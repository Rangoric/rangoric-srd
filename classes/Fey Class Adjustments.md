# Fey Class Adjustments

All classes are available to the fey races, and many choose to follow as many as three or four different dedications over the course of their extended lives.

Some fey discover that the magic of the fey realm unlocks passageways between arcane and divine magic and reveals a few arcane schools and divine domains unknown by mortal casters. As an optional rule, players with [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^fey-characters|fey characters]] who want something slightly different from the core classes can take their characters down these alternate paths of class abilities. Since some of the techniques used by these spells are quite foreign to the caster, they sometimes must be learned at a higher level (as noted below).

The following adjustments can be made, with the GM’s approval, to add some “fey flavor” to a campaign:

**[[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|Bard]]**: Extend the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bard Spell List|spell list]] to include the 1st- through 6th-level spells of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain|Earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain|Water]] domains.

**[[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|Cleric]]**: Allow additional spell access through the choice of one free domain from the following: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Enchantment Domain|Enchantment]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Illusion Domain|Illusion]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Magic Domain|Magic]].

**[[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|Druid]]**: Extend the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Druid Spell List|spell list]] by adding the divination and illusion schools.

**[[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|Paladin]]**: Extend the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Paladin Spell List|spell list]] to include the 1st- through 4th-level spells of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Good Domain|Good]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Luck Domain|Luck]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain|Strength]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War]] domains.

**[[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|Ranger]]**: Extend the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ranger Spell List|spell list]] to include the 1st- through 4th-level spells of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain|Earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain|Water]] domains.

**[[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]]**: Extend the spell list to include the spells of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|Plant]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Sun Domain|Sun]] domains. Divine spells are learned at one level higher than listed.

**[[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|Wizard]]**: Extend the spell list to include the spells of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Protection Domain|Protection]] domains. Divine spells are learned at one level higher than listed.

The two domains, Enchantment and Illusion, are open to any cleric (their deities are “any”), not only those taking them through this adjustment.
