## Character Classes

### Nymph (Racial Class)

**Race**: only [[rangoric-srd/races/Nymph|nymphs]] can take levels in this class. A nymph who takes it has level adjustment +1 (see the race).

**Racial classes**: as a rule of thumb, any monster can be played as a PC if the appropriate level adjustment can be determined (see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monsters as Races|Monsters as Races]]). That isn’t the only approach: a monster can also be broken down as if it were a class or prestige class, without the special abilities it gained through Hit Dice advancement, so that a character grows into the full monster level by level. The nymph racial class is built that way from the monster [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Nymph|nymph]]. ^racial-class

**Alignment:** Always chaotic good.

**Hit Die:** d6. The Hit Dice column gives the nymph’s total Hit Dice: she gains her Hit Dice at the levels where the column changes (1d6 at 1st level, 9d6 by 20th).

**Class Skills:** Faeries gives none for the racial class, so it has the skills of the monster [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Nymph|nymph]]: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]].

**Skill Points at Each Level:** 6 + Int modifier, as the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fey|fey type]].

**Proficiencies:** Faeries gives none for the racial class.

###### Class Features

**Spellcasting:** A nymph casts divine spells as a druid of the level the table shows (spellcasting ability only), from the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]]’s spell list, with the spells per day in the table. ^nymph-spellcasting

**Blinding Beauty (Su):** At 4th level, a nymph gains the blinding beauty of the monster [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Nymph|nymph]]. ^nymph-blinding-beauty

**Dimension Door (Sp):** At 11th level, a nymph can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]* once per day. ^nymph-dimension-door

**Unearthly Beauty (Su):** At 15th level, a nymph’s beauty can kill: a person who sees it and fails a DC 17 Will save dies, an effect that resembles *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]]*. She can use it once per day, for up to 10 minutes. ^nymph-unearthly-beauty

##### How the Nymph Was Broken Down

Faeries’ worked example, for breaking down other monsters the same way.

Based on the theory that what the monster entry describes is a “full-level” nymph, we must work backwards and reduce the abilities to what a 1<sup>st</sup> level nymph might be like.

- **Physical Abilities:** Looking at the nymph, we see that there are no bonuses to attack other than the +1 gained from Strength. Unfortunately, the nymph doesn’t get a better base attack as she goes up in level. No ability adjustments are necessary.

- **Saving Throws:** These appear to be “weak” as well. We can use the druid as a comparison, and see that the best fit is a 12<sup>th</sup> level druid. Comparing the saving throw progression of a wizard, which has a “weaker” set of saving throws, we get a closer fit to the numbers we need. Since the best fit is also at 12<sup>th</sup> level, we’ll note the nymph’s in the 12<sup>th</sup> level slot. Fill in the progression with the numbers from the Reflex and Will save columns. Since the other numbers are evenly distributed across the levels, we’ll do the same for the Fortitude save.

- **Blinding beauty:** The nymph has a permanent effect that eternally blinds a person if they fail a DC 15 Fortitude save. This is similar to the 3<sup>rd</sup> level cleric spell *blindness/deafness*. Since clerics and druids are able to cast 3<sup>rd</sup> level spells at 5<sup>th</sup> level and nymphs have druidic abilities, we’ll temporarily put this in the 5<sup>th</sup> level slot.

- **Unearthly beauty:** The nymph’s beauty kills a person if they fail a DC 17 Will save and can be used every 10 minutes. In its ultimate effect, this resembles *finger of death*. Since *finger of death* is an 8<sup>th</sup> level spell that druids get at 15<sup>th</sup> level, we’ll put this temporarily in the 15<sup>th</sup> level slot.

- **Spellcasting:** The nymph can cast spells like a 7<sup>th</sup> level druid, and the DC to resist them is normal. For now, let’s place this ability in the 7<sup>th</sup> level slot. Let’s fill out the spells per day while we’re there. We may need to shift it lower because we don’t get the base attack bonus or same saves as a druid of the same level.

- ***Dimension door:*** This ability is a 4<sup>th</sup> level spell. This would be a second 4<sup>th</sup> level spell. So, following the logic we used above, we’ll put this in the 8<sup>th</sup> level slot.

- **Character Advancement:** To make the level advancement more gradual, let’s go back and adjust the spell progression and abilities while sprinkling in the additional hit dice beyond the one gained at 1<sup>st</sup> level. Try to have only one ability or change in spells for each level between 1<sup>st</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> level.

With the exception of Unearthly Beauty, we have a complete nymph at 12<sup>th</sup> level. Compared to the known ECL for this monster (+12), we’re just about right.

Advancing the Nymph Racial Class table further, we can increase the levels in Nymph just as we would in any other class, increasing access to spells, adding to the saving throw progression, and adding the rest of the Hit Dice specified in the “Advancement” of the creature.
