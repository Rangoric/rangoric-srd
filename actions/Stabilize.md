# Stabilize

A [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] action: make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check.

**DC**: 15.

You usually do this to save a dying character. If a character has negative hit points and is losing hit points (at the rate of 1 per round, 1 per hour, or 1 per day), you can make him or her stable. A stable character regains no hit points but stops losing them.

**Try Again**: Yes, as long as the character is still alive.
