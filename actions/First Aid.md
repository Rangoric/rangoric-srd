# First Aid

The First Aid actions (tag *first-aid*) keep people alive and help them recover. Either [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] can make the check, so a ranger and a doctor can both stop someone bleeding out. Fieldcraft checks use Wisdom and Medicine checks use Intelligence.

| Action | Takes | DC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[rangoric-srd/actions/Stabilize\|Stabilize]] | Standard action | 15 |
| [[rangoric-srd/actions/Long-Term Care\|Long-Term Care]] | 8 hours of light activity | 15 |
| [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Wound\|Treat Wound]] | Standard action (10 minutes for spike growth or spike stones) | 15 or the spell’s save DC |
| [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Poison\|Treat Poison]] | Standard action | The poison’s save DC |
| [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Disease\|Treat Disease]] | 10 minutes | The disease’s save DC |
| [[rangoric-srd/actions/Quicken Recovery\|Quicken Recovery]] | 1 hour | 50 |
| [[rangoric-srd/actions/Perfect Recovery\|Perfect Recovery]] | 1 hour | 100 |

**Try Again**: Varies. Generally, you can’t try again without proof that the first check failed. You can always try again to stabilize someone who is still alive.

**Tools**: A [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services|healer’s kit]] gives a +2 circumstance bonus on First Aid checks.

Medicine has more uses of its own, such as restoring hit points and surgery; see the skill.
