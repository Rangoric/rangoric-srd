# Perfect Recovery

A [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] action: make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check.

**DC**: 100.

The patient regains hit points in a single hour as if you had provided [[rangoric-srd/actions/Long-Term Care|long-term care]] for a full week (2 or 3 hit points per level per day, based on activity). You can use perfect recovery on up to six patients at a time. No character’s recovery can be perfected more than once per day, and perfect recovery and quicken recovery can’t both be used on the same patient in the same day, even by different healers.
