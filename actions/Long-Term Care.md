# Long-Term Care

A [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] action: make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check.

**DC**: 15.

Providing long-term care means treating a wounded person for a day or more. If your check succeeds, the patient recovers hit points or ability score points (lost to ability damage) at twice the normal rate: 2 hit points per level for a full 8 hours of rest in a day, or 4 hit points per level for each full day of complete rest; 2 ability score points for a full 8 hours of rest in a day, or 4 ability score points for each full day of complete rest.

You can tend as many as six patients at a time. You need a few items and supplies (bandages, salves, and so on) that are easy to come by in settled lands. Giving long-term care counts as light activity for the healer. You cannot give long-term care to yourself.
