# Treat Deadly Wounds

A [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] action: make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check.

**DC**: 20.

You restore hit points to a damaged creature: 1 hit point per level of the creature. If you exceed the DC by 5 or more, add your Wisdom modifier (if positive) to this amount. A creature can benefit from having its deadly wounds treated only within 24 hours of being injured, and never more than once per day.

You must use up two uses of a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services|healer’s kit]]. You take a –2 penalty on the check for each use you lack.

The Healing unlocks of Fieldcraft and Medicine improve this action.
