# Quicken Recovery

A [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] action: make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check.

**DC**: 50.

The patient regains hit points in a single hour as if you had provided [[rangoric-srd/actions/Long-Term Care|long-term care]] for a full day (2 or 3 hit points per level, based on activity). You can quicken the recovery of up to six patients at a time. No character’s recovery can be quickened more than once per day, even by different healers.
