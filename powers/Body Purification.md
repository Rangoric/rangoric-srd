# Body Purification

Psychometabolism (Healing)  
Level: Psion/wilder 3, psychic warrior 2  
Display: Auditory and material  
Manifesting Time: 1 round  
Range: Personal  
Target: You  
Duration: Instantaneous  
Power Points: Psion/wilder 5, psychic warrior 3  

You restore up to 2 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]] to a single ability score. You cannot use body purification to heal [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|ability drain]].

Augment: For every additional power point you spend, this power heals 1 additional point of damage to the same ability score.
