# Retrieve

Psychoportation (Teleportation)  
**Level:** Psion/wilder 6  
**Display:** Visual  
**Manifesting Time:** 1 standard action  
**Range:** Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./ level)  
**Target:** One object you can hold or carry in one hand, weighing up to 10 lb./level  
**Duration:** Instantaneous  
**Defense:** Will; see text  
**Power Resistance:** No  
**Power Points:** 11

You automatically teleport an item you can see within range directly to your hand. If the object is in the possession of an opponent, it comes to your hand if the power hits your opponent’s Will.

**Augment:** For every additional power point you spend, the weight limit of the target increases by 10 pounds.
