# Truevenom

Psychometabolism  
**Level:** Psychic warrior 4  
**Display:** Material; see text  
**Manifesting Time:** 1 swift action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Target:** You  
**Duration:** 1 min./level or until discharged  
**Defense:** None and Fortitude; see text  
**Power Points:** 7

If you have a claw attack (either from an actual natural weapon or from an effect such as [[rangoric-srd/powers/Claws of the Beast|claws of the beast]]), you can use this power to produce a horrible poison that coats one of your claws. On your next successful melee attack with the claw during the power’s duration, the target is exposed to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]]: injury; _attack_ vs Fortitude (the power’s attack bonus); _frequency_ 1/minute for 2 minutes; _effect_ 1d8 Con [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]].
