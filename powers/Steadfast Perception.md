# Steadfast Perception

Clairsentience  
**Level:** Psychic warrior 4  
**Display:** Visual  
**Manifesting Time:** 1 standard action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Target:** You  
**Duration:** 10 min./level (D)  
**Power Points:** 7  

Your vision cannot be distracted or misled, granting you immunity to all figments and glamers (such as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]). Moreover, your [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks receive a +6 enhancement bonus for the duration of this power. This power also grants you another saving throw against someone using [[rangoric-srd/powers/False Sensory Input|false sensory input]] on you, but you must realize that that power has been used in order to know enough to manifest steadfast perception.
