# Teleport Trigger

Psychoportation (Teleportation)  
**Level:** Nomad 5  
**Display:** Material  
**Manifesting Time:** 1 standard action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Target:** You  
**Duration:** 1 hour/level (D)  
**Power Points:** 9  

You specify a situation that triggers your automatic manifestation of a [[rangoric-srd/powers/Teleport, Psionic|psionic teleport]], taking you to a predetermined location. You must know the psionic teleport power and have sufficient power points to manifest it when the specified situation occurs.

The teleport trigger goes off on the initiative count immediately after the specified situation occurs, even if you are flat-footed or you have already taken your turn in the current round. The specified situation can be described in general terms or specific terms.
