# Dismiss Ectoplasm

Metacreativity  
Level: Psion/wilder 3  
Display: Auditory and visual  
Manifesting Time: 1 standard action  
Range: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./ level)  
Area: 30-ft.-radius burst  
Duration: Instantaneous  
Defense: Will; see text  
Power Resistance: No  
Power Points: 5

You dismiss creatures, objects, or effects composed of ectoplasm, such as astral constructs or the [[rangoric-srd/powers/Ectoplasmic Cocoon|ectoplasmic cocoon]] power, or that were formerly composed of ectoplasm, such as items created by metacreativity (creation) powers.

An ectoplasmic creature the power hits dissipates into so much constituent ectoplasm, which evaporates immediately.

A creature under the effect of the ectoplasmic form power that this power hits is either destroyed out right or physically shifted to a random location on the Astral Plane (50% chance for either result).

Other ongoing powers that create ectoplasmic objects or effects, such as ectoplasmic cocoon, are dismissed if you succeed on a manifester level check (1d20 + your manifester level, maximum +10) against a DC of 11 + the power’s manifester level.
