## Prestige Classes

### Rune Caster

Rune casters (also known as rune mages) are the true masters of the art of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]]. While they begin their studies in the more mundane forms of arcane and divine magic, most rune casters make the study of runes their life’s work after they learn the basics. The runes, as tools of the gods and symbols of their power, hold a far deeper meaning than simply being a conduit for magical energies. They offer a mixture of philosophy, guidance, and information about the structure and function of the universe. To rune casters, studying dusty tomes in dark libraries pales in comparison.

Beginning rune casters learn their craft from the solitary hermits who make up the upper echelons of the rune caster hierarchy. These hermits have only one or two students at a time and spend years forcing their students to perform menial tasks like cleaning and caring for the livestock. Suffering is an important part of rune magic, so rune casters wish to know their students can take some punishment before imparting real knowledge to them.

Rune casters spend their time in pursuit of complete comprehension of the runes, but this study takes many forms. Some sit on high mountaintops and watch the fury of nature about them, trying to see how each rune connects itself to the world. Others involve themselves in great quests using the magical properties of the runes to better understand them. In either case, while worldly goods are sometimes important to a rune caster, they mean nothing before the value of knowledge.

Sorcerers and druids are the most likely to take up the study of runes because their magical gifts are of the same primal nature as rune magic. Sorcerers do not have to study books for their power but instead master their own internal powers while druids are already deeply involved in the study of nature. Wizards and clerics also become rune casters but do not take to it like sorcerers or druids. Bards rarely study runes because it detracts from their other pursuits, but it is possible for bards to become rune casters. Other classes cannot become rune casters without first multiclassing to sorcerer, bard, wizard, cleric, or druid.

Rune casters of all alignments can be found, but most lean in some fashion toward the neutral alignments. The majority of rune casters are human, yet a sizable number of dwarves can be found. Despite not being fond of arcane magic, dwarves find the permanent and steady nature of runes attractive and employ them in magically enhancing items. Elves and gnomes occasionally practice rune magic, while halfling and half-orc rune casters are almost unheard of.

**Hit Die**: d6.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a rune caster, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Spellcasting**: Ability to cast 2nd-level arcane spells and 2nd-level divine spells.

**Ability**: Con 12.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 1 rank.

**Base Will Save**: +5.

###### Class Skills

The rune caster’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the rune caster prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: The rune caster is proficient with simple weapons but not armor or shields. Armor of any type interferes with a rune caster’s arcane gestures, which can cause his spells to fail.

**Rune Spells (Ex)**: Rune casters cast spells using runes to focus magical energies ([[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#Casting Rune Spells|casting rune spells]]). These spells are a mixture of the arcane and divine. A rune caster’s spells are limited to those made available by runes he is currently attuned to. A rune caster may only be attuned to a number of runes equal to his level in the attune rune class ability plus his Constitution bonus. Each rune has a list of ten spells, one of each level, that are available to the rune caster who is attuned to the rune ([[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]). The rune caster may use his rune spell slots ([[#^table-rune-spells-per-day|Table: Rune Spells per Day]]) to cast any spells made available to him by his attuned runes. ^rune-caster-rune-spells

Rune casters begin with the number of rune spell slots listed for a 1st-level rune caster. Wizards, bards, and clerics keep their original spellcasting abilities and may use them normally. Druids and sorcerers may keep their original spellcasting abilities if they choose, or they can choose to transfer their caster levels to rune magic. When sorcerers and druids take their first level in rune caster they may choose to reduce their caster level in their original classes and add them to their caster level in rune caster. This does not affect the class abilities, Hit Dice, saving throws or attack bonuses of the character, merely their effective caster level and number of spell slots. This allows sorcerers and druids to become far more effective rune casters than other classes. Each level in rune caster the character gains increases his rune caster caster level by one, increasing the number of available spell slots.

*Example*: A 4th-level sorcerer and 3rd-level druid has just become a 1st-level rune caster. He has five 0-level and three 1st-level rune spell slots. He also has six 0-level, six 1st-level and three 2nd-level sorcerer spell slots, and four 0-level, two 1st-level and one 2nd-level druid spell slots. He chooses to change two of his sorcerer caster levels and one of his druid caster levels over to rune magic. He now has the spell slots of a 4th-level rune caster, 2nd-level sorcerer, and 2nd-level druid, and the class abilities, hit points, attack bonus and saving throws of a 1st-level rune caster, 4th-level sorcerer and 3rd-level druid. When he goes up another level he is a 2nd-level rune caster, 4th-level sorcerer and 3rd-level druid but has the spell slots of a 5th-level rune caster, 2nd-level sorcerer and 2nd-level druid.

A rune caster casts spells spontaneously, in the same fashion a bard or sorcerer does, but only those spells available to the runes the rune caster is attuned to. The rune caster receives bonus rune spells based on his Wisdom, Charisma, or Intelligence, whichever is higher. So, a rune caster with an 18 Wisdom would have a bonus 1st-, 2nd-, 3rd-, and 4th-level spell.

A rune caster may cast a lower-level spell using a higher-level slot if desired. For purposes of all [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] and saving throws, the spell level is considered that of the lower-level spell, but the higher-level spell slot is used.

To cast a spell from an attuned rune, a rune caster must have an Intelligence or Charisma (whichever was the primary attribute of the rune caster’s arcane magic class) and Wisdom equal to 10 + the spell’s level. The Difficulty Class for saving throws against rune caster spells is 10 + the spell’s level + the Intelligence, Charisma, or Wisdom modifier of the rune caster, whichever is higher.

If a rune caster wishes to use a metamagic feat on one of his rune spells and the spell takes 1 standard action to cast, he must instead spend a full-round action casting the spell. If the spell requires more than a standard action, then an extra full-round action is added to the casting time for the spell.

**Runecasting (Ex)**: At 1st level, in addition to using runes to work magic, rune casters may use the runes to perform a type of divination called runecasting. Runecasting does not actually predict the future but allows the rune caster to look back on what he has done, see what he is doing, and what path he is on. The rune caster may then choose to try to alter this path. It does not give a glimpse of a certain, unchangeable future. Instead, it helps the rune caster puzzle out what is likely to happen on his own, allowing him to take appropriate actions. ^rune-caster-runecasting

Casting runestones takes at least ten minutes of uninterrupted concentration. The rune caster must sit comfortably and concentrate for a few moments, after which he tosses all of his runestones to the ground, reading the placement and orientation of the runes. The rune caster then makes a class level check (d20 + his rune caster level) against DC 10. If successful, the rune caster has gleaned some guidance from the runes. The rune caster gains a +1 insight bonus on (1) all skill checks, (2) all attack rolls, or (3) all saving throws for a 24-hour period at the discretion of the rune caster. Once chosen, the bonus cannot be switched. The GM should feel free to impart some real wisdom on the rune caster if he feels the future holds something important.

Runecastings may be performed for others as well, but they do not receive the inspiration bonus unless the GM feels they have a suitable faith in the runes (anyone with the rune caster prestige class or who has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat qualifies).

**Create Runestone (Ex)**: At 1st level, a rune caster learns to make runestones. Rune casters (except the highest levels) cannot work their magic without runestones. Runestones are small stones, about the size of a coin, inscribed with a single rune on one side. They are made from smooth stone but may be made from metal, wood, or even bone. A rune caster may only attune to runes for which he has an appropriate runestone (or runestaff, see below). Because of this, making runestones is one of the first things a rune caster learns. Rune casters are given a set of runestones by their instructor, but soon prefer using runestones made with their own hands. When using a runestone made by someone else, rune casters take a –1 penalty on attunement checks. ^rune-caster-create-runestone

Creating a runestone requires a complete day of work during which the rune caster may cast no spells and undertake no strenuous activity or travel. The process takes eight hours, during which the rune caster meditates and carefully carves the rune. Runestones may be made from almost any material, but stone is the most common. At the end of eight hours, the rune caster must make a Lore (runes) check (DC 10). If successful, the runestone is properly made. The rune caster then spends 100 XP to bind it to him. If the check fails, the rune caster starts again another day and the material used is destroyed.

Runestones are made from materials that have some link to the rune to be inscribed, such as a Hagalaz rune inscribed on a piece of wood from a tree struck by lightning. Such runes are more powerful than normal runestones. They can add anywhere from a +1 to a +5 bonus on attunement checks when using that runestone, depending on the power of the connection the material has to the rune. Creating such runestones requires more XP, costing 500 XP per point of bonus.

**Attune Rune (Su)**: A rune caster may know many runes but can only use a certain number at a time. Each level of attune rune (I at 1st level, II at 5th, III at 10th, IV at 15th) allows the rune caster to attune to one rune, enabling him to use the powers of that rune ([[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#Casting Rune Spells|attuning]]). A rune caster may only attune to a number of runes equal to his level in attune rune plus his Constitution bonus. ^rune-caster-attune-rune

**Inscribe Rune (Ex)**: At 3rd level a rune caster is able to inscribe runes, [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#Inscribing Runes|as described in rune magic]]. ^rune-caster-inscribe-rune

**Draw Rune (Sp)**: Once a rune caster reaches 8th level he is no longer tied down by needing runestones. If the rune caster does not have the correct type of runestone on hand, he simply draws the rune on any available surface. The rune must be visible; a rune caster cannot simply run his fingers over a surface. This takes a standard action. The rune caster then attunes the rune (as long as he is within ten feet of the drawn rune and the rune remains intact). Any marring of the drawn rune destroys it; any creature within 5 feet of a rune may take a standard action to render it useless. ^rune-caster-draw-rune

**Create Runestaff (Ex)**: At 11th level, a rune caster can create runestaves, enabling him to attune to runes in greater numbers. A runestaff is one foot long for each rune it carries. Creating a runestaff is a long and difficult process that only the most powerful rune casters dare attempt. ^rune-caster-create-runestaff

The staff itself must be made out of a very stout substance, such as oak, metal, or stone. Most runestaves are wood. Once a suitable staff is obtained, the rune caster must spend one day per rune inscribing each rune carefully on the staff. At the end of each day a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (runes)]] check must be made at DC 25 plus the number of runes on the staff. If successful, the rune caster spends 1,000 XP to empower the rune and continues to the next. If the roll fails, the staff is ruined and must be started over completely; any XP spent on the process thus far is lost.

If all the runes are inscribed correctly, the staff is enchanted over the course of five days, during which the rune caster must fast. At the end of this time, the rune caster makes a Lore (runes) check (DC 25 + the number of runes on the runestaff) and a Fortitude save (DC 15 + the number of runes on the staff). If both checks are successful, the runestaff is complete. If either check fails, the rune caster starts again from scratch and any XP or material costs are lost.

Once created, a runestaff allows the rune caster who constructed it to attune to all the runes on the runestaff by simply attuning to the staff once, which also only counts as a single rune in terms of how many runes the rune caster may attune to. So, a runestaff with four runes on it allows the rune caster attuned to it to use any of the four runes on the runestaff but it only counts as being attuned to a single rune. A rune caster may only attune to one runestaff at a time.

If a rune caster finds a runestaff built by another rune caster, he may attune himself to it by spending one full day in meditation and 500 XP per rune on the staff. Until then, the rune caster cannot use the staff.

If used in combat, runestaves with 1–3 runes count as clubs and those with 4–5 runes count as quarterstaffs ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons|weapons]]). A finished runestaff is treated as having an enhancement bonus equal to the number of runes inscribed upon it when determining if the runestaff breaks. Other runes may be added to a runestaff later, if desired, up to a maximum of 5 runes. The process of adding runes to a staff is the same as above.

**Rune Mastery (Su)**: At 12th level, the rune caster learns to find the power of the runes inside himself, allowing him to permanently attune himself to a rune. This is done with a rune that in some way embodies the beliefs of the rune caster; warriors bond to the Thurisaz or Uruz rune while a nomadic rune caster may master the Raidho rune. In any case, the rune caster brings the power of the rune into himself, forever altering his relationship with the world. ^rune-caster-rune-mastery

Mastering a rune requires permanently inscribing the rune on the surface of the rune caster’s body. This is done with tattoos or scarring. Once this is accomplished, the rune caster retires to an area to which his chosen rune is linked (such as a glacier or snow-capped mountain for the Isa rune). The rune caster invokes his chosen rune, pulling its power into himself. The rune caster then meditates and prays for five days. Each day, he must make a Fortitude save (DC 20 + the number of previous days spent in meditation) to withstand the energy of the rune seeping into him. Failure causes a number of points of damage equal to the amount by which the rune caster failed his Fortitude save. After making five successful saves in a row, the rune caster is permanently linked with the rune and gains mastery of it. The rune caster is permanently attuned to this rune, which does not count against his normal allotment of attuned runes.

Rune mastery II (15th level) allows the rune caster to forge a permanent bond with a second rune if desired, using the process described above.
