## Prestige Classes

### Fae-walker

Mortal scholars believe the first fae-walkers were [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadowdancers]] who found an alternative to their normal shadow-travel. Fae-walkers skillfully navigate the borderland between the world and the fey realm. They lurk in the periphery of social life, never completely merging with, nor being accepted by, the mainstream of either culture. Those fey that lurk in shadow welcome the fae-walker, but in the end, they always seek to exploit the fae-walker for their own purposes.

Fae-walkers prefer to work alone, or at least in a capacity where a (perhaps necessary) prolonged absence will be acceptable. Some use their abilities to steal and act as thieves, while others use their higher-level abilities to handle trade between the mortal world and the fey realm.

Rogues, bards, and monks make excellent fae-walkers and find their abilities advantageous in combat. Barbarians and rangers, in addition to the abilities gained, enjoy the mystical relationship with the fey realm. Wizards, sorcerers, clerics, and druids find the abilities useful when trying to safely cast spells or escape melee combat. Despite the use of the borderland when stepping sideways and fae-walking, one is just as likely to find a good or neutral fae-walker as an evil one.

**Hit Die**: d8.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a fae-walker (abbreviated Fwk), a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any chaotic.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +4.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (fey)]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 5 ranks.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]].

###### Class Skills

The fae-walker’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] (Dex), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] (Str), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] (Dex), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] (Dex), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 6 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the fae-walker prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Fae-walkers are proficient with all simple weapons and light armor, but not shields. ^fae-walker-weapon-and-armor-proficiency

**Evasion (Ex)**: At 1st level, a fae-walker gains evasion. If exposed to any effect that normally allows a Reflex saving throw for half damage (such as a fireball), he takes no damage with a successful saving throw. Evasion can only be used if the fae-walker is wearing light armor or no armor. ^fae-walker-evasion

**Step Sideways (Su)**: At 1st level, the fae-walker gains the supernatural ability to step sideways into the borderland between the world and the fey realm. This ability can be used if no other move action has been taken. To onlookers, the fae-walker disappears as if stepping through a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]*. While in the borderland, the fae-walker cannot move other than to step sideways back into the mortal world. He can choose to wait there for a number of rounds equal to his fae-walker level before stepping sideways back to the mortal world. If this duration lapses, the fae-walker risks being lost in the borderland (25% cumulative chance per round beyond the duration). Using this ability does not provoke an attack of opportunity. ^fae-walker-step-sideways

At 5th level, he can choose to wait in the borderland for a number of minutes equal to his fae-walker level before stepping back to the mortal world. If this duration lapses, he risks being lost there (with the dangers noted above).

At 7th level, the fae-walker can choose to return to the mortal world, or he can now move and instead try his luck with the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Paths|fey path]] that appears before him in the fey realm. A fae-walker chooses a fey path willingly by moving while in the borderland, or accidentally by not stepping back to the mortal world in time. Once the path is chosen, he cannot step sideways back into the mortal world, but must instead find another way back.

**Low-Light Vision (Su)**: At 2nd level, a fae-walker permanently gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], as if he were a fey. If the character already has low-light vision, this ability has no effect. ^fae-walker-low-light-vision

**Uncanny Dodge (Ex)**: At 2nd level and above, the fae-walker retains his Dexterity bonus to AC (if any) if caught flat-footed or struck by an invisible attacker. ^fae-walker-uncanny-dodge

At 5th level, most characters can no longer flank the fae-walker. Only a rogue at least four levels higher than the fae-walker can still flank him.

At 10th level, the fae-walker receives a +1 bonus on Reflex saves made to avoid traps and a +1 dodge bonus to AC against attacks made by traps.

If the fae-walker already has uncanny dodge abilities (such as the rogue’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]]) before gaining them in this prestige class, the character simply advances to the next level of the ability when gained here.

**Slippery Mind (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the fae-walker gains [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slippery Mind (Rogue Talent)|slippery mind]], as the rogue’s advanced talent. ^fae-walker-slippery-mind

**Fae-walk (Sp)**: The fae-walker gains the ability to travel from one location to another through the borderland, as by means of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]* spell. At 4th level, the fae-walker can walk up to a total of 15 feet each day in this way, although this may be a single walk of 15 feet or one walk of 10 feet and another of a mere 5 feet. Every two levels thereafter, the distance a fae-walker can walk each day doubles (30 feet at 6th level, 60 feet at 8th level, and 120 feet at 10th level). This amount can be split among many walks, but each one, no matter how small, counts as a 10-foot increment. (A 6th-level fae-walker who walks 22 feet cannot use this ability again until the next day.) ^fae-walker-fae-walk

**Defensive Jaunt (Su)**: Starting at 6th level, the fae-walker can use his step sideways ability to evade a potentially lethal blow or to take less damage from it. Once per day, when a fae-walker would be reduced to 0 hit points or fewer in combat (from a weapon or other blow, not a spell or special ability), the fae-walker can attempt to jaunt away the damage. He makes a Reflex saving throw (DC = damage dealt) and, if successful, takes only half damage from the blow. He must be aware of the attack and able to react to it to execute his defensive jaunt. If the situation denies him any Dexterity bonus to AC (whether he has one or not), he can’t attempt the defensive jaunt. ^fae-walker-defensive-jaunt

**Improved Evasion (Ex)**: This extraordinary ability, gained at 9th level, works like evasion (see above). The fae-walker takes no damage at all on successful saving throws against attacks that allow a Reflex saving throw for half damage (breath weapon, fireball, and so on). What’s more, he takes only half damage even if he fails his saving throw, since the fae-walker’s reflexes allow him to get out of harm’s way with incredible speed. ^fae-walker-improved-evasion

**Stray (Su)**: At 10th level, a fae-walker gains the supernatural ability to stray from a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Paths|fey path]] for up to 10 rounds before the effects of straying from the path affect him and he risks becoming lost in the wilds of the fey realm. ^fae-walker-stray
