## Prestige Classes

### Mage of the Circle

Over the centuries, fey spellcasters deigned to teach others their unorthodox magics, and mages of the circle became the inheritors of arcane powers bent toward the talents of the fey. The mage of the circle has learned techniques enabling him to tap into the magical power of fey circles. This arcane ability is coveted by wizards, since it offers them limited access to the spellcasting flexibility that only sorcerers normally have. Characters who become mages of the circle thoroughly embrace the magical powers and techniques of the fey. They seek out arcane lore and mystical materials to aid them in the use of their newfound abilities.

**Hit Die**: d4.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a mage of the circle (abbreviated Moc), a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (fey)]] 5 ranks.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fey Magic|Fey Magic]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Scribe Scroll|Scribe Scroll]].

**Spells**: Able to prepare and cast arcane spells of 3rd level or higher.

###### Class Skills

The mage of the circle’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (Int), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the mage of the circle prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: The mage of the circle gains no armor or weapon proficiencies beyond those he already possesses. ^mage-of-the-circle-weapon-and-armor-proficiency

**Spells per Day**: A mage of the circle continues to study magic and gains new spells per day as if he had gone up a level in a previous spellcasting class. If the character is multiclassed, apply the spellcasting level to it, but do not adjust hit points or any other factors tied to levels. ^mage-of-the-circle-spells-per-day

**Encircle**: The mage of the circle has tapped into the power of circle magic, which allows him to inscribe any spell he knows into a complex circle diagram on a scroll only he can use. Once a spell is thus encircled, the scroll can only be used to cast spells in this manner; it cannot be used as a standard spell scroll. ^mage-of-the-circle-encircle

At 1st level, a mage of the circle can take one prepared spell slot and allocate three “encircled” spells of that level for potential use in that slot. A mage of the circle can choose one of these three encircled spells and cast it as if he had normally prepared that particular spell. To cast an encircled spell, the mage of the circle only needs to utter the verbal component of the spell. Once one of these three spells is cast, the encircled spell slot is exhausted for the day.

When preparing spells for the day, only one spell slot per spell level may be used to prepare three encircled spells. Spells prepared using this ability use up a spell slot of the spell’s normal level, or the modified level if prepared using metamagic feats. Any spells used as encircled spells can still be prepared as normal in the remaining spell slots.

The encircling process can be done for each of the spell levels the caster knows or can use in accord with the encircle abilities:

- *Encircle I* (1st level) allows the mage of the circle to inscribe spells of 1st to 3rd level.
- *Encircle II* (4th level) allows spells of 4th to 6th level.
- *Encircle III* (7th level) allows spells of 7th to 9th level.

All scrolls created for this use cost double the cost of a standard scroll (materials, XP, and so on), but the spells are not expended and the scrolls are not destroyed when casting the spells they contain.

A special encircled scroll case (below) must be constructed for each spell level before spells of that level can be cast in this manner. The encircled scrolls containing the desired spells need to be placed into the case for use that day.

**Encircled Scroll Case**: These scroll cases are especially made by mages of the circle to contain their prepared and encircled spells. Appearing as elaborate oversized scroll tubes, these scroll cases allow a mage of the circle to cast the spells contained within them as part of his standard repertoire. These scroll cases frequently take on the shape of three or four standard scroll tubes bound together and adorned with precious metals, materials, and gemstones. Unlike the scrolls they contain (which are keyed to individual casters only), any mage of the circle can use these cases. ^mage-of-the-circle-encircled-scroll-case

CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]*; Price 2,000 gp; Weight —.

**Sand Circles**: At 2nd level, mages of the circle can use [[rangoric-srd/rules/Faercana#^magic-sand|magic sand]] to bolster their encircled spells. The use of magic sand adds +1 to each die rolled for the spell effect, and it also inserts a random effect (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Faercana#^table-magic-sand-wild-characteristics|Table: Magic Sand Wild Characteristics]]). ^mage-of-the-circle-sand-circles

This ability does not give the caster magic sand; the mage of the circle must find it, or harvest it in the fey realm with the *harvest magic sand* spell (not in this SRD yet), which this ability requires him to know but does not grant. This ability duplicates but does not stack with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fey Magic|Fey Magic]] feat.

**Extra Slot**: At 5th level, and again at 9th, the mage of the circle gains the ability to cast one more spell beyond his normal daily allotment. This extra slot can be used for any spell level except the highest the mage of the circle can cast. Once selected, this extra slot does not change in spell level. This extra slot can be used to gain additional encircled spells as long as additional encircled scrolls and scroll cases are crafted. This is the only method by which a spell level may be duplicated for the use of more encircled spells. ^mage-of-the-circle-extra-slot

**Circle Mastery**: At 10th level, a mage of the circle surpasses the spell level restrictions of the encircle ability, and adds a fourth spell to all encircled slots. ^mage-of-the-circle-circle-mastery
