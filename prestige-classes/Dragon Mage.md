## Prestige Classes

### Dragon Mage

The only way for characters to make use of [[rangoric-srd/rules/dragon-magic/Dragon Magic|dragon magic]] is to enter the dragon mage prestige class. While the [[rangoric-srd/rules/dragon-magic/Dragon Magic#The Trovebond|trovebond]] can be performed anytime the character is able to perform the ritual, it does little good until the character takes his first level of dragon mage. Then, and only then, are the realms of dragon magic open for the character.

Unlike many other prestige classes, the benefits gained from advancing as a dragon mage are variable. In the advancement table of each aspect, the character only gains the hit points, abilities, spells, saves, and base attack bonus appropriate to the current attunement of his trove. This allows dragon mages to determine how they grow in power and offers them a great many options.

**Hit Die**: By the trove’s aspect: [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Breath)|breath]] d4, [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Scales)|scales]] d10, [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)|talons]] d8, [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Wings)|wings]] d6.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a dragon mage, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] 10 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 7 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (dragons)]] 7 ranks.

**Spellcasting**: Ability to cast 1st-level spells.

**Special**: The character must have undergone the [[rangoric-srd/rules/dragon-magic/Dragon Magic#The Trovebond|trovebond ritual]], and have the required amount of treasure in his trove, as shown on the table for its aspect ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Breath)#^table-the-dragon-mage-breath|breath]], [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Scales)#^table-the-dragon-mage-scales|scales]], [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)#^table-the-dragon-mage-talon|talon]], [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Wings)#^table-the-dragon-mage-wings|wings]]).

###### Class Skills

The dragon mage’s class skills are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] (Int) and the same as the class skills of his highest-level non-prestige character class. If there is a tie among classes, the dragon mage must choose one for purposes of skills.

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 3 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the dragon mage prestige class. Each one is marked with the aspect and level it comes with; a dragon mage has only those of his trove’s current aspect, which are in that aspect’s note ([[rangoric-srd/rules/dragon-magic/Dragon Magic#Attuning the Trove to a Different Aspect|attuning the trove]]).

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Dragon mages gain no additional proficiency with any armor, but any dragon mage with a trove currently attuned to the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Scales)|scales]] aspect suffers no [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Armor|arcane spell failure]] for wearing armor. If the trove’s attunement changes to another aspect at any time, this ability is lost until the trove is reattuned to the scales aspect. ^dragon-mage-weapon-and-armor-proficiency

Dragon mages with a trove currently attuned to the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)|talons]] aspect gain proficiency with all simple and martial weapons. If the trove’s attunement changes to another aspect, at any time, this proficiency is lost until the trove is reattuned to the talons aspect.

**Spell Ability**: When a level of dragon mage is gained, the character receives new spells per day as if he had also gained a level of the divine or arcane (as noted in each aspect above) spellcasting class he belonged to before this prestige class. The character does not gain any other abilities associated with gaining a level in the previous class, however. If the dragon mage had more than one appropriate caster class before taking this prestige class, he must decide which class to apply the new level to for purposes of determining new spells each day. ^dragon-mage-spell-ability

**Siphon Magic (Su)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Breath)|breath]] and [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Wings)|wings]], 2nd level): This is the core of dragon magic, the original purpose behind the trovebond. Dragon mage characters gain this ability at 2nd level. The danger of this ability is that its careless use can actually destroy a trove, burning up the items that provide the energy. ^dragon-mage-siphon-magic

When this ability is used, it can provide as many as three benefits to the caster calling on the power of the trove. First, the power to siphon magic allows spellcasters to ignore the need for material components worth up to 100 gp per dragon mage level. If the caster would like to use the energy of the trove to replace a material component that exceeds the value listed above, he may do so, but one-half of the component’s value is immediately removed from the trove itself. The sudden ripping of so much energy from the trove destroys many items, leaving the dragon mage that much poorer.

The second power available through the siphon magic ability is the means to exceed the daily allotment of spells available to spellcasters. The amount of spells the character can cast in excess of his normal maximum per day is dependent on the size of the trove, as shown on [[#^table-siphon-magic|Table: Siphon Magic]].

In [[#^table-siphon-magic|Table: Siphon Magic]], the **Extra Spell Levels/Day** column indicates the total number of spell levels gained through this ability each day and the **Max. Spell Level** column indicates the highest level of spell that the extra spell levels can be used to power. For example:

_A dragon mage has a trove worth 49,500 gp and needs to cast a few extra spells to get through a particularly dangerous encounter. He is entitled to seven extra spell levels each day, and the highest level of spell these can be applied to is 3rd. He could cast two 3rd-level spells and one 1st-level spell (3 + 3 + 1 = 7), three 2nd-level spells and one 1st-level spell (2 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 7), or any other combination of spells in which the total number of levels is equal to 7 and the highest spell level does not exceed 3._

Note that the dragon mage may only use the extra spell levels each day to power spells that he either already memorized (or prayed for) that day or is capable of casting as spontaneous magic. A cleric can use the extra spell levels gained each day to cast healing (or harming magic) as per their spontaneous casting ability, even if they have no spells to convert.

The third power of the siphon magic ability is exercised either when a spell is prepared (if the character must prepare spells ahead of time), or at the moment that the spell is cast (if the character is capable of spontaneous casting). Use of the bond in the first case extends the time required for preparation (or prayer) by 10 minutes per extra spell level used. If this option is exercised at the moment the spell is cast, the casting time for the spell is tripled. These extra spell levels may pay the cost for metamagic feats, as detailed below.

Any metamagic feat that uses up spell slots of a higher level than the spell they affect can be powered by using the extra spell levels gained each day through dragon magic. To offset this increase in spell level, the dragon mage must use a number of extra spell levels equal to the original level of the spell for each one-level increase. For example, a 3rd-level _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]_ prepared with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Empower Spell|Empower Spell]] feat (requiring a spell slot 2 levels higher than spell’s actual level) would be prepared as a 5th-level spell. However, a dragon mage can expend 6 extra spell levels (3 extra levels for each level increase required by the metamagic feat) to prepare the empowered spell at its normal level.

Note that the last two uses of the siphon magic ability both draw from the same pool of extra spell levels each day.

**Trove Awareness (Su)** (every aspect, 1st level): The first ability gained after undergoing the trovebond provides the character with an instinctual knowledge of the trove’s location and condition. This allows the character to find his trove at any time and to know instantly if it is being tampered with in any way, regardless of distance. Though no detailed information is sensed, the trovebound character knows the instant that anyone touches his trove. This ability even works across planar boundaries, so tightly is he bonded to his trove. ^dragon-mage-trove-awareness

This ability continues to function regardless of what aspect the character’s trove is currently attuned to, even if the attunement changes.
