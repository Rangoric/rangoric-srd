## Prestige Classes

### Knight of the Fey

Knights of the fey live to defend the inhabitants of the fey realm from any hostile threats. This simple duty has evolved into protecting visitors and natural wonders from the stray evils that haunt the realm from their dark holes. Knights of the fey are dedicated to their charge, and will defend their charges to the death. Knights of the fey belong to a close-knit organization that upholds the laws of the fey courts and enforces their will. They are loners by necessity, finding that the best way to maintain an acceptable level of peace in the fey realm is to patrol as much of it as they can.

**Hit Die**: d10.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a knight of the fey, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any nonevil and nonchaotic.

**Race**: Any fey race (see [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^fey-characters|fey characters]]).

**Base Attack Bonus**: +4.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (fey)]] 2 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] 2 ranks.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Armor Proficiency (Heavy)|Armor Proficiency (Heavy)]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Cleave|Great Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Combat|Mounted Combat]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]].

###### Class Skills

The knight of the fey’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] (Int).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the knight of the fey prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Knights of the fey are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, all types of armor, and shields. ^knight-of-the-fey-weapon-and-armor-proficiency

**Blind-Fight**: A knight of the fey gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]] as a bonus feat at 1st level. ^knight-of-the-fey-blind-fight

**Shining Beacon (Ex)**: The knight of the fey is the physical and spiritual embodiment of the noble fey and feeorin. Any fey allies get a +4 morale bonus on saves against fear effects when they stand within 10 feet of the knight of the fey. If the knight is held, unconscious, or otherwise rendered helpless, his allies lose this bonus. ^knight-of-the-fey-shining-beacon

**Lucky Hit (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a knight of the fey gains the ability to add his prestige class level as an attack bonus to a missed attack roll once per day. At 6th level, this ability can be used twice per day. At 9th level, this ability can be used three times per day. This stacks with all attack bonuses. ^knight-of-the-fey-lucky-hit

**Luck of the Fey (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the knight of the fey gains [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Class Features#^luck-of-the-fey|luck of the fey]]. ^knight-of-the-fey-luck-of-the-fey

**Call Knight of the Fey (Su)**: At 5th level, a knight of the fey is awarded a magical horn, giving him the ability to call another knight to his aid. The knight, summoned through force of will and the sounding of the horn, arrives in 1d10 rounds. The arriving knight is less or more experienced than the summoner (1d6: 1–3 less, 4–6 more) by 1d4 levels. The knight fights for the remainder of that combat, ensures the safety of the fellow knight and his companions, and then leaves. ^knight-of-the-fey-call-knight-of-the-fey

**Luck of the True Fey (Ex)**: At 7th level, the knight of the fey gains [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Class Features#^luck-of-the-true-fey|luck of the true fey]]. ^knight-of-the-fey-luck-of-the-true-fey

**Destined (Ex)**: At 10th level, the knight of the fey gains [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Class Features#^destined|destined]]. ^knight-of-the-fey-destined
