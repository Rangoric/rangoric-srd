## Prestige Classes

### Apothecary

The apothecary reflects the teachings and priorities of a school of herbalism just as the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Herbalist|herbalist]] does, sharing a respect for both the herbs themselves and those to whom the herbs are administered. Where the apothecary differs is simply the tight focus on healing and the production, distribution, and use of medicines.

Apothecaries start out as herbalists, but this class grants them a focus and a new profession with skills beyond the grasp of NPC experts or those who learned their skills via self-teaching or a lone tutor.

Apothecaries practise the medicinal side of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism|herbalism]] (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|Alchemy and Herbalism]]).

**Hit Die**: d4.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become an apothecary, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Abilities**: Wisdom 12 (minimum for casting limited spell repertoire).

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 1 rank; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (herbalist) 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (apothecary) 5 ranks.

###### Class Skills

The apothecary’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] (Wis).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the apothecary prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Apothecaries gain no proficiency with any weapon or armor.

**Spells**: An apothecary does not necessarily know magic before beginning this prestige class. She does gain some small knowledge of natural magics and learns some limited magics normally available to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druids]], but she uses them more in the manner of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]], not learning by memorization so much as learning and using by gut instinct and a close connection to the healing powers of nature. Her spells are divine, as druid spells are, and her spellcasting ability is Wisdom. ^apothecary-spells

The spells on the table are marked with two numbers divided by a slash. The first number is how many spells the apothecary knows from her potential spell list. The second number is how many times per day she can actually cast any spells of that level.

The apothecary’s spell list is designed to be very restricted and linked to her knowledge and understanding of plants and herbs and healing, and very little else.

- **0-level**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Minor Wounds|cure minor wounds]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Purify Food and Drink|purify food and drink]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Virtue|virtue]]*.
- **1st-level**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Calm Animals|calm animals]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Goodberry|goodberry]]*.
- **2nd-level**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]*.

These spells carry “Apo” levels.

If a character had a spellcasting class before she became an apothecary, she foregoes advancement in that class while she studies and walks the path of the apothecary.

**Bonus Feats**: At 1st level and at every level thereafter, apothecaries gain a bonus feat tied to their talents, taught in a specific order: 1st, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Profession (apothecary)); 2nd, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Herbal Restoratives|Craft Herbal Restoratives]]; 3rd, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bodily Awareness|Bodily Awareness]]; 4th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diagnosis|Diagnosis]]; 5th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Toxins|Craft Toxins]]. An apothecary must still meet all feat prerequisites to use it. ^apothecary-bonus-feats

*Important:* These feats are in addition to the feats a character of any class gets as she rises in level. The apothecary is not limited to the list given here when choosing those feats.

**Skill Restrictions**: Any skill points earned while learning this prestige class go first to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (apothecary) and then to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]]. Those two skills must be at the maximum allowed per level before skill points can be placed among other skills. ^apothecary-skill-restrictions
