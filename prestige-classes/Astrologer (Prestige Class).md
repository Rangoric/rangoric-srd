## Prestige Classes

### Astrologer

An astrologer watches the night sky from her observatory and reads in the stars the causes behind events, and from them what is to come.

**Hit Die**: d4.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a astrologer, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (astrology) 5 ranks.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Astrologer (Feat)|Astrologer]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Lore (astrology)).

**Spells**: Able to call at least two 5th-level [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^constellations|constellations]].

**Special**: Must have built an observatory to watch the sky from, costing at least 50,000 gp.

###### Class Skills

The astrologer’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (Int), all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]] (Int), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (Wis).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the astrologer prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: An astrologer is proficient with the club, dagger, heavy crossbow, light crossbow and quarterstaff, but not with any armor or shield. Arcane spell failure applies to any armor she wears, as usual.

**Spells per Day/Spells Known**: When a new astrologer level is gained, the character gains new spells per day (and spells known, if applicable) as if she had also gained a level in an arcane spellcasting class she belonged to before she added the prestige class. She does not, however, gain any other benefit a character of that class would have gained. This means she adds her astrologer levels to the level of one arcane spellcasting class she has, then works out spells per day, spells known and caster level, which also count for [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^other-arcane-casters|star magic]]. If she had more than one arcane spellcasting class before, she decides to which class each new level is added.

**Star Vision (Ex)**: An astrologer gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]. If she already has it, she sees three times as far as a human in dim light instead of twice as far. ^astrologer-star-vision

**Prediction (Ex)**: An astrologer always has a sense of what is coming and of when things turn dangerous. She is never surprised: she can act normally in a surprise round. ^astrologer-prediction

**Astrological Lore (Ex)**: At 3rd level, an astrologer can know facts about particular people, places and deeds from the movements of the stars. This works like the bard's special [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-knowledge|bardic knowledge]] check, with a bonus equal to her astrologer level + her Intelligence modifier. A bard adds her astrologer levels to her bard level for bardic knowledge, and gains a +2 bonus on the check. ^astrologer-astrological-lore

**Truths Unknown (Ex)**: At 4th level, an astrologer gains a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks. Four times per day, by spending 10 minutes drawing up a chart, she learns what a person truly wants: their motives, intentions and needs, though not how they mean to get them or where they are. ^astrologer-truths-unknown

**Unclouded Vision (Sp)**: At 5th level, an astrologer can take a full-round action and make a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] check to gain *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]* for 5 rounds. She can do this successfully five times per day; a failed attempt uses the full-round action but not a daily use. ^astrologer-unclouded-vision
