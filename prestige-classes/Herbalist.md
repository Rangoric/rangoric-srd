## Prestige Classes

### Herbalist

This prestige class directly reflects the teachings and standards of a school of herbalism. While not always embracing the religious nature of druidism, herbalists have a sharply honed respect for nature and a broad knowledge and understanding of nature, its cycles, and the balances between plants and animals.

Herbalists of this class are neither common herbalists nor common students of the school—they are the advanced students who carry the message of Nature’s Gifts in Balance throughout the world. They are also learned in secrets of erlcana beyond the grasp of most self-taught or tutored herbalists (meaning NPC experts or those with random ranks of Profession (herbalist)).

Herbalists practise [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism|herbalism]] (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|Alchemy and Herbalism]]).

**Hit Die**: d4.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become an herbalist, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (herbalist) 5 ranks. (It is highly recommended that characters place one or two skill points each level into their Profession (herbalist) skill in order to meet the prerequisites of many feats gained in this prestige class.)

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Profession (herbalist)).

**Special**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-sense|Nature sense]] (the druid’s ability).

**Spellcasting**: Ability to cast 2nd-level spells.

**Saves**: Fortitude save +2.

###### Class Skills

The herbalist’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] (Str), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the herbalist prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Herbalists gain no proficiency with any armor. They do gain proficiency with one weapon—the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons|sickle]].

**Spells per Day**: An herbalist continues to study magic at the same time he studies herbalism, as all herbalists of this class are spellcasters to some degree. However, he only gains an additional level of spellcasting mastery every even level gained as an herbalist. He does not gain any other benefits a character of that class would have gained (hit points, save bonuses, metamagic or item creation feats, and so on). This essentially means he adds half the levels gained as an herbalist (rounding down) to the level of some other spellcasting class the character has, and then determines spells per day and caster level accordingly. For example, if a 5th-level druid gains his 2nd level as an herbalist, he gains new spells as if he had risen to 6th level in druid, but uses the other herbalist aspects of level progression such as attack bonus and save bonus. If a character had more than one spellcasting class before he became an herbalist, he must decide which class gains half his herbalist levels for purposes of determining spells per day. ^herbalist-spells-per-day

**Bonus Feats**: At 1st level and at every odd level thereafter, herbalists gain a bonus item creation feat tied to their herbal specialties, taught in a specific order: 1st, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Toxins|Craft Toxins]]; 3rd, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Herbal Restoratives|Craft Herbal Restoratives]]; 5th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Powders & Dusts|Craft Powders & Dusts]]; 7th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Grow Swathe|Grow Swathe]]; 9th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Create Erlcana|Create Erlcana]]. An herbalist must still meet all prerequisites for a feat to use it. ^herbalist-bonus-feats

*Important:* These feats are in addition to the feats a character of any class gets as she rises in level. The herbalist is not limited to the list given here when choosing those feats.

***Bloomagic***: At 4th level, herbalists gain a unique ability to heal plants using spell energies. They can cast any 0- or 1st-level spell but negate its normal effects or abilities in favor of using the energies to restore plant life. This is a lesser version of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flourish Spell|Flourish Spell]] feat or ability, though unlike that ability, the *bloomagic* talent allows the herbalist to do any one of the following with said energies: ^herbalist-bloomagic

- Restore preserved herbs or plants to their healthiest, freshest state;
- Heal any nonmagical damage to a plant that can be touched directly (a broken stalk, trampled leaves and body, stripped bark, etc.) and encompassed entirely by the herbalist’s hands. (Equivalent to a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]* spell.)

**Flourish Spell**: At 6th level, herbalists gain the spell-like ability Flourish Spell, allowing them to use spell energies (either arcane or divine) to heal or affect the growth of plants. See the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flourish Spell|Flourish Spell]] feat for more details on this ability’s use. The only difference between that feat and the herbalist ability is that Flourish Spell can be used twice a day at will. ^herbalist-flourish-spell
