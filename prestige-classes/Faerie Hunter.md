## Prestige Classes

### Faerie Hunter

The faerie hunter seeks to destroy all fey life—especially the faeries. She sees faeries as magical mutants that are an affront to the natural order of life and the logical proliferation of species. Faerie hunters seek to undo all works of faeriers and will even kill them to stop their efforts. To accomplish her goals, the faerie hunter spends her time learning everything she can about the fey, their homeland, and any weaknesses they have.

However misguided her reasons—usually claiming to have experienced some traumatic situation in which faeries were at fault—the faerie hunter uses everything in her power to fight the faeries. Her melee skills match those of any fighter, and to aid in her struggle, she possesses several supernatural abilities.

Most faerie hunters are rangers who follow the nature of crueler predators. Barbarians, bards, fighters, and fallen ex-paladins make skilled faerie hunters. The most dreaded of all faerie hunters are clerics, druids, sorcerers, and wizards, due to their wide arrays of available spells.

**Hit Die**: d10.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a faerie hunter (abbreviated Fhn), a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any nongood.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +5.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (fey)]] 2 ranks.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mite Fighter|Mite Fighter]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]].

**Special**: The character must harm or kill a fey for no other reason than to become a faerie hunter.

###### Class Skills

The faerie hunter’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] (Str), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] (Wis), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the faerie hunter prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Faerie hunters are proficient with all simple and martial weapons as well as all types of armor and shields. ^faerie-hunter-weapon-and-armor-proficiency

**Fey Wary (Su)**: Due to their absolute hatred for the fey and anything they create, faerie hunters develop a resistance to enchantment and illusion spells. This supernatural ability provides them with a +1 insight bonus on saves to resist spells and spell-like abilities of these schools. This bonus increases by +1 at 4th, 7th and 10th level. ^faerie-hunter-fey-wary

At 10th level, this faerie hunter ability extends to a radius emanating 5 feet from her.

**Fey Hunter (Ex)**: Beginning at 2nd level, a faerie hunter gains fey as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]]. This ability works the same as the ranger’s favored enemy class ability. If a faerie hunter has chosen fey for her favored enemy as a ranger, these abilities stack. This bonus increases by one every other level to a total of +5 at 10th level. ^faerie-hunter-fey-hunter

**Sneak Attack (Ex)**: Any time a faerie hunter’s target would be denied a Dexterity bonus to AC (whether it actually has a Dexterity bonus or not), the faerie hunter’s attack deals +1d6 points of damage. This extra damage increases to +2d6 points at 5th level. Should the faerie hunter score a critical hit with a sneak attack, this extra damage is not multiplied. This works like the rogue’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]]. ^faerie-hunter-sneak-attack

**Smite Fey (Su)**: Once a day, a faerie hunter of 3rd level or higher may attempt to smite fey with one normal melee attack. She adds her Charisma modifier (if positive) to her attack roll and deals 1 extra point of damage per faerie hunter class level. If a faerie hunter accidentally smites a creature that is not fey, the smite has no effect, but it is still used up for the day. ^faerie-hunter-smite-fey

**Faerie Fighter (Ex)**: When fighting [[rangoric-srd/races/Faerie|faeries]], faerie hunters gain a +1 bonus at 2nd level on their attack and damage rolls. This bonus increases to +2 for both attack and damage rolls at 5th level. If the faerie is smaller than the faerie hunter, this bonus stacks with any bonuses gained from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mite Fighter|Mite Fighter]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Mite Fighter|Improved Mite Fighter]]. (This is only against the faerie race, not all fey.) ^faerie-hunter-faerie-fighter

**Anchor (Sp)**: At 6th level, a faerie hunter gains the ability to keep a fey from escaping the mortal world, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]*. To accomplish this, the fey must be hit by a crossbow bolt or arrow tipped with a cold iron spike. Some particularly nasty faerie hunters commission all their bolts or arrows to be fashioned from iron for potential use as an anchor. ^faerie-hunter-anchor

**Slippery Mind (Ex)**: At 7th level, the faerie hunter gains [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slippery Mind (Rogue Talent)|slippery mind]], as the rogue’s advanced talent. ^faerie-hunter-slippery-mind

**Craft Blood Item (Ex)**: At 8th level, a faerie hunter gains this specialized form of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]] feat, which allows her to create healing potions from the blood and remains of a slain fey. The type of potion produced depends on the Hit Dice of the fey creature slain: 1 HD yields a potion of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]*, 2 HD yields a potion of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]*, and so on. The faerie hunter’s caster level for these potions is her faerie hunter level plus all other caster levels from any other spellcasting classes. All the normal requirements for a potion remain the same (such as race or spells). All other rules for creating potions apply. Only the faerie hunter is foul enough to research the magical uses of fey blood and escape the bad luck it could bring. ^faerie-hunter-craft-blood-item

**Destroy Doorway (Su)**: At 9th level, faerie hunters gain the supernatural ability to close doorways to the fey realm. Not in Rangoric yet: it works through the fey realm’s doorways, a subsystem of *Faeries* that waits with Feat Topics S18. ^faerie-hunter-destroy-doorway

**Spells**: Beginning at 1st level, a faerie hunter gains the ability to cast a small number of arcane spells. To cast a spell, the faerie hunter must have a Charisma score of at least 10 + the spell’s level. Faerie hunter bonus spells are based on Charisma, and saving throws against these spells have a DC of 10 + spell level + the faerie hunter’s Charisma modifier. When the faerie hunter gets 0 spells of a given level, such as 0 1st-level spells at 1st level, she gets only bonus spells. (A faerie hunter without a bonus spell for that level cannot yet cast a spell of that level.) The faerie hunter’s spell list appears below. A faerie hunter can freely choose, prepare, and cast any spell on the list, just like a bard. ^faerie-hunter-spells
