## Prestige Classes

### Dragon Mage (Wings)

The wings aspect of the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Mage|dragon mage]]: a dragon mage has what this note gives while his trove is attuned to wings. Requirements, class skills and the features of every aspect are in [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Mage|Dragon Mage]].

**Hit Die**: d6.

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Base Attack Bonus|Base Attack Bonus]]**: Medium (3/4 per level).

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Saving Throws|Saving Throws]]**: Good Fortitude and Will, poor Reflex.

Dragons should not fly—their bodies and wings are not suitable to airborne travel and their sheer mass makes the whole prospect ludicrous. And yet, the dragons still take to the air, day after day, eon after eon, without showing any signs of faltering. This aspect represents the mystery and majesty of creation that allows dragons to fly, despite the patent impossibility of such a feat.

Dragon mages who attune their troves to this aspect discover an increased awareness of the world around them and the divine energies that permeate the multiverse. An improved capacity for casting and directing divine magic results and the dragon mage gains a deeper understanding of his ability to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turn or rebuke undead]]. While it requires at least some training in the divine arts to truly master this aspect, even religious neophytes have experienced dramatic increases in their ability to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel divine energy]] after attuning their troves to this aspect. Dragons with a religious bent make frequent use of this aspect, and few opponents are more difficult to defeat than a dragon with the divine favor to master clerical magic.

**Attunement**: Attuning a trove to the wings aspect requires at least 40% of the trove’s value consist of divine magical items. The god to whom these items are consecrated is not important (though some clerics may be uncomfortable with blasphemous objects in their trove); it is their value and divine resonance that is necessary.

###### Class Features

**Attuned Rings (Su)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Wings)|wings]], 7th level): This ability allows the dragon mage to attune to the magical rings stored within his trove. As a full-round action, the dragon mage can then activate one of the rings he has attuned himself to, and be thereafter treated as if he were actually wearing the ring. The dragon mage may only attune one ring per two levels of the dragon mage prestige class. In order to successfully ‘wear’ an attuned ring, the dragon mage must not be currently wearing any other magical rings. If the dragon mage activates a second ring while ‘wearing’ an attuned ring, the effects of the first ring end immediately, and the effects of the second ring begin. ^dragon-mage-attuned-rings

For every attuned ring after the first that is ‘worn’ in a given day, there is a 10% chance that a flare of energy through the trovebond destroys the last-attempted ring in the trove. Dragons, with their perfected trovebond, never destroy one of their rings in this way.

**Distant Potions (Su)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Wings)|wings]], 5th level): This ability is activated with a full-round action after which the dragon mage may use a potion within his trove as if he actually held the potion in his hand. This immediately reduces the value of the trove by the value of the potion, which could be problematic if the ability is frequently used. The dragon mage may imbibe the potion himself or administer it to another (the target must be within 5 feet of the dragon mage and accept the potion willingly). If the target refuses the potion, it is still lost from the trove but no one gains its benefits. ^dragon-mage-distant-potions

**Healing Sacrifice (Sp)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Wings)|wings]], 9th level): Dragon mages that are able to cast healing magic may use this ability to sacrifice from their trove in order to gain additional healing spells in a day. These additional spells may only come from [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Mage#^table-healing-sacrifice|Table: Healing Sacrifice]] and do not count against the extra spell levels each day that are granted through the trovebond and the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Mage#^dragon-mage-siphon-magic|siphon magic]] ability. This table lists each of the spells available through this trove and the value of the trove that is depleted to cast them. ^dragon-mage-healing-sacrifice
