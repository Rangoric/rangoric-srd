## Prestige Classes

### Dragon Mage (Scales)

The scales aspect of the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Mage|dragon mage]]: a dragon mage has what this note gives while his trove is attuned to scales. Requirements, class skills and the features of every aspect are in [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Mage|Dragon Mage]].

**Hit Die**: d10.

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Base Attack Bonus|Base Attack Bonus]]**: Medium (3/4 per level).

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Saving Throws|Saving Throws]]**: All good.

The skin of a dragon is covered in supple, interlocking scales that provide amazing protection without inhibiting the dragon’s movements or speed. Troves attuned to this aspect provide similar defensive benefits to the trovebound, sheathing them in magical defenses and improving their resilience and resistance to damage. Feeble spellcasters quickly become anything but when their troves are attuned to this aspect—their flesh becomes tough and their health improves dramatically. Dragons with a paranoid streak keep their troves attuned to this aspect to protect themselves from sneak attacks.

**Attunement**: Attuning a trove to the scales aspect requires at least 40% of the trove’s value consist of magical armor or shields.

###### Class Features

**Constitution Increase (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Scales)|scales]], 6th level): The dragon mage’s Constitution score increases by 1 while his trove is attuned to the scales aspect. ^dragon-mage-constitution-increase

**Damage Reduction (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Scales)|scales]], 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th level): If the creature already possesses [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] from another ability, this ability adds to the amount of damage ignored from the attack. So, a young adult [[rangoric-srd/monsters/chromatic-dragons/Black Dragon|black dragon]] (with DR 5/magic) that is a 9th-level dragon mage with his trove attuned to the scales aspect (DR 10/magic) would have a total DR of 15/magic. ^dragon-mage-damage-reduction

**Improved Uncanny Dodge (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Scales)|scales]], 10th level): The dragon mage can no longer be [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]]. This works like the barbarian’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]], with his dragon mage levels counting as barbarian levels (they stack with the levels of any other class that grants uncanny dodge). ^dragon-mage-improved-uncanny-dodge

**Keen Senses (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Scales)|scales]], 2nd level): The dragon mage gains a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks. ^dragon-mage-keen-senses

**Uncanny Dodge (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Scales)|scales]], 8th level): The dragon mage can’t be caught [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]]: he gains the barbarian’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]]. If he already has uncanny dodge from another class, he gains improved uncanny dodge instead. ^dragon-mage-uncanny-dodge
