## Prestige Classes

### Dragon Mage (Talons)

The talons aspect of the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Mage|dragon mage]]: a dragon mage has what this note gives while his trove is attuned to talons. Requirements, class skills and the features of every aspect are in [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Mage|Dragon Mage]].

**Hit Die**: d8.

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Base Attack Bonus|Base Attack Bonus]]**: Full (1 per level).

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Saving Throws|Saving Throws]]**: Good Fortitude, poor Reflex and Will.

Few dragons or trovebound characters make use of this aspect of the trove because of its focus on purely physical power. Still, those characters who use magic as a fast way to improve their combat skills or resilience in battle find this aspect to be very beneficial and those wizards and sorcerers who attune their troves toward talons may be surprised to discover just how effective raw combat ability can make them.

Troves with a talons aspect are renowned for providing their trovebound with an enhanced ability to strike and damage targets in combat (both melee and ranged) and an impressive increase in size brought on by the transformative powers of the trovebond. The benefits of this aspect are most notable in non-dragons, as their natural forms are not as innately powerful as a dragon’s and do not dramatically increase in size during their lifetimes. Though dragons can benefit, somewhat, from attuning their trove to this aspect, most prefer to pursue an aspect that better supplements the areas in which they are weaker.

**Attunement**: Attuning a trove to the talons aspect requires that at least 40% of the trove’s value consist of magical weapons.

###### Class Features

**Brutal Knockdown (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)|talons]], 8th level): This ability improves the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragon, True#Combat|tail sweep]] attack, allowing dragons to apply 3 times their Strength bonus to any tail sweep attack. Creatures without a tail sweep attack may still use this ability in a more primitive form that allows them to use a full-round action to perform a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip attack]] against all opponents within 5 feet. Note that this ability may not be used in conjunction with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]] feat. ^dragon-mage-brutal-knockdown

**Fracture (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)|talons]], 10th level): Creatures with this ability have an innate knack for damaging objects. Any time this ability is used to strike at an inanimate object, the attacker may add twice his Strength bonus to damage caused by the attack. See [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Carrying, Movement, and Exploration#Smashing an Object|smashing an object]]. ^dragon-mage-fracture

**Hop Attack (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)|talons]], 4th level): Creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hover|Hover]] feat may use this ability to make an attack with their bite, all four feet, tail, and wings all in the same round, but cannot move during the round in which this ability is used. The creature hops up from the ground and unleashes its devastating rain of attacks before landing amid its opponents. ^dragon-mage-hop-attack

For creatures that do not have the Hover feat, this feat allows them to leap off the ground in order to exert greater force through a single attack during the round, allowing the creature to double its Strength bonus to damage for a single attack. The creature may only take a 5-foot step in the same round this action is used, but may perform the rest of its attacks as normal.

**Improved Flyby (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)|talons]], 2nd level): This ability gives dragons with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flyby Attack|Flyby Attack]] feat a +1 to all attacks made using this feat. For creatures without Flyby Attack, this ability enables them to move both before and after an action, if the total distance moved is not greater than their speed. Using this ability does allow an attack of opportunity. ^dragon-mage-improved-flyby

**Size Increase (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)|talons]], 7th level): The transformative powers of the trove make the dragon mage one size category larger, with the changes to his ability scores, natural armor, Armor Class and attack bonus that a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Improving Monsters#Size Increases|size increase]] brings, while his trove is attuned to the talons aspect. ^dragon-mage-size-increase

**Stunning Grapple (Ex)** ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/dragon-mage/Dragon Mage (Talons)|talons]], 6th level): If a creature has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snatch|Snatch]] feat, this ability enables them to exert extra force during the initial [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] attempt, possibly stunning their foes with the power of the attack. A snatch attack also attacks the Fortitude of a creature it hits, its attack bonus the attacker’s Hit Dice or levels, and on a hit the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round (until just before the attacker’s next action). The stunning grapple ability may be used once per day for every 5 Hit Dice or levels and must be declared before the attack is made. ^dragon-mage-stunning-grapple

If the attacker does not have the Snatch feat, they may attempt to stun their foes with a ferocious attack. If the attack hits, it also attacks the foe’s Fortitude, its attack bonus ½ the attacker’s Hit Dice or levels, and on a hit the foe is stunned for one round (until just before the attacker’s next action). The attacker must declare their use of the ability before making the attack, and may use this ability once per day for every 5 Hit Dice or levels the attacker has attained.
