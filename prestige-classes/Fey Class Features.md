# Fey Class Features

Several of the *Faeries* prestige classes share these class features, which work the same in each class that has them. Each class says at what level it gains them; where a feature speaks of “the character”, read the class.

Two more shared features already have a home elsewhere: **slippery mind** is the rogue’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slippery Mind (Rogue Talent)|advanced talent]] ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fae-walker|fae-walker]] (3rd), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Faerie Hunter|faerie hunter]] (7th), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Prankster|fey prankster]] (7th), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey-Touched|fey-touched]] (7th)), and **timeless body** is the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-timeless-body|druid’s]] ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Faerier|faerier]] (7th), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey-Touched|fey-touched]] (8th)).

## Luck of the Fey

**Luck of the Fey (Ex)**: The character develops the supernatural ability to affect the luck of herself and others. Once per day, the character can choose to reroll one roll that she or another character or creature within a 50-foot radius has just made. The targeted creature must take the results of the reroll, even if they are worse. ^luck-of-the-fey

*Gained by*: [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Guardian|fey guardian]] (1st), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Faemancer|faemancer]] (3rd), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Prankster|fey prankster]] (3rd), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey-Touched|fey-touched]] (3rd), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Knight of the Fey|knight of the fey]] (3rd).

## Luck of the True Fey

**Luck of the True Fey (Ex)**: The character expands the supernatural ability to affect the luck of herself and others. This ability provides two more chances per day to reroll any roll that she or another character or creature (again, within a 50-foot radius of the character) has just made. The targeted creature must take the results of the reroll, even if they are worse. This ability stacks with Luck of the Fey (providing a total of three die rolls that can be rerolled per day). ^luck-of-the-true-fey

*Gained by*: [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Prankster|fey prankster]] (6th), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey-Touched|fey-touched]] (6th), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Knight of the Fey|knight of the fey]] (7th).

## Destined

**Destined (Ex)**: The character can reroll a result multiple times in one round and choose between the rerolls gained through Luck of the Fey and Luck of the True Fey. This ability does not grant additional rerolls but allows more than one reroll per round and also allows worse results to be ignored. ^destined

*Gained by*: [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey-Touched|fey-touched]] (9th), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Prankster|fey prankster]] (10th), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Knight of the Fey|knight of the fey]] (10th).

## Fey Friend

**Fey Friend (Ex)**: Fey creatures come to see the character as a sympathetic being. The character gains a +4 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks when interacting with fey creatures. ^fey-friend

*Gained by*: [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Faemancer|faemancer]] (2nd), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Faerier|faerier]] (2nd), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey-Touched|fey-touched]] (4th).

## Fey Sense

**Fey Sense (Su)**: The character develops a special sensitivity to the presence of fey beings. As a free action, the character can detect the presence of fey creatures within 100 feet. The character does not know the type or specific location of the fey creatures, and this ability does not give the character the ability to see invisible fey creatures. The character senses the number and general direction of all fey within range. ^fey-sense

*Gained by*: [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Faerier|faerier]] (1st), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey-Touched|fey-touched]] (1st).

## Prestidigitator

**Prestidigitator (Sp)**: The character gains the ability to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]* as per the spell of the same name three times per day. If the character has other spellcasting abilities that grant cantrips, this is in addition to those spells. ^prestidigitator

*Gained by*: [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Prankster|fey prankster]] (1st), [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey-Touched|fey-touched]] (2nd).
