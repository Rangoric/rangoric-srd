## Prestige Classes

### Faemancer

To an outsider, characters who become faemancers may appear to be taking advantage of, and sometimes harming, the fey. This, however, couldn’t be further from the truth. The relationship between the faemancer and the fey is at least collaborative if not symbiotic. The fey retrieve spells and magics from the wilds of the fey realm in exchange for the opportunity to do things normally off limits to other fey. Fey who work with faemancers serve them of their own free will and are able to leave their service at any time. The fey themselves combine the magical energies of the fey realm with the skills of the spellcaster to most creatively bring about the requested effect. In a sense, the fey see the relationship as an exciting game of life and death.

Most faemancers say that this path was predestined and the fey chose them, not the other way around. The faemancer sees this mutual bond as a chance to be more than a normal arcane spellcaster. Some theorize that faemancers are all latent spellcasters who, if not gifted by fey companionship, would never have realized their potential.

Faemancers often come from the ranks of druids and barbarians, since these classes enjoy and understand the natural spirits and the fey seem to prefer working with them. Fighters, rangers, bards, and rogues also make acceptable faemancers; monks and paladins are entirely too serious and orderly to be good faemancers, at least from a fey’s perspective. Clerics, sorcerers, and wizards, conversely, cannot understand why people would forgo reliable spellcasting methods for those dependent upon the whims, moods, and cooperation of such a fickle race. The fey simply respond, “Together, we can cast spells that are normally beyond our skills separately.”

**Hit Die**: d4.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a faemancer (abbreviated Fmc), a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any chaotic, any nonevil.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +4.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (fey)]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 5 ranks.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Leadership|Leadership]].

**Special**: The character must have been chosen by the fey to become a faemancer (at the GM’s discretion).

###### Class Skills

The faemancer’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] (Wis).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the faemancer prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Faemancers gain no additional proficiency with any weapon or armor. ^faemancer-weapon-and-armor-proficiency

**Fey Companion (Ex)**: Before a character adopts this prestige class, a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sprite/Grig|grig]] approaches a latent faemancer and presents him or her with the knowledge the fey have about the character’s potential. If the character wishes to become a faemancer, the grig agrees to serve as a companion, and this makes the character a 1st-level faemancer. The grig (or other fey companion) disappears once a spell has been requested, and returns no earlier than the next round with the spell being sent on its way. At 4th level, a faemancer gains the services of a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sprite/Pixie|pixie]] to retrieve and deliver spells for her. ^faemancer-fey-companion

**Life Bond (Su)**: The strong magical connection between the faemancer and her fey companion allows a faemancer to absorb any damage that might affect the companion. The fey still rolls any necessary saves to avoid damage, but any damage it takes transfers to the faemancer without further saves or chances to reduce the damage. If the fey companion is in direct physical contact with the faemancer, it can stack both the faemancer’s save bonuses and its own together before determining the damage. The life bond unfortunately does not improve the saves of the faemancer herself. ^faemancer-life-bond

**Fey Companion Advancement (Ex)**: Fey companions of a faemancer gain experience and insight of their own while adventuring. At 2nd, 4th, and 6th levels, the grig companion increases in Hit Dice according to the advancement in its monster entry; at 6th and 8th levels, the pixie companion increases in the same way (each up to a maximum of 3 HD). ^faemancer-fey-companion-advancement

**Fey Friend (Ex)**: At 2nd level, the faemancer gains [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Class Features#^fey-friend|fey friend]]. ^faemancer-fey-friend

**Domain (Su)**: The faemancer, through experience and luck and the blessings of the fey realm, gains access to divine spells: one [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains|domain]]. The domain should not be one available to the faemancer from any previous class, and can be one of the following: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Dream Domain|Dream]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fey Domain|Fey]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Luck Domain|Luck]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Magic Domain|Magic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|Plant]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Protection Domain|Protection]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Travel Domain|Travel]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain|Trickery]]. These spells are retrieved by the fey companions the same as arcane spells are. All faemancer levels count as divine levels when casting these spells. Divine material foci are still required when listed in the spell description, but the spells are otherwise unchanged. The first domain comes at 3rd level, and new domains are gained at 7th and 9th levels. ^faemancer-domain

**Luck of the Fey (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the faemancer gains [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Class Features#^luck-of-the-fey|luck of the fey]]. ^faemancer-luck-of-the-fey

**Spirit Bond (Su)**: At 5th level, the magical connections between the faemancer and her fey companions strengthen. Fey companions permanently stack the faemancer’s save bonuses with their own, regardless of contact or distance between them. This ability can be used in combination with the life bond ability above. ^faemancer-spirit-bond

**Mutual Bond (Su)**: At 10th level, the magics among the faemancer and her fey companions strengthen once more, and now the faemancer can permanently stack the best save bonuses from either of her fey companions with her own. ^faemancer-mutual-bond

**Spells (Sp)**: The faemancer gains access to all spells normally available to wizards and sorcerers (the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|sorcerer/wizard spell list]]), with the spells per day shown on the table. She casts them as she casts the spells of a spellcasting class she already has: they use that class’s key ability score (for bonus spells, save DCs and the score needed to cast a spell), and she prepares them or casts them spontaneously as that class does. A faemancer with more than one spellcasting class chooses one at 1st level; one with none casts them as a sorcerer does (Charisma, spontaneously). A faemancer does not gain any additional levels in other spellcasting classes while walking this path. She still has access to any previous knowledge and spells, but casting those spells in the presence of a fey companion betrays what they are learning together. There is a 10% chance per level of spell cast that a fey companion will be offended by this “falling back away from our work” and disappear for five times as many minutes as the cast spell’s level. ^faemancer-spells
