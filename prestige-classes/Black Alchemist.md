## Prestige Classes

### Black Alchemist

Masters of all inanimate matter, the black alchemists have sacrificed much knowledge and ability to speed themselves toward their one true goal of creating the Five Great Stones of Alchemy. To them, all matter is equal, and such cosmetic differences as size, texture, hardness, liquidity, or flammability are equally malleable and changeable. A black alchemist sees no permanence in any state or structure, only change waiting to be triggered.

Black alchemists—or eagles as they sometimes call themselves—are closest to the classic alchemists, as their ultimate goals do reach toward the purification of matter to gold. However, they do not stop with that but search for the perfect form in all matter. Because they must know materials to such a degree, it is mandatory that the prospective black alchemist already have some basic respect for and knowledge of a craft that works with the materials they hope to transmute.

Black alchemists practise [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy|alchemy]] (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|Alchemy and Herbalism]]).

**Hit Die**: d6.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a black alchemist, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) 5 ranks; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (any one of the following: blacksmith, carpentry, gemcutting, leatherworking, pottery, sculpture, stonemasonry) 1 rank. It is also highly recommended that characters put one or two skill points each level into their Craft (alchemy) skill, in order to meet the prerequisites of many feats gained in this prestige class.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Craft (alchemy)).

**Spellcasting**: Ability to cast 2nd-level spells.

###### Class Skills

The black alchemist’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] (Int) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the black alchemist prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Black alchemists gain no proficiency with any weapon or armor.

**Spells per Day**: A black alchemist continues to study magic at the same time he studies alchemy and gains a level in spellcasting knowledge with each level as a black alchemist. He does not, however, gain any other benefits a character of that class would have gained (hit points, save bonuses, metamagic or item creation feats, and so on). For example, if a 5th-level wizard gains her 1st level as a black alchemist, she gains new spells as if she had risen to 6th level in wizard, but uses the other black alchemist aspects of level progression such as attack bonus and save bonus. If she next gains a level of wizard, making her a 6th-level wizard/1st-level black alchemist, she gains and casts spells as if she had risen to 7th level wizard. If a character had more than one spellcasting class before she became a black alchemist, she must decide which class adds to her levels of black alchemist for purposes of determining spells per day. ^black-alchemist-spells-per-day

**Bonus Feats**: At every even level, black alchemists gain a bonus item creation feat tied to their specialties: 2nd, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Chymicals|Craft Chymicals]]; 4th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Create Alchana|Create Alchana]]; 6th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Perfect Materials|Perfect Materials]]; 8th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Create Greater Alchana|Create Greater Alchana]]; 10th, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]]. If the character does not meet the prerequisites for the feat, it cannot be used until they are met (a character may have knowledge of [[rangoric-srd/feats/Create Alchana|Create Alchana]] but cannot use it until she gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], etc.) ^black-alchemist-bonus-feats

*Important:* These feats are in addition to the feats a character of any class gets as she rises in level. The black alchemist is not limited to the list given here when choosing those feats.

**Enhanced Spellcraft**: Due to the black alchemist’s deep understanding of matter and materials, he can gain benefits in spellcasting. ^black-alchemist-enhanced-spellcraft

- *Enhanced Illusions*: At 1st level, illusory images of inanimate objects are more sharply defined and focused. Saving throw DCs are at +5 versus an opponent’s disbelief or saves. ^black-alchemist-enhanced-illusions
- *Enhanced Transmutations*: At 3rd level, due to the intensive knowledge on matter gained, the alchemist can boost the integrity of transmutations done via spells. Saving throw DCs are at +1 per two class levels for all transmutation spells cast by the black alchemist. The alchemist gains a +1 insight bonus toward spell resistances in the same manner as spell DCs. ^black-alchemist-enhanced-transmutations

**Note on Stonecrafting**: Given the highly focused attention put to inanimate matter, the black alchemist can only create the following Alchana Stones using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Create Greater Alchana|Create Greater Alchana]]: Alchemist’s Stone, Mystic’s Stone, Philosopher’s Stone (the Five Stones are alchemy’s greater alchana; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemical and Herbal Items#Alchana|Alchana]]). ^black-alchemist-stonecrafting
