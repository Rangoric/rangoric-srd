## Prestige Classes

### Battle Herald

In every army there are leaders who surpass the average cold-hearted mercenary, battle-scarred veteran, or wizened drill instructor, not so much in their skill at arms but in their ability to inspire and lead others. Battle heralds are just such captains—skilled warriors to be sure, but ones who have honed the art of leadership to a keen edge and keep it as ready as their sword as a means to victory. Battle heralds tap into reservoirs of courage, skill, bravery, and perseverance that those who serve with them never knew they had, and their allies often find themselves ascending the mount of triumph following the battle herald's glorious (if often tattered) banner.

**Role**: A veteran whose masterful tactics and air of command inspire allies to greater feats of heroism.

**Hit Die**: d10.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a battle herald, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +4.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (oratory) 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (soldier) 2 ranks.

**Special**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-challenge|Challenge]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-courage|inspire courage]] class features.

###### Class Skills

The battle herald’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] (Int), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Tactics|Tactics]] (Int).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the battle herald prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A battle herald gains no proficiency with any weapon or armor.

**Inspiring Command (Ex)**: A battle herald uses her keen tactical acumen and decisive judgment to lead others to victory, as well as her mastery of command to assist herself and her allies in battle. ^battle-herald-inspiring-command

All battle heralds may use inspiring command to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-courage|inspire courage]] (as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] ability); [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] and battle herald levels stack to determine the bonuses provided by inspire courage.

At 1st level, and every two levels thereafter, the battle herald chooses one command to learn. Unless otherwise noted, these abilities provide a competence bonus equal to the battle herald's inspiring command bonus to her and to all allies within 60 feet able to see or hear her. Commands marked “limited” have the same range as above but only affect a certain number of allies (which can include the battle herald).

Issuing an inspiring command is a move action. At 5th level, this becomes a swift action, and at 10th level, it becomes an immediate action. Maintaining an inspiring command is a free action that cannot be disrupted, but its effects end immediately if the battle herald is killed or otherwise prevented from taking actions (such as being [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]]). The battle herald cannot have more than one command in effect at a time. She may use this ability for a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + her Charisma modifier, plus 2 additional rounds per level after 1st. She may use rounds of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] to issue inspiring commands, but not vice versa. Inspiring commands are language-dependent, mind-affecting effects. The battle herald cannot maintain an inspiring command and a bardic performance at the same time (this does not preclude abilities such as [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Battle Herald#^battle-herald-persistent-commands|persistent command]] or the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lingering Performance|Lingering Performance]] feat, which continue an inspiring command or bardic performance after the battle herald stops maintaining it).

**Improved Leadership (Ex)**: A battle herald with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Leadership|Leadership]] feat adds her inspiring command bonus to her Leadership score. ^battle-herald-improved-leadership

**Voice of Authority (Ex)**: A battle herald is skilled at shouting commands over the din of the battlefield and signaling allies when speech is impossible. She gains a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks with creatures with which she shares a language. In addition, her allies gain a bonus equal to the battle herald's class level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks to hear her commands or interpret her secret messages conveyed with [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]]. The battle herald's levels stack with cavalier levels for the purpose of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-tactician|tactician]] ability. ^battle-herald-voice-of-authority

**Easy March (Ex)**: At 2nd level, allies within 60 feet of the battle herald can [[rangoric-srd/rules/Carrying, Movement, and Exploration#Overland Movement|hustle or force march]] during overland travel without ill effects for a number of hours per day equal to 1 hour times her inspiring command bonus. ^battle-herald-easy-march

**Inspire Greatness (Ex)**: At 4th level, a battle herald may use her inspiring command ability to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-greatness|inspire greatness]] (as the 9th-level bardic performance ability). This ability affects one creature at 4th level, two at 7th, and three at 10th. ^battle-herald-inspire-greatness

**Banner (Ex)**: At 5th level, a battle herald may fly a battle standard that inspires her allies. This ability is identical to the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-banner|banner]] ability, and cavalier levels stack with battle herald levels to determine the bonuses provided by the banner. ^battle-herald-banner

**Teamwork Feat**: At 6th level, a battle herald receives a [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^teamwork-feats|teamwork feat]] as a bonus feat. She must meet the prerequisites for this feat. She can use her cavalier [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-tactician|tactician]] ability as a move action to grant this feat to allies (if she has the cavalier [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-greater-tactician|greater tactician]] ability, this is a swift action). ^battle-herald-teamwork-feat

**Demanding Challenge (Ex)**: At 7th level, a battle herald gains demanding challenge. This ability is identical to the 12th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-demanding-challenge|demanding challenge]] ability. ^battle-herald-demanding-challenge

**Persistent Commands (Ex)**: At 8th level, a battle herald may allow her inspiring commands to persist even if she is incapacitated and unable to maintain them. If the player chooses, the effects of the battle herald's inspiring commands persist for a number of rounds equal to her Charisma bonus (these count toward her number of rounds per day limit). This ability does not apply if the battle herald intentionally stops maintaining an inspiring command—only if she is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], held, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], killed, and so on, and is unable to maintain them. If the battle herald recovers from incapacity while an inspiring command is ongoing, she may resume maintaining it as a free action. ^battle-herald-persistent-commands

**Inspire Last Stand (Ex)**: At 9th level, a battle herald may use inspiring command to grant herself and all allies within 30 feet the benefits of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]] feat. Conscious affected creatures also gain the benefits of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-courage|inspire courage]] while at [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death|negative hit points]]. ^battle-herald-inspire-last-stand

**Complex Commands (Ex)**: At 10th level, a battle herald can have more than one command ability in effect at one time. Each must be begun separately and requires a separate maintenance cost. This allows the battle herald to have more than one single-target command (such as battle magic) in effect at the same time. The effects of multiple instances of the same command ability do not stack, even if the bonuses provided by that ability would normally stack (for example, if the battle herald maintains two pincer maneuver commands, her allies do not get double the bonus, even though dodge bonuses normally stack). ^battle-herald-complex-commands

The battle herald can also maintain one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] in addition to her inspiring commands, though each must be begun separately and each requires its own maintenance cost.
