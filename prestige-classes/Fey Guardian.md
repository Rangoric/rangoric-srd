## Prestige Classes

### Fey Guardian

Characters who become fey guardians often seek to share the knowledge, gifts, and magic of the fey realm with the mortals of other worlds and planes. They frequently adopt one or two people—often people with little means and low status—and seek to change their lives in the most positive way through their abilities. Upon helping the person, they seek out their next “project” by observing the people surrounding them and selecting one who would benefit and most appreciate the help.

**Hit Die**: d4.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a fey guardian (abbreviated Fgd), a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 12 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (fey)]] 12 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 5 ranks.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fey Magic|Fey Magic]], any two [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#Metamagic Feats|metamagic feats]].

**Spells**: Able to prepare and cast arcane spells of 4th level or higher.

**Special**: Able to cast divine spells of 1st level or higher.

###### Class Skills

The fey guardian’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (Int), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the fey guardian prestige class.

**Spells per Day**: At each level, a fey guardian gains new spells per day as if he had also gained a level in a spellcasting class he belonged to before adding the prestige class. He does not, however, gain any other benefit a character of that class would have gained. If he had more than one spellcasting class before becoming a fey guardian, he must decide to which class he adds each level for the purpose of determining spells per day. ^fey-guardian-spells-per-day

**Luck of the Fey (Ex)**: At 1st level, the fey guardian develops the supernatural ability to affect the luck of himself and others. Once per day, the fey guardian can choose to reroll one roll that he or another character or creature within a 50-foot radius has just made. The targeted creature must take the results of the reroll, even if they are worse. ^fey-guardian-luck-of-the-fey

**Will of the Fey (Ex)**: At 1st level, a fey guardian is gifted with the ability to expend less XP than required to complete a spell. When casting spells that benefit others, the XP cost is 75% of the listed cost, while spells that benefit the fey guardian himself cost 90% of the listed cost. ^fey-guardian-will-of-the-fey

**Polymorph Other (Sp)**: Upon reaching 2nd level, the fey guardian is granted the ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Baleful Polymorph|baleful polymorph]]* three times per day as per the spell of the same name. This extra spell is in addition to the character’s regular spell allotment. ^fey-guardian-polymorph-other

**Mote Form (Su)**: Once a fey guardian reaches 3rd level, he gains the ability to change into a cloud of small glowing spheres. This ability can be used three times per day and functions like the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]* spell; since the fey guardian is light rather than a gas, he is not subject to winds and can enter water and liquids. ^fey-guardian-mote-form

**Transfer (Sp)**: At 4th level, the fey guardian gains the ability to cast spells with a range of personal on other beings as if they were touch spells, allowing them to use spells normally limited to the caster. ^fey-guardian-transfer

**Permanency (Sp)**: When fey guardians reach 5th level, they gain the ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Permanency|permanency]]* once per day. This extra spell is in addition to the character’s regular spell allotment. ^fey-guardian-permanency

**Limited Wish (Sp)**: At 7th level, the fey guardian gains the ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]* once per week. This extra spell is in addition to the character’s regular spell allotment. ^fey-guardian-limited-wish

**Polymorph Any Object (Sp)**: At 9th level, the fey guardian is able to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph Any Object|polymorph any object]]* three times per day. This extra spell is in addition to the character’s regular spell allotment. ^fey-guardian-polymorph-any-object

**Wish (Sp)**: At 10th level, a fey guardian is able to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]* once per week. This extra spell is in addition to the character’s regular spell allotment. ^fey-guardian-wish
