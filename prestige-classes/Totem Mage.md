## Prestige Classes

### Totem Mage

Totem mages spend their days trying to enhance their connection with their totems through meditation and mimicry of the totem, or merely roaming the wilderness. They are mostly found in the regions their totems naturally inhabit. They practice [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]].

**Hit Die**: d8.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a totem mage, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Base Will Save**: +4.

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 5 ranks.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]].

**Special**: Able to transform into her chosen totem, either by spellcasting (such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shapechange|shapechange]]*) or by a class ability (such as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|druid’s wild shape]]).

###### Class Skills

The totem mage’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] (Str), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the totem mage prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Totem mages gain proficiency with simple weapons and light armor.

**Totem Link (Su)**: A totem mage must choose a single animal to link to, and this must be the same animal she chose for the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat. Once the character becomes a totem mage, she gains enlarged spell selections and some special abilities based on her totem. Once chosen, a totem cannot be changed short of taking the Totem Link feat again with another animal and starting all over again, taking levels in totem mage with the second totem. The totems are [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Bear (Totem)|Bear]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Boar (Totem)|Boar]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Cat (Totem)|Cat]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Dog (Totem)|Dog]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Eagle (Totem)|Eagle]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Horse (Totem)|Horse]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Insect (Totem)|Insect]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Rat (Totem)|Rat]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Wolf (Totem)|Wolf]]. ^totem-mage-totem-link

**Spells per Day**: Totem mages are able to cast divine spells by channeling power through their totem. This energy comes from the very source of the totem, essentially the spirit of the species of the totem. Bear totem mages may call on the Great Bear, for example. The spirits that grant these abilities are not gods, but are the true incarnation of the totem. Totem mages can’t spontaneously cast *cure* or *inflict* spells. ^totem-mage-spells-per-day

To cast a spell, the totem mage must have a Charisma of at least 10 + the spell’s level. Saving throws against a totem mage’s spells have a DC of 10 + spell level + the totem mage’s Charisma modifier.

The totem mage gains spells each day by communing with her totem, which requires behavior appropriate to the totem. Weasel totem mages may play with shiny objects while dog totem mages may gnaw bones or chase small animals. These actions can’t involve combat, skill checks, saving throws or spellcasting. The communion can only be done after eight hours of rest and takes fifteen minutes. Each day, the totem mage gets the spell slots on the table below, plus bonus spell slots for her Charisma.

The totem mage’s spell slots from her totem mage levels are only used to prepare the spells on the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Totem Mage Spell List|Totem Mage Spell List]] (their levels show as “Tot”) and the five spells on her totem’s list.

**Totem Shape (Sp)**: This ability functions as the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Changeform|changeform]]*, except that the only form the totem mage can assume is that of her totem; her totem’s note gives the forms open to her by level. She can use it once per day at 1st level, and once more per day at every even level (as the table shows). ^totem-mage-totem-shape

At 5th level, the totem mage gains the ability to change into a dire version of her totem. If the creature does not have a dire version, add to the normal version of the creature 3 Hit Dice, +2 on all saving throws and attack bonuses, increase speed by 10 feet, increase the damage die of the creature by one die type, and increase the size category to Large. Totem mages may cast spells while in totem form so long as the spell does not require a material component.

**Totem Friendship (Sp)**: The totem mage may use the equivalent of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Animal|charm animal]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]* at will on animals of her totem. This requires a standard action. ^totem-mage-totem-friendship

**Totem Companion (Ex)**: If the totem mage has not already done so, she automatically attracts a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar]] of the same species as her totem. The familiar gains abilities based on the totem mage’s character level as if she were a sorcerer with a +4 to the level, and the creature has maximum hit points for its species. If the totem mage already has a familiar of her totem, it gains these bonuses immediately. ^totem-mage-totem-companion

**Totem Fear (Sp)**: At 3rd level, once per day per class level, the totem mage may cast a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]* spell as a spell-like ability, but it only affects creatures that fear the totem mage’s totem. Animals may flee from a wolf totem mage while men may run from a bear totem mage. The caster level is equal to the class level of the totem mage. It is up to the GM to determine what is afraid of each totem at a given time. ^totem-mage-totem-fear

**Totem Facet (Su)**: At 5th level, the totem mage gains the ability to partially change her shape, taking on a part of the totem’s abilities. When using the totem facet ability, the totem mage gains one ability score, saving throw, natural weapon, skill bonus, or movement rate from the statistics for her totem. The totem mage may use this facet instead of her own statistics for a number of rounds equal to her class level. Only one facet may be adopted at a time using this ability and each separate facet requires a use of this ability (once per day at 5th level, twice at 7th, three times at 9th). The totem mage also adopts aspects of the appearance of her totem. Bear totem mages who gain the claws of their totem suddenly develop oversized, claw-bearing hands, while a totem mage of the horse totem who increases his speed may have his legs grow and gain hooves. ^totem-mage-totem-facet

**Totem Ability (Ex)**: At 9th level, the totem mage gains a special ability granted by her totem, which is described in the totem’s note. ^totem-mage-totem-ability
