## Prestige Classes

### Faerier

Those who wander the land sometimes encounter wild faeries. Few, however, notice their encounters for what they really are—brushes with primal magics of wondrous charm and terrible danger. Those who understand the true nature of the fey become enchanted by, or covetous of, the powerful natural magic of the faerie. Those who devote themselves to the study of fey creatures are known as faeriers. While faeriers form a rarefied community of magical study, they are not formally affiliated with wizardly studies.

Almost all faeriers come from the ranks of bards, druids, and rangers. A few rogues, and even a scattering of barbarians, have devoted themselves to the study of faerie ways. However, clerics, sorcerers, and wizards generally disdain the primitive magic of the fey. Fighters, monks and paladins rarely exhibit any sensitivity toward the world of the faeries.

**Hit Die**: d4.

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Base Attack Bonus|Base Attack Bonus]]**: Full (1 per level).

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Saving Throws|Saving Throws]]**: Good Will, poor Fortitude and Reflex.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a faerier (abbreviated Frr), a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Size**: Medium or smaller.

**Languages**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics#^fae|Fae]].

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]]).

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 5 ranks.

**Knowledge**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore (fey)]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 5 ranks, or a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-knowledge|bardic knowledge]] bonus of +6.

**Spellcasting**: Able to cast spells or use magic devices.

###### Class Skills

The faerier’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] (Dex), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] (Wis), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the faerier prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: The faerier gains no armor or weapon proficiencies beyond those he already possesses. ^faerier-weapon-and-armor-proficiency

**Fey Sense (Su)**: At 1st level, the faerier gains [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Class Features#^fey-sense|fey sense]]. ^faerier-fey-sense

**Fey Arts (Sp)**: At 1st level, the faerier chooses whether to study the magic of Seelie or Unseelie faeries. Faeriers of evil alignment may not study Seelie magic, and those of good alignment may not study Unseelie magic. Once the choice is made, the faerier selects his fey arts from the matching list below, and he may not switch between these studies at any later time. He chooses one fey art at 1st level, and an additional fey art at 3rd, 5th, 6th, 8th, and 10th levels; he may not select the same fey art more than once. Each fey art is a spell-like ability, detailed below. ^faerier-fey-arts

**_Seelie Arts_**

_Dryad Form_: Once per day, you may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tree Shape|tree shape]]_ as if cast by a 6th-level druid.

_Gnome Brew_: Once per day, as a full-round action, you may imbue up to 1 barrel of liquor with an enchanting potency. The enchanted liquor attacks the Fortitude of a creature that drinks a mug of it at +1 + faerier level, and on a hit the creature immediately falls asleep for 1d4 days. Creatures sleeping off the effects of a _gnome brew_ do not suffer from starvation or dehydration due to the magical nature of the draught. Dwarves and fey creatures are not affected. Any creatures put to sleep by the enchanted liquor awaken if they receive a damage-causing blow. The attack bonus increases by 1 for each mug consumed without being hit.

_Grig Fiddle_: Once per day, you may play an enchanting tune on a fiddle or other stringed instrument. While you play this enchanted tune, the tune attacks the Will of all non-fey creatures within 60 feet at +5 + faerier level, and those it hits are affected as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Irresistible Dance|irresistible dance]]_ spell. The effect ends when you stop playing. Any creature attacked while affected by this ability stops dancing at the beginning of the following round.

_Hanid Hiding_: Once per day as a full-round action, you may become immaterial and house your spirit in any plant within 10 feet. Your body, clothing, and gear become invisible and immaterial as well and rematerialize when you do. You may remain hidden in the plant for up to 3 hours. Emerging from the plant requires a full-round action. If a plant in which you are hiding is killed, its death attacks your Fortitude at +5, and on a hit you die. A miss causes you to rematerialize at a randomly determined location within 10 feet.

_Hybsil Sight_: As a free action, you may choose to see invisible objects and creatures for up to 10 minutes three times per day.

_Nixie Breath_: Three times per day, you may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_ as if cast by a 6th-level druid.

**_Unseelie Arts_**

_Bogan’s Fog_: Three times per day as a full-round action, you may cause a cloud of fog to form. The fog is centered on your location when the ability is activated and extends to a 40-foot radius, providing total concealment to everything inside the cloud. The fog dissipates in 2d4 minutes.

_Hob Lick_: Once per day as a full-round action, you may lick an edged weapon to coat it with fey poison. A successful hit with that weapon exposes the target to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]]: injury; _attack_ +3 + faerier level vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/minute for 2 minutes; _effect_ 1d4 Con [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]]. The fey poison lasts for 5 minutes or until an attack using the weapon causes damage.

_Pixie Invisibility_: Three times per day, you may become [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] for up to 10 minutes. You remain invisible even while casting spells or attacking but become visible if you are successfully attacked or otherwise suffer damage.

_Satyr Pipe_: Once per day, you may play an enchanting tune on pipes or some other wind instrument. While you play this enchanted tune, the tune attacks the Will of all non-fey creatures within 60 feet at +5 + faerier level, and those it hits are affected as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]_. The effect ends when you stop playing.

_Siren Song_: Once per day, you may call out to a creature, attacking its Will at +12, –2 for every 20 feet of distance from you; on a hit it feels compelled to walk toward you. An affected creature disregards all considerations other than his compulsion to move toward you in a straight line. If a dangerous obstacle appears in the creature’s path (such as the edge of a cliff or a body of water), the song immediately attacks the target’s Will again, the target gaining a +5 natural bonus on its Will Defense. If the attack hits, he ignores any dangers or obstacles in his path. Affected creatures attempt to walk the entire distance at their normal movement rate; they do not run, fly, or swim, even if doing so would make sense to someone not enchanted.

_Sprite Charm_: Three times per day, you may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Animal|charm animal]]_ as a 6th-level druid.

**Fey Friend (Ex)**: At 2nd level, the faerier gains [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Fey Class Features#^fey-friend|fey friend]]. ^faerier-fey-friend

**Detect Doorways (Su)**: At 3rd level, the faerier gains an innate sense for the presence of fey [[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Doorways|doorways]]. Once per day per faerier level, the faerier can detect nearby doorways and backroads as if casting _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Doorway|detect doorway]]_. ^faerier-detect-doorways

**Create Doorways and Backroad (Sp)**: At 4th level, the faerier gains the ability to create doorways and connecting backroads once every 10 days, as if he were a fey creature. This ability works like the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Doorway and Backroad|create doorway and backroad]]_ spell and requires the faerier to pay the listed XP cost. ^faerier-create-doorways-and-backroad

**Fey Form (Su)**: Due to his extreme study of fey beings, the faerier may assume the form of a fey creature once per day. This special ability works like a druid’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]], but is limited to the forms of fey creatures within one size category of the faerier. A faerier gains this ability at 5th level. ^faerier-fey-form

**Timeless Body (Ex)**: At 7th level, the faerier gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-timeless-body|timeless body]], as the druid’s. ^faerier-timeless-body

**True Fey Form (Su)**: At the culmination of his study of fey beings, the faerier may assume the form of a fey creature once per day. This special ability works like a druid’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]], but is limited to fey creatures within two size categories of the faerier. A faerier gains this ability at 9th level. ^faerier-true-fey-form

**Create Doorway and Sideroad (Sp)**: At 10th level, the faerier can create doorways and connecting sideroads once every 30 days, as if he were a fey creature. This ability works like the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Doorway and Sideroad|create doorway and sideroad]]_ spell and requires the faerier to pay the listed XP cost. ^faerier-create-doorway-and-sideroad
