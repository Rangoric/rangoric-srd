## Prestige Classes

### Star Magus

A star magus has given her studies wholly to [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|star magic]], and calls the stars with an authority few others reach. (The book calls this class the magus; it is the Star Magus here, so it isn't confused with the magus class.)

**Hit Die**: d4.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a star magus, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Mastery|Spell Mastery]], chosen for at least three stars.

**Spells**: Able to cast 6th-level arcane spells. Knows at least ten constellations.

**Special**: Must be a [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^wizardly-science|specialist in star magic]], and must pass a deadly test set by another star magus.

###### Class Skills

The star magus’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (Int), all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]] (Int), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (Wis).

**Skill Points at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the star magus prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A star magus is proficient with the club, dagger, heavy crossbow, light crossbow and quarterstaff, but not with any armor or shield. Arcane spell failure applies to any armor she wears, as usual.

**Spells per Day/Spells Known**: When a new star magus level is gained, the character gains new spells per day (and spells known, if applicable) as if she had also gained a level in an arcane spellcasting class she belonged to before she added the prestige class. She does not, however, gain any other benefit a character of that class would have gained. This means she adds her star magus levels to the level of one arcane spellcasting class she has, then works out spells per day, spells known and caster level, which also count for [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^other-arcane-casters|star magic]]. If she had more than one arcane spellcasting class before, she decides to which class each new level is added.

**Superior Spell Focus (Ex)**: The save DCs of the stars and constellations a star magus calls are 2 higher. This becomes +4 at 3rd level and +6 at 5th level, and stacks with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]]. ^star-magus-superior-spell-focus

**Focused Spell Penetration (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a star magus gains a +2 bonus on caster level checks to overcome spell resistance with stars and constellations. This becomes +4 at 4th level. ^star-magus-focused-spell-penetration

**Bonus Feat**: At 3rd level, a star magus gains one of these as a bonus feat: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Secret Star of Change|Secret Star of Change]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Secret Star of Power|Secret Star of Power]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Secret Star of Reality|Secret Star of Reality]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Secret Star of Time|Secret Star of Time]]. ^star-magus-bonus-feat
