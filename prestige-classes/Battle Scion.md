## Prestige Classes

### Battle Scion

Battle scions include battleblade scions and battlestaff scions. The base prestige classes are identical, even if one wields a blade and one wields a staff.

A battle scion has earned the right to fight with a particular legendary weapon, either because the weapon is an ancestral heirloom or because he upholds the principles for which the weapon was originally created. While characters of any class can eventually qualify for the battle scion class, those who wield weapons professionally — fighters, barbarians, rangers, and paladins — are ideal candidates.

No two battle scions are identical, by virtue of the weapons that grant them access to this prestige class. One scion might wield an heirloom weapon that provides him with the abilities needed to uphold the family’s honor. Another could be the scourge of an evil temple, using his legendary sword to prove that might does indeed make right. Yet another could be wielding an axe, hammer, or staff dedicated to his profession, or to his race, with abilities that draw on the strengths thereof.

For all the powers his sword grants him, a battle scion isn’t terribly different from any other fighter, barbarian, ranger, or paladin who uses his share of the treasure to upgrade his favorite weapon every so often. What really sets him apart is the fact that his weapon is leading him toward a particular destiny, if only by shaping his future advancement choices.

Sometimes a magic staff is not so much a device for storing spells as it is a magic weapon in the shape of a staff. For such legendary staves, the battlestaff scion is the ideal wielder. Their weapons are items of immense arcane power, and often have a great magical destiny. They might be wielders of fey staves, or of demon rods, or the item might actually be an important symbol of a military order.

**Hit Die:** d10.

###### Requirements

To qualify to become a battle scion, a character must fulfill the criteria given in his particular weapon’s entry. (For an example, see [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/legendary-weapons/Drakeblade|Drakeblade]], below.)

######## Class Skills

The battle scion’s class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] (Str), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] (Cha).

**Skill Points at Each Level:** 2 + Int modifier.

###### Class Features

The following are class features of the battle scion prestige class. A legendary weapon associated with the class provides a unique set of additional special abilities, which are detailed in the weapon’s description. (For an example, see [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/legendary-weapons/Drakeblade|Drakeblade]], below.) These abilities accrue in addition to those already noted in the Special column on [[#^table-the-battle-scion|Table: The Battle Scion]]. However, because those abilities are functions of the weapon rather than the wielder, they are lost if the legendary weapon that granted them is lost. The features mentioned on Table: The Battle Scion are retained even if the weapon is lost.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Battle scions gain no proficiency with any weapon or armor, not even with their legendary weapon (see [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Legendary Weapons & Familiar Items#^scion-proficiency|Rules and Restrictions]]). ^battle-scion-proficiency

**Bonus Feats:** At 1st level, a battle scion receives a bonus feat. Thereafter, he gains an additional bonus feat at 4th, 7th, and 10th level. These bonus feats must be [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#Fighter Bonus Feats|fighter bonus feats]] (the tag *fighter-bonus*). A battle scion must still meet all prerequisites to take a bonus feat.

**Two-Weapon Fighting ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Legendary Weapons & Familiar Items#^blade-and-staff-scions|staff scions]] only)**: If a staff scion does not already have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]] feat, he gains it at 1st level. The wielder does not benefit from the Two-Weapon Fighting feat unless he meets the feat’s prerequisites, however. If the legendary weapon does not actually require two hands to use in combat, the wielder gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with the weapon instead. ^battle-scion-two-weapon-fighting

**Weapon Specialization ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Legendary Weapons & Familiar Items#^blade-and-staff-scions|blade scions]] only)**: If a blade scion does not already have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Specialization|Weapon Specialization]] feat that applies to his legendary weapon, he gains it at 1st level as a bonus feat. However, he cannot benefit from the Weapon Specialization feat unless he meets the prerequisites for it ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Legendary Weapons & Familiar Items#^weapon-specialization|a scion needs no fighter levels for it]]). ^battle-scion-weapon-specialization
