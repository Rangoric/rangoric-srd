### Skill Descriptions

The skills below relate to the use of psionics. A new category is provided for the Knowledge skill, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Psionics (Skill)|Psionics]], which also identifies powers and psionic effects. Psionic items are used with [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] and Autohypnosis is now [[rangoric-srd/skills/Meditation|Meditation]], both of which anyone can learn, and the psionic uses of Concentration are in [[rangoric-srd/psionics/Psionics#Concentration|Psionics]].
