Regarded as living legends, simurghs are held in high regard by desert dwellers. Those who live in the desert lands where these benevolent creatures sometimes reside consider it a lifetime's worth of luck even to spot one soaring through the sky. Simurghs prefer to keep to themselves, well out of the way of lesser creatures and their often dubious morals, though they can be relied upon for aid when called by those in true need and with a pure heart. A simurgh can live for thousands of years, and frequent mentions of these giant avian beings throughout a region's historical record are more often than not sightings of the same creature.

Simurghs speak Celestial, Common and Draconic.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 16th; concentration +22): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Zone of Truth|zone of truth]]_ (+8 vs Will); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|flame strike]]_ (+11 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds, Mass|mass cure critical wounds]]_.

**Aura of Peace (Su)**: Creatures within a 50-foot spread from a simurgh feel a sensation of peace wash over them, as if affected by calm emotions, except the simurgh can choose which creatures are affected. It attacks at +18 vs Will each round, and a miss negates the effects of this aura for that round. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Banishing Swipe|Banishing Swipe]] (Su)**: A simurgh can use its radiant tail to return creatures to their native planes. In addition to taking damage, any extraplanar creature touched by a simurgh's tail is attacked at +18 vs Will, and on a hit is affected as if by banishment. A creature the swipe misses cannot be affected by the same simurgh's banishing swipe for the next 24 hours. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Glaring Ray|Glaring Ray]] (Su)**: A simurgh can blast a fiery ray of brilliant light from its eyes as a standard action to a range of 100 feet.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Radiant Feathers|Radiant Feathers]] (Su)**: Once per day as a standard action, a simurgh can fan out its glimmering tail feathers and blast its foes with a 100-foot cone of radiant light from its tail. Aside from its size, this attack is identical to a prismatic spray (+18). The attack bonus is Charisma-based.
