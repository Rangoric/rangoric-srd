A titan is about 25 feet tall and weighs about 14,000 pounds.

Titans speak Abyssal, Common, Celestial, Draconic, and Giant.

### Combat

A titan’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as chaotic-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].

**Change Shape (Su):** A titan can assume the form of any Small or Medium humanoid. The titan retains its oversized weapon special attack regardless of form.

**Oversized Weapon (Ex)**: A titan wields a great, two-handed warhammer (big enough for Gargantuan creatures) without penalty.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 20th. The attack bonuses are Charisma-based.

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] (+13 vs Reflex), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]] (+11 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|cure critical wounds]] (+11), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]] (+14 vs Reflex), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]] (+12 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility Purge|invisibility purge]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Persistent Image|persistent image]] (+12)

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Etherealness|etherealness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Word of Chaos|word of chaos]] (+12 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IX|summon nature’s ally IX]];

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate|gate]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Maze|maze]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]] (+16 vs Reflex). 

In addition, titans of good or neutral alignment can use the following additional spell-like abilities. Caster level 20th. The attack bonuses are Charisma-based:

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]] (+11 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]] (+11 vs Will);

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Greater|greater restoration]].

Titans of evil alignment can use the following additional spell-like abilities. Caster level 20th. The attack bonuses are Charisma-based:

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]] (+11 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]] (+11 vs Will);

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Crushing Hand|crushing hand]] (+16).

### Tactics Round-By-Round

Titans enjoy combat and usually close with their foes. If that proves ineffective, they swiftly back off and pelt the foe with spell-like abilities and magical effects. Because of a titan’s Quicken Spell-Like Ability feat, it can use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] as a free action, and frequently attacks in melee while lashing out with this ability at the same time.

_Prior to combat_: [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility Purge|Invisibility purge]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]].

_Round 1_: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|Charge]] and attempt to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] the weapon of the most dangerous foe. Hurl [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] at opponents standing away from the fight.

_Round 2_: Full attack against the disarmed opponent, and hurl [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] at other opponents.

_Round 3_: Back away from first opponent and use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Maze|maze]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]] on any spellcaster causing trouble.

_Round 4_: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|Sunder]] the weapon of the next most effective combatant, or use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]] on all nearby opponents.

_Round 5_: Full attack against any nearby opponent, or use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]].

Use another quickened [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] if foes seem really dangerous.

A titan usually reserves its [[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate|gate]] and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Etherealness|etherealness]] abilities to escape a fight that is not going well.
