A titan is about 25 feet tall and weighs about 14,000 pounds.

Titans speak Abyssal, Common, Celestial, Draconic, and Giant.

### Combat

A titan’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as chaotic-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Change Shape (Su):** A titan can assume the form of any Small or Medium humanoid. The titan retains its oversized weapon special attack regardless of form.

**Oversized Weapon (Ex)**: A titan wields a great, two-handed warhammer (big enough for Gargantuan creatures) without penalty.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 20th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] (DC 23), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|cure critical wounds]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]] (DC 24), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]] (DC 22), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility Purge|invisibility purge]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Persistent Image|persistent image]] (DC 22)  

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Etherealness|etherealness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Word of Chaos|word of chaos]] (DC 22), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IX|summon nature’s ally IX]];  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate|gate]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Maze|maze]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]] (DC 26).   

In addition, titans of good or neutral alignment can use the following additional spell-like abilities. Caster level 20th. The save DCs are Charisma-based:  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]] (DC 21);  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Greater|greater restoration]].  

Titans of evil alignment can use the following additional spell-like abilities. Caster level 20th. The save DCs are Charisma-based:  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]] (DC 21);  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Crushing Hand|crushing hand]] (DC 26).  

### Tactics Round-By-Round

Titans enjoy combat and usually close with their foes. If that proves ineffective, they swiftly back off and pelt the foe with spell-like abilities and magical effects. Because of a titan’s Quicken Spell-Like Ability feat, it can use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] as a free action, and frequently attacks in melee while lashing out with this ability at the same time.

*Prior to combat*: [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility Purge|Invisibility purge]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]].

*Round 1*: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|Charge]] and attempt to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Sunder|sunder]] the weapon of the most dangerous foe. Hurl [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] at opponents standing away from the fight.

*Round 2*: Full attack against the disarmed opponent, and hurl [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] at other opponents.

*Round 3*: Back away from first opponent and use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Maze|maze]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]] on any spellcaster causing trouble.

*Round 4*: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Sunder|Sunder]] the weapon of the next most effective combatant, or use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]] on all nearby opponents.

*Round 5*: Full attack against any nearby opponent, or use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]].

Use another quickened [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] if foes seem really dangerous.

A titan usually reserves its [[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate|gate]] and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Etherealness|etherealness]] abilities to escape a fight that is not going well.
