A bugbear lurker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/bugbear/Bugbear|bugbear]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the bugbear sneaks. Many bugbear sneaks serve as scouts and assassins for hobgoblin armies. They sometimes become enamored of the act of killing itself, and use their natural affinity for moving quietly to become unseen murderers in cities.

A bugbear lurker speaks Common, Goblin, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: Using fast stealth to approach quickly, the bugbear lurker positions herself to land a surprise attack.
