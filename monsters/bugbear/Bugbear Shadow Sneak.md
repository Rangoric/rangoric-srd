A bugbear shadow sneak is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/bugbear/Bugbear|bugbear]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja|ninja]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadowdancer]], one of the bugbear sneaks. Many bugbear sneaks serve as scouts and assassins for hobgoblin armies. They sometimes become enamored of the act of killing itself, and use their natural affinity for moving quietly to become unseen murderers in cities.

A bugbear shadow sneak speaks Common and Goblin.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The shadow sneak uses hide in plain sight to ambush foes.

**Shadowdancer Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +5): 2/day—_[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadow illusion]]_ (+1 vs Will); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadow call]]_ (+5 vs Will).
