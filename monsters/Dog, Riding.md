# Dog, Riding

  - Medium Animal
  - **Hit Dice**: 2d8+4 (13 hp)
  - **Initiative**: +2
  - **Speed**: 40 ft. (8 squares)
  - **Armor Class**: 16 (+2 Dex, +4 natural), touch 12, flat-footed 14
  - **Base Attack**: +1; **CMB**: +3; **CMD**: 15 (19 vs. trip)
  - **Attack**: Bite +3 melee (1d6+3)
  - **Full Attack**: Bite +3 melee (1d6+3)
  - **Space/Reach**: 5 ft./5 ft.
  - **Special Attacks**: —
  - **Special Qualities**: Low-light vision, scent
  - **Saves**: Fort +5, Ref +5, Will +1
  - **Abilities**: Str 15, Dex 15, Con 15, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 6
  - **Skills**: Athletics +11 (+15 jumping), Fieldcraft +1\*, Perception +4
  - **Feats**: Skill Focus (Perception), TrackB
  - **Environment**: Temperate plains
  - **Organization**: Solitary or pack (5–12)
  - **Challenge Rating**: 1
  - **Advancement**: —
  - **Level Adjustment**: —

This category includes working breeds such as collies, huskies, and St. Bernards.

**Carrying Capacity**: A light load for a riding dog is up to 100 pounds; a medium load, 101–200 pounds; and a heavy load, 201–300 pounds. A riding dog can drag 1,500 pounds.

#### Combat

If trained for war, these animals can make trip attacks just as wolves do (see the Wolf entry). A riding dog can fight while carrying a rider, but the rider cannot also attack unless he or she succeeds on an Animal Handling check.

**Skills**: Riding dogs have a +4 racial bonus on Athletics checks.

\*Riding dogs have a +4 racial bonus on Fieldcraft checks when tracking by scent.
