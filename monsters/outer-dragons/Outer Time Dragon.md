This dragon’s cracked scales creak with frightening, patient power. Some of its horns are ancient and brittle, others new and pristine.

Guardians of history, time dragons are the most powerful of the outer dragons. Watchers and waiters, time dragons guard the universe against those that would interfere with the natural temporal order.

Time dragons speak Common, Draconic, and Elven; adult and older time dragons also speak Dwarven and Sylvan, and ancient and older ones Celestial and Infernal.

## Combat

**Breath Weapon (Su)**: A time dragon has a cone-shaped breath weapon that deals 2d10 points of electricity damage per age category (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragon, True#Combat|rules for true dragons]]).

**Immortal (Ex)**: Time dragons age, but don't die from old age.

**Read the Threads (Su)**: A time dragon gains a racial bonus on initiative checks equal to its age category. In addition, a time dragon is immune to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] condition.

**Alien Presence (Su)**: A creature that fails its saving throw against a time dragon's alien presence is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 5d6 rounds (or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] if it has 4 Hit Dice or fewer).

**Shifting Breath (Su)**: Instead of a cone of electricity, an old or older time dragon can use its breath weapon to displace creatures in time. Each creature in the cone must succeed at a Will save or be shifted forward in time a number of rounds equal to 1/2 the dragon's age category (DC equal to breath weapon). For an affected creature, it's as if no time passed. Creatures that succeed at their saves are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round.

**Second Chance (Su)**: Three times per day as an immediate action, an ancient or older time dragon can force a creature (including itself) to reroll any d20 roll. The target must use the result of the second roll.

**Time Travel (Su)**: Up to three times in its life, a great wyrm time dragon can travel to any point in time, taking with it a number of willing creatures equal to its Charisma modifier.
