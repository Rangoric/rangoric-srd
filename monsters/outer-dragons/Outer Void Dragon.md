This dragon’s ebony scales and horns are flecked with a substance that glows an eerie green. The folds of its wings reflect a starry sky.

Void dragons have been tainted by long exposure to the terrible alien entities that dwell in deep space. Though some continue to struggle against the inevitable tide of annihilation, many have embraced the encroaching void and exist only to feed and destroy.

Void dragons speak Abyssal, Aklo, Draconic, and Infernal; adult and older void dragons also speak Celestial and Ignan, and ancient and older ones Auran and Terran.

## Combat

**Breath Weapon (Su)**: A void dragon has a cone-shaped breath weapon that deals 2d8 points of cold damage per age category (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragon, True#Combat|rules for true dragons]]).

**Agile (Ex)**: Void dragons have good aerial maneuverability regardless of their age or size.

**Alien Presence (Su)**: : A void dragon's alien presence causes an opponent it hits to be overcome with dizziness, becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] (or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] if the target has 4 or fewer Hit Dice) for 5d6 rounds.

**Obliterate (Su)**: A young or older void dragon's bite deals an additional 2d6 points of negative energy damage. A creature reduced to 0 or fewer hit points by this attack is attacked against Fortitude, and on a hit is immediately slain and reduced to ashes (as the disintegrate spell). The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**Suffocating Breath (Su)**: Instead of dealing cold damage, a very young or older void dragon can breathe a cone-shaped suffocation effect. The breath attacks Fortitude, and an air-dependent creature it hits suffocates for a number of rounds equal to the dragon's age category. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**Void Gaze (Su)**: A creature within 30 feet of an adult or older void dragon is attacked against Will, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d6 rounds. This gaze attack is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.
