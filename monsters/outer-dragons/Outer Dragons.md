# Outer Dragons

Outer dragons are natives of the vast reaches of space, and can be met on many worlds. They are generally neutral, but their alignments vary more than those of other dragons. They radiate an alien presence in place of the frightful presence of other dragons.

The age categories, attacks, breath weapons and other rules the true dragons share are in [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragon, True|Dragon, True]]; each note below prints only what differs.
