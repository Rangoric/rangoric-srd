# Archon

Archons are celestials from a lawful good-aligned plane.

Archons speak Celestial, Infernal, and Draconic, but can speak with almost any creature because of their truespeech.

### Combat

Archons generally prefer to meet a foe head-on if it is prudent to do so, but if outmatched, they do what they can to even the odds (usually by employing hit-and run tactics or standing off and engaging a foe with magic before moving into melee).

Archons have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^archon|archon subtype]]: darkvision and low-light vision, an aura of menace, immunity to electricity and petrification, a bonus on defenses against poison, greater teleport at will, and truespeech.
