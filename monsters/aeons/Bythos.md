The bythos are guardians of time and planar travel—indeed, to bythos, the act of aging is nothing more than a highly specialized method of travel. Although bythos themselves have no additional method of traveling through time, they scour the multiverse, hunting for creatures that do have the ability to time-travel and may have abused this ability.

Far more often, though, bythos seek out abuses of planar travel, such as tears in reality, regions where planes overlap, or creatures that abuse the use of planar travel. In some cases, such distortions are ignored, but in others, a bythos or even a full tribunal comes to assess and repair the damage. In most cases, “repair” is analogous to the death of the creature responsible for the distortions, but placing such creatures in temporal stasis can also solve the problem.

While a bythos's body may seem to be made of smoke and vapor, it is strangely solid to the touch, feeling not dissimilar to dry stone. A bythos is 13 feet tall and weighs 600 pounds.

Bythos speak envisaging.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 18th; concentration +23): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ (+8 vs Will); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ (+10 vs Will); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Lock|dimensional lock]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Moment of Prescience|moment of prescience]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Temporal Stasis|temporal stasis]]_ (+13 vs Fortitude).

**Aging Strike (Su)**: If a bythos strikes a living target with two slam attacks in a single round, the bythos ages the creature, causing it to advance to the next age category if the strike hits it at +14 vs Fortitude. The victim gains all of the penalties from this aging and none of the bonuses. A venerable victim targeted by this ability dies if the strike hits it at +14 vs Fortitude. This process is reversible with greater restoration, limited wish, miracle, or wish. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Confusion Gaze|Confusion Gaze]] (Su)**: Confusion for 1d4 rounds, 30 feet, +14 vs Fortitude. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Temporal Strike|Temporal Strike]] (Su)**: As a standard action, a bythos can touch a creature or object to displace it from time. It attacks the target at +14 vs Fortitude, and on a hit the target disappears from the present moment and reappears in the same location 1d4 rounds later as if no time had passed. If an object occupies that space, the creature appears in the closest available space to its original location—this displacement does not cause the creature any additional harm. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.
