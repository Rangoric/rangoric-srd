# Aeon

Aeons are a race of neutral outsiders who roam the planes maintaining the balance of reality. They communicate wordlessly, by envisaging.

### Combat

Aeons have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^aeon|aeon subtype]]: immunity to cold, poison, and critical hits, resistance to electricity and fire, the wordless communication of envisaging, a bonus on Knowledge checks from their connection to the multiverse (extension of all), and a void form that adds a deflection bonus to their Armor Class.
