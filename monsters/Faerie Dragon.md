Related to pseudodragons, faerie dragons are whimsical, playful pranksters that spend most of their time either relaxing in cool forest glades or engaged in some sort of prank. Although such mischief is usually spontaneous, some faerie dragons have been known to spend months or even years in preparation of one truly spectacular joke. Travelers making their way through lands known to be inhabited by these good-natured—but sometimes annoying—tricksters are advised to keep up their guard lest they face all manner of creative annoyances (horses painted with berry juice, squirrels placed inside bedrolls, and clothing decorating high branches being a few examples).

Unlike true dragons, faerie dragons do not grow larger with age, but their scales do change color, starting with red and moving through the rainbow to reach dark violet at old age. Faerie dragons grow in power as spellcasters as they age (typically gaining levels in [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]), learning more powerful spells to complement their innate abilities. The spells of faerie dragons tend to focus on tools that can be used to create or enhance pranks, and illusion and enchantment spells are particularly popular.

Faerie dragons avoid combat unless there is no other option. If forced to fight, faerie dragons attempt to confound enemies rather than kill them, using both spells and breath weapon to weaken and scatter foes before fleeing. The only exception to this is when a faerie dragon's clan or allies are in danger. As capricious as these tiny dragons can be, many prove quick to bond with goodly creatures, especially fey and others they find interesting or hilarious—adventurers often fall into this latter camp, particularly those most outraged by a faerie dragon's tricks.

Faerie dragons sometimes ally with spellcasters, serving as curious, occasionally reckless, but always true familiars. An arcane spellcaster of at least 7th level with an alignment within 1 step of chaotic good who has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Familiar|Improved Familiar]] feat may select a faerie dragon as a familiar.

Faerie dragons speak Common, Draconic, Elven and Sylvan.

The faerie dragon as a fey of twelve ages, from wyrmling to great wyrm, is [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Faerie Dragon, Ages|Faerie Dragon, Ages]].

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 3rd; concentration +6): 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_ (self only).

**Spells**: A faerie dragon casts spells as a 3rd-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]].

**Spells known** (CL 3rd; concentration +6): 1st (6/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]_ (+4), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silent Image|silent image]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]_ (+4 vs Will); 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]_ (+3 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (+3 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Open-Close|open/close]]_.

**Breath Weapon (Su)**: 5-foot cone, euphoria for 1d6 rounds, +2 vs Fortitude. Creatures affected by euphoria are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]], and immune to fear effects for the duration. A faerie dragon can use this breath weapon once every 1d4 rounds. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
