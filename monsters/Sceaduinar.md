Sceaduinars are strange creatures born of pure entropy, the antithesis of creation and life. In the cold heart of the Negative Energy Plane, the un-substance of that realm coalesces into snowflake-like crystals, and it is from these strange formations that sceaduinars arise, breaking free from their jagged “eggs” fully grown. They hate the living and the undead with equal passion, perhaps out of jealousy for those who have a spark of life (even if that spark is provided by a corruption of life in the form of undeath), though they usually ignore creatures from the Outer Planes. They believe their positive energy counterparts, the jyoti, long ago stole their ability to create, breaking the parallel between the two energy planes and forcing these void-dwellers into an unwanted role of pure destruction.

In a way, their hatred parallels that of another native of the Negative Energy Plane—the nightshade. Yet despite their similar goals, the sceaduinars see nightshades as just another corruption of life worthy of destruction—even though very few sceaduinars are powerful enough to directly oppose one of these deadly undead. Sceaduinars are quite intelligent, yet they have no real society to speak of. When they gather together, it is always to form a larger band to strike against a particularly dangerous foe.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 9th; concentration +12): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entropic Shield|entropic shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hide from Undead|hide from undead]]_ (+4 vs Will); At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_ (+3 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_ (self only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_; 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Knell|death knell]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Serious Wounds|inflict serious wounds]]_ (+6 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antilife Shell|antilife shell]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Harm|harm]]_ (+9 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]_ (+8 vs Fortitude).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Entropic Flesh|Entropic Flesh]] (Ex)**: Any creature that hits a sceaduinar with a melee attack takes 1d6 points of negative energy damage. Attacking with a weapon that provides reach allows a creature to avoid taking this damage.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Entropic Touch|Entropic Touch]] (Ex)**: A sceaduinar's natural attacks can strike incorporeal creatures as if they were ghost touch weapons. All of a sceaduinar's natural attacks deal +1d6 points of negative energy damage to the target. This energy does not heal creatures healed by inflict spells.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Void Child|Void Child]] (Ex)**: Sceaduinars are immune to effects that target souls (such as trap the soul) or require knowledge of a creature's identity (such as scrying). When one is slain, it cannot be restored to life by magic save by a miracle or wish, or by divine intervention.
