Breath Weapon (Su): Cone of poisonous gas, 80 feet, as a free action every 1d4 rounds, initial damage 3d6 temporary Con, secondary damage 2d6 temporary Con, Fort half (DC 58). The DC is Constitution-based.

Magic Immunity (Ex): An iron colossus is immune to all magical and supernatural effects, except as follows. An electricity effect restores 1 hit point to the iron colossus for each 3 points of damage it would otherwise deal.

Rustproof (Ex): An iron colossus is not affected by rust attacks, unlike smaller [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Iron Golem|iron golems]].

### Construction

An iron colossus’s body is sculpted from 150,000 pounds of pure iron. The colossus costs 1,000,000 gp to create, which includes 100,500 gp for the body. Assembling the body requires a successful [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (armorsmithing) or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (weaponsmithing) check (DC 42). The creator must be 35th level and able to [[rangoric-srd/feats/Epic Spellcasting|cast epic spells]]. Completing the ritual drains 50,000 XP from the creator and requires [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cloudkill|cloudkill]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Geas-Quest|geas/quest]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]], and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph Any Object|polymorph any object]].
