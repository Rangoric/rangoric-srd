These omnivorous rodents thrive almost anywhere.

#### Combat

Rats usually run away. They bite only as a last resort.

**Skills**: Rats have a +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks, and a +8 racial bonus on Acrobatics checks. Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|climb speed]], a rat is better at climbing.

A rat uses its Dexterity modifier instead of its Strength modifier for Athletics checks to climb and swim.

Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]], a rat is better at swimming.
