Jinmenjus are trees that grow in hilly regions far from civilized lands, and prey on those who come too close. They are remarkably intelligent and crafty, and use both scent and magical compulsion to lure prey.

Jinmenjus speak Common.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 13th; concentration +15): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Share Memory|share memory]]_ (with a range of 55 feet, targeting the jinmenju and 1 creature in range, +4 vs Will); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sculpt Sound|sculpt sound]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_(+6).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Enticing Head-Fruits|Enticing Head-Fruits]] (Su)**: Any creature that begins its turn within 5 feet of a jinmenju is attacked at +12 vs Will, and on a hit is magically compelled to immediately grab a head-fruit and eat it. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect. A creature it misses is immune to that jinmenju's enticing head-fruits for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. Anyone who takes a bite out of one suffers from the following effect.

[[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Head-Fruit Poison|Head-Fruit Poison]]: ingested; _attack_ +12 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d3 Wis damage and confused for 1 round; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Intoxicating Stench|Intoxicating Stench]] (Su)**: Once per day as a swift action, a jinmenju can cause its fruits to emit an unnaturally sweet aroma in a 60-foot spread for 6 rounds. All creatures within the area are attacked at +12 vs Will each round, and on a hit are captivated. A captivated creature takes no actions except to approach the jinmenju via the most direct route possible. If this path leads it into a dangerous area or the jinmenju attacks it, the effect attacks the captivated creature again, and a miss ends it. This is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Unsettling Drone|Unsettling Drone]] (Su)**: A jinmenju emits a low, persistent hum that unnerves living creatures that hear it. Those within 30 feet of it are attacked at +8 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] until they leave the affected area and for 1d4 rounds thereafter. A creature it misses is immune to that jinmenju's unsettling drone for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.
