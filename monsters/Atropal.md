An atropal’s natural weapons are treated as epic, evil, and lawful-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

#### Combat

Spell-Like Abilities: Caster level 30th; save DC 26 + spell level. The save DCs are Charisma-based  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blasphemy|blasphemy]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Greater Undead|create greater undead]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead|create undead]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cone of Cold|cone of cold]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spectral Hand|spectral hand]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]];  

5/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Project Image|project image]], weird. 

Rebuke/Command Undead (Su): Atropals can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|rebuke or command]] undead as a cleric with a level equal to the atropal’s HD + 6.

Negative Energy Aura (Su): A 30-foot-radius spread negative energy aura spreads from each atropal. All undead in the field (including the atropal) are treated as if having [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^turn-resistance|turn resistance]] +20 and a negative energy version of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fast-healing|fast healing]] 20. Living creatures in the aura are treated as having ten [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative levels]] unless they have some sort of negative energy protection or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Evil|protection from evil]]. Creatures with 10 or fewer HD or levels perish (and, at the atropal’s option, rise as [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Spectre|spectres]] under the atropal’s command 1 minute later).

Constitution Drain (Su): When the atropal hits a living opponent with a touch attack, the opponent takes 5 points of permanent Constitution [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], or 10 points on a critical hit. The atropal heals 20 points of damage, or 40 points on a critical hit, whenever it drains Constitution, gaining any excess as temporary hit points. These temporary hit points last a maximum of 1 hour. The attack allows a Fortitude save (DC 59). The DC is Charisma-based.

Energy Drain (Su): When the atropal hits with a ranged touch attack (a ray of darkness that it shoots from one eye to a range of 400 feet), the resultant [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|energy drain]] bestows four [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative levels]], or eight negative levels on a critical hit. For each negative level bestowed on an opponent, the atropal heals 10 points of damage, or 20 on a critical hit, gaining any excess as temporary hit points. These temporary hit points last a maximum of 1 hour. After 24 hours have passed, the afflicted opponent must attempt a Fortitude save (DC 59) for each negative level. If successful, the negative level goes away with no harm to the creature; otherwise, the creature’s level decreases by one. The DC is Charisma-based.

Regeneration (Ex): Atropals take normal damage from good weapons or sentient weapons (or otherwise living weapons).

Summon Nightcrawler (Sp): Five times per day an atropal can summon a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nightshade/Nightcrawler|nightcrawler]].

Abomination Traits: Immune to polymorphing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrification]], and other form-altering attacks; not subject to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|energy drain]], ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], or ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]], or death from massive damage; immune to mind-affecting effects; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|fire resistance]] 20; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|cold resistance]] 20; [[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]; [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]] at will; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^blindsight|blindsight]] 500 ft.; telepathy out to 1,000 ft.

Undead Traits: Immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|poison]], sleep, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralysis]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Disease|disease]], death, effects, necromantic effects, mind-affecting effects, and any effect requiring a Fortitude save unless it also works on objects. Not subject to critical hits, subdual damage, ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]], ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|energy drain]]. Negative energy heals. Not at risk of death from massive damage, but destroyed at 0 hit points or less. Darkvision 60 ft. Cannot be raised; resurrection works only if creature is willing.
