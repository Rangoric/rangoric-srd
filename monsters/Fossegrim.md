Pale and possessing unnatural beauty, fossegrims are wicked fey who haunt idyllic waterfalls, where they lure the unsuspecting near with song and the promise of treasure, only to drown them. Well built and elflike in appearance, fossegrims are gifted musicians, and their lairs often echo with the sound of bewitching music. They are never without their enchanting instruments.

Fossegrims speak Common and Sylvan.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 9th; concentration +14): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Walk|water walk]]_; 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Torrent|hydraulic torrent]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Drowning Touch|Drowning Touch]] (Su)**: A fossegrim can flood the lungs of a creature that is willing, is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], is affected by its enchanted music ability, touches it while it's in treasure form, or is touched by it (traditionally by kissing the creature on the lips). If the target cannot breathe water, it cannot hold its breath and immediately begins to drown slowly. On its turn, the water attacks the target at +6 vs Fortitude, and on a miss it coughs up the water; on a hit, it falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] at 0 hp. On the next round, the water attacks a fallen target again at +6 vs Fortitude, and on a hit it drops to -1 hit points and is dying; on the next round it attacks again, and on a hit the target loses 1d6 hit points. On the first miss, the water clears from the target's lungs and the target stabilizes. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Enchanting Music|Enchanting Music]] (Su)**: As a full-round action, a fossegrim can play a musical stringed instrument (usually a harp or violin) and target a single creature within 45 feet that can hear and see the fossegrim with an effect identical to the unnatural lust spell (CL 9th; +9 vs Will). This action provokes an attack of opportunity. If the target touches or kisses the fossegrim, the fey can affect the target with its drowning touch ability. This is a sonic mind-affecting compulsion effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Transparency|Transparency]] (Su)**: When underwater, a fossegrim's body becomes transparent, effectively rendering it invisible. It can become visible or transparent at will as a free action.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Treasure Form|Treasure Form]] (Su)**: As a standard action when completely underwater, a fossegrim can create an illusion causing it to resemble a Medium pile of gold and silver coins, glittering jewels, and valuable art objects. If a fossegrim speaks, moves, attacks, or uses any of its special abilities or spell-like abilities while in treasure form, the illusion dissipates. The illusion attacks at +9 vs Will, and a miss disbelieves it. If a living creature within the fossegrim's melee reach touches or physically interacts with the illusion, the fossegrim reaches out to kiss or otherwise touch that creature, automatically affecting the creature with its drowning touch ability. The fossegrim can maintain this illusion indefinitely and can revert to its normal natural form as a free action. This is a mind-affecting illusion (glamer) effect. The attack bonus to disbelieve is Charisma-based.
