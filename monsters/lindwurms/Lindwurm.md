# Lindwurm

Lindwurms are powerful, primeval dragons of the wild regions far north of where most civilizations dare to tread: immense, serpentine and wingless, yet able to fly. Favored, if not by the gods, then by some primal intelligence of the mysterious world of the fey, a lindwurm bestows a powerful curse on any who manage to slay it (its [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Death Curse|death curse]]).

Lindwurms speak Aklo, Draconic, and Sylvan.

Sorcerers can carry a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Lindwurm (Mutated Bloodline)|lindwurm bloodline]], and barbarians and skalds can channel a lindwurm through its death curse rage power: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Lindwurm Death Curse, Cairn (Rage Power)|lindwurm death curse, cairn]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Lindwurm Death Curse, Crag (Rage Power)|lindwurm death curse, crag]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Lindwurm Death Curse, Fjord (Rage Power)|lindwurm death curse, fjord]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Lindwurm Death Curse, Ice (Rage Power)|lindwurm death curse, ice]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Lindwurm Death Curse, Taiga (Rage Power)|lindwurm death curse, taiga]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Lindwurm Death Curse, Tarn (Rage Power)|lindwurm death curse, tarn]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Lindwurm Death Curse, Tor (Rage Power)|lindwurm death curse, tor]].
