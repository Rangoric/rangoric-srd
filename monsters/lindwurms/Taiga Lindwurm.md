A beast covered with hundreds of spines, the taiga lindwurm is a terror to all those who venture through the arctic forests of the north. Taiga lindwurms take down foes and prey first with their electrifying breath and finish their victims off with their devastating bite.

In winter, when light is sparse and the nights are long, it is easy for a taiga lindwurm to blend in with the coniferous forests it dwells in, hiding among the trees and ambushing imprudent travelers or unwary adventurers setting up camp. A taiga lindwurm often lies in wait for days—sometimes weeks—just for the opportunity to maim and devour unsuspecting victims. When this subtler tactic does not work, a taiga lindwurm simply rampages through the woods in order to find nearby settlements to pillage, taking twisted delight in shocking the inhabitants before shredding them with its massive jaws or destructive forelimbs. In any event, taiga lindwurms rarely venture far from their woodland territories save for short flights to attack outlying villages.

A taiga lindwurm is 50 feet long and weighs just over 13,000 pounds.

Taiga lindwurms speak Aklo, Draconic and Sylvan.

### Combat

**Breath Weapon (Su)**: Once every 1d4 rounds as a standard action, a taiga lindwurm can expel a 60-foot cone of electrified vapor, dealing 21d8 points of electricity damage to all creatures struck (+20 vs Reflex; a miss halves). The vapor itself persists for 1d4 rounds, filling its cone-shaped area with electrified mist that deals 4d6 points of electricity damage (no attack against a defense) to any creature that ends its turn in the mist. The mist does not hinder vision. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Death Curse|Death Curse]] (Su)**: When a creature slays a taiga lindwurm, the slayer is affected by the curse of electrocution, which the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Lindwurm Death Curse, Taiga (Rage Power)|lindwurm death curse, taiga]] rage power also bestows. _Curse of Electrocution_: _attack_ +19 vs Will; _effect_ the creature gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Vulnerabilities|vulnerability to electricity]].

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: Bite—injury; _attack_ +20 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 10 rounds; _effect_ 4d6 electricity damage and 1d8 Dex drain; _cure_ 3 consecutive misses.

**Spines (Ex)**: Any creature that makes a melee attack against a taiga lindwurm takes 1d6 points of piercing damage per attack from the lindwurm's spines. A melee weapon with reach provides protection from these spines.
