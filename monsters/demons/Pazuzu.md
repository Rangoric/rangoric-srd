Pazuzu is among the oldest and most powerful of all demon lords. His Abyssal realm is located in one of that plane's greatest rifts. This vertical realm includes an immense city, at the heart of which can be found Pazuzu's personal rookery and palace.

Pazuzu appears as a four-winged, 15-foot-tall fiend. He takes great delight in corrupting mortals, particularly those of a pure heart and soul, offering them any one _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ in return for nothing but their innocence.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 30th; concentration +43): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|detect law]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ (winged animals only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]]_ (+21); At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection|astral projection]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blasphemy|blasphemy]]_\* (+20 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Winds|control winds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]]_\*, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Person|dominate person]]_\* (+18 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shapechange|shapechange]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_\* (+18 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unhallow|unhallow]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_\* (+17 vs Will); 3/day—quickened _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Person|dominate person]]_\* (+18 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] demons, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Persuasion|symbol of persuasion]]_ (+19 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sympathy|sympathy]]_ (+21 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Whirlwind|whirlwind]]_\*; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Monster|dominate monster]]_ (+22 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Time Stop|time stop]]_\*, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_\*; \* Pazuzu can use the mythic version of this ability in his realm.

**Aura of Locusts (Su)**: Pazuzu exhales clouds of locusts. In any round in which he does not move more than 5 feet, he is surrounded by a 10-foot-radius spread of these creatures. Any creature that enters this area is attacked at +33 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Avian Mastery|Avian Mastery]] (Su)**: Any creature flying under its own power (flight from a source other than a spell, spell-like ability, or magic item) that attempts to attack Pazuzu with a melee attack is attacked at +30 vs Will. On a hit, the creature can't follow through with the attack, that part of its action is lost, and it can't directly attack Pazuzu for 1d4 rounds. Once this ability misses a creature, it is immune to this ability for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Hear Name|Hear Name]] (Su)**: Pazuzu hears his name whenever it is spoken, regardless of distance—this ability functions even across planar boundaries. If a creature speaks Pazuzu's name aloud three times in the same breath, Pazuzu automatically knows that creature's precise location and name. If Pazuzu is on the same plane as someone who speaks his name three times in a single breath, he can immediately attempt to possess that creature.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: Sting—injury; attack +33 vs Fortitude; frequency 1/round for 6 rounds; effect 1d6 Wisdom drain and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]]; cure 3 consecutive misses.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Possession (Pazuzu)|Possession]] (Su)**: Once per day as a swift action, Pazuzu can attempt to possess a single living creature within 1 mile, provided he knows the target's name. The attempt attacks the target at +33 vs Will, and a miss resists it. A lawful creature gains a +10 bonus to its Will Defense against it, and a good creature gains a +20 bonus (these bonuses stack). If the attempt misses, the creature is immune to possession attempts by Pazuzu for the rest of its life. If it hits, Pazuzu can control the possessed creature from afar. While possessing a creature, Pazuzu automatically knows every thought that creature has. By concentrating, he can sense the creature's surroundings using that creature's senses. As a swift action, he can cause the creature to perform any ability it can perform on its own. Pazuzu can use any of his spell-like abilities through a possessed target, with the effects resolving as if the possessed creature had created the effect. Possession is permanent, but Pazuzu can only possess one creature at a time. When Pazuzu isn't actively controlling the target, it can take its own actions. Dispel chaos or dispel evil ends this possession effect as if it were an enchantment spell, but unless the caster of the spell succeeds at a DC 30 caster level check, as a swift action Pazuzu can attempt to possess the caster as he is driven out of the target. A creature possessed by Pazuzu is immune to protection from evil, magic circle against evil, and any similar effects. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Profane Wishcraft|Profane Wishcraft]] (Su)**: A creature that accepts a wish from Pazuzu immediately becomes chaotic evil if the wish hits it at +33 vs Will. A creature that becomes chaotic evil in this way gains the benefits of a good hope spell for 1 week, followed by the effects of crushing despair for 1d6 months (CL 30th). The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swarm Master|Swarm Master]] (Su)**: Pazuzu is immune to swarm damage and other swarm effects (such as distraction). As a swift action, he can direct the movement of any swarm within 30 feet.
