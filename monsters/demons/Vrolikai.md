A vrolikai is 14 feet tall but weighs only 500 pounds. Unlike other demons, it does not form from a sinful soul—it instead manifests from a nabasu demon that returns to the Abyss after growing to maturity on the Material Plane. Not all nabasus survive this transformation, but those who do become powerful indeed—vrolikai usually rule large regions of unclaimed Abyssal land, and often serve as assassins or ambassadors to demon lords in need of an agent in a distant realm.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 19th; concentration +27): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_ (+13 vs Will); 3/day—quickened _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ (+10 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster, Mass|mass hold monster]]_ (+17 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 6, 1 marilith 50% or 1d4 glabrezus 75%), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]]_ (+16 vs Fortitude).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Black Flame Knives|Black Flame Knives]] (Su)**: A vrolikai can manifest daggers made of crystallized black flames in each of its four hands as a free action. These weapons function as +1 daggers that bestow one permanent negative level on a successful hit. It attacks at +17 vs Fortitude, and a miss negates the negative level, although on a critical hit it hits automatically. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Death-Stealing Gaze|Death-Stealing Gaze]] (Su)**: 1 permanent negative level, 30 ft., +17 vs Fortitude. Creatures slain by these negative levels become juju zombies under the vrolikai's control. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**Madness (Su)**: A creature stung by a vrolikai's tail is attacked at +17 vs Will, and on a hit takes 1d6 points of Charisma drain and becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d4 rounds. On a miss, the victim is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d4 rounds as strange visions assault its mind. This is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Multiweapon Mastery|Multiweapon Mastery]] (Ex)**: A vrolikai never takes penalties on its attack roll when fighting with multiple weapons.
