Slimy demodands are more muscular than shaggy demodands, but aren't as lithe or quick as their tarry brethren. They have froglike heads, similar to those of the shaggy demodands, but their constantly darting eyes give them a more feral look. A slimy demodand stands 6 feet tall and weighs 500 pounds.

Slimy demodands often serve as shock troops in demodand armies, but they are also frequently put in charge of gathering and keeping slaves. Slimy demodands have no magical mind control powers, but prefer to rely upon intimidation and physical threats anyway to manage their slaves and keep them in line.

Slimy demodands speak Abyssal, Celestial and Common.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 16th; concentration +19): Constant—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]*; At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* (DC 15), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]* (DC 17); 3/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Fog|acid fog]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]*; 1/day—[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 6, 1d4 tarry demodands 50%).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Acid (Slimy Demodand)|Acid]] (Su)**: A slimy demodand is coated in an ever-dripping layer of acid that deals an extra 2d6 points of acid damage on a successful natural attack. In addition, opponents that successfully strike a slimy demodand with an unarmed strike or natural attack take 2d6 points of acid damage.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Dread Claws|Dread Claws]] (Ex)**: A slimy demodand adds 1-1/2 times its strength bonus on all attack rolls made when using its claws.
