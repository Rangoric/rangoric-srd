A con artist is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Con artists are never what they first appear to be. They hustle the gullible using confidence schemes.

A con artist speaks Common, Draconic, and Elven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The bard uses her potion of invisibility to hide and minor image to make illusory combatants. If pressed, she uses suggestion to make her enemies leave the fight.

**Bard spells known** (CL 6th; concentration +10): 2nd (4/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ (DC 16), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Minor Image|minor image]]_ (DC 17), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_ (DC 16); 1st (5/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Comprehend Languages|comprehend languages]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silent Image|silent image]]_ (DC 16), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ventriloquism|ventriloquism]]_ (DC 16); 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (DC 15), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]_.
