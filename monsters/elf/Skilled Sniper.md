A skilled sniper is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The skilled sniper waits for the perfect opportunity to strike.

A skilled sniper speaks Common, Dwarven, and Elven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The rogue picks a perch that's hard to reach and waits for targets to come into range.

**During Combat**: The rogue uses her sleep arrow against ranged attackers or anyone she needs to keep alive.
