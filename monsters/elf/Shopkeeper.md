A shopkeeper is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Commoner|commoner]]. A shopkeeper stocks a mix of useful supplies and gear for townsfolk and adventurers alike. The stat block above can be used for any typical merchant in a cosmopolitan settlement.

A shopkeeper speaks Common, Elven, Gnome, and Halfling.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The commoner calls for help, and uses alchemical items to delay attackers.
