A swampwalker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. The swampwalker is a savage predator of the stinking marsh. He is familiar with its threats and uses them as tools to defeat his opponents.

A swampwalker speaks Common and Elven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin and pass without trace.

**During Combat**: The ranger attacks from stealth using his bow. He might drag a Small opponent underwater, relying on Endurance to outlast his drowning foe (though he has his potion of water breathing just in case).

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, the ranger's statistics are **AC** 16, touch 12, flat-footed 15.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 6th; concentration +8): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Snare|snare]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ (2).
