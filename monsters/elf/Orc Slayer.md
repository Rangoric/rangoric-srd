An orc slayer is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. The orc slayer is driven by vengeance to kill all orcs.

An orc slayer speaks Common, Elven, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin, cat's grace, and resist energy (fire).

**During Combat**: If ranger acts in the surprise round, she casts entangle. She prefers to attack at range and from cover.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, cat's grace, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **Init** +4; **AC** 20, touch 15, flat-footed 16; **Ref** +11; **Melee** masterwork elven curve blade +13/+8 (1d10+1/18-20); **Ranged** +1 longbow +13/+8 (1d8+1/×3); **Dex** 19; **CMD** +24; **Skills** Acrobatics +11, Animal Handling +10, Stealth +14.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 5th; concentration +6): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_.
