A master storycrafter is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] and 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Wandering Chronicler|wandering chronicler]]. These chroniclers travel with intrepid adventurers to record tales of their exploits.

A master storycrafter speaks Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Elf, Gnome, Halfling, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The wandering chronicler casts heroism and protection from arrows.

**During Combat**: The chronicler casts shield on herself, then supports allies with bardic performance and illusions.

**Base Statistics**: Without heroism and protection from arrows, the wandering chronicler's statistics are **Fort** +8; **Ref** +11; **Will** +13; **DR** none; **Melee** quarterstaff +9/+4 (1d6-1); **Ranged** dagger +12/+7 (1d4-1/19-20); **Skills** -2 on all skills.
