An officer is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Aristocrat|aristocrat]]. The officer is a capable and seasoned commander, and has earned the loyalty of her troops.

An officer speaks Common and Elven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The aristocrat directs her troops from horseback, saving her personal attention for the toughest foes. She uses potions of cure light wounds to save her most wounded subordinates from death.
