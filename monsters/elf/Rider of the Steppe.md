A rider of the steppe is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 14 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. A rider of the steppe works individually or in a group to bring safety and law to the wild and arid flatlands, often protecting caravans and traveling dignitaries.

A rider of the steppe speaks Common, Elven, and Goblin.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The paladin tries to remain out of melee range and attack enemies with her bow.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 14th; concentration +15): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.
