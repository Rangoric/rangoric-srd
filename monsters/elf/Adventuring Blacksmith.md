An adventuring blacksmith is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. Smiths who craft weapons may also be skilled in their use.

An adventuring blacksmith speaks Common, Dwarven, and Elven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The fighter stands and deals mighty blows until her foes drop or she dies on her feet.
