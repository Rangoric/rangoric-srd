A blade lord is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 20 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The blade lord has attained mastery with swords by forsaking all other weapons.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The rogue uses Whirlwind Attack if engaging multiple foes, otherwise focusing all attacks with her longsword against a target whose defenses the weapon easily ignores.
