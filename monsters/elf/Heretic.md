A heretic is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Adept|adept]]. The heretic seeks to convert the faithful away from a church that blinds them with lies.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The adept casts bull's strength and drinks his potion of shield of faith.

**During Combat**: The adept blasts his enemies with divine fire and lightning.

**Base Statistics**: Without bull's strength and shield of faith, the adept's statistics are **AC** 10, touch 10, flat-footed 10; **Melee** masterwork spear +5 (1d8/×3); **Ranged** masterwork spear +5 (1d8/×3); **Str** 11; **CMB** +4; **CMD** 14.

**Adept spells prepared** (CL 8th; concentration +11): 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]_ (+7 vs Reflex); 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]_(+5 vs Reflex); 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]_ (+5 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guidance|guidance]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Purify Food and Drink|purify food and drink]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]_.
