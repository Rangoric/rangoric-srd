A mithral master is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 19 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. The mithral master is a gleaming symbol of honor.

A mithral master speaks Common and Elven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The paladin casts delay poison and resist energy (fire) on himself and greater magic weapon on his longsword.

**During Combat**: The paladin fights on horseback or on foot as the situation warrants. He casts eagle's splendor to enhance his smite evil attacks, but otherwise relies on standard melee tactics and healing himself with lay on hands.

**Base Statistics**: Without delay poison, greater magic weapon, and resist energy the paladin's statistics are **Immune** charm, compulsion, disease, fear, sleep; **Resist** none; **Melee** +1 axiomatic longsword +26/+21/+16/+11 (1d8+6/17-20).

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 19th; concentration +22): At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]*.

**Paladin spells prepared** (CL 16th; concentration +19): 4th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]*; 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon, Greater|greater magic weapon]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|prayer]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Blindness-Deafness|remove blindness/deafness]]*; 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]* (2), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*; 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]* (2), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]*.
