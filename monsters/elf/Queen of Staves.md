A queen of staves is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] and 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Abjuration School|abjuration]] school) and 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Eldritch Knight|eldritch knight]]. A lone, lightly armored traveler carrying a walking staff, a queen of staves mixes spell power with whirling quarterstaff attacks.

A queen of staves speaks Common, Draconic, Elven, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The eldritch knight casts shield and displacement on herself, then casts lightning bolt and magic missile before casting mirror image and entering melee.
