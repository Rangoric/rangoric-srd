A fiendslayer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 13 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. The fiendslayer's ultimate goal is to rid the mortal world of evil outsiders.

A fiendslayer speaks Abyssal, Celestial, Common, Elven, and Infernal.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin, bear's endurance, delay poison, longstrider, protection from energy (fire), and resist energy (electricity).

**During Combat**: If fighting demons or devils, the ranger applies oil of bless weapon to one weapon and drinks her potion of heroism.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, bear's endurance, delay poison, longstrider, protection from energy, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **AC** 24, touch 16, flat-footed 18; **hp** 99; **Fort** +10; **Immune** sleep; **Resist** none; **Speed** 30; **Con** 12; **Skills** Acrobatics +15.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 10th; concentration +12): 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]*; 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear's endurance]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]*; 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]* (2).
