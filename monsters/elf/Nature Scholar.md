A nature scholar is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Loremaster|loremaster]]. More aggressive than most other loremasters, nature scholars use terrain and the magic of the natural world to avoid enemies while calling down destruction.

A nature scholar speaks Auran, Common, Draconic, Druidic, Elven, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The loremaster drinks her potion of resist energy (fire) and casts freedom of movement.

**During Combat**: The loremaster casts call lightning storm and flame strike.

**Base Statistics**: Without resist energy and freedom of movement, the loremaster's statistics are **Defensive Abilities** none; **Resist** none.
