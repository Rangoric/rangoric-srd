A storm sorcerer is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. The storm sorcerer battles his enemies with wind and lightning, reveling in nature's destructive power.

A storm sorcerer speaks Auran, Common, Draconic, and Elven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The sorcerer casts mage armor.

**During Combat**: The sorcerer favors his electricity spells, casting lightning bolt or scorching ray, or using his spectral hand to deliver shocking grasp attacks. He prefers ranged combat, using a scroll of fly or levitate to avoid opponents on the ground.

**Base Statistics**: Without mage armor, the sorcerer's base statistics are **AC** 14, touch 14, flat-footed 11.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 6th; concentration +9): 6/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Elemental Bloodline|elemental ray]] (1d6+3 electricity).
