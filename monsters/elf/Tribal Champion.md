A tribal champion is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] and 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Duelist|duelist]]. These brave warriors gain prestige within their tribes and clans by challenging and defeating enemy spellcasters.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The duelist uses Mobility to press her advantage against spellcasters. In the thick of the fray, she uses parry and Combat Expertise to augment her defenses.
