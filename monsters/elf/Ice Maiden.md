An ice maiden is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] and 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Disciple|dragon disciple]]. With the patience of elves and the majesty of dragons, ice maidens are masters of subtle machinations—and of the battlefield.

An ice maiden speaks Auran, Common, Draconic, Elven, and Goblin.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The dragon disciple casts stoneskin.

**During Combat**: The dragon disciple casts shield and spell resistance on herself, then takes flight and rains down destructive spells augmented with Empower Spell.

**Base Statistics**: Without stoneskin, the dragon disciple's statistics are **DR** none.
