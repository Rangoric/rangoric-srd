A green warden is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] and 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Conjuration School|conjuration]] school) and 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Arcane Archer|arcane archer]]. Protectors of the forest, green wardens are sworn to defend their sylvan homes from enemy encroachment, using magic arrows to kill from the trees' canopy.

A green warden speaks Common, Elven, and Goblin.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The arcane archer attempts to start combat from a hard-to-reach spot, such as a high tree branch or steep elevation. He casts heroism and protection from arrows on himself from scrolls.

**During Combat**: The archer keeps his distance and uses his magic arrows first. He casts glitterdust and web to slow down any approaching enemies, using imbue arrow to increase the range of such spells if needed. He uses Arcane Armor Training each round.

**Base Statistics**: Without heroism and protection from arrows, the archer's base statistics are **Senses** Perception +12; **Fort** +5; **Ref** +7; **Will** +8; **DR** none; **Melee** +2 short sword +12/+7 (1d6+4/19-20); **Ranged** masterwork composite longbow +16/+11 (1d8+5/×3); **Skills** Arcana +7 (+9 to identify magic item properties), Athletics +8, Knowledge (arcana, nature) +7, Perception +12, Stealth +12.

**Conjurer Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 3rd; concentration +4): 4/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Conjuration School|acid dart]].

**Conjurer spells prepared** (CL 3rd; concentration +4; arcane spell failure 15%): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glitterdust|glitterdust]]_ (+3 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]_ (+3 vs Reflex); 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]_ (2, +2 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]_ (2, +2 vs Will); 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_ (+1 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (+1 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_; **Opposition Schools**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Divination School|divination]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Necromancy School|necromancy]].
