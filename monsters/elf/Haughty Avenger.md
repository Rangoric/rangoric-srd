A haughty avenger is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. Haughty avengers often serve as diplomats and bodyguards.

A haughty avenger speaks Common and Elven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The paladin uses her quick speed and athletic skill to outmaneuver opponents, wearing them down with smite evil.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 3rd; concentration +5): At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]*.
