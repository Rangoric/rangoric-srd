A jungle wizard is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Transmutation School|transmutation]] school). Jungle wizards live in harmony with nature. They're frequently mistaken for druids, and often use such misunderstandings to their advantage. Many jungle wizards use natural materials for their magical gear, such as large leaves or hides for spellbooks and scrolls, unworked tree branches for wands, or grasses that can be knotted into the shapes of rings.

A jungle wizard speaks Common, Draconic, Elven, Gnoll, Goblin, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The wizard casts mage armor. When she prepares spells, she uses physical enhancement to increase her Constitution. She studies the combat area for the best places to use spells like grease and web, then hides in ambush.

**During Combat**: The wizard casts web on her opponents or in their path (especially if there is a pit or ravine present). She casts summon swarm into the web. If trapped opponents are escaping from the web, she casts empowered burning hands on them.

**Base Statistics**: Without mage armor, the wizard's statistics are **AC** 14, touch 13, flat-footed 11.

**Transmuter spells prepared** (CL 5th; concentration +9): 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]_, empowered*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]* (+6 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Swarm|summon swarm]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]_ (+6 vs Reflex); 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]_ (+6 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Fatigue|touch of fatigue]]_ (+4 vs Fortitude); **Opposition Schools**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Divination School|divination]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Enchantment School|enchantment]].
