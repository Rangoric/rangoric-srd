A battle mage (wizard) is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elf/Elf|elf]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Universalist School|universalist]] school). These mercenary wizards are able to fill many roles, and demand a high fee for their versatility.

A battle mage (wizard) speaks Common, Draconic, Elven, Orc, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The wizard uses her wand to cast mage armor.

**During Combat**: The wizard attacks with scorching ray, her longbow, and hand of the apprentice. If forced into melee, she casts mirror image and obscuring mist.

**Base Statistics**: Without mage armor, the wizard's statistics are **AC** 12, touch 12, flat-footed 10.

**Wizard spells prepared** (CL 3rd; concentration +6): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]_ (DC 13), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_.
