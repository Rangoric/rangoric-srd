The statistics presented here describe small, nonvenomous lizards of perhaps a foot or two in length, such as an iguana.

#### Combat

Lizards prefer flight to combat, but they can bite painfully if there is no other option.

**Skills**: Lizards have a +8 racial bonus on Acrobatics checks. Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|climb speed]], they are also better at climbing. Lizards use their Dexterity modifier instead of their Strength modifier for Athletics checks to climb.
