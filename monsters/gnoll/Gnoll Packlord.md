A gnoll packlord is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnoll/Gnoll|gnoll]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/barbarian/Pack Rager (Archetype)|pack rager]] archetype), one of the gnoll barbarians. Gnoll barbarians feed off one another's rage, and use pack tactics to savage their enemies.

A gnoll packlord speaks Gnoll.

### Combat

**Base Statistics**: When she's not raging, the barbarian's statistics are **AC** 20, touch 11, flat-footed 19; **hp** 104; **Fort** +14; **Will** +5; **Melee** +1 falchion +15/+10 (2d4+8/18–20); **Ranged** masterwork javelin +11/+6 (1d6+5); **Str** 20; **Con** 17; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 25.
