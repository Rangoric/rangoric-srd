A gnoll ravager is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnoll/Gnoll|gnoll]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladin]], one of the gnoll devotees.

Gnoll clerics, devoted to the goddess of monsters, serve the pack by culling the weak and enforcing pack hierarchy.

A gnoll ravager speaks Gnoll.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ravager casts bull's strength on himself.

**Base Statistics**: Without bull's strength, the ravager's base statistics are **Melee** +1 human-bane falchion +17/+12/+7 (2d4+7/15–20); **Str** 19; **CMB** +15; **CMD** 25.

**Antipaladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +11): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_.

**Antipaladin spells prepared** (CL 7th; concentration +8): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Doom|doom]]_ (+2 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Litany of Sloth|litany of sloth]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Good|protection from good]]_.
