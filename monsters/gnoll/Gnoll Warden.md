A gnoll warden is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnoll/Gnoll|gnoll]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/ranger/Skirmisher (Archetype)|skirmisher]] archetype), one of the gnoll skirmishers. Employing a mix of cunning and brute force, skirmishers thrive in a pack hierarchy.

The warden often serves as a gnoll pack's forward scout, tracking prey and wearing it down with pursuit before her packmates swoop in to attack.

A gnoll warden speaks Gnoll.
