A gnoll sergeant is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnolls/Gnoll|gnoll]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the gnoll war leaders. The alpha of a pack assigns leadership roles as she sees fit.

The lowest of gnoll war leaders, sergeants are always eager to prove their mettle in order to move up in the dominance hierarchy of the pack.

A gnoll sergeant speaks Gnoll.
