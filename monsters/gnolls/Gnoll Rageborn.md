A gnoll rageborn is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnolls/Gnoll|gnoll]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/barbarian/Pack Rager (Archetype)|pack rager]] archetype), one of the gnoll barbarians. Gnoll barbarians feed off one another's rage, and use pack tactics to savage their enemies.

A gnoll rageborn speaks Gnoll.

### Combat

**Base Statistics**: When he's not raging, the barbarian's statistics are **AC** 18, touch 11, flat-footed 17; **hp** 41; **Fort** +9; **Will** +1; **Melee** masterwork greataxe +8 (1d12+6/×3), bite +7 (1d6+6); **Str** 19; **Con** 16; **CMB** +7; **CMD** 18; **Skills** Athletics +6.
