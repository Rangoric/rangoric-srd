A troglodyte skulker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troglodyte/Troglodyte|troglodyte]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the troglodyte sneaks. Using their natural stealth or magical invisibility, these troglodytes sneak up on enemies, then either slip away to report what they've seen to the tribe or attack enemies that look weak.

Far-ranging and mobile, troglodyte skulkers spend most of their time away from the tribe.

A troglodyte skulker speaks Draconic.
