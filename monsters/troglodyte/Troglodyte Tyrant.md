A troglodyte tyrant is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troglodyte/Troglodyte|troglodyte]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] and 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. Troglodyte tyrants are sovereigns of advanced troglodyte societies found in the lowest depths of the world's subterranean wilderness.

Troglodyte tyrants come from the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troglodyte/Troglodyte#^enlightened-troglodyte|enlightened troglodytes]] who live far deeper underground than common troglodytes. Stronger, more intelligent, and longer-lived than their degenerate kin, they find it easy to assert dominance over tribes of normal troglodytes. Warlike in the extreme, tyrants are seasoned veterans of numerous military campaigns and they are expert tacticians, willing to sacrifice large numbers of troglodyte warriors to probe an enemy's strengths and weaknesses.

A troglodyte tyrant speaks Abyssal, Draconic, and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The tyrant casts amplify stench on herself and bull's strength on a trusted ally. She then uses her wand of bear's endurance.

**During Combat**: When combat begins, the tyrant casts rage on her allies—though she doesn't concentrate on it. She then casts divine power on herself if the enemies haven't reached her yet. She casts mark of the reptile god and bestow curse on strong enemies, then fights in melee.

**Base Statistics**: Without amplify stench and bear's endurance, the tyrant's statistics are **Aura** stench (30 ft., DC 19, 10 rounds); **hp** 113; **Fort** +15; **Con** 18.
