A troglodyte sorcerer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troglodyte/Troglodyte|troglodyte]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]], one of the troglodyte sneaks. Using their natural stealth or magical invisibility, these troglodytes sneak up on enemies, then either slip away to report what they've seen to the tribe or attack enemies that look weak.

Arcane casters often become scouts, as their divine counterparts monopolize the positions of power within the tribe. Most troglodyte sorcerers have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Abyssal Bloodline|Abyssal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Deep Earth Bloodline|Deep Earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Draconic Bloodline|Draconic]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Undead Bloodline|Undead]] bloodline.

A troglodyte sorcerer speaks Draconic.
