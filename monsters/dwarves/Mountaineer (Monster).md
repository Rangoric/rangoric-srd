A mountaineer (monster) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarves/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]].

A mountaineer (monster) speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts longstrider and resist energy (fire), then drinks his potion of bull's strength.

**During Combat**: The ranger uses Shield Slam to push opponents into difficult terrain, off cliffs, or into the area of spike growth.

**Base Statistics**: Without bull's strength, longstrider, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **Resist** none; **Speed** 20 ft; **Melee** +1 warhammer +7/+2 (1d8+4/×3), masterwork heavy shield +7/+2 (1d4+3 plus bull rush); **Str** 16; **CMB** +10; **CMD** 22 (26 vs. bull rush or trip); **Skills** Athletics +9.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 4th; concentration +6): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spike Growth|spike growth]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_.
