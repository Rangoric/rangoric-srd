An iron duelist is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarves/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] and 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Duelist|duelist]]. Nimble for a dwarf, an iron duelist excels at hit-and-run tactics.

An iron duelist speaks Common, Dwarven, and Orc.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The duelist drinks his potion of invisibility, and gets into a tactical position. He uses Spring Attack, Lunge, and Improved Vital strike to make hit-and-run attacks.
