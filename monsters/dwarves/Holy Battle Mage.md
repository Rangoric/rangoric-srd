A holy battle mage is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarves/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] of [[rangoric-srd/divine/Pantheons#Greek and Roman|Hephaestus]] and 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Universalist School|universalist]] school) and 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Mystic Theurge|mystic theurge]]. These master theurges mix support for allies with offensive might.

A holy battle mage speaks Aklo, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Elven, Giant, and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The mystic theurge casts heroes' feast, protection from energy (electricity, fire), see invisibility, and stoneskin.

**During Combat**: The mystic theurge uses spells to foil opponents and bolster allies. If entering melee combat, he casts righteous might and transformation.

**Base Statistics**: Without heroes' feast, protection from energy, and stoneskin, the mystic theurge's statistics are **hp** 223; **Will** +22; +2 vs. poison, spells, and spell-like abilities; **DR** none; **Immune** none; **Melee** +1 holy warhammer +12/+7 (1d8+1/×3).

**Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 5th; concentration +12): 10/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Artifice Domain|artificer's touch]] (1d6+2), [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Protection Domain|resistant touch]].
