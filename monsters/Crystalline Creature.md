# Crystalline Creature

Crystalline creatures are beings that have been altered by magical forces, most commonly by being trapped in a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Jewel Mage#^jewel-mage-create-binding-jewel|binding jewel]] or affected by a spell. They are usually in service to a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Jewel Mage|jewel mage]], whether voluntarily or involuntarily.

Crystalline creatures retain their basic form and features, but appear to be mobile crystal statues. However ugly the original creature may have been, the transformation creates a being of stunning beauty. They are often mistaken for earth elementals or constructs of some kind.

Most crystalline creatures occupy this state temporarily, but for some it becomes permanent.

### Creating a Crystalline Creature

“Crystalline” is a template that can be added to any aberration, animal, dragon, fey, giant, humanoid, magical beast, monstrous humanoid, ooze, outsider, plant, shapechanger, or vermin (referred to hereafter as the base creature). The creature’s type becomes [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^elemental|elemental]]. It uses all the base creature’s statistics and abilities except as noted here. Do not recalculate the creature’s Hit Dice, base attack bonus, saves, or skill points for its new type.

**Speed**: A crystalline creature with wings has its maneuverability changed to clumsy, due to the immense weight of its crystal body. A crystalline creature’s weight is triple that of the base creature.

**Armor Class**: Natural armor increases by +4.

**Attacks**: Creatures without natural attacks gain a slam attack at their base melee attack bonus.

**Damage**: The slam attack deals 1d6 points of damage if the base creature was Medium. Small creatures deal 1d4 points of damage, and Large ones deal 1d8.

**Special Attacks**: A crystalline creature retains any special attacks of the base creature. Crystalline creatures with an Intelligence or Wisdom score of 8 or higher gain the following spell-like abilities, using their Hit Dice or level as the caster level, as specified in the table below. Unless otherwise specified, each ability is usable once per day. A crystalline creature has the abilities for its Hit Dice and every lower row. These abilities are a result of the magical energies coursing through the creature’s crystalline body.

| Hit Dice or Level | Spell-Like Abilities |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 | *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]* 3/day |
| 4–6 | *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]* |
| 7–9 | *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]* |
| 10–12 | *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness/deafness]]* |
| 13–15 | *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rainbow Pattern|rainbow pattern]]* |
| 16+ | *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]* |

**Special Qualities**: A crystalline creature keeps any special qualities that the base creature had, and is now an elemental, granting it immunity to poison, sleep, paralysis, and stunning. It is no longer subject to critical hits, and gains darkvision out to 60 feet. It also has resistance to cold 10 and fire 10. It has no metabolism, and therefore needs no food, water, or air.

**Saves**: Same as the base creature.

**Abilities**: Strength +4, Dexterity –2, Constitution +4.

**Skills**: Crystalline creatures take a –4 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks, due to their increased weight.

**Feats**: Same as the base creature.

**Environment**: Same as the base creature.

**Organization**: Same as the base creature.

**Challenge Rating**: Up to 3 HD, as base creature +1; 4 HD to 9 HD, as base creature +2; 10 or more HD, as base creature +3.

**Treasure**: Same as the base creature.

**Alignment**: Same as the base creature.

**Advancement**: Same as the base creature.

**Crystalline Equipment**: When a creature is granted the crystalline template, whatever equipment it is carrying is transformed along with it. While the template alters the appearance of the equipment to appear as though it was made of crystal, this has no effect on its performance.

The [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Soul Gem Guardian|soul gem guardian]] can take on crystalline form for a short time ([[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Soul Gem Guardian#^soul-gem-guardian-crystalline-form|crystalline form]]).

### Sample Crystalline Creature

This example uses a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragonne|dragonne]] as the base creature.

**Crystalline Dragonne**

  - Large [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^elemental|Elemental]]
  - **Hit Dice**: 9d10+45 (94 hp)
  - **Initiative**: +5
  - **Speed**: 40 ft. (8 squares), fly 30 ft. (clumsy)
  - **Armor Class**: 21 (–1 size, +1 Dex, +11 natural), touch 10, flat-footed 20
  - **Base Attack**: +9; **CMB**: +16; **CMD**: 27 (31 vs. trip)
  - **Attack**: Bite +14 melee (2d6+6)
  - **Full Attack**: Bite +14 melee (2d6+6) and 2 claws +9 melee (2d4+3)
  - **Space/Reach**: 10 ft./5 ft.
  - **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Pounce|Pounce]], roar, spell-like abilities
  - **Special Qualities**: Darkvision 60 ft., elemental traits, low-light vision, resistance to cold 10 and fire 10, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]]
  - **Saves**: Fort +11, Ref +7, Will +4
  - **Abilities**: Str 23, Dex 13, Con 21, Int 6, Wis 12, Cha 12
  - **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +17
  - **Feats**: Blind-Fight, Combat Reflexes, Improved Initiative, Track
  - **Environment**: Temperate deserts
  - **Organization**: Solitary, pair, or pride (5–10)
  - **Challenge Rating**: 9
  - **Treasure**: Double standard
  - **Alignment**: Usually neutral
  - **Advancement**: 10–12 HD (Large); 13–27 HD (Huge)

A crystalline dragonne looks like a dragonne carved from crystal, and weighs about 2,100 pounds.

#### Combat

**Pounce (Ex)**: If a dragonne charges, it can make a full attack in the same round.

**Roar (Su)**: A dragonne can loose a devastating roar every 1d4 rounds. All creatures except dragonnes within 120 feet must succeed on a DC 15 Will save or become fatigued. Those within 30 feet who fail their saves become exhausted. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: 3/day—*flare*; 1/day—*color spray*, *hypnotic pattern*. Caster level 9th.

**Elemental Traits**: Immune to poison, sleep, paralysis, and stunning; not subject to critical hits; no need to eat, drink or breathe.
