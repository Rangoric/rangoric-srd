#### Combat

Cerebriliths favor telepathic attacks, usually beginning a battle by launching a furious barrage of ego whip and id insinuation attacks. In melee, cerebriliths often meditate to gain psionic focus, so that they can use Psionic Fist or Unavoidable Strike (the latter in conjunction with Power Attack).  

A cerebrilith’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as Chaotic-aligned and Evil-aligned for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

Psi-Like **Abilities:** At will - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Brain Lock|brain lock]] (any nonmindless, DC 17\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Detect Psionics|detect psionics]], [[rangoric-srd/powers/Ego Whip|ego whip]] (2d4, DC 19\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Id Insinuation|id insinuation]] (four targets, DC 20\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Mind Trap|mind trap]](5 rounds\*); 3/day - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Dominate, Psionic|psionic dominate]] (24 hours, DC 20\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Ectoplasmic Form|ectoplasmic form]], [[rangoric-srd/powers/Mind Probe|mind probe]] (DC 20); 1/day - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Mind Thrust|mind thrust]] (9d10, DC 20\*). Manifester level 9th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.

\*Includes augmentation for the cerebrilith’s manifester level.

Spell-Like **Abilities:** At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Good|dispel good]] (DC 20), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]] (self plus 50 pounds of objects only), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Good|magic circle against good]] (DC 18), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]] (DC 19). Caster level 9th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.

Summon Demon (Sp): Once per day a cerebrilith can attempt to summon 4d6 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monsters Da to De|dretches]] or another cerebrilith with a 35% chance of success. This ability is equivalent to a 4th-level spell.

Outsider Traits: A cerebrilith cannot be raised, reincarnated or resurrected (though a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]], or [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]] spell can restore life). It has darkvision out to 60 feet.

**Skills:** Cerebriliths have a +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks.
