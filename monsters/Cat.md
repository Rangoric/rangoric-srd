The statistics presented here describe a common housecat.

#### Combat

Cats prefer to sneak up on their prey.

**Skills**: Cats have a +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks and a +8 racial bonus on Athletics checks. Cats have a +8 racial bonus on Acrobatics checks. They use their Dexterity modifier instead of their Strength modifier for Athletics checks to climb. \*In areas of tall grass or heavy undergrowth, the bonus on Stealth checks is +4 higher.
