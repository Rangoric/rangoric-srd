# Lammasu

  - Large [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^magical-beast|Magical Beast]]
  - **Hit Dice**: 7d10+21 (59 hp)
  - **Initiative**: +1
  - **Speed**: 30 ft. (6 squares), fly 60 ft.(average)
  - **Armor Class**: 20 (–1 size, +1 Dex, +10 natural), touch 10, flat-footed 19
  - **Base Attack**: +7; **CMB**: +14; **CMD**: 25 (29 vs. trip)
  - **Attack**: Claw +12 melee (1d6+6)
  - **Full Attack**: 2 claws +12 melee (1d6+6)
  - **Space/Reach**: 10 ft./5 ft.
  - **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^pounce|Pounce]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^rake|rake]] 1d6+3, spells
  - **Special Qualities**: Darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]], spell-like abilities
  - **Saves**: Fort +8, Ref +8, Will +7
  - **Abilities**: Str 23, Dex 12, Con 17, Int 16, Wis 17, Cha 14
  - **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] +13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +15, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] +12, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] +13
  - **Unspent Skill Points**: 7
  - **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Iron Will|Iron Will]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats All|Lightning Reflexes]]
  - **Environment**: Temperate deserts
  - **Organization**: Solitary
  - **Challenge Rating**: 8
  - **Treasure**: Standard
  - **Alignment**: Always lawful good
  - **Advancement**: 8–10 HD (Large); 11–21 HD (Huge)
  - **Level Adjustment**: +5

A typical lammasu is about 8 feet long and weighs about 500 pounds. Lammasus speak Common, Draconic, and Celestial.

### Combat

A lammasu attacks with spells or its razor-sharp claws. It almost always enters combat if it observes a good creature being threatened by evil.

**Spells**: A lammasu casts spells as a 7th-level cleric, and can choose spells from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List|cleric spell list]], plus any two of the following domains: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|Good]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|Healing]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|Knowledge]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|Law]].

*Typical Cleric Spells Prepared (6/6/5/4/2; save DC 13 + spell level)*:  

[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List#0-Level Cleric Spells (Orisons)|0]] - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Guidance|guidance]] (2), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]];  

[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List#1st-Level Cleric Spells|1st]] - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]] (2), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Entropic Shield|entropic shield]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Evil|protection from evil]]\*;  

[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List#2nd-Level Cleric Spells|2nd]] - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]\*, [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear’s endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]];  

[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List#3rd-Level Cleric Spells|3rd]] - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]\*, [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]];  

[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List#4th-Level Cleric Spells|4th]] - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]\*, [[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]].

\*Domain spell. Domains: Good and Healing.

**Magic Circle against Evil (Su)**: A lammasu radiates a continuous [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]] that affects a 20-foot radius.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 7th.  

2/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]] (self only);  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]].  

**Pounce (Ex)**: If a lammasu [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charges]] a foe, it can make a full attack, including two [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^rake|rake]] attacks.

**Rake (Ex)**: Attack bonus +12 melee, damage 1d6+3.

**Skills**: Lammasus have a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks.
