# Grippli

- Small [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] (Grippli)
- **Hit Dice**: 1d10+2 (12 hp)
- **Initiative**: +3
- **Speed**: 30 ft. (6 squares), climb 20 ft.
- **Armor Class**: 16 (+2 armor, +3 Dex, +1 size), touch 14, flat-footed 13
- **Base Attack**: +1; **CMB**: +1; **CMD**: 14
- **Attack**: [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Short Sword|Short sword]] +3 melee (1d4+1/19–20) or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dart|dart]] +5 ranged (1d3+1) or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Net|net]] +5 ranged (special)
- **Full Attack**: [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Short Sword|Short sword]] +3 melee (1d4+1/19–20); or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dart|dart]] +5 ranged (1d3+1); or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Net|net]] +5 ranged (special)
- **Space/Reach**: 5 ft./5 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: Favored enemy (vermin +2)
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swamp Stride|swamp stride]], track, wild empathy
- **Defenses**: Fort 13, Ref 15, Will 11
- **Abilities**: Str 12, Dex 17, Con 13, Int 12, Wis 12, Cha 8
- **Skills**: (+15 in forests or marshes) Survival +7, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] +4, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] +3, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] +13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] +5, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +5, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] +11
- **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Fieldcraft)
- **Environment**: Warm forests or marshes
- **Organization**: Solitary, gang (2–5), pack (6–11), or tribe (21–30 plus 1 cleric leader of 3rd level)
- **Challenge Rating**: 1/2
- **Treasure**: NPC gear (leather armor, 4 darts, net, short sword, other treasure)
- **Alignment**: Usually neutral
- **Advancement**: —
- **Level Adjustment**: —

These small, intelligent, humanoid tree frogs live in primitive societies deep in the forests and marshes of the world. They hunt giant insects and eat fruits and flowers from their tree homes. They often barter with more advanced creatures for metal and gemstone ornaments.

Grippli hatchlings develop into adults in less than a year. Unless they meet a savage end from a forest threat, they can live 60 years, their skin graying and losing its luster as they age. A grippli stands just over 2 feet in height and weighs 30 pounds.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swamp Stride|Swamp Stride]] (Ex)**: A grippli can move through difficult terrain at its normal speed while within a swamp. Magically altered terrain affects a grippli normally.

### Grippli as Characters

Grippli characters use the [[rangoric-srd/races/Grippli|grippli]] race, which has their racial traits and level adjustment.
