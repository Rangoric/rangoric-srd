A triton has silvery skin that fades into silver-blue scales on the lower half of its body. A triton’s hair is deep blue or blue-green.

A triton is about the same size and weight as a human.

Tritons speak Common and Aquan.

### Combat

The reclusive tritons prefer to avoid combat, but they fiercely defend their homes. They attack with either melee or ranged weapons as the circumstances warrant. When encountered outside their lair, they are 90% likely to be mounted on friendly sea creatures such as porpoises.

**Spell-Like Abilities**:  Caster level 7th. Tritons often choose water elementals for their companions.  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IV|summon nature’s ally IV]].

**Skills**: Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]], a triton is better at swimming.
