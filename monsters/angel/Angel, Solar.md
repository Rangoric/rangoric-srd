A solar has a deep and commanding voice, and stands about 9 feet tall. It weighs about 500 pounds.

## Combat

Solars are puissant champions of good. Only the most powerful fiends approach their power. Even more fearsome than their +5 dancing greatswords are their +2 composite longbows that create any sort of slaying arrow when drawn.

A solar’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as good-aligned and epic for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

**Change Shape (Su):** A solar can assume the form of any Small or Medium humanoid.

**Regeneration (Ex)**: A solar takes normal damage from epic evil-aligned weapons, and from spells or effects with the evil descriptor.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 20th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Objects|animate objects]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spells F to G|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Imprisonment|imprisonment]] (DC 26), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]] (self only), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]] (DC 19), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]] (DC 20), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]] (DC 20), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]][[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks|summon monster VII]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]] (DC 20), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Waves of Fatigue|waves of fatigue]] (DC 18);

The following abilities are always active on a solar’s person, as the spells (caster level 20th): [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Snares and Pits|detect snares and pits]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]. They can be dispelled, but the solar can reactivate them as a free action.

**Spells**: Solars can cast divine spells as 20th-level clerics. A solar has access to two of the following domains: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|Air]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|Destruction]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|Good]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|Law]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|War]] (plus any [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains|others]] from its deity). The save DCs are Wisdom-based.
