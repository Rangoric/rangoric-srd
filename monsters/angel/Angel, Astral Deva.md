Astral devas are messengers of the gods of good. They watch over planar travelers and sponsor powerful mortals, pushing them to take on good causes. A typical astral deva looks human except for its wings, though some look like other humanoid races and a rare few have even more unusual forms. An astral deva is 7-1/2 feet tall and weighs 250 pounds.

Astral devas are usually created by deities from the souls of good mortals, though some souls spontaneously transform into astral devas without the intervention of a deity. Their skills and abilities make them excellent scouts and elite agents for celestial armies. They often carry long, flowing scrolls upon which are writ messages and judgments from the gods. An astral deva's scrolls frequently contain hints of prophecies and major events yet to come, and as such the devas are fiercely protective of the contents of these scrolls and let none, not even other astral devas, peruse them.

Astral devas speak Celestial, Draconic and Infernal, and can speak with almost any creature by [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^angel|truespeech]].

### Combat

An astral deva is not afraid to enter melee combat. It takes a fierce joy in bashing evil foes with its +2 disrupting warhammer.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 13th): At Will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]]* (DC 20), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Evil|dispel evil]]* (DC 21), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Aura|holy aura]]* (DC 24), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]* (DC 20), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Word|holy word]]* (DC 23), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]* (self only), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]* (DC 23), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]*; 7/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blade Barrier|blade barrier]]* (DC 22), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]*.

**Stun (Su)**: If an astral deva strikes an opponent twice in one round with its warhammer, that creature must succeed on a DC 25 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d6 rounds. The save DC is Strength-based.

**Uncanny Dodge (Ex)**: This ability functions identically to the rogue ability. If a deva gains uncanny dodge from a class level, he instead gains improved uncanny dodge.
