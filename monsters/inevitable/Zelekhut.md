Zelekhuts are charged with hunting down those who would deny justice—especially those who flee to escape punishment. Expert trackers, they use a combination of natural skill and magic to find fugitives wherever they may hide.

A zelekhut may initially appear rather non-threatening—but when it is about to enter combat, it can cause two spiked chains to spring forth from its forearms as a free action. In similar fashion, it can cause a pair of golden metallic wings to emerge from its back. Retracting the chains or the wings is also a free action.

## Combat

Once it has found its fugitive, a zelekhut uses its speed and its spell-like abilities to cover the most likely escape routes. Then it immobilizes any defenders while attempting to protect any innocent bystanders. Finally, it apprehends the fugitive with its spiked chains, [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Trip|tripping]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Disarm|disarming]] the foe as needed.

A zelekhut’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as lawful-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 8th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Clairaudience-Clairvoyance|clairaudience/clairvoyance]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]] (DC 16), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]] (DC 15), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]];  

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]] (DC 17), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mark of Justice|mark of justice]];  

1/week - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Geas, Lesser|lesser geas]] (DC 16).  

**Skills**: A zelekhut has a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks.

**Feats**: Due to its centaur-like construction, a zelekhut qualifies for feats as if it had the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Combat|Mounted Combat]] feat.
