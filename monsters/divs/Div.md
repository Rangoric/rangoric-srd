# Div

Divs are neutral evil outsiders that sow misfortune and ruin. They speak Abyssal, Celestial, and Infernal.

### Combat

Divs have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^div|div subtype]]: immunity to fire and poison, resistance to acid and electricity, telepathy, and often see in darkness and the power to summon others of their kind.
