# Fleshwarp

Fleshwarps are creatures twisted by the drow's alchemical torture of living flesh, each made from a different victim: the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarps/Ghonhatine|ghonhatine]] from troglodytes, the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarps/Grothlut|grothlut]] from humans, the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarps/Halsora|halsora]] from vegepygmies and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarps/Irnakurse|irnakurse]] from elves.
