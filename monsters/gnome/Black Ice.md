A black ice is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Illusion School|illusion]] school) and 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadowdancer]]. These evasive spellcasters make frustrating enemies.

A black ice speaks Aklo, Common, Draconic, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The shadowdancer casts mage armor and stoneskin.

**During Combat**: The shadowdancer casts greater invisibility and shield, then harries his foes with phantasmal killer, hypnotic pattern, and spells from his staff of frost.

**Base Statistics**: Without mage armor and stoneskin, the shadowdancer's statistics are **AC** 19, touch 17, flat-footed 16; **DR** none.

**Shadowdancer Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 7th; concentration +9): 3/day—[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadow illusion]] (DC 13); 2/day—[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadow call]] (DC 16).

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +19): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_.
