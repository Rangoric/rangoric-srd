A chaos arrow is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] and 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Arcane Archer|arcane archer]]. Often whimsical in their destruction, chaos arrows roam the world playing the cruelest pranks for their own twisted amusement.

A chaos arrow speaks Common and Gnome.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The arcane archer casts see invisibility and haste. She prepares flaming burst arrows as her enhance arrows ability.

**During Combat**: A arcane archer uses imbue arrows to fire off cloudkill, stinking cloud, and entangle from a distance.

**Base Statistics**: Without see invisibility, the arcane archer's statistics are **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +26.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 11th; concentration +15): 7/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Fey Bloodline|laughing touch]].
