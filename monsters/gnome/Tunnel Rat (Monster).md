A tunnel rat (monster) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. A tunnel rat patrols twisting passages underground.

A tunnel rat (monster) speaks Common, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts delay poison.

**During Combat**: The ranger uses Precise Shot to help allies in melee.

**Base Statistics**: Without delay poison, the ranger's statistics are **Immune** none.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 3rd; concentration +4): 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]*.
