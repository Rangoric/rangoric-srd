A skirmishing crusader is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. A skirmishing crusader often serves as a scout in the army of a noble, or roams the boundaries of civilization, working with rangers to hold the line against evil. She spends her life in the wilds, and as a result can often be awkward and crass.

A skirmishing crusader speaks Common, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The paladin attempts to stay out of melee, preferring to use cover and attack with her bow.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +8): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +8): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_.

**Paladin spells prepared** (CL 1st; concentration +5): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]_.
