A fastidious sharpshooter is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]].

A fastidious sharpshooter speaks Common, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The fighter drinks her potion of shield of faith.

**During Combat**: The fighter drinks her potion of haste and launches full attacks with screaming bolts.

**Base Statistics**: Without shield of faith, the fighter's statistics are **AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 17; **CMD** 20.

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +9): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, ghost sound* (+1 vs Will), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, speak with animals_.
