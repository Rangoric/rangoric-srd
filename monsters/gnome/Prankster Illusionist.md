A prankster illusionist is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Illusion School|illusion]] school). These irreverent illusionists are the bane of humorless adventurers. Their tricks are sometimes mistaken for enemy attacks, and even in dangerous situations, their own amusement comes first.

A prankster illusionist speaks Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Elven, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The wizard casts mage armor from her wand.

**During Combat**: The wizard prefers to prank others rather than cause deliberate harm. She casts greater invisibility, then harasses and annoys her targets with glitterdust, grease, major image, persistent image, and solid fog. If attacked with lethal force, she retaliates with cloudkill, empowered scorching ray, and magic missile.

**Base Statistics**: Without mage armor, the wizard's statistics are **AC** 16, touch 15, flat-footed 13.

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +11): 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]*.
