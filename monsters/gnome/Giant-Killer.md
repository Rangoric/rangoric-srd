A giant-killer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 11 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. The giant-killer is trained to kill monsters many times her size, using speed, specialized weapons, and time honored techniques to bring down even the most daunting foes.

A giant-killer speaks Common, Giant, and Gnome.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin, bear's endurance, delay poison, and resist energy (fire).

**During Combat**: If facing multiple opponents with reach, the ranger uses Lunge.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, bear's endurance, delay poison, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **hp** 94; **Fort** +10; **AC** 21, touch 15, flat-footed 18; **Immune** none; **Resist** none; **Con** 14.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 8th; concentration +10): 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear's endurance]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_.
