A lion tamer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 15 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. These bards tame and control wild animals.

A lion tamer speaks Common, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The bard casts greater invisibility on himself, and sends forth summoned creatures and trained lions.
