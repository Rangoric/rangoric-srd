A daring bravo is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. These small, quick warriors are also clever and brave. They know when to strike fast and hard—and when to run.

A daring bravo speaks Common, Gnome, Halfling, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The fighter shifts to maintain an advantageous position. If facing a similarly mobile foe, she resorts to her tanglefoot bag.
