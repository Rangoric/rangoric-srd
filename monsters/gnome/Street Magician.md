A street magician is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Enchantment School|enchantment]] school). The street magician uses his talents to make money.

A street magician speaks Common, Dwarven, Gnome, and Halfling.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The wizard casts color spray, then casts grease between himself and foes. If threatened, he drinks his potion of invisibility.

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 2nd; concentration +3): 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]*.

**Enchanter spells prepared** (CL 2nd; concentration +4): 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]* (DC 14), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]* (DC 13); 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]* (2, DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]*; **Opposition Schools**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Abjuration School|abjuration]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Necromancy School|necromancy]].
