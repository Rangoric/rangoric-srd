A giant hunter (monster) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 15 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. This paladin's lifelong mission is to eradicate evil giants.

A giant hunter (monster) speaks Common, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The paladin uses divine bond to give her weapon a mix of the defending, flaming, and holy special abilities.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th; concentration +18): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th; concentration +18): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_.
