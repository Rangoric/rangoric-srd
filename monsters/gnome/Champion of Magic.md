A champion of magic is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]] and 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Eldritch Knight|eldritch knight]]. These eldritch knights seek out evil spellcasters and dispense justice for their misdeeds.

A champion of magic speaks Common, Draconic, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The eldritch knight casts see invisibility and stoneskin.

**During Combat**: The eldritch knights separates large groups with a wall of force, then attacks opponents outside of the wall before passing through it with dimension door to finish off the remainder of his assailants.

**Base Statistics**: Without see invisibility and stoneskin, the eldritch knight's statistics are **Senses** low-light vision; **DR** none.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 2nd; concentration +5): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 13th; concentration +16): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_.
