A mad prophet is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Wandering Chronicler|wandering chronicler]]. These Pathfinders know some paths are twisted and mad.

A mad prophet speaks Abyssal, Aklo, Common, Draconic, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The wandering chronicler drinks his potion of fly. While flying above his enemies, he activates his aura of madness, casts chaos hammer, and swoops down to cast slay living on spellcasters.

**Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +15): At will—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Knowledge Domain|lore keeper]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Knowledge Domain|remote viewing]] (9 rounds/day); 7/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Madness Domain|vision of madness]].
