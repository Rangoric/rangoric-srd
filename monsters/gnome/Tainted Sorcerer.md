A tainted sorcerer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. The tainted sorcerer's mind and body have been warped by alien or extraplanar magic.

A tainted sorcerer speaks Common, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The sorcerer casts shield, targets approaching enemies with acidic ray, then uses her long limbs ability to deliver touch spells. In melee, she casts enlarge person on herself and attacks with her spear.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +8): 7/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Aberrant Bloodline|acidic ray]] (1d6+2 acid).

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +8): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_.

**Sorcerer spells known** (CL 4th; concentration +8): 2nd (4/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]_; 1st (7/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]_ (+6 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]_ (+6 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Splash|acid splash]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Fatigue|touch of fatigue]]_ (+4 vs Fortitude); **Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Aberrant Bloodline|aberrant]].
