A trickster mage is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. The trickster mage thinks everyone deserves to be the butt of his jokes, even if they don't want to be.

A trickster mage speaks Common, Elven, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The sorcerer casts mirror image, then attempts to control or humiliate opponents with charm person, hideous laughter, suggestion, or his wand of grease.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +12): 7/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Fey Bloodline|laughing touch]].

**Gnome Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +12): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_.
