A trapsmith is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnome/Gnome|gnome]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. This rogue excels at building, setting, and bypassing traps.

A trapsmith speaks Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The rogue uses her crossbow to make direct attacks.
