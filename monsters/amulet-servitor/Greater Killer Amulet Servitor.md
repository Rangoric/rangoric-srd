Unlike the other amulet servitors, a greater killer amulet servitor’s collapsed form is not an amulet. Rather, a greater killer amulet servitor collapses to a two-foot long cylinder of tightly wrapped metal wires weighing about 10 pounds. When deployed, a greater killer amulet servitor takes the shape of a large horse, with four sharp blades protruding forward from the shoulders and back of the servitor, and a sharp spike in place of the head. Embedded in the center of the killer amulet servitor’s spine is a massive black sapphire. Although a greater killer amulet servitor can simply be carried, some groups that employ these mechanical assassins pair them with a humanoid handler who sacrifices a limb to conceal the servitor. If used to replace an arm or a leg, the servitor can function as a prosthetic limb with Strength 16. When the handler identifies the target, the servitor immediately launches forward at high speed and attacks.

## Combat

A greater killer amulet servitor, like its lesser cousin, concentrates all of its attacks on its designated target until either the servitor is destroyed or the target is slain. After the target’s death, the killer amulet servitor uses its ability to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Soul Bind|soul bind]]* the victim and then breaks off combat to bring the target’s soul to its master.

**Activation Attack (Ex):** A greater killer amulet servitor typically activates by hurling itself forward at its target. It immediately makes all of its attacks, beginning with its gore attack. The initial gore attack is made at the better of the servitor’s gore attack bonus or the handler’s ranged attack bonus +4, with a range increment of 30 feet.

**Improved grab (Ex):** If the servitor hits with one of its claws it may attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity. If it successfully gains a hold on its opponent, it draws him into its ribcage and begins using its *constrict* ability the following round.

**Constrict (Ex):** A successfully grappled foe takes 2d10+12 points of constriction damage.

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|Poison]] (Ex):** The poison in a greater killer amulet servitor’s horn is extremely deadly. Injury, Fortitude DC 21, initial and secondary damage 1d8 Con.

***Haste* (Sp):** Once per combat as a free action, a greater killer amulet servitor may cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]* upon itself as if cast by a sorcerer of level equal to the construct’s Hit Dice.

**Soul Bind (Sp):** As a standard action, a greater killer amulet servitor may cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Soul Bind|soul bind]]* upon a slain victim. A Will save (DC 21) negates the effect.
