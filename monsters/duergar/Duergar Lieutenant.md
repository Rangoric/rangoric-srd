A duergar lieutenant is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/duergar/Duergar, 1st-Level Warrior|duergar]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the duergar rangers. Spending much time scouting and exploring alone, rangers prove their toughness to their kin.

A duergar lieutenant speaks Common, Dwarven, and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The lieutenant casts ironskin and lead blades on himself.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 6th; concentration +3): 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dust of Twilight|dust of twilight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ironskin|ironskin]]*.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 3rd; concentration +5): 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lead Blades|lead blades]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]*.
