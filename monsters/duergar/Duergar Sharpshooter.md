A duergar sharpshooter is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/duergar/Duergar, 1st-Level Warrior|duergar]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the duergar rangers. Spending much time scouting and exploring alone, rangers prove their toughness to their kin.
