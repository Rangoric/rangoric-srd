A duergar slaver is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/duergar/Duergar, 1st-Level Warrior|duergar]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the duergar rogues. Duergar rogues primarily capture and discipline slaves.

A duergar slaver speaks Common, Dwarven, and Undercommon.
