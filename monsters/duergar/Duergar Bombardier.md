A duergar bombardier is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/duergar/Duergar, 1st-Level Warrior|duergar]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]]. These eccentric duergar enjoy nothing more than concocting new alchemical methods to kill and torture their enemies.

A duergar bombardier spends her days fiddling with reagents and bizarre ingredients that she either finds deep underground or purchases from scouts who have brought them back from the surface or distant caverns. Essential ingredients, especially plants, can be hard to find, and a bombardier must work for money or favors if she's to continue practicing her craft.

Though the military and slavers are loath to employ these unpredictable duergar, bombardiers can be very effective when subtlety is not required. Though most bombardiers wouldn't hurt their own kind—the bonds of kin and law are too strong—they often inadvertently kill potential slaves instead of capturing them.

A duergar bombardier speaks Aklo, Common, Draconic, Dwarven, and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The bombardier quaffs a Dexterity mutagen and casts ironskin.

**During Combat**: The bombardier spends most of the combat lobbing bombs or alchemist's fire at her foes, using invisibility and potions of longstrider to gain superior tactical positions or make a hasty retreat.

**Base Statistics**: When she's not under the effect of her mutagen and ironskin, the bombardier's statistics are **Init** +2; **AC** 14, touch 12, flat-footed 12; **Ref** +5; **Will** +1; **Ranged** bomb +4 (1d6+2 fire) or light crossbow +3 (1d8/19–20); **Dex** 14; **Wis** 12; **CMD** 14 (18 vs. bull rush, 18 vs. trip); **Skills** Engineering +7, Fieldcraft +6, Medicine +6, Perception +6.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 2nd; concentration -1): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_ (self only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ironskin|ironskin]]_.

**Alchemist extracts prepared** (CL 2nd): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bomber’s Eye|bomber's eye]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_.
