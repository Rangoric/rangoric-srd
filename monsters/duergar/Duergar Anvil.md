A duergar anvil is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/duergar/Duergar, 1st-Level Warrior|duergar]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]], one of the duergar monks. Martial mystics expunge corruption from duergar society.

A duergar anvil speaks Common, Dwarven, and Undercommon.
