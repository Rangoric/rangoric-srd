A duergar taskmaster is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/duergar/Duergar, 1st-Level Warrior|duergar]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadowdancer]], one of the duergar rogues. Duergar rogues primarily capture and discipline slaves.

A duergar taskmaster speaks Common, Dwarven, and Undercommon.
