A duergar hammer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/duergar/Duergar, 1st-Level Warrior|duergar]] with 12 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/monk/Gray Disciple (Archetype)|gray disciple]] archetype), one of the duergar monks. Martial mystics expunge corruption from duergar society.

A duergar hammer speaks Common, Dwarven, and Undercommon.
