Blue-ringed octopuses are aquatic animals capable of a surprising amount of cunning. They cannot survive out of water.

### Combat

**Ink Cloud (Ex)**: While within water, an octopus can emit a 5-foot-radius sphere of ink once per minute as a swift action. This ink provides total concealment and persists for 1 minute.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Jet|Jet]] (Ex)**: The octopus can jet 60 feet in a straight line as a full-round action. This does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: Bite—injury; _save_ Fort DC 10; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1 Str; _cure_ 1 save.
