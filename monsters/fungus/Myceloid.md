A creature that dies of the purple pox becomes bloated over the course of 24 hours, after which its body bursts open, releasing a fully grown myceloid. Additionally, as long as a creature takes at least 7 points of Wisdom damage from the purple pox, the pox attacks it at +5 vs Will each day, and on a hit it is affected by a lesser geas (no HD limit) that compels the sickly character to seek out the nearest myceloid colony in order to offer itself up for spore domination. The attack bonuses are Constitution-based.

The walking fungi known as myceloids feed off of decaying organic matter like many other fungi, yet unlike typical mushrooms or molds, they take particular pleasure in feeding from the rotting bodies of humanoids. Myceloids claim to be able to taste things like “innocence,” “despair,” and “hope” in the ripeness of rancid meat, although whether this is true or simply part of the myceloids' twisted sense of humor is unclear.

Most myceloids have deep purple caps studded with white lumps, and paler purple necks and bodies of tough, leathery fungus. Their spores grow tenaciously in living flesh, causing a rapid spread of painful purple lesions that, in advanced stages of the sickness, sprout tiny purple mushrooms; plucking these mushrooms is painful to the victim and causes bleeding. This condition, known as purple pox, is the method by which myceloids both season their meat and procreate.

Myceloids prefer to capture victims alive for later infection and control. To a myceloid, a living creature has three uses—first as a slave, second as a host from which to birth new myceloids, and finally as a banquet to feast upon once the first two destinies have played out.

Myceloids speak Undercommon.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 6th; concentration +6): 1/day-spore domination (+4 vs Will).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Disease|Disease]] (Su)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Purple Pox|Purple Pox]]: inhaled or injury; _attack_ +5 vs Fortitude; _onset_ 1 minute; _frequency_ 1/day; _effect_ 1d2 Wis and 1d2 Con damage; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spore Cloud|Spore Cloud]] (Ex)**: Once per day as a standard action, a myceloid can expel a 10-foot-radius burst of spores centered on itself. This cloud persists for 1d3 rounds. Any creature caught in this cloud or that moves through it is exposed to the myceloid's [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Purple Pox|purple pox]] disease—any one spore cloud attacks a creature only once, however, before becoming permanently immune to that particular spore cloud's effects. The spore cloud does not hamper vision.

**Spore Domination (Sp)**: This spell-like ability functions as charm monster, but functions only against creatures currently infected with [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Purple Pox|purple pox]].
