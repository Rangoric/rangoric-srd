# Dolphin

- Medium [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|Animal]]
- **Hit Dice**: 2d8+2 (11 hp)
- **Initiative**: +2
- **Speed**: swim 80 ft.
- **Armor Class**: 13 (+2 Dex, +1 natural), touch 12, flat-footed 11
- **Base Attack**: +1; **CMB**: +2; **CMD**: 14
- **Attack**: Slam +3 melee (1d4+1)
- **Full Attack**: Slam +3 melee (1d4+1)
- **Space/Reach**: 5 ft./5 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: —
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Blindsight|Blindsight]] 120 ft., [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Hold Breath|hold breath]]

- **Advancement**: —

Dolphins are social predators that hunt shallow seas and rivers in large family groups called pods. Sailors are fond of dolphins and frequently tell tales of dolphins saving drowning fishermen or killing sharks with blows from their powerful snouts.

### Combat

**Hold Breath (Ex)**: A dolphin can hold its breath for a number of minutes equal to 6 times its Constitution score before it risks drowning.
