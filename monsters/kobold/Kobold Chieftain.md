A kobold chieftain is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kobold/Kobold|kobold]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], and 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Assassin|assassin]], one of the kobold leaders. It has the [[rangoric-srd/races/class-bonuses/Kobold Fighter (Class Bonus)|kobold fighter class bonus]] (+2 on damage rolls with weapon attacks against an opponent it is flanking or that is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC), and its danger sense includes the [[rangoric-srd/races/class-bonuses/Kobold Rogue (Class Bonus)|kobold rogue class bonus]]. Kobold leaders are the undisputed masters of their tribes.

A kobold chieftain speaks Common, Dark Folk, Draconic, Dwarven, and Undercommon.
