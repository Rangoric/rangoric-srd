A kobold sniper is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kobold/Kobold|kobold]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the kobold fighters. Kobold warriors are more prudent than brave. They usually opt to attack from hiding with ranged weapons, and move into melee only when absolutely necessary—such as when the chief doesn't give them any other option.

A kobold sniper speaks Common and Draconic.
