A kobold scalecaster is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kobold/Kobold|kobold]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]], one of the kobold sorcerers. Kobolds see sorcery as proof of their draconic heritage.

A kobold scalecaster speaks Draconic.
