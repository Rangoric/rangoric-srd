A kobold monster wrangler is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kobold/Kobold|kobold]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]], one of the kobold riders. It has the [[rangoric-srd/races/class-bonuses/Kobold Cavalier (Class Bonus)|kobold cavalier class bonus]]: its mount’s speed is 10 feet higher when it charges or withdraws. The boldest kobolds enjoy riding bizarre beasts into battle.

A kobold monster wrangler speaks Common and Draconic.
