A kobold yapper is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kobold/Kobold|kobold]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/bard/Dragon Yapper (Archetype)|dragon yapper]] archetype), one of the kobold leaders. Kobold leaders are the undisputed masters of their tribes.

A kobold yapper speaks Common, Draconic, and Sylvan.
