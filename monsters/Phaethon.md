A phaethon’s natural weapons are treated as epic and evil for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

#### Combat

Fiery Touch (Ex): Touching or being touched by a phaethon deals 2d6 points of fire damage.

Fiery Overrun (Ex): A foe who is successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]] by a phaethon is treated as if swallowed whole.

Improved Grab (Ex): If the phaethon hits with a pseudopod, it deals normal damage and attempts to start a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity. Phaethons can use improved grab on a Huge or smaller creature. The phaethon has the option to conduct the grapple normally, simply maintaining a pseudopod hold, or attempting to absorb the opponent by swallowing the foe whole. Each successful grapple check it makes during successive rounds automatically deals the damage listed for the pseudopod.

Swallow Whole (Ex): The phaethon can absorb opponents it holds with a second successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] check after a grab. The opponent must be Huge or smaller. Absorbed creatures take 20d6 points of fire damage and 10d6 points of bludgeoning damage each round they remain inside a phaethon. Victims must make a successful grapple check to “swim” free of the living magma of the phaethon.

Spell-Like Abilities: Caster level 34th; save DC 24 + spell level.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Delayed Blast Fireball|delayed blast fireball]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|flame strike]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Fire|wall of fire]];  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]].   

Summon Elder Fire Elemental (Sp): Phaethons can summon up to ten [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Fire Elemental|elder fire elementals]] per day.

Abomination Traits: Immune to polymorphing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrification]], and other form-altering attacks; not subject to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|energy drain]], ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], or ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]], or death from massive damage; immune to mind-affecting effects; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|fire resistance]] 20; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|cold resistance]] 20; [[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]; [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]] at will; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^blindsight|blindsight]] 500 ft.; telepathy out to 1,000 ft.

Oozelike Immunities (Ex): Immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|poison]], sleep, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralysis]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]], polymorphing, and mind-influencing effects; not subject to critical hits or flanking; [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blind]].

Regeneration (Ex): Phaethons take normal damage from lawful weapons and cold-forged weapons, and double damage from cold or ice weapons.
