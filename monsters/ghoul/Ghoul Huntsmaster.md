A ghoul huntsmaster is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ghoul/Ghoul|ghoul]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. Though most ghouls hunt and kill to satiate their hunger, a huntsmaster relishes the chase as much as the feast after.

A ghoul huntsmaster speaks Common and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The huntsmaster casts longstrider and uses his wand of greater magic fang on himself as well as on his [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Corpse Cat|animal companion]].

**Base Statistics**: Without longstrider and greater magic fang, the huntsmaster's statistics are **Speed** 30 ft; **Melee** bite +12 (1d6+2 plus disease and paralysis), 2 claws +13 (1d8+2 plus paralysis).

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 3rd; concentration +6): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Fang|magic fang]]_.
