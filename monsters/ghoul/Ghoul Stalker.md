A ghoul stalker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ghoul/Ghoul|ghoul]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the sneaky ghouls. While all ghouls are quiet and deadly in the night, these ghouls specialize in striking from cover or exploiting tactical advantages. They set up ambushes or fight using group tactics to claim their prey.

A ghoul stalker speaks Common.
