A ghoul commander is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ghoul/Ghoul|ghoul]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladin]]. Ghoul commanders have stoked a burning hatred for the living. A ghoul commander might lead a pack of ghouls or may serve as a guardian or lieutenant for a more powerful ghoul monarch.

A ghoul commander speaks Common and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ghoul commander is usually accompanied by a fiendish dire boar mount. If he is defending an area, he casts desecrate on the area (these bonuses are not included in his statistics).

**During Combat**: The commander begins combat by charging in on his boar. He prefers to attack with his greataxe.

**Antipaladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 7th; concentration +11): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_.

**Antipaladin spells prepared** (CL 4th; concentration +8): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Light Wounds|inflict light wounds]]_ (+5 vs Will).
