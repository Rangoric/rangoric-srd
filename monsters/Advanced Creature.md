# Advanced Creature

Creatures with the advanced template are fiercer and more powerful than their ordinary cousins.

“Advanced” is a simple template: it can be added to any creature. Its changes come in two forms. The quick rules are a fast way to adjust a creature’s rolls in play without rebuilding its statistics, which works well for summoned creatures. The rebuild rules are the exact changes to make to its statistics when there’s time to rebuild it. The two give similar, if not identical, results. A complete rebuild gives an animal too much Intelligence, for example.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +1.

**Quick Rules**: +2 on all rolls (including damage rolls) and special ability DCs; +4 to AC and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|CMD]]; +2 hp/HD.

**Rebuild Rules**: **AC** increase natural armor by +2; **Ability Scores** +4 to all ability scores (except Int scores of 2 or less).
