A lizardfolk vanguard is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lizardfolk/Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]], one of the lizardfolk barbarians. Some warriors enter war-trances to increase their prowess.

A lizardfolk vanguard speaks Draconic.

### Combat

**During Combat**: Lizardfolk vanguards use shock tactics to eliminate the most threatening opponents first.

**Base Statistics**: When he's not raging, the vanguard's statistics are **AC** 19, touch 12, flat-footed 17; **hp** 27; **Fort** +7; **Will** +1; **Melee** masterwork morningstar +6 (1d8+3), bite +3 (1d6+1) or claw +5 (1d4+3), bite +3 (1d6+1); **Str** 17; **Con** 15; **CMB** +5; **Skills** Athletics +16.
