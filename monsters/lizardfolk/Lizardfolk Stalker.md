A lizardfolk stalker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lizardfolk/Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the lizardfolk rangers. Submerged in shallow water or hidden in tangles of rushes, lizardfolk rangers give away their position only once it is too late for their prey to do anything about it.

A lizardfolk stalker speaks Draconic.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The stalker prefers to soften up lightly armored targets with his longbow before moving in for the kill.
