A lizardfolk berserker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lizardfolk/Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]], one of the lizardfolk barbarians. Some warriors enter war-trances to increase their prowess.

A lizardfolk berserker speaks Draconic.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The berserker uses the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Knockdown Stance (Rage Power)|knockdown]] rage power on enemies who are heavily armored or wield heavy weapons.

**Base Statistics**: When she's not raging, the berserker's statistics are **AC** 21, touch 12, flat-footed 19; **hp** 54; **Fort** +9; **Will** +2; **Melee** masterwork morningstar +10 (1d8+4), bite +7 (1d6+3) or claw +9 (1d4+3), bite +7 (1d6+2); **Str** 18; **Con** 15; **CMB** +9; **Skills** Athletics +19.
