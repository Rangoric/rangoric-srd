A lizardfolk champion is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lizardfolk/Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]]. Among most lizardfolk tribes, the greatest mark of status a warrior can achieve is a mighty dinosaur steed, allowing her to thunder terrifyingly into enemy lines.

A lizardfolk champion speaks Draconic.
