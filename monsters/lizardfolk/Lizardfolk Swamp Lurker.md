A lizardfolk swamp lurker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lizardfolk/Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the lizardfolk rangers. Submerged in shallow water or hidden in tangles of rushes, lizardfolk rangers give away their position only once it is too late for their prey to do anything about it.

A lizardfolk swamp lurker speaks Draconic.
