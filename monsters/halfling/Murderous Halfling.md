A murderous halfling is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halfling/Halfling|halfling]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. Small, sly, and seemingly innocuous, evil halflings make perfect murderers.

A murderous halfling speaks Common, Goblin, and Halfling.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The fighter hurls daggers until her foes close with her. In melee, she fights defensively (and gains a +3 dodge bonus from her ranks in Acrobatics). She keeps her feather token in reserve for dangerous spellcasters or when battle turns against her.
