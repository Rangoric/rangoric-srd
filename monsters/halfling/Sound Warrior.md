A sound warrior is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halfling/Halfling|halfling]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Mystic Theurge|mystic theurge]]. Using a mix of rousing oratory, song, and thunderous spells, a sound warrior is often heard before she's seen.

A sound warrior speaks Common, Druidic, and Halfling.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The mystic theurge casts barkskin, freedom of movement, and pass without trace.

**During Combat**: While aiding her companions with bardic performance, the mystic theurge harasses her opponents with call lightning, charm monster, and wind wall.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, the mystic theurge's statistics are **AC** 21, touch 17, flat-footed 17.

**Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +6): 5/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|lightning arc]] (1d6+2 electricity).
