A thunder wizard is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halfling/Halfling|halfling]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Evocation School|evocation]] school). The thunder wizard manipulates sound and electricity to destroy her enemies.

A thunder wizard speaks Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Goblin, Halfling, and Infernal.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The wizard casts mage armor and protection from arrows. If she has an ally who attacks in melee, she casts protection from energy (electricity) on that ally to protect him from her lightning bolt spells; otherwise, she casts it on herself (warding against fire).

**During Combat**: The wizard tries to catch multiple opponents with black tentacles, then follows up with a lightning bolt to hit as many targets as possible. She uses her imp to invisibly deliver shocking grasp and empowered shocking grasp.

**Base Statistics**: Without mage armor, the wizard's statistics are **AC** 15, touch 15, flat-footed 12.

**Evoker spells prepared** (CL 7th; concentration +10): 4th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]* (DC 17); 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]* (2, DC 16), *protection from energy, * empowered*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]*; 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkvision|darkvision]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Arrows|protection from arrows]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]* (2); 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]* (DC 14), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]* (2); 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]*; **Opposition Schools**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Divination School|divination]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Necromancy School|necromancy]].
