A town watcher is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halfling/Halfling|halfling]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. The town watcher protects a settlement from hostile creatures.

A town watcher speaks Common and Halfling.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The ranger shoots at opponents who attempt to harm innocents or his allies. If trying to capture someone alive, he uses a tanglefoot bag. If trying to disperse a hostile crowd, he throws a thunderstone.
