A dog rider knight is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halfling/Halfling|halfling]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]].

A dog rider knight speaks Common and Halfling.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The paladin casts bull's strength.

**During Combat**: The paladin uses Ride-By Attack to charge his foes, and uses smite evil.

**Base Statistics**: Without bull's strength, the paladin's statistics are **Melee** +1 lance +12/+7 (1d6+3/×3) or masterwork longsword +11/+6 (1d6+2/19-20); **Str** 14; **CMB** +8; **CMD** 20; **Skills** Athletics +0.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 7th; concentration +10): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.

**Paladin spells prepared** (CL 4th; concentration +7): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless Weapon|bless weapon]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_.
