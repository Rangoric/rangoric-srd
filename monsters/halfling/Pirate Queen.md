A pirate queen is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halfling/Halfling|halfling]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. The pirate queen is a deadly foe, whether she's encountered in a scummy port town or out on the high seas, flying the black flag. She keeps a full crew of unruly, vicious buccaneers in line, and rewards them with the treasure and carnage they crave.

A pirate queen speaks Common and Halfling.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin, endure elements, longstrider, and resist energy (fire).

**During Combat**: The ranger prefers melee, using Mobility and Spring Attack to engage the enemy leader while her pirate allies deal with underlings.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, longstrider, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **AC** 22, touch 17, flat-footed 17; **Resist** none; **Speed** 20 ft.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 7th; concentration +9): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Wall|wind wall]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_.
