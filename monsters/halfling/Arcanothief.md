An arcanothief is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halfling/Halfling|halfling]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] and 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Arcane Trickster|arcane trickster]]. Masters at breaking into wizard towers and sorcerer societies, arcanothieves steal magic items, supplying local fences or selling directly to visiting adventurers.

An arcanothief speaks Common, Draconic, and Halfling.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The arcane trickster casts false life.

**During Combat**: The arcane trickster stays out of melee, using invisibility, gaseous form, and dimension door to keep her distance while pelting foes with crossbow bolts. When in dire straits, she uses her scroll of teleport to flee.

**Base Statistics**: Without false life, the arcane trickster's statistics are **hp** 97.
