A troll fury is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Troll|troll]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Troll Fury (Archetype)|troll fury]] archetype). The troll fury is an experienced steward of nature, watching over nearby troll individuals and tribes to make sure they don't consume too much game.

A troll fury speaks Druidic and Giant.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The fury attacks her foes with fire and electricity spells, typically starting with fireball. She avoids melee combat until her offensive spells are exhausted. She then casts bull's strength on herself and starts attacking.

**Druid spells prepared** (CL 6th; concentration +9): 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning|call lightning]]* (DC 18), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]* (DC 18), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]* (2, DC 16); 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flaming Sphere|flaming sphere]]* (DC 17), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Animal|hold animal]]* (DC 15), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Produce Flame|produce flame]]* <sup>D</sup>; 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]* <sup>D</sup> (DC 16), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]* (DC 14), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Fang|magic fang]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]*; 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guidance|guidance]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]*.
