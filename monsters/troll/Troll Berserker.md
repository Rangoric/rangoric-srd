A troll berserker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Troll|troll]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]], one of the troll ragers. More feral than typical trolls, ragers are bloodthirsty maniacs in battle, known for tearing off and swallowing pieces of living opponents. These trolls are the most likely to overeat and devastate the food supply in an area.

A troll berserker speaks Giant.

### Combat

**Base Statistics**: When he's not raging, the barbarian's statistics are **AC** 22, touch 12, flat-footed 19; **hp** 148; **Fort** +18; **Will** +3; bite +14 (1d8+7), 2 claws +15 (1d6+9); rend (2 claws, 1d6+14); **Str** 25; **Con** 28; **CMB** +16; **CMD** 29; **Skills** Intimidation +16.
