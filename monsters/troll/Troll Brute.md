A troll brute is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Troll|troll]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]], one of the troll ragers. More feral than typical trolls, ragers are bloodthirsty maniacs in battle, known for tearing off and swallowing pieces of living opponents. These trolls are the most likely to overeat and devastate the food supply in an area.

A troll brute speaks Giant.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The brute drinks her potion and rages.

**Base Statistics**: When she's not raging, the barbarian's statistics are **AC** 23, touch 13, flat-footed 20; **hp** 109; **Fort** +17; **Will** +3; bite +12 (1d8+7), 2 claws +13 (1d6+7); rend (2 claws, 1d6+10); **Str** 25; **Con** 27; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 28; **Skills** Intimidation +11.
