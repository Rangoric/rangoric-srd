A troll render is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Troll|troll]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the troll fighters. These trolls are a cut above a common troll in terms of combat ability, but even less intelligent. They wear armor made from the hides of creatures they hunted and killed.

A troll render speaks Giant.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The render drinks his potions.
