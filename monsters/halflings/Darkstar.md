A darkstar is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halflings/Halfling|halfling]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] and 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadowdancer]]. Short but deadly, a darkstar skirts the shadows with his shadow companion.

A darkstar speaks Common and Halfling.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The shadowdancer drinks his potion of invisibility, summons a shadow to flank with him, and focuses on dropping foes one at a time.

**Shadowdancer Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +10): 5/day—[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadow illusion]] (+1 vs Will); 4/day—[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadow call]] (+4 vs Will); 2/day—[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadowdancer|shadow power]] (+5 vs Will).
