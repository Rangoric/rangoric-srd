A guild initiate is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halflings/Halfling|halfling]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The guild initiate is a rogue who is still seeking to prove his worth to a thieves' guild.

A guild initiate speaks Common and Halfling.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The rogue uses surprise and flanking to make sneak attacks, as well as his slow reactions rogue talent.
