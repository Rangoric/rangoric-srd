A taunting comedian is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halflings/Halfling|halfling]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. These jokers' jeers and pranks create humor from misfortune.

A taunting comedian speaks Common and Halfling.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The bard hides using ventriloquism, and snares enemies with animate rope.

**Bard spells known** (CL 4th; concentration +8): 2nd (2/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_ (+6 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]_; 1st (4/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope|animate rope]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion, Lesser|lesser confusion]]_ (+5), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ventriloquism|ventriloquism]]_ (+5 vs Will); 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]_ (+4 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_.
