A feisty hotspur is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/halflings/Halfling|halfling]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Duelist|duelist]]. Anyone who questions the martial prowess of halflings should keep quiet around feisty hotspurs, as these diminutive bravos excel at poking holes in large people who underestimate them.

A feisty hotspur speaks Common and Halfling.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The duelist drinks her potion of haste. She attempts to disarm opponents, then makes sneak attacks even if she must feint to do so.
