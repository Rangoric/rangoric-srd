Bone golems are most often crafted as guardians of tombs—and sometimes formed from the very corpses they guard.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Bone Prison|Bone Prison]] (Ex)**: As a standard action, a bone golem can throw some of its bones at a creature within 30 feet—it must make a ranged touch attack to hit. These bones magically duplicate and form a cage surrounding struck creatures. Each round, the cage makes a combat maneuver check to deal the golem's slam damage, using the golem's CMB. If the check fails, the target is still trapped but takes no damage. The target can escape the grapple normally, or can break out of the bones by dealing 15 points of damage to the prison, which has the same AC, DR, and defenses as the bone golem itself. Damage to the prison has no effect on the golem. The golem can only have one bone prison active at a time. If it wishes to create a second one, it (or some other creature) must first destroy the existing one.

**Immunity to Magic (Ex)**: A bone golem is immune to any spell or spell-like ability that allows spell resistance. In addition, certain spells and abilities function differently against the creature, as noted below.

- Magical effects that heal living creatures slow a bone golem (as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ spell) for 1d4 rounds (no attack against a defense).
- A magical attack that deals negative energy damage breaks any _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ effect on the golem and heals 1 point of damage for every 3 points of damage the attack would otherwise deal. If the healing would cause the golem to exceed its full normal hit points, it gains any excess as temporary hit points. Attacks that deal negative energy damage hit a bone golem's defenses automatically.
- A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Raise Dead|raise dead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resurrection|resurrection]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]_ spell negates its DR and immunity to magic for 1 minute.
