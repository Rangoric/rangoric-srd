Carrion golems are made from the partially decayed parts of numerous dead creatures—no two carrion golems are exactly alike in appearance. Most are created from the corpses of humanoid creatures that are then augmented here and there with parts taken from animals, resulting in a monster that stands 6 feet tall and weighs 120 pounds.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Foul Stench|Foul Stench]] (Ex)**: This functions as the stench ability, but causes affected creatures to be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] rather than [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]].

**Immune to Magic (Ex)**: A carrion golem is immune to any spell or spell-like ability that allows spell resistance. In addition, certain spells and effects function differently against the creature, as noted below.

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gentle Repose|Gentle repose]]_ causes a carrion golem to become stiff and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] for 1d4 rounds if it fails a Will save against the spell.
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|Animate dead]]_ causes the various parts of the golem's body to shudder and tear, dealing 1d6 points of damage per caster level to the golem (no save).
- Any magical attack that deals cold or fire damage slows a carrion golem (as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ spell) for 2d6 rounds (no save).
- Any magical attack that deals electricity damage hastes a carrion golem (as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_ spell) for 2d6 rounds.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Plague Carrier|Plague Carrier]] (Ex)**: When a carrion golem is created, its creator infects it with a specific disease. The carrion golem can then infect those it strikes with its slams with this disease—most carrion golems inflict [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]]. The save DC is Constitution-based and includes a +2 racial bonus.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Disease|Disease]] (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|Filth Fever]]: Slam—injury; _save_ Fort DC 14; _onset_ 1d3 days; _frequency_ 1/day; _effect_ 1d3 Dex damage and 1d3 Con damage; _cure_ 2 consecutive saves.
