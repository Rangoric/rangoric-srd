Junk golems are assembled from trash and spare parts, and often leave trails of garbage in their wakes.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Discorporate|Discorporate]] (Ex)**: A junk golem can break apart into its constituent pieces as a standard action, becoming a swarming mass of Tiny parts. It gains the swarm subtype, its space changes to 10 feet, and its reach changes to 0. In this form, it gains a swarm attack that deals 1d6 points of damage and inflicts disease, but loses its slam attacks. It can revert back to its normal form as a full-round action.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Disease|Disease]] (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Tetanus|Tetanus]]: Slam—injury; _attack_ +2 vs Fortitude; _onset_ 1d6 days; _frequency_ 1 day; _effect_ 1d4 Dex damage. Each time someone takes Dex damage from tetanus, there's a 50% chance his jaw muscles stiffen, preventing speech and the use of spells with verbal components for 24 hours; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**Immunity to Magic (Ex)**: A junk golem is immune to any spell or spell-like ability that allows spell resistance. In addition, certain spells and effects function differently against a junk golem, as noted below.

- A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shatter|shatter]]_ spell causes a junk golem to discorporate and dazes it for 1 round.
- A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]_ spell affects the junk golem as if it were _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_ for 1d6 rounds and ends any _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ effect on it.
- An _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Lock|arcane lock]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Portal|hold portal]]_ spell affects the junk golem as if it were a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ spell for 1d6 rounds and breaks any _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_ effect on it.
- A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wood Shape|wood shape]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rusting Grasp|rusting grasp]]_ spell deals 2d6 points of damage to a junk golem.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Junk Repair|Junk Repair]] (Su)**: A junk golem has fast healing 2 when in an area with a high concentration of debris, such as a refuse heap or the area created by another junk golem's discorporate ability.

### Construction

A junk golem's body is made up of 250 pounds of assorted rubbish with 200 gp of spare metal and copper wire.
