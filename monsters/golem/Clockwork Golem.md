Forged from thousands of gears, the clockwork golem is a precision creation. In combat, a clockwork golem is ruthlessly efficient, moving with swift conviction to grind and slice its foes to ribbons.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Death Burst|Death Burst]] (Ex)**: When a clockwork golem is reduced to 0 or fewer hit points, it explodes in a shower of razor-sharp gears and debris. All creatures within a 10-foot burst take 12d6 points of slashing damage—it attacks at +8 vs Reflex, and a miss halves the damage. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grind (Clockwork Golem)|Grind]] (Ex)**: A clockwork golem deals an additional 2d10+12 points of slashing damage when it makes a successful grapple check as razor-sharp gears and blades emerge from its body to grind and slice its foe.

**Immunity to Magic (Ex)**: A clockwork golem is immune to any spell or spell-like ability that allows spell resistance. In addition, certain spells and effects function differently against a clockwork golem, as noted below.

- A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]_ spell cast on the golem causes it to move quickly for 1d6 rounds, as if under the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_.
- A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rusting Grasp|rusting grasp]]_ spell deals damage to a clockwork golem normally, and makes the golem [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d6 rounds (no attack against a defense).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Wall of Gears|Wall of Gears]] (Su)**: As a standard action, a clockwork golem can fold into a whirling wall of grinding gears measuring 10 feet by 10 feet or 5 feet by 20 feet. Anyone passing through the wall takes 15d6 points of slashing damage. If the wall appears in a creature's space, the wall attacks that creature at +8 vs Reflex, and on a miss the creature leaps to one side and avoids the damage entirely. The clockwork golem can take no actions while in this form except to resume its normal form as a move action. A clockwork golem's AC and immunities remain the same while it is in this form.
