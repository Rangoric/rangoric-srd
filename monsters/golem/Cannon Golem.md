A cannon golem's internal workings are a mechanical labyrinth; its extradimensional pockets constantly process new black powder. A cannon golem stands 12 feet tall.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Alloyed|Alloyed]] (Ex)**: A cannon golem's slam and cannon attacks count as adamantine, cold iron, and silver for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Blasting Critical|Blasting Critical]] (Ex)**: When a cannon golem confirms a critical hit with a slam attack, it can make one cannon attack against that target as a free action (as long as the cannon is loaded).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Cannon|Cannon]] (Ex)**: The golem's cannon has a range increment of 100 feet and deals 6d6 points of bludgeoning and piercing damage on a hit with a ×4 critical modifier. The cannon's magazine can hold up to 20 cannonballs at a time—reloading a single cannonball into this magazine is a standard action for the golem, while loading a cannonball into the cannon itself to fire it is a swift action; this allows the golem to take two shots per round with the cannon.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Gun Training|Gun Training]] (Ex)**: A cannon golem adds its Dex modifier to the damage dealt by its cannon.

**Immunity to Magic (Ex)**: A cannon golem is immune to spells and spell-like abilities that allow spell resistance. Certain spells and effects function differently against it, as noted below.

- Any spell with the water descriptor that affects a cannon golem renders its cannon unusable for 1 round (no save).
- A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heat Metal|heat metal]]_ spell causes the golem's cannon to instantly backfire, dealing 6d6 points of damage to the golem and staggering it for 1 round (no save).
