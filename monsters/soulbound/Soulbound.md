# Soulbound

Soulbound constructs are animated by a fragment of a creature's soul, bound into a focus set in the body. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/soulbound/Soulbound Mannequin|mannequin]] is a human-sized improvement on the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/soulbound/Soulbound Doll|doll]], and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/soulbound/Soulbound Shell|shell]] is the perfected version of the mannequin.
