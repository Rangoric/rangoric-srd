A soulbound shell is the perfected version of a soulbound mannequin, allowing the soul to retain its spellcasting.

Soulbound shells speak Aklo, Common and Draconic.

### Combat

**Spells**: A spellbound shell casts spells as a 12th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|witch]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]], but does not gain any other class abilities.

**Wizard spells prepared** (CL 12th; concentration +17): 6th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape IV|beast shape IV]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_ (+11 vs Fortitude); 5th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cloudkill|cloudkill]]_ (2, +12 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Person|dominate person]]_ (+11 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Passwall|passwall]]_; 4th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]_ (2), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Shield|fire shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_ (+9); 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]_ (2, +8 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stinking Cloud|stinking cloud]]_ (2, +10 vs Fortitude); 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life|false life]]_ (already cast), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glitterdust|glitterdust]]_ (+9 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hideous Laughter|hideous laughter]]_ (+8 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Make Whole|make whole]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]_ (2, +7 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]_ (+8), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_ (already cast), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unseen Servant|unseen servant]]_; 0—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Splash|acid splash]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Cemented Mind|Cemented Mind]] (Ex)**: A soulbound shell can never change its known or prepared spells.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Soul Focus (Soulbound Shell)|Soul Focus]] (Su)**: The soul bound to the shell lives within a gem inside its chest. As long as this soul focus remains intact, it can be used to animate another shell, at the same cost as creating a new soulbound shell, and retains its personality and memories. A soul focus has hardness 8, 12 hit points, and a break DC of 20.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Susceptible to Mind-Affecting Effects|Susceptible to Mind-Affecting Effects]] (Ex)**: A soulbound shell is not immune to mind-affecting effects.

### Construction

A soulbound shell is constructed from marble, a 5,000 gp gem for the soul focus, and the soul of a willing spellcaster of at least 12th level who dies at the culmination of the creation process and animates the stone shell.
