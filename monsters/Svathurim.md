Svathurims and frost giants consider each other allies and distant kin. The hindquarters of an adult svathurim are up to 10 feet tall and 16 feet long, while its giant trunk and head reach up to 18 feet in height. They typically weigh more than 5 tons and may live up to 500 years. Svathurims dwell in arctic regions, galloping along the shores of polar seas or between high mountain peaks, with individuals organized into clans headed by the strongest hunters.

Svathurims speak Common and Giant.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Natural Jouster|Natural Jouster]] (Ex)**: A svathurim can wield a lance one-handed as if it were mounted, and it deals double damage with a lance while charging.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Skyrunner|Skyrunner]] (Su)**: When charging, running, or taking a double move, a svathurim can stride across open air as if it were using air walk. This movement is very taxing; a svathurim must succeed at a DC 14 Constitution check at the end of each round it uses this ability or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] for 1 minute. This DC increases by 2 for each round after the first. Returning to the ground resets the DC to 14, but doesn't negate any ongoing fatigue or exhaustion. A svathurim cannot trample when using this ability.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Thunderous Trample|Thunderous Trample]] (Su)**: Any creature damaged by a svathurim's trample attack is attacked at +12 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
