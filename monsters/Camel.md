# Camel

- Large [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|Animal]]
- **Hit Dice**: 2d8+4 (13 hp)
- **Initiative**: +3
- **Speed**: 50 ft. (10 squares)
- **Armor Class**: 13 (+3 Dex, +1 natural, –1 size), touch 12, flat-footed 10
- **Base Attack**: +1; **CMB**: +6; **CMD**: 19 (23 vs. trip)
- **Attack**: Bite +4 melee (1d4+6)
- **Full Attack**: Bite +4 melee (1d4+6)
- **Space/Reach**: 10 ft./5 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: Spit (+3 ranged touch)
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|Low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]]
- **Defenses**: Fort 15, Ref 16, Will 10
- **Abilities**: Str 18, Dex 16, Con 14, Int 2, Wis 11, Cha 4
- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +5
- **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]]

- **Organization**: Solitary, domesticated, or herd (6–30)
- **Challenge Rating**: 1
- **Treasure**: None
- **Alignment**: Usually neutral
- **Advancement**: —
- **Level Adjustment**: —

Camels are large, desert-dwelling herd animals noted for their stamina and ill tempers. A typical camel stands about 6 feet at the shoulder and 7 feet at the hump.

Camels are known for their ability to travel long distances without food or water.

The statistics presented here are for the dromedary, or one-humped camel, which thrives in warm deserts.

The two-humped, or Bactrian, camel is suited to cooler, rocky areas. It is stockier, slower (speed 40 feet), and has a higher Constitution score (16).

**Carrying Capacity**: A light load for a camel is up to 300 pounds; a medium load, 301–600 pounds; and a heavy load, 601–900 pounds. A camel can drag 4,500 pounds.

### Combat

**Spit (Ex)**: Once per hour, a camel can regurgitate the contents of its stomach, spitting the foul material at a single target within 10 feet. The spit attacks the target at +3 vs Fortitude, and on a hit it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d4 rounds. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
