# Young Creature

Creatures with the young template are immature specimens of the base creature. You can also use this simple template to easily create a smaller variant of a monster. This template cannot be applied to creatures that increase in power through aging or feeding (such as dragons or barghests) or creatures that are Fine-sized.

“Young” is a simple template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Simple Templates|Simple Templates]]): it can be added to a creature during play. The quick rules adjust its rolls without rebuilding its statistics; the rebuild rules are the exact changes to make when there’s time to rebuild it.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature –1.

**Quick Rules**: +2 on all Dex-based rolls, –2 on all other rolls, –2 hp/HD.

**Rebuild Rules**: **Size** decrease by one category ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Improving Monsters#Size Increases|Table: Changes to Statistics by Size]], read in reverse); **AC** reduce natural armor by –2 (minimum +0); **Attacks** decrease damage dice by 1 step; **Ability Scores** –4 Strength, –4 Con, +4 size bonus to Dex.
