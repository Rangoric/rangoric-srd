# Barbarian Creature

A barbarian creature can fly into a rage, granting it numerous bonuses in combat. It also gains additional hit points and a few valuable defensive abilities. A barbarian creature's CR increases by 3 if the creature has 10 or more HD. A barbarian creature must be chaotic.

“Barbarian” is a simple class template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters|Modifying Monsters]]): it gives a creature the flavor and abilities of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]] without adding class levels.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +2 or +3.

**Quick Rules**: +2 on all rolls based on Str; can [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] for a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + its HD + its Con modifier (this functions as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-greater-rage|greater rage]] if the creature has 10 or more HD); gains DR 1/— and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]] (DR 3/— and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]] if the creature has 10 or more HD).

**Rebuild Rules**: **Defensive Abilities** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]] ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]] if the creature has 10 or more HD); **DR** 1/— (3/— if the creature has 10 or more HD); **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] (can be used a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + its HD + its Con modifier, functions as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-greater-rage|greater rage]] class feature if the creature has 10 or more HD); **Ability Scores** +4 Strength.
