# Entropic Creature

Creatures with the entropic template live in planes where chaos is paramount. They can be summoned using spells such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_. An entropic creature's quick and rebuild rules are the same.

“Entropic” is a simple template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Simple Templates|Simple Templates]]): it can be added to a creature during play.

**Challenge Rating**: An entropic creature's CR increases by +1 only if the base creature has 5 or more HD.

**Rebuild Rules**: **Senses** gains darkvision 60 ft.; **Defensive Abilities** gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|DR]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Resistance|energy resistance]] as noted on the table; **SR** gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell Resistance|SR]] equal to new CR +5; **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Smite Law (Template)|smite law]] 1/day as a swift action (adds Cha bonus on attack rolls and damage bonus equal to HD against lawful foes; smite persists until the target is dead or the entropic creature rests).
