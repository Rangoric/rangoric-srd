# Wizard Creature

A wizard creature is skilled in the ways of arcane magic, and its spellcasting ability is unmatched. Beyond casting damaging spells, a wizard creature can call upon mighty forces to serve the will of the creature and its allies. Select a wizard creature's arcane school when the template is added. A wizard creature's CR increases by 2 if the creature has 7 or more HD, and it increases by 3 if the creature has 13 or more HD.

“Wizard” is a simple class template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters|Modifying Monsters]]): it gives a creature the flavor and abilities of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] without adding class levels.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +1, +2, or +3.

**Quick Rules**: +2 on all rolls based on Int; gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-arcane-school-feature|arcane school abilities]] of its chosen school (using its HD – 2 as its wizard level to determine the effect and DC [minimum 1]); can cast a small number of wizard spells (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters#^cleric-druid-and-wizard-spell-slots|Cleric, Druid, and Wizard Spell Slots table]]) using its HD as its CL; can designate one item as its arcane bond and use that item to cast any one spell it knows once per day.

**Rebuild Rules**: **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-arcane-bond|arcane bond]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-arcane-school-feature|arcane school abilities]] (using its HD – 2 as its wizard level to determine the effect and DC [minimum 1]); **Wizard Spells** can cast a small number of wizard spells (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters#^cleric-druid-and-wizard-spell-slots|Cleric, Druid, and Wizard Spell Slots table]]) using its HD as its CL; **Ability Scores** +4 Intelligence.
