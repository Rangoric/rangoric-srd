# Ranger Creature

A ranger creature gains a favored enemy. It also gains a basic combat style, tracking, and some defensive abilities. A ranger creature's CR increases by 2 if the creature has 10 or more HD.

“Ranger” is a simple class template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters|Modifying Monsters]]): it gives a creature the flavor and abilities of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]] without adding class levels.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +1 or +2.

**Quick Rules**: Choose either Str or Dex. The creature gains +2 on all rolls based on the chosen ability score (and +2 to AC if Dex is chosen). The creature gains either [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point-Blank Shot]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]] as a bonus feat (if the creature has 10 or more HD, choose two more bonus feats from the lists in [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|combat style]] class feature—from archery if the creature took Point-Blank Shot, or from two-weapon combat if the creature took Two-Weapon Fighting). The creature also gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] (choosing only one favored enemy; the favored enemy bonus increases by 2 at 5 HD and every 5 HD thereafter, to a maximum of +10 at 20 HD). Lastly, the creature gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-track|track]] (and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-evasion|evasion]] if the creature has 10 or more HD).

**Rebuild Rules**: **Defensive Abilities** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-evasion|evasion]] (if the creature has 10 or more HD); **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] (choosing only one favored enemy; the favored enemy bonus increases by 2 at 5 HD and every 5 HD thereafter, to a maximum of +10 at 20 HD); **Special Qualities** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-track|track]]; **Ability Scores** +4 Strength or Dexterity; **Feats** either [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point-Blank Shot]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]] as a bonus feat (if the creature has 10 or more HD, choose two more bonus feats from the lists in the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|combat style]] ranger class feature—either from archery if the creature took Point-Blank Shot, or from two-weapon combat if the creature took Two-Weapon Fighting).
