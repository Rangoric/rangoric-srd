# Giant Creature

Creatures with the giant template are larger and stronger than their normal-sized kin. This template cannot be applied to creatures that are Colossal.

“Giant” is a simple template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Simple Templates|Simple Templates]]): it can be added to a creature during play. The quick rules adjust its rolls without rebuilding its statistics; the rebuild rules are the exact changes to make when there’s time to rebuild it.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +1.

**Quick Rules**: +2 on all rolls based on Str or Con, +2 hp/HD, –1 penalty on all rolls based on Dex.

**Rebuild Rules**: **Size** increase by one category ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Improving Monsters#Size Increases|Table: Changes to Statistics by Size]]); **AC** increase natural armor by +3; **Attacks** increase dice rolled by 1 step; **Ability Scores** +4 size bonus to Str and Con, –2 Dex.
