# Druid Creature

Drawn to the raw might and power of nature, a druid creature gains the ability to change shape and cast druid spells. A druid creature's CR increases by 2 if the creature has 7 or more HD, and it increases by 3 if the creature has 13 or more HD.

“Druid” is a simple class template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters|Modifying Monsters]]): it gives a creature the flavor and abilities of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]] without adding class levels.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +1, +2, or +3.

**Quick Rules**: +2 on all rolls based on Wis; can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] if it has 7 or more HD (using its HD – 3 as its druid level to determine the effect of the wild shape); can cast a small number of druid spells (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters#^cleric-druid-and-wizard-spell-slots|Cleric, Druid, and Wizard Spell Slots table]]) using its HD as its CL; [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Rebuild Rules**: **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] (if it has 7 or more HD, using its HD – 3 as its druid level to determine the effect of the wild shape); **Druid Spells** can cast a small number of druid spells (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters#^cleric-druid-and-wizard-spell-slots|Cleric, Druid, and Wizard Spell Slots table]]) using its HD as its CL; **Ability Scores** +4 Wisdom; **SQ** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Animal Companion**: These rules assume the druid creature doesn't have an animal companion. If you want to add one, select a creature from the list of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally]]* spell. The spell must have a level no higher than 1/2 the creature's CR. Treat the companion as an additional creature in the encounter, awarding XP for defeating it as if it was not an animal companion.
