# Rogue Creature

A rogue creature gains sneak attack. It also gains defensive abilities and rogue talents if it has 10 or more Hit Dice. A rogue creature's CR increases by 2 if the creature has 10 or more HD.

“Rogue” is a simple class template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters|Modifying Monsters]]): it gives a creature the flavor and abilities of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] without adding class levels.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +1 or +2.

**Quick Rules**: +2 to AC and on all rolls based on Dex; gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] with a number of sneak attack dice equal to 1/2 its HD (maximum 10d6 at 20 HD); gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|evasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]] (if the creature has 10 or more HD, it also gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]] and two [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]], one of which can be an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]]).

**Rebuild Rules**: **Defensive Abilities** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|evasion]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]] ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]] if the creature has 10 or more HD); **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] (with a number of sneak attack dice equal to 1/2 the creature's HD, to a maximum of 10d6 at 20 HD); **Special Qualities** if the creature has 10 or more HD, it gains two [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]], one of which can be an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]]; **Ability Scores** +4 Dexterity.
