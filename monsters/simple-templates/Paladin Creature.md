# Paladin Creature

Paladin creatures can battle evil using smite evil and heal using lay on hands, and they possess some defensive abilities as well. A paladin creature's CR increases by 3 if the creature has 10 or more HD. A paladin creature must be lawful good.

“Paladin” is a simple class template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Modifying Monsters|Modifying Monsters]]): it gives a creature the flavor and abilities of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]] without adding class levels.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +2 or +3.

**Quick Rules**: +2 on all rolls based on Str and Cha; can [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|smite evil]] once per day (treating its HD as its paladin level for the purposes of damage); can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] once per day (healing 1d6 hit points for every 2 HD it possesses instead of using its paladin level); gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-detect-evil|detect evil]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-grace|divine grace]] (if the creature has 10 or more HD, it also gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-resolve|aura of resolve]]).

**Rebuild Rules**: **Defensive Abilities** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-grace|divine grace]] (if the creature has 10 or more HD, it also gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-resolve|aura of resolve]]); **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|smite evil]] ability once per day (treating its HD as its paladin level for the purposes of damage); **Special Qualities** [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-detect-evil|detect evil]] as the paladin class feature, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] once per day (healing 1d6 hit points for every 2 HD the creature possesses instead of using its paladin level); **Ability Scores** +4 Strength and Charisma.
