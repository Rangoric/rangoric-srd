- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Ice Elemental, Elder|Ice Elemental, Elder]]

Ice elementals speak Aquan.

- **Environment**: Any land or water (Elemental Plane of Water)
- **Organization**: Solitary, pair, or gang (3–8)
- **Treasure**: None

### Combat

**Ice Glide (Su)**: A burrowing ice elemental can pass through nonmagical ice and snow as easily as a fish swims through water. Its burrowing leaves behind no tunnel or hole, nor does it create any ripple or other sign of its presence. A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]_ spell cast on an area containing a burrowing ice elemental flings the elemental back 30 feet, and the spell attacks its Fortitude at +5, stunning it for 1 round on a hit. ^ice-glide

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Icewalking|Icewalking]] (Ex)**: This ability works like the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_ spell, but the surfaces the elemental climbs must be icy. The elemental can move across icy surfaces without penalty and does not need to make Acrobatics checks to run or charge on ice.

**Numbing Cold (Su)**: When an ice elemental deals cold damage to a creature, it attacks that creature’s Fortitude, and on a hit the creature is staggered for 1 round. The attack bonus is listed in the elemental's stat block and is Constitution-based. ^numbing-cold

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Snow Vision|Snow Vision]] (Ex)**: An ice elemental can see perfectly well in snowy conditions and does not take any penalties on Perception checks while in snow.
