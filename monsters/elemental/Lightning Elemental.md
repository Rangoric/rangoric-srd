# Lightning Elemental

- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Lightning Elemental, Small|Lightning Elemental, Small]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Lightning Elemental, Medium|Lightning Elemental, Medium]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Lightning Elemental, Large|Lightning Elemental, Large]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Lightning Elemental, Huge|Lightning Elemental, Huge]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Lightning Elemental, Greater|Lightning Elemental, Greater]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Lightning Elemental, Elder|Lightning Elemental, Elder]]

Lightning elementals speak Auran.

- **Environment**: Any (Elemental Plane of Air)
- **Organization**: Solitary, pair, or gang (3–8)
- **Treasure**: None

### Combat

**Metal Mastery (Ex)**: A lightning elemental gains a +3 bonus on attack rolls if its opponent is wearing metal armor, is wielding a metal weapon, or is made of metal (such as an iron golem). ^metal-mastery

**Spark Leap (Ex)**: A lightning elemental gains a +10 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]], and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks when it charges a creature against whom its metal mastery ability applies. ^spark-leap
