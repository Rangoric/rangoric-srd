A water elemental can’t venture more than 180 feet from the body of water from which it was conjured.

Water elementals speak Aquan but rarely choose to do so.

## Combat

A water elemental prefers to fight in a large body of water where it can disappear beneath the waves and suddenly swell up behind its opponents.

**Water Mastery (Ex)**: A water elemental gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls if both it and its opponent are touching water. If the opponent or the elemental is touching the ground, the elemental takes a –4 penalty on attack and damage rolls. These modifiers apply to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]] maneuvers, whether the elemental is initiating or resisting these kinds of attacks. (These modifiers are not included in the statistics block.) ^water-mastery

A water elemental can be a serious threat to a ship that crosses its path. An elemental can easily overturn small craft (5 feet of length per Hit Die of the elemental) and stop larger vessels (10 feet long per HD). Even large ships (20 feet long per HD) can be slowed to half speed.

**Drench (Ex)**: The elemental's touch puts out nonmagical flames of Large size or smaller. The creature can dispel magical fire it touches as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_ (caster level equals elemental's HD). ^drench

**Vortex (Su)**: A water elemental can create a whirlpool as a standard action, at will. This ability functions identically to the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Whirlwind|whirlwind]] special attack, but can only form underwater and cannot leave the water. Its attack bonus is in each water elemental's stat block, and its height in the table below. ^vortex

**Skills**: Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]], a water elemental is better at swimming.
