Mud elementals speak Terran.

  - **Environment**: Any land or water (Elemental Plane of Earth)
  - **Organization**: Solitary, pair, or gang (3–8)
  - **Treasure**: None

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Earth Glide|Earth Glide]] (Ex)**: A burrowing mud elemental can pass through dirt, gravel, or other loose or porous solid matter as easily as a fish swims through water. It cannot use this ability to pass through a solid barrier such as a stone or brick wall. Its burrowing leaves behind no tunnel or hole, nor does it create any ripple or other sign of its presence. A *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Move Earth|move earth]]* spell cast on an area containing a burrowing mud elemental flings the elemental back 30 feet, stunning the creature for 1 round unless it succeeds on a DC 15 Fortitude save.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Entrap|Entrap]] (Ex)**: The mud from an elemental's entrap ability can be washed away in 1d3 rounds of immersion in water.
