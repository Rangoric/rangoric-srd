# Slicer Beetle

The slicer beetle sports specialized mouthparts that scissor apart flesh for easier consumption. Each side of its horizontally closing mandibles fits into the other with barely a hair's breadth to spare. These creatures prefer to dwell in compact caves or warrens. A typical slicer beetle measures 10 feet long and weighs 800 pounds.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Crippling Bite|Crippling Bite]] (Ex)**: A slicer beetle's bite attack threatens a critical hit on a roll of 19–20. If a slicer beetle scores a critical hit on a target, its mandibles cut deep, resulting in a wound that causes 1d6 bleed and leaving its foe [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d3 rounds from the tremendous pain dealt.
