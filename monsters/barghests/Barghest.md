Said to be fiendish relations of all goblinoid races, the hateful barghests come to the Material Plane to feed. As they consume the bodies of innocents, they grow increasingly powerful. A barghest eventually sheds its skin to transform into a greater barghest, at which point it often seeks out a tribe of goblins or other creatures to rule.

A full-grown barghest is about 6 feet long and weighs 180 pounds.

Barghests speak Common, Goblin and Infernal.

### Combat

Barghests can claw and bite, no matter what their form, and usually disdain weapons. Though they love killing, they have little stomach for direct combat and attack from ambush whenever possible. Barghests start a combat by using [[rangoric-srd/spells/Crushing Despair|crushing despair]] and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]] to keep opponents off balance. They try to stay away from the enemy’s main strength.

Its natural weapons are treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 6th): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Misdirection|misdirection]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ (+6 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Crushing Despair|crushing despair]]_ (+6 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_.

**Feed (Su)**: Once per month, a barghest can devour a nonevil humanoid's corpse as a full-round action to gain a growth point. It gains a bonus equal to its growth point total on attack rolls, CMB rolls, and skill checks, and to its defenses. Its maximum hit points increase by 5 for each growth point it gains. For every 2 growth points, a barghest's caster level for its spell-like abilities and its CR increase by +1. When a barghest reaches 4 growth points, it sheds its skin and becomes a greater barghest, losing all of its growth points (and bonuses) but gaining the stats presented on this page for a greater barghest.

**Pass Without Trace (Ex)**: A barghest in wolf form can use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]] (as the spell) as a free action.
