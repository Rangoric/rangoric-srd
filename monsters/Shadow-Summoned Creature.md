# Shadow-Summoned Creature

Creatures with the shadow-summoned creature template dwell on the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#Plane of Shadow|Plane of Shadow]], only rarely venturing onto other, brighter planes, and can be summoned by [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/summoner/Shadow Caller (Archetype)|shadow callers]]. This template is for summoned creatures; a creature native to the Plane of Shadow uses the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shadow Creature|shadow creature]] template instead.

### Creating a Shadow-Summoned Creature

“Shadow-summoned creature” is a simple template. A shadow-summoned creature’s CR increases by +1. A shadow-summoned creature’s [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Advanced Creature|quick and rebuild rules]] are the same.

**Senses**: Gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] 60 ft.

**Defensive Abilities**: Gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Resistance|energy resistance]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|DR]] as noted on the table below.

**SR**: Gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell Resistance|SR]] equal to new CR + 5.

**Special Abilities**: _Shadow Blend (Su)_: In any condition of illumination other than bright light, a shadow-summoned creature blends into the shadows, giving it [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] (20% miss chance). A shadow-summoned creature can suspend or resume this ability as a free action.

| Hit Dice | Resist Cold and Electricity | DR       |
| -------- | --------------------------- | -------- |
| 1–4      | 5                           | —        |
| 5–10     | 10                          | 5/magic  |
| 11+      | 15                          | 10/magic |
