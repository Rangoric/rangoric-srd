The Forever Storm is believed by many cultures to be the cause of seasonal typhoons or other great storms—such tempests being a manifestation of the Forever Storm's monstrous breaths as she exhales during her long slumber every season. While this kaiju is not in fact responsible for the world's storms, her powers over wind and lightning are significant, and when she is vexed to rampage, the effects of her hurricanes can be as devastating to a region as her more direct attacks.

The Forever Storm has the appearance of a primeval, two-headed flying reptile with a wingspan of over 150 feet. Her tail is perhaps the most fearsome of her aspects. This lengthy and flailing appendage is covered with razor-sharp blades and wickedly barbed spines capable of slashing through buildings and tearing through armor with great force and from great distances.

The Forever Storm dwells on the upper slopes of a long-dormant volcano that sits on a remote tropical island beyond the main shipping lanes and the scope of most explorers' travels. As with many kaiju, she spends the bulk of her time in deep and peaceful slumber. She is ferociously protective of the strange peoples who dwell on the shores of this island, and has been known to rouse herself to come to their defense against slavers, pirates, or other external threats to their way of life. Despite this strange protective streak, the Forever Storm is not a kindly creature—and none know this more plainly than those who share her island. Her responses to intrusions to the island may simply be defenses of her perceived territories, for certainly the villages of the locals suffer significant damage during her local rampages. She's been known to take wing to travel to distant cities as well, often as a result of some of her island's inhabitants being taken from the shores as slaves. Yet the devastation she wreaks upon the slavers' destination cities pays no regard to the safety of the slaves themselves—they are in as much danger as anyone else from the kaiju's vengeful wrath in this situation.

There are rumors that certain [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shamans]] on the isle conceal sacred words or some ancient ritual to waken and command the Forever Storm, and that many of her rampages are not the result of the kaiju being territorial or protective, but simply due to an ancient bargain the natives' ancestors forged with the mighty beast in some forgotten era. Some speculate that such rituals require the sacrifice of trespassers, but this may be nothing more than fearful superstition or rumors spread by shamans to persuade foreigners to stay clear of their island.

The Forever Storm has a particular hatred of the kaiju known as the Final King, and often leaves her volcanic lair to fly west to the remote jungle lake that serves as the Final King's home to clash with her nemesis. The Final King's penchant for attacking coastal cities often results in a devastating escalation when the Forever Storm arrives to do battle with the land-bound kaiju. Sometimes, methods of conjuring the Forever Storm are used to deliberately lure the kaiju to a city besieged by the Final King, but just as often her arrival seems to be driven by self-interest. The Forever Storm typically manages to weaken the Final King enough to drive him off, but is often slain herself in these battles, resurrecting and returning to her home at the same time the Final King returns to his. The nature of this rivalry is unclear, but kaiju scholars believe that the two monsters have been enemies for longer than humanity has existed.

The Forever Storm understands Auran but can't speak.

### Combat

**Blinding Flash (Su)**: By spreading her wings as a full-round action while she stands upon the ground, the Forever Storm can create a blinding flash of light that targets all creatures within 100 feet. Affected creatures must succeed at a DC 41 Fortitude save or be permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d6 rounds by the blast of light. A successful saving throw reduces the blindness to 1 round and negates the staggered effect. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Breath Weapon (Su)**: Once every 4 rounds as a standard action, each of the Forever Storm's twin heads can breathe out a line of electricity to a range of 1,200 feet. The Forever Storm can breathe each line in a different direction. Each creature caught in a line of electricity must succeed at a DC 41 Reflex save or take 20d6 points of electricity damage and be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d4 rounds. A successful saving throw halves the damage and negates the staggered effect. A creature struck by both breath weapons simultaneously must attempt separate saving throws against each breath weapon, but takes a -4 penalty on both—the damage and staggered condition duration stack with each other. A creature wearing medium or heavy metal armor or a creature composed mostly of metal that fails its saving throw is also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. A creature slain by the Forever Storm's breath weapon remains electrified for 2d4 rounds after death—any creature that touches the body automatically takes 3d6 points of electricity damage. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Electrified Corpse (Su)**: If the Forever Storm is slain, her corpse crackles and flashes with pulses of electrical light if she has not used her rebirth ability that year. Any creature that touches her corpse takes 3d6 points of electricity damage. Even if the Forever Storm's body is destroyed, the site of her death continues to carry this electrical charge for 1 minute, or until the Forever Storm is reborn, whichever comes first.

**Hurricane (Sp)**: Once per day, the Forever Storm can create a supernatural hurricane surrounding herself. This effect functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]]_, save that it affects an area with a 4-mile radius, lasts for 24 hours, and can only be used to create hurricane-force winds. The hurricane created is static, and features a calm eye at the center with a 500-foot radius.

**Rebirth (Su)**: The Forever Storm remains dead for only 1 minute before she comes back to life, as if via a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]_ spell. The Forever Storm rises from the exact same position she occupied when she died, and typically takes advantage of this second chance to use her swift flight to escape as soon as possible. The Forever Storm can only be reborn in this manner once per year; if she is slain a second time before a year has passed, her death is permanent and her corpse does not become electrified. This ability replaces the Forever Storm's ability to immediately heal damage that would normally kill her, granted by her [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Recovery|recovery]] ability, but does not replace the other defenses granted by recovery.

**Storm Flier (Ex)**: The Forever Storm does not take penalties on Acrobatics checks when flying in strong or more powerful winds.

**Swift Flight (Su)**: As a standard action once per hour, the Forever Storm can fly in a straight line at an incredible speed. She may travel up to 1 mile in this manner—doing so does not provoke attacks of opportunity. When the Forever Storm activates this ability, her thunderous blast ability is automatically triggered from the swift flight's point of origin.

**Thunderous Blast (Su)**: When the Forever Storm uses her swift flight, she creates a thunderous sonic boom. This devastating explosion of sound occurs at her location when she activates swift flight, filling an area equal to her space and her reach combined—resulting in a 100-foot-diameter burst of sonic energy. All creatures in this area of effect take 20d10 points of sonic damage, are permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]], are knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]], and are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. A successful DC 41 Reflex save halves the damage, reduces the deafened condition to 1d4 rounds, and negates the knocked prone and stun effects entirely. The save DC is Constitution-based.
