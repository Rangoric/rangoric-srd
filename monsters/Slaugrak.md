The taint of demonkind has long corrupted the troglodyte bloodline. Each generation a few mutant creatures marked with the taint of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/The Abyss|Abyss]] are born, and fiendish deformities and stillbirths reeking of brimstone are all too common. The rapacious slaugrak is one of these fiendish mutants.

A slaugrak is born with an unnatural and incessant hunger for living flesh. Young slaugraks grow at an unnatural rate, reaching full and awful maturity in just 2 weeks. From birth, a slaugrak is little more than a walking collection of fangs and claws with no thought other than to slay and eat.

Slaugraks are sterile and solitary, holding no special love for troglodytes. A well-fed captive slaugrak might form an affectionate bond with its troglodyte keeper, but this affection merely makes the slaugrak more likely to eat its keeper last.

A typical slaugrak stands 12 to 14 feet tall and weighs 6,000 pounds.

Slaugraks speak Abyssal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 8th; concentration +10): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_ (+6 vs Will).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Corrupting Bite|Corrupting Bite]] (Su)**: A slaugrak's caustic saliva is infused with the corrupting power of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/The Abyss|Abyss]]. Any living creature that takes acid damage from a slaugrak's bite is attacked at +7 vs Fortitude, and on a hit takes 2 points of Constitution bleed damage as its blood boils and its internal organs liquefy. Creatures without a discernible anatomy (blood and internal organs) are immune to the Constitution bleed. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Vicious Jaws|Vicious Jaws]] (Ex)**: A slaugrak's bite attack threatens a critical hit on a roll of 19–20.
