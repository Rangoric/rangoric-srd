Priests and prophets, keketars are the leaders of their race, guiding proteans in their sacred mission to return all existence to primal chaos.

Keketars speak Abyssal and Protean.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 17th; concentration +24): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|detect law]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]]_ (+11 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Creation|major creation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Move Earth|move earth]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shatter|shatter]]_ (+9); 3/day—quickened _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_ (+11 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Law|dispel law]]_ (+12), empowered _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]]_ (+11 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph Any Object|polymorph any object]]_ (+15 vs Fortitude); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_ (+13 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]_ (+14), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Sphere|prismatic sphere]]_ (+16), _reshape reality_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Reshape Reality|Reshape Reality]] (Sp)**: This ability functions as the spell mirage arcana heightened to a 9th-level spell, except the changes created are quasi-real, like those created by shadow conjuration. The illusion attacks the Will of a creature that interacts with reshaped reality at +16, and on a miss the creature sees through it. Terrain can provide concealment, and against foes who do not see through the facade, reshaped reality can provide cover. For disbelievers, quasi-real objects and terrain have only 20% normal hardness and hit points, and break DCs are 10 lower than normal. Dangerous terrain cannot exceed 5d6 points of damage per round (1d6 per round against disbelievers). This ability cannot damage existing structures, nor does it function in areas where planar travel is prohibited.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spatial Riptide|Spatial Riptide]] (Su)**: Any non-protean teleporting into or out of the protean's aura is attacked at +18 vs Fortitude, and on a hit enters a state of suspended animation (identical to temporal stasis) for 1d3 rounds; a miss means the creature is merely [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**Warpwave (Su)**: A creature struck by a keketar's claw or bite is attacked at +18 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is affected by a warpwave. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
