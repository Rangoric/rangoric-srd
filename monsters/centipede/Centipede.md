# Centipede

Centipedes are many-legged, segmented predators with venomous mandibles. The giant ones are [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^vermin|vermin]]; those that come in sizes from Tiny to Colossal are described under [[rangoric-srd/monsters/monstrous-centipede/Monstrous Centipede|monstrous centipedes]], and the kinds below are particular species.

- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/centipede/Centipede Swarm|Centipede Swarm]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/centipede/Giant Whiptail Centipede|Giant Whiptail Centipede]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/centipede/House Centipede|House Centipede]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/centipede/Titan Centipede|Titan Centipede]]

**Species**

Other species of giant centipedes exist as well, some smaller but most quite a bit larger. You can adjust the stats of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/monstrous-centipede/Monstrous Centipede, Medium|giant centipede]] by changing Hit Dice and size (changing Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution as appropriate) to represent a wide range of giant centipede species.
