# Colour-Blighted Creature

A creature with the colour-blighted simple template appears hideously deformed and glows with the same unnamable color as the creature that blighted it. A colour-blighted creature's quick and rebuild rules are the same.

“Colour-blighted” is a simple template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Simple Templates|Simple Templates]]). A creature gains it when a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Colour Out of Space|colour out of space]]'s feed attack drains its Charisma to 0.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +0.

**Rebuild Rules**: A colour-blighted creature's ability scores are already drained as a result of being fed upon by a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Colour Out of Space|colour out of space]], but once a creature gains this template it becomes immune to further feed attacks from colours out of space until it loses the colour-blighted simple template. A Charisma score drained to 0 by a colour out of space's feed attack is raised to 1; otherwise, its ability scores are not altered by this template. In order to remove this simple template from a creature, one only has to restore all of its [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drained]] ability scores to normal. As long as a creature suffers the colour-blighted template, it becomes aggressive toward creatures that do not exude the colors of a colour out of space, and gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls against such targets. Every 24 hours, the template attacks a creature suffering from it at +2 vs Fortitude, and on a hit the creature crumbles into fine, white ash—such a doom means instant death and, for many color-blighted creatures, the only chance at escape from a life filled with pain.
