The queens of a race consumed by bitterness and predatory instinct, lamia matriarchs mastermind all manner of foul plots in hopes of breaking the bestial curse that afflicts their race. They move with shocking ease from silken-tongued temptresses to dervishes, striking with all the deadly precision of vipers. Quick to covet, enslave, and overindulge, lamia matriarchs luxuriate in gory feasts, violent trysts, and bloody entertainments, reveling until their playthings are broken or until they tire and move on.

Lamia matriarchs speak Abyssal, Common and Draconic.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th; concentration +17): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ (+9 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ventriloquism|ventriloquism]]_ (+6 vs Will); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deep Slumber|deep slumber]]_ (+8 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dream|dream]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Image|major image]]_ (+8 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_ (+8 vs Will).

**Spells known** (CL 6th; concentration +11): 3rd (4/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_; 2nd (6/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Knell|death knell]]_ (+7 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_; 1st (8/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_.

**Spells**: A lamia matriarch casts spells as a 6th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]], and can cast spells from the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] list as well as those normally available to a sorcerer. Cleric spells are considered arcane spells for a lamia matriarch.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Wisdom Drain|Wisdom Drain]] (Su)**: A lamia matriarch drains 1d4 points of Wisdom each time she hits with her melee touch attack. The first time each round that she strikes a foe with a melee weapon, she also drains 1 point of Wisdom. It attacks at +11 vs Will, and a miss negates the Wisdom drain. Unlike with other kinds of ability drain attacks, a lamia matriarch does not heal damage when she uses her Wisdom drain. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.
