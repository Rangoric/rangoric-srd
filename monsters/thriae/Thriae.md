# Thriae

Thriae live in hive colonies, each ruled by a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/thriae/Thriae Queen|queen]], guided by [[rangoric-srd/monsters/thriae/Thriae Seer|seers]] and defended by [[rangoric-srd/monsters/thriae/Thriae Soldier|soldiers]]. All three make use of merope, which the queen makes in vast stores.
