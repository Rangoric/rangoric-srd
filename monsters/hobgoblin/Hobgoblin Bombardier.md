A hobgoblin bombardier is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hobgoblin/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/alchemist/Grenadier (Archetype)|grenadier]] archetype). Hobgoblins who have high intelligence or find themselves drawn to destruction, flames, and experimentation often take on the role of bombardiers in the military.

These [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemists]] focus their race's penchant for fire into more productive and efficient means of destruction than mere torches or bonfires. Instead, they hone the craft of concocting bombs, mutagens, and extracts. Though they possess the means of enhancing their physical prowess, many bombardiers forgo imbibing their mutagens prior to battle, believing that increasing one's weaknesses, even for potential gain in other areas, is a tactical mistake. When the situation calls for it, however, they drink mutagens to increase their physical stamina or ranged accuracy, preferring to decrease their perception and influence before compromising their mental faculties.

A hobgoblin bombardier speaks Aklo, Common, Goblin, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The bombardier hurls bombs at enemies, or uses directed blast if the foes are grouped closely together. He uses unstable accelerants with the bombs that seem most likely to hit. He saves most of his alchemical items to use with the alchemical weapon ability if he gets stuck fighting in melee range. In this circumstance, he drinks his mutagen before attacking.

**Alchemist extracts prepared** (CL 8th): 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life|false life]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bomber’s Eye|bomber's eye]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_ (2).

**Alternate Racial Features (Ex)**: The bombardier has the [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Engineer|engineer]] racial trait in place of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hobgoblin/Hobgoblin|sneaky]], granting him a +2 bonus on Craft (alchemy) and Engineering checks. He has the [[rangoric-srd/races/class-bonuses/Hobgoblin Alchemist (Class Bonus)|hobgoblin’s alchemist class bonus]], granting him four additional uses of his bomb ability each day.
