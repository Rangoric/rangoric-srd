A hobgoblin forerunner is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hobgoblin/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the hobgoblin leaders. Veteran soldiers become leaders of hobgoblin warbands. Some inspire their troops, but most lead by the lash.

A hobgoblin forerunner speaks Common and Goblin.
