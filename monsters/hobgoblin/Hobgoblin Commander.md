A hobgoblin commander is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hobgoblin/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]] with 13 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Samurai|samurai]]. The commanders of hobgoblin armies are among the most formidable warriors and military tacticians the world over.

A hobgoblin commander speaks Common, Goblin, and Infernal.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The commander challenges an enemy leader and fires flaming burst arrows from horseback until the initial target is dead, then uses Chain Challenge to challenge another.
