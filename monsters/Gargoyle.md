Gargoyles often appear to be winged stone statues, for they can perch indefinitely without moving and use this disguise to surprise their foes. They require no food, water, or air, but often eat their fallen foes out of fondness for inflicting pain.

Gargoyles speak Common and Terran.

### Combat

Gargoyles either remain still, then suddenly attack, or dive onto their prey.

A gargoyle’s natural weapons are treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Freeze (Ex)**: A gargoyle can hold itself so still it appears to be a statue. An observer must succeed on a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check to notice the gargoyle is really alive.

**Skills**: Gargoyles have a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks. \*The Stealth bonus increases by +8 when a gargoyle is concealed against a background of stone.

### Kapoacinth

These cousins of the gargoyle have the aquatic subtype. They have a base land speed of 40 feet and a swim speed of 60 feet (no fly speed) and are found only in aquatic environments.

### Gargoyles as Characters

Gargoyle characters possess the following racial traits.

  - \+4 Strength, +4 Dexterity, +8 Constitution, –4 Intelligence, –4 Charisma.

  - Medium size.

  - A gargoyle’s base land speed is 40 feet. It also has a fly speed of 60 feet (average).

  - Darkvision out to 60 feet.

  - Racial Hit Dice: A gargoyle begins with four levels of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^monstrous-humanoid|monstrous humanoid]], which provide 4d8 Hit Dice, a base attack bonus of +4, and base saving throw bonuses of Fort +1, Ref +4, and Will +4.

  - Racial Skills: A gargoyle’s monstrous humanoid levels give it skill points equal to 4 x (2 + Int modifier). Its class skills are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]]. A gargoyle has a +2 racial bonus on Stealth and Perception checks, and an additional +8 bonus on Stealth checks when it is concealed against a background of stone.

  - Racial Feats: A gargoyle’s monstrous humanoid levels give it two feats.

  - \+4 natural armor bonus.

  - Special Qualities (see above): [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|Damage reduction]] 10/magic, freeze.

  - Automatic Languages: Common. Bonus Languages: Auran, Dwarven, Elven, Gnome, Halfling, Giant, Terran.

  - Favored Class: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|Fighter]].

  - Level adjustment +5.
