# Giant Toad

- **Hit Dice**: 3d8+9 (22 hp)

- **Attack**: Bite +5 melee (1d6+6 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]])
- **Full Attack**: Bite +5 melee (1d6+6 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]])
- **Space/Reach**: 10 ft./10 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swallow Whole|Swallow whole]] (1d4 bludgeoning, AC 12, 2 hp)

- **Alignment**: Usually neutral
- **Advancement**: —

Most giant toads live in dryer areas after maturing, returning to the water only to mate and give birth.

Giant toad companions are identical to giant frog companions, save that they lack the frog's tongue and pull abilities. Instead, they gain the poison skin ability at 4th level.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison Skin|Poison Skin]] (Ex)**: A creature that strikes a giant toad with an unarmed strike or natural weapon exposes itself to the toad's poisonous skin. [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Toad Venom|Toad Venom]]: Skin—contact; _attack_ +4 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 4 rounds; _effect_ 1d2 Wis damage; _cure_ 1 miss.
