# Imperial Dragons

Although chromatic and metallic dragons are the most widely known, other families of true dragon exist. The five imperial dragons are regal guardians of ancient lands and servants of cosmic balance. They are wingless, and their flight is a supernatural ability; a Medium or larger imperial dragon makes a gore attack with its horns where other dragons make wing attacks.

The age categories, attacks, breath weapons and other rules the true dragons share are in [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragon, True|Dragon, True]]; each note below prints only what differs.
