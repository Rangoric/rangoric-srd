# Stench (Iathavos)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “stench”, on the Special Qualities line.

The iathavos's stench ability is supernaturally disgusting—creatures it hits are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], while those it misses are still [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]]. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Iathavos|iathavos]] has it.
