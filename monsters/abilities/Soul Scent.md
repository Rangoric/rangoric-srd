# Soul Scent

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “soul scent”, on the Special Qualities line.

This ability functions as the scent ability, save that it also grants the cerberi a +4 racial bonus on Fieldcraft checks made to track undead. This ability even allows a cerberi to track the passage of an incorporeal creature. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Cerberi|cerberi]] has it.
