# Prehensile Tail

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “prehensile tail”, on the Special Qualities line.

All vanaras have long, flexible tails that they can use to carry objects. They cannot wield weapons with their tails, but the tails do allow them to retrieve small stowed objects carried on their persons as a swift action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Vanara|vanara]] has it.
