# Bleed

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “bleed (2d6)”, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines.

A creature with this ability causes wounds that continue to bleed: the target takes the listed [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage each round at the start of its turn, and stops bleeding as that condition says. The amount of damage each round is given in the creature's entry.
