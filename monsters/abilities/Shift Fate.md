# Shift Fate

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “shift fate”, on the Special Attacks line.

As an immediate action, a norn can force an attack against the defense of any one target within 120 feet to be rerolled—this ability must be used immediately after the attack is rolled, and the attack must abide by the result of this second roll. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Norn|norn]] has it.
