# Confusion (Gloomwing)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “confusion”, on the Special Attacks line.

The eerie shifting of patterns on a gloomwing's wings is hypnotic—any creature within 30 feet that does not avert its gaze from the gloomwing is attacked at +4 vs Will at the start of each turn, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting effect—gloomwings and tenebrous worms are immune to this effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Gloomwing|gloomwing]] has it.
