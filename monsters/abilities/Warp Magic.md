# Warp Magic

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “warp magic”, on the Special Qualities line.

Anytime a spell targets the creature, there is an 80% chance that it produces a random effect instead of affecting the creature. Only spells that directly target the creature are warped; area effect spells are not affected. If a spell is warped, roll 1d10 and consult the table in its entry. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Flail Snail|flail snail]] has it.
