# Dispelling Bite

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “dispelling bite”, on the Special Attacks line.

When the creature scores a critical hit with its bite, the victim is affected as if targeted by a greater dispel magic spell. The caster level for this effect is equal to the creature's Hit Dice (CL 14th for most lukwatas). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Lukwata|lukwata]] has it.
