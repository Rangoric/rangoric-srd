# Stunning Shock

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “stunning shock”, on the Special Attacks line.

A siyokoy can emit a strong electrical charge from its tail. When it hits a creature with its tail slap, the attack deals 3d6 points of electrical damage and the target must succeed at a DC 22 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Siyokoy|siyokoy]] has it.
