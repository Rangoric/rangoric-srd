# Trip

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “trip”, on the Attack line.

A creature with the trip special attack can attempt to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] its opponent as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity if it hits with the specified attack. If the attempt fails, the creature is not tripped in return.
