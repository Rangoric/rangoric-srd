# Otherworldly Mind

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “otherworldly mind”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature attempting to contact a cerebric fungus's mind or read its thoughts with a divination spell or similar ability is attacked at +6 vs Will, and on a hit is overwhelmed by the alien thoughts in the creature's head. Those it hits take 1d6 points of nonlethal damage and are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d6 rounds, and the divination effect immediately ends. The attack bonus is Charisma-based and includes a +2 racial bonus. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fungi/Cerebric Fungus|cerebric fungus]] has it.
