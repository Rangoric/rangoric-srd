# Final Judgment

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “final judgment”, on the Special Qualities line.

A yamaraj can only use its miracle spell-like ability to restore a slain outsider to life or to reproduce the following spell effects: banishment, dimensional anchor, greater restoration, plane shift, true resurrection. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/psychopomps/Yamaraj|yamaraj]] has it.
