# Coordinated Swarm

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “coordinated swarm”, on the Special Qualities line.

The swarm coordinates its attacks more than a typical swarm, and deals swarm damage one step higher than a swarm of its Hit Dice would normally cause. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/swarms/Monkey Swarm|monkey swarm]] has it.
