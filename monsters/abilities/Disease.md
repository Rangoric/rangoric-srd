# Disease

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex) or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines*: **Disease Name** (Ex) Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 15; *onset* 1d3 days; *frequency* 1/day; *effect* 1 Con damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves.

A creature with this ability causes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]] in those it contacts. The effects of the disease, including its save, onset, frequency, and cure, are included in the creature's entry. The saving throw to negate the disease is usually a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 the infecting creature's racial HD + the creature's Con modifier; the exact DC is given in the creature's entry). Disease can be removed through *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]* and similar effects.
