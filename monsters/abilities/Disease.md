# Disease

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex) or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines_: **Disease Name** (Ex) Bite—injury; _save_ Fort DC 15; _onset_ 1d3 days; _frequency_ 1/day; _effect_ 1 Con damage; _cure_ 2 consecutive saves.

A creature with this ability causes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]] in those it contacts. The effects of the disease, including its save, onset, frequency, and cure, are included in the creature's entry. The saving throw to negate the disease is usually a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 the infecting creature's racial HD + the creature's Con modifier; the exact DC is given in the creature's entry). Disease can be removed through _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]_ and similar effects.
