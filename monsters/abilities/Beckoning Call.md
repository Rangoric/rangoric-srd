# Beckoning Call

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “beckoning call”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a rusalka can sing or speak, causing all non-fey creatures within a 300-foot spread to approach its position as if compelled to do so via a suggestion spell (+17 vs Will). A creature the call misses is not subject to the same rusalka's beckoning call for 24 hours. When an affected creature begins its turn adjacent to the rusalka, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for that round. These effects continue as long as the rusalka takes a standard action to maintain the effect, plus 1 additional round. This is a sonic mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Rusalka|rusalka]] has it.
