# Thunderbolt (Tempest Behemoth)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “thunderbolt”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a swift action once every 1d4 rounds, the creature can shoot a bolt of lightning that deals 15d6 points of electricity damage and 15d6 points of sonic damage, and that causes permanent deafness in a 200-foot line (+24 vs Reflex; a miss halves damage and negates deafness). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/behemoth/Tempest Behemoth|tempest behemoth]] has it.
