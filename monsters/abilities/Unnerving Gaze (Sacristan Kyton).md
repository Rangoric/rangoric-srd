# Unnerving Gaze (Sacristan Kyton)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “unnerving gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature that succumbs to a sacristan's unnerving gaze becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Sacristan Kyton|sacristan kyton]] has it.
