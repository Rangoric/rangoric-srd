# Muddy Field

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “muddy field”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a standard action when on sand, soil, or other types of loose earth, the creature can gush standing water into the area surrounding it. Upon doing so, the land within 15 feet of the creature is treated as a shallow bog. This water remains as long as the creature is within 15 feet and wishes to maintain the water. The bog instantly disperses as soon as the creature is killed or moves out of the area. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Grodair|grodair]] has it.
