# Star-Shriek

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “star-shriek”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day as a full-round action, a cerebric fungus can unleash a shrill scream of madness. All creatures (except other cerebric fungi) within 30 feet are attacked at +5 vs Will, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d4 rounds. This is a sonic, mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fungus/Cerebric Fungus|cerebric fungus]] has it.
