# Paralyzing Gaze

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “paralyzing gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

Paralysis for 1 round, 30 feet, +11 vs Fortitude. Evil creatures are immune to this effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Shemhazian|shemhazian]] has it.
