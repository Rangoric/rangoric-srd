# Dazzling Burst

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “dazzling burst”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day, a d'ziriak can cause its body to flare with intense, colorful light as a swift action. Non-d'ziriaks within a 20-foot radius is attacked at +3 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute. After using this ability, the d'ziriak's brilliant glow is extinguished for 24 hours. This ability is a light effect, and creatures that cannot see are immune to it. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/D'ziriak|d'ziriak]] has it.
