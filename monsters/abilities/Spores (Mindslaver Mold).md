# Spores (Mindslaver Mold)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “spores”, on the Special Attacks line.

Whenever a mindslaver mold hits a creature with its spore pod, or whenever a creature touches a mindslaver mold (including when a creature hits the mold with a touch attack, unarmed strike, or natural attack), the spores attack the creature at +5 vs Fortitude, and on a hit it takes 1d4 points of Wisdom damage as the mold's spores swiftly drain away the victim's willpower and sense of self. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fungus/Mindslaver Mold|mindslaver mold]] has it.
