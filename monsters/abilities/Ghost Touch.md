# Ghost Touch

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “ghost touch”, on the Special Qualities line.

A festering spirit can manipulate corporeal objects that weigh up to 25 pounds as if those objects had the ghost touch special ability. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Festering Spirit|festering spirit]] has it.
