# Intoxicating Stench

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “intoxicating stench”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day as a swift action, a jinmenju can cause its fruits to emit an unnaturally sweet aroma in a 60-foot spread for 6 rounds. All creatures within the area are attacked at +12 vs Will each round, and on a hit are captivated. A captivated creature takes no actions except to approach the jinmenju via the most direct route possible. If this path leads it into a dangerous area or the jinmenju attacks it, the effect attacks the captivated creature again, and a miss ends it. This is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jinmenju|jinmenju]] has it.
