# Blinding Light

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “blinding light”, on the Special Qualities line.

A shining child can radiate a 60-foot-radius aura of blinding light as a free action. Creatures within the affected area must succeed on a DC 25 Fortitude save or be permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]]. A creature that successfully saves cannot be affected again by the same shining child's aura for 24 hours. The save is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shining Child|shining child]] has it.
