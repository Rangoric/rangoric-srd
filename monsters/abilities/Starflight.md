# Starflight

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “starflight”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can survive in the void of outer space and flies through it at incredible speed. Exact travel times vary; the creature must know the way to its destination. A trip to another planet of the same solar system takes an oma 3d20 days, a shantak 3d20 hours and a mi-go 3d20 months; a trip to another solar system takes an oma 3d20 weeks, a shantak 3d20 days and a mi-go 3d20 years (or more, at the GM's discretion). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Oma|oma]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Shantak|shantak]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Mi-Go|mi-go]] have it.
