# Starflight

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “starflight”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can survive in the void of outer space and flies through it at incredible speed. Exact travel times vary; the creature must know the way to its destination. A trip to another planet of the same solar system takes an oma 3d20 days and a shantak 3d20 hours; a trip to another solar system takes an oma 3d20 weeks and a shantak 3d20 days (or more, at the GM's discretion). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Oma|oma]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shantak|shantak]] have it.
