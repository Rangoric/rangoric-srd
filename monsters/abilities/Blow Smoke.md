# Blow Smoke

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “blow smoke”, on the Special Attacks line.

Smoke constantly drifts from a kapre's mouth, and as a standard action it can exhale a 30-foot cone of smoke. Any creature in the area is attacked at +10 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round. This is a poison effect, and the attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Kapre|kapre]] has it.
