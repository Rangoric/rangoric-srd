# Deaf

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “deaf”, on the Special Qualities line.

Akatas cannot hear. They are immune to spells and effects that rely on hearing to function, but they also cannot make Perception checks to listen. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Akata|akata]] has it.
