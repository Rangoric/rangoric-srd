# Vulnerable to Dispel Magic

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “vulnerable to dispel magic”, on the Special Qualities line.

When the creature is targeted by dispel magic, it takes 1d6 points of damage per caster level (maximum 10d6, vs Fortitude; a miss halves). Greater dispel magic functions similarly (maximum 20d6 damage, vs Fortitude; a miss halves). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Disenchanter|disenchanter]] has it.
