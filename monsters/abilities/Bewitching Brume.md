# Bewitching Brume

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “bewitching brume”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature within 10 feet of a hungry fog or currently being affected by its enveloping mists is attacked at –2 vs Will at the start of that creature's turn, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round at the half-glimpsed shapes of phantoms floating within the fog. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Hungry Fog|hungry fog]] has it.
