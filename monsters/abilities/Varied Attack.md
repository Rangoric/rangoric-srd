# Varied Attack

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “varied attack”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's slam attack deals both bludgeoning and slashing damage since the creature is formed completely of biting eels. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Galvo|galvo]] has it.
