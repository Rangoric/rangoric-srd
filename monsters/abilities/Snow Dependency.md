# Snow Dependency

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “snow dependency”, on the Special Qualities line.

A yuki-onna is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] if she is ever in an area without snow while her snowstorm aura is suppressed or otherwise not functioning. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Yuki-Onna|yuki-onna]] has it.
