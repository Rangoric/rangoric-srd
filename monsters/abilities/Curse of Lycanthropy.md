# Curse of Lycanthropy

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “curse of lycanthropy”, on the Attack line.

A natural [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lycanthrope/Lycanthrope|lycanthrope]]'s bite attack in animal or hybrid form infects a humanoid target with lycanthropy (+5 vs Fortitude). If the victim's size is not within one size category of the lycanthrope's size, this ability has no effect.
