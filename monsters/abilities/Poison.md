# Poison

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex) or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines_: **Poison** (Ex) Sting—injury; _attack_ +12 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d4 Con; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses.

A creature with this ability can poison those it attacks. The effects of the poison, including its attack, frequency, and cure, are included in the creature's entry as a poison block (type, attack, frequency, effect, and cure; how poisons work is under [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]]). A poison usually attacks Fortitude (attack bonus 1/2 the poisoning creature's racial HD + the creature's Con modifier; the exact bonus is given in the creature's entry), and a miss resists it. Poisons can be removed through _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_ and similar effects.

A creature with a poison attack is immune to its own poison and the poison of others of its kind.

Older stat blocks give a poison's effect as initial and secondary damage instead of a frequency and cure; those follow [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Initial and Secondary Damage|initial and secondary damage]].
