# Death Curse

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “death curse”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a creature slays a creature that has a death curse, the slayer is affected by the curse named in the slain creature's entry. The slayer can resist it with a Will save; the curse's name, save DC and effect are given in the creature's entry, written the same way as any other [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Curse|curse]] (how curses work is under [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Curse|curses]]). The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/linnorm/Linnorm|linnorms]] have death curses, each its own.
