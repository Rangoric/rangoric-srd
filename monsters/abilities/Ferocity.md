# Ferocity

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “ferocity”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature with ferocity remains conscious and can continue fighting even if its hit point total is below 0. The creature is still [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] and loses 1 hit point each round. The creature still dies when its hit points fall low enough to kill it (see [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#^death|dead]]).
