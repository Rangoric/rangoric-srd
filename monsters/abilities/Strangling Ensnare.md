# Strangling Ensnare

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “strangling ensnare”, on the Special Attacks line.

A jack-o'-lantern's slam attack entangles the target in its vines for 2d4 rounds if it hits the target at +4 vs Reflex. The target can attempt to burst these entangling vines before the duration expires with a successful DC 12 Strength check as a full-round action. While the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]], its vines also grasp at the target's mouth. The target cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components while it is entangled by the jack-o'-lantern. When a creature is entangled by this ability, the jack-o'-lantern loses its slam attack, but it can end this ability as a free action. The ensnare's attack bonus and the burst DC are Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jack-o’-Lantern|jack-o’-lantern]] has it.
