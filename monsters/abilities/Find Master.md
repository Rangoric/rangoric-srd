# Find Master

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “find master”, on the Special Qualities line.

As long as an abrakarn viper and its diadem are on the same plane, the viper can unerringly find the diadem's wearer (or just the diadem, if it is removed). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Abrakarn Viper|abrakarn viper]] has it.
