# Battle-Trained

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “battle-trained”, on the Special Qualities line.

An einherji or a valkyrie is proficient with all armor. Armor never impacts its speed, and it takes no armor check penalties on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Einherji|einherji]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Valkyrie|valkyrie]] have it.
