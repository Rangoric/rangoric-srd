# Goblin Breath

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “goblin breath”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once every 1d4 rounds, a goblin snake can release a disgusting belch as a standard action. Any creature within 5 feet of the goblin snake is attacked at +3 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d6 rounds by the stench. Creatures it misses cannot be affected by the same goblin snake's goblin breath for 24 hours. Goblin snakes and goblins are immune to this effect. This is a poison effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Goblin Snake|goblin snake]] has it.
