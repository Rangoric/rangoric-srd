# Elemental Affinity

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “elemental affinity”, on the Special Qualities line.

Undine sorcerers with the elemental (water) bloodline treat their Charisma score as 2 points higher for all sorcerer class abilities. Undine clerics with the Water domain cast their Water domain powers and spells at +1 caster level. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/planetouched/Undine, 1st-Level Cleric|undine, 1st-level cleric]] has it.
