# Powerful Slam

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “powerful slam”, on the Special Attacks line.

Kostchtchie's slam attack is considered a primary attack even when he uses a weapon in his other hand, and he always adds his full Strength bonus to damage dealt with his slam attack. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Kostchtchie|kostchtchie]] has it.
