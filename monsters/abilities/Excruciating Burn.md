# Excruciating Burn

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “excruciating burn”, on the Special Attacks line.

A living creature that takes fire damage from a shard slag is attacked at +6 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. This is a fire effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Shard Slag|shard slag]] has it.
