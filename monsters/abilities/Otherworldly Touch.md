# Otherworldly Touch

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “otherworldly touch”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's natural attacks can strike incorporeal creatures as if they were ghost touch weapons. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Zoog|zoog]] has it.
