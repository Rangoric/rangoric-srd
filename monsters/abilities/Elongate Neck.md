# Elongate Neck

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “elongate neck”, on the Special Attacks line.

A rokurokubi can stretch her neck from a normal human length up to 20 feet, giving her reach with her bite attack and threatening all squares within that range. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Rokurokubi|rokurokubi]] has it.
