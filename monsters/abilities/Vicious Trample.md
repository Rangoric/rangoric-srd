# Vicious Trample

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “vicious trample”, on the Special Attacks line.

A juggernaut's massive rollers deal 8d6+38 points of damage on a successful trample attack. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Juggernaut|juggernaut]] has it.
