# Mucus

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “mucus”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a free action, the creature can excrete a trail of mucus that covers its space and lasts for 10 minutes. The mucus comes in two types: slimy and sticky. A character who attempts to move through an area covered in slippery mucus must make a Reflex save each round or fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. Sticky mucus transforms squares into difficult terrain. Only one type of mucus can be in effect at a time in any one square. The creature can move through either type with ease. A square of mucus exposed to a fire source dries and reverts to normal. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Flail Snail|flail snail]] has it.
