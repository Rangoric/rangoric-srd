# Daze

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “daze”, on the Special Attacks line.

An isitoq's nerves form grotesque wings. Any creature struck by the creature's wings must succeed at a DC 11 Will save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1 round as the target's mind is flooded with images of the last seconds of the isitoq's existence as a living creature. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Isitoq|isitoq]] has it.
