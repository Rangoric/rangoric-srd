# Hex-Prone

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “hex-prone”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature takes a -2 penalty on all defenses against harmful witch hexes. The duration of any beneficial hexes longer than 1 round that affect the creature is increased by 50%. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Almiraj|almiraj]] has it.
