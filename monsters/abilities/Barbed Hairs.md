# Barbed Hairs

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “barbed hairs”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can throw barbed hairs from its back at a creature as a ranged touch attack (range increment 20 feet). The hairs attack the Fortitude of a creature they strike, and on a hit it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d6 rounds. A creature that attacks it with a non-reach melee weapon is attacked against Reflex, and on a hit is struck by these hairs. The attack bonus is Constitution-based; the creature's entry gives it. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Giant Tarantula|giant tarantula]] has it.
