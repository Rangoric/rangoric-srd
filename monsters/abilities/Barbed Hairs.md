# Barbed Hairs

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “barbed hairs”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can throw barbed hairs from its back at a creature as a ranged touch attack (range increment 20 feet). A creature struck by these hairs must make a Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d6 rounds. A creature that attacks it with a non-reach melee weapon must make a Reflex save to avoid being struck by these hairs. The save DC is Constitution-based; the creature's entry gives it. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Giant Tarantula|giant tarantula]] has it.
