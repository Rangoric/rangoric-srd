# Infestation (Deathweb)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “infestation”, on the Special Qualities line.

An infestation of poisonous spiders, which nest within this creature's exoskeleton, constantly surrounds a deathweb, to a radius of 5 feet. Any creature within the infestation takes 1d6 points of damage at the end of each round it remains in the area. A creature that takes this damage is attacked at +7 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round. In addition, those damaged by this infestation are poisoned as well. Any area effect attack that deals 10 or more points of damage to a deathweb destroys its infesting spiders, removing its aura for 3 rounds, after which a new batch of spiders swarms out of the undead's body to replenish the infestation. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Deathweb|deathweb]] has it.
