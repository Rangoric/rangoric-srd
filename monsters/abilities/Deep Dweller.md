# Deep Dweller

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “deep dweller”, on the Special Qualities line.

Deep ones are immune to damage from water pressure; their bodies are capable of instantly adjusting to different water depths or even the surface with ease. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Deep One|deep one]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Elder Deep One|elder deep one]] have it.
