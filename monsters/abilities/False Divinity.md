# False Divinity

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “false divinity”, on the Special Qualities line.

Each advodaza chooses one cleric domain and gains the domain spells (5th level and lower) of that domain as spell-like abilities. Each of these abilities can be used 3 times per day. The advodaza does not gain any of the domain's granted powers. Most advodazas possess powers from the Evil, Fire, Law, Trickery, War, or Weather domains, though any domains except Good or Chaos are possible. These spell-like abilities are not included in the stat block above.

- **[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Evil Domain|Evil Domain]]**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|align weapon]]* (evil only), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Good|dispel good]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Good|magic circle against good]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Good|protection from good]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]*
- **[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire Domain]]**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Shield|fire shield]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Produce Flame|produce flame]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Fire|wall of fire]]*
- **[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Law Domain|Law Domain]]**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|align weapon]]* (law only), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Chaos|dispel chaos]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Chaos|magic circle against chaos]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Order’s Wrath|order's wrath]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Chaos|protection from chaos]]*
- **[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain|Trickery Domain]]**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Vision|false vision]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]*
- **[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War Domain]]**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Power|divine power]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|flame strike]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Vestment|magic vestment]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon|magic weapon]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spiritual Weapon|spiritual weapon]]*
