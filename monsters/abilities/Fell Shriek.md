# Fell Shriek

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “fell shriek”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a sabosan can emit a deafening cry in a 30-foot cone. Creatures in this area are attacked at +5 vs Fortitude, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute. Sabosan are immune to this ability. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Sabosan|sabosan]] has it.
