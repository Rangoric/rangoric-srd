# Smite Law (Template)

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “smite law 1/day (+N attack, +N damage)”, on the Special Attacks line. A template grants it, and says how often it can be used.

As a swift action, the creature chooses one lawful target it can see to smite, as the smite evil of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]] of the same level as its Hit Dice: it adds its Charisma bonus (if any) on attack rolls and its Hit Dice on damage rolls against that target. The smite persists until the target is dead or the creature rests.

The entropic creature simple template grants it once per day ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Entropic Creature|Entropic Creature]]).
