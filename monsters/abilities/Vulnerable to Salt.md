# Vulnerable to Salt

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “vulnerable to salt”, on the Special Qualities line.

Half a pound of salt or a gallon of salt water poured over an alpluachra or consumed by a creature an alpluachra has implanted itself in deals 1d3 points of damage to the alpluachra. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Alpluachra|alpluachra]] has it.
