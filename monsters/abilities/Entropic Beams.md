# Entropic Beams

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “entropic beams”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action once every minute, an iathavos can fire beams of entropic energy from its 10 eyes. Each of these beams of energy can be directed at a single target within 300 feet of the iathavos, but no more than one beam may be directed at any one creature. Beams that are not directed at a creature are wasted. The qlippoth must make a +15 ranged touch attack to hit with each beam. Each beam has the same effect as a CL 20th disintegrate (40d6 damage, +22 vs Fortitude, a miss dealing 5d6 damage), except a creature killed by this damage explodes in a 5-foot burst of energy, flesh, shadow, and smoke instead of turning into dust. Any creature in this burst is attacked at +22 vs Will, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. The attack bonuses are Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Iathavos|iathavos]] has it.
