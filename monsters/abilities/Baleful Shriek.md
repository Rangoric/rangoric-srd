# Baleful Shriek

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “baleful shriek”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once every 1d4 rounds, a revenant can shriek as a standard action. All creatures within a 60-foot spread are attacked at +8 vs Will, and on a hit cower in fear for 1d4 rounds. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Revenant|revenant]] has it.
