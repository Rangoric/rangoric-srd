# Shake Faith

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “shake faith”, on the Special Attacks line.

Anytime an akvan strikes a divine spellcaster with any of its melee attacks, it attacks the target at +20 vs Will, and on a hit the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d4 rounds. On a miss, the target is instead shaken for 1 round. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Akvan|akvan]] has it.
