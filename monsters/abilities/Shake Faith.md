# Shake Faith

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “shake faith”, on the Special Attacks line.

Anytime an akvan strikes a divine spellcaster with any of its melee attacks, the target must make a DC 30 Will save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d4 rounds. If the save is successful, the target is instead shaken for 1 round. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Akvan|akvan]] has it.
