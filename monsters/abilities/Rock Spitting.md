# Rock Spitting

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “rock spitting”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can spit rocks from the essentially inexhaustible store in its gizzard. It can spit up to four rocks as a standard action, with a range increment of 60 feet. A creature that is critically hit by one of these rocks is attacked at +28 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. The attack bonus is Strength-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/behemoth/Thunder Behemoth|thunder behemoth]] has it.
