# False Heads

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “false heads”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's false heads can be severed. To sever a head, an opponent must make a sunder attempt with a slashing weapon targeting the head. A head is considered a separate weapon with hardness 0 and hit points equal to the creature's Hit Dice (typically 16 hp). To sever a head, an opponent must deal enough damage to reduce the head's hit points to 0 or fewer. Severing a head deals an amount of damage to the creature's body equal to the creature's Hit Dice. The creature can't attack with a severed head. The creature with no remaining heads can't use its cacophony ability or its spell-like abilities. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Seaweed Siren|seaweed siren]] has it.
