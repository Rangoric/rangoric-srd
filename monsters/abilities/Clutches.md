# Clutches

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “clutches”, on the Special Attacks line.

A nightgaunt has a +4 racial bonus on grapple combat maneuver checks. If a nightgaunt uses its fly speed to move itself and a grappled target, it can fly at full speed. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Nightgaunt|nightgaunt]] has it.
