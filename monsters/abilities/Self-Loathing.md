# Self-Loathing

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “self-loathing”, on the Special Qualities line.

When confronted with its reflection or any object that was important to it in life, it attacks a revenant at +10 vs Will, and on a hit the revenant becomes overwhelmed with self-pity. This condition renders the revenant [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], and lasts until the revenant is attacked or sees its murderer. If a revenant resists becoming overwhelmed, the revenant becomes obsessed with the source that triggered the attack and does everything it can to destroy it, reacting to the trigger as if the trigger were its murderer and gaining bonuses from its reason to hate ability. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Revenant|revenant]] has it.
