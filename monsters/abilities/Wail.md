# Wail

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “wail”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per minute, a banshee may wail as a full-round action. The wail lasts until the beginning of her next turn. All creatures within 40 feet of the banshee when she begins her wail, as well as all creatures that end their turn within that radius, are attacked at +13 vs Fortitude. (A wail attacks a creature only once.) Creatures under the effects of a fear effect take a –4 penalty to their Fortitude Defense against it. Creatures it misses are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d6 rounds. Those it hits take 140 points of damage (as if affected by a CL 14 wail of the banshee). If a wailing banshee is damaged during a wail, she must make a Will check (1d20 + Will save bonus; DC 15 + damage taken) to maintain the wail; otherwise it ends. This is a sonic death effect. Banshee wails are supernaturally powerful, and penetrate the effect of any spell of 3rd level or lower that creates silence. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Banshee|banshee]] has it.
