# Crystallize

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “crystallize”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature entrapped by a carnivorous crystal's attack is attacked at +12 vs Fortitude each round, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]]. If this attack hits a helpless creature, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] as its body crystallizes. In 1d4 hours, the petrified victim shatters and a new carnivorous crystal emerges from the remains. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/oozes/Carnivorous Crystal|carnivorous crystal]] has it.
