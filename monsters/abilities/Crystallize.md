# Crystallize

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “crystallize”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature entrapped by a carnivorous crystal's attack must succeed at a DC 22 Fortitude save each round or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]]. If a helpless creature fails this save, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] as its body crystallizes. In 1d4 hours, the petrified victim shatters and a new carnivorous crystal emerges from the remains. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Carnivorous Crystal|carnivorous crystal]] has it.
