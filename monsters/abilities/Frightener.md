# Frightener

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “frightener”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per minute as a standard action, a poltergeist can temporarily drop its natural invisibility, revealing itself to be a skeletal, ghost-like humanoid. All creatures within 30 feet when a poltergeist uses this ability are attacked at +4 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1d4 rounds. The poltergeist then resumes its invisibility at the end of its turn as a free action. A creature it misses is immune to the fear effect of that poltergeist for 24 hours. If the poltergeist's natural invisibility is negated via other methods, it cannot use this ability. Likewise, those that can see invisible creatures are immune to this special attack. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Poltergeist|poltergeist]] has it.
