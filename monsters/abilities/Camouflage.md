# Camouflage

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “camouflage”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature is difficult to spot when it is at rest among plant growth, looking like a normal part of the plant. A successful DC 15 Perception check is required to notice it before it attacks for the first time (the weedwhip's check can be made with Fieldcraft or Nature instead). The garden ooze automatically hits with a slam against any creature that fails to notice it and enters its square. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/oozes/Garden Ooze|garden ooze]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Weedwhip|weedwhip]] have it.
