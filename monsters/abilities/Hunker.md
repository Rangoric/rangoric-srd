# Hunker

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “hunker”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature gains a +4 bonus to its CMD to avoid bull rush or overrun attempts. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Slurk|slurk]] has it.
