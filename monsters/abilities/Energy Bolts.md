# Energy Bolts

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “energy bolts”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once every 1d4 rounds as a standard action that provokes an attack of opportunity, a marai's six snake arms can each spit a bolt of energy to a maximum range of 60 feet. Each bolt deals 1d8 points of damage and has an additional effect if it hits the target at +9 vs Fortitude, as summarized below. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/rakshasas/Marai|marai]] has it.
