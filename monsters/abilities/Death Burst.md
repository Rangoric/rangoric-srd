# Death Burst

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “death burst”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a clockwork golem is reduced to 0 or fewer hit points, it explodes in a shower of razor-sharp gears and debris. All creatures within a 10-foot burst take 12d6 points of slashing damage—it attacks at +8 vs Reflex, and a miss halves the damage. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Clockwork Golem|clockwork golem]] has it.
