# Disgusting Demise

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “disgusting demise”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a grothlut reaches 0 or fewer hit points, its digestive organs rupture, freeing alchemical agents that have a violent effect on the rest of the grothlut's internal organs and flesh. This causes an explosion of grothlut viscera within a 30-foot-radius burst of the creature. Though this viscera deals no damage, it's disgusting to behold and its smell can cause living creatures within that radius to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d4 rounds (+4 vs Fortitude). This is a poison effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarps/Grothlut|grothlut]] has it.
