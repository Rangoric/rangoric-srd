# Manipulate Terrain

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “manipulate terrain”, on the Special Qualities line.

A jinushigami can help any number of creatures travel within its territory. Creatures aided in this way receive a +10 bonus on Fieldcraft checks and do not suffer reductions to overland movement from terrain or lack of a highway or other roadway, regardless of the presence of roads or the type of terrain they actually travel through. Alternatively, a jinushigami can make terrain more difficult for any number of creatures to travel through, imparting a –10 penalty on all Fieldcraft checks and causing overland movement to be reduced to one-tenth its normal speed. A jinushigami can affect as many creatures as it wishes with this ability, selecting whether it aids or impedes travel for each, as long as it can notice the creature and the creature is within its regional ward. A jinushigami may use this ability while merged with its ward. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kamis/Jinushigami|jinushigami]] has it.
