# Master's Bond

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “master's bond”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can form a bond with a willing creature by touching that creature. This allows the creature to communicate telepathically with the bonded creature with no range restriction (provided the creature and its master are on the same plane). Both the creature and master can sense the other's condition as if both were under the effect of a status spell. The creature can maintain a bond with only one master at a time. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Thrasfyr|thrasfyr]] has it.
