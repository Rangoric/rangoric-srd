# Infestation

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “infestation”, on the Special Attacks line.

Any living creature that takes damage from the swarm is attacked against Reflex, and on a hit becomes infested. The infested creature takes 1d4 points of Constitution damage per round as the rot grubs burrow through and consume its flesh; this effect continues as long as the victim remains in the swarm and for 1d6 rounds after it leaves the swarm. Any energy-based attack (including damage from negative energy) that deals at least 5 points of damage to the victim automatically destroys all of the rot grubs infesting it, ending the effect prematurely. Additionally, any effect that removes disease instantly ends a rot grub infestation. Immunity to disease offers no defense. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/swarm/Rot Grub Swarm|rot grub swarm]] has it.
