# Thundering Path

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “thundering path”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's charge shakes the ground it crosses. The path of its charge is considered difficult terrain until the start of the creature's next turn, and creatures with tremorsense that are within 100 feet of the end of the creature's charge are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for that same amount of time. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ypotryll|ypotryll]] has it.
