# Lash Out

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “lash out”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a swift action, the creature can make a single attack with a bite, claw, or tail slap. The creature cannot lash out on the same round it charges. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bandersnatch|bandersnatch]] has it.
