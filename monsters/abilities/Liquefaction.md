# Liquefaction

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “liquefaction”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature killed by the creature's poison dissolves into an acidic liquid that deals 2d6 points of acid damage per round to anything in its square (including the dead creature's equipment). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Seps|seps]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Juvenile Seps|juvenile seps]] have it.
