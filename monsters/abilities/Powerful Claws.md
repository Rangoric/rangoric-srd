# Powerful Claws

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “powerful claws”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's claw attacks deal damage equal to 1-1/2 times its Strength modifier. In addition, it ignores up to 5 points of hardness when damaging objects. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shipwrecker Crab|shipwrecker crab]] has it.
