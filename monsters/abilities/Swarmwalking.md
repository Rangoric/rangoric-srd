# Swarmwalking

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “swarmwalking”, on the Special Qualities line.

A derghodaemon is immune to damage or distraction effects caused by swarms. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Derghodaemon|derghodaemon]] has it.
