# Sin-Scent

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “sin-scent”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature has scent against creatures whose nature reflects its related sin. For example, a wrathful fleshdreg or sinspawn can scent creatures using rage effects. The GM should adjudicate what creatures a particular fleshdreg or sinspawn can scent. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Fleshdreg|fleshdreg]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Sinspawn|sinspawn]] have it.
