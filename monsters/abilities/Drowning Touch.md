# Drowning Touch

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “drowning touch”, on the Special Attacks line.

A fossegrim can flood the lungs of a creature that is willing, is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], is affected by its enchanted music ability, touches it while it's in treasure form, or is touched by it (traditionally by kissing the creature on the lips). If the target cannot breathe water, it cannot hold its breath and immediately begins to drown slowly. On its turn, the water attacks the target at +6 vs Fortitude, and on a miss it coughs up the water; on a hit, it falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] at 0 hp. On the next round, the water attacks a fallen target again at +6 vs Fortitude, and on a hit it drops to -1 hit points and is dying; on the next round it attacks again, and on a hit the target loses 1d6 hit points. On the first miss, the water clears from the target's lungs and the target stabilizes. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Fossegrim|fossegrim]] has it.
