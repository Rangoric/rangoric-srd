# Self-Destruction (Clockwork Goliath)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “self-destruction”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a goliath's hit points are reduced to 10% its total (21 hp for most clockwork goliaths) or less but still above 0, it self-destructs on its next turn, bursting in an explosion of metal scraps and steam that deals 12d6 points of slashing damage plus 12d6 points of fire damage to all creatures within a 20-foot-radius burst. The explosion attacks at +14 vs Reflex, and a miss halves the damage. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/clockwork/Clockwork Goliath|clockwork goliath]] has it.
