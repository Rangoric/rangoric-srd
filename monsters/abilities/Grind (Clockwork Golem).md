# Grind (Clockwork Golem)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “grind”, on the Special Attacks line.

A clockwork golem deals an additional 2d10+12 points of slashing damage when it makes a successful grapple check as razor-sharp gears and blades emerge from its body to grind and slice its foe. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golems/Clockwork Golem|clockwork golem]] has it.
