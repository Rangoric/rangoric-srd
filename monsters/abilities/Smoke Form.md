# Smoke Form

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Spell-Like]] (Sp). In a stat block*: “smoke form”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a fire yai can turn into a cloud of smoke. This functions like gaseous form, except the cloud has the properties of the smoke cloud from a pyrotechnics spell (Fort DC 26 negates the effects of the smoke cloud). The fire yai can end this ability as a standard action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/onis/Fire Yai|fire yai]] has it.
