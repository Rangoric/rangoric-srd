# Smoke Form

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Spell-Like]] (Sp). In a stat block_: “smoke form”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a fire yai can turn into a cloud of smoke. This functions like gaseous form, except the cloud has the properties of the smoke cloud from a pyrotechnics spell (+16 vs Fortitude; a miss negates the effects of the smoke cloud). The fire yai can end this ability as a standard action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/onis/Fire Yai|fire yai]] has it.
