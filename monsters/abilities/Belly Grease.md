# Belly Grease

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “belly grease”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature exudes a slippery grease from its belly that grants it a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] checks and to its CMD against grapples. Once per minute, it can wallow on a solid surface as a full-round action to coat the floor in a 5-foot radius with this grease. The smear created turns that area of floor into difficult terrain for 10 minutes, after which the grease dries to a nasty crust. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Slurk|slurk]] has it.
