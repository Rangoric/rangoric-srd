# Protected Life Force

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “protected life force”, on the Special Qualities line.

Movanic devas are never harmed by positive-dominant or negative-dominant planar traits. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/angels/Angel, Movanic Deva|angel, movanic deva]] has it.
