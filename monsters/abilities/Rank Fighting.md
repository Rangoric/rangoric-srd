# Rank Fighting

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “rank fighting”, on the Special Attacks line.

Whenever a terra-cotta soldier is adjacent to another terra-cotta soldier, it gains a +2 dodge bonus to its AC and a +2 bonus on defenses, attack rolls, and damage rolls. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Terra-Cotta Soldier|terra-cotta soldier]] has it.
