# Stone Knowledge

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “stone knowledge”, on the Special Attacks line.

A pech's knowledge of earth and stone grants a +1 racial bonus on attack and damage rolls and the benefits of the Improved Critical feat against creatures and objects made of stone or earth or with the earth subtype. Dungeoneering, Engineering and Profession (miner) are always class skills for a pech. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Pech|pech]] has it.
