# Second Skin

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “second skin”, on the Special Qualities line.

A legion archon is proficient in all forms of armor. It takes no reduction to its speed or any armor check penalties from wearing any sort of armor. Most legion archons wear full plate armor. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archon/Legion Archon|legion archon]] has it.
