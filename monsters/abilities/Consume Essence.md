# Consume Essence

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “consume essence”, on the Special Attacks line.

A shira's deadliest attacks drain away a portion of its victim's essence. Whenever a shira hits with a coup de grace attack using its bite, or confirms a critical hit with its claws or bite, the target is attacked at +13 vs Fortitude, and on a hit takes 1d4 points of Constitution drain. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Shira|shira]] has it.
