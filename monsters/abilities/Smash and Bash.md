# Smash and Bash

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “smash and bash”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a free action, a buggane can attempt to trip an opponent it damages with its rend attack without provoking an attack of opportunity. If the trip attempt fails, the buggane cannot be tripped in return. If the trip attempt is successful, the target must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. The save DC is Strength-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Buggane|buggane]] has it.
