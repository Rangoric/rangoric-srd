# Fire Healing

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “fire healing”, on the Special Qualities line.

A magical attack that deals fire damage heals the wickerman 1 point of damage for every 3 points of damage the attack would otherwise deal. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Wickerman|wickerman]] has it.
