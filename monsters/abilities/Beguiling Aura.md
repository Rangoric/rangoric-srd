# Beguiling Aura

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “beguiling aura”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature sexually attracted to women runs the risk of being beguiled by a nereid if it looks upon her beauty from a distance of 30 feet or less. If the aura hits the creature at +13 vs Will, it is immediately [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]]. A nereid may use her suggestion spell-like ability at will against creatures that are fascinated by her beguiling aura. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymph/Nereid|nereid]] has it.
