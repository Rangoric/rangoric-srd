# Powerful Tail

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “powerful tail”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's tail slap is always a primary attack. When the creature deals damage to an object with its tail slap or via constrict damage, it ignores the first 10 points of hardness that object possesses. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Deep Sea Serpent|deep sea serpent]] has it.
