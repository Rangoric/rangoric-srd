# Sleep (Sandman)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “sleep”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature struck by a sandman's slam attack is attacked at +4 vs Will, and on a hit immediately falls asleep, as if affected by a sleep spell (caster level 8th). There is no limit to the number of Hit Dice a sandman can affect with this ability. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Sandman|sandman]] has it.
