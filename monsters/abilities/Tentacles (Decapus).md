# Tentacles (Decapus)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “tentacles”, on the Special Qualities line.

A decapus's tentacles must all strike at a single target, but they do so as a primary attack. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Decapus|decapus]] has it.
