# Alchemy

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “alchemy”, on the Special Attacks line.

When an alchemical golem strikes a foe, the attack has an additional random effect, chosen from the options below. The attack can either deal 1d6 points of acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage, or cause the target to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] (Fortitude DC 16 negates) or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] (Reflex DC 16 negates) for 1d4 rounds. These save DCs are Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Alchemical Golem|alchemical golem]] has it.
