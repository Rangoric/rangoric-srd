# Mind-Warping Gaze

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “mind-warping gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

Fear, 30 feet, +8 vs Will. A creature this attack hits becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d6 rounds. A creature currently suffering from a fear effect that this attack hits instead takes 1d4 points of Wisdom damage. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Mothman|mothman]] has it.
