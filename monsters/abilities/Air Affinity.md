# Air Affinity

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “air affinity”, on the Special Qualities line.

Sylph sorcerers with the elemental (air) bloodline treat their Charisma score as 2 points higher for all sorcerer spells and class abilities. Sylph clerics with the Air domain cast their domain powers and spells at +1 caster level. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/planetouched/Sylph, 1st-Level Rogue|sylph, 1st-level rogue]] has it.
