# Crippling Bite

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “crippling bite”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's bite attack threatens a critical hit on a roll of 19–20. If it scores a critical hit on a target, its mandibles cut deep, resulting in a wound that causes 1d6 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Bleed|bleed]] and leaving its foe [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d3 rounds from the tremendous pain dealt. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Slicer Beetle|slicer beetle]] has it.
