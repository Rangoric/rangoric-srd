# Destructive Charge

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “destructive charge”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's charge attack ignores up to 1 point of a target object's hardness for each 5 feet charged. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ypotryll|ypotryll]] has it.
