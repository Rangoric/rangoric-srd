# Baleful Whinny

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “baleful whinny”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a sagari can emit a monstrous whinny. Any creature within 60 feet is attacked at +2 vs Will, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d4 rounds by the hideous sound. This is a sonic, mind-affecting effect. Whether the attack hits or misses, the affected creature is immune to the same sagari's baleful whinny for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Sagari|sagari]] has it.
