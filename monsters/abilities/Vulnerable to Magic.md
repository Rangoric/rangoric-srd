# Vulnerable to Magic

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “vulnerable to magic”, on the Special Qualities line.

A transmute mud to rock spell deals 1d6 points of damage per caster level to any mudlord in the area and automatically staggers it for 2d6 rounds. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elementals/Mudlord|mudlord]] has it.
