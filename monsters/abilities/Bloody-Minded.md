# Bloody-Minded

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “bloody-minded”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature is not easily swayed from its bloody business. It gains a +6 bonus on defenses against charm and compulsion effects. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ypotryll|ypotryll]] has it.
