# Diseased

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “diseased”, on the Special Qualities line.

Due to its filthy eating habits and constant exposure to decaying flesh, a creature with this ability is a harbinger of disease. Any creature it bites has a 10% chance of being exposed to [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Blinding Sickness|blinding sickness]], or a similar disease. Once this check is made, a victim can no longer be infected by this particular creature, though attacks by different diseased creatures are resolved normally and may result in multiple illnesses. A diseased creature gains a +4 racial bonus on defenses against disease, though it isn't immune to all diseases. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Vulture|giant vulture]] has it.
