# Wounding Weapons

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “wounding weapons”, on the Special Attacks line.

Any weapon wielded by a lhaksharut gains the wounding weapon quality as long as it remains in the creature's grasp. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/inevitables/Lhaksharut|lhaksharut]] has it.
