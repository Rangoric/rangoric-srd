# Dread Howl

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “dread howl”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day, a baykok can unleash a blood-curdling howl. Any living creature within a 30-foot-radius burst becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] with fear for 1 round if the burst hits it at +9 vs Will. Any creature it misses is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round. This is a fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Baykok|baykok]] has it.
