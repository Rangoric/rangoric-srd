# Alignment Variation (Soulbound Doll)

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “alignment variation”, on the Special Qualities line.

Soulbound dolls are at least partially neutral in alignment, although they can also be chaotic, evil, good, or lawful. They have an alignment-dependent spell-like ability usable once per day as listed below.

- *Chaotic Neutral*: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]*
- *Lawful Neutral*: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]* (DC 12)
- *Neutral*: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deep Slumber|deep slumber]]* (DC 12)
- *Neutral Evil*: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Serious Wounds|inflict serious wounds]]* (DC 12)
- *Neutral Good*: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]*

The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/soulbound/Soulbound Doll|soulbound doll]] has it.
