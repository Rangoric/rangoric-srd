# Tenacious Grapple

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “tenacious grapple”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature does not gain the grappled condition when it grapples a foe. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ahuizotl|ahuizotl]] has it.
