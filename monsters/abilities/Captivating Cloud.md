# Captivating Cloud

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “captivating cloud”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day as a swift action, a viper vine can emit a transparent cloud of pollen in a 60-foot spread that has the power to lull the minds of those that smell it. Once activated, the cloud persists for 5 rounds unless dispersed by moderate or stronger wind. All creatures in the cloud are attacked at +14 vs Will each round, and on a hit become captivated. Once a creature becomes captivated, it takes no actions save to approach the viper vine via the most direct route possible. If this path leads it into a dangerous area, such as through fire or off a cliff, the effect attacks that creature again, and a miss ends it, before it moves into peril. The effect attacks a victim again during each of the vine's attacks on it, and a miss overcomes the effect. This is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Viper Vine|viper vine]] has it.
