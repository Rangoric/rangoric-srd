# Devour Soul (Astradaemon)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “devour soul”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, an astradaemon that begins its turn with a grappled opponent can attempt to draw out and consume the soul of its victim, killing it instantly. This ability only works on living creatures, which it attacks at +15 vs Fortitude, a miss resisting it. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. For every 5 HD of the slain creature, the daemon gains a +1 profane bonus on attacks and checks and to defenses for 24 hours. This ability does not consume all of the soul, and pieces of it still exist after the daemon completes its feast (enough to be able to resurrect the slain victim normally). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Astradaemon|astradaemon]] has it.
