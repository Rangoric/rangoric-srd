# Darting Claw

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “darting claw”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature is exceptionally quick with its claws. When making a full attack, if it misses with both of its claw attacks, it can make an additional claw attack at a –2 penalty against any creature within its reach. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shark-Eating Crab|shark-eating crab]] has it.
