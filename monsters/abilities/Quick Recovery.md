# Quick Recovery

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “quick recovery”, on the Special Qualities line.

A debilitated creature of this kind recovers with frightening speed. If the creature starts its turn affected by any or all of the following conditions, these conditions end at the end of its turn: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]], and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]]. Furthermore, if the creature is affected by ability damage, ability drain, or a mind-affecting effect that allows a save, it receives a single additional save against the effect of its choice at the original DC at the end of its turn in order to shake off the effect. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bandersnatch|bandersnatch]] has it.
