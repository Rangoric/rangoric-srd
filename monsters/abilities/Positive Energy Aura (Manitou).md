# Positive Energy Aura (Manitou)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “positive energy aura”, on the Special Qualities line.

Living creatures within the manitou's aura heal 5 hit points on its turn each round. A manitou can exclude a number of creatures equal to its Charisma modifier (typically 7) from the aura's effect, and can cease or resume the aura as a free action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Manitou|manitou]] has it.
