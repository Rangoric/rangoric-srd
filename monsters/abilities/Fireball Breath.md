# Fireball Breath

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “fireball breath”, on the Special Attacks line.

A flame drake can, as a standard action, breathe a ball of flame that explodes like a fireball. This attack has a range of 180 feet and deals 5d6 points of fire damage (+6 vs Reflex; a miss halves) to all creatures within a 20-foot-radius spread. Once a flame drake has used its fireball breath, it cannot do so again for 1d6 rounds. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drake/Flame Drake|flame drake]] has it.
