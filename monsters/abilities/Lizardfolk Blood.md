# Lizardfolk Blood

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “lizardfolk blood”, on the Special Qualities line.

A lizard scion counts as a lizardfolk for all effects related to race. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lizardfolk/Lizard Scion|lizard scion]] has it.
