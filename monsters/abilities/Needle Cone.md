# Needle Cone

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “needle cone”, on the Special Attacks line.

Three times per day, as a standard action a saguaroi can eject a barrage of needles from its body. This effect creates a 15-foot-cone burst of needles that deals 4d6 points of piercing damage to all creatures in the area (+10 vs Reflex; a miss halves). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Saguaroi|saguaroi]] has it.
