# Smashing Breach

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “smashing breach”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a full-round action, the creature can make a special charge attack against creatures on the surface of the water. At the end of its charge, it breaches, then slams down onto the target with incredible force. Any creatures of the size its entry names or smaller in its space are attacked against Reflex, and on a hit take bludgeoning damage (the attack bonus and damage are in its entry) and are forced into the nearest square that is adjacent to it. This breach automatically attempts to capsize any boats caught wholly or partially in this area ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Capsize|capsize]]). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/whale/Great White Whale|great white whale]] has it.
