# Smashing Breach

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “smashing breach”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a full-round action, the creature can make a special charge attack against creatures on the surface of the water. At the end of its charge, it breaches, then slams down onto the target with incredible force. Any creatures of the size its entry names or smaller in its space must make a Reflex save or take bludgeoning damage (the DC and damage are in its entry) and be forced into the nearest square that is adjacent to it. This breach automatically attempts to capsize any boats caught wholly or partially in this area ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Capsize|capsize]]). The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/whale/Great White Whale|great white whale]] has it.
