# Soul Corruption

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “soul corruption”, on the Special Attacks line.

If a spire drake successfully performs a coup de grace upon a creature afflicted by its accursed breath, the slain target arises 1d4 rounds later as a wight. This wight is not under the drake's control, and the wight and any spawn that it creates perish 1d6 days after arising. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drake/Spire Drake|spire drake]] has it.
