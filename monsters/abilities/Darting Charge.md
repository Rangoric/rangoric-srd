# Darting Charge

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “darting charge”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature is adept at swooping in to attack prey with a powerful bite and then, just as quickly, swooping back up out of reach. As a result, it gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flyby Attack|Flyby Attack]] as a bonus feat. In addition, if it charges while flying, it receives a +4 bonus on CMB checks made to grapple foes. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Dragonfly|giant dragonfly]] has it.
