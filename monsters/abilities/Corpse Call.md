# Corpse Call

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “corpse call”, on the Special Attacks line.

Zuvembies cannot speak, but their strange calls and whistles captivate the minds of those who hear them. Once per day, a zuvembie may call out, and all living creatures with an Intelligence score of 3 or higher within a 100-foot spread are attacked at +6 vs Will, and on a hit move toward the zuvembie using the most direct means possible. If this path leads them into a dangerous area such as through fire or off a cliff, the call attacks the creatures again, and a miss ends the effect, before they move into peril. Captivated creatures can take no actions other than to defend themselves. A victim within 5 feet of the zuvembie simply stands and offers no resistance to the zuvembie's attacks. This effect continues for as long as the zuvembie continues its call as a standard action each round. This is a sonic mind-affecting charm effect, and has no effect on deaf creatures. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Zuvembie|zuvembie]] has it.
