# Inflict Warpwave

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “inflict warpwave”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, an imentesh can inflict a warpwave upon any corporeal creature within 100 feet. The warpwave attacks the target at +10 vs Fortitude, and a miss resists its effects. If the imentesh wishes, it can use this ability as a swift action, but if it does so, the warpwave attacks its Fortitude as well, and affects it on a hit. See the warpwave table for a list of possible effects caused by a warpwave. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/proteans/Imentesh|imentesh]] has it.
