# Spores (Halsora)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “spores”, on the Special Attacks line.

A halsora's claws are covered in mutated russet mold spores. A creature hit by a claw attack is attacked at +8 vs Fortitude, and on a hit takes 2 points of Constitution damage per round. The spores attack it again each round, and a miss halts the growth. A creature reduced to 0 Constitution by these spores explodes in a mess of viscera and spores. Creatures within the burst are attacked at +8 vs Fortitude, and on a hit are affected as if they were hit by the halsora's claw attack. This is a disease effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarp/Halsora|halsora]] has it.
