# Staggering Strikes

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “staggering strikes”, on the Special Attacks line.

An ice yai can strike twice per round with its two slam attacks. A creature struck by more than two of these slam attacks in a round must make a DC 27 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. The save DC is Strength-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/onis/Ice Yai|ice yai]] has it.
