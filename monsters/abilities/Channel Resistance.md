# Channel Resistance

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “channel resistance +4”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature with this special quality (usually an undead) is less easily affected by channeled negative or positive energy. The creature adds the listed bonus on saves made to resist the effects of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-channel-energy|channel energy]], including effects that rely on the use of channel energy (such as the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Command Undead (Feat)|Command Undead]] feat).
