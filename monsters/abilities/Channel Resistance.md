# Channel Resistance

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “channel resistance +4”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature with this special quality (usually an undead) is less easily affected by channeled negative or positive energy. The creature adds the listed bonus on defenses against the effects of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-channel-energy|channel energy]], including effects that rely on the use of channel energy (such as the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Command Undead (Feat)|Command Undead]] feat), and is treated as having that many more Hit Dice against attempts to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turn or rebuke undead]] it (turn, rebuke, command, or bolster).

Older stat blocks print it as “+4 turn resistance”, which is the same ability. An eidolon can gain it with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Channel Resistance (Evolution)|channel resistance evolution]].

Also see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Turn Resistance|Turn Resistance]] under Special Abilities.
