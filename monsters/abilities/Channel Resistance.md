# Channel Resistance

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “channel resistance +4”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature with this special quality (usually an undead) is less easily affected by channeled negative or positive energy. The creature adds the listed bonus on saves made to resist the effects of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-channel-energy|channel energy]], including effects that rely on the use of channel energy (such as the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Command Undead (Feat)|Command Undead]] feat), and is treated as having that many more Hit Dice against attempts to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turn or rebuke undead]] it (turn, rebuke, command, or bolster).

Older stat blocks print it as “+4 turn resistance”, which is the same ability. An eidolon can gain it with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Channel Resistance (Evolution)|channel resistance evolution]].

Also see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Turn Resistance|Turn Resistance]] under Special Abilities.
