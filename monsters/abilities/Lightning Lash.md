# Lightning Lash

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “lightning lash”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action that doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity, a comozant wyrd can shock any creature or object within 30 feet to which it has line of effect, dealing 2d8 electricity damage. The wyrd can choose for this damage to be nonlethal. If the target is also affected by the wyrd's illuminating flames, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round (+6 vs Fortitude) and the flames are dispelled. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Comozant Wyrd|comozant wyrd]] has it.
