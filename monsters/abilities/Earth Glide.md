# Earth Glide

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “earth glide”, on the Special Qualities line.

When the creature burrows, it can pass through stone, dirt, or almost any other sort of earth except metal as easily as a fish swims through water. If protected against fire damage, it can even glide through lava. Its burrowing leaves behind no tunnel or hole, nor does it create any ripple or other sign of its presence. A *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Move Earth|move earth]]* spell cast on an area containing the burrowing creature flings it back 30 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]] it for 1 round unless it succeeds at a DC 15 Fortitude save.
