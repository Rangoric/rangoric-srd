# Fatal Faker

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “fatal faker”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action three times per day, the creature can teleport as per the spell dimension door, but only within a range of 30 feet. Upon teleporting, the creature leaves behind a perfect replica of itself amid a colored flash and the sound of a reptilian choke. This replica duplicates the creature in all ways, though it is obviously dead and the colorless stone in its head is reduced to worthless dust. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Carbuncle|carbuncle]] has it.
