# Wand Magic

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “wand magic”, on the Special Attacks line.

A clockwork mage's wand crystal allows it to cast spells as if using a spell trigger magic item (CL 9th). The arcane school of the wand crystal determines a clockwork mage's spells. They cast 1st-level spells at will, 2nd-level spells 3 times per day, and 3rd level spells 1 time per day.

- *Abjuration*: 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Portal|hold portal]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]*; 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Arrows|protection from arrows]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*; 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*

- *Conjuration*: 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]* (DC 11), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster I]]*; 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glitterdust|glitterdust]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]* (DC 13); 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stinking Cloud|stinking cloud]]* (DC 14)

- *Enchantment*: 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bungle|bungle]]* (DC 11), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]* (DC 11); 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze Monster|daze monster]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Idiocy|touch of idiocy]]*; 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deep Slumber|deep slumber]]* (DC 14)

- *Evocation*: 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]* (DC 11); 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]*; 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]* (DC 14)

- *Illusion*: 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]* (DC 11), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vanish|vanish]]*; 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]* (DC 13); 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]*

- *Necromancy*: 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]* (DC 11), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|ray of enfeeblement]]* (DC 11); 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness/deafness]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scare|scare]]* (DC 13); 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Exhaustion|ray of exhaustion]]* (DC 14)

- *Transmutation*: 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]* (DC 11); 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]*; 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*

The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/clockwork/Clockwork Mage|clockwork mage]] has it.
