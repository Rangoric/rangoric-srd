# Soul Focus (Soulbound Mannequin)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “soul focus”, on the Special Qualities line.

The soul bound to the mannequin lives within a focus integrated into the doll or its apparel, typically a carved mask. As long as this soul focus remains intact, it can be used to animate another mannequin, at the same cost as creating a new soulbound mannequin. The new mannequin retains its personality and memories. A soul focus has hardness 8, 12 hit points, and a break DC of 20. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/soulbound/Soulbound Mannequin|soulbound mannequin]] has it.
