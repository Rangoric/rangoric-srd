# Move through Hedges

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “move through hedges”, on the Special Qualities line.

A living topiary may move through any mass of brambles or other dense plant growth without penalty. It must begin and end its turn outside of the mass. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Living Topiary|living topiary]] has it.
