# Shell

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “shell”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a move action, the creature can pull its extremities and head into its shell. It can't move or attack as long as it remains in this state, but its armor bonus from natural armor increases by 4 as long as it does. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Snapping Turtle|snapping turtle]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Snapping Turtle|giant snapping turtle]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Tortoise|giant tortoise]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Immense Tortoise|immense tortoise]] have it.
