# Infused Weapons (Upasunda)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “infused weapons”, on the Special Attacks line.

In addition to being evil and lawful, weapons an upasunda wields are considered to be magic for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/asuras/Upasunda|upasunda]] has it.
