# Thunderous Trample

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “thunderous trample”, on the Special Attacks line.

Any creature damaged by a svathurim's trample attack is attacked at +12 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Svathurim|svathurim]] has it.
