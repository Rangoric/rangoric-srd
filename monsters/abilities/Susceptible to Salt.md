# Susceptible to Salt

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “susceptible to salt”, on the Special Qualities line.

A handful of salt burns a creature with this weakness as if it were a flask of acid, causing 1d6 points of damage per use. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Leech|giant leech]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Slug|giant slug]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/swarm/Leech Swarm|leech swarm]] have it.
