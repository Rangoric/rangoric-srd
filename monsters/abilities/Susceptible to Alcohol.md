# Susceptible to Alcohol

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “susceptible to alcohol”, on the Special Qualities line.

Strong alcohol harms the hood. Every quart poured on it deals 1 point of damage. Four quarts of alcohol incapacitates it and forces it to release a grappled target. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Executioner’s Hood|executioner’s hood]] has it.
