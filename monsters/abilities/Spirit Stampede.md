# Spirit Stampede

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “spirit stampede”, on the Special Attacks line.

Every 1d4 rounds, a manitou can call upon animal spirits to attack, affecting a 60-foot cone and dealing 10d6 points of force damage (+16 vs Reflex; a miss halves). Using this ability temporarily weakens the manitou's connection with the natural world, halting its positive energy aura until the stampede is ready to be used again. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Manitou|manitou]] has it.
