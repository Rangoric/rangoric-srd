# Mudball (Mudlord)

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “mudball”, on the Special Attacks line.

A mudlord can throw a ball of sticky mud up to 30 feet at an opponent's face as a ranged touch attack. It if hits, the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]]. Each round on its turn, the target can attempt a DC 16 Reflex save as a free action; if successful, the target shakes off the mud. The target or an adjacent creature can wipe off the mud as a standard action. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Mudlord|mudlord]] has it.
