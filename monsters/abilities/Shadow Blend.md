# Shadow Blend

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “shadow blend”, on the Special Qualities line.

In conditions of illumination other than bright light, a shadow drake disappears into the shadows, giving it concealment (20% miss chance). It can resume or suspend this ability as a free action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drake/Shadow Drake|shadow drake]] has it.
