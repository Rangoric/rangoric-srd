# Pheromones

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “pheromones”, on the Special Attacks line.

After the first round of combat, a gloomwing can emit a strange, musky scent in a 30-foot radius as a free action. All creatures within this area (save for other gloomwings or tenebrous worms) are attacked at +4 vs Fortitude each round, and on a hit become weakened by the pheromones. Once this effect hits a creature, it takes a –4 penalty to its Strength score—this penalty lasts for as long as the battle continues and for 1 hour thereafter. Lesser restoration or any other effect capable of healing ability damage immediately removes this Strength penalty. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Gloomwing|gloomwing]] has it.
