# Kiss of Death

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “kiss of death”, on the Special Attacks line.

A catrina can kill a grappled, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], or willing target with a long, passionate kiss. The kiss attacks the target at +7 vs Fortitude, and on a hit the target takes 5d6 points of damage. Any creature damaged by the same catrina's kiss for three consecutive rounds instantly dies, regardless of how many hit points it has remaining. Creatures of the old age category take a -2 penalty on defenses against this ability, while those in the venerable age category take a -4 penalty. This is a death effect. The attack bonuses are Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/psychopomps/Catrina|catrina]] has it.
