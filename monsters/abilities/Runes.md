# Runes

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “runes”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a free action, whenever a rune giant uses its spark shower or spell-like abilities, it can cause the runes on its body to flash with light. All creatures within 10 feet of the giant must make a DC 24 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1 round. The saving throw is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Rune Giant|rune giant]] has it.
