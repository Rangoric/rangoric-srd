# Endless Coils

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “endless coils”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a full-round action, the creature can attempt a single combat maneuver check to grapple up to two Large or four Medium or smaller creatures within its reach. Any targets successfully grabbed take constrict damage. The creature only needs to succeed at one grapple check to maintain a grapple against multiple opponents. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Abaia|abaia]] has it.
