# Numbing Touch

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “numbing touch”, on the Special Attacks line.

Each time a freezing flow deals cold damage with its slam attack or constrict ability, the cold attacks the target at +7 vs Fortitude, and on a hit the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] with numbing cold for 1 round. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Freezing Flow|freezing flow]] has it.
