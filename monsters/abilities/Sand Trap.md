# Sand Trap

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sand trap”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can create a pit of the size its entry gives in any sand or soft earth surface. Creating a sand trap takes 1 hour. A Perception check allows a creature to realize such a depression in the sand is in fact a trap. Any creature that steps into the trap slides to the center if the trap hits its Reflex; such victims take no damage, but they do fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. The creature can make an attack of opportunity against any creature that falls to the bottom of its sand trap. These creatures can move across sand traps at their normal speed and are immune to the trap's effects. Other creatures can navigate the trap's walls with an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check. The attack bonus is Dexterity-based; the creature's entry gives the DCs. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Ant Lion|giant ant lion]] has it.
