# Stunning Slam

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “stunning slam”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a hekatonkheires may forgo any weapon attacks to make a single slam attack against any creature in reach. If it hits, the target takes damage and is attacked at +31 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d6 rounds. The attack bonus is Strength-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/titans/Hekatonkheires Titan|hekatonkheires titan]] has it.
