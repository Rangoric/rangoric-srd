# Enticing Head-Fruits

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “enticing head-fruits”, on the Special Attacks line.

Any creature that begins its turn within 5 feet of a jinmenju must succeed at a DC 22 Will save or be magically compelled to immediately grab a head-fruit and eat it. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect. A creature that successfully saves is immune to that jinmenju's enticing head-fruits for 24 hours. The save DC is Constitution-based. Anyone who takes a bite out of one is exposed to [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Head-Fruit Poison|Head-Fruit Poison]]. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jinmenju|jinmenju]] has it.
