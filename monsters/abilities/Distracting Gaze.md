# Distracting Gaze

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “distracting gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

Staggered for 1d4 rounds, 30 feet, +6 vs Will. This is a mind-affecting effect that requires the kodama to be visible to those it wishes to affect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kamis/Kodama|kodama]] has it.
