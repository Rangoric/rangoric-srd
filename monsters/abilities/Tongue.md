# Tongue

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “tongue”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can grab a foe with its tongue and draw the victim to its mouth. This tongue attack has the reach its entry gives. The attack does no damage, but allows the creature to grab. It does not gain the grappled condition while using its tongue in this manner. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Chameleon|giant chameleon]] has it.
