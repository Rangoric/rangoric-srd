# Augmented Critical

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “augmented critical”, on the Special Qualities line.

A piscodaemon's claws threaten a critical hit on an 18–20 and inflict ×3 damage on a successful critical hit. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Piscodaemon|piscodaemon]] has it.
