# Talon or Wing

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “talon or wing”, on the Special Attacks line.

A garuda cannot use its wing attacks while flying, and cannot use its talon attacks while not flying. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Garuda|garuda]] has it.
