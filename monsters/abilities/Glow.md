# Glow

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “glow”, on the Special Qualities line.

The colorful runes that decorate a d'ziriak's body create dim light in a 20-foot radius from its body. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/D'ziriak|d'ziriak]] has it.
