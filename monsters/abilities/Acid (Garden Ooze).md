# Acid (Garden Ooze)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “acid”, on the Special Attacks line.

A garden ooze excretes digestive acid that can dissolve flesh. Creatures made of harder materials or plant matter are immune to the ooze's acid. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Garden Ooze|garden ooze]] has it.
