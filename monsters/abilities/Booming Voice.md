# Booming Voice

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “booming voice”, on the Special Qualities line.

A derhii can use its own booming voice as a signal. The sound can be heard up to 12 miles away as a thrumming in the air that conveys 20 words of information in 5 minutes. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Derhii|derhii]] has it.
