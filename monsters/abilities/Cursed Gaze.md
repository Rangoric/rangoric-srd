# Cursed Gaze

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “cursed gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

Aghash's choice: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1 round, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round, or deals 1d4 points of damage, 30 feet, +6 vs Fortitude. Any creature under the effects of protection from evil is immune to an aghash's gaze. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Aghash|aghash]] has it.
