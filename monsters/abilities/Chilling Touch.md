# Chilling Touch

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “chilling touch”, on the Special Attacks line.

A yuki-onna's touch causes 4d6 cold damage. Whenever a creature takes cold damage in this manner, it is attacked at +10 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] by the supernatural cold for 1 round. This duration stacks. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Yuki-Onna|yuki-onna]] has it.
