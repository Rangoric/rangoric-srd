# Wind Walk (Wendigo)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Spell-Like]] (Sp). In a stat block_: “wind walk”, on the Special Attacks line.

If a wendigo pins a grappled foe, it can attempt to wind walk with the target by using its spell-like ability—it automatically succeeds on all concentration checks made to use wind walk. If the spell hits the victim, the wendigo hurtles into the sky with him. Each round, the spell attacks the victim again at +13 vs Will, and on a miss he turns solid again, but at this point he falls if he cannot fly. Eventually, the wendigo strands the victim in some rural area, usually miles from where it began. A creature that wind walks with a wendigo is exposed to wendigo psychosis. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Wendigo|wendigo]] has it.
