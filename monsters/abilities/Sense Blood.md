# Sense Blood

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sense blood”, on the Special Qualities line.

A vampiric mist can immediately sense the presence of warm-blooded creatures in a 60-foot radius as if by scent. It can detect exposed blood within a mile. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Vampiric Mist|vampiric mist]] has it.
