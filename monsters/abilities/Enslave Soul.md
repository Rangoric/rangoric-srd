# Enslave Soul

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “enslave soul”, on the Special Attacks line.

A kalavakus can attempt to enslave the soul of any mortal creature within 60 feet as a swift action. The kalavakus must have line of sight to the target. It attacks the target at +8 vs Will, and a miss resists it, but the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round even on a miss. On a miss, the creature is immune to this ability for 24 hours. On a hit, the target's soul is enslaved—this creature takes a –6 penalty on all attack rolls and defenses against that kalavakus. If a creature with an enslaved soul is slain by that kalavakus, the soul immediately infuses the demon's body, affecting it with a heal spell (CL 12th). A kalavakus can have only one mortal soul enslaved at a time—if it enslaves a second soul, the first is released. This is a mind-affecting death effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Kalavakus|kalavakus]] has it.
