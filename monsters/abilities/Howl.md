# Howl

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “howl”, on the Special Attacks line.

Three times per day as a standard action, a wendigo can emit a forlorn howl that can be heard up to a mile away. Any who hear the howl are attacked at +18 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for an hour. Creatures within 120 feet become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for 1d4+4 rounds, and those within 30 feet cower with fear for 1d4 rounds. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Wendigo|wendigo]] has it.
