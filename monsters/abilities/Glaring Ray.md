# Glaring Ray

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “glaring ray”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can blast a fiery ray of brilliant light from its eyes as a standard action to a range of 100 feet. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Simurgh|simurgh]] has it.
