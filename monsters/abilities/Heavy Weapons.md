# Heavy Weapons

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “heavy weapons”, on the Special Qualities line.

A redcap can wield weapons sized for Medium creatures without penalty. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Redcap|redcap]] has it.
