# Gale

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “gale”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can generate winds of up to hurricane force as a free action, blowing in as many as three different directions at once. The winds blow either away from or directly toward the creature in a 30-foot-wide path that extends for 1,000 feet. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/behemoth/Tempest Behemoth|tempest behemoth]] has it.
