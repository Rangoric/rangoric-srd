# Glare

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “glare”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, in any area of normal or brighter light, the creature can ruffle its metallic feathers in such a way as to blind all creatures nearby. Any creature within 30 feet must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1d6 minutes. A creature can defend against this effect in the same way it would a gaze attack. This is a sight-based effect. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Stymphalidies|stymphalidies]] has it.
