# Aerial Charge (Derhii)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “aerial charge”, on the Special Attacks line.

When airborne, a derhii can dive at twice its normal flying speed. This is the equivalent of a charge, granting a +2 bonus on the attack roll and a –2 penalty to AC. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Derhii|derhii]] has it.
