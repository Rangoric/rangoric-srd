# Cold Vigor

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “cold vigor”, on the Special Qualities line.

When a charda is in a cold environment, it becomes more animated and gains a +1 racial bonus on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Charda|charda]] has it.
