# Fiery Regeneration

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “fiery regeneration”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature with fiery regeneration does not take damage from fire-based attacks. Additionally, when it would normally take fire damage, it heals that number of hit points, to a maximum of 5 hit points per round. It can't use its breath weapon to heal itself. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Kyrana|kyrana]] has it.
