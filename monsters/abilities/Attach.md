# Attach

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “attach”, on the Attack line.

The creature automatically latches onto its target when it successfully makes the listed attack. The creature is considered [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]], but the target is not. The target can attack or grapple the creature as normal, or break the attach with a successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] check. Most creatures with this ability have a racial bonus to maintain a grapple (listed in its CMB entry).
