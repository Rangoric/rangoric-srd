# Rending Claws

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “rending claws”, on the Special Attacks line.

A charybdis's claws are particularly devastating when used against objects, and ignore the first 10 points of an object's hardness rating. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Charybdis|charybdis]] has it.
