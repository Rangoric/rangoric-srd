# Demon Lord Traits

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “demon lord traits”, on the Special Qualities line.

A demon lord is a powerful, unique demon that rules a layer of the Abyss. All demon lords are chaotic evil outsiders that are, at a minimum, CR 26. Demon lords have a particular suite of traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature’s entry) as summarized here.

- A demon lord can grant spells to its worshipers as if it were a deity. A demon lord’s domains are [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Evil Domain|Evil]], and two other domains relevant to its theme and interests. Like a deity, a demon lord has a favored weapon.
- A demon lord’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as chaotic, epic, and evil for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Abyssal Resurrection|Abyssal Resurrection]] (Ex): a demon lord that rules an Abyssal realm returns to life if it is slain, as described in its own note.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Frightful Presence|Frightful Presence]] (Ex): a demon lord can activate its frightful presence as a free action as part of any attack, spell-like ability, special attack, or by speaking aloud.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Immunity|Immunity]] to ability damage, ability drain, charm effects, compulsion effects, death effects, energy drain, and petrification.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Regeneration|Regeneration]] (Ex): only epic and good damage, or damage from a creature of equal or greater power (such as an archdevil, deity, demon lord or protean lord) interrupts a demon lord’s regeneration.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Resistance|Resistance]] to acid 30, cold 30, and fire 30.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|Summon Demon]] (Sp): three times per day as a swift action, a demon lord can summon any demon or combination of demons whose total combined CR is 20 or lower. This otherwise works like the summon universal monster rule with a 100% chance of success, and counts as a 9th-level spell effect.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Telepathy|Telepathy]] 300 feet.

[[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Dagon|Dagon]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Kostchtchie|Kostchtchie]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Pazuzu|Pazuzu]] have it.
