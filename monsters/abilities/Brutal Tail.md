# Brutal Tail

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “brutal tail”, on the Special Attacks line.

The quills and barbs on the creature's tail cause triple damage on a critical hit from its tail slap. The creature adds 1-1/2 times its strength bonus on attack rolls when using its tail slap. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bandersnatch|bandersnatch]] has it.
