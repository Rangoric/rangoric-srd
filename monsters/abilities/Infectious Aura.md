# Infectious Aura

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “infectious aura”, on the Special Qualities line.

All creatures within 50 feet of a leukodaemon take a –4 penalty on Fortitude Defense against disease effects. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Leukodaemon|leukodaemon]] has it.
