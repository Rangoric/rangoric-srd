# Water Magic

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “water magic”, on the Special Qualities line.

An oceanid can use her spell-like abilities only when she is in the ocean or within 1 mile of the ocean. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Oceanid|oceanid]] has it.
