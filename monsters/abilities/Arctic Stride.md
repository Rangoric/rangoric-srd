# Arctic Stride

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “arctic stride”, on the Special Qualities line.

An adlet can move through any sort of difficult terrain at its normal speed while within arctic or snowy terrain. Magically altered terrain affects an adlet normally. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Adlet|adlet]] has it.
