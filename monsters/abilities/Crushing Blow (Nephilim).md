# Crushing Blow (Nephilim)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “crushing blow”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a nephilim makes a successful critical hit with a melee attack, the blow attacks the target at +12 vs Fortitude, and on a hit the target takes an additional 3d6 points of nonlethal damage and is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. The attack bonus is Strength-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Nephilim|nephilim]] has it.
