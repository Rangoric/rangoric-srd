# Crushing Blow (Nephilim)

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “crushing blow”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a nephilim makes a successful critical hit with a melee attack, the target must make a DC 22 Fortitude save or take an additional 3d6 points of nonlethal damage and be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. The save DC is Strength-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Nephilim|nephilim]] has it.
