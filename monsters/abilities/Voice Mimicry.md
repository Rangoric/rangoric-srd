# Voice Mimicry

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “voice mimicry”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can perfectly mimic the sound of humanoid sobbing and can even attempt to mimic voices it has heard. When mimicking a voice, the creature must make a Deception check opposed by its listener's Sense Motive check. If the listener has never heard the voice the creature is attempting to mimic, the listener takes a –8 penalty on the Sense Motive check. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ahuizotl|ahuizotl]] has it.
