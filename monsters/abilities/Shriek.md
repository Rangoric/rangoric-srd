# Shriek

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “shriek”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once every 1d6 rounds as a standard action, the creature can voice a piercing screech. All creatures (other than jubjub birds) within a 60-foot-radius spread are attacked at +16 vs Fortitude, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d4 rounds. This is a sonic effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jubjub Bird|jubjub bird]] has it.
