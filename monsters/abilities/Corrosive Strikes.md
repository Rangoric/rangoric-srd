# Corrosive Strikes

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “corrosive strikes”, on the Special Attacks line.

A tiberolith reduced to 30 or fewer hit points leaks acid, and deals an additional 1d6 points of acid damage with its slam attacks. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tiberolith|tiberolith]] has it.
