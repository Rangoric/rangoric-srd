# Mighty Roar

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “mighty roar”, on the Special Attacks line.

Every 1d4 rounds as a standard action, the creature can issue a mighty roar in a 60-foot cone that duplicates the effect of an empowered greater shout (+20 vs Fortitude; a miss halves). This is a sonic effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/behemoth/Thunder Behemoth|thunder behemoth]] has it.
