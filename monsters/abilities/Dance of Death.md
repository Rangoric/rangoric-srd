# Dance of Death

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “dance of death”, on the Special Attacks line.

A necrophidius can entrance opponents by swaying back and forth as a full-round action. All creatures within 30 feet who can see the necrophidius when it uses its dance of death are attacked at +5 vs Will, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 2d4 rounds. This is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based and includes a +4 racial bonus. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Necrophidius|necrophidius]] has it.
