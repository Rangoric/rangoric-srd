# Glide (Hydrodaemon)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “glide”, on the Special Qualities line.

A hydrodaemon can launch itself into the air and glide along for 1 minute, gaining a fly speed of 40 feet with average maneuverability. While gliding, the hydrodaemon gains the pounce ability. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Hydrodaemon|hydrodaemon]] has it.
