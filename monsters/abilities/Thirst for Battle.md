# Thirst for Battle

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “thirst for battle”, on the Special Qualities line.

As long as an einherji is in battle (i.e., each round it makes an attack roll), it gains fast healing 5. An einherji loses this ability outside of combat or if knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Einherji|einherji]] has it.
