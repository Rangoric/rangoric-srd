# Fear Aura

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “fear aura (30 ft., DC 17)”, on the Special Qualities line.

The use of this ability is a free action. The aura can freeze an opponent (as in the case of a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Mummy|mummy]]’s despair) or function like the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]_ spell. Other effects are possible. A fear aura is an area effect. The descriptive text gives the size and kind of the area.

A few creatures have a fear aura of their own that works differently: the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jack-o’-Lantern|jack-o’-lantern]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Quickwood|quickwood]].
