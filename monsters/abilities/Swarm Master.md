# Swarm Master

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “swarm master”, on the Special Attacks line.

Pazuzu is immune to swarm damage and other swarm effects (such as distraction). As a swift action, he can direct the movement of any swarm within 30 feet. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demons/Pazuzu|pazuzu]] has it.
