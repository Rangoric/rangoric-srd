# Devour Soul

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “devour soul”, on the Special Attacks line.

A baykok can take a standard action to devour the soul of an adjacent dead or dying creature. This attacks a dying creature at +9 vs Fortitude, and a miss resists it. On a hit, the target is instantly slain. If the creature is already dead, it hits automatically, although the body cannot be more than 1 hour dead. A creature subjected to this attack cannot be brought back to life via raise dead (resurrection and more powerful effects work normally). When a baykok devours a soul in this way, it heals 5d6+10 points of damage and becomes hasted for 4 rounds (as if affected by haste). This is a death effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Baykok|baykok]] has it.
