# Slime Trail

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “slime trail”, on the Special Qualities line.

A hungry flesh leaves behind a trail of slime that acts as a grease spell (DC 17). Any living creature that touches this slime with bare flesh must succeed at a Fortitude save (with a +4 bonus) or contract [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Tumor Infestation|tumor infestation]]. The slime dries up after 1 minute. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Hungry Flesh|hungry flesh]] has it.
