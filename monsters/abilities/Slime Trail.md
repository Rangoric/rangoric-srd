# Slime Trail

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “slime trail”, on the Special Qualities line.

A hungry flesh leaves behind a trail of slime that acts as a grease spell (+7 vs Reflex). Any living creature that touches this slime with bare flesh is attacked against Fortitude (with a +4 bonus to its Fortitude Defense), and on a hit contracts [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Tumor Infestation|tumor infestation]]. The slime dries up after 1 minute. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/oozes/Hungry Flesh|hungry flesh]] has it.
