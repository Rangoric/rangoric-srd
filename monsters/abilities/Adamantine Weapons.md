# Adamantine Weapons

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “adamantine weapons”, on the Special Attacks line.

The teeth and claws of a clockwork dragon are made of adamantine and have the qualities of a weapon made from that material. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/clockwork/Clockwork Dragon|clockwork dragon]] has it.
