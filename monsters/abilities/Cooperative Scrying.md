# Cooperative Scrying

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Spell-Like]] (Sp). In a stat block_: “cooperative scrying”, on the Special Qualities line.

Three or more caulborn joining hands can scry on a place or creature as if using the scrying spell (+10 vs Will), but with no limit to the spell's duration so long as at least three of the caulborn involved continue to join hands and concentrate. This ability functions at CL 7th (or at the highest CL available to the most powerful caulborn in the group). The attack bonus is Charisma-based, adjusted by the modifier of the caulborn with the highest Charisma score. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Caulborn|caulborn]] has it.
