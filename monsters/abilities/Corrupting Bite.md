# Corrupting Bite

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “corrupting bite”, on the Special Attacks line.

A slaugrak's caustic saliva is infused with the corrupting power of the Abyss. Any living creature that takes acid damage from a slaugrak's bite is attacked at +7 vs Fortitude, and on a hit takes 2 points of Constitution bleed damage as its blood boils and its internal organs liquefy. Creatures without a discernible anatomy (blood and internal organs) are immune to the Constitution bleed. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Slaugrak|slaugrak]] has it.
