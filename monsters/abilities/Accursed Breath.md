# Accursed Breath

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “accursed breath”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a spire drake can spit a compressed ball of jet-black dust that bursts into a spray of clinging motes that sap the spiritual strength of creatures in an area. The attack has a range of 60 feet and deals 5d6 points of negative energy damage (+8 vs Will; a miss halves) to all creatures in a 20-foot radius. Affected creatures take a -2 penalty on attack rolls, defenses, skill checks, and ability checks for 1d4 rounds (or for 1 round on a miss against Will). As long as these motes cling to a creature, the effect of any conjuration (healing) effect used on that creature is halved. This is a curse effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drake/Spire Drake|spire drake]] has it.
