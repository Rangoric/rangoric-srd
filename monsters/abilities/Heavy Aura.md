# Heavy Aura

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “heavy aura”, on the Special Qualities line.

Whenever a creature enters the drowning devil's heavy aura, the aura attacks it at +8 vs Will, and on a hit it reduces its speed as if carrying a load one step higher or wearing armor one category heavier (whichever is worse), and its armor check penalty increases by 2. A creature already carrying a heavy load or wearing heavy armor that the aura hits can't move as long as it remains in the affected area. A creature a drowning devil's heavy aura misses is immune to that devil's aura for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Drowning Devil|drowning devil]] has it.
