# Vortex

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “vortex”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can create a whirlpool as a standard action, at will. This ability functions identically to the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Whirlwind|whirlwind]] special attack, but the whirlpool can form only underwater and cannot leave the water. The vortex attacks a creature at +15 vs Reflex, and on a hit the creature is caught by the churning waters. The vortex is 20 feet across; the charybdis's is 120 feet deep and deals 2d6+12 points of damage per round, the lorelei's is 80 feet deep and deals 2d8+9 points of damage. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Charybdis|charybdis]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Lorelei|lorelei]] have it.
