# Vortex

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “vortex”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can create a whirlpool as a standard action, at will. This ability functions identically to the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Whirlwind|whirlwind]] special attack, but the whirlpool can form only underwater and cannot leave the water. A creature must succeed at a DC 25 Reflex save or be caught by the churning waters. The vortex is 20 feet across; the charybdis's is 120 feet deep and deals 2d6+12 points of damage per round, the lorelei's is 80 feet deep and deals 2d8+9 points of damage. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Charybdis|charybdis]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Lorelei|lorelei]] have it.
