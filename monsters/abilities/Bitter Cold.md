# Bitter Cold

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “bitter cold”, on the Special Qualities line.

All creatures within 20 feet of the creature take 1d6 points of cold damage each round on the creature's turn. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Glacier Toad|glacier toad]] has it.
