# Plant Defenses

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “plant defenses”, on the Special Qualities line.

A fungal crawler is part plant and shares many of the immunities that plant creatures possess. A fungal crawler is immune to mind-affecting effects, paralysis, poison, polymorph, sleep, and stunning. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Fungal Crawler|fungal crawler]] has it.
