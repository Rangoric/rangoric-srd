# Insane Beauty

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “insane beauty”, on the Special Qualities line.

This ability affects all humanoids within 30 feet who are viewing a lampad in conditions brighter than dim light. Those who look directly upon the lampad must succeed at a DC 18 Will save or gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] condition for 1d6 rounds. A creature that succeeds at the save is immune to the same lampad's insane beauty for 24 hours. A lampad can suppress or resume this ability as a free action. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymph/Lampad|lampad]] has it.
