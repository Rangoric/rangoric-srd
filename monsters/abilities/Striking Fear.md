# Striking Fear

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “striking fear”, on the Special Attacks line.

If a bogeyman confirms a critical hit or a sneak attack with one of its claws on a target currently suffering a fear effect, that effect automatically becomes one step more severe ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] creatures become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], frightened creatures become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], and panicked creatures cower in fear). The claw attacks at +15 vs Will, and a miss negates this increase. In addition, a critical hit from the bogeyman's claw makes the creature's fear aura attack the Will of any target it has missed again, even if 24 hours have not yet passed. This is a fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bogeyman|bogeyman]] has it.
