# Positive Energy Affinity

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “positive energy affinity”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature is healed by positive energy and harmed by negative energy, as a living creature is, even if it is [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^undead|undead]].
