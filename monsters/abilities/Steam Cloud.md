# Steam Cloud

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “steam cloud”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, the creature can emit a cloud of steam from its snout in a spread. The initial burst of steam deals fire damage to any creature in the area. The cloud remains for 1d4 rounds once created, acting as an _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_, and any creature that begins its turn in the area of the steam must succeed at a Fortitude save or take more fire damage (the spread, damage and DC are in the creature's entry). It can use its steam cloud only once every 1d6 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Steam Hog|steam hog]] has it.
