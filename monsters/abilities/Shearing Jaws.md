# Shearing Jaws

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “shearing jaws”, on the Special Attacks line.

All tupilaqs are crafted with oversized jaws capable of tearing and pulling. A tupilaq applies twice its Strength modifier to damage with a successful bite attack and threatens a critical hit on a roll of 19–20. A tupilaq is treated as Large for all checks made to move a grappled opponent. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tupilaq|tupilaq]] has it.
