# Spawn Undead

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “spawn undead”, on the Special Attacks line.

Any creature slain by a pukwudgie's poisonous quills rises in 24 hours as a zombie. Undead created by this ability are not immediately under the control of a pukwudgie, but they receive a –4 penalty on defenses against a pukwudgie's control undead spell-like ability. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Pukwudgie|pukwudgie]] has it.
