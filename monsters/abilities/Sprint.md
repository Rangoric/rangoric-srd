# Sprint

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sprint”, on the Special Qualities line.

Once per minute, a werecrocodile can increase its land speed to 60 feet for 1 round. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lycanthropes/Werecrocodile|werecrocodile]] has it.
