# Produce Water

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “produce water”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a standard action once per hour, a saguaroi can cut itself (taking 1 point of damage) to produce 1 gallon of water. After producing 4 gallons, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]]. After producing 8 gallons, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]] and cannot produce more water for 24 hours. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Saguaroi|saguaroi]] has it.
