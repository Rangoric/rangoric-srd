# Entropic Flesh

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “entropic flesh”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature that hits a sceaduinar with a melee attack takes 1d6 points of negative energy damage. Attacking with a weapon that provides reach allows a creature to avoid taking this damage. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Sceaduinar|sceaduinar]] has it.
