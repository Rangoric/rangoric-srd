# Profane Wishcraft

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “profane wishcraft”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature that accepts a wish from Pazuzu immediately becomes chaotic evil if the wish hits it at +33 vs Will. A creature that becomes chaotic evil in this way gains the benefits of a good hope spell for 1 week, followed by the effects of crushing despair for 1d6 months (CL 30th). The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demons/Pazuzu|pazuzu]] has it.
