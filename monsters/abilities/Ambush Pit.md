# Ambush Pit

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “ambush pit”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can spend 1 minute digging itself into the soil or other ground debris. It then waits for its [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] to detect prey. When a creature comes within range, it can erupt from the ground as a free action as part of a charge, allowing it to use its [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Pounce|pounce]] ability. When dug in, it has improved cover, granting it a +10 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/scorpions/Giant Emperor Scorpion|giant emperor scorpion]] has it.
