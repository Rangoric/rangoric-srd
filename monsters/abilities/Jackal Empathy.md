# Jackal Empathy

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “jackal empathy”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can communicate and empathize with jackals (use stats for Small dog), and can use Deception as if it were Diplomacy to change a jackal's attitude, receiving a +4 racial bonus to do so. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jackalwere|jackalwere]] has it.
