# Powerful Bite

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “powerful bite”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's bite attack always applies 1-1/2 times its Strength modifier on damage rolls and threatens a critical hit on a roll of 19–20. When the creature bites an object, its bite treats the object as having a hardness of 5 less than the object's actual hardness rating. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Leucrotta|leucrotta]] has it.
