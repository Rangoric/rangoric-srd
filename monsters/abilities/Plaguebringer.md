# Plaguebringer

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “plaguebringer”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature increases the disease DC of rats (including rat kings, rat swarms, and dire rats) within 100 feet by +2. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Rat King|rat king]] has it.
