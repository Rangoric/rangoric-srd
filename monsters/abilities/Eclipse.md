# Eclipse

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “eclipse”, on the Special Attacks line.

Anytime a tzitzimitl casts deeper darkness, any creatures in the area of darkness when it is created take 8d6 points of cold damage (+21 vs Fortitude; a miss halves). Any creature that takes damage from this effect becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] as long as it remains in the area of darkness and for 1d4 rounds after it leaves that area. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tzitzimitl|tzitzimitl]] has it.
