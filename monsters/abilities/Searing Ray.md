# Searing Ray

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “searing ray”, on the Special Attacks line.

A shining child's primary attack is a ray of searing light. This attack has a range of 120 feet. The ray deals double damage to undead creatures. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shining Child|shining child]] has it.
