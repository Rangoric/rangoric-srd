# Maker's Gift

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “maker's gift”, on the Special Qualities line.

Each creature with this ability gains one additional ability that is based on its specific form. Typical additional abilities include the following.

*Flight*: The creature gains a fly speed of 30 feet (clumsy).

*Grab and Constrict*: The creature's tentacle attack gains grab and constrict. The creature's constrict damage is equal to its slam damage plus its Strength bonus.

*Poison*: One claw or tentacle attack is replaced with a sting attack (same damage as the replaced attack) plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|poison]]. Sting—injury; *save* Fort DC 19; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d2 Strength damage; *cure* 1 save. The save DC is Constitution-based.

*Pounce and Rake*: The creature gains pounce and rake (2 claws +14, 1d8+7).

*Quills*: A creature attacking it with a melee weapon, an unarmed strike, or a natural weapon takes 1d8+7 points of piercing damage from the creature's quills. Melee weapons with reach do not endanger their users in this way.

*Trample*: The creature gains a trample attack that deals an amount of damage equal to its claw damage plus 1-1/2 times its Strength modifier. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Yaoguai|yaoguai]] has it.
