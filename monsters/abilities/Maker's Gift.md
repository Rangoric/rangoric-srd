# Maker's Gift

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “maker's gift”, on the Special Qualities line.

Each creature with this ability gains one additional ability that is based on its specific form. Typical additional abilities include the following.

_Flight_: The creature gains a fly speed of 30 feet (clumsy).

_Grab and Constrict_: The creature's tentacle attack gains grab and constrict. The creature's constrict damage is equal to its slam damage plus its Strength bonus.

_Poison_: One claw or tentacle attack is replaced with a sting attack (same damage as the replaced attack) plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|poison]]. Sting—injury; _save_ Fort DC 19; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d2 Strength damage; _cure_ 1 save. The save DC is Constitution-based.

_Pounce and Rake_: The creature gains pounce and rake (2 claws +14, 1d8+7).

_Quills_: A creature attacking it with a melee weapon, an unarmed strike, or a natural weapon takes 1d8+7 points of piercing damage from the creature's quills. Melee weapons with reach do not endanger their users in this way.

_Trample_: The creature gains a trample attack that deals an amount of damage equal to its claw damage plus 1-1/2 times its Strength modifier. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Yaoguai|yaoguai]] has it.
