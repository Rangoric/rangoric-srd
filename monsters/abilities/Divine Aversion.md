# Divine Aversion

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “divine aversion”, on the Special Qualities line.

Jyoti dislike deities and are never divine spellcasters. Jyoti gain a +2 racial bonus on defenses against divine magical effects. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jyoti|jyoti]] has it.
