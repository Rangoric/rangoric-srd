# Lashing Strike

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “lashing strike”, on the Special Attacks line.

Although a sagari is Tiny, it does not provoke attacks of opportunity when it makes melee attacks. In addition, its tentacle attack is a primary attack. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Sagari|sagari]] has it.
