# Confusing Critical

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Spell-Like]] (Sp). In a stat block_: “confusing critical”, on the Special Attacks line.

If an abrakarn viper confirms a critical hit against an opponent with its bite attack, it attacks the opponent at +13 vs Will, and on a hit the opponent is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1 round (as lesser confusion). The attack bonus is Charisma-based and includes a +9 racial bonus. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Abrakarn Viper|abrakarn viper]] has it.
