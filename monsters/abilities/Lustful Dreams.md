# Lustful Dreams

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “lustful dreams”, on the Special Attacks line.

Pairakas can torment sleeping creatures. While an intelligent creature sleeps, a pairaka can slip into the target's mind and twist its dreams to lusty nocturnal visions. The victim must be asleep for the pairaka to use this ability and the pairaka must be within 100 feet. The dreams attack the victim at +11 vs Will, and on a hit it experiences vivid hallucinations of a lurid nature that leave it breathless and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] upon waking. The victim, even a depraved soul, rarely considers the sexual nature of these dreams enjoyable, as the images exploit any number of taboos the pairaka suspects its victim might harbor. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. Creatures that do not sleep or dream are immune to this effect. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Pairaka|pairaka]] has it.
