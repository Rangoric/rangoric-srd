# Water Skating

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “water skating”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can move on the surface of water as if it were on land. When swimming at the water's surface, it can pull itself onto the water with a successful [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Water Strider|giant water strider]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Water Strider Nymph|water strider nymph]] have it.
