# Savage Stingers

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “savage stingers”, on the Special Attacks line.

If a thulgant hits a single target with all three stings in the same round, it tears through the victim's body, dealing an extra 3d6+12 points of damage and draining an additional 2 ability points from all six of the victim's ability scores. The drain attacks once at +19 vs Fortitude, and a miss negates all of it. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Thulgant|thulgant]] has it.
