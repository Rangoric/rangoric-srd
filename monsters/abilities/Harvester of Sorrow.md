# Harvester of Sorrow

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “harvester of sorrow”, on the Special Qualities line.

A popobala heals as a result of the suffering of others. It gains fast healing 10 if at least one creature within 15 feet has one or more of the following conditions: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^cowering|cowering]], dying, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]]. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Popobala|popobala]] has it.
