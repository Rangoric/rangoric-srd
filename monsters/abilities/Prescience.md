# Prescience

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “prescience”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can see all the possible outcomes of any of its own futures. This grants the creature an insight bonus to its AC and on initiative checks and to Reflex Defense equal to its Wisdom bonus (+6 for most shedus). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shedu|shedu]] has it.
