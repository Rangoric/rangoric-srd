# Shape Flesh

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “shape flesh”, on the Special Attacks line.

By spending 1 minute in contact with a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] creature, a totenmaske can reshape the target's face, causing flesh to cover vital features. It attacks the target at +9 vs Fortitude, and a miss resists it. Changes are permanent, but can be reversed with heal, restoration, or regeneration, or by surgically opening the sealed flesh with a DC 15 Medicine check that takes 1d3 rounds and deals 1d4 points of damage even if the check is not successful. A totenmaske can use this ability on one of four different features per use: ears (target becomes deaf), eyes (target becomes blind), mouth (target cannot speak or eat), or nose (target cannot smell). Multiple uses can have increasingly serious effects (such as sealing the mouth and nose, which causes suffocation). The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Totenmaske|totenmaske]] has it.
