# Pus Burst

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “pus burst”, on the Special Attacks line.

When damaged by a piercing or slashing melee weapon, a spray of vile pus strikes the attacker unless the attacker makes a DC 15 Reflex save. Weapons that provide reach protect the attacker completely from a pus burst. Creatures struck by pus become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d3 rounds and take 2d6 points of negative energy damage. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Crawling Hand|giant crawling hand]] has it.
