# Transparency

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “transparency”, on the Special Qualities line.

When underwater, the creature's body becomes transparent, which effectively renders it invisible. It can become visible or transparent at will as a free action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Fossegrim|fossegrim]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Nereid|nereid]] have it.
