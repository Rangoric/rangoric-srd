# Powerful Jaws

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “powerful jaws”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's muscular jaws threaten a critical hit on a natural roll of 19 or 20. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Thylacine|thylacine]] has it.
