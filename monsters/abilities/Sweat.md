# Sweat

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sweat”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's reddish sweat protects it from nonlethal damage from hot environments, and grants it a +2 racial bonus on defenses against nonmagical disease. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Hippopotamus|hippopotamus]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/behemoth/Behemoth Hippopotamus|behemoth hippopotamus]] have it.
