# Runic Tattoos

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “runic tattoos”, on the Special Qualities line.

The tattoos scribed on a maftet's body power its spell-like abilities and glow when those abilities are used. A maftet can activate up to two of its spell-like abilities as a standard action. An erase spell cast on its tattoos attacks its Fortitude, and on a hit it loses access to its spell-like abilities for 24 hours. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Maftet|maftet]] has it.
