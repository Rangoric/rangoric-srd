# Shake

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “shake”, on the Special Attacks line.

On a successful critical hit with its bite attack, a selkie automatically violently shakes a Large or smaller target. The target must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1 round. Even on a successful save, the target still takes a -2 penalty on all attack rolls and skill checks for the next 2 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Selkie|selkie]] has it.
