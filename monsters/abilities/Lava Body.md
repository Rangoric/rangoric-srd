# Lava Body

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “lava body”, on the Special Qualities line.

The magma ooze is formed of molten rock. Whenever a creature strikes a magma ooze with a weapon, the heat attacks the attacker at +9 vs Fortitude, and on a hit that weapon takes 4d6 points of fire damage. Damage caused to weapons in this manner is not halved, but hardness does help prevent some of the damage dealt. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Magma Ooze|magma ooze]] has it.
