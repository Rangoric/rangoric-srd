# Noxious Breath

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “noxious breath”, on the Special Attacks line.

Three times per day, as a standard action a magaav can exhale a breath that reeks of pure corruption upon a creature within 5 feet. The target is attacked at +6 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d4 rounds. Creatures the breath misses cannot be affected by the same magaav's noxious breath for 24 hours. This is a poison effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Host Devil|host devil]] has it.
