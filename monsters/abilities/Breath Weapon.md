# Breath Weapon

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “breath weapon (60-ft. cone, 8d6 fire damage, +10 vs Reflex; a miss halves, usable every 1d4 rounds)”, on the Special Attacks line.

A breath weapon attack usually deals damage and is often based on some type of energy.

Such breath weapons attack Reflex, a miss halving the damage (attack bonus 1/2 breathing creature’s racial HD + breathing creature’s Con modifier; the exact bonus is given in the creature’s descriptive text). A creature is immune to its own breath weapon unless otherwise noted. Some breath weapons attack Fortitude or Will instead of Reflex.

Each breath weapon also says how often it can be used, even if that is limited to a number of times per day. If the breath is more complicated than damage, the creature’s entry also describes it under its special abilities.

Also see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Breath Weapon|Breath Weapon]] under Special Abilities.
