# Unsettling Drone

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “unsettling drone”, on the Special Qualities line.

A jinmenju emits a low, persistent hum that unnerves living creatures that hear it. Those within 30 feet of it are attacked at +8 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] until they leave the affected area and for 1d4 rounds thereafter. A creature the drone misses is immune to that jinmenju's unsettling drone for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jinmenju|jinmenju]] has it.
