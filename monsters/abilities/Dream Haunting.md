# Dream Haunting

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “dream haunting”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a wendigo uses its nightmare spell-like ability, the victim is also exposed to wendigo psychosis. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Wendigo|wendigo]] has it.
