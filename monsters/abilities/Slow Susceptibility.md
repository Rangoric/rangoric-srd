# Slow Susceptibility

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “slow susceptibility”, on the Special Qualities line.

A quickling that succumbs to a slow effect loses its supernatural speed ability and is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] as long as the effect persists. This sickened condition persists for 1 round after the slow effect ends. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Quickling|quickling]] has it.
