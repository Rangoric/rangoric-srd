# Repair Clockwork

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “repair clockwork”, on the Special Qualities line.

Clockwork servants are adept at repairing other clockwork constructs. As a standard action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity, a clockwork servant can repair damage done to either itself or an adjacent clockwork creature, healing 1d10 points of damage to the target. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/clockwork/Clockwork Servant|clockwork servant]] has it.
