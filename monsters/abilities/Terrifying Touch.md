# Terrifying Touch

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “terrifying touch”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature struck by a frightful haunter's touch attack takes 3d8 points of damage and is attacked at +7 vs Will, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 minute. A creature the touch misses can't be made shaken by the terrifying touch for 24 hours, but can still take damage from it. Successfully using terrifying touch multiple times extends only the duration of its effect; it does not create a stronger fear condition. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/bugbears/Frightful Haunter|frightful haunter]] has it.
