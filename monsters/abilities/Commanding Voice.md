# Commanding Voice

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “commanding voice”, on the Special Attacks line.

A void oni gains a +4 racial bonus on the attack bonus of any charm or compulsion effects it uses against humanoids. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/onis/Void Yai|void yai]] has it.
