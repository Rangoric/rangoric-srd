# Vengeful Strike

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “vengeful strike”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per round, Koschei may make an attack of opportunity against any creature that strikes him in melee combat. This attack of opportunity has a critical threat range of 18-20. Against female targets, the vengeful strike is also a crushing blow. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demons/Koschei|Koschei]] has it.
