# Razor Sharp

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “razor sharp”, on the Special Attacks line.

A carnivorous crystal's slam attack deals devastating piercing and slashing damage, and threatens a critical hit on a roll of 18, 19, or 20. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/oozes/Carnivorous Crystal|carnivorous crystal]] has it.
