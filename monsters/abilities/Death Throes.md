# Death Throes

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “death throes”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a soulsliver is killed, its body explodes into jagged glass shards that deal 2d6 points of piercing and slashing damage to creatures within a 20-foot-radius burst (+2 vs Reflex; a miss halves). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Soulsliver|soulsliver]] has it.
