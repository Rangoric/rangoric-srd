# Channel Negative Energy

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “channel negative energy”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a hollow serpent can channel negative energy in a 30-foot burst as a 16th-level evil cleric. This ability requires no divine focus. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Hollow Serpent|hollow serpent]] has it.
