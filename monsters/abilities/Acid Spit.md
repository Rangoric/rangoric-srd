# Acid Spit

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “acid spit”, on the Special Attacks line.

An azruverda can spit a stream of acid at a target within 60 feet as a ranged touch attack that deals 10d6 points of acid damage. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Azruverda|azruverda]] has it.
