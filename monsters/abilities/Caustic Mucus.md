# Caustic Mucus

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “caustic mucus”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a river drake can spit a ball of caustic mucus that explodes in a 5-foot-radius spread. This attack has a range of 50 feet, deals 2d8 points of acid damage, and entangles creatures in the area. It attacks at +4 vs Reflex, and a miss halves the damage and negates the entangle effect. An [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] creature takes 1d4 points of acid damage each round on its turn, and the mucus attacks it again at the end of its turn every round, and a miss frees it from the entanglement and ends the acid damage. Once a river drake spits mucus, it cannot do so again for 1d6 rounds. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/River Drake|river drake]] has it.
