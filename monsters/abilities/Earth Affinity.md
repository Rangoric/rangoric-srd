# Earth Affinity

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “earth affinity”, on the Special Qualities line.

Oread sorcerers with the Elemental (earth) bloodline treat their Charisma score as 2 points higher for all sorcerer spells and class abilities. Oread clerics with the Earth domain cast their domain powers and spells at +1 caster level. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/planetouched/Oread, 1st-Level Fighter|oread, 1st-level fighter]] has it.
