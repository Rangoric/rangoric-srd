# One-Armed Hold

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “one-armed hold”, on the Special Attacks line.

A baregara's huge arms allow it to initiate and maintain a grapple without the standard –4 penalty for not having both hands free. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Baregara|baregara]] has it.
