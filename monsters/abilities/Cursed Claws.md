# Cursed Claws

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “cursed claws”, on the Special Attacks line.

A ghul's claws count as both cold iron and magic for the purpose of bypassing damage reduction. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ghul|ghul]] has it.
