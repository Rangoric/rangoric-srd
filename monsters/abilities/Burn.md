# Burn

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “burn (2d6, +5 vs Reflex)”, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines.

A creature with the burn special attack deals fire damage in addition to damage dealt on a successful hit in melee. A creature affected by the burn ability is also attacked against Reflex (attack bonus 1/2 the attacking creature's racial HD + the attacking creature's Con modifier), and on a hit catches fire, taking the listed damage at the start of its turn for an additional 1d4 rounds. A burning creature can spend a full-round action to make the fire attack again, and a miss puts it out. Dropping and rolling on the ground grants a +4 bonus to its Reflex Defense against this attack. Creatures that hit the monster with natural weapons or unarmed attacks take fire damage as though hit by the monster's burn attack and are attacked against Reflex, catching on fire on a hit (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|catching on fire]]).
