# Bristles

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “bristles”, on the Special Qualities line.

Long bristles of shadowstuff extend from between the tenebrous worm's armor plates. These bristles react swiftly to attacks, stabbing at any creature that attempts to harm the worm. Each time a creature attacks a tenebrous worm, the bristles attack it at +8 vs Reflex, and several puncture it on a hit. Each time a creature is punctured by these bristles, it takes 1d4 points of piercing damage and is exposed to the tenebrous worm's poison. A creature that grapples a tenebrous worm is automatically hurt by these bristles. The attack bonus to avoid the bristles is Dexterity-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tenebrous Worm|tenebrous worm]] has it.
