# Flying Charge

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “flying charge”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature gains a +4 bonus on damage rolls if it charges while flying. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragon Horse|dragon horse]] has it.
