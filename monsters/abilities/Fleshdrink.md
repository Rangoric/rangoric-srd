# Fleshdrink

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “fleshdrink”, on the Special Attacks line.

If a totenmaske hits a single creature with both claw attacks, the hollow claws drain away some of the target's flesh, dealing 1d6 points of Constitution damage and making the victim [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d4 rounds. A successful DC 19 Fortitude save negates the Constitution damage and reduces the sickened condition duration to 1 round. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Totenmaske|totenmaske]] has it.
