# Godslayer

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “godslayer”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a thanatotic titan damages a creature capable of casting divine spells, it attacks that creature at +18 vs Will, and on a hit the creature is unable to cast any divine spells for 1d4 rounds and be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]]. On a miss, the creature struck is merely shaken for 1 round. A thanatotic titan's attacks are treated as epic and evil for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/titans/Thanatotic Titan|thanatotic titan]] has it.
