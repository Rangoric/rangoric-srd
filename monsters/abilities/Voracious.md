# Voracious

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “voracious”, on the Special Attacks line.

A voracious creature’s bite or swarm attack deals double damage to [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^plant|plant]] creatures and to objects made of paper, wood, or other plant materials.
