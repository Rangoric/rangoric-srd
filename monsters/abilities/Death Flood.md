# Death Flood

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “death flood”, on the Special Attacks line.

When the creature is killed, it immediately explodes in a 15-foot-radius burst of highly pressurized water that deals 5d6 points of bludgeoning damage (+7 vs Reflex; a miss halves). After the explosion, a successful DC 25 Fieldcraft check allows a creature to recover a cluster of strange organs from the remains. This cluster functions as a decanter of endless water for 2d6 hours, but can only produce a “stream” or “fountain” effect. Failing this Fieldcraft check by 5 or more causes the cluster to burst, dealing an additional 2d6 points of damage to that creature (no attack against a defense) and destroying the organs entirely. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Grodair|grodair]] has it.
