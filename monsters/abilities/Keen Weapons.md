# Keen Weapons

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “keen weapons”, on the Special Attacks line.

After it has engaged in at least 1 round of combat, a terra-cotta soldier's weapons automatically gain the benefits of keen weapon (CL 6th). This effect persists until the end of the battle. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Terra-Cotta Soldier|terra-cotta soldier]] has it.
