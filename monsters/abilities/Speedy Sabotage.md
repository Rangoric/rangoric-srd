# Speedy Sabotage

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “speedy sabotage”, on the Special Attacks line.

Vexgits are adept at disassembling machinery, reducing even complex devices to trash with shocking speed. When using the Engineering skill, these gremlins treat all devices as being one category simpler for the purposes of determining how long it takes to use the skill. Thus, difficult devices count as tricky, tricky devices count as simple, and simple devices can be dismantled as a free action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gremlin/Vexgit|vexgit]] has it.
