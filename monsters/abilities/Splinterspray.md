# Splinterspray

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “splinterspray”, on the Special Attacks line.

A twigjack can eject a barrage of splinters and brambles from its body three times per day as a standard action. This effect creates a 15-foot conical burst of jagged splinters, dealing 4d6 points of piercing damage to all creatures in the area. It attacks at +4 vs Reflex, and a miss halves this damage. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Twigjack|twigjack]] has it.
