# Sandwalking

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “sandwalking”, on the Special Qualities line.

A desert giant travels at full speed across sand, rocky ground, or dust, and leaves no trail behind unless it chooses to. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Desert Giant|desert giant]] has it.
