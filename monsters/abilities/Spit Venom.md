# Spit Venom

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “spit venom”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, the creature can spit venom up to 30 feet. This is a ranged touch attack with no range increment. Any opponent hit by this attack is exposed to the creature's poison. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Seru|seru]] has it.
