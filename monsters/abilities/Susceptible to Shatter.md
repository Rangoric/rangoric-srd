# Susceptible to Shatter

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “susceptible to shatter”, on the Special Qualities line.

A shatter spell deals 3d6 points of damage to a cold rider (no save) and reduces its armor bonus by 2 for 1 minute. Shatter spells automatically overcome a cold rider's spell resistance. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Cold Rider|cold rider]] has it.
