# Adhesion

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “adhesion”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature striking a tarry demodand with a manufactured weapon must make a DC 23 Reflex save; failure means the weapon sticks to the demodand and cannot be used to make attacks until freed. Freeing a stuck weapon requires a successful grapple check. This adhesion grants a +4 racial bonus to CMD against disarm attempts. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demodands/Tarry Demodand|tarry demodand]] has it.
