# Abyssal Resurrection

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “Abyssal resurrection”, on the Special Qualities line.

A demon lord rules an Abyssal realm, a vast world that not only serves as its home but provides it with power. If a demon lord is slain, its body rapidly melts into corruption (leaving behind any gear it held or carried), its soul returns to a hidden location within its realm, and it is immediately restored to life (as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]_) at that location. Once this occurs, a demon lord can’t use this ability again until a full year has passed.

A demon lord realizes it is vulnerable during this time and usually doesn’t risk further battles for the remainder of that year, relying on the defenses of its realm and its legions of minions to protect it.

A demon lord that is slain again during this year or is killed by unusual methods (such as by a true deity or an artifact created for this purpose) is slain forever—its remains appearing somewhere deep in the Abyss among other dead demon lords from the ages. A demon lord who does not control a domain does not gain this ability.

[[rangoric-srd/monsters/demons/Dagon|Dagon]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demons/Koschei|Koschei]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demons/Pazuzu|Pazuzu]] have it.
