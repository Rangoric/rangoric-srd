# Dream Claws

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “dream claws”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's claws are treated as cold iron and magic for purposes of overcoming damage reduction. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Baku|baku]] has it.
