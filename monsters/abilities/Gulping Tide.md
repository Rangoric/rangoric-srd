# Gulping Tide

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “gulping tide”, on the Special Attacks line.

An astral leviathan can create a 60-foot cone of roiling astral material, pulling all Large or smaller creatures and objects into its mouth so it can swallow them. The tide attacks the Fortitude of each creature in the area at +20; a creature it misses moves up to 60 feet toward the cone's origin, and creatures it hits are swallowed. The leviathan can use this ability only once per minute. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Astral Leviathan|astral leviathan]] has it.
