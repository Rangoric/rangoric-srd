# Constrict

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “constrict (1d8+6)”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature with this special attack can crush an opponent, dealing bludgeoning damage, when it makes a successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] check (in addition to any other effects caused by a successful check, including additional damage). The amount of damage is given in the creature’s entry and is typically equal to the amount of damage caused by the creature’s melee attack.
