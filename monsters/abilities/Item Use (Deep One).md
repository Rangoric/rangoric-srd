# Item Use (Deep One)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “item use”, on the Special Qualities line.

A deep one can activate spell-trigger items like staves and wands as if it were a spellcaster of the appropriate class. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Deep One|deep one]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Elder Deep One|elder deep one]] have it.
