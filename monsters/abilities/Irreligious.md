# Irreligious

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “irreligious”, on the Special Qualities line.

Bitter and blasphemous, redcaps cannot stand the symbols of good-aligned religions. If a foe spends a standard action presenting such a holy symbol, the symbol attacks any redcap that can see the creature at +5 vs Will, and on a hit the redcap becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 minute and attempt to flee. A redcap the symbol misses is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 minute. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Redcap|redcap]] has it.
