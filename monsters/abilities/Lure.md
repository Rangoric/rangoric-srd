# Lure

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “lure”, on the Special Attacks line.

At any point that the creature's targets are unaware of it (for example, if the creature is hiding or concealed in darkness), the creature can call out to the targets, who must be in line of sight and within 60 feet. When the creature calls out, the targets must make a DC 16 Will save or fall under the effects of a suggestion to approach the sound of the creature's voice. This effect functions identically to a mass suggestion spell with a caster level equal to the creature's Hit Dice. A creature that saves cannot be affected again by that creature's lure for 24 hours. The lure is a language-dependent effect, and if the creature uses the victim's name during the lure, the victim takes a –4 penalty on its saving throw. This is a sonic mind-affecting charm effect. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Leucrotta|leucrotta]] has it.
