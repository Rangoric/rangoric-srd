# Vile Croak

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “vile croak”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action once every 1d4 rounds, the creature can unleash a thunderous croak. Any non-boggard or non-mobogo within 50 feet of the creature is attacked at +9 vs Will, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d4 rounds. Once a particular creature's vile croak misses a creature, it is immune to that creature's croak for 24 hours. Any boggards or mobogos within the area of the creature's vile croak gain a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls and defenses against fear effects for 1 round. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Mobogo|mobogo]] has it.
