# Adaptive Defense

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “adaptive defense”, on the Special Qualities line.

When first encountered, the creature has no energy resistance. When damaged by an attack that causes cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage, it gains resistance 30 to that energy type until the end of its next turn. If an attack causes more than one type of energy damage, the creature gains resistance 30 to all the types of energy damage dealt. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jubjub Bird|jubjub bird]] has it.
