# Phasing (Hypnalis)

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “phasing”, on the Special Qualities line.

A hypnalis can shift from the Ethereal Plane to the Material Plane as a free action and shift back again as a move action (or part of a move action). The ability is otherwise identical to ethereal jaunt (CL 15th). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Hypnalis|hypnalis]] has it.
