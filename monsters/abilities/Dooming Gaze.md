# Dooming Gaze

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “dooming gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

Paralyzed with fear for 1 round, 60 feet, Will DC 27 negates. A creature that is already [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] by this gaze attack and fails a saving throw to resist being paralyzed for 1 more round gains 1 negative level (Fortitude DC 27 to remove after 24 hours). The paralysis effect of this gaze is a mind-affecting fear effect, but the negative level is not. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Sea Bonze|sea bonze]] has it.
