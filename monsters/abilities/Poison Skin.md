# Poison Skin

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “poison skin”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature that strikes a creature with poison skin with an unarmed strike or natural weapon exposes itself to its poisonous skin; the poison is given in the creature's entry. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Toad|giant toad]] has it.
