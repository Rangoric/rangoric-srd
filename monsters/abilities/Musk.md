# Musk

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “musk”, on the Special Attacks line.

Up to twice per day, the creature can spray a stream of noxious musk at a single target within the range its entry gives as a standard action. With a successful ranged touch attack, the creature struck by this spray must make a Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for the listed rounds and then [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for the listed minutes by the horrific stench. A successful save reduces the effect to only 1d4 rounds of being sickened. A creature cannot use the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]] ability as long as it is affected by this musk. The save DC is Constitution-based, and includes a +2 racial bonus; the creature's entry gives it. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Skunk|skunk]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Skunk|giant skunk]] have it.
