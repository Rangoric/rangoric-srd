# Overwhelming Strength

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “overwhelming strength”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature always applies 1-1/2 times its Strength modifier on all natural weapon attacks. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Bhole|bhole]] has it.
