# Thunderous Stomp

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “thunderous stomp”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a full-round action, the creature can rear up on its serpentine body to bring its massive forelimbs down with awesome force. The creature chooses two adjacent targets to be affected as though trampling them. In addition, each creature within 10 feet must make a DC 28 Reflex save or be knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Grootslang|grootslang]] has it.
