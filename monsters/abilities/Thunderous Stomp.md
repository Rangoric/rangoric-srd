# Thunderous Stomp

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “thunderous stomp”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a full-round action, the creature can rear up on its serpentine body to bring its massive forelimbs down with awesome force. The creature chooses two adjacent targets to be affected as though trampling them. In addition, each creature within 10 feet is attacked at +18 vs Reflex, and on a hit is knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Grootslang|grootslang]] has it.
