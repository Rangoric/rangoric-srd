# Gnaw

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “gnaw”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature that is grappling a foe and chooses to damage the foe with an additional grapple check deals twice its normal bite damage, in addition to injecting an additional dose of poison with each successful check. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Rot Grub|giant rot grub]] has it.
