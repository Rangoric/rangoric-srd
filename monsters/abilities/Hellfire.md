# Hellfire

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “hellfire”, on the Special Attacks line.

Any fire damage caused by an immolation devil's abilities and spells is half fire damage, half unholy damage. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devils/Immolation Devil|immolation devil]] has it.
