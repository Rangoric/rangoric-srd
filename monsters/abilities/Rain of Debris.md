# Rain of Debris

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “rain of debris”, on the Special Attacks line.

Three times per day as a standard action, a sepid can call forth a hail of stones, wood, metal, and similar debris. The debris rains down and pelts all creatures in a 10-foot-high, 40-foot-radius cylinder centered on the sepid, dealing 15d6 points of bludgeoning damage (+15 vs Reflex; a miss halves). This attack does not harm the sepid, and counts as an evil attack for the purpose of damage reduction. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Sepid|sepid]] has it.
