# Unnerving Gaze (Interlocutor Kyton)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “unnerving gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

A creature that succumbs to an interlocutor's unnerving gaze becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round as it becomes convinced that it recognizes some of its own body parts entangled in the interlocutor's body. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Interlocutor Kyton|interlocutor kyton]] has it.
