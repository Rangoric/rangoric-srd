# Disorienting Gaze

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “disorienting gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

An ijiraq can level an icy stare at a creature within 30 feet. That creature takes a -20 penalty on Perception checks and Fieldcraft checks for 24 hours (Fortitude DC 23 negates). The save DC is Charisma-based. An ijiraq can use this ability while in the shape of an elk. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ijiraq|ijiraq]] has it.
