# Cacophonous Roar

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “cacophonous roar”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once every 1d4 rounds as a standard action, a jotund troll can emit a cacophonous roar from its nine heads. All creatures within a 60-foot spread of the troll must make a DC 20 Will save or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d4 rounds. This is a mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Charisma-based and includes a +4 racial bonus. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Jotund Troll|jotund troll]] has it.
