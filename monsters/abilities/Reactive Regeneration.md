# Reactive Regeneration

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “reactive regeneration”, on the Special Qualities line.

Whenever a hungry flesh takes piercing or slashing damage, it regenerates 5 hit points and gains 1 growth point. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Hungry Flesh|hungry flesh]] has it.
