# Master Grappler

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “master grappler”, on the Special Qualities line.

A magaav can wield a weapon and still attempt grapple checks. While not wielding a weapon, a magaav gains a +4 bonus on grapple checks. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Host Devil|host devil]] has it.
