# Woodcraft

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “woodcraft”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature has a racial bonus on Craft checks involving wood (+8 for the hamadryad, +6 for the dryad), and is always treated as having masterwork artisan's woodworking tools when it makes such checks. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymph/Hamadryad|hamadryad]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymph/Dryad|dryad]] have it.
