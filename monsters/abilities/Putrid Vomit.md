# Putrid Vomit

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “putrid vomit”, on the Special Attacks line.

Every 1d4 rounds, a grimple can spew a 30-foot line of vomit as a standard action. Treat this as a ranged touch attack with no range increment. The vomit attacks anyone struck at +1 vs Fortitude, and on a hit they are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d4 rounds. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gremlins/Grimple|grimple]] has it.
