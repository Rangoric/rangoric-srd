# Elusive (Tripurasura)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “elusive”, on the Special Qualities line.

The tripurasura and items it carries are considered to be under the effects of a nondetection spell. A caster must succeed at a DC 14 caster level check to overcome this effect. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/asuras/Tripurasura|tripurasura]] has it.
