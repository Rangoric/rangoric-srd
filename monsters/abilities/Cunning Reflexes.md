# Cunning Reflexes

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “cunning reflexes”, on the Special Attacks line.

A lhaksharut uses its Wisdom modifier, rather than its Dexterity modifier, to determine how many additional attacks of opportunity it gains with the Combat Reflexes feat. For most lhaksharut inevitables, this benefit equates to 5 additional attacks of opportunity per round. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/inevitable/Lhaksharut|lhaksharut]] has it.
