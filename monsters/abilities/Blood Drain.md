# Blood Drain

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “blood drain (1d2 Constitution)”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature drains blood at the end of its turn if it grapples a foe, dealing [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|Constitution damage]].
