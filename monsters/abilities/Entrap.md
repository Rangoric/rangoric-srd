# Entrap

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “entrap (+3 vs Fortitude, 1d10 minutes, hardness 5, hp 10)”, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines.

The creature has an ability that restricts another creature's movement, usually with a physical attack such as ice, mud, lava, or webs. An entrap attack attacks its target's Fortitude, and on a hit the target becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] for the listed duration. If a target is already entangled by this ability, a second entrap attack attacks the target's Fortitude, and on a hit it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] for the listed duration. The attack bonuses are Constitution-based. A target made helpless by this ability is conscious but can take no physical actions (except attempting to break free) until the entrapping material is removed. The target can use spells with only verbal components or spell-like abilities if it can make a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]]. An entangled creature can make a Strength check (DC 10 + the entrap's attack bonus) as a full-round action to break free; the DC for a helpless creature is 5 higher. Destroying the entrapping material frees the creature.
