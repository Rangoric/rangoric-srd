# Entrap

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “entrap (DC 13, 1d10 minutes, hardness 5, hp 10)”, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines.

The creature has an ability that restricts another creature's movement, usually with a physical attack such as ice, mud, lava, or webs. The target of an entrap attack must make a Fortitude save or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] for the listed duration. If a target is already entangled by this ability, a second entrap attack means the target must make a Fortitude save or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] for the listed duration. The save DCs are Constitution-based. A target made helpless by this ability is conscious but can take no physical actions (except attempting to break free) until the entrapping material is removed. The target can use spells with only verbal components or spell-like abilities if it can make a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]]. An entangled creature can make a Strength check (at the same DC as the entrap saving throw DC) as a full-round action to break free; the DC for a helpless creature is +5 greater than the saving throw DC. Destroying the entrapping material frees the creature.
