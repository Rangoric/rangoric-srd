# Devour

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “devour”, on the Special Attacks line.

While a creature is under the effects of a xtabay's soporific pollen, the plant may, as a full-round action, occupy the same square as the sleeping creature and slowly sap the life from it. Every round the xtabay uses this ability, it attacks the affected creature at +4 vs Fortitude, and on a hit the creature takes 1d2 Con damage. This feeding is curiously painless, and normally isn't enough to waken a foe put to sleep by the plant. Each round this feeding continues, the sleep attacks the victim again at +4 vs Will, and the victim awakens on a miss. This attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Xtabay|xtabay]] has it.
