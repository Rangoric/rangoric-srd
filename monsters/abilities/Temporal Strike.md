# Temporal Strike

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “temporal strike”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a bythos can touch a creature or object to displace it from time. It attacks the target at +14 vs Fortitude, and on a hit the target disappears from the present moment and reappears in the same location 1d4 rounds later as if no time had passed. If an object occupies that space, the creature appears in the closest available space to its original location—this displacement does not cause the creature any additional harm. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/aeons/Bythos|bythos]] has it.
