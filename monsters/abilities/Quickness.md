# Quickness

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “quickness”, on the Special Attacks line.

A mithral golem is incredibly quick. It can take an extra move action during its turn each round. This means it can move up to its speed and still make a full attack. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Mithral Golem|mithral golem]] has it.
