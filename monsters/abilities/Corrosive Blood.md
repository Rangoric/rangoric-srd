# Corrosive Blood

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “corrosive blood”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's blood can corrode metal on contact. If a creature damages it with a piercing or slashing weapon made of metal, the blood deals acid damage to the metal weapon (the amount is in the creature's entry; unlike most forms of energy damage, this damage is not halved when applied to a metal object, although it does still have to penetrate the metal's hardness). The blood attacks the weapon's wielder's Reflex, and a miss halves the damage the weapon takes. Creatures made of metal that deal slashing or piercing damage to it with a natural attack take the same acid damage (vs Reflex; a miss halves). The corrosive elements of the blood fade 1 round after it leaves the creature's body or the creature dies. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Death Worm|death worm]] has it.
