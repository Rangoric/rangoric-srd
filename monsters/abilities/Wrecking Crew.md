# Wrecking Crew

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “wrecking crew”, on the Special Attacks line.

A group of up to six vexgits can work together to dismantle a device. This ability functions like the aid another action, but a single vexgit can receive help from up to five other vexgits, granting it up to a +10 bonus on its Engineering check. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gremlin/Vexgit|vexgit]] has it.
