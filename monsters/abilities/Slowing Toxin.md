# Slowing Toxin

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “slowing toxin”, on the Special Attacks line.

A shriezyx's web is coated with a supernatural toxin that numbs and deadens the nerves on contact. Any creature struck by a shriezyx's web is attacked at +4 vs Fortitude, and on a hit becomes slowed (as the slow spell) for 1 minute. Each round, the toxin attacks a victim again, and a miss ends the effect early. This toxin fades quickly from spun webs—it can only affect targets on the round the web is spun. Existing webs remain sticky and tangled, but do not have this slowing effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shriezyx|shriezyx]] has it.
