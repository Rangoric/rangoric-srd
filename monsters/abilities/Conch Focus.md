# Conch Focus

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “conch focus”, on the Special Attacks line.

When holding its conch, an ocean giant can cast great shout (DC 22) instead of shout, and gains Augment Summoning as a bonus feat. If the conch is destroyed (hardness 5, 20 hp), the giant can spend 1 week to craft a replacement. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Ocean Giant|ocean giant]] has it.
