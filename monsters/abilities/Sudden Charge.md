# Sudden Charge

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sudden charge”, on the Special Attacks line.

When making a charge attack, the creature makes a single bite attack. If successful, it may also attempt to trip its opponent as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity. If the attempt fails, the creature cannot be tripped in return. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Axe Beak|axe beak]] has it.
