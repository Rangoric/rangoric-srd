# Paired Weapons

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “paired weapons”, on the Special Attacks line.

Because of its intense training wielding a scimitar in each hand, a maftet's off-hand scimitar is treated as a light weapon. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Maftet|maftet]] has it.
