# Hallucinatory Spores

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “hallucinatory spores”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day as a standard action, a hangman tree can release a cloud of spores in a 50-foot-radius spread. The spores attack creatures in the area at +10 vs Will, and those they hit believe the hangman tree to be a perfectly ordinary tree—or at worst, a treant or some other friendly tree-like creature. An affected creature becomes passive for 2d6 minutes and refuses to attack the hangman tree during this time. The spores attack an affected creature again each round that the tree attacks an ally, and a miss ends the effect—if a hallucinating creature is attacked by the tree, it gains a +4 bonus to its Will Defense against the hallucination. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Hangman Tree|hangman tree]] has it.
