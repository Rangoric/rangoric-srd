# Starlight Blast

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “starlight blast”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action once every 1d4 rounds, a lyrakien can tap into the divine power of Elysium, unleashing a blast of holy starlight in a 5-foot burst. All creatures in this area take 1d4 points of holy damage, plus 1 point for each step their alignment deviates from chaotic good. For example, a chaotic neutral or neutral good creature would take 1d4+1 points of damage, a neutral creature would take 1d4+2 points of damage, and a lawful evil creature would take 1d4+4 points of damage. It attacks at +2 vs Reflex, and a miss negates this damage. Chaotic good creatures are unaffected by this ability. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/azata/Lyrakien|lyrakien]] has it.
