# Multi-Armed

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “multi-armed”, on the Special Qualities line.

A kasatha has four arms. One hand is considered its primary hand; all others are considered off hands. It can use any of its hands for other purposes that require free hands. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Kasatha|kasatha]] has it.
