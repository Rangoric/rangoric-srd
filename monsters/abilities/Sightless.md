# Sightless

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sightless”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature is blind and is not affected by any effect that relies on sight, such as gaze attacks or blindness. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Sea Anemone|giant sea anemone]] has it.
