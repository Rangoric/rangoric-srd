# Drowning Kiss

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “drowning kiss”, on the Special Attacks line.

A nereid can flood the lungs of a willing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] creature by touching it (traditionally by kissing the creature on the lips). If the target cannot breathe water, it cannot hold its breath and immediately begins to drown. On its turn, the target can attempt a DC 23 Fortitude save to cough up this water; otherwise it falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] at 0 hp. On the next round, the target must save again or drop to –1 hit points and be dying; on the third round it must save again or die. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymph/Nereid|nereid]] has it.
