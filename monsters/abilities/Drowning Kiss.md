# Drowning Kiss

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “drowning kiss”, on the Special Attacks line.

A nereid can flood the lungs of a willing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] creature by touching it (traditionally by kissing the creature on the lips). If the target cannot breathe water, it cannot hold its breath and immediately begins to drown. On its turn, the water attacks the target at +13 vs Fortitude; on a miss it coughs up this water, and on a hit it falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] at 0 hp. On the next round, it attacks again, and a hit drops the target to –1 hit points and dying; on the third round it attacks again, and a hit kills it. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymph/Nereid|nereid]] has it.
