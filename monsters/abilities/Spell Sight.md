# Spell Sight

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “spell sight”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's eyes radiate normal light in a 15-foot cone and increase the light level by one step for an additional 15 feet beyond that area. Any magic effect in this area glows faintly. This glow does not reveal the magic effect's school or strength. The creature can quell or renew this light as a swift action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Zoog|zoog]] has it.
