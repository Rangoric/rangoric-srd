# Unsettling Appearance

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “unsettling appearance”, on the Special Qualities line.

A cerebric fungus constantly scans the minds of those around it, projecting around itself a confusing collage of images gleaned from their thoughts. Creatures within 60 feet that can see the fungus are attacked at +4 vs Will, and on a hit take a –2 penalty on attack rolls. This is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fungus/Cerebric Fungus|cerebric fungus]] has it.
