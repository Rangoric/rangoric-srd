# Tree Meld

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “tree meld”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can meld with any tree, as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]_ spell works, and can remain melded with a tree for as long as it wishes. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Hamadryad|hamadryad]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Kapre|kapre]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Dryad|dryad]] have it.
