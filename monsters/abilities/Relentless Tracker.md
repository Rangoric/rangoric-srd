# Relentless Tracker

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “relentless tracker”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can move at up to double its speed and still track without penalty. It gains a +10 competence bonus on Fieldcraft checks made to track creatures it has wounded. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bandersnatch|bandersnatch]] has it.
