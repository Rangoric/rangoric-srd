# Transfer Memories

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “transfer memories”, on the Special Qualities line.

Once per day as a standard action, a hyakume can touch a willing creature with either its hand or one of its eye probes to transfer memories it has stored to the creature touched. This is a mind-affecting effect. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Hyakume|hyakume]] has it.
