# Spawn Soldiers

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “spawn soldiers”, on the Special Attacks line.

Three times per day as a standard action, a thriae queen can spawn a large swarm of wasps. This functions as four separate [[rangoric-srd/monsters/swarm/Wasp Swarm|wasp swarms]] that occupy all of the squares adjacent to the thriae queen. These swarms do not harm any thriae, and while they move with the queen as she moves, the swarms cannot leave her side. The swarms last until they are destroyed or 1 hour passes, at which point the swarms die on their own. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/thriae/Thriae Queen|thriae queen]] has it.
