# Devouring grapple

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “devouring grapple”, on the Special Attacks line.

The mouth at the center of a baregara's chest automatically deals 2d8+4 points of damage per round to any creature the baregara successfully grapples. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Baregara|baregara]] has it.
