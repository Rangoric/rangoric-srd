# Fire Affinity

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “fire affinity”, on the Special Qualities line.

Ifrit sorcerers with the elemental (fire) bloodline treat their Charisma score as 2 points higher for all sorcerer spells and class abilities. Ifrit spellcasters with the Fire domain use their domain powers and spells at +1 caster level. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/planetouched/Ifrit, 1st-Level Sorcerer|ifrit, 1st-level sorcerer]] has it.
