# Shadow Mark

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “shadow mark”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a free action, the creature can make a ranged touch attack by flying over a humanoid target—the maximum range of this attack is 300 feet. If the creature hits, its shadow transforms to match the shadow of the creature struck. Once the creature has established this link, it gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls made against that target, and every time the creature hits that target with an attack, the link attacks the creature at +3 vs Will, and on a hit it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Peryton|peryton]] has it.
