# Heroic Strength

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “heroic strength”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day, a silvanshee can grant itself a +8 enhancement bonus to Strength for 1 minute. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/agathion/Silvanshee|silvanshee]] has it.
