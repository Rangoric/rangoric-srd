# Quills

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “quills”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature attacking a creature with quills using the weapons or attacks its entry names takes the piercing damage listed there, and a creature that grapples it takes piercing damage each round it does so. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Porcupine|porcupine]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Porcupine|giant porcupine]] have it.
