# Hatred

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “hatred”, on the Special Attacks line.

Garudas receive a +1 racial bonus on attack and damage rolls against nagas and other serpentine monsters of the aberration type. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Garuda|garuda]] has it.
