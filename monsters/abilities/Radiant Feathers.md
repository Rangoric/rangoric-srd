# Radiant Feathers

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “radiant feathers”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day as a standard action, the creature can fan out its glimmering tail feathers and blast its foes with a 100-foot cone of radiant light from its tail. Aside from its size, this attack is identical to a prismatic spray (DC 28). The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Simurgh|simurgh]] has it.
