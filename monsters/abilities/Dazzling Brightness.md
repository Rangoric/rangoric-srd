# Dazzling Brightness

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “dazzling brightness”, on the Special Attacks line.

A glass golem in an area of bright light dazzles any creature within 30 feet that sees it for 1 round (Fortitude DC 16 negates). Once a creature makes its save against this ability, it is immune to that golem's brightness for 24 hours. The DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Glass Golem|glass golem]] has it.
