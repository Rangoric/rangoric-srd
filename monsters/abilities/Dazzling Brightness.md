# Dazzling Brightness

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “dazzling brightness”, on the Special Attacks line.

A glass golem in an area of bright light dazzles any creature within 30 feet that sees it for 1 round (+6 vs Fortitude). Once this ability misses a creature, it is immune to that golem's brightness for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Glass Golem|glass golem]] has it.
