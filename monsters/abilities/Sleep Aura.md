# Sleep Aura

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “sleep aura”, on the Special Qualities line.

A sandman radiates a 20-foot-radius spread that puts creatures to sleep. The aura attacks any creature in the area at +4 vs Will, and on a hit it falls asleep, as if affected by a sleep spell (caster level 8th). There is no limit to the number of Hit Dice a sandman can affect with this ability. A creature the aura misses is immune to that sandman's sleep aura for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elementals/Sandman|sandman]] has it.
