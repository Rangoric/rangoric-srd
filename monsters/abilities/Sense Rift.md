# Sense Rift

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “sense rift”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a standard action, a shulsaga can detect any planar rift within 20 miles, automatically learning its direction and distance. This includes natural portals, non-instantaneous planar spells such as gate, and other tears in reality. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shulsaga|shulsaga]] has it.
