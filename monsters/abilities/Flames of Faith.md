# Flames of Faith

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “flames of faith”, on the Special Attacks line.

A legion archon can manifest a +1 flaming burst greatsword or +1 flaming javelin as a move-equivalent action. The legion archon's sword vanishes if it leaves its hand, and its javelin vanishes after striking or missing its target. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archon/Legion Archon|legion archon]] has it.
