# Hypnosis

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “hypnosis”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day, by weaving and coiling its body and hissing as a standard action, a lunar naga can cause its scales to shimmer and glow with moonlike radiance. All creatures within 30 feet who can see the naga are attacked at +7 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]]. The lunar naga can maintain this fascination effect as long as it concentrates; fascinated creatures follow the still-glowing naga if it moves as long as it maintains the effect by concentrating. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/naga/Lunar Naga|lunar naga]] has it.
