# Sunlight Petrification

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sunlight petrification”, on the Special Qualities line.

A rock troll that is exposed to natural sunlight is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] and must make a DC 20 Fortitude save each round to resist permanently turning to stone. A stone to flesh spell (or similar effect) restores a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] rock troll, but if it remains exposed to sunlight, it must immediately start making new Fortitude saves to avoid petrification. Spells like sunray or sunburst that create powerful natural sunlight cannot petrify a rock troll, but the troll is staggered for 1d4 rounds after being exposed to such an effect. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Rock Troll|rock troll]] has it.
