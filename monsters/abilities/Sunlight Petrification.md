# Sunlight Petrification

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sunlight petrification”, on the Special Qualities line.

A rock troll that is exposed to natural sunlight is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] and the sunlight attacks it at +10 vs Fortitude each round, and on a hit it permanently turns to stone. A stone to flesh spell (or similar effect) restores a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] rock troll, but if it remains exposed to sunlight, the sunlight immediately starts attacking its Fortitude again. Spells like sunray or sunburst that create powerful natural sunlight cannot petrify a rock troll, but the troll is staggered for 1d4 rounds after being exposed to such an effect. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/trolls/Rock Troll|rock troll]] has it.
