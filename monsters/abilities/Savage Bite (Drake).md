# Savage Bite (Drake)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “savage bite”, on the Special Attacks line.

A drake with this ability applies 1-1/2 times its Strength modifier to damage dealt with its bite attack, and it threatens a critical hit on a 19–20. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drake/Desert Drake|desert drake]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drake/Rift Drake|rift drake]] have it.
