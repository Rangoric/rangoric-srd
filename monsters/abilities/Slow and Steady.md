# Slow and Steady

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “slow and steady”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's speed is never modified by armor or encumbrance. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Tortoise|giant tortoise]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Immense Tortoise|immense tortoise]] have it.
