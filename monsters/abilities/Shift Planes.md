# Shift Planes

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “shift planes”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can enter the Ethereal Plane, Astral Plane, Plane of Air, or Material Plane once per day as a standard action. This functions as plane shift, but the creature can only bring up to two other willing creatures with it, and only if they are on its back. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragon Horse|dragon horse]] has it.
