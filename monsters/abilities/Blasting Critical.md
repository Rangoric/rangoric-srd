# Blasting Critical

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “blasting critical”, on the Special Qualities line.

When a cannon golem confirms a critical hit with a slam attack, it can make one cannon attack against that target as a free action (as long as the cannon is loaded). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Cannon Golem|cannon golem]] has it.
