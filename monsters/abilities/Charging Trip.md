# Charging Trip

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “charging trip”, on the Special Attacks line.

A festrog that hits with its bite after making a charge attack on all fours can attempt to trip its opponent (+4 bonus). This trip does not provoke attacks of opportunity. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Festrog|festrog]] has it.
