# Polymorph Susceptibility

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “polymorph susceptibility”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature is immune to polymorph effects or effects that otherwise change its shape, but the attempt to alter its shape causes it great pain and if the effect attacks a defense, it attacks the creature's. If it hits, or the effect doesn't attack a defense, the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] and loses its damage reduction and fast healing for 1d4 rounds. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Yaoguai|yaoguai]] has it.
