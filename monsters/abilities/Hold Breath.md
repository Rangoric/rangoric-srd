# Hold Breath

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “hold breath”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can hold its breath for a number of minutes equal to 6 times its Constitution score before it risks [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Drowning|drowning]] (in place of the usual time under [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Suffocation|suffocation]]).
