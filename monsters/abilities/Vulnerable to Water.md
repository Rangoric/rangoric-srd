# Vulnerable to Water

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “vulnerable to water”, on the Special Qualities line.

If a significant amount of water (the contents of a large bucket, the liquid of a create water spell, or a blow from a water elemental) strikes the creature, it must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] (for 2d4 rounds for the magma ooze, 1d6 rounds for the shard slag). A creature that is immersed in water must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save each round (the DC rises by 1 each round after the first) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]], reverting once the water is gone (the shard slag is automatically staggered while it is immersed). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Magma Ooze|magma ooze]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Shard Slag|shard slag]] have it.
