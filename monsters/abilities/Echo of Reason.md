# Echo of Reason

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “echo of reason”, on the Special Qualities line.

A selkie can instinctively alter the intonation of its voice to make anything it says sound more pleasing to those who understand it. When using the Deception skill, a selkie treats its lies as one step more believable for the purposes of bonuses or penalties on the check. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Selkie|selkie]] has it.
