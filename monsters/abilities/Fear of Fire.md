# Fear of Fire

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “fear of fire”, on the Special Qualities line.

A shriezyx within 30 feet of a fire the size of a torch or larger becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] as long as it remains within that range. If damaged by fire, a shriezyx must make a successful Will save (DC equals the amount of fire damage dealt) or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 round. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shriezyx|shriezyx]] has it.
