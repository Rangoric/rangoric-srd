# Fear of Fire

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “fear of fire”, on the Special Qualities line.

A shriezyx within 30 feet of a fire the size of a torch or larger becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] as long as it remains within that range. If damaged by fire, the fire attacks a shriezyx's Will (attack bonus equal to the amount of fire damage dealt – 10), and on a hit it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 round. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shriezyx|shriezyx]] has it.
