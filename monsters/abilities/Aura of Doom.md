# Aura of Doom

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “aura of doom”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a free action, a vulnudaemon can radiate an aura of dread and hopelessness. Any creature within 30 feet of the vulnudaemon must succeed at a DC 18 Will save or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for as long as it remains in the aura. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Vulnudaemon|vulnudaemon]] has it.
