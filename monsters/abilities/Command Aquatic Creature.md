# Command Aquatic Creature

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “command aquatic creature”, on the Special Attacks line.

Dagon can command aquatic creatures to do his bidding as a move action, either via using his ability to speak with animals or via telepathy. This affects all aberrations, animals, magical beasts, oozes, and vermin within 300 feet that have the aquatic subtype (+26 vs Will). This functions like mass suggestion, but can affect mindless creatures. Dagon can suggest obviously harmful or suicidal acts (though non-mindless creatures gain a +10 bonus to their Will Defense against these suggestions). The commanded course of activity can have a duration of up to 1 hour. If Dagon issues a new command to a creature, the previous command is discarded. Once this effect misses a creature, it is immune to further commands from Dagon for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Dagon|dagon]] has it.
