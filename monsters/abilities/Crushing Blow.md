# Crushing Blow

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “crushing blow”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per round, Koschei may designate any attack with his warhammer to be a crushing blow. He must designate this attack before making the attack roll. If he hits, the blow ignores the target's hardness and damage reduction, and allows him to make a free trip attempt against the target. This trip attempt does not provoke an attack of opportunity, can target a foe of any size, and has no chance of causing Koschei to fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] if he fails. A crushing blow attacks the creature it strikes at +34 vs Fortitude; a miss means the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round, and a hit means it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round and staggered for 1d6 rounds afterward. The attack bonus is Strength-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demons/Koschei|Koschei]] has it.
