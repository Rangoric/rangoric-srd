# Unnatural Aura

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “unnatural aura (30 ft.)”, on the Special Qualities line.

Animals do not willingly approach the creature unless the animal's master makes a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|wild empathy]] check.
