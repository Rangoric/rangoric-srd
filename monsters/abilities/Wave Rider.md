# Wave Rider

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “wave rider”, on the Special Qualities line.

When the creature moves onto land, it brings a sheath of water with it, allowing it to swim on land. Its swim speed drops by 10 feet at the start of its turn if it is out of the water, and the sheath dissipates entirely when the creature's swim speed reaches 20 feet. While wave riding on land, the creature retains its spell resistance but loses its bonus to Stealth. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Abaia|abaia]] has it.
