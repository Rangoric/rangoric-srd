# Cloud Form

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “cloud form”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can change into the form of a 10-foot black cloud or back to its normal form as a standard action. The creature in cloud form is otherwise treated as if under the effects of gaseous form, except that it obscures vision like fog cloud. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Nue|nue]] has it.
