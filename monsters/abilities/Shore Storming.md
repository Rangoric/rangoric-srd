# Shore Storming

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “shore storming”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature automatically transforms into an orca whenever it is fully immersed in water, losing its legs and fur. Likewise, when the creature emerges from the water, it automatically transforms into its wolf-orca hybrid form. If the creature moves from water to land (or vice versa) on the round before initiating combat, it gains a +8 bonus on its initiative check. This initial attack resolves as a charge. The creature has the same statistics in both forms. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Akhlut|akhlut]] has it.
