# Savage Bite (Devilfish)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “savage bite”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can attack with its savage bite whenever it makes a successful grapple check. This attack is in place of any other action made with a successful grapple check. The bite threatens a critical hit on a roll of 18–20, and injects the target with poison as well. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Devilfish|devilfish]] has it.
