# All-Around Vision

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “all-around vision”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature sees in all directions at once. It gains a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks, and it cannot be [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]].
