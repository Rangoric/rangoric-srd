# Piteous Moan

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “piteous moan”, on the Special Attacks and Special Qualities lines.

Whenever a grothlut sees another creature, it begins to moan as free action. Anyone within 60 feet who can hear the moan must succeed at a DC 15 Will saving throw or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] by the moaning for as long as she can hear it. Every two additional grothluts with the area increase the DC by 1, to a maximum of DC 18. This is a mind-affecting sonic effect. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarp/Grothlut|grothlut]] has it.
