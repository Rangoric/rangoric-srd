# Piteous Moan

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “piteous moan”, on the Special Attacks and Special Qualities lines.

Whenever a grothlut sees another creature, it begins to moan as free action. Anyone within 60 feet who can hear the moan are attacked at +5 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] by the moaning for as long as she can hear it. Every two additional grothluts with the area increase the attack bonus by 1, to a maximum of +8. This is a mind-affecting sonic effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarp/Grothlut|grothlut]] has it.
