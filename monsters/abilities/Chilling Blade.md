# Chilling Blade

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “chilling blade”, on the Special Attacks line.

A dullahan is proficient with all simple and martial slashing weapons. When it wields a slashing weapon, the blade inflicts +1d6 cold damage and gains the keen weapon property. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dullahan|dullahan]] has it.
