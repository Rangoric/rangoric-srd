# Unstoppable Force

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “unstoppable force”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's bonus on a charge attack is +4, and it may add this bonus on overrun combat maneuver checks attempted using Charge Through. The creature may charge over difficult terrain, provided the terrain does not cost more than 2 squares to enter. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ypotryll|ypotryll]] has it.
