# Trap Making

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “trap making”, on the Special Attacks line.

A crucidaemon can use Engineering to disarm magic traps. When it uses its greater glyph of warding spell-like ability to create a spell glyph, it may utilize any 6th-level or lower spell from the cleric or the wizard spell list, even though it otherwise can't cast these spells. The Perception and Engineering DCs for any traps a crucidaemon creates gain a +2 bonus. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Crucidaemon|crucidaemon]] has it.
