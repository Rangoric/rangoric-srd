# Acid Blood

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “acid blood”, on the Special Qualities line.

A metal, wooden, or natural weapon that deals piercing or slashing damage to the creature takes acid damage unless the weapon's wielder succeeds at a Reflex save. The creature's entry gives the damage and the DC, which is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Seps|seps]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Juvenile Seps|juvenile seps]] have it.
