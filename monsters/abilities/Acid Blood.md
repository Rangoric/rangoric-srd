# Acid Blood

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “acid blood”, on the Special Qualities line.

A metal, wooden, or natural weapon that deals piercing or slashing damage to the creature takes acid damage if the acid hits the Reflex of the weapon's wielder. The creature's entry gives the damage and the attack bonus, which is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Seps|seps]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Juvenile Seps|juvenile seps]] have it.
