# Lesser Shadow Evocation

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Spell-Like]] (Sp). In a stat block*: “lesser shadow evocation”, on the Special Attacks line.

This spell-like ability functions identically to the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Evocation|shadow evocation]]*, but it can only create quasi-real versions of sorcerer or wizard evocation spells of 2nd level or lower. This spell-like ability is the equivalent of a 3rd-level spell. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shae|shae]] has it.
