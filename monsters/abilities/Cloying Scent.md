# Cloying Scent

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “cloying scent”, on the Special Qualities line.

Qallupilluks exude a vaguely sweet and unexpectedly pleasant briny scent. This smell comforts and distracts humanoids and animals that come within 15 feet of the qallupilluk, causing such creatures to take a -2 penalty on defenses against mind-affecting effects. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hags/Qallupilluk|qallupilluk]] has it.
