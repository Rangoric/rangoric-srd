# Adamantine Claws

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “adamantine claws”, on the Special Qualities line.

Able to tear through stone, an olethrodaemon's claws are treated as though they were adamantine. This ability also allows an olethrodaemon to make use of its burrow speed through stone. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Olethrodaemon|olethrodaemon]] has it.
