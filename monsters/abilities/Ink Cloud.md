# Ink Cloud

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “ink cloud”, on the Special Qualities line.

While within water, the creature can emit a 5-foot-radius sphere of ink once per minute as a swift action. This ink provides total concealment and persists for 1 minute. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Blue-Ringed Octopus|blue-ringed octopus]] has it.
