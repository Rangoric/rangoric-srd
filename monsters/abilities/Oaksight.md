# Oaksight

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “oaksight”, on the Special Qualities line.

A quickwood may observe the area surrounding any oak tree within 360 feet as if using clairaudience/clairvoyance. It can use this ability on any number of oak trees in the area. Although the quickwood does not need line of sight to establish this link, if it does have line of sight to even a single oak tree, it can't be [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]]. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Quickwood|quickwood]] has it.
