# Explosive Rebirth

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “explosive rebirth”, on the Special Qualities line.

When killed, a star archon explodes in a blinding flash of energy that deals 50 points of damage (half fire, half holy damage) to anything within 100 feet (+19 vs Reflex; a miss halves). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The slain archon reincarnates 1d4 rounds later as an advanced shield archon. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archon/Star Archon|star archon]] has it.
