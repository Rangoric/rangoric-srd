# Wing Razors

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “wing razors”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's metallic feathers are razor-sharp. In addition to being able to slash creatures with its wings as a melee attack, it may beat its wings, flinging two large, feathered shards at a single target. These wing razors deal 2d6 points of damage and cause bleed, with a range increment of 50 feet. The creature can use this attack a number of times per day equal to its Constitution modifier (5 times per day for most stymphalidies). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Stymphalidies|stymphalidies]] has it.
