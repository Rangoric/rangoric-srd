# Spores (Ascomoid)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “spores”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per round as a free action, an ascomoid can release a jet of deadly spores to a range of 30 feet. Upon impacting a solid surface, such as a wall or creature, the jet billows out into a cloud of spores that fills a 10-foot-radius spread. This cloud lasts for 1 round before dispersing. Any creature in the cloud is attacked at +6 vs Fortitude, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] as long as it remains in the cloud. Any creature this nausea hits is also exposed to the ascomoid's poison (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Ascomoid Spores|Ascomoid Spores]]). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fungus/Ascomoid|ascomoid]] has it.
