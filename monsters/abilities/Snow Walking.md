# Snow Walking

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “snow walking”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can walk on snow or thin sheets of ice as though affected by water walk. It only leaves a trail on such surfaces when it wants to. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Akhlut|akhlut]] has it.
