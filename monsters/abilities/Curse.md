# Curse

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines*: **Curse Name** (Su) Slam—contact; *save* Will DC 14; *frequency* 1/day; *effect* 1d4 Str drain.

A creature with this ability bestows a curse upon its enemies. The effects of the curse, including its save, frequency, and cure, are included in the creature's entry, written the same way as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|poison]] (how curses work is under [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Curse|curses]]). If a curse allows a saving throw, it is usually a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 the cursing creature's racial HD + the creature's Cha modifier; the exact DC is given in the creature's entry). Curses can be removed through *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]* and similar effects.
