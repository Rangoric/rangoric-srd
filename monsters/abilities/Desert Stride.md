# Desert Stride

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “desert stride”, on the Special Qualities line.

A kasatha moves through nonmagical difficult terrain in desert environments at normal speed. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Kasatha|kasatha]] has it.
