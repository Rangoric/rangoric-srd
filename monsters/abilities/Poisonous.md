# Poisonous

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “poisonous”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature that attacks a weedwhip with a natural piercing or slashing weapon is exposed to its poison. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Weedwhip|weedwhip]] has it.
