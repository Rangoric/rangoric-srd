# Protective Aura

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “protective aura”, on the Special Qualities line.

Against attacks made or effects created by evil creatures, this ability provides a +4 deflection bonus to AC and a +4 resistance bonus on defenses to anyone within 20 feet of the agathion. Otherwise, it functions as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]_ effect and a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Globe of Invulnerability, Lesser|lesser globe of invulnerability]]_, both with a radius of 20 feet (caster level equals the agathion's HD). The defensive benefits from the circle are not included in the stat block. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/agathions/Cetaceal|cetaceal]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/agathions/Draconal|draconal]] have it.
