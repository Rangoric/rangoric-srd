# Blood-Freezing Howl

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “blood-freezing howl”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, the creature can unleash a bloodcurdling howl. All creatures within a 30-foot-radius burst must succeed at a DC 15 Will save or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1 round and then [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for an additional 1d6 rounds. Any creature that makes a successful save against the creature's howl is immune to the same the creature's howl for 24 hours. This is a sonic mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Voonith|voonith]] has it.
