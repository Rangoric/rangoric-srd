# Blood-Freezing Howl

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “blood-freezing howl”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, the creature can unleash a bloodcurdling howl. All creatures within a 30-foot-radius burst are attacked at +5 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1 round and then [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for an additional 1d6 rounds. Any creature the howl misses is immune to the same the creature's howl for 24 hours. This is a sonic mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Voonith|voonith]] has it.
