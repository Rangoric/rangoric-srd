# Powerful Stench

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “powerful stench”, on the Special Qualities line.

An enraged ghonhatine secretes a tarry, musk-like chemical. Any living, non-ghonhatine creature within 10 feet must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] as long as it remains within the affected area and for 1d4 rounds afterward. A creature that saves is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] as long as it remains in the area, and can't be affected again by the same ghonhatine's stench for 24 hours. This is a poison effect. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarp/Ghonhatine|ghonhatine]] has it.
