# Startle

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “startle”, on the Special Attacks line.

If an undead ningyo attacks in the surprise round, its target must attempt a DC 11 Will save. Success means the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round; failure means it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1d4 rounds. The target can only be affected by that ningyo's startle once every 24 hours. Creatures with 6 or more Hit Dice are immune to this ability. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ningyo|ningyo]] has it.
