# Drown

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “drown”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a full-round action, a drowning devil can summon murky water into the lungs of a single target within 30 feet. If the target can't breathe water, it's unable hold its breath and immediately begins to drown. At the start of its next turn, the water attacks the target at +8 vs Fortitude; on a miss it coughs up this water, and on a hit it falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] and is brought to 0 hit points. On the next round, it attacks again, and a hit drops the target to -1 hit point and starts it dying; on the third round it attacks again, and a hit kills it. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Drowning Devil|drowning devil]] has it.
