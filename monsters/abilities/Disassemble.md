# Disassemble

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “disassemble”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a move-equivalent action, a harbinger can break apart into its components. This is treated as gaseous form, except the archon retains its full flight speed and DR, and cannot fit through gaps smaller than 1 inch in diameter. Reforming into an orrery is a standard action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archon/Harbinger Archon|harbinger archon]] has it.
