# Indestructible

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “indestructible”, on the Special Qualities line.

An adamantine golem is nearly impossible to destroy. Even if reduced below 0 hit points, its fast healing continues to restore hit points, though the golem is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] unless above 0 hit points. It can only be permanently destroyed if reduced to negative hit points and then decapitated using an adamantine vorpal weapon—alternatively, miracle or wish can be used to slay it while it is at negative hit points. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Adamantine Golem|adamantine golem]] has it.
