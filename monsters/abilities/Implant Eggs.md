# Implant Eggs

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “implant eggs”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day, the creature can implant eggs into a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] target or a target it is grappling. As a full-round action, the creature extends an ovipositor from its abdomen and penetrates the victim's flesh by making a successful sting attack (+5 melee). On a hit, the ovipositor deals 1 point of damage and implants 1d4 eggs in the victim. The eggs draw nutrients from the target's flesh, and give the target the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] condition. The eggs grow swiftly, hatching in a mere 1d4 minutes into ravenous chicks that immediately burrow out of the victim's body. This deals 2 points of Constitution damage per chick, after which the hatchlings immediately take wing and fly away (if needed, use game statistics for a bat familiar to represent a hatchling). Removing implanted eggs requires a DC 20 Medicine check (a full-round action); each attempt deals 1 hit point of damage. Although immunity to disease offers no special protection against egg implantation, remove disease, heal, or similar effects automatically destroy any implanted eggs. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Gryph|gryph]] has it.
