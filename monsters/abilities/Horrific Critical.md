# Horrific Critical

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “horrific critical”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's gore attack threatens a critical hit on an 18–20. If the creature kills a humanoid foe with a critical hit, it can tear out the victim's heart with its wolf-like teeth as a free action. Any creature that witnesses this savage event must make a DC 13 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Peryton|peryton]] has it.
