# Wall of Gears

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “wall of gears”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a clockwork golem can fold into a whirling wall of grinding gears measuring 10 feet by 10 feet or 5 feet by 20 feet. Anyone passing through the wall takes 15d6 points of slashing damage. If the wall appears in a creature's space, the wall attacks that creature at +8 vs Reflex, and on a miss the creature leaps to one side and avoids the damage entirely. The clockwork golem can take no actions while in this form except to resume its normal form as a move action. A clockwork golem's AC and immunities remain the same while it is in this form. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golems/Clockwork Golem|clockwork golem]] has it.
