# Druid Magic

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “druid magic”, on the Special Qualities line.

A hamadryad can use any magic item as if she were a 20th-level druid. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Hamadryad|hamadryad]] has it.
