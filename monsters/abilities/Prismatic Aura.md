# Prismatic Aura

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “prismatic aura”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a swift action once per day, a humbaba can activate its prismatic aura. Doing so causes brilliant light to radiate from the humbaba to a radius of 30 feet. Each round at the start of the humbaba's turn, roll 1d8 and consult the table of prismatic spray effects to determine the color and effect of the light (reroll results of 8). If a creature begins its turn in the prismatic aura's area, that effect attacks it at +16 against the defense the effect names. Once activated, a humbaba's prismatic aura lasts for 7 rounds. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Humbaba|humbaba]] has it.
