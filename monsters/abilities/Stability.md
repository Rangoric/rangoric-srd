# Stability

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “stability”, on the Special Qualities line.

Shield archons receive a +4 racial bonus to CMD when resisting a bull rush or trip attempt. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archon/Shield Archon|shield archon]] has it.
