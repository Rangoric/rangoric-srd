# Ironwood Mastery

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “ironwood mastery”, on the Special Attacks line.

Any ironwood armor an erlking wears gains a +3 enhancement bonus, and any ironwood weapon it wields is treated as a +3 cold iron keen weapon. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Erlking|erlking]] has it.
