# Soul Crush

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “soul crush”, on the Special Attacks line.

A thanadaemon can crush a soul gem (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Cacodaemon|cacodaemon]]) as a standard action to gain fast healing 15 for 15 rounds (this is a standard action). This action condemns the crushed soul to the daemons' home plane—resurrecting this victim requires a DC 28 caster level check. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Thanadaemon|thanadaemon]] has it.
