# Profane Magic

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “profane magic”, on the Special Qualities line.

Protection from evil blocks attacks from a warsworn's spell-like abilities. Weapons animated by the warsworn are damaged by channeled energy as if undead. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Warsworn|warsworn]] has it.
