# Breath of Flies

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “breath of flies”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per minute as a standard action, a leukodaemon can unleash a cloud of corpse-bloated, biting black flies in a 20-foot cone. Those caught in the cone take 8d6 points of slashing damage. It attacks at +11 vs Reflex, and a miss halves this damage. Those who take any damage are also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute. In addition, the flies linger for 1d4+1 rounds, congealing into a buzzing 20-foot-square cloud centered on the cone's original point of origin. Any creature that ends its turn in this cloud is attacked at +11 vs Reflex, and on a hit takes 4d6 points of damage and becomes sickened for 1 minute. This cloud of flies may be dispersed by any area effect that does damage or creates wind of at least strong wind force. All daemons are immune to this effect. The attack bonuses are Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Leukodaemon|leukodaemon]] has it.
