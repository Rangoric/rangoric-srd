# Shrine

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “shrine”, on the Special Qualities line.

A juggernaut counts as a movable shrine for the deity or religion it is dedicated to. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Juggernaut|juggernaut]] has it.
