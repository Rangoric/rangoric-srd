# Push

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “push (slam, 10 feet)”, on the Special Attacks and Attack lines.

A creature with the push ability can choose to make a free [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver check]] with a particular successful attack (often a slam attack). If successful, this check pushes an opponent directly away as with a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], but the distance moved is set by this ability. The type of attack that causes the push and the distance pushed are included in the creature's entry. This ability only works on creatures of a size equal to or smaller than the pushing creature. Creatures pushed in this way do not provoke attacks of opportunity and stop if the push would move them into a solid object or creature.
