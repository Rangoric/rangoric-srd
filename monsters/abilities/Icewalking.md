# Icewalking

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “icewalking”, on the Special Qualities line.

This ability works like the spider climb spell, but the surfaces the hag climbs must be icy. The hag can move across icy surfaces without penalty and doesn't need to make Acrobatics checks to run or charge on ice. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hag/Winter Hag|winter hag]] has it.
