# All-Seeing Attacks

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “all-seeing attacks”, on the Special Attacks line.

A jotund troll can make nine additional attacks of opportunity in a round, one for each head, although no more than a single attack for any given opportunity. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Jotund Troll|jotund troll]] has it.
