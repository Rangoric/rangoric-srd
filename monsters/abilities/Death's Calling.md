# Death's Calling

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “death's calling”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day as a standard action, a dullahan may place death's calling on a target within 60 feet (+12 vs Fortitude). If the dullahan knows and speaks the target's name, the target takes a –2 penalty to its Fortitude Defense against it. On a hit, the victim becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d6 rounds. For the next 24 hours (or until the dullahan is slain), all critical hits against the victim automatically confirm. Finally, the victim automatically fails all Constitution checks to stabilize while dying. This is a mind-affecting curse effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dullahan|dullahan]] has it.
