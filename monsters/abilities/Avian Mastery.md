# Avian Mastery

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “avian mastery”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature flying under its own power (flight from a source other than a spell, spell-like ability, or magic item) that attempts to attack Pazuzu with a melee attack is attacked at +30 vs Will. On a hit, the creature can't follow through with the attack, that part of its action is lost, and it can't directly attack Pazuzu for 1d4 rounds. Once this ability misses a creature, it is immune to this ability for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Pazuzu|pazuzu]] has it.
