# Magic Horn

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “magic horn”, on the Special Attacks line.

While on the creature's head, the creature's horn is treated as a +1 weapon. Any living creature slain by the creature's gore attack immediately turns to stone (as if by the flesh to stone spell, with no attack against a defense, and the creature is still dead). A severed horn retains a wisp of its former magic, and counts as a masterwork weapon if used to create a magical dagger or similar small piercing weapon. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Almiraj|almiraj]] has it.
