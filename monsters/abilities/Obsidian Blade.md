# Obsidian Blade

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “obsidian blade”, on the Special Qualities line.

At will as a free action, a brijidine can create a blade of jagged volcanic glass that functions as a +1 flaming burst keen longsword. One round after it leaves the brijidine's grasp, the weapon decays into useless powder. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/azata/Brijidine|brijidine]] has it.
