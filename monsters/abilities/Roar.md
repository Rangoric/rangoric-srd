# Roar

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “roar”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's roar is supernaturally loud and horrifying. When the creature roars (a standard action the creature can perform at will), all hearing creatures with 4 or fewer HD within a 100-foot spread are attacked at +3 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for 2d4 rounds. Whether the attack hits or misses, creatures in the area are immune to the roar of that particular creature for 24 hours. This is a sonic, mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bunyip|bunyip]] has it.
