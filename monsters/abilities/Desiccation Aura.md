# Desiccation Aura

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “desiccation aura”, on the Special Qualities line.

A cloud of moisture-draining dust fills the air in a 30-foot radius around a hollow serpent. The aura attacks living creatures within this area at +16 vs Fortitude, and those it hits take 1d6 points of Strength damage as water is leached from their flesh. Whether the attack hits or misses, a creature cannot be affected again by the same hollow serpent's desiccation aura for 24 hours. Creatures without fleshy bodies are immune to this aura, while creatures with the aquatic or water subtypes take a –4 penalty to their Fortitude Defense against it. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Hollow Serpent|hollow serpent]] has it.
