# Summon Mount

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “summon mount”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a dullahan can summon a war-trained heavy horse with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creature/Alternate Fiendish Creature|fiendish creature simple template]]. This horse remains until it is slain or the dullahan dismisses it. He can only have one such horse in his service at a time. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dullahan|dullahan]] has it.
