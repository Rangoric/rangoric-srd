# Aquatic Reconstruction

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “aquatic reconstruction”, on the Special Qualities line.

Whenever a coral golem is touching a body of saltwater of a size equal to or greater than its own size, it gains fast healing 5. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Coral Golem|coral golem]] has it.
