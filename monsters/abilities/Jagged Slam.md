# Jagged Slam

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “jagged slam”, on the Special Attacks line.

A freezing flow's slam attack is a slashing tendril of crystalline ice. It deals slashing damage instead of bludgeoning damage and has a critical range of 19-20. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Freezing Flow|freezing flow]] has it.
