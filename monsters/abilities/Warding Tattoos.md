# Warding Tattoos

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “warding tattoos”, on the Special Qualities line.

A jungle giant's magical tattoos grant it a +4 deflection bonus to Armor Class. The giant loses this bonus if it wears armor. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Jungle Giant|jungle giant]] has it.
