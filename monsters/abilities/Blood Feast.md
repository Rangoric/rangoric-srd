# Blood Feast

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “blood feast”, on the Special Qualities line.

When a creature within 30 feet of the creature takes bleed damage (including ongoing damage), the creature heals an equal amount of damage. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Feeder in the Depths|feeder in the depths]] has it.
