# Speed Surge

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “speed surge”, on the Special Qualities line.

Three times per day as a swift action, a drake can draw on its draconic heritage for a boost of strength and speed that allows it to take an additional move action that round. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Desert Drake|desert drake]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Flame Drake|flame drake]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Forest Drake|forest drake]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Frost Drake|frost drake]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Lava Drake|lava drake]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Mist Drake|mist drake]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Rift Drake|rift drake]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/River Drake|river drake]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Sea Drake|sea drake]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Shadow Drake|shadow drake]] have it.
