# Aerial Charge (Snallygaster)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “aerial charge”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a snallygaster charges downward at an angle of 45 degrees or more, its bite attack deals double damage (or triple damage on a critical hit). Bleed damage is not multiplied for this attack. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Snallygaster|snallygaster]] has it.
