# Specious Suggestion

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “specious suggestion”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action three times per day, the creature can concentrate intently on one creature within its line of sight and attempt to impose its will upon the target. The compulsion attacks at +1 vs Will, and a miss resists it. On a hit, roll 1d6. On a result of 1–2, the target gains a flash of insight and a +2 insight bonus to its Armor Class for 1 minute. On a result of 3–4, the victim is affected as if by suggestion for 1 minute, and must follow a single (usually embarrassing, always harmless) suggestion from the creature. On a result of 5–6, the victim's thoughts are garbled with those of the creature, imposing a –2 penalty to the victim's Will Defense for 1 minute. This is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Carbuncle|carbuncle]] has it.
