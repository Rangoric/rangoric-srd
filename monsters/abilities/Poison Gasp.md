# Poison Gasp

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “poison gasp”, on the Special Attacks line.

A tatzlwyrm's breath contains a poisonous vapor. While grappling, instead of making a bite or rake attack, a tatzlwyrm can breathe poison into its victim's face. A tatzlwyrm must begin its turn grappling to use this ability—it can't begin a grapple and use its poison gasp in the same turn. [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Tatzlwyrm Poison|Tatzlwyrm Poison]]: Breath—inhaled; *save* Fort DC 12; *frequency* 1/round for 2 rounds; *effect* 1d2 Str damage; *cure* 1 save. The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tatzlwyrm|tatzlwyrm]] has it.
