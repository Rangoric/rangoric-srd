# Acid (Slimy Demodand)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “acid”, on the Special Attacks line.

A slimy demodand is coated in an ever-dripping layer of acid that deals an extra 2d6 points of acid damage on a successful natural attack. In addition, opponents that successfully strike a slimy demodand with an unarmed strike or natural attack take 2d6 points of acid damage. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demodands/Slimy Demodand|slimy demodand]] has it.
