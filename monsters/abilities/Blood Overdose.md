# Blood Overdose

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “blood overdose”, on the Special Qualities line.

When a vampiric mist gorges on blood to an extent that it gains temporary hit points, it moves much more quickly. It gains a +2 bonus to its Armor Class and on Reflex saves, and can take one additional move action each round. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Vampiric Mist|vampiric mist]] has it.
