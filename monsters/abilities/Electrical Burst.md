# Electrical Burst

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “electrical burst”, on the Special Attacks line.

An arbiter can release electrical energy from its body in a 10-foot-radius burst that deals 3d6 electricity damage (+3 vs Reflex; a miss halves). Immediately following such a burst, the arbiter becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 24 hours. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/inevitable/Arbiter|arbiter]] has it.
