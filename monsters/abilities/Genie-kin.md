# Genie-kin

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “genie-kin”, on the Special Qualities line.

For all race-related effects (such as a ranger's favored enemy), a ghul is considered a genie even though its type is undead. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Ghul|ghul]] has it.
