# Regurgitate

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “regurgitate”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day, the creature can empty its putrid stomach upon one creature within 5 feet. The target must make a Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute (or until the target spends a full-round action with at least a gallon of water to wash off the filth). The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Maggot|giant maggot]] has it.
