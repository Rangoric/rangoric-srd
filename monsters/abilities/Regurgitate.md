# Regurgitate

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “regurgitate”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day, the creature can empty its putrid stomach upon one creature within 5 feet. The filth attacks the target's Fortitude, and on a hit the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute (or until the target spends a full-round action with at least a gallon of water to wash off the filth). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Maggot|giant maggot]] has it.
