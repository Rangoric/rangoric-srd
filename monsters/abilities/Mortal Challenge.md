# Mortal Challenge

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “mortal challenge”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a nephilim spends a standard action to roar a battle challenge, all humanoids and monstrous humanoids within 100 feet of the nephilim must make a DC 18 Will save to avoid becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 5d6 rounds. On a successful save, the creatures only become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for that time. Creatures with more HD than the nephilim become shaken if they fail their save, and suffer no effect if they succeed. A creature that succeeds at the save is immune to that nephilim's mortal challenge for 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Nephilim|nephilim]] has it.
