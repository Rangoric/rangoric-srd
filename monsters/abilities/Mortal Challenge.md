# Mortal Challenge

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “mortal challenge”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a nephilim spends a standard action to roar a battle challenge, all humanoids and monstrous humanoids within 100 feet of the nephilim are attacked at +8 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 5d6 rounds. On a miss, the creatures only become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for that time. Creatures with more HD than the nephilim become shaken on a hit, and suffer no effect on a miss. A creature the challenge misses is immune to that nephilim's mortal challenge for 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Nephilim|nephilim]] has it.
