# Thunderbolt (Thunderbird)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “thunderbolt”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can fire a ray of thunder and lightning from its outspread wings as a standard action. This attack has a range of 200 feet with no range increment, and requires a ranged touch attack to hit. A creature critically hit by a thunderbolt is stunned and deafened for 1 round if the thunderbolt hits it at +12 vs Fortitude. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Thunderbird|thunderbird]] has it.
