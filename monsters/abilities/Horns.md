# Horns

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “horns”, on the Special Attacks line.

The kalavakus's numerous horns can easily catch weapons and yank them away from opponents. The demon gains a +4 racial bonus on all disarm attempts as a result. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Kalavakus|kalavakus]] has it.
