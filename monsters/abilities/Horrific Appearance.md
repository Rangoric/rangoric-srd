# Horrific Appearance

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “horrific appearance”, on the Special Attacks line.

All qlippoth have such horrific and mind-rending shapes that those who gaze upon them suffer all manner of ill effects. A qlippoth can present itself as a standard action to assault the senses of all living creatures within 30 feet. The exact effects caused by a qlippoth's horrific appearance vary by the type of qlippoth. It attacks Will (attack bonus 1/2 the qlippoth's Hit Dice + the qlippoth's Charisma modifier), and a miss reduces or negates the effect. This ability is a mind-affecting gaze attack.

The effects, by qlippoth:

- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Augnagar|Augnagar]]: Creatures that succumb to an augnagar's horrific appearance are driven momentarily insane. This results in 2 points of Charisma damage and leaves the victim [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d3 rounds.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Chernobue|Chernobue]]: Creatures that succumb to a chernobue's horrific appearance become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] with disgust for 2d6 rounds; the appearance attacks a paralyzed creature again each round, and a miss ends the effect, provided he is no longer aware of the chernobue or within 30 feet of it.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Cythnigot|Cythnigot]]: Creatures that succumb to a cythnigot's horrific appearance become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 round—a cythnigot's horrific appearance only functions to a range of 10 feet. Once a particular cythnigot's horrific appearance has attacked a creature, that creature is immune to the horrific appearance of all cythnigots for 24 hours. A spellcaster that has a cythnigot as a familiar is immune to the horrific appearance of all cythnigots, and also gains a +4 bonus on defenses against any qlippoth's horrific appearance.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Iathavos|Iathavos]]: Creatures that succumb to the iathavos's horrific appearance are affected by a feeblemind effect and permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]].
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Nyogoth|Nyogoth]]: Creatures that succumb to a nyogoth's horrific appearance become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d8 rounds.
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Shoggti|Shoggti]]: Creatures that succumb to a shoggti's horrific appearance become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] by the creature's hypnotically wriggling tentacles and the strange, shimmering colors in its eyes. This effect persists for 1d6 rounds (but can be ended by the normal methods of defeating the fascinated condition).
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Thulgant|Thulgant]]: Creatures that succumb to a thulgant's horrific appearance are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d4 rounds and take 1d6 points of Wisdom damage.

The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Augnagar|augnagar]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Chernobue|chernobue]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Cythnigot|cythnigot]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Iathavos|iathavos]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Nyogoth|nyogoth]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Shoggti|shoggti]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Thulgant|thulgant]] have it.
