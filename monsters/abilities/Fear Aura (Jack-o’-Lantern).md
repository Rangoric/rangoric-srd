# Fear Aura (Jack-o’-Lantern)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “fear”, on the Special Qualities line.

Any creature within 30 feet of a jack-o'-lantern is attacked at +3 vs Will, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 2d6 minutes. If the aura misses, the creature is immune to that jack-o'-lantern's fear aura for 24 hours. This is a vision-based mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based and includes a +4 racial bonus. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jack-o’-Lantern|jack-o’-lantern]] has it.
