# Wrappings

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “wrappings”, on the Special Attacks line.

A morrigna's web wrappings grant her a +2 shield bonus to AC and can make secondary natural attacks. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/psychopomps/Morrigna|morrigna]] has it.
