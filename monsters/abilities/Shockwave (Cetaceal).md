# Shockwave (Cetaceal)

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “shockwave”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once per day, a cetaceal can release a 100-foot-radius burst of energy. All creatures in the area take 17d6 damage; half of this damage is cold, and half is electricity (DC 25 Reflex save halves). The save DC is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/agathion/Cetaceal|cetaceal]] has it.
