# Sudden Strike

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “sudden strike”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature is adept at moving quickly when its foes are surprised. During a surprise round, it may act as if it had a full round to act, rather than just one standard action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/scorpion/Deadfall Scorpion|deadfall scorpion]] has it.
