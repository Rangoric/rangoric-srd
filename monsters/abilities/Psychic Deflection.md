# Psychic Deflection

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “psychic deflection”, on the Special Qualities line.

A caulborn adds its Charisma modifier as a deflection bonus to its AC. The caulborn loses this bonus when [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Caulborn|caulborn]] has it.
