# Infernal Investment

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “infernal investment”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a subclause of all infernal contracts, a contract devil can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying, Greater|greater scrying]]_ at will upon any creature it has a contract with. The devil's scrying attempt always hits the target creature—this ability otherwise functions at caster level 20th. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Contract Devil|contract devil]] has it.
