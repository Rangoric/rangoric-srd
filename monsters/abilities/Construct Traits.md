# Construct Traits

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “construct traits”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature with construct traits has the immunities and other traits of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^construct|construct type]], which owns them. In a stat block, “construct traits” on the Immune line stands for that list; immunities the creature has beyond it are listed after it.
