# Share Defenses

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “share defenses”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a free action, the creature can extend its no breath ability and cold immunity to a single creature touching it. It can withdraw this protection as a free action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Shantak|shantak]] has it.
