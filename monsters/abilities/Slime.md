# Slime

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “slime”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's back is crusted with thick, dry slime and dozens of nodules. As a standard action at will, it can squirt a jet of this slime from one of these nodules as a ranged touch attack against any target within 30 feet. The slime quickly hardens to the texture of cold tar, entangling the foe. Anyone the creature successfully bull rushes or overruns is automatically squirted with back slime. The hardened slime can be removed as a full-round action with a DC 15 Strength check. The back slime grants a creature riding it a +8 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks made to stay in the saddle, but a –8 penalty on Animal Handling checks to dismount. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Slurk|slurk]] has it.
