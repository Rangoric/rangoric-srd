# Toss

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “toss”, on the Special Attacks line.

If the creature charges 20 feet or more and damages a foe with an attack, it can throw its foe with a special combat maneuver check. The opponent must be corporeal and at least one size category smaller than the creature. If the combat maneuver check succeeds, the creature's opponent is thrown 10 feet through the air in a direction chosen by the creature and falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. The creature can only toss its opponent in a straight line. If an obstacle prevents the creature's movement, both the creature tossed and the object struck take 1d6 points of damage, and the creature falls prone in the space adjacent to the obstacle. The creature can also toss an opponent 10 feet up into the air. The victim lands in the same square it started in, falls prone, and takes 1d6 points of damage. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Hodag|hodag]] has it.
