# Dazing Gaze

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “dazing gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

A cephalophore can make a gaze attack that affects all seeing creatures within a 60-foot radius. These creatures are attacked at +6 vs Will, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. Creatures it misses are instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting fear effect, and the attack bonus is Wisdom-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Cephalophore|cephalophore]] has it.
