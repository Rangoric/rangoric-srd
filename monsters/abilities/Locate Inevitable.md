# Locate Inevitable

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “locate inevitable”, on the Special Qualities line.

An arbiter can always sense the direction of the nearest non-arbiter inevitable on the plane, the better to help it report back to its superiors. It cannot sense the range to this inevitable. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/inevitable/Arbiter|arbiter]] has it.
