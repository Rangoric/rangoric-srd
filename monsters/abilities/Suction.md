# Suction

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “suction”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's foot adheres to surfaces so well that its climb speed applies even to perfectly sheer surfaces and ceilings, with no chance of its falling off unless it is actively pinned and peeled away as part of a grapple. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Flail Snail|flail snail]] has it.
