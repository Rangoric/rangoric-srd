# Touch of Madness (Aoandon)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “touch of madness”, on the Special Attacks line.

The touch of an aoandon causes maddening fear. As a standard action it can make an incorporeal touch attack that deals 10d6 damage. Any living creature damaged by an aoandon's touch attack is attacked at +14 vs Will, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d4 rounds. A creature the touch misses can't be confused again by this aoandon's touch of madness for 10 minutes. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Aoandon|aoandon]] has it.
