# Eldritch Encrustation

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “eldritch encrustation”, on the Special Qualities line.

Crystalline growths along the creature's digestive tract contain large deposits of magical energy. These nodes are responsible for the magical feedback that surrounds each creature, granting it its spell resistance, damage reduction, dispelling bite, and digest magic abilities. Dispel magic cast on the creature can negate these powers for 1 minute—the CL of the effect is treated as the creature's Hit Dice (CL 14th for most lukwatas). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Lukwata|lukwata]] has it.
