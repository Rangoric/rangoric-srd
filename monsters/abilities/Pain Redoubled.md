# Pain Redoubled

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “pain redoubled”, on the Special Attacks line.

When an incubus confirms a critical hit with a melee weapon or a natural weapon, that attack deals an additional 2d6 points of nonlethal damage and the incubus attacks the target at +9 vs Fortitude, and on a hit the target is wracked by pain, becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d6 rounds. Multiple uses of this ability extend the duration. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Incubus|incubus]] has it.
