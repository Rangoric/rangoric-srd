# Staggering Gaze

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “staggering gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature's gaze staggers creatures within 30 feet for 1d6 rounds unless they succeed on a Will save. This is a mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Charisma-based; the creature's entry gives it. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Seaweed Siren|seaweed siren]] has it.
