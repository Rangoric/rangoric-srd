# Undead Traits

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “undead traits”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature with undead traits has the immunities and other traits of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^undead|undead type]], which owns them. In a stat block, “undead traits” on the Immune line stands for that list; immunities the creature has beyond it are listed after it.
