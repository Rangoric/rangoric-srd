# Fear Gaze

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “fear gaze”, on the Special Attacks line.

Cower in fear for 1d6 rounds, 30 feet, +11 vs Will. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Thanadaemon|thanadaemon]] has it.
