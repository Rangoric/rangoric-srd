# Consume Thoughts

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “consume thoughts”, on the Special Attacks line.

A caulborn can consume the thoughts of a willing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] creature with a touch attack. If the caulborn hits the target at +8 vs Will, it can alter the victim's memory as if using modify memory. This process deals 1d4 points of Intelligence and Wisdom damage to the target. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Caulborn|caulborn]] has it.
