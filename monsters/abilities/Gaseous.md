# Gaseous

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “gaseous”, on the Special Qualities line.

A hungry fog has a body composed of eerie mist. It can pass through small holes or narrow openings, even mere cracks, but cannot enter water or other liquid. It has no Strength score, and cannot manipulate objects as a result. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ooze/Hungry Fog|hungry fog]] has it.
