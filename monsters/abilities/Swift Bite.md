# Swift Bite

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “swift bite”, on the Special Attacks line.

When making a full attack, the creature can make a second bite attack at a –5 penalty. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Feeder in the Depths|feeder in the depths]] has it.
