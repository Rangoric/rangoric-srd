# Smother

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “smother”, on the Special Attacks line.

When a lurker above grapples a target, it forms an airtight seal around its prey. A grappled target cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components, and must hold its breath (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#^hold-breath|Suffocation]]). The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Lurker Above|lurker above]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Trapper|trapper]] have it.
