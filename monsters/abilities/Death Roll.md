# Death Roll

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “death roll”, on the Special Attacks line.

When grappling a foe of its size or smaller, a werecrocodile can perform a death roll upon making a successful grapple check. As it clings to its foe, the werecrocodile deals its bite damage, knocks the creature [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]], and maintains the grapple. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lycanthrope/Werecrocodile|werecrocodile]] has it.
