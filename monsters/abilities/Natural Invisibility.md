# Natural Invisibility

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “natural invisibility”, on the Special Qualities line.

This ability is constant—the creature remains [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] at all times, even when attacking. As this ability is inherent, it is not subject to the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility Purge|invisibility purge]]_ spell.
