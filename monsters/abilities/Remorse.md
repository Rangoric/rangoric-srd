# Remorse

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “remorse”, on the Special Qualities line.

Whenever a forlarren kills a living creature, its remorse attacks it at +5 vs Will, and a miss avoids becoming overwhelmed. On a hit, the forlarren becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d6 rounds. This is a mind-affecting effect. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Forlarren|forlarren]] has it.
