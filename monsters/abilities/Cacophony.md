# Cacophony

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “cacophony”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature's noises disrupt spellcasting; casting within 100 feet of the creature requires a concentration check (DC 15 + the level of the spell being cast). All other concentration checks and Perception checks involving hearing made inside the aura have their DCs increased by 5. The creature can begin or end this ability as a free action. This is a sonic effect. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Seaweed Siren|seaweed siren]] has it.
