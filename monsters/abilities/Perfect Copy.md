# Perfect Copy

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “perfect copy”, on the Special Qualities line.

When a soulsliver uses change shape, it can assume the appearance of a specific individual. However, it is always the mirror image of the person it copies, which might give away that something is wrong. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Soulsliver|soulsliver]] has it.
