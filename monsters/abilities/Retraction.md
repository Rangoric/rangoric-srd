# Retraction

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “retraction”, on the Special Qualities line.

The creature can pull its fleshy parts into its shell as a swift action, increasing its natural armor bonus by the amount its entry gives, but it cannot move or attack while retracted. It can return to normal as a free action. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Flail Snail|flail snail]] has it.
