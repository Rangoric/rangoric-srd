# Explode

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “explode”, on the Special Attacks line.

One round after a jack-o'-lantern is reduced to 0 or fewer hit points, it explodes in a burst of flaming spores. Creatures within 10 feet of an exploding jack-o'-lantern are attacked at +2 vs Reflex, and on a hit take 2d6 points of fire damage and are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. A miss halves the damage and negates the stun. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Jack-o’-Lantern|jack-o’-lantern]] has it.
