# Leviathan's Call

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “leviathan's call”, on the Special Attacks line.

The tone of a bakekujira's eerie song varies from that of a wistful memory to a burning hatred. As a standard action, a bakekujira can sing. Living creatures within a 120-foot radius of a singing bakekujira are attacked at +18 vs Will, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] (bakekujira's choice) for 1 round. Any creature the call misses cannot be affected by the same bakekujira's leviathan's call ability for 24 hours. This is a sonic mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bakekujira|bakekujira]] has it.
