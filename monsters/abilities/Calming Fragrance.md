# Calming Fragrance

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “calming fragrance”, on the Special Qualities line.

A supernaturally sweet perfume that calms the nerves and blunts aggression constantly surrounds an alraune to a radius of 60 feet. The fragrance attacks the Will of any creature in this area of effect at +14 at the start of its turn, and on a hit it falls under the effects of calm emotions for 1 round. Creatures that could be attracted to the alraune's current apparent gender take a –2 penalty to their Will Defense against it, while all other creatures gain a +2 bonus to theirs. This is a mind-affecting effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Alraune|alraune]] has it.
