# Digestive Spew

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “digestive spew”, on the Special Attacks line.

In order to ingest food, a grothlut must first regurgitate its digestive liquids upon its victim. As a standard action, it can spit these liquids on a creature within 5 feet. This spew deals 3d6 acid damage (+5 vs Reflex; a miss halves). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fleshwarp/Grothlut|grothlut]] has it.
