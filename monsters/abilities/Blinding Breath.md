# Blinding Breath

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “blinding breath”, on the Special Attacks line.

Once every 1d4 rounds, a gholdako can exhale its noxious breath in a 20-foot cone, attacking any creatures in the affected area at +11 vs Fortitude and permanently blinding those it hits. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Gholdako|gholdako]] has it.
