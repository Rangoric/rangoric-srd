# Weep

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “weep”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, a lampad can unsettle those near her when she cries. Any creature within 30 feet who can hear a lampad weeping is attacked at +8 vs Will, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]]. This ability can't cause a creature to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]]. This is a mind-affecting fear effect that relies on audible components. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymph/Lampad|lampad]] has it.
