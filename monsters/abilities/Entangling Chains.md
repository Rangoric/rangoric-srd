# Entangling Chains

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “entangling chains”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can control the six chains that hang from its body as if they were its own limbs. As a standard action, it can cause these chains to snake outward to a radius of 30 feet. All creatures in this area take 10d6 points of slashing damage and become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]]—the chains attack at +10 vs Reflex, and a miss halves the damage and negates the entangled condition. An entangled creature can escape with a DC 20 Reflex check (1d20 + Reflex save bonus) or a DC 30 Escape Artist check made as a full-round action. The chains can also be sundered (hardness 10, hp 20, Break DC 28). The creature creates these chains from its own body—destroyed chains regrow in 24 hours. The attack bonus is Dexterity-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Thrasfyr|thrasfyr]] has it.
