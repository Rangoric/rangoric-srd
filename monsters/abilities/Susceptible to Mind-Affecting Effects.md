# Susceptible to Mind-Affecting Effects

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “susceptible to mind-affecting effects”, on the Special Qualities line.

Unlike most constructs, the creature is not immune to mind-affecting effects: the weakened conviction of the soul bound into it makes it susceptible to them. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/soulbound/Soulbound Doll|soulbound doll]], the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/soulbound/Soulbound Mannequin|soulbound mannequin]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/soulbound/Soulbound Shell|soulbound shell]] have it.
