# Immunity

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “Immune acid, fire, paralysis”, on the Special Qualities line.

A creature with immunities takes no damage from listed sources. Immunities can also apply to afflictions, conditions, spells (based on school, level, or the defense it attacks), and other effects. A creature that is immune does not suffer from these effects, or any secondary effects that are triggered due to an immune effect.
