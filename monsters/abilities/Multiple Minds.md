# Multiple Minds

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “multiple minds”, on the Special Qualities line.

A jotund troll has nine different minds that are in constant communication with each other. The resulting jumble of tangled thoughts grants the troll a +4 racial bonus on Will Defense against mind-affecting effects. In addition, whenever something attacks a jotund troll's Will, the attacker rolls twice and takes the worse of the two results. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Jotund Troll|jotund troll]] has it.
