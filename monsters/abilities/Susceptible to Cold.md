# Susceptible to Cold

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “susceptible to cold”, on the Special Qualities line.

Magical cold stuns a mihstu rather than damaging it. If the creature fails its save against a magical cold effect, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round and then [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for an additional 1d4 rounds. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Mihstu|mihstu]] has it.
