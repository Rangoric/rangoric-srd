# Nature's Pacifism

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “nature's pacifism”, on the Special Qualities line.

Animals and plant creatures do not willingly attack a movanic deva, though they can be forced to do so by magic. If the deva attacks a plant or animal, its protection against that creature ends. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/angel/Angel, Movanic Deva|angel, movanic deva]] has it.
