# Strength Damage (Urdefhan)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “strength damage”, on the Special Attacks line.

An urdefhan's bite drains vitality, turning the skin and muscle around the wound transparent and, if it hits the target at +4 vs Fortitude, causing 2 points of Strength damage. The flesh remains transparent until the Strength damage is healed, but this does not have any other effects. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Urdefhan|urdefhan]] has it.
