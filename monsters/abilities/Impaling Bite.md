# Impaling Bite

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block*: “impaling bite”, on the Special Attacks line.

The creature can make a bite attack against any creature it is grappling. If this attack hits, it is automatically treated as a critical threat. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Grootslang|grootslang]] has it.
