# Shadow Traveler

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “shadow traveler”, on the Special Qualities line.

When a kyton with this ability uses plane shift to travel to the Plane of Shadow, it arrives at its intended destination with complete accuracy. When it uses shadow walk, it moves at 100 miles per hour. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Eremite Kyton|eremite kyton]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Ostiarius Kyton|ostiarius kyton]] have it.
