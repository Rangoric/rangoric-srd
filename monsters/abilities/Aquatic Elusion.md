# Aquatic Elusion

*[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block*: “aquatic elusion”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a standard action, the creature, if fully immersed in water, can teleport to another body of water without error. This ability functions like tree stride, but the creature can exit from any body of water within 1 mile so long as both the entry and exit are wide enough for the creature to enter. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Grootslang|grootslang]] has it.
