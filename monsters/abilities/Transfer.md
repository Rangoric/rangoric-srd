# Transfer

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “transfer”, on the Special Attacks line.

A bodythief can transfer a creature grappled with a tentacle to its mouth with a move action and a successful grapple combat maneuver check. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bodythief|bodythief]] has it.
