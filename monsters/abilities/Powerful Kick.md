# Powerful Kick

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “powerful kick”, on the Special Attacks line.

As a standard action, the clockwork steed can make two hoof attacks with its rear hooves; if both hit, it can perform an awesome blow combat maneuver as the Awesome Blow feat. A clockwork steed gains a +4 racial bonus on this combat maneuver check. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/clockwork/Clockwork Steed|clockwork steed]] has it.
