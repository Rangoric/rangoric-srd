# Deepest Fear

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “deepest fear”, on the Special Qualities line.

A bogeyman is cloaked in a 30-foot aura of fear. This aura manifests as a shifting haze of images that reflect the viewer's deepest fears. The first time it ends its turn within the aura, the aura attacks a creature at +15 vs Will, and on a hit it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for as long as it stays within the aura. If the aura misses the creature, it cannot be affected again by the aura for another 24 hours. This is a fear effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bogeyman|bogeyman]] has it.
