# Quill Barrage

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “quill barrage”, on the Special Attacks line.

Three times per day, a peluda can fire dozens of spearlike barbs in all directions. All creatures within 15 feet take 6d6 points of piercing damage (+10 vs Reflex; a miss halves) and are subject to its poison quills. The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Peluda|peluda]] has it.
