# Elusive

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “elusive”, on the Special Qualities line.

As a full-round action while in water, the creature can move up to its run speed (200 feet) without leaving any trace of its passing (identical in effect to pass without trace). While doing so it gains a +40 circumstance bonus on its [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] check. In addition, except when in combat, the creature is considered to be under the effects of a nondetection spell. Both of these effects function at caster level 20th and cannot be dispelled. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Deep Sea Serpent|deep sea serpent]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Water Orm|water orm]] have it.
