# Infused Weapons (Aghasura)

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Supernatural]] (Su). In a stat block_: “infused weapons”, on the Special Attacks line.

Weapons an aghasura wields are considered to be magic for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. In addition, such weapons gain the ability to deliver the aghasura's poison on a successful attack. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/asuras/Aghasura|aghasura]] has it.
