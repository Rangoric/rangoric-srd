# Rough Hide

_[[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^special-abilities|Extraordinary]] (Ex). In a stat block_: “rough hide”, on the Special Qualities line.

Ghawwas have rough hides studded with jagged barbs and spiny protrusions. Any creature striking a ghawwas with a natural weapon or an unarmed strike takes 1d6 points of slashing and piercing damage. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Ghawwas|ghawwas]] has it.
