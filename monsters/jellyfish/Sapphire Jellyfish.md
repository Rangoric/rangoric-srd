Like other giant jellyfish, a sapphire jellyfish is an active hunter. It stores powerful electrical charges in its domelike bell, which is 16 feet in diameter. Its sensory tentacles can trail for twice that length, but the dangerous toxic tentacles are positioned within about 20 feet of the creature's bell.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Electricity Blast|Electricity Blast]] (Ex)**: Once every 1d6 rounds as a standard action, a sapphire jellyfish can discharge a 20-foot-radius blast of electricity, dealing 8d6 points of electricity damage (+13 vs Reflex; a miss halves). Any creature that takes damage from this electricity is also attacked at +13 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d4 rounds. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Sapphire Jellyfish Venom|Sapphire Jellyfish Venom]]: Tentacle—injury; _attack_ +13 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d4 Dex and 1d4 Con; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
