A frost giant hunter is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Frost Giant|frost giant]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the frost giant rangers. Frost giant rangers hunt big game to feed their tribes, and take point on raids.

A frost giant hunter speaks Common and Giant.
