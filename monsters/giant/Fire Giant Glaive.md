A fire giant glaive is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Fire Giant|fire giant]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/fighter/Polearm Master (Archetype)|polearm master]] archetype), part of the fire giant vanguard. When the fire giants unleash their savagery, the glaives and the strongarms are often in the lead.

A fire giant glaive speaks Common and Giant.
