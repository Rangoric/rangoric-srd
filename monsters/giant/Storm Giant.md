Very rarely, storm giants have violet skin. Violet-skinned storm giants have deep violet or blue-black hair with silvery gray or purple eyes. Adults are about 21 feet tall and weigh about 12,000 pounds. Storm giants can live to be 600 years old. Storm giants’ garb is usually a short, loose tunic belted at the waist, sandals or bare feet, and a headband. They wear a few pieces of simple but finely crafted jewelry, anklets (favored by barefoot giants), rings, or circlets being most common. They live quiet, reflective lives and spend their time musing about the world, composing and playing music, and tilling their land or gathering food.

## Combat

Storm giants use weapons and spell-like abilities instead of throwing rocks. Their composite longbows have a range increment of 180 feet.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 20th.

2/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]].  

Caster level 15th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning|call lightning]] (DC 15), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]] (DC 18).   
**  
Freedom of Movement (Su)**: Storm giants have a continuous [[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]] ability as the spell (caster level 20th). The effect can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispelled]], but the storm giant can create it again on its next turn as a free action.

**Water Breathing (Ex)**: Storm giants can breathe underwater indefinitely and can freely use their spell-like abilities while submerged.

**Skills**: Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]], a storm giant is better at swimming. \*Storm giants ignore all weight penalties for gear carried when swimming.

## Storm Giant Characters

About 20% of adult storm giants are sorcerers or clerics. A storm giant cleric has access to two of the following domains: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Good Domain|Good]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Protection Domain|Protection]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War]].
