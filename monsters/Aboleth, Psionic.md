# Aboleth, Psionic

**Huge** [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^aberration|Aberration]] ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^aquatic|Aquatic]], Psionic)  
**Special Attacks:** Psi-like abilities, slime  
**Feats:** [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats All|Combat Manifestation]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Iron Will|Iron Will]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Perception)  

#### Combat

A psionic aboleth does not possess spell-like abilities or the enslave ability of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Aboleth|standard aboleth]]. Instead, it has psi-like abilities, including psionic dominate.  

Psi-Like Abilities: At will - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Disable|disable]] (30-ft. cone, 12 HD, DC 20\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/False Sensory Input|false sensory input]] (five targets, DC 16\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Mental Disruption|mental disruption]] (20-ft. radius, DC 18\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Mindlink|mindlink]] (unwilling, nine targets, DC 14\*); 3/day - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Ego Whip|ego whip]] (ML 7th, 2d4, DC 17\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Id Insinuation|id insinuation]] (ML 7th, three targets, DC 17\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Dominate, Psionic|psionic dominate]] (any target, 24 hours, DC 20\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Thought Shield|thought shield]] (power resistance 21, 3 rounds\*); 1/day - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Modify Memory, Psionic|psionic modify memory]] (DC 17), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Remote Viewing|remote viewing]] (DC 17), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Wall of Ectoplasm|wall of ectoplasm]] (DC 17).

Manifester level 13th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.

\*Includes augmentation for the aboleth’s manifester level.
