A shape of fire’s natural weapons are treated as epic for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].

#### Combat

Blazefire (Su): Living creatures taking Damage from a shape of fire’s incorporeal touch attack find themselves ignited with blazefire; white-hot, lambent flames cascade over their bodies, and the flames attack them at +27 vs Fortitude, and on a hit they permanently lose 10 hit points. The flames attack the opponent again every round for the next 6 rounds (7 rounds total), and each hit permanently drains 10 more hit points. The shape of fire heals the same amount of Damage whenever a creature is drained, gaining any excess hit points as temporary hit points. These temporary hit points last a maximum of 1 hour. If the opponent is slain by blazefire, only blackened ash remains of the victim. Hit points lost to the blazefire never heal naturally and cannot be magically restored-they are gone for good. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

Create Spawn (Su): Any humanoid slain by a shape of fire becomes a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Lavawight|lavawight]] in 1d4 rounds. Lavawights are under the command of the shape of fire that created them and remain enslaved until its death. They do not possess any of the abilities they had in life.

Spell-Like Abilities: Caster level 24th; attack bonus +10 + spell level. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Incendiary Cloud|incendiary cloud]].

Incorporeal Traits: Can be harmed only by other incorporeal creatures, magic weapons, or magic, with a 50% chance to ignore any Damage from a corporeal source. Can pass through solid objects at will, and own attacks pass through armor. Always moves silently.

Undead Traits: Immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]], sleep, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralysis]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]], death, effects, necromantic effects, mind-affecting effects, and any effect that attacks Fortitude unless it also works on objects. Not subject to critical hits, subdual damage, ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]], ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|energy drain]]. Negative energy heals. Not at risk of death from massive damage, but destroyed at 0 hit points or less. Darkvision 60 ft. Cannot be raised; resurrection works only if creature is willing.

Fire Subtype: Immunity to fire. [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Vulnerabilities|Vulnerability to cold]] (takes half again as much (+50%) damage as normal from cold, regardless of whether the effect attacks a defense, or whether that attack hits or misses).

Heat Aura (Su): A 10-foot-radius spread heat aura surrounds a shape of fire. All creatures of the fire subtype in the area (including the shape of fire) are treated as if having [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Channel Resistance|turn resistance]] +6 (if undead) and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]] 10. Creatures subject to fire Damage take 2d10 points of fire Damage each round they remain within the heat aura.
