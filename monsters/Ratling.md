# Ratling

- Tiny [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^magical-beast|Magical Beast]]
- **Hit Dice**: 3d10+3 (19 hp)
- **Initiative**: +2
- **Speed**: 30 ft. (6 squares), burrow 10 ft., climb 20 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Armor Class**: 16 (+2 Dex, +1 dodge, +1 natural, +2 size), touch 15, flat-footed 13
- **Base Attack**: +3; **CMB**: +3; **CMD**: 12 (16 vs. trip)
- **Attack**: Bite +3 melee (1d3–2 plus bleed 1)
- **Full Attack**: Bite +3 melee (1d3–2 plus bleed 1)
- **Space/Reach**: 2-1/2 ft./0 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: Sneak attack +1d6, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|spell-like abilities]]
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft., detect magic, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], evasion, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scroll Use|scroll use]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Immunity|immunity]] to disease and poison
- **Defenses**: Fort 14, Ref 15, Will 11
- **Abilities**: Str 6, Dex 15, Con 13, Int 12, Wis 10, Cha 13
- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] +6, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +6, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] +16, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] +4
- **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]]

- **Treasure**: Standard
- **Alignment**: Usually chaotic evil
- **Advancement**: —
- **Level Adjustment**: —

The ratling is a hideous amalgamation of rat and human: a long-haired rodent with handlike front paws and a face reminiscent of the face of a leering old man. Within the ratling's humanoid mouth are long, yellow incisors more akin to those one might find in the jaws of a rat.

Ratlings are carnivores. While they can subsist on grubs, other rodents, and carrion, they prefer living food and fresh blood, particularly that of humanoid children and elderly folk. They also have a sense for magic, and lurk around temples and universities searching for scraps of knowledge or overlooked books and scrolls to steal.

Ratlings associate with common rats and even mate with them, producing anything from large, aggressive rats (often with vestigial humanlike features or other sickening deformities) to infant ratlings to deformed rats. In a mixed litter, the infant ratling usually kills and eats its siblings, then arranges the dismembered and disemboweled corpses in semi-occult patterns.

Elder ratlings often gain the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Advanced Creature|advanced simple template]] or levels in [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle|oracle]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|witch]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (a rare few gain [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] levels, especially if they associate with a thieves' guild or wererats). These elders often become leaders of their own kind, creating cabals organized like the universities they frequently inhabit. If the ratling can gain a familiar, it usually chooses a rat—an association that often brings with it more than mere supernatural companionship. Ratlings can never select another ratling as a familiar.

A chaotic evil spellcaster can gain a ratling as a familiar at 7th level by taking the Improved Familiar feat. A ratling familiar gains _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]]_ once per week as a spell-like ability, which it can use on its master's behalf. The master usually allows the familiar to drink her blood at least once per week.

A ratling measures just over 2 feet long (although half that length is its long, ratty tail) and weighs 10 pounds.

Ratlings speak Aklo.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 6th; concentration +7): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ (rodents only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]_; 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]_ (+2 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_ (self only); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Swarm|summon swarm]]_ (rat swarm only); 1/week—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]]_ (only when serving as a familiar; 6 questions, CL 12th).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scroll Use|Scroll Use]] (Ex)**: A ratling can cast spells from any magic scroll as if it had the spell on its spell list.
