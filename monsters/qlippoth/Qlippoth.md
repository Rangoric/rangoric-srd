# Qlippoth

Qlippoth are chaotic evil outsiders from the deepest reaches of the Abyss. They speak Abyssal.

### Combat

Qlippoth have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^qlippoth|qlippoth subtype]]: immunity to cold, mind-affecting effects, and poison, resistance to acid, electricity, and fire, telepathy, and a horrific appearance whose effect depends on the kind of qlippoth.
