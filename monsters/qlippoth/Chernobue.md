The thick, orange poison injected by a chernobue is semi-alive. As soon as the poisoned victim is cured of the poison (by making a save, being targeted with an effect like neutralize poison, or enduring the full duration of the poison), the orange fluid bursts from the victim's body, causing 1d6 Charisma damage and rendering the victim [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] for 2d6 rounds unless he makes a final DC 23 Fortitude save.

The chernobue is a living manifestation of the vile fecundity of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/The Abyss|Abyss]]—a monstrous, alien pregnancy made flesh. By infecting creatures with the Abyssal taint they carry, they spread pain and misfortune wherever they flop and writhe—and with their _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ ability, they are ready to spread their filth throughout the multiverse. A chernobue is 13 feet long and weighs 500 pounds.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th; concentration +16): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Air Walk|air walk]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Sight|arcane sight]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]]_ (+8 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_; 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_ (+8 vs Will), quickened _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Law|protection from law]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ (+11 vs Will).

**Aura of Misfortune (Su)**: A chernobue radiates an aura of evil malaise to a radius of 30 feet. All lawful or good creatures in this area take a –1 penalty on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls. Lawful good creatures take a –3 penalty and upon first entering the aura are attacked at +10 vs Fortitude, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for as long as they remain in the area. This ability is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Horrific Appearance|Horrific Appearance]] (Su)**: Creatures that succumb to a chernobue's horrific appearance become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] with disgust for 2d6 rounds; the appearance attacks a paralyzed creature again each round, and a miss ends the effect, provided he is no longer aware of the chernobue or within 30 feet of it.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Light Vulnerability|Light Vulnerability]] (Ex)**: A chernobue within an area of bright light takes 1 point of Constitution damage per minute it remains in the area.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Su)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Chernobue Venom|Chernobue Venom]]: Bite—injury; _attack_ +13 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d2 Con drain; _cure_ 1 miss. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
