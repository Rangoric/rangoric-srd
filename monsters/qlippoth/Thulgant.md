The dreaded thulgant is among the most dangerous of the qlippoth, for it supports an array of deadly and painful physical attacks with a wide range of potent magical powers. Born from the cannibalistic orgies of augnagar qlippoth, each thulgant exists for one purpose only—the eradication of all demons from the [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/The Abyss|Abyss]].

Yet thulgants do not spend all of their lives hunting and destroying demons. They rule horrific hives deep in the [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/The Abyss|Abyss]] populated by all manner of hideous minions, many of which are bound into servitude via _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Binding|binding]]_ spells. These qlippoth are fond of decorating their lairs with [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] or enstasised victims of great power—the more powerful the victims, the greater the prestige held by the thulgant.

Thulgants speak Abyssal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 18th; concentration +25): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cloak of Chaos|cloak of chaos]]_ (DC 25), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_ (DC 22); 3/day—quickened _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]_ (DC 23), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Word of Chaos|word of chaos]]_ (DC 24); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Binding|binding]]_ (DC 25), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ (DC 24), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinetic Sphere|telekinetic sphere]]_ (DC 25), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Temporal Stasis|temporal stasis]]_ (DC 25).

**Ability Drain (Su)**: A thulgant's stingers each drain a different ability score on a hit. One stinger drains 1d4 points of Strength, another drains 1d4 points of Dexterity, and the third drains 1d4 points of Charisma. Any sting's drain is negated by a DC 29 Fortitude save. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Demon Hunter (Thulgant)|Demon Hunter]] (Ex)**: A thulgant gains a +10 racial bonus on caster level checks to penetrate the spell resistance of any demon. Its attacks are treated as cold iron and good against demons.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Horrific Appearance|Horrific Appearance]] (Su)**: Creatures that succumb to a thulgant's horrific appearance are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d4 rounds and take 1d6 points of Wisdom damage.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Savage Stingers|Savage Stingers]] (Ex)**: If a thulgant hits a single target with all three stings in the same round, it tears through the victim's body, dealing an extra 3d6+12 points of damage and draining an additional 2 ability points from all six of the victim's ability scores. A single DC 29 Fortitude save negates all of this additional ability drain. The save DC is Constitution-based.
