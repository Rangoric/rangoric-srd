A kobold bomber is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kobolds/Kobold|kobold]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/alchemist/Alchemical Trapper (Archetype)|alchemical trapper]] archetype), one of the kobold tricksters. Its bombs per day include the [[rangoric-srd/races/class-bonuses/Kobold Alchemist (Class Bonus)|kobold alchemist class bonus]]. Kobold tricksters are experts at crafting deadly traps—both magical and mundane—and at striking from concealment while their enemies are distracted by the traps' effects.

A kobold bomber speaks Common, Draconic, Gnome, and Goblin.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The kobold uses her mutagen and extracts to strengthen her defense, and throws bombs at her enemies.

**Alchemist extracts prepared** (CL 2nd): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Adjuring Step|adjuring step]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_.
