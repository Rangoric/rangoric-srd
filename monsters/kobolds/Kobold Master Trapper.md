A kobold master trapper is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kobolds/Kobold|kobold]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the kobold tricksters. Its danger sense includes the [[rangoric-srd/races/class-bonuses/Kobold Rogue (Class Bonus)|kobold rogue class bonus]]. Kobold tricksters are experts at crafting deadly traps—both magical and mundane—and at striking from concealment while their enemies are distracted by the traps' effects.

A kobold master trapper speaks Draconic.

### Combat

**During Combat**: If the master trapper's enemies aren't near her traps, she fires arrows or uses her necklace of fireballs.
