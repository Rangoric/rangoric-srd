A serpentfolk high priest is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/serpentfolk/Serpentfolk|serpentfolk]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]]. Serpentfolk high priests are the leaders of their people.

A serpentfolk high priest speaks Aklo, Common, Draconic, and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: A high priest prepares for battle by casting divine power and freedom of movement, then uses its scythe of evil ability to enhance its weapon.
