A serpentfolk illusionist is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/serpentfolk/Serpentfolk|serpentfolk]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]]. Serpentfolk cherish knowledge—and hence know how valuable obscuring the truth can be.

A serpentfolk illusionist has greatly enhanced its racial gifts for deception with decades of intensive arcane study. On the surface world, it is often the leader of a group of serpentfolk or a cult of lesser reptilian humanoids.

A serpentfolk illusionist speaks Abyssal, Aklo, Common, Draconic, Elven, and Undercommon.

### Combat

**During Combat**: A serpentfolk illusionist seeks to deceive opponents with spells until it can debilitate them with blindness/deafness, color spray, or a venomous bite enhanced with pernicious poison. Otherwise, it attacks with its wand or uses suggestion and dominate person to turn enemies into allies.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 4th; concentration +8): At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]* (DC 15, humanoid form only), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ventriloquism|ventriloquism]]* (DC 15); 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Person|dominate person]]* (DC 19), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Image|major image]]* (DC 17), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]* (DC 17).
