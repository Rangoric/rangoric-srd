The statistics presented here can describe any arboreal monkey that is no bigger than a housecat, such as a colobus or capuchin.

#### Combat

Monkeys generally flee into the safety of the trees, but if cornered can fight ferociously.

**Skills**: Monkeys have a +8 racial bonus on Acrobatics checks. Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|climb speed]], monkeys are better at climbing. They use their Dexterity modifier instead of their Strength modifier for Athletics checks to climb.
