# Dragonne

- Large [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^magical-beast|Magical Beast]]
- **Hit Dice**: 9d10+27 (76 hp)
- **Initiative**: +7
- **Speed**: 40 ft. (8 squares), fly 30 ft. (poor)
- **Armor Class**: 20 (+3 Dex, +8 natural, –1 size), touch 12, flat-footed 17
- **Base Attack**: +9; **CMB**: +15; **CMD**: 28 (32 vs. trip)
- **Attack**: Bite +13 melee (1d8+5) or claw +13 melee (1d6+5)
- **Full Attack**: Bite +13 melee (1d8+5) and 2 claws +13 melee (1d6+5)
- **Space/Reach**: 10 ft./10 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Pounce|Pounce]], roar
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]]
- **Defenses**: Fort 19, Ref 19, Will 14
- **Abilities**: Str 21, Dex 17, Con 17, Int 6, Wis 12, Cha 12
- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] +5, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +12
- **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flyby Attack|Flyby Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]]
- **Environment**: Temperate deserts
- **Organization**: Solitary, pair, or pride (5–10)
- **Challenge Rating**: 7
- **Treasure**: Standard
- **Alignment**: Usually neutral
- **Advancement**: 10–12 HD (Large); 13–27 HD (Huge)
- **Level Adjustment**: +4 (cohort)

Possessing the savage instincts of lions with the cunning of brass dragons, dragonnes combine the fiercest features of these noble creatures into predators both awe-inspiring and deadly.

The origin of dragonnes generates endless speculation. The odds of these creatures being direct crossbreeds of dragons and lions are quite remote, for numerous reasons: the two creatures rarely share the same territories; few creatures as clever as brass dragons would choose to mate with simple lions; and dragonne abilities differ significantly from those of metallic dragons. While otherworldly breeding experiments and magical mishaps remain possibilities, few satisfying explanations make themselves apparent.

Although many creatures rightly fear these ferocious hunters, few dragonnes are blatantly evil—most are just highly territorial and seek to defend their homes and hunting grounds from interlopers. Creatures that draw too close to a dragonne's lair are typically met by the resident's fearsome roar, followed by its claws and fangs if this warning is ignored. Those who attempt to settle in a dragonne's territory find themselves harassed constantly until they decide to leave or the dragonne is slain. These intimidating predators spend the majority of their time on the ground, even when in combat, since their wings prove somewhat ungainly. They typically search for prey and intruders from the air, then land nearby to charge and pounce.

Despite their deadliness, dragonnes form strong bonds with those they consider members of their pride, sometimes even adopting creatures of other races. A character with the Leadership feat can take a dragonne as a cohort. Such characters must have an effective leadership level of 10th. Most dragonne cohorts gain levels in [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]].

A dragonne measures between 10 and 12 feet long and weighs up to 1,200 pounds.

A dragonne possesses huge claws and fangs, and large eyes, usually the color of its scales.

Dragonnes speak Draconic.

### Combat

A dragonne’s wings are useful only for short flights, carrying the creature for 10 to 30 minutes at a time. Nevertheless, it uses its wings effectively in battle. If opponents attempt to charge or encircle it, the dragonne simply takes to the air and finds a more defensible position.

**Roar (Su)**: A dragonne can unleash a devastating roar every 1d4 rounds as a standard action. All creatures except dragonnes within 120 feet are attacked at +7 vs Fortitude, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]]. Those within 30 feet it hits are also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] for 2d4 rounds. This is a sonic effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
