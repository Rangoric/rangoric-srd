A drow house guard is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drow/Drow, 1st-Level Warrior|drow]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the drow house soldiers. A house guard is better trained and equipped than a common warrior defending a drow city or caravan.

A drow house guard speaks Elven and Undercommon.

### Combat

A drow house guard’s poisoned weapons carry [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Drow Poison|drow poison]] (+3 vs Fortitude).

**During Combat**: The house guard uses alchemical weapons and poison against spellcasters.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 3rd; concentration +3): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_.
