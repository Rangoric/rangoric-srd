A drow poisoner is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drow/Drow, 1st-Level Warrior|drow]] with 12 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]]. This drow makes use of deadly venoms and alchemy.

A drow poisoner speaks Elven and Undercommon.

### Combat

A drow poisoner’s poisoned weapons carry [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Drow Poison|drow poison]] (Fortitude DC 13).

**Before Combat**: The poisoner drinks her Dexterity mutagen and her extracts of anticipate peril, countless eyes, and greater invisibility.

**Base Statistics**: Without the Dexterity mutagen, anticipate peril, and countless eyes, the drow's statistics are **Init** +7; **Senses** no all-around vision; **AC** 21, touch 14, flat-footed 18; **Ref** +12; **Will** +6; **Ranged** masterwork hand crossbow +13 (1d4/19–20 plus poison), or bomb +12/+7 (6d6+5 fire); **Dex** 17; **Wis** 12; **CMD** 23; **Skills** Acrobatics +11, Engineering +17, Fieldcraft +10, Medicine +10, Perception +18, Stealth +14.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th; concentration +12): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_.

**Alchemist extracts prepared** (CL 12th): 4th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|cure critical wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fluid Form|fluid form]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life, Greater|greater false life]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_; 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Countless Eyes|countless eyes]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eruptive Pustules|eruptive pustules]]_ (DC 18), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alchemical Allocation|alchemical allocation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_ (2), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch Injection|touch injection]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vomit Swarm|vomit swarm]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Anticipate Peril|anticipate peril]]_ (DC 16), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bomber’s Eye|bomber's eye]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Negate Aroma|negate aroma]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polypurpose Panacea|polypurpose panacea]]_ (DC 16), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_ (2).
