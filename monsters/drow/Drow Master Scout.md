A drow master scout is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drow/Drow, 1st-Level Warrior|drow]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/rogue/Sniper (Rogue Archetype)|sniper]] archetype), one of the drow scouts. Scouts are cunning and resourceful, but still must prove themselves to higher-ranked drow before being considered anything but expendable.

A drow master scout speaks Common, Dwarven, Elven, and Undercommon.

### Combat

A drow master scout’s poisoned weapons carry [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Drow Poison|drow poison]] (Fortitude DC 13).

**During Combat**: A drow master scout focuses on disabling enemies—especially healers—with ranged attacks using sneak attacks, poison, and energy arrows.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 8th, concentration +8): 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*.
