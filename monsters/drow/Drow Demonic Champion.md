A drow demonic champion is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drow/Drow, 1st-Level Warrior|drow]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladin]]. Chosen by the most powerful nobles to serve as generals and bodyguards, demonic champions embody all of the brutal, cruel, and murderous impulses instilled in the drow race and valued by their society.

A demonic champion has been tested by the powers of a chaotic evil-aligned plane and emerged stronger for it. Her soul is scarred by centuries of depraved acts, and she is irredeemable in her evil—something she is happy to prove. Demonic champions enjoy many creature comforts within drow society thanks to the spoils they claim in war, but their bloodlust motivates them to repeatedly return to battle.

A drow demonic champion speaks Elven and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The demonic champion casts invisibility and protection from good on herself, then uses her scroll of defile armor on her full plate. She prefers to use her fiendish boon ability to grant her scimitar the anarchic, flaming, unholy, or vicious weapon special ability.

**During Combat**: The demonic champion uses smite good and Channel Smite with her scimitar attacks to harm foes, or uses her cruelties and touch of corruption to disable opponents. She casts silence to interfere with spellcasters employing ranged spells against her.

**Antipaladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +13): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 10th; concentration +3): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_.

**Antipaladin spells prepared** (CL 7th; concentration +10): 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Litany of Sight|litany of sight]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ (+5 vs Will); 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Litany of Sloth|litany of sloth]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Murderous Command|murderous command]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Good|protection from good]]_.
