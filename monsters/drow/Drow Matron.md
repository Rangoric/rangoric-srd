A drow matron is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drow/Drow, 1st-Level Warrior|drow]] with 15 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/cleric/Demonic Apostle (Archetype)|demonic apostle]] archetype). Born with unusual powers, the matron has survived coup attempts by countless rivals to remain the head of her house.

A drow matron speaks Abyssal, Common, Elven, and Undercommon.
