Swan maidens are fey shapechangers who vow to protect unspoiled wilds from the encroachment of civilization or evil. They live in small flocks along secluded lakeshores. Because stealing a swan maiden's cloak robs her of her shapechanging ability, most maidens avoid humanoids and take up armor and weapons to defend themselves.

Swan maidens speak Common and Sylvan.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 10th; concentration +12): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_ (DC 16), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deep Slumber|deep slumber]]_ (DC 15), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_ (DC 13), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glitterdust|glitterdust]]_ (DC 14), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Image|major image]]_ (DC 15).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Feather Cloak|Feather Cloak]] (Su)**: Without her feather cloak, a swan maiden can't use her change shape ability.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Transformation Ritual|Transformation Ritual]] (Su)**: A swan maiden can transform a willing good female humanoid into a swan maiden via a ritual that takes 24 hours. The humanoid loses her class and racial abilities.
