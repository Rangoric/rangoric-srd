Couatls are servants of lawful and good deities, though some operate independently of any greater being. Respected and admired for their wisdom and beauty, they try to steer mortals onto the right path and use their powers to fight evil, particularly those known to shift between the planes. Some couatls are viewed as benevolent gods by isolated societies, and while most couatls cringe at the thought of pretending to be a god, they allow such misconceptions to continue since they allow the couatls to guide and coax these societies onto paths of peace and cooperation with their neighbors. A couatl is about 12 feet long, with a wingspan of about 15 feet. It weighs 1,800 pounds.

As native outsiders, couatls must eat. They prefer the same foods as true snakes, such as mammals and birds, though they have been known to eat evil humanoids.

As they would rather spend their time promoting their agenda than hunting, couatls appreciate offers of food, particularly small boars and large game fowl.

A couatl sometimes shows its favor to an adventurer or party that has done it a service by gifting the group with 1d4 of its brightly colored feathers. Such a freely given feather, if used as an additional material component, allows a spellcaster to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ to conjure that specific couatl without expending the typical payment of gold or other valuables—provided the couatl approves of the service asked for by the spellcaster.

Couatls speak Celestial, Common, and Draconic, and also have the power of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Telepathy|telepathy]].

### Combat

A couatl uses its [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]] ability on any creature that arouses its suspicions. Since it is highly intelligent, a couatl usually casts spells from a distance before closing. If more than one couatl is involved, they discuss their strategy before a battle.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 9th): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Chaos|detect chaos]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|detect law]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt|ethereal jaunt]]_ (CL 16th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ (+10 vs Will).

**Spells known** (CL 9th): 4th (4/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ (+7 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_; 3rd (7/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_; 2nd (7/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ (+5 vs Will); 1st (7/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Chaos|protection from chaos]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disrupt Undead|disrupt undead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Frost|ray of frost]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]_.

**Spells**: A couatl casts spells as a 9th-level sorcerer, and can cast spells from the cleric list as well as those normally available to a sorcerer. Cleric spells are considered arcane spells for a couatl, meaning that the creature does not need a divine focus to cast them.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: Bite—injury; _attack_ +6 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/minute for 10 minutes; _effect_ 1d4 Str; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
