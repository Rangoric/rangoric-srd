# Snake

Snakes usually are not aggressive and flee when confronted.

**Skills**: Snakes have a +4 racial bonus on Stealth and Perception checks and a +8 racial bonus on Acrobatics checks. Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|climb speed]], a snake is better at climbing. Snakes use either their Strength modifier or Dexterity modifier for Athletics checks to climb, whichever is higher. Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]], a snake is better at swimming.

- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/snakes/Constrictor Snake|Constrictor Snake]]

#### Combat

Constrictor snakes hunt by grabbing prey with their mouths and then squeezing it with their powerful bodies.

**Constrict (Ex)**: On a successful grapple check, a constrictor snake deals 1d3+4 points of damage.

**Improved Grab (Ex)**: To use this ability, a constrictor snake must hit with its bite attack. It can then attempt to start a grapple as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity. If it wins the grapple check, it establishes a hold and can constrict.

Viper snakes rely on their venomous bite to kill prey and defend themselves.
