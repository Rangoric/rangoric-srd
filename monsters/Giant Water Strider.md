A giant water strider is a long-legged insect with the ability to walk on water. It feeds on fish and birds. Water striders use their weak flight only to escape predators or for seasonal migrations to new breeding areas. They are sometimes kept as mounts by coast-dwelling humanoids. A giant water strider lives 1-2 years.

Carrying more than a light load (200 pounds) prevents a giant water strider from using its water skating ability.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Tremorsense|Tremorsense]] (Ex)**: A water strider's tremorsense functions only when the creature is in contact with the water's surface.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Water Skating|Water Skating]] (Ex)**: A water strider can move on the surface of water as if it were on land. A water strider swimming at the water's surface can pull itself onto the water with a successful Athletics check.
