# Alternate Celestial Creature

Celestial creatures dwell in the higher planes, but can be summoned using spells such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_. A celestial creature's quick and rebuild rules are the same.

“Celestial” is a simple template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Simple Templates|Simple Templates]]): it can be added to a creature during play.

**Challenge Rating**: A celestial creature's CR increases by +1 only if the base creature has 5 or more HD.

**Rebuild Rules**: **Senses** gains darkvision 60 ft.; **Defensive Abilities** gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|DR]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Resistance|energy resistance]] as noted on the table; **SR** gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell Resistance|SR]] equal to new CR +5; **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Smite Evil (Template)|smite evil]] 1/day as a swift action (adds Cha bonus on attack rolls and damage bonus equal to HD against evil foes; smite persists until the target is dead or the celestial creature rests).

**Table: Celestial Creature Defenses**

| Hit Dice | Resist Cold, Acid, and Electricity | DR      |
| -------- | ---------------------------------- | ------- |
| 1–4      | 5                                  | —       |
| 5–10     | 10                                 | 5/evil  |
| 11+      | 15                                 | 10/evil |

This is the second version of the celestial creature template, a simple template; the first is [[rangoric-srd/monsters/celestial-creatures/Celestial Creature|Celestial Creature]].
