Nightwalkers are human-shaped horrors that haunt the darkness.

A nightwalker is about 20 feet tall and weighs about 12,000 pounds.

## Combat

Nightwalkers lurk in dark areas where they can almost always surprise the unwary.

A nightwalker’s natural weapons are treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Crush Item (Su)**: A nightwalker can destroy any weapon or item of Large size or smaller (even magic ones, but not artifacts) by picking it up and crushing it between its hands. The nightwalker must make a successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] attempt to grab an item held by an opponent. The item is entitled to a DC 34 Fortitude save to resist destruction. The save DC is Strength-based.

**Evil Gaze (Su)**: Fear, 30 feet. A creature that meets the nightwalker’s gaze must succeed on a DC 24 Will save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] with fear for 1d8 rounds. Whether or not the save is successful, that creature cannot be affected again by the same nightshade’s gaze for 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 21st. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]] (DC 18), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]], see [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]], and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]] (DC 18);  

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]] (DC 18), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]] (DC 19), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]];  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cone of Cold|cone of cold]] (DC 19), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]] (DC 21).  

**Summon Undead (Su)**: A nightwalker can summon undead creatures once per night: 7–12 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Shadow|shadows]], 2–5 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Shadow|greater shadows]], or 1–2 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/wraith/Wraith|dread wraiths]]. The undead arrive in 1d10 rounds and serve for 1 hour or until released.

**Skills**: \*When hiding in a dark area, a nightwalker gains a +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.

## Tactics Round-by-Round

The nightwalker is an exceedingly intelligent foe that makes the best use of all its abilities. It favors using its spell-like abilities to divide and disable its enemies, then closing to melee with opponents it has isolated from their allies.

*Prior to combat*: The nightwalker keeps its [[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]] power active most of the time. It uses [[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]] and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]] to prepare for battle.

*Round 1*: Move to within 30 feet to make use of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^gaze|gaze]] attack and strike with [[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]], coupled with a quickened [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]].

*Round 2*: Hit a spellcaster with [[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]] and another quickened [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]].

*Round 3*: Move up to engage the enemy and attempt to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] an enemy fighter.

*Round 4*: Crush the disarmed weapon (or use [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^gaze|gaze]] attack if disarm attempt failed).

*Round 5*: Full attack against the unarmed foe (or on a nearby spellcaster).
