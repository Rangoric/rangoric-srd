Nightwings are batlike flyers that hunt on the wing.

A nightwing has a wingspan of about 40 feet and weighs about 4,000 pounds.

## Combat

Nightwings prowl the night sky and dive onto their victims. They are all but invisible, detectable only because of the stars they obscure in their passing.

A nightwing’s natural weapons are treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Magic Drain (Su)**: A nightwing can weaken magic armor, weapons, and shields by making a successful touch attack. The targeted item must succeed on a DC 22 Fortitude save or lose 1 point of its enhancement bonus. The save DC is Charisma-based. An item that loses its entire enhancement bonus becomes merely a masterwork item and loses any special abilities (such as flaming) as well. Casting [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Evil|dispel evil]] upon the item reverses the effect of the magic drain, provided this occurs within a number of days after the attack equal to the caster’s level and the caster succeeds on a DC 29 caster level check.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 17th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]] (DC 18), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]] (DC 18);  

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]] (DC 18), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]] (DC 19), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]];  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cone of Cold|cone of cold]] (DC 19), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]] (DC 21).  

**Summon Undead (Su)**: A nightwing can summon undead creatures once per night: 5–12 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Shadow|shadows]], 2–4 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Shadow|greater shadows]], or 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/wraith/Wraith|dread wraith]]. The undead arrive in 1d10 rounds and serve for 1 hour or until released.

**Skills**: \*When hiding in a dark area or flying in a dark sky, a nightwing gains a +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.
