The most powerful of the known types of nightshade is the ravenous nightwave, an unholy personification of the remorseless gluttony of death given the form of a shark the size of the largest whales. Although the nightwave is most at home in the ocean's deeps, it has no need to breathe, and its constant _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]_ spell-like ability allows it to bring ruin above the waves as the need presents itself.

A nightwave is 100 feet long and weighs 200 tons.

Nightwaves speak Abyssal, Common and Infernal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 20th; concentration +27): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Fang|magic fang]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_ (+11 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]_ (+11 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_ (+11 vs Will); 3/day-quickened _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cone of Cold|cone of cold]]_ (+12 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]]_ (+14 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]]_ (+12 vs Will); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster, Mass|mass hold monster]]_ (+16 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ (+14 vs Will), summon (level 9, 1 nightwing), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wail of the Banshee|wail of the banshee]]_ (+16 vs Fortitude).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Blackest Depths|Blackest Depths]] (Su)**: The waters in which a nightwave swims become as chill, dark, and heavy as those in the ocean's deepest reaches. All waters within 60 feet are completely dark (as deeper darkness), and creatures within this radius take 6d6 points of damage (half cold, half bludgeoning) at the end of their turn each round if they remain in the area at this time. It attacks at +21 vs Fortitude, and a miss negates the crushing damage. Incorporeal creatures and creatures with the aquatic or water subtypes native to deep waters do not take this damage, and freedom of movement protects completely against the damage. Any magical light effect within this radius at the beginning of the nightwave's turn is dispelled (treat as greater dispel magic). This effect does not extend out of the water. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Energy Drain|Energy Drain]] (Su)**: A creature that has been swallowed whole by a nightwave gains 2 negative levels each round.
