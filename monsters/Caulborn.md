Caulborn are a race of telepathic prophets and historians who subsist on the psychic energy of others. They wander the planes in search of new facts and concepts to add to the pulsating brain-sacks that serve as their collective memories. When a band of caulborn find a particularly interesting site, they settle down to dwell there for many years until they feel that they have learned all there is to know about the location.

A caulborn is literally a creature of the mind. Its body is self-sustaining, and it exists solely to record and catalogue new thoughts. While they are not evil, caulborn have little interest in alliances or friendship with other races.

Caulborns speak Abyssal, Aklo, Aquan, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Giant and Infernal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 7th; concentration +11): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ (+6 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_; 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ (+8 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze Monster|daze monster]]_ (+6 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]]_ (+9 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]_ (+6 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]_ (+7); 1/week—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ (willing targets only).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Consume Thoughts|Consume Thoughts]] (Ex)**: A caulborn can consume the thoughts of a willing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] creature with a touch attack. If the caulborn hits the target at +8 vs Will, it can alter the victim's memory as if using modify memory. This process deals 1d4 points of Intelligence and Wisdom damage to the target. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Cooperative Scrying|Cooperative Scrying]] (Sp)**: Three or more caulborn joining hands can scry on a place or creature as if using the scrying spell (+10 vs Will), but with no limit to the spell's duration so long as at least three of the caulborn involved continue to join hands and concentrate. This ability functions at CL 7th (or at the highest CL available to the most powerful caulborn in the group). The attack bonus is Charisma-based, adjusted by the modifier of the caulborn with the highest Charisma score.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Hive Mind|Hive Mind]] (Ex)**: As long as there are at least two caulborn within 300 feet of each other, if one caulborn in the group is aware of a particular danger, they all are. No caulborn in a group is considered flanked or flat-footed unless all of them are.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Psychic Deflection|Psychic Deflection]] (Su)**: A caulborn adds its Charisma modifier as a deflection bonus to its AC. The caulborn loses this bonus when [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]].

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Thoughtsense (Caulborn)|Thoughtsense]] (Su)**: A caulborn notices and locates living, conscious creatures within 60 feet, just as if it possessed the blindsight ability. Spells such as nondetection or mind blank make an affected creature undetectable by this sense.
