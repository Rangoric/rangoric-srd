An anaxim’s natural weapons are treated as epic and lawful-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

#### Combat

Sonic Blast (Ex): As a standard action, an anaxim can emit a 60-foot cone of sonic energy that deals 20d6 points of sonic damage to all creatures that fail a Reflex save (DC 29); those that succeed take half damage.  
The save DC is Constitution-based

Rend (Ex): If the anaxim hits with both spinning blades, it slices particularly well. This attack automatically deals an additional 4d6+18 points of damage.

Spell-Like Abilities: Caster level 22nd. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]] (DC 18), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]] (DC 19), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt|ethereal jaunt]].  

Summon Iron Golem (Sp): An anaxim can summon an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Iron Golem|iron golem]] up to four times per day.

Abomination Traits: Immune to polymorphing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrification]], and other form-altering attacks; not subject to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|energy drain]], ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], or ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]], or death from massive damage; immune to mind-affecting effects; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|fire resistance]] 20; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|cold resistance]] 20; [[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]; [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]] at will; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^blindsight|blindsight]] 500 ft.; telepathy out to 1,000 ft.

Construct Traits: Immune to mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, phantasms, patterns, and morale effects), and to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]], sleep, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralysis]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]], death effects, necromantic effects, and any effect that requires a Fortitude save unless it also works on objects. Cannot heal damage (though [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^regeneration|regeneration]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fast-healing|fast healing]] still apply, if present). Not subject to critical hits, subdual damage, ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]], ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|energy drain]]. Not at risk of death from massive damage, but destroyed when reduced to 0 hit points or less; cannot be raised or resurrected. Darkvision 60 ft.
