Created from the body of a drow, warped and mutated through special poisons and elixirs to take on the characteristics of a giant spider, the drider is a dangerous creature.

Driders are sexually dimorphic. A female drider's lower spider body is sleek and graceful, often similar to a black widow's body, while its upper drow torso retains its alluring curves and beautiful face (with the exception of sharp, poisonous fangs). A male drider's lower body is bulky like a tarantula, while its upper body is wiry and bears a hideous face more spider than drow, complete with fanged mandibles.

Driders speak Elven, Common, and Undercommon.

### Combat

Driders seldom pass up an opportunity to attack other creatures, especially from ambush. They usually begin with a spell assault and often levitate out of the enemy’s reach.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 9th): Constant—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|detect law]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*; At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Clairaudience-Clairvoyance|clairaudience/clairvoyance]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]* (DC 16).

**Spells**: A drider casts spells as a 6th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]], but does not gain any other class abilities. Drider clerics choose from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Destruction Domain|Destruction]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Evil Domain|Evil]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain|Trickery]] domains.

**Spells known** (CL 6th): 3rd (4/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]* (DC 16); 2nd (6/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]* (DC 15); 1st (7/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|ray of enfeeblement]]* (DC 14)*, [[rangoric-srd/spells/Silent Image|silent image]]* (DC 14); 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]* (DC 13)*, [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Frost|ray of frost]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]*.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Drider Venom|Drider Venom]]: Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 18; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d2 Str; *cure* 1 save. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Undersized Weapons (Ex)**: Although a drider is Large, its upper torso is the same size as that of a Medium humanoid's upper torso. As a result, it wields weapons as if it were one size category smaller than its actual size (Medium for most driders).
