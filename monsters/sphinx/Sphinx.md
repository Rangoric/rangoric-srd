# Sphinx

Sphinxes are enigmatic creatures with great, feathery wings and leonine bodies. All sphinxes are territorial, but the more intelligent ones can differentiate between deliberate intrusion and temporary or inadvertent trespass.

A typical sphinx is about 10 feet long and weighs about 800 pounds.

Sphinxes speak Sphinx, Common, and Draconic.

#### Combat

Most sphinxes fight on the ground, using their wings to help them [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^pounce|pounce]] much as lions do. If outnumbered by earthbound creatures, a sphinx takes wing and attacks on the fly.

**Pounce (Ex)**: If a sphinx charges a foe, it can make a full attack, including two [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^rake|rake]] attacks.

**Rake (Ex)**: A sphinx that pounces onto a creature can make two [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^rake|rake]] attacks with its hind legs. Each sphinx’s description provides its attack bonus and damage.
