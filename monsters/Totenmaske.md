Consumed by the same lusts and excesses that led them in life, the souls of some sinners rise as totenmaskes, drinking the flesh and memories of living creatures and even stepping into their lives to once more pursue their base desires. Incapable of resuming their sinful pursuits in their natural form, totenmaskes often keep their victims alive for as long as possible, renewing their stolen identities regularly in order to continue indulging in pleasures of the flesh.

A totenmaske can be created from the corpse of a sinful mortal by a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] of at least 18th level using the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Greater Undead|create greater undead]]_ spell.

A totenmaske is 6 feet tall and weighs 140 pounds.

Totenmaskes speak Abyssal, Celestial, Common and Infernal.

### Combat

**Charisma Drain (Su)**: A totenmaske can eat the hopes and dreams of a creature it bites, attacking it at +11 vs Will and dealing 1d4 points of Charisma drain on a hit. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Fleshdrink|Fleshdrink]] (Su)**: If a totenmaske hits a single creature with both claw attacks, the hollow claws drain away some of the target's flesh, dealing 1d6 points of Constitution damage and making the victim [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d4 rounds. It attacks at +9 vs Fortitude, and a miss negates the Constitution damage and reduces the sickened condition duration to 1 round. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Shape Flesh|Shape Flesh]] (Su)**: By spending 1 minute in contact with a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] creature, a totenmaske can reshape the target's face, causing flesh to cover vital features. It attacks the target at +9 vs Fortitude, and a miss resists it. Changes are permanent, but can be reversed with heal, restoration, or regeneration, or by surgically opening the sealed flesh with a DC 15 Medicine check that takes 1d3 rounds and deals 1d4 points of damage even if the check is not successful. A totenmaske can use this ability on one of four different features per use: ears (target becomes deaf), eyes (target becomes blind), mouth (target cannot speak or eat), or nose (target cannot smell). Multiple uses can have increasingly serious effects (such as sealing the mouth and nose, which causes suffocation). The attack bonus is Charisma-based.
