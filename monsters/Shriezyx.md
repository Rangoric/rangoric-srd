# Shriezyx

- **Initiative**: +7

- **Base Attack**: +5; **CMB**: +6; **CMD**: 19 (31 vs. trip)

Devised by a cabal of ancient [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizards]] to serve as guardians for monuments and laboratories, shriezyx are horrific spiderlike aberrations created in arcane vats out of the flesh of many different creatures. Having outlived their creators, they continue to lurk in ruins, caves, and the deep places of the world, spreading far beyond the original city where they were created.

Shriezyx have no natural place in the ecology, but easily fill the niche of any predator of comparable strength. They can subsist on rats and other vermin for long periods of time, but relish the opportunity for a more substantial meal. Derros, drow, and other subterranean races sometimes use them as guards or mounts, or subject them to torturous experimentation. These races use bribes of food and the threat of fire to keep the creatures in line.

Shriezyx can reproduce with their own kind or lay unfertilized eggs that hatch into identical copies of their parent. Eggs remain viable for years, hatching after exposure to water, fresh air, or consistent warmth. A typical shriezyx is 3 feet tall and weighs 300 pounds.

Shriezyxes speak Aklo.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Fear of Fire|Fear of Fire]] (Ex)**: A shriezyx within 30 feet of a fire the size of a torch or larger becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] as long as it remains within that range. If damaged by fire, the fire attacks a shriezyx's Will (attack bonus equal to the amount of fire damage dealt – 10), and on a hit it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 round.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: Bite—injury; attack +4 vs Fortitude; frequency 1/round for 6 rounds; effect 1d2 Str; cure 1 miss.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Slowing Toxin|Slowing Toxin]] (Su)**: A shriezyx's web is coated with a supernatural toxin that numbs and deadens the nerves on contact. Any creature struck by a shriezyx's web is attacked at +4 vs Fortitude, and on a hit becomes slowed (as the slow spell) for 1 minute. Each round, the toxin attacks a victim again, and a miss ends the effect early. This toxin fades quickly from spun webs—it can only affect targets on the round the web is spun. Existing webs remain sticky and tangled, but do not have this slowing effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
