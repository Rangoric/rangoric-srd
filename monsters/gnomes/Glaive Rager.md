A glaive rager is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/gnomes/Gnome|gnome]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]].

A glaive rager speaks Aquan, Auran, Common, Gnome, and Sylvan.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The barbarian leads with trip maneuvers, seeking to trigger attacks of opportunity. He uses knockback to force creatures to advance on him again through the threatened area of his glaive.

**Base Statistics**: When not raging, the barbarian's statistics are **AC** 19, touch 12, flat-footed 18; **hp** 89; **Fort** +10; **Will** +3; no bonus vs. spells and spell-like or supernatural abilities; **Melee** masterwork cold iron glaive +13/+8 (1d8+4/×3) or kukri +12/+7 (1d3+3/18-20); **Ranged** masterwork composite longbow +11/+6 (1d6+3/×3); **Str** 16; **Con** 16; **CMB** +10 (+14 trip); **Skills** Athletics +8.
