A wight’s appearance is a weird and twisted reflection of the form it had in life. A wight is about the height and weight of a human.

Wights speak Common.

### Combat

Wights attack by hammering with their fists.

**Create Spawn (Su)**: Any [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|humanoid]] slain by a wight becomes a wight in 1d4 rounds. Spawn are under the command of the wight that created them and remain enslaved until its death. They do not possess any of the abilities they had in life.

**Energy Drain (Su)**: Living creatures hit by a wight’s slam attack gain one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative level]]. The DC is 14 for the Fortitude save to remove a negative level. The save DC is Charisma-based. For each such negative level bestowed, the wight gains 5 temporary hit points.

**Skills**: Wights have a +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.
