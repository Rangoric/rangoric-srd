A sahuagin shark sentry is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the sahuagin shark speakers. Shark speakers prefer the company of vicious fish over other sahuagin.

A sahuagin shark sentry speaks Aquan and Common, and can speak with sharks.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The shark sentry drinks its potion of greater magic fang, then reads scrolls of barkskin for itself and an ally.

**Base Statistics**: Without greater magic fang and barkskin, the shark sentry's statistics are **AC** 21, touch 12, flat-footed 19; **Melee** 2 claws +11 (1d6+5), bite +11 (1d4+5).

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 1st; concentration +2): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hunter’s Howl|hunter's howl]]_ (+2 vs Will).
