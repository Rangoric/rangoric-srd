A sahuagin infiltrator is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the sahuagin agents. Agents lead the way for other sahuagin by scouting enemy patrols and hidden fortifications.

A sahuagin infiltrator speaks Aquan, Common, Elven, Giant, and Goblin, and can speak with sharks.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The infiltrator darts around the battlefield, attempting to flank its opponents. If it's forced to fight alone, it instead tries to ambush its foes one by one.
