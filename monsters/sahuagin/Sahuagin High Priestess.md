A sahuagin high priestess is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]], one of the sahuagin priestesses. Priestesses lead ceremonies and support military actions.

A sahuagin high priestess speaks Aquan, Common, Elven, and Infernal, and can speak with sharks.
