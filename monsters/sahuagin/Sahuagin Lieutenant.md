A sahuagin lieutenant is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the sahuagin soldiers. Powerful warriors occupy the top ranks of sahuagin society.

A sahuagin lieutenant speaks Aquan and Common, and can speak with sharks.
