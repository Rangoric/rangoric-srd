A sahuagin baron is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the sahuagin soldiers. Powerful warriors occupy the top ranks of sahuagin society.

A sahuagin baron speaks Aquan, Common, and Infernal, and can speak with sharks.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The sahuagin baron drinks its potions of bear's endurance and bull's strength.

**Base Statistics**: Without bear's endurance and bull's strength, the baron's statistics are **hp** 83; **Fort** +9; **Melee** masterwork trident +15/+10 (1d8+5), claw +14 (1d4+5/19–20 plus 1d6 cold), bite +13 (1d6+3 plus 1d6 cold); **Str** 20; **Con** 17; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 27.
