A sahuagin prince is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/cavalier/Beast Rider (Archetype)|beast rider]] archetype), one of the sahuagin soldiers. Powerful warriors occupy the top ranks of sahuagin society.

A sahuagin prince is a paragon of ferocity and political skill.

A sahuagin prince speaks Aquan and Common, and can speak with sharks.

### Combat

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin and heroism, the prince's statistics are **AC** 26, touch 14, flat-footed 23; **Fort** +14; **Ref** +11; **Will** +9; **Melee** lance of jousting +18/+13/+8 (1d8+8/19–20/×3), bite +15 (1d6+2), 2 claws +17 (1d4+5) or 4 claws +17 (1d4+5), bite +17 (1d6+5); **Ranged** masterwork underwater heavy crossbow +16 (1d10/19–20); **Skills** Animal Handling +13, Athletics +17, Perception +16, Sense Motive +8, Society +14.
