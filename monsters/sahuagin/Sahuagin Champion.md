A sahuagin champion is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]], one of the sahuagin marauders. These sahuagin revel in their rage and blood frenzy.

A sahuagin champion speaks Aquan and Common, and can speak with sharks.

### Combat

**Base Statistics**: When not raging and not affected by barkskin, the champion's statistics are **AC** 21, touch 11, flat-footed 18; **hp** 105; **Fort** +12; **Will** +8; **Melee** +1 trident +16/+11 (1d8+10), claw +13 (1d4+3), masterwork spiked heavy steel shield +16 (1d6+6), bite +13 (1d4+3) or 4 claws +15 (1d4+6), bite +15 (1d4+6); **Ranged** masterwork heavy crossbow +13 (1d10/19–20); **Str** 22; **Con** 17; **CMB** +15; **CMD** 28; **Skills** Athletics +25, Intimidation +18.
