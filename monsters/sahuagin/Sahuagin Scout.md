A sahuagin scout is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the sahuagin agents. Agents lead the way for other sahuagin by scouting enemy patrols and hidden fortifications.

A sahuagin scout speaks Aquan, Common, and Elven, and can speak with sharks.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The scout shoots the appropriate bane bolts before closing to melee.
