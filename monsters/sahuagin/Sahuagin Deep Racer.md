A sahuagin deep racer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the sahuagin shark speakers. Shark speakers prefer the company of vicious fish over other sahuagin.

A sahuagin deep racer speaks Aquan and Common, and can speak with sharks.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The deep racer regularly uses Pushing Assault.
