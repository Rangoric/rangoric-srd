# Kyton

Kytons are lawful evil outsiders native to the Plane of Shadow who feed on fear and pain.

### Combat

Kytons have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^kyton|kyton subtype]]: darkvision, a regeneration that good weapons, good spells, and silver weapons overcome, immunity to cold, and the unnerving gaze that manipulates the perceptions of those who look upon them.
