# Scorpion

Scorpions are armored hunters with grasping claws and a venomous sting at the end of a curved tail. The giant ones are [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^vermin|vermin]]; those that come in sizes from Tiny to Colossal are described under [[rangoric-srd/monsters/monstrous-scorpion/Monstrous Scorpion|monstrous scorpions]], and the kinds below are particular species.

  - [[rangoric-srd/monsters/scorpion/Black Scorpion|Black Scorpion]]
  - [[rangoric-srd/monsters/scorpion/Cave Scorpion|Cave Scorpion]]
  - [[rangoric-srd/monsters/scorpion/Deadfall Scorpion|Deadfall Scorpion]]
  - [[rangoric-srd/monsters/scorpion/Ghost Scorpion|Ghost Scorpion]]
  - [[rangoric-srd/monsters/scorpion/Giant Emperor Scorpion|Giant Emperor Scorpion]]
  - [[rangoric-srd/monsters/scorpion/Greensting Scorpion|Greensting Scorpion]]

**Species**

Other species of scorpions exist as well, some smaller but most quite a bit larger and favoring different terrains, such as forests, plains, or even underground. You can adjust the stats for the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/monstrous-scorpion/Monstrous Scorpion, Large|giant scorpion]] by changing Hit Dice and size (adjusting Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution as appropriate) to represent a wide range of species.
