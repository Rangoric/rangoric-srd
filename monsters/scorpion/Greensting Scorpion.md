Deadly greensting scorpions normally live in forests, though they can survive nearly anywhere. Their pincers are too small to be dangerous to anything but other insects, but they have a poisonous sting that sickens even larger creatures.

A greensting scorpion familiar grants a +4 bonus on Initiative checks so long as the familiar is within 1 mile of the spellcaster. A greensting scorpion familiar loses the mindless trait and has an Intelligence score appropriate for its master's level.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: Sting—injury; _attack_ +0 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 round; _cure_ 1 miss. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
