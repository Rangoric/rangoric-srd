# Tendriculos

- Huge [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^plant|Plant]]
- **Hit Dice**: 9d8+36 (76 hp)
- **Initiative**: +3
- **Speed**: 20 ft. (4 squares)
- **Armor Class**: 19 (–1 Dex, +12 natural, –2 size), touch 7, flat-footed 19
- **Base Attack**: +6; **CMB**: +15 (+19 grapple); **CMD**: 24 (can't be tripped)
- **Attack**: Bite +11 melee (2d6+7 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]]) or tentacle +11 melee (1d6+3 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]])
- **Full Attack**: Bite +11 melee (2d6+7 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]]) and 2 tentacles +6 melee (1d6+3 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]])
- **Space/Reach**: 15 ft./15 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Paralysis|Paralysis]] (3d6 rounds, +8 vs Fortitude), [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swallow Whole|swallow whole]] (2d6 acid damage plus paralysis, AC 15, 7 hp)
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|Low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Regeneration|regeneration]] 10 (bludgeoning or fire), [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Immunity|immunity]] to acid, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Plant Traits|plant traits]]

A tendriculos is a creature brought into being through a corruption of nature, often where foul magic has seeped into the environs for many years, or where the boundaries between the Material Plane and the mysterious realm of the fey have worn thin. Tales and myths speak of arcane manipulation from other planes, while others speak of a tendriculos as being the manifestation of an angered spirit of nature.

A voracious carnivore, the tendriculos is an active hunter when it needs to be but it prefers to rely on ambush tactics, choosing well-traveled areas in the forest where it can lie in wait in the undergrowth. The creature is quick to swallow any prey it happens to catch in its vines, relying upon the acid-filled reservoir in its trunk to finish off prey that may still have a bit of fight left in it. This acid not only consumes organic material with shocking ease, but also contains a powerful paralytic enzyme that further reduces a creature's chance of escape once it's been gulped down by the plant.

Although incapable of speech itself, the tendriculos generally understands a handful of words in a single language (usually Sylvan). While they tend to see all smaller creatures as nothing more than food, tales exist of certain creatures, particularly fey or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druids]], having secured a sort of alliance with local tendriculoses. In such cases, the tendriculos is more than willing to serve its ally as a guardian, so long as the ally is diligent at providing the plant with a regular supply of food.

The fact that a tendriculos displays a curious mix of plant, fungal, and even animal traits (in the form of its almost fleshy, toothed maw) has long intrigued sages. That the creature is a plant is firmly established, yet it lays egg-like spheres when the need to reproduce strikes. These “eggs” are in fact massive, puffball-like fungi filled with spores—when jostled, the cloud of spores that is released can carry for miles, ensuring that a single tendriculos can seed a huge territory.

A tendriculos is 20 feet tall and weighs 3,500 pounds.

Animals and other plant creatures are unnerved by the presence of a tendriculos; they avoid it and any place it has been within the last 24 hours.

### Combat

Prowling deep forests or waiting in vegetated areas (looking like nothing more than a small hillock), a tendriculos attacks savagely, showing no fear. It attempts to [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swallow Whole|swallow]] as much flesh as it can, as quickly as it can.

A tendriculos can also use its [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]] ability on a tentacle attack. If it succeeds on the grapple check, it establishes a hold, picks up the opponent, and transfers it to the mouth as a free action, automatically dealing bite damage.

A tendriculos that loses part of its body mass can regrow it in 1d6 minutes. Holding the severed portion against the mass enables it to reattach instantly.
