A hoary hunter’s natural weapons are treated as epic for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].

#### Combat

A critical hit (or the blow that would kill or render a foe [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]) from the hoary hunter’s blade does not wound the hunter’s prey. Instead, the victim is bodily transported as per a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Soul Bind|soul binding]] spell heightened to 16th level (DC 30) into a diamond at the end of the sword’s hilt.

Spell-Like Abilities: Caster level 23rd; save DC 18 + spell level.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Location|discern location]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]];  

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Monster|dominate monster]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spells F to G|greater dispel magic]];  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Disjunction|mage’s disjunction]], contingent recall and resurrection\*.  

\*Contingent recall and resurrection is a variation on an epic spell. When slain, the hoary hunter is actually transported, with its mount, back to the fey realm, where it is resurrected. The only way to truly slay the hoary hunter is to defeat it in its home realm after this epic spell has been exhausted for the day.
