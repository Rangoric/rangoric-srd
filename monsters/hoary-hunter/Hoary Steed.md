A hoary steed’s natural weapons are treated as magic for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].

### Combat

Air Walk (Su): A hoary steed can ride through the air as well as on the ground, as though affected by a permanent [[rangoric-srd/spells/Air Walk|air walk]] spell.

Astral Projection and Etherealness (Su): These abilities function just like the spells of the same names as cast by a 20th-level caster.

Magic Circle Against Good (Su): This ability continuously duplicates the effects of [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Good|the spell]]. A hoary steed cannot suppress this ability.

Misty Breath (Su): The breath of a hoary steed functions like a constant [[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]] spell.

Immunities (Ex): A hoary steed is immune to all charm and hold spells or abilities.
