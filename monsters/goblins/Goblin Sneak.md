A goblin sneak is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/goblins/Goblin|goblin]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the goblin rogues. Its sneak attack includes the [[rangoric-srd/races/class-bonuses/Goblin Rogue (Class Bonus)|goblin rogue class bonus]], taken at each rogue level. Goblins adore theft, and are adept at slinking about undetected. Goblin sneaks are master ambushers and spectacular hiders. The most talented also incorporate their racial love of fire into their tactics.

A goblin sneak speaks Goblin.

### Combat

**During Combat**: A sneak tries to ambush opponents so it can make sneak attacks, then uses Acrobatics to flank.
