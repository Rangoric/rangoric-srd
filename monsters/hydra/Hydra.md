Hydras are reptile-like monsters with multiple heads.

A hydra is gray-brown to dark brown, with a light yellow or tan underbelly. The eyes are amber and the teeth are yellow-white. It is about 20 feet long and weighs about 4,000 pounds.

Hydras do not speak.

### Hydras by Heads

A hydra has one head per Hit Die. Each added Hit Die increases the hydra’s statistics as appropriate, but also gives it one additional head and a +1 increase to its natural armor. A hydra’s CR increases by +1 for each Hit Die it gains. The head-count notes are built this way from the five-headed hydra: every hydra stays Huge, its Strength rises by 1 at nine heads, and its added feats are [[rangoric-srd/feats/Toughness|Toughness]] at seven heads, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (bite) at nine and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Natural Attack|Improved Natural Attack]] (bite) at eleven.

### Combat

Every hydra has [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Hydra Traits|hydra traits]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Regenerate Head|regenerate head]], and can [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Pounce|pounce]]. Hydras can attack with all their heads at no penalty, even if they move or charge during the round.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Fast Healing|Fast Healing]] (Ex)**: A hydra’s fast healing ability is equal to its current number of heads (minimum fast healing 5). This fast healing applies only to damage inflicted on the hydra’s body.

**Skills**: Hydras have a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, thanks to their multiple heads.

Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]], a hydra is better at swimming.

**Feats**: A hydra’s [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]] feat allows it to use all its heads for attacks of opportunity.
