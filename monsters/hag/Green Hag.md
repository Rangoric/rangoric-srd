Green hags are found in desolate swamps and dark forests.

A green hag is about the same height and weight as a female human.

## Combat

Green hags prefer to attack from hiding, usually after distracting foes. They often use darkvision to their advantage by attacking during moonless nights.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 9th. The save DC is Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]] (DC 12), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]].  

**Weakness (Su)**: A green hag can weaken a foe by making a special touch attack. The opponent must succeed on a DC 16 Fortitude save or take 2d4 points of Strength [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]]. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Mimicry (Ex)**: A green hag can imitate the sounds of almost any animal found near its lair.

**Skills**: Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]], a green hag is better at swimming.
