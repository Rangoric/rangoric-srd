An ogre boss is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ogre/Ogre|ogre]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the ogre bruisers. Violence is a way of life for ogres. Most of them learn the best ways to cause pain from their clans’ bosses, who mete out beatings for even the slightest infraction.

An ogre boss speaks Giant.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The ogre boss closes with soft-looking enemies and uses her massive reach (with Lunge, if necessary) to box them in.
