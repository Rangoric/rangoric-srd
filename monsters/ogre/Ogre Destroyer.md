An ogre destroyer is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ogre/Ogre|ogre]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladin]], one of the ogre destroyers. Ogre destroyers are chosen by the powers of chaos and evil to walk the world on a dark quest to break, maim, eat, torture, and kill everything beautiful, peaceful, or pure.

An ogre destroyer speaks Giant.
