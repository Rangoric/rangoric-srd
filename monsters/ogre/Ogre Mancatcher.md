An ogre mancatcher is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ogre/Ogre|ogre]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the ogre devourers. Ogres who specialize in catching delicious humanoid morsels are popular among the clans.

An ogre mancatcher speaks Giant.
