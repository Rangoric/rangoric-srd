# Shaggra Ogre

Ogres with the shaggra template are covered in long, matted fur, and have stunted legs and massive oversized arms, similar to apes. They move on all fours and smash enemies with their giant fists.

“Shaggra” is an inherited template for [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ogre/Ogre|ogres]]; like a simple template, it has quick rules and rebuild rules.

**Challenge Rating**: As base creature +1.

**Quick Rules**: +2 on all attack rolls, damage rolls, and Strength checks; gains two slam attacks (1d6); gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Constrict|constrict]] monster special abilities.

**Rebuild Rules**: **Ability Scores** +4 Strength; **Attacks** gains 2 slam attacks (1d6, grab, constrict); **Special Abilities** [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Constrict|constrict]].
