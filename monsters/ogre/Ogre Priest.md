An ogre priest is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ogre/Ogre|ogre]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]], one of the ogre schemers. Ogres belittle those who value smarts over strength, but clever ogres find their own paths to power.

An ogre priest speaks Giant.
