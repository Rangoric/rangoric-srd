An ogre stalker is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ogre/Ogre|ogre]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the ogre schemers. Ogres belittle those who value smarts over strength, but clever ogres find their own paths to power.

An ogre stalker speaks Giant.
