A ratfolk bravo is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ratfolk/Ratfolk|ratfolk]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger|gunslinger]], one of the ratfolk weapon masters. Because they trade so widely, ratfolk acquire interesting equipment—including black powder and rare metals.

A ratfolk bravo speaks Common and Undercommon.
