A ratfolk warden is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ratfolk/Ratfolk|ratfolk]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the ratfolk weapon masters. Because they trade so widely, ratfolk acquire interesting equipment—including black powder and rare metals.

A ratfolk warden speaks Common.

### Combat

**During Combat**: Focused on defending her allies against attacks, the warden tries to disarm enemies so they pose less of a threat.
