A ratfolk tinkerer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ratfolk/Ratfolk|ratfolk]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the ratfolk scroungers. Ratfolk are adept at finding and disarming traps, which they scavenge to add to their warrens' defenses.

A ratfolk tinkerer speaks Common, Gnome, Goblin, and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: If he can, the tinkerer sets an ambush in an area with multiple entrances and exits.

**During Combat**: The tinkerer shares his space with allies to make sneak attacks. If he needs to escape, the tinkerer uses his potion of pass without trace.
