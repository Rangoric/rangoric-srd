A ratfolk expedition leader is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ratfolk/Ratfolk|ratfolk]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the ratfolk travelers. Many nomadic ratfolk take on jobs that require travel.

A ratfolk expedition leader speaks Common and Gnoll.
