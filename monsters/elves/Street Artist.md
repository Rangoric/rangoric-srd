A street artist is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elves/Elf|elf]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Street artists are active in urban politics, a little crazy, or both. Their art and messages delight some, but annoy landowners whose buildings become the artists' medium.

A street artist speaks Common and Elven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The bard posts political screeds in alleys, imbuing them with sepia snake sigils to trap those reading them. If anticipating combat, the bard drinks his potion of eagle's splendor.

**During Combat**: The bard starts by casting haste and mirror image. He then shoots at opposing spellcasters or deafens them with sound burst.

**Base Statistics**: Without eagle's splendor, the bard's statistics are **Bard Spells Known** reduce spell DCs by 2; **Cha** 17; **Skills** Deception +10, Perform (dance) +14.
