A tavern singer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elves/Elf#Half-Elf|half-elf]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. These performers entertain to earn drinks and tips.

A tavern singer speaks Common and Elven.

**During Combat**: The bard uses grease to escape.
