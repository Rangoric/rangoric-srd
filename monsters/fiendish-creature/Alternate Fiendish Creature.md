# Alternate Fiendish Creature

Creatures with the fiendish template live in the Lower Planes, such as the Abyss and Hell, but can be summoned using spells such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]*. A fiendish creature's quick and rebuild rules are the same.

“Fiendish” is a simple template ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Simple Templates|Simple Templates]]): it can be added to a creature during play.

**Challenge Rating**: A fiendish creature's CR increases by +1 only if the base creature has 5 or more HD.

**Rebuild Rules**: **Senses** gains darkvision 60 ft.; **Defensive Abilities** gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|DR]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Resistance|energy resistance]] as noted on the table; **SR** gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell Resistance|SR]] equal to new CR +5; **Special Attacks** [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Smite Good (Template)|smite good]] 1/day as a swift action (adds Cha bonus on attack rolls and damage bonus equal to HD against good foes; smite persists until target is dead or the fiendish creature rests).

**Table: Fiendish Creature Defenses**

| Hit Dice | Resist Cold and Fire | DR      |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–4      | 5                    | —       |
| 5–10     | 10                   | 5/good  |
| 11+      | 15                   | 10/good |

This is the second version of the fiendish creature template, a simple template; the first is [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creature/Fiendish Creature|Fiendish Creature]].
