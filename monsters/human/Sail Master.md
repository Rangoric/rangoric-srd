A sail master is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]]. Mastery of the wind and weather is a boon on any ship, and many northern druids thus serve as captains, navigators, and battle support.

A sail master speaks Common and Druidic.
