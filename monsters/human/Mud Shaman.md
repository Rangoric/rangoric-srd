A mud shaman is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]]. Stalking through boiling and sometimes acidic mud pits, these druids have caustic personalities and abilities.

A mud shaman speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The druid casts barkskin and bull's strength.

**During Combat**: The druid casts wall of stone between his opponents to separate them. On the following rounds, he spontaneously casts summoning spells, bolstering summoned creatures with greater magic fang. He eventually wild shapes into a stegosaurus to enter melee.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin and bull's strength, the druid's statistics are **AC** 19, touch 10, flat-footed 19; **Melee** masterwork club +8/+3 (1d6+1); **Ranged** masterwork shortspear +7/+2 (1d6+1); **Str** 13; **CMB** +7; **CMD** 17; **Skills** Athletics +4.

**Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +14): 8/day—_[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain|acid dart]]_.
