A pirate captain is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] and 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. Pirate captains are the deadly mistresses of pirate bands, villainous cutthroats who have cursed, looted, ravaged, betrayed, and carved their way to the top. These steel-handed stingrays lead their crews by sheer force of will. Pirate captains make excellent champion pit fighters and deadly assassins. A pirate captain is usually found with a first mate and a dozen shipmates (CR 12).

A pirate captain speaks Aquan and Common.
