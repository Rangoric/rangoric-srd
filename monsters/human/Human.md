# Human

Humans are the most adaptable and widespread of the common races, found in every land and climate. Most humans encountered are ordinary folk with a few levels of an NPC class; those with levels in a character class are the soldiers, priests, mages and rogues described below. A human's statistics come from its class levels.

Human characters use the [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]] race, which has their racial traits.
