A cutpurse is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The cutpurse is a dirty sneak who uses surprise and stealth to rob unsuspecting victims.

A cutpurse speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The rogue hides so he can use sneak attack.

**During Combat**: The rogue continues to engage his initial opponent in melee, or throws acid if all his opponents are at range.
