A cautious mage is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Universalist School|universalist]] school). These mages are new adventurers looking for protectors.

A cautious mage speaks Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Elven, and Infernal.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The wizard casts mage armor.

**During Combat**: The wizard uses hand of the apprentice to throw her quarterstaff or casts burning hands. She offers to use her scroll of enlarge person on an ally who'll protect her.

**Base Statistics**: Without mage armor, the wizard's statistics are **AC** 11, touch 11, flat-footed 10.

**Wizard spells prepared** (CL 1st; concentration +4): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]_ (DC 14), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_ (DC 13), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_.
