A celestial theurge is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] and 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Mystic Theurge|mystic theurge]]. These theurges support righteous causes, especially ones that involve destroying undead.

A celestial theurge speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The mystic theurge casts bull's strength.

**During Combat**: The mystic theurge casts haste and shield, then supports her companions with spells. She targets undead with channeled energy and searing light.

**Base Statistics**: Without bull's strength, the mystic theurge's statistics are **Melee** +1 spear +11 (1d8+5/×3); **Str** 18; **CMB** +9; **CMD** 19.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +5): 4/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Celestial Bloodline|heavenly fire]] (1d4+2).

**Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 3rd; concentration +5): 5/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Healing Domain|rebuke death]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Glory Domain|touch of glory]].

**Sorcerer spells known** (CL 6th; concentration +7; 25% spell failure): 3rd (3/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*; 2nd (5/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Arrows|protection from arrows]]*; 1st (7/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]* (DC 12), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]*; 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]*; **Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Celestial Bloodline|celestial]].
