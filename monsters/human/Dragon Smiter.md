A dragon smiter is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 13 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. A dragon smiter is sworn to slay dragons.

A dragon smiter speaks Common and Draconic.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The paladin casts resist energy against the breath weapon energy type of dragons he expects to fight.

**During Combat**: The paladin casts eagle's splendor to improve his smite and divine grace. He uses Improved Vital Strike and Lunge to land solid hits on creatures with reach.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 13th; concentration +14): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.

**Paladin spells prepared** (CL 10th; concentration +11): 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|prayer]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_ (2), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_ (2).
