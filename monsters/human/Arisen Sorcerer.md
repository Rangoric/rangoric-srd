An arisen sorcerer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 20 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]].

An arisen sorcerer speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The sorcerer casts false life and mage armor.

**During Combat**: The sorcerer casts energy drain, power word kill, and wail of the banshee. She may deter opponents with solid fog, waves of exhaustion, or her grasp of the dead ability.

**Base Statistics**: Without false life and mage armor, the sorcerer's statistics are **AC** 26, touch 17, flat-footed 23; **hp** 202.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +28): 11/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Undead Bloodline|grave touch]] (10 rounds); 3/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Undead Bloodline|grasp of the dead]] (20d6 slashing, DC 28); 1/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Undead Bloodline|incorporeal form]] (20 rounds).
