A doomsayer (bard) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 20 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Some doomsayers walk in solitude; others form cults to witness the end.

A doomsayer (bard) speaks Abyssal and Common.
