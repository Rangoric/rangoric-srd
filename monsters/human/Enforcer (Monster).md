An enforcer (monster) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Adept|adept]]. The enforcer uses threats and violence to serve a dual purpose: to intimidate enemies of his religion, and to pass judgment on members who believe they can betray or desert the holy cause.

An enforcer (monster) speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The adept casts bull's strength.

**During Combat**: The adept casts mirror image, then attacks with his club. If he has trouble landing blows, he switches to his wand of burning hands.

**Base Statistics**: Without bull's strength, the adept's statistics are **Melee** masterwork club +5 (1d6+3); **Ranged** dagger +3 (1d4+2/19-20); **Str** 14; **CMB** +4; **CMD** 15.

**Adept spells prepared** (CL 5th; concentration +7): 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]*; 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]*; 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]* (DC 12), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]*.
