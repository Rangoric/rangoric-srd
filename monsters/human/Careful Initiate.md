A careful initiate is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]]. Neophyte monks are often eager to prove their mettle in battle, but just as often their strict training means they tend to act tentatively when deprived of their master's guidance.

A careful initiate speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The monk uses Stealth to catch enemies off-guard, starting any surprise round with Stunning Fist.

**During Combat**: The monk never fights multiple opponents if she can help it, and prefers to use her shuriken with a flurry of blows before entering melee.
