A master spy (monster) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 19 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. A master spy lingers in the shadows or at parties, where he can easily misdirect people.

A master spy (monster) speaks Auran, Common, and Draconic.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The bard keeps misdirection in place at all times.

**During Combat**: The bard opens combat by using a scroll of disintegrate.
