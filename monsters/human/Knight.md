A knight is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Aristocrat|aristocrat]] and 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. Knights are noble warriors, proud of bearing and lineage and yet humble in service to their liege. Though merciful and generous of spirit, a true knight is always ready to level lance or bare steel in pursuit of justice and to protect the innocent. Knights may also serve as local lord-stewards, judges, or fortress commanders. Knights are usually found singly or accompanied by a squire, escorting a pair of pilgrims (CR 8), guarding two nobles (CR 11), or leading a lance of four cavalry (CR 10).

A knight speaks Celestial, Common, and Sylvan.
