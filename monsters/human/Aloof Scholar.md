An aloof scholar is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] and 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Wandering Chronicler|wandering chronicler]]. Often lost in their own thoughts, sometimes these scholars forget others are around them. They're usually solitary, but have to go out exploring in search of information—or pay adventurers to retell their stories.

An aloof scholar speaks Celestial, Common, Draconic, and Dwarven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The wandering chronicler casts blur, then casts haste from her scroll. Preferring to stay out of melee, she uses bardic performance to aid her allies, and casts daze monster or charm person on enemies who come close. If threatened, she withdraws, drinks her potion of invisibility, and makes her escape.
