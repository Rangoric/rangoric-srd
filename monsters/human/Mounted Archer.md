A mounted archer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. The mounted archer fires deadly arrows from horseback.

A mounted archer speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts endure elements and resist energy (fire).

**During Combat**: The ranger shoots from horseback. When fighting dangerous opponents, she uses oil of magic weapon on her bow and drinks her potion of blur.

**Base Statistics**: Without resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **Resist** none.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 2nd; concentration +3): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_.
