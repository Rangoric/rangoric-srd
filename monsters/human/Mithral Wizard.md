A mithral wizard is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] and 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Evocation School|evocation]] school) and 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Eldritch Knight|eldritch knight]]. Clad in full plate, these eldritch knights mix an impenetrable defense with a relentless spell offense.

A mithral wizard speaks Abyssal, Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Elven, Giant, Goblin, Ignan, and Infernal.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The eldritch knight casts stoneskin.

**During Combat**: The knight casts time stop, then shield, expeditious retreat, fly, and mirror image.

**Base Statistics**: Without stoneskin, the knight's statistics are **DR** none.

**Evoker Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +25): At will—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Evocation School|elemental wall]] (8 rounds/day); 11/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Evocation School|force missile]] (1d4+4).
