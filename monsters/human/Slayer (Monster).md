A slayer (monster) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Assassin|assassin]]. Slayers are cold-blooded killers, assassins for hire. They are cunning tricksters, deadly snipers, masters of the knife in the back and the storm of shuriken whistling from either hand. Slayers may also be used as ninjas, spies, or particularly deadly enforcers.

Slayers often work alone, sometimes as chief enforcer for a guild master (CR 11), but many work with accomplices, such as three burglars (CR 9), a pair of highwaymen (CR 10), or a half-dozen slavers (CR 10).

A slayer (monster) speaks Common and Elven.
