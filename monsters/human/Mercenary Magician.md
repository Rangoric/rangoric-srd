A mercenary magician is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. The mercenary magician serves whoever can pay her, but is not particularly loyal to her employers.

A mercenary magician speaks Common and Draconic.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The sorcerer casts color spray to neutralize groups of foes, followed by magic missile at any foes left standing.

**Sorcerer spells known** (CL 1st; concentration +4): 1st (4/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]_ (DC 14), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]_(DC 13), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Frost|ray of frost]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_; **Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Arcane Bloodline|arcane]].
