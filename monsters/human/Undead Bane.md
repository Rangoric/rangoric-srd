An undead bane is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]] and 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] and 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Arcane Archer|arcane archer]]. Though these archers primarily hunt undead, they are dangerous foes for any creatures.

An undead bane speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The arcane archer casts barkskin and uses her wand of shield. She prepares frost burst arrows using her enhance arrows ability.

**During Combat**: Preferring to stay out of the reach and sight of powerful enemies, the arcane archer casts fly and greater invisibility on herself, takes flight, and pelts her enemies with arrows from relative safety.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, the arcane archer's statistics are **AC** 24, touch 17, flat-footed 19.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 6th; concentration +8): 5/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Destined Bloodline|touch of destiny]].

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 6th; concentration +8): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Snare|snare]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_.
