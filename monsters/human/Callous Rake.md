A callous rake is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. The callous rake is a young paladin exhibiting unpaladinlike behavior. If unchecked, these habits become the first step toward disgrace.

A callous rake speaks Common.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The paladin begins with Dazzling Display, then uses smite evil on the strongest evil opponent. If he is confident he will win the battle, he continues to demoralize opponents.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 1st; concentration +3): At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]*.
