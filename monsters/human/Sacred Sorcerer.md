A sacred sorcerer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. The sacred sorcerer is an agent of the gods, less constrained than a priest and armed with magic rarely used by other servants of the divine.

A sacred sorcerer speaks Celestial and Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The sorcerer casts false life and mage armor.

**During Combat**: The sorcerer casts summon monster IV to summon a celestial giant wasp, then shoots ray spells at her foes.

**Base Statistics**: Without false life and mage armor, the sorcerer's statistics are **AC** 15, touch 14, flat-footed 12; **hp** 61.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +13): 7/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Celestial Bloodline|heavenly fire]] (1d4+4 divine energy).
