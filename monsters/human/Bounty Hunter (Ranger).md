A bounty hunter (ranger) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 12 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. A bounty hunter brings back his targets dead or alive.

A bounty hunter (ranger) speaks Common, Elven, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin, cat's grace, delay poison, longstrider, and resist energy (fire).

**During Combat**: The ranger attacks with his bow using Deadly Aim. In melee, he uses his kukris or attempts to grapple.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, cat's grace, delay poison, longstrider, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **Init** +6; **Senses** normal; **AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 17; **Ref** +11; **Immune** none; **Resist** none; **Speed** 30 ft; **Ranged** masterwork longbow +15/+10/+5 (1d8+4/×3); **Dex** 14; **CMD** 30 (32 vs. grapple); **Skills** Acrobatics +12, Stealth +17.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 9th; concentration +10): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Wall|wind wall]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_.
