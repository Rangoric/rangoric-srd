A champion (monster) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]] and 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. The champion is mistress of the arena, leaving in her wake a trail of wrecked armaments, battered bodies, and shattered dreams. Raising her bloodied blade, she exults in the roar of the crowd and the terror she inspires in her foes. Champions can also be used as barbarian chieftains or mercenary captains. A champion is usually encountered alone or in the company of a pair of lesser gladiators (CR 10).

A champion (monster) speaks Common.

### Combat

**Base Statistics**: When not raging, the champion has **AC** 19, touch 12, flat-footed 17; **hp** 85; **Fort** +10; **Will** +2; **Melee** +1 adamantine vicious greatsword +17/+12 (2d6+10/17–20 plus 2d6 vicious) or spiked gauntlet +14/+9 (1d3+4); **Ranged** javelin +12 (1d6+4); **Str** 18; **Con** 14; **CMB** +14 (+18 sunder); **CMD** 26 (28 vs. sunder); **Skills** Athletics +8.
