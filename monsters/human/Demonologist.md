A demonologist is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Conjuration School|conjuration]] school) and 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Loremaster|loremaster]]. Demonologists compel and manipulate their foes, and summon demon minions to do their dirty work.

A demonologist speaks Abyssal, Aklo, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Elven, Ignan, Infernal, Orc, and Undercommon.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The loremaster casts see invisibility.

**During Combat**: The loremaster casts greater invisibility. He summons demons, then casts maze, hold monster, and mass suggestion.
