A bounty hunter is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 12 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. Bounty hunters rarely hunt animals or beasts. Instead, they make not only sport but a lucrative business of capturing humanoid targets at the behest of wealthy patrons. Bounty hunters often work alone, but may lead a press gang of a sellsword, torturer, and two slavers (CR 12).

A bounty hunter speaks Common, Elven, and Halfling.
