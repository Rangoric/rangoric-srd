A merchant prince is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Expert|expert]] and 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. Merchant princes are the captains of commerce, canny mercantilists who deal with nation-spanning trade contracts in bulk commodities as well as backroom deals over baubles of great price. They are clever negotiators and can serve as highly skilled spies, diplomats, or charlatans emulating true magicians. A merchant prince often retains a sellsword as a bodyguard (CR 10).

A merchant prince speaks Common, Dwarven, Gnome, and Halfling.
