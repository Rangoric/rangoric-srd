A cruel devotee is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]]. Cruel devotees serve as the guardians and agents of monastic orders, taking on secret missions and even assassinations in order to protect the orders' interests.

A cruel devotee speaks Common.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The monk uses Stunning Fist to make opponents drop weapons, attacking if foes try to retrieve them.
