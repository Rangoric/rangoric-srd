A seance medium is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Mediums prey on those who have lost loved ones, or sentimental souls who wish to contact the great beyond.

A seance medium speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The bard drinks a potion of eagle's splendor.

**During Combat**: The bard disrupts spellcasters with dispel magic or readied blowgun attacks, and casts blink to protect himself in melee.

**Base Statistics**: Without eagle's splendor, the bard's statistics are **Bard Spells Known** reduce spell DCs by 2; **Cha** 20; **Skills** Deception +21, Imitation +7, Perform (act) +14, Perform (keyboard) +18, Perform (oratory) +14, Persuasion +18.
