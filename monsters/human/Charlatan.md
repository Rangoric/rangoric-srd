A charlatan is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. Using tricks, the charlatan imitates a mage, a priest, or an official.

A charlatan speaks Common and Elven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: Using Bluff or Disguise, the rogue avoids combat.

**During Combat**: The rogue uses Improved Feint to make sneak attacks while trying to escape.

**Rogue Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th, concentration +5): 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_; 2/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_.
