A barmaid (commoner) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Commoner|commoner]]. A barmaid isn't above passing messages or slipping a dose of sleeping poison into a drink if the price is right.

A barmaid (commoner) speaks Common.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The commoner tries to talk her way out of a fight, and uses her dagger if that fails.
