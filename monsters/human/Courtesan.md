A courtesan is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 12 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Courtesans specialize in music and other pleasures. They often serve as information brokers and spies.

A courtesan speaks Common and Gnome.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The bard casts greater invisibility, then confusion or rainbow pattern to lead enemies astray.
