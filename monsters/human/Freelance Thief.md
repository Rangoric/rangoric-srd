A freelance thief is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The freelance thief sells his loyalty for gold.

A freelance thief speaks Common and Halfling.

### Combat
