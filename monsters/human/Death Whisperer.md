A death whisperer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 19 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The death whisperer ends lives with a quiet sigh, freeing a last breath and a soul with one strike.

A death whisperer speaks Common.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The rogue uses Spring Attack and Lightning Stance to reach opponents, and uses his rapier of puncturing to make sneak attacks.
