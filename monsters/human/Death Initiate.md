A death initiate is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] and 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Assassin|assassin]]. These monks deal swift and dispassionate death to those who threaten or cross their monastery.

A death initiate speaks Auran, Common, and Infernal.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The assassin drinks her potion of barkskin, attempts to study her victim for 3 rounds, and drinks her potion of haste.

**During Combat**: Disguised as a modest pilgrim, the assassin sidles up to her mark and makes her death attack with a Stunning Fist attack. She then retreats to throw flurries of shuriken.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, the assassin's statistics are **AC** 23, touch 21, flat-footed 17.

**Assassin spells known** (CL 4th; concentration +6): 2nd (2/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat’s grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_; 1st (4/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_.
