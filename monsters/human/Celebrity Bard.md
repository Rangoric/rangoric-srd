A celebrity bard is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 11 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. A celebrity bard is a legendary performer, and the wealthy and powerful clamor for her to grace them with a performance. Celebrity bards often entertain at the request of a queen or general (CR 12).

A celebrity bard speaks Common, Draconic, and Elven.
