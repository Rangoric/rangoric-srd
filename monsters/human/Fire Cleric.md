A fire cleric is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 11 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]]. It is a cleric of Asmodeus. The fire cleric serves infernal or elemental powers and uses his magic to purge weakness from the world.

A fire cleric speaks Common, Ignan, and Infernal.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The cleric casts freedom of movement and resist energy (electricity).

**During Combat**: The cleric casts fire shield (warm shield), uses his wand of shield of faith, then attacks with fire spells, switching to blade barrier, channeled energy, and flame strike against fire-resistant opponents.
