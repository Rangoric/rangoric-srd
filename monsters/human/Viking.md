A viking is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]] and 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. Vikings are riders of the waves, marauding plunderers from the frozen northern lands who are always ready to pillage with axe and spear and blazing torch. They are boastful and proud, shouting battle cries to their savage gods for the glory of combat. Vikings can serve as elite marines or mobile shock troops. A lone viking may travel with a minstrel to chronicle his journeys (CR 8) or with a first mate and captain (CR 12), while five vikings might serve as honor guard to a chieftain (CR 13).

A viking speaks Common.

### Combat

**Base Statistics**: When not raging, the viking has **AC** 22, touch 12, flat-footed 20; **hp** 69; **Fort** +12; **Will** +5; **Melee** +1 battleaxe +14/+9 (1d8+6/19–20/×3) or shortspear +12/+7 (1d6+4); **Ranged** throwing axe +11 (1d6+5) or shortspear +10 (1d6+4); **Str** 18; **Con** 14; **CMB** +12 (+16 to overrun); Climb +6, Swim +10.
