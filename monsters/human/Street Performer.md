A street performer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. A bard without a regular tavern as a base can busk in various streets and markets.

A street performer speaks Common.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The bard stays at a safe distance, supporting allies with music and using spells to deter fights.

**Bard spells known** (CL 2nd; concentration +5): 1st (3/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]* (DC 14), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]* (DC 14); 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]*.
