An undead slayer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 16 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. An undead slayer's sole purpose is to destroy undead.

An undead slayer speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin, delay poison, freedom of movement, longstrider, protection from energy (cold, fire), and resist energy (electricity).

**During Combat**: The ranger allows herself to be surrounded by weak undead so she can use Great Cleave and Power Attack.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, delay poison, longstrider, protection from energy, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 18; **Immune** none; **Resist** none; **Speed** 30 ft; **Skills** Acrobatics +17.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 13th; concentration +15): 4th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]*; 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]* (2); 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom|owl's wisdom]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]* (2); 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Calm Animals|calm animals]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*.
