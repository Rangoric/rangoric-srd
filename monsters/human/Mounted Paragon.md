A mounted paragon is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 16 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. This holy warrior is a skilled rider who dispenses harsh justice at the point of a lance.

A mounted paragon speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The paladin casts delay poison and resist energy (fire).

**During Combat**: The paladin fights from horseback with his lance, positioning himself where he can attack the enemy leader using Spirited Charge. He uses Improved Overrun and Trample to pass and crush creatures in his way. If he is unhorsed and mounting again is dangerous or impractical, he casts holy sword on his mace and fights on foot.

**Base Statistics**: Without delay poison and resist energy, the paladin's statistics are **Immune** charm, disease, fear; **Resist** none.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +18): At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]*.

**Paladin spells prepared** (CL 13th; concentration +15): 4th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Sword|holy sword]]*; 3rd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal Mount|heal mount]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|prayer]]*; 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Paralysis|remove paralysis]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*; 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]* (2), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]* (2).
