A local celebrity is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/human/Human|human]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Celebrity bards love the spotlight and their throngs of admiring fans.

A local celebrity speaks Common, Elven, and Infernal.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The bard uses bardic performance to bolster his entourage, and spells to impede enemies.
