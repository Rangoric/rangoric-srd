A grizzled rider is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/goblin/Goblin|goblin]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the goblin riders. Goblins love riding ferocious mounts into battle.

A grizzled rider speaks Goblin.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The grizzled rider prefers to stay out of melee, using Rapid Shot to attack enemies multiple times while staying away from dangerous opponents.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 3rd; concentration +4): 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*.
