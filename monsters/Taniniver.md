Taninivers are a degenerate race of diseased dragons. A taniniver's body is alive but constantly rotting. Wracked by never-ending pain, with the stench of its own decaying flesh so strong it nearly overwhelms the vile creature's enhanced senses, the foul taniniver spends most of its time in magical research to reverse the progression of its diseases or, failing that, to stave off further deterioration. Taninivers often ally with cults of undeath or dragonkind.

Taninivers speak Common, Draconic and Undercommon.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 20th; concentration +25): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deathwatch|deathwatch]]_; 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Serious Wounds|inflict serious wounds]]_ (+8 vs Will); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eyebite|eyebite]]_ (+11 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Horrid Wilting|horrid wilting]]_ (+13 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Pain|symbol of pain]]_ (+10 vs Fortitude).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Breath Weapon|Breath Weapon]] (Ex)**: A taniniver's breath weapon is a hideous gray cloud of disease particles. Any creature in the area is attacked at +17 vs Fortitude, and on a hit contracts [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Mummy Rot|mummy rot]]. The disease is contracted immediately (the onset period does not apply) and is an instantaneous effect. The disease's ongoing attacks use the dragon's breath weapon attack bonus. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Disease|Disease]] (Ex)**: A taniniver's natural attacks infect its opponent with a random disease from the following list: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Blinding Sickness|blinding sickness]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Bubonic Plague|bubonic plague]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Cackle Fever|cackle fever]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Leprosy|leprosy]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Mindfire|mindfire]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Shakes|shakes]]. The first attack of these diseases uses the breath weapon's attack bonus.
