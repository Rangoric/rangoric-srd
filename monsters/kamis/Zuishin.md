While all kami are enemies of oni, none are so fervent as the zuishin. Known also as shrine kami, zuishin take as their wards gates, doorways, religious places, and the spiritual archways known as torii, ensuring that the gates are respected. Zuishin can use many weapons, but tend to favor the bow. A zuishin treats its equipment with utmost care, viewing weapons as extensions of itself. It never discards its bow voluntarily; if the weapon is ever lost or destroyed, a zuishin can craft a new one in a month.

Zuishin treat other kami with respect, and are always willing to lend a helping hand. Creatures other than kami must earn a zuishin's trust before receiving its aid, and do so by honoring the gate it guards or providing an offering to the kami, such as a tree branch or other small piece of nature.

Zuishin manifest as suits of armor with a partly manifest [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warrior|warrior]] within, and wield glowing weapons. They stand 5 feet tall and weigh 120 pounds.

Zuishins speak Common.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 13th; concentration +18): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_; 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Evil|dispel evil]]_ (+10), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Healing Arrow|Healing Arrow]] (Su)**: As a swift action, a zuishin can infuse an arrow it fires to carry any of the following effects: breath of life, cure light wounds, heal, neutralize poison, remove curse, remove disease, or restoration. Using one of these effects consumes a use of the same spell-like ability. The zuishin must make a touch attack to deliver the effect to the target—the target takes no damage from the arrow.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Holy Weapons|Holy Weapons]] (Su)**: Any weapon wielded by a zuishin is treated as if it had the holy special ability. A zuishin creates arrows out of nothing as part of its attacks with any bow it wields.
