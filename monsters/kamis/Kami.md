# Kami

Kami are a race of native outsiders who serve to protect what they call “wards”—animals, plants, objects, and even locations—from being harmed or dishonored.

### Combat

Kami have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^kami|kami subtype]]: immunity to bleed, mind-affecting effects, petrification, and polymorph effects, resistance to acid, electricity, and fire, telepathy, no need to eat, drink, or breathe, fast healing near their ward, and the power to merge with it.
