# Psychopomp

Psychopomps are neutral outsiders native to Purgatory. They speak Abyssal, Celestial, and Infernal.

### Combat

Psychopomps have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^psychopomp|psychopomp subtype]]: darkvision and low-light vision, damage reduction, immunity to death effects, disease, and poison, resistance to cold and electricity, natural weapons that count as ghost touch, and spiritsense that tells the living from the undead.
