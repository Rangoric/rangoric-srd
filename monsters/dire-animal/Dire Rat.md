Dire rats grow up to 2 feet long and weigh up to 25 pounds. They are common menaces in dungeons and city sewers alike.

Dire rats are omnivorous scavengers, but will attack to defend their nests and territories.

### Combat

Dire rat packs attack fearlessly, biting and chewing with their sharp incisors.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Disease|Disease]] (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|Filth Fever]]: Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 11; *onset* 1d3 days; *frequency* 1/day; *effect* 1d3 Dex damage and 1d3 Con damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves. The save DC is Constitution-based.
