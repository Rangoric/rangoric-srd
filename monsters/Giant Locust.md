Giant locusts have a hunger akin to their smaller cousins, but only rarely do they gather in the endless numbers of their tiny kin.

A mature giant locust measures just over 4 feet long and weighs 50 pounds.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Leap|Leap]] (Ex)**: A giant locust can take 10 on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to jump even if distracted or in danger.

**Spit (Ex)**: A giant locust can spit a brown jet of acid and partially digested food up to 30 feet with no range increment.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Voracious|Voracious]] (Ex)**: A giant locust's bite attack deals double damage to creatures with the plant subtype and to objects made of paper, wood, or other plant materials.
