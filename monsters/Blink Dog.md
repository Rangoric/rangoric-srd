The blink dog is an intelligent canine that has a limited teleportation ability.

Blink dogs have their own language, a mixture of barks, yaps, whines, and growls that can transmit complex information.

### Combat

Blink dogs hunt in packs, teleporting in a seemingly random fashion until they surround their prey, allowing some of them to take advantage of flanking.

**Blink (Su)**: A blink dog can use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]] as the spell (caster level 8th), and can evoke or end the effect as a free action.

**Dimension Door (Su)**: A blink dog can teleport, as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]] (caster level 8th), once per round as a free action. The ability affects only the blink dog, which never appears within a solid object and can act immediately after teleporting.
