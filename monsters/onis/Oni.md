# Oni

Oni are evil spirits who take humanoid form to become native outsiders.

### Combat

Oni have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^oni|oni subtype]]: darkvision and low-light vision, the power to change shape into other humanoid forms, a humanoid subtype of their own, and regeneration whose weakness depends on the kind of oni.
