The tyrannosaurus is an apex predator that measures 40 feet long and weighs 14,000 pounds.

Despite its enormous size, a tyrannosaurus is a swift runner. Its head is nearly 6 feet long, and its teeth are from 3 to 6 inches in length.

### Combat

A tyrannosaurus pursues and eats just about anything it sees. Its tactics are simple—charge in and bite.

**Powerful Bite (Ex)**: A tyrannosaurus applies twice its Strength modifier to bite damage.
