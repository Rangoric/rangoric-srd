# Tylosaurus

- Gargantuan [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|Animal]]

- **Initiative**: +1
- **Speed**: 20 ft. (4 squares), swim 50 ft.
- **Armor Class**: 20 (+1 Dex, +1 dodge, +12 natural, –4 size), touch 8, flat-footed 18
- **Base Attack**: +7; **CMB**: +23 (+27 grapple); **CMD**: 35 (39 vs. trip)
- **Attack**: Bite +15 melee (2d8+18 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]])
- **Full Attack**: Bite +15 melee (2d8+18 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]])
- **Space/Reach**: 15 ft./15 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swallow Whole|Swallow whole]] (2d6+12 bludgeoning, AC 16, hp 10)

- **Defenses**: Fort 25, Ref 18, Will 14
- **Abilities**: Str 34, Dex 13, Con 22, Int 2, Wis 13, Cha 9
- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] +20, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +14
- **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]]

- **Challenge Rating**: 8
- **Treasure**: None
- **Alignment**: Usually neutral
- **Advancement**: —
- **Level Adjustment**: —

This predatory marine lizard swims with four flippered limbs and a powerful, fluke-like tail. Its extended snout is filled with sharp teeth, and it feeds on both aquatic and surface prey. They are known to attack ships, mistaking them for basking whales or other creatures. A tylosaurus grows up to 50 feet in length and weighs 30,000 pounds.
