The ankylosaurus is a powerful, squat dinosaur more than capable of defending itself against enemies. Its back is heavily armored with thick bony plates and spikes. A solid blow from an ankylosaurus's tail can leave most creatures [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] long enough for the armored dinosaur to make good an escape, although once confronted, most ankylosauruses are too ill-tempered and stubborn to flee. Instead, they stand their ground and use their tails to great effect in battle. An ankylosaurus is 30 feet long and weighs 6,000 pounds.

### Combat

**Stun (Ex)**: The ankylosaurus's tail can deliver a powerful, stunning blow. A creature struck by this attack must make a DC 23 save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1 round. If the strike is a critical hit and the target fails its save, it is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Strength-based.
