Werecrocodiles merge the ruthless determination of a crocodile with the intelligence and adaptability of a humanoid. This fusion creates a maliciously cunning creature with no doubt of its own martial superiority.

The werecrocodile presented here is based on a human with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]; the class levels are the human form’s.

Werecrocodiles speak Common.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Death Roll|Death Roll]] (Ex)**: When grappling a foe of its size or smaller, a werecrocodile can perform a death roll upon making a successful grapple check. As it clings to its foe, the werecrocodile deals its bite damage, knocks the creature [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]], and maintains the grapple.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Sprint|Sprint]] (Ex)**: Once per minute, a werecrocodile can increase its land speed to 60 feet for 1 round.
