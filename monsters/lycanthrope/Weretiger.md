Weretigers in humanoid form tend to be sleekly muscular, taller than average, and very agile.

## Combat

Weretigers can assume a hybrid form as well as an animal form. In tiger form, they can [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Pounce|pounce]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]] just as normal tigers do. In hybrid form, they rely on their deadly claws.

**Alternate Form (Su)**: A weretiger can assume a bipedal hybrid form or the form of a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tiger|tiger]].

**Curse of Lycanthropy (Su)**: Any humanoid or giant hit by a weretiger’s bite attack in animal or hybrid form must succeed on a DC 15 Fortitude save or contract lycanthropy.

**Improved Grab (Ex)**: To use this ability, a weretiger in tiger form must hit with a claw or bite attack. It can then attempt to start a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity. If it succeeds on the grapple check, it establishes a hold and can rake.

**Pounce (Ex)**: If a weretiger in tiger form [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charges]] an opponent, it can make a full attack, including two [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Rake|rake]] attacks.

**Rake (Ex)**: Attack bonus +9 melee, damage 1d8+3.

**Tiger Empathy (Ex)**: Communicate with tigers and dire tigers, and +4 racial bonus on Charisma-based checks against tigers and dire tigers.

**Skills**: In any form, weretigers have a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. \*In areas of tall grass or heavy undergrowth, the bonus on Stealth checks is +4 higher in the tiger form.

The weretiger presented here is a 1st-level human warrior and natural lycanthrope, using the following base ability scores: Str 13, Dex 11, Con 12, Int 10, Wis 9, Cha 8.
