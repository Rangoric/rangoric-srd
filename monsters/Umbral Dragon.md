# Umbral Dragon

The umbral dragon, a primal dragon, is named in the [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|fetchling]]’s description. The general dragon rules (age categories, frightful presence, crush and tail sweep) are in [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dragon, True|Dragon, True]]. An umbral dragon has [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Negative Energy Affinity|negative energy affinity]] through its umbral scion ability.

The stat blocks are Pathfinder’s, with skills renamed to Rangoric’s; their totals are Pathfinder’s, close enough.

## Umbral Dragon Abilities

**Breath Weapon (Su)**: Although it deals negative energy damage, an umbral dragon's breath weapon does not heal undead creatures.

**Create Shadows (Su)**: Any creature slain by an ancient or older umbral dragon rises as a shadow (if 8 HD or less) or greater shadow (if above 8 HD) under the umbral dragon's control 1d4 rounds later.

**Energy Drain (Su)**: A great wyrm umbral dragon deals 1 negative level with each successful bite or claw attack (1 level, +22 vs Fortitude).

**Ghost Bane (Su)**: A young or older umbral dragon's physical attacks deal damage to incorporeal creatures normally.

**Umbral Scion (Ex)**: Umbral dragons have negative energy affinity and are immune to energy drain and death effects.

**Shadow Breath (Su)**: Three times per day, an adult or older umbral dragon can breathe a cone of shadows. The shadows attack Fortitude, and creatures they hit are blinded for 1d4 rounds and take 1 point of Str drain per age category possessed by the dragon. A miss negates the blindness and reduces Str drain to 1d4 points.

**Spell-Like Abilities (Sp)**: An umbral dragon gains the following spell-like abilities, usable at will (unless indicated otherwise) on reaching the listed age category. Young—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_; Juvenile—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]_; Adult—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]]_; Old—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Project Image|project image]]_; Ancient—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]]_ (3/day), Great wyrm—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shades|shades]]_. An umbral dragon can cast cleric spells as arcane spells.
