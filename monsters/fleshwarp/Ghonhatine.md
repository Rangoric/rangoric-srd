# Ghonhatine

- **Hit Dice**: 12d8+108 (162 hp)
- **Initiative**: –2
- **Speed**: 40 ft. (8 squares)
- **Armor Class**: 24 (–2 Dex, +17 natural, –1 size), touch 7, flat-footed 24
- **Base Attack**: +9; **CMB**: +18 (+20 bull rush); **CMD**: 26 (28 vs. bull rush)
- **Attack**: Claw +16 melee (1d6+8) or bite +16 melee (2d6+8) or tail slap +19 melee (1d8+4) or regurgitate +6 ranged (2d6 acid plus [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]])
- **Full Attack**: 2 claws +16 melee (1d6+8) and bite +16 melee (2d6+8) and tail slap +14 melee (1d8+4); or regurgitate +6 ranged (2d6 acid plus [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]])
- **Space/Reach**: 10 ft./10 ft. (15 ft. with tail)
- **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Feed|Feed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]]
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Powerful Stench|powerful stench]] (10 ft., DC 24, 1d4 rounds), [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Immunity|immunity]] to acid, critical hits, disease and poison

Troglodytes forced to undergo fleshwarping by the drow, ghonhatines harken to what troglodytes might have been in savage prehistory. They stand over 10 feet tall (hunched to about 8 feet) and weigh upward of 5,000 pounds.

Ghonhatines speak Draconic.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Feed|Feed]] (Su)**: By spending a full-round action devouring the body of a dead or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] creature, a ghonhatine gains 1d8+9 temporary hit points and a +2 bonus on attack and damage rolls for 1 minute. The bonus to hit points is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|Filth Fever]] (Ex)**: Disease—injury; save Fort DC 25; onset 1d3 days; frequency 1 day; effect 1d3 Dex damage and 1d3 Con damage; cure 2 consecutive saves. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Powerful Stench|Powerful Stench]] (Ex)**: An enraged ghonhatine secretes a tarry, musk-like chemical. Any living, non-ghonhatine creature within 10 feet must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] as long as it remains within the affected area and for 1d4 rounds afterward. A creature that saves is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] as long as it remains in the area, and can't be affected again by the same ghonhatine's stench for 24 hours. This is a poison effect. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Regurgitate|Regurgitate]] (Ex)**: A ghonhatine can expel the contents of its stomach as a ranged attack with a splash weapon that has a range increment of 20 feet. It deals 2d6 acid damage to the target and splashes all adjacent creatures. In addition to taking damage, a target directly hit by a ghonhatine's regurgitation must make two DC 24 Fortitude saves, the first to resist contracting [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]], and the second to avoid being [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 minute. A nauseated creature can end its nausea early by dousing itself in a gallon of water. All creatures adjacent to the target must make DC 24 Fortitude saves to avoid being [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute. Once a ghonhatine uses this ability it can't use it again until it feeds. The save DCs are Constitution-based.
