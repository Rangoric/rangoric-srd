An infernal’s natural weapons are treated as epic, evil, and chaotic-aligned or lawful-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

#### Combat

Improved Grab (Ex): If an infernal hits with a claw, it deals normal damage and attempts to start a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Grapple|grapple]] as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity. Infernals can use this ability on Large and smaller creatures. The infernal has the option to conduct the grapple normally, or simply use its claw to hold the opponent. Each successful grapple check it makes during successive rounds automatically deals standard claw damage, in addition to automatic spell suck damage.

Spell Suck (Su): If an infernal bites a foe, the foe loses one of its highest-level prepared spells or one of the spell slots for this day that it has not used. The victim chooses the prepared spell to lose. If the victim has no prepared spells or unused spell slots (either because it has exhausted its spellcasting for the day or because the victim is not a spellcaster), the bite instead deals 2 points of temporary Intelligence [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]].

Learned Spell Immunity (Su): If an infernal is affected by a spell cast by a particular spellcaster, the infernal thereafter becomes immune to that spell when cast by that spellcaster.

Spell-Like Abilities: Caster level 26th; save DC 19 + spell level. The DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blasphemy|blasphemy]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead|create undead]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Chaos|detect chaos]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|detect law]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Good|magic circle against good]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Image|major image]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Produce Flame|produce flame]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph|polymorph]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Pyrotechnics|pyrotechnics]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]] (self plus 1,000 pounds), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unhallow|unhallow]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Fire|wall of fire]];

Summon Fiend (Sp): An infernal can summon four [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Balor|balors]] or four [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Pit Fiend|pit fiends]] per day (lawful infernals summon pit fiends, and chaotic infernals summon balors).

Abomination Traits: Immune to polymorphing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrification]], and other form-altering attacks; not subject to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|energy drain]], ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], or ability [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]], or death from massive damage; immune to mind-affecting effects; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|fire resistance]] 20; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|cold resistance]] 20; [[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]; [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]] at will; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^blindsight|blindsight]] 500 ft.; telepathy out to 1,000 ft.

Regeneration (Ex): Infernals take normal damage from good weapons. Lawful infernals also take normal damage from chaotic weapons, and vice versa.
