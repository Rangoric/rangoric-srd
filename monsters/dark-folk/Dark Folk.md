# Dark Folk

Dark folk are reclusive subterranean humanoids with an aversion to light; what every one of them shares is in the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^dark-folk|dark folk subtype]]. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dark-folk/Dark Creeper|dark creepers]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dark-folk/Dark Stalker|dark stalkers]] are the clans’ thieves and masters; the other dark folk are below.
