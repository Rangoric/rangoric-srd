# Dark Dancer

- **Hit Dice**: 2d8+4 (13 hp)
- **Initiative**: +4
- **Speed**: 30 ft. (6 squares)
- **Armor Class**: 15 (+4 Dex, +1 size), touch 15, flat-footed 11
- **Base Attack**: +1; **CMB**: +0; **CMD**: 14

- **Full Attack**: [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Club|Club]] +2 melee (1d4); or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dagger|dagger]] +6 melee (1d3/19–20 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|poison]])
- **Space/Reach**: 5 ft./5 ft.

Though still clad in the tattered dark clothing worn by other dark folk, dark dancers appear to be the most decorated and festive of these strange humanoids. They are intermediaries between clans and factions, and thus clans can't restrict the actions of dark dancers in their realms. Dark dancers answer only to dark stalkers and dark callers.

Though fickle and erratic, dark dancers are well liked and usually welcome among clans for as long as they wish to stay. Yet they rarely stay long, instead insinuating themselves into dozens of underground communities over the course of a year. They serve as storytellers, entertainers, and messengers. One of their favorite stories to share is about the relationship between their kind and the owbs.

Though dark dancers are rare and weak, they are highly appreciated by dark folk society. Dark folk consider it a bad omen when a dark dancer dies. Dark dancers stand just under 4 feet tall and weigh 60 pounds.

Dark dancers speak Dark Folk.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Bardic Performance|Bardic Performance]] (Su)**: A dark dancer can use its dancing to help its allies. A dark dancer can use bardic performance as a 2nd-level bard, but only for distraction and inspire courage effects.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Dark Curse|Dark Curse]] (Su)**: As a standard action, a dark dancer can make a touch attack against a foe and curse it. The curse attacks the foe at +2 vs Will, and a miss resists the effects. Creatures it hits take a -2 penalty on all Dexterity and Charisma-based skill checks. This curse is permanent, but it attacks the victim again each day, and a miss ends it.

**Death Throes (Su)**: When a dark dancer is slain, its body combusts in a flash of bright white light, leaving its gear in a heap on the ground. All creatures within a 10-foot burst are attacked at +3 vs Fortitude, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1d6 rounds. Other dark folk within 20 feet are attacked at +3 vs Will, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
