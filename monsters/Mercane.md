# Mercane

Large [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^outsider|Outsider]]   

Hit Dice: 7d8+21 (52 hp)      
Initiative: +2 (Dex)      
Speed: 30 ft.      
AC: 15 (-1 size, +2 Dex, +4 natural)      
Base Attack/Grapple: +7/+13     
Attack: Masterwork falchion +9(2d4+3) melee     
Full Attack: Masterwork falchion +9/+4 (2d4+3) melee      
Space/Reach: 10 ft./10 ft.      
Special Qualities: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^SR|SR]] 25, spell-like abilities, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^telepathy|telepathy]]     
Saves: Fort +8, Ref +7, Will +8      
Abilities: Str 15, Dex 15, Con 16, Int 20, Wis 17, Cha 15      
Skills: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] +19, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] +15, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] +12, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] +12, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] +12, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (bookkeeper) +13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] +13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] +12, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] +15      
**Unspent Skill Points**: 21
Feats: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats All|Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Disarm|Improved Disarm]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats All|Improved Feint]]     
Climate/Terrain: Any land and underground      
Organization: Company (1-4 mercanes and 3-18 5th-level fighter bodyguards) or progression (1 mercane, 3 14th-level fighters, 1 21st-level fighter/2nd-level Union Sentinel)  
Challenge Rating: 5      
Treasure: Double standard      
Alignment: Always lawful neutral      
Advancement: By character class      
Level Adjustment: +7     

Mercanes speak Abyssal, Celestial, Draconic, Infernal, and at least two other languages.

#### Combat

Spell-Like Abilities: Caster level 14th; save DC 12 + spell level.  

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]];  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Secret Chest|secret chest]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]].   

Telepathy (Su): Mercanes can communicate telepathically with any creature within 100 feet that has a language.

Skills: Mercanes receive a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] checks.

### Mercane Characters

A mercane’s favored class is [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]]. Player character mercanes have a level adjustment of +7.
