# Spider

Spiders are eight-legged hunters and web-spinners. The giant ones are [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^vermin|vermin]]; those that come in sizes from Tiny to Colossal are described under [[rangoric-srd/monsters/monstrous-spider/Monstrous Spider|monstrous spiders]], and the kinds below are larger or stranger.

- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Giant Black Widow|Giant Black Widow]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Giant Crab Spider|Giant Crab Spider]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Giant Tarantula|Giant Tarantula]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Goliath Spider|Goliath Spider]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Ogre Spider|Ogre Spider]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Scarlet Spider|Scarlet Spider]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Spider Swarm|Spider Swarm]]
