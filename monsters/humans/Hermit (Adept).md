A hermit (adept) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/humans/Human|human]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Adept|adept]]. This filthy, ill-mannered hermit wants nothing but to be left alone. He knows many secrets that others have forgotten, but will fight anyone who dares to ask about them.

A hermit (adept) speaks Common.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The adept casts endure elements at the start of each day. He drinks his potion of barkskin if expecting a fight.

**During Combat**: The adept casts web, then hurls shock bullets with his sling at any exposed foes, all the while uttering vile epithets.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, the adept's statistics are **AC** 11, touch 11, flat-footed 11.

**Adept spells prepared** (CL 7th; concentration +10): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animal Trance|animal trance]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]_ (+5 vs Reflex); 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Purify Food and Drink|purify food and drink]]_.
