A master duelist is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/humans/Human|human]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] and 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Duelist|duelist]]. Paragons of swordplay, these master duelists constantly search for foes to defeat in order to enhance their already formidable reputations.

### Combat

**During Combat**: Constantly moving in order to take advantage of his Wind Stance, the duelist strikes other sword wielders first, attempting to suss out who among them is the most talented and then focusing his attentions on that one.
