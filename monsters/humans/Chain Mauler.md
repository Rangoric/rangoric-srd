A chain mauler is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/humans/Human|human]] with 14 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The chain mauler is a vicious sadist whose weapon allows her the bloodiest satisfaction.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The rogue uses trip and disarm combat maneuvers to hinder opponents before finishing them off with damaging attacks.
