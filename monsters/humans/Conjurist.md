A conjurist is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/humans/Human|human]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Conjuration School|conjuration]] school). Conjurists are arcanists who have studied the planes, irresistibly drawn to forbidden lore that shatters mind and morality. Conjurists may be exotic court mages or unusual war wizards. A conjurist will often keep a battle monk bodyguard or may have a hedge wizard or medium as an assistant (CR 7).

A conjurist speaks Abyssal, Aquan, Auran, Celestial, Common, Ignan, Infernal, and Terran.
