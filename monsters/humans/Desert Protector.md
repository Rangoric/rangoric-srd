A desert protector is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/humans/Human|human]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]] of [[rangoric-srd/divine/Pantheons#Japanese|Amaterasu]]. A desert protector watches over the bodies and souls of a nomadic desert community.

A desert protector speaks Common.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The paladin draws attention away from weaker allies, and heals allies who are competent fighters.
