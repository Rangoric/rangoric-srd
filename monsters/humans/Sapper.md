A sapper is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/humans/Human|human]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Expert|expert]]. Sappers specialize in undermining fortifications and tunneling under walls. Using a combination of time, engineering, and explosives, sappers can rig a tunnel to collapse to protect a retreat or create a path over a mountainside. Their enthusiasm for blowing things up means they take unnecessary risks, and the lifespan of a professional sapper is often cut short in a dramatic blast.

A sapper speaks Common, Dwarven, and Goblin.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The sapper throws alchemical splash weapons, avoiding melee combat as long as possible.
