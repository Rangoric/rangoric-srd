A dilettante is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/humans/Human|human]] with 12 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The dilettante developed magical abilities, but eventually decided to avoid both the arcane and divine paths.

A dilettante speaks Common, Dwarven, Elven, Gnome, and Halfling.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The rogue uses his wand of mage armor.

**During Combat**: If forced to fight, the rogue uses the disarm combat maneuver against his opponent's weapon to defuse the situation.

**Base Statistics**: Without mage armor, the rogue's statistics are **AC** 14, touch 14, flat-footed 11.

**Rogue Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th, concentration +16): 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]_; 2/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Comprehend Languages|comprehend languages]]_.
