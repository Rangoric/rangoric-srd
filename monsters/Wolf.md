# Wolf

  - Medium Animal
  - **Hit Dice**: 2d8+4 (13 hp)
  - **Initiative**: +2
  - **Speed**: 50 ft. (10 squares)
  - **Armor Class**: 14 (+2 Dex, +2 natural), touch 12, flat-footed 12
  - **Base Attack/Grapple**: +1/+2
  - **Attack**: Bite +3 melee (1d6+1)
  - **Full Attack**: Bite +3 melee (1d6+1)
  - **Space/Reach**: 5 ft./5 ft.
  - **Special Attacks**: Trip
  - **Special Qualities**: Low-light vision, scent
  - **Saves**: Fort +5, Ref +5, Will +1
  - **Abilities**: Str 13, Dex 15, Con 15, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 6
  - **Skills**: Fieldcraft +1\*, Perception +6, Stealth +2
  - **Feats**: TrackB, Weapon Focus (bite)
  - **Environment**: Temperate forests
  - **Organization**: Solitary, pair, or pack (7–16)
  - **Challenge Rating**: 1
  - **Advancement**: 3 HD (Medium); 4–6 HD (Large)
  - **Level Adjustment**: —

Wolves are pack hunters known for their persistence and cunning.

#### Combat

A favorite tactic is to send a few individuals against the foe’s front while the rest of the pack circles and attacks from the flanks or rear.

**Trip (Ex)**: A wolf that hits with a bite attack can attempt to trip the opponent (+1 check modifier) as a free action without making a touch attack or provoking an attack of opportunity. If the attempt fails, the opponent cannot react to trip the wolf.

**Skills**: \*Wolves have a +4 racial bonus on Fieldcraft checks when tracking by scent.
