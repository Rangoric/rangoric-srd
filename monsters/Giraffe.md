# Giraffe

- Huge [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|Animal]]
- **Hit Dice**: 4d8+12 (30 hp)
- **Initiative**: +2
- **Speed**: 50 ft. (10 squares)
- **Armor Class**: 16 (+2 Dex, +6 natural, –2 size), touch 10, flat-footed 14
- **Base Attack**: +3; **CMB**: +10; **CMD**: 22 (26 vs. trip)
- **Attack**: Hoof +11 melee (1d8+5) or slam +1 melee (1d8+2)
- **Full Attack**: 2 hooves +6 melee (1d8+5) and slam +1 melee (1d8+2)
- **Space/Reach**: 15 ft./10 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: —
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|Low-light vision]]
- **Defenses**: Fort 17, Ref 16, Will 12
- **Abilities**: Str 20, Dex 15, Con 16, Int 2, Wis 13, Cha 7
- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +8
- **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Run|Run]]
- **Environment**: Warm plains
- **Organization**: Solitary, pair, or herd (3–10)
- **Challenge Rating**: 3
- **Treasure**: None
- **Alignment**: Usually neutral
- **Advancement**: —
- **Level Adjustment**: —

These long-necked animals roam across plains in loose-knit groups, grazing on the upper reaches of acacia trees. Giraffes usually avoid conflict. However, when its young are threatened or if the giraffe is cornered, it attacks ruthlessly, striking with its powerful hooves. Giraffes rarely use their necks to make slam attacks unless they are fighting among themselves as part of their mating displays.

A giraffe is between 16 and 20 feet tall and weighs 3,500 pounds.

### Combat

**Natural Weapons (Ex)**: A giraffe's hoof attacks are primary attacks and its slam attack is a secondary attack.
