An axe dancer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orcs/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 8 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]]. Augmenting a monk's grace with the power of a greataxe, these half-orcs hack and crush their way to victory.

An axe dancer speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The monk uses Stunning Fist, Scorpion Style, and Greater Grapple.
