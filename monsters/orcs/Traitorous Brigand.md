A traitorous brigand is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orcs/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. Brigands are hard as nails and always look out for themselves first.

A traitorous brigand speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The fighter opens with an Intimidate check to demoralize the nearest enemy, then wades in with his axe using Cleave when possible.
