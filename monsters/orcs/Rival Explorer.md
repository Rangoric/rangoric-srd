A rival explorer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orcs/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 18 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Rival explorers compete against other adventurers, going after the same rewards.

A rival explorer speaks Common, Draconic, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The bard casts cat's grace and freedom of movement.

**During Combat**: The bard casts haste on herself, and makes hit-and-run attacks.

**Base Statistics**: Without cat's grace, the bard's statistics are **Init** +4; **Dex** 18; **Skills** Engineering +18, Escape Artist +18, Stealth +20.
