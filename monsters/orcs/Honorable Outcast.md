A honorable outcast is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orcs/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]] of [[rangoric-srd/divine/Pantheons#Greek and Roman|Hephaestus]]. Honorable outcasts are far from their homes and often found alone. Some have been questing in the wild for so long that they have lost any semblance of social graces and tact.

A honorable outcast speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The paladin prefers to attack with her bow before entering melee with her greataxe. She uses Power Attack unless she fights heavily armored enemies.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 2nd; concentration +3): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.
