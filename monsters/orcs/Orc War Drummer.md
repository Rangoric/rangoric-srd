An orc war drummer is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orcs/Orc|orc]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] (the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/bard/Savage Skald (Archetype)|savage skald]] archetype), one of the orc prodigies. The rare clever or artistic orcs may use their skills to bolster comrades—or to better compete with brutish tribemates.

An orc war drummer speaks Common, Goblin, and Orc.
