A skulking brute is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orcs/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The skulking brute extorts illegal tolls from travelers, attacking those who refuse to pay.

A skulking brute speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The rogue charges at the nearest foe.
