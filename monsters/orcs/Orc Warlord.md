An orc warlord is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orcs/Orc|orc]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]], one of the orc rulers. Its rage rounds include the [[rangoric-srd/races/class-bonuses/Orc Barbarian (Class Bonus)|orc barbarian class bonus]]. Leadership of an orc tribe or horde falls not to the smartest or most canny, but to the strongest.

An orc warlord speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The warlord rages before combat.
