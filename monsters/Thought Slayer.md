#### Combat

A thought slayer can spend a maximum of 10 continuous minutes on the Material Plane before its wispy flesh finally dissipates and it falls dead. If badly wounded, it escapes to the Ethereal Plane rather than continuing the fight.

Mind-Consuming Gaze (Su): Instantly kill (by draining the mind away), range 30 feet, Fortitude DC 19 negates. The save DC is Charisma-based. This is considered a mind-affecting death effect. If a thought slayer’s gaze is met indirectly (such as through a mirror or a reflection in water), the opponent does not die on a failed save but instead is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 14 rounds. This otherwise is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Gaze|gaze]] effect.

The gaze cannot affect creatures on the Material Plane while the thought slayer remains ethereal, but it can affect ethereal creatures while the thought slayer is on the Material Plane.

Psi-Like **Abilities:**At will - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Brain Lock|brain lock]] (any nonmindless, DC 14\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Chameleon|chameleon]], [[rangoric-srd/powers/Detect Psionics|detect psionics]], [[rangoric-srd/powers/Distract|distract]] (DC 13); 3/day - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Body Adjustment|body adjustment]] (5d12\*), [[rangoric-srd/psionics/Elocater|mental barrier]] (+6 AC, 2 rounds\*), [[rangoric-srd/psionics/Elocater|mind trap]] (10 rounds\*), [[rangoric-srd/powers/Precognition|precognition]], [[rangoric-srd/powers/Wall Walker|wall walker]]; 1/day - [[rangoric-srd/powers/Escape Detection|escape detection]]. Manifester level 14th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.

\*Includes augmentation for the thought slayer’s manifester level.

Ethereal Jaunt (Su): A thought slayer can shift from the Ethereal Plane to the Material Plane as part of any move action, and then shift back again as a free action. The ability is otherwise identical to the [[rangoric-srd/powers/Ethereal Jaunt, Psionic|psionic ethereal jaunt]] power.
