Garudas are noble, birdlike creatures that inhabit rugged hills. While they remain detached from humanoid societies, they are impetuous and gallant, often serving as protectors of nearby communities.

Most garudas stand around 6 feet tall with a wingspan of 15 feet and weigh approximately 150 pounds.

Garudas speak Common and Garuda.

### Combat

**Spells**: Garudas cast spells as 7th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerers]].

**Spells known** (CL 7th; concentration +10): 3rd (5/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_; 2nd (7/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Arrows|protection from arrows]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_; 1st (7/day)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]_ (+4), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Hatred|Hatred]] (Ex)**: Garudas receive a +1 racial bonus on attack and damage rolls against nagas and other serpentine monsters of the aberration type.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swooping Pounce|Swooping Pounce]] (Ex)**: When a garuda makes a diving aerial charge, it can make a full attack with its natural weapons.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Talon or Wing|Talon or Wing]] (Ex)**: A garuda cannot use its wing attacks while flying, and cannot use its talon attacks while not flying.
