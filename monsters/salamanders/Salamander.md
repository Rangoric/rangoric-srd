# Salamander

- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/salamanders/Flamebrother Salamander|Flamebrother Salamander]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/salamanders/Average Salamander|Average Salamander]]
- [[rangoric-srd/monsters/salamanders/Noble Salamander|Noble Salamander]]

Salamanders speak Ignan. Some average salamanders and all nobles also speak Common.

### Combat

If a salamander has damage reduction, its natural weapons are treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].

**Constrict (Ex)**: A salamander deals automatic tail slap damage (including fire damage) with a successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] check. A noble salamander can constrict multiple creatures simultaneously, provided they are all at least two sizes smaller than it.

**Heat (Ex)**: A salamander generates so much heat that its mere touch deals additional fire damage. Salamanders’ metallic weapons also conduct this heat.

**Improved Grab (Ex)**: To use this ability, a salamander must hit a creature of up to one size larger than itself with its tail slap attack. It can then attempt to start a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity. If it wins the grapple check, it establishes a hold and can constrict.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: (Noble salamanders only) Caster level 15th. The attack bonuses are Charisma-based.

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]] (+3 vs Reflex), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]] (+5 vs Reflex), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Flaming Sphere|flaming sphere]] (+4 vs Reflex), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Fire|wall of fire]] (+6);

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VII|summon monster VII]] (Huge fire elemental).

**Skills**: Salamanders have a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (blacksmithing) checks.

**Feats**: Salamanders have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Multiattack|Multiattack]] feat even though they do not have the requisite three natural weapons.
