A dragon chieftain is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 10 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] and 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Dragon Disciple|dragon disciple]]. These half-orcs become chieftains of savage tribes by brutally and publicly assassinating the former chieftains.

A dragon chieftain speaks Common, Draconic, Goblin, and Orc.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The dragon disciple casts greater heroism and haste, then uses dominate person and charm person to create allies among his enemies. In melee, he uses his breath weapon and Dazzling Display.
