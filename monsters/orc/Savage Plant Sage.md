A savage plant sage is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]].

A savage plant sage speaks Common, Druidic, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The druid casts barkskin on himself.

**During Combat**: The druid casts entangle or summon swarm.

**Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +5): 4/day—_[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|wooden fist]]_.
