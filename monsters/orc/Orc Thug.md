An orc thug is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc|orc]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], one of the orc prodigies. The rare clever or artistic orcs may use their skills to bolster comrades—or to better compete with brutish tribemates.

An orc thug speaks Common, Goblin, and Orc.
