An angel slayer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 15 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. An angel slayer decries false gods and their messengers.

An angel slayer speaks Abyssal, Celestial, Common, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin, cat's grace, freedom of movement, longstrider, pass without trace, protection from energy (cold), and resist energy (electricity, fire).

**During Combat**: The ranger uses her bow against flying targets, but prefers melee and uses a potion of fly if her prey won't land.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, cat's grace, longstrider, protection from energy, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **Init** +5; **AC** 19, touch 12, flat-footed 18; **Ref** +15; **Immune** none; **Resist** none; **Speed** 30 ft; **Ranged** masterwork composite longbow +17/+12/+7 (1d8+6/×3); **Dex** 13; **CMD** 33; **Skills** Animal Handling +8, Stealth +18.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 12th; concentration +14): 4th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_; 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_ (2).
