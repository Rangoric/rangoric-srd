An orc scout is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc|orc]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], one of the orc seekers. Any orc warband needs skilled members such as scouts or gorthek riders to make first contact with the enemy.

An orc scout speaks Common and Orc.
