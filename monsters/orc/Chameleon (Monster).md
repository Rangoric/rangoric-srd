A chameleon (monster) is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 11 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Chameleons are more comfortable portraying other people than appearing in their own natural forms.

A chameleon (monster) speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The bard drinks her potion of bull's strength and potion of eagle's splendor.

**During Combat**: The bard casts greater invisibility, then enters melee, using haste to augment her attacks or slow to hinder foes.

**Base Statistics**: Without bull's strength and eagle's splendor, the bard's statistics are **Bard Melee** +2 falchion +13/+8 (2d4+5/18-20); **Spells Known** reduce spell DCs by 2; **Str** 14; **Cha** 17; **CMB** +10 (+12 sunder); **CMD** 23; **Skills** Athletics +7, Intimidation +10, Perform (act) +17, Perform (dance) +17, Perform (oratory) +17.
