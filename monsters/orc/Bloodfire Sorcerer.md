A bloodfire sorcerer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. The bloodfire sorcerer withers her enemies with flame and quickens her allies with hot-blooded ferocity.

A bloodfire sorcerer speaks Common, Ignan, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The sorcerer casts false life and mage armor.

**During Combat**: The sorcerer casts haste before targeting her foes with fireball or scorching ray. In melee combat, she casts shield on herself, and magic weapon on her falchion.

**Base Statistics**: Without false life and mage armor, the sorcerer's statistics are **AC** 13, touch 11, flat-footed 12; **hp** 48.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 7th; concentration +10): 6/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Elemental Bloodline|elemental ray]] (1d6+3 fire).
