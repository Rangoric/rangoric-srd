A desert stalker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 14 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]. The desert stalker snares his prey and bleeds it dry.

A desert stalker speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The ranger casts barkskin, endure elements, freedom of movement, longstrider, pass without trace, protection from energy (fire), and resist energy (electricity).

**During Combat**: The ranger casts wind wall to separate foes, then uses Improved Shield Bash to manipulate positioning.

**Base Statistics**: Without barkskin, longstrider, protection from energy, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics are **AC** 23, touch 14, flat-footed 21; **Immune** none; **Resist** none; **Speed** 30 ft; **Skills** Acrobatics +11.

**Ranger spells prepared** (CL 11th; concentration +13): 4th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_; 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Wall|wind wall]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_.
