A rage flame is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 19 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]]. These druids dwell in the most explosive spots in the world.

A rage flame speaks Auran, Common, Draconic, Druidic, Giant, Ignan, Orc, and Terran.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The druid casts shambler once per week and casts ironwood on his armor every 19 days.

**During Combat**: The druid sends shambling mounds into combat, casts mass cat's grace, wild shapes into a Huge fire elemental, and moves in to flank.
