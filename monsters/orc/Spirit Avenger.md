A spirit avenger is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]] and 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] and 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Eldritch Knight|eldritch knight]]. Dedicated to protecting the world from evil outsiders and undead, spirit avengers realize it's better to contain an enemy than to destroy it.

A spirit avenger speaks Celestial, Common, Giant, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The eldritch knight casts false life.

**During Combat**: The knight starts combat with shield and summoning spells, letting conjured allies run interference while he contains or damages his foes.

**Base Statistics**: Without false life, the eldritch knight's statistics are **hp** 125.
