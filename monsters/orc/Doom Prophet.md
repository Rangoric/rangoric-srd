A doom prophet is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Adept|adept]]. A doom prophet preaches his dire portents to any who listen.

A doom prophet speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The adept reads his scrolls of bear's endurance and bull's strength.

**During Combat**: The adept casts darkness on his mace, then casts scorching ray or burning hands.

**Base Statistics**: Without bear's endurance and bull's strength, the adept's statistics are **hp** 22; **Fort** +1; **Melee** masterwork heavy mace +5 (1d8+2); **Ranged** masterwork sling +3 (1d4+2); **Str** 14; **Con** 11; **CMB** +4; **CMD** 14.

**Adept spells prepared** (CL 4th; concentration +6): 2nd—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*; 1st—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]* (DC 13), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]* (DC 13); 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guidance|guidance]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]*.
