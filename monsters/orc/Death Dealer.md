A death dealer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 19 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]]. These fearsome killers seek to feed their dark gods with the souls of those they slice apart with their powerful scythes—weapons powerful enough to cut through both flesh and spirit.

A death dealer speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The barbarian uses potions if expecting a major confrontation. His ring of blinking operates continuously.

**During Combat**: The barbarian begins combat with Dazzling Display and terrifying howl. Against a small group, he makes Flyby Attacks, using Lunge and potions of enlarge person to extend his reach and avoid attacks of opportunity, and Great Cleave against targets clustered together. In close combat, he uses intimidating glare along with Great Cleave attacks, using knockback to force enemies into close quarters. He relies on his ring of blinking to avoid most attacks, and with his tireless rage he exits and re-enters rage whenever it's convenient to regain the use rage powers he can use only once per rage.

**Base Statistics**: When not raging, the barbarian's statistics are **AC** 15, touch 12, flat-footed 13; **hp** 186; **Fort** +17; **Will** +10; no bonus vs. spells and spell-like or supernatural abilities, no bonus vs. enchantments; **Melee** +1 unholy ghost touch adamantine scythe +28/+23/+18/+13 (2d4+11/19-20/×4); **Ranged** masterwork composite longbow +22/+17/+12/+7 (1d8+7/×3); **Str** 24; **Con** 14; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 38; **Skills** Athletics +11, Intimidation +37.
