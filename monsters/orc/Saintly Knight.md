A saintly knight is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc#Half-Orcs|half-orc]] with 9 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. A saintly knight accomplishes great acts with his indomitable spirit and martial prowess.

A saintly knight speaks Common and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The paladin casts resist energy (fire) unless he expects damage from a different energy type. He applies silversheen to his weapon if he expects to fight devils or lycanthropes.

**During Combat**: The paladin uses Ride-By Attack and Spirited Charge, preferring to attack the opposing leader. When on foot, he uses his mace. If facing a powerful foe, he drinks his potions.

**Base Statistics**: Without resist energy, the paladin's statistics are **Resist** none.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +11): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.

**Paladin spells prepared** (CL 6th; concentration +8): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Zone of Truth|zone of truth]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Evil|protection from evil]]_.
