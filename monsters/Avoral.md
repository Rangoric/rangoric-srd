An avoral’s bones are strong but hollow, so even the largest specimens weigh no more than 120 pounds. An avoral is about 7 feet tall. Each of an avoral’s wings has a small hand at the midpoint. When the wings are folded, these appendages are about where human hands would be and can do nearly anything hands can do.

An avoral’s visual acuity is virtually unmatched. It can see detail on objects up to 10 miles away and is said to be able to discern the color of a creature’s eyes at 200 paces.

Avorals speak Celestial, Infernal, and Draconic, but can speak with almost any creature, thanks to their tongues ability.

#### Combat

On the ground, an avoral can lash out with its wings to deliver punishing blows. However, it prefers to meet its foes in the air, where it can employ its talons and make full use of its aerial speed and agility. It can’t make wing attacks while flying, however.

An avoral’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as good-aligned for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 8th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]] (self only), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]] (DC 14), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]] (DC 15), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]] (DC 16), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]] (self only), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]];

3/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]] (DC 16).

**Fear Aura (Su)**: Once per day an avoral can create an aura of fear in a 20-foot radius. It is otherwise identical with [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]] from an 8th-level caster (save DC 17). The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Lay on Hands (Su)**: As the paladin class feature, except that each day, an avoral can heal an amount of damage equal to its full normal hit points.

**Speak with Animals (Su)**: This ability works like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]] (caster level 8th) but is a free action and does not require sound.

**True Seeing (Su)**: This ability is identical with [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]] (caster level 14th), except that it has personal range and the avoral must concentrate for 1 full round before it takes effect. Thereafter the ability remains in effect as long as the avoral concentrates on it.

**Skills**: An avoral’s sharp eyes give it a +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks.
