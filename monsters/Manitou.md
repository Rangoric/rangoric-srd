A manitou is a powerful, benevolent spirit of nature that safeguards entire regions from danger, heals the sick and wounded, and preserves the balance among all creatures. When not invisible, a manitou looks like a powerfully built, long-haired humanoid with skin made of tree bark and rose quartz. A manitou stands 20 feet tall and weighs over 5,000 pounds.

Manitous speak Celestial, Common and Sylvan.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 19th; concentration +26): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_; 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally VIII|summon nature's ally VIII]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Earthquake|earthquake]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Positive Energy Aura (Manitou)|Positive Energy Aura]] (Su)**: Living creatures within the manitou's aura heal 5 hit points on its turn each round. A manitou can exclude a number of creatures equal to its Charisma modifier (typically 7) from the aura's effect, and can cease or resume the aura as a free action.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spirit Stampede|Spirit Stampede]] (Su)**: Every 1d4 rounds, a manitou can call upon animal spirits to attack, affecting a 60-foot cone and dealing 10d6 points of force damage (+16 vs Reflex; a miss halves). Using this ability temporarily weakens the manitou's connection with the natural world, halting its positive energy aura until the stampede is ready to be used again.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Token of Fortune|Token of Fortune]] (Su)**: A manitou can imbue an small object, such as a stone or necklace, with its blessing. The object's bearer and any allies within 10 feet gain a +2 luck bonus on defenses. The manitou can use scrying on the token at will. The manitou can teleport to it (without error) once per day as a swift action. These abilities are lost if the token leaves the manitou's territory. A manitou may only have one token active at a time.
