# Shadow Lord

### Creating a Shadow Lord

“Shadow lord” is an acquired template that can be added to any [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shadow Creature|shadow creature]] with 5 or more Hit Dice (referred to hereafter as the base creature). Most shadow lords were originally humanoids. A shadow lord retains all the base creature's statistics and abilities (including those granted by the shadow creature template) except as noted here.

**Challenge Rating**: Same as the base creature +2.

**Alignment**: Any evil.

**Senses**: A shadow lord gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/See in Darkness|see in darkness]] ability.

**Armor Class**: Same as the base creature (see also the incorporeal step ability).

**Defensive Abilities (Su)**: A shadow lord gains the following defensive abilities.

*Energy Resistance and Damage Reduction*: A shadow lord gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Resistance|resistance]] to cold and electricity based on its Hit Dice, as noted on the table below. This replaces the energy resistance and damage reduction granted by the shadow creature template.

| Hit Dice | Resist Cold and Electricity | DR       |
|----------|-----------------------------|----------|
| 5-10     | 15                          | 10/magic |
| 11-15    | 20                          | 15/magic |
| 16+      | 30                          | 20/magic |

*Incorporeal Step*: When a shadow lord moves, it gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] subtype and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Incorporeal|quality]], including a deflection bonus to AC equal to its Charisma bonus. It loses the incorporeal subtype and special ability when it stops moving.

**Attacks**: A shadow lord gains a melee touch attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (Fortitude negates). The save DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 the shadow lord's Hit Dice + the shadow lord's Charisma modifier. On a critical hit, the shadow lord's touch attack also deals 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|Constitution damage]] (also negated by the saving throw).

**Special Attacks (Su)**: A shadow lord gains the following special attacks. Their saving throw DCs for these attacks are equal to 10 + 1/2 the shadow lord's Hit Dice + the shadow lord's Charisma modifier, unless otherwise noted.

*Cloying Gloom Blast*: Three times per day, the shadow lord can unleash a 30-foot cone of cloying gloom. On a failed Fortitude saving throw, creatures in the cone are affected by a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]* spell (caster level equal to the shadow lord's Hit Dice) and are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for the duration of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]* effect.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: A shadow lord gains the following spell-like abilities, with a caster level equal to its Hit Dice: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Sickening|ray of sickening]]*; 3/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Conjuration|shadow conjuration]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Step|shadow step]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Conjuration, Greater|greater shadow conjuration]]* (if the shadow lord has 11 or more Hit Dice), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]]*.

A creature created with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Conjuration|shadow conjuration]]* or *greater shadow conjuration* that would normally have a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/celestial-creature/Celestial Creature|celestial]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creature/Fiendish Creature|fiendish]] template (such as a bear) instead gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shadow Creature|shadow creature]] template.

**Special Qualities (Su)**: A shadow lord gains the following special quality.

*Planar Thinning*: Once per day as a full-round action, a shadow lord can thin the barriers between the Material Plane and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#Plane of Shadow|Plane of Shadow]], making it considerably easier for creatures to cross between the two. This functions like the planar travel aspect of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate|gate]]* spell (caster level equal to the shadow lord's Hit Dice). This planar thinning is immediately dispelled if in an area of normal or bright light.

**Ability Scores**: Dex +4, Cha +4.

**Skills**: A shadow lord gains a +8 racial bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.
