Even the fiendish inhabitants of the lawful evil planes know to stay clear of the maddening buzz of hellwasp swarms. While individual hellwasps are mindless, when swarmed together they gain a hive mind that torments the living and the dead alike. Sages and diabolists argue as to why this happens, but a favored explanation is that all hellwasps contain the dispersed soul of some greater devil, scattered and imprisoned by his rivals for infractions lost to mortal knowledge.

Though intelligent, these swarms are often difficult to understand and reason with. A hellwasp swarm can speak in a fashion with its myriad droning wings. The chatter is often little more than repetitive mantras of insults and the voicing of the twisted and base desires of the swarm. A hellwasp swarm has little use for reason, diplomacy, or discourse in other circumstances, for its desires are as savage as its home plane.

A single hellwasp resembles a thumb-sized normal wasp, except its carapace is gleaming black with ruby-red stripes, and its compound eyes are an iridescent green.

### Combat

Like any swarm, a hellwasp swarm seeks to surround and attack any living prey it encounters. The swarm can take over the bodies of its prey and infest both the living and the dead, using them as horrible living (or unliving) puppets to accomplish acts of wickedness that a swarm of insects could never attempt.

A hellwasp swarm’s attack is treated as an evil-aligned weapon and a magic weapon for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].

**Inhabit (Ex)**: A hellwasp swarm can enter the body of a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] or dead creature by crawling into its mouth and other orifices. Inhabiting a body requires 1 minute, and the victim must be Small, Medium, or Large. The swarm can abandon the body at any time as a full-round action. Any attack against the host deals half damage to the hellwasp swarm as well, although the swarm's DR, resistances, and immunities may negate some or all of this damage. When a hellwasp swarm inhabits a dead body, it effectively transforms the corpse into a zombie of the appropriate size under the swarm's control. If a hellwasp swarm inhabits a living victim, it can neutralize the effects of its own poison and control the victim's movement and actions as if using dominate monster. Hellwasps quickly consume a living victim, dealing 2d4 points of Constitution damage per hour.

A hellwasp-inhabited creature is easy to spot, since its skin crawls with the forms of the insects inside. A swarm can attempt an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] check to conceal its inhabitation of a host, with a –4 penalty if currently inhabiting a Small host.

A [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]] spell cast on an inhabited victim forces the hellwasp swarm to abandon its host.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Hellwasp Swarm Venom|Hellwasp Swarm Venom]]: Swarm—injury; _attack_ +10 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d6 Dex; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses.

**Hive Mind (Ex)**: Any hellwasp swarm with at least 1 hit point per Hit Die (or 12 hit points, for a standard hellwasp swarm) forms a hive mind, giving it an Intelligence of 6. When a hellwasp swarm is reduced below this hit point threshold, it becomes mindless.
