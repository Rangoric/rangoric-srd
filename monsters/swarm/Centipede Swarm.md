## Combat

A centipede swarm seeks to surround and attack any living prey it encounters. A swarm deals 2d6 points of damage to any creature whose space it occupies at the end of its move.

**Distraction (Ex)**: Any living creature that begins its turn with a centipede swarm in its space must succeed on a DC 13 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Poison (Ex)**: Injury, Fortitude DC 13, initial and secondary damage 1d4 Dex. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Skills**: A centipede swarm has a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, and uses its Dexterity modifier instead of its Strength modifier for Athletics checks to climb. Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|climb speed]], a centipede swarm is better at climbing.
