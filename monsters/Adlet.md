Adlets are cunning hunters of the arctic wilds. Tall, sinewy, nimble, and very quick, they see themselves as the true heritors of untamed arctic lands, and are offended by any other humanoid species that attempts to settle in such regions. Although not normally evil, adlets are very aggressive and warlike. They also have no social taboo against cannibalism, and their practice of eating their dead rather than burying them only further builds misconceptions about their morality.

Deeply religious, adlets worship the power and cruelty of nature, seeing divinity in the lash of the blizzard's wind, the ferocity of the polar bear, and the immensity of the towering iceberg. Many become [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle|oracles]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druids]], but all adlets know their place in the natural world. One in every dozen adlets is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]]: an adlet with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Advanced Creature|advanced creature template]] and the ability to summon a greater ice elemental or 1d4+1 large ice elementals and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune with Nature|commune with nature]]* once per day each as spell-like abilities. An adlet is 6 feet tall and weighs 250 pounds.

Adlets speak Adlet and Common.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 10th; concentration +11): Constant—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]*; At will-ray of frost; 3/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleet Storm|sleet storm]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ice Storm|ice storm]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Walk|wind walk]]* (self only).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Arctic Stride|Arctic Stride]] (Ex)**: An adlet can move through any sort of difficult terrain at its normal speed while within arctic or snowy terrain. Magically altered terrain affects an adlet normally.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Frozen Breath|Frozen Breath]] (Su)**: An adlet's breath is supernaturally cold, and deals an additional 1d6 points of cold damage with its bite as a result. Once every 1d4 rounds as a swift action, it can exhale, filling a 10-foot-radius spread around it with frigid air that deals 2d6 points of cold damage and staggers those in the area with numbing cold for 1d6 rounds. A DC 21 Fortitude save negates the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] effect but not the cold damage. The save DC is Constitution-based.
