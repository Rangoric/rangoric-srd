# Mothman

- Medium [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^monstrous-humanoid|Monstrous Humanoid]]
- **Hit Dice**: 9d10+27 (76 hp)
- **Initiative**: +8
- **Speed**: 30 ft. (6 squares), fly 60 ft. (good)
- **Armor Class**: 20 (+4 Dex, +6 natural), touch 14, flat-footed 16
- **Base Attack**: +9; **CMB**: +13; **CMD**: 24
- **Attack**: Claw +13 melee (1d6+1)
- **Full Attack**: 2 claws +13 melee (1d6+1)
- **Space/Reach**: 5 ft./5 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Mind-Warping Gaze|Mind-warping gaze]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|spell-like abilities]]
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Telepathy|telepathy]] 100 ft., [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] 17
- **Saves**: Fort +6, Ref +10, Will +10
- **Abilities**: Str 12, Dex 19, Con 16, Int 17, Wis 19, Cha 18
- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] +20, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] +12, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] +12, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +16, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] +13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] +16
- **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Agile Maneuvers|Agile Maneuvers]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flyby Attack|Flyby Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Finesse|Weapon Finesse]]
- **Environment**: Any land
- **Organization**: Solitary
- **Challenge Rating**: 6
- **Treasure**: Standard
- **Alignment**: Usually chaotic neutral
- **Advancement**: —
- **Level Adjustment**: —

Little is known of these strange creatures, save that when they appear, calamity follows. Mothmen see themselves as agents of fate, exhibiting extraordinary powers to guide the hands of destiny. More often than not, citizens encounter a mothman and never recall the meeting, yet fall right into the creature's obscure plans.

Mothmen stand almost 7 feet tall and weigh 100 pounds.

Mothmen understand Common, Sylvan and Undercommon but can't speak.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th; concentration +16): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ (DC 16), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ (DC 14), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Misdirection|misdirection]]_ (DC 16); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Image|major image]]_ (DC 17), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Modify Memory|modify memory]]_ (DC 18), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nightmare (Spell)|nightmare]]_ (DC 19), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantasmal Killer|phantasmal killer]]_ (DC 18), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]]_ (DC 20), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_ (DC 17); 1/day-agent of fate, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Vision|false vision]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Fog|mind fog]]_ (DC 19), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mislead|mislead]]_ (DC 20), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Project Image|project image]]_ (DC 21).

**Agent of Fate (Sp)**: A mothman may recreate the effects of any spell of 5th level or lower once per day as a spell-like ability, but only if doing so steers the flow of fate in its proper course. What the proper flow of fate entails is determined by the GM. Typical uses of this ability include casting major image to coax someone to a portentous location, casting raise dead to return someone with an important fate to life, or using rusting grasp to weaken a structure and cause some necessary calamity.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Mind-Warping Gaze|Mind-Warping Gaze]] (Su)**: Fear, 30 feet, Will DC 18 negates. A creature that fails a save against this attack becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d6 rounds. A creature currently suffering from a fear effect that fails this save instead takes 1d4 points of Wisdom damage. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.
