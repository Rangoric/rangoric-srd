Dark and moody cousins to nymphs, lampads sulk along natural caverns and dank tunnels, their weeping cries echoing through the darkness. These creatures are often found carrying light in caverns and dungeons, drawing creatures to them like moths to flame. Though they prefer the darkness, they know that exposing their forms under bright conditions gives them an edge over creatures viewing them.

Just as nymphs guard nature's purest places and dryads protect their sacred trees, lampads watch over the dark places of the world. They speak to the stone that forms their murky world, and their forlorn cries ring out through the belly of the earth.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 7th; concentration +12): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Tell|stone tell]]_.

**Spells**: A lampad casts spells as a 5th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]], but can't swap out prepared spells to cast summon spells.

**Druid spells prepared** (CL 5th; concentration +8): 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Shape|stone shape]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Call|stone call]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Stone|magic stone]]_; 0—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Guarded|Guarded]] (Su)**: A lampad adds her Charisma modifier as a deflection bonus to her Armor Class.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Insane Beauty|Insane Beauty]] (Su)**: This ability affects all humanoids within 30 feet who are viewing a lampad in conditions brighter than dim light. Those who look directly upon the lampad are attacked at +8 vs Will, and on a hit gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] condition for 1d6 rounds. A creature the challenge misses is immune to the same lampad's insane beauty for 24 hours. A lampad can suppress or resume this ability as a free action. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Weep|Weep]] (Su)**: As a standard action, a lampad can unsettle those near her when she cries. Any creature within 30 feet who can hear a lampad weeping is attacked at +8 vs Will, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]]. This ability can't cause a creature to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]]. This is a mind-affecting fear effect that relies on audible components. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.
