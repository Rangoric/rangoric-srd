Nereids are capricious and often dangerous aquatic fey that appear as strikingly beautiful women, often seen bathing unclothed in the water. Many sailors have met their doom following a nereid, for though a nereid's beauty is otherworldly, her watery kiss is death. Others seek out nereids, for if one can secure control over the creature's shawl, the cloth can be used to force the nereid's compliance. A nereid forced to obey in this manner immediately attempts to slay her master as soon as she can secure her shawl's safety.

Nereids speak Aquan, Common and Sylvan.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th; concentration +17): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_ (+8 vs Will; only against creatures that are currently fascinated by her beguiling aura); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]_ (water elementals only).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Beguiling Aura|Beguiling Aura]] (Su)**: Any creature sexually attracted to women runs the risk of being beguiled by a nereid if it looks upon her beauty from a distance of 30 feet or less. If the aura hits the creature at +13 vs Will, it is immediately [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]]. A nereid may use her suggestion spell-like ability at will against creatures that are fascinated by her beguiling aura. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Drowning Kiss|Drowning Kiss]] (Su)**: A nereid can flood the lungs of a willing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] creature by touching it (traditionally by kissing the creature on the lips). If the target cannot breathe water, it cannot hold its breath and immediately begins to drown. On its turn, the water attacks the target at +13 vs Fortitude; on a miss it coughs up this water, and on a hit it falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] at 0 hp. On the next round, it attacks again, and a hit drops the target to –1 hit points and dying; on the third round it attacks again, and a hit kills it. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Nereid Poison|Nereid Poison]]: contact; _attack_ +13 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d2 Con plus blindness; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Shawl|Shawl]] (Ex)**: A nereid's shawl (hardness 2, hp 6) contains a portion of her life force. If the shawl is ever destroyed, the nereid takes 1d6 points of Constitution drain per hour until she dies. A nereid can craft a new shawl from water by making a DC 25 Will check (1d20 + Will save bonus), but each attempt takes 1d4 hours to complete. Attempts to destroy or steal a nereid's shawl require the sunder or disarm attempts.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Transparency|Transparency]] (Su)**: When underwater, a nereid's body becomes transparent, effectively rendering her invisible. She can become visible or transparent at will as a free action.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Unearthly Grace|Unearthly Grace]] (Su)**: A nereid adds her Charisma bonus as a deflection bonus to her Armor Class and CMD if she wears no armor.
