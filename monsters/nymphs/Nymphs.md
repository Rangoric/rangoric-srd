# Nymphs

Nymphs are fey of great beauty, each tied to a particular part of the natural world. The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Dryad|dryad]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Nymph|nymph]] are the first kinds; the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Hamadryad|hamadryad]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Lampad|lampad]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Nereid|nereid]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nymphs/Oceanid|oceanid]] are others.
