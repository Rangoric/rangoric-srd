Few demons understand the inner workings of the chaotic evil planes like the nalfeshnee, and it is not unusual to see a nalfeshnee seeming to serve its plane itself rather than a demon lord. Some claim stewardship over the fleshy realms that birth new demons, while others guard sites of particular significance deep in the plane's secret reaches. Often, a nalfeshnee's realm there surpasses the strength and size of the largest of mortal kingdoms, for nalfeshnees display a singular gift for managing and ordering the chaos of its plane. Mortal summoners often seek them out for their unparalleled yet mad intellects, ever taking care to comb through bargains with such demons for hidden and unwanted consequences, for there is little a nalfeshnee will agree to do that does not, in some sinister way, advance the needs and desires of its plane.

Nalfeshnees stand 20 feet tall and weigh 8,000 pounds. They form from the souls of greedy or avaricious evil mortals, particularly those who ruled over empires of slavery, theft, banditry, and more violent vices.

Nalfeshnees speak Abyssal, Celestial and Draconic.

### Combat

When fulfilling their duties in the underworld, nalfeshnees usually disdain combat as being beneath them. Given the opportunity, they succumb to blood lust and do battle. They disable opponents with their unholy nimbus and slaughter them while they can’t fight back.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th): Constant—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]]* (DC 23); At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning|call lightning]]* (DC 18), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feeblemind|feeblemind]]* (DC 20), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]* (DC 18), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only); 1/day—[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 5, 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Nalfeshnee|nalfeshnee]] 20%, 1d4 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Hezrou|hezrous]] 40%, or 1d4 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Vrock|vrocks]] 50%).

**Unholy Nimbus (Su)**: Three times per day as a free action a nalfeshnee can create a nimbus of unholy light, causing nauseating beams of writhing color to play around its body. One round later, the light bursts in a 60-foot radius. Any non-demon creature caught within this area must succeed on a DC 22 Will save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1d10 rounds as visions of madness hound it. The save DC is Charisma-based.
