Profane champions of the chaotic evil planes, vrocks embody all the rage, hatred, and violence of those despicable realms. As ravenous and grotesquely opportunistic as the scavengers they resemble, vrocks delight in bloodshed, relishing the sounds and sensations of ripping the still-pulsing entrails from a living husk.

A typical vrock stands 8 feet tall and weighs 400 pounds. Vrocks generally form from the evil souls of hateful and wrathful mortals, particularly those who were career criminals, mercenaries, or assassins.

Vrocks speak Abyssal, Celestial and Common.

### Combat

Vrocks are vicious fighters who like to fly down into the enemy and cause as much damage as possible. They prance about in battle, taking briefly to the air and bringing their clawed feet into play. Despite their advantage in mobility, the vrocks’ deep love of battle frequently leads them into melee combats against heavy odds.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_ (DC 18); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]_, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 3, 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Vrock|vrock]] 35%).

**Dance of Ruin (Su)**: A vrock can dance and chant as a full-round action—at the end of 3 rounds, a crackling wave of energy explodes from the vrock, dealing 5d6 points of electricity damage to all creatures within 100 feet. A DC 17 Reflex save halves this damage. For each additional vrock that joins in the dance, the damage increases by 5d6 and the DC to avoid the effect increases by +1, to a maximum of 20d6 when four or more vrocks are dancing (the DC continues to increase with additional vrocks, but the damage does not). The dance immediately ends and must be started anew if any of the participating vrocks is slain, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], or otherwise prevented from dancing. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Spores (Ex)**: A vrock can release a cloud of spores from its body once every 3 rounds as a free action. Adjacent creatures take 1d8 points of damage from the spores, plus 1d4 points of damage per round for 10 rounds as the spores grow into thick green vines. Although ugly, the vines are harmless and wither away in 1d4 days if not shaved off before then. The spores can be destroyed by casting _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_ on the affected creatures or by sprinkling them with holy water. This attack can also be halted by effects that remove or provide immunity to disease.

**Stunning Screech (Su)**: Once per hour, a vrock can emit a shrill screech. All creatures except demons within a 30-foot-radius spread must succeed on a DC 21 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. The save DC is Constitution-based.
