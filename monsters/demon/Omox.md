Amorphous beings of living slime, these repulsive demons lurk in fetid pools and lakes of filth, eager to drown unwary passersby. When summoned to the Material Plane, omoxes typically guard places of sacred filth or waters watched over by cults of Jubilex, the demon lord with which these foul demons are most commonly associated.

A typical omox stands 7 feet tall and weighs 1,200 pounds. They form from the souls of those who destroyed beautiful things in life, or who befouled and desecrated objects of purity.

Omoxes speak Abyssal, Celestial and Draconic.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th; concentration +16): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _liquid leap_ (see below), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_ (+9 vs Will); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]_ (+8 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stinking Cloud|stinking cloud]]_ (+7 vs Fortitude); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Fog|acid fog]]_, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 4, 1 omox 30% or 1d4 babaus 60%).

**Liquid Leap (Sp)**: As long as an omox is in contact with liquid, it can use dimension door as a swift action (CL 12th); its starting and ending points must be connected by a contiguous mass of liquid.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Slime|Slime]] (Su)**: An omox's nauseating body is composed of sticky, acidic slime. As an attack action, it can hurl a glob of slime (range increment 20 feet). Any creature that is struck by the glob is attacked at +13 vs Reflex, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] for 1d6 rounds. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Smothering|Smothering]] (Ex)**: An omox can use its grab ability against a creature of any size. When it grabs a foe, it attempts to flow over and into the victim's mouth and nose to smother it. Each round the omox maintains its grapple, its victim cannot breathe or speak. See the rules for [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Suffocation|suffocation]] for how long a victim can hold its breath and the consequences of suffocation.
