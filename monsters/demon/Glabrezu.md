Like succubi, glabrezu tempt victims into ruin, but they lure their prey with power or wealth rather than passion.

Glabrezu have penetrating violet eyes, and their skin color ranges from deep russet to pitch black. A glabrezu stands about 15 feet tall and weighs about 5,500 pounds.

## Combat

Glabrezu prefer subterfuge to combat. However, if their attempts to entice or deceive fail, these enormous demons attack with a vengeance. They follow a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]] attack with melee attacks, hoping to finish off wounded foes with [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]].

A glabrezu’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as chaotic-aligned and evil-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Improved Grab (Ex)**: To use this ability, a glabrezu must hit a Medium or smaller opponent with a pincer attack. It can then attempt to start a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Grapple|grapple]] as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 14th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]] (DC 19), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]] (DC 19), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Reverse Gravity|reverse gravity]] (DC 22), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]] (self plus 50 pounds of objects only), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]] (DC 19);  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Power Word Stun|power word stun]].  

Once per month, a glabrezu can fulfill a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]] for a mortal humanoid. The demon can use this ability to offer a mortal whatever he or she desires - but unless the wish is used to create pain and suffering in the world, the glabrezu demands either terrible evil acts or great sacrifice as compensation.

**Summon Demon (Sp)**: Once per day a glabrezu can attempt to summon 4d10 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Dretch|dretches]] or 1d2 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Vrock|vrocks]] with a 50% chance of success, or another glabrezu with a 20% chance of success. This ability is the equivalent of a 4th-level spell.

**True Seeing (Su)**: Glabrezu continuously use [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]] as the spell (caster level 14th).

**Skills**: Glabrezu have a +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks.
