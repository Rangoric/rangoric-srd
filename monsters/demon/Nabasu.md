Nabasus are birthed directly into the Material Plane from the chaotic evil planes, where they feed on innocent souls to mature. Only when finally sated can a nabasu return to those planes. Rumor holds that even then the nabasu's lifecycle does not change, and that further developments await them as they continue to grow. These vile demons form from the souls of evil gluttons, particularly from cannibals, blood-drinkers, and those who prefer the tang of undead flesh.

Nabasus speak Abyssal, Celestial and Draconic.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 8th): At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]* (DC 19); 3/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]* (DC 16), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person, Mass|mass hold person]]* (DC 21), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]*, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 4, 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Nabasu|nabasu]] 30% or 1d4 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Babau|babaus]] 30%).

**Consume Life (Su)**: When a nabasu creates a ghoul with its gaze attack, it gains a growth point. It gains a bonus equal to its growth point total on attack rolls, CMB rolls, saving throws, caster level checks, and skill checks. Its maximum hit points increase by 10 for each growth point, and its caster level for spell-like abilities increases by 1. For every 2 growth points, its natural armor bonus, SR, and CR increase by 1. Every time it gains a growth point it makes a DC 30 caster level check—success indicates it matures (gaining both the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Advanced Creature|advanced]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Giant Creature|giant]] simple templates) and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shifts]] to the chaotic evil planes in a burst of smoke. A nabasu can have a maximum of 20 growth points—it automatically matures if it has not done so already when it reaches 20 growth points.

**Death-Stealing Gaze (Su)**: As a free action once per day per growth point (minimum of 1/day), a nabasu can activate its death-stealing gaze for a full round. All living creatures within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Fortitude save or gain a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]]. A humanoid slain in this manner immediately transforms into a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ghoul/Ghoul|ghoul]] under the nabasu's control. A nabasu's gaze can only create one ghoul per round—if multiple humans perish from the gaze in a round, the nabasu picks which human becomes a ghoul. The save DC is Charisma-based.
