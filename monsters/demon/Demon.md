# Demon

Demons are a race of creatures native to chaotic evil-aligned planes. They are ferocity personified and will attack any creature just for the sheer fun of it - even other demons.

Demons have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^demon|demon subtype]]: immunity to electricity and poison, resistance to acid, cold, and fire, the power to summon others of their kind, telepathy, and the languages they speak.
