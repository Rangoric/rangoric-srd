# Demon

Demons are a race of creatures native to chaotic evil-aligned planes. They are ferocity personified and will attack any creature just for the sheer fun of it - even other demons.

**Demon Traits**: Most demons possess the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature’s entry).

  - Immunity to electricity and poison.

  - [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^resistance-to-energy|Resistance to acid]] 10, cold 10, and fire 10.

  - *Summon (Sp)*: Many demons share the ability to summon others of their kind (the success chance and type of demon summoned are noted in each monster description). Demons are often reluctant to use this power until in obvious peril or extreme circumstances.

  - [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^telepathy|Telepathy]].

Except where otherwise noted, demons speak Abyssal, Celestial, and Draconic.

### Combat

A bebilith attacks any creature it sees. It usually picks one target and concentrates its attacks on that opponent, using its webs to isolate the target from its comrades. Should the bebilith become overwhelmed by tougher opponents, it often attempts to bite one or more of its victims and retreats, allowing its [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^poison|poison]] to do its work.

A bebilith’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as chaotic-aligned and evil-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Poison (Ex)**: Injury, Fortitude DC 24, initial damage 1d6 Con, secondary damage 2d6 Con. Bebilith venom is highly perishable, losing its potency and becoming inert, foul-smelling goo almost as soon as it comes into contact with air. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Rend Armor (Ex)**: If a bebilith hits with both claw attacks, it pulls apart any armor worn by its foe. This attack deals 4d6+18 points of damage to the opponent’s armor. Creatures not wearing armor are unaffected by this special attack. Armor reduced to 0 hit points is destroyed. Damaged armor may be repaired with a successful [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (armorsmithing) check.

**Web (Ex)**: A bebilith can throw a web up to four times per day. This is similar to an attack with a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Net|net]] but has a maximum range of 30 feet, with a range increment of 10 feet. This attack is effective against targets of up to Gargantuan size. The web anchors the target in place, allowing no movement.

An [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] creature can escape with a DC 24 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] check or burst the web with a DC 24 Strength check. The check DCs are Constitution-based. The web has 14 hit points and hardness 0. There is a 75% chance that the webbing will not burn if any sort of fire is applied to it (check each round).

**Plane Shift (Su)**: This ability affects only the bebilith. It is otherwise similar to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|the spell]] (caster level 12th).

**Skills**: A bebilith has mottled coloration that gives it a +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.
