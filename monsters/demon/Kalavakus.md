Known to some as “horned demons,” the kalavakus demons are hulking, muscled beasts. They work as slavers on the Abyss, sometimes as harem keepers or captains of the guard for a more powerful demon, and at other times as mercenaries who sell their captured victims for profit to anyone with the funds to pay.

A kalavakus is 7 feet tall and weighs 450 pounds. They form from the souls of evil mortals who were slavers in their mortal lives.

Kalavakus speak Abyssal, Celestial, Common and Draconic.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th; concentration +15): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_ (+8 vs Will); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Air Walk|air walk]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Person|dominate person]]_ (+8 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command, Greater|greater command]]_ (+8 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 4, 1 kalavakus 40%), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Persuasion|symbol of persuasion]]_ (+9 vs Will).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Enslave Soul|Enslave Soul]] (Su)**: A kalavakus can attempt to enslave the soul of any mortal creature within 60 feet as a swift action. The kalavakus must have line of sight to the target. It attacks the target at +8 vs Will, and a miss resists it, but the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round even on a miss. On a miss, the creature is immune to this ability for 24 hours. On a hit, the target's soul is enslaved—this creature takes a –6 penalty on all attack rolls and defenses against that kalavakus. If a creature with an enslaved soul is slain by that kalavakus, the soul immediately infuses the demon's body, affecting it with a heal spell (CL 12th). A kalavakus can have only one mortal soul enslaved at a time—if it enslaves a second soul, the first is released. This is a mind-affecting death effect. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Horns|Horns]] (Ex)**: The kalavakus's numerous horns can easily catch weapons and yank them away from opponents. The demon gains a +4 racial bonus on all disarm attempts as a result.
