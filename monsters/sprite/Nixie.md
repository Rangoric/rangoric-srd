Nixies are guardians of ponds, rivers, lakes, and springs. They prefer dwelling in idyllic places, which they protect from the depredations of predators and careless humanoids. While this often involves confusing enemies and driving foes away, nixies are not afraid of spilling blood to protect their homes—though they prefer not to resort to such means unless completely necessary. Nixies are 4 feet tall and rarely weigh more than 45 pounds.

Nixies avoid combat if possible, using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]_ to lead humanoids away from their lairs. They sometimes use this ability to enslave people to act as protectors or help with a task that is simply too big for them to deal with. They cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_ on charmed creatures helping with tasks below the surface. Nixies are reclusive and prefer to keep their presence hidden. They make lairs underwater, forming small communities if their numbers are great enough. Legends speak of a secret nixie kingdom at the bottom of a great lake, but no one has yet confirmed its location.

Most nixies are slim and comely, with lightly scaled, pale green skin and dark green hair. Females often twine shells and pearl strings in their hair and dress in wraps woven from colorful seaweed. Males wear loincloths of the same materials. Nixies prefer not to leave their lakes.

Nixies speak Aquan and Sylvan. Some also speak Common.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 6th; concentration +10): 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]_ (DC 15); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_ (CL 12th).

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: This ability works like the druid ability of the same name. The nixie's total includes a +8 racial bonus on wild empathy checks.
