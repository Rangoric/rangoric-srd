Most nixies are slim and comely, with lightly scaled, pale green skin and dark green hair. Females often twine shells and pearl strings in their hair and dress in wraps woven from colorful seaweed. Males wear loincloths of the same materials. Nixies prefer not to leave their lakes.

A nixie stands about 4 feet tall and weighs about 45 pounds.

Nixies speak Aquan and Sylvan. Some also speak Common.

## Combat

Nixies rely on their [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]] ability to deter enemies, entering combat only to protect themselves and their territory.

**Charm Person (Sp)**: A nixie can use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]] three times per day as the spell (caster level 4th). Those affected must succeed on a DC 15 Will save or be charmed for 24 hours. Most charmed creatures are used to perform heavy labor, guard duty, and other onerous tasks for the nixie community. Shortly before the effect wears off, the nixie escorts the charmed creature away and orders it to keep walking. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Amphibious (Ex)**: Although nixies are aquatic, they can survive indefinitely on land.

**Water Breathing (Sp)**: Once per day a nixie can use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]] as the spell (caster level 12th). Nixies usually bestow this effect on those they have charmed.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: This ability works like the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]]’s wild empathy class feature, except that a nixie has a +6 racial bonus on the check.

**Skills**: Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]], a nixie is better at swimming.

\*Nixies have a +5 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks when in the water.
