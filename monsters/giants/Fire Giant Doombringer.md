A fire giant doombringer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giants/Fire Giant|fire giant]] with 16 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle|oracle]]. With a single pronouncement from a doombringer, an entire settlement can be reduced to cinder and ash.

A fire giant doombringer speaks Common and Giant.
