A frost giant ice mage is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giants/Frost Giant|frost giant]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. Frost giant sorcerers excel in harnessing the power of the elements and bolstering raiders in combat.

Ice mages inherit the magic of the icy north. While some are tricksters and manipulators—perhaps using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]_ to grow to Huge size and trick travelers into thinking they're cloud giants—many act as support and artillery for their tribe's raiders, killing at a distance with the power of a storm or increasing their allies' size and ferocity. As powerful as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerers]] may be, frost giant society still places more value in physical strength and melee prowess, meaning sorcerers are usually advisors rather than leaders.

A frost giant ice mage speaks Abyssal, Auran, Common, and Giant.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: A frost giant ice mage casts shield and expeditious retreat.

**During Combat**: A frost giant ice mage uses his wand of solid fog to trap opponents, then blasts them at range with his wand of ice storm or wand of lightning bolt. He casts ice slick and uses his icewalker bloodline ability to outmaneuver his opponents in icy and snowy areas.

**Bloodline Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 6th; concentration +9): 7/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Boreal Bloodline|cold steel]] (grant weapon or 50 ammunition _[[path srd 3.5/items/weapon-special-abilities/Frost|frost]]_ weapon special ability for 3 rounds).
