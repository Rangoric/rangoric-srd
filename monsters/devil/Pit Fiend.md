A pit fiend often wraps its wings around itself like a grotesque cloak, and appears wreathed in flames.

A pit fiend is 12 feet tall and weighs 800 pounds.

## Combat

Pit fiends are wily and resourceful fighters, using [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]] to gain the upper hand and biting at foes seemingly able to see them. They don’t hesitate to blanket an area with [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireballs]], and can call down the wrath of an inferno with [[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]].

A pit fiend’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as evil-aligned and lawful-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Constrict (Ex)**: A pit fiend deals 2d8+26 points of damage with a successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] check.

**Disease (Su)**: A creature struck by a pit fiend’s bite attack must succeed on a DC 27 Fortitude save or be infected with a vile [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Disease|disease]] known as devil chills (incubation period 1d4 days, damage 1d4 Str). The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Fear Aura (Su)**: A pit fiend can radiate a 20-foot-radius [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fear|fear aura]] as a free action. A creature in the area must succeed on a DC 27 Will save or be affected as though by a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]] spell (caster level 18th). A creature that successfully saves cannot be affected again by the same pit fiend’s aura for 24 hours. Other devils are immune to the aura. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Improved Grab (Ex)**: To use this ability, a pit fiend must hit with its tail slap attack. It can then attempt to start a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity.

If it succeeds on the grapple check, it establishes a hold and can constrict.

**Poison (Ex)**: Injury, Fortitude DC 27, initial damage 1d6 Con, secondary damage death. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Spell-Like Abilities**: Caster level 18th. The save DCs are Charisma-based.  

At will - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blasphemy|blasphemy]] (DC 25), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead|create undead]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]] (DC 21), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]] (self plus 50 pounds of objects only), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Good|magic circle against good]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster, Mass|mass hold monster]] (DC 27), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Persistent Image|persistent image]] (DC 23), [[rangoric-srd/spells/Power Word Stun|power word stun]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]] (DC 26);  

1/day - [[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]] (DC 27).  

Once per year a pit fiend can use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]] as the spell (caster level 20th).

**Summon Devil (Sp)**: Twice per day a pit fiend can automatically summon 2 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Lemure|lemures]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Bone Devil (Osyluth)|bone devils]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Bearded Devil (Barbazu)|bearded devils]], or 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Erinyes|erinyes]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Horned Devil (Cornugon)|horned devil]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Ice Devil (Gelugon)|ice devil]]. This ability is the equivalent of an 8th-level spell.

**Regeneration (Ex)**: A pit fiend takes normal damage from good-aligned silvered weapons, and from spells or effects with the good descriptor.

## Tactics Round-by-Round

A pit fiend typically opens combat by using its spell-like abilities, attempting to neutralize dangerous opponents before entering melee.

*Prior to combat*: [[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|Unholy aura]]; activate [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fear|fear aura]], summon devil.

*Round 1*: Quickened [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]] and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spells H to L|mass hold monster]] if facing three or more visible, active opponents; otherwise [[rangoric-srd/spells/Power Word Stun|power word stun]] against unarmored opponent (preferably a spellcaster).

*Round 2*: [[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|Meteor swarm]] against as many foes as possible, approach worst-injured enemy.

*Round 3*: Full attack against injured enemy.

*Round 4*: Continue melee against injured enemy, or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Power Word Stun|power word stun]] against annoying spellcaster.

*Round 5*: Repeat from round 1, or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]] to safety if endangered.
