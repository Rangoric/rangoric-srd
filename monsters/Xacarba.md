Fiends hailing from the darkest reaches of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/The Abyss|Abyss]], xacarbas are manipulation and destruction intertwined. With their infamous ability to redirect spells, these serpentine goliaths wreak havoc on the mind as well as the body, turning allies against one another and reveling in the destruction doing so produces.

Xacarbas speak Abyssal, Common and Draconic.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 18th; concentration +25): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Sight|arcane sight]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ (DC 19), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_ (DC 20); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ (DC 21), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion, Mass|mass suggestion]]_ (DC 23), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]_ (DC 21), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Pain|symbol of pain]]_ (DC 22), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Idiocy|touch of idiocy]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vision|vision]]_; 1/day—[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 5, 1 hezrou or 1d4 succubi, 50%).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Poison|Poison]] (Su)**: The xacarba can choose between three poisons. The save DC of each is Constitution-based. [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Fiendish Bile|Fiendish Bile]]: Bite—injury; _save_ Fort DC 25; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d4 Str damage (good-aligned creatures also take 2d8 points of damage); _cure_ 2 consecutive saves. [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Mysterious Blood|Mysterious Blood]]: Bite—injury; _save_ Fort DC 25; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d4 Dex and 1d4 Wis damage plus confusion for 1 round; _cure_ 2 consecutive saves. [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Vile Disjunction|Vile Disjunction]]: Bite—injury; _save_ Fort DC 25; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ targeted _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]_ (CL 18th) on the creature; _cure_ 2 consecutive saves.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Redirect Spell|Redirect Spell]] (Su)**: Any creature that attempts to cast a spell within 30 feet of a xacarba must cast the spell defensively. If the caster fails the concentration check to do so (or if the caster opts to not cast defensively), the xacarba can choose the target of the spell as a immediate action. The new target must be a legal target—if there's no legal alternative target to choose from, this ability cannot be used.
