Leukodaemons spread plagues and pandemics, both in the daemons' home plane and across the planes.

Leukodaemons stand upward of 14 feet tall but weigh just over 200 pounds. The skulls that serve as their heads can be replaced with any skulls, yet these creatures choose horse skulls. The creature's true head is merely a blistered knob between its shoulders.

Leukodaemons speak Abyssal, Draconic and Infernal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 10th; concentration +13): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deathwatch|deathwatch]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]_ (+7 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Harm|harm]]_ (+9 vs Will), [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Summon|summon]] (level 3, 1 leukodaemon only, 35%).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Breath of Flies|Breath of Flies]] (Su)**: Once per minute as a standard action, a leukodaemon can unleash a cloud of corpse-bloated, biting black flies in a 20-foot cone. Those caught in the cone take 8d6 points of slashing damage. It attacks at +11 vs Reflex, and a miss halves this damage. Those who take any damage are also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute. In addition, the flies linger for 1d4+1 rounds, congealing into a buzzing 20-foot-square cloud centered on the cone's original point of origin. Any creature that ends its turn in this cloud is attacked at +11 vs Reflex, and on a hit takes 4d6 points of damage and becomes sickened for 1 minute. This cloud of flies may be dispersed by any area effect that does damage or creates wind of at least strong wind force. All daemons are immune to this effect. The attack bonuses are Constitution-based.

**Contagion (Su)**: Any arrow a leukodaemon fires from a bow is tainted with disease. If a creature is damaged by a leukodaemon's arrow, it is attacked at +9 vs Fortitude, and on a hit is affected as if by the spell contagion. A leukodaemon can manifest arrows at will and never runs out of ammunition.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Infectious Aura|Infectious Aura]] (Su)**: All creatures within 50 feet of a leukodaemon take a –4 penalty on Fortitude Defense against disease effects.
