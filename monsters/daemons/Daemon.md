# Daemon

Daemons are neutral evil outsiders that eat souls and thrive on disaster and ruin. They speak Abyssal, Draconic, and Infernal.

### Combat

Daemons have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^daemon|daemon subtype]]: immunity to acid, death effects, disease, and poison, resistance to cold, electricity, and fire, the power to summon others of their kind, telepathy, and the languages they speak.
