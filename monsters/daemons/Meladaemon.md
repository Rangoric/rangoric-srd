As personifications of death from starvation and thirst, these withered fiends spend their time destroying resources and spreading hunger. These creatures visit worlds throughout the planes, destroying acres of crops and slaughtering livestock in order to harvest souls. Meladaemons delight in the slow death of starvation, going so far as to experiment with various bodily deficiencies and mortal weaknesses. Arrogant and solitary, meladaemons rarely work with others of their kind.

Meladaemons stand approximately 12 feet tall and weigh 350 pounds.

Meladaemons speak Abyssal, Draconic and Infernal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 11th; concentration +15): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blight|blight]]_ (+9 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Diminish Plants|diminish plants]]_, quickened _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Horrid Wilting|horrid wilting]]_ (+12 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Waves of Fatigue|waves of fatigue]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Consumptive Aura|Consumptive Aura]] (Su)**: A meladaemon radiates an aura of hunger to a radius of 20 feet. Every round a creature begins its turn within this aura, it is attacked at +12 vs Fortitude, and on a hit takes 1d6 nonlethal damage and becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] from extreme hunger. Creatures that do not need to eat are immune to this effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Disease|Disease]] (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Daemonic Wasting|Daemonic Wasting]]: Bite—injury; _attack_ +12 vs Fortitude; _onset_ 1 day; _frequency_ 1/day; _effect_ 1d4 Con damage and 1d4 Cha damage; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

**Hunger (Su)**: A meladaemon's claw attack deals an additional 1d6 points of nonlethal damage as it causes sudden pangs of horrific hunger in its foe. Creatures that do not need to eat are immune to this effect.
