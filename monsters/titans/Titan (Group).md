# Titan (Group)

Titans are a race that was old when the world was young, very near to the divine. When the mightiest of them grew jealous of the adulation the gods received from mortals, they began a crusade to destroy mortal life. The Elysian titans turned against their kin and were allowed to wander the planes; the Thanatotic titans were banished to the Abyss; and the hekatonkheires, shunned by the gods, were cast into the furthest reaches of the multiverse. Unless slain by violence, a titan is immortal.

The [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Titan|titan]] and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Titan, Elder|elder titan]] are described on their own.

### Combat

Every titan has a long list of spell-like abilities, and the powerful ones rule over realms of their own.
