While workers cannot speak, they can convey simple concepts (such as danger) by body movements. Through the hive mind, however, they can communicate just fine—although their intelligence still limits the concepts that they can grasp.

A worker is about 3 feet long and about 2-1/2 feet high at the front. It weighs about 60 pounds. Its hands are suitable only for manual labor.

### Combat

Formian workers fight only to defend their hive-cities, using their mandibled bite.

A formian worker’s natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as lawful-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^DR|damage reduction]].

**Cure Serious Wounds (Sp)**: Eight workers together can heal a creature’s wounds as though using the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]] spell (caster level 7th). This is a full-round action for all eight workers.

**Make Whole (Sp)**: Three workers together can repair an object as though using the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Make Whole|make whole]] spell (caster level 7th). This is a full-round action for all three workers.
