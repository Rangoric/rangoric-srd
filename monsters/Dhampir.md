# Dhampir

- Medium [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] (Dhampir)
- **Hit Dice**: 1d10+3 (13 hp)
- **Initiative**: +3
- **Speed**: 30 ft. (6 squares)
- **Armor Class**: 17 (+4 armor, +3 Dex), touch 13, flat-footed 14
- **Base Attack**: +1; **CMB**: +2; **CMD**: 15
- **Attack**: [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Rapier|Rapier]] +4 melee (1d6+1/18–20)
- **Full Attack**: [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Rapier|Rapier]] +4 melee (1d6+1/18–20)
- **Space/Reach**: 5 ft./5 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-like abilities]]
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Negative Energy Affinity|negative energy affinity]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Resist Level Drain|resist level drain]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Light Sensitivity|light sensitivity]]
- **Saves**: Fort +2, Ref +3, Will –1
- **Abilities**: Str 13, Dex 17, Con 10, Int 10, Wis 8, Cha 16
- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] +5, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] +5, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] +3, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] +7, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +1

- **Environment**: Any land
- **Organization**: Solitary, pair, or court (3–8 and 1 vampire)
- **Challenge Rating**: 1/2
- **Treasure**: NPC gear (chain shirt, rapier, other treasure)
- **Alignment**: Usually chaotic evil
- **Advancement**: —
- **Level Adjustment**: —

Cursed from birth, dhampirs result from the rare and unnatural union of vampires and humans. Although not driven to consume blood for survival as their undead progenitors are, dhampirs nonetheless know a lifelong desire for blood that nothing else can truly sate. Those who survive their early years face a life of fear and mistrust, their unnatural beauty and incredible reflexes marking them as scions of the night just as surely as their sensitivity to light. Although polluted by undeath, dhampirs do grow old and die, aging at a rate similar to elves.

Dhampirs speak Common.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 1st): 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Resist Level Drain|Resist Level Drain]] (Ex)**: A dhampir takes no penalties from energy draining effects, though he can still be killed if he accrues more negative levels then he has Hit Dice. After 24 hours, any negative levels a dhampir takes are removed without the need for an additional saving throw.

### Dhampir as Characters

Dhampir characters use the [[rangoric-srd/races/Dhampir|dhampir]] race, which has their racial traits and level adjustment.
