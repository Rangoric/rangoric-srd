The chaotic evil planes are terrible places, yet even in these horrific realms there exist predators and wild beasts that prey upon the demonic horde—the bebilith being the most notorious, a creature evolved to hunt and slay demons.

Far more intelligent than its verminous shape would suggest, it is perhaps a blessing that the bebilith is such a focused and devoted hunter of demons, for had these dangerous outsiders more of a mind to conquer and expand empires, their ability to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ would make them a menace indeed. Although, as an outsider, the bebilith has no biological need to eat, it does enjoy the sensation of chewing on demonic flesh.

Bebiliths understand but do not speak Abyssal.

### Combat

A bebilith attacks any creature it sees. It usually picks one target and concentrates its attacks on that opponent, using its webs to isolate the target from its comrades. Should the bebilith become overwhelmed by tougher opponents, it often attempts to bite one or more of its victims and retreats, allowing its rot to do its work.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 12th): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ (bebilith only).

**Dismantle Armor (Ex)**: If a bebilith hits a foe with both claw attacks, it can attempt to peel away the target's armor and shield as a free action by making a CMB check. If the bebilith is successful, the target's armor and shield are torn from his body and dismantled, falling to the ground. Armor subjected to this attack loses half its hit points and gains the broken condition if the attack hits the target at +15 vs Reflex. The attack bonus is Strength-based.

**Penetrating Strike (Su)**: A bebilith's natural weapons are treated as chaotic and magical for the purposes of penetrating damage reduction. Against creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^demon|demon subtype]], its natural weapons are also treated as cold iron and good.

**Rot (Su)**: A bebilith's bite causes a horrible withering and weakening of the flesh, resulting in a hideous melting and foul rotting effect. This catastrophic withering begins on the round the creature is bitten and continues for another 4 rounds thereafter, for 5 rounds of withering in all. Each round the rot persists, the rot attacks the target at +13 vs Fortitude, and on a hit it takes 2 points of Constitution damage. If the rot misses the target twice in a row, the effect is cured. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|Heal]]_ can also halt the rot effect. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.
