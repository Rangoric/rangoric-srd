Graven guardians are human-sized constructs of clay, stone, wood, or similar materials, crafted and painted to resemble a deity or a deity's favored servants. When not in motion, a graven guardian appears identical to a temple or crypt's statue, but while active, graven guardians look extremely lifelike.

Graven guardians are inexorably tied to a specific deity's faith. While unintelligent and thus lacking any real personality, a graven guardian has the ability to recognize fellow worshipers or minions of its deity and does not treat them as enemies unless they first attack. This recognition does not extend so far as to allow these worshipers (or those cunningly disguised as worshipers) to give a graven guardian new orders. As with all constructs, a graven guardian follows only the orders of its creator.

Each graven guardian gains a special ability from two domains assigned during construction. Attack bonuses for these abilities are Wisdom-based (+4 for most guardians). Caster level for these abilities is equal to the graven guardian's Hit Dice (CL 6th for most graven guardians).

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 6th; concentration +0): 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_ (self only).

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Faith Bound|Faith Bound]] (Su)**: A graven guardian cannot attack any creature that openly wears or displays the holy or unholy symbol of the deity to which the graven guardian is dedicated unless that creature first attacks the graven guardian.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Magic Weapon|Magic Weapon]] (Su)**: A graven guardian that carries its deity's favored weapon treats that weapon as a +1 weapon as long as it is wielded by the guardian. If the weapon is a melee weapon, it gains the keen weapon special ability (even if the weapon is a bludgeoning weapon). If it is a thrown weapon, it gains the returning weapon special ability. If it is a ranged weapon, it gains the seeking weapon special ability, and generates new ammunition with each attack (this ammunition is destroyed whether or not it hits).

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]] (Su)**: _Flight_ —The graven guardian gains a fly speed equal to its base speed with average maneuverability and an Acrobatics skill of +6 (this includes a +4 racial bonus).

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]] (Su)**: _Beast Ward_ —The ward attacks an animal's Will before it can attack the graven guardian, and on a hit it can't. Any attack on an animal (but not its allies) by the graven guardian breaks this protection. Once the ward misses an animal, that particular graven guardian's ward doesn't attack it again on subsequent attacks.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Artifice Domain|Artifice]] (Ex)**: _Repair_ —The graven guardian's fast healing increases to 5.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]] (Su)**: _Chaotic Weapons_ —The graven guardian's weapon and slam attacks are treated as chaotic-aligned. Its magic weapon deals +1d6 points of damage to lawful targets.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Charm Domain|Charm]] (Su)**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|Command]]_ —The graven guardian can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]_ three times per day as a spell-like ability.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Community Domain|Community]] (Su)**: _Alliance_ —Adjacent worshipers and graven guardians dedicated to the same god gain a +1 luck bonus on attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, and defenses.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain|Darkness]] (Su)**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|Darkness]]_ —The graven guardian can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_ two times per day as a spell-like ability.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Death Domain|Death]] (Ex)**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|Bleed]]_ —The graven guardian's weapon deals 2 points of bleed damage on a hit.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Destruction Domain|Destruction]] (Ex)**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shatter|Shatter]]_ —The graven guardian can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shatter|shatter]]_ two times per day as a spell-like ability.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain|Earth]] (Ex)**: _Rocky_ —The graven guardian's natural armor bonus increases by +2.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Evil Domain|Evil]] (Su)**: _Evil Weapons_ —The graven guardian's weapon and slam attacks are treated as evil-aligned. Its magic weapon deals +1d6 points of damage to good targets.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire]] (Su)**: _Flaming_ —The graven guardian's weapon gains the _flaming_ property.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Glory Domain|Glory]] (Su)**: _Glorious_ —The graven guardian's magic weapon and slam attack deal an additional 1d6 points of damage against undead creatures.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Good Domain|Good]] (Su)**: _Good Weapons_ —The graven guardian's weapon and slam attacks are treated as good-aligned. Its magic weapon deals +1d6 points of damage to evil targets.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Healing Domain|Healing]] (Su)**: _Recovery_ —Once per day, when reduced below half hit points, the graven guardian automatically heals 3d10+6 points of damage.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Knowledge Domain|Knowledge]] (Su)**: _Familiarity_ —The graven guardian gains a +2 bonus on attack rolls again any creature it attacked in the past round.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Law Domain|Law]] (Su)**: _Lawful Weapons_ —The graven guardian's weapons and slam attacks are treated as lawful-aligned. Its magic weapon deals +1d6 points of damage to chaotic targets.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Liberation Domain|Liberation]] (Su)**: _Unfettered_ —The graven guardian gains a +4 bonus to its CMD.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Luck Domain|Luck]] (Su)**: _Lucky_ —Once per day, the graven guardian may make an attack against its defense roll twice and take the worse of the two results as the actual result.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Madness Domain|Madness]] (Su)**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|Confusion]]_ —The graven guardian can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_ once per day as a spell-like ability.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Magic Domain|Magic]] (Su)**: _Resistant_ —The graven guardian's SR increases by 5.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain|Nobility]] (Su)**: _Honored_ —The graven guardian gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls and combat maneuver checks.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|Plant]] (Su)**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|Entangle]]_ —The graven guardian can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_ three times per day as a spell-like ability.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Protection Domain|Protection]] (Su)**: _Protected_ —The graven guardian gains a +2 bonus on all defenses.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Repose Domain|Repose]] (Su)**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rest Eternal|Rest Eternal]]_ —Damage dealt by the graven guardian resists any magical healing. Attempting to use magical healing to cure this damage requires a successful caster level check against the guardian's SR. This ability does not affect natural healing or healing provided by extraordinary abilities.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Rune Domain|Rune]] (Su)**: _Rune Carved_ —Runes cover the graven guardian. Whenever the graven guardian takes energy damage or energy damage fails to overcome its SR, for the next round, its attacks deal +1d6 points of damage of that energy type. If more than one type of energy attack occurs in a round, roll randomly to determine what kind of extra energy damage it deals.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain|Strength]] (Ex)**: _Powerful_ —The graven guardian gains Power Attack as a bonus feat.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Sun Domain|Sun]] (Su)**: _Dazzling_ —When the graven guardian scores a critical hit with its magic weapon, a bright flash blinds the target for 1d4 rounds (vs Fortitude).

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Travel Domain|Travel]] (Su)**: _Speedy_ —The guardian gains Mobility as a bonus feat, and all of its modes of movement increase by +10 feet.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain|Trickery]] (Su)**: _Trip_ —The graven guardian gains the trip ability with its melee weapon and slam attacks.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War]] (Su)**: _Enhanced_ —The enhancement bonus of the graven guardian's weapon increases to +2.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain|Water]] (Ex)**: _Swimmer_ —The guardian gains a swim speed equal to its base speed, and an Athletics skill of +11.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]] (Su)**: _Shocking Weapon_ —The graven guardian's weapon gains the _shock_ special ability.

### Construction

A graven guardian's body must be sculpted from clay, stone, or wood, and adorned with precious stones, rare woods, or metal inlays worth 500 gp. The Hit Dice and size of graven guardians can be increased as though they were golems.
