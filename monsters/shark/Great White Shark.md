# Great White Shark

- Huge [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|Animal]] (Aquatic)
- **Hit Dice**: 5d8+20 (42 hp)
- **Initiative**: +6
- **Speed**: swim 60 ft.
- **Armor Class**: 19 (+2 Dex, +9 natural, –2 size), touch 10, flat-footed 17
- **Base Attack**: +3; **CMB**: +12; **CMD**: 24 (can't be tripped)
- **Attack**: Bite +9 melee (2d8+10 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Bleed|bleed]])
- **Full Attack**: Bite +9 melee (2d8+10 plus [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Bleed|bleed]])
- **Space/Reach**: 15 ft./10 ft.
- **Special Attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Bleed|Bleed]] (1d6)
- **Special Qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Blindsense|Blindsense]] 30 ft., [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Keen Scent|keen scent]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]
- **Defenses**: Fort 20, Ref 16, Will 14
- **Abilities**: Str 25, Dex 14, Con 19, Int 1, Wis 16, Cha 4
- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] +15, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] +11
- **Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (bite)
- **Environment**: Any oceans
- **Organization**: Solitary, pair, school (3–6), or pack (7–13)
- **Challenge Rating**: 4
- **Treasure**: None
- **Alignment**: Usually neutral
- **Advancement**: 11–17 HD (Huge)
- **Level Adjustment**: —

The great white shark is one of the largest predatory fish in the ocean, and preys on seals, small whales, seabirds, dolphins, and turtles. Its strength and speed even allow it to leap out of the water to reach prey on land, rafts, or boats.

Though fierce and ruthless, great white sharks are often careful when they encounter a new type of prey. They make many biting passes to evaluate the threat their quarry poses.

An adult great white shark is 20 feet long and weighs 5,000 pounds.
