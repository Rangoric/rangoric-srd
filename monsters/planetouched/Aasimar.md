Aasimars are humans with a significant amount of celestial or other good outsider blood in their ancestry. Aasimars are not always good, but it is a natural tendency for them, and they gravitate to good faiths or organizations associated with celestials. Aasimar heritage can hide for generations, only to appear suddenly in the child of two apparently human parents. Most societies interpret aasimar births as good omens. Aasimars look mostly human except for some minor physical trait that reveals their unusual heritage. Typical aasimar features are hair that shines like metal, unusual eye or skin color, or even glowing golden halos.

Aasimars are defined by class levels—they do not possess racial Hit Dice. Their racial traits are the [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|aasimar]] race’s. The aasimar presented here is a 1st-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Good Domain|Good]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Healing Domain|Healing]] domains.

Aasimars are usually tall, good-looking, and generally pleasant. Some have a minor physical trait suggesting their heritage, such as silver hair, golden eyes, or an unnaturally intense stare.

Most aasimars are decidedly good-aligned. They fight against evil causes and attempt to sway others to do the right thing. Occasionally they take on the vengeful, judgmental aspect of their celestial ancestor, but this is rare.

Aasimars speak Celestial, Common and Draconic.

### Combat

Aasimars usually like a fair, straightforward contest. Against a particularly evil foe, however, they fight with utter conviction and to the death.
