# Undine, 1st-Level Cleric

- **Hit Dice**: 1d8 (8 hp)
- **Initiative**: +2

- **Armor Class**: 17 (+5 armor, +2 Dex), touch 12, flat-footed 15
- **Base Attack**: +0; **CMB**: +0; **CMD**: 12
- **Attack**: [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Trident|Trident]] +0 melee (1d8) or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Sling|sling]] +2 ranged (1d4)
- **Full Attack**: [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Trident|Trident]] +0 melee (1d8); or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Sling|sling]] +2 ranged (1d4)

- **Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] +4, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] +6, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] +4

- **Alignment**: Usually neutral
- **Advancement**: —

Undines are humans whose ancestry includes elemental beings of water, such as marids. This connection with the Elemental Plane of Water is most noticeably manifested in their coloration, which tends to mimic that of lakes or oceans—all undines have limpid, blue eyes, and their skin and hair can range from pale blue-white to the deep blue or green of the sea.

An undine, 1st-level cleric is an undine with one level of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]] class. Undine characters use the [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|undine]] race, which has their racial traits.

Undines speak Common and Aquan.
