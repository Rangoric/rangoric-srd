Ghaeles are the most knightly of the azatas, hunting fiends, dragons, and undead with equal vigor. Most appear like idealized humans or elves and are quick to smile—and equally quick to strike against those they perceive as wicked.

A ghaele is about 6 feet tall and weighs about 170 pounds.

Ghaeles speak Celestial, Infernal, and Draconic, but can communicate with almost any creature, thanks to their [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^azata|truespeech]].

### Combat

Ghaeles who enter combat prefer direct confrontation and damaging attacks to more subtle or insidious methods. They usually fight in their humanoid form, wielding incandescent +2 holy greatswords. If a ghaele desires mobility, it assumes its light form and blasts the enemy with light rays.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 13th): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Aura|holy aura]]_ (+11), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ (+7 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]]_ (+8 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_ (self only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Image|major image]]_ (+6 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only); 3/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Globe of Invulnerability|globe of invulnerability]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]]_ (+9 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]_ (+10), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Force|wall of force]]_.

**Spells**: Ghaeles cast divine spells as 13th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|clerics]]. They do not gain access to domains or other cleric abilities.

**Spells prepared** (CL 13th): 7th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Word|holy word]]_ (+11 vs Will); 6th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Banishment|banishment]]_ (+10 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_ (+10 vs Will); 5th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|flame strike]]_ (+9 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Raise Dead|raise dead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_; 4th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dismissal|dismissal]]_ (2) (+8 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Power|divine power]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]_; 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]_ (3), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Searing Light|searing light]]_ (2); 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|align weapon]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear's endurance]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]_ (2); 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]_ (+5 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield of Faith|shield of faith]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Purify Food and Drink|purify food and drink]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Virtue|virtue]]_.

**Gaze (Su)**: In humanoid form, a ghaele's gaze attack slays evil creatures of 5 HD or less (range 60 feet, +8 vs Will, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 2d10 rounds on a miss). Nonevil creatures, and evil creatures with more than 5 HD, are attacked at +8 vs Will, and on a hit are shaken for 2d10 rounds. A creature a ghaele's gaze misses is immune to that particular ghaele's gaze for 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The attack bonuses are Charisma-based.

**Light Form (Su)**: A ghaele can shift between its solid body and one made of light as a standard action. In solid form, it cannot fly or use light rays. In light form, it can fly and gains the incorporeal quality—it can make light ray attacks or use spell-like abilities in this form, but can't make physical attacks or cast spells. This ability otherwise functions similarly to a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/azatas/Bralani|bralani]]'s wind form ability.

**Light Ray (Ex)**: A ghaele's light rays have a range of 300 feet. This attack bypasses all damage reduction.

**Protective Aura (Su)**: Against attacks made or effects created by evil creatures, this ability provides a +4 deflection bonus to AC and a +4 resistance bonus on defenses to anyone within 20 feet of the ghaele. Otherwise, it functions as a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]] effect and a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Globe of Invulnerability, Lesser|lesser globe of invulnerability]], both with a radius of 20 feet (caster level equals ghaele’s HD). (The defensive benefits from the circle are not included in a ghaele’s statistics block.)
