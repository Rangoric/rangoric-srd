Monstrous centipedes tend to attack anything that resembles food, biting with their jaws and injecting their poison.

| Size       | Fort DC | Damage  |
| ---------- | ------- | ------- |
| Tiny       | 10      | 1 Dex   |
| Small      | 10      | 1d2 Dex |
| Medium     | 10      | 1d3 Dex |
| Large      | 11      | 1d4 Dex |
| Huge       | 14      | 1d6 Dex |
| Gargantuan | 17      | 1d8 Dex |
| Colossal   | 23      | 2d6 Dex |

**Poison (Ex)**: A monstrous centipede has a poisonous bite. The details vary by the centipede’s size, as shown on the table above. The save DCs are Constitution-based. The indicated damage is both initial and secondary damage.

**Skills**: Monstrous centipedes have a +4 racial bonus on Perception checks, and a +8 racial bonus on Stealth checks. They can use either their Strength or Dexterity modifier for Athletics checks to climb, whichever is higher. Like any creature with a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|climb speed]], monstrous centipedes are better at climbing.
