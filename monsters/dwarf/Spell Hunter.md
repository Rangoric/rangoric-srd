A spell hunter is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]]. Spell hunters specialize in locking down and then murdering enemy spellcasters.

A spell hunter speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The fighter drinks her potion of bull's strength.

**During Combat**: When fighting with a group, the fighter uses bull rush to grant attacks of opportunity to her allies. She uses the combination of Step Up and Disruptive to interrupt spellcasting.

**Base Statistics**: Without bull's strength, the fighter's statistics are **Melee** masterwork cold iron dwarven waraxe +12/+7 (1d10+6/×3); **Ranged** masterwork throwing axe +11 (1d6+3); **Str** 15; **CMB** +9 (+13 bull rush); **CMD** 21 (27 vs. bull rush, 25 vs. trip); **Skills** Athletics +8.
