A miner is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Commoner|commoner]]. A miner labors long hours in the dark, and spends his earnings freely during rare visits to the surface.

A miner speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The commoner uses whatever materials are available to avoid melee combat, such as a lantern he can smash to create a fire.
