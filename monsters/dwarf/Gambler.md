A gambler is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Gamblers lounge in dark corners of taverns and gambling dens, using their keen wits and talents to make a living.

A gambler speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The bard casts eagle's splendor.

**During Combat**: The bard brings his fists to any brawl that breaks out when he or someone else gets caught cheating. He alternates between making melee attacks and using confusion to reduce the number of effective combatants.

**Base Statistics**: Without eagle's splendor, the bard's statistics are **Bard Spells Known** reduce spell DCs by 2; **Cha** 14; **Skills** Deception +12, Intimidation +14, Perform (comedy) +15, Perform (oratory) +12, Persuasion +4.

**Bard spells known** (CL 7th; concentration +11): 3rd (1/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]* (DC 17), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glibness|glibness]]*; 2nd (4/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]* (DC 16), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* (DC 16), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*; 1st (5/day)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]* (DC 15), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Comprehend Languages|comprehend languages]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unseen Servant|unseen servant]]*; 0 (at will)—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]* (DC 14), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]*.
