A war priest is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]]. The dwarven war priest serves the god of strength, and uses her divine powers for the glory of war itself.

A war priest speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The cleric casts magic weapon.

**During Combat**: The cleric uses destructive smite as often as possible.

**Base Statistics**: Without magic weapon, the cleric's statistics are **Melee** masterwork greatsword +4 (2d6+3/19-20).

**Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 2nd; concentration +5): 6/day—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|touch of chaos]].

**Cleric spells prepared** (CL 2nd; concentration +5): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bane|bane]]_ (+4 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Stone|magic stone]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon|magic weapon]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_ <sup>D</sup>; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_ (+3 vs Will), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_; <sup>D</sup> domain spell; **Domains**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Destruction Domain|Destruction]].
