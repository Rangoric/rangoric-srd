A hammer of justice is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]. A hammer of justice lives to crush evil with his divinely enhanced weapon, and is beholden to none but his god.

A hammer of justice speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The paladin prefers melee. He uses Power Attack if easily able to hit opponents, and his divine bond to give his weapon the flaming property.

**Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 5th; concentration +5): At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.

**Paladin spells prepared** (CL 2nd; concentration +2): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_.
