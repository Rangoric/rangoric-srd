A sundering axe is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 7 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]]. A sundering axe whittles away at her enemies' abilities to attack and defend themselves.

A sundering axe speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The barbarian uses her guarded stance and alternates between dealing damage and sundering weapons.

**Base Statistics**: When not raging, the barbarian's statistics are **AC** 20, touch 11, flat-footed 19; **hp** 79; **Fort** +9; **Will** +5; **Melee** masterwork adamantine greataxe +11/+6 (1d12+4/×3); **Ranged** light hammer +8 (1d4+3) or throwing axe +8 (1d6+3); **Str** 16; **Con** 16; **CMB** +10 (+14 sunder); **Skills** Athletics +8.
