An initiate of flame is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]]. These hostile guardians of volcanic mountain regions have tempers to match their fiery environs, and tolerate no intruders.

An initiate of flame speaks Common, Druidic, Dwarven, and Giant.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The druid casts endure elements each day.

**During Combat**: The druid casts faerie fire, then drops a smokestick at his feet, letting foes come to him, and possibly sets his spear against a charge. He then fights with his spear or casts burning hands.

**Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 1st; concentration +3): 5/day—_[[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|fire bolt]]_.

**Druid spells prepared** (CL 1st; concentration +3): 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]_ <sup>D</sup> (+3 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]_ (+2 vs Fortitude), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]_; <sup>D</sup> domain spell; **Domain**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire]].
