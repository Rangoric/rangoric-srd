A fiery dragonkin is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 17 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. The fiery dragonkin embodies all the greedy, violent, and territorial impulses of red dragons. Heedless of the lives they crush in their rise to glory, the dragonkin sorcerers are born conquerors, and see in their noble draconic blood an undeniable right to rule over lesser beings.

A fiery dragonkin speaks Common, Draconic, Dwarven, and Orc.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The sorcerer casts stoneskin.

**During Combat**: The sorcerer casts mass hold person, then uses his breath weapon and area spells against his paralyzed foes. If forced into melee, he casts greater magic weapon on his battleaxe and transformation on himself.

**Base Statistics**: Without stoneskin, the sorcerer's statistics are **DR** none.
