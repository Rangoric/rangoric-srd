A guildmaster is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 13 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The guildmaster manipulates crime and politics—often the same thing—to create wealth and stability for himself and his devoted thieves.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The rogue uses flanking and sneak attacks as often as possible. He relies on his resiliency and defensive roll rogue talents to stay alive against dangerous opponents.

**Rogue Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 13th, concentration +14): 3/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*.
