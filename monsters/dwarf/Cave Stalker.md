A cave stalker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]]. The cave stalker is an experienced spelunker and treasure hunter.

A cave stalker speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The rogue prefers to find an elevated spot so she can shoot opponents while they waste time climbing to her.

**During Combat**: The rogue uses Rapid Shot, making the most of Point Blank Shot and her darkvision.
