A tunnel drummer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 14 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]]. Tunnel drummers keep time for acts of work and war carried out in deep warrens and mine tunnels.

A tunnel drummer speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**Before Combat**: The bard drinks a potion of eagle's splendor.

**During Combat**: The bard uses mind fog and dirge of doom.

**Base Statistics**: Without eagle's splendor, the bard's statistics are **Bard Spells Known** reduce spell DCs by 2; **Cha** 16; **Skills** Emulation +14, Perform (comedy, oratory) +16, Perform (percussion) +20, Perform (string) +12, Persuasion +12.
