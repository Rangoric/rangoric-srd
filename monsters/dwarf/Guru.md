A guru is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dwarf/Dwarf|dwarf]] with 6 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Adept|adept]]. The guru achieved inner peace and developed magical talents through meditating and abandoning her attachment to physical burdens and obligations. She lives on the street, seeking students to enlighten and free from their bonds, and though most people ignore her as a beggar, she is wise and content.

A guru speaks Common and Dwarven.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The adept attacks for nonlethal damage only (the attack penalty for this is not included in her melee entry). She tries to escape combat entirely with her spells and items.

**Adept spells prepared** (CL 6th; concentration +9): 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]_; 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_; 0 (at will)—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guidance|guidance]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|mending]]_.
