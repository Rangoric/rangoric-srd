Shield archons are the mighty rocks of celestial armies, withstanding waves of demons and devils without complaint. Though more than capable of tearing apart lesser demons and devils, their true strength lies in their ability to shrug off deadly attacks from superior opponents, giving their offense-oriented allies time to flank and overwhelm their mutual foes. Shield archons are 9 feet tall and weigh 800 pounds.

Shield archons speak Celestial, Draconic and Infernal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 9th; concentration +11): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disrupting Weapon|disrupting weapon]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Power|divine power]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield Other|shield other]]_.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spear and Shield|Spear and Shield]] (Su)**: At will as a free action, a shield archon can transform his hands into a +1 tower shield and a +3 shortspear, or either individually, or back to hands again. He cannot transform both hands into shields or both into shortspears. A shield archon never takes the typical –2 penalty on attack rolls while wielding a tower shield. A shield archon's weapons cannot be disarmed, but they can be sundered. If a shield archon loses his spear or shield, he can manifest a new one as a full-round action. When a shield archon is slain, these two items fade away—they cannot be looted or wielded by any other creature.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Stability|Stability]] (Ex)**: Shield archons receive a +4 racial bonus to CMD when resisting a bull rush or trip attempt.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Transpose Ally|Transpose Ally]] (Su)**: Once per day as a standard action, a shield archon can teleport to the location of a willing (or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]) ally and immediately teleport that ally to the archon's previous position, in effect switching places with the ally. The archon must have line of effect to the target.
