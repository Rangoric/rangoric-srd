Star archons are the tacticians and strategists of [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/Heaven|Heaven]]. Gifted with insight and powerful magic, they spend much of their time steering long-term plans for [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/Heaven|Heaven]]'s armies and good folk in the world.

Star archons speak Celestial, Draconic and Infernal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 19th; concentration +25): Constant—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]*; At will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunbeam|sunbeam]]* (DC 23); 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]]* (DC 25), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polar Ray|polar ray]]* (DC 24), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]* (DC 23), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]* (DC 24).

**Spells**: Star archons cast divine spells as 19th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|clerics]]. They do not gain access to domains or other cleric abilities.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Explosive Rebirth|Explosive Rebirth]] (Su)**: When killed, a star archon explodes in a blinding flash of energy that deals 50 points of damage (half fire, half holy damage) to anything within 100 feet (Reflex DC 29 half). The save DC is Constitution-based. The slain archon reincarnates 1d4 rounds later as an advanced shield archon.
