Star archons are the tacticians and strategists of [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/Heaven|Heaven]]. Gifted with insight and powerful magic, they spend much of their time steering long-term plans for [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/Heaven|Heaven]]'s armies and good folk in the world.

Star archons speak Celestial, Draconic and Infernal.

### Combat

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Spell-Like Abilities|Spell-Like Abilities]]** (CL 19th; concentration +25): Constant—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_; At will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunbeam|sunbeam]]_ (+13 vs Reflex); 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]]_ (+15 vs Reflex), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polar Ray|polar ray]]_ (+14), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]_ (+13), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]_ (+14 vs Reflex).

**Spells**: Star archons cast divine spells as 19th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|clerics]]. They do not gain access to domains or other cleric abilities.

**[[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Explosive Rebirth|Explosive Rebirth]] (Su)**: When killed, a star archon explodes in a blinding flash of energy that deals 50 points of damage (half fire, half holy damage) to anything within 100 feet (+19 vs Reflex; a miss halves). The attack bonus is Constitution-based. The slain archon reincarnates 1d4 rounds later as an advanced shield archon.
