A boggard farcroaker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggard/Boggard|boggard]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]], one of the boggard oddities. A few boggards serve their tribes in specialized roles.

A boggard farcroaker speaks Boggard, Common, and Draconic.
