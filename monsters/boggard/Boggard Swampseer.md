A boggard swampseer is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggard/Boggard|boggard]] with 3 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]], one of the boggard mystics. These casters are often the children of the priest-king.

The poison known as [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Swampseer Poison|swampseer poison]] is named for them.

A boggard swampseer speaks Boggard and Druidic.
