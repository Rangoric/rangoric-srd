A boggard brute is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggard/Boggard|boggard]] with 1 level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the boggard fighters. Boggard fighters make up the majority of war parties.

Boggard brutes may be armed with primitive-looking weapons, yet their terrifying strength makes them as deadly as any knight in armor.

A boggard brute speaks Boggard.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The boggard brute throws his javelins before rushing into combat.
