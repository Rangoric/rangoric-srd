A boggard savage is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggard/Boggard|boggard]] with 2 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]], one of the boggard barbarians. Boggard barbarians are terrifying and unpredictable.

A boggard savage speaks Boggard.

### Combat

**Base Statistics**: When not raging, the barbarian's statistics are **AC** 17, touch 10, flat-footed 17; **hp** 51; **Fort** +10; **Will** +2; **Melee** masterwork greatclub +10 (1d10+6), tongue +3 touch (sticky tongue); **Str** 19; **Con** 18; **CMB** +8; **Skills** Athletics +17.
