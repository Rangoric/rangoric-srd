A boggard prophet is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggard/Boggard|boggard]] with 4 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]], one of the boggard mystics. These casters are often the children of the priest-king.

A boggard prophet speaks Boggard.
