A boggard champion is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggard/Boggard|boggard]] with 5 levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]], one of the boggard fighters. Boggard fighters make up the majority of war parties.

Most boggard champions forsake tactics in favor of mayhem. In magic-deficient tribes, champions are often second only to priest-kings in status.

A boggard champion speaks Boggard.

### Combat

**During Combat**: The boggard champion uses her terrifying croak before engaging in melee.
