# Stuff of Shadow

*A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/potions/Strange Brew Potions|strange brew]].*

This strange liquid is possessed of a weird colorless dimness and seems to lack weight and substantiality in some subtle fashion. It is in fact essence drawn from the Plane of Shadow. Ingesting it confers the ability to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]]* as the spell (but for the user alone) for a 12 hour period. For each hour spent walking along the border of the Plane of Shadow, the user must make a Will check (DC 15). Failure means he is drawn into the Plane of Shadow and loses the path back to the Material Plane.

CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]]*; Price 6,825 gp.
