# Restorative Elixir

*A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/potions/Strange Brew Potions|strange brew]].*

While many clerics have sought the secrets of creating evermore powerful healing potions, only certain dark clerics have managed to brew a potion with the power of removing negative levels and restoring abilities as per the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]* spell. Unfortunately, their success involved taking a dangerous shortcut—the energy powering the potion draws on the negative energy plane. With each dose the imbiber must make a Fortitude check (DC 15 + number of ability points restored by the potion, or DC 20 if a negative level has been removed, whichever is greater) or fade into nonexistence, to be reborn as a ruthless shadow a minute later.

CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]*; Price 2,200 gp.
