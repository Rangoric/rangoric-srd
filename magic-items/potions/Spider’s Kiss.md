# Spider’s Kiss

_A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/potions/Strange Brew Potions|strange brew]]._

While under the effects of this foul sticky liquid, the imbiber grows pronounced fangs and gains several attributes of a monstrous spider—the ability to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_ as the spell and spin webs, and the poisonous bite of a Medium-size monstrous spider (+4 vs Fortitude; 1d4 Strength). The potion lasts for 1 hour. Alas, the brew is addictive (see the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/potions/Strange Brew Potions#^strange-brew-addiction|addiction drawback]] of strange brews).

CL 2nd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_, spellcaster level 8+; Price 1,200 gp.
