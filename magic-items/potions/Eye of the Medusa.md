# Eye of the Medusa

_A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/potions/Strange Brew Potions|strange brew]]._

Upon quaffing this potion, the imbiber’s eyes glow red and for 1 hour he has the ability to turn creatures to stone with his gaze as a medusa (+5 vs Fortitude). Though a powerful elixir, eye of the Medusa is also dangerously unstable; with each dose, the potion attacks the character’s Fortitude at +5. A hit means that the imbiber sprouts snakes for hair and the gaze attack becomes permanent. It also attacks the character’s Will at +5, and on a hit the character also changes alignment to lawful evil over the period of a week. The key ingredient of this potion are two medusa eyes.

CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]_; Price 4,950 gp.
