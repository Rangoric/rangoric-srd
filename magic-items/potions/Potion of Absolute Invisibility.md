# Potion of Absolute Invisibility

_A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/potions/Strange Brew Potions|strange brew]]._

The imbiber of this clear potion turns invisible, and remains so even when attacking, as per the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_ spell. The potion lasts 1 hour. It has two major drawbacks. The first is that it dramatically increases the aggression level of the person using it. While under its influence, the potion attacks the imbiber’s Will at +5 whenever his wishes or desires are opposed in any way, and on a hit he flies into a rage and attack the object of his frustration with lethal force, regardless of how much danger this may place him in, or how close he may be to this person under ordinary circumstances. While the user is lost in this blood rage, he retains his natural cunning and all of his abilities, and uses his _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_ to best effect. The rage lasts until the target of his aggression is dead or the potion wears off. The other drawback is a person who falls prey to this blind rage a third time (while under the same or different doses) becomes permanently _invisible_ and dangerously deranged. Needless to say, the inventor of this potion came to a bad end…as did her husband and five children.

CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_; Price 2,100 gp.
