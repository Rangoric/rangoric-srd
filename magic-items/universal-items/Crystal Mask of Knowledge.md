# Crystal Mask of Knowledge

Each of the various crystal masks of knowledge grants the wearer a +5 competence bonus on a specific type of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] check.  
Faint clairsentience; ML 3rd; Craft Universal Item, creator must have 2 ranks in the Knowledge skill of the appropriate type; Price 2,500 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
