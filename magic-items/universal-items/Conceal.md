# Conceal

While worn, a third eye conceal protects the wearer from view by all devices, powers, and spells that detect, inf luence, or read emotions or thoughts. This power protects against all mind-affecting powers and effects as well as information-gathering by clairsentience powers or effects (except for [[rangoric-srd/powers/Metafaculty|metafaculty]]); this item affects the wearer as if he enjoyed the benefit of the [[rangoric-srd/powers/Mind Blank, Psionic|psionic mind blank]] power.  
Strong telepathy; ML 15th; Craft Universal Item, psionic mind blank; Price 120,000 gp.
