# Gloves of Titan’s Grip

While wearing these oversize gloves, the user gains a +8 enhancement bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Grapple|grapple]] checks. The power is activated when the user enters a grapple. The enhancement bonus lasts for a maximum of 7 rounds per use, up to three uses per day.  
Moderate psychometabolism; ML 9th; Craft Universal Item, grip of iron; Price 14,000 gp.
