# Skin of Iron

This psychoactive skin affects the wearer as the [[rangoric-srd/powers/Iron Body, Psionic|psionic iron body]] power up to three times per day for 15 minutes with each use. When the skin’s power is not active, it has no protective qualities. Activating the psionic iron body effect is a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity.  
Strong metacreativity; ML 15th; Craft Universal Item, psionic iron body; Price 129,600 gp; Weight 2 lb.
