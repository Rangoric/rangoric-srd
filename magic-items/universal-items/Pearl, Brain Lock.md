# Pearl, Brain Lock

This tiny pearly sphere speeds toward its target when hurled by its owner. He can hurl the pearl at any target within 130 feet that he can see and to which he has line of effect. If the target fails a DC 13 Will saving throw, the pearl seems to impact and then enter the flesh of the target. The target stands mentally paralyzed, as if by [[rangoric-srd/powers/Brain Lock|brain lock]], for 3 rounds. The use destroys the item.  
Faint telepathy: ML 3rd; Craft Universal Item, brain lock; Price 300 gp.
