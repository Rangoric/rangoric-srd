# Repudiate

While worn, this powerful item allows the wearer to manifest [[rangoric-srd/powers/Dispel Psionics|dispel psionics]] once per day with a +20 modifier on the dispel check (the wearer uses the +20 modifier in place of his manifester level).  
Strong psychokinesis; ML 20th; Craft Universal Item, dispel psionics; Price 43,200 gp.
