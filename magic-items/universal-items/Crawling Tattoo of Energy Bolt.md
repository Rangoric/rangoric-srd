# Crawling Tattoo of Energy Bolt

Each of these tattoos is keyed to one energy type: cold, electricity, fire, or sonic. This crawling tattoo delivers the [[rangoric-srd/powers/Energy Bolt|energy bolt]] power, dealing 5d6 points of damage of its energy type.  
Faint psychokinesis; ML 5th; Craft Universal Item, energy bolt; Price 750 gp.
