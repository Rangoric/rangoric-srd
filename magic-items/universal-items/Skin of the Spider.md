# Skin of the Spider

This psychoactive skin continually grants the wearer a +20 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks and continually affects the wearer as the [[rangoric-srd/powers/Body Equilibrium|body equilibrium]] power. Three times per day, the wearer can manifest [[rangoric-srd/powers/Entangling Ectoplasm|entangling ectoplasm]] against a target within 30 feet.  
Faint psychometabolism; ML 3rd; Craft Universal Item, creator must have 7 ranks in Athletics, body equilibrium, entangling ectoplasm; Price 79,080 gp; Weight 2 lb.
