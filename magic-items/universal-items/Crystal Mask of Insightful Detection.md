# Crystal Mask of Insightful Detection

The wearer of a crystal mask of insightful detection can make [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks checks with a +9 insight bonus.  
Moderate clairsentience; ML 7th; Craft Universal Item, creator must have 6 ranks in both Investigation and Perception; Price 20,250 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
