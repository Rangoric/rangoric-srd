# Crystal Mask of Dread

The wearer of a crystal mask of dread can attempt to frighten other beings to her will. The wearer makes all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks with a +10 competence bonus.  
Moderate clairsentience; ML 7th; Craft Universal Item, creator must have 7 ranks in Intimidation; Price 10,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
