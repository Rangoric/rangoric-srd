# Rod of Nonresistance

When this rod strikes a creature that has spell resistance, the rod attacks her Fortitude at +15. On a hit, her SR is reduced to SR 0 for the next 24 hours. If, however, it misses, the power of the rod lashes back at the wielder instead. This backlash drains magical energy from the owner. This can take the form of a single charge from one magical item, a plus from a weapon, or whatever effect is desired. If there is nothing to take the backlash, it clings to the owner of the rod and makes the next attack against his defenses hit automatically. Targets that have a SR 0 or no SR at all are not attacked and cause no backlashes. This rod is also a +2 weapon, but is never affected by its backlash effects.

CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Penetration|Spell Penetration]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Resistance|spell resistance]]_; Price 40,000 gp.
