# Rod of Viper

This rod strikes as a +2 heavy mace. Once per day, upon command, the head of the rod becomes that of an actual serpent for 10 minutes. During this period, any successful strike with the rod deals its usual damage and also poisons the creature hit. The [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]] is injury; _attack_ +6 vs Fortitude; _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds; _effect_ 1d3 Con damage; _cure_ 1 miss. More hits stack as [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Multiple Doses|multiple doses]]. The rod only functions if its possessor is evil.

Moderate necromancy; CL 10th; Craft Rod, Craft Magic Arms and Armor, poison, creator must be evil; Price 19,000 gp.
