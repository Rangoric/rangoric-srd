# Rod of Interminable Hexes

Three times per day when a wielder of this rod uses a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (but not a major hex or a grand hex), she can use this rod's power to double the duration of the hex, as long as the hex's normal duration is longer than 1 round. When augmented with this rod, a hex that might have a longer duration on a hit but only a 1-round duration on a miss (such as [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Evil Eye (Hex)|evil eye]]) still has only a 1-round duration for a target it misses.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Extend Spell|Extend Spell]], creator must have the hex class feature; Price 11,000 gp; Cost 5,500 gp.
