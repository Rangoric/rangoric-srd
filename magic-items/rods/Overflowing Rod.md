# Overflowing Rod

An overflowing rod amplifies and focuses the side effects of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]]. While holding the rod, the kineticist counts as 3 levels higher for the purpose of determining the effects of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-overflow|elemental overflow]] ability. If used by a character without elemental overflow, the rod grants its wielder the benefit of elemental overflow as a 3rd-level kineticist (which is generally helpful only to low-level kineticists or kineticists with archetypes that replace elemental overflow with another class feature).

Moderate evocation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], creator must be a 10th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]; Price 25,000 gp; Cost 12,500 gp.
