# Rod of Abrupt Hexes

Three times per day when a wielder of this rod uses a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (but not a major hex or a grand hex), she can use this rod's power to activate the hex as a swift action rather than a standard action.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|Quicken Spell]], creator must have the hex class feature; Price 75,500 gp; Cost 37,750 gp.
