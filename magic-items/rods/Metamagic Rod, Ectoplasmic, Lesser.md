# Metamagic Rod, Ectoplasmic, Lesser

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that have full effect against incorporeal creatures, as though she were using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ectoplasmic Spell|Ectoplasmic Spell]] feat.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ectoplasmic Spell|Ectoplasmic Spell]]; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp.
