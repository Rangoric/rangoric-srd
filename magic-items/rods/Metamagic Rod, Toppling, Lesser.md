# Metamagic Rod, Toppling, Lesser

The wielder can cast up to three spells with the force descriptor per day that are affected as though the spells were augmented with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Toppling Spell|Toppling Spell]] feat.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Toppling Spell|Toppling Spell]]; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp.
