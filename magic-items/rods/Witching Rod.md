# Witching Rod

This especially powerful [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Dowsing Rod|dowsing rod]] is usually made from a branch of an ancient tree or from shafts of rare metal. Used for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft#Dowsing|dowsing]] occult skill unlock in place of a mundane dowsing rod, it gives a +5 circumstance bonus on the Fieldcraft check.

A witching rod can also work on the water, graves and metal it finds: it can produce the effect of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]_ (+4 vs Will) or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Shape|stone shape]]_. This normally works once per day, but a wielder who can use dowsing more than once per day can produce a spell effect one more time per day for each additional use of dowsing she has, without spending those uses. Only one creature per day can gain extra uses of a witching rod this way.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Shape|stone shape]]_; Price 31,000 gp; Cost 15,500 gp.
