# Witching Rod

This especially powerful [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Dowsing Rod|dowsing rod]] is usually made from a branch of an ancient tree or from shafts of rare metal. Used for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft#Dowsing|dowsing]] occult skill unlock in place of a mundane dowsing rod, it gives a +5 circumstance bonus on the Fieldcraft check.

A witching rod can also work on the water, graves and metal it finds: it can produce the effect of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]* (DC 14) or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Shape|stone shape]]*. This normally works once per day, but a wielder who can use dowsing more than once per day can produce a spell effect one more time per day for each additional use of dowsing she has, without spending those uses. Only one creature per day can gain extra uses of a witching rod this way.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Shape|stone shape]]*; Price 31,000 gp; Cost 15,500 gp.
