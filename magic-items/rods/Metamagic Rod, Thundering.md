# Metamagic Rod, Thundering

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that deafen affected creatures as though using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Thundering Spell|Thundering Spell]] feat.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Thundering Spell|Thundering Spell]]; Price 32,500 gp; Cost 16,250 gp.
