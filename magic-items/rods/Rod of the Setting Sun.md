# Rod of the Setting Sun

In dim light or darkness, this crystal-studded rod glows faintly, enough to turn ordinary darkness into dim light (but not magical darkness).

Its holder gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] out to 120 feet and ignores the miss chance from [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]], including that of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisibility]].

CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], *[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/constellations/The Hound|The Hound]]*; Price 15,000 gp; Cost 7,500 gp + 600 XP.
