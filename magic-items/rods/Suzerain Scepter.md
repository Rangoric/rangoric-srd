# Suzerain Scepter

This ornately bejeweled baton confers the mark of rightful rulership on its bearer. The wielder of this scepter gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks. Furthermore, once per day, and on command, the wielder can affect up to seven living creatures with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Good Hope|good hope]]_, as the spell. If the wielder of a _suzerain scepter_ has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Leadership|Leadership]] feat, she may attract double the normal number of followers for her Leadership score; however, if the rod is out of the wielder's possession for more than 1 week these extra followers leave. In addition, a _suzerain scepter_ radiates a continuous *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]*effect that affects any cohort, follower, animal companion, familiar, special mount, or creature charmed or summoned by the wielder (but not called creatures or those under _dominate_ or other compulsion effects). Whenever the wielder creates an effect that provides a morale bonus to such creatures (not including the scepter's *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]*effect), that bonus increases by 1.

Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Leadership|Leadership]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Good Hope|good hope]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command, Greater|greater command]]_, the creator must have the Leadership feat; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp.
