# Rod of Grasping Hexes

This rod is crafted from a gnarled branch covered in sharp thorns. Three times per day when a wielder of this rod uses a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (but not an advanced hex or grand hex), she can use this rod's power to double the range of the hex, so long as the hex has a range measured in feet.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Reach Spell|Reach Spell]], creator must possess the hex class feature; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp.
