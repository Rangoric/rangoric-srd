# Rod of Grasping Hexes

This rod is crafted from a gnarled branch covered in sharp thorns. Three times per day when a wielder of this rod uses a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (but not a major hex or a grand hex), she can use this rod's power to double the range of the hex, as long as the hex has a range measured in feet.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Reach Spell|Reach Spell]], creator must have the hex class feature; Price 11,000 gp; Cost 5,500 gp.
