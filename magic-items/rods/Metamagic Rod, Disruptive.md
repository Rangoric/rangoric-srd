# Metamagic Rod, Disruptive

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that are disruptive as though using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disruptive Spell|Disruptive Spell]] feat.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disruptive Spell|Disruptive Spell]]; Price 3,000 gp (lesser), 11,000 gp (normal), 24,500 gp (greater); Cost 1,500 gp (lesser), 5,500 gp (normal), 12,250 gp (greater).
