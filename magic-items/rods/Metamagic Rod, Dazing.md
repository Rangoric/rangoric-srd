# Metamagic Rod, Dazing

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that daze affected creatures as though using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazing Spell|Dazing Spell]] feat.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazing Spell|Dazing Spell]]; Price 14,000 gp (lesser), 54,000 gp (normal), 121,500 gp (greater); Cost 7,000 gp (lesser), 27,000 gp (normal), 60,750 gp (greater).
