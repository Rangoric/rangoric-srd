# Metamagic Rod, Dazing

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that daze affected creatures as though using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazing Spell|Dazing Spell]] feat.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazing Spell|Dazing Spell]]; Price 54,000 gp; Cost 27,000 gp.
