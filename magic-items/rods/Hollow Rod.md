# Hollow Rod

Slightly smaller and shorter than vril staves, hollow rods are hollow silver tubes covered in intricate glyphs and small, flute-like keys. Three times per day, a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] can utilize the hollow rod in conjunction with a blast wild talent or the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Healer|kinetic healer]] wild talent to augment that talent’s destructive or healing capabilities, treating the wild talent as maximized. A hollow rod can’t be used on a wild talent affected by [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-metakinesis|metakinesis]].

Strong (no school); CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], creator must be a 10th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]; Price 121,500 gp; Cost 60,750 gp.
