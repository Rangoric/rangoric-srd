# Metamagic Rod, Selective

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day as though using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Selective Spell|Selective Spell]] feat. For each spell, he can select up to four creatures of his choice within the area that are unaffected.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Selective Spell|Selective Spell]]; Price 3,000 gp (lesser), 11,000 gp (normal), 24,500 gp (greater); Cost 1,500 gp (lesser), 5,500 gp (normal), 12,250 gp (greater).
