# Refocusing Rod

Once per day, a refocusing rod lets an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] move mental focus with his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-shift-focus|shift focus]] class feature without losing any points of focus. He moves focus from one implement into the rod, then from the rod into another implement, taking twice as long as shifting focus normally takes (since he's making two transfers). He must move the focus out of the rod within 10 minutes, or it's lost.

If the occultist has chosen the rod as one of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-implements|implements]] for the day, he can instead use this ability to move focus into the rod and leave it there, or move focus stored in the rod into another implement. Either way, no focus is lost and the transfer takes the normal time for shifting focus. If the rod isn't one of his implements for the day, focus stored in it can be used only to move it to another implement. Focus moved into the rod still belongs to the occultist who moved it; the rod can't move mental focus between two users.

Moderate (no school); CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], creator must be a 10th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]]; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp.
