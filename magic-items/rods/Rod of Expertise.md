# Rod of Expertise

When held, this rod grants a bonus to some of the wielder’s skill checks. Class skills get a +4 bonus and cross-class skills get a +2 bonus. Since the rod must be held, one cannot gain this bonus on skills that require the use of both hands, such as Athletics, Craft or Engineering.

CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], any one skill with 15 ranks; Price 16,000 gp; Weight 4 lb.
