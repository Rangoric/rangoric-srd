# Scepter of Heaven

This ornate scepter of bronze and mithral has a flanged head like a golden-white sunburst. A _scepter of heaven_ acts as a _+1 evil outsider bane morningstar_. The scepter has the following powers when wielded by a good-aligned creature.

- The wielder of a scepter of heaven gains a +2 sacred bonus on caster level checks made to dispel magic against with the evil descriptor or effects created by evil outsiders.
- The wielder can use the scepter to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless Weapon|bless weapon]]_ on command and at will as the spell.
- Once per day on command, if the wielder can shoot a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spear of Purity|spear of purity]]_ from the scepter.

If the wielder of a _scepter of heaven_ wears a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Crown of Heaven|crown of heaven]]_, the scepter's caster level increases to 15th (increasing its aura to strong) and, when used as a weapon, it gains the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Holy|holy]]*property. A good-aligned wielder may also use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Sword|holy sword]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Word|holy word]]_, as the spells, once per day each.

A _scepter of heaven_ is of good alignment, and any evil creature attempting to wield one gains one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]]. The negative level remains as long as the rod is in hand and disappears when the rod is no longer held. This negative level cannot be overcome in any way (including _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]_ spells) while the rod is being held.
