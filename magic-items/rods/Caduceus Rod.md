# Caduceus Rod

Two entwined wooden snakes run the length of this laurel rod, which is capped with a pair of brass wings. Its possessor gains a +5 insight bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) checks and a +1 insight bonus on attack and damage rolls with alchemical items, alchemists' [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bombs]] included. Held in hand, the rod can be invoked five times per day for one of the following boons, each a 6th-level spell-like ability.

*Awaken*: The rod wakes all sleeping creatures in a 40-foot-radius burst centered on the wielder. Creatures in the burst are immune to sleep effects for 1 minute.

*Exchange*: The wielder changes one of his prepared alchemical [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-alchemy|extracts]] into an extract of another formula of the same or lower level in his formula book.

*Mercy*: Every [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] creature (at –1 or fewer hit points) in a 40-foot-radius burst centered on the wielder dies. Each can attempt a DC 19 Fortitude save to instead take 1d6 points of negative energy damage. This is a death effect.

*Revive*: The wielder targets a creature within 40 feet with an [[rangoric-srd/feats/Empower Spell|empowered]] *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]*.

*Slumber*: As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deep Slumber|deep slumber]]*, except that it affects a 20-foot-radius burst centered on the wielder, can affect up to 20 HD of creatures and has a Will save DC of 19. The wielder is unaffected and can exclude up to four other targets.
