# Duelist’s Comate

This _+2 countering dueling rapier_ is particularly well balanced, and features a stout steel ball on the pommel. Three times per day, the wielder can make a surprise melee attack with the pommel as a swift action, as if proficient with the attack and at her normal attack bonus. The attack deals 1d6+2 points of bludgeoning damage, and the critical multiplier is 20/×2.

Strong transmutation; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]_; Price 35,320 gp; Cost 17,820 gp.
