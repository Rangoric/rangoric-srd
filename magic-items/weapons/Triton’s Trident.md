# Triton’s Trident

This curve-tined _+1 monstrous humanoid bane trident_ is etched with a pattern of alternating scales and shells. A _triton's trident_ can unleash a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|hydraulic push]]_ three times per day, once from each tine; alternatively, their power can be combined to release a single _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Torrent|hydraulic torrent]]_ once per day. The [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]] for either effect is +6.

Moderate evocation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|hydraulic push]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Torrent|hydraulic torrent]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster I]]_; Price 15,065 gp; Cost 7,690 gp.
