# Swift Obsidian Greataxe

The blade of this _+1 keen greataxe_ is carved from jet-black obsidian. When the wielder [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charges]] an enemy, he receives a +10 foot enhancement bonus to his speed that round. Once per day, he can command the axe to grant all allies within 30 feet a +10 foot enhancement bonus to base speed for 1 round.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]_; Price 11,320 gp; Cost 5,820 gp.
