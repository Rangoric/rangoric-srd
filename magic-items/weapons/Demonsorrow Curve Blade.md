# Demonsorrow Curve Blade

This blade's hilt is shaped as a beautiful angel with elven features. Elaborately detailed feathered wings spread from the hilt to form the guard, with the angel's face at the base of the blade. This _+3 cold iron holy demon bane elven curve blade_ hinders those it was created to destroy. It bars all creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^demon|demon subtype]] that are within 60 feet of the wielder from teleporting, as if they were under the effect of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]_ spell (hitting automatically).

Strong evocation and necromancy; CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]_; Price 90,469 gp; Cost 46,460 gp.
