# Tentacle of the Fiend

A *tentacle of the fiend* is a greasy, rubbery whip that inflicts lethal damage. It is a +2 *unholy* weapon. In the hands of an evil wielder, it moves in a sinuous fashion, whipping and cracking as if it had a mind of its own. The wielder also gains a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver checks and a further +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] combat maneuver checks (for a total of a +6 bonus on disarm checks, with the whip’s own +2). (Originally, the disarm bonus also went on the roll to keep from being disarmed if the attempt failed.)

Moderate transmutation and conjuration [evil]; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]*; Price 35,501 gp; Cost 17,751 gp + 1,420 XP.
