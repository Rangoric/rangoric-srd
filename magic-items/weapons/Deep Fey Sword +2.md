# Deep Fey Sword +2

These ornately sculpted bastard swords are used by the deep fey in their battles. The sword is enchanted with strong glamours, making it appear to be more devastating than it really is. Anyone struck by a deep fey sword is attacked against its Will at the sword’s attack bonus, and on a hit takes an additional amount of illusionary damage. Anyone reduced to 0 hit points or less by this illusionary damage falls unconscious. This damage is [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]] and can be healed accordingly.
