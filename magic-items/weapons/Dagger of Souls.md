# Dagger of Souls

This murky gray *+1 dagger* has an uneasy presence about it, as the tormented faces of the dead seem to be etched onto the blade itself. It allows the wielder to cast a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Trap the Soul|trap the soul]]* spell (DC 22) against a victim upon a successful melee attack once per day. The wielder must designate the use of the dagger’s ability before he attacks (as the spell needs to triggered), but doing so is a free action.

Strong necromancy; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Trap the Soul|trap the soul]]*; Price 218,302 gp; Cost 109,151 gp + 8,732 XP.
