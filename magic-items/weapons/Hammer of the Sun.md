# Hammer of the Sun

This sturdy golden warhammer is a potent weapon against both undead and living evildoers. Anyone other than a paladin that picks up this hammer receives one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative level]], which cannot be restored and remains until the hammer is put down again. In the hands of a paladin, the weapon’s true power manifests; it is powerless in the hands of any other class.

This +4 *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Disruption|disrupting]]* warhammer is *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Weightless (Weapon Special Ability)|weightless]]* and sheds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, as the spell, whenever held. Once per day, the hammer allows its wielder to cast the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]*, with the hammer as the focus of the spell.

Strong conjuration; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunbeam|sunbeam]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]*; Price 240,800 gp; Cost 120,400 gp + 9,632 XP.
