# Frostblood Axe

This +1 _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Frost|frost]]_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Wounding|wounding]]_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Battleaxe|battleaxe]] sacrifices the souls of those it kills to the foul gods of the frost giants. Whenever it kills a creature with at least 1 Hit Die (including creatures killed by its [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage), as an immediate action the wielder can activate either _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_ on himself or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_ on himself and his allies.

Moderate necromancy [evil]; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Metal|chill metal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Knell|death knell]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_; Price 40,310 gp; Cost 20,310 gp.
