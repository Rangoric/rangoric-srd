# Scythe of Life-Harvesting

This weapon appears to be a simple farm implement, although it has a slight aura of unease about it. Wielders soon discover that the scythe is unnaturally cool to the touch, but not painfully so.

The weapon functions as a _+1 scythe_, but in the hands of a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Blackguard|blackguard]] it becomes a _+3 unholy scythe._ In addition, upon scoring a successful critical hit with the weapon, the blade attacks the victim’s Fortitude at +10, and on a hit the victim is instantly slain. This has no effect on creatures immune to critical hits. The scythe’s enhancement bonus rises to +5 for one hour after killing a victim in this manner.

Strong necromancy [evil]; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]]_, creator must be evil; Price 89,518 gp; Cost 44,918 gp + 3,568 XP.
