# Serpentine Sword

This *+2 longsword* appears to have a segmented blade. When held, it grants the bearer a +5 resistance bonus to all poison saving throws. At will, the user can call upon the blade’s *serpent strike* ability, causing the blade to separate at each segment and spring out up to 10 feet. A strong wire that snaps the blade back into place at the end of the strike holds the segments of the blade together. Besides gaining reach, the serpent strike can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] a hit opponent (if the user so desires, and succeeds on the combat maneuver check) without the wielder being knocked prone if the attempt fails.

Moderate transmutation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]*; Price 43,315 gp; Cost 21,658 gp + 1,733 XP.
