# Unholy Vindicator

This *+2 cold iron longsword* becomes a *+5* *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Unholy|unholy]]* *cold iron longsword* in the hands of a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Blackguard|blackguard]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladin]]. It provides spell resistance of 5 + the blackguard’s level to the wielder and anyone adjacent to her. It also enables the wielder to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]* (once per round as a standard action) at the class level of the blackguard. (Only the area dispel is possible, not the targeted dispel or counterspell versions of *greater dispel magic.*)

Strong abjuration; CL 18th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]]*, creator must be evil; Price 120,630 gp; Cost 60,630 gp + 4,800 XP.
