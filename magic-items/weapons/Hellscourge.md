# Hellscourge

This _+1 unholy scorpion whip_ is fashioned from skin flayed from a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Barbed Devil (Hamatula)|barbed devil]]'s back. It grants its wielder a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks. A [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] creature struck by the _hellscourge_ must succeed at a DC 17 Will save or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 round. A frightened or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] creature struck must instead save or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^cowering|cower]] for 1d4 rounds. Both of these are mind-affecting fear effects.

If wielded by a person who wears _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Hamatula Hide|hamatula hide]]_ armor, the _hellscourge_ functions as a _+1 flaming unholy whip_. Such a wielder can lash the whip once per day to hurl flames as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]_ (creating two rays); doing so extinguishes the whip's flames until the next day.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_; Price 39,305 gp; Cost 19,805 gp.
