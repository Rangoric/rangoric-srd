# Cave Dweller’s Greataxe

A _cave dweller's greataxe_ appears to be a thin slab of stone crudely lashed to a bone haft. It's as serviceable as a metal weapon of the same type would be, and functions as a +1 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Greataxe|greataxe]]. Once per day when the wielder of a _cave dweller's greataxe_ hits a creature, the wielder can speak a command word to make the axe attack the hit creature’s Fortitude at +6. On a hit, it becomes slowed for 1 round. At the end of that round, the creature is no longer slowed, but the axe attacks again, and on a second hit it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] for 1d4 rounds.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Calcific Touch|calcific touch]]_; Price 8,305 gp; Cost 4,305 gp.
