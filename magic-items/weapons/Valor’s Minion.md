# Valor’s Minion

The blade of this *+2 holy bastard sword* always appears lustrous and polished, with a hilt wrapped in alternating colors of deep black and royal purple. Effective on its own, a *valor's minion* is even more potent in the hands of those who dedicate their efforts to vanquishing a single foe. Whenever a wielder uses either the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-challenge|challenge]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|smite evil]] class feature, she can sacrifice one additional use of that class feature to treat the smite or challenge as if she were three class levels higher.

Strong evocation; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]*; Price 41,335 gp; Cost 20,168 gp.
