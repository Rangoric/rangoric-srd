# Doom Bullet

An evil touch clings to these +2 sling bullets. A target struck by a _doom bullet_ is attacked against its Will at +5, and on a hit suffers the effects of a _curse_, losing 6 points from its highest ability score. The effects of a _doom bullet_ may be reversed as per a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]_ spell. A _doom bullet_ is destroyed upon use, whether or not it hits its target.

Moderate necromancy; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]_; Price 1,031 gp; Cost 516 gp + 41 XP.
