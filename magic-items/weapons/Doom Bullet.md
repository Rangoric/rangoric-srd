# Doom Bullet

An evil touch clings to these +2 sling bullets. A target struck by a *doom bullet* must succeed at a Will save (DC 15) or suffer the effects of a *curse*, losing 6 points from its highest ability score. The effects of a *doom bullet* may be reversed as per a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]* spell. A *doom bullet* is destroyed upon use, whether or not it hits its target.

Moderate necromancy; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]*; Price 1,031 gp; Cost 516 gp + 41 XP.
