# Sizzling Arrow

This _+1 corrosive arrow_ continues to leak acid onto its target each round, dealing 1d6 acid damage on the attacker's turn for the next 3 rounds. Removing the arrow is a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] check and prevents any further damage from the arrow (the DC increases to 15 if the target tries to remove the arrow from himself). Removing the arrow destroys it, and it dissolves up once the 3 rounds pass.

Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Arrow|acid arrow]]_; Price 1,516 gp; Cost 758 gp.
