# Dustburst Bullet

This gritty and irregular _+1 sling bullet_ explodes into a cloud of choking dust when it successfully strikes its target. This fills a 5-foot cube and follows the target for 1d6 rounds before dispersing. Any creature ending its turn in the cloud is attacked against its Fortitude at +3, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stinking Cloud|stinking cloud]]_; Price 196 gp; Cost 98 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
