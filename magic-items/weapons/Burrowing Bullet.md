# Burrowing Bullet

This *+1 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Firearm Bullet|firearm bullet]]* deals normal damage, but when it hits a living creature, it burrows into the creature's flesh, causing wracking pain until removed or until the bullet burrows its way out of the creature. While these bullets burrow, the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]]. This effect lasts for 1d3 rounds or until the bullet is removed with a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check made as a standard action. *Greater burrowing bullets* take longer to pass though the bodies of living creatures (the staggered effect lasts 1d3+2 rounds) and are harder to remove (DC 20 Fieldcraft or Medicine check as a standard action).

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th (*burrowing bullet*) or 13th (*greater burrowing bullet*); [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Pain|symbol of pain]]* (*burrowing bullet*) or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Stunning|symbol of stunning]]* (*greater burrowing bullet*); Price 1,722 gp (*burrowing bullet*) or 3,447 gp (*greater burrowing bullet*).
