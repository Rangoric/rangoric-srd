# Burrowing Bullet

This _+1 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Firearm Bullet|firearm bullet]]_ deals normal damage, but when it hits a living creature, it burrows into the creature's flesh, causing wracking pain until removed or until the bullet burrows its way out of the creature. While these bullets burrow, the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]]. This effect lasts for 1d3 rounds or until the bullet is removed with a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check made as a standard action. _Greater burrowing bullets_ take longer to pass though the bodies of living creatures (the staggered effect lasts 1d3+2 rounds) and are harder to remove (DC 20 Fieldcraft or Medicine check as a standard action).

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Pain|symbol of pain]]_; Price 1,722 gp.
