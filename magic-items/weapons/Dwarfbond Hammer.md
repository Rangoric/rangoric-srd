# Dwarfbond Hammer

In the hands of a [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]], this _+1 warhammer_ functions as a _+2 returning warhammer_. It can be hurled with a 20-foot range increment. When hurled, a _dwarfbond hammer_ deals an extra 1d8 points of damage against creatures of the giant subtype.

Moderate evocation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], creator must be a dwarf of at least 7th level; Price 25,312 gp; Cost 12,812 gp.
