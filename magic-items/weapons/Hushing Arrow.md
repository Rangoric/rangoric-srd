# Hushing Arrow

This *+1 arrow* makes no sound on release or as it flies through the air. A creature struck by a *hushing arrow* must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be affected by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]* (as the spell) for 5 rounds. The *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]* effect is not triggered by arrows that miss or those that strike an inanimate object.

A *greater hushing arrow* functions just like a normal *hushing arrow*, except the target must succeed at a DC 16 Will save to avoid being silenced. *Hushing bolts* and *greater hushing bolts* can also be made; they share the same statistics as *hushing arrows* and *greater hushing arrows*, except they weigh 1/10 of a pound per bolt.

Faint illusion; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*; Price 547 gp; Cost 277 gp.
