# Axe of the Berserker

This axe functions as an ordinary *+2 battleaxe* under most conditions. When wielded by a barbarian while under the effects of a barbarian [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]], its additional powers activate. The axe increases the wielder’s Strength by an additional +4 (an enhancement bonus) and provides a +2 deflection bonus to his AC. Additionally, the wielder is not [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] when the rage ends, as long as the axe remained in his hands for the entire duration of the rage. Most axes of these types are primitive and etched with brutal and violent scenes of battle. A quarter of these weapons are greataxes.

Faint transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]]*; Price 24,310 gp; Cost 12,155 gp +922 XP.
