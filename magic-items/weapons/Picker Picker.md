# Picker Picker

The _picker picker_ is a powerful magical device developed by picker sorcerers to help them in their worldly pursuits. This sharp, slender _+2 dagger_ bears a delicately crafted key at its pommel. If the wielder of the picker uses the key with his thieves’ tools, he gains a +10 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks to pick locks.

Moderate transmutation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Knock|knock]]_; Price 18,302 gp; Cost 9,151 gp + 732 XP.
