# Hushing Arrow, Greater

This _+1 arrow_ makes no sound on release or as it flies through the air. A creature struck by a _hushing arrow_ is attacked against its Will at +3, and on a hit is affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ (as the spell) for 5 rounds. The _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ effect is not triggered by arrows that miss or those that strike an inanimate object.

A _greater hushing arrow_ functions just like a normal _hushing arrow_, except it attacks the target’s Will at +6. _Hushing bolts_ and _greater hushing bolts_ can also be made; they share the same statistics as _hushing arrows_ and _greater hushing arrows_, except they weigh 1/10 of a pound per bolt.

Faint illusion; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_; Price 1,047 gp; Cost 527 gp.
