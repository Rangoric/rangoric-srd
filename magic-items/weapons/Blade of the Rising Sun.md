# Blade of the Rising Sun

This golden-bladed *+1 glorious undead bane katana* shines like the first rays of the morning sun. Whenever the wielder of a *blade of the rising sun* threatens a critical threat (regardless of whether the critical is confirmed or whether the target is subject to critical hits), the target is surrounded by a halo of golden sunlight equivalent to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]* until the beginning of the wielder's next turn. Undead surrounded by this glow take 1d6 points of damage at the beginning of their turn.

The wielder of a *blade of the rising sun* may declare a sacrificial strike (similar to the retributive strike of a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/staffs/Staff of Power|staff of power]]*) as a standard action, or as an immediate action before losing consciousness upon gaining the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dead|dead]] condition. A sacrificial strike converts the wielder's body and spirit into raw energy equivalent to a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]* (DC 22). Allies of the wielder within the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]* are not harmed, and the wielder may choose to affect all allies within the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]* as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds, Mass|mass cure light wounds]]* or all allies within 10 feet as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]*. The caster level for the effects of the sacrificial strike is equal to the sword's caster level plus 1/2 the wielder's level (maximum 25th level).

A sacrificial strike destroys the wielder's body (as the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]*), but she has a 50% chance of being reincarnated (as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reincarnate|reincarnate]]* spell) 24 hours after performing a sacrificial strike in a random safe location within 1 mile. The wielder's gear is left behind unharmed after the sacrificial strike; however, the *blade of the rising sun* is teleported to a random location at least 100 miles distant.

Strong evocation and necromancy; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness/deafness]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]*; Price 51,850 gp; Cost 28,100 gp.
