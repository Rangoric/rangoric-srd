# Blade of the Rising Sun

This golden-bladed _+1 glorious undead bane katana_ shines like the first rays of the morning sun. Whenever the wielder of a _blade of the rising sun_ threatens a critical threat (regardless of whether the critical is confirmed or whether the target is subject to critical hits), the target is surrounded by a halo of golden sunlight equivalent to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_ until the beginning of the wielder's next turn. Undead surrounded by this glow take 1d6 points of damage at the beginning of their turn.

The wielder of a _blade of the rising sun_ may declare a sacrificial strike (similar to the retributive strike of a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/staffs/Staff of Power|staff of power]]_) as a standard action, or as an immediate action before losing consciousness upon gaining the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dead|dead]] condition. A sacrificial strike converts the wielder's body and spirit into raw energy equivalent to a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]_ (DC 22). Allies of the wielder within the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]_ are not harmed, and the wielder may choose to affect all allies within the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]_ as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds, Mass|mass cure light wounds]]_ or all allies within 10 feet as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]_. The caster level for the effects of the sacrificial strike is equal to the sword's caster level plus 1/2 the wielder's level (maximum 25th level).

A sacrificial strike destroys the wielder's body (as the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_), but she has a 50% chance of being reincarnated (as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reincarnate|reincarnate]]_ spell) 24 hours after performing a sacrificial strike in a random safe location within 1 mile. The wielder's gear is left behind unharmed after the sacrificial strike; however, the _blade of the rising sun_ is teleported to a random location at least 100 miles distant.

Strong evocation and necromancy; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness/deafness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]_; Price 51,850 gp; Cost 28,100 gp.
