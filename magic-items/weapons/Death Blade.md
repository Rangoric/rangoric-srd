# Death Blade

This finely made steel katana bears a skeleton motif and death runes carved into its hilt. This is a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Vorpal|vorpal]]* blade with a +3 enchantment, and anyone wielding it is able to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] (negative energy) as if he were an evil cleric four levels lower than his actual character level. This ability can be used 3/day. Evil clerics who wield this weapon channel energy as if they were two levels higher than they are. (Originally, both powers were rebuking and commanding undead.)

Strong necromancy and transmutation; CL 18th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Halt Undead|halt undead]]*; Price 133,915 gp; Cost 66,958 gp + 5,357 XP.
