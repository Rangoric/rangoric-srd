# Skysplitter

This powerful weapon is a +5 _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Jagged|jagged]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Shocking Burst|shocking burst]]_ greataxe. Three times per day, on a successful hit, the wielder can choose to unleash a powerful bolt of lightning (10d6 points of damage, +8 vs Fortitude; a miss halves) into the target struck. The axe also grants the wielder [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance to electricity]] 10.

Strong evocation; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning|call lightning]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Moderate Wounds|inflict moderate wounds]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_; Price 266,320 gp; Cost 133,160 gp + 10,653 XP.
