# Skysplitter

This powerful weapon is a +5 *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Jagged|jagged]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Shocking Burst|shocking burst]]* greataxe. Three times per day, on a successful hit, the wielder can choose to unleash a powerful bolt of lightning (10d6 points of damage, Fortitude save versus DC 18 for half damage) into the target struck. The axe also grants the wielder [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance to electricity]] 10.

Strong evocation; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning|call lightning]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Moderate Wounds|inflict moderate wounds]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*; Price 266,320 gp; Cost 133,160 gp + 10,653 XP.
