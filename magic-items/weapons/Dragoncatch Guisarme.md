# Dragoncatch Guisarme

This _+1 dragon bane guisarme_ has a shaft wound tightly with dragonhide. A _dragoncatch guisarme_ can be used to make a special [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] maneuver against creatures using wings to fly. If the maneuver succeeds, the target's wings are fouled and the creature is knocked off balance, falling to the ground and gaining the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] condition. A creature tripped in this way can attempt a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check to fly as a move action to remove the entangled condition.

Moderate conjuration; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster I]]_; Price 13,308 gp; Cost 6,808 gp.
