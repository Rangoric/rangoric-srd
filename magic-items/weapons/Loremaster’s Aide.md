# Loremaster’s Aide

This *+1 quarterstaff* is typically adorned with arcane symbols, often in the form of eye- and book sigils embossed near the staff’s head. Its holder can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]* at will, and can once per day cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Analyze Dweomer|analyze dweomer]]*. The true value of this item shows only in the hands of someone with at least one level in the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Loremaster|loremaster]] prestige class. Such a wielder gains the additional benefits of a +10 insight bonus on checks with his [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]], and the ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]* once per day.

Moderate divination; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Analyze Dweomer|analyze dweomer]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]*, 7 ranks in the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]]; Price 120,600 gp; Cost 60,300 gp + 4,824 XP.
