# Loremaster’s Aide

This _+1 quarterstaff_ is typically adorned with arcane symbols, often in the form of eye- and book sigils embossed near the staff’s head. Its holder can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_ at will, and can once per day cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Analyze Dweomer|analyze dweomer]]_. The true value of this item shows only in the hands of someone with at least one level in the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Loremaster|loremaster]] prestige class. Such a wielder gains the additional benefits of a +10 insight bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] checks, and the ability to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]_ once per day.

Moderate divination; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Analyze Dweomer|analyze dweomer]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]_, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] 7 ranks; Price 120,600 gp; Cost 60,300 gp + 4,824 XP.
