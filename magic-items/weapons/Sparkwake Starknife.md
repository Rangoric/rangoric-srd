# Sparkwake Starknife

A faint trail of sparks marks the passage of this _+1 shock starknife_ whenever it is thrown. Once per day its wielder may throw it so it transforms into an 8d6 _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]_ (+4 vs Reflex; a miss halves), returning to its physical form at the end of the bolt's path.

Moderate evocation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]_; Price 21,324 gp; Cost 10,824 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
