# Reaver’s Scythe

The black blade of this *+2 [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Keen|keen]] [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Unholy|unholy]] [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Scythe|scythe]]* reflects faint images of the souls of those it has claimed. Whenever the scythe is used to drop a creature to –1 hit points or less, the creature must immediately make a DC 14 Will save or be instantly slain. If the *reaver’s scythe* kills a creature, either through damage or because the creature failed its Will save, the wielder immediately receives 1 [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points|hero point]]. This hero point must be used within 1 hour or it is lost. The wielder cannot gain another hero point from the scythe until the first is used or lost.

Moderate necromancy; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Malediction|malediction]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]*; Price 80,318 gp; Cost 40,318 gp; Weight 10 lbs.
