# Chortlebomb

These glass flasks contain a few ounces of chortler dander. When thrown or dropped, the flasks break on impact and the dander explodes into the air. The dander attacks the Fortitude of anyone within five feet of the area of impact at +6, and on a hit they fall into painful fits of laughter and choking. Those affected are unable to perform any other actions for 1d4+1 rounds.

Price 100 gp.
