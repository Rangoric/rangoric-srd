# Grimdeath Arrow

Grimdeath arrows plague their victims by breathing life to the victim’s most terrifying fears. Any creature struck by a grimdeath arrow must make a Will save (DC 16); otherwise, the creature mistakenly views his adversaries as the physical manifestations of his darkest phobias. If one of his enemies strikes him in the next 1d4 rounds, he must make a Fortitude save (DC 16) or die from fright. A successful saving throw still results in 3d6 points of damage, but it also ends the effect. Grimdeath arrows act as +2 weapons.

Moderate illusion (phantasm); CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantasmal Killer|phantasmal killer]]*; Price 707 gp; Cost 357 gp + 14 XP.
