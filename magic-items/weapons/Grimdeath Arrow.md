# Grimdeath Arrow

Grimdeath arrows plague their victims by breathing life to the victim’s most terrifying fears. Any creature struck by a grimdeath arrow is attacked against its Will at +6; on a hit, the creature mistakenly views his adversaries as the physical manifestations of his darkest phobias. If one of his enemies strikes him in the next 1d4 rounds, the fear attacks his Fortitude at +6, and on a hit he dies from fright. A miss still results in 3d6 points of damage, but it also ends the effect. Grimdeath arrows act as +2 weapons.

Moderate illusion (phantasm); CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantasmal Killer|phantasmal killer]]_; Price 707 gp; Cost 357 gp + 14 XP.
