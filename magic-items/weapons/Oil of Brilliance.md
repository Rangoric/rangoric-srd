# Oil of Brilliance

This small vial of light green potion is not for consuming, but for increasing the effectiveness of metal blades. With a normal cloth, a thin layer of the oil can be applied to any type of bladed weapon. Once coated, it increases the critical multiplier of the weapon by one, and does not wash off easily, be it by rain, water, or blood. One vial of *oil of brilliance* can be used to fully cover a Medium weapon or two Small or smaller weapons, and it stays on for one week until its magic fades away, at which time the oil turns white and flakes off.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]*; Price 4,000 gp.
