# Bloodletting Kukri

On a successful critical hit, this *+1 kukri* deals 2 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage, which stacks with any bleed damage already caused by the attack. Each time the wielder strikes a blow that deals bleed damage with this weapon, he gains temporary hit points equal to the amount of bleed damage dealt. No temporary hit points are gained if the target was already bleeding, even for a lesser amount. Temporary hit points bestowed by the *bloodletting kukri* last for 1 minute.

Faint necromancy; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]*; Price 6,308 gp; Cost 3,308 gp.
