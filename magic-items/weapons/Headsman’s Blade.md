# Headsman’s Blade

This scalpel-sharp greatsword has a simple black handle and crosspiece, and is made of unusually dark steel etched with an organic wave pattern. Once per day, the wielder of this _+1 keen greatsword_ can attempt to [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Assassinate (Slayer Talent)|assassinate]] a target, per the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|slayer advanced talent]] of the same name. If the wielder is not a slayer, he must spend a full-round action studying the target before attempting the assassination, and the target's DC to avoid death is 15. In the hands of a slayer, the sword's enhancement bonus on attack rolls is 2 better against a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied target]], and the DC to avoid death from an assassination attempt increases by 2. Additionally, if the assassination attempt is successful, the target is decapitated instead of merely slain.

Moderate necromancy and transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]_; Price 13,850 gp; Cost 7,100 gp.
