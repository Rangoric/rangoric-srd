# Snakebiter Arrow

This animated wood and metal snake squirms and hisses when handled, attempting to bite its holder just as a real snake might. To use the _snakebiter arrow_ as a projectile, the bearer must first succeed at a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] check to get the snake to shape itself into a +1 _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Seeking|seeking]]_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Arrows|arrow]] or a +1 _seeking_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Crossbow Bolts|bolt]] (this is considered a trick known by the snake) for 1 minute. Failure means the snake attacks the bearer for 1 round, using the statistics of a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/snakes/Snake, Tiny Viper|Tiny viper]] familiar. A target struck by the projectile is attacked by a powerful variant [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Black Adder Venom|black adder venom]] (_attack_ +10 vs Fortitude) and affected by it on a hit. Normally, these items are carried in a pouch rather than a quiver to prevent them from getting loose.

Strong divination; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Objects|animate objects]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_; Price 306 gp; Cost 156 gp.
