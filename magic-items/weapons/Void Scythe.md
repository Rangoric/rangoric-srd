# Void Scythe

Instead of a blade, a swathe of absolute darkness inimical to life tips this scythe. It functions as a *+1 brilliant-energy scythe*, except the blade is formed of negative energy and gives off no light whatsoever. The wielder can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel negative energy]] directly into the scythe to charge it with life-destroying energy for 1 round per die of channeling. For example, channeling negative energy for 5d6 points of damage fuels the scythe for 5 rounds. While so charged, the *void scythe* bestows one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]] with each hit. One day after being struck, subjects must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save for each negative level or the negative levels [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^permanent-negative-levels|become permanent]].

Black energy utterly consumes any creature that is slain by the *void scythe*, leaving no body behind but leaving the creature's equipment unharmed. A victim of the *void scythe* can be restored only by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]* or carefully worded *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]*.

Strong necromancy; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]*; Price 95,318 gp; Cost 47,815 gp.
