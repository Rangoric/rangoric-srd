# Void Scythe

Instead of a blade, a swathe of absolute darkness inimical to life tips this scythe. It functions as a _+1 brilliant-energy scythe_, except the blade is formed of negative energy and gives off no light whatsoever. The wielder can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel negative energy]] directly into the scythe to charge it with life-destroying energy for 1 round per die of channeling. For example, channeling negative energy for 5d6 points of damage fuels the scythe for 5 rounds. While so charged, the _void scythe_ bestows one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]] with each hit. Each day the negative levels attack the subject’s Fortitude again at +6, as [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-level-save|temporary negative levels]] do.

Black energy utterly consumes any creature that is slain by the _void scythe_, leaving no body behind but leaving the creature's equipment unharmed. A victim of the _void scythe_ can be restored only by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]_ or carefully worded _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_.

Strong necromancy; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]_; Price 95,318 gp; Cost 47,815 gp.
