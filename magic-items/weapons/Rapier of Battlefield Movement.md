# Rapier of Battlefield Movement

This *+2 rapier* has elegant lines and a stylish basket hilt. The wielder of this weapon gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks made to move through an enemy's threatened area or through its space. As a swift action after hitting with a melee attack using this weapon, the wielder can spend 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] point to move 10 feet without provoking attacks of opportunity.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]*, creator must have 10 ranks in Craft (weapons); Price 30,320 gp; Cost 15,350 gp.
