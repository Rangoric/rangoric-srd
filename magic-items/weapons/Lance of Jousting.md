# Lance of Jousting

This ornately designed _+1 lance_ streams with ribbons and small favors, allowing a wielder to more effectively knock an opponent from his mount. A successful hit with a _lance of jousting_ on a mounted opponent forces the target to make an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] check (DC 10 + damage dealt) to avoid being knocked from the saddle. Although used in combat to remove a mounted foe's advantage, use of a _lance of jousting_ in a tournament or similar contest is generally considered a dishonorable form of cheating.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]_; Price 4,310 gp; Cost 2,310 gp; Weight 10 lbs.
