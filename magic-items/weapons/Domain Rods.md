# Domain Rods

These sturdy rods are crafted out of the finest materials available and are designed to aid specialist spellcasters, both martially and spiritually. In addition to providing magical power, each rod can be used as a formidable weapon, and each can be imprinted with the user’s holy symbol, increasing its potency. When the wielder uses [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] through a holy symbol that is imprinted into a domain rod, its save DC increases by 2 (in *Arms & Armor*, turn attempts made this way received a +2 enhancement bonus). Each rod, when held, provides its user a +2 enhancement bonus to his DC for spells cast within the rod’s domain and a +2 enhancement bonus to saving throws vs. spells and effects of the same domain as the rod.

Additionally, each rod allows a single spell from its domain, of any level, to be cast into it and held. This spell can be regained by the caster without losing it from the rod. A spell stored in a domain rod can be cast at any time as if it were a prepared spell. An empty rod can be used as a counterspell to any spell of its domain. When a spell of the correct domain targets the wielder of an empty domain rod, he makes a check with his [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] to allow the rod to absorb the magic.

Although these weapons are made for specialists, the wielder of one of these rods does not need to be a specialist, or even a spellcaster in order to take advantage of its properties.

## Air Rod

This delicate shaft consists of a thin crystal tube filled with shimmering mercury. The end is pointed, and the rod is sharpened along one side, allowing it to be used as a *+3 rapier*. In addition to all other powers granted by the rod, the wielder can take *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]* once per day.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]*; Price 90,602 gp; Cost 45,300 gp + 3,624 XP.

## Animal Rod

This rod is made of carved ivory, and in addition to the powers outlined above, can be wielded as a *+3 club*. This rod allows its wielder to cast the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph|polymorph]]* once per day.

Moderate transmutation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph|polymorph]]*; Price 107,800 gp; Cost 53,900 gp + 4,312 XP.

## Chaos Rod

This heavy lead rod is weighted so as to function as a powerful bludgeoning tool. In addition to all other powers above, this rod serves as a +3 *chaotic* warhammer.

Moderate evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]]*, creator must be chaotic; Price 74,312 gp; Cost 37,156 gp + 2,972 XP.

## Death Rod

This gruesome bone rod is topped with a shrunken skull. It can be wielded as a *+3 heavy mace*, and in addition to all other powers, allows its wielder to cast the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]* once per day.

Moderate necromancy; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]*; Price 124,437 gp; Cost 62,219 gp + 4,977 XP.

## Destruction Rod

This withered shaft is made from charred wood, and can be wielded as a *+3 light mace*. In addition to the standard powers, this rod allows its user to cast the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]* once per day.

Moderate transmutation; CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]*; Price 161,105 gp; Cost 80,553 gp + 6,444 XP.

## Earth Rod

This heavy granite cudgel serves its wielder as a *+3 greatclub*, as well as providing all the standard powers above, and allowing him to cast his choice of either *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone to Flesh|stone to flesh]]* once per day.

Moderate transmutation; CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone to Flesh|stone to flesh]]*; Price 161,105 gp; Cost 80,553 gp + 6,444 XP.

## Evil Rod

This ornate jeweled rod is crafted from pure gold. In addition to all other powers, it functions as a +3 *unholy heavy mace*.

Moderate evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]*, creator must be evil; Price 74,312 gp; Cost 37,156 gp + 2,972 XP.

## Fire Rod

This sturdy amber rod functions as a +3 *flaming burst morningstar* in addition to all other powers outlined above.

Moderate evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Blade|flame blade]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|flame strike]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]*; Price 74,308 gp; Cost 37,154 gp + 2,972 XP.

## Good Rod

This elegantly crafted platinum rod, in addition to all standard powers, functions as a +3 *holy heavy mace*.

Moderate evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]*, creator must be good; Price 74,312 gp; Cost 37,156 gp + 2,972 XP.

## Healing Rod

This small gnarled shaft is crafted out of congealed blood. It does not function as a weapon in any way, but in addition to all standard powers, allows its user to cast all his healing spells as if they are *empowered*.

Strong conjuration [healing]; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|cure critical wounds]]*, creator must have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Empower Spell|Empower Spell]] feat; Price 97,000 gp; Cost 48,500 gp + 3,880 XP.

## Knowledge Rod

This delicate glass rod appears fragile, but it stands up to the most intense of punishments. This rod, in addition to all standard properties, functions as a *+3 heavy pick*. Also, when it is held, it prevents its wielder from being caught flat-footed.

Moderate divination; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Clairaudience-Clairvoyance|clairaudience/clairvoyance]]*; Price 60,308 gp; Cost 30,154 gp + 2,413 XP.

## Law Rod

This heavy gavel is crafted of solid iron. In addition to the standard powers, this rod serves as a +3 *lawful warhammer*.

Moderate evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Order’s Wrath|order’s wrath]]*, creator must be lawful; Price 74,315 gp; Cost 37,158 gp + 2,973 XP.

## Luck Rod

This light rod is crafted from beaten copper. Upon command, the shaft retracts, revealing a sharp blade. This blade can be wielded as a *+3 longsword*. In addition to all other powers, once per day, a missed attack roll with this rod can be rerolled.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*; Price 74,315 gp; Cost 37,158 gp + 2,973 XP.

## Magic Rod

This exquisitely sculpted mithral rod bears a retractable head, allowing it to serve as either a *+3 morningstar*, or a *+3 heavy flail.* This weapon provides its user with all the above powers, although it holds two spells instead of the standard one.

Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]]; Price 52,315 gp; Cost 26,158 gp + 2,093 XP.

## Plant Rod

This shaft is made from living wood, and is covered with green leaves, thorns, and vines. As a weapon, this rod functions like a *+3 club*. In addition to all standard powers, it allows its wielder to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Plants|speak with plants]]* whenever it is held.

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Plants|speak with plants]]*; Price 114,300 gp; Cost 57,150 gp + 4,572 XP.

## Protection Rod

This stark rod is crafted from pure silver, and can be wielded as a *+3 morningstar*. It also affords its user a +2 deflection bonus to his AC.

Moderate abjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]]; Price 50,308 gp; Cost 25,154 gp + 2,012 XP.

## Strength Rod

This unusual rod is crafted from hundreds of individually woven strands of sinew. It functions quite effectively as a *+3 greatclub*. In addition to all other powers above, this rod increases its user’s Strength by +4 (an enhancement bonus) when it is wielded as a club.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]]*; Price 58,305 gp; Cost 29,153 gp + 2,332 XP.

## Sun Rod

This fantastic rod is crafted out of pure shimmering sunlight. In addition to all standard powers, this rod can be used as a *+3 heavy mace* of *brilliant energy*.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]]*; Price 122,312 gp; Cost 61,156 gp + 4,892 XP.

## Travel Rod

This slender shaft appears to be fabricated out of thousands of individually set feathers. Upon command, a hidden blade slides out of the shaft and allows the rod to be used as a *+3 battleaxe*. In addition to all standard powers, while wielding this rod, a creature can cast the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]* spell once per day.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]*; Price 74,310 gp; Cost 37,155 gp + 2,972 XP.

## Trickery Rod

This twisted rod is made from the petrified body of a serpent. A command word causes the head to slide out upon a chain, a second command orders the rod to fade from view, and a third initiates both effects. In addition to all other powers, this rod can be used as a *+3 invisible light flail*, and allows its wielder to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]* once per day.

Moderate illusion; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]*; Price 88,708 gp; Cost 44,354 gp + 3,548 XP.

## War Rod

This adamantine shaft sprouts two solid blades upon command, transforming it into a *+3 greataxe*. In addition to all other powers above, three times a day this rod can be wielded with a +20 insight bonus to a single attack roll.

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]*; Price 90,920 gp; Cost 45,460 gp + 3,637 XP.

## Water Rod

This jagged rod appears to be made of solid ice, although it does not melt. It can be wielded as a *+3 icy burst morningstar* in addition to all other powers defined above.

Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Metal|chill metal]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ice Storm|ice storm]]*; Price 74,308 gp; Cost 37,154 gp + 2,972 XP.
