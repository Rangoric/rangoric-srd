# Silent Dagger

This is a favored weapon of assassins and is often poisoned for extra effect. When drawn from its sheath, this *+2 dagger* creates a one-way sound barrier around it with a radius of five feet. Sound moves into the barrier normally, but does not move out. Anyone within the barrier is not able to notice any differences in the sounds around them, although they are fully silenced to anyone outside the barrier. Rogues commonly use this blade to sneak attack guards without alerting others. It also allows its wielder to move around without being heard. Most silent daggers are cruel affairs, with grotesquely exaggerated blades and razor-sharp edges. Many of these blades are magically blackened so as not to reflect any light.

Moderate illusion; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*; Price 18,302 gp; Cost 9,151 gp + 732 XP.
