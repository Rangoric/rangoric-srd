# Hurricane Quarterstaff

This _+1/+1 quarterstaff_ is hollow, drilled through with numerous tiny holes, and whistles when swung and spun. Once per day as a standard action, the wielder may spin the _hurricane quarterstaff_ and create a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]_ (DC 13 Fortitude negates). A monk or ninja wielding a _hurricane quarterstaff_ can also spend 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki*]] point to activate the staff's _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]_ power; the save DC when activating this power with _ki_ is equal to 12 + the wielder's Wisdom modifier.

Faint evocation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]_; Price 7,840 gp; Cost 4,220 gp.
