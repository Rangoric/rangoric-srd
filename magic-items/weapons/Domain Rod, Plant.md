# Domain Rod, Plant

These sturdy rods are crafted out of the finest materials available and are designed to aid specialist spellcasters, both martially and spiritually. In addition to providing magical power, each rod can be used as a formidable weapon, and each can be imprinted with the user’s holy symbol, increasing its potency. When the wielder uses [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] through a holy symbol that is imprinted into a domain rod, its attack bonus increases by 2 (originally, turn attempts made this way received a +2 enhancement bonus). Each rod, when held, provides its user a +2 enhancement bonus to his DC for spells cast within the rod’s domain and a +2 enhancement bonus to defenses vs. spells and effects of the same domain as the rod.

Additionally, each rod allows a single spell from its domain, of any level, to be cast into it and held. This spell can be regained by the caster without losing it from the rod. A spell stored in a domain rod can be cast at any time as if it were a prepared spell. An empty rod can be used as a counterspell to any spell of its domain. When a spell of the correct domain targets the wielder of an empty domain rod, he makes a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] check to allow the rod to absorb the magic.

Although these weapons are made for specialists, the wielder of one of these rods does not need to be a specialist, or even a spellcaster in order to take advantage of its properties.

**Plant Rod**: This shaft is made from living wood, and is covered with green leaves, thorns, and vines. As a weapon, this rod functions like a _+3 club_. In addition to all standard powers, it allows its wielder to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Plants|speak with plants]]_ whenever it is held.

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Plants|speak with plants]]_; Price 114,300 gp; Cost 57,150 gp + 4,572 XP.
