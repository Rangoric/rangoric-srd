# Foul Feyslayer Sword

Kept alive by life-preserving magics long beyond his human lifespan, a mortal fey hunter spent his extended life hunting fey with this longsword. Originally nothing more than a *+2 fey [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Bane (Weapon Special Ability)|bane]] longsword*, it grew more powerful with each fey life it took (and perhaps due to some unknown magics cast against its wielder). Over the centuries, it apparently gained the following abilities and curses:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Anchor Fey|anchor fey]]* within a 10-ft. radius of its blade (against all fey in range and at will);
- instill *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]* in those who look upon the *foul feyslayer* (3/day);
- wielder gains years of life equal to half the damage done to any fey victim if he successfully kills said fey victim (reduces wielder’s age by that number of years but never to less than full adulthood).
- wielder shares and suffers damage equal to half that inflicted on the sword’s victims;
- wielder must make a Will save (DC 18) or attack any fey he sees within 60 feet.

The last known wielder of the *foul feyslayer*, one of the most malefic faerie hunters ever known, died in battle against a fey king, though the king was sorely wounded in the process. The king had the sword thrown into the shadowed reaches of the fey realm in hopes that no mortal would find it and the shadows of that realm would hide it forever.

CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Anchor Fey|anchor fey]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]*; Price 115,145 gp; Cost 57,573 gp + 4,600 XP.
