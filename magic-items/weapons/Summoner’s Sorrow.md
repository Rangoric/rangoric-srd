# Summoner’s Sorrow

The *summoner's sorrow* is a *+2 cold iron glaive* that deals an extra 2d6 points of damage against summoned creatures, including [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]]s. Once each round when the *summoner's sorrow* damages a summoned or illusory creature, the wielder can make a dispel check as a free action to send away or destroy the creature. This is treated as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]* spell targeted against the spell that created the creature, except that the wielder's base attack bonus is used in place of the wielder's or item's caster level. This ability has no effect on creatures that are called rather than summoned, or on summoned creatures that cannot be dispelled, such as a summoner's eidolon.

Once per day as a free action, the wielder can attempt a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dismissal|dismissal]]* against a creature damaged by the weapon instead of dispelling it. This ability functions only against extraplanar creatures (whether summoned or not), and works as the spell of the same name with a Will save DC of 17. The wielder cannot both dispel and dismiss in the same round.

Moderate abjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dismissal|dismissal]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*; Price 42,816 gp; Cost 21,566 gp.
