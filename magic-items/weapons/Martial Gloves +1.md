# Martial Gloves +1

These masterwork leather gloves can be worn on the hands and allow characters to apply an enhancement bonus to their unarmed strike attack and damage rolls. These gloves are quite thin and light, and the wearer magically feels as if he is not wearing gloves at all.

Moderate enchantment; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]]; Price 2,303 gp; Cost 1,152 gp + 92 XP.
