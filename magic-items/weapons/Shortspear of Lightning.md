# Shortspear of Lightning

This item resembles a common shortspear except for a jagged line of runes running its entire length and a knob of silver at its end. Most *shortspears of lightning* are made from ash or oak, but any material that has been struck by lightning (magical or natural) suffices. These items combine the properties of a *+1 shortspear* with that of a 5th-level *wand of lightning bolt*. A charge from the *shortspear* can be expended when the weapon successfully strikes a target, adding the lightning damage to the weapon damage. When the charges are expended, the shortspear loses all magical properties.
