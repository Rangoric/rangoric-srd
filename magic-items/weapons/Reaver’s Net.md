# Reaver’s Net

This magical net is made from supple green cords of specially treated seaweed. The cords are studded with tiny suckers, hooks, and tendrils that constantly seethe and roil as if the net were underwater. A _reaver’s net_ functions as a _+1 net_ for all users.

Its powers truly awaken when its wielder has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Specialization|Weapon Specialization]] (net) feat or at least 7 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (sailor). The net then takes on a life of its own, functioning as a _+1 net_ of _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Distance|distance]]_. Additionally, any creature entangled by the net is attacked against its Fortitude at +4 each round the entanglement persists, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 3d6 minutes. The semi-living nature of the net allows the trained user to refold the net for proper throwing as a move action.

Moderate divination, enchantment, and transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Clairaudience-Clairvoyance|clairaudience/clairvoyance]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]]_; Price 12,600 gp; Weight 10 lb.
