# Fear Arrow

When one of these black *+1 arrows* strikes its target, it explodes in a shower of black motes. The target takes nonlethal damage only and must make a Will save (DC 16) to avoid becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for 7 rounds. On a successful save, the target is only [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]*; Price 746 gp; Cost 376 gp + 29 XP.
