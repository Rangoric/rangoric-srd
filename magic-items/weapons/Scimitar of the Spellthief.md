# Scimitar of the Spellthief

This curved blade of golden metal, a _+1 keen heartseeking spellstealing scimitar_, is the bane of spellcasters. Inscribed with flowing magical script that purports to reveal the inner secrets of magic, the _scimitar of the spellthief_ grants the wielder a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] checks. The scimitar also reveals magical auras by touch; this functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_ but affects only a single object or creature at a time, which must be touched with the blade while the wielder concentrates on detection, and the magical auras revealed are clearly visible to all.

Strong divination; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Knell|death knell]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Sight, Greater|greater arcane sight]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_; Price 75,815 gp; Cost 37,815 gp.
