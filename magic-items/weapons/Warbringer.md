# Warbringer

This massive, wickedly curved red-bladed sword is a _+3 keen vicious falchion_ that murmurs a low, rumbling battle chant when drawn. Whenever the wielder confirms a critical hit with a _warbringer_, the wielder gains the benefits of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]_ for 1 round (if a barbarian, the wielder gains 1 additional round of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] for that day), while the creature struck is compelled to move toward and attack its nearest ally with a melee weapon, natural weapon, or unarmed strike on its next turn (+4 vs Will), as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Murderous Command|murderous command]]_ spell.

Strong enchantment; CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Murderous Command|murderous command]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]_; Price 61,375 gp; Cost 30,875 gp.
