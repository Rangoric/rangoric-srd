# Screaming Bolt

One of these +2 bolts screams when fired, attacking the Will of all enemies of the wielder within 20 feet of the path of the bolt at +4; on a hit they become shaken. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; Craft Magic Arms and Armor, doom; Price 267gp; Cost 128 gp and 5 sp + 10 XP.
