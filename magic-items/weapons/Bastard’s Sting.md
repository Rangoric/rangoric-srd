# Bastard’s Sting

This _+2 adamantine bastard sword_ becomes a _+5 unholy adamantine bastard sword_ in the hands of an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladin]].

While wielded by an antipaladin, a _bastard's sting_ deals 2d6 points of negative energy damage to all living creatures that start their turn adjacent to the wielder. The antipaladin also gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] 5 as long as at least one living creature takes damage from this negative energy. This negative energy has no effect on undead. The sword also enables the antipaladin to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_ at will on command at the class level of the antipaladin. Paladins take a –2 penalty on defenses against this effect.

Strong necromancy; CL 18th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_, creator must be evil; Price 123,035 gp; Cost 63,035 gp.
