# Sword of Revealing

This *+1 longsword* has the special ability of cutting through illusion. Whenever the blade passes through an illusion (such as a figment produced by one of the many *image* spells, or the substance of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Conjuration|shadow conjuration]]*), it leaves a rent of reality in its wake; the illusion isn’t dispelled, but the fabric of the illusion in the places actually contacted by the sword are sliced apart, revealing to all whatever lies behind them. Even if the illusion is constantly changing (such as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]* or a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Persistent Image|persistent image]]*) the rent caused by the sword cannot be patched over by that spell (although casting another illusion in the same location would cover over the slice of reality).

A *sword of revealing* has no effect on non-visual illusions (such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]*), on illusions that disguise the form of a real person (unless the sword cuts through the illusion in the process of cutting into the person), or on spells like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]* that create a visual effect but are not illusions. The wielder of a *sword of revealing* is not given any indication by the sword of the presence of illusions, nor does it make him immune to the effects of the illusions that the sword hasn’t yet punctured.

Moderate divination; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]*; Price 121,115 gp; Cost 60,558 gp + 4,845 XP.
