# Lash of the Howler

The _lash of the howler_ is a _+1 vicious scorpion whip_ tipped with quills torn from a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Howler|howler]]'s mane. Each time a creature takes damage from the _lash of the howler_, the lash attacks its Reflex at +4, and on a hit a quill breaks off and lodges in its flesh. The whip instantly sprouts a new quill to replace the one lost. The quill leaves the target [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] until it is removed. Removing a single quill from a creature requires a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check made as a full-round action. For every 5 by which the check is exceeded, one additional quill can be removed. On a failed check, a quill is still removed but the process deals 1d4+1 points of damage to the victim.

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Sickening|ray of sickening]]_; Price 18,305 gp; Cost 9,305 gp.
