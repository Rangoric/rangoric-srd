# Exterminator

These +2 vermin *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Bane (Weapon Special Ability)|bane]]* swords typically bear spider, insect, or rat motifs upon their blades. The holder of an *exterminator* is able to operate normally in webs as if under the effect of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]* spell, and can walk along web strands as if moving along solid ground. Additionally, the sword grants a +3 resistance bonus to the wielder’s saving throws against poison. These exceptional powers only function while the weapon is drawn. This enchantment is most often placed upon short swords, small longswords, or other small blades. A few *exterminator* daggers are even known to exist.

Moderate conjuration; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster I]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]*; Price 67,315 gp; Cost 33,658 gp + 2,693 XP.
