# Stinging Smoke

This glass flask contains a light, acrid dust. When the flask breaks open, the dust flies into the air in a great cloud, looking like smoke. The dust is extremely painful to the eyes, attacking the Fortitude of all within 10 feet of the point of impact at +4; on a hit they are forced to shut their eyes, effectively [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]]. The cloud lasts 2d4 rounds before dissipating.

Price 35 gp.
