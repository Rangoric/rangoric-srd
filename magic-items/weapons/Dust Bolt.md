# Dust Bolt

This barbed _+1 outsider (water) bane bolt_ is etched with runes of thirst and desiccation. Any living creature struck by a _dust bolt_ begins dying of thirst, as the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cup of Dust|cup of dust]]_ (+4 vs Fortitude). Creatures with the aquatic or water subtype take a –4 penalty on that defense, and on a hit they are also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cup of Dust|cup of dust]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster I]]_; Price 1,730 gp; Cost 880 gp.
