# Gauntlets of the Master

These enchanted gauntlets appear to be ordinary leather hand gear, but they are imbued with powerful enchantments. In addition to acting as a +3 weapon, they have the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Intense|intense]]_ quality and are a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Bane (Weapon Special Ability)|bane]]_ weapon against undead. The _intense_ quality doubles the critical range of an unarmed strike, while the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bane|bane]]_ quality increases the gauntlets to +5 and inflicts an additional +2d6 points of damage against undead creatures.

Moderate conjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Forceful Hand|forceful hand]]_; Price 50,303 gp; Cost 25,152 gp + 2,012 XP.
