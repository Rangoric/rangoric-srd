# Tangle Bolt

Often etched with images of spider webs or swampy vines, this sickly green *+1 seeking bolt* transforms into sticky goo when it hits, entangling its target and becoming tough and resilient upon exposure to air. In addition to the bolt damage, the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] as if it had been hit by a tanglefoot bag.

Strong conjuration; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]* or creator must have 5 ranks in Craft (alchemy); Price 226 gp; Cost 113 gp.
