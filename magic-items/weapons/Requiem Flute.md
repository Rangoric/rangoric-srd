# Requiem Flute

This odd looking flute seems to have a shroud of light about it, and darkness fades in and out with a slow rhythm. On command, a barely visible force blade appears from the lower end of the flute, and it may be used as a longsword. The sword’s touch attack deals 2d8 force damage, but the wielder may not apply any Strength modifier to it. When used as the instrument for [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] or spellcasting abilities, the bard’s effective level increases by 4.

Strong evocation [force]; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Sword|mage’s sword]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]*; Price 165,500 gp; Cost 82,750 gp + 6,620 XP.
