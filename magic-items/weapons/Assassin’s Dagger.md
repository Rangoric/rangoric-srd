# Assassin’s Dagger

This wicked-looking, curved +2 dagger provides a +1 bonus on the Fortitude attack of an assassin’s death attack. The dagger must be used in the death attack to grant the bonus.

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; Craft Magic Arms and Armor, slay living; Price 18,302 gp; Cost 9,302 gp + 720 XP.
