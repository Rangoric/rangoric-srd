# Trackless

_Trackless_ armor lightens the wearer's step and camouflages his appearance. [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to track the wearer take a –5 penalty, and the wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. Only leather armor or hide armor can be _trackless_.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_; Price +7,500 gp.
