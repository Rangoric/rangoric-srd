# Revealing

*Revealing* armor holds the light of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Revelation|Star of Revelation]]. Its wearer gains a +6 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks against targets within 15 feet.

CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Revelation|Star of Revelation]]*; Price +1 bonus.
