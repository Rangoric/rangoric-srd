# Impervious

A shield or suit of armor with this special ability is especially hardy. It gains double its enhancement bonus to hardness and hit points (instead of just the enhancement bonus), its break DC increases by double its enhancement bonus, and it gains a bonus on defenses against direct attacks (such as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Rust Monster|rust monster]]'s rust ability) equal to its enhancement bonus.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fabricate|fabricate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Make Whole|make whole]]_; Price +1 bonus.
