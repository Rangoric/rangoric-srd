# Imprinted

Armor with this quality is made for one particular person, family, gender, group, lineage, or race, specified at the armor’s creation. Anyone other than an intended user that dons the armor receives 4d8 points of electricity damage each time it is worn. Once discharged, the armor can be used normally until removed, although it functions only as ordinary nonmagical armor.

Moderate divination; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glyph of Warding|glyph of warding]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]]*; Price +2,000 gp.
