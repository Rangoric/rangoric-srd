# Quick Don

Armor and shields possessing this enchantment allow the wearer or wielder to instantly don them when needed. _Quick don_ armor takes half the time to don (round down) as its nonmagical counterparts.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_; Price +1 bonus.
