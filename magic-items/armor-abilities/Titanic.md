# Titanic

A suit of armor with the *titanic* ability is almost comically oversized, though the effect is superficial and the interior fits a creature normally, with no adjustments necessary. A creature wearing *titanic* armor is considered one size category larger for the purpose of using or being affected by special attacks that depend on size, such as swallow whole and trample. Once per day on command, the wearer can increase her size as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]* (even if the wearer is not humanoid) for 1 minute. In addition, once per minute as a free action the wearer can add the armor's enhancement bonus to a single Strength check or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] check, or to her CMD against a single [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]]. Because of its great bulk, the armor check penalty of *titanic* armor is increased by an amount equal to its enhancement bonus.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*; Price +3 bonus.
