# Spellcasting

Attuned to magical energies, armor with the spellcasting quality reduces the arcane spell failure percentage by 10%. This quality cannot be applied to heavy armor. This ability stacks with the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Weightless (Armor Special Ability)|weightless]]_ property (offering up to a 20% reduction for armor with both qualities).

CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Imbue with Spell Ability|imbue with spell ability]]_; Price +1 bonus for light armor, +2 bonus for medium armor.
