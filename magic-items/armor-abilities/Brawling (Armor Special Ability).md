# Brawling

The wearer of *brawling* armor gains a +2 bonus on unarmed attack and damage rolls, including [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] checks made to grapple. Her unarmed strikes count as magic weapons for the purpose of bypassing damage reduction. These bonuses do not apply to natural weapons. This special ability does not prevent the wearer's unarmed strikes from provoking attacks of opportunity or make the wearer's unarmed strikes count as armed attacks. The *brawling* ability can be applied only to light armor.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]*; Price +1 bonus.
