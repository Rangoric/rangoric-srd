# Retributive

The quality can only be cast upon armor. When the wearer is struck by a critical hit, the burst attacks the attacker’s Reflex (base +10; this increases by +2 per point of enhancement), and on a hit it suffers 1d8 damage per point of enhancement bonus of the armor from a burst of fire, frost, electricity, or acid (as specified during the armor’s creation). Normal resistances versus the retribution effect apply (so a creature protected by a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_ spell gains the benefit of that magic).

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_, plus either _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heat Metal|heat metal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Metal|chill metal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Arrow|acid arrow]]_; Price +3 bonus.
