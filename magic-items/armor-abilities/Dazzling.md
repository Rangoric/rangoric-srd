# Dazzling

In normal or bright light, *dazzling* armor flashes and gives a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against attackers that rely on sight. It does nothing in dim light or darkness, or against blind attackers and those using other senses to target the wearer.

CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Light|Star of Light]]*; Price +1 bonus.
