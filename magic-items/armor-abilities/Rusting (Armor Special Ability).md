# Rusting

Nonmetallic armor only can be enchanted with this ability. Anyone that strikes someone wearing rusting armor with a metal weapon must succeed at a Reflex save (DC 16) or the weapon rusts away, effectively destroyed. If the wearer successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]]s another creature, that creature’s armor, if metallic, must succeed at a similar save or be destroyed.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rusting Grasp|rusting grasp]]*; Price +3 bonus.
