# Rusting

Nonmetallic armor only can be enchanted with this ability. Anyone that strikes someone wearing rusting armor with a metal weapon is attacked by the armor, at +6 against the weapon’s Reflex, and on a hit the weapon rusts away, effectively destroyed. If the wearer successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]]s another creature, that creature’s armor, if metallic, is attacked the same way, and on a hit is destroyed.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rusting Grasp|rusting grasp]]_; Price +3 bonus.
