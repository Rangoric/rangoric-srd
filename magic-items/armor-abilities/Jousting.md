# Jousting

This suit of armor helps its wearer remain mounted and in control of his mount. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling#Riding|Animal Handling]] checks to ride.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], creator must have 5 ranks in Animal Handling; Price +3,750 gp.
