# Fiendish Bone

This powerful enchantment literally changes an item from one of wood, metal, or stone to one of *fiendish bone*, the very essence of evil and darkness. Such armors (or shields) radiate with a profane gloom, possessing a constant umbra as if a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]* spell has been cast upon them. These items cannot be broken by any physical means (although powerful spells such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Disjunction|mage’s disjunction]]* do have a chance of destroying them) and they retain all prior bonuses and abilities that they had before undergoing this enchantment. In addition, armors (or shields) with this enchantment confer the following powers upon their wearers (or wielders) once per day: *bane, entropic shield*, and the ability to smite good (similar to the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]*). Clerics, [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Blackguard|blackguards]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladins]] possessing one of these items can also add its enhancement bonus to the save DC of their [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] (originally, the bonus went on the rebuke or command undead checks of clerics or blackguards).

Any non-evil creature that equips the item suffers two [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative levels]] that cannot be overcome in any way, although they never result in actual level loss and disappear when the item is set down again.

Strong conjuration; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gentle Repose|gentle repose]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]*; Price +3 bonus.
