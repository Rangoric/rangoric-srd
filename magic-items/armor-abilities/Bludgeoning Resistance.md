# Bludgeoning Resistance

Armor with this type of enchantment is endowed with [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] 5/magic, although only against bludgeoning attacks.

*Arms & Armor* prints this quality twice, the second time as *impact resistance* (caster level 7th).

Moderate abjuration; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]*; Price +1 bonus.
