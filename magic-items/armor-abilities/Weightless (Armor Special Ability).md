# Weightless

Armor given this enchantment has no weight at all. Weightless armor removes any adjustment for speed from a given armor type and reduces the arcane spell failure percentage chance by 10%. It also decreases the armor check penalty by 2 and increases the maximum Dexterity bonus by 2.

CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]*; Price +1 bonus for light or medium armor and +2 bonus for heavy armor.
