# Storing

The breast piece (or the front in case of a shield) of this armor has an innocuous catch ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] DC 25 to find) that allows the wearer to open the armor. Inside is an extra dimensional space that can be used in the exact same way as a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Bag of Holding|bag of holding]]* (type I).

Moderate conjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Secret Chest|secret chest]]*; Price +1 bonus.
