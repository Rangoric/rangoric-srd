# Moonbeam

Named because of the color, reflective quality, and location of its creation, *moonbeam* armor is actually made of magically woven spider webs. This process is exclusively known to the fey of [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/places/Between|Between]] (see *Faeries*), and can only be created there. *Moonbeam* armor has the physical properties of leather armor but the AC bonus of a chain shirt. Other armor types cannot be made with this ability. The armor creation process also imparts the ability to emit a burst of soft white luminescence three times per day (effects of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]* on all within a 5-foot radius).

Moderate transmutation and evocation [light]; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]*; Price +3 bonus.
