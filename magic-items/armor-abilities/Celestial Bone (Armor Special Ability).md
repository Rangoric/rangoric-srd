# Celestial Bone

This powerful enchantment literally changes an item from one of wood, metal, or stone to one of _celestial bone_, the very essence of goodness and light. Such armors (or shields) shine with a divine light, constantly radiating as if a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_ spell had been cast upon them. These items cannot be broken by any physical means (although spells such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Disjunction|mage’s disjunction]]_ do have a chance of destroying them) and they retain all prior bonuses and abilities from before receiving this enchantment. In addition, _celestial bone_ items confer the following powers upon their wearers once per day: _bless, shield of faith_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]_. Clerics or paladins possessing a _celestial bone_ item can add its enhancement bonus to the save DC of their [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] (originally, the bonus went on their turn undead checks).

Any non-good creature that equips the item incurs two [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative levels]] that cannot be overcome in any way, although they never result in actual level loss and disappear when the item is set down again.

Strong conjuration; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gentle Repose|gentle repose]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_; Price +3 bonus.
