# Shifting, Greater

Greater shifting items are similar to those with the standard shifting enchantment, except that they allow the wielder to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]* once per day in addition to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]*.

Strong (no school); CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]*; Price +4 bonus.
