# Shifting, Greater

Greater shifting items are similar to those with the standard shifting enchantment, except that they allow the wielder to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_ once per day in addition to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]_.

Strong (no school); CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]_; Price +4 bonus.
