# Spiteful

This armor's ability functions only if its wearer has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] class feature. When a melee attack made by an enemy within the wearer's melee reach would reduce the wearer to fewer than 0 hit points, the wearer can spend 1 panache point as an immediate action. This allows her to make a single melee attack against the creature that reduced her to 0 hit points or fewer. This occurs prior to the wearer falling [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] or dead.

Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*; Price +7,000 gp.
