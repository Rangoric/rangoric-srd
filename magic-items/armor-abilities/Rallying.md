# Rallying

This armor or shield is brightly polished, and its mirror-bright surface is magically endowed with the ability to bring comfort to the wearer's companions. Allies within 30 feet of the wearer gain a +4 morale bonus on defenses against fear effects. If the wearer has an ability that grants a morale bonus on defenses against fear (such as a paladin's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-courage|aura of courage]], a bard's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^countersong|countersong]], or a cavalier's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-banner|banner]] ability), the morale bonus increases to +6.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_; Price +5,000 gp.
