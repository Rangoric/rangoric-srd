# Stanching

A _stanching_ suit of armor helps stop the flow of blood from the wearer's wounds, automatically tightening like a tourniquet in the appropriate places while also magically reducing the severity of the wound. _Stanching_ armor reduces hit point damage, ability damage, or ability drain by an amount equal to its enhancement bonus if the damage or drain was from a [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] effect. The wearer also adds the armor's enhancement bonus to Constitution checks to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stable|stable]].

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|cure critical wounds]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]_; Price +1 bonus.
