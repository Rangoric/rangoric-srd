# Girding

*Girding* armor absorbs some of the damage done by a slashing weapon and disperses it across the entire suit of armor. Damage dealt by nonmagical slashing weapons is reduced to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]]. One half of the damage done by a +1 slashing weapon is reduced to nonlethal damage. One quarter of the damage done by a +2 slashing weapon is reduced to nonlethal damage. Slashing weapons with the *keen* quality or an enchantment of +3 or better bypasses this ability. Bludgeoning and piercing weapons do normal damage.

Moderate abjuration; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entropic Shield|entropic shield]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]*; Price +3 bonus.
