# Righteous

Armor with this special ability is often engraved or enameled with religious symbols. Once per day on command, the wearer can invoke an effect, as per the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*, lasting for 10 rounds. *Righteous* armor is always aligned toward good (positive energy), for the effects of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*. *Righteous* armor bestows one permanent [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative level]] on any evil creature attempting to wear it. The negative level remains as long as the armor is worn and disappears when it is removed. This negative level cannot be overcome in any way (including by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]* spells) while the armor is worn.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*; Price +27,000 gp.
