# Enduring

The wearer of _enduring_ armor can go without sleep for up to a week. It can become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], but no magical or mundane effect can make it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].
