# Enduring

The wearer of *enduring* armor can go without sleep for up to a week. It can become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], but no magical or mundane effect can make it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].

CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Life|Star of Life]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/constellations/The Drum|The Drum]]*; Price +1 bonus.
