# Offensive Spell Storing

This armor allows a spellcaster to store a single touch spell of up to 3rd level in it. Anytime a creature hits the wearer with a melee attack or melee touch attack, the armor can cast the spell on that creature as a swift action if the wearer desires. Once the spell has been cast from the armor, a spellcaster can cast any other targeted touch spell of up to 3rd level into it. The armor magically imparts to the wielder the name of the spell currently stored within it. A randomly rolled suit of *offensive spell storing* armor has a 50% chance to have a spell stored in it already. *Offensive spell storing* armor emits a strong aura of the evocation school, plus the aura of the stored spell.

Strong evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], creator must be a caster of at least 12th level; Price +1 bonus.
