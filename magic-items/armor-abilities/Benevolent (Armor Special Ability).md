# Benevolent

This suit of armor best serves a wearer who focuses on assisting and protecting his allies. *Benevolent* armor is usually decorated with motifs of prosperity, plenty, and generosity, such as helping hands, cornucopias, and flourishing plants. When the wearer of a suit of *benevolent* armor uses the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] action to give an ally a bonus to AC against an opponent's next attack, he also adds the *benevolent* armor's enhancement bonus to the ally's AC.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]*; Price +2,000 gp.
