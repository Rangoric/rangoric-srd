# Martyring

This armor allows the wearer to capitalize on her own injuries to heal her allies. Once per day as an immediate action, when an enemy confirms a critical hit against someone wearing *martyring* armor, the wearer can heal up to nine allies within 30 feet as if using the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds, Mass|mass cure light wounds]]* spell (1d8+9 hit points each). This special ability cannot be placed on armor that possesses an ability that reduces the chance of or negates a critical hit (such as the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]* special ability).

Moderate conjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds, Mass|mass cure light wounds]]*; Price +18,000 gp.
