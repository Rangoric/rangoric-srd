# Journeying

Armor of *journeying* weighs half as much as normal and has no armor check penalty. Its wearer's [[rangoric-srd/rules/Carrying, Movement, and Exploration#Overland Movement|overland movement]] is always ×1, whatever the terrain.

CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/constellations/The Steed|The Steed]]*; Price +2 bonus.
