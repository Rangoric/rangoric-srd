# Runic

This suit of armor has several runes scattered across its surface. In addition to whatever abilities the armor has, it also has one rune built into it that may be attuned to when worn by a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] ([[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#Casting Rune Spells|attuning]]). Also, the armor reduces the chance of arcane spell failure for rune magic by 10%. This reduction does not stack with other qualities that reduce the chance of spell failure.

CL 14th (rune caster); [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/runes/Eihwaz|Eihwaz]] rune; Price +3 bonus.
