# Divine Conduit

Forged by dwarven priestsmiths, *divine conduit* armor allows wearers with the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] ability to channel energy into the armor, enhancing its protective qualities. As a free action, you may spend one use of channel energy (in *Arms & Armor*, a turn or rebuke undead attempt) to gain one of the following effects: acid resistance 5, cold resistance 5, electricity resistance 5, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Evil|protection from evil]]*. The chosen effect lasts for 1 minute. The armor can have only one protective quality active at any given time, but you can end an effect at will.

Moderate abjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blasphemy|blasphemy]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Word|holy word]]*; Price +2 bonus.
