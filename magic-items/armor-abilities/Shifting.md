# Shifting

Many seek out armors and shields that possess this enchantment, but rogues and spell casters particularly covet them. Items with the shifting enchantment are almost always pitch black with intricate and arcane etchings all over their surfaces, making them appear more like a mystical map than an article of war or defense. Characters wearing or wielding them can cast the following spells as a standard action, once per day: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]*.

Strong (no school); CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]*; Price +2 bonus.
