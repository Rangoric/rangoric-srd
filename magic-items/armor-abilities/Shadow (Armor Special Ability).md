# Shadow

This armor is jet black and blurs the wearer whenever she tries to hide, granting a +5 competence bonus on Stealth checks. (The armor’s armor check penalty still applies normally.)

Faint illusion; CL 5th; Craft Magic Arms and Armor, invisibility; Price +3,750 gp.
