# Aporter

As a standard action, a suit of aporter armor or an aporter shield transports the wearer and her equipment to any spot within 800 feet that she can visualize or specify, as the [[rangoric-srd/powers/Dimension Door, Psionic|psionic dimension door]] power. The armor or shield can transport the wearer in this fashion twice per day.  
Moderate psychoportation; ML 10th; Craft Psionic Arms and Armor, psionic dimension door; Price +40,320 gp.
