# Dread

Upon command, a suit of *dread* armor causes the wearer’s clothes and appearance to become an illusion of horror. This has the same effect as frightful presence, which affects all creatures within 30 feet. Opponents within range become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] (if the save is failed) or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] if they make a Will save (DC 10 + ½ wearer’s level + wearer’s Cha modifier). An opponent that succeeds on the saving throw is immune to any further effects from the dread armor for another 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]*; Price +2 bonus.
