# Dread

Upon command, a suit of _dread_ armor causes the wearer’s clothes and appearance to become an illusion of horror. This has the same effect as frightful presence, which affects all creatures within 30 feet. It attacks the Will of opponents within range, its attack bonus ½ wearer’s level + wearer’s Cha modifier; those it hits become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], and those it misses [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]]. An opponent it misses is immune to any further effects from the dread armor for another 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]_; Price +2 bonus.
