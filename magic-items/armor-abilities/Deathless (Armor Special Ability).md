# Deathless

This armor protects its wearer from harmful negative and positive energy, including [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channeled energy]]. The armor absorbs the first 10 points of positive or negative energy damage per attack that the wearer would normally take. The wearer has a 25% chance to ignore negative levels from any attack. *Deathless* armor does not block healing of any kind and does not protect against positive or negative energy effects that do not deal damage or bestow negative levels. The *deathless* ability can be applied to armor of any sort, but not shields.

Moderate abjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]*; Price +1 bonus.
