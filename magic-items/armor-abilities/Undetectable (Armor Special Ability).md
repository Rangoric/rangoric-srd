# Undetectable

Upon command, armor with this quality changes shape and form to assume the appearance of a normal set of clothing. It also hides the aura of its wearer and shields him from magical means of detection, including scrying and divinations such as _clairaudience/clairvoyance, locate object,_ and _detect_ spells. A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_ spell or similar magic can see through this illusion after 1d4+1 rounds of observation.

Moderate illusion and abjuration; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Misdirection|misdirection]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscure Object|obscure object]]_; Price +3 bonus.
