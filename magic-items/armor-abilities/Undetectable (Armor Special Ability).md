# Undetectable

Upon command, armor with this quality changes shape and form to assume the appearance of a normal set of clothing. It also hides the aura of its wearer and shields him from magical means of detection, including scrying and divinations such as *clairaudience/clairvoyance, locate object,* and *detect* spells. A *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]* spell or similar magic can see through this illusion after 1d4+1 rounds of observation.

Moderate illusion and abjuration; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Misdirection|misdirection]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscure Object|obscure object]]*; Price +3 bonus.
