# Vouchsafing

_Vouchsafing_ armor grants its wearer DR 5/— while she is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Pinned (Condition)|pinned]]. This DR stacks with any other DR the wearer already has. If the wearer is somehow able to take standard actions while unconscious, nauseated, or stunned, or is able to make attacks while pinned, the armor does not grant its DR.

Moderate abjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]_; Price +1 bonus.
