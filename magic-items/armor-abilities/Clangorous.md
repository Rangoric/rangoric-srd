# Clangorous

A shield with this ability rings loudly when struck in combat. As an immediate action when the wearer is hit by an attack, the wearer can activate the shield to create a deafening bang equivalent to a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Thunderstone|thunderstone]]. If the attack is a critical hit, the wearer can instead create a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sound Burst|sound burst]]_ (+3 vs Fortitude, partial on a miss). The wearer decides the area for either effect, but must include the attacker in the area. Neither ability affects the wearer.

Moderate evocation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness/deafness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sound Burst|sound burst]]_; Price +1 bonus.
