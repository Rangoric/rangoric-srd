# Emergency Reservoirs

Emergency reservoirs are small magical pockets that can be fitted inside any type of heavy or medium armor. The reservoir holds one dose of a potion that is administered subdermally each time a critical hit is scored against the wearer. Typically the reservoirs are filled with healing potions. Medium armor can hold one emergency reservoir, and heavy armor can hold two. Only one reservoir can be triggered in a single round; the wearer presets the order of discharge.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Open-Close|open/close]]*; Price 540 gp; Cost 270 gp + 22 XP.
