# Reshaping

This quality is a favorite of stalkers, assassins and spies. Armor enchanted with the _reshaping_ property can reform itself upon command, changing its style and appearance. Plain black leather armor enchanted with this quality can transform into golden plate mail with an eagle crest, for example. Specific uniforms can be adapted to with a successful [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] check (DC 15). Only a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_ spell or similar magic sees through this enchantment.

Strong transmutation; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph Any Object|polymorph any object]]_; Price +2,700 gp.
