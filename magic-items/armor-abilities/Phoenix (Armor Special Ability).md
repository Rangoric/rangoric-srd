# Phoenix

Armor with the *phoenix* ability allows its wearer to experience an unusual rebirth; if he is slain by a particular type of attack, he may make a Will save (DC 10 + the slayer’s CR). If successful, the wearer is *resurrected* one round later, at full hit points. The *phoenix* armor is destroyed in the process.

The known subtypes of *phoenix* armor are: fire, cold, lightning, venom (which works against all poisons), and nemesis (which works against an assassin’s [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Death Attacks|death attack]]).

Strong necromancy; CL 18th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resurrection|resurrection]]*; Price +4 bonus.
