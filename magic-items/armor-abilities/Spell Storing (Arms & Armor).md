# Spell Storing

These items can store a single spell up to 3rd level. The stored spell must require only one action to cast. The wearer of the armor can cast a stored spell as a standard action. Once the spell has been cast, a spellcaster may replenish it with another spell. Spell storing items can hold either arcane or divine spells. Arcane spell failure percentages do not apply when casting spells from *spell storing* items.

Strong evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], creator must be a caster of at least 12th level; Price +1 bonus.
