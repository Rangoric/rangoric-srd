# Dastard

This armor special ability works only for evil creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-challenge|challenge]] ability (such as cavaliers) or the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-smite-good|smite good]] ability (such as antipaladins, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/half-fiend/Half-Fiend|half-fiends]], and creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creature/Fiendish Creature|fiendish creature]] template). A wearer with one of these abilities gains a +2 profane bonus to AC against attacks from the chosen opponent.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Good|protection from good]]_; Price +1 bonus.
