# Champion

This armor special ability works only for good creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-challenge|challenge]] ability (such as cavaliers) or the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|smite evil]] ability (such as paladins, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/half-celestials/Half-Celestial|half-celestials]], and creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/celestial-creatures/Celestial Creature|celestial creature]] template). A wearer with one of these abilities gains a +2 sacred bonus to AC against attacks from the chosen opponent.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Evil|protection from evil]]_; Price +1 bonus.
