# Gaze Protection

Shields are the only devices that can receive this particular enchantment, which gives them a semitransparent appearance. While the bearer interposes the shield directly between himself and his opponent, he is immune to any [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Gaze Attacks|gaze attack]] the opponent may have. However, while the shield is being used in this way, its Armor Class and enhancement bonuses are not effective versus melee attacks.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*; Price +1 bonus.
