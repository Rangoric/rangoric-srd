# Aspected

*Aspected* armor accommodates not only its wearer’s physical form but also incorporates cosmetic enhancements that suit the wearer’s tastes. The armor displays a holy symbol, family crest, clan emblem, or the like. While wearing *aspected* armor, the wearer suffers a –5 circumstance penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks to disguise himself but receives a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks in situations where his identity or affiliation are important.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]*; Price +250 gp.
