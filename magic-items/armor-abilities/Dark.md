# Dark

Armors and shields that possess the _dark_ enchantment are generally a dusky gray color, and are often adorned with strange inlays of dark stones, feathers, or fur. However, it is not for their appearance, but for their powers, that warriors and rogues alike love them. Items enchanted with the _dark_ enchantment allow the wearer to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_ (centered on the armor or shield) three times per day. Further, the wearer (wielder) gains the ability to see in this (but no other) magical _darkness_ normally.

Moderate evocation [darkness]; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_; Price +2 bonus.
