# Dark

Armors and shields that possess the *dark* enchantment are generally a dusky gray color, and are often adorned with strange inlays of dark stones, feathers, or fur. However, it is not for their appearance, but for their powers, that warriors and rogues alike love them. Items enchanted with the *dark* enchantment allow the wearer to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]* (centered on the armor or shield) three times per day. Further, the wearer (wielder) gains the ability to see in this (but no other) magical *darkness* normally.

Moderate evocation [darkness]; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*; Price +2 bonus.
