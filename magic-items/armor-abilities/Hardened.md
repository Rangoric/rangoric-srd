# Hardened

Armor with this quality has a higher DR than regular armor of its type. Light armor can be hardened once, medium armor can be hardened twice, and heavy armor can be hardened three times. Each application of this quality increases its base DR by 1 point. This quality is only available when using [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Defence Variants#Armor as Damage Reduction (Arms & Armor)|armor as damage reduction]] (*Arms & Armor*’s version).

CL 12th; 9 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (armorsmithing), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Interposing Hand|interposing hand]]*; Price +1 bonus.
