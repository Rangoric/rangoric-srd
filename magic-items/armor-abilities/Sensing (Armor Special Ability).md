# Sensing

A set of *sensing* armor compensates when its wearer has been [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]], is in total darkness (as long as the wearer doesn't have darkvision or the see in darkness ability), or is in magical darkness (as long as the wearer doesn't have the see in darkness ability). When one of these conditions affects the wearer, *sensing* armor immediately grants its wearer [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsight]] with a 5-foot range and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blindsense|blindsense]] with a 60-foot range. As soon as the wearer can see, the additional senses end. The wearer can't gain these abilities by closing her eyes.

Strong divination; CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]*; Price +3 bonus.
