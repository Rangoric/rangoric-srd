# Morphic

Both shields and armors with this enchantment typically have a varied array of different-sized creatures emblazoned upon them. These items confer the ability to alter the proportions of one’s body, as per the spells *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]*, as a standard action, up to three times per day. *Morphic* items always shrink and stretch to fit their wearer’s new size.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]*; Price +2 bonus.
