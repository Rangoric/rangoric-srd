# Negative Energy Resistance

A suit of armor or a shield with this property normally has a white or cream appearance. The armor absorbs the first 10 points of negative energy damage per attack (similar to the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]* spell). The armor does not protect against negative levels, ability damage, death effects, or any other negative energy attack except one that deals hit point damage.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]*; Price +18,000 gp.
