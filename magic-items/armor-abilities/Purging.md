# Purging

A favorite of healers, *purging* armor helps the body fight off attacks by poisons and toxins. Creatures wearing such armor while being tended to by someone treating poison with the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] skill (a [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] action) receive a +4 circumstance bonus on their ministrations to the poisoned creature. While wearing this armor, a creature gains a +2 circumstance bonus to all saving throws versus poison.

Moderate conjuration [healing]; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]*; Price +1 bonus.
