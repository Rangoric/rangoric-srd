# Time Buttress

This kind of shield gives the wielder a chance to avoid telling blows by using time itself as a shield. Once per day, the wielder can use [[rangoric-srd/powers/Timeless Body|timeless body]] as though manifesting the power.  
Strong psychoportation; ML 17th; Craft Psionic Arms and Armor, timeless body; Price +5 bonus.
