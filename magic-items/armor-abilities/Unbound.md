# Unbound

Armor with this special ability is often engraved or enameled with chaotic religious symbols. Once per day on command, the wearer can invoke an effect as per the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*, except the wearer gains DR 5/lawful instead of DR/evil or DR/good. This effect lasts for 10 rounds. *Unbound* armor bestows one permanent [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative level]] on any lawful creature attempting to wear it. The negative level remains as long as the armor is worn and disappears when it is removed. This negative level cannot be overcome in any way (including by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]* spells) while the armor is worn.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*, creator must be chaotic; Price +27,000 gp.
