# Quick Release

Armor and shields possessing this enchantment allow the wearer or wielder to instantly remove them when needed. These items are particularly handy when one falls into a deep, rushing stream while wearing his brand new double plate armor.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*; Price +1 bonus.
