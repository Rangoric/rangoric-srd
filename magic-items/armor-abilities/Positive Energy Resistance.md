# Positive Energy Resistance

A suit of armor or a shield with this property normally has a dark gray or black appearance. It absorbs the first 10 points of positive energy damage per attack (similar to the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]* spell). Because undead suffer damage from *cure* spells, they are typically the only wearers of such armor.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]*, creator must be undead; Price +18,000 gp.
