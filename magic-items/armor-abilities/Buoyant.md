# Buoyant

Armor given this property becomes buoyant and floats up to the surface upon mental command and remains active until deactivated. The _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Buoyancy|buoyancy]]_ enchantment can affect items that weigh up to ten times the weight of the buoyant item.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]_; Price +1 bonus.
