# Dire Totem

Items with the _dire totem_ enchantment are similar to those with the _totem_ enchantment, excepting that the circumstance bonus on wild empathy and Animal Handling checks and the ability bonus granted is increased to +4. The wearer can also speak with animals at will, instead of only three times per day.

Strong enchantment; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Animal|charm animal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally V|summon nature’s ally V]]_; Price +3 bonus.
