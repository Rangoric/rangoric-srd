# Dire Totem

Items with the *dire totem* enchantment are similar to those with the *totem* enchantment, excepting that the circumstance bonus on wild empathy and Animal Handling checks and the ability bonus granted is increased to +4. The wearer can also speak with animals at will, instead of only three times per day.

Strong enchantment; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Animal|charm animal]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally V|summon nature’s ally V]]*; Price +3 bonus.
