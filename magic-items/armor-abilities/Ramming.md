# Ramming

These shields are sturdily built and often bear the device of a ram or bull. When the wielder of a _ramming_ shield makes a shield bash attack as part of a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]], the shield's enhancement bonus to Armor Class applies to the attack and damage rolls. This doesn't stack with any existing enhancement the shield has. If the attack hits, as a swift action the wielder can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush combat maneuver]] against that target, adding the +2 bonus for charging and the shield's enhancement bonus to the combat maneuver check. Only shields that can make a shield bash attack can have the _ramming_ ability.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Force Punch|force punch]]_; Price +1 bonus.
