# Balm

Weapons that possess this enchantment constantly radiate an aura of soothing, positive energy that heals wounds, protects from diseases, and aids in fighting off infections and impurities. _Balm_ weapons commonly appear as normal items that possess a golden sheen, occasionally displaying sigils of healing and positive energy upon their surfaces. These weapons grant [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] 2 to characters wielding them, as well as a +2 luck bonus to Fortitude Defense versus disease.

Strong necromancy; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gentle Repose|gentle repose]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_; Price +3 bonus.
