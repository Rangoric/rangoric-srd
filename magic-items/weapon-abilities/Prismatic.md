# Prismatic

Wreathed in shifting colors, a *prismatic* weapon releases a spray of colors upon hitting a target. On a successful critical hit, colored rays spring from the weapon, affecting the target with a random prismatic effect. Except as noted, the effects are similar to those caused by a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]* spell. Bows, crossbows, and slings that are so ensorcelled bestow the *prismatic* ability upon their ammunition.

| 1d8 | Color | Effect (save DC 20) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Red | 20 points of fire damage (Reflex half) |
| 2 | Orange | 40 points of acid damage (Reflex half) |
| 3 | Yellow | 80 points of electricity damage (Reflex half) |
| 4 | Green | [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Green Prismatic Poison\|Poison]] (frequency 1/round for 6 rounds; effect death/1 Con damage; cure 2 consecutive Fortitude saves) |
| 5 | Blue | Turned to stone (Fortitude negates) |
| 6 | Indigo | Insane (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insanity (Spell)|insanity]]* spell; Will negates) |
| 7 | Violet | Sent to another plane (Will negates) |
| 8 | — | Struck by two rays (roll twice more, ignoring any other “8” results) |

Strong evocation; CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]*; Price +3 bonus.
