# Coup de Grace

Coup de grace weapons are exceptionally dangerous. On a successful critical hit, the weapon attacks the foe’s Will at +17, and on a hit it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round. While this ability does work on creatures that are immune to extra damage from critical hits, it does not work on creatures without an Intelligence score. Bows, crossbows, and slings bestow this ability on their ammunition.  
Strong telepathy; ML 19th; Craft Psionic Arms and Armor, psionic dominate; Price +5 bonus.
