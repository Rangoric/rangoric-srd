# Deathchill

This weapon inflicts double normal damage to all incorporeal creatures struck by it. On a critical hit against an incorporeal creature, instead of doubling the damage, the deathchill quality simply increases the damage multiplier by 1. This enchantment can only be applied to weapons that also bear the *ghost touch* quality.

Moderate conjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Metal|chill metal]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heat Metal|heat metal]]*, [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]] ability; Price +2 bonus.
