# Lucky, Greater

This special ability can only be placed on one-handed or two-handed firearms. A _greater lucky firearm_ is nearly identical to a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Lucky (Firearm Special Ability)|lucky]]_ gun, but its reservoir holds 3 grit points instead of 1. A firearm cannot have both this special ability and the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Lucky (Firearm Special Ability)|lucky]]_ special ability.

Moderate enchantment; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater|greater heroism]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]] user; Price +3 bonus.
