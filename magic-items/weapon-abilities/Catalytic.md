# Catalytic

This ability can be placed only on a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Corrosive|corrosive]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Acid Burst|corrosive burst]]_ weapon. When the wielder of a _catalytic_ weapon hits a creature, the weapon triggers a devastating chemical reaction that burns the struck creature. The reaction attacks the struck creature’s Fortitude at +5, and on a hit it takes 1d6 points of acid damage at the start of its turn for 1d4 rounds. Successive hits with the _catalytic_ weapon increase the duration but don't add to the damage. The creature can attempt to neutralize the reaction as a full-round action as if it were [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|on fire]] (including bonuses for dropping and rolling on the ground or jumping into water). Anyone who hits a creature subject to this chemical reaction with a natural weapon or unarmed strike takes 1d6 points of acid damage.

Strong evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Arrow|acid arrow]]_; Price +1 bonus.
