# Catalytic

This ability can be placed only on a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Corrosive|corrosive]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Acid Burst|corrosive burst]]* weapon. When the wielder of a *catalytic* weapon hits a creature, the weapon triggers a devastating chemical reaction that burns the struck creature. The creature struck must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save, or take 1d6 points of acid damage at the start of its turn for 1d4 rounds. Successive hits with the *catalytic* weapon increase the duration but don't add to the damage. The creature can attempt to neutralize the reaction as a full-round action as if it were [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|on fire]] (including bonuses for dropping and rolling on the ground or jumping into water). Anyone who hits a creature subject to this chemical reaction with a natural weapon or unarmed strike takes 1d6 points of acid damage.

Strong evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Arrow|acid arrow]]*; Price +1 bonus.
