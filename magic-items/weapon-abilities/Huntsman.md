# Huntsman

A _huntsman_ weapon helps its wielder locate and capture quarry. When the weapon is held in hand, the wielder gains an enhancement bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks made to track any creature the weapon has damaged in the past day. It also deals +1d6 points of damage to creatures the wielder has tracked with Fieldcraft in the past day.

Moderate divination; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Animals or Plants|detect animals or plants]]_; Price +1 bonus.
