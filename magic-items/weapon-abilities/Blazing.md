# Blazing

When a *blazing* weapon is drawn, it sends up a flare of light that can be seen for miles. Every creature within 20 feet other than the wielder must succeed on a DC 18 Reflex save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1d4 rounds. Only melee weapons can be blazing.

CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Light|Star of Light]]*; Price +2 bonus.
