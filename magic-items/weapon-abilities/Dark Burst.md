# Dark Burst

Weapons that possess this enchantment are generally indistinguishable from those with only basic enhancements. Occasionally, one may be found with a shadowy or dark motif and is typically mistaken for an unholy item. A *dark burst* weapon that scores a critical hit against a foe immediately casts *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]* upon the target.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*; Price +1 bonus.
