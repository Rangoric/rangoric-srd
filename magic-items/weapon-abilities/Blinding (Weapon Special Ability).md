# Blinding

Weapons with this quality dim the vision of those struck with a critical hit. The weapon attacks their Fortitude at +9, and on a hit they are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]], as per the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness]]_.

Moderate necromancy; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness]]_; Price +2 bonus.
