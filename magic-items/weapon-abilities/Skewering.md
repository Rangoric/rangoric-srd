# Skewering

This enchantment creates essentially the piercing version of a *vorpal* weapon. Upon a successful critical hit with a piercing weapon, it skewers the heart of the target, killing it immediately. Creatures without hearts or with unusual anatomies may not be affected by this quality, requiring the GM to use his best judgment in such circumstances. *Skewering* weapons must be of the piercing type.

Strong necromancy and transmutation; CL 18th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]*; Price +5 bonus.
