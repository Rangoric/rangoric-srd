# Will Sapping

A living creature struck with a weapon possessing this enhancement is attacked against its Will at +10, and on a hit takes 1 point of Wisdom damage. On a critical hit, the point is Wisdom drain instead.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]_; Price +2 bonus.
