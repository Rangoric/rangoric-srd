# Impact

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons that are not light weapons. An *impact* weapon delivers a potent kinetic jolt when it strikes, dealing damage as if the weapon were one size category larger. In addition, any [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush combat maneuver]] the wielder attempts while wielding the weapon gains a bonus equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus; this includes all bull rush attempts, not only those in which a weapon is used, such as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bull Rush Strike|Bull Rush Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Slam|Shield Slam]], or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Unseat|Unseat]].

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lead Blades|lead blades]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Form I|giant form I]]*; Price +2 bonus.
