# Benevolent

This ability can only be placed on a melee weapon. When the wielder of a *benevolent* weapon uses the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] action to grant an ally a bonus on attack rolls, he increases the aid another bonus by the enhancement bonus of the weapon.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]*; Price +1 bonus.
