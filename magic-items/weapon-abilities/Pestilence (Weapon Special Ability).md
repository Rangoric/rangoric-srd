# Pestilence

A *pestilence* weapon is a diseased weapon that passes on its sickness whenever it succeeds in a critical hit. A *pestilence* weapon is a carrier of one of the following [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|diseases]]: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Blinding Sickness|blinding sickness]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Cackle Fever|cackle fever]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Mindfire|mindfire]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Red Ache|red ache]], the [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Shakes|shakes]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Slimy Doom|slimy doom]]. The disease is chosen when the item is created. No matter what the disease, if the target fails the saving throw (Fortitude DC 18), he contracts it immediately, with no onset. A creature struck with a disease cannot be further infected with the same disease while still suffering its effects.

Moderate necromancy; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]*; Price +2 bonus.
