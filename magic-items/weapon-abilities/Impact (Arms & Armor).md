# Impact

The magical might of an *impact* weapon helps its wielder deliver tremendously powerful blows. Upon a successful hit with an *impact* weapon, an additional 1d4 points of damage is dealt. This quality may only be applied to bludgeoning weapons.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]]*; Price +1 bonus.
