# Spawning

These weapons allow the creation of minions to assist the wielder in combat. Any creature slain by a spawning weapon rises as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Zombie|zombie]] of the appropriate size with average hit points. The newly risen dead obey the verbal commands of the wielder for ten minutes or until slain again. Zombies created in this manner may be [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turned, destroyed, rebuked, or commanded]] as normal, but commanded undead become corpses again after the ten-minute duration ends.

A creature is considered slain by the weapon if a successful attack by it was the last external source of hit point damage to that creature. A *spawning* weapon does not raise the bodies of foes who are constructs or already undead.

Strong necromancy; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]*; Price +3 bonus.
