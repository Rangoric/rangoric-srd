# Repositioning

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A _repositioning_ weapon grants the wielder a +2 enhancement bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|combat maneuver checks to reposition]] a foe. If the wielder confirms a critical hit with the weapon, he can attempt to reposition his opponent as a free action. These reposition attempts still provoke attacks of opportunity as normal. A weapon cannot have both the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Anchoring|anchoring]]_ and _repositioning_ special abilities.

Moderate enchantment; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_; Price +3 bonus.
