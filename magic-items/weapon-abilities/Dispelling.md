# Dispelling

A *dispelling* weapon functions like a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Spell Storing|spell storing]]* weapon, but it may only store *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*; however, the caster level check to dispel gains an additional bonus equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus. This bonus also applies to a magus's [[rangoric-srd/class-options/magus-arcana/Dispelling Strike (Magus Arcana)|dispelling strike]] arcana or a barbarian's [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Spell Sunder (Rage Power)|spell sunder]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Sunder Enchantment (Rage Power)|sunder enchantment]] [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] check.

Strong abjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*, creator must be a caster of at least 10th level; Price +1 bonus.
