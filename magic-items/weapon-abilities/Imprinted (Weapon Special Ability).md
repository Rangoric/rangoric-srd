# Imprinted

This weapon was made for one particular person, family, gender, group, lineage, or race, specified at its creation. Anyone other than an intended user that picks up the weapon suffers 4d8 points of electricity damage (Fortitude save DC 15 for half damage) each time it is grasped. Once discharged, the weapon can be used normally until set down again, although it functions only as an ordinary nonmagical weapon. All other magical properties of an imprinted weapon remain hidden except for the imprinted owner.

Moderate divination; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glyph of Warding|glyph of warding]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]]*; Price +2,000 gp.
