# Silent

Sought by rogues and assassins, this ammunition appears no different from their ordinary counterparts. They are not hard to identify, however, for they create absolutely no noise when handled. Once fired, the ammunition created by this quality radiates a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]* spell in a 20-foot-radius. The *silence* persists for 5 minutes before dissipating. All ammunition fired activates, so the magical quality is wasted whether the projectile strikes its target or not.

Faint illusion [glamer]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*; Price +1 bonus.
