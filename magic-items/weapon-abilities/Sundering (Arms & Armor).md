# Sundering

Sundering weapons appear to be more reinforced and sturdier than other weapons of their type. Using a *sundering* weapon grants the wielder the use of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Sunder|Improved Sunder]] feat. A wielder who already has the Improved Sunder feat gains a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] attempts.

Moderate evocation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], creator must have the Improved Sunder feat; Price +2 bonus.
