# Corruption

This evil enhancement grants a defense adjustment to whomever it deals damage. Against good or neutral creatures, a _corruption_ weapon bestows a –1 profane penalty to all defenses. If the weapon strikes an evil creature, however, it grants it a +1 profane bonus to its defenses. The profane bonuses from weapons of _corruption_ stack and are cumulative up to a +5 bonus. The corruption effect lasts 1d4+1 minutes.

Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Doom|doom]]_; Price +2 bonus.
