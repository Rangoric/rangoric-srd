# Menacing

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A *menacing* weapon helps allies deal with [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]] foes. When the wielder is adjacent to a creature that is being flanked by an ally, the flanking bonus on attack rolls for all flanking allies increases by +2. This ability works even if the wielder is not one of the characters flanking the creature.

Moderate illusion; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantasmal Killer|phantasmal killer]]*; Price +1 bonus.
