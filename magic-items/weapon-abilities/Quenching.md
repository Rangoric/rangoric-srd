# Quenching

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A _quenching_ weapon thrust into a nonmagical fire of Medium size or smaller extinguishes it. When used against a creature of the fire subtype, it deals an extra 1d6 points of damage. The wielder of a _quenching_ weapon receives a +2 competence bonus on defenses against fire-based effects, and the weapon itself is immune to fire damage.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Metal|chill metal]]_; Price +1 bonus.
