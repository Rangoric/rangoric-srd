# Invisible

Only the grip of this weapon is visible. Blows from it are thus more difficult to avoid, and opponents suffer a –4 penalty to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] attempts against the weapon’s wielder. The wielder gains a +4 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feinting]] attempts with the weapon. This all comes at a price, however. This may only be applied to melee weapons.

Moderate illusion; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*; Price +1 bonus.
