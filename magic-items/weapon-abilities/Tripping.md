# Tripping

This weapon grants its wielder the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]] feat. A wielder who already has the Improved Trip feat gains a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attempts. This bonus can only be applied to weapon types that can be used to make trip attacks.

Moderate enchantment; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], creator must have the Improved Trip feat; Price +2 bonus.
