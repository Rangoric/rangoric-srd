# Solace, Greater

These weapons are similar to *solace* weapons except that they further boost a paladin’s healing abilities (adding an additional 2d8 points), add a +2 sacred bonus to all her saves, and upon her death, immediately transport her soul to her deity’s realm, where she is then honored among the chosen of her god. *Resurrection* is still possible, however.

Moderate enchantment; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|cure critical wounds]]*; Price +4 bonus.
