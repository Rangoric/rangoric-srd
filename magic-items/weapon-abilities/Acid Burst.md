# Acid Burst

Weapons with this quality function as [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Corrosive|acidic]] (corrosive) weapons that also splash their targets with acid on a successful critical hit. This magical acid does not damage inanimate objects. On a critical hit an acidic burst weapon with a x2 critical multiplier deals +1d10 points of acid damage, a x3 weapon deals +2d10, and a x4 weapon deals +3d10. Missile weapons with this enchantment bestow the acid burst quality upon their ammunition.

Moderate conjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Arrow|acid arrow]]*; Price +2 bonus.
