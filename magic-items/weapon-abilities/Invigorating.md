# Invigorating

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. After an attack with this weapon drops a living foe (kills it or drops it to negative hit points), the weapon infuses its wielder with vigor. If its wielder is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], the weapon removes the fatigue. If [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]], the wielder becomes fatigued instead. If neither exhausted nor fatigued, the wielder gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls and a +10 foot enhancement bonus to all modes of movement possessed until the end of the wielder's next turn.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Good Hope|good hope]]*; Price +2 bonus.
