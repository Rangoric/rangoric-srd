# Boring

This enhancement may only be placed upon ranged piercing weapons. The heads of weapons with this special ability appear to have a pair of insect-like mandibles. Upon a successful hit they animate and chew through flesh and bone, dealing an additional +1d6 points of damage to the target for 1d3 rounds thereafter, unless a full-round action is taken to remove the ammunition (inflicting an additional 2 points of damage per projectile in the process).

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Vermin|giant vermin]]*; Price +3 bonus.
