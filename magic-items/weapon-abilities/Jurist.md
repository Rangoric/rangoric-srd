# Jurist

When an inquisitor wielder uses her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|judgment]] class ability while wielding a _jurist_ weapon, it grants her an enhancement bonus on her [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks and to CMD. The bonus is +1 on the first round of her judgment, and increases by +1 each round, to a maximum of +3 on the third and following rounds.

Faint transmutation; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom|owl's wisdom]]_; Price +1 bonus.
