# Igniting

An _igniting_ weapon functions as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]_ weapon that also causes the target to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|catch fire]] upon striking it with a successful critical hit. Catching fire hits the target automatically, but the fire attacks again each round on its turn, and a miss puts it out. The _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]_ ability must be active for the weapon to set enemies on fire.

Strong evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Blade|flame blade]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|flame strike]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]_; Price +2 bonus.
