# Igniting

An *igniting* weapon functions as a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]* weapon that also causes the target to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|catch fire]] upon striking it with a successful critical hit. The target does not get a saving throw to avoid catching fire, but can make a save each round on its turn to put out the fire. The *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]* ability must be active for the weapon to set enemies on fire.

Strong evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Blade|flame blade]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|flame strike]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]*; Price +2 bonus.
