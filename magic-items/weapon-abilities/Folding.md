# Folding

Weapons that possess this enchantment usually display a hazy, almost insubstantial quality about them, which seems to come and go from moment to moment. Once the command word is spoken, the weapon folds into a small, thin square of nondescript, gray colored cloth that is easily stored. This enchantment is widely sought after by many, especially rogues and assassins. Unfolding these items is a standard action, usually keyed to a command phrase or sound, such as a whistle.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shrink Item|shrink item]]*; Price +1 bonus.
