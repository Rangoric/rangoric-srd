# Cunning

This special ability allows a weapon to find chinks in a foe's defenses using the wielder's knowledge of the target. Whenever the weapon's attack is a critical threat, the wielder gains a +2 bonus on the confirmation roll if she has 5 or more ranks in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|Knowledge skill]] that is related to the target's creature type (such as [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] for an outsider opponent).

Moderate divination; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]*; Price +1 bonus.
