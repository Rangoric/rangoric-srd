# Distracting

Weapons with this special ability cause foes struck by them to have difficulty controlling their magic. Any time a creature that has been struck by a *distracting* weapon would have to succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] to complete a spell or use a special ability or item, the DC of that check increases by 5. This distraction lasts for 1 minute. Once a *distracting* weapon affects a creature in this way, that creature can't be affected by a *distracting* effect for 24 hours, though it can be affected by a *greater distracting* effect.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Qualm|qualm]]*; Price +1 bonus.
