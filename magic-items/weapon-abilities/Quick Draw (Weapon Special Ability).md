# Quick Draw

A weapon with this ability seems to spring into the wielder’s hand when he needs it, acting as if he had the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]] feat: A normal weapon is drawn as a free action instead of a move action, and a concealed weapon is drawn as a move action instead of as a standard action. If the wielder personally possessed the Quick Draw feat, then drawing a hidden weapon becomes a free action, while drawing a normal weapon occurs so rapidly that those watching one would swear that the weapon *teleported* itself from its scabbard to the user’s hand.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*; Price +1 bonus.
