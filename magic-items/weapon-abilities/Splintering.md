# Splintering

This ability can be placed only on ranged piercing weapons. Upon striking a creature, a _splintering_ weapon shatters into multiple shards that dig into the target’s body. Until the splinters are removed, the creature cannot heal the damage inflicted by the weapon either naturally or magically. All splinters can be extracted with a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check and 10 minutes of work, or a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]_ spell. A vampire struck by a _splintering_ weapon made of wood is attacked against its Fortitude at +15, and on a hit is affected as if staked.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Warp Wood|warp wood]]_; Price +1 bonus.
