# Ki Intensifying

The weapon channels and amplifies the wielder's _ki_, allowing her to use her special _ki_ attacks through the weapon as though they were unarmed attacks. These attacks include the monk's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-strike|*ki* strike]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-quivering-palm|quivering palm]], and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]] feat. The attack bonus of _ki_ abilities used through the weapon increases by half the enhancement bonus of the weapon (minimum 1).

After a successful strike with a _ki intensifying_ weapon, the wielder can spend 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki* point]] as a swift action to perform a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] to bull rush, disarm, reposition, or trip as a free action without provoking attacks of opportunity. Any bonuses or penalties applicable to the weapon attack also apply to this combat maneuver check. The wielder cannot move from her space as a part of the chosen maneuver.

Only melee weapons can have the _ki intensifying_ ability. It cannot be placed on an _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Amulet of Mighty Fists +1|amulet of mighty fists]]_ or similar non-weapon item. Weapons with the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ki Focus|ki focus]]_ ability can be upgraded to be _ki intensifying_.

Strong transmutation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], creator must be a monk; Price +2 bonus.
