# Tracking

This enchantment allows the wielder to track a target he has engaged in battle and blooded with his *tracking* weapon. Any foe struck by the ranger with the tracking weapon grants the wielder a +4 insight bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/feats/Track|tracking]] attempts versus that creature for 1d4 days. In addition, the wielder gets a +4 insight bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to discover his quarry’s location.

Faint divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]*; Price +1 bonus.
