# Dark Solace, Greater

These weapons are similar to _dark solace_ weapons except that they further boost a blackguard’s sneak attack ability (adding an additional +2d6 points of damage), add a +2 profane bonus to all his defenses, and upon his death, immediately transport his soul to the sinister realm of his deity, where he then becomes a lesser devil or demon.

Moderate enchantment; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Critical Wounds|inflict critical wounds]]_; Price +4 bonus.
