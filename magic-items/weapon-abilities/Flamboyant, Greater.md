# Flamboyant, Greater

This special ability can be placed only on one-handed or light piercing melee weapons. A _greater flamboyant_ weapon is nearly identical to a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flamboyant|flamboyant]]_ weapon, but its reservoir holds 3 panache points instead of 1. A weapon cannot have both this special ability and the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flamboyant|flamboyant]]_ special ability.

Faint abjuration and divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater|greater heroism]]_, creator must be a panache user; Price +3 bonus.
