# Glorious

A *glorious* weapon glows with a dazzling light equal to a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]* spell when drawn. The wielder cannot suppress this light, though it can be temporarily suppressed by any effect that can suppress *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*. A *glorious* weapon flashes with light, and any creature the wielder attacks in melee is automatically [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] until the beginning of the wielder's next turn. When a *glorious* weapon confirms a critical hit, the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] until the beginning of the wielder's next turn (DC 14 Will negates); if the weapon's critical multiplier is greater than ×2, this blindness lasts 1 additional round per multiple over ×2. Only a melee weapon can have the *glorious* ability.

Moderate evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness/deafness]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]*; Price +2 bonus.
