# Dispelling

Weapons with the dispelling quality dispel magical spells and effects on a successful hit. For example, striking a creature under the effects of *improved invisibility* may make it visible. On a successful hit, roll a targeted dispel check, as per the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]*, at the weapon’s caster level vs. caster level of the effect. Only one spell or effect may be dispelled per hit. If more than one effect is in place, the weakest (in terms of caster level) is dispelled first. This weapon cannot be used to dispel effects not applied to a creature.

Strong abjuration; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]*; Price +3 bonus.
