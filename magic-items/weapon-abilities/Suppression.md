# Suppression

An opponent or object struck by this kind of weapon is subject to a targeted [[rangoric-srd/powers/Dispel Psionics|dispel psionics]] power. The wielder makes a dispel check (1d20 + 5 + manifester level, maximum +15) against a DC of 11 + the manifester level of the power to be dispelled. Bows, crossbows, and slings bestow this ability upon their ammunition, but can do so only three times per day.  
Moderate psychokinesis ML 10th; Craft Psionic Arms and Armor, dispel psionics; Price +2 bonus.
