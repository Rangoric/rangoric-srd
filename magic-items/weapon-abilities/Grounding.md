# Grounding

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A _grounding_ weapon can safely touch electrically charged surfaces without harm to its wielder. When used against a creature of the air subtype, it deals an extra 1d6 points of damage. The wielder of a _grounding_ weapon receives a +2 competence bonus on defenses against air- and electricity-based effects, and the weapon itself is immune to electricity damage.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]_; Price +1 bonus.
