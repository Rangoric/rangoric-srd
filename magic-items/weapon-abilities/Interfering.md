# Interfering

A successful strike with an interfering weapon disrupts any spellcasting ability the target possesses. For the three rounds following a hit, the target must succeed on a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] (DC 17) in order to cast a spell. A critical hit from an interfering weapon increases the effect’s duration to 6 rounds and the concentration check DC to 21. The duration of the enchantment is not cumulative for multiple strikes. Bows, crossbows, and slings so enchanted bestow this power upon their ammunition.

Moderate enchantment; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*; Price +2 bonus.
