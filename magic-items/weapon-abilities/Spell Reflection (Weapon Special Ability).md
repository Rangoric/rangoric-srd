# Spell Reflection

Those who must do battle with spellcasters seek out weapons with the _spell reflection_ enhancement. These items constantly shimmer with an aura of mirrorlike motes, their surfaces silvered and highly reflective. A _spell reflecting_ weapon’s enchantment takes effect when a spell of a specific school specifically targets the wielder. The spell attacks the wielder’s Reflex (even if it normally makes no attack), and on a miss, the spell’s effect is reflected back upon the caster as per the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]]_ spell. Each such weapon is attuned to only one school of magic, which must be specified at the time of creation. This quality can be used no more than three times per day, and a weapon can shield against no more than a single school of magic (_spell reflection_ qualities do not stack).

Effect and area spells do not activate the _spell reflection_ quality. In addition, a weapon must be wielded in order for the _spell reflection_ quality to activate (it is dormant when sheathed or stored).

Strong abjuration; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]]_; Price +2 bonus.
