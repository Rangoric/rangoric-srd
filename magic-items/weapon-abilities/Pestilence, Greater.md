# Pestilence, Greater

The more powerful version of the *pestilence* enchantment, *greater pestilence* weapons carry all of the diseases of their lesser counterparts and randomly spread them upon a successful critical hit (Fortitude DC 20). In all other respects, *greater pestilence* weapons function identically to their lesser counterparts.

Moderate necromancy; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]*; Price +3 bonus.
