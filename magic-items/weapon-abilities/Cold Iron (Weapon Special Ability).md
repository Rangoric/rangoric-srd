# Cold Iron

Over the years, many weaponsmiths have sought a way to blend the strength of steel with the supernatural powers of cold forged iron. It wasn’t until 300 years ago that a half-insane wizard developed a means to merge the two into a magical alloy. A weapon with this ability inflicts an additional 2 hit dice of damage (whatever the weapon’s normal damage dice) to fey creatures. Damage inflicted by a cold iron weapon must be healed magically.

For weapons simply made of cold iron, see [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Special Materials#^cold-iron|cold iron]].

Price +2 bonus.
