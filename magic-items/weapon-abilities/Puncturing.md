# Puncturing

*Puncturing* weapons are able to penetrate a single substance (such as stone, iron, or gem-stone) far more easily than usual weapons, granting a +2 luck bonus to attack and damage rolls when used against objects made of or opponents wearing armor made of the appropriate substance. Such weapons must have part of their structure (such as the hilt, the haft, the head, and so on) forged from the substance. This is often simply a round or oval inset of the material somewhere on the weapon’s surface. These items can be very handy for tasks such as scaling walls, fighting armored opponents, or even combating particular golem types.
