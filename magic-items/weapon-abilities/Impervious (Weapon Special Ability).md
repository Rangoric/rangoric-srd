# Impervious

An _impervious_ weapon is warded from damage and decay. A metallic weapon cannot rust and a wooden weapon cannot rot or warp, even by magical or supernatural means. An _impervious_ weapon gains double the normal bonus to its hardness and hit points for each point of its enhancement bonus. The break DC for an _impervious_ weapon and the wielder's combat maneuver defense against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder maneuvers]] against the _impervious_ weapon each gain a bonus equal to twice the weapon's enhancement bonus.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fabricate|fabricate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Make Whole|make whole]]_; Price +3,000 gp.
