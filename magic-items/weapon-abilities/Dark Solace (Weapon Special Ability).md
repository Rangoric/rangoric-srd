# Dark Solace

Only [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Blackguard|blackguards]] can truly utilize the full abilities of *dark solace* weapons. These items usually appear to have been completely scorched to a flat, dull, black finish. Normally, as a part of the enchantment, the wielder’s unholy symbol is added to the item in bas-relief. When the weapon’s powers are used, the symbol glows with an unholy, searing, crimson light.

Blackguards using these items gain the corrupted assistance of their dark gods when harming others via their sneak attack (which adds an additional +1d6 points of damage to the attack). Also, the blackguard herself gains a +1 profane bonus to all saves as long as she wields the item. Lastly, a *dark solace* weapon also precludes that the wielder, if she should perish while using it, immediately becomes a sentient undead in the servitude of the dark power that fueled the item.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Minor Wounds|inflict minor wounds]]*; Price +2 bonus.
