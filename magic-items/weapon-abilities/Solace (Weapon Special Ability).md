# Solace

Many may wield weapons that possess this enchantment, but only paladins can truly utilize a *solace* weapon’s full abilities. These items usually appear as if completely bleached to a dull white finish. Normally, as a part of the enchantment, the wielder’s holy symbol is added to the item in bas-relief. When the powers of a *solace* item are used, the symbol glows with a holy, silvered light.

A paladin in contact with a *solace* weapon gains divine assistance (an additional 1d8 points) when healing others via his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|lay on hands]] power. Also, the paladin gains a +1 sacred bonus to all saves as long as he wields the item. Lastly, a *solace* weapon also prevents its wielder, if he should perish while using it, from becoming undead.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]*; Price +2 bonus.
