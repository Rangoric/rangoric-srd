# Limning

When a _limning_ weapon is wielded, a pale glow outlines it. On a successful strike against a creature affected by a means of magical concealment, the weapon outlines the creature in _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_ (as the spell) for 1 round. Magical means of concealment include _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, and similar effects, whether from a spell, spell-like ability, or supernatural means, but not those provided by extraordinary abilities. A _limning_ weapon has no special effect against creatures merely hiding or concealed by nonmagical means, or those hidden by environmental conditions (even magical) such as darkness or fog.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_; Price +1 bonus.
