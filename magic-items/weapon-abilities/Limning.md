# Limning

When a *limning* weapon is wielded, a pale glow outlines it. On a successful strike against a creature affected by a means of magical concealment, the weapon outlines the creature in *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]* (as the spell) for 1 round. Magical means of concealment include *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, and similar effects, whether from a spell, spell-like ability, or supernatural means, but not those provided by extraordinary abilities. A *limning* weapon has no special effect against creatures merely hiding or concealed by nonmagical means, or those hidden by environmental conditions (even magical) such as darkness or fog.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*; Price +1 bonus.
