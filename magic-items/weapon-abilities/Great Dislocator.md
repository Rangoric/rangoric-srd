# Great Dislocator

The wielder of this kind of weapon can attempt to greatly dislocate a designated foe up to three times per day. On a successful hit, the weapon attacks the foe’s Will at +10, and on a hit it is cast into a random alternate plane of existence. If the weapon misses, the use is wasted. Bows, crossbows, and slings bestow this ability upon their ammunition.  
Strong psychoportation; ML 12th; Craft Psionic Arms and Armor, psionic plane shift; Price +4 bonus.
