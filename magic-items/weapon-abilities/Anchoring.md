# Anchoring

This special ability can only be added to a melee weapon or a thrown weapon. An *anchoring* weapon pins a target in place and prevents it from moving. As a swift action, the weapon can be fixed in place in a point in space, functioning as an *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rods/Immovable Rod|immovable rod]]*. This ability can also be used when the wielder hits a creature with a melee attack using an *anchoring* weapon. This anchors the target to the weapon, preventing it from moving away from the weapon. The target is not [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]]; it simply cannot move from its location without first destroying the weapon or making a successful DC 30 Strength check as a full-round action to move with the weapon up to 10 feet. An *anchoring* weapon remains motionless and cannot be used to attack while it is anchoring a creature. An *anchoring* weapon has no effect on amorphous creatures, including elementals, oozes, and creatures in gaseous or liquid form. It also cannot anchor incorporeal creatures unless the weapon also has the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]]* special ability.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]*; Price +2 bonus.
