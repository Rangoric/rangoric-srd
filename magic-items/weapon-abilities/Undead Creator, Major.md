# Undead Creator, Major

This type of weapon is similar to a *minor undead creator* except that slain creatures rise again as wights instead of zombies. Also, the maximum number of undead the wielder may control rises by +15 HD. A wielder without the ability to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|command undead]] can control up to 15 HD worth of wights created only through use of his weapon.

Strong necromancy; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead|create undead]]*; Price +5 bonus.
