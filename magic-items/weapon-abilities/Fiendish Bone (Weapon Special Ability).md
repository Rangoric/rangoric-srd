# Fiendish Bone

Weapons possessing this quality not only have their appearances altered, but their entire composition. This powerful enchantment literally changes the weapon from one of wood, metal, or stone to one of fiendish bone – the very essence of evil and darkness.

These items radiate with a profane gloom, possessing a constant umbra as if a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]* spell had been cast upon them. These weapons cannot be broken by any physical means (although powerful spells such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Disjunction|mage’s disjunction]]* have a chance of destroying them) and retain all prior bonuses and abilities. In addition, a weapon with this enchantment confers the following powers upon its wielder once per day: *bane*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entropic Shield|entropic shield]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]*. Clerics or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladins]] possessing these weapons can also add the weapon’s enhancement bonus to the save DC of their [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] (in *Arms & Armor*, the bonus went on the rebuke or command undead checks of clerics or blackguards). Any non-evil creature that picks up the weapon gains two [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative levels]] that cannot be overcome in any way, although they never result in actual level loss and disappear when the weapon is set down again.

Strong conjuration; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gentle Repose|gentle repose]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]*; Price +3 bonus.
