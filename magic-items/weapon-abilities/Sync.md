# Sync

Extremely rare, _sync_ weapons are usually only found in the possession of their deadly creators, who give these weapons their name (as detailed in _Complete Minions_). Weapons with the _sync_ enchantment behave like those with the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Alterable, Major|major alterable]]_ quality in that they can change their form to match their masters’ wills, however, they can only take the form of melee weapons. _Sync_ weapons also display a strikingly different and far more lethal power.

Just as their creators’ abilities and skills increase in each round of single, focused combat, weapons that possess the _sync_ enchantment also increase their power. For each round of focused, one-on-one combat against the same individual opponent, the weapon’s user gains a cumulative luck bonus of +1 (up to a max bonus of +10) to attack and damage rolls made with the weapon. Once a different opponent is targeted with the weapon, the enhancement bonus drops back to its original level. When wielded by a sync, this type of weapon mimics the shape of its foe’s weapon.

Strong enchantment; CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph Any Object|polymorph any object]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_, creator must be a sync; Price +6 bonus.
