# Truthcutter

A _truthcutter_ weapon has the ability to reveal hidden truths, showing things as they truly are. In the instant a _truthcutter_ weapon strikes an opponent whose true self is somehow hidden by magic (whether it be the result of an _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph|polymorph]]_ spell, a doppelganger in altered form, or an invisible enemy) the truth of his situation is shown, just for an instant. This does not dispel the opponent’s magic, it merely reveals the truth about him for a split-second, but this is often enough to give the wielder of the _truthcutter_ weapon important knowledge about who (or what) he’s fighting. If used against an illusion, the illusion flickers out when “struck” by the _truthcutter_ weapon but returns again almost immediately. A strike by a _truthcutter_ weapon dispels a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]_ just as a normal weapon does, however. Bows, crossbows, and slings bestow the truthcutter ability upon their ammunition.

Moderate divination; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_; Price +2 bonus.
