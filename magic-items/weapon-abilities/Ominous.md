# Ominous

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. An *ominous* weapon trails a shadowy haze behind every stroke, and moans a menacing dirge in battle. An *ominous* weapon adds its enhancement bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks made by the wielder. In addition, when an *ominous* weapon confirms a critical hit, the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 minute (DC 13 Will negates); if the weapon's critical multiplier is greater than ×2, this condition lasts 1 additional minute per multiple over ×2. A creature that gains the shaken condition from an *ominous* weapon cannot gain that condition again from the same weapon for 24 hours.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Doom|doom]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scare|scare]]*; Price +1 bonus.
