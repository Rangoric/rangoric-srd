# Lucky (Firearm Special Ability)

This special ability can only be placed on one-handed or two-handed firearms. A *lucky firearm* has its own magical reservoir of grit. Usually this grit is stored within the marks of an engraving or in a trinket that hangs from the firearm. Often these marks take the form of holy symbols or fetishes, but such a reservoir can take just about any form. This reservoir holds 1 grit point, which is refreshed at the start of each day. Whether or not the wielder of a *lucky firearm* has any deeds, she can always spend 1 grit point from the *lucky firearm* to reroll an attack from it that would result in a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms#Misfires|misfire]]. When the wielder does so, she must take the second result, even if that attack roll also results in a misfire.

Moderate transmutation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]*, creator must be a grit user; Price +1 bonus.
