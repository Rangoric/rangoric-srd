# Muse

Muse weapons are always elaborate affairs, resplendent with etchings of famous legends and tooled inlays of precious stones and metals, which are pleasures for the eye to behold. A bard in possession of a *muse* weapon receives a +5 insight bonus to all [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-knowledge|bardic knowledge]] checks. Anyone possessing a *muse* weapon gains a +2 bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] checks and is able to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]* as a standard action once per day.

Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]*, caster must have at least one level in bard; Price +2 bonus.
