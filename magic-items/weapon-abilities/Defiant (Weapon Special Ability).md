# Defiant

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A *defiant* weapon helps its wielder stay alive in desperate conditions. It stays in its wielder's hand even if she is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]. She adds the weapon's enhancement bonus as a bonus on checks to stabilize when [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] and on saving throws to end ongoing conditions such as disease, poison, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]]*. If the wielder possesses [[rangoric-srd/feats/Heroic Defiance|Heroic Defiance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Heroic Recovery|Heroic Recovery]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Great Fortitude|Improved Great Fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Iron Will|Improved Iron Will]], or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Lightning Reflexes|Improved Lightning Reflexes]], she gains a number of additional daily uses equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus that can be used on any of these feats.

Strong abjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Heroic Defiance|Heroic Defiance]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]*; Price +2 bonus.
