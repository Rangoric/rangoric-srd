# Valiant

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. When a cavalier wielding a _valiant_ weapon targets a foe with his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-challenge|challenge]] ability, the _valiant_ weapon deals an additional 1d6 points of damage against that foe. The wielder receives a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] checks made to disarm or sunder a challenged foe's weapon as well as a +4 bonus to his CMD against disarm and sunder attacks from that foe.

Faint divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_; Price +1 bonus.
