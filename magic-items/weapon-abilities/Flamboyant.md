# Flamboyant

This special ability can be placed only on one-handed or light piercing melee weapons. A *flamboyant* weapon has its own magical reservoir of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] that its wielder can spend to use her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|deeds]]. Usually this panache is stored within a jewel or other adornment. This reservoir holds 1 panache point, which is refreshed at the start of each day.

Whether or not the wielder of a *flamboyant* weapon has any deeds, she can always spend 1 panache point from the *flamboyant* weapon to reroll an attack made with it that missed due to rolling a natural 1. When the wielder does so, she must take the second result, even if it also misses.

Moderate transmutation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]*, creator must be a panache user; Price +1 bonus.
