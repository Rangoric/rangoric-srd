# Deflecting

*Deflecting* items project an invisible field of force that provides additional protection beyond that of the weapon itself and its enhancement bonus. This field can either be invisible, or appear as a shadowy shell around the wielder. This enchantment adds a deflection bonus to the weapon, its cost varying depending on its potency.

+1 deflection bonus: Moderate abjuration [force]; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]*; Price +2 bonus.

+2 deflection bonus: Moderate abjuration [force]; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]*; Price +3 bonus.

+3 deflection bonus: Moderate abjuration [force]; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]*; Price +4 bonus.

+4 deflection bonus: Moderate abjuration [force]; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]*; Price +5 bonus.
