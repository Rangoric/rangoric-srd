# Deflecting

_Deflecting_ items project an invisible field of force that provides additional protection beyond that of the weapon itself and its enhancement bonus. This field can either be invisible, or appear as a shadowy shell around the wielder. This enchantment adds a deflection bonus to the weapon, its cost varying depending on its potency.

+1 deflection bonus: Moderate abjuration [force]; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_; Price +2 bonus.

+2 deflection bonus: Moderate abjuration [force]; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_; Price +3 bonus.

+3 deflection bonus: Moderate abjuration [force]; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_; Price +4 bonus.

+4 deflection bonus: Moderate abjuration [force]; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_; Price +5 bonus.
