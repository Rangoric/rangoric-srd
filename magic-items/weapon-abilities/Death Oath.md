# Death Oath

A set of up to ten weapons can be crafted at one time with this enchantment. Each weapon can be of a different type and each may bear various other additional enchantments. The culmination of the *death oath* requires one person for each weapon to be present for a daylong ceremony during which they vow the death of a single, named individual or entity (as per the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Geas-Quest|geas/quest]]* spell). It is not necessary that the original oath takers use the weapons; anyone can gain the full benefits of one but the oath takers are the ones subject to the *geas*.

If the named individual is killed by one of these weapons, his soul is drawn into the weapon. Unless the weapon is later destroyed, this prevents resurrection by any means, including *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]* or a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]*. After the named individual is killed by one of these weapons, all benefits and special abilities granted by the *death oath* to all weapons in the set permanently disappear (excepting that the soul remains trapped in one weapon).

Whenever the oath taker for a particular weapon is killed, that weapon permanently disappears, and all remaining weapons in the set gain a cumulative +1 luck bonus to damage. For instance, if two oath takers died, the remaining weapons would each receive a +2 luck bonus to damage. Death oath weapons that have disappeared do not reappear if their oath takers are resurrected. In addition, other benefits accrue after a certain number of oath takers are killed. These effects are cumulative, and initiate whenever the weapon scores a critical hit. See the table below for details.

| Oath Takers Killed | Additional Effect |
|---|---|
| 3 or more | [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|Poison]] (Fortitude DC 18, 1d6 Str/1d6 Str) |
| 6 or more | *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|Dimensional anchor]]* (Will DC 19) |
| 9 | Death (Fortitude DC 22; on a success, still 3d6+19 points of additional damage) |

If the last oath taker for a weapon in a set is killed, the weapon vanishes, leaving the oath forever unfulfilled. If the oath can no longer be accomplished due to the death of the targeted individual by other means, the weapons still remain and receive their full powers until all oath takers have passed away.

Strong (all schools); CL 19th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death|finger of death]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Geas-Quest|geas/quest]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|ray of enfeeblement]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Soul Bind|soul bind]]*, an additional 1,000 gp per HD of the target of the oath must be spent in the ritual; Price Per weapon, based on the number of weapons in the set: 1–3 (+1 bonus), 4–6 (+2 bonus), 7–9 (+3 bonus), 10 (+4 bonus).
