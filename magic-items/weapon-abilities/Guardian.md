# Guardian

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A _guardian_ weapon allows the wielder to transfer some or all of the weapon's enhancement bonus to his defenses as a bonus that stacks with all others. As a free action, the wielder chooses how to allocate the weapon's enhancement bonus at the start of his turn before using the weapon. The bonus on defenses lasts until his next turn. Only the weapon's own enhancement bonus can be sacrificed, not any enhancement bonus provided by other effects such as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon, Greater|greater magic weapon]]_ spell. However, the total of such effects is still diminished by the amount allocated to improving defenses.

If a weapon has both the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Defending|defending]]_ and _guardian_ abilities, allocating a single point of enhancement bonus increases either AC or defenses, but not both.

Moderate abjuration; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_; Price +1 bonus.
