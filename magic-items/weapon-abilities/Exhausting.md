# Exhausting

This ability can be placed only on melee weapons. An *exhausting* weapon can bring a great weariness to opponents in battle. On a critical hit, the wielder can choose to forgo the additional damage dice to instead sap the struck creature's strength and stamina, causing it to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]]. Opponents that are already exhausted don't suffer any additional effects, and creatures immune to the effects of exhaustion or to critical hits are not affected.

Strong necromancy; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Exhaustion|ray of exhaustion]]*; Price +3 bonus.
