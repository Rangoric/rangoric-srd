# Feybrand

Generally used in some form of polearm, these weapons sense the presence of fey and the metal head or tip of the weapon begins to heat up, glowing red hot when within close proximity. When fey creatures are within 60 ft. of the weapon the tip visibly heats up, glowing softly and doing an additional +1d4 points of fire damage. When a fey creature comes within 30 ft. the weapon’s tip becomes red hot, glowing brightly. Its heat can be felt from up to 5 ft. away. When a feybrand weapon strikes, it inflicts an additional 2d4 points of fire damage.

Moderate evocation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heat Metal|heat metal]]*; Price +1 bonus.
