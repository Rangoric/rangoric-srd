# Force Bubble

This enchantment can only be added to ammunition. Any creature hit by a *force bubble* missile must make a Reflex save (DC 17) or be surrounded by an impenetrable bubble of force for 2d4 rounds, similar to a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resilient Sphere|resilient sphere]]* spell.

Moderate evocation [force]; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resilient Sphere|resilient sphere]]*; Price +2 bonus.
