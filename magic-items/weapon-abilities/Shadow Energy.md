# Shadow Energy

A shadow energy weapon has its effective portion (blade, axe head, spearhead, etc.) transformed into shadow. A shadow energy weapon ignores nonliving matter. Armor bonuses provided by physical armor (as opposed to spells) are ineffective against weapons imbued with Shadow Energy. Other bonuses to Armor Class (dexterity, deflection, dodge, natural armor, etc.) still apply. A shadow energy weapon automatically dispels a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]]* spell if touched to the object upon which the spell was cast. A shadow energy weapon causes no damage to targets that are not alive. Bows, crossbows, and slings cannot be enchanted with this ability. This does not alter the item’s weight.

CL 16th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Touch|chill touch]]*; Price +4 bonus.
