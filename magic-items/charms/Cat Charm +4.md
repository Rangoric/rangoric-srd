# Cat Charm +4

_A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charms|charm]], worn on a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charm Bracelet|charm bracelet]]._

Varying in type of feline represented (though always some form of cat) these onyx _charms_ add to the wearer’s Dexterity score with an enhancement bonus of +2, +4, or +6.

CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Charms and Bracelets|Craft Charms and Bracelets]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat’s grace]]_; Price 16,000 gp.
