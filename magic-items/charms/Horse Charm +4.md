# Horse Charm +4

_A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charms|charm]], worn on a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charm Bracelet|charm bracelet]]._

In the form of a galloping horse, this polished jet figurine is mounted on delicately braided silver wires. These _charms_ grant an enhancement bonus to the wearer’s Constitution score of +2, +4, or +6.

CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Charms and Bracelets|Craft Charms and Bracelets]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear’s endurance]]_; Price 16,000 gp.
