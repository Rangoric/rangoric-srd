# Cat Charm

*A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charms|charm]], worn on a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charm Bracelet|charm bracelet]].*

Varying in type of feline represented (though always some form of cat) these onyx *charms* add to the wearer’s Dexterity score with an enhancement bonus of +2, +4, or +6.

CL 3rd (+2), 6th (+4), 10th (+6); [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Charms and Bracelets|Craft Charms and Bracelets]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat’s grace]]*; Price 4,000 gp (+2), 16,000 gp (+4), 36,000 gp (+6).
