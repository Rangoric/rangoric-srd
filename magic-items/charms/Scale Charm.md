# Scale Charm

*A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charms|charm]], worn on a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charm Bracelet|charm bracelet]].*

Made from small slices of dragon scales and strung on miniscule steel chains, these *charms* grant the wearer +2, +4, or +6 deflection bonus to AC.

CL 3rd (+2), 6th (+4), 10th (+6); [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Charms and Bracelets|Craft Charms and Bracelets]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield of Faith|shield of faith]]*; Price 8,000 gp (+2), 32,000 gp (+4), 72,000 gp (+6).
