# Charm Bracelet

Magical *charm bracelets* found in the fey realm are typically used to confer on the wearer the abilities of magical [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charms|charms]] attached to them by fine chain or metal threads. The *charm bracelet* has only the slightest weight to it, and is always made of a single elaborately carved loop of silver, gold, sculpted gemstone, precious wood, or bone. The *bracelet* must have a value of no less than 75 gp. The *charm bracelet* has an AC of 16, 5 hit points, and a break DC of 18. Some *bracelets* are only equipped to carry smaller numbers of charms, while others can carry as many as eight.
