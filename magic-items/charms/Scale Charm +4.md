# Scale Charm +4

_A [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charms|charm]], worn on a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/charms/Charm Bracelet|charm bracelet]]._

Made from small slices of dragon scales and strung on miniscule steel chains, these _charms_ grant the wearer +2, +4, or +6 deflection bonus to AC.

CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Charms and Bracelets|Craft Charms and Bracelets]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield of Faith|shield of faith]]_; Price 32,000 gp.
