# Staff of Accompaniment

This staff is adorned with musical notes. When held and used with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]], it accompanies the song with the sounds of wind instruments, granting a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chord of Shards|Chord of shards]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Saving Finale|Saving finale]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Allegro|Allegro]]_ (2 charges)

Moderate evocation and transmutation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Allegro|allegro]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chord of Shards|chord of shards]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Saving Finale|saving finale]]_; Price 14,800 gp; Cost 7,400 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
