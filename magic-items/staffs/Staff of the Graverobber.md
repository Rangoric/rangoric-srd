# Staff of the Graverobber

This 5foot staff of black oak, carved with images of falling leaves, skulls, and bat-winged creatures, is shod with iron at both ends. The _staff of the graverobber_ can be used as a quarterstaff +2, even after all its charges are used up. It allows the use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Secret Doors|Detect secret doors]]_ (1 charge)

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|Detect undead]]_ (1 charge)

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disrupt Undead|Disrupt undead]]_ (1 charge)

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|Fear]]_ (+6 vs Will) (2 charges)

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Knock|Knock]]_ (1 charge)

CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Secret Doors|detect secret doors]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disrupt Undead|disrupt undead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Knock|knock]]_; Price 49,550 gp; Weight 5 lb.
