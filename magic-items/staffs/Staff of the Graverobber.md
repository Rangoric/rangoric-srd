# Staff of the Graverobber

This 5foot staff of black oak, carved with images of falling leaves, skulls, and bat-winged creatures, is shod with iron at both ends. The *staff of the graverobber* can be used as a quarterstaff +2, even after all its charges are used up. It allows the use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Secret Doors|Detect secret doors]]* (1 charge)

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|Detect undead]]* (1 charge)

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disrupt Undead|Disrupt undead]]* (1 charge)

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|Fear]]* (DC 16) (2 charges)

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Knock|Knock]]* (1 charge)

CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Secret Doors|detect secret doors]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disrupt Undead|disrupt undead]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Knock|knock]]*; Price 49,550 gp; Weight 5 lb.
