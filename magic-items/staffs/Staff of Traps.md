# Staff of Traps

The tip of this dark wooden shaft holds a piece of hard, chalk-like stone between black, clawlike protrusions. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Mouth|magic mouth]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantom Trap|phantom trap]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Explosive Runes|explosive runes]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Trap|fire trap]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sepia Snake Sigil|sepia snake sigil]]* (2 charges)
