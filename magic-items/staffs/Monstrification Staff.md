# Monstrification Staff

The figures on this scrimshawed ivory staff slowly morph from one monstrous figure into another, and often grow to take up more space on the staff's surface. An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-alchemy-4|alchemist]] or other character capable of creating extracts can pour an extract of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]* over this staff to restore 1 charge to the staff or pour an extract of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Monstrous Physique I|monstrous physique I]]* over it to restore 3 charges.

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|Enlarge person]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Monstrous Physique I|Monstrous physique I]]* (3 charges)

Faint transmutation (polymorph); CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Monstrous Physique I|monstrous physique I]]*, creator must be an alchemist; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,150 gp; Weight 4 lbs.
