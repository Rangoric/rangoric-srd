# Staff of Revelations

The forked top of this walnut shaft resembles a combination of a dowsing rod and the curled horns of a beast, and a delicate gemstone hovers between its enfolding arms. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divination|divination]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]]* (3 charges)

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divination|divination]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]*; Price 51,008 gp; Cost 25,504 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
