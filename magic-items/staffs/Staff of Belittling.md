# Staff of Belittling

Someone who carries this blue-and-white staff need never feel small nor weak, as the staff brings everybody and everything down to size. This staff allows the use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|Reduce person]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Animal|Reduce animal]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shrink Item|Shrink item]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person, Mass|Mass reduce person]]* (4 charges)

Moderate transmutation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person, Mass|mass reduce person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Animal|reduce animal]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shrink Item|shrink item]]*; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
