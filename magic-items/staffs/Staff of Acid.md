# Staff of Acid

This staff is made of a rust-colored metal that has been corroded by acid, with a sickly green gem at its tip. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Corrosive Touch|Corrosive touch]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Arrow|Acid arrow]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Fog|Acid fog]]* (3 charges)

Moderate conjuration; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Arrow|acid arrow]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Fog|acid fog]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Corrosive Touch|corrosive touch]]*; Price 28,600 gp; Cost 14,300 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
