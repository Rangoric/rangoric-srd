# Animate Staff

Created from a number of iron rods, copper gears, and silver screws, this staff constantly shifts and changes. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Make Whole|Make whole]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mending|Mending]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Objects|Animate objects]]_ (2 charges)

In addition, once per day, the staff can become a Medium [[rangoric-srd/monsters/animated-object/Animated Object, Medium|animated object]] made of metal (hardness 10 and +2 natural armor). It can remain in this form for up to 10 minutes. If destroyed while in this form, it reverts to being a staff, but it loses all of its charges and this ability cannot be used again until it is fully recharged.
