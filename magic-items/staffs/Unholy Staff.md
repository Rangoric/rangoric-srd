# Unholy Staff

This twisted iron staff bears ominous stains along its length, and ends in a black skull with red gemstone eyes. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|Align weapon]]* (evil only, 1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|Detect good]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Good|Magic circle against good]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|Unholy blight]]* (2 charges)

Moderate evocation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|align weapon]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Good|magic circle against good]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]*; Price 29,600 gp; Cost 14,800 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
