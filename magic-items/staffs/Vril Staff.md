# Vril Staff

This hollow silver staff is covered in intricate glyphs and flute-like keys. It allows the use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]_ (2 charges)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_ (2 charges)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Repulsion|repulsion]]_ (3 charges)

A spellcaster wielding a vril staff can, as a standard action, spend 1 charge and sacrifice a prepared spell or unused spell slot of 1st level or higher to channel its power through the staff as a ray of raw magical energy called vril. The ray is a ranged touch attack with a range of 30 feet, and the wielder chooses whether it deals damage or heals: 1d6 points per level of the sacrificed spell + 1 point per caster level of the wielder.

In a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]'s hands, the staff works differently. By spending 1 charge as part of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-kinetic-blast|kinetic blast]], she can use a new simple energy blast, the vril blast, which deals untyped damage. A vril blast works only with form and substance [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusions]] that say they work with any blast. If she spends 3 charges instead, her vril blast is stronger and ignores spell resistance. A kineticist can use a vril staff without an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] check.

A spellcaster can recharge a vril staff as normal (see [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Scrolls, Staffs, and Wands#^recharging-staffs|recharging staffs]]). A kineticist can restore 1 charge to it by accepting 3 points of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]].
