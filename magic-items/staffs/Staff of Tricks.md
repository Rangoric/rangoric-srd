# Staff of Tricks

This thin staff is made of stiff wood that has been wrapped in colorful bands of cloth and topped with a number of feathers, bells, and other decorative baubles. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|Ghost sound]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|Mage hand]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|Prestidigitation]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silent Image|Silent image]]_ (2 charges)

Moderate illusion; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silent Image|silent image]]_; Price 8,800 gp; Cost 4,400 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
