# Staff of the Hierophant

The *staff of the hierophant* is a powerful magic item with the ability to both heal and harm, which also gives its wielder a number of protections. The staff is crafted from platinum and topped with a glowing blue diamond. The staff allows the use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|Bless]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|Cure moderate wounds]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|Freedom of movement]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|Hold person]]* (heightened to 5th level, 1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|Prayer]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Searing Light|Searing light]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|Cure critical wounds]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|Flame strike]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|Righteous might]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|True seeing]]* (2 charges)

The wielder of a *staff of the hierophant* gains a +2 luck bonus to AC and on saving throws. The staff is also a *+2/+2 quarterstaff*, and its wielder may use it to smite opponents. If 1 charge is expended (as a free action), the staff deals double damage (×3 on a critical hit) for 1 round.

A *staff of the hierophant* can be used to restore the life of a dead creature by calling upon the wielder's deity to take the staff as a blessing. This causes the staff to vanish, but restores life to any one dead creature as if casting *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]*. In addition, after using this effect, the wielder who triggered it receives a +2 luck bonus on all ability checks, attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks for 1 day.
