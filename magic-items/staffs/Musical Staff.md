# Musical Staff

This staff is made from high-quality wood and festooned with brass rods and keys, looking almost like an instrument. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|Ghost sound]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Piercing Shriek|Piercing shriek]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sculpt Sound|Sculpt sound]]_ (2 charges)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Sound|Wall of sound]]_ (2 charges)

In addition, as a swift action, the wielder of the _musical staff_ can cause it to transform into a string or wind instrument. Any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks made with the _musical staff_ gain a +5 competence bonus. The staff can still be used to cast spells while it is an instrument, and it can revert back to a staff as a free action.
