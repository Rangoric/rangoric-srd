# Lawful Staff

This staff is crafted from a perfectly smooth triangular rod of silver, and its top is crowned with a golden sphere. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|Align weapon]]_ (lawful only, 1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Chaos|Detect chaos]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Chaos|Magic circle against chaos]]_ (2 charges)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Order’s Wrath|Order's wrath]]_ (2 charges)

Moderate evocation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|align weapon]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Chaos|detect chaos]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Chaos|magic circle against chaos]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Order’s Wrath|order's wrath]]_; Price 29,600 gp; Cost 14,800 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
