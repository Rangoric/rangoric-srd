# Staff of Hungry Shadows

This staff is made of scorched and twisted wood and is set with a crystal sphere filled with swirling smoke. It allows the use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|Darkness]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|Ray of enfeeblement]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|Vampiric touch]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|Enervation]]* (2 charges)
- Summon shadow (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]* but summons 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Shadow|shadow]], 2 charges)
- Call devourer (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]* but calls 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Devourer|devourer]], 3 charges)

Strong varies; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|ray of enfeeblement]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]*; Price 69,300 gp; Cost 34,650 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
