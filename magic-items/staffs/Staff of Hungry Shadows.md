# Staff of Hungry Shadows

This staff is made of scorched and twisted wood and is set with a crystal sphere filled with swirling smoke. It allows the use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|Darkness]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|Ray of enfeeblement]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|Vampiric touch]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|Enervation]]_ (2 charges)
- Summon shadow (as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]_ but summons 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Shadow|shadow]], 2 charges)
- Call devourer (as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_ but calls 1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Devourer|devourer]], 3 charges)
