# Holy Staff

This staff is made from polished birch, inlaid with ivory, and topped with a pristine white gemstone. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|Align weapon]]* (good only, 1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|Detect evil]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|Holy smite]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|Magic circle against evil]]* (2 charges)
