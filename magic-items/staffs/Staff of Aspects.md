# Staff of Aspects

This stout staff is made of ash. Four faces of animals—a bear, a falcon, a stag, and a wolf—are carved at its top. The _staff of aspects_ functions as a _+1/+1 quarterstaff_ and allows the use of the following spells:

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aspect of the Bear|Aspect of the bear]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aspect of the Falcon|Aspect of the falcon]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aspect of the Stag|Aspect of the stag]]_ (2 charges)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aspect of the Wolf|Aspect of the wolf]]_ (3 charges)

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aspect of the Bear|aspect of the bear]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aspect of the Falcon|aspect of the falcon]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aspect of the Stag|aspect of the stag]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aspect of the Wolf|aspect of the wolf]]_; Price 37,600 gp; Cost 19,100 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
