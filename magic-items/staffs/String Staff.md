# String Staff

Unlike most staffs that are made of wood, bone, or metal, the *string staff* takes the form of a five foot length of stiffened, braided rope. It is somewhat flexible, of +2 enchantment, and less substantial than other magical staffs when used in combat.

But the *string staff* is quite versatile in other ways, allowing the use of the following spells:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope|animate rope]]* (1 charge)

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rope Trick|rope trick]]* (1 charge)

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]* (1 charge, DC 13)

When used to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope|animate rope]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rope Trick|rope trick]]* the staff extends itself into the form of a long, thin cord, and performs the magical activities. Even while extended, the staff retains its full toughness (hardness 3, 10 hit points, +5 save). The sticky strands of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]* spell seem to fly out of the top if the *string staff*.

It can also, on command, change form into a +1 whip. When the staff is in whip form or extended to perform a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rope Trick|rope trick]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope|animate rope]]* spell, it cannot be used for other magical effects until it’s restored to its staff form.

CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope|animate rope]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Baleful Polymorph|baleful polymorph]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rope Trick|rope trick]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]*; Price 8,500 gp; Weight 4 lb.
