# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Daemon

This ring is carved from bone. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Cacodaemon|cacodaemon]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters she can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Ceustodaemon|ceustodaemon]] to the 5th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Hydrodaemon|hydrodaemon]] to the 6th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Leukodaemon|leukodaemon]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Piscodaemon|piscodaemon]] to the 7th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Derghodaemon|derghodaemon]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Meladaemon|meladaemon]] to the 8th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Thanadaemon|thanadaemon]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a cacodaemon, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [evil]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be neutral evil; Price 10,600 gp; Cost 5,300 gp.
