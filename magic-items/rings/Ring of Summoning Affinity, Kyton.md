# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Kyton

This iron ring has several sharp barbs. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Augur Kyton|augur]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Sacristan Kyton|sacristan]] to the 7th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Interlocutor Kyton|interlocutor]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon an augur, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [evil, law]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be lawful evil; Price 7,300 gp; Cost 3,650 gp.
