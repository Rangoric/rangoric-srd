# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Protean

This ring is made from an opalescent material that is never the same color twice. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/proteans/Voidworm|voidworm]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters she can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/proteans/Naunet|naunet]] to the 6th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/proteans/Imentesh|imentesh]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a voidworm, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [chaos]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be chaotic neutral; Price 7,200 gp; Cost 3,600 gp.
