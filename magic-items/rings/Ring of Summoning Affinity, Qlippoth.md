# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Qlippoth

This ring has an odd, disquieting texture that always reveals greater layers of complexity the more closely it is examined. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Cythnigot|cythnigot]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Shoggti|shoggti]] to the 6th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Nyogoth|nyogoth]] to the 7th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Chernobue|chernobue]] to the 8th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Augnagar|augnagar]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a cythnigot, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [chaos, evil]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be chaotic evil; Price 8,600 gp; Cost 4,300 gp.
