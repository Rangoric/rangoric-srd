# Ring of Swimming

This silver ring has a wave pattern etched into the band. It continually grants the wearer a +5 competence bonus on Athletics checks.

Faint transmutation; CL 2nd; Forge Ring, creator must have 2 ranks in the Athletics skill; Price 2,500 gp.
