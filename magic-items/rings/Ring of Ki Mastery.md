# Ring of Ki Mastery

This ring is half onyx and half clear-white crystal, magically fused together and hardened to the strength of steel. It allows a wearer with a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki* pool]] to store up to 2 _ki_ points in the ring as a swift action. These points remain in the ring until used. The wearer can use the _ki_ points normally, or can gain the following benefits when he has _ki_ points stored in the ring.

As long as there is at least one _ki_ point stored in the ring, the wearer gains a +2 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|CMD]] against grapple, reposition, and trip attempts.

As long as there are at least 2 _ki_ points stored in the ring, the wearer reduces the number of _ki_ points needed to use a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-tricks|ninja trick]] or _ki_ ability by 1 (minimum 1 _ki_ point).

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ki Leech|ki leech]]_, the creator must be able to use ki; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 3,420 gp.
