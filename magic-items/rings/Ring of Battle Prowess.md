# Ring of Battle Prowess

When this ring is worn, it increases the chance that attacks are successful. Whenever the character makes an attack while wearing this ring, roll two dice for each attack, and take the more favorable result. This power does not enact until it has been worn continuously for at least 24 hours.

A *ring of battle prowess* can only function twice per day for up to three rounds per activation.

CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]*, *random action*; Price 42,000 gp.
