# Ring of Arcane Mastery

This ring is a thin but sturdy piece of forged steel crafted to resemble a serpent swallowing its tail, and is magically imbued to reveal the secrets of arcane mastery to its wearer. The ring allows a wearer with an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-arcane-pool|arcane pool]] to store up to 4 points from his arcane pool in the ring as a swift action. These points remain in the ring until used. A wearer can spend arcane pool points stored in the ring as if they were his own, or can spend them in each of the following ways.

- As a free action, the wearer can expend 1 arcane pool point stored in the ring to gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|Concentration checks]] for 1 round.
- As a standard action, the wearer can expend 1 arcane pool point stored in the ring to use the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/magus-arcana/Pool Strike (Magus Arcana)|pool strike]] magus arcana using his own magus level to determine the effect.
- As an immediate action, the wearer can expend up to 4 arcane pool points stored in the ring to use the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/magus-arcana/Reflection (Magus Arcana)|reflection]] magus arcana.

Strong abjuration and evocation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Imbue with Spell Ability|imbue with spell ability]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]]*; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp.
