# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Div

This ring is shaped to resemble a snake consuming its own tail. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Doru|doru]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Aghash|aghash]] to the 4th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Pairaka|pairaka]] to the 6th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Ghawwas|ghawwas]] to the 7th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Shira|shira]] to the 8th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/divs/Sepid|sepid]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a doru, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [chaos, evil]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be chaotic evil; Price 9,100 gp; Cost 4,550 gp.
