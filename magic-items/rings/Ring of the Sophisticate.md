# Ring of the Sophisticate

A favorite ring of urban half-elves, humans, and halflings, this small and plain silver band is set with a single radiant-cut sapphire and decorated with a mazelike pattern engraved into the ring's metal. Often worn on the little finger, the ring grants its wearer a +4 competence bonus on Sense Motive and Society checks. Also, once per day on command, the wearer can use the ring to cast either *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Object|locate object]]* (the wearer's choice). Furthermore, on command and at will, the wearer can instantly know the direction from her to the nearest tavern, pub, or similar watering hole as long as that establishment is in a settlement whose population is equivalent to that of a village or greater and which is within 20 miles of the wearer.

Moderate transmutation and divination; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Know Direction|know direction]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Object|locate object]]*, creator must have at least 4 ranks in both Sense Motive and Society; Price 11,000 gp; Cost 5,500 gp.
