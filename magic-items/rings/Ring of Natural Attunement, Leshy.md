# Ring of Natural Attunement, Leshy

This ring is made of thin but strong vines braided together in an elegant weave. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/leshies/Leaf Leshy|leaf leshy]] to the 1st-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/leshies/Gourd Leshy|gourd leshy]] to the 2nd-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/leshies/Fungus Leshy|fungus leshy]] to the 3rd-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/leshies/Seaweed Leshy|seaweed leshy]] to the 4th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a leaf leshy, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally I]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plant Growth|plant growth]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally I]]_; Price 2,800 gp; Cost 1,400 gp.
