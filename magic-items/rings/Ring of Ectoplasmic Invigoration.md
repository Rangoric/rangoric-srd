# Ring of Ectoplasmic Invigoration

A summoner wearing a *ring of ectoplasmic invigoration* can use his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-makers-call|maker’s call]] class feature to absorb his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]] (dismissing it in the process) instead of bringing it to his side. He gains 2 temporary hit points per Hit Die of the eidolon and the benefits of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]* spell. The eidolon must still be within range of his maker's call ability. Any temporary hit points gained fade after 1 hour or when the eidolon is summoned again. A wearer with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-transposition|transposition]] ability can transport himself to the eidolon's square (still absorbing and dismissing it) rather than calling the eidolon to him.

Moderate conjuration; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], the creator must be able to summon an eidolon and have the maker's call class feature; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp.
