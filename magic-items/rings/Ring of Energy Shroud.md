# Ring of Energy Shroud

A *ring of energy shroud* always appears to be glass or crystal in construction, tinted in a red, green, blue, or yellow hue. Closer inspection reveals a small band of metal within the glass or crystal. Treasured by frontline fighters and anyone with a trusted evoker ally, *rings of energy shroud* normally function as a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Energy Resistance|minor ring of energy resistance]]*. However, once per day as an immediate action, the ring's wearer can negate the damage of an incoming acid, cold, electricity, or fire energy effect for himself (but not for other creatures affected by the spell or effect), transforming that damage into a shroud of energy that wreathes the wearer's body acting as if under the effects of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Aura|elemental aura]]* spell. The elemental aura effect has the same energy type of the damage negated.

Strong abjuration; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Aura|elemental aura]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*; Price 19,500 gp; Cost 9,750 gp.
