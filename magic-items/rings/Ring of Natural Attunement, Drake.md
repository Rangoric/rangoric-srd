# Ring of Natural Attunement, Drake

This coiling band is made of jade carved into the shape of a sea drake. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Forest Drake|forest drake]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Shadow Drake|shadow drake]] to the 4th-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Flame Drake|flame drake]] to the 5th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Frost Drake|frost drake]] to the 6th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/drakes/Desert Drake|desert drake]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a shadow drake, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IV|summon nature’s ally IV]]_ spell.

Moderate conjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Fang, Greater|greater magic fang]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IV|summon nature’s ally IV]]_; Price 12,880 gp; Cost 6,440 gp.
