# Ring of Resilience

This tarnished copper band sheds its patina when worn by anyone with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]]. Whenever the wearer regains a panache or grit point through her actions (not just a daily refresh), she can try to end a single ongoing condition affecting her, making it attack her again with its original attack bonus. The effect must be one that attacked a defense, can be removed by either _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_, and be an effect whose first attack hit the wearer. If the new attack misses, the effect is removed even if a miss would normally have other consequences, and even if the effect normally needs multiple misses to remove. If the effect attacked more than one defense, use the defense and attack bonus of the first attack that hit the wearer. As the wearer must regain a panache or grit point to make the effect attack again, the ring provides no benefit if she hasn't used any grit or panache.

Moderate conjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]_, creator must be a grit or panache user; Price 15,000 gp; Cost 7,500 gp.
