# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Aeon

This ring is forged from black iron and shining silver. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, he adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/aeons/Paracletus|paracletus]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/aeons/Theletos|theletos]] to the 6th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/aeons/Akhana|akhana]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day the wearer can summon a paracletus, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be neutral with no other alignment components; Price 7,800 gp; Cost 3,900 gp.
