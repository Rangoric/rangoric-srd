# Dungeon Ring

A *jailer's dungeon ring* is worked gold, set with a carnelian. The ring is magically attuned to one or more iron *prisoner's dungeon rings*. When the wearer of a *jailer's ring* places a *prisoner's ring* on a subject, the prisoner cannot remove the ring without the use of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]* spell. The wearer of the *jailer's ring* can also remove a linked *prisoner's ring* at any time.

The jailer is aware of any wearer of a linked *prisoner's ring* like a constant *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Status|status]]* spell. Additionally, all wearers of linked *prisoner's rings* count as familiar to the jailer for purposes of spells such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]*.

Moderate divination; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Status|status]]*; Price 16,000 gp (jailer's ring), 250 gp (prisoner's ring); Cost 8,000 gp (jailer's ring), 125 gp (prisoner's ring).
