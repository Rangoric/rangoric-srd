# Ring of Psychic Mastery

The large sapphire on this platinum ring darkens as its wearer uses up her psychic spellcasting, turning black when her spells for the day are spent or if she can't cast psychic spells. The ring grants its wearer 1 additional [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^psychic-magic|psychic spell]] per day of each spell level from 1st to 4th that she can cast.

Strong (no school); CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_; Price 50,000 gp; Cost 25,000 gp.
