# Ring of Retribution

Once per day, the wearer of this charred bone ring may, as an immediate action, cause it to detonate in a 30-foot-radius burst that deals 10d6 points of fire damage (+4 vs Reflex; a miss halves). If the wearer is killed by the detonation, it also attacks all magical equipment he carries, dealing fire damage on a hit, and all nonmagical equipment takes this damage automatically. This explosion never destroys the ring.

Strong evocation; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|Quicken Spell]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]_; Price 15,000 gp; Cost 7,500 gp.
