# Ring of Jumping

This ring continually allows the wearer to leap about, providing a +5 competence bonus on all his Athletics checks.

Faint transmutation; CL 2nd; Forge Ring, creator must have 2 ranks in the Athletics skill; Price 2,500 gp.
