# Ring of Natural Attunement, Kami

This ring carved from a single piece of cherry wood bears an inscription. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kamis/Shikigami|shikigami]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kamis/Kodama|kodama]] to the 5th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kamis/Zuishin|zuishin]] to the 7th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/kamis/Toshigami|toshigami]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a shikigami, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally III|summon nature’s ally III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune with Nature|commune with nature]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally III|summon nature’s ally III]]_; Price 7,800 gp; Cost 3,900 gp.
