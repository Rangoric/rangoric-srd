# Dungeon Ring, Prisoner's

A _jailer's dungeon ring_ is worked gold, set with a carnelian. The ring is magically attuned to one or more iron _prisoner's dungeon rings_. When the wearer of a _jailer's ring_ places a _prisoner's ring_ on a subject, the prisoner cannot remove the ring without the use of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ spell. The wearer of the _jailer's ring_ can also remove a linked _prisoner's ring_ at any time.

The jailer is aware of any wearer of a linked _prisoner's ring_ like a constant _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Status|status]]_ spell. Additionally, all wearers of linked _prisoner's rings_ count as familiar to the jailer for purposes of spells such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]_.

Moderate divination; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Status|status]]_; Price 250 gp; Cost 125 gp.
