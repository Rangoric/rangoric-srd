# Ring of Heroes

Sculpted in the shape of two noble looking bronze lions charging toward a centrally placed garnet, this ring grants the wearer the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Luck of Heroes|Luck of Heroes]] feat. The ring must be worn for 24 hours before it takes effect. The wearer can also call upon the ring’s power to gain a [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points|hero point]] that must be used immediately, although this causes the ring to lose its powers permanently.

Moderate (no school); CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Luck of Heroes|Luck of Heroes]]; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp.
