# Ring of the Hidden Psyche

This ring looks like a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Psychic Mastery|ring of psychic mastery]], but its flawless sapphire is a vessel for the wearer's soul. It has all the abilities of a ring of psychic mastery, and also the following.

The wearer's soul enters the ring the instant she dies, even if her body is utterly destroyed. This counts as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Object Possession|object possession]]*, except that the soul can't animate the ring and must wait for someone to wear it. From inside the ring, she can attempt *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession, Greater|greater possession]]* on anyone who willingly puts the ring on (Will DC 23 negates); a creature that saves is immune to further attempts for 24 hours. Dispelling the object possession effect on the ring banishes the soul to its proper afterlife; otherwise a soul can stay in the ring indefinitely. While in the ring, the soul knows when a creature puts it on, but can't otherwise sense its surroundings or do anything but attempt the greater possession.

The ring resists attempts to learn its true nature. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]* reveals only the properties of a ring of psychic mastery; *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Identify|identify]]* or stronger magic can reveal its full powers if the caster succeeds at a DC 23 Will save.

Strong (no school); CL 18th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession, Greater|greater possession]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Object Possession|object possession]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]*; Price 160,000 gp; Cost 80,000 gp.
