# Spiritualist Rings

These four garish and gaudy rings must be worn on one hand, though they take up only a single ring slot. They grant the wearer the ability to commune with and affect the dead in the following ways.

- The wearer can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]_, as the spell, three times per day. It takes 10 minutes to use this ability.
- The wearer can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spectral Hand|spectral hand]]_, as the spell, three times per day.
- The wearer's weapons gain the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]]_ weapon special ability while the wearer is wielding the weapons.
- The wearer gains a +4 sacred or profane bonus on defenses against positive and negative energy effects. The bonus depends on the wearer's alignment—good-aligned wearers gain a sacred bonus, whereas evil-aligned wearers gain a profane bonus. Neutral-aligned wearers pick either a sacred or profane bonus when the ring is first put on, which cannot be changed later.

Strong necromancy; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spectral Hand|spectral hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_; Price 70,000 gp; Cost 35,000 gp.
