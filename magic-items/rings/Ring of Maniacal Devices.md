# Ring of Maniacal Devices

This deceptively precious looking ring grants the wearer a +5 competence bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (traps) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks. Even if he has no ranks in these skills, the wearer may make [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (traps) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks as if he were trained in them.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fox’s Cunning|fox's cunning]]*, creator must have 5 ranks in the relevant skill; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp.
