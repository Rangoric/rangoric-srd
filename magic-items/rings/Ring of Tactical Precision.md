# Ring of Tactical Precision

This steel ring is inlaid with adamantine swords and shields. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (soldier) checks, and anytime the wearer gives or receives a numeric bonus from a teamwork feat or the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] action, that bonus increase by +1. By wearing the ring for 1 hour, a creature may imbue it with a teamwork feat he knows for 24 hours. During this time, any creature wearing the ring may use that feat when cooperating with the creature that imbued it, but not with other creatures even if they possess the same teamwork feat. If the ring is imbued with a new teamwork feat, it replaces the previous feat.

Moderate transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], creator must have 5 ranks of Profession (soldier) and at least one teamwork feat; Price 11,000 gp; Cost 5,500 gp.
