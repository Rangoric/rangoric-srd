# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Asura

This ring is made of intricately marked brass. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/asuras/Tripurasura|tripurasura]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/asuras/Adhukait|adhukait]] to the 6th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/asuras/Upasunda|upasunda]] to the 7th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/asuras/Aghasura|aghasura]] to the 8th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a tripurasura, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [evil, law]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be lawful evil; Price 7,600 gp; Cost 3,800 gp.
