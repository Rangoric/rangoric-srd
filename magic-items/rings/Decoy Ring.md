# Decoy Ring

This ring is a thick loop of mirrorlike metal. Whenever the wearer of this ring takes the withdraw action or becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] (including falling [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]), it instantly makes her [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] for 3 rounds and creates four illusory duplicates that either run off in opposite directions or perform other plausible actions that could draw enemy attention away from her. The duplicates last for 3 rounds before disappearing, but they instantly pop out of existence if struck by an attack (AC 10) and can be disbelieved (+9 vs Will disbelief). Allies of the wearer always know her true location and can freely provide aid or assistance to her.

Moderate illusion; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mislead|mislead]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp.
