# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Psychopomp

This silver ring is marked with a skull emblem. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/psychopomps/Nosoi|nosoi]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/psychopomps/Catrina|catrina]] to the 4th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/psychopomps/Vanth|vanth]] to the 6th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/psychopomps/Morrigna|morrigna]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a nosoi, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be neutral with no other alignment components; Price 7,600 gp; Cost 3,800 gp.
