# Ring of Powerful Blood Magic

When the wearer of this ring is [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodraging]] and casts a spell, as a swift action he can increase the save DC of the spell by 1. The wearer can do this a maximum of three times per day. This has no effect on spells that do not require saving throws.

Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]], creator must have the bloodrage class feature; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp.
