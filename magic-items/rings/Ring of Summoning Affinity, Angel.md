# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Angel

This ring is cut from transparent crystal. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/angels/Angel, Cassisian|cassisian]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters she can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/angels/Angel, Movanic Deva|movanic deva]] to the 7th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/angels/Angel, Monadic Deva|monadic deva]] to the 8th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a cassisian, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [good]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be good; Price 7,200 gp; Cost 3,600 gp.
