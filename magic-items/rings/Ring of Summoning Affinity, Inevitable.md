# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Inevitable

This ring resembles a gear or cog. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/inevitable/Arbiter|arbiter]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters she can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/inevitable/Zelekhut|zelekhut]] to the 7th-level list, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/inevitable/Kolyarut|kolyarut]] to the 8th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/inevitable/Marut|marut]] to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon an arbiter, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [law]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be lawful neutral; Price 8,100 gp; Cost 4,050 gp.
