# Ring of Lingering Blood Magic

When the wearer of this ring is [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodraging]], he can expend 1 round of bloodrage to extend the duration of a beneficial spell affecting him by 2 rounds. This is a free action the wearer can take once per round.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Extend Spell|Extend Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], creator must have the bloodrage class feature; Price 2,000 gp; Cost 1,000 gp.
