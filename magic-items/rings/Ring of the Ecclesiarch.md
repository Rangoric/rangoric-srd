# Ring of the Ecclesiarch

This ornate ring of sanctified silver is engraved with a golden signet that incorporates the holy symbol of the wearer's deity after being worn for 24 hours. The ring can be used to seal parchments or to mark any surface as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Mark|arcane mark]]* spell at will. The wearer is marked as a scholarly leader of the faith and gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks. If the wearer has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Leadership|Leadership]] feat, she may attract double the normal number of followers, and her followers' zealous devotion grants them a +4 morale bonus on Will saves against enchantment spells and effects.

Once per day, the wearer of the ring can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|prayer]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enter Image|enter image]]*, often using the latter power to speak and observe through portraits, statues, and even minted coinage bearing their likeness.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Mark|arcane mark]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enter Image|enter image]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enthrall|enthrall]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|prayer]]*, creator must have 5 ranks of Persuasion and Theology and the ability to cast divine spells; Price 28,500 gp; Cost 14,250 gp.
