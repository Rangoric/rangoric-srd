# Ring of Ferocious Action

This ring allows its wearer to persevere through physical or mental trauma that hampers his actions. The ring has 5 charges, renewed each day. At the start of his turn, if the wearer is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]], as a free action he may spend a charge to activate the ring and ignore the staggered condition until his next turn.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]*; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp.
