# Ring of Foe Focus

This ring of jagged steel gives the impression that it was salvaged from a suit of armor and then crudely bent into a circle. The wearer of the ring can, at will, designate any one opponent she can see as a particularly dangerous foe. She gains a +2 bonus to her AC and defenses against attacks made by that opponent until she designates a new enemy.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp.
