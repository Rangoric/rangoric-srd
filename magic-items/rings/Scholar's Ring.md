# Scholar's Ring

A *scholar's ring* grants complete understanding of any written text or spoken language, even one that the wearer has never encountered before (as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Comprehend Languages|comprehend languages]]* spell), as well as a +5 competence bonus on Society checks.

In addition, once every 5 days the wearer can invoke a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]* spell related to a subject that she has been studying in the previous week, if she has attempted a Society check aided by the ring.

Moderate conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Comprehend Languages|comprehend languages]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]*; Price 8,700 gp; Cost 4,350 gp.
