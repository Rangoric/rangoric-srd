# Ring of Ancestral Blood Magic

While the wearer is [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodraging]], he can cast a bonus spell gained from his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodline|bloodrager bloodline]] without expending a spell slot. He can do this three times per day, and must expend a number of rounds of bloodrage equal to the level of the spell to be cast. The wearer must meet all the other requirements for spellcasting.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Extend Spell|Extend Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], creator must have the bloodrage class feature; Price 4,000 gp; Cost 2,000 gp.
