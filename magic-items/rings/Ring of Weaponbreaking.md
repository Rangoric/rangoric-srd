# Ring of Weaponbreaking

A ring of weaponbreaking is identical to a ring of ironskin, and has one additional power. The ring also attacks the Fortitude of any weapon that successfully strikes the wearer at +10, and on a hit the weapon is shattered into pieces.  
Caster Level: 20th; Prerequisites: Forge Ring, Forge Epic Ring, iron body, shatter; Market Price: 600,000 gp; Cost to Create: 300,000 gp + 16,000 XP.
