# Ring of Climbing

This ring is actually a magic leather cord that ties around a finger. It continually grants the wearer a +5 competence bonus on Athletics checks.

Faint transmutatation; CL 5th; Forge Ring, creator must have 2 ranks in the Athletics skill; Price 2,500 gp.
