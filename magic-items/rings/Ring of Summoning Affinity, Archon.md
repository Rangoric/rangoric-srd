# Ring of Summoning Affinity, Archon

This ring is made of hammered steel. If the wearer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ spells, the wearer adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archons/Harbinger Archon|harbinger archon]] to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archons/Legion Archon|legion archon]] to the 6th-level list, and adds [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archons/Shield Archon|shield archon]] to the 7th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a harbinger archon, as if by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_ spell.

Faint conjuration [good, law]; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_, creator must be lawful good; Price 7,000 gp; Cost 3,500 gp.
