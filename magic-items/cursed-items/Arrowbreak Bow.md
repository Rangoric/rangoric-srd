# Arrowbreak Bow

This shortbow is beautifully made and inlaid with iridescent designs, appearing to be a _+2 shortbow_. When it fires its arrows, though, it does it with so much force that the arrows are always broken on either a hit or a miss. This bow must be used as its owner's primary ranged weapon and can only be discarded after the owner is subject to a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ spell or similar effect.

Moderate evocation; CL 6th; Intended magic item: +2 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Shortbow|shortbow]].
