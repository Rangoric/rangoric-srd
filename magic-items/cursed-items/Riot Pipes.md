# Riot Pipes

This simple musical instrument seems and behaves in all ways like any other kind of magical pipes until its owner plays it in an urban setting. Once he does, it attacks the Will of all people within hearing range at +5, and those it hits fall into a murderous fury. They gain the effects of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]_ spell for the next 1d6 hours and experience a strong compulsion (+5 vs Will) to attack any stranger or disliked person whom they encounter during this time. If the owner spends more than 3 consecutive days in an urban setting, the pipes attack his Will at +5 each day, and on a hit he plays the _riot pipes_ at some point during each subsequent day. The pipes remain with the player until the curse is broken.

Moderate enchantment; CL 10th; Intended magic item: _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Pipes of Haunting|pipes of haunting]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Pipes of the Sewers|pipes of the sewers]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Pipes of Sounding|pipes of sounding]]_.
