# Flask of Curses

This item looks like an ordinary beaker, bottle, container, decanter, flask, or jug. It may contain a liquid, or it may emit smoke. When the flask is first unstoppered, it attacks the Will of all within 30 feet at +7, and those it hits are cursed, taking a –2 penalty on attack rolls, defenses, and skill checks until a remove curse spell is cast upon them.

Moderate conjuration; CL 7th; Create Wondrous Item, bestow curse; Price 2,100 gp.
