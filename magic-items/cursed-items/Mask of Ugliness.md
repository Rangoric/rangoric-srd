# Mask of Ugliness

This strange, featureless mask seems to have the powers of a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Headband of Alluring Charisma +4|headband of alluring charisma +4]]*, but instead of granting a bonus to Charisma, the mask melds to the wearer's face, making him seem uglier and his words come off as crude and insulting. The wearer takes a –4 penalty to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks but gains a +2 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks.

Moderate transmutation; CL 8th; Intended magic item: any magical mask or a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Headband of Alluring Charisma +2|headband of alluring charisma]]*.
