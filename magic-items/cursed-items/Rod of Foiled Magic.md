# Rod of Foiled Magic

This rod is similar in appearance to a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Potions, Rings, and Rods#Rods|metamagic rod]]_. Once a creature picks up such a rod, it cannot be discarded without use of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ spell or similar magic. Each time the wielder casts a spell, he takes the _rod of foiled magic_ in hand and treats the caster level of the spell as 2 levels lower. If this would reduce the caster level to 0 or lower, then the caster has only a 50% chance of casting the spell (any chance of arcane spell failure is added to this percentile chance), but if he is able to cast the spell, the spell has a caster level of 1st.

Strong (no school); CL 17th; Intended magic item: any [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Potions, Rings, and Rods#Rods|metamagic rod]].
