# Maniac Hand

This dried, withered hand ends in a jagged stump. Touched to the arm stump of a humanoid who has lost a hand, it bonds instantly and takes on a lifelike look. It shrinks or grows to fit its new owner and becomes a right or left hand to match the one lost. After it merges, an uneven scar and an unhealthy hue mark it as foreign to its owner. It takes no magic item slot.

Attacks made with a maniac hand gain a +2 competence bonus on attack and damage rolls. When making a full attack, the wielder can accept a –2 penalty to AC to make one extra attack with the hand at his full base attack bonus; this doesn't stack with similar effects, such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*. Any weapon held in the hand (or in both hands) qualifies, and the hand itself can deal damage as a slam attack for the wielder's size (1d4 for a Medium creature).

The hand has an insidious will that slowly takes control from its owner. It has Intelligence 8, Wisdom 12 and Charisma 10. Unable to communicate and without rational thought, it knows only an endless urge to destroy. When first attached, it has an [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Intelligent and Cursed Items and Artifact#Item Ego|Ego]] score of 5. At least once each day, when given a chance to murder a friend or innocent unwitnessed, the hand tries to assert control by the rules for [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Intelligent and Cursed Items and Artifact#Items Against Characters|items against characters]]. If it succeeds, it controls its owner until it commits the murder or 5 minutes pass, whichever comes first; the owner remembers nothing of the crime but a blinding rage, and the hand's Ego returns to 5. If it fails, the owner feels a moment of anger he can't place. Each day that passes without the hand killing a creature of Intelligence 3 or higher, its Ego rises by 1. Once its Ego is above 20, it no longer cares about caution and tries to murder whether or not it might be caught.

A maniac hand denied the death of friends or innocents for more than 30 days turns on its owner. When it's in a position to sabotage him (such as by letting go of a rope), it tries to assert control; it can also do so while he sleeps, strangling him or seizing a weapon to deliver a coup de grace. It tries such an act at most once per day. An owner willing to slaughter innocents can keep it satisfied by finding a victim and willingly giving up control to the hand.

If the owner tries to rid himself of the hand by destroying or amputating it, it resists as best it can, making attacks and one-handed [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] attempts with its owner's Strength and Dexterity scores and its own bonuses. Its AC equals its owner's touch AC with a +4 size bonus that stacks with the owner's size modifier. The owner loses his Dexterity bonus against the hand's attacks, but the hand keeps its Dexterity bonus against him. The hand has hit points equal to 1/4 of its owner's. Casting *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]* on the hand stops it from resisting amputation for 1 hour.

Strong necromancy; CL 13th; Intended magic item: *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Hand of Glory|hand of glory]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Hand of the Mage|hand of the mage]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Hand of Stone|hand of stone]]*.
