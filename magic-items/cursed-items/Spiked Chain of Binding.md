# Spiked Chain of Binding

This is a *+1* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Spiked Chain|spiked chain]], but each time it is used in melee against a foe and the attack roll is a natural 1, the spiked chain wraps itself about the wielder and binds them. The spiked chain makes a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver check against the wielder (CMB +10). If it succeeds, it grapples the wielder and deals its normal damage (2d4+1). In later rounds, the spiked chain attempts to pin its victim and keeps dealing damage, so long as the victim is pinned.

Moderate evocation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]*; Price 2,500 gp.
