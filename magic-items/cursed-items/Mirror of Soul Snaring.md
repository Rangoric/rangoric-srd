# Mirror of Soul Snaring

This ordinary-looking mirror, about 4 feet long and 3 feet wide, is like a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Mirror of Opposition|mirror of opposition]] or [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Mirror of Life Trapping|mirror of life trapping]], but has no command word. It has 15 extradimensional compartments. Any creature within 30 feet that sees its own reflection is attacked against its Will at +13, and on a hit has its soul drawn into one of them. A creature unaware that the mirror is cursed always sees its reflection; one that knows and tries to avoid looking has a 50% chance of seeing it (treat it as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Gaze|gaze attack]]).

A creature whose soul is trapped leaves its body outside the mirror, where a malignant spirit possesses it and behaves in a way wholly opposed to the trapped creature. Treat the spirit as the creature with its alignment reversed (and possibly other things reversed, such as being an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladin]] instead of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]]); for a neutral creature, the GM decides whether it's chaotic evil or lawful good. The original body's death doesn't free the soul, but a soul freed with no body to return to passes on to the afterlife at once.

If the mirror's capacity is exceeded, one trapped soul (chosen at random) is set free and rejoins its body, if available. If the mirror is destroyed (hardness 1, 5 hit points), all its victims are freed. Unless it's destroyed, not even _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ can restore a trapped spirit to its body.

Strong necromancy; CL 15th; Intended magic item: _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Mirror of Life Trapping|mirror of life trapping]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Mirror of Opposition|mirror of opposition]]_.
