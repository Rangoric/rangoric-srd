# Petrifying Cloak

This gray magical cloak seems to be a protective cloak, but when it is donned, the cloak attacks the wearer’s Fortitude at +10, and on a hit the wearer is instantly [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]]. If the first attack misses, the cloak attacks again at the start of each of the wearer’s turns. On any hit the creature becomes petrified. A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone to Flesh|stone to flesh]]_ transforms the wearer back, at least long enough for someone to take the cloak off the wearer.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; Intended magic item: any cloak.
