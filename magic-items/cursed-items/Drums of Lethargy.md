# Drums of Lethargy

This set of kettle drums appears to be _drums of haste_. When a creature plays the drums, it affects all creatures (including the creature playing the drums) as though subject to a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ spell. It attacks their Will at +4, and a miss negates the effect. No [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] check is needed to create the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ effect.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; Intended magic item: _drums of haste_.
