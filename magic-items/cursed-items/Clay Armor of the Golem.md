# Clay Armor of the Golem

This _+1_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Clay Armor|clay armor]] radiates magic (enchantment [compulsion]) and consists of a helm, breastplate, backplate, bracers and leggings. The armor is cumbersome and requires assistance when donning. When the wearer dons the entire set of armor it attacks their Will at +5, and on a hit they are unable to move, though they can still speak and are aware of their surroundings. They then act as if under a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]_ spell cast by a 5th-level caster and obey the commands of the person assisting them. The armor can be removed with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ spell, leaving _+1 clay armor_, or a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_ spell, which leaves ordinary clay armor.

Moderate enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command, Greater|greater command]]_; Price 2,000 gp.
