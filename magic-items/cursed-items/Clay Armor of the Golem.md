# Clay Armor of the Golem

This *+1* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Clay Armor|clay armor]] radiates magic (enchantment [compulsion]) and consists of a helm, breastplate, backplate, bracers and leggings. The armor is cumbersome and requires assistance when donning. When the wearer dons the entire set of armor they must pass a Will save (DC 15) or be unable to move, though they can still speak and are aware of their surroundings. They then act as if under a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]* spell cast by a 5th-level caster and obey the commands of the person assisting them. The armor can be removed with a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]* spell, leaving *+1 clay armor*, or a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]* spell, which leaves ordinary clay armor.

Moderate enchantment; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command, Greater|greater command]]*; Price 2,000 gp.
