# Monkey's Paw (Cursed Item)

This mummified monkey's hand can grant three wishes, at a terrible cost. It grants any request within the power of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ spell, but its curse twists each result to bring about despair: a wish to keep an erupting volcano from burying a town might drain the magma beneath the volcano, so the town is swallowed when the emptied chamber collapses. Careful wording meant to avoid a bad result only makes the attempt fail, without using up a wish. The paw writhes in the holder's grasp when it grants a wish.

Once its third wish is used, the paw loses its power for those who used the wishes and their acquaintances, but regains it once passed on to a stranger. It isn't the minor artifact of the same name, _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/artifacts/Monkey's Paw|monkey's paw]]_.

Strong universal; CL 20th; Intended magic item: none (a unique cursed item).
