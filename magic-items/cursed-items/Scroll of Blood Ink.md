# Scroll of Blood Ink

This scroll can seem to contain any spell. When the wielder attempts to cast the spell, the ink turns to blood and runs off the page, ruining the spell automatically. The scroll's edges turn sharp and cut the wielder's hands and wrists, dealing 4 points of Constitution [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]]. The edges attack the wielder’s Reflex at +5, and a miss halves the bleed. The bleed can be stopped with any magical healing or a successful DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check.

Aura varies; CL varies; Intended magic item: any [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Scrolls, Staffs, and Wands#Scrolls|scroll]].
