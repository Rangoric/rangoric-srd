# Cape of Anchoring

This bright red and gold cape appears to be a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Cape of the Mountebank|cape of the mountebank]]*. Once worn, however, it affects its wearer as if subject to a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]* spell. The cape can only be removed after its wearer is subject to a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]* spell or similar effect.
