# Cape of Anchoring

This bright red and gold cape appears to be a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Cape of the Mountebank|cape of the mountebank]]_. Once worn, however, it affects its wearer as if subject to a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]_ spell. The cape can only be removed after its wearer is subject to a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ spell or similar effect.

Moderate abjuration; CL 9th; Intended magic item: _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Cape of the Mountebank|cape of the mountebank]]_.
