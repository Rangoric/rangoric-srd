# Ring of Spell Devouring

This delicate ring appears to be a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Spell Storing|ring of spell storing]]_ or a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Spell Turning|ring of spell turning]]_, but instead of aiding casting or counterspelling, the ring hinders such efforts. Each time the wearer casts an arcane spell, he suffers a 25% spell failure chance. Furthermore, the wearer of the ring takes a –4 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] checks to identify a spell when attempting to counterspell. The ring can only be removed by a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ spell or a similar effect.

Strong abjuration; CL 13th; Intended magic item: _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Spell Storing|ring of spell storing]]_ (any) or a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Spell Turning|ring of spell turning]]_.
