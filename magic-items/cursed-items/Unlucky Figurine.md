# Unlucky Figurine

This figurine appears to be a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Figurine of Wondrous Power, Bronze Griffon|figurine of wondrous power]]_, but instead of having the ability to animate and serve its owner, it curses that owner with bad luck. While in possession of this figurine, the owner takes a –2 penalty to AC and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|CMD]], and a –2 penalty on attack rolls, skill checks, ability checks, and defenses.

Moderate enchantment; CL 11th; Intended magic item: any _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Figurine of Wondrous Power, Bronze Griffon|figurine of wondrous power]]_.
