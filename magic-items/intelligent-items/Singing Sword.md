# Singing Sword

This +2 _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Keen|keen]]_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Dancing|dancing]]_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Longsword|longsword]] is infused with the soul of a bard who chose to instill his consciousness into a weapon rather than to see his voice die. The sword constantly sings. The only way to silence it to a whisper is with a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check, and even this only quiets the sword for 10 minutes plus another 10 minutes for every 5 points by which the DC is exceeded. The sword speaks, though it typically communicates through song. A _singing sword_ has the following powers.

- Eight times per day, a _singing sword_ can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]], but can only use the countersong, distraction, fascinate, inspire courage, or inspire competence abilities. The sword is treated as an 8th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] for the purposes of these abilities.
- Three times per day, the _singing sword_ can use the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroic Finale|heroic finale]]_ spell.

**Intelligent item**: Int 14, Wis 12, Cha 18, Ego 17; chaotic good; Senses 30 ft.; Language speech (Common and four other languages).

Strong enchantment; CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Objects|animate objects]]_, the soul of a willing bard of at least 8th level; Price 165,315 gp; Cost 82,815 gp.
