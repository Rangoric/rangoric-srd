# Armor of the Shadow Lord

This suit of +4 *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Shadow, Greater|greater shadow]]* *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Slick, Greater|greater slick]]* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Leather Armor|leather armor]] fits as snugly as a tight suit of silk against its wearer's skin and harbors an amoral and bloodthirsty intelligence. Via the telepathy it shares with its wielder, the armor gives constant advice on how to be stealthy, reach out of the way spots, and get the drop on foes. It also grants an extra +1d6 points of sneak attack damage, but the wearer must already have the sneak attack class feature to gain this bonus. Furthermore the armor has a maximum Dexterity bonus of +10 and no arcane spell failure.

**Intelligent item**: Int 14, Wis 16, Cha 14, Ego 14; neutral; Senses 120 ft., darkvision; Language telepathy (Aklo, Common, and Elven).

Moderate illusion; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*, creator must have the sneak attack class feature; Price 117,160 gp; Cost 58,660 gp.
