# Rod That Should Not Be, Lesser

This rod is a fusion of magic and the limb of a dangerous, nearly mindless aberration. This rod features a single writhing tentacle at one end. The rod contains the mind of a living creature, but it does not have the ability to communicate via speech, empathy, or telepathy. If allowed, the rod attempts to attack creatures within reach, and must be stowed in a bag or backpack when the wielder is sleeping to keep it from grasping at the wielder or other creatures.

On command, the tentacle attacks an adjacent creature as a melee touch attack (attack bonus +8). On a successful hit, the tentacle deals 2d8 points of acid damage and the tentacle attacks the target’s Will at +4; on a hit, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round. This paralysis is a mind-affecting fear effect.

The greater version of this rod has four tentacles, which attack the same target simultaneously on command (attack bonus +12). The tentacles collectively deal 4d8 points of acid damage and the tentacles attack the target’s Will at +8; on a hit, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round. This paralysis is a mind-affecting fear effect. The greater rods have a dedicated purpose: serve a bloodthirsty master.

**Intelligent item**: Int 5, Wis 10, Cha 8, Ego 3; chaotic evil; Senses 30 ft.; Language none.

Moderate abjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Rod|Craft Rod]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp.
