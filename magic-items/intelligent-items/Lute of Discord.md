# Lute of Discord

This lute is a masterwork instrument constructed of polished ebony, with mother of pearl embellishments and gold frets. It grants any creature playing it a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (string instruments) checks as it gently telepathically whispers ways the creature can improve its current performance. It also grants those who can play the lute the following abilities.

- Three times per day on command, a creature with at least 6 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (string instruments) can play a chord on the lute of discord to employ the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Murderous Command|murderous command]]_ spell.
- Three times per day on command, a creature with at least 6 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (string instruments) can play a chord on the _lute of discord_ to use the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Distracting Cacophony|distracting cacophony]]_ spell (3rd level).
- Once per day on command, a creature with at least 6 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (string instruments) can play a chord to use the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Echolocation|echolocation]]_ spell (4th level caster).

**Intelligent item**: Int 14, Wis 14, Cha 18, Ego 14; chaotic neutral; Senses 120 ft., darkvision; Language telepathy (Common and four other languages).
