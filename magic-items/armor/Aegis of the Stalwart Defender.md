# Aegis of the Stalwart Defender

This *+2 adamantine* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Mountain Kite Shield|mountain kite shield]] provides excellent protection to any warrior, in the form of its (total) +5 shield bonus to AC. When carried in conjunction with the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Mantle of the Stalwart Defender|mantle of the stalwart defender]]*, it also grants [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] 2/–, increasing the total granted by the two to 5/– in normal situations, and 10/– when in a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Stalwart Defender|defensive stance]].

Faint abjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]*; Price 16,250 gp.
