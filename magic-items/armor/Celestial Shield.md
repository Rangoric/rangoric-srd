# Celestial Shield

This bright silver or gold _+2 blinding heavy steel shield_ is impossibly light and handy despite its size. It has no armor check penalty or arcane spell failure chance, and it allows the wielder to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_ on himself once per day. A creature wearing _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Celestial Armor|celestial armor]]_ while wielding a _celestial shield_ may command the armor to provide _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Overland Flight|overland flight]]_ rather than _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]_ once per day.

Moderate evocation [good]; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Overland Flight|overland flight]]_; Price 13,170 gp; Cost 6,670 gp.
