# Celestial Shield

This bright silver or gold *+2 blinding heavy steel shield* is impossibly light and handy despite its size. It has no armor check penalty or arcane spell failure chance, and it allows the wielder to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]* on himself once per day. A creature wearing *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Celestial Armor|celestial armor]]* while wielding a *celestial shield* may command the armor to provide *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Overland Flight|overland flight]]* rather than *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]* once per day.

Moderate evocation [good]; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Overland Flight|overland flight]]*; Price 13,170 gp; Cost 6,670 gp.
