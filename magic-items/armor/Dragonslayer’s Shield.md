# Dragonslayer’s Shield

This _+2 heavy steel shield_ has a distinct curve to its shape and often bears the device of a valiant knight. The wearer of this shield gains a +2 bonus on Reflex Defense against effects that originate or burst from a point outside his own square, as though he had [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|cover]]. It does not protect against spread effects. This bonus does not stack with that granted by actual cover or give cover to any creature other than the wearer. In addition, the _dragonslayer's shield_ grants its wielder a +2 morale bonus on Will Defense against the frightful presence ability.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield of Faith|shield of faith]]_; Price 7,170 gp; Cost 3,670 gp.
