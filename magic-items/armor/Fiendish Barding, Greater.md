# Fiendish Barding, Greater

This suit of _+3 mithral heavy barding_ resizes to fit on any Medium to Huge animal or magical beast. It transforms and infuses the creature with the essence of evil, giving them the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creatures/Fiendish Creature|fiendish]] template while worn. Good-aligned creatures are immediately [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] when this armor is placed on them, and the armor attacks their Fortitude each hour at +6, dealing 1d4 points of Constitution damage on a hit.

A creature that already has the fiendish template that wears this armor, finds its fiendish powers increased, adding a +2 profane bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, defenses and skill checks.

Strong transmutation [evil]; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]]_; Price 63,800 gp; Cost 31,900 gp + 2,552 XP.
