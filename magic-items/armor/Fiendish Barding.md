# Fiendish Barding

This suit of *+2 heavy barding* resizes to fit on any Medium to Huge animal. It transforms and infuses the animal with the essence of evil, giving it the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Fiendish Creature|fiendish]] template while worn. Good-aligned animals are immediately [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] when this armor is placed on them, taking 1d4 points of Constitution damage (DC 16 Fortitude save to resist each hour).

Strong transmutation [evil]; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Aura|unholy aura]]*; Price 27,400 gp; Cost 13,700 gp + 1,096 XP.
