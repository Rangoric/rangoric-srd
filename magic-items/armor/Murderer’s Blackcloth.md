# Murderer’s Blackcloth

This silk *+1 [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Shadow (Armor Special Ability)|shadow]] padded armor* is as dark as night and stitched with thread the color of dried blood. When worn by a rogue with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Bleeding Attack (Rogue Talent)|bleeding attack]] talent, it increases the bleed damage of the rogue's sneak attacks by +1.

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*; Price 12,405 gp; Cost 6,280 gp.
