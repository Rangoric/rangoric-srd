# Emperor’s Scale

Crafted for the emperor’s seasoned troops, this *+2* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Scale Mail|scale mail]] armor has had the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Weightless (Armor Special Ability)|weightless]]* property applied (to allow his troops quick movement on the battlefield), and *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Hardened|hardened]]* (to allow them to survive the vicious melees they’ll endure in his service). Some senior members of the Empire’s army further enchant the armor to include moderate *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]* and *energy resistance*.

Moderate enchantment; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], 9 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (armorsmithing), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]*; Price 16,200 gp.
