# Folding Plate

This item normally looks like a heavy steel brooch or cloak clasp, often depicting a heavy helm or sturdy shield. On command, the brooch transforms in a clatter of metallic plates and panels to instantly cover the wearer in a complete suit of _+1 full plate_, with the design of the brooch displayed on the armor's breastplate. The same command word causes the armor to transform into the brooch. The brooch only transforms if the wearer's armor slot is unoccupied, thus it won't work if the wearer is already armored. As a brooch it occupies the neck slot and weighs less than a pound; unfolded, it is a suit of armor in the armor slot and weighs 50 pounds.

Moderate conjuration (summoning); CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Instant Summons|instant summons]]_; Price 12,650 gp; Cost 7,650 gp.
