# Enchanted Eelskin

This _+1 slick leather armor_ is made of shining, slippery eelskin that allows the wearer to move with deadly grace and sudden swiftness, especially underwater. When immersed in water, _enchanted eelskin_ gains the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Shadow (Armor Special Ability)|shadow]]_ property and grants the wearer a swim speed of 30 feet and the ability to breathe water. In addition, during a surprise round the wearer can act as if she had a full round of actions, rather than a single standard action, as long as those actions do not require leaving the water. If she does leave the water, her surprise round turn immediately ends.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of the Sea|touch of the sea]]_; Price 11,160 gp; Cost 5,660 gp.
