# Tireless Tracking Hide

This _+1 hide armor_ is always made from the pelt of a migratory herd animal. Anyone wearing this armor can maintain a hustle for a full 8 hours without growing [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], doubling her normal daily overland movement rate. After that, the wearer begins to take nonlethal damage each hour, per the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Carrying, Movement, and Exploration#Overland Movement|hustle rules]]. This armor does not increase tactical movement during combat. The benefits of this armor aren't replenished until the character has had a full night's rest.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear's endurance]]_; Price 11,165 gp; Cost 5,665 gp.
