# Elysian Shield

This *+2 ghost touch tower shield* is engraved with funerary runes depicting the peaceful rest of the contented dead. The wielder of an *elysian shield* is immune to the create spawn ability of undead, and once per day as an immediate action can negate a single [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|energy drain]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|ability drain]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|ability damage]] attack by an undead creature. In addition, once per day as a standard action, the wielder can release a wave of positive energy that panics undead, as the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Turn Undead|Turn Undead]] feat (DC 20 Will negates).

Strong conjuration; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Turn Undead|Turn Undead]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Etherealness|etherealness]]*; Price 52,620 gp; Cost 26,390 gp.
