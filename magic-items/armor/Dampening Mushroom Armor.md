# Dampening Mushroom Armor

This armor functions as a suit of dampening leafy leather armor (the dampening and plant armor qualities, not in this SRD yet). The armor appears to be made from large pieces of pale tan leather, when in fact it is composed of several large mushrooms.

CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plant Growth|plant growth]]*; Price 35,000 gp; Cost 17,500 gp + 1,400 XP.
