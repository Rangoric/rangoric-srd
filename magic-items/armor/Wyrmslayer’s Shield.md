# Wyrmslayer’s Shield

This _+4 heavy steel shield_ acts in all ways as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Dragonslayer’s Shield|dragonslayer’s shield]]_, except it grants a +6 morale bonus on Will Defense against the frightful presence ability instead of a +2 morale bonus.

Moderate abjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield of Faith|shield of faith]]_; Price 20,170 gp; Cost 10,170 gp.
