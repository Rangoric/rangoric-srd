# Nightwarden’s Leaf

This *+3* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Elven Leafweave|elven leafweave]] is crafted from dark autumn leaves and has a somewhat ominous appearance. Nightwarden’s leaf is always *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Aspected|aspected]]* in some way, often bearing a druidic circle’s symbol. It also bears the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Dopple|dopple]]* ability, allowing it to be worn even as the wearer shifts forms.

Strong transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph Any Object|polymorph any object]]*; Price 26,750 gp.
