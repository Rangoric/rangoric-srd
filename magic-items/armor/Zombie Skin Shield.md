# Zombie Skin Shield

This *+1 light steel shield* is wrapped in a layer of gray skin. Once per day, the wearer can animate the skin as a human [[rangoric-srd/monsters/zombie/Zombie|zombie]] under her control, as if she had created it with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]*, for a period of 5 minutes. When the time expires, the zombie returns to the shield. If the zombie is destroyed or cannot reach the shield by the time it expires, placing the shield on a Medium humanoid corpse for 1 hour recreates the shield's skin.

Moderate abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]*; Price 2,159 gp; Cost 1,159 gp.
