# Ironwood Plate Armor of Fire Protection

As the name suggests, this *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ironwood|ironwood]]* plate armor confers upon the wearer protection of up to 156 hit points of fire damage. Once this threshold has been met or exceeded, the armor’s magic goes dormant for 12+1d12 hours before activating again with its full protections.

CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ironwood|ironwood]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plant Growth|plant growth]]*; Price 78,000 gp; Cost 39,000 gp + 3,100 XP.
