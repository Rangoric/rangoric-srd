# Mantle of the Stalwart Defender

This massive suit of armor is often found on the belabored backs of dwarven defenders, and is well suited to their cause. The armor itself is *+2* [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Dwarven Plate|dwarven plate]] and is expertly crafted from adamantine, granting a total +12 bonus to AC, and granting [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] 3/–. When the wearer enters a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Stalwart Defender|defensive stance]], the damage reduction granted by the armor increases to 8/– for the entire duration of the stance.

Faint abjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]*; Price 25,650 gp.
