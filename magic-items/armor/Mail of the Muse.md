# Mail of the Muse

This suit of *+2* [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Elven Chain|elven chain]] is engraved with golden symbols of musical notation. When worn by a singing bard the symbols emanate a golden light (as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]* spell), which provides a +1 deflection bonus to AC and a further +1 deflection bonus to AC for every 4 levels of bard. When the wearer stops using his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]], the golden light lingers for a number of rounds equal to the wearer’s Charisma modifier. The armor has a spell failure chance of 20%, a maximum Dexterity bonus of +4 and an armor check penalty of –2. It is considered light armor and weighs 20 pounds.

Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]*, creator must be a bard; Price 44,150 gp; Cost 22,075 gp + 1,766 XP.
