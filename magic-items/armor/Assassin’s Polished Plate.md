# Assassin’s Polished Plate

This suit of *+1* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Daggered Plate|daggered plate]] is highly ornate and often bears the crest of a royal house or noble family. Upon the users command, the armor turns jet black and gains the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Shadow, Improved|improved shadow]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Silent Moves, Improved|improved silent moves]]* properties, granting a +10 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. The daggers locked to either shoulder function as *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapons/Assassin’s Dagger|assassin’s daggers]]*, though they only retain their wicked, curved edge when the plate is in its clandestine form.

Moderate illusion; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]*; Price 68,504 gp; Cost 34,252 gp + 2,740 XP.
