# Buccaneer’s Breastplate

This _+1 breastplate_ is made of shining amber metal and decorated with faint motifs of the waves and water. It allows the wearer to continually utilize the effects of the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Walk|water walk]]_. If a creature puts on the armor while underwater, the wearer is borne toward the surface at a rate of 60 feet per round.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Walk|water walk]]_; Price 23,850 gp; Cost 12,100 gp.
