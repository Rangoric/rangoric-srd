# Morlock Hide

This _+1 shadow leather armor_ causes the wearer's skin to warp and distort into a welter of horrid scarification. The armor itself disappears from view under this coat of scars and the wearer's face appears as a loathsome, eyeless veil of flesh. This change is illusory, similar to _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Glamered|glamered]]_ armor; however, _morlock hide_ does cause the wearer's arms and legs to extend and her posture to become hunched. She gains a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb, and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] checks, and she can move through narrow spaces while squeezing at normal speed and with no penalty to AC or on attack rolls.

Moderate illusion and transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_; Price 8,910 gp; Cost 4,535 gp.
