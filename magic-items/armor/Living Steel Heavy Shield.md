# Living Steel Heavy Shield

This nonmagical heavy steel shield is made out of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Special Materials#^living-steel|living steel]]. It has no enhancement bonus, but its material and construction can damage metal weapons that strike it. Whenever the wielder of a metal weapon (other than one made of adamantine) rolls a natural 1 on an attack against a creature wielding this shield, the item must make a DC 20 Fortitude save or gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition. If the weapon already has the broken condition, it is instead destroyed.

No aura (nonmagical); Price 120 gp.
