# Avalanche Shield

The front of this _+1 bashing heavy steel shield_ looks as though it is crafted from hundreds of bits of rubble and larger stones. When used to make a shield bash attack, stones fall from shield and cling to the target of the shield bash. The target must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude saving throw or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] for 1d4 rounds. Once per day on command, the shield's wielder can choose to petrify the target of a shield bash instead. The target must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude saving throw or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] for 1 minute.

Strong evocation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]_, shape stone, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]_; Price 19,170 gp; Cost 9,670 gp.
