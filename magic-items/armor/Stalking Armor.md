# Stalking Armor

This *+2 studded leather armor* comes in a number of varieties, each corresponding to a specific type of terrain from the ranger's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-terrain|favored terrain]] list. The armor takes on visual characteristics related to that type of terrain (cold *stalking* armor might be gray and white with a white fur trim, jungle *stalking* armor could be lush green with vine patterns, and so on) and makes the wearer blend in more naturally with his surroundings. The *stalking armor* grants the wearer a +5 competence bonus on Stealth and Perception checks attempted while in that specific terrain.

Moderate transmutation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chameleon Stride|chameleon stride]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Perceive Cues|perceive cues]]*; Price 8,575 gp; Cost 4,375 gp.
