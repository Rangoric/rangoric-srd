# Catskin Leather

Rather than the hide of a cat, this extremely supple suit of jet-black _+1 shadow leather armor_ grants a portion of the grace and fortune of a feline. It has no armor check penalty or maximum Dexterity bonus. The wearer receives a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks and takes only half normal damage from falling. When the wearer's hit points would be reduced to a negative amount equal to her Constitution score from an attack or spell, the armor falls to pieces and is destroyed, and the wearer takes only half damage from the attack or spell.

Strong transmutation; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_; Price 18,910 gp; Cost 9,535 gp.
