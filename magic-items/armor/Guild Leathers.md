# Guild Leathers

A sign of favor among the thieves’ guild, this suit of *+3* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Leather Armor|leather]] is imbued with *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Silent Moves|silent moves]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Shadow, Improved|improved shadow]]* (granting a +10 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks, since the two competence bonuses don’t stack; originally, +5 on Move Silently and +10 on Hide checks). While some members of the guild *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Hardened|harden]]* their armors, most tend to invest in *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Glamered|glamer]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Spell Resistance (Armor Special Ability)|spell resistance]]*, or energy resistance.

Moderate enchantment; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*; Price 18,910 gp.
