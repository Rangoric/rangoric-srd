# Arcane Weave

This item is a *+1 mithral chain shirt* with pockets that hold four arcane scrolls. The wearer may activate the armor to invoke one of these scrolls as a move action (this does not provoke an attack of opportunity). When activated, a deep, resonant voice reads the scroll (a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]* spell negates the scroll’s activation). The entire contents of the scroll are consumed upon casting, even if a single scroll holds multiple spells.

The armor can invoke spells flawlessly that are equal or less than its caster level, and all scrolls are cast at the armor’s caster level regardless of the level of the scroll’s creator. To determine the success or failure of *arcane weave* on more powerful magic, treat the armor as a 6th-level caster and roll for failure as described for [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Scrolls, Staffs, and Wands#Scrolls|scrolls]].

Strong evocation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Mouth|magic mouth]]*, creator must have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Casting|Combat Casting]] feat; Price 23,850 gp; Cost 11,925 gp + 954 XP.
