# Hamatula Hide

Crafted from the flayed skin of a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Barbed Devil (Hamatula)|barbed devil]], this imposing _+2 spiked hide armor_ weighs half the normal weight. When its wearer [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rages]], the armor's spikes elongate and the hide bonds to her skin, providing fire resistance 10. Any creature that strikes the wearer with a melee weapon, unarmed strike, or a natural weapon while the spikes are elongated takes 1d8+6 points of piercing damage from the armor's barbs (unless using a weapon with reach). While the wearer is enraged, spells that cause additional damage to evil outsiders deal that increased damage to her.

A barbarian with fiend totem rage powers gains additional benefits from _hamatula hide_. If she has the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Fiend Totem, Lesser (Rage Power)|lesser fiend totem]] rage power, she gains a +1 profane bonus to Armor Class while raging. If she has the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Fiend Totem (Rage Power)|fiend totem]] rage power, the damage caused by her barbs increases to 2d8+6 and stacks with the barbed defense granted by the armor. If she has the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Fiend Totem, Greater (Rage Power)|greater fiend totem]] ability, she gains fire resistance 20 when raging.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph|polymorph]]_, resist fire; Price 44,215 gp; Cost 22,215 gp.
