# Giant-Hide Armor, Cloud Giant

This drooping, many-folded suit of _+3 hide armor_ is made from the tanned skin of an actual giant. Once per day on command, the wearer can take the form of a cloud giant, as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Form II|giant form II]]_. This transformation lasts up to 15 minutes, and ends when the wearer commands. Most good folk consider wearing the skins of intelligent creatures to be abhorrent. Giants gain a +2 morale bonus on attack and damage rolls against the wearer, or +4 if the attacker is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Cloud Giant|cloud giant]].

Strong transmutation; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], giant form II; Price 69,165 gp; Cost 34,665 gp.
