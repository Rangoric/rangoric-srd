# Winged Armor

Crafted with winged humanoids in mind, this suit of *+3* [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Elven Chain|elven chain]] armor is specially designed to accommodate flying creatures. At first glance the armor appears as if a section has been removed from the back. When pressed against the chest of a winged humanoid, the armor magically melds around the wearer and links together in the back around the wings. The magically formed armor allows for full free movement of the wearer’s wings, and can be removed by a command word.

The armor grants a bonus of +10 feet on their flight speed, and improves their maneuverability by one class. It has no effect on creatures that don’t have the ability to fly, or that fly without the use of wings.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fabricate|fabricate]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]*; Price 38,150 gp.
