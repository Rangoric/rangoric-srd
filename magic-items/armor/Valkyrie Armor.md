# Valkyrie Armor

This unusual suit of _+3_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Weightless (Armor Special Ability)|weightless]]_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Full Plate|full plate]] armor appears to be crafted for a human female. Valkyrie armor normally functions only for females, but males who don it are in for a surprise when their armor transforms them into females. Once the armor is removed, this effect can be reversed by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_. Female characters that equip this finely sculpted suit are able to fly into a rage twice a day, receiving a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength and Dexterity and a +2 morale bonus to all defenses. This effect lasts 2–20 rounds. Also, once per day, anyone wearing this armor can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantom Steed|phantom steed]]_.

Moderate enchantment; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Baleful Polymorph|baleful polymorph]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantom Steed|phantom steed]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]]_; Price 54,370 gp; Cost 28,010 gp + 2,109 XP.
