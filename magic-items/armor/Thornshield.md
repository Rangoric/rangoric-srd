# Thornshield

Popular among halfling, gnome, and elven warriors, *thornshields* are *+2* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Light Wooden Shield|light wooden shields]]. *Thornshields* sprout long, curved spines and thorns from the face of the shield when the command word is uttered; these function as *+2 shield spikes*. The wielder gains a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] combat maneuver checks, and cannot be disarmed of the shield himself.

Moderate transmutation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Warp Wood|warp wood]]*; Price 9,153 gp; Cost 4,577 gp + 366 XP.
