# Scurry’s Dampening Mushroom Armor

This armor, created for the bogie Scurry the Tunnelrat (gifted to him upon his release from service), functions as a suit of [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Dampening|dampening]] leafy leather [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Plant|plant armor]]. The armor appears to be made from large pieces of pale tan leather, when in fact it is composed of several large mushrooms.

CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plant Growth|plant growth]]_; Price 35,000 gp; Cost 17,500 gp + 1,400 XP.
