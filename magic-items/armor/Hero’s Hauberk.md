# Hero’s Hauberk

The damage reduction of this dull gray _+1 adamantine chain shirt_ stacks with any other damage reduction the wearer possesses. For example, a wearer with DR 5/magic would reduce damage from nonmagical attacks by 6, and magical attacks by 1. While the wearer [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rages]] or maintains a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]], the armor emits a low droning sound and grants the benefits of _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|light fortification]]_.

While the wearer maintains a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]], the armor's links jangle in harmony with his song or movements, aiding his focus. The wearer receives a +1 luck bonus on all ability checks and skill checks he attempts during such a performance. In addition, any penalties applied to his attack rolls, ability checks, skill checks, and defenses are reduced by 1. If a die roll has multiple penalties, the total of the penalties is reduced by 1, not each individual penalty.

Strong abjuration; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_, creator must have the raging song class feature; Price 16,600 gp; Cost 11,850 gp.
