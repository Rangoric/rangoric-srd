# Woven Assistant

This simple *+1* [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Woven Cord Armor|woven cord]] armor can be commanded to unwind from its wearer and act as a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Rope of Climbing|rope of climbing]]* or a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Rope of Entanglement|rope of entanglement]]*. Winding or unwinding the rope is a move action. The armor amounts to 60 feet of rope when unwound, thus when acting as a *rope of entanglement*, its reach is doubled to 40 ft. forward or 20 ft. up to entangle its victim. Just as the *rope of entanglement*, the armor (in any form) recovers 1 point of damage every 5 minutes.

Moderate transmutation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope|animate rope]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Objects|animate objects]]*; Price 21,386 gp.
