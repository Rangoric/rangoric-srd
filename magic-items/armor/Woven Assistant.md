# Woven Assistant

This simple _+1_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Woven Cord Armor|woven cord]] armor can be commanded to unwind from its wearer and act as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Rope of Climbing|rope of climbing]]_ or a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Rope of Entanglement|rope of entanglement]]_. Winding or unwinding the rope is a move action. The armor amounts to 60 feet of rope when unwound, thus when acting as a _rope of entanglement_, its reach is doubled to 40 ft. forward or 20 ft. up to entangle its victim. Just as the _rope of entanglement_, the armor (in any form) recovers 1 point of damage every 5 minutes.

Moderate transmutation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope|animate rope]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Objects|animate objects]]_; Price 21,386 gp.
