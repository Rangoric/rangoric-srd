# Portal Network

Each gate of a portal network is a massive ring of iron adorned with runes, bound to at least one other such gate. A creature that steps through is instantly transported to the gate's mate, anywhere on the same plane or another. Gates let Large or smaller creatures through; Huge creatures aren't transported, even if they squeeze through the ring. If the destination gate is blocked by an immovable object, the network doesn't work.

A network of more than two gates tells destinations apart by focal objects its creators chose, such as gems, minor magic items or carefully drawn sigils. Some gates don't work at all without a focal object; others send the traveler to a default location. Creatures can share a focal object by touching as they pass through. A gate with several focal objects has a set priority, fixed when the network was made, that decides which objects take precedence. If a network needs a focal object, only creatures using one, or touching someone who is, can pass through a gate, even an activated one.

Portal networks work an unlimited number of times. Once a gate is activated, impenetrable darkness fills its ring until 1 round after the last creature passes through. A creature doesn't reach the other end, or see the other side, until it has passed fully through the ring. Blocking a gate (such as by building a wall against its opening) stops anyone from passing through, but a creature standing in the way doesn't; it's pushed aside when anyone passes through.

**Destruction**: Destroying a portal network entirely means sending a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/artifacts/Sphere of Annihilation|sphere of annihilation]] through one of its gates. This destroys the sphere too (as its own destruction describes), except that a gap is always torn in the fabric of space.

Strong conjuration; CL 20th; Weight 64,000 lb.
