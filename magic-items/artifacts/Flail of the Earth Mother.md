# Flail of the Earth Mother

The Earth Mother gave this mighty weapon to her mortal worshippers long ago. It is currently lost somewhere deep in the earth, its whereabouts known only to her. Legend tells that in time of need, when the land and its people are in grave danger, she will send one of her chosen mortal worshippers on a quest to prove their worth. If successful, she will bestow upon them this [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dire Flail|dire flail]], with the haft of purest silver and the chains on either end solid gold. The spiked balls are as clear as crystal, each bearing a sparkling light within. Although it appears delicate, the weapon is indestructible and powerful. But until the hero of prophecy wields it, scholars say it appears to be a common flail, as much like a farm implement as a weapon of war.

The flail acts as a *+5* *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Holy|holy]]* *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Disruption|disruption]]* weapon. While bearing the flail, the wielder receives *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Aura|holy aura]]* as the spells. The flail’s wielder may cast the following spells from the flail once per day: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Earthquake|earthquake]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Shape|stone shape]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Transmute Rock to Mud|transmute rock to mud]]* (or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Transmute Mud to Rock|transmute mud to rock]]*, as the wielder chooses).

CL 25th.
