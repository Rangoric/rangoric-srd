# The Saint’s Mace

This relic appears to be a simple, well-used cudgel, but its simple appearance hides great power. The saint’s mace has a +5 enhancement bonus and functions as a heavy mace with the holy, axiomatic, and disruption special abilities. The wielder can project searing light from the mace at will, at caster level 20th.
