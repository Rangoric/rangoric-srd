# Demon Prince Armor

This armor looks like a more massive and dangerous suit of _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor/Demon Armor|demon armor]]_. It is a suit of +5 _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|heavy fortification]]_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Full Plate|full plate]] that allows its wearer to make claw attacks that deal 2d10 points of damage, and strike as +3 weapons that are treated as chaotic and evil for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. Additionally, the claws attack the Fortitude of those they strike at +10, and those they hit are affected as per the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]_. The armor's claws are built into the armor so these weapons cannot be disarmed. While wearing the armor, the wearer benefits from the following demonic traits.

- The wearer gains darkvision 60 ft. If it already has darkvision, the range of its darkvision is increased by 60 ft.
- The wearer does not need to breathe or eat.
- The wearer is immune to electricity and poison.
- The wearer gains resistance to acid 10, cold 10 and fire 10.
- The wearer gains telepathy and speaks Abyssal, Celestial, and Draconic.

**Destruction**: The Demon Prince Armor dissolves if submerged in the tears of a demon lord. If that foul concoction is then drank by an angel, the armor is destroyed.

Overwhelming necromancy; CL 22nd; Weight 80 lbs.
