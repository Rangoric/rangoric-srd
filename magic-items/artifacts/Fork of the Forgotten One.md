# Fork of the Forgotten One

This +5 _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Wounding|wounding]]_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Unholy|unholy]]_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Ranseur|ranseur]] was once the weapon of a powerful archdevil who they say controlled an entire layer of Hell. When he attempted to defy the Prince of Darkness, he was overthrown and banished into some cold and remote prison at the edge of the multiverse. Still bearing some infernal vestige of the forgotten fiend's power, the fork grants the bearer a +10 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks and the power to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]_ three times per day.

Any nonevil creature who touches the _Fork of the Forgotten One_ is given a choice: become lawful evil immediately, or die. Those who choose death are instantly slain (_attack_ +20 vs Fortitude). Those who choose to become lawful evil change alignment immediately (hitting automatically) and are subject to a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Demand|demand]]_ spell (+15 vs Will) requiring that they find the Forgotten One and free him from his icy prison—presumably thereafter he will press them into service for his plans of revenge against those who imprisoned him.

**Destruction**: The _Fork of the Forgotten One_ is destroyed when a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]] of 19th level or higher touches it and chooses death rather than becoming lawful evil. This sacrifice still kills the paladin, though she can be raised from the dead.

Strong transmutation; CL 20th; Weight 12 lbs.
