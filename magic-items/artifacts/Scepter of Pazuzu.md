# Scepter of Pazuzu

The Scepter of Pazuzu, a staff of black metal with a large hook at its tip, is [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Pazuzu|Pazuzu]]'s weapon. The scepter functions as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rods/Rod of Enemy Detection|rod of enemy detection]]_ that can be used any number of times per day. If Pazuzu presents the scepter as a standard action, all creatures within 30 feet who view it become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for 1d6 rounds if it hits their Will (+13) (this is a mind-affecting fear effect). Once per day, Pazuzu can use the Scepter of Pazuzu as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rods/Rod of Cancellation|rod of cancellation]]_ (this does not deplete or harm the scepter). As a standard action, he can call the scepter to his hand from any distance, even across planar boundaries. As an immediate action, Pazuzu can transform the scepter into a +5 _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Anarchic|anarchic]]_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Keen|keen]]_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Unholy|unholy]]_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Longsword|longsword]] or return it to its normal shape. None of its other powers can be used in sword form.

**Destruction**: The Scepter of Pazuzu can be broken by a redeemed demon lord if Pazuzu's regenerating towers are already destroyed.

Overwhelming all; CL 30th; Weight 4 lb.
