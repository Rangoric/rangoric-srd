# Skill Blade

This wondrous [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Longsword|longsword]] is exquisitely crafted and decorated with wild flourishes and fantastic jewels. The secret of the sword’s creation is long lost. The sword is imbued with an evil intelligence, requiring anyone who possesses it who is not Chaotic Evil to fight its will in order to set it down or to sheathe it.

The sword itself is a *+5* *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Keen|keen]]* *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Clamping (Weapon Special Ability)|clamping]]* longsword. Every time the sword slays a victim, it gains a single rank in the victim’s highest skill. These skill ranks are cumulative, and add on to the skill ranks of anyone holding the sword. For example, if the sword kills a creature whose highest skill is [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], the sword’s wielder gains a +1 bonus on Athletics checks while holding the sword. For purposes of the skill sword, killing a creature means performing the final blow to it before its death. This can be a blow that brings it to just below 0 hp and causes it to bleed to death, or it can be a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Helpless Defenders|coup de grace]] performed on an already dying foe.

The sword’s wielder has the option to expend the sword’s skill ranks as if they were charges for the sword’s special powers. Spent ranks can be taken from any of the sword’s skills, as decided by the wielder. The sword can use the following powers when charges are spent:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]* (2 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Iron Body|iron body]]* (3 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Spray|prismatic spray]]* (3 charges)

Each time any of these powers are used for purposes not in accordance with the sword’s alignment, the wielder must win a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Intelligent and Cursed Items and Artifact#Items Against Characters|personality conflict]] with the sword. The skill sword is telepathic, and has the following attributes: Int: 17, Wis: 9, Cha: 19, Ego: 25.

When first recovered, it is likely that the sword has skill ranks in it. Roll d6 to determine its state, or choose:

| d6 | Skill Ranks |
|---|---|
| 1 | None |
| 2–5 | 1d10 random skills have 1d8 ranks each |
| 6 | Every skill has 1d10–5 ranks |

CL 25th.
