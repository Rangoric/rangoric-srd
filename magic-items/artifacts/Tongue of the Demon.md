# Tongue of the Demon

Rumored to have been created from the severed tongue of a demon lord—by none other than the demon himself—it has appeared throughout the ages in the possession of numerous champions of evil who were devoted to the demon. The _Tongue of the Demon_ is a _+5_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Anarchic|anarchic]]_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Unholy|unholy]]_ _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Icy Burst|icy burst]]_ [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Trident|trident]] that is infused with a sadistic and truly evil intelligence. As an intelligent weapon, it has an Intelligence of 16, a Wisdom of 16, and a Charisma of 18. It can communicate with its wielder via empathy and often imposes its emotions upon the wielder when the creature takes actions of which the _Tongue_ does not approve. The _Tongue of the Demon_ is chaotic evil, and detests being wielded by anyone of a different alignment. It tries at all times to impose its will over such an undesirable user.

As long as its owner remains the chosen wielder of the _Tongue of the Demon_, he gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]] feat and does not need to sleep, provided the _Tongue_ is within 30 feet of his location. If the owner of the _Tongue_ can cast spells, he still needs to rest for 8 hours before preparing spells, but during this rest he is fully aware of his surroundings. Three times a day, the user can afflict a creature struck by the _Tongue_ with an empowered _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]_ spell (as the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Empower Spell|Empower Spell]] feat). The _Tongue of the Demon_ was created by its demonic maker to defeat good outsiders. Each time it strikes such a creature (but only once per round), the _Tongue_ attacks the good outsider’s Fortitude at +9, and on a hit it suffers the effects of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Destruction|destruction]]_ spell. Even on a miss, the outsider takes an additional 10d6 points of damage from the attempted _destruction_. The _Tongue of the Demon_ has an Ego score of 29.

CL 21st.
