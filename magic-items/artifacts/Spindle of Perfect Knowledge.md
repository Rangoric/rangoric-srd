# Spindle of Perfect Knowledge

The *spindle of perfect knowledge* is large, bright, and flawless marquise-cut diamond *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Ioun Stone, Clear Spindle|ioun stone]]* the size of a halfling's fist. The gemstone would be a treasure in itself if it lacked magic, but it is also a powerful *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Ioun Stone, Clear Spindle|ioun stone]]*. The *spindle of perfect knowledge* functions like a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Headband of Mental Superiority +4|headband of mental superiority +4]]* and grants 5 ranks in two random [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] skills. The wearer can communicate and understand languages as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Comprehend Languages|comprehend languages]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]*, and telepathy with a range of 100 feet.

**Destruction**: The *spindle of perfect knowledge* is destroyed if worn by a mindless creature for an entire year.

Strong transmutation; CL 20th.
