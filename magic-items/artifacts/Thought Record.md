# Thought Record

This tube looks like a plain copper pipe a foot long, but its exotic metal bends the flow of time. Despite its light weight, it resists sudden acceleration as if it were a massive tree trunk, and the patterns etched inside it shift constantly without ever repeating. A thought record stores and replays memories, absorbing the whole of a person's experience in an instant; the memories stay in it indefinitely and can't be changed, though later recordings from the same person can be added. It absorbs these imprints unpredictably, with no sign of when it will trigger or how to activate it on purpose. When it records a life, its etched surface fades into a tiny window on a starscape. No one knows of any limit to how many lives one record can hold.

The memories in a thought record can be used with reality-altering magic such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ to bring back those lost to death beyond other recall.

A thought record can let its holder live parts of another's life in an instant. What triggers a replay is unclear, though most often it happens the moment a creature first touches it. The holder then lives the stored memories as if she were there and making the choices herself, and suffers any mental conditions suffered in them, such as insanity, damage to mental ability scores and mind-affecting effects. A record almost never replays more than one creature's memories for one user: later activations replay other memories of the same creature, skipping back and forth across its life rather than in order.

The time-bending properties that let it carry memories make the rod and the memories in it all but immune to physical harm or alteration.

**Destruction**: A thought record can be destroyed by making it absorb the memories of a senile god.

Strong divination; CL 15th; Weight 2 lb.
