# Monkey's Paw

This dimension-hopping artifact waits patiently until a creature picks it up. Each time it is picked up by a new user, the _monkey's paw_ grants that creature three _wishes,_ as if it were a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Three Wishes|ring of three wishes]]_. Once a new user picks up the _monkey's paw_, it cannot be discarded, not even by way of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ or a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_ spell. Even if stolen, it reappears among the user's possessions within 1d4 hours. It can only be parted from its bearer when all three of its wishes are used or it is destroyed—but such a feat takes time and patience. When a bearer uses a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ granted by the _monkey's paw_, he is then subject to a curse, as per the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Curse, Major|major curse]]_ (DC 25 to remove). This curse manifests as the creature taking a –4 penalty to attack rolls, defenses, ability checks, and skill checks until the curse is removed. These penalties are cumulative in cases where multiple _wishes_ are used before past curses are removed. Once all three _wishes_ are used, the monkey paw _plane shifts_ to a random plane and begins the process anew.

**Destruction**: The monkey's paw is destroyed when its user retains the artifact and is cursed by it for 1,000 years.

Strong necromancy and universal; CL 20th; Weight 2 lbs.
