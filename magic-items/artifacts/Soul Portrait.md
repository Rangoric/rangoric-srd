# Soul Portrait

A soul portrait is a painting of immense magical power on a solid wooden frame. Its image varies, and it has a magical effect only if it shows the likeness of its current owner (other figures in the background don't interfere).

The owner of a soul portrait doesn't appear to age and no longer takes the penalties of aging, though she still gains its mental bonuses; the portrait ages in her place, and she doesn't die of old age. If she stops being the owner, she immediately takes all the physical penalties for her current age and her appearance changes to match; if she's past her maximum age, she withers and dies at once.

When the owner is examined by magical means (such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aura Sight|aura sight]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]]_), she gains the benefit of a special _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Misdirection|misdirection]]_ that hits the investigator automatically, giving the results she would have given when she first acquired the portrait. Unlike a normal misdirection, it isn't limited to spells that detect her aura: whenever it would benefit her, she counts as her younger self for any spell or effect that gathers information. If she denies a crime she committed after commissioning the portrait, for instance, all truth magic shows her denial as true, and even _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ reveals her younger self's thoughts.

When the owner would take [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|ability damage]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|ability drain]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative levels]], she suffers no ill effects until they would be fatal; she still accrues them in the portrait, where her image twists in agony as if suffering for her. As soon as the accrued harm would be fatal (such as negative levels equal to her Hit Dice), she dies. Effects that heal these maladies can heal the portrait to reduce what it holds.

When the owner dies, her soul is drawn into the canvas and her likeness becomes a figure in the background, leaving room for a new portrait. A creature claims ownership by having an artist paint her likeness over the image; she must be present and willing while the artist paints, and the artist must succeed at a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (painting) check for the artifact to accept her. If the previous owner is still alive, she then stops being the owner and ages as above. The portrait can't be cut, burned or otherwise damaged, except as its destruction describes.

**Destruction**: Only a soul portrait's current owner can destroy it. She must slay the artist who painted her likeness on it, then destroy the portrait with the same weapon.

Strong necromancy; CL 20th; Weight 25 lb.
