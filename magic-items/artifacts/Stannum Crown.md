# Stannum Crown

This velvet-capped crown is polished to a silvery gleam, but its metal is simple tin. It feeds on its wearer's paranoia and mistrust, making him immune to mind-affecting effects and to divinations that gather information about him; he can't lower this immunity without taking the crown off. Wearing it saps his mind, imposing a –2 penalty to Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma. Taking it off removes the penalty, but his paranoia makes him unwilling to: once per day, he can try to remove it with a Will check (1d20 + his Will save bonus; DC 25 + the number of consecutive days he has worn it). Once the DC is 35 or higher, his paranoia no longer lets him treat any other creature as an ally, and even harmless spells and abilities must attack his defenses. From then on, a natural 20 isn't an automatic success on the check to remove the crown, and he never counts as a willing target for another's spell or ability, even when unconscious. Other creatures can't remove the crown by force; only the wearer can take it off.

**Destruction**: A stannum crown can be destroyed only by placing it on the head of a sentient being truly incapable of mistrust. This destroys the crown, but leaves that creature with its powers and drawbacks forever, unable to remove or lessen them.

Strong abjuration; CL 15th; Weight 2 lb.
