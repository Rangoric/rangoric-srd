# Perfect Golden Lute

The _perfect golden lute_ is said to be a flawless instrument; its tone and clarity are unrivaled. It is an instrument so fine it practically performs itself, granting anyone who plays it a +10 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (string instruments) checks with it. A character with ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (string instruments) can play the lute to activate countersong, distraction, or fascinate (as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] abilities) as if he were a 1st-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]], performing a number of rounds per day equal to his Charisma modifier. If the user of the _perfect golden lute_ is a bard, he adds +2 to the DC of all bardic performance abilities using the lute, and can use bardic performance with the lute for an additional 6 rounds per day.

**Destruction**: The _perfect golden lute_ shatters into splinters if a day passes in which no music is played within 1,000 miles of it, or if its bearer is killed by a bard spell or bardic performance.

Strong transmutation; CL 20th; Weight 3 lbs.
