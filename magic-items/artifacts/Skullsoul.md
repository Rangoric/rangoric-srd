# Skullsoul

This grim, alabaster figurine is the size of a human skull with an elaborately sculpted base. The *Skullsoul* is inert until it is bonded with a living creature. It then becomes a powerful and never-questioning companion. The *Skullsoul* functions as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Demilich|demilich]] without the devour soul, rejuvenation, telekinetic storm, and torpor abilities, and with a hardness of 10. It is under the complete control of whoever bonds with the skull. Bonding with the *Skullsoul* is a ritual that takes 24 hours to perform, and doing so is an evil act. Paladins and good clerics who do so become ex-paladins and ex-clerics respectively. If they wish to atone, they must intercede with their deities (expending 2,500 gp in rare incense and offerings) and must promise to contain the skull.

**Destruction**: The *Skullsoul* cannot be destroyed but it can be contained. As long as the *Skullsoul* stays pierced by either a weapon with the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Vorpal|vorpal]]* special ability or a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapons/Holy Avenger|holy avenger]]*, it is contained and entirely inert until the sword is removed.

Strong necromancy; CL 18th; Weight 8 lbs.
