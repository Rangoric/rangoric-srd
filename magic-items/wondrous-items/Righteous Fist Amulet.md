# Righteous Fist Amulet

This golden amulet is used by religious sects as a way to help the faithful capture fugitives or defend themselves without doing undue harm to potential converts. While the wearer is using her divine powers of retribution (such as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric|cleric]]'s smite, an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]]'s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Smiting (Judgment)|smiting judgment]], or a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|paladin]]'s smite), the amulet grants her the ability to make unarmed strikes in melee combat as if she had the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]] feat.

Faint transmutation; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Fist|stone fist]]_; Price 4,000 gp; Cost 2,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
