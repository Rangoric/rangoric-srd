# Gravewatch Pendant

Favored by the grave-wardens of sites known to be infested with the spirits of the dead, this pendant is an iron ring strung on a necklace made from polished and discolored bones, nails, and teeth. A faint blue light limns the wearer, protecting him from attack. Undead creatures are repulsed by this light and take a –2 penalty on all attack rolls against the wearer of a _gravewatch pendant_. [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Incorporeality|Incorporeal]] undead creatures are further repulsed and instead take a –4 penalty on all attack rolls against the wearer. Undead creatures can sense this light easily, however, and receive a +4 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to detect a creature wearing this pendant.

Faint abjuration; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Evil|protection from evil]]_; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
