# Amulet of the Spirits, Stone

Each different type of _amulet of the spirits_ grants its wearer powers from one type of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Spirits|spirit]] bond, but makes her vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with that spirit bond (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Spirit-Hunting|spirit-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Mysteries|mystery]] or spirit wears the amulet, that wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of her mystery or spirit powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of mystery or spirit powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Stone Spirit|Stone]]**: This rounded river rock hangs from a copper chain. Once per day as a swift action, the wearer can activate a ward of stone, granting herself [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]] 5/adamantine against the next two melee attacks against her. This ward lasts for 1 minute, after which it fades away if not expended. In addition, once per day as a swift action the wearer can cause a creature within 30 feet to become slightly magnetic; this effect lasts for 2 rounds. Whenever the creature is attacked by a melee or ranged weapon made primarily of metal, it takes a –4 penalty to AC.

Moderate abjuration; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heat Metal|heat metal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Stone Spirit|stone]] spirit; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
