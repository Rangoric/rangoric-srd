# Belt of the Weasel

This surprisingly soft belt has an absurdly large buckle, made even more absurd by the fact that it's crafted in the shape of a smiling weasel's face. It grants its wearer a +2 enhancement bonus to Dexterity, and the ability to move at half normal speed while [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. Treat the enhancement bonus to Dexterity as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the belt is worn. The wearer does not take a penalty on melee attack rolls or to AC against melee attacks while prone. Additionally, the wearer gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Compression|compression]] ability.

Moderate transmutation; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
