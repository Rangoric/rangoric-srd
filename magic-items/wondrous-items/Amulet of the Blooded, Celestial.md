# Amulet of the Blooded, Celestial

Each different type of _amulet of the blooded_ grants its wearer powers from one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines|bloodline]], but makes him vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with the appropriate bloodline (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Blood-Hunting|blood-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated bloodline wears the amulet, the wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of his bloodline powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of bloodline powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Celestial Bloodline|Celestial]]**: This large, polished opal hangs from a chain of smoky gray steel. The wearer gains acid [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 5 and cold resistance 5. Three times per day as a standard action, the wearer can unleash a ray of heavenly fire as a ranged touch attack, targeting any foe within 30 feet. Against evil creatures, the ray deals 1d4+4 points of damage. The damage is divine and not subject to energy resistance or immunity. The ray heals good creatures of 1d4+4 points of damage, but a good creature cannot benefit from the heavenly fire more than once per day. Neutral creatures are unaffected by the ray.

Moderate abjuration and evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Celestial Bloodline|celestial]] bloodline; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
