# Vest of Surgery

This emerald vest has four prominent and bulging pockets along its front. The wearer is always treated as if using a healer's kit when making [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks. Once per day, the wearer can make a DC 20 Medicine check as if attempting to [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Deadly Wounds|treat deadly wounds]]. If the check is successful, it cures 1d4 points of ability damage instead of hit point damage.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]_, creator must have 5 ranks in Medicine; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp; Weight 4 lbs.
