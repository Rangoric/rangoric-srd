# Grappler's Mask

This mask is fashioned from dark leather decorated with geometrical patterns that emphasize the wearer's fearsomeness. It covers the wearer's face but leaves his mouth and eyes uncovered. The wearer may attempt to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] without provoking attacks of opportunity.

Faint transmutation; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
