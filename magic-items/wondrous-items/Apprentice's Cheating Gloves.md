# Apprentice's Cheating Gloves

These nondescript white gloves are a boon to minor spellcasters and fakes who are limited or lacking in magical ability or versatility. The wearer of these gloves can employ _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_ at will.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_; Price 2,200 gp; Cost 1,100 gp.
