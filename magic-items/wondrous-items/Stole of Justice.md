# Stole of Justice

This long stole of unrelenting black is marked on one end with golden scales and the other with the embroidered image of hooded justice. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks and can create a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Zone of Truth|zone of truth]]_ for up to 5 minutes per day; these rounds need not be consecutive. When the wearer uses _detect chaos_, _detect evil_, _detect good_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|detect law]]_, at the beginning of her next turn after casting she gains information as if she had concentrated for 3 full rounds. Finally, if the wearer is an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]], when using her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|judgment]] she receives a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks and her combat maneuver defense as if she were wielding a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Jurist|jurist]]_ weapon.

Faint divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Zone of Truth|zone of truth]]_, creator must have 5 ranks of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]]; Price 18,000 gp; Cost 9,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
