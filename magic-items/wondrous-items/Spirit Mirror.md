# Spirit Mirror

This hand mirror lets its user talk with the spirits of the recently dead, at great risk. On command, the user looks into the mirror and names a recently dead person, trying to call that person's spirit into it. The attempt has a 70% chance of success, less 10% for each week the person has been dead (to a minimum of 0%). On a failure, the mirror stops working for 1d4 days and can never contact that spirit. On a success, the person's image appears in the mirror and the user can ask up to three questions, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Spirit|call spirit]]*. The spirit can also move objects within 20 feet of the mirror, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unseen Servant|unseen servant]]*.

The mirror lets malevolent spirits through, too: each time it fails to contact a spirit, another spirit comes instead. Usually it makes mischief, giving wrong answers or moving objects around, but there is a 10% chance it appears as a random incorporeal undead and attacks the user.

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Spirit|call spirit]]*; Price 18,900 gp; Cost 9,450 gp; Weight 4 lb.
