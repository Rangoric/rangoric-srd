# Anatomy Doll

This stuffed rag doll might resemble a child's toy if not for the vital organs depicted on its cloth. By dabbing the doll with a few drops of blood from a living creature (a standard action), the bearer can form a sympathetic link between the doll and the victim. If the chosen victim has [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]], the doll must beat the target's spell resistance to create the link. As a standard action, the bearer can twist or stab the doll; this gives the target the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] condition for one round and deals 1 point of negative energy damage plus 1 point for each die of the doll bearer's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage (if any). Each time the target is affected by the doll, the link attacks its Will at +4, and a miss ends the link. The doll's holder needs line of effect to the victim, but not line of sight. Once linked to a particular creature, an _anatomy doll_ cannot be linked to another creature, but the bearer can re-establish a broken link by anointing it with more of the target's blood.

Faint necromancy; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
