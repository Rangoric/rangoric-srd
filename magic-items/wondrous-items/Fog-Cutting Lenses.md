# Fog-Cutting Lenses

These goggles are made of polished rock crystal, with frames of polished brass and a simple leather strap and buckle sized for a giant's head (but easily adjustable for smaller wearers). The goggles allow the wearer to see through magical and normal fogs, mists, and similar obscurement. They do not confer [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]. Further, the goggles distort and skew vision strangely, imposing a –4 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkvision|darkvision]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]_; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
