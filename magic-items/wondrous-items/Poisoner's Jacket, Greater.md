# Poisoner's Jacket, Greater

This jacket has the abilities of a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Poisoner's Jacket, Lesser|lesser poisoner's jacket]]_, and once per day the wearer can create a vial that reproduces the effect of any [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]] that costs 4,000 gp or less. The jacket's poison always looks like a murky lime fluid. This poison becomes inert after 1 hour.

Strong conjuration and necromancy; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Creation|major creation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]_; Price 58,000 gp; Cost 29,000 gp; Weight 4 lbs.
