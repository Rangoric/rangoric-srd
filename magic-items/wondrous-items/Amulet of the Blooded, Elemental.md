# Amulet of the Blooded, Elemental

Each different type of _amulet of the blooded_ grants its wearer powers from one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines|bloodline]], but makes him vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with the appropriate bloodline (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Blood-Hunting|blood-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated bloodline wears the amulet, the wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of his bloodline powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of bloodline powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Elemental Bloodline|Elemental]]**: This amulet comes in a variety of minerals depending on its powers, as shown in the table below. The wearer gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 5 against the energy type shown in the table. Once per day as a standard action, the wearer can unleash a blast of elemental power. This 20-foot-radius burst deals 4d6 points of damage (+4 vs Reflex; a miss halves) of the energy type listed. Creatures it hits gain vulnerability to the energy type until the end of the wearer's next turn.

| d%     | Material   | Energy Type |
| ------ | ---------- | ----------- |
| 01–25  | Anhydrite  | Electricity |
| 26–50  | Hematite   | Acid        |
| 51–75  | Beryl      | Fire        |
| 76–100 | Forsterite | Cold        |

Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Aura|elemental aura]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Elemental Bloodline|elemental]] bloodline; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
