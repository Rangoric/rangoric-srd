# Bead of Newt Prevention

This bead is carved with a tiny pictogram representing a small, harmless animal such as a newt, rabbit, or frog. If a hostile polymorph effect hits the bearer such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Baleful Polymorph|baleful polymorph]]_, the bead is destroyed and the effect does not affect the bearer.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 500 gp.
