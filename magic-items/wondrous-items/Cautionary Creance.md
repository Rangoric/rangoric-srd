# Cautionary Creance

This slim leather leash is just over 100 feet long, incredibly light, and uncannily strong, making it an excellent tool for training birds of prey and other flying predators. It grants a +4 circumstance bonus on any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] check made to train such animals. When used to bind a flying [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar]] to its master, the leash allows the master to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_ on the creature at will. If the master has the share [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^familiar-deliver-touch-spell|touch spells]] class ability, once per day he can use that ability on the leashed creature, even if they are not touching. The delivered spell can be no higher than 3rd level. The creance attaches to the master's forearm; attack rolls and skill checks made with that arm take a –1 penalty.

Faint transmutation; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Reach Spell|Reach Spell]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_; Price 4,000 gp; Cost 2,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
