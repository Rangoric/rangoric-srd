# Gloves of Swimming and Climbing

These apparently normal lightweight gloves grant a +5 competence bonus on Athletics checks. Both gloves must be worn for the magic to be effective.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; Craft Wondrous Item, bull’s strength, cat’s grace; Price 6,250 gp.
