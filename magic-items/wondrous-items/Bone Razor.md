# Bone Razor

This jagged bone knife seems too thin and frail for combat but easily cuts flesh. If used to perform a coup de grace on a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] living creature and the creature dies from the attack, the creature's flesh begins to peel off and its bones animate as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeleton/Skeleton|skeleton]] under the command of the bearer of the razor. The creature's flesh is not destroyed, but decays at a steady rate. The bearer of the razor can spend 1 minute reattaching the flesh to the animate skeleton, which ends the necromantic magic and results in a normal corpse (though a corpse of a person killed or mutilated by knife wounds). If the bearer uses the razor to flense and animate another creature's bones, the previous animate skeleton is immediately destroyed.

Faint necromancy; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]_; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
