# Meridian Belt

This narrow cloth belt has a silver buckle in the shape of four rings. The belt allows a creature to wear a magic ring on each foot in addition to the ring on each hand, though only two rings function at any given time. As a swift action, the wearer can change which of his rings are active (both hands, both feet, left hand and right foot, and so on). For example, a creature could wear a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Protection +1|ring of protection]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Energy Resistance, Minor|ring of energy resistance]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Swimming|ring of swimming]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Counterspells|ring of counterspells]]*, switching between any two of them as a swift action each round as it desires.

The belt does not change the type of action required to activate a ring (for example, activating a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Invisibility|ring of invisibility]]* is still a standard action), but allows the wearer to easily switch between the constant powers of several worn rings. While the belt is worn, wearing a ring on a foot counts toward the attunement process of certain rings (such as a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Sustenance|ring of sustenance]]*) even if the belt isn't used to make that ring active during that attunement period.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph|polymorph]]*; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
