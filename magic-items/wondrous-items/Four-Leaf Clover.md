# Four-Leaf Clover

This four-leaf clover was picked in the wild and preserved, usually encased in glass or crystal, sometimes pressed or bronzed. Three times per day, before attempting an ability check or skill check, or before an attack against its defenses is rolled, a creature carrying it can call on its luck to gain a +2 luck bonus on that check or defense. Doing so is a free action she can take even when it isn't her turn.
