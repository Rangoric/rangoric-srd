# Earring of Overhearing

When worn, this small earring bestows upon the wearer the ability to hear anyone who speaks her name for the next three breaths (treat as about 25 words). This does not grant the wearer the ability to communicate back, or grant any special method of understanding other languages. The person speaking the wearer’s name does not necessarily know that they are being overheard, but can become aware of it by making a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] check at DC 25. The speaker and the earring’s owner must be on the same plane of existence for the power to work.

CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]*; Price 5,000 gp.
