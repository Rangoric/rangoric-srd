# Vambraces of the Genie, Marid

These bracers are decorated with icons representing air, earth, fire, or water, and depictions of genies appropriate to that element. The item protects the wearer against hot and cold environments as if she were using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_. The vambraces' materials and other powers depend on their associated genie type.

**Marid**: These coral bracers allow the wearer to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Torrent|hydraulic torrent]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_ each once per day.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Torrent|hydraulic torrent]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_; Price 18,900 gp; Cost 9,450 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
