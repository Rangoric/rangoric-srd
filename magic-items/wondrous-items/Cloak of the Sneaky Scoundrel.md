# Cloak of the Sneaky Scoundrel

This dark burgundy cloak shifts its hue and pattern to blend in against its surroundings, granting the wearer a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. The cloak holds a concealed scabbard in which a light blade (as defined in the fighter weapon group) can be hidden. The wearer can draw the blade as if from a normal scabbard, but anyone searching the wearer for the item must succeed at a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check, as if he were looking for a secret door.

If a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler|swashbuckler]] is wearing the cloak, she can spend 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] point to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vanish|vanish]]_, as the spell.

Faint illusion; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shrink Item|shrink item]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vanish|vanish]]_; Price 6,400 gp; Cost 3,200 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
