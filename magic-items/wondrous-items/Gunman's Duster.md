# Gunman's Duster

This long, loose-fitting coat grants a +4 armor bonus to AC and a +2 luck bonus to the wearer's touch AC against firearm attacks. If the wearer is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger|gunslinger]] or has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Gunslinger|Amateur Gunslinger]] feat, she begins each day with 1 additional [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]] point. This extra point does not increase her maximum grit total. When the wearer uses the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Gunslinger's Dodge (Deed)|gunslinger's dodge]] deed, the deed's dodge bonuses to AC increase by +1. If the wearer is not a gunslinger, she may use the gunslinger's dodge deed once per day.

Strong abjuration; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Gunslinger|Amateur Gunslinger]] or creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger|gunslinger]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bullet Shield|bullet shield]]_; Price 36,000 gp; Cost 18,000 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
