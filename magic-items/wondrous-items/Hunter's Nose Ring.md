# Hunter's Nose Ring

This nose ring seems to be braided from five platinum strands. It grants its wearer the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]] ability along with a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks involving smell and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to track using scent.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bloodhound|bloodhound]]_, platinum ring worth 250 gp; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp.
