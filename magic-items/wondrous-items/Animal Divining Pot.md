# Animal Divining Pot

These small clay vessels are shaped like small animals; fish, lizards, mice and sparrows are the most common. If the user places a living animal of the pot's shape inside, she can talk with it in whispers, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_. This works three times per day.

Faint divination; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_; Price 1,400 gp; Cost 700 gp; Weight 5 lb.
