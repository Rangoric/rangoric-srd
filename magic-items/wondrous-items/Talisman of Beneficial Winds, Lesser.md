# Talisman of Beneficial Winds, Lesser

A talisman works as described under [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#^talismans|Talismans]] in the rules for magic items on the body. A lesser talisman has only enough magic to protect its wearer once, after which it crumbles to dust.

The first time the wearer falls at least 5 feet, he is automatically affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_.

Faint transmutation; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_; Price 50 gp; Cost 25 gp; Weight 1 lb.
