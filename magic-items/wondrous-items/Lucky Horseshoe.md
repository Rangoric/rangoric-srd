# Lucky Horseshoe

Though it looks like an ordinary horseshoe, a lucky horseshoe focuses the energies of good fortune. While its owner carries it, she gains a +1 luck bonus on saving throws. Once per day, she can call on it to gain a +4 luck bonus on one saving throw, declaring it before the roll.

Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_; Price 6,800 gp; Cost 3,400 gp; Weight 2 lb.
