# Belt of Mighty Hurling, Greater

This item functions as the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Belt of Mighty Hurling, Lesser|lesser belt of mighty hurling]]_, except it grants a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength and any thrown weapons its wearer hurls are treated as though they had the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Returning|returning]]_ quality. This does not apply to improvised ranged weapons, splash weapons, or weapons the wearer lacks proficiency with. Treat the bonus to Strength as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the belt is worn.

Moderate transmutation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longshot|longshot]]_; Price 42,000 gp; Cost 21,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
