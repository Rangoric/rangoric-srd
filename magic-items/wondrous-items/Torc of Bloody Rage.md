# Torc of Bloody Rage

This thick iron torc appears crudely made, and a few dents from the maker's hammer are visible. Once per day as a free action, the wearer can enter a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]* for 6 rounds as the spell, except that the wearer is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] for 12 rounds after the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]* ends. If the wearer has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] class feature, once per day as a free action the wearer can grant herself 3 additional rounds of rage instead.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]*, creator must have the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodrage]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] class feature; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
