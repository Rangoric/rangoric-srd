# Arrowmaster's Bracers

This simple pair of leather shooting bracers are designed to protect the wearer's wrist from being snapped by bowstrings. They appear similar to a set of _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Bracers of Archery, Lesser|bracers of archery]]_, but a wearer notices the difference as soon as she dons them.

The wearer of these bracers gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] 5/magic against ranged weapons, as well as a +1 deflection bonus against ranged attacks. In addition, once per day, as a swift action, the wearer can grant herself a +20 insight bonus on her next ranged attack roll before the end of her next turn.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Arrows|protection from arrows]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_; Price 13,900 gp; Cost 6,950 gp; Weight 1 lb.
