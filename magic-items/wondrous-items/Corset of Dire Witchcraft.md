# Corset of Dire Witchcraft

This slimming garment is fastened with laces, buckles, and buttons and reinforced with ribs of leather or bone. A _corset of dire witchcraft_ grants a +4 armor bonus to AC. If the wearer is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|witch]], each day when she communes with her familiar to prepare spells, she may enhance one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] she knows, increasing its caster level by +2 for 24 hours. This enhancement ends if the corset is removed or if she uses it to enhance a different hex.

Moderate abjuration; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|witch]]; Price 22,000 gp; Cost 11,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
