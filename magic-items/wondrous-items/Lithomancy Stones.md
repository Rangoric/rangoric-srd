# Lithomancy Stones

A set of lithomancy stones is 15 semiprecious stones of varied shape and color, standing for elements, emotions and spiritual presences of the Outer Planes. Once per day, the possessor can frame a question in her mind, cast the stones on the ground and attempt a reading with a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] check. The check takes 1 minute, and its DC depends on the question's time frame: 20 for the next 30 minutes, 30 for the next day, 40 for the next week. The GM rolls the check in secret. If it fails by 5 or more, the possessor gets a false or misleading reading; if it fails by less, no result. A success reveals information like that of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]_ (for the next 30 minutes) or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divination|divination]]_ (for longer), shown by how the stones lie.

Lithomancy stones can also be used as an additional focus for _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divination|divination]]_ or similar spells, adding 10% to the chance of correct information (to a maximum of 90%).
