# Insistent Doorknocker

This iron gargoyle face holds an iron ring in its mouth. Once per day, when the flat back of the gargoyle face is placed against a wall, floor, or ceiling, it fuses into place, creating a usable door and penetrating the space beyond up to 1 foot as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Passwall|passwall]]*. Speaking the command word again ends the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Passwall|passwall]]* effect, returns the surface to its previous doorless shape, and releases the doorknocker from the surface.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Passwall|passwall]]*; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
