# Headband of Deathless Devotion

This white headband, often painted or embroidered with calligraphy or a stylized pictogram such as a sunburst, helps focus the wearer's resolve and absolute determination to survive and succeed. The wearer gains a +2 morale bonus on stabilization checks and on saving throws against ongoing effects with recurring saves, such as diseases, poisons, or compulsions such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Person|dominate person]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]]*.

If the wearer is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Samurai|samurai]], he treats his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Samurai#^samurai-resolve|resolve]] ability as if he were two class levels higher.

Faint enchantment; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]*; Price 6,400 gp; Cost 3,200 gp; Weight 1 lb.
