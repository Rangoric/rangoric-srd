# Incense of Corporeality

Until it's lit, this bitter-smelling stick looks much like ordinary [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Incense|incense]]. In still air, its smoke fills a 40-foot-by-40-foot area to a height of 20 feet. In that area, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures take on some substance: they can no longer pass through objects, and take full damage from all attacks (even nonmagical ones). Their incorporeal touch attacks there are melee attacks, but they add their Charisma bonus on them. They still lack cohesion and mass: they keep their ability to fly and their immunity to combat maneuvers and critical hits, and they can pick up and handle objects as though their Strength scores equaled their Charisma scores, but they still aren't solid enough to wear equipment.

Moderate wind halves the area; strong wind disperses the smoke, making it useless while the wind lasts. A stick burns for 1 hour. If it's put out, the rest of the stick loses its power.

Moderate abjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghostbane Dirge|ghostbane dirge]]_; Price 1,400 gp; Cost 700 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
