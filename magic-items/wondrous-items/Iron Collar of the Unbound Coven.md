# Iron Collar of the Unbound Coven

This grotesque collar is useful to any witch who is eager to form a coven, but would rather avoid associating with one or more evil hags. The collar is a crude band of iron from which hang the fist-sized shrunken heads of three [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hags/Hag|hags]] (an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hags/Annis|annis hag]], a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hags/Green Hag|green hag]], and a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hags/Sea Hag|sea hag]]). The heads remain semi-animate—they occasionally twitch, blink, coo softly, or whisper curses at the wearer, each other, or anyone else nearby. A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|witch]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Coven (Hex)|coven]] hex who wears the collar gains access to the following powers.

**Champion of the Unbound Coven**: If the wearer joins a coven, the coven can ignore the requirement to have at least one hag as a member. Each of the coven members must still have the coven hex, and one of the members must be at least a 9th-level witch.

**Dreadful Gaze**: Three times per day as a swift action, the wearer can command the hags' heads to lock their terrible gazes on one humanoid within 30 feet. The target of this [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Gaze Attacks|gaze attack]] is attacked against its Will at +5, and on a hit becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] with terror (as the effect of the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]]_ spell). This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

**Hag Form**: Once per day as a standard action, the wearer can assume the form of a powerfully built Medium crone that resembles the horrific union of an annis hag, a green hag, and a sea hag. This metamorphosis is identical to the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Transformation|transformation]]_ spell, including the violent change to the wearer's mindset and the loss of spellcasting ability.
