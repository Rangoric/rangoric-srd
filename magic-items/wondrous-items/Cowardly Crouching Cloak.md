# Cowardly Crouching Cloak

This nondescript gray cloak has a hood that can fit over the wearer's entire face. The wearer can, as a move action, cover his face and drop to a huddling crouch. Any creature seeking to directly attack him while he does this is attacked against its Will at +1 and can do so only on a miss (as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sanctuary|sanctuary]]_ spell). The wearer cannot see or take any actions while crouching. This makes him effectively [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blind]], with all the normal penalties, but not [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]]. The wearer can end his crouch simply by standing up or moving.

Faint abjuration; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sanctuary|sanctuary]]_; Price 1,800 gp; Cost 900 gp; Weight 4 lbs.
