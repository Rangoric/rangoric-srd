# Circlet of Dreams

These ornate headpieces provide to the wearer additional spells related to sleep, dreaming, or nightmares. Additionally, wearing one of these circlets also provides a +2 luck bonus to AC and saving throws. These circlets are hard to come by, though they occasionally are available from fey craftsmen. A new circlet comes with 50 charges, which can be spent on the following abilities:

- *snooze* (DC 14) (3/day, 0 charges)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]* (heightened to 5th level, DC 17) (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dreamspeak|dreamspeak]]* (heightened to 5th level, DC 17) (1 charge)
- *sleepwalking* (heightened to 5th level, DC 17) (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fitful Sleep|fitful sleep]]* (heightened to 5th level, DC 17) (1 charge)
- *nap* (DC 18) (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eon of Sleep|eon of sleep]]* (DC 19) (2 charges)
- *sleepless curse* (DC 19) (2 charges)
- *improved nap* (DC 19) (3 charges)
- *recurring nightmare* (DC 20) (3 charges)

CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *snooze*, heightened *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]*, heightened *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dreamspeak|dreamspeak]]*, heightened *sleepwalking*, heightened *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fitful Sleep|fitful sleep]]*, *nap*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eon of Sleep|eon of sleep]]*, *sleepless curse*, *improved nap*, *recurring nightmare*, 1 lb. of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Faercana#^magic-sand|magic sand]]; Price 200,000 gp; Cost 100,000 gp + 8,000 XP; Weight 3 lb.
