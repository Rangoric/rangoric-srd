# Circlet of Dreams

These ornate headpieces provide to the wearer additional spells related to sleep, dreaming, or nightmares. Additionally, wearing one of these circlets also provides a +2 luck bonus to AC and defenses. These circlets are hard to come by, though they occasionally are available from fey craftsmen. A new circlet comes with 50 charges, which can be spent on the following abilities:

- _snooze_ (+4) (3/day, 0 charges)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]_ (heightened to 5th level, +7) (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dreamspeak|dreamspeak]]_ (heightened to 5th level, +7) (1 charge)
- _sleepwalking_ (heightened to 5th level, +7) (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fitful Sleep|fitful sleep]]_ (heightened to 5th level, +7) (1 charge)
- _nap_ (+8) (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eon of Sleep|eon of sleep]]_ (+9) (2 charges)
- _sleepless curse_ (+9) (2 charges)
- _improved nap_ (+9) (3 charges)
- _recurring nightmare_ (+10) (3 charges)
