# Bottled Scream

Wax and resin seal this smoked glass bottle. When the bottle is opened or shattered, a shrill scream erupts in a 20-foot-radius burst. Any [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performances]] with audible components immediately end. Any spellcaster concentrating on or casting a spell must succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] (DC 15 + spell level) or lose the spell. In addition, the scream dispels any _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ spells that overlap its area. The bottle can be thrown as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]] or opened by hand. If the bottle is opened, its burst is centered on the opener's space.

Moderate evocation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_; Price 900 gp; Cost 450 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
