# Moon Circlet

This fine silver circlet typically has a carved moonstone inset on the front or a crescent moon design. The wearer of a _moon circlet_ gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] out to 60 feet (if she does not already possess it). Its other effects only benefit a wearer capable of casting arcane spells.

During the waxing moon, the wearer's caster level is considered one higher for purposes of caster level checks and spell effects; during the three nights of the full moon, this benefit increases to two levels higher than normal. However, this additional power comes with a price. During the waning moon, the wearer's caster level is considered one lower for purposes of caster level checks and spell effects, and during the three nights of the new moon, this penalty increases to two levels lower.

Removing a _moon circlet_ imposes 2 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative levels]] on the wearer until the next full moon (or until the _circlet_ is worn again, whichever comes first). These negative levels cannot be restored by any means short of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_.

Strong evocation; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkvision|darkvision]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp.
