# Void Pennant

This long tapering pennant resembles a stylized serpentine figure wearing a shimmering crown. The figure's claws function as clasps, attaching it to a lance, polearm, frame, or staff. The pennant has no effect when not mounted properly, or when activated by a lawfully aligned creature.

Planting the pennant in the ground generates an area of twisted space around the flag out to a range of 30 feet. Any creature that teleports into or out of this area is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round (a DC 18 Fortitude save reduces this penalty to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]]). Any creature may end the effect by uprooting the pennant as a standard action. The pennant functions for 10 rounds per day. These rounds do not need to be consecutive.

Moderate abjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]_; Price 14,000 gp; Cost 7,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
