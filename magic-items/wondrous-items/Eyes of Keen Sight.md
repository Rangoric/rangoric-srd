# Eyes of Keen Sight

These clear crystals fit over the wearer's eyes. They grant the wearer a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, and the wearer gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]. If the wearer has low-light vision already, these lenses double the distance the wearer can see with this vision.

Faint transmutation; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Senses|keen senses]]*; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp.
