# Dryad Sandals

These sandals are woven from grass, lengths of pliant vine, living leaves, and supple bark. In forest terrain, the wearer leaves no tracks or scent and gains a +2 competence bonus on Stealth checks. The wearer can move through natural undergrowth without impediment or harm as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]] using [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]], and once per day she may step inside a tree and remain there for up to 9 hours, or teleport to a similar tree within range, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tree Stride|tree stride]]_ but allowing only a single teleport.

Moderate conjuration and transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tree Stride|tree stride]]_, creator must be a druid; Price 24,000 gp; Cost 12,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
