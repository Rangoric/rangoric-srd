# Cape of Effulgent Escape

This shining white cloak is trimmed with golden sunbursts and continuously radiates light (as per the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_ spell, but only in a 10-foot radius). Twice per day, the wearer can command the cape to erupt in a burst of light, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinding]] all creatures other than the wearer within 20 feet for 1d4 rounds (+4 vs Reflex). After triggering this burst, the wearer becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] (as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_ spell, with a duration of 1 minute) and the cape's light is extinguished as long as the wearer is invisible.

Faint evocation and illusion; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_; Price 14,000 gp; Cost 7,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
