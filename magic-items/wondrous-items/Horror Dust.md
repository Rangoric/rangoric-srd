# Horror Dust

*Horror dust* is the compressed stuff of nightmares. It is contained in a glass vial that can be thrown as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]] with a range increment of 10 feet. It shatters on impact, creating a cloud of hallucinogenic magic in a 20-foot radius that causes victims to suffer terrible visions and hear phantom screams. Targets in this area must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d6 rounds. If a creature is directly struck with the *horror dust* and fails its save, it is also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1 round. Goblinoids actually enjoy the visions created by *horror dust*, and are immune to its fear effect.

Faint necromancy; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]*; Price 600 gp; Cost 300 gp.
