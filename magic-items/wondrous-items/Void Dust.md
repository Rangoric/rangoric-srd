# Void Dust

Infused with the essence of destruction, a pinch of void dust unmakes force effects. If sprinkled on or thrown at a force effect, it acts as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*, unless the force effect is immune to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*, in which case it acts like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]*. *Void dust* can be sprinkled on an adjacent force effect or thrown up to 10 feet. No attack roll is required. A single pinch affects only a single force effect. A found container holds three pinches.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]*; Price 4,500 gp; Cost 2,250 gp.
