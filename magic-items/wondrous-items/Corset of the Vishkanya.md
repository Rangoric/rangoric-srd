# Corset of the Vishkanya

This black corset fits neatly beneath armor. As a free action, the wearer can compress herself to fit through tight spaces as though affected by a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Squeeze|squeeze]]* spell for up to 10 rounds per day. While using the corset's magic, she gains a +5 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] checks.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Squeeze|squeeze]]*; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
