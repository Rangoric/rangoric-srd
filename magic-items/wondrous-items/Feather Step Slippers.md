# Feather Step Slippers

Once per day for 10 minutes, these fine silken slippers allow their wearer to ignore the adverse movement effects of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement#Terrain and Obstacles|difficult terrain]] as if subject to the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Step|feather step]]* spell, including granting the ability to take 5-foot steps in difficult terrain.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Step|feather step]]*; Price 2,000 gp; Cost 1,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
