# Mask of the Trickster

No sane mage admits to creating one of these garish masks, and some sages hold that a deity of chaos or trickery is actually behind them. Whatever their source, they fuse with the wearer’s features, granting him a +5 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks, but imposing a –5 luck penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.

Any lawful creature that wears a *trickster’s mask* suffers a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative level]]. This negative level never results in actual level loss, but it cannot be removed by any means, including *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]* spells, as long as the mask is worn.

Moderate enchantment [mind-affecting]; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]*; Price 8,000 gp.
