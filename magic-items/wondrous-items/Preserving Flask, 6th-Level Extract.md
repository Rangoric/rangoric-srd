# Preserving Flask, 6th-Level Extract

This small, durable flask is designed to preserve alchemical extracts indefinitely. An extract stored in a *preserving flask* remains potent until it is consumed or otherwise removed from the flask. Preserved extracts count against an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-alchemy|extracts]] per day on the day they are prepared, but not on subsequent days. Each flask is capable of preserving an extract of a particular level. An extract stored in the flask retains duration, caster level, and other properties it had when it was created. The flask works on extracts and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Infusion (Discovery)|infusions]], but not [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|mutagens]].

Strong abjuration; CL 17th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must be able to create [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-alchemy|alchemical extracts]] of the extract level to be stored; Price 36,000 gp; Cost 18,000 gp.
