# Flaming Brand Glove, Lesser

This single glove is made of dye-stained red leather. Tooled designs of flames, fire elementals and forest fires cover the back of the glove. Upon command, the wearer of the Glove can summon or dismiss a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Blade|flame blade]]*, per the spell, in the gloved hand. With a successful touch attack, the wearer deals 1d8+1 fire damage with this flaming weapon. The blade can only be used for ten minutes each day. If use beyond this limit is attempted, the glove is consumed by its own fiery power. In addition to the weapon power, the wearer gains fire resistance 5.

CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Blade|flame blade]]*; Price 5,700 gp.
