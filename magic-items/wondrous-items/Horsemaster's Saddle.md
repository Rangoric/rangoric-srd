# Horsemaster's Saddle

This ornate military saddle, tooled with an elaborate equine motif, grants the mount a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks and the mount's rider a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks to ride. In addition, the mount gains the benefits of any [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^teamwork-feats|teamwork feats]] possessed by the rider.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mount|mount]]_, creator must have 5 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]]; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
