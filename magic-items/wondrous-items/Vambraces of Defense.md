# Vambraces of Defense

These polished steel vambraces grant a +1 deflection bonus to AC. Once per day, as an immediate action, if the wearer has at least one hand free, when he would normally be hit with an attack from a ranged weapon, he may deflect it with the bracers, taking no damage from it, as if he had the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deflect Arrows|Deflect Arrows]] feat.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
