# Amulet of Epic Natural Armor +7

This amulet toughens the wearer’s body and flesh, giving him or her an enhancement bonus to his or her natural armor of +6 or higher, depending on the type of amulet.  
Caster Level: 20th; Prerequisites: Craft Wondrous Item, Craft Epic Wondrous Item, barkskin, creator’s caster level must be three times the amulet’s bonus; Market Price: 980,000 gp; Weight: -.
