# Breastplate of Blinding

This black multi-faceted [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Gemstone Mail|gemstone]] breastplate gives the wearer a +1 armor bonus. In addition, twice per day as a free action, the wearer can call forth a brilliant flash of light. Anyone in the 120 degrees directly in front of the wearer and within 30 feet is attacked against its Reflex at +4, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1d4 rounds. This can be worn with other forms of armor and shield that do not cover the torso or over chain mail or padded armor. This item can be used in conjunction with a breastplate or as a piece in a suit of plate armor.

Faint evocation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_; Price 600 gp.
