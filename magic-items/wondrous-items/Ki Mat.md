# Ki Mat

This humble pad of woven river reeds allows its owner to recover his mental fortitude and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki* pool]] by practicing breathing exercises and other forms of meditation. Each hour its owner spends sitting on the mat without taking any other action, he can attempt to center himself by making a Wisdom check with a DC of 10 plus the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]]'s current number of _ki_ points (note that ability checks, like skill checks, do not automatically succeed on a 20). If the check is successful, the monk regains 1 _ki_ point.

Moderate conjuration (healing); CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]]; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
