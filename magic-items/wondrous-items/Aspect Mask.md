# Aspect Mask

This leather mask is vaguely animalistic, and is inlaid with various primitive designs. Three times per day on command, the wearer gains the benefit of a single aspect of an animal attuned to the mask, as if by the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter|hunter]]’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]] class feature. Each mask is attuned to one type of animal from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/animal-foci/Animal Foci|animal focus]] list, chosen when the mask is created.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must have [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] class feature; Price 6,500 gp; Weight 2 lb.
