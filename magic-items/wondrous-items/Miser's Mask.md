# Miser's Mask

This wooden mask depicts the sunken face of a miser with an upturned nose. The mask grants the wearer the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]] ability, but she can use it only to sense coins and gems. Each type and quality of gem has its own unique aura to the wearer, granting her a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] checks made to ascertain the value of gems.

Faint divination; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bloodhound|bloodhound]]_, the creator must have 4 ranks in Appraise; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
