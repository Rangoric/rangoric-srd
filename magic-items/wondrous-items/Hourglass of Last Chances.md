# Hourglass of Last Chances

This tiny hourglass is filled with blood-red sand. The sand flows only one way and turning it to start the flow activates its magic. It grants a target creature within 30 feet the following benefits for a limited time—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_ (as the spell), and suspends any and all of the following conditions: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|diseased]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], feebleminded, insanity, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poisoned]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]], and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]]. These effects are suspended rather than dispelled; they resume when the hourglass is deactivated or expended and this suspended time does not count toward the duration of the suppressed effect.

Ending the hourglass's effects is a swift action. The hourglass can be used for up to 5 rounds in total before its magic is consumed. These rounds need not be consecutive.

Moderate conjuration and transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp.
