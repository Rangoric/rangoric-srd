# Kitsune Star Gem, 1st-Level Spell

This magical jewel glows like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*, shedding light as a candle. It acts as *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Pearl of Power, 1st-Level Spell|pearl of power]]*, except it only allows a [[rangoric-srd/races/Kitsune|kitsune]] to recover a spent use of a spell-like ability of the appropriate spell level.

Faint transmutation; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/races/Kitsune|kitsune]]; Price 750 gp; Cost 375 gp.
