# Helm of the Valkyrie

This shining helm glints with the multicolored hues of the aurora, and features wings carved of fine whalebone that sweep back from the temples. While it is in good shape, it shows signs of having seen battle. Once per day the wearer can summon a giant spectral wolf. Treat this as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantom Steed|phantom steed]]* cast by a 14th-level caster, but with an AC of 24 (–1 size, +8 natural armor, +7 Dexterity) and 100 hit points. The wolf can persist for up to 24 hours, as long as the creature that summoned it is still wearing the *helm of the valkyrie*. Even though it takes the form of a fierce predator and is more powerful than other *phantom steeds*, the mount is nothing more than mist and can't attack.

Strong conjuration; CL 14th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantom Steed|phantom steed]]*; Price 16,000 gp; Cost 8,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
