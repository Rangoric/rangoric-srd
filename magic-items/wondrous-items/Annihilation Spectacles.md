# Annihilation Spectacles

Creatures viewed through these darkly tinted glasses sometimes seem to flicker briefly in and out of existence. A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Transmutation School|transmuter]] can use these spectacles to spontaneously convert any prepared wizard transmutation spell into any other wizard transmutation spell he knows; the desired spell must be of the same level or lower than the prepared spell. Once per day, he may use the spectacles to spontaneously cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_ in place of one of his prepared transmutation wizard spells of 6th level or higher, even if he doesn't know that spell.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_, creator must be a transmuter; Price 25,000 gp; Cost 12,500 gp; Weight 1/4 lb.
