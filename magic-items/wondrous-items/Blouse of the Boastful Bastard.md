# Blouse of the Boastful Bastard

This stylish, purple silk shirt trimmed with designs embroidered in golden thread brings out reckless behavior in even the most conservative swashbucklers. If the wearer has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] class feature, when she takes damage from a successful critical hit, she regains 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] point. This shirt has no effect on someone who is not a panache user.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guidance|guidance]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp.
