# Talisman of Protection from Flames, Greater

A talisman works as described under [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#^talismans|Talismans]] in the rules for magic items on the body. A greater talisman works as a lesser talisman of the same type, but lasts far longer: it can give its benefit once per day.

The first time the wearer takes 5 or more points of fire damage in one round, he is automatically affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]_, which protects him from further fire damage. Talismans that protect against other energy types are much less common.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]_; Price 12,500 gp; Cost 6,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
