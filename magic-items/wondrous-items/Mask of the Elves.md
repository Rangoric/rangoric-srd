# Mask of the Elves

Elven mages typically craft their masks in order to assist them in dealing with their natural environments. Wearers of elven masks gain a +6 insight bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks. In addition, they gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] of 60 feet, and can _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_ at all times.

This is one of the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Racial Masks|racial masks]].

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkvision|darkvision]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_; Price 43,600 gp; Cost 21,800 gp + 1,744 XP.
