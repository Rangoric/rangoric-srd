# See Invisibility Sight

This sight can be attached to a single two-handed firearm. When this is done, the sight becomes part of the weapon, but can be removed from that weapon with a full-round action. A firearm wielder using a firearm that has this sight can choose to spend a full-round action to either locate an [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] creature within line of sight or make a single shot that ignores the invisibility of a creature that she knows is in the area.

Moderate divination; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]*; Price 12,000 gp; Weight 1 lb..
