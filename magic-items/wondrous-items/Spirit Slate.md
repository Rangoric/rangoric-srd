# Spirit Slate

This book-sized wooden frame holds a thin slab of black slate. Placing a fragment of a dead creature's bone on the slate makes the fragment animate and scribble the answers to four questions on its surface:

- Who were you? (The name the creature was most commonly known by.)
- What were you? (Its gender, race and profession or role.)
- How did you die? (A brief outline of the events that caused its death, to its best knowledge.)
- When did you die? (As exactly as it can place its death.)

The answers always appear in a language the user can read, even if the creature spoke no language. The slate can be used up to three times per day.

Faint divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blood Biography|blood biography]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 2 lb.
