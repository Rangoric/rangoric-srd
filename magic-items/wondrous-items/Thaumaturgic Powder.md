# Thaumaturgic Powder

This silver powder usually comes in a fist-sized leather bag. Poured on the ground, it animates over 1 round, scribbling silver script and symbols in a circle of 20-foot radius. The circle is the perfectly drawn special diagram of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Chaos|magic circle against chaos]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|evil]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Good|good]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Law|law]]*, and bars extradimensional movement as if *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]* had been attached to it. The circle lasts 24 hours, and a bag can be used only once.

An extraplanar creature of the matching alignment called or summoned into the circle can try once during its duration to break through it, as if breaking free from *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding, Lesser|lesser planar binding]]*, with its spell resistance or a DC 26 Charisma check.
