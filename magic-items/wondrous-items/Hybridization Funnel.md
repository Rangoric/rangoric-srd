# Hybridization Funnel

This two-spouted funnel is used to safely mix two alchemical splash weapons into a single flask. Using the vial requires 10 minutes and a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check; a [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]] has a +5 bonus on this check. Failing the check means both splash weapons are destroyed. Success means the two materials are safely mixed into one vial that is the same volume as a normal splash weapon vial; when thrown as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]], the mixture has the effects of both component substances and creatures are affected as if hit by both. The mixture becomes inert after 24 hours. Only liquids may be mixed with the funnel. The bearer can use it to mix an alchemical splash weapon with either [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Holy Water|holy water]] or unholy water, but the Craft (alchemy) DC increases to 30; half-elves still get the +5 bonus on this check. The funnel does not work on potions, elixirs, extracts, or other materials. Mixing a substance with a similar or identical substance (such as alchemist's fire with alchemist's fire) has no effect. A mixture cannot be combined with another mixture.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fabricate|fabricate]]_ or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Combine Extracts (Discovery)|combine extracts]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]]; Price 200 gp; Cost 100 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
