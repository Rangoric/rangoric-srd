# Headband of Mental Superiority

This ornate headband is decorated with numerous clusters of small gemstones. The headband grants the wearer an enhancement bonus to all mental ability scores (Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma) of +2, +4, or +6. Treat this as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the headband is worn. The headband also grants skill ranks as a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Headband of Vast Intelligence|headband of vast intelligence]]*.

Strong transmutation; CL 16th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fox’s Cunning|fox's cunning]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom|owl's wisdom]]*; Price 16,000 gp (+2), 64,000 gp (+4), 144,000 gp (+6); Cost 8,000 gp (+2), 32,000 gp (+4), 72,000 gp (+6); Weight 1 lb.
