# Necromancer's Athame

This pallid length of sharpened thighbone is carved into the shape of a dagger, but with tiny holes bored into it at equal intervals, almost like a flute. A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Necromancy School|necromancer]] who selects this athame as his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-arcane-bond|bonded object]] can use it to spontaneously convert any prepared wizard necromancy spell into any other wizard necromancy spell he knows; the desired spell must be of the same level or lower than the prepared spell.

Strong necromancy; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead|create undead]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Necromancy School|necromancer]]; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
