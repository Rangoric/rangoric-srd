# Jingasa of the Fortunate Soldier

This conical iron jingasa, or war hat, grants the wearer a +1 deflection bonus to AC. When struck by a critical hit or sneak attack, the wearer can spend an immediate action to negate the critical hit or sneak attack (similar to the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]_ armor special ability, but without requiring a roll). The damage is instead rolled normally. This ability functions only once, though the jingasa continues to grant its deflection bonus even after the other ability is expended.

Strong divination; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Moment of Prescience|moment of prescience]]_; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
