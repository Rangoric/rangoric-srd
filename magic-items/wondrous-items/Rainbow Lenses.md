# Rainbow Lenses

These lenses interfere with the wearer's ability to see color, and everything appears in shades of black and white, as if the wearer were using [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]]. Once per day the wearer can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rainbow Pattern|rainbow pattern]]_.

Strong enchantment; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hypnotic Pattern|hypnotic pattern]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rainbow Pattern|rainbow pattern]]_; Price 21,000 gp; Cost 10,500 gp.
