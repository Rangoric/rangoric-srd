# Agile Alpenstock

This gnarled wooden walking stick aids a creature's travel, granting a +5 foot enhancement bonus to speed and a +2 enhancement bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attempts and on saving throws to avoid being tripped. These bonuses apply as long as the *agile alpenstock* is held in hand. It can be wielded in combat as a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Quarterstaff|quarterstaff]].

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]*; Price 2,000 gp; Cost 1,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
