# Falcon Spaulders

These ornate armored shoulder pieces have an enhancement bonus of +1. _Falcon spaulders_ grant their wearer the ability to sprout a pair of beautiful, angelic wings once per day, which allow him to fly for a number of hours equal to his Wisdom modifier, with a minimum of one hour. Flying in this manner gives the wearer a fly speed of 70 feet, with average maneuverability.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]_; Price 12,175 gp; Cost 6,086 gp + 487 XP.
