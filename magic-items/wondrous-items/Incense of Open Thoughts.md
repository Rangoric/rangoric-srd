# Incense of Open Thoughts

While a block of this sweet-scented [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Incense|incense]] burns, creatures breathing its fumes become unusually open to thought. In still air, its smoke fills a 40-foot-by-40-foot area to a height of 20 feet. Creatures in that area with an Intelligence of 3 or higher can communicate telepathically with each other, without needing a shared language. The openness also gives a +5 insight bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks against creatures in the area, and imposes a –5 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks against them and a –2 penalty on their saving throws against mind-affecting effects.

Moderate wind halves the area; strong wind disperses the smoke, making it useless while the wind lasts. A block burns for 1 hour. It can be put out and relit, but each use takes at least 10 minutes of its remaining time.

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telepathic Bond|telepathic bond]]_; Price 2,800 gp; Cost 1,400 gp; Weight 1 lb.
