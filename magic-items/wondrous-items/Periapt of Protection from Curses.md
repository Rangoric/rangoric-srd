# Periapt of Protection from Curses

This amethyst stone strung on a coarse iron chain protects the wearer from curses. Three times per day, whenever a curse attacks the wearer’s defenses (such as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|witch]]'s hex, or a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]_ spell), he may make it reroll its attack as an immediate action, keeping the result better for him. The wearer must decide to use this ability before the result of the attack is revealed. He must take the second roll, even if it is worse.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp.
