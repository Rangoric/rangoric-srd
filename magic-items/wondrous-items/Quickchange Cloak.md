# Quickchange Cloak

One side of this reversible gray hooded cape is exquisitely embroidered and richly decorated, while the other appears shabby and worn. On command, by passing the edge of the cloak over her face, the wearer may remove a mundane disguise (one applied with the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] skill) and store it in the cloak. This does not change the cloak's appearance in any way. Once a disguise is stored in the cloak, it can be reapplied to the wearer who placed it within the cloak on command, using the original result of the Imitation check. The cloak can store up to three disguises at once, and any wearer of the cloak can discard any number of disguises within the cloak as a free action.

Faint transmutation; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, creator must have at least 5 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]]; Price 1,500 gp; Cost 750 gp; Weight 1 lb.
