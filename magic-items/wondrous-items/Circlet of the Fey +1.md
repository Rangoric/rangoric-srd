# Circlet of the Fey +1

This thin band of interwoven flowers, grass, and silver thread provides the wearer with the resistances of the fey, namely a +1 resistance bonus on defenses against enchantments and illusions. The finest and most powerful versions of these circlets (+5) are crafted for members of the fey courts, but other varying potencies are available (+2, +3, and +4) from fey craftsmen.
