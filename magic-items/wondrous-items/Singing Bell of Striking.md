# Singing Bell of Striking

This simple bowl-shaped bell made of either cold iron or silver is played by rubbing a leather mallet along its rim, creating a ringing sound. By playing the bell while meditating or resting to regain [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki*]], the bearer may attune his *ki* points to the instrument. Once attuned, the bearer's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-strike|*ki* strikes]] count as cold iron (if the bell is cold iron) or silver (if the bell is silver) instead of magic for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]]. This lasts until the next time the bearer rests or meditates to regain *ki* points. A creature can only be attuned to one singing bell at a time, and a singing bell can only be attuned to one person at a time. Only creatures whose *ki* strikes normally act as magic weapons can use a *singing bell of striking*.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Versatile Weapon|versatile weapon]]*; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
