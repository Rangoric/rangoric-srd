# Rope of Knots

This rope is 100 feet long and can perform all the functions of a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Rope of Climbing|rope of climbing]]*. Furthermore, upon command the *rope of knots* snakes out and knots itself into a rope structure. The rope can tie itself into any structure which can be created out of 100 feet of rope. Possible structures include (but are not limited to) a sturdy rope bridge up to 25 feet long, a 10-foot-square net or hammock, or a 40-foot-tall rope ladder. The rope takes up to 10 rounds to create a complex structure or half that time to return itself to a simple coil. The rope has hardness 1 and 20 hit points. It repairs damage to itself at a rate of 2 hit points every 10 minutes.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope|animate rope]]*; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp.
