# Censer of Conjuring Air Elementals

This 6-inch-wide, perforated metallic vessel resembles a thurible found in a place of worship. The censer must be filled with incense to function. On command as a full-round action, the bearer may summon an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elementals/Air Elemental|air elemental]]. If the censer is filled with _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Incense of Meditation|incense of meditation]]_, it conjures a greater air elemental as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VII|summon monster VII]]_. If filled with any other incense, it conjures a Large air elemental as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]_. Conjuring an elemental consumes any normal incense in the censer, or one-fourth of a block of incense of meditation. The censer cannot summon another elemental until the previous one is dismissed, killed, or the duration of its summoning expires.

This is a lesser version of the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Censer of Controlling Air Elementals|censer of controlling air elementals]]_, which calls its elementals as stronger summoning spells and costs more.

Moderate conjuration; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VII|summon monster VII]]_; Price 90,000 gp; Cost 45,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
