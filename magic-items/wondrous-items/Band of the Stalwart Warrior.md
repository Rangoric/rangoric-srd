# Band of the Stalwart Warrior

This plain iron band protects its wearer from fear effects. Whenever the wearer suffers a fear effect, she is merely [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fear-shaken|shaken]] (even if the effect would normally make the target [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fear-frightened|frightened]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fear-panicked|panicked]]). Furthermore, the subject gains a +2 competence bonus on Will Defense against fear. If the wearer has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]] class feature, she is considered four levels higher when determining that class feature's effect.

Strong abjuration; CL 16th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_; Price 14,000 gp; Cost 7,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
