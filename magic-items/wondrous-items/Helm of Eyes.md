# Helm of Eyes

A *helm of eyes* is a full-faced iron helm that covers the eyes and upper bridge of the nose of the wearer, making him entirely dependent of the magic of the helm to see. The helm adds a +2 deflection bonus to the armor class of the wearer. The wearer also gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsight]] (range 60 feet), and an additional +10 insight bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks. The helm protects the wearer from being blinded or hampered by bright light or by spells such as *light, daylight*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]]*. Once per day the helm grants it wearer the power of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]*.

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkvision|darkvision]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]*; Price 161,000 gp; Cost 80,500 gp + 6,440 XP; Weight 2 lb.
