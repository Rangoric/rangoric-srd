# Slayer's Robe

This ragged, brown robe could be mistaken for a monk's cassock, but the devotees who wear it are dedicated only to the art of killing. This robe grants the wearer a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks to conceal a weapon, and a +1 resistance bonus on all saving throws. If worn by a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer|slayer]], the resistance bonus on saving throws increases to match the slayer's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied target]] bonus when he is facing a studied target.

On command, the robe's wearer is able to turn into a brown mist (as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_ spell) for up to 5 minutes per day. This duration doesn't need to be continuous, but must be used in 1-minute increments. While the wearer is in this form, the bonus on Stealth checks increases to +10. The wearer can end this effect as a standard action.

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guidance|guidance]]_; Price 15,000 gp; Cost 7,500 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
