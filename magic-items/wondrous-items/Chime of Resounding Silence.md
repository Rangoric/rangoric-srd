# Chime of Resounding Silence

This foot-long tube of gold absorbs nearby sound when struck, as if it were the target of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ spell. This ability can be used once per day. The chime vibrates silently for the next 5 rounds, increasing in strength as time passes. On the bearer's turn each round there is a cumulative 20% chance that the chime prematurely ends the magical silence and releases the pent-up sound as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_ spell as a free action, oriented in a random direction. The bearer may spend a standard action to strike the vibrating chime again, ending its _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ early and unleashing the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_ in a direction of his choosing. If dropped while vibrating, the chime automatically gains the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition and releases the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_ effect in a random direction. A broken _chime of resounding silence_ cannot be used until it is repaired. The chime has no effect if struck in an area of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ it did not create.

Moderate evocation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
