# Acrobat Slippers

These supple gray leather slippers enable the wearer to keep his balance in precarious positions. The wearer retains his Dexterity bonus to AC when climbing, running, or using the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] skill to move on narrow surfaces or uneven ground without falling. In addition, the wearer gains a +2 competence bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] maneuvers and to Reflex Defense against falling (including falling [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]).

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_; Price 3,000 gp; Cost 1,500 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
