# Pirate's Eye Patch

This black silk eye patch is adorned by a skull and crossbones worked in silver thread. The wearer of this patch gains a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks. In addition, once per day, the wearer of this eye patch can gain the effects of either _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of the Sea|touch of the sea]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_ on command (wearer's choice).

Faint transmutation; CL 2nd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of the Sea|touch of the sea]]_; Price 2,600 gp; Cost 1,300 gp.
