# Werewhistle

This whistle is shaped like a wolf's head and is crafted out of silver. Once per day the bearer may blow the whistle to cause one [[rangoric-srd/monsters/lycanthrope/Lycanthrope|lycanthrope]] within 30 feet with a +7 attack against its Will; on a hit it is forced back into its humanoid form for the next 24 hours.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Baleful Polymorph|baleful polymorph]]_; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp.
