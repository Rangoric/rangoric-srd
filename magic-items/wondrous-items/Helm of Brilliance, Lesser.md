# Helm of Brilliance, Lesser

This helm functions like a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Helm of Brilliance|helm of brilliance]]* but is lower-powered. Instead of the standard helm's allotment of gems, the lesser helm has 10 diamonds (*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]*, 10d6 damage, Reflex DC 20 half), 20 corals (*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flaming Sphere|flaming sphere]]*), 30 garnets (*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]*), and 40 agates (*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]*). It still glows near and harms undead, has the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]* weapon special ability, and provides fire resistance (though only 20 instead of 30).

Just like the standard helm, the wearer risks detonation if damaged by magical fire; diamonds become *fireballs* centered on the wearer, corals become *flaming spheres* in and adjacent to the wearer's space, garnets become *scorching rays* that fire randomly at creatures in range, and the agates and the helm itself are destroyed.
