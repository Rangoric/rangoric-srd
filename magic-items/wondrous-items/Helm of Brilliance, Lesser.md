# Helm of Brilliance, Lesser

This helm functions like a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Helm of Brilliance|helm of brilliance]]_ but is lower-powered. Instead of the standard helm's allotment of gems, the lesser helm has 10 diamonds (_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]_, 10d6 damage, Reflex DC 20 half), 20 corals (_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flaming Sphere|flaming sphere]]_), 30 garnets (_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]_), and 40 agates (_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_). It still glows near and harms undead, has the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]_ weapon special ability, and provides fire resistance (though only 20 instead of 30).

Just like the standard helm, the wearer risks detonation if damaged by magical fire; diamonds become _fireballs_ centered on the wearer, corals become _flaming spheres_ in and adjacent to the wearer's space, garnets become _scorching rays_ that fire randomly at creatures in range, and the agates and the helm itself are destroyed.
