# Horn of the Huntmaster

This elongated bull's horn curves sharply at the end, almost making it seem more like an enormous smoking pipe than a tool for the hunt. Once each day, a person with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-hunters-bond|hunter’s bond]] (companions) class feature may blow the horn as a standard action; this grants his full [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] bonus (instead of half) against a single target to all allies within 30 feet who can see and hear him. The effect lasts for 1 minute.

Moderate enchantment; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater|greater heroism]]*; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
