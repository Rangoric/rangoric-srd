# Amulet of Channeled Life

This amulet consists of a pale translucent gem in a dull gray metal setting etched with ancient runes. When worn by an individual with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Negative Energy Affinity|negative energy affinity]] racial trait, it alters the effects of positive energy on his body. When targeted by positive energy that heals living creatures, the wearer instead gains half the hit points the positive energy heals as temporary hit points. These temporary hit points go away after 10 minutes. This effect does not stack with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Life Channel|life channel]]_ (see below) or other effects that convert healing positive energy to temporary hit points.

This item does not actually transpose positive energy into negative energy, it just affects the way the wearer's body reacts to positive energy. Only a living creature can benefit from this amulet; it provides no benefit to undead.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Life Channel|life channel]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
