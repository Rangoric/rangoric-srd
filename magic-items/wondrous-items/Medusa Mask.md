# Medusa Mask

This intricate mask is made of gold-plated iron. Writhing snake tails radiate from a gemstone upon the brow, almost as if they were medusa-like hair. The mask grants a +4 bonus on all defenses against visual effects, including [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Gaze Attacks|gaze attacks]] and sight-based illusions. Once per day as a standard action, the wearer can cause the central gemstone to glow with pale green light, at which point she may target any one creature within 30 feet. The targeted creature is attacked against its Fortitude at +5, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] for 1 minute, as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]_.

Moderate transmutation; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
