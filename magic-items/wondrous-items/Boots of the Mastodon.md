# Boots of the Mastodon

These massive boots are made from mastodon hide. The boots are so large and cumbersome that their wearer takes a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks, but gains a +2 enhancement bonus on overrun attempts and a +2 dodge bonus to AC while attempting an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]] combat maneuver. In addition, once per day the wearer can make a trample attack. Regardless of his actual size, the creature wearing the boots can [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Trample|trample]] creatures of size Large or smaller in his path. The trample attack deals 2d8+18 points of damage (Reflex DC 29 halves).

Moderate transmutation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]_; Price 10,500 gp; Cost 5,250 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
