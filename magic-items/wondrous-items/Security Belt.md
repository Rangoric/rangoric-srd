# Security Belt

A *security belt* increases the DC of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made against the wearer and checks to locate items or weapons hidden on the wearer's person by 5. The wearer also gets a +5 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] maneuver. Once per day on command, the wearer can touch a single item of up to 10 cubic feet and shrink it into a tiny cloth duplicate as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shrink Item|shrink item]]* spell. With a move action, the wearer can draw one of these items from one of the belt's pockets and return it to normal size. It is difficult to detect a *security belt* and any items in the pockets as magic. They are affected as though a constant *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]* spell were making them appear nonmagical.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shrink Item|shrink item]]*; Price 12,500 gp; Cost 6,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
