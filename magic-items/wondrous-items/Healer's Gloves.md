# Healer's Gloves

These pure-white leather gloves bear symbols of healing and faith on the back of the hands. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must have 5 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]]; Price 2,500 gp; Cost 1,250 gp.
