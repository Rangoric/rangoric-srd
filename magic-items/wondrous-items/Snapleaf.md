# Snapleaf

This crystalline carving looks like a hand-sized tree leaf. It is usually worn around the neck on a strap so it is within easy reach, but can be affixed to a belt or clothing just like a badge. While falling, the bearer can snap and destroy a *snapleaf* as an immediate action to gain the benefits of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*. The duration of the two effects work independently; ending one early does not affect the other. The item cannot be activated to provide just one of these two effects; they are always activated simultaneously.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*; Price 750 gp; Cost 375 gp.
