# Jellyfish Cape

This billowing white cape renders the wearer almost invisible the moment she is submerged in salt water, granting her concealment (20% miss chance) so long as she remains entirely submerged. The wearer can breathe underwater and gains a swim speed of 20 ft. While the wearer is submerged, the cape surrounds her with tiny stinging tentacles. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to the wearer takes 1 point of electricity damage and is attacked against its Fortitude at +6, and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 round. The sickened effect is a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]] effect.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape II|beast shape II]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_; Price 19,200 gp; Cost 9,600 gp; Weight 1 lb.
