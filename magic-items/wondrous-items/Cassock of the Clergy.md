# Cassock of the Clergy

The cut and color of this solemn vestment varies from religion to religion, but its properties are the same. The wearer may command the _cassock of the clergy_ to display the holy symbol of her deity or hide this symbol. At the most basic level, the garment functions as a divine focus. Additionally, the wearer gains a +3 competence bonus on Charisma-based checks to influence creatures that match her alignment or that of her patron deity. Also, the wearer may use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sanctuary|sanctuary]]_ (+1 vs Will) each once per day, and may prepare one additional orison each day. If the wearer is a spontaneous divine caster, once per day she may spend 1 hour in prayer to gain an orison of her choice as a spell known for the next 24 hours.

Moderate abjuration; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sanctuary|sanctuary]]_, creator must be a divine spellcaster; Price 4,600 gp; Cost 2,300 gp; Weight 1 lb.
