# Headband of Mental Superiority +2

This ornate headband is decorated with numerous clusters of small gemstones. The headband grants the wearer an enhancement bonus to all mental ability scores (Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma) of +2, +4, or +6. Treat this as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the headband is worn. The headband also grants skill ranks as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Headband of Vast Intelligence +2|headband of vast intelligence]]_.

Strong transmutation; CL 16th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fox’s Cunning|fox's cunning]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom|owl's wisdom]]_; Price 16,000 gp; Cost 8,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
