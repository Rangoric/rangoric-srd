# Dust of Darkness

This shimmering black dust causes one creature to be coated in a layer of darkness for 1 minute. In normal light, the creature gains [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] (20% miss chance). In dim light, the creature gains [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|total concealment]] (50% miss chance). It has no effect in areas of bright light or darkness (though in bright light, a viewer can make a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check to notice the target is slightly shadowed). Creatures with [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] see through the dust's darkness as if it were normal darkness. The dust dispels and counters the outlining effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glitterdust|glitterdust]]_, but doesn't affect the spell's blinding effects. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glitterdust|Glitterdust]]_ dispels and counters the effects of the _dust of darkness_.

Faint evocation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_; Price 600 gp; Cost 300 gp.
