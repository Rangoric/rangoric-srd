# Highwayman's Cape

This reversible multicolored cape marks the wearer's flamboyant audacity when turned with its bright colors outward, granting a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks. Reversible as a move action, the dappled camouflage of its other side grants a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. Regardless of the cape's current facing, the wearer can spend an immediate action at will to roll an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] check against being [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]], bound, or restrained as if the cape targeted her with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Liberating Command|liberating command]]*.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Liberating Command|liberating command]]*; Price 32,500 gp; Cost 16,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
