# Candle of Spirit Protection

Made of collected ectoplasm and wax, this candle wards off creatures of the spirit world. It must be placed where it's to work. When it's lit, fine lines of smoke coil out into a circle of 10-foot radius around it. Creatures using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection|astral projection]]_, ethereal creatures, haunts, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit|mediums channeling a spirit]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-phantom|phantoms]] (and, at the GM's discretion, other spirits or creatures made of ectoplasm) can't enter the area, as though affected by an _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antilife Shell|antilife shell]]_ that targets only them.

The candle can't be moved once it's lit; trying to move it ends the effect. Otherwise a lit candle burns for 1 hour. Either way, it's used up after one use.

Moderate necromancy; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antilife Shell|antilife shell]]_; Price 1,500 gp; Cost 750 gp; Weight 1 lb.
