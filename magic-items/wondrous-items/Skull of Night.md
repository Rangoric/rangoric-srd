# Skull of Night

This is a human skull, inlaid with copper with small rubies set into the metal. This [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Necromantic Foci|necromantic focus]] provides a bonus of +6 to two shadetouched ability checks per day, as if the wielder were attuned to a source of necromantic energy ([[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#The Gray Road|the Gray Road]]).

This focus also allows a character with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shadecall|Shadecall]] feat to create two undead creatures with one check, and to control twice the number of undead he would ordinarily be able to. This expanded control only lasts so long as he retains physical possession of the focus.

CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Necromantic Focus|Craft Necromantic Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shadecall|Shadecall]], access to a source of necromantic energy providing a bonus of at least +12; Price 46,000 gp.
