# Boots of the Enduring March

These travel-stained boots of stout brown leather enable the wearer to traverse great distances without tiring. When making a forced march, for each hour of marching beyond 8 hours, the wearer needs only to make a Constitution check (DC 10, +1 per extra hour) instead of the normal check. Furthermore, if the wearer fails the check, she only takes 1d4 points of nonlethal damage and does not become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] the first time she takes nonlethal damage in this way.

Faint conjuration; CL 2nd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]]; Price 1,500 gp; Cost 750 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
