# Doomharp

Made from bone and sun-dried sinew, this masterwork harp produces a vague sense of unease in anyone near it. If the owner of this item has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] class feature he can use it to perform a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-dirge-of-doom|dirge of doom]] even if he is not of sufficient level to normally have access to that ability. A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] who can use dirge of doom and who plays the harp as part of his performance increases the effective radius to 60 feet.

Faint necromancy; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
