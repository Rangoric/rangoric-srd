# Cloak of the Hedge Wizard

This cloak comes in eight varieties—one for each school of magic. The cloak grants its wearer the ability to use a certain number of spells on command (caster level 1st) based on its school of magic. It also grants the wearer the ability to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_ on command and at will.

**Abjuration**: at will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_

**Conjuration**: at will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Splash|acid splash]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unseen Servant|unseen servant]]_

**Divination**: at will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Secret Doors|detect secret doors]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_

**Enchantment**: at will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]_

**Evocation**: at will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Floating Disk|floating disk]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]]_

**Illusion**: at will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silent Image|silent image]]_

**Necromancy**: at will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Fatigue|touch of fatigue]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|ray of enfeeblement]]_

**Transmutation**: at will—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]_; 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_

Faint, school based on cloak type; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_ and any spell the cloak allows the caster to use; the creator must have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] in the cloak's school of magic; Price 2,500 gp; Cost 1,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
