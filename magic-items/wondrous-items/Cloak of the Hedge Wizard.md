# Cloak of the Hedge Wizard

This cloak comes in eight varieties—one for each school of magic. The cloak grants its wearer the ability to use a certain number of spells on command (caster level 1st) based on its school of magic. It also grants the wearer the ability to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]* on command and at will.

**Abjuration**: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]*

**Conjuration**: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Splash|acid splash]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unseen Servant|unseen servant]]*

**Divination**: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Secret Doors|detect secret doors]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]*

**Enchantment**: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]*

**Evocation**: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Floating Disk|floating disk]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]]*

**Illusion**: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silent Image|silent image]]*

**Necromancy**: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Fatigue|touch of fatigue]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|ray of enfeeblement]]*

**Transmutation**: at will—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]*; 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]*

Faint, school based on cloak type; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]* and any spell the cloak allows the caster to use; the creator must have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] in the cloak's school of magic; Price 2,500 gp; Cost 1,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
