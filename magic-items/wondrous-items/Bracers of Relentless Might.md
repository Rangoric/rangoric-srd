# Bracers of Relentless Might

These bracers grant a +12 enhancement bonus to the wearer’s Strength and Constitution. The wearer is treated as two size categories larger than normal (to a maximum of Colossal) for purposes of combat-related opposed checks that apply a modifier based on size, and for its [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] (such as for bull rush, grapple, and trip).  
Caster Level: 20th; Prerequisites: Craft Wondrous Item, Craft Epic Wondrous Item, bull’s strength, endurance, enlarge; Market Price: 4,384,000 gp; Weight: 1 lb.
