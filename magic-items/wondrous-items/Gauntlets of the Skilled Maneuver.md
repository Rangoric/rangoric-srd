# Gauntlets of the Skilled Maneuver

The wearer of these gauntlets gains a +2 bonus on one type of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] check (such as bull rush, disarm, or steal) chosen by the creator when the item is created.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must have the appropriate Improved combat maneuver feat; Price 4,000 gp; Cost 2,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
