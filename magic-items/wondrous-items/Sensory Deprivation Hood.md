# Sensory Deprivation Hood

This especially heavy leather [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Hoodwink Cowl|hoodwink cowl]] quiets its wearer's mundane senses so he can focus on his supernatural ones. Putting it on or taking it off takes 1 minute, or 5 rounds with help.

As with a hoodwink cowl, the wearer is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]], and takes a –10 penalty on all other Perception checks. A wearer with [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Blindsense|blindsense]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Blindsight|blindsight]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] or a similar ability can take a full-round action to suppress those senses for as long as he wears the hood or until he uses them again (another full-round action). While he benefits from one of those senses, the hood gives him nothing.

While his senses are suppressed, the wearer can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics#Automatic Writing|automatic writing]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft#Dowsing|dowsing]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise#Psychometry|psychometry]] occult skill unlocks even without psychic spellcasting or the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] feat. If he has either, he gains a +5 bonus on checks for those unlocks.

Once per day, the wearer can also open his mind to passing spiritual phenomena for insight into a specific issue. This works as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contact Other Plane|contact other plane]]*, allowing up to 5 questions, but the wearer contacts a nearby spirit or other psychic entity rather than an entity on another plane, with these values: Avoid Int/Cha Decrease DC 10/24 hours; True Answer 01–34; Don't Know 35–62; Lie 63–83; Random Answer 84–100. Unlike with the spell, the wearer can attempt either an Intelligence check or a Charisma check to avoid the decrease.

Moderate divination; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contact Other Plane|contact other plane]]*; Price 27,500 gp; Cost 13,750 gp; Weight 5 lb.
