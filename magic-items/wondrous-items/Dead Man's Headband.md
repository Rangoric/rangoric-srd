# Dead Man's Headband

This taut black band, clipped with a black metal skull, can be attached on the outside of a hat or other head slot item or can be worn as a normal headband. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks and increases the DC of any fear effect he creates by +1. In addition, if this headband is attached to the outside of a hat, the wearer's hat or headgear cannot be blown off or removed by wind, water, or environmental effects, and the wearer gains a +5 circumstance bonus to his [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] attempts targeting his headgear.

Faint enchantment and abjuration; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Doom|doom]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shrink Item|shrink item]]*; Price 3,600 gp; Cost 1,800 gp; Weight 1 lb.
