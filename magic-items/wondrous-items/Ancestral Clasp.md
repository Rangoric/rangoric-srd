# Ancestral Clasp

Favored by [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elves]] seeking to distill their heritage into a combat edge, an ancestral clasp resembles a metal tube, open on both sides and covered in delicate elven calligraphy. Whenever a half-elf fits an ancestral clasp to the pommel, grip, or haft of a longbow, longsword, rapier, or shortbow, the character is treated as though she were proficient with that weapon. In addition, when an ancestral clasp is fitted to any weapon with the word "elven" in the title, the character treats the weapon as if it were a martial weapon, mirroring the weapon familiarity [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elven]] racial trait. A half-elf who already is proficient with one of the above weapons adorned with an ancestral clasp receives a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls with that weapon. Although this ability stems from the item's magic, the affected weapon is treated as nonmagical for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]]. Affixing and removing an _ancestral clasp_ is a standard action.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Paragon Surge|paragon surge]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]]; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
