# Inquisitor's Bastion Vambraces

Constructed of blackened steel, these vambraces augment the protection granted to the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]] by the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Protection (Judgment)|protection judgment]]. While under that judgment's effects, the inquisitor gains a sacred bonus both to CMD and on checks made to cast spells defensively. This bonus is equal to the sacred bonus the judgement grants to AC.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield of Faith|shield of faith]]_, creator must be an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]] with access to the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Protection (Judgment)|protection judgment]]; Price 4,000 gp; Cost 2,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
