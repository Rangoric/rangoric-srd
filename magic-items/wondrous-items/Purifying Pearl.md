# Purifying Pearl

A favored tool of diplomats and those tasked with protecting the lives of regents and other important people, this small luminescent white pearl purifies putridity and poison in two ways. First, up to 10 cubic feet of food or drink that it touches per day is affected as if a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Purify Food and Drink|purify food and drink]]_ spell had been cast upon the food or drink. Second, once per day, if placed in the mouth of a creature (as a standard action that provokes [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]]), the pearl acts as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_ spell cast on that creature (caster level 10th).

Moderate conjuration and transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Purify Food and Drink|purify food and drink]]_; Price 19,000 gp; Cost 9,500 gp.
