# Necklace of Ki Serenity

This simple leather cord is tightly knotted at even intervals and gives its wearer a sense of inner calmness and connection with the larger world. The wearer gains a +4 bonus to his effective level when determining the size of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki* pool]] and the level-based effects of his _ki_ pool ability (such as bypassing damage reduction).

Moderate enchantment; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Calm Emotions|calm emotions]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom|owl's wisdom]]_; Price 16,000 gp; Cost 8,000 gp.
