# Belt of Equilibrium

As long as this belt's wearer doesn't move more than 5 feet during her turn, she ignores the penalties for being [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] until the end of her next turn or until she moves more than 5 feet, whichever occurs first. While she does not take any of the penalties of those conditions, she is considered to be under the effect of those conditions until they end or are removed.

Faint conjuration; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
