# Oil of Rust Protection

Applying this oil to metal objects protects them from rusting, eliminating one of the major avenues in which such equipment, or even animated metal constructs, can utterly be destroyed over the passage of time. This oil lasts forever unless removed, a process done by one either of two methods. First, subjecting the anointed item or construct to a medium strength or stronger acidic solution—for example that of a tar-beast, a gelatinous cube, or the breath weapon of a black dragon. Secondly, subjecting the anointed item to three magical rust attacks, such as those from a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Rust Monster|rust monster]] or a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rusting Grasp|rusting grasp]]* spell, negates the effects of this wondrous oil. Either one suit of Large armor (or smaller) or up to five weapons of Huge size (or smaller) may be protected by a single dose of this oil.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]*; Price 750 gp; Cost 375 gp + 30 XP.
