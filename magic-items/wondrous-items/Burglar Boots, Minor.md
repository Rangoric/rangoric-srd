# Burglar Boots, Minor

These form-fitting, calfskin boots are dyed black and lightly decorated with raven feathers. grant their wearer a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to spot or locate traps and devices on the floor with 10 feet. This includes pits, pressure plates, and other mechanical and magical devices located on or attached to the floor. This bonus also applies to AC and Reflex Defense against such traps.

Faint divination; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must have 4 ranks in Perception; Price 4,000 gp; Cost 2,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
