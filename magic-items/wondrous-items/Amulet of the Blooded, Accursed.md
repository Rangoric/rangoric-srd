# Amulet of the Blooded, Accursed

Each different type of _amulet of the blooded_ grants its wearer powers from one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines|bloodline]], but makes him vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with the appropriate bloodline (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Blood-Hunting|blood-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated bloodline wears the amulet, the wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of his bloodline powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of bloodline powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Accursed Bloodline|Accursed]]**: This swirling green malachite hangs from a chain of oxidized copper. Sinister, unblinking eyes are etched upon its surface. The wearer gains a +2 bonus on defenses against charm, cold, fear, fire, and sleep effects. Three times per day as a standard action, the wearer can fix his gaze on any one creature within 60 feet. The target becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 4 rounds unless a +8 attack against its Will misses. Only one target can be affected by the wearer's gaze at a time; if the wearer attempts to stagger a new opponent, the previous target immediately recovers whether the new attempt is successful or not.

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Accursed Bloodline|accursed]] bloodline; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
