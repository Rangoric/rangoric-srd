# Nullfield Stone

This item is barely portable, being a large, plain stone weighing approximately 45 pounds. Atop the stone is a rough, graven rune about the size of a human’s palm. Once per day, as a standard action, the rune may be touched to activate the stone. This produces an antimagic field in a 10foot radius. A nullfield has 25 charges when created, and each use drains one charge.

CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antimagic Field|antimagic field]]*; Price 5,900 gp; Weight 45 lb.
