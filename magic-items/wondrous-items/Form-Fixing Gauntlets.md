# Form-Fixing Gauntlets

These leather gauntlets grant the wearer a +2 morale bonus on [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|unarmed strikes]], natural attacks, and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver checks]] versus creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Change Shape|change shape]] special ability or similar abilities that allow it to change shape at will. While the wearer has such a creature [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Pinned (Condition)|pinned]], it cannot use any shape-changing spells or abilities unless it succeeds at a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]]; the DC of this check is 4 + the DC of casting the spell while grappled. If the wearer has such a creature pinned, a successful grapple check allows him to force the target into a specific shape it can take naturally (without the use of a spell). This involuntary transformation only forces the creature into a shape it is normally capable of and does not cost the creature an action.

Strong abjuration; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph Any Object|polymorph any object]]*; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
