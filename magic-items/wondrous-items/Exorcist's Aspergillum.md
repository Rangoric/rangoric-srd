# Exorcist's Aspergillum

This short aspergillum is made of silver. An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]] who carries it may use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]_ as if it were one of the spells associated with her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-detect-alignment|detect alignment]] ability. Once per day, the item can alter an inquisitor's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|judgment]] ability; by spending one use of her judgment ability, she can allow her spells of 3rd level and lower to have full effect against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures, as though using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ectoplasmic Spell|Ectoplasmic Spell]] feat. This lasts until the combat ends.

Moderate divination; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ectoplasmic Spell|Ectoplasmic Spell]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]_, creator must be an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]]; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
