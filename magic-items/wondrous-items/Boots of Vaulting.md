# Boots of Vaulting

The wearer of these suede boots ignores an additional 10 feet of falling distance when she successfully uses [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] to soften a fall. Once per round as a free action, the wearer can use the boots to gain a +10 competence bonus on an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|jump]], treating such jump as though she had a running start. The wearer also receives this +10 competence bonus on Acrobatics checks to avoid [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] during the jump or to balance after landing.

Using the boots in this fashion strains the lower body. If the wearer attempts a second such jump before at least 1 minute has passed, the strain attacks her Fortitude, its attack bonus the DC of her second jump attempt – 10, and on a hit she injures her legs. This injury reduces her speed by half for 24 hours, or until she is successfully treated with a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check or at least 1 point of magical healing. The attack bonus increases by 5 for each additional jump attempted until at least 1 minute passes between jumps.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Jump (Spell)|jump]]_; Price 3,500 gp; Cost 1,750 gp; Weight 1 lb.
