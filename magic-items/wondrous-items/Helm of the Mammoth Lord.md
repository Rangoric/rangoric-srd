# Helm of the Mammoth Lord

This hide helm is set with plates of ivory carved with primitive runes, with a pair of tusks curving down on either side of the wearer's face for cheek guards. The tusks of the helm provide a gore attack dealing 1d6 points of damage for a Medium wearer (or 1d4 points of damage for Small wearers) and counts as a magic weapon for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

The _helm of the mammoth lord_ also protects the wearer from cold environments as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_ spell. Furthermore, it provides a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] checks with elephants, mammoths, mastodons, and other similar elephant-like creatures.

On command, the wearer may use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Animals or Plants|detect animals or plants]]_, but can only detect elephant-like creatures. On command, the wearer may use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_, but only to communicate with elephant-like creatures.
