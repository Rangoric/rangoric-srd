# Vest of Escape

Hidden within secret pockets of this simple silk vest are lockpicks that provide a +4 competence bonus on Sleight of Hand checks. The vest also grants its wearer a +6 competence bonus on Escape Artist checks.

Faint conjuration and transmutation; CL 4th; Craft Wondrous Item, knock, grease; Price 5,200 gp.
