# Boots of the Battle Herald

These boots are made of fine white leather that has been tooled with scenes of epic battles. Once per day as a move action, the wearer can grant himself the effect of _greater heroism_ for 11 minutes.

Moderate enchantment; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater|greater heroism]]_; Price 30,000 gp; Cost 15,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
