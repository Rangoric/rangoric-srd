# Talisman of Danger Sense, Greater

A talisman works as described under [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#^talismans|Talismans]] in the rules for magic items on the body. A greater talisman works as a lesser talisman of the same type, but lasts far longer: it can give its benefit once per day.

The first time the wearer rolls initiative, she is immediately affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Anticipate Peril|anticipate peril]]_.

Faint divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Anticipate Peril|anticipate peril]]_; Price 7,500 gp; Cost 3,750 gp; Weight 1 lb.
