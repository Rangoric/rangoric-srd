# Gloves of Dueling

These supple gloves grant the wearer a +4 bonus to her [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] attacks, attempts to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] her wielded weapons, and effects that cause her to lose her grip on her weapons (such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]*). The wearer doesn't drop held weapons when [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]]. If the wearer has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] class feature and is using an appropriate weapon, her weapon training bonus increases by +2.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon, Greater|greater magic weapon]]*; Price 15,000 gp; Cost 7,500 gp.
