# Red Feather Fan

This fan is made of copper fashioned into a dozen small feathers mounted on a ring-shaped bone handle. Once per day, the bearer can hold it and speak a command word to counterspell a darkness effect (as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*) or counterspell a light effect (as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]*). If activated in an area of overlapping light and darkness effects, the fan randomly counterspells one of them, leaving the other intact.

Moderate evocation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]*; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
