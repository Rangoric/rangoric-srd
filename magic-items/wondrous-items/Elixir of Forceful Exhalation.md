# Elixir of Forceful Exhalation

This fizzy, sky-blue liquid leaves its drinker feeling buoyant, yet bloated. The drinker gains a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks made to make high or long jumps and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks for up to 1 hour after consuming the elixir. As a standard action, the drinker can exhale a 15-foot-long _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]_. The drinker can exhale up to three gusts, after which the elixir's effect is discharged and the skill bonuses end. Unused gusts dissipate after 1 hour.

Faint evocation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]_; Price 900 gp; Cost 450 gp; Weight 1 lb.
