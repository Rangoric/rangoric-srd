# Grave Salt

When poured on ground in a circle, this pure white salt sanctifies the enclosed area as the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]*. Applying the salt takes 1 round per dose. A single dose is sufficient to form a 5-foot radius circle, with each additional dose increasing the circle's radius by 5 feet. The area within the circle remains consecrated for 1 hour per dose, though any corporeal creature can spend a standard action to break the circle and end the effect prematurely. A newly created pouch of *grave salt* holds 5 doses.

*Grave salt* can also be used directly against restless spirits. A single dose can be hurled up to 15 feet as a ranged touch attack. An [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] undead creature struck by the salt must make a DC 14 Will save or take 1d4 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|Charisma damage]]. Strong winds and other effects that penalize ranged attacks make it impossible to employ the salt in this manner.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]*; Price 1,100 gp; Cost 550 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
