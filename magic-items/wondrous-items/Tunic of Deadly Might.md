# Tunic of Deadly Might

This tunic is crafted from golden thread, and has silver-hemmed sleeves. The wearer gains a +2 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|Combat Maneuver Defense]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] combat maneuvers. If the wearer is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Samurai|samurai]], twice per day as a swift action he can apply the effects of the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Thundering|thundering]]_ weapon special ability to a successful critical hit against the target of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Samurai#^samurai-challenge|challenge]].

Faint evocation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blindness-Deafness|blindness/deafness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Armor|mage armor]]_; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
