# Cloak of the Diplomat

The forest-green cloak grants a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks. In addition, once per day, before making a Persuasion or Sense Motive check, the wearer can decide to call upon the powers of the cloak to roll twice and take the better result. Finally, the wearer of this cloak can adjust a creature's attitude up to three steps when using Persuasion, instead of the normal limit of two steps. However, the cloak's power does have one drawback. Should the wearer ever fail a Persuasion check made to adjust a creature's attitude by 5 or more, the creature's attitude is reduced by two steps instead of one.

Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]*, creator must have 5 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]]; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
