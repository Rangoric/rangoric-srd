# Incense of Transcendence

The heady vapors of this coiled incense facilitate a deep meditative trance. When an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle|oracle]] lights a coil of _incense of transcendence_ and then spends 8 hours praying and meditating nearby, she can replace one of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelations]] with another revelation. The new revelation must be one available to her (such as from the same [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|mystery]]) and must be available at the same class level as the original revelation (for example, she cannot replace a revelation available at 1st level with a revelation requiring a minimum of 7th level). If she replaces a revelation that is a prerequisite for another revelation or ability, she cannot use that other revelation or ability while under the influence of the incense.

The coil of incense burns for 8 hours. Its effects persist for 24 hours, after which the oracle's original revelations return.

Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|prayer]]_, creator must be [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle|oracle]] level 7th; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
