# False Coin

The possessor activates this well-worn gold coin by rubbing it between thumb and forefinger while willing it to work. Only a creature with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] feat or the ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^psychic-magic|psychic spells]] can activate it. Once activated, the coin keeps a link to its activator, and if it's passed to someone else, it shares empathic cues about its new possessor with the activator. The activator gains a +2 insight bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks against the coin's current possessor, and the possessor takes a –1 penalty on saving throws against the activator's spells with the emotion, fear or scrying descriptor. While activated, the coin appears nonmagical, as though protected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]_ (Will DC 11).

The link lasts 1 week, after which the coin loses all its powers and becomes a mundane coin.
