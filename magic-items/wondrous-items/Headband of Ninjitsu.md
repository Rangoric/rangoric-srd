# Headband of Ninjitsu

This simple black cloth band focuses the wearer's _ki_, helping her use subtle misdirection against opponents in combat. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks made to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|combat maneuver checks]] made to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]], and once per day she can attempt a feint or reposition maneuver as a swift action. The wearer also gains a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls on [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attacks]] and can sneak attack creatures with [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] or total concealment, though miss chances apply normally.

Faint abjuration; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_; Price 15,000 gp; Cost 7,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
