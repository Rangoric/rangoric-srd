# Whistle of Calling

This whistle emits a loud, shrill note. Once per day when the user blows this whistle his mount or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] is instantly teleported to a safe space of the user's choice that's within 30 feet of him. The creature must be within 1 mile, and any effect that bars or hampers _teleportation_ also impedes this effect. The whistle is useless unless attuned to a specific mount or animal companion; attuning the whistle is a special ritual that takes 10 minutes. The whistle can be attuned to a new mount or animal companion at will.

Moderate conjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]_; Price 6,300 gp; Cost 3,150 gp.
