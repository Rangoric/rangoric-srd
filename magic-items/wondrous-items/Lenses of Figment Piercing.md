# Lenses of Figment Piercing

When the wearer first sees an illusion effect with the figment subschool with a visual element, and that figment attacks a defense, it immediately attacks her, and on a miss she realizes the image is illusory instead of waiting to interact with it. This has no effect on illusions of other subschools or on illusions that do not normally attack a defense. If the wearer fails to disbelieve the illusion and later interacts with it in such a way as to qualify normally for an attack she can disbelieve, she gains a +4 competence bonus on this defense.

Faint divination; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp.
