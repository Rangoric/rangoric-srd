# Spikebones

_Spikebones_ are a collection of bleached humanoid bones—typically the ribs, vertebrae, and finger bones—carried in a crude pouch made of the humanoid's skin and connective tissue. Three times per day, the _spikebones_ can be cast upon the ground as a standard action to create an area of dangerous spikes. Bony in color and shape, these sharp protrusions function like a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spike Growth|spike growth]]_ spell. The spikes fill four contiguous 10-foot-square areas, shaped by the user at the time of deployment. The spikes remain effective for up to 3 hours, after which they vanish and the _spikebones_ magically reappear in their pouch.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spike Growth|spike growth]]_; Price 22,000 gp; Cost 11,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
