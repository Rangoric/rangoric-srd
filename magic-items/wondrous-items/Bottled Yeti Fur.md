# Bottled Yeti Fur

This small opaque flask releases a thick stream of coarse white fur when opened. This fur creeps up the user's arm and across her entire body until she is completely covered in a tangled pelt. While coated with this fur she gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|cold resistance]] 5, a +2 natural armor bonus to AC, and a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks in areas of ice and snow. This fur sheds from her body after 24 hours, or can be removed early by soaking it in alcohol. The flask is expended after one use.

Faint abjuration; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Barkskin|barkskin]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*; Price 800 gp; Cost 400 gp.
