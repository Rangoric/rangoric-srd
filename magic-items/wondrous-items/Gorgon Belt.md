# Gorgon Belt

This belt grants its wearer a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength, and the ability to ignore [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement#Terrain and Obstacles|difficult terrain]] while charging or making an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Trample|trample]] attempt. Furthermore, once per day on command, its wearer can spew a 60-foot cone of poisonous green gas as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Breath Weapon|breath weapon]]. Any creature caught in the area of the gas can attempt a DC 18 Fortitude saving throw to resist its effect, but if the creature fails the saving throw, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1d4 rounds. At the end of its turn, a creature paralyzed by this breath weapon can attempt a new save to end the paralysis effect. Treat the enhancement bonus to Strength as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the belt is worn.

Moderate transmutation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Step|feather step]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]*; Price 23,000 gp; Cost 11,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
