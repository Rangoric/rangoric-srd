# Talisman of Arrow Protection, Greater

A talisman works as described under [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#^talismans|Talismans]] in the rules for magic items on the body. A greater talisman works as a lesser talisman of the same type, but lasts far longer: it can give its benefit once per day.

The first time the wearer is hit by two or more ranged attacks in one round, he is immediately affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Arrows|protection from arrows]]_.

Faint abjuration; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Arrows|protection from arrows]]_; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
