# Pipes of the Warren Guardian

These finely crafted yellow-ivory pipes are bound in bronze and decorated with bits of amber. If the possessor learns the proper tune, once per day he can use these pipes to summon 1d4+1 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dire-animal/Dire Rat|dire rats]] as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_. The summoned dire rats also have the swarming racial trait, and count as [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|ratfolk]] for the purposes of the ratfolk's swarming racial trait.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]_; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp.
