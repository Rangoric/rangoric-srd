# Merciful Baldric

This sash of white silk embroidered with golden suns never tears, fades, or soils. Once per day, a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]] wearing this baldric can select three extra [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-mercy|mercies]] of her level or lower she does not already know and add them to the list of mercies she can use for the day. She has access to these mercies for 24 hours. When the wearer uses a mercy to remove a condition caused by a curse, disease, or poison, the mercy actually cures the source curse, disease, or poison rather than merely suppressing it for 1 hour.

Moderate conjuration; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Extra Mercy|Extra Mercy]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]*, creator must be lawful good; Price 60,000 gp; Cost 30,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
