# Mask of a Thousand Tomes

The random snippets of script that write across the surface of this parchment-colored eyeless mask hint at the knowledge of the many tomes stored within. The mask grants its wearer a +10 competence bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]] checks, but the wearer is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] while wearing the mask. The mask must be worn for 10 minutes before the wearer gains its bonus.

Faint divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], clairaudience/clairvoyance; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
