# Headband of Knucklebones

This headband is made of interlocked finger bones woven together by leathery tendons and gristle. The wearer may use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command Undead|command undead]]_ up to three times per day, but only affecting [[rangoric-srd/monsters/zombie/Zombie|zombies]]. Once per day, the wearer may use the headband to animate one Medium zombie as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]_.

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command Undead|command undead]]_; Price 27,500 gp; Cost 13,750 gp; Weight 1 lb.
