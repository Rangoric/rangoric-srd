# Boots of Diminished Velocity

Creatures within ten feet of the wearer are attacked against their Will at +6, and on a hit are _slowed_, per the spell. Creatures that fail their save are slowed until they leave the area. The boots attack those they miss again each round that they remain in the area. The wearer of the boots is immune to slow spells.

CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (Transmutation), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_; Price 72,000 gp.
