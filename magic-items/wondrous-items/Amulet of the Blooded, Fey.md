# Amulet of the Blooded, Fey

Each different type of _amulet of the blooded_ grants its wearer powers from one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines|bloodline]], but makes him vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with the appropriate bloodline (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Blood-Hunting|blood-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated bloodline wears the amulet, the wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of his bloodline powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of bloodline powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Fey Bloodline|Fey]]**: This drop of amber hangs on a silk cord. The wearer can turn invisible for 9 rounds per day (as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_). The rounds don't need to be consecutive. Three times per day, the wearer can make a melee touch attack to cause a creature to burst out laughing. That creature can take only a move action, but can defend itself normally. Once a creature has been affected by this laughter, it is immune to it for 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting effect.

Moderate illusion; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hideous Laughter|hideous laughter]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Fey Bloodline|fey]] bloodline; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
