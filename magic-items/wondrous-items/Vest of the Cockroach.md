# Vest of the Cockroach

This padded and slightly bulky vest, made from red-brown cloth, causes its wearer to feel vaguely uncomfortable whenever she is lying [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. If the wearer is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]], she gains the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Resiliency (Rogue Talent)|resiliency]] rogue talent. If she already has this talent, activating the talent grants her 2 hit points per rogue level instead of 1.

Faint necromancy; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life|false life]]_; Price 16,000 gp; Cost 8,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
