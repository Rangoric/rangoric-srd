# Cauldron of Resurrection

The mouth of this heavy, dark iron cauldron is shaped like a monstrous maw and is large enough to accommodate a single Medium creature. When the cauldron is filled with pure water and certain rare and sacred herbs, and a deceased creature is placed inside it, the cauldron can restore the creature to life as if with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Raise Dead|raise dead]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resurrection|resurrection]]_ spell. The spell effect depends on the components used: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Raise Dead|raise dead]]_ costs 5,000 gp worth of materials, while _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resurrection|resurrection]]_ costs 10,000 gp worth of materials. The cauldron is usable once per day.

Strong conjuration (healing); CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resurrection|resurrection]]_; Price 33,000 gp; Cost 16,500 gp; Weight 35 lbs.
