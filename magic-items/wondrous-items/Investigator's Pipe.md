# Investigator's Pipe

This long-stemmed, wooden pipe can burn any number of substances, but is primarily used for smoking tobacco. The pipe smoker can command a small, smoky servant to emerge from the pipe to do minor tasks. The servant can move or fetch small objects, and in general acts as an _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unseen Servant|unseen servant]]_, except that it is visible as a small, wispy cloud of gray smoke. Additionally, the servant is capable of clearing one 5-foot square of smoke or fog per round. The servant can be used for up to 1 hour per day; this time doesn't need to be continuous, but must be used in 10-minute increments.

An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator|investigator]] gains some additional benefits while smoking the pipe. He can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|inspiration]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to track or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to notice fine details without spending a use of inspiration. Additionally, once per day he can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] check as if he had the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Eidetic Recollection (Investigator Talent)|eidetic recollection]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]].

Faint conjuration and divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guidance|guidance]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unseen Servant|unseen servant]]_; Price 9,000 gp; Cost 4,500 gp.
