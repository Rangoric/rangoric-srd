# Lenses of Darkness

Crafted from a dark-colored crystalline material and infused with magical darkness, these lenses fit over the wearer's eyes. When worn by a creature with [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Sensitivity|light sensitivity]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Blindness|light blindness]], these lenses protect the wearer against the effect of sunlight, the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_ spell, or similar light effects. Both lenses must be worn for the magic to be effective.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp.
