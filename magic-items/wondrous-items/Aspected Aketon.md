# Aspected Aketon

This magical aketon gives the ability to modify the look of any armor worn but is limited to surface appearance only. It cannot alter the appearance of the type or amount of armor. The aketon can be used to cover scratches and dents when trying to make a good impression or it can make the armor to appear more used than it actually is when attempting to intimidate someone.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]*; Price 300 gp.
