# Poet's Cloak

Even after cleaning and mending, this traveler's cloak appears worn and weathered. For wearers with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] class feature, it grants the use of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]] as a 4th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald|skald]] by spending rounds of bardic performance. For wearers with the raging song class feature, it grants the use of inspire courage and inspire competence as a 4th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] by spending rounds of raging song.

In addition, each _poet's cloak_ allows its wearer to grant one specific [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] when using raging song in place of his own rage powers (if any). The specific rage power varies by cloak. It must be usable by a 4th-level skald, and can't have any prerequisites other than level.
