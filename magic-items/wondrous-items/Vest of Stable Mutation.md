# Vest of Stable Mutation

This leather vest is fitted with a number of pockets that are useful for storing the ingredients necessary for alchemical tinkering. The main ability of this vest, however, is to lessen the debilitating effects of an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|alchemist]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|mutagen]]. While under the effect of any type of mutagen, the wearer takes no penalty to mental ability scores from that mutagen.

Moderate abjuration; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fox’s Cunning|fox's cunning]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom|owl's wisdom]]*, creator must be an alchemist; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
