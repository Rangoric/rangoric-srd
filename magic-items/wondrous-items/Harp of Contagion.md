# Harp of Contagion

This grand-looking wooden harp is carved with images of various venomous creatures. Once per day the bearer may play the harp to target a single opponent with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]_ spell (DC 16). Alternatively, the bearer may play the harp to grant allies within 30 feet a +2 resistance bonus on saving throws against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]]; maintaining this benefit each round is a standard action. If the player has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^countersong|countersong]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] ability, he may expend rounds of bardic performance to use the harp to refocus his countersong against disease and poison instead of sonic or language-dependent effects.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_; Price 13,000 gp; Cost 6,500 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
