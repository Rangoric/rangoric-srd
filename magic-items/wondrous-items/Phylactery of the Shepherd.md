# Phylactery of the Shepherd

Three times per day on command, the phylactery's wearer can determine the current health and overall well-being of any one ally within 30 feet. This effect acts as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Status|status]]* spell. If the wearer has the ability to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-channel-energy|channel energy]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]], he can as a swift action choose to apply the effects of that class feature to an ally he is currently evaluating so long as that ally is within 30 feet of him, but doing so ends the effect of the phylactery's *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Status|status]]*.

Faint divination; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Status|status]]*; Price 7,000 gp; Cost 3,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
