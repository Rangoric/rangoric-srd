# Feychild Necklace

This necklace is set with a bloodstone sculpted to look like a scintillating green eye. It gives the wearer a +4 competence bonus on Charisma checks and Charisma-based checks made to interact with gnomes, a +2 competence bonus on similar checks when dealing with beings of the fey creature type, and a –2 penalty on similar checks when interacting with anyone else. The wearer can read, speak, and understand the Gnome language. If the wearer is not a gnome, she gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], and may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ each once per day as a spell-like ability.

Moderate divination; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]]; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
