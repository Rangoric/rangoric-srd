# Vambraces of the Genie, Efreeti

These bracers are decorated with icons representing air, earth, fire, or water, and depictions of genies appropriate to that element. The item protects the wearer against hot and cold environments as if she were using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*. The vambraces' materials and other powers depend on their associated genie type.

**Efreeti**: These scorched bronze bracers allow the wearer to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]* (self only), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Produce Flame|produce flame]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pyrotechnics|pyrotechnics]]* each once per day.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Produce Flame|produce flame]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pyrotechnics|pyrotechnics]]*; Price 14,400 gp; Cost 7,200 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
