# Torc of the Primal Song

This heavy gold neck ring resembles a braided rope. When the wearer performs a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]] or benefits from an ally's raging song, she gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]] feat for the duration of the song. In addition, she benefits from any helpful raging song or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] for 1 round after the performance ends or would normally cease affecting the wearer. This does not stack with other means of prolonging the effects of a performance that has ended.

Moderate enchantment; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater|greater heroism]]*; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 4 lbs.
