# Headband of Unshakeable Resolve

This simple white cotton headband is decorated with a stylized imperial dragon coiled above the wearer's forehead. The headband grants the wearer a +2 enhancement bonus to her Wisdom score. Treat this as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the headband is worn.

Whenever the wearer suffers a fear effect, she may spend an immediate action to reduce the severity of the fear effect by one step. If the wearer would become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fear-panicked|panicked]], she is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fear-frightened|frightened]]. If she would become frightened, she is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fear-shaken|shaken]]. If the wearer would become shaken, the effect is negated entirely. The wearer can use this ability up to three times per day.

Moderate abjuration and transmutation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom|owl's wisdom]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_; Price 5,600 gp; Cost 2,800 gp; Weight 1 lb.
