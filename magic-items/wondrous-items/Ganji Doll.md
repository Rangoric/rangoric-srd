# Ganji Doll

A ganji doll is a fetish bound so closely to its victim that good or ill can be done to the victim through it. When it's made, the creator chooses a humanoid to bind it to and works a bit of that creature's body (typically hair, blood or a tooth) into it. After that the doll affects only its bound target, and the target can't be changed once the doll is finished.

Three times per day, any bearer can use the doll to create one of the following effects, which target only the bound creature. The target takes a –2 penalty on defenses against them.

_Blind_: Covering the doll's head with a cloth [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinds]] the target for 1 minute (+6 vs Will).

_Damage_: Stabbing the doll with something sharp, holding it over a flame, wrapping a cord around its neck or otherwise damaging it deals 3d6 points of damage to the target (+6 vs Fortitude; a miss halves). This damage ignores all resistances and immunities.

_Touch Spell_: The doll counts as the bound target for any spell with a range of touch. The target receives the effect of any spell cast on the doll (within the doll's three effects per day) as if the caster had touched him, and the spell attacks his defenses as normal, with the doll's –2 penalty. Beneficial spells, such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_, can be used this way too.
