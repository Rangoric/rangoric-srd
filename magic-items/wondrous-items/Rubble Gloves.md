# Rubble Gloves

These gloves are made of a fine metal mesh. If a creature wearing rubble gloves touches a 5-foot square of stone or earth floor, rubble roils to the surface, making the square [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement#Terrain and Obstacles|difficult terrain]]. Using the gloves is a standard action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity. The wearer can use the gloves to create up to 20 squares of difficult terrain per day.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Excavation|expeditious excavation]]_; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
