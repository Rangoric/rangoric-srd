# Ganji Doll, Greater

A greater ganji doll looks like and works as a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Ganji Doll|ganji doll]], except that it can be used five times per day, imposes a –4 penalty on the target's defenses against its effects, and has the following changed or added abilities besides all of a ganji doll's.

_Damage_: Damaging the doll deals 6d6 points of damage to the target (+6 vs Fortitude; a miss halves). This damage ignores all resistances and immunities.

_Locate Creature_: The bearer can use the doll to find its bound target, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]]_.

_Suggestion_: The user whispers in the doll's ear, creating the effect of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_ (+6 vs Will). Unlike with the spell, the target needn't understand the user's language.

Moderate necromancy; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_, a piece of the body of the humanoid to be affected; Price 36,000 gp; Cost 18,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
