# Monk’s Robe

When worn, this simple brown robe confers great ability in unarmed combat. If the wearer has levels in [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]], her AC and unarmed damage are treated as a monk of 5 levels higher. If donned by a character with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]] feat, the robe lets her make one additional stunning attack per day. If the character is not a monk, she gains the AC and unarmed damage of a 5th-level monk (although she does not add her Wisdom bonus to her AC). This AC bonus functions just like the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ac-bonus|monk’s AC bonus]].

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Transformation|transformation]]*; Price 13,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
