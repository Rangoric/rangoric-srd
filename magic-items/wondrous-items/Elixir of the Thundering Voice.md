# Elixir of the Thundering Voice

This light gray elixir makes the user's voice fiercer and louder, and gives it an inhuman quality that rattles those he talks to, granting the user a +10 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks for 1 hour.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sound Burst|sound burst]]*; Price 250 gp; Cost 125 gp.
