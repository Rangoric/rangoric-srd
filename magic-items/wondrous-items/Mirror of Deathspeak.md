# Mirror of Deathspeak

The creation of this 1-1/2-foot by 1-foot mirror requires that a monk hold the item as the final spell to create it is cast. This attunes the mirror so that it taps into a ki user’s spirit when it is activated. To use this item, it must be placed on the chest of a dead monk and the corpse’s name is spoken. Within seconds, the translucent image of the deceased as he looked while alive appears in the mirror. It answers and speaks as it did while it was alive. The catch is that the item only provides this medium within the 12 hours immediately after the monk’s demise. (Because monks are greater receptacles, and therefore more intertwined with *ki* energies, their spirits require a full 12 hours to separate from and leave their bodies.)

CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]*, Monk of any level; Price 60,000 gp.
