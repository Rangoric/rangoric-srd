# Sash of Flowing Water

If this belt's wearer has at least one hand free (holding nothing), he can attempt to deflect a successful melee attack with an immediate action. To do so, he rolls an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|unarmed strike]] against the opponent, including any applicable modifiers such as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] bonus or the penalty for [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]]. If his attack roll is greater than the opponent's attack roll, the attack is negated without effect. The unarmed strike itself causes no damage and does not count as a successful attack against the opponent. The opponent does not need to be within the wearer's reach to use this ability. Whether the attack succeeds or not, the wearer takes a –4 penalty on attack rolls until the end of his next turn.

Moderate abjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]]; Price 25,000 gp; Cost 12,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
