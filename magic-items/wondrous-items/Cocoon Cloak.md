# Cocoon Cloak

This ruffled silk cloak is incredibly durable despite its soft, delicate construction. Anytime the wearer falls asleep (whether the sleep is natural or brought on by a magical effect), the cloak immediately transforms into sticky strands that envelop the wearer's body, hardening into a solid silk cocoon. While wrapped in the cocoon the wearer gains a +4 enhancement bonus to natural armor and is protected from critical hits and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attacks]] as if subject to the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|light fortification]]_ armor ability (25% chance for normal damage on a critical hit). The cocoon opens and transforms back into a cloak once the wearer awakens.

Moderate transmutation; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vermin Shape I|vermin shape I]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
