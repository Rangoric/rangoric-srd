# Ghost Powder

This mixture of salt and powdered iron, usually kept in bags or tubes, forms a barrier spirits can't see or pass when it's sprinkled on the ground. One dose traces a line up to 50 feet long, in 5-foot increments. Corporeal creatures can cross it, but [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] and ethereal creatures are blocked as though it were a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Force|wall of force]]_. Teleportation effects can cross it, as can an incorporeal creature [[rangoric-srd/rules/Possession (Rules)|possessing]] a corporeal one. The barrier is 10 feet high, and incorporeal creatures can't cross through the floor directly beneath it, or through the ceiling directly above it if the barrier reaches the ceiling. If ghost powder is sprinkled in front of every entrance of a building (chimneys, air vents and the like included), incorporeal creatures can't enter the building through its walls, floor or ceiling at all.

Sweeping the powder away destroys the barrier, but incorporeal and ethereal creatures can't disturb the powder or affect it with their abilities unless they deal enough damage to destroy the barrier itself. Laid down quickly (up to 10 feet as a standard action), it lasts 1 hour; laid down carefully (1 minute per 5 feet), it lasts 8 hours.

Moderate abjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Anti-Incorporeal Shell|anti-incorporeal shell]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]_; Price 1,400 gp; Cost 700 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
