# Symbol of Luck

This translucent sphere of iron-hard glass changes shape to match its bearer's holy symbol within an hour of coming into her possession. After that transformation is complete, when this symbol is used by a [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]] with the luck or [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Adaptable Luck|adaptable luck]] racial trait to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] in order to heal, it also grants those healed a +1 luck bonus on saving throws for a number of rounds equal to the number of dice healed by the channel energy.

Faint evocation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_, the creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]]; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
