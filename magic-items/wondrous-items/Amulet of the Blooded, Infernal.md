# Amulet of the Blooded, Infernal

Each different type of *amulet of the blooded* grants its wearer powers from one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines|bloodline]], but makes him vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with the appropriate bloodline (such as a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Blood-Hunting|blood-hunting]]* weapon). If a creature that already has the associated bloodline wears the amulet, the wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of his bloodline powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of bloodline powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Infernal Bloodline|Infernal]]**: This chunk of black marble hangs from a chain of brass. The wearer gains fire [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 5 and a +2 bonus on saving throws against poison. Three times per day the wearer can make a melee touch attack to cause a creature to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 4 rounds. Multiple touches do not stack, but do add to the duration.

Moderate abjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Touch|chill touch]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Infernal Bloodline|infernal]] bloodline; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
