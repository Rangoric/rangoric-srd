# Master's Perfect Golden Bell

This lustrous golden bell produces a deep and resonant ring which vibrates in perfect harmony with the Material Plane and destructively interferes with otherworldly beings. These vibrations reduce the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] of all [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^extraplanar|extraplanar]] creatures within 30 feet by 5 (for example, changing DR 10/magic to DR 5/magic). The bell has no clapper; to ring it, the bearer must attack it with an unarmed strike (this counts as an attack, with no attack roll needed, and does not damage the bell). The ringing lasts 1d6 rounds; stifling the ringing early is a standard action. It can ring for a maximum of 10 rounds per day.

Strong abjuration; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Banishment|banishment]]_; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp; Weight 10 lbs.
