# Concealing Pocket

This 4-inch-square cloth pocket has no powers unless it is sewn onto a garment (which takes 1d4 minutes). Once sewn, it changes its appearance to match the garment. Any magic item inside it cannot be magically detected, as if protected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]_. Hiding an item in the pocket gives the garment's wearer a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks to conceal the item. The pocket can be removed from the garment with a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check or an appropriate [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] check such as Craft (cloth) or Craft (leather); failing this roll by 5 or more gives the pocket the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition.

Faint illusion; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]_; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 500 gp.
