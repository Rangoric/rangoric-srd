# Gloves of Larceny

These supple beige calfskin gloves greatly enhance a thief's talents. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on Sleight of Hand checks.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must have 5 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]]; Price 2,500 gp; Cost 1,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
