# Amulet of the Spirits, Bones

Each different type of _amulet of the spirits_ grants its wearer powers from one type of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Spirits|spirit]] bond, but makes her vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with that spirit bond (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Spirit-Hunting|spirit-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Mysteries|mystery]] or spirit wears the amulet, that wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of her mystery or spirit powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of mystery or spirit powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Bones Spirit|Bones]]**: This amulet is a necklace made of petrified bones. Once per day as a swift action, the wearer can activate a bone ward, which causes a group of flying bones to encircle her, granting her a +2 deflection bonus to AC for 8 rounds. In addition, once per day as a swift action the wearer can target a creature within 30 feet, causing it to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 round (Will DC 13 negates).

Moderate necromancy; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entropic Shield|entropic shield]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scare|scare]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Bones Spirit|bones]] spirit; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
