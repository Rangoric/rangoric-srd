# Boots of Striding and Springing

These boots increase the wearer’s base land speed by 10 feet. The base speed is then modified based on the armor worn and any other modifiers. In addition to this striding ability (considered an enhancement bonus), these boots allow the wearer to make great leaps. She can jump with a +5 competence bonus on Athletics checks.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; Craft Wondrous Item, longstrider, creator must have 2 ranks in the Athletics skill; Price 5,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
