# Elixir of Sneaking

This draught of liquid grants the drinker the ability to walk softly and dampens sound around her slightly, granting a +10 competence bonus on Stealth checks for 1 hour.

Faint illusion; CL 5th; Craft Wondrous Item, silence; Price 250 gp.
