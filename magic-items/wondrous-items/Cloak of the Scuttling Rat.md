# Cloak of the Scuttling Rat

This unassuming cloak initially appears to be made of plain leather. When the wearer speaks the command word, however, the _cloak of the scuttling rat_ wraps itself around her, transforming her into a dire rat as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]_. The wearer can remain in dire rat form for up to 5 minutes per day. This duration need not be consecutive, but it must be used in 1-minute increments.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]_; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
