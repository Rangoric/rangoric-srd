# Mask of the Krenshar

This simple leather mask depicts an earless catlike creature. When worn, it melds into its wearer's face and vanishes, leaving the wearer's face just as it looked before. Three times per day the wearer can command the mask to appear to retract the wearer's skin, revealing an illusion of the wearer's glistening skull and musculature. Creatures within 100 feet who see this display are attacked against their Will at +3, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] (if the target has 6 or fewer Hit Dice) or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] (if the target has more than 6 HD) for 1d4 rounds. This is a mind-affecting fear effect and a glamer. Anyone who can see through illusions (such as by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_) is unaffected.

Faint illusion; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scare|scare]]_; Price 7,200 gp; Cost 3,600 gp; Weight 1 lb.
