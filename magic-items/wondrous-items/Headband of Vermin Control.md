# Headband of Vermin Control

The wearer of this black leather headband can spend 10 minutes attuning the headband to one touched [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Vermin|vermin]] of Medium or larger size. Once the headband is attuned, any creature wearing it can attempt [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks (handling and riding) to influence or control that vermin as if it were an animal and had animal-level intelligence. The attunement lasts until the vermin dies, the headband is destroyed, or the headband is attuned to a different vermin.

[[rangoric-srd/races/Duergar|Duergar]]-created items of this type are usually protected by a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dust Ward|dust ward]]* spell.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Vermin|control vermin]]*; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
