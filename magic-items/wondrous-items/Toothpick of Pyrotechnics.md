# Toothpick of Pyrotechnics

This wooden toothpick is useful for creating flashy diversions on stage or in a fight. Breaking the toothpick causes a blinding flash and a dark cloud of smoke to appear. Creatures other than the user within 20 feet are attacked against their Fortitude at +3, and on a hit are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1d4+1 rounds. The cloud of smoke spreads 20 feet in all directions, obscures all sight (including darkvision), and lasts for 3 rounds.

Faint evocation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pyrotechnics|pyrotechnics]]_; Price 400 gp; Cost 200 gp.
