# Prayer Wheel of Ethical Strength

Sacred or profane mantras cover the outer face of this cylindrical wooden block, which rotates around a decorative brass spindle. By holding the spindle in one hand while meditating or resting to regain _ki_, the bearer may attune his _ki_ points to the wheel. Once attuned, the bearer's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-strike|*ki* strikes]] count as good (if the wheel has sacred mantras) or evil (if the wheel has profane mantras) instead of lawful for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]]. This lasts until the next time the bearer rests or meditates to regain _ki_ points. A creature can only be attuned to one prayer wheel at a time, and a prayer wheel can only be attuned to one person at a time. Only creatures who can make lawfully-aligned _ki_ strikes can use a prayer wheel.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Align Weapon|align weapon]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
