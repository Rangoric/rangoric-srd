# Talisman of Freedom, Greater

A talisman works as described under [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#^talismans|Talismans]] in the rules for magic items on the body. A greater talisman works as a lesser talisman of the same type, but lasts far longer: it can give its benefit once per day.

The first time the wearer becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]], he is automatically affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_ for 3 rounds.

Moderate abjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_; Price 9,000 gp; Cost 4,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
