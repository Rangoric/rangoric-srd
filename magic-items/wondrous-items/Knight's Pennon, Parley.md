# Knight's Pennon, Parley

This narrow cloth flag is made to attach to the end of a knight's lance, though it can be flown from a spear, polearm, or even a staff. It has no effect if not mounted appropriately. A _knight's pennon_ grants different benefits depending on its color and design.

**Parley**: The white _knight's pennon of parley_, when displayed approaching a negotiation, grants the bearer a +4 enhancement bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks for 1 hour, so long as the bearer does not initiate any hostile action.

Moderate abjuration or transmutation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle's splendor]]_; Price 4,500 gp; Cost 2,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
