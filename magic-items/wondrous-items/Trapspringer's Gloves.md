# Trapspringer's Gloves

These studded leather gloves look similar to workman's gloves, though they are reinforced with numerous metal strips that the user can extend or retract as she wills, turning the tip of each finger into a customizable tool useful in examining and disarming traps. While worn, these gloves grant a +5 competence bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks made to disarm traps. In addition, the gloves grant the user a +1 luck bonus on all defenses against traps.

Faint divination; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Find Traps|find traps]]_; Price 4,000 gp; Cost 2,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
