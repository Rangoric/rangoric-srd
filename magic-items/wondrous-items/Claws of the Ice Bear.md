# Claws of the Ice Bear

Strapped to the hands, these spiked climbing claws grant a +2 competence bonus on Athletics checks and Acrobatics checks made when using both hands to aid in movement, and allow the wearer to ignore the normal penalties on those skills for slippery or icy surfaces. In addition, up to 3 times per day, the wearer can activate the claws as a swift action to gain the benefits of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_ for 1 round. The wearer cannot hold anything else in her hands while using claws of the ice bear. If used as a weapon, _claws of the ice bear_ function as [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Spiked Gauntlet|spiked gauntlets]].

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_; Price 1,300 gp; Cost 650 gp; Weight 1 lb.
