# Wind-Caller Compass

This light gray compass is made of fine metal and is engraved with various depictions of the sun, moon, and clouds. It allows a creature to increase or decrease the intensity of the wind four times per day as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Winds|alter winds]]_.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Winds|alter winds]]_; Price 4,400 gp; Cost 2,200 gp.
