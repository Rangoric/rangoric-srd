# Boots of the Burning Wastes

The wearer of these boots gains two special abilities that will help when traveling through immensely hot locales such as burning wastes. First, she is able to travel across the ground at normal speed without leaving tracks. Second, the boots also cool the wearer as if she were under the effects of an *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]* spell (against heat).

CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]*; Price 2,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
