# Flying Skiff

A flying skiff is a long, flat-bottomed vessel covered in metallic veins. Without a psychic pilot, it is in all ways a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Keelboat|keelboat]]. One Large [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Direct-Fire Ranged Siege Engines|direct-fire siege engine]] can be mounted on its front.

A control helmet that fits a Small, Medium or Large creature is linked by long, curling cables to the skiff's metal veins. Whenever a psychic creature (one with levels in an occult class, the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] feat or the ability to use psychic spell-like abilities) wears the helmet, the skiff gains these abilities:

- It can fly, with a maximum speed of 100 feet and an acceleration of 30 feet.
- It gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]] 5 while below half its starting hit points. Damage above that threshold must be repaired normally.

The pilot also gains these abilities:

- When she casts _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Intellect Fortress I|intellect fortress]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mental Barrier I|mental barrier]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Thought Shield I|thought shield]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tower of Iron Will I|tower of iron will]]_ (any version), the spell affects the skiff's whole crew. It ends for a creature that leaves the skiff before the spell's duration ends.
- She can aim and fire the skiff's siege engine with her mind, taking the same actions as normal, but can't reload it that way.
- Once per day, as a full-round action, she can transport the skiff and everyone on board, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_.
