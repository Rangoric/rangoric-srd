# Cloak of Human Guise

This plain cloak only has any effect when worn by a member of a half-human race, such as a [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]] or [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]]. It alters the wearer's appearance similarly to a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Hat of Disguise|hat of disguise]]*, but only to the extent that it conceals or alters the wearer's non-human physical traits so the creature appears fully human. Any feature that is plausibly human remains. For example, a half-orc wearing the cloak loses his green or gray skin color and pointed ears, has no visible tusks, and is otherwise completely able to pass as a human version of his normal self. Likewise, a half-elf wearing the cloak has round ears, humanlike eyes, and no other traits indicating elven ancestry. The wearer has no control over the specific guise, and those familiar with his normal appearance can recognize him in his human guise.

Faint illusion; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]*; Price 900 gp; Cost 450 gp; Weight 1 lb.
