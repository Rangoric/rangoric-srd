# Alluring Golden Apple

This enchanted apple appears to be made of solid gold. When dropped or thrown, it draws the attention of the nearest hostile creature within 20 feet. The apple attacks the creature’s Will at +3, and on a hit it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]]_ as it focuses its undivided attention on the golden apple. If the attack misses on a later round, it realizes it has been affected by magic and the apple is not actually gold. While focusing on the apple, the target takes a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks. The apple is consumed upon being dropped or thrown.

Faint enchantment; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beguiling Gift|beguiling gift]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]]_; Price 400 gp; Cost 200 gp; Weight 1 lb.
