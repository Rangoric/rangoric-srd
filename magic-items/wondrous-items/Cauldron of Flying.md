# Cauldron of Flying

This iron pot is large enough for two Medium humanoids to stand in comfortably. Upon command, the cauldron and up to 500 pounds of additional weight can fly as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Overland Flight|overland flight]]_. The cauldron moves under the direction of the person who spoke the activating command word. It can carry up to double its capacity, but doing so reduces its speed to 30 feet. It can hover in place without the need for an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|fly]]. The cauldron provides partial [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|cover]] for anyone standing in it. It has a hardness of 10 and 60 hit points.

Moderate transmutation; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Overland Flight|overland flight]]_; Price 40,000 gp; Cost 20,000 gp; Weight 100 lbs.
