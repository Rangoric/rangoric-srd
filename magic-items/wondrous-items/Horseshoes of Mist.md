# Horseshoes of Mist

When a horse is shod with these horseshoes, they give off a puff of mist each time a hoof strikes the earth. On the rider's command, the mount and the rider are both affected as if subject to a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_ spell. If for some reason the rider and the mount become separated from one another while in _gaseous form_, the effect automatically ends for both. The horseshoes can be used for 10 minutes per day. The duration need not be consecutive, but it must be spent in 1-minute increments.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_; Price 27,000 gp; Cost 13,500 gp; Weight 12 lbs.
