# Seafoam Shawl

This shawl of delicate lace can be activated once per day to grant the benefits of the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fins to Feet|fins to feet]]_ spell, transforming the wearer's lower fins or appendages into feet whenever he is on dry land—unless dismissed, this effect lasts for 5 hours.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fins to Feet|fins to feet]]_; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
