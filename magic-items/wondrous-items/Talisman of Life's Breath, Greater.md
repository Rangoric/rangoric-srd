# Talisman of Life's Breath, Greater

A talisman works as described under [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#^talismans|Talismans]] in the rules for magic items on the body. A greater talisman works as a lesser talisman of the same type, but lasts far longer: it can give its benefit once per day.

The first time the wearer dies, he is automatically affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]_.

Moderate conjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]_; Price 35,000 gp; Cost 17,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
