# Apple of Eternal Sleep

This ripe red apple looks fresh and delicious. Should anyone bite into it (even a small taste), he must make an immediate DC 19 Will saving throw or fall into an eternal slumber as though by the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|witch]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-grand-hex|grand hex]] of the same name. The victim can only be awakened by a casting of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]*, or by a kiss from someone of royal blood.

Moderate enchantment; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Potion|Brew Potion]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|witch]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Eternal Slumber (Hex)|eternal slumber]] grand hex; Price 2,500 gp; Cost 1,250 gp.
