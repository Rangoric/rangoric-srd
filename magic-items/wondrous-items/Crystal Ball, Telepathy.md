# Crystal Ball, Telepathy

This is the most common form of scrying device, a crystal sphere about 6 inches in diameter. A character can use the device to see over virtually any distance or into other planes of existence, as with the spell scrying (+6 vs Will).

Certain crystal balls have additional powers that can be used through the crystal ball on the target viewed.

| Crystal Ball Type                | Market Price |
| -------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Crystal ball with telepathy (\*) | 70,000 gp    |

\* _The viewer is able to send and receive silent mental messages with the person appearing in the crystal ball. Once per day the character may attempt to implant a suggestion (as the spell, +4 vs Will) as well._

Moderate divination; CL 10th; Craft Wondrous Item, scrying (plus any additional spells put into item); Price 70,000 gp; Weight 7 lb.
