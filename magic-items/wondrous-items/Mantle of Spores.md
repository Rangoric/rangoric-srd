# Mantle of Spores

This mantle grants the wearer a +4 resistance bonus on defenses against disease. Once per day, the wearer can flourish the cape as a swift action to surround himself with a cloud of deadly spores. Creatures adjacent to the wearer take 1d6 points of damage from the spores, plus 1d3 points of damage per round for 3 rounds as the spores grow into fuzzy green mold. After these rounds the mold becomes harmless, and can be washed away with a minute of scrubbing. The spores can be destroyed by making a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check on an affected creature, or by effects that remove diseases. The spores are a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]] effect.

Faint necromancy; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]_; Price 13,400 gp; Cost 6,700 gp; Weight 1 lb.
