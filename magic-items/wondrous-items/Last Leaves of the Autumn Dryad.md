# Last Leaves of the Autumn Dryad

This vibrant cluster of leaves constantly changes hue through the different colors of the autumn season, from brown and red to orange and yellow. Once per day, the bearer may touch the leaves to her tongue to assume _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tree Shape|tree shape]]_ (as the spell) for up to 8 hours, appearing as a healthy tree that is lush with thousands of autumn leaves for the duration of the effect.

While in tree form, a creature may release these leaves upon the wind, consciously controlling them as a scrying sensor with the abilities of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Eye|arcane eye]]_. The leaves have the statistics of an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/swarms/Army Ant Swarm|army ant swarm]], except the swarm has [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^plant|plant traits]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] 5, and vulnerability to fire.

The creature may shape the scrying leaves into a semblance of her face, allowing her to speak through the leaves. The leaves' voice is a quiet, dry rustle, and they are capable of conveying most of the creature's intended facial expressions (they can smile, frown, and so on). If the creature has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] class ability or is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Fey Bloodline|fey bloodline]], she may have any _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Plants|speak with plants]]_ spell she casts originate from the leaves instead of herself, and her _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tree Shape|tree shape]]_ does not prevent her from casting these spells.

If the scrying leaves fail to return before the creature returns to its normal shape, the magic item does not function for 1 week. During this time, the leaves fade to brown and appear to have a light coating of frost on them.

Moderate divination and transmutation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Eye|arcane eye]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tree Shape|tree shape]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Whispering Wind|whispering wind]]_; Price 52,000 gp; Cost 26,000 gp.
