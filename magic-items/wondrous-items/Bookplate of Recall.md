# Bookplate of Recall

This metal bookplate is inscribed with mystical words in Draconic, leaving space for a single written name. When the bookplate is glued to the inside cover of a book, the named individual may speak the title of the book to summon it as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Instant Summons|instant summons]]*. This ability functions once per day.

Strong conjuration; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Instant Summons|instant summons]]*; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 500 gp.
