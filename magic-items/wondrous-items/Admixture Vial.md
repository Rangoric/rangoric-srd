# Admixture Vial

Once per day, an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]] can use this simple-looking glass vial to combine two extracts into one usable extract as if he had the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Combine Extracts (Discovery)|combine extracts]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]]. An *admixture vial* can combine extracts of 3rd level or lower. Extracts combined with an *admixture vial* cannot be combined again using this item or the combine extracts discovery.

Moderate universal; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must be an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Combine Extracts (Discovery)|combine extracts]] discovery; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp.
