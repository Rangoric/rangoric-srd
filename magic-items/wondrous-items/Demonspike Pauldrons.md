# Demonspike Pauldrons

These black iron pauldrons are studded with jagged spikes of various lengths. _Demonspike pauldrons_ act as _+2 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Armor Spikes|armor spikes]]_ and also deal 1d2 [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage on a successful grapple attack or melee attack made with the pauldrons. If these pauldrons are worn with normal armor spikes, increase the damage done by the armor spikes to 1d6 (Small) or 1d8 (Medium), and use the greater of the two enhancement bonuses to attack and damage—either from the armor spikes (if any) or the _demonspike pauldrons_.

Moderate necromancy; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_; Price 14,350 gp; Cost 7,175 gp; Weight 8 lbs.
