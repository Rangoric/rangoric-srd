# Physician's Spectacles

These nondescript, brass-rimmed spectacles have green-tinted lenses and are favored by practicing doctors and adventurers alike. When worn, they grant the wearer a constant awareness of poisons and diseases as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Diagnose Disease|diagnose disease]]* spells. Also, once per day on command the wearer can touch a single creature to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]* on that creature.

Faint conjuration and divination; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Diagnose Disease|diagnose disease]]*; Price 4,900 gp; Cost 2,450 gp.
