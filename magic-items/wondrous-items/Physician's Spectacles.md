# Physician's Spectacles

These nondescript, brass-rimmed spectacles have green-tinted lenses and are favored by practicing doctors and adventurers alike. When worn, they grant the wearer a constant awareness of poisons and diseases as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Diagnose Disease|diagnose disease]]_ spells. Also, once per day on command the wearer can touch a single creature to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]_ on that creature.

Faint conjuration and divination; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Diagnose Disease|diagnose disease]]_; Price 4,900 gp; Cost 2,450 gp.
