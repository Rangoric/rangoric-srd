# Bane Baldric

This ornate sash of embroidered velvet stretches across the chest from shoulder to waist. If the wearer is an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]], she is treated as five levels higher when using her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-bane|bane]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-greater-bane|greater bane]] abilities. If the wearer is not an inquisitor, she gains the bane ability of a 5th-level inquisitor, but must first attune a light or one-handed melee weapon to the baldric by hanging it from the cloth for 24 hours, and can only use the bane ability with the attuned weapon. Attuning a new weapon to the baldric ends the attunement for the previous weapon.

Moderate conjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster I]]*, creator must be an inquisitor; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
