# Knight's Pennon, Battle

This narrow cloth flag is made to attach to the end of a knight's lance, though it can be flown from a spear, polearm, or even a staff. It has no effect if not mounted appropriately. A *knight's pennon* grants different benefits depending on its color and design.

**Battle**: The red *knight's pennon of battle* allows its bearer to benefit from *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]* once per day.

Moderate abjuration or transmutation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]*; Price 4,500 gp; Cost 2,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
