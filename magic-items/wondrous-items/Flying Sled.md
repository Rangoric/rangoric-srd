# Flying Sled

A flying sled is a small vessel covered in metallic veins that holds one occupant. Without a psychic pilot, it is in all ways a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rowboat|rowboat]].

A control helmet that fits a Small or Medium creature is linked by cables to the sled's metal veins. When a psychic creature (one with levels in an occult class, the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] feat or the ability to use psychic spell-like abilities) wears the helmet, the sled gains these abilities:

- It can fly, with a maximum speed of 100 feet and an acceleration of 30 feet.
- It gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]] 2 while below half its starting hit points. Damage above that threshold must be repaired normally.

The pilot also gains this ability:

- Once per day, as a full-round action, she can transport herself and the sled between the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Astral Plane|Astral Plane]] and the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Material Plane|Material Plane]], as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_.

Strong conjuration; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Overland Flight|overland flight]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_; Price 80,000 gp; Cost 40,000 gp; Weight 100 lb.
