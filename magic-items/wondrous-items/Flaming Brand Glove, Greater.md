# Flaming Brand Glove, Greater

This single red glove appears to be a gauntlet, except that it is made of thick sections of oil hardened leather. Representations of fire elementals decorate the leather. Upon command, the wearer of the Glove can summon or dismiss a maximized *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Blade|flame blade]]*, per the spell, in the gloved hand. With a successful touch attack, the wearer deals 12 fire damage with this flaming weapon. The blade can only be used for twenty minutes each day. If use beyond this limit is attempted, the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Blade|flame blade]]* power simply does not function. In addition to the weapon power, the wearer gains fire resistance 12.

CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Maximize Spell|Maximize Spell]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Blade|flame blade]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*; Price 38,000 gp.
