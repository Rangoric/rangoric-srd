# Stone of Conjuring Earth Elementals

A stone of this nature is typically an oddly shaped bit of roughly polished rock. On command as a full-round action, the bearer may summon an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elementals/Earth Elemental|earth elemental]]. If there is an unbroken piece of unworked stone of at least Huge size nearby, the stone conjures a Huge earth elemental as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]_. If there is no such stone available (for example, if there is only sand, earth, worked stone, or smaller pieces of stone), the stone conjures a Large earth elemental as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]_. The stone cannot summon another elemental until the previous one is dismissed, killed, or the duration of its summoning expires.

This is a lesser version of the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals|stone of controlling earth elementals]]_, which calls its elementals as stronger summoning spells and costs more.

Moderate conjuration; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]_; Price 90,000 gp; Cost 45,000 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
