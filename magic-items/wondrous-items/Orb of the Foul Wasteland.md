# Orb of the Foul Wasteland

This grapefruit-sized sphere of glossy black stone glows with faint whorls of orange fire deep within its inky substance. When held in hand, it can be commanded to unleash a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dread Bolt|dread bolt]]_ once per day. When used as an additional focus for casting a spell with the evil descriptor, the bearer gains a +1 bonus to her caster level for these spells. It bestows one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^permanent-negative-levels|permanent negative level]] on any good creature holding or carrying it. The negative level remains as long as the creature has the orb and disappears immediately when the orb is no longer held or carried. This negative level cannot be overcome in any way (including by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]_ spells) while the bearer has the orb.

Moderate conjuration; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dread Bolt|dread bolt]]_, creator must be evil; Price 18,000 gp; Cost 9,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
