# Fan of Dusting

This magical fan distributes [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/dusts/Fey Dusts|dust]] in a semi-circular burst. (Its effective area is similar to the area of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]* spell.) The fan is made with a series of hollow spines that hold the magic *dusts* for release into the area of effect. Residual air turbulence caused by the fan confines the suspended dust—treated as a cloud, once released—in the initial area of effect. A fan can deliver a pinch (or pinches) with a 15-ft. range.

CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Secret Chest|secret chest]]*; Price 2,250 gp; Cost 1,125 gp + 90 XP; Weight 1 lb.
