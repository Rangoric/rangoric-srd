# Glowing Gourd

This gourd has been hollowed out and carved with the symbol of a good-aligned deity. It sheds light as a candle. If the gourd is smashed onto the ground, the 40-foot-radius area around the crushed gourd is affected as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hallow|hallow]]_ with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_ spell fixed to it. This magic item is often crafted from a pumpkin, though other types of gourds, or even large turnips, can be used.

Moderate evocation [good]; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hallow|hallow]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_; Price 4,250 gp; Cost 2,125 gp; Weight 8 lbs.
