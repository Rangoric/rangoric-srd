# Juggernaut's Pauldrons

These massive pauldrons take the shape of a pair of clenched fists made of beaten bronze. They bestow a +4 bonus on CMD, and grant the wearer the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Ferocity|ferocity]] ability. On command, the wearer can increase its size, as though subject to an _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]_ spell, and can revert to regular size with another command. Three times per day, as an immediate action after the pauldrons' wearer kills an opponent, the wearer gains the benefit of the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deadly Juggernaut|deadly juggernaut]]_ spell for 1 minute.

Faint necromancy; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deadly Juggernaut|deadly juggernaut]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]_; Price 40,000 gp; Cost 20,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
