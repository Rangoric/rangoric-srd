# Serpent Belt, Greater

This belt functions as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Serpent Belt|serpent belt]]_, except it grants the wearer a +2 enhancement bonus to Strength and Dexterity. Treat this as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the belt is worn. When the wearer animates the belt as a venomous or constrictor snake, she may speak a second command word to give the snake the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Advanced Creature|advanced]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/celestial-creature/Celestial Creature|celestial]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Entropic Creature|entropic]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creature/Fiendish Creature|fiendish]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Resolute Creature|resolute]] simple template. Adding a planar template changes the snake's alignment to that of the corresponding planes.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat's grace]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster II|summon monster II]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally II|summon nature's ally II]]_; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
