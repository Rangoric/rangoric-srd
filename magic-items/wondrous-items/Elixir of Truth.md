# Elixir of Truth

This elixir forces the individual drinking it to say nothing but the truth for 10 minutes (+3 vs Will). She is compelled to answer any questions put to her in that time, but each question attacks her Will again at +3. If one of these secondary attacks misses, she doesn’t break free of the truth-compelling enchantment but also doesn’t have to answer that particular question. No more than one question can be asked each round. This is a mind-affecting compulsion enchantment.

Faint enchantment; CL 5th; Craft Wondrous Item, zone of truth; Price 500 gp.
