# Resistant Padding

This padded cloth outfit is worn beneath medium and heavy armor; its effects work with that of any armor worn and it does not count as wearing two sets of armor. Resistant padding can be worn with any suit of armor and is enchanted to protect its wearer from one or more types of energy damage. Choose from fire, acid, electricity, sonic, or cold resistance. The suit absorbs 10 points of damage per round from its protected energies.
