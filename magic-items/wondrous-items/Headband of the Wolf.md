# Headband of the Wolf

This silver band is cast to resemble a pack of hunting wolves. On command, the wearer can gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] ability with a range of 10 feet (20 feet upwind, 5 feet downwind) as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/animal-foci/Wolf (Animal Focus)|wolf aspect]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter|hunter]]’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]] class feature. If the wearer is a hunter with that aspect, this headband instead increases the wearer's effective hunter level for the wolf aspect by 8 levels.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bloodhound|bloodhound]]*, creator must have the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]] class feature; Price 5,000 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
