# Pipes of Dissolution

Carvings of winter scenes decorate this set of pan pipes. Activating the pipes requires a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (wind instruments) check. Creatures within 30 feet gain a +2 competence bonus on caster level checks to dispel magical effects and overcome [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]]. Maintaining the performance is challenging, requiring a move action and a new [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] check each round, with the DC increasing by +1 for each round the instrument is played. The magic ends when the player voluntarily stops or fails the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] check, after which the pipes cannot be used again for 1 hour.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
