# Amulet of the Spirits, Flame

Each different type of _amulet of the spirits_ grants its wearer powers from one type of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Spirits|spirit]] bond, but makes her vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with that spirit bond (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Spirit-Hunting|spirit-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Mysteries|mystery]] or spirit wears the amulet, that wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of her mystery or spirit powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of mystery or spirit powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Flame Spirit|Flame]]**: This amulet is a single fire opal hanging from a chain of brass. Twice per day as a swift action, the wearer can activate a fire ward. The next time she is struck with a melee weapon, the creature making the attack takes 1d6+4 points of fire damage. The ward lasts for 1 minute, after which it fades away if not expended. In addition, twice per day as a swift action the wearer can cause a creature within 30 feet to become vulnerable to fire until the end of the wearer's turn. Whenever the creature takes fire damage, it takes half again as much damage (+50%). If the creature is already vulnerable to fire, this hex has no effect. Fire immunity and resistances apply as normal, and a miss by the effect that caused the damage reduces it as normal.

Moderate evocation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Flame Spirit|flame]] spirit; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
