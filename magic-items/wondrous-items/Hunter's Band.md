# Hunter's Band

This simple headband of braided hide grants a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks and inures the wearer to the strain of long travel, as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tireless Pursuit|tireless pursuit]]_ spell. If the wearer is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]], the _hunter's band_ increases his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] bonus on attack rolls by +1 for all his favored enemies. If the ranger has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-hunters-bond|hunter's bond]] (companions) class feature, this increase also applies to the attack roll bonus his allies gain from that ability.

Faint transmutation; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Instant Enemy|instant enemy]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tireless Pursuit|tireless pursuit]]_, creator must have 5 ranks in Fieldcraft; Price 11,000 gp; Cost 5,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
