# Pipe of the Little Folk

This small wooden pipe is said to have been first created by a Halfling god, and the knowledge of its creation passed on to the Halflings. When smoked by a Halfling, it bestows upon the user the effects of a bless spell that lasts for six hours. If any non-halfling partakes of the Pipe, they must make a Fortitude Save (DC 26) or be instantly transformed into a Halfling-sized version of themselves (i.e. 2 ½ to 3 feet tall). Characters so transformed can only regain their original form after a full night’s sleep (8 hours). A favorite game of Halflings is to pass this around the campfire when humans are visiting. They consider it a test of character as to how the humans react to their new size, and quite a good jest, too.
