# Belt of Superior Maneuvers +5

This thin, black canvas belt is typically tied loosely about the waist to secure a robe, a kimono, or a martial artist's dobok. Three times per day as a free action, the wearer can select a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] and for 1 round receive an enhancement bonus of +1 to +5 on combat maneuver checks for this combat maneuver and a +1 to +5 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when defending against this maneuver. Additionally, when worn by a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler|brawler]], the belt grants one additional use of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-martial-flexibility|martial flexibility]] class feature each day.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]*, creator's caster level must be at least triple the belt's bonus (15); Price 50,000 gp; Cost 25,000 gp.
