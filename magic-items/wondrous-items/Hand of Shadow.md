# Hand of Shadow

This is a preserved human hand, bound with silver wire and set with an onyx gem in the palm. This [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Necromantic Foci|necromantic focus]] provides a bonus of +3 to one shadetouched ability check per day, as if the wielder were attuned to a source of necromantic energy ([[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#The Gray Road|the Gray Road]]).

CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Necromantic Focus|Craft Necromantic Focus]], access to a source of necromantic energy providing a bonus of at least +6; Price 6,000 gp.
