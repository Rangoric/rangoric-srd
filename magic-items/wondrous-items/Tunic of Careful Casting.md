# Tunic of Careful Casting

This woolen garment is decorated with a pattern of linked chains embroidered in silver and black thread. It assists the wearer with maintaining focus during spellcasting, granting a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]].

Faint abjuration; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Casting|Combat Casting]]; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
