# Boots of Escape

These sturdy leather-soled cloth boots have straps at the ankles and calves to ensure a snug fit. Once per day, when the wearer of the boots is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^grappling|grappled]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Pinned (Condition)|pinned]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]], she may transfer herself to any spot within 30 feet as if using a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]_ spell. A [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]] wearing these boots may instead transfer herself up to 400 feet away.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]] or a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] specialized in the school of conjuration; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
