# Mask of Beauty

While worn, this mask transforms the wearer into an idealized version of beauty. The wearer of the *mask of beauty* gains a +6 enhancement bonus to Charisma.

Moderate illusion; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eagle’s Splendor|eagle’s splendor]]*; Price 36,000 gp; Cost 18,000 gp + 1,440 XP.
