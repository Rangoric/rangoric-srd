# Bowl of Conjuring Water Elementals

This large container is usually fashioned from silver with a rippled pattern. It is about 1 foot in diameter and half that deep. The bowl must be filled with fresh or salt water to function. On command as a full-round action, the bearer may summon a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Water Elemental|water elemental]]. If the bowl is filled with salt water, it conjures a Huge water elemental as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]_. If filled with fresh water, it conjures a Large water elemental as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]_. Conjuring an elemental empties the bowl. The bowl cannot summon another elemental until the previous one is dismissed, killed, or the duration of its summoning expires.

This is a lesser version of the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals|bowl of commanding water elementals]]_, which calls its elementals as stronger summoning spells and costs more.

Moderate conjuration; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]_; Price 90,000 gp; Cost 45,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
