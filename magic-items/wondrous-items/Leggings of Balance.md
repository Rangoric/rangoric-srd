# Leggings of Balance

These leggings allow their wearer to remain standing no matter what the circumstances. The wearer cannot be [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|tripped]] or knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. The wearer may still fall off of things, but always lands on his feet. Characters with these leggings remain standing even if killed or knocked unconscious. In addition, the wearer’s Dexterity is increased by an enhancement bonus of +4.

Faint abjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]*; Price 17,250 gp; Cost 8,625 gp + 690 XP.
