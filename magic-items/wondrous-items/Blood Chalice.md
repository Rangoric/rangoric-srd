# Blood Chalice

This large iron chalice allows a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Blood Mage|blood mage]] to catch the life force of others as it pours out in their blood. If used to catch the blood from a new wound, the chalice can store one half of the hit points lost. These hit points are used as per the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blood Power|Blood Power]] feat but, if they are not used, the hit points disperse at a rate of one per day. The chalice can store a maximum of 60 hit points and becomes a dark red color as it approaches this maximum.

CL 18th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]*; Price 120,000 gp.
