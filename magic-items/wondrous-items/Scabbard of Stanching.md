# Scabbard of Stanching

This fine, red leather sheath is decorated with gold filigree. It fits any bladed slashing weapon. While worn, a _scabbard of stanching_ protects against [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage. Anytime the wearer suffers a bleed effect, the scabbard automatically stanches it. It has no effect on bleed damage from effects that require a DC 16 or higher caster level or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check.

Faint conjuration (healing); CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 2,500 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
