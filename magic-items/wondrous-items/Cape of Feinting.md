# Cape of Feinting

This hooded cape is crafted from fine fabric with colorful embroidery and elegant weasel-fur trim. Three times per day, the wearer can spend a standard action to purposely miss an opponent, performing a dramatic feint that causes that foe to lose its Dexterity bonus to AC until the wearer's next turn. If a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler|swashbuckler]] wearing a *cloak of feinting* performs the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Superior Feint (Deed)|superior feint]] deed or uses this cape's ability, the foe is also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] until the start of the swashbuckler's next turn if the foe fails a DC 13 Will save.

Moderate enchantment; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]*, creator must be able to perform the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Superior Feint (Deed)|superior feint]] deed; Price 14,000 gp; Cost 7,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
