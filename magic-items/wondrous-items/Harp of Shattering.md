# Harp of Shattering

This finely crafted masterwork harp fits comfortably in one hand, but it requires two hands to play. The harp has 10 charges, which refresh each day at daybreak. By making a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (string) check, the bearer can produce the following effects:

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break|break]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shatter|shatter]]* (2 charges)

Faint evocation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break|break]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shatter|shatter]]*; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 5 lbs.
