# Verdant Vine

Consisting of a vine with five green berries, a _verdant vine_ is worn tied around the wrist. Once per day on command, the wearer can pinch a berry off the vine, and throw it up to 30 feet away, like a splash weapon. Viciously thorny vines erupt in a 20-foot radius from the point of impact, even if the berry misses its target. Creatures within the area are attacked against their Reflex at +6, and on a hit become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]], as the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_, as the vines coil around the creature's legs. The vines persist, but at the start of the affected creature's turn, the vines attack it again, and a miss ends the effect. If the attack hits, the creature also takes 1d4 points of damage.

A DC 20 Strength check, made as a standard action, allows an affected creature to break free and end the effect. Failing the check causes the vines to constrict, dealing 1d4 points of damage.

Faint conjuration; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plant Growth|plant growth]]_; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp.
