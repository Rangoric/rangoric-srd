# Amulet of the Spirits, Waves

Each different type of _amulet of the spirits_ grants its wearer powers from one type of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Spirits|spirit]] bond, but makes her vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with that spirit bond (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Spirit-Hunting|spirit-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Mysteries|mystery]] or spirit wears the amulet, that wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of her mystery or spirit powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of mystery or spirit powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Waves Spirit|Waves]]**: This amulet is a small conch hanging from a cord of woven seaweed. Once per day as a swift action, the wearer can activate a ward of mist, granting herself concealment as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]_ spell. The mist dissipates after it causes two attacks to miss because of concealment or after 1 minute, whichever comes first. In addition, as a swift action once per day the wearer can cause one creature within 30 feet to become more susceptible to the sapping powers of cold for 1 minute. When a creature takes cold damage while under this effect, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] for 1 round. If the creature takes cold damage while already entangled by this effect, the duration of the entangled condition increases by 1 round.

Moderate illusion; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Touch|chill touch]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Waves Spirit|waves]] spirit; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
