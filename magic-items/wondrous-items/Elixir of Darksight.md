# Elixir of Darksight

This dark, syrupy draught doubles the range of the drinker's [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] and also enables her to see through _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]_ when using darkvision. The effects last for 1 hour.

Moderate transmutation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkvision|darkvision]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]_; Price 1,200 gp; Cost 600 gp.
