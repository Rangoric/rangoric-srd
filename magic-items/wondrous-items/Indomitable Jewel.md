# Indomitable Jewel

This black opal, set in a burnished copper pendant, shifts color with its wearer's mood. While the wearer is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] by a mind-affecting effect, at the start of his turn he can take 2 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|ability damage]] to each of Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma to take a standard action that turn anyway (in place of the move action, if nauseated), even if he couldn't otherwise act. If several such conditions affect him, he must take the ability damage for each to take his standard action. Ability damage from the jewel can't be prevented or reduced, and must heal naturally. The jewel also makes the wearer immune to sleep caused by mind-affecting effects.

Moderate abjuration; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Thought Shield I|thought shield I]]_; Price 12,000 gp; Cost 6,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
