# Lucky Horseshoe, Communal

This item looks like and works as a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Lucky Horseshoe|lucky horseshoe]]. Also, if it's hung prominently over the entrance of a building for at least 8 hours, it brings good luck to those who stay inside: a creature who spends at least 8 consecutive hours in the building gains a lucky horseshoe's benefit for 24 hours. One communal lucky horseshoe can give its benefit to any number of creatures per day, but each creature can benefit from only one lucky horseshoe at a time. Once a creature calls on a lucky horseshoe's power, she can't benefit from another lucky horseshoe for 24 hours, though time she spends in the building meanwhile counts toward the 8 consecutive hours she needs for the next day's benefit.

Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hallow|hallow]]_; Price 15,000 gp; Cost 7,500 gp; Weight 2 lb.
