# Shrunken Head

The mouth of this shriveled head is stitched shut with coarse thread. A shrunken head works held in one hand or worn in the neck slot, hung from a thong.

Once per day as an immediate action, the owner can make the head absorb a mind-affecting spell of up to 5th level that targets her; this protects only her, not other targets. The head's eyes then open and burn with pale green fire for 1 round per level of the absorbed spell. While they burn, the owner can release the energy as a standard action as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]_, the ray giving 1 negative level per level of the absorbed spell.

Used as an additional focus component for a spell with the fear descriptor, a shrunken head adds 1 to any save DC of the spell, whether or not it has absorbed a spell.

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]_; Price 90,000 gp; Cost 45,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
