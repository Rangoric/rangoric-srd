# Magic Talking Board

This rectangular wooden board shows an arch of letters, numbers and short affirmative and negative messages, and comes with a small, heart-shaped, three-legged pointer. It can be used in place of a mundane [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Talking Board|talking board]], but its magic doesn't depend on a haunt or a psychically sensitive person to make contact. Used for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics#Automatic Writing|automatic writing]] occult skill unlock in place of a mundane talking board, it gives a +5 circumstance bonus on the Linguistics check.

Once per day, the board can call the spirit of one dead humanoid, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Spirit|call spirit]]_. The board attacks the spirit’s Will at +7; if it misses, a malevolent spirit always comes in its place. The spirit doesn't appear or speak, but answers through the board, guiding the pointer to letters at one word per round for as long as the user concentrates. Up to four creatures can use the board at once, and the spirit takes a penalty on its Will Defense equal to the number of users beyond the first.

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Spirit|call spirit]]_; Price 20,000 gp; Cost 10,000 gp; Weight 2 lb.
