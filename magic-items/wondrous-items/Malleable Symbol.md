# Malleable Symbol

This undistinguished metal lump has no powers in its default state. By concentrating on it for 1 minute, a creature who worships a deity may transform it into a masterwork holy symbol of that deity. Whenever this holy symbol is used as a focus for [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channeled energy]], as a free action the bearer can alter the channel area to one of the following options:

- 10-foot burst centered anywhere within 30 feet
- 60-foot line
- 30-foot cone

The symbol reverts to its nondescript lump form after being away from its bearer for 24 hours.

Moderate conjuration; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds, Mass|mass cure light wounds]]_, creator must be able to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]]; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
