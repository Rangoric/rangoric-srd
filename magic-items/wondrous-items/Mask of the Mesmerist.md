# Mask of the Mesmerist

This carved wooden mask in the shape of a mythical creature covers its wearer's face, leaving only two eyeholes. A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist|mesmerist]] wearing it adds 10 feet to the range of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-hypnotic-stare|hypnotic stare]], and creatures under his hypnotic stare take a –2 penalty on attack rolls against him.

Once per day, if the wearer has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-tricks|mesmerist tricks]] class feature, he can implant a trick without spending a use of it, and that trick doesn't count against his limit of tricks active at once.

Faint divination and illusion; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aversion (Spell)|aversion]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Focused Scrutiny|focused scrutiny]]_; Price 25,000 gp; Cost 12,500 gp; Weight 4 lb.
