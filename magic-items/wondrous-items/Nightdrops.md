# Nightdrops

This narrow vial holds 5 doses of viscous liquid. When dripped onto a willing creature's eyes, it gives the creature [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] for 1 hour. The affected creature takes a –2 penalty on defenses against light-based spells and effects and gains the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Sensitivity|light sensitivity]] weakness. A dose affects one creature, regardless of how many eyes it has.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]_; Price 250 gp; Cost 125 gp.
