# Orb of Utter Chaos

This globe of varicolored material constantly shifts hue and texture, from steel-hard to spongy or even gelatinous. The bearer of the orb is constantly protected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entropic Shield|entropic shield]]_. Three times per day, when the bearer holds the orb and casts a spell with the polymorph subtype, chaotic descriptor, or one that gives creatures the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] condition, she may modify her caster level for that spell by 1d6–2.

Once per day the bearer may hold the orb and command it to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_. If the dispel check succeeds, the wielder absorbs the unraveled magical energy through the orb and gains the benefit of an _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]_ spell.

The orb bestows one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^permanent-negative-levels|permanent negative level]] on any lawful creature holding or carrying it. The negative level remains as long as the creature has the orb and disappears immediately when the orb is no longer held or carried. This negative level cannot be overcome in any way (including by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]_ spells) while the bearer has the orb.
