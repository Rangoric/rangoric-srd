# Helm of Electric Radiance

The *helm of electric radiance* is similar to a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Helm of Brilliance|helm of brilliance]]*, except its spells focus on electricity rather than fire. Other aspects of the helm, such as detecting and damaging undead, remain unchanged. The helm has 10 sapphires that can be used for *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]]*, 20 pieces of amber that can be used for *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]*, 30 pieces of petrified wood that can be used for *shocking sphere* (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flaming Sphere|flaming sphere]]* but dealing electricity damage), and 40 opals that can be used for *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*. In addition, the wearer gains resistance to electricity 30 and his weapons become *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Shock|shocking]]* weapons instead of *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]* weapons.

Strong varied; CL 13th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flaming Sphere|flaming sphere]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]*; Price 125,000 gp; Cost 62,500 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
