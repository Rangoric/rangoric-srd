# Vambraces of the Tactician

These vambraces change their appearance depending on who wears them. If the wearer is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]], these vambraces display the symbol of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-order|order]]. When unworn or in the hands of a non-cavalier, they appear to be an ordinary pair of steel vambraces. A cavalier wearing these vambraces is considered two class levels higher for purposes of determining the effects of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-challenge|challenge]] class feature. Furthermore, once per day as a swift action, a cavalier with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-tactician|tactician]] class feature can increase the area in which he grants teamwork feats. Before the end of his next turn, when the cavalier grants his allies a teamwork feat, he can grant it to all allies within 60 feet, though they must still be able to see and hear him.

Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]_; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 3 lbs.
