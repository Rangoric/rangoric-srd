# Talisman of Healing Power, Lesser

A talisman works as described under [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#^talismans|Talismans]] in the rules for magic items on the body. A lesser talisman has only enough magic to protect its wearer once, after which it crumbles to dust.

The first time the wearer is reduced to fewer than half his maximum hit points, he automatically heals 4d8+7 hit points.

Moderate conjuration; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|cure critical wounds]]_; Price 2,400 gp; Cost 1,200 gp; Weight 1 lb.
