# Summon-Slave Crystal

This thumb-sized crystalline skull is carved with necromantic runes. Once per day, a spellcaster can grasp the stone while casting a summoning spell. When the spellcasting is finished, the caster may immediately attempt to possess the summoned creature, as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Jar|magic jar]]_, except as a free action. It hits the creature automatically, though [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] still applies. If successful, the caster possesses the creature's body until the duration of the summoning spell ends, at which time his mind returns to his body, the creature's mind returns to its body, and the creature disappears.

Moderate necromancy; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Jar|magic jar]]_; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp.
