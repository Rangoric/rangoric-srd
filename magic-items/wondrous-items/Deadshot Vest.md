# Deadshot Vest

This rough vest of black snakeskin is decorated with a fringe of rawhide tassels, each knotted around a bit of bone or a small animal skull. A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger|gunslinger]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Deadeye (Deed)|deadeye]] deed reduces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]] point cost to use that deed by 1 (minimum 1 grit).

In addition, once per day when the wearer reduces a creature to 0 or fewer hit points with a firearm she can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Knell|death knell]]_ (+3 vs Will) against that target as a swift action. She does not have to touch the target to activate this ability, but her range increment penalty for shooting the target also applies to the attack bonus of the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Knell|death knell]]_ (for example, if the range increment penalty is –2, the bonus is +1).

Faint necromancy; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Knell|death knell]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]_; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
