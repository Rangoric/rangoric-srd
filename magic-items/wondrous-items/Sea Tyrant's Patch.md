# Sea Tyrant's Patch

This ornate silk eye patch is embroidered with gold thread and studded with pearls in a pattern representing a whirlpool. The wearer of this item, which is a favorite patch of ruthless or particularly driven pirate captains, is constantly affected as if subject to the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of the Sea|touch of the sea]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]* spells. Furthermore, once per day on command, the wearer can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster, Mass|mass charm monster]]*, as per the spell.

Strong enchantment; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster, Mass|mass charm monster]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of the Sea|touch of the sea]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]*; Price 70,000 gp; Cost 35,000 gp.
