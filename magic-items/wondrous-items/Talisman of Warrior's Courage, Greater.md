# Talisman of Warrior's Courage, Greater

A talisman works as described under [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#^talismans|Talismans]] in the rules for magic items on the body. A greater talisman works as a lesser talisman of the same type, but lasts far longer: it can give its benefit once per day.

The first time the wearer would become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], he becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] instead.

Faint abjuration; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_; Price 4,500 gp; Cost 2,250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
