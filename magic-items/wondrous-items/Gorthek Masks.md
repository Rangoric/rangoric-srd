# Gorthek Masks

This set consists of two masks of hammered iron, one sized for a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Gorthek|gorthek]]'s face and the other sized for the face of its [[rangoric-srd/monsters/orc/Orc|orc]] handler. To bond a gorthek with its handler, orcs permanently nail the _gorthek masks_ to the respective wearers' faces. The magic functions only when the _gorthek masks_ are affixed to one orc and one gorthek. It doesn't work with animals other than gortheks. There's only a 60% chance an orc's mask will function when affixed to a non-orc humanoid—and it's impossible to tell whether a mask will work until after it has been permanently attached.

The orc's mask is fashioned with horns on the side, resembling those of a gorthek. With its bonded gorthek, the wearer gains the benefits of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ and a +10 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks.

The gorthek's mask depicts fearsome tusks, scales, or other features meant to make the creature look more terrifying. A gorthek wearing its mask ignores the first 5 points of hardness of any object it hits with a gore attack when performing a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder combat maneuver]] or when smashing an object. Any creature other than its handler takes a –10 penalty on Animal Handling checks against a bonded gorthek.

Moderate transmutation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Versatile Weapon|versatile weapon]]_; Price 5,800 gp; Cost 2,900 gp; Weight 75 lbs.
