# Hand of Stone

This mummified dwarf hand hangs on a steel chain around a character's neck, bringing the wearer more in tune with the earth beneath her feet. If the wearer spends a standard action concentrating on the hand, she gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] out to a range of 30 feet until the start of her next turn. This ability only allows her to sense creatures and objects that are in contact with earth or stone.

Moderate divination; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], clairaudience/clairvoyance; Price 27,000 gp; Cost 13,500 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
