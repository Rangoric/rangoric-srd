# Wound Paste

Prized by slavers as an affordable way to stop a captive from bleeding to death, a dose of _wound paste_ acts as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]_ spell when slathered on a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] creature. Applying _wound paste_ is a standard action that provokes an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]]. A pot of _wound paste_ contains 5 doses.

Faint conjuration; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]_; Price 50 gp; Cost 25 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
