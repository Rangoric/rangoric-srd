# Aquatic Cummerbund

Long believed to have been gnome in origin, this stately cummerbund features a number of dancing dolphins embroidered on its deep blue material. The cummerbund grants its wearer a +4 competence bonus on Athletics checks, and allows her to take 10 on such checks even if distracted or endangered.

Faint transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of the Sea|touch of the sea]]_; Price 2,600 gp; Cost 1,300 gp.
