# Headband of Seduction

This plain white band makes the wearer seem more attractive. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on Charisma-based checks with other members of his creature type. If the wearer is of the humanoid type, he gains a +5 competence bonus on creatures of his subtype instead. Once per day, the wearer can attempt to affect a creature as the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unnatural Lust|unnatural lust]]_ but with a duration of 10 minutes.

Faint enchantment and transmutation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unnatural Lust|unnatural lust]]_; Price 40,000 gp; Cost 20,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
