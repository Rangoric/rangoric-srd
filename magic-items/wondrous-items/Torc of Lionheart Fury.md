# Torc of Lionheart Fury

Each end of this heavy copper neckwear bears the image of a snarling lion. The wearer gains a +2 morale bonus on saving throws against fear. If the wearer is a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]] of at least 12th level, she gains [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Fearless Rage (Rage Power)|fearless rage]] as a bonus [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]]. If she already has this power, she also becomes immune to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] condition while raging, and any panic-causing attack against her while she rages instead causes her to regain 1 round of rage up to once per round.

Faint abjuration; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]*; Price 8,000 gp; Cost 4,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
