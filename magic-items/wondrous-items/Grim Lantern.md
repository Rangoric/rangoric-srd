# Grim Lantern

This macabre lantern is composed of a humanoid skull dangling from a short length of chain. A small fragment of soulstuff is trapped inside, burning slowly in a heatless flame. Its flickering light shines from the skull's eye sockets, casting light as a bullseye lantern. On command, the lantern can consume its soulstuff in a sudden blast of heat, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]_. Doing so extinguishes the lantern. Whenever a living creature with at least 1 HD is slain within 30 feet of an empty _grim lantern_, the lantern absorbs a fragment of that creature's soul, restoring its glow.

Faint evocation; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]_, creator cannot be good; Price 5,800 gp; Cost 2,900 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
