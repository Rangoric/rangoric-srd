# Minotaur Belt

This belt grants its wearer a +2 enhancement bonus to Strength, and the ability to ignore [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement#Terrain and Obstacles|difficult terrain]] while charging or making an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Trample|trample]] attempt. Treat the enhancement bonus to Strength as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the belt is worn.

Faint transmutation; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull's strength]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Step|feather step]]*; Price 11,000 gp; Cost 5,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
