# Eyes of the Dragon

These tigereye gems fit over and then meld into the eyes, transforming the wearer's eyes into something slitted and draconic. These eyes grant the wearer [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] 120 ft. and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsense]] 60 ft. The wearer sees four times as well as a human in dim light and twice as well in normal light.

Strong transmutation; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Form of the Dragon III|form of the dragon III]]*; Price 110,000 gp; Cost 55,000 gp.
