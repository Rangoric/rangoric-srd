# Elixir of Oppression

This elixir is normally made from the juice of tomb herald brambles, though other formulae have also been discovered. It allows creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]] ability to smell fear, and instills a bloodlust in the imbiber when it's facing a fearful creature. The imbiber gains a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks made with the scent ability, and a successful DC 15 Perception check allows the imbiber to determine by scent whether a creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]]. Once a creature's condition has been identified, the imbiber gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls and opposed skill checks against that opponent. These effects last for 1d4+1 hours. If a creature drinks a second elixir of oppression within 24 hours of the first, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 24 hours and does not gain the benefits of the second dose.

Faint transmutation; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scare|scare]]_; Price 600 gp; Cost 300 gp.
