# Blazing Robe

This red and orange silk robe gives viewers the impression of a flame roiling just below the fabric's slick surface. The robe grants the wearer fire [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 5 and increases the wearer's caster level for all spells with the fire descriptor by +1. Once per day on command, the wearer of the robe can emit a 20-foot-radius burst of fire. Creatures other than the wearer within the area take 2d6 points of fire damage (+6 vs Reflex; a miss halves).

Moderate evocation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Heighten Spell|Heighten Spell]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]_; Price 11,000 gp; Cost 5,500 gp; Weight 1 lb.
