# Lantern of Auras

A *lantern of auras* is a bullseye lantern made of cold iron with a series of lenses and colored glass filters that can be flipped down in front of its main aperture. The lantern functions as a bullseye lantern but needs no oil to shed its light. If its command word is spoken, it begins to reveal magic auras as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]* spell. It will reveal all auras *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]* reveals over the normal duration of 3 rounds, as long as its light is held on the same area for this duration. Anyone who takes a full-round action to observe the revealed auras during each of these rounds can attempt an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] skill check to determine the school of any magic aura revealed, or a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] check to identify the properties of a magic item within the area.

Roughly 45% of these lanterns have the command word etched on the bottom of the lantern. Casting *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Identify|identify]]* on a *lantern of auras* reveals the command word.

Faint divination; CL 1st; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]*; Price 2,000 gp; Cost 1,000 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
