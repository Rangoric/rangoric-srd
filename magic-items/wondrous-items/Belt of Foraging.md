# Belt of Foraging

This belt allows its wearer to easily forage while in the wilderness. As long as the wearer of this belt has at least 1 rank in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]], she need not make a check to get along in the wild; she always succeeds at that Fieldcraft check as long as she moves at half her overland speed. She can still choose to make that check to provide food and water for one other creature for every 2 points by which her result exceeds DC 10. Furthermore, she can instead make a DC 20 Fieldcraft check to get along in the wild while moving at full speed instead of half speed. While doing so, she can provide food and water for one other creature for every 2 points by which her check result exceeds 20.

Faint divination; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Animals or Plants|detect animals or plants]]_, creator must have at least 1 rank in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]]; Price 6,000 gp; Cost 3,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
