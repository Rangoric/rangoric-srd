# Comfort's Cloak

A favored cloak of rangers, this cloak is a patchwork of hides and fur. _Comfort's cloak_ grants a constant _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_ effect to the wearer, and grants a +4 competence bonus to defenses against disease, energy drain, effects that cause [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigue]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhaustion]], and poisons. Furthermore, while the wearer is resting he regains twice his Hit Dice in hit points for 8 hours of rest instead of the typical 1 hit point per Hit Die. When taking a full 24 hours of rest, the character regains 5 times his Hit Dice in hit points instead of twice his Hit Dice in hit points.

Faint abjuration and necromancy; CL 5th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_; Price 15,600 gp; Cost 7,800 gp; Weight 1 lb.
