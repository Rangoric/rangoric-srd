# Silk Robe of Sunbeams

A very rare and unique item, this silk robe looks to be made of thin but broad rays of light. The _silk robe of sunbeams_ grants the wearer a +3 armor bonus. Additionally, while the armor is active the wearer may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_ at will, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Searing Light|searing light]]_ once per day, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]_ once per week.

If the wearer is neutral good, he can also cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_ three times per day, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]_ twice a day, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_ once a day, and receives [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] 1 as long as he stays in broad, cloudless daylight while wearing the robe.

Strong evocation; CL 15th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst|sunburst]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_, must be created in broad daylight; Price 99,150 gp; Cost 50,150 gp + 3,916 XP.
