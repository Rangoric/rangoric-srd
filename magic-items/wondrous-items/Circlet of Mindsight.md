# Circlet of Mindsight

This elaborate circlet of gold-and-platinum filigree bears tiny gems in settings that look disturbingly like human eyes. When worn, the circlet lets the wearer sense the presence of other thinking creatures in her immediate area. The wearer gains the benefits of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blindsense|blindsense]] 30 feet, but only against creatures with an Intelligence score that are susceptible to mind-affecting effects.

Undead, constructs, and mindless creatures like most oozes and vermin cannot be perceived when using the circlet, nor can creatures under the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Blank|mind blank]]_ or a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rings/Ring of Mind Shielding|ring of mind shielding]]_.

The circlet does not interfere with the wearer's ability to see normally. If the wearer has blindsense or blindsight, he is able to differentiate creatures detected with those senses from creatures detected with the _circlet of mindsight_.

Moderate divination; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], clairaudience/clairvoyance, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_; Price 22,000 gp; Cost 11,000 gp.
