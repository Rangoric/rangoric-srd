# Headband of Ponderous Recollection

This black leather headband has two small amber gemstones set so they rest on the wearer's forehead. The headband grants the wearer a +2 enhancement bonus to Intelligence. Treat this as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the headband is worn. It also grants skill ranks as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Headband of Vast Intelligence +2|headband of vast intelligence]]_, but always grants ranks in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]].

As a swift action three times per day, the wearer can call upon the headband to reveal the abilities and weaknesses of a creature within line of sight. The headband fills the wearer's mind with information as though the wearer had made a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|Knowledge check]] with a natural roll of a 5, adding her bonuses to the check as normal. At the start of her next turn, the wearer receives additional information as though she had made the check with a natural roll of 10. At the start of her turn after that, the wearer receives a final piece of information, treating her natural roll as 15.

Moderate divination and transmutation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Know the Enemy|know the enemy]]_; Price 5,100 gp; Cost 2,550 gp; Weight 1 lb.
