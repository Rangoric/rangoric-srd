# Amulet of the Blooded, Destined

Each different type of _amulet of the blooded_ grants its wearer powers from one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines|bloodline]], but makes him vulnerable to attacks and effects that target creatures with the appropriate bloodline (such as a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Blood-Hunting|blood-hunting]]_ weapon). If a creature that already has the associated bloodline wears the amulet, the wearer does not gain the abilities listed below; instead, the effective level of his bloodline powers increases by 2. This effect does not stack with other abilities that increase the effective level of bloodline powers.

**[[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Destined Bloodline|Destined]]**: This chunk of uncut quartz hangs on a platinum chain. The wearer gains a +2 luck bonus on all defenses and to AC during surprise rounds or any time he is otherwise unaware of an attack. Once per day, the wearer can reroll one attack roll, critical hit confirmation roll, or caster level check to overcome spell resistance. He must decide to use this ability after the first roll is made but before the result is revealed. He must take the second result, even if it's lower.

Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid|aid]]_, creator must be a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Destined Bloodline|destined]] bloodline; Price 10,000 gp; Cost 5,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
