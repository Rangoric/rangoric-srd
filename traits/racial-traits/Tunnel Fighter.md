# Tunnel Fighter

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]].

Caves and tunnels are a second home to you. While underground, you receive a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on initiative checks and a +1 trait bonus on weapon damage rolls for critical hits (this damage is multiplied on a critical hit).
