# Almost Human

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]].

You have enough human features that it's easy for you to pass for a pureblooded human. You gain a +4 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks to pass as human, and Imitation is always a class skill for you.
