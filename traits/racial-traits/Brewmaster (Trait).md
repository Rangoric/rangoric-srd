# Brewmaster

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]].

Your family brought the secrets of dwarven brewing to non-dwarven lands. Though this has given you skill in the brewer's craft, it's also earned you distrust among dwarven purists. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (brewer) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) checks, but you take a –1 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks made to change the attitude of dwarves who know that your family has shared brewing secrets with non-dwarves.
