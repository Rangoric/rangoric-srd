# Brute

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]].

You have worked for a crime lord, either as a low-level enforcer or as a guard, and are adept at frightening away people. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks, and Intimidation is always a class skill for you.
