# Goldsniffer

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]].

Your keen senses lead you to hidden treasures. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks related to metals, jewels, and gemstones.
