# Elven Reflexes

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]].

One of your parents was a member of a wild elven tribe, and you've inherited a portion of your elven parent's quick reflexes. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on Initiative checks.
