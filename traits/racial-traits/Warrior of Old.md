# Warrior of Old

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]].

As a child, you put in long hours in combat drills, and though time has made this training a dim memory, you still have a knack for quickly responding to trouble. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on Initiative checks.
