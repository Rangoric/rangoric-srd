# Forlorn

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]].

Having lived outside of traditional elf society for much or all of your life, you know the world can be cruel, dangerous, and unforgiving of the weak. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on Fortitude Defense.
