# Scrapper

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]].

A childhood spent scrabbling for every possession and scrap of food has made you fierce and wary. You receive a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks made to demoralize opponents and a +1 trait bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to avoid being surprised.
