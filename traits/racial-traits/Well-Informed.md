# Well-Informed

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]].

You make it a point to know everyone and to be connected to everything around you. You frequent the best taverns, attend all of the right events, and graciously help anyone who needs it. Because of this, you gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks to gather information and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks. One of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
