# Scholar of Ruins

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]].

From the moment you could walk and talk, the ruins of ancient civilizations have fascinated you. Because of this, you have special insight into geography as well as expertise in exploring lost places. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] checks. One of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
