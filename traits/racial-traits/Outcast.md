# Outcast

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]]. **Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]].

Driven from town after town because of your heritage, you have become adept at living apart from others. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] skill checks, and Fieldcraft is always a class skill for you.
