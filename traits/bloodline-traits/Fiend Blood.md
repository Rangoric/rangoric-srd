# Fiend Blood

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Bloodline Race Traits|bloodline race trait]].

The blood of fiends taints your line, manifesting physically, though it may be barely noticeable. Choose one of the following skills: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]]. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on checks with that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
