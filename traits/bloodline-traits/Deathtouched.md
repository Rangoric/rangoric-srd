# Deathtouched

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Bloodline Race Traits|bloodline race trait]].

At some point you were tainted with the corruption of the undead, and you have gained some of their defenses. You gain either a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on Fortitude saving throws or a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting effects (your choice).
