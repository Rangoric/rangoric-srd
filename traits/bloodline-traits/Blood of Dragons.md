# Blood of Dragons

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Bloodline Race Traits|bloodline race trait]].

Long ago, your ancestors' blood mixed with that of dragons. Choose one of the following: gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, gain low-light vision, or gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against effects that cause sleep or paralysis.
