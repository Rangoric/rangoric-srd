# Sentimental

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Drawbacks|drawback]].

You are sentimental, and your thoughts often stray to the past at inappropriate times. You take a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to avoid being surprised and on Reflex Defense against traps or hazards.
