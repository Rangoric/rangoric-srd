# Xenophobic

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Drawbacks|drawback]].

You have a hard time understanding and trusting those with unfamiliar ways and appearances. You take a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks made against creatures of a different race or from a different culture.
