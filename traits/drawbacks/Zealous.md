# Zealous

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Drawbacks|drawback]].

You are fanatical in your beliefs, ruled by emotion over reason. When you attack a creature that you know worships a different religion than you do, you take a –5 penalty on the attack roll and a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on the damage roll with your first attack.
