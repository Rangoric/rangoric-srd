# Dependent

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Drawbacks|drawback]].

You are dependent upon the acceptance of others. Whenever you fail a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] check, you become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 hour.
