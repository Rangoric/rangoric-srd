# Family Ties

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Drawbacks|drawback]].

Your family is extremely important to you, and you feel disheartened when you can't do what they ask. When a family member makes a request of you, you must fulfill that request or take a –2 penalty on all Wisdom- and Charisma-based ability checks and skill checks until you either do what was requested or shake it off: once per day at the start of each day, the request attacks your Will at +10, and the penalty ends on a miss. You can't take this drawback if you have no family. If you ever lose your family or lose contact with your family, exchange this drawback for the [[rangoric-srd/traits/drawbacks/Doubt|Doubt]] drawback.
