# Provincial

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Drawbacks|drawback]].

You have only one way of looking at things: the right way. You take a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks made against all creatures whose religion or alignment differs from your own.
