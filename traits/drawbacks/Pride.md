# Pride

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Drawbacks|drawback]].

You can't abide challenges to your dignity, authority, or honor. When someone threatens, accuses, or challenges you, you take a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks involving that creature until the creature apologizes to you.
