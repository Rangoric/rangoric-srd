# Anatomist

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You have studied the workings of anatomy, either as a student at university or as an apprentice mortician or necromancer. You know where to aim your blows to strike vital organs, and you gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all rolls made to confirm critical hits.
