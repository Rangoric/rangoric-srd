# Reckless

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You have a tendency for rash behavior, often disregarding your own safety as you move across the battlefield. You gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks, and Acrobatics is always a class skill for you.
