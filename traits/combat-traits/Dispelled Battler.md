# Dispelled Battler

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

When deprived of magic, you fight harder. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on weapon attack and damage rolls while within an area of antimagic such as that created by an *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antimagic Field|antimagic field]]*. You also gain this bonus for 1 round after you are subject to an area or targeted *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]* spell, regardless of the effect's success or failure in dispelling your spells and magical effects.
