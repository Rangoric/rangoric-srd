# Nature's Mimic

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Your knowledge of nature informs your fighting style, and that style gives you insight into related aspects of the natural world. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] checks, and Nature is always a class skill for you. You can make Nature checks pertaining to animals that correspond to your [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#Style Feats|style feats]] untrained.
