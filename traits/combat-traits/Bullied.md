# Bullied

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You were bullied often as a child, and you are now constantly ready to defend yourself with your fists when an enemy comes near. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] attack rolls made with unarmed strikes. Note that this trait does not grant the ability to make attacks of opportunity with your unarmed strikes—you must have a level of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]], the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]] feat, or some other similar power to gain the use of this character trait. However, that doesn't prevent you from selecting this trait. You simply cannot make use of it until a later point if you do.
