# Axe to Grind

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

There is a fire in your heart that can't be quenched. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on damage against foes who are threatened by only you.
