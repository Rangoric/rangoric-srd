# Never Stop Shooting

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Even when the chips are down, you keep on shooting. If your hit point total drops to 0 or lower but you are not dead, you may act as if [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^disabled|disabled]] rather than [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] (as if you possessed the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]] feat). However, you can use your actions only to draw a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]], reload a firearm, or attack with a firearm. If you have the Diehard feat, this trait also allows you to substitute your Wisdom score for your Constitution score for the purpose of determining the negative hit point total at which you die.
