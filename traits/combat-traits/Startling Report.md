# Startling Report

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Through either perfect timing or the determination of your action, the sound of your firearm going off sets your enemies on edge. When you shoot a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]], you gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks for 1 round against those who heard the shot.
