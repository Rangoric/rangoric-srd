# Indelible Ire

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

When you are gravely wounded, you're stirred to greater combat focus. Each time a critical hit is confirmed against you, you gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on attack rolls for 1 round.
