# Surprise Weapon

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You are skilled at fighting with objects not traditionally considered weapons. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on attack rolls with improvised weapons.
