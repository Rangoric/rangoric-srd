# Tactician

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You know how to take advantage of enemies who are unprepared for your assault. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on initiative checks. In addition, once per day when you make an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]], you gain a +2 trait bonus on the attack roll.
