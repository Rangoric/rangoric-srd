# Black Powder Interjection

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Your confidence rises when you foil spellcasting. Once per day, when you interrupt the casting of a spell with a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]] attack, you may regain 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit point]].
