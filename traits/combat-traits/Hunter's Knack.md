# Hunter's Knack

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

When you watch and wait before attacking, your strike is true. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on attack rolls against your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemies]] when you make such attacks as part of a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Special Initiative Actions#Ready|readied action]].
