# Deft Dodger

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Growing up in a rough neighborhood or a dangerous environment has honed your senses. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on Reflex saves.
