# Kin Guardian

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You are dedicated to defending members of your family. When you use the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] action to give a member of your family a bonus to AC, increase the bonus by 2. This increase is a [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] (and therefore doesn't stack with increases granted by other family members using this trait). This trait has no effect when using the aid another action to increase a family member's next attack roll.
