# Unblemished Barrel

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Your skill with firearms speeds your repair of such weapons. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) and Background (weaponsmithing) checks, and it takes you only 30 minutes to remove the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken condition]] from a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]].
