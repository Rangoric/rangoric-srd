# Battlefield Disciple

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You are skilled at supporting your allies in the heat of battle. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks made to [[rangoric-srd/actions/Stabilize (Action)|stabilize]] a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] creature. In addition, when you successfully use the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] action in combat to grant an ally a bonus on her next attack roll, the bonus you grant increases by 1.
