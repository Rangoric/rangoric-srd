# Hidden Hand

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Your concealed weapons strike fast and true. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made to conceal light weapons and a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls when making an attack with a light weapon during a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]].
