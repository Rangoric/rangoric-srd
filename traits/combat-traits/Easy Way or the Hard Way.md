# Easy Way or the Hard Way

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You are skilled at subduing your enemies. Whenever you attack one of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemies]] with a weapon that deals lethal damage and choose to deal nonlethal damage, you take only a –2 penalty on the attack roll instead of the normal –4.
