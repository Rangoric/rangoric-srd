# Evasive Sting

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Larger enemies have a hard time striking you. You gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC against enemies that are both your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] and at least two size categories larger than you.
