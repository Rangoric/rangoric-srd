# Faith's Hunter

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Divine powers smile upon you each time you grievously wound a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]]. When you confirm a critical hit against a favored enemy, you may extend the duration of any divine spell affecting you by 1 round. The benefits from multiple critical hits stack. Divine spells with an instantaneous duration can't be extended in this manner.
