# Vigilant Battler

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You are hard to fool and harder to trick with a feint. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks. This trait bonus increases to +2 when used to counter a feint in combat.
