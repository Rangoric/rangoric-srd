# Dedicated Defender

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

When a companion is down, you fight harder. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on attack rolls and on checks made to overcome spell resistance while you are adjacent to a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^disabled|disabled]] ally, animal companion, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]], familiar, or mount.
