# Reckless Contempt

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You do not fear the threat of lesser weapons. Whenever you provoke an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] by firing a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]], you gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on the attack roll that provoked the attack of opportunity.
