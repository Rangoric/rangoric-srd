# Martial Performer

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Your training has made you a martial performer. When you gain a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|monk bonus feat]], you may instead take a [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^performance-feats|performance feat]] for which you meet the prerequisites.
