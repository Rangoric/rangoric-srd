# Resolve of the Rejected

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Some disagree with your choice to wield a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]], and their scorn has only hardened your resolve in battle. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all defenses against charm and compulsion effects. Also, once per day when such an effect misses you, you regain 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit point]].
