# Scarred Descendant

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

Your own people are your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]]. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on rolls to confirm critical hits against favored enemies of your own subtype and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks made to track such creatures.
