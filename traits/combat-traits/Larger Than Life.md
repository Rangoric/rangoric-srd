# Larger Than Life

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You wield firearms with truly terrifying menace. When you use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] skill on a creature while wielding a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]], you are considered one size category larger than your actual size.
