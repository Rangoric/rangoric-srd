# Black Powder Bravado

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Combat Traits|combat trait]].

You are particularly successful when using [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-deeds|deeds]]. Once per day, when you perform a deed that requires an attack roll and you miss with that roll, you can reroll it. You must take the second result even if it is worse.
