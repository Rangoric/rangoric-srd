# Sea-Souled

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

You are at home at sea. You receive a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim, and you can always take 10 on them while swimming.
