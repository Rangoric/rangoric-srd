# Savanna Child

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

You were born and raised among rolling plains or savannas. You spent much of your youth exploring these vast reaches and know many of the savanna's secrets. Pick one of the following skills: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]]. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
