# Desert Child

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

You were born and raised in rocky deserts. You are accustomed to high temperatures, and gain a +4 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on any defenses against the effects of being in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Heat Dangers|hot conditions]] and a +1 trait bonus on all defenses against fire effects.
