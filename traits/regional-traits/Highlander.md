# Highlander

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

You were born and raised in rugged badlands or hills, and you've become something of an expert at evading the predators, monsters, and worse that haunt the highlands. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks, and Stealth is always a class skill for you. This trait bonus increases to +2 in hilly or rocky areas.
