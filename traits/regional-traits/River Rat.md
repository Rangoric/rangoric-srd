# River Rat

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

You learned to swim right after you learned to walk. As a youth, a gang of river pirates put you to work swimming in nighttime rivers and canals with a dagger in your teeth so you could sever the anchor ropes of merchant vessels. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on damage dealt with a dagger and a +1 trait bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim. Athletics is always a class skill for you.
