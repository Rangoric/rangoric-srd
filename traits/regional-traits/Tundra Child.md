# Tundra Child

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

You were born and raised in the cold wastes of the far north or far south, and are accustomed to low temperatures. You gain a +4 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on saving throws made to resist the effects of being in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Cold Dangers|cold conditions]] and a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against cold effects.
