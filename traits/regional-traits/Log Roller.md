# Log Roller

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

The time you spent leaping between slippery logs as they whirled down the river to market taught you how to keep your footing. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks and a +1 trait bonus to your CMB when attempting to resist [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks.
