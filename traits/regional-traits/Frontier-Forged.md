# Frontier-Forged

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

A hard life on the edge of civilization has made you resourceful but has also given you a streak of self-preservation bordering on paranoia. You receive a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks and a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks made to get along in the wild.
