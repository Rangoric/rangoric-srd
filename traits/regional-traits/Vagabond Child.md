# Vagabond Child

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

You grew up among the outcasts and outlaws of your society, learning to forage and survive in an urban environment. Select one of the following skills: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]]. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
