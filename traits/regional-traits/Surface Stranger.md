# Surface Stranger

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Regional Traits|regional trait]].

You were born and raised in a subterranean home with little or no bright light. Your miss chance for concealment or total concealment due to either mundane or magical darkness decreases by 10%. You are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round when you are suddenly exposed to bright light.
