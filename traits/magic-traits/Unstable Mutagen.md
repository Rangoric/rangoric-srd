# Unstable Mutagen

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You discovered or were given a secret to make your mutagens more unstable—but also more potent. Once per day, you can create an unstable [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|mutagen]]. It is like a normal mutagen in most ways, but also gains a single benefit or hindrance due to its instability. Roll 1d6 to determine the result of the instability.

| d6  | Result                                                                                         |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | The unstable mutagen lasts for 5 minutes per alchemist level.                                  |
| 2   | The unstable mutagen lasts for 20 minutes per alchemist level.                                 |
| 3   | The unstable mutagen does not grant a natural armor bonus.                                     |
| 4   | The natural armor bonus granted by the mutagen increases by 2.                                 |
| 5   | The penalty that the mutagen applies to the corresponding mental ability score increases by 2. |
| 6   | The unstable mutagen does not apply a penalty to the corresponding mental ability score.       |
