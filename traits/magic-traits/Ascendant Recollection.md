# Ascendant Recollection

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Your first bloodline power is particularly strong. Your sorcerer level is considered to be 1 level higher when determining the effects of your 1st-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-bloodline|bloodline]] power.
