# Eldritch Delver

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You have an unquenchable thirst for knowledge about the world and desire to obtain it firsthand. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks, and you may treat your caster level as 1 level higher for all conjuration spells of the teleportation subschool.
