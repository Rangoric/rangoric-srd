# Focused Burn

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You know the secret of making punishing fire bombs. Any [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bombs]] you have that deal fire damage deal 1 additional point of fire damage on a direct hit per 2d6 of fire damage normally dealt (minimum 1 point). This additional damage does not apply to the bomb's splash damage.
