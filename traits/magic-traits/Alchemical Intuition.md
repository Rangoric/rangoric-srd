# Alchemical Intuition

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You are often struck with epiphanies about alchemical processes and substances. Once per day as a free action, you may gain a [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 0) on a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) check you make. You can apply this bonus after you roll the check, possibly turning a failure into a success.
