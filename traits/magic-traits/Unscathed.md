# Unscathed

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You are amazingly resistant to energy attacks because of either your upbringing or magical experimentation. Each type of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|energy resistance]] you have (if any) increases by 2 points.
