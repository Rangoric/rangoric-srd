# Greater Link

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

The link between you and your eidolon is stronger than most. Your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]]'s current and maximum hit point totals are not reduced by 50% until you are separated by 110 feet or more. Your eidolon's current and maximum hit point totals are not reduced by 75% until you are separated by 1,100 feet or more. All other aspects of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-life-link|life link]] class feature are unaffected.
