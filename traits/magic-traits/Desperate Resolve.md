# Desperate Resolve

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You are adept at casting spells even in the most precarious situations. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration]] checks. This trait bonus increases to +4 when you are grappled, pinned, in violent weather, or entangled.
