# Greater Purpose

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You are convinced you're alive to achieve a great purpose. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all defenses against death effects and a +1 trait bonus on Constitution checks made to [[rangoric-srd/actions/Stabilize (Action)|stabilize]] while [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]].
