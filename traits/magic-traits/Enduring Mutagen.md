# Enduring Mutagen

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Because of special training, your unique body chemistry, or the uncovering of an alchemical secret, your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|mutagens]] last longer. The bonuses and penalties granted by your mutagens last for 1 additional minute per alchemist level.
