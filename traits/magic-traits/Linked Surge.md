# Linked Surge

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Once per day, you may make a Strength- or Constitution-based ability check or skill check using your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]]'s ability score in place of your own. Your eidolon must be summoned and within 30 feet for you to use this trait.
