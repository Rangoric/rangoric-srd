# Partial Protege

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You are adept at disguising your true talents. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] checks made to emulate a class feature.
