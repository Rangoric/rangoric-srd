# Twinned Presence

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]]—and your connection to it—makes others ill at ease. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks, and Intimidation is always a class skill for you. In addition, if your eidolon is summoned and within 30 feet, and its size exceeds your own, use its size modifier on any Intimidation checks you make.
