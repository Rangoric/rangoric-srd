# Cross-Disciplined

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Your dabbling has granted you greater insight into the proper casting of certain spells. Once per day, you may cast a spell you have prepared that appears on both the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magus Spell List|magus]] and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|wizard]] spell lists as if your caster level were 1 level higher.
