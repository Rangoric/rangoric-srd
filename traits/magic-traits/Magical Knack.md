# Magical Knack

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You were raised, either wholly or in part, by a magical creature, either after it found you abandoned in the woods or because your parents often left you in the care of a magical minion. This constant exposure to magic has made its mysteries easy for you to understand, even when you turn your mind to other devotions and tasks. Pick a class when you gain this trait—your caster level in that class gains a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] as long as this bonus doesn't raise your caster level above your current Hit Dice.
