# Shrouded Casting

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You have learned the secret to casting some spells without lesser material components. Choose one school of magic when you pick this trait. You can cast spells from that school as if you had the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Eschew Materials|Eschew Materials]] feat.
