# Mathematical Prodigy

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Mathematics has always come easily for you, and you have always been able to "see the math" in the physical and magical world. You gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
