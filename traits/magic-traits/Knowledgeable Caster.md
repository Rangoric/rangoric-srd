# Knowledgeable Caster

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Once per day when you cast a divination spell, you can cast that spell as if your caster level were 1 level higher in the appropriate class. You also gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on any Knowledge checks associated with your sorcerer [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-bloodline|bloodline]], if any.
