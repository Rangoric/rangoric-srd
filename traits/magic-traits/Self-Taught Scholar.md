# Self-Taught Scholar

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Being self-taught has made it necessary for you to scour all documentation you can get your hands on. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] checks to decipher unfamiliar languages, and Linguistics is always a class skill for you. In addition, you gain a +1 trait bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] checks made to decipher the writing on a scroll.
