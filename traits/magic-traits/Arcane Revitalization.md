# Arcane Revitalization

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Your martial prowess can feed your arcane power. Once per day, when you confirm a critical hit with a weapon attack, you regain 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-arcane-pool|arcane pool]] point. You can't exceed your maximum number of arcane pool points.
