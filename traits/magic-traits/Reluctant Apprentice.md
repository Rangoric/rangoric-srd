# Reluctant Apprentice

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Your early training grants you knowledge of the arcane. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] checks, and are considered trained in that skill even if you have no ranks in it.
