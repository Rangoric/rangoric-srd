# Skeptic

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Growing up, you were always around magical effects to the extent that you realized much of it was mere smoke and mirrors. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all defenses against illusions.
