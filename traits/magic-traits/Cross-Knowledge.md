# Cross-Knowledge

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Once per day, you may treat an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-alchemy|extract]] you drink as if your caster level were 1 level higher as long as that extract appears on the wizard spell list and the alchemist list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-formulae|formulae]].
