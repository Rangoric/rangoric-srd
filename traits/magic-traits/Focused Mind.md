# Focused Mind

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Your childhood was either dominated by lessons of some sort (whether musical, academic, or other) or by a horrible home life that encouraged your ability to block out distractions and focus on the immediate task at hand. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration]] checks.
