# Desperate Speed

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]] excels at a movement type you do not possess. Your eidolon receives a +5-foot enhancement bonus to one type of speed it has that you do not, such as a climb or swim speed. If your eidolon doesn't already possess such a mode of movement, you may apply this [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] later if it gains an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-evolutions|evolution]] that grants it such a mode. Once this bonus is applied to a mode of movement, it can't be switched to another mode.
