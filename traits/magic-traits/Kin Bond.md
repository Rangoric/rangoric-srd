# Kin Bond

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

The bond between you and a close sibling is nearly mystical. Choose a single sibling with whom to share this bond. Once per day when an effect hits you while your sibling is within 30 feet, you may make the attacker reroll the attack against your sibling's defense (10 + your sibling's save bonus) instead of yours, and take the worse result. If you and the sibling are twins or otherwise part of a multiple birth, you gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on that defense. Your sibling must be willing to grant you the reroll. If the reroll also hits, you and your sibling are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1 round.
