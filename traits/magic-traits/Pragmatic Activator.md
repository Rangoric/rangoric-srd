# Pragmatic Activator

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

While some figure out how to use magical devices with stubborn resolve, your approach is more pragmatic. You may use your Intelligence modifier when making [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] checks instead of your Charisma modifier.
