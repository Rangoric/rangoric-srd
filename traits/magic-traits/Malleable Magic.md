# Malleable Magic

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Once per day as a swift action, you can lose a single 1st-, 2nd- or 3rd-level magus spell to regain 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-arcane-pool|arcane pool]] point. You can't exceed the maximum number of arcane pool points in your arcane pool by using this trait.
