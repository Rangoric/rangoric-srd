# Outcast's Intuition

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You are able to sense the motives of others and use that sense to bolster your magic against dispelling. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks, and Sense Motive is always a class skill for you. In addition, your caster level is treated as 1 level higher for the purposes of any attempts to dispel your magic.
