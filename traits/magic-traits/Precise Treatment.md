# Precise Treatment

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You treat others with a clear and calculating intellect. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks, and you may use your Intelligence modifier when making Fieldcraft checks instead of your Wisdom modifier.
