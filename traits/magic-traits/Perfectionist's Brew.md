# Perfectionist's Brew

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

You know that potion recipes should be followed with exact precision. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) checks and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic#Each class’s Magic skill|Magic skill]] checks made to brew potions.
