# Magic Crafter

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Magic Traits|magic trait]].

Your early magical training involved study of the properties and creation of magic items. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] checks and a +1 bonus on any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] checks attempted while making magic items.
