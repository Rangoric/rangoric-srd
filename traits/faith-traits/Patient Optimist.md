# Patient Optimist

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

You know that all things pass in time, and are used to having to repeat arguments multiple times to convince even the most stubborn believer. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks to influence hostile or unfriendly creatures, and if you fail at such an attempt, you may retry it once.
