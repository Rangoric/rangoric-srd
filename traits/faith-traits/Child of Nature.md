# Child of Nature

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

You have been blessed to be as comfortable in the wilderness as you are at home. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to find food and water, and a +1 trait bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] checks. One of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
