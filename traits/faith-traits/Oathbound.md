# Oathbound

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You have made a solemn oath, and you pursue that oath with headstrong determination. Once per day, when a charm or compulsion effect hits you, you may make the attacker reroll the attack and take the worse result.
