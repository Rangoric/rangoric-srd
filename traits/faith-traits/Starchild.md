# Starchild

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

Your goddess sensed your love of travel and promised you would always be able to find your way home. You can automatically determine where true north is. You gain a +4 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to avoid becoming lost.
