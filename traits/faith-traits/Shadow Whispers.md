# Shadow Whispers

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

You have a penchant for just knowing others' secrets. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks and a +2 trait bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks made to demoralize opponents.
