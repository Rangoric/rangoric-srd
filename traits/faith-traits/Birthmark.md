# Birthmark

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You were born with a strange birthmark that looks very similar to the holy symbol of the god you chose to worship later in life. This birthmark can serve you as a divine focus for casting spells, and as a physical manifestation of your faith, it increases your devotion to your god—you gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all defenses against charm and compulsion effects as a result.
