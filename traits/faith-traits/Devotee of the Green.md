# Devotee of the Green

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

Your faith in the natural world or one of the gods of nature makes it easy for you to pick up on related concepts. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] checks, and Nature is always a class skill for you.
