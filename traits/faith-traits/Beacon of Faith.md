# Beacon of Faith

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You wield the might of your faith with power and clarity. Once per day as a free action, you may treat your caster level as if it were 2 levels higher when using one of the granted powers of your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains|domain]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/inquisitions/Inquisitions|inquisition]], or when casting one of your domain spells.
