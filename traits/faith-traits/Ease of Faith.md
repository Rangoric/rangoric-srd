# Ease of Faith

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

Your mentor, the person who invested your faith in you from an early age, took steps to ensure that you understood that what powers your divine magic is no different than that which powers the magic of other religions. This philosophy makes it easier for you to interact with others who may not share your views. You gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks, and Persuasion is always a class skill for you.
