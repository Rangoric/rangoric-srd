# Perpetual Companion

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You are bolstered against fear when your eidolon is near. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all defenses against fear effects whenever your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]] is within 30 feet.
