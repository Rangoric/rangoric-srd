# Guardian of the Forge

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

Your god's sacred duties are to protect the faithful, take lessons from the great craftsmen and strategists of the past, and prepare against dark times. You receive a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks. One of these skills (your choice) is a class skill for you.
