# Fortified Drinker

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

Your god's holy brews invigorate your mind, making you less susceptible to mental attacks. Whenever you imbibe any alcoholic beverage, you gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on defenses against mind-affecting effects for 1 hour.
