# Focused Disciple

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

When weaker wills falter, you keep a clear mind. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on defenses against charm and compulsion effects.
