# Martial Manuscript

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You either carry or have memorized a sacred text from your school, temple, or monastery, and its wisdom gives you insight that makes your attacks more devastating. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on rolls to confirm critical hits when you're using an unarmed strike or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-groups|monk weapon]].
