# Prophesied

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

Your coming was foretold in prophecy, and people familiar with your legend regard you with awe and fear. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks when interacting with anyone familiar with you or your reputation.
