# Omen

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You are the harbinger of some future event. Whether this event bodes good or ill, you exude an ominous presence. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks, and Intimidation is always a class skill for you. Once per day, you may attempt to demoralize an opponent as a swift action.
