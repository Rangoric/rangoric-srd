# Veteran of Battle

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

You have fought in several battles, and each time felt the presence of your god guiding your sword-arm, making you ready to act at a moment’s notice. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on Initiative checks, and if you are able to act during a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]], you may draw a weapon (but not a potion or magic item) as a free action during that round.
