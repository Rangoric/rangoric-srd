# Scholar of the Planes

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

Your great interests as a child did not lie with current events or the mundane—you have always felt out of place, as if you were born in the wrong era. You take to philosophical discussions of the planes and of historical events with ease. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
