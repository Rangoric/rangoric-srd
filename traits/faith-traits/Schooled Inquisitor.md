# Schooled Inquisitor

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

Your additional training aids in identifying the wiles of your faith's enemies. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks made to identify the abilities and weaknesses of creatures.
