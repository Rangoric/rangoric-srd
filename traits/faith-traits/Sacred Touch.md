# Sacred Touch

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You were exposed to a potent source of positive energy as a child, perhaps by being born under the right cosmic sign, or maybe because one of your parents was a gifted healer. As a standard action, you may automatically [[rangoric-srd/actions/Stabilize (Action)|stabilize]] a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] creature merely by touching it.
