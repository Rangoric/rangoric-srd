# Principled

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You hold yourself to a strict code of behavior that guides all of your decisions and actions. You take a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks and gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on defenses against charm, compulsion, and emotion effects.
