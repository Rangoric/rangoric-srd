# Divine Warrior

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

From an early age, you were trained by a militaristic order of clerics. You are devoted both to the teachings of your goddess and to spreading those teachings by force. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on melee weapon damage when you cast a divine spell that affects weapons.
