# Spirit Sense

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You are so attuned to the spiritual world that it is hard to get the jump on you. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to avoid being surprised and to detect invisible or incorporeal creatures.
