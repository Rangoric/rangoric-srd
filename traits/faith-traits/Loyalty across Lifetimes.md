# Loyalty across Lifetimes

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You and your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]] share a link that seems to span multiple lifetimes. Your eidolon treats its Constitution score as if it were 2 points higher for the purposes of determining when its negative hit point total sends it back to its home plane. In addition, your eidolon gains a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on Will saves against enchantment spells and effects.
