# Eyes and Ears of the City

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

Your religious training involved serving in the city watch of a large city, the primary duty of which was standing sentinel on a city wall. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, and Perception is always a class skill for you.
