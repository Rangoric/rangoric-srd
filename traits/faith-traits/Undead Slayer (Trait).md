# Undead Slayer

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

Instructed at a young age in the tenets of your faith, you view the undead as abominations that must be destroyed, so their souls can journey beyond to be judged. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on weapon damage against undead.
