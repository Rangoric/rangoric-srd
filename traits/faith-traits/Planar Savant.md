# Planar Savant

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You have always had an innate sense of the workings of the planes and their denizens. You may use your Charisma modifier when making [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks instead of your Intelligence modifier.
