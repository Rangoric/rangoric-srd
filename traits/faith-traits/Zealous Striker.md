# Zealous Striker

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

Your zeal is a danger to those of "lesser" faiths. While you have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Destruction (Judgment)|destruction judgment]] active, you gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on damage rolls when attacking a foe who can cast divine spells and worships a different deity than you.
