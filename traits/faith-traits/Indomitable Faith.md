# Indomitable Faith

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You were born in a region where your faith was not popular, but you never abandoned it. Your constant struggle to maintain your own faith has bolstered your drive; you gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on Will saves as a result.
