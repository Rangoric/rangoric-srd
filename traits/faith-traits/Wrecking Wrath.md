# Wrecking Wrath

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

Your ferocity is devastating, sometimes even to your own weapons. Once per day, after successfully hitting a foe with a melee weapon, you can add your Strength modifier to the damage roll a second time (your Strength modifier is not doubled if you are using a two-handed weapon). Doing so has a 25% chance of giving your weapon the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition.
