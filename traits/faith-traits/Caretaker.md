# Caretaker

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

As the child of an herbalist or an assistant in a temple infirmary, you often had to assist in tending to the sick and wounded. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
