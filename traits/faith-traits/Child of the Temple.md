# Child of the Temple

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

You have long served at a temple in a city, and not only did you pick up on many of the nobility's customs, you spent much time in the temple libraries studying your faith. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
