# Divine Courtesan

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

You worked in one of your goddess's temples as a sacred courtesan, and you know how to flatter, please, and (most of all) listen. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks to gather information, and one of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
