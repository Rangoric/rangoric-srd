# Ear for Music

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Religion Traits|religion trait]].

You spent countless hours of your youth in one of your goddess's temples, listening to wonderful musicians and singers. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on one category of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks and a +2 trait bonus on any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks that deal with the local art or music scene.
