# Sacred Conduit

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Faith Traits|faith trait]].

Your birth was particularly painful and difficult for your mother, who needed potent divine magic to ensure that you survived (your mother may or may not have). In any event, that magic infused you from an early age, and you now channel divine energy with greater ease than most. Whenever you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]], you gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] to the attack bonus of your channeled energy.
