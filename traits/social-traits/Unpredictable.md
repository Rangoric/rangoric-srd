# Unpredictable

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

Your actions often seem random and chaotic to others, but there is a method to your madness. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks, and Deception is always a class skill for you.
