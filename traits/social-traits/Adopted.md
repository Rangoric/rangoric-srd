# Adopted

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You were adopted and raised by someone not of your actual race, and raised in a society not your own. As a result, you picked up a race trait from your adoptive parents and society, and may immediately select a [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Race Traits|race trait]] from your adoptive parents' race.
