# Bully

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You grew up in an environment where the meek were ignored and you often had to resort to threats or violence to be heard. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks, and Intimidation is always a class skill for you.
