# Merchant

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You lived your life as a merchant, buying and selling goods. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks you attempt while bargaining for the price of goods. Appraise is always a class skill for you.
