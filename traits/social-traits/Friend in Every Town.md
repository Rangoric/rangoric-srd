# Friend in Every Town

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You have no problem making friends and learning information from them wherever you go. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks. One of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
