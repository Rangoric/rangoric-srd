# Mercenary

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

For everything there is a price, and you are a negotiator at heart. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks made while negotiating payment for a quest or a service that you provide.
