# Poverty-Stricken

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

Your childhood was tough, and your parents had to make every copper piece count. Hunger was your constant companion, and you often had to live off the land or sleep in the wild. You gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks, and Fieldcraft is always a class skill for you.
