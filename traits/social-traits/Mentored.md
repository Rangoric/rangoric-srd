# Mentored

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

A tutor or private instructor guided you in learning your art, profession, or trade, and through your education, you became capable of teaching and guiding others. Choose a single [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] skill. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on checks with that skill. You also gain a +1 trait bonus when you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]]'s skill check with any skill.
