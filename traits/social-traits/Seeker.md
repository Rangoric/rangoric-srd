# Seeker

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You are always on the lookout for reward and danger. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, and Perception is always a class skill for you.
