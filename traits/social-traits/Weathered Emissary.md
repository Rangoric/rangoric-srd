# Weathered Emissary

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

Your travels in the wildlands gave you insight into how to survive their dangers and communicate with their inhabitants. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks, and Linguistics is always a class skill for you.
