# Civilized

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You are well versed in the local laws, customs, and politics. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks, and Society is always a class skill for you.
