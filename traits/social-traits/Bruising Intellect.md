# Bruising Intellect

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

Your sharp intellect and rapier-like wit bruise egos. [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] is always a class skill for you, and you may use your Intelligence modifier when making Intimidation checks instead of your Charisma modifier.
