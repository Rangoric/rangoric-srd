# Fast Talker

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You had a knack for getting yourself into trouble as a child, and as a result developed a silver tongue at an early age. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks, and Deception is always a class skill for you.
