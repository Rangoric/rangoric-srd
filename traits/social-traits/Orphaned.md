# Orphaned

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You grew up separated from your birth parents, and had to learn to watch out for yourself. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks, and Fieldcraft is always a class skill for you.
