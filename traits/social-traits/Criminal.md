# Criminal

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You spent your early life robbing and stealing to get by. Select one of the following skills: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]]. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
