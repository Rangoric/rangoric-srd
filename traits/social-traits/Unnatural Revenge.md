# Unnatural Revenge

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks against creatures of the animal, fey, or plant type, and Intimidation is always a class skill for you.
