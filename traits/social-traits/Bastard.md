# Bastard

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You were born out of wedlock. You have always been an outsider in society, and in your own family. This perspective has sharpened your insight. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks, and Sense Motive is always a class skill for you.
