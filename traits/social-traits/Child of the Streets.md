# Child of the Streets

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You grew up on the streets of a large city, and as a result you have developed a knack for picking pockets and hiding small objects on your person. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks, and Sleight of Hand is always a class skill for you.
