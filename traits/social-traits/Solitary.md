# Solitary

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

_You are skilled at slipping away from crowds._

**Benefits**: Once per day, you can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check to create a diversion to hide with a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on the check, and if successful, you take only a –5 penalty on your [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] check to get to an unobserved place while observers are distracted (instead of the normal –10 penalty).
