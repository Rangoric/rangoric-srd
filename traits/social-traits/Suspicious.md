# Suspicious

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You discovered at an early age that someone you trusted, perhaps an older sibling or a parent, had lied to you, and lied often, about something you had taken for granted, leaving you quick to question the claims of others. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks, and Sense Motive is always a class skill for you.
