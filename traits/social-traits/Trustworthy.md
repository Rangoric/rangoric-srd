# Trustworthy

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

People find it easy to put their faith in you. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks made to fool someone. You also gain a +1 trait bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks, and Persuasion is always a class skill for you.
