# Talented

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You are a virtuoso musician, actor, or storyteller. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on checks with a single [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] skill (your choice), and all Performance skills are always class skills for you.
