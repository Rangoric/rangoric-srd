# Destined Diplomat

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

Your relationship with your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]] gives you some clout with other outsiders. You gain a +2 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks toward outsiders, and Persuasion is always a class skill for you.
