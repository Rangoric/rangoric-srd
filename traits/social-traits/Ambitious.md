# Ambitious

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You exude confidence in the presence of those more powerful than you—sometimes unreasonably so. You gain a +4 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks made to influence creatures with at least 5 Hit Dice more than you possess.
