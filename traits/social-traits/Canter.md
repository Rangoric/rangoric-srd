# Canter

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You grew up among thieves and scoundrels, and their unusual speech patterns and turns of phrase don't faze you in the slightest today as a result. Anyone who attempts to use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] to deliver a secret message to you gains a +5 bonus on his Deception check. When you attempt to intercept a secret message using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]], you gain a +5 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on the attempt.
