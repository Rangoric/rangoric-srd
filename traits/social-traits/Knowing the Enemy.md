# Knowing the Enemy

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You know a good deal about your most dire enemies. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on checks with the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] skill corresponding to your first [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] choice, and that Knowledge skill is always a class skill for you.
