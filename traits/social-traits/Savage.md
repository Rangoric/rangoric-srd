# Savage

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You were born and raised in untamed lands far from civilization. You learned to survive in the elements among brutal humanoids and beasts. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] checks and a +1 trait bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to get along in the wild. Nature is always a class skill for you.
