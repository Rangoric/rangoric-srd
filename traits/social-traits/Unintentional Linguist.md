# Unintentional Linguist

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You can speak with outsiders. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] checks, and you begin play knowing one of the following [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics#Languages|languages]] (in addition to those granted by your race and Intelligence modifier): Abyssal, Aquan, Celestial, Ignan, Infernal, Protean, or Terran.
