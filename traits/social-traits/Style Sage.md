# Style Sage

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You have a passion for history and news concerning monastic disciplines. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks, and Society is always a class skill for you. In addition, you gain a +1 trait bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks made to gather information about any person with levels in [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]].
