# Truth's Agent

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You are skilled at weeding out information. You gain a +1 [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#^trait-bonus|trait bonus]] on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks made to gather information and all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks. Society is always a class skill for you.
