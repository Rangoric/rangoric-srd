# Beast Bond

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

You share a close bond with animals. You gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks, and Animal Handling is always a class skill for you.
