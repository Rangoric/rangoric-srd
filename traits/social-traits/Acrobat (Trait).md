# Acrobat

**Category**: [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits#Social Traits|social trait]].

Having trained from a young age, you're capable of amazing feats of daring. You gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks, and you take only a –2 penalty instead of the normal –5 penalty when using the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] skill to attempt an accelerated climb.
