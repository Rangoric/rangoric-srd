# Unbound Steps

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-master-tricks|master trick]] (Su).*

This trick allows a ninja to use her ki to walk through the air. Whenever the ninja uses the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-light-steps|light steps]] class feature, she can walk on air, rising or descending as she desires. She must end her move on a solid surface. Each use of this ability uses up 1 ki point.
