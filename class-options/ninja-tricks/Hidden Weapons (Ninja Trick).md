# Hidden Weapons

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-tricks|ninja trick]] (Ex).*

A ninja with this ability can easily conceal weapons on her body. The ninja adds her level on opposed [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made to conceal a weapon. In addition, she can draw hidden weapons as a move action, instead of as a standard action.
