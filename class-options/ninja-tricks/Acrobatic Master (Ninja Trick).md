# Acrobatic Master

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-tricks|ninja trick]] (Su).*

As a swift action, a ninja with this trick can focus her ki to grant her a +20 bonus on one [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check of her choice made before the start of her next turn. Using this ability expends 1 ki point from her ki pool.
