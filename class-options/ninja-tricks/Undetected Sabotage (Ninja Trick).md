# Undetected Sabotage

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-tricks|ninja trick]] (Ex).*

Whenever a ninja with this trick uses [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] to sabotage or disable a device, she can make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] check with a bonus equal to her level. Anyone who inspects the device does not notice the sabotage and assumes the object works unless he beats the ninja's check with an opposed [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check.
