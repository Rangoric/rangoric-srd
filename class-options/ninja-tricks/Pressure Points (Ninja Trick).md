# Pressure Points

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-tricks|ninja trick]] (Su). It adds to sneak attack: only one such trick applies to an attack (see [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-tricks|ninja tricks]]).*

A ninja with this trick can strike at an opponent's vital pressure points, causing weakness and intense pain. Whenever the ninja deals sneak attack damage, she also deals 1 point of Strength or Dexterity damage, decided by the ninja. Unlike normal ability damage, this damage can be healed by a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check. Each successful check heals 1 point of damage caused by this trick. A ninja with this trick receives a +10 insight bonus on this Medicine check.
