# Psychic Disciplines

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)|psychic]]’s disciplines, one note each. Each discipline note gives the ability score the psychic uses for her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-pool|phrenic pool]] and phrenic amplifications, her bonus spells (one at 1st level and one every 2 levels from 4th to 18th), and her discipline powers (at 1st, 5th and 13th level, with any that improve later). The class note has the rules ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-discipline|psychic discipline]]).
