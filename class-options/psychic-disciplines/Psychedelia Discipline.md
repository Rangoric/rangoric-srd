# Psychedelia Discipline

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-discipline|psychic discipline]]._

You ingest hallucinogens to expand your mind. Experimentation and study show you which ones will have the greatest effect. Your psychedelic forays put you into a different mental space from others, and normal people don’t really understand you.

**Phrenic Pool Ability**: Wisdom.

**Bonus Spells**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polypurpose Panacea|polypurpose panacea]]_ (1st), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mad Hallucination|mad hallucination]]_ (4th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Synesthesia|synesthesia]]_ (6th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_ (8th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirage Arcana|mirage arcana]]_ (10th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Joyful Rapture|joyful rapture]]_ (12th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Waves of Ecstasy|waves of ecstasy]]_ (14th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Euphoric Tranquility|euphoric tranquility]]_ (16th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection|astral projection]]_ (18th).

## Discipline Powers

You distort your own mind and perceptions, and can impress your altered states onto others.

**Drug Resistance (Ex)**: When you ingest [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|drugs]], you take half as much ability damage (minimum 1). You also gain a +4 bonus on defenses to avoid becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#^addiction|addicted]] to a drug or to overcome being addicted.

**Cognatogen (Su)**: Once per day, you can create a cognatogen, a mutagen-like mixture that heightens one mental ability score at the expense of a physical ability score. When you imbibe a cognatogen, you gain a +2 natural armor bonus and a +4 alchemical bonus to the selected ability score for 1 minute per psychic level. In addition, while the cognatogen is in effect, you take a –2 penalty to one of your physical ability scores. If the cognatogen enhances your Intelligence, it applies a penalty to your Strength. If it enhances your Wisdom, it applies a penalty to your Dexterity. If it enhances your Charisma, it applies a penalty to your Constitution. Otherwise, this ability works just like the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|alchemist’s mutagen]] ability. When the effect of the cognatogen ends, you take 2 points of ability damage to the ability score penalized by the cognatogen. If you have both alchemist and psychic levels, these levels stack to determine the duration of your cognatogen and the attack bonus against a non-alchemist who drinks your cognatogen. If you gain discoveries, you can take the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Grand Cognatogen (Discovery)|grand cognatogen]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Greater Cognatogen (Discovery)|greater cognatogen]] discoveries to improve your cognatogen. The [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Infuse Mutagen (Discovery)|infuse mutagen]] discovery and the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-persistent-mutagen|persistent mutagen]] class ability apply to cognatogens. However, even if you have alchemist levels, the duration of your cognatogen remains 1 minute per level (instead of 10 minutes per level) and you can still create only one per day (instead of an unlimited number) unless you also possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Cognatogen (Discovery)|cognatogen]] discovery.

**Warped Brain (Su)**: At 5th level, your mind becomes difficult to comprehend. When another creature uses a mind-affecting spell or ability against you, your mind attacks that creature’s Will. On a hit, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round. This ability triggers even if the spell or ability misses you (or you are otherwise unaffected by it), but doesn’t apply if you’re a willing subject of the spell. This is a mind-affecting effect.

**Hallucinogenic Aura (Su)**: At 13th level, a mental field emanates from you, touching the minds of those nearby. It attacks the Will of any creature within 30 feet of you, and on a hit the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d4 rounds. A creature the aura misses is immune to your hallucinogenic aura for 24 hours. A creature it hits isn’t attacked again while it’s confused by this aura, and becomes immune for 24 hours once its confusion ends. This is a mind-affecting effect. You’re immune to your own hallucinogenic aura, as well as that of any other psychic. You can brew an antidote that protects the imbiber from your aura. Brewing 1 dose requires 1 hour and a successful DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) check. One dose’s effects last for 1 month.
