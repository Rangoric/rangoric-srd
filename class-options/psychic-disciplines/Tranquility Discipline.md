# Tranquility Discipline

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-discipline|psychic discipline]]._

Psychic magic flows through you when you attain a peaceful mental state. Regular meditation expands your mind, allowing new powers to develop.

**Phrenic Pool Ability**: Wisdom.

**Bonus Spells**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telempathic Projection (Spell)|telempathic projection]]_ (1st), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ (4th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mantle of Calm|mantle of calm]]_ (6th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze, Mass|mass daze]]_ (8th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Serenity|serenity]]_ (10th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Psychic Surgery|psychic surgery]]_ (12th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Blank|mind blank]]_ (14th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Euphoric Tranquility|euphoric tranquility]]_ (16th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Time Stop|time stop]]_ (18th).

## Discipline Powers

Your stable mind protects you and your allies.

**Mental Placidity (Su)**: As an immediate action, you can gain a +2 bonus to your Will Defense against an attack about to be rolled against it. This bonus increases to +4 against an enchantment spell or effect. Any ally the same effect attacks gains a +1 bonus to Will Defense against it, or a +2 bonus if the effect is an enchantment. If the attack misses you, you regain 1 point in your phrenic pool. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier.

**Calming Presence (Su)**: At 5th level, you become a calming force. You can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Calm Emotions|calm emotions]]_ as a spell-like ability a number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier.

**Purge Disquiet (Su)**: At 13th level, you can banish mental distress. You become immune to fear spells and effects and to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] condition. As a standard action, you can choose a willing creature that is either within 30 feet or in telepathic contact with you. For 1 round per psychic level you possess, you suppress any fear effect or confused, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^cowering|cowering]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] condition affecting that creature. You can suppress only one effect at a time with this ability, and choosing a new target ends the suppression for the character you previously chose.
