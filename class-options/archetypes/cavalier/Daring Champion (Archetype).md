# Daring Champion

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-archetypes|cavalier]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

While many cavaliers are the champions of old fighting forms, some younger, more daring cavaliers mix a martial style influenced by the lighter armored and more flamboyant [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler|swashbuckler]] forms with the dedication of cavalier orders.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Daring champions are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, light and medium armor, and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Buckler|bucklers]]. This replaces the cavalier's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#Class Features|weapon and armor proficiencies]].

**Champion's Finesse (Ex)**: At 1st level, a daring champion gains the benefits of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Finesse|Weapon Finesse]] feat with light or one-handed piercing melee weapons, and he can use Charisma in place of Intelligence for the purpose of [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Fighter Bonus|combat feats]] prerequisites. A daring champion also counts as having the Weapon Finesse feat for the purpose of meeting feat requirements. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-mount|mount]].

**Nimble (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a daring champion gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC when wearing light or no armor and carrying no more than a light load. Anything that causes the daring champion to lose his Dexterity bonus to AC also causes him to lose this dodge bonus. This bonus increases by 1 for every 4 levels beyond 3rd (to a maximum of +5 at 19th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-cavaliers-charge|cavalier's charge]].

**Panache and Deeds (Ex)**: At 4th level, a daring champion gains the swashbuckler's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] class feature, along with the following swashbuckler [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|deeds]]: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Dodging Panache (Deed)|dodging panache]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Opportune Parry and Riposte (Deed)|opportune parry and riposte]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Precise Strike (Deed)|precise strike]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Swashbuckler Initiative (Deed)|swashbuckler initiative]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-expert-trainer|expert trainer]].

**Advanced Deeds (Ex)**: At 11th level, a daring champion gains the following swashbuckler deeds: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Superior Feint (Deed)|superior feint]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Targeted Strike (Deed)|targeted strike]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Subtle Blade (Deed)|subtle blade]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Dizzying Defense (Deed)|dizzying defense]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-mighty-charge|mighty charge]].

**Champion's Weapon Mastery (Ex)**: At 20th level, when a daring champion threatens a critical hit with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon, that critical is automatically confirmed. Furthermore, the critical modifier of those weapons increases by 1 (×2 becomes ×3, for example). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-supreme-charge|supreme charge]].
