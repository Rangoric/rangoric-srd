# Shigenjo

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-archetypes|oracle]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|tengus]].*

The shigenjo walks the path of enlightenment and transcendence by seeking oneness with the celestial spirits. In doing so, she unlocks the martial potential of her own spiritual power. A shigenjo has the following class features.

**Class Skills**: A shigenjo adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis) to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#Class Skills|class skills]] in place of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha).

**Alignment**: Any neutral.

**Recommended Mysteries**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ancestor Mystery|ancestor]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Battle Mystery|battle]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Flame Mystery|flame]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Heavens Mystery|heavens]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Lore Mystery|lore]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Metal Mystery|metal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Nature Mystery|nature]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Stone Mystery|stone]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Time Mystery|time]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Waves Mystery|waves]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Wood Mystery|wood]].

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Strike|true strike]]* (2nd), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]* (4th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Power|divine power]]* (8th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Jar|magic jar]]* (12th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ki Shout|ki shout]]* (14th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Moment of Prescience|moment of prescience]]* (16th). These bonus spells replace the shigenjo's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|mystery]] bonus spells at these levels.

**Ki Pool (Su)**: At 7th level, a shigenjo gains a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|pool of *ki* points]], supernatural energy she can use to accomplish amazing feats. The number of points in the shigenjo's *ki* pool is equal to 1/3 her oracle level + her Charisma modifier. The *ki* pool is replenished each morning after 8 hours of rest or meditation; these hours do not need to be consecutive. If the shigenjo possesses levels in another class that grants points to a *ki* pool, *ki* points gained from the shigenjo class stack with those gained from the other class to determine the total number of *ki* points in the combined pool, but only one ability score modifier is added to the total. The choice of which score to use is made when the second class ability is gained, and once made, the choice is permanent. The shigenjo can use *ki* points from this pool to power the abilities of every class she possesses that grants a *ki* pool.

As long as she has at least 1 point in her *ki* pool, a shigenjo can make a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-strike|*ki* strike]] as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] whose level is equal to her oracle level – 3. As a swift action, she may spend 1 point of *ki* to gain one of the following benefits.

*Ki Magic*: Add +1 to the DC of the next spell she casts on her turn.

*Ki Curse*: Treat her oracle level as 5 higher for the purpose of determining the effects of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-curse|curse]] for the next round.

*Ki Insight*: Gain a +4 insight bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic#Each class’s Magic skill|Magic skill]] checks for 1 round.

This ability replaces the shigenjo's 7th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]].

**Quivering Palm (Su)**: At 15th level, a shigenjo may learn [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-quivering-palm|quivering palm]] as the monk ability of the same name in place of a revelation. She treats her oracle level as her monk level for this ability, and the DC is based on her Charisma bonus instead of her Wisdom bonus.

**Final Revelation**: Upon reaching 20th level, you achieve true enlightenment and become one with the celestial spirits. You gain the ability to speak with any creature that uses a language. For a number of days equal to your Wisdom score, you can ignore the negative effects of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Weather|extreme weather]], starvation, thirst, and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhaustion]]. If you die, your powerful connection to the celestial realm allows you to be reborn 3 days later (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reincarnate|reincarnate]]*). This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-final-revelation|final revelation]] of the shigenjo's mystery.
