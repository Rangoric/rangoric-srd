# Purifier

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-archetypes|oracle]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|aasimars]]._

The purifier seeks out signs of possession or mind control that manifest from unwilling (and often unwitting) servants for fiendish corruptors and their mortal minions. A purifier seeks liberation of mind, body, and spirit from the bondage of sin and the taint of the unholy. A purifier gains the following class features.

**Recommended Mysteries**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ancestor Mystery|ancestor]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Battle Mystery|battle]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Heavens Mystery|heavens]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Lore Mystery|lore]].

**Bonus Spells**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Veil of Heaven|veil of heaven]]_ (2nd), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confess|confess]]_ (4th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cast Out|cast out]]_ (6th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Denounce|denounce]]_ (8th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Evil|dispel evil]]_ (10th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Banishment|banishment]]_ (12th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Word|holy word]]_ (14th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Blank|mind blank]]_ (16th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom|freedom]]_ (18th).

**Diminished Spellcasting**: A purifier can use one fewer [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-spells|spell]] per day of each level and does not automatically learn cure or inflict spells. Her number of oracle spells known is unchanged.

**Revelations**: A purifier must take the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelations]] at the listed levels.

_See Sin (Sp)_: At 3rd level, a purifier gains a bonus equal to 1/2 her oracle level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks to sense enchantments, which she can make as a full-round action. She also gains a bonus equal to 1/2 her oracle level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] checks to identify enchantment school spells and spells with the curse or emotion descriptor.

_Celestial Armor (Su)_: At 7th level, a purifier's armor takes on a golden or silvery sheen and becomes light as a feather. Her armor weighs half as much as long as she wears it, and she also gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] 4 levels lower than her oracle level. At 11th level, a purifier gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Armor Proficiency (Heavy)|heavy armor proficiency]].

_Sin Eater (Su)_: At 11th level, a purifier can consume a curse, enchantment, or emotion effect by touch as a full-round action. She can do this a number of times per day equal to her Charisma modifier, and must make a Charisma check with a bonus equal to her oracle level against a DC of 11 + the caster level of the effect (or the Hit Dice of the creator for a supernatural effect). If the check succeeds, the effect is negated; however, the purifier is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1d4 rounds.

If the target is possessed (such as by a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Jar|magic jar]]_ effect or a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ghost/Ghost|ghost]]'s malevolence ability), the possessor is forced out on a successful check. Whether the check succeeds or fails, the possessor is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 2d4 rounds.

**Sacred Scourge (Su)**: At 5th level, a purifier may channel holy power to harm evil outsiders as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-channel-energy|cleric]] of her level using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Alignment Channel|Alignment Channel]] feat. She may use this ability a number of times per day equal to 1 + her Charisma modifier.

**Holy Terror (Su)**: At 9th level, a purifier may use her sacred scourge to panic evil outsiders as if using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Turn Undead|Turn Undead]] feat against undead.

**Celestial Master (Su)**: At 13th level, a purifier may use her sacred scourge to compel good outsiders to serve her, as if using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Command Undead (Feat)|Command Undead]] feat against undead.
