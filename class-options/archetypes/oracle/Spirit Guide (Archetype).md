# Spirit Guide

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-archetypes|oracle]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Through her exploration of the universe's mysteries, a spirit guide opens connections to the spirit world and forms bonds with the entities that inhabit it.

**Class Skills**: A spirit guide gains all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]] (Int) as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#Class Skills|class skills]]. This replaces the bonus class skills gained from the oracle's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|mystery]].

**Bonded Spirit (Su)**: At 3rd level, a spirit guide can form a temporary bond with a spirit, as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-wandering-spirit|wandering spirit]] class feature. She must make this selection each day when she refreshes her spells. A spirit guide cannot bond with a spirit that is incompatible with her alignment, ethos, or mystery (GM's discretion).

A spirit guide gains one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|hex]] of her choice from the list of hexes available from that [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Spirits|spirit]]. She uses her oracle level as her shaman level, and she switches Wisdom for Charisma and vice versa for the purpose of determining the hex's effects.

At 4th level, she adds the bonded spirit's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit-magic|spirit magic]] spells to her oracle spells known for that day, but only of spell levels she can cast. At 7th level, she gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit|spirit ability]] of her current bonded spirit. At 15th level, she gains the greater spirit ability of her current bonded spirit.

This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelations]] gained at 3rd, 7th, and 15th levels.

**Recommended Mysteries**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ancestor Mystery|Ancestor]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Heavens Mystery|heavens]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Lore Mystery|lore]].
