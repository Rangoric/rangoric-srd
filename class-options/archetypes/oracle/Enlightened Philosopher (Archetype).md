# Enlightened Philosopher

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-archetypes|oracle]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The enlightened philosopher seeks enlightenment through compassion, moderation, and humility. By following her philosophy's teachings, the enlightened philosopher seeks to reach enlightenment and become one with the universe.

**Alignment**: Any lawful.

**Recommended Mysteries**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ancestor Mystery|ancestor]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Flame Mystery|flame]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Heavens Mystery|heavens]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Lore Mystery|lore]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Metal Mystery|metal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Nature Mystery|nature]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Stone Mystery|stone]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Time Mystery|time]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Waves Mystery|waves]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Wood Mystery|wood]].

**Class Skills**: An enlightened philosopher adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] (Int) and all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]] (Int) to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#Class Skills|class skills]]. These replace the additional class skills from her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|mystery]].

**Bonus Spells**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom|owl's wisdom]]_ (4th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Walk|water walk]]_ (6th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]]_ (8th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_ (10th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Walk|wind walk]]_ (12th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt|ethereal jaunt]]_ (14th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Moment of Prescience|moment of prescience]]_ (16th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection|astral projection]]_ (18th). These bonus spells replace the oracle's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|mystery]] bonus spells at these levels.

**Revelations**: An enlightened philosopher must take the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] at 7th level.

**Mental Acuity (Ex)**: As the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Lore Mystery|lore]] mystery revelation ([[rangoric-srd/class-options/revelations/Mental Acuity (Revelation)|mental acuity]]).

**Final Revelation**: Upon achieving 20th level, you achieve true enlightenment and become one with the universe. You receive a bonus on all saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier. You become immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confusion]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhaustion]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigue]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nausea]], and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] effects. You can take 20 on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]] checks. Should you die, you are reborn 3 days later as a living example of the summoning of your chosen philosophy (treat as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reincarnate|reincarnate]]_ spell). This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-final-revelation|final revelation]] of the oracle's mystery.
