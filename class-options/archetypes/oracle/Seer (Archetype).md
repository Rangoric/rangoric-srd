# Seer

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-archetypes|oracle]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

While all oracles possess some ability at divination, the seer is a true prophet, able to see things as they really are, or will be.

**Recommended Mysteries**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Lore Mystery|lore]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Nature Mystery|nature]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Time Mystery|time]].

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* (4th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Clairaudience-Clairvoyance|clairaudience/clairvoyance]]* (6th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]* (8th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]* (10th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]* (12th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying, Greater|greater scrying]]* (14th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vision|vision]]* (16th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Foresight|foresight]]* (18th). These bonus spells replace the oracle's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|mystery]] bonus spells at these levels.

**Revelations**: A seer must take the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelations]] at the listed levels.

**Natural Divination (Ex)**: As the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Nature Mystery|nature]] mystery revelation ([[rangoric-srd/class-options/revelations/Natural Divination (Revelation)|natural divination]]). You must take this revelation at 1st level.

**Gift of Prophecy (Su)**: Once per day, you can enter a trance to gain a glimpse of the future. This trance lasts for 1 minute, which must be uninterrupted and during which you can take no other actions. At 3rd level, you gain the benefits of an *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]* spell with 90% effectiveness. At 5th level, you gain the benefits of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divination|divination]]* spell with 90% effectiveness. At 9th level, the knowledge you gain is equivalent to a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]]* spell. None of these spell effects require material components. You must take this revelation at 3rd level.
