# Community Guardian

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-archetypes|oracle]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halflings]]._

The community guardian is chosen to protect and succor the weak and innocent within her community. Her calling also allows her to draw upon and focus the collective will in order to achieve those goals. A community guardian has the following class features.

**Alignment**: Any good.

**Recommended Mysteries**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ancestor Mystery|ancestor]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Life Mystery|life]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Lore Mystery|lore]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Nature Mystery|nature]].

**Class Skills**: A community guardian adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis) to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#Class Skills|class skills]]. These replace the additional class skills from her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|mystery]].

**Bonus Spells**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless Water|bless water]]_ (2nd), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]_ (4th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]_ (6th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hallow|hallow]]_ (10th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroes’ Feast|heroes' feast]]_ (12th). These bonus spells replace the oracle's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|mystery]] bonus spells from these levels.

**Revelations**: A community guardian must take the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelations]] at the listed levels.

_Spirit of Community (Ex)_: As a move action, you call upon the spirits of community. For the next round, you grant every ally within 30 feet a +1 competence bonus on a single skill check (of the ally's choice) that it makes before the end of this revelation's duration. Furthermore, allies within 30 feet can, as a free action, choose to forgo this bonus, and instead grant a single ally a +1 increase to its competence bonus granted by this ability (maximum +5). You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. You must take this revelation at 1st level.

_Renewing Radiance (Su)_: Once per day you can produce a burst of swirling white light that provides a measure of protection and renewal to allies within 30 feet for 1 round. On their turn, the allies can choose either to gain a +1 sacred bonus to AC for 1 round or to heal a number of hit points equal to 1d6 + your Charisma bonus (their choice). If an ally is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]], it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stable|stabilized]] instead. At 7th level, the bonus to AC increases to +2, and the healing increases to 2d6 + your Charisma bonus hit points, and at 14th level the bonus to AC increases to +3, and the healing increases to 3d6 + your Charisma bonus hit points. You must take this revelation at 3rd level.
