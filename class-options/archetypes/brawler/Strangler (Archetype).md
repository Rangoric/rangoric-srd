# Strangler

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-archetypes|brawler]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A strangler is trained to choke the life out of her victims with her vise-like grip. Some stranglers are self-taught and are little more than brutish murderers, unhinged sociopaths, or opportunistic alley-bashers. Others are members of murder cults or specialized schools of assassination, trained since the cradle to kill with their bare hands.

**Class Skills**: A strangler adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex) to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#Class Skills|class skills]].

**Strangle (Ex)**: At 1st level, a strangler deals +1d6 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage whenever she succeeds at a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] check to damage or pin an opponent. The strangler is always considered [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] her target for the purpose of using this ability. This damage increases by +1d6 at 2nd, 8th and 15th levels. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-unarmed-strike|unarmed strike]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-flurry|brawler's flurry]].

**Practiced Strangler (Ex)**: At 2nd level, when a strangler has the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^grappling|grappled]] condition, she does not take a –4 penalty to Dexterity and does not lose her Dexterity bonus to AC. At 9th level, a grappling or grappled strangler still threatens an area and is still able to make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] while grappling or grappled; when [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^pinned|pinned]], she is not denied her Dexterity bonus and does not take a –4 penalty to Armor Class. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-ac-bonus|AC bonus]].

**Sleeper Hold (Ex)**: At 4th level, a strangler can instantly render a pinned opponent [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]. This works like the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-knockout|knockout]] ability, but requires a successful grapple combat maneuver check against the pinned opponent and does not have to damage the opponent. Creatures that do not need to breathe, are immune to critical hits, or are immune to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]] are immune to this ability. At 10th level, a strangler can use this ability twice per day. At 16th level, she can use it three times per day. This alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-knockout|knockout]].

**Neckbreaker (Ex)**: At 16th level, a strangler can attempt to instantly kill a pinned opponent as a standard action. This works like the sleeper hold ability, but imposes a –5 penalty on her grapple combat maneuver check. If the opponent succeeds at its Fortitude save, the strangler deals damage as if she had attempted the grapple check to damage her opponent; if the opponent fails its Fortitude save, it dies. Creatures that are immune to critical hits are immune to this ability. At 20th level, the strangler can attempt this as a move action instead of a standard action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-awesome-blow|awesome blow]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-improved-awesome-blow|improved awesome blow]].
