# Snakebite Striker

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-archetypes|brawler]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

With her lightning quickness and guile, a snakebite striker keeps her foes' attention focused on her, because any one of her feints might be an actual attack. By giving up some of a brawler's versatility, she increases her damage potential and exposes opponents to deadly and unexpected strikes.

**Class Skills**: The snakebite striker adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] (Cha) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex) to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#Class Skills|class skills]] and removes [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] (Cha) from it.

**Sneak Attack (Ex)**: At 1st level, the snakebite striker can make a sneak attack. This is as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|rogue ability of the same name]]. At 1st level, her sneak attack damage is +1d6. This increases by 1d6 at 6th, 10th, 12th, and 20th levels. If she gets a sneak attack bonus from another source, the bonuses on damage stack. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-martial-flexibility|martial flexibility]].

**Snake Feint (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a snakebite striker who uses a standard action to move can combine that move with a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]]. If she is able to feint as a move action (such as from having the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]] feat), she can combine a move action to move with her feint. At 11th level, once per round she can declare her square and one adjacent square as the origin of her attacks until her next turn (allowing her to use one or both squares to determine whether she or allies are [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] an opponent). At 15th level, she counts an additional adjacent square for this purpose. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-maneuver-training|maneuver training]] gained at 3rd and 7th levels.

**Opportunist (Ex)**: At 11th level, once per round the snakebite striker can make an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] against an opponent who has just been struck for damage in melee by another character. This attack counts as an attack of opportunity for that round. She cannot use this ability more than once per round, even if she has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]] feat or a similar ability. At 19th level, she can use this ability twice per round. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-maneuver-training|maneuver training]] gained at 11th and 19th levels.
