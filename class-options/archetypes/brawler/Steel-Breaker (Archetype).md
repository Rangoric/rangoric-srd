# Steel-Breaker

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-archetypes|brawler]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The steel-breaker studies destruction and practices it as an art form. She knows every defense has a breaking point, and can shatter those defenses with carefully planned strikes.

**Class Skills**: The steel-breaker adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] (Int) to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#Class Skills|class skills]] and removes [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] (Int) from it.

**Exploit Weakness (Ex)**: At 5th level, as a swift action a steel-breaker can observe a creature or object to find its weak point by succeeding at a Wisdom check, adding her brawler level against a DC of 10 + the object's hardness or the target's CR. If it succeeds, the steel-breaker gains a +2 bonus on attack rolls until the end of her turn, and any attacks she makes until the end of her turn ignore the creature or object's [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]] or hardness.

A steel-breaker can instead use this ability as a swift action to analyze the movements and expressions of one creature within 30 feet, granting a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks and to Reflex Defense, as well as a dodge bonus to AC against that opponent equal to 1/2 her brawler level until the start of her next turn. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-strike|brawler's strike]].

**Sunder Training (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a steel-breaker receives additional training in [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] combat maneuvers. She gains a +2 bonus on sunder combat maneuver checks and a +2 bonus to her [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when defending against this maneuver. At 7th level, these bonuses increase by 1, and she gains a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] combat maneuver checks and a +2 bonus to her CMD when defending against a disarm maneuver. At 11th, 15th, and 19th levels, all of these bonuses increase by 1. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-maneuver-training|maneuver training]].
