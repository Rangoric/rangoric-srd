# Gravewalker

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-archetypes|witch]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Having much in common with necromancers, the gravewalker is obsessed with the occult manipulations of the dead, particularly mindless undead such as zombies. Unlike the creations of standard necromancers, a gravewalker's creations remain forever tied to her will, and she can produce vile apparitions of tremendous power.

**Spells**: A gravewalker replaces some of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|patron spells]] with the following: 4th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command Undead|command undead]]*, 6th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]*, 12th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead|create undead]]*, 14th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Undead|control undead]]*, 16th—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Greater Undead|create greater undead]]*. These replace the patron spells.

**Spell Poppet**: Each gravewalker carries around a gristly, inanimate poppet stitched from human skin and stuffed with shards of bone, fingernails, and grave dirt. A gravewalker's spells come from the will of evil spirits residing in the poppet, and its ability to hold spells functions in a manner identical to the way a witch's spells are granted by her familiar. The gravewalker must commune with her poppet each day to prepare her spells and cannot prepare spells that are not stored in the poppet. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-familiar|witch’s familiar]]. The following familiar ability works differently for a gravewalker:

**[[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#Witch’s Familiar|Deliver Touch Spells]] (Su)**: At 3rd level or higher, a gravewalker can use her poppet to deliver touch spells. After casting a touch spell, as a full-round action, the witch can designate a target and stab a pin into her poppet, delivering the spell as a ranged touch attack. The target must be within range of her aura of desecration ability (see below).

**Aura of Desecration (Su)**: At 1st level, a gravewalker can create a 20-foot-radius aura of evil power. This aura increases the DC of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channeled negative energy]] by +1 and the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Turn Resistance|turn resistance]] of undead by +1. At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the radius of the aura increases by 5 feet, to a maximum of 70 feet at 20th level. This ability replaces the witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|1st-level hex]].

**Bonethrall (Su)**: At 1st level, a gravewalker can take control of an undead creature within her aura of desecration by forcing her will upon it (Will negates, using her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex DC]]). If it fails the save, the creature falls under her control as if she had used *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command Undead|command undead]]* (once control is established, the undead remain controlled even if outside the witch's aura). Intelligent undead receive a new saving throw each day to resist her command. The witch can control up to 1 HD of undead creatures per caster level. If an undead creature is under the control of another creature, the witch must make an opposed Charisma check whenever her orders conflict with that creature's. This replaces the witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex gained at 4th level]].

**Possess Undead (Sp)**: A gravewalker may take direct control of one of her undead minions within her aura of desecration, as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Jar|magic jar]]*; the witch's poppet acts as the soul receptacle for this ability. The minion gets no saving throw against this ability. This replaces the witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex gained at 8th level]].

**Hexes**: The following witch hexes complement the gravewalker archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Beast of Ill-Omen (Hex)|beast of ill-omen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Evil Eye (Hex)|evil eye]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Misfortune (Hex)|misfortune]].

**Major Hexes**: The following major hexes complement the gravewalker archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Ice Tomb (Hex)|ice tomb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Infected Wounds (Hex)|infected wounds]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Waxen Image (Hex)|waxen image]].

**Grand Hexes**: The following grand hexes complement the gravewalker archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Death Curse (Hex)|death curse]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Summon Spirit (Hex)|summon spirit]].
