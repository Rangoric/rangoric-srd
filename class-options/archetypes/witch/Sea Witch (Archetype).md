# Sea Witch

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-archetypes|witch]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A sea witch's affinities are tied to the vast oceans and the rolling waves. Her magic concerns the moon, tides, water, and winds, and she is most at peace when she is upon or near the sea.

**Patron**: A sea witch cannot choose a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|patron]] whose interests or theme opposes that of water (for example, earth or fire).

**Spells**: A sea witch replaces some of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|patron spells]] with the following: 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of the Sea|touch of the sea]]_, 4th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]_, 6th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_, 8th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]_, 10th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cloudkill|cloudkill]]_, 12th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Winds|control winds]]_, 14th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]]_, 16th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vortex|vortex]]_, 18th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tsunami|tsunami]]_.

**Know Direction (Sp)**: So long as she is near a sizable body of water (at least a lake with a diameter of 1 mile or more), a sea witch may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Know Direction|know direction]]_ at will as a spell-like ability.

**Sea Creature Empathy (Ex)**: A sea witch can influence the attitude of water-dwelling animals and animals that live along coasts and shores, including birds, as if using [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]]. The sea witch uses her witch level as her druid level for this ability. If the sea witch has wild empathy from another class, her witch levels stack with the other class's levels to determine her wild empathy bonus for these kinds of creatures. This ability replaces the witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|1st-level hex]].

**Hexes**: The following witch hexes complement the sea witch archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Charm (Hex)|charm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Flight (Hex)|flight]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Water Lung (Hex)|water lung]].

**Major Hexes**: The following major hexes complement the sea witch archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Hag's Eye (Hex)|hag's eye]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Hidden Home (Hex)|hidden home]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Weather Control (Hex)|weather control]].

**Grand Hexes**: The following grand hex complements the sea witch archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Natural Disaster (Hex)|natural disaster]].
