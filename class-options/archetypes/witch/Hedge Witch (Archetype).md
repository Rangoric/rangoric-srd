# Hedge Witch

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-archetypes|witch]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Among witches, there are those who devote themselves to the care of others and restrict their practices to the healing arts. They often take the place of clerics in rural communities and may wander the countryside servicing the needs of several small communities.

**Patron**: A hedge witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|patron]] is normally one with a healing theme, such as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/patrons/Healing Patron|healing]] patron.

**Spontaneous Healing (Su)**: A hedge witch can channel stored spell energy into healing spells that she did not prepare ahead of time. The witch can “lose” any prepared spell that is not an orison in order to cast any _cure_ spell of the same spell level or lower, even if she doesn't know that cure spell. This replaces the witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex gained at 4th level]].

**Empathic Healing (Su)**: A hedge witch can minister to a diseased or poisoned target, redirecting the affliction into herself. For a poisoned target, the witch must tend to him as a standard action; the poison makes its next attack against him as normal, but the witch suffers the effects of a hit instead of the poisoned creature. For a diseased target, the witch must tend to the sick person for an hour; the disease makes its next attack against him as normal, but the witch suffers the effects of a hit instead of the diseased creature. The witch does not actually become poisoned or diseased (and is not contagious and does not need to be cured), but suffers the effects of the affliction as if she had been. The witch normally uses this ability to extend the life of someone near death, giving him time to recover. This ability has no effect if the witch is immune to disease or poison. This replaces the witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex gained at 8th level]].

**Hexes**: The following witch hexes complement the hedge witch archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Cauldron (Hex)|cauldron]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Feral Speech (Hex)|feral speech]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Healing (Hex)|healing]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Tongues (Hex)|tongues]].

**Major Hexes**: The following major hexes complement the hedge witch archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Major Healing (Hex)|major healing]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Weather Control (Hex)|weather control]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Witch's Brew (Hex)|witch's brew]].

**Grand Hexes**: The following grand hex complements the hedge witch archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Life Giver (Hex)|life giver]].
