# Elemental Annihilator

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-archetypes|kineticist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

An elemental annihilator uses her powers only to harm others.

**Devastating Infusion (Su)**: At 1st level, an elemental annihilator can either shoot her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-kinetic-blast|kinetic blast]] at a target within 30 feet or make a single melee attack as if she were using [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Blade|kinetic blade]] as an attack action. For this attack, the elemental annihilator's base attack bonus from her kineticist levels is equal to her full kineticist level. A devastating infusion always deals an amount of damage equal to 1d8 + the elemental annihilator's Constitution modifier, regardless of the annihilator's class level or use of composite blasts. When making a melee attack with devastating infusion, the elemental annihilator doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity, and if she uses two hands, the attack's damage is equal to 1d8 + 1-1/2 times her Constitution modifier. This is a 1st-level form infusion that costs 0 points of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] and can be used with any physical blast (but not energy blasts). Unlike with kinetic blade, the elemental annihilator can use [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]] with devastating infusion. The damage bonus from [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-overflow|elemental overflow]] doesn't apply to devastating infusion's damage rolls. This ability replaces the 1st-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusion]] and the basic utility wild talent normally granted by selecting an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|element]].

**Dampened Versatility (Ex)**: An elemental annihilator can never gain utility [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talents]].

**Bonus Feats**: At 2nd level, an elemental annihilator can select a bonus feat from the following list: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deadly Aim|Deadly Aim]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Double Slice|Double Slice]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Shot|Rapid Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Finesse|Weapon Finesse]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]]. Rapid Shot and Two-Weapon Fighting can be used with a kinetic blast only if the annihilator also possesses flurry of devastation (see below). She gains an additional bonus feat at 8th, 10th, 14th, and 18th levels. At 8th level, she can also select [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Two-Weapon Fighting|Improved Two-Weapon Fighting]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Specialization|Weapon Specialization]]. At 10th level, she can also select [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Weapon Focus|Greater Weapon Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Precise Shot|Improved Precise Shot]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Rend|Two-Weapon Rend]]. An elemental annihilator doesn't need to meet the prerequisites for these feats to select them as bonus feats. This ability replaces the 2nd-, 8th-, 10th-, 14th-, and 18th-level utility [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talents]].

**Increased Range (Su)**: At 3rd level, an elemental annihilator gains the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Extended Range|extended range]] infusion. At 9th level, she gains the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Extreme Range|extreme range]] infusion. This ability replaces the 3rd- and 9th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusions]].

**Ever-Present Threat (Su)**: At 4th level, an elemental annihilator threatens all foes within her natural reach. If anyone provokes an attack of opportunity from her, she can form her melee devastating infusion and make the attack, after which the weapon disappears. This ability replaces the 4th-level utility [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talent]].

**Blast Training (Ex)**: At 5th level, an elemental annihilator gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls with her devastating infusion form infusion. This bonus increases by 1 at 9th level and every 4 levels thereafter. This ability replaces the 5th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusion]].

**Flurry of Devastation (Su)**: At 6th level, an elemental annihilator can make a full attack of devastating infusions against targets within 120 feet as a full-attack action. Each individual attack deals damage as a devastating infusion. This is a 3rd-level form infusion that costs 1 point of burn and can be used with the same blast types as devastating infusion. Abilities such as Rapid Shot and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_ that grant additional attacks apply normally to this full attack. If she makes only melee attacks with her flurry of devastation, she can use the Two-Weapon Fighting feats (unlike with the kinetic blade wild talent), but ranged devastating infusions do not work with Two-Weapon Fighting. For the elemental annihilator's off-hand attacks with Two-Weapon Fighting, she adds half her Constitution modifier to the damage (increasing to her full Constitution modifier if she has Double Slice). Though she can normally mix melee and ranged attacks with her flurry of devastation, she can't do so when she uses Two-Weapon Fighting. She can never use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-metakinesis|metakinesis]] or substance infusions with flurry of devastation. This ability replaces the 6th-, 12th-, and 16th-level utility [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talents]].

**Omnicide (Su)**: At 20th level, an elemental annihilator can unleash omnicide, a unique physical composite blast. Omnicide deals 10d6+10 points of bludgeoning damage, 10d6+10 points of cold damage, 10d6+10 points of electricity damage, 10d6+10 points of fire damage, and 10d6+10 points of force damage, and counts as a blast of all five elements. Omnicide costs 4 points of burn, which can be reduced by [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-composite-specialization|composite specialization]] as normal. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-omnikinesis|omnikinesis]] and the 20th-level utility [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talent]].
