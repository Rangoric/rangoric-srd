# Blood Kineticist

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-archetypes|kineticist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A blood kineticist works the water in a creature's blood.

**Blood Focus (Ex)**: A blood kineticist must choose water as her primary [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|element]] and must choose [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Water Blast|water blast]] as her first simple blast. All of her infusions granted by this archetype work only on targets with blood and automatically fail if the target is immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^bleed|bleed]] damage.

**Wrack (Su)**: At 1st level, a blood kineticist can use her abilities to wrack a target within 30 feet, harming the target by manipulating its blood from the inside. The target takes half the normal damage of the blood kineticist's blast, and can attempt a Fortitude save to reduce that amount to 1/4 normal damage, but the damage is untyped and ignores [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]]. This is a 1st-level form infusion that costs 0 points of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] and can be used with water and blood blasts. This ability replaces the 1st-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusion]].

**Bleeding Infusion (Su)**: At 5th level, a blood kineticist can infuse her blasts with a vicious power that causes internal or external bleeding. On a failed Fortitude save, the target takes 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^bleed|bleed]] damage for every die of the blast's damage. A target that fails its saving throw against the wrack form infusion does not receive a saving throw to avoid the bleed damage from bleeding infusion on the same blast. This is a 2nd-level substance infusion that costs 2 points of burn and can be used with water and blood blasts. This ability replaces the 5th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusion]].

**Blood Tell (Sp)**: At 6th level, a blood kineticist's connection to blood allows her to learn much from the blood of another. This works as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blood Biography|blood biography]]_, except the blood kineticist suddenly knows the information, rather than having it appear on a parchment. This is a 3rd-level utility wild talent and costs 0 points of burn. She can accept 1 point of burn to reduce the casting time to a standard action instead of 1 minute. This ability replaces the 6th-level utility [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talent]].

**Blood Blast (Su)**: The first time a blood kineticist selects water with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]], she doesn't gain the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Cold Blast|cold]] simple blast or the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Ice Blast|ice]] composite blast; instead, she gains the blood composite blast, which costs 2 points of burn. A blood blast is a physical blast that deals bludgeoning damage. It is associated with the same infusions as the water blast. If the blood kineticist selects water with expanded element a second time, she gains the cold simple blast and ice composite blast as normal. This ability alters expanded element.

**Blood Throw (Su)**: At 8th level, a blood kineticist gains the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Foe Throw|foe throw]] infusion as a bonus infusion, and can use it with the water element on water and blood blasts, in addition to its normal associated element and blast. This ability replaces the 8th-level utility [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talent]].

**Gut-Wrenching Infusion (Su)**: At 9th level, a blood kineticist can infuse her blasts with the ability to manipulate her opponent's insides to the point of impairing their function. On a failed Fortitude save, the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute. A target that fails its saving throw against the wrack form infusion does not receive a saving throw to avoid the sickened condition from gut-wrenching infusion on the same blast. This is a 4th-level substance infusion that costs 3 points of burn and can be used with water and blood blasts. This ability replaces the 9th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusion]].

**Vampiric Infusion (Su)**: At 11th level, a blood kineticist can drain the vitality from her enemy's blood to restore her own. If her blast hits (or the enemy fails its saving throw against a blast without an attack roll) the blood kineticist can activate the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Healer|kinetic healer]] utility wild talent on herself by accepting its burn cost; she doesn't need to take an action to do so. If she doesn't possess the kinetic healer utility wild talent, she can still use this ability, but it heals only half as much. This is a 5th-level substance infusion that costs 3 points of burn and can be used with water and blood blasts. This ability replaces the 11th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusion]].

**Blood Mastery (Su)**: At 20th level, a blood kineticist no longer ages and can't be magically aged. She doesn't take penalties to her ability scores for aging and removes any such penalties she already possesses. She still accrues age bonuses as normal, but she doesn't die of old age when her time would be up. Because of her utter control over her own blood, she is immune to bleed, injected poisons, injury diseases, and the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] conditions unless she chooses to be affected. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-omnikinesis|omnikinesis]].
