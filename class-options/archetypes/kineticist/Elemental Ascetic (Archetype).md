# Elemental Ascetic

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-archetypes|kineticist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

An elemental ascetic channels his powers through his body, as a monk does.

**Elemental Flurry (Su)**: At 1st level, an elemental ascetic gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]] as a bonus feat. He gains the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Fist|kinetic fist]] form infusion and it costs 0 points of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] instead of 1 point of burn. When using the kinetic fist form infusion with a full attack, he can make a flurry of blows as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-flurry-of-blows|monk class feature]]. He must use only his fists to make this flurry, no matter what other abilities he possesses. Like a monk, he can use this ability only when unarmored, not using a shield, and unencumbered. He can't use his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-kinetic-blast|kinetic blast]] without a form infusion, nor can he ever use his kinetic blast with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Chain|chain]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Extended Range|extended range]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Extreme Range|extreme range]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Foe Throw|foe throw]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Flurry of Blasts|flurry of blasts]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Many Throw|many throw]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Snake (Wild Talent)|snake]] form infusions, or with any other form infusion that requires a ranged attack roll or ranged touch attack roll. This ability alters kinetic blast and replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-overflow|elemental overflow]].

**Elemental Wisdom (Su)**: An elemental ascetic can use his Wisdom modifier instead of his Constitution modifier to determine the DCs of Constitution-based wild talents, the duration of wild talents with a Constitution-based duration, and his bonus on concentration checks for wild talents. This ability alters the key ability scores of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talents]].

**AC Bonus (Ex)**: At 2nd level, when unarmored, not using a shield, and unencumbered, an elemental ascetic adds his Wisdom bonus (if any) to his AC and his CMD. These bonuses to AC apply even against touch attacks or when the elemental ascetic is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]]. He loses these bonuses when he is immobilized or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]]. This bonus increases by 1 for every 4 kineticist levels the elemental ascetic possesses beyond 2nd. The elemental ascetic's kineticist levels stack with monk levels for the purpose of determining when the bonus increases. An elemental ascetic can never take the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Expanded Defense|expanded defense]] utility wild talent. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-defense|elemental defense]].

**Powerful Fist (Su)**: At 5th level, an elemental ascetic can accept 2 additional points of burn when using kinetic fist to increase that infusion's damage dice from d6s to d8s. At 9th level, he can instead accept 3 additional points of burn to increase the damage dice from d6s to d10s. At 13th level, he can instead accept 4 additional points of burn to increase the damage dice from d6s to d12s. All of these options count as burn from a form infusion and can thus be reduced by [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion-specialization|infusion specialization]]. This ability replaces the 5th-, 9th-, and 13th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion|infusions]].
