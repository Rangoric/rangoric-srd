# Treetop Monk

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Vanara|vanaras]]._

While many vanaras follow traditional monastic training and traditions, others learn to blend exotic combat and the mysterious forces of _ki_ with the natural world, allowing them to move through trees and overgrowth to deliver devastating attacks. A treetop monk has the following class features.

**Branch Runner (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a treetop monk adds half the base speed bonus from his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-fast-movement|fast movement]] ability to his racial climb speed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-still-mind|still mind]].

**Wood Affinity (Su)**: At 5th level, a treetop monk may expend 1 point from his _ki_ pool as a free action to treat a wooden object as if it were not [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^broken|broken]] for 1 minute (this includes a weapon with a wooden haft such as an axe or spear). At 8th level, as a free action, a treetop monk can expend 1 point from his _ki_ pool to use the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lunge|Lunge]] feat with any wooden or wood-hafted melee weapon. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-purity-of-body|purity of body]].

**Freedom of Movement (Su)**: At 12th level, a treetop may expend 1 point from his _ki_ pool as a swift action to gain the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_ for 1 round. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-abundant-step|abundant step]].
