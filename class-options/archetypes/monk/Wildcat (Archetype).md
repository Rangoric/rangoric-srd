# Wildcat

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A wildcat is a student of the school of hard knocks who dedicates himself to learning how to take down foes by any means necessary. A wildcat isn’t afraid to smash a tankard over a foe’s head, stomp an opponent’s foot, gouge an eye, or generally create mayhem to gain any possible advantage.

**Ready for Anything (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a wildcat gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to act in a surprise round. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-still-mind|still mind]].

**Brawler Maneuver Training (Ex)**: At 4th level, a wildcat gains additional training with the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]]. He gains a +1 bonus on combat maneuver checks when attempting a dirty trick and a +1 bonus to his CMD to resist this maneuver.

At 7th, 10th, and 16th levels, a wildcat becomes further trained in another combat maneuver, gaining the above +1 bonus on combat maneuver checks and to his CMD to resist that combat maneuver. In addition, each of the bonuses granted by previous maneuver training increases by 1. For example, when a wildcat reaches 7th level, he gains a +1 bonus on one type of combat maneuver and +1 to his CMD to resist that combat maneuver, and the bonuses for the dirty trick combat maneuver increase to +2.

This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-high-jump|high jump]].

**Improvised Weapon Mastery (Ex)**: Starting at 4th level, a wildcat’s damage with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^improvised-weapons|improvised weapons]] increases. When wielding an improvised weapon, he uses the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|unarmed strike]] damage of a monk 4 levels lower instead of the base damage for that weapon (minimum monk level 1st). For example, a 6th-level Medium wildcat wielding a broken bottle deals 1d6 points of damage instead of the weapon’s normal 1d4. If the weapon normally deals more damage than this, its damage is unchanged. This increase in damage does not affect any other aspect of the weapon. The wildcat can decide to use the weapon’s base damage instead of his adjusted unarmed strike damage. This decision must be declared before the attack roll is attempted. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-slow-fall|slow fall]].

**Bonus Feat**: A wildcat adds the following feats to his list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feats]] at 6th level: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Dirty Trick|Improved Dirty Trick]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Reposition|Improved Reposition]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Steal|Improved Steal]]. He adds the following feats to his list of bonus feats at 10th level: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Dirty Trick|Quick Dirty Trick]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Reposition|Quick Reposition]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Steal|Quick Steal]].

**Knockout (Ex)**: At 9th level, a wildcat can unleash a devastating attack once per day that can instantly knock a target [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]. He must announce this intent before making his attack roll. If the wildcat’s strike is successful and the target takes damage from the blow, the blow attacks the target’s Fortitude at 1/2 the wildcat’s level + the higher of the wildcat’s Strength and Dexterity modifiers, and on a hit it falls unconscious for 1d6 rounds. Each round on its turn, an unconscious target can make the effect attack it again, and a miss ends it; this is a full-round action that does not provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]]. Creatures immune to critical hits or nonlethal damage are immune to this ability. At 12th level, a wildcat can use this ability twice per day. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-improved-evasion|improved evasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-abundant-step|abundant step]].

**Turn the Tables (Ex)**: At 13th level, whenever an opponent fails a combat maneuver check against the wildcat, it provokes an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] from the wildcat. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-diamond-soul|diamond soul]].

**Dirty Blow (Ex)**: At 19th level, when a wildcat succeeds at a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] combat maneuver, he can deal his unarmed strike damage to that opponent. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-empty-body|empty body]].
