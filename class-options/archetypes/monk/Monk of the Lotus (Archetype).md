# Monk of the Lotus

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Monks are warriors who hone their bodies into deadly weapons, but some monks eschew violence in favor of a more peaceful philosophy. While a monk of the lotus realizes that combat cannot always be avoided—and is more than capable in a fight—he understands that all creatures are connected, and to harm another is to harm the self. Instead, he strives to find peaceful resolutions to conflicts, and in doing so, hopes to achieve inner peace. A monk of the lotus has the following class features.

**Touch of Serenity (Su)**: At 1st level, a monk of the lotus gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Touch of Serenity|Touch of Serenity]] as a bonus feat, even if he does not meet the prerequisites. At 6th level, and every six levels thereafter, the duration of Touch of Serenity increases by 1 round. Each round on its turn, the effect attacks the target’s Will again, and a miss ends it. This duration does not stack; only the longest remaining duration applies. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-stunning-fist|Stunning Fist]].

**Touch of Surrender (Su)**: At 12th level, a monk of the lotus makes a foe into a friend with a single show of mercy. As an immediate action, when one of his melee attacks would reduce a creature to 0 or fewer hit points, the monk can spend 6 _ki_ points to make the target of that attack surrender. When the target surrenders, it is reduced to 0 hit points, becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^disabled|disabled]], and is charmed, as if the monk had cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ with a caster level equal to the monk's level. This effect hits the target automatically. This charm lasts until its duration expires, until the monk dismisses it or uses it on another creature, or until the target is again reduced to 0 or fewer hit points, whichever happens first. The monk can only have one creature charmed with touch of surrender at a time. This is a mind-affecting [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Charm and Compulsion|charm]] effect. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-abundant-step|abundant step]].

**Touch of Peace (Su)**: At 15th level, a monk of the lotus can set up vibrations within the body of another creature to win over the creature's mind. The monk can use touch of peace once per day, and must announce his intent before making his attack roll. On a successful hit, the attack deals no damage, but the target is charmed as if the monk had cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ with a caster level equal to the monk's level. This effect hits the target automatically. The creature is charmed for 1 day per level. If the monk or his allies attack the charmed creature, or if the monk asks or commands the charmed creature to take hostile actions, the effect ends. This is a mind-affecting charm effect. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-quivering-palm|quivering palm]].

**Learned Master (Ex)**: At 17th level, a monk of the lotus gains all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]] and the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] skill as class skills. The monk uses Wisdom instead of Intelligence as the key ability for these skills. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-tongue-of-the-sun-and-moon|tongue of the sun and the moon]].
