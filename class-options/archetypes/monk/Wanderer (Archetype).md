# Wanderer

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|humans]]._

Some monks wander the world in humility to learn and to share wisdom and philosophy from their teachers with those they meet, often aiding those who are in need. A wanderer has the following class features.

**Class Skills**: The wanderer adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] to his list of class skills.

**Far Traveler (Ex)**: At 1st level, the wanderer gains either one additional language known or proficiency in one exotic or martial weapon. At 4th level and every four levels thereafter, the wanderer may gain an additional language known or may retrain her weapon proficiency from this ability to a different exotic or martial weapon. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feat]] gained at 1st level.

**Long Walk (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the wanderer gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]] as a bonus feat, and the feat bonus doubles when he makes Constitution checks because of a forced march. In addition, a wanderer gains a +2 bonus on defenses against spells and effects that cause [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhaustion]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigue]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-still-mind|still mind]].

**Light Step (Su)**: At 5th level, a wanderer leaves no trail and cannot be tracked, though he can leave a trail if desired. By spending 1 point from his _ki_ pool, he can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ant Haul|ant haul]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Step|feather step]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pass without Trace|pass without trace]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tireless Pursuit|tireless pursuit]]_ as a spell-like ability (with a caster level equal to his monk level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-slow-fall|slow fall]].

**Inscrutable (Su)**: At 5th level, the wanderer gains a supernatural air of mystery. The DC to gain information or insight into the wanderer with [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] increases by 5. In addition, by spending 1 point from his _ki_ pool, the wanderer gains _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]_ for 24 hours with a caster level equal to his monk level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-high-jump|high jump]].

**Wanderer's Wisdom (Ex)**: At 7th level, the wanderer can dispense excellent advice in the form of philosophical proverbs and parables. As a swift action, the wanderer can [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-courage|inspire courage]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-competence|inspire competence]] as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] of his monk level by spending 2 points from his _ki_ pool. This affects one creature within 30 feet and lasts a number of rounds equal to the wanderer's Wisdom modifier (minimum 1 round). This ability is language-dependent. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-wholeness-of-body|wholeness of body]].

**Disappear Unnoticed (Ex)**: At 12th level, the wanderer may use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] to hide even while being directly observed or when no [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|cover]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] is available, as long as he is adjacent to at least one creature of his size or larger, by spending 1 point from his _ki_ pool. This effect lasts until the beginning of the wanderer's next turn and may be continued in consecutive rounds by spending 1 _ki_ point each round. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-abundant-step|abundant step]].

**Free Step (Su)**: At 13th level, the wanderer gains continuous _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_ as a continuous spell-like ability. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-diamond-soul|diamond soul]].
