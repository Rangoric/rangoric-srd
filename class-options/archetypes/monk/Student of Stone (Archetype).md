# Student of Stone

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|oreads]]._

By following the path of the stone, students of stone give up much of monks' mobility in favor of sheer resilience. A student of stone has the following class features.

**Hard as Stone (Ex)**: At 2nd level, whenever an opponent rolls to confirm a critical hit against a student of stone, treat the student of stone's AC as +4 higher than normal. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-evasion|evasion]].

**Strength of Stone (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a student of stone learns to draw strength from the earth. So long as both he and his opponent are touching the ground, the student of stone gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver rolls, and to his CMD when resisting a bull rush or trip attempt. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Bonus Feat**: At 6th level, a student of stone adds [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]] to his list of available [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feats]]. If the student of stone selects Elemental Fist as a bonus feat, he may only deal acid damage when using the feat. At 10th level, the student of stone adds [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shaitan Style|Shaitan Style]] to his list of bonus feats, at 14th level, he adds [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shaitan Skin|Shaitan Skin]], and at 18th level, he adds [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shaitan Earthblast|Shaitan Earthblast]]. This otherwise functions as and alters the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feat]] ability.

**Bones of Stone (Su)**: At 7th level, as a swift action, a student of stone can spend 1 _ki_ point to gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]] 2/magic until the beginning of his next turn. At 10th level, he can spend 1 _ki_ point to gain DR 2/chaotic until his next turn. At 15th level, he can spend 1 _ki_ point to gain DR 5/chaotic until his next turn. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-high-jump|high jump]].

**Body of Stone (Ex)**: At 9th level, a student of stone gains the benefits of the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|light fortification]]_ armor property. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-improved-evasion|improved evasion]].

**Soul of Stone (Su)**: At 12th level, as a swift action, a student of stone can spend 1 _ki_ point to gain [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] 15 feet until his next turn. At 16th level, the range of this tremorsense increases to 30 feet. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-abundant-step|abundant step]].

**Stone Self**: At 20th level, a student of stone becomes an earth outsider. He gains the earth subtype, as well as DR 5/chaotic, burrow speed 20 feet, and tremorsense 20 feet. This ability doesn’t replace [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-perfect-self|perfect self]]: a student of stone gains both, is an outsider either way, and the damage reduction and other benefits of the two overlap.
