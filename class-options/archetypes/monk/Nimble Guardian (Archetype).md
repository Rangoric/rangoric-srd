# Nimble Guardian

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|catfolk]]._

Some catfolk monks dedicate their graceful prowess to the defense of others, especially those dedicated to a similar ethos or who prove themselves as stalwart allies of the monk's cause. A nimble guardian has the following class features.

**Defensive Aid (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a number of times per day equal to his Wisdom bonus, a nimble guardian can interpose herself between one adjacent ally and an attack or damage dealt in an area of effect. If an adjacent ally is the target of the attack or is attacked against its Reflex by a damaging effect, as an immediate action the nimble guardian can grant that ally a +4 circumstance bonus to AC or to Reflex Defense against the effect. The nimble guardian must use this ability before the attack roll is made. The nimble guardian can only use this ability if he is wearing light or no armor. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-evasion|evasion]].

**Nimble Reflexes (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a nimble guardian gains a +2 bonus on Reflex Defense. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-still-mind|still mind]].

**Defensive Mastery (Ex)**: At 5th level, a nimble guardian gains 3 additional uses of her defensive aid ability per day. Furthermore, if an effect misses the Reflex of an ally that gained the benefit of a use of defensive aid, and the effect still deals damage on a miss, the nimble guardian can spend 1 _ki_ point to negate that damage. Doing so is not an action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-purity-of-body|purity of body]].

**Guardian Feline (Su)**: At 7th level, a nimble guardian can transform himself into a feline creature by spending 2 _ki_ points. The effect lasts for 1 hour or until the nimble guardian changes back. Changing form (to animal or back) is a standard action and does not provoke an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]]. The chosen form must be some form of feline (cheetah, lion, etc.). This ability is otherwise identical to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape II|beast shape II]]_. At 9th level, this ability functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]_. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-wholeness-of-body|wholeness of body]].

**Evasion (Ex)**: At 9th level, a nimble guardian gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-evasion|evasion]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-improved-evasion|improved evasion]].
