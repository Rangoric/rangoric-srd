# Martial Artist

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The martial artist pursues a mastery of pure martial arts without the monastic traditions. He is a master of form, but lacks the ability to harness his _ki_.

**Alignment**: A martial artist may be of any alignment.

**Pain Points (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a martial artist's advanced knowledge of anatomy grants a +1 bonus on critical hit confirmation rolls and increases the DC of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-stunning-fist|stunning fist]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-quivering-palm|quivering palm]] by 1. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-still-mind|still mind]].

**Martial Arts Master (Ex)**: At 4th level, a martial artist may use his monk level to qualify for feats with a fighter level prerequisite when those feats are applied to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|unarmed strikes]] or weapons with the monk special quality. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-slow-fall|slow fall]].

**Exploit Weakness (Ex)**: At 4th level, as a swift action, a martial artist can observe a creature or object to find its weak point by making a Wisdom check and adding his monk level against a DC of 10 + the object's hardness or the target's CR. If the check succeeds, the martial artist gains a +2 bonus on attack rolls until the end of his turn, and any attacks he makes until the end of his turn ignore the creature or object's [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]] or hardness.

A martial artist may instead use this ability as a swift action to analyze the movements and expressions of one creature within 30 feet, granting a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks and to Reflex Defense and a dodge bonus to AC against that opponent equal to 1/2 his monk level until the start of his next turn.

This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki* pool]].

**Extreme Endurance (Ex)**: At 5th level, a martial artist gains immunity to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigue]]. At 10th level, he also gains immunity to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhaustion]]. At 15th level, he gains immunity to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]]. At 20th level, he gains immunity to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Death Attacks|death effects]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|energy drain]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-purity-of-body|purity of body]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-diamond-body|diamond body]], and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-perfect-self|perfect self]].

**Physical Resistance (Ex)**: At 7th level, if a martial artist suffers any effect that causes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|ability damage]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|ability drain]], or temporary ability score penalties, the effect is reduced by 1 point. This reduction increases by 1 for every three levels beyond 7th (to a maximum reduction of 5 at 19th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-wholeness-of-body|wholeness of body]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-timeless-body|timeless body]], and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-tongue-of-the-sun-and-moon|tongue of the sun and moon]].

**Bonus Feat**: At 12th level, a martial artist gains an additional bonus feat, selected from those available in the monk [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feat]] class feature. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-abundant-step|abundant step]].

**Defensive Roll (Ex)**: At 13th level, a martial artist may use the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Defensive Roll (Rogue Talent)|defensive roll]] advanced [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|rogue talent]] once per day, plus once per three levels beyond 13th (to a maximum of 3 times a day at 19th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-diamond-soul|diamond soul]].

**Quivering Palm**: A martial artist may use this ability ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-quivering-palm|quivering palm]]) one additional time per day per level above 15th, but may not have more than one in effect at a time.

**Greater Defensive Roll (Ex)**: At 19th level, a martial artist suffers no damage on a successful defensive roll, and only half damage if the Reflex check fails. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-empty-body|empty body]].
