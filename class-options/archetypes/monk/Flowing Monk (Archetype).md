# Flowing Monk

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The flowing monk is the wind and the river. He knows how the world flows, and forces his enemies to flow with it. Even the most powerful stone breaks under the graceful and persistent pressure of wind and water.

**Bonus Feat**: A flowing monk replaces the normal monk [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feats]] with the following: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Agile Maneuvers|Agile Maneuvers]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deflect Arrows|Deflect Arrows]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Reposition|Improved Reposition]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Moves|Nimble Moves]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Finesse|Weapon Finesse]]. At 6th level, the following feats are also available: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Acrobatic Steps|Acrobatic Steps]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bodyguard|Bodyguard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Disarm|Improved Disarm]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ki Throw|*Ki* Throw]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Second Chance (Feat)|Second Chance]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sidestep|Sidestep]]. At 10th level, the following feats are added: [[rangoric-srd/feats/In Harm's Way|In Harm's Way]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Repositioning Strike|Repositioning Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snatch Arrows|Snatch Arrows]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spring Attack|Spring Attack]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tripping Strike|Tripping Strike]].

**Redirection (Ex)**: At 1st level, as an immediate action, a flowing monk can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver against a creature that the flowing monk threatens and that attacks him. If the combat maneuver is successful, the attacker is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 round (Reflex DC = 10 + 1/2 the monk's level + monk's Wisdom modifier to halve the duration), plus 1 additional round at 4th level and for every four levels afterward (to a maximum of 6 rounds at 20th level). The monk gains a +2 bonus on the reposition or trip combat maneuver check and the attack bonus for redirection increases by 2 if the attacker is using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]] or is charging when attacking him. The benefit increases to a +4 bonus and an increase of the attack bonus by 4 if both apply.

At 4th level, a flowing monk can use redirection against an opponent that the flowing monk threatens and that attacks an ally with a melee attack. At 8th level, a flowing monk can make both a reposition and a trip maneuver as part of a single immediate action with this ability. At 12th level, a flowing monk can use redirection against any opponent that attacks him in melee, even if the flowing monk is not threatening the opponent who attacks him. A flowing monk can use this ability once per day per monk level, but no more than once per round. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-stunning-fist|stunning fist]].

**Unbalancing Counter (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a flowing monk's [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] render a struck creature [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] until the end of the flowing monk's next turn (vs Reflex, attack bonus 1/2 the monk's level + Wisdom modifier). This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feat]] gained at 2nd level.

**Flowing Dodge (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a flowing monk gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC for each enemy adjacent to him, up to a maximum bonus equal to his Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Elusive Target (Ex)**: At 5th level, as an immediate action, a flowing monk may spend 2 points from his _ki_ pool to attempt a Reflex check (1d20 + Reflex save bonus) opposed by an attacker's attack roll to halve damage from that attack. At 11th level and above, the flowing monk suffers no damage on a successful check, or half damage on a failed check. If the attacker is [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] the monk, the flanking opponent who is not attacking becomes the target of the attack. Use the same attack roll, and if the attack hits the new target, that creatures takes half damage (or full damage if the attack is completely avoided). Any associated effects from the attack (such as bleed, poison, or spell effects) apply fully even if the attack deals only half damage. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-purity-of-body|purity of body]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-diamond-body|diamond body]].

**Volley Spell (Su)**: At 15th level, when a targeted spell or spell-like ability fails to overcome a flowing monk's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-diamond-soul|spell resistance]], he may reflect the effect onto its caster as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]]_ by spending a number of points from his _ki_ pool equal to 1/2 the spell's level (minimum 1). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-quivering-palm|quivering palm]].
