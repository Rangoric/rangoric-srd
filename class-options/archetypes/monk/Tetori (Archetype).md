# Tetori

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The style of the tetori is that of the majestic wrestler—a warrior with a dizzying array of grabs, holds, and locks with which to bewilder and incapacitate his foes.

**Bonus Feat**: A tetori gains the following bonus feats: 1st level—[[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], 2nd level—[[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Pin|Stunning Pin]], 6th level—[[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Grapple|Greater Grapple]], 10th level—[[rangoric-srd/feats/Pinning Knockout|Pinning Knockout]], 14th level—[[rangoric-srd/feats/Chokehold|Chokehold]], 18th level—[[rangoric-srd/feats/Neckbreaker|Neckbreaker]]. These feats replace a monk's normal [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feats]].

**Graceful Grappler (Ex)**: A tetori uses his monk level in place of his base attack bonus to determine CMB and CMD for [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grappling]]. At 4th level, he suffers no penalties on attack rolls, can make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] while grappling, and retains his Dexterity bonus to AC when pinning an opponent or when grappled. At 8th level, a tetori gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]] special attack when using [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|unarmed strikes]], and can use this ability against creatures his own size or smaller by spending 1 point from his _ki_ pool, or against larger creatures by spending 2 points from his _ki_ pool. At 15th level, a tetori gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Constrict|constrict]] special attack, inflicting his unarmed strike damage on any successful grapple check. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-flurry-of-blows|flurry of blows]].

**Counter-Grapple (Ex)**: At 4th level, a tetori wrestler may make an attack of opportunity against a creature attempting to grapple him. This ability does not allow the tetori to make an attack of opportunity against a creature with the Greater Grapple feat. At 6th level, he may use counter-grapple even if his attacker has [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] or total concealment, at 8th level even if he is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]], and at 10th level even if his attacker has exceptional reach. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-slow-fall|slow fall]].

**Break Free (Ex)**: At 5th level, a tetori adds his monk level on combat maneuver or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] checks made to escape a grapple. If an effect hits a tetori and causes him to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]], slowed, or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]], he may spend 1 point from his _ki_ pool as an immediate action to make it attack again, and a miss ends it. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-high-jump|high jump]].

**Inescapable Grasp (Su)**: At 9th level, a tetori can spend 1 point from his _ki_ pool to suppress his opponents' _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_ and magical bonuses to Escape Artist or on checks to escape a grapple. At 13th level, this ability also duplicates the effect of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]]_. At 17th level, the tetori's unarmed strike gains the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]]_ special ability, and an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] creature that he strikes gains the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]] condition (vs Reflex, attack bonus 1/2 the wrestler's level + his Wisdom modifier). Inescapable grasp is a swift action and lasts until the beginning of the wrestler's next turn. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-abundant-step|abundant step]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-improved-evasion|improved evasion]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-timeless-body|timeless body]], and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-tongue-of-the-sun-and-moon|tongue of the sun and moon]].

**Form Lock (Su)**: At 13th level, a tetori can negate a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Polymorph|polymorph]] effect by touch with a Wisdom check, adding a bonus equal to his monk level, against a DC of 11 + the caster level of the effect (or HD of the creature, for supernatural polymorph effects). This is a standard action requiring 2 points from the tetori's _ki_ pool, or an immediate action if a creature the tetori is grappling attempts to use a polymorph effect. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-diamond-soul|diamond soul]].

**Iron Body (Su)**: At 19th level, as a move action, a tetori can make his tissues ultra-dense for 1 minute as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Iron Body|iron body]]_ spell by spending 3 points from his _ki_ pool. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-empty-body|empty body]].
