# Kata Master

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-archetypes|monk]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The kata master takes the visual aspect of his martial art to its logical extreme, harnessing his flowing movements and skilled maneuvers as a psychological weapon against his enemies. A kata master forsakes the mental discipline of his more contemplative brethren in favor of flamboyant exhibitions. He often performs in staged fights and tournaments, utilizing stylized forms to amaze the audience and shock and dismay his opponents.

**Panache**: At 1st level, a kata master gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler|swashbuckler’s]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] class feature. At the start of each day, a kata master gains a number of panache points equal to his Charisma bonus (minimum 1). His panache goes up or down throughout the day, but usually cannot exceed his Charisma bonus (minimum 1). A kata master gains the swashbuckler’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Derring-Do (Deed)|derring-do]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Dodging Panache (Deed)|dodging panache]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|deeds]]. A kata master can use an unarmed strike or a monk special weapon in place of a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon for the purpose of swashbuckler class features and deeds. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-stunning-fist|stunning fist]].

**Menacing Swordplay (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a kata master gains the swashbuckler’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Menacing Swordplay (Deed)|menacing swordplay]] deed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-still-mind|still mind]].

**Ki Pool (Su)**: At 4th level, a kata master can treat ki points as panache points for any swashbuckler deed gained through this archetype. This ability modifies [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]].

**Targeted Strike (Ex)**: At 7th level, a kata master gains the swashbuckler’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Targeted Strike (Deed)|targeted strike]] deed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-wholeness-of-body|wholeness of body]].

**Dizzying Defense (Ex)**: At 15th level, a kata master gains the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Dizzying Defense (Deed)|dizzying defense]] swashbuckler deed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-quivering-palm|quivering palm]].
