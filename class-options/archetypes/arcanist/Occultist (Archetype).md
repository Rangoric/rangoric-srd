# Occultist

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-archetypes|arcanist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Not all arcanists peer inward to discern the deepest secrets of magic. Some look outward, connecting with extraplanar creatures and bartering for secrets, power, and favor.

**Planar Spells**: An occultist adds all _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]_ spells to her spell list (using her arcanist level as the cleric level), and treats _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]_ as a 5th-level arcanist spell.

**Conjurer's Focus (Sp)**: An occultist can spend 1 point from her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-arcane-reservoir|arcane reservoir]] to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster I]]_. She can cast this spell as a standard action and the summoned creatures remain for 1 minute per level (instead of 1 round per level). At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the power of this ability increases by one spell level, allowing her to summon more powerful creatures (to a maximum of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster IX|summon monster IX]]_ at 17th level), at the cost of an additional point from her arcane reservoir per spell level. An occultist cannot have more than one _summon monster_ spell active in this way at one time. If this ability is used again, any existing _summon monster_ immediately ends. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-exploits|arcanist exploit]] gained at 1st level.

**Planar Contact (Sp)**: At 7th level, an occultist can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]_ once per day and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contact Other Plane|contact other plane]]_ once per week, using her arcanist level as her caster level. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-exploits|arcanist exploit]] gained at 7th level.

**Perfect Summoner**: At 20th level, an occultist can use her conjurer's focus without spending points from her arcane reservoir, and the creatures summoned last until dismissed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-magical-supremacy|magical supremacy]].
