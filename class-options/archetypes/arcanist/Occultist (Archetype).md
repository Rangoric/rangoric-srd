# Occultist

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-archetypes|arcanist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Not all arcanists peer inward to discern the deepest secrets of magic. Some look outward, connecting with extraplanar creatures and bartering for secrets, power, and favor.

**Planar Spells**: An occultist adds all *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]* spells to her spell list (using her arcanist level as the cleric level), and treats *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]* as a 5th-level arcanist spell.

**Conjurer's Focus (Sp)**: An occultist can spend 1 point from her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-arcane-reservoir|arcane reservoir]] to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster I]]*. She can cast this spell as a standard action and the summoned creatures remain for 1 minute per level (instead of 1 round per level). At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the power of this ability increases by one spell level, allowing her to summon more powerful creatures (to a maximum of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster IX|summon monster IX]]* at 17th level), at the cost of an additional point from her arcane reservoir per spell level. An occultist cannot have more than one *summon monster* spell active in this way at one time. If this ability is used again, any existing *summon monster* immediately ends. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-exploits|arcanist exploit]] gained at 1st level.

**Planar Contact (Sp)**: At 7th level, an occultist can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]* once per day and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contact Other Plane|contact other plane]]* once per week, using her arcanist level as her caster level. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-exploits|arcanist exploit]] gained at 7th level.

**Perfect Summoner**: At 20th level, an occultist can use her conjurer's focus without spending points from her arcane reservoir, and the creatures summoned last until dismissed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-magical-supremacy|magical supremacy]].
