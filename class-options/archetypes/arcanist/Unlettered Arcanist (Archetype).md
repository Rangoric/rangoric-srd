# Unlettered Arcanist

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-archetypes|arcanist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some arcanists store their spells as whispered secrets within familiars instead of on paper.

**Familiar**: An unlettered arcanist does not keep a spellbook. Instead, she gains a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-familiar|familiar]] in which she stores her spells as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|witch]] does, though she does not gain a witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|patron]]. Treat her arcanist level as her witch level for determining the abilities and benefits granted by the familiar. Anything that would allow an unlettered arcanist to add spells to her spellbook allows her to add spells to her familiar instead. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-spellbooks|spellbooks]].

**Witch Spells**: An unlettered arcanist follows a different arcane tradition. She uses the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Witch Spell List|witch spell list]] instead of the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|wizard/sorcerer spell list]]. This ability alters the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-spells|spells]] class feature.
