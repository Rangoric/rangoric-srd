# Verminous Hunter

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-archetypes|hunter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A verminous hunter calls on the ceaseless, single-minded dedication of vermin to hunt and overwhelm her prey. Where other hunters invoke the cunning, animalistic powers of the alpha predators, she calls on the powers of the lowest life forms, reaching out to the spider instead of the monkey, the mantis instead of the snake, or the moth instead of the owl.

**Vermin Companion (Ex)**: At 1st level, a verminous hunter must choose a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^vermin|vermin]] companion instead of an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-companion|animal companion]]. This ability alters animal companion. The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-tactics|hunter tactics]] class ability allows a verminous hunter to grant her teamwork feats to a mindless vermin companion.

**Vermin Empathy (Ex)**: A verminous hunter gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] ability, but can use it only to influence vermin (not animals or magical beasts). This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-wild-empathy|wild empathy]].

**Vermin Focus (Su)**: A verminous hunter can take on the aspect of a vermin. This functions as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]], but allows only the following choices, each with its own note beside the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/animal-foci/Animal Foci|animal foci]].

**Swarm Stride (Ex)**: At 5th level, a verminous hunter learns to move through vermin without danger. He can safely pass through [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^swarm|swarms]] of vermin and does not take [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^swarm-attack|swarm damage]] while within a vermin swarm's space. In addition, he is immune to a swarm's [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Distraction|distraction]] ability. If the hunter or his animal companion attacks a swarm, they lose this protection against only that swarm. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].
