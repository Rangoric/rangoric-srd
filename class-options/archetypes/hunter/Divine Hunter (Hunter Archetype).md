# Divine Hunter

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-archetypes|hunter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While most hunters heed the call of nature and fight to protect its bounty, some are inspired to serve a higher power. These divine hunters use faith to aid them in their struggles, and their faith infuses their [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-companion|animal companions]], making these companions champions of their deities.

**Alignment**: A divine hunter's alignment must be within one step of her deity's, along either the law/chaos axis or the good/evil axis. A divine hunter can otherwise be of any alignment.

**Class Skills**: A divine hunter gains [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] as a class skill, but she does not gain [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] as a class skill.

**Domain**: At 3rd level, a divine hunter learns to call upon the power of her deity. The divine hunter must select one [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains|domain]] from those available to her deity. She gains the granted powers of this domain, using her hunter level – 2 as her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric]] level for determining when the powers are gained and what effects they have. Once she chooses this domain, it cannot be changed.

If the divine hunter selects the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|animal domain]], she does not gain a second animal companion upon reaching an effective cleric level of 4th. When the divine hunter would gain that ability, her animal companion instead gains two ability score increases (gaining +1 to two different ability scores or +2 to one ability score). If her animal companion dies or is released, when she gains a new one, it benefits from this ability score increase.

In addition, the divine hunter adds the 1st-level domain spell from her domain to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-spells|spells known]]. She adds the 2nd-level domain spell at 6th level, the 3rd-level domain spell at 9th level, the 4th-level domain spell at 12th level, the 5th-level domain spell at 15th level, and the 6th-level domain spell at 18th level.

This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-teamwork-feat|teamwork feats]].

**Otherworldly Companion (Su)**: At 3rd level, a hunter's companion takes on otherworldly features. If the divine hunter is good (or worships a good deity), the animal companion gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/celestial-creature/Celestial Creature|celestial template]]. If the hunter is evil (or worships an evil deity), the animal companion gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creature/Fiendish Creature|fiendish template]]. If the hunter is neutral and worships a neutral deity, she must choose either the celestial or fiendish template; once this choice is made, it cannot be changed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-tactics|hunter tactics]].
