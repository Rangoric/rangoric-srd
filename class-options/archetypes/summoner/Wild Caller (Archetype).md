# Wild Caller

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-archetypes|summoner]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elves]]._

Often a half-elf's ties to nature and elven heritage are so strong that they can dramatically affect the nature of his summonings. The wild caller calls eidolons that take more natural and savage forms and summons from nature rather than the Outer Planes. A wild caller has the following class features.

**Spells**: A wild caller does not have access to _summon monster_ spells. Instead, he swaps out those spells on his spell list with _summon nature's ally_ spells of the same level. This ability alters the normal [[rangoric-srd/spells/Summoner Spell List|spell list]] of the summoner.

**Eidolon**: The eidolon of a wild caller tends to take more natural and more savage forms than other summoners' eidolons. A wild caller gains 1/4 his class level as bonus evolution points in his eidolon's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-evolutions|evolution pool]], but his eidolon cannot take the following evolutions:

_1-point evolutions_: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Basic Magic (Evolution)|basic magic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Magic Attacks (Evolution)|magic attacks]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Skilled (Evolution)|skilled]] (while the eidolon can select this evolution, it can only do so with the following skills: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]]), [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Unnatural Aura (Evolution)|unnatural aura]]; _2-point evolutions_: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Energy Attacks (Evolution)|energy attacks]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Immunity (Evolution)|immunity]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Weapon Training (Evolution)|weapon training]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Channel Resistance (Evolution)|channel resistance]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Head (Evolution)|head]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Minor Magic (Evolution)|minor magic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Undead Appearance (Evolution)|undead appearance]]; _3-point evolutions_: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Damage Reduction (Evolution)|damage reduction]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Frightful Presence (Evolution)|frightful presence]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Major Magic (Evolution)|major magic]]; _4-point evolutions_: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Breath Weapon (Evolution)|breath weapon]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Spell Resistance (Evolution)|spell resistance]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Dimension Door (Evolution)|dimension door]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Incorporeal Form (Evolution)|incorporeal form]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Lifesense (Evolution)|lifesense]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/No Breath (Evolution)|no breath]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Ultimate Magic (Evolution)|ultimate magic]].

This ability alters the normal summoner's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]] class feature, but is otherwise identical to that class feature.

**Summon Nature's Ally I (Sp)**: Starting at 1st level, a wild caller can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]_ a number of times per day equal to 3 + his Charisma modifier. At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the power of this ability increases by one spell level, allowing him to summon more powerful creatures (to a maximum of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IX|summon nature's ally IX]]_ at 17th level). Furthermore, at 19th level, the wild caller cannot use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate|gate]]_ as a spell-like ability, but can use either _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Elder Worm|summon elder worm]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Froghemoth|summon froghemoth]]_ instead. This ability otherwise functions like the standard summoner's _[[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-summon-monster|summon monster I]]_ ability and replaces that ability.
