# Shadow Caller

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-archetypes|summoner]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|fetchlings]].*

While most summoners can call any manner of creature from across the planes to serve them in combat, supplementing the skills of their eidolon with a diverse range of creatures, others eschew this broad utility and instead concentrate upon calling forth entities from the Plane of Shadow. A shadow caller has the following class features.

**Class Skills**: The shadow caller adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex) to his list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#Class Skills|class skills]] and removes [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] (Cha).

**Shadow Summoning (Sp)**: When a shadow caller uses his *[[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-summon-monster|summon monster]]* ability or casts the *summon monster* spell, he typically summons creatures from the Plane of Shadow or creatures closely associated with shadow. When a creature on a *summon monster* spell list is summoned with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/celestial-creature/Celestial Creature|celestial]] or the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creature/Fiendish Creature|fiendish]] template, the creature summoned by the shadow caller has the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shadow-Summoned Creature|shadow-summoned creature template]] instead. Furthermore, the *summon monster* lists are modified in the following ways (these changes also apply to using a higher-level summon spell to summon multiple creatures from a lower-level list).

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|Summon Monster I]]*: No changes.
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster II|Summon Monster II]]*: A shadow caller cannot summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Lemure|lemures]], but instead can summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Zoog|zoogs]].
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|Summon Monster III]]*: A shadow caller cannot summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Elemental|Small elementals]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Dretch|dretches]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hell-hound/Hell Hound|hell hounds]], but can summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Augur Kyton|augur kytons]].
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster IV|Summon Monster IV]]*: A shadow caller cannot summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archon/Lantern Archon|lantern archons]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mephit/Mephit|mephits]], but can summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Allip|allips]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Gloomwing|gloomwings]], and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Shadow|shadows]].
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|Summon Monster V]]*: A shadow caller cannot summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Bearded Devil (Barbazu)|bearded devils]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/archon/Hound Archon|hound archons]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Elemental|Medium elementals]], but can summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shae|shae]] (and the list's [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Shadow Mastiff|shadow mastiffs]]).
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|Summon Monster VI]]*: A shadow caller cannot summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/azata/Bralani|bralani]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Elemental|Large elementals]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Xill|xills]], but can summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Cloaker|cloakers]].
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VII|Summon Monster VII]]*: A shadow caller cannot summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/demon/Babau|babau]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Bone Devil (Osyluth)|bone devils]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Elemental|Huge elementals]], but can summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Greater Shadow|greater shadows]].
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VIII|Summon Monster VIII]]*: A shadow caller can only summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Derghodaemon|derghodaemons]] and young [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Umbral Dragon|umbral dragons]].
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster IX|Summon Monster IX]]*: A shadow caller can only summon [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/kytons/Interlocutor Kyton|interlocutor kytons]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/nightshade/Nightwing|nightwings]].

This ability otherwise functions as and replaces the summoner's normal *[[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-summon-monster|summon monster]]* spell-like abilities.

**Shadow Eidolon**: A shadow caller's eidolon is at once a thing of shadow called from the deep of the Plane of Shadow and his own shadow; the two are inseparable. When his eidolon manifests, his shadow lengthens and finally detaches from him as a creature unto itself. For as long as the shadow caller's eidolon is manifested, he and the eidolon do not have distinct shadows, regardless of the presence or absence of light. This lack of a shadow replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|magical symbol]] that identifies the summoner and his eidolon.

This ability alters the summoner's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]] ability.
