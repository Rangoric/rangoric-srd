# Wild Rager

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Rages are barely controlled, but there are those who wholly give in to their more savage side, letting their rages take them to a confusing and uncontrolled place of terrible savagery. These barbarians become beasts, consumed with absolute bloodlust to the point where they cannot tell friend from foe.

**Uncontrolled Rage (Ex)**: A wild rager's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] functions as normal, except that when she reduces a creature to 0 or fewer hit points, the rage attacks her Will at 1/2 the barbarian's level + the barbarian's Constitution modifier, and on a hit she becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]]. For the remainder of her current turn, she attacks the nearest creature other than herself. On the following round, refer to the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_ spell to determine her actions. At the end of this round, and each round thereafter, it attacks again, and a miss ends the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_ effect. The rounds during which she is confused do not count against the rounds she has spent raging that day, but she cannot end her rage voluntarily, nor can she use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] while confused.

**Wild Fighting (Ex)**: At 2nd level, even when not raging, wild ragers often fight with reckless, savage abandon. A wild rager using the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Full Attack|full-attack action]] can make one extra attack per round at her highest base attack bonus. Until the beginning of her next turn, however, she takes a –2 penalty on attack rolls and –4 penalty to AC. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]].

**Rage Conversion (Ex)**: At 5th level, when a mind-affecting effect hits a wild rager, it attacks her again at the beginning of her next turn. If it misses, that effect ends and she instead rages and becomes confused as noted above. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].
