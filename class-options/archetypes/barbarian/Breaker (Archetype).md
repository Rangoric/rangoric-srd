# Breaker

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While most barbarians are skilled at breaking things, some find the need to destroy their surroundings an almost uncontrollable urge when in the middle of a rage. These barbarians are a danger not only to their foes, but also to the very environment around them. A breaker barbarian has the following class features.

**Destructive (Ex)**: Whenever the breaker barbarian makes a melee attack that targets an unattended object or makes a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] combat maneuver, she adds half her barbarian level (minimum +1) on the damage roll. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Battle Scavenger (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the breaker barbarian suffers no penalty on attack rolls when using an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^improvised-weapons|improvised weapon]] or a weapon with the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^broken|broken]] condition. In addition, she gains a +1 bonus on damage rolls with improvised or broken weapons for every three levels beyond 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]].

**Rage Powers**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the breaker archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Ground Breaker (Rage Power)|ground breaker]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Smasher (Rage Power)|smasher]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Strength Surge (Rage Power)|strength surge]].
