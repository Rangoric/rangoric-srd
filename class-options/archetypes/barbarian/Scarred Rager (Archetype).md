# Scarred Rager

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some barbarians wear marks of prowess and savagery upon their bodies. The scarred rager believes each wound tells the tales of her prowess and bravery. She augments these ragged trophies with brands and tattoos in order to win the notice and favor of her gods, ancestors, or totem spirits. The scarred rager's gnarled and exotic appearance terrifies civilized onlookers, but is awe-inspiring to her savage kin.

**Terrifying Visage (Ex)**: A scarred rager adds 1/2 her barbarian level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks against humanoids who are not members of barbarian tribes. When dealing with barbarians, the scarred rager may choose to add this bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks instead. The DC of any [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] effect created by the scarred rager also increases by 1. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Tolerance (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a scarred rager who fails a save against an effect that causes her to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]] can make a second save to negate the effect on the start of her next turn. Only one additional save is allowed. If the effect does not allow a saving throw, its duration is halved instead (minimum of 1 round). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]].

**Scarification (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a scarred rager can ignore 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^bleed|bleed]] damage per round. This amount increases by 1 every three levels beyond 3rd. At 15th level, a scarred rager can ignore 1 bleed effect each round. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]].

**Improved Tolerance (Ex)**: At 5th level, a scarred rager's tolerance ability also applies to effects that would cause her to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]]. This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].

**Rage Powers**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the scarred rager archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Auspicious Mark (Rage Power)|auspicious mark]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Body Bludgeon (Rage Power)|body bludgeon]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Taunting Stance (Rage Power)|come and get me]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Increased Damage Reduction (Rage Power)|increased damage reduction]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Inspire Ferocity (Rage Power)|inspire ferocity]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Internal Fortitude (Rage Power)|internal fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Intimidating Glare (Rage Power)|intimidating glare]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Reckless Stance (Rage Power)|reckless abandon]], regenerative vigor, renewed life, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Renewed Vigor (Rage Power)|renewed vigor]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Renewed Vitality (Rage Power)|renewed vitality]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Roused Anger (Rage Power)|roused anger]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Terrifying Howl (Rage Power)|terrifying howl]].
