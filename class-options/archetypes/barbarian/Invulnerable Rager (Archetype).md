# Invulnerable Rager

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some barbarians learn to take whatever comes their way, shrugging off mortal wounds with ease. These barbarians invite their enemies to attack them, and use pain to fuel their rage. An invulnerable rager has the following class features.

**Invulnerability (Ex)**: At 2nd level, the invulnerable rager gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]]/— equal to half her barbarian level. This damage reduction is doubled against nonlethal damage. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]], and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-damage-reduction|damage reduction]].

**Extreme Endurance (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the invulnerable rager is inured to either hot or cold climate effects (choose one) as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_. In addition, the barbarian gains 1 point of fire or cold [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] for every three levels beyond 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]].

**Rage Powers**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the invulnerable rager archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Taunting Stance (Rage Power)|come and get me]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Guarded Life (Rage Power)|guarded life]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Increased Damage Reduction (Rage Power)|increased damage reduction]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Inspire Ferocity (Rage Power)|inspire ferocity]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Reckless Stance (Rage Power)|reckless abandon]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Renewed Vigor (Rage Power)|renewed vigor]].
