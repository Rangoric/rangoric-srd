# Sea Reaver

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Not all barbarians hunt forests, plains, and mountains. Some are raiding terrors on the sea and coasts, pillaging those who hoard treasure and pursuing monsters of the deep. Some sea reavers are no more than hunters of the open sea, while others are raiders striking fear into coastal settlements within reach of the sea reavers' longships.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A sea reaver is not proficient with medium armor ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-weapon-and-armor-proficiency|weapon and armor proficiency]]).

**Marine Terror (Ex)**: A sea reaver can hold her breath for a number of rounds equal to four times her Constitution score. In addition, a sea reaver can move normally through squares of standing water or bog that is 1 foot deep. It does not cost her extra movement to traverse these terrains. Lastly, a sea reaver ignores the normal [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|cover]] bonus to AC when attacking creatures that are partially immersed in water. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Eyes of the Storm (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a sea reaver ignores any [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] provided by fog, rain, sleet, mist, wind, or other weather effects that is less than total concealment, and any penalties weather applies on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks are halved. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]].

**Savage Sailor (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a sea reaver gains a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession (sailor)]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks made in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Aquatic Terrain|aquatic terrain]], including aboard a ship or along shorelines. These bonuses improve by +1 every three levels after 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]].

**Sure-Footed (Ex)**: At 5th level, a sea reaver takes no penalties when moving across slick surfaces, whether natural or magical (e.g., _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Grease|grease]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ice Storm|ice storm]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleet Storm|sleet storm]]_). She is not at risk of falling, is not denied her Dexterity bonus when moving across such areas, and does not treat them as difficult terrain. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].

**Rage Powers**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the sea reaver archetype: bestial leaper, bestial swimmer, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Taunting Stance (Rage Power)|come and get me]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Hurling Charge (Rage Power)|hurling charge]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Raging Leaper (Rage Power)|raging leaper]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Raging Swimmer (Rage Power)|raging swimmer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Rolling Dodge (Rage Power)|rolling dodge]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Smasher (Rage Power)|smasher]].
