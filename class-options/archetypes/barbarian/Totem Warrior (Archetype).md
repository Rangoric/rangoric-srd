# Totem Warrior

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A barbarian often has a special totem that is the patron of her tribe. While individual totems vary, those in the tribe that call upon a totem receive similar abilities. Totem warrior barbarians can select from the following rage powers.

**Rage Powers**: The totem warrior is based entirely upon his totem [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Rage Powers|rage powers]]. In addition to the totem powers themselves, the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the totem warrior archetype (depending on the totem chosen): [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Animal Fury (Rage Power)|animal fury]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Low-Light Vision (Rage Power)|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Night Vision (Rage Power)|night vision]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Raging Climber (Rage Power)|raging climber]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Raging Leaper (Rage Power)|raging leaper]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Raging Swimmer (Rage Power)|raging swimmer]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Swift Foot (Rage Power)|swift foot]].
