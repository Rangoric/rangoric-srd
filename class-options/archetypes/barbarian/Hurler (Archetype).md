# Hurler

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A raging barbarian is frightening enough in melee, but some become skilled at throwing objects at their foes before closing in for the kill. A hurler has the following class feature.

**Skilled Thrower (Ex)**: The hurler is skilled at throwing objects in combat. Increase the range increment of any thrown weapon or object by 10 feet. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Rage Powers**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the hurler archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Hurling (Rage Power)|hurling]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Hurling, Greater (Rage Power)|greater hurling]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Hurling Charge (Rage Power)|hurling charge]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Hurling, Lesser (Rage Power)|lesser hurling]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Strength Surge (Rage Power)|strength surge]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Surprise Accuracy (Rage Power)|surprise accuracy]].
