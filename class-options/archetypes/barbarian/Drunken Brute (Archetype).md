# Drunken Brute

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Barbarians are known for their ability to consume potent drink, but drunken brutes turn drinking into a combat tactic, using the potent liquor to fuel their rage and grant them additional powers. A drunken brute has the following class feature.

**Raging Drunk (Ex)**: While [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|raging]], the drunken brute can drink a potion, or a tankard of ale or similar quantity of alcohol, as a move action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity. A potion has its normal effect, while an alcoholic drink allows the barbarian to maintain her rage that round without expending a round of rage for the day (instead of the alcohol's normal effects). For each alcoholic drink consumed while raging, the barbarian is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round when her rage expires, in addition to the normal fatigue that follows a rage. [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-tireless-rage|Tireless rage]] does not negate this nauseated condition but the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Internal Fortitude (Rage Power)|internal fortitude]] rage power does. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Rage Powers**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the drunken brute archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Boasting Taunt (Rage Power)|boasting taunt]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Good for what Ails you (Rage Power)|good for what ails you]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Internal Fortitude (Rage Power)|internal fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Liquid Courage (Rage Power)|liquid courage]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Moment of Clarity (Rage Power)|moment of clarity]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Roaring Drunk (Rage Power)|roaring drunk]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Staggering Drunk (Rage Power)|staggering drunk]].
