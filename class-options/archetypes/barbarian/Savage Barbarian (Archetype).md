# Savage Barbarian

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some barbarians are truly savage, having little training in modern arms. These savage barbarians learn to avoid blows and toughen up their skin. A savage barbarian has the following class features.

**Naked Courage (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the savage barbarian gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC and a +1 morale bonus on defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] when wearing no armor (shields are allowed). This bonus increases by +1 for every six levels after 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]].

**Natural Toughness (Ex)**: At 7th level, the savage barbarian gains a +1 natural armor bonus to AC when wearing no armor (shields are allowed). This bonus increases by +1 for every three levels beyond 7th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-damage-reduction|damage reduction]].

**Rage Powers**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the savage barbarian archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Flesh Wound (Rage Power)|flesh wound]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Guarded Stance (Rage Power)|guarded stance]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Increased Damage Reduction (Rage Power)|increased damage reduction]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Intimidating Glare (Rage Power)|intimidating glare]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Rolling Dodge (Rage Power)|rolling dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Superstition (Rage Power)|superstition]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Terrifying Howl (Rage Power)|terrifying howl]].
