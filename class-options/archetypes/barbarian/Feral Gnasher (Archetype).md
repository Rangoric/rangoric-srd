# Feral Gnasher

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|goblins]]._

Feral gnashers grow up in the wild, either raised by animals or scraping by on their own, and soon learn to fend for themselves. These barbarians often utilize pieced-together armor and fight with their sharp teeth and whatever improvised weapons are within reach. A feral gnasher has the following class features.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A feral gnasher loses all martial weapon proficiencies except for [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Greatclub|greatclub]] and loses proficiency with medium armor ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-weapon-and-armor-proficiency|weapon and armor proficiency]]).

**Savage Bite (Ex)**: At 1st level, a feral gnasher gains a savage bite attack. This is a primary natural attack that deals 1d4 points of damage. If the goblin already has the [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Hard Head, Big Teeth|hard head, big teeth]] racial trait, the damage increases to 1d6. At 10th level, the damage from a feral gnasher's bite increases to 1d6 (or 1d8 if the goblin has the hard head, big teeth racial trait) and deals ×3 damage on a critical hit. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Impromptu Armament (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a feral gnasher gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Throw Anything|Throw Anything]] as a bonus feat and can pick up an unattended object that can be wielded in one hand as a free action. Additionally, the feral gnasher can take [[rangoric-srd/feats/Catch Off-Guard|Catch Off-Guard]] in place of a rage power. This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] gained at second level.

**Lockjaw (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a feral gnasher gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]] ability with her bite attack. A feral gnasher can use this ability on a creature up to one size category larger than she is. This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]] +1.

**Improvised Weapon Mastery (Ex)**: At 5th level, a feral gnasher gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improvised Weapon Mastery|Improvised Weapon Mastery]] as a bonus feat. This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].

**Improved Lockjaw (Ex)**: At 6th level, as long as a feral gnasher is controlling the grapple with her lockjaw attack, she does not gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]] condition, but is unable to move or use her mouth for anything other than grappling. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]] +2.

**Greater Lockjaw (Ex)**: At 9th level and again at 15th level, the size of a creature a feral gnasher is able to use her lockjaw's grab ability on increases by one size increment. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]] +3 and trap sense +5.

**Wicked Improvisation (Ex)**: At 12th level, a feral gnasher becomes more capable with improvised weapons and natural attacks. The feral gnasher gains a +1 competence bonus on damage rolls when using natural attacks or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^improvised-weapons|improvised weapons]] while [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|raging]]. At 14th level and every two levels thereafter, the damage bonus increases by +1. This increase is not precision damage and is thus multiplied on a critical hit. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]] +4.

**Rage Powers**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage powers]] complement the feral gnasher archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Increased Damage Reduction (Rage Power)|increased damage reduction]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Scent (Rage Power)|scent]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Superstition (Rage Power)|superstition]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Beast Totem (Rage Power)|beast totem]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Beast Totem, Greater (Rage Power)|beast totem (greater)]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Beast Totem, Lesser (Rage Power)|beast totem (lesser)]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Eater of Magic (Rage Power)|eater of magic]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Ghost Rager (Rage Power)|ghost rager]].
