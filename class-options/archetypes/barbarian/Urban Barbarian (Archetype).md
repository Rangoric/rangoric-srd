# Urban Barbarian

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-archetypes|barbarian]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Every barbarian knows that city life can soften the spirit and the body, but some barbarians take on the trappings and ways of their adoptive homes and bend their savage powers to its challenges. While these urban barbarians' rough edges are smoothed into civility, they can use their primal nature and upbringing to move with the ebb and flow of civilization's natural rhythms.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: An urban barbarian is not proficient with medium armor ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-weapon-and-armor-proficiency|weapon and armor proficiency]]).

**Skills**: An urban barbarian does not gain [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] (Int), or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis) as class skills; instead, she gains [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] (Int), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (Wis) as class skills.

**Crowd Control (Ex)**: At 1st level, an urban barbarian gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls and a +1 dodge bonus to AC when adjacent to two or more enemies. In addition, her movement is not impeded by crowds, and she gains a bonus equal to 1/2 her barbarian level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks to influence crowds. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-fast-movement|fast movement]].

**Controlled Rage (Ex)**: When an urban barbarian rages, instead of making a normal [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] she applies a +4 morale bonus to her Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution. This bonus increases to +6 when she gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-greater-rage|greater rage]] and +8 when she gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-mighty-rage|mighty rage]]. She may apply the full bonus to one ability score or may split the bonus between several scores in increments of +2. When using a controlled rage, an urban barbarian gains no bonus on Will Defense, takes no penalties to AC, and can still use Intelligence-, Dexterity-, and Charisma-based skills. This ability otherwise follows the normal rules for rage. This ability alters rage.
