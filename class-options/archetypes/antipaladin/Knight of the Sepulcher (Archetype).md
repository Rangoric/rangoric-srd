# Knight of the Sepulcher

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-archetypes|antipaladin]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Not content with the antipaladin's mere corruption of the soul, the knight of the sepulcher sacrifices mortality along with morality.

The knight of the sepulcher archetype is available only to the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin|antipaladin]] alternate class.

**Touch of the Crypt (Ex)**: At 5th level, a knight of the sepulcher gains a +2 bonus on defenses against mind-affecting effects, death effects, and poison. He is harmed by positive energy effects and healed by negative energy effects as though he were undead, although negative energy effects that don't heal undead (such as enervation) affect him normally. The knight of the sepulcher has a 25% chance of ignoring critical hits and the bonus damage from sneak attacks as though he were wearing _armor of light [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]_. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-fiendish-boon|fiendish boon]] ability.

**Fortitude of the Crypt (Ex)**: At 8th level, a knight of the sepulcher gains immunity to poison. He also gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] 60 feet if he does not already possess it. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-aura-of-despair|aura of despair]] ability.

**Cloak of the Crypt (Ex)**: At 10th level, the knight of the sepulcher gains immunity to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|energy drain]] and harmful negative energy effects. His chance of ignoring critical hits and sneak attacks increases to 50%, as though he were wearing _armor of moderate [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]_. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-smite-good|smite good]] use gained at 10th level.

**Will of the Crypt (Ex)**: At 11th level, a knight of the sepulcher's bonus on defenses against mind-affecting effects and death effects increases to +4. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-aura-of-vengeance|aura of vengeance]] ability.

**Weapons of Sin (Su)**: At 14th level, a knight of the sepulcher's weapons are treated as evil-aligned for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-aura-of-sin|aura of sin]].

**Crypt Lord (Ex)**: At 15th level, a knight of the sepulcher's chance of ignoring critical hits and sneak attacks increases to 75%, as though he were wearing _armor of heavy [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]_. He gains immunity to death effects, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralysis]], sleep effects, and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]]. He no longer sleeps. The knight of the sepulcher also gains immunity to effects that cause fatigue, and effects that would cause him to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]] instead cause him to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-cruelty|cruelty]].

**Soul of the Crypt (Su)**: At 17th level, a knight of the sepulcher gains DR 5/bludgeoning and good. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-aura-of-depravity|aura of depravity]].

**Undying Champion (Ex)**: At 20th level, a knight of the sepulcher joins the ranks of the undead. His DR increases to 10/bludgeoning and good. His type changes to [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^undead|undead]], and he acquires all undead traits. Although immune to disease, he can still carry and spread diseases with the antipaladin's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-plague-bringer|plague bringer]] ability. The undying champion no longer has a Constitution score. He uses his Charisma score for calculating hit points, Fortitude Defense, and any special abilities that rely on Constitution. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-unholy-champion|unholy champion]].
