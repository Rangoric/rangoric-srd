# Herald of the Horn

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-archetypes|skald]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Even the loudest voice can often times be drowned out by the din of battle. Whether with the polished metal trumpet of a standing army or the crude curved animal horn of savage raiders, a herald of the horn sounds his raging song with thunderous blasts, which can bolster allies or shatter castle walls.

**Arcane Bond (Ex)**: At 1st level, a herald of the horn forms a powerful bond with a horn (musical instrument). This functions like an [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Arcane Bloodline|arcane bloodline]] sorcerer's arcane bond with an object. Like a weapon, wand, or staff, the horn must be held in one hand when the herald of the horn casts skald [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-spells|spells]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-scribe-scroll|Scribe Scroll]].

**Rousing Retort (Su)**: At 5th level, a herald of the horn can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]] to free allies from enchantment effects and fear. When beginning a raging song, he can expend 4 rounds of that ability to make an ongoing enchantment or fear effect attack all allies within 60 feet again, and a miss ends it. The allies gain a +2 bonus to their defenses against this new attack. This ability replaces the first daily use of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-spell-kenning|spell kenning]] gained at 5th level.

**Horn Call (Su)**: At 7th level, a herald's horn enhances his sonic spells. If a skald spell with the sonic descriptor is cast using the horn, its DC increases by 1. These DCs increase by an additional 1 at 13th and 19th levels. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-lore-master|lore master]].

**Crumbling Blast (Su)**: At 11th level, a herald of the horn can use his horn to create a devastating shock wave of energy. Once per day, he can sound a note on the horn that functions like a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Horn of Blasting|horn of blasting]]_ (DC = 10 + 1/2 the herald of the horn's level + his Charisma bonus). At 17th level, the herald of the horn can use this ability twice per day and it functions like a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Horn of Blasting, Greater|greater horn of blasting]]_. Unlike an actual _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Horn of Blasting|horn of blasting]]_, this ability does not carry the risk of destroying the horn. This ability replaces the second and third daily uses of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-spell-kenning|spell kenning]] gained at 11th and 17th levels.

**Rage Powers**: The following rage powers complement the herald of the horn archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Battle Roar (Rage Power)|battle roar]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Energy Absorption (Rage Power)|energy absorption]], energy eruption, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Fearless Rage (Rage Power)|fearless rage]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Knockback (Rage Power)|knockback]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Knockdown Stance (Rage Power)|knockdown]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Smasher (Rage Power)|smasher]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Swift Foot (Rage Power)|swift foot]].
