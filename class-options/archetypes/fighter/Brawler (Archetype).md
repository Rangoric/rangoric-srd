# Brawler

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

All melee is up close and personal, but some warriors bring it as close as they can get. Brawlers can be found anywhere, among all races and societies. A brawler could be hired muscle in a tavern, a local crime syndicate enforcer, or a hotheaded recruit among the ranks of a baron's guard. Often brawlers' in-your-face attitudes are as powerful as their tactics.

**Close Control (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a brawler becomes skilled at forcefully moving his opponent around the battlefield. The brawler gains a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Drag|drag]], and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]] combat maneuver checks. The brawler also gains a +1 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when attacked with the bull rush, drag, and reposition maneuvers. These bonuses increase by +1 for every four levels after 2nd (to a maximum of +5 at 18th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1.

**Close Combatant (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a brawler gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls and a +3 bonus on damage rolls with weapons in the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-groups|close weapon group]]. Both of these bonuses increase by +1 for every four levels beyond 3rd (to a maximum of +5 on attack rolls and +7 on damage rolls at 19th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1 and 2.

**Menacing Stance (Ex)**: At 7th level, a brawler constantly harries and distracts his enemies. While adjacent to the brawler, enemies take a –1 penalty on attack rolls and a –4 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]]. These penalties increase by 1 for every four levels after 7th level (to a maximum of –4 on attack rolls and –7 on concentration checks at 19th level). Creatures do not take these penalties if the brawler is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 2, 3, and 4 and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-mastery|armor mastery]].

**No Escape (Ex)**: At 9th level, taking a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Take 5-Foot Step|5-foot step]] out of the area of a brawler's menacing stance or moving out of the area of a brawler's menacing stance with a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Withdraw|withdraw]] action provokes an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] from the brawler. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3 and 4.

**Stand Still**: At 13th level, a brawler gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stand Still|Stand Still]] as a bonus feat, even if he does not have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]] feat. If the brawler already has the Stand Still feat, he can take any other [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Fighter Bonus|combat feat]] instead. Furthermore, he gains a bonus equal to 1/2 his fighter level on combat maneuver checks when using the Stand Still feat.

**Weapon Mastery (Ex)**: A brawler must select a close weapon for [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-mastery|this ability]].
