# Airborne Ambusher

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Strix|strix]]._

Driven by suspicion and hatred, strix doggedly guard their territories, making deadly use of their flight. Using swift strikes from above, strix plummet onto their foes with lethal force.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: An airborne ambusher is not [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-and-armor-proficiency|proficient]] with heavy armor or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Tower Shield|tower shields]].

**Class Skills**: An airborne ambusher adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|Acrobatics]] to his list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#Class Skills|class skills]].

**Combat Flyer (Ex)**: At 2nd level, an airborne ambusher may use his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|fighter bonus feats]] to select [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flyby Attack|Flyby Attack]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hover|Hover]]. This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Aerobatics (Ex)**: At 5th level, an airborne ambusher may make an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Tumbling|Acrobatics]] check to move through a threatened area or an enemy's space while flying. This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1.

**Flying Dodger (Ex)**: At 9th level, when an airborne ambusher flies at least half its fly speed on its turn, it gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC for 1 round. This bonus increases to +2 at 11th level and +4 at 17th level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 2.

**Plummeting Charge (Ex)**: At 13th level, if an airborne ambusher flies at least half its fly speed as part of a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]], it gains a +2 racial bonus on the attack roll (in addition to the normal charge bonus) and a +4 bonus on its critical confirmation roll. These bonuses improve to +4 and +6 at 17th level. This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3 and 4.
