# Cavern Sniper

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]].*

Perfectly at home in the darkness, the cavern sniper capitalizes on stealth and ranged attacks imbued with his spell-like abilities to harass his opponents. The cavern sniper focuses on surprise, his innate magical abilities, and poison to take down unwary foes. The cavern sniper has the following class features.

**Class Skills**: The cavern sniper adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] to his list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#Class Skills|class skills]] and removes [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] from his list of class skills.

**Imbued Shot (Su)**: At 1st level, the cavern sniper gains the ability to imbue his arrows or bolts with the effect of one of his [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]] *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]* [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^spell-like-abilities|spell-like abilities]] (provided he has access to the ability) as a swift action. When such an arrow or bolt is fired, the spell's area is centered where the arrow or bolt lands. If the target of the attack has a space larger than 5 feet, the cavern sniper can choose which square of the creature's space is the center of the spell-like ability's effect, as long as that square is within line of sight of the cavern sniper. The cavern sniper can instead choose to target a single square within line of sight with an imbued arrow or bolt, and uses that square as the center of the spell-like ability's area of effect on a hit (AC 5). The arrow must be fired during the round it was imbued, or the spell-like ability is wasted. If the arrow or bolt misses, the use of the spell-like ability is wasted. This ability replaces the 1st-level fighter [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|bonus feat]].

**Silent Shooter (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a cavern sniper gains a +2 bonus on Stealth checks made when loading a bow or crossbow, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|poisoning]] ammunition, and making sniping attempts. This bonus increases by +2 for every four levels beyond 2nd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Quick and Deadly (Ex)**: At 4th level, the cavern sniper can move at full speed while using Stealth at no penalty and can apply poison to a single arrow or crossbow bolt as a swift action. This ability replaces the 4th-level fighter [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|bonus feat]].

**Sniper Training (Ex)**: At 5th level, the cavern sniper chooses the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-groups|bow or crossbow weapon group]] and gains a +2 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 5th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1, 3, and 4.

**Greater Imbued Shot (Su)**: At 9th level, the cavern sniper gains two extra uses of both his *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]* spell-like abilities, but can only use these extra uses to imbue arrows and bolts with the imbued shot class feature. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 2.

**Weapon Mastery (Ex)**: At 20th level, when the cavern sniper gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-mastery|weapon mastery]], he must choose a weapon in either the bow or crossbow weapon group.
