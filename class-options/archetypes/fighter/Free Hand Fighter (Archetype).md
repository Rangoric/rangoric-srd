# Free Hand Fighter

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The free hand fighter specializes in the delicate art of handling a single weapon in one hand while using his free hand to balance, block, tip, and distract his opponents. While not a brawler, his open hand is as much a weapon as a bow or blade. His fighting school benefits only apply when he is using a one-handed weapon and carrying nothing in his other hand.

**Deceptive Strike (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a free hand fighter gains a +1 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] checks and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth#^creating-a-diversion|create a diversion to hide]]. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels after 2nd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Elusive (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a free hand fighter gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels after 2nd. This bonus does not apply when wearing medium or heavy armor or carrying a medium or heavier load. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1, 2, 3, and 4.

**Singleton (Ex)**: At 5th level, a free hand fighter gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls when wielding a melee weapon in one hand and leaving his other hand free. This bonus increases by +1 per six levels after 5th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1 and 4.

**Timely Tip (Ex)**: At 9th level, a free hand fighter can make a disarm combat maneuver against a target he threatens as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^move-action|move action]] to push aside the target's shield. If successful, the target loses its shield bonus to AC against the free hand fighter's next attack. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 2.

**Interference (Ex)**: At 13th level, a free hand fighter can make a disarm or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver against a target he threatens as a move action to push his opponent off balance. If successful, the target becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]]. This condition lasts until the target takes damage from a melee or ranged attack or until the beginning of the free hand fighter's next turn, whichever comes first. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3.

**Reversal (Ex)**: At 19th level, a free hand fighter can make a disarm combat maneuver against a creature he threatens as an immediate action when he is the target of a melee attack from another creature. If successful, the attack changes to target the target of the free hand fighter's maneuver instead of the free hand fighter himself. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-mastery|armor mastery]].
