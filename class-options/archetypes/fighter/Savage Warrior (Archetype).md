# Savage Warrior

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Warriors' might is not measured only by their skill with steel, but also by their ability to inflict death with fang and claw, horn and hoof, and every exotic appendage the natural and unnatural world has to offer.

**Spark of Life (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a savage warrior gains a +1 bonus on defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|energy drain]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Death Attacks|death effects]]. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 2nd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Natural Savagery (Ex)**: At 5th level, a savage warrior gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with natural weapons. This bonus also applies to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] for [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grappling]]. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 5th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1.

**Savage Charge (Ex)**: At 9th level, when a savage warrior attacks with a natural weapon at the end of a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]], he gains a bonus on his natural weapon attack rolls equal to half his fighter level, while suffering a penalty to his AC equal to half his fighter level. These replace the normal attack roll bonus and AC penalty for charging. This bonus also applies to his CMB for a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]] combat maneuvers made when charging. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 2.

**Careful Claw (Ex)**: At 13th level, when using a natural weapon to attack a creature using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Shield|fire shield]]_ or a similar effect that damages creatures attacking it (such as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/devil/Barbed Devil (Hamatula)|barbed devil’s]] barbed defense), a savage warrior reduces the damage from such effects by an amount equal to 1/2 his fighter level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3.

**Greater Savage Charge (Ex)**: At 17th level, when using savage charge, the AC penalty is reduced to 1/4 his fighter level instead of 1/2 his fighter level. In addition, a savage warrior can charge through friendly creatures and difficult terrain. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 4.

**Natural Weapon Mastery (Ex)**: At 20th level, a savage warrior must choose one natural weapon. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-mastery|weapon mastery]].
