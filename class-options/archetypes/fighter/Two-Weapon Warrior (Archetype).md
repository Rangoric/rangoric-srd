# Two-Weapon Warrior

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Trained under great masters who preached the simple truth that two are better than one when it comes to weapons, the two-weapon warrior is a terror when his hands are full. From paired daggers to exotic double weapons, all combinations come equally alive in his skilled hands.

**Defensive Flurry (Ex)**: At 3rd level, when a two-weapon warrior makes a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Full Attack|full attack]] with both weapons, he gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC against melee attacks until the beginning of his next turn. This bonus increases by +1 every four levels after 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1 and 2.

**Twin Blades (Ex)**: At 5th level, a two-weapon warrior gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls when making a full attack with [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Two-Weapon Fighting|two weapons]] or a double weapon. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels after 5th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1.

**Doublestrike (Ex)**: At 9th level, a two-weapon warrior may, as a standard action, make one attack with both his primary and secondary weapons. The penalties for attacking with two weapons apply normally. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 2.

**Improved Balance (Ex)**: At 11th level, the attack penalties for fighting with two weapons are reduced by –1 for a two-weapon warrior. Alternatively, he may use a one-handed weapon in his off-hand, treating it as if it were a light weapon with the normal light weapon penalties. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 3.

**Equal Opportunity (Ex)**: At 13th level, when a two-weapon warrior makes an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]], he may attack once with both his primary and secondary weapons. The penalties for attacking with two weapons apply normally. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3.

**Perfect Balance (Ex)**: At 15th level, the penalties for fighting with two weapons are reduced by an additional –1 for a two-weapon warrior. This benefit stacks with improved balance. If he is using a one-handed weapon in his off hand, treating it as a light weapon, he uses the normal light weapon penalties. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Deft Doublestrike (Ex)**: At 17th level, when a two-weapon warrior hits an opponent with both weapons, he can make a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] attempt (or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]], if one or both weapons can be used to trip) against that opponent as an immediate action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 4.

**Deadly Defense (Ex)**: At 19th level, when a two-weapon warrior makes a full attack with both weapons, every creature that hits him with a melee attack before the beginning of his next turn provokes an attack of opportunity from the warrior. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-mastery|armor mastery]].
