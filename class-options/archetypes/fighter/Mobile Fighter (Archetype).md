# Mobile Fighter

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Where some fighters focus on strength and raw power, the mobile fighter relies on swiftness and mobility, gliding across the battlefield like a steel whirlwind and leaving destruction in his wake.

**Agility (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a mobile fighter gains a +1 bonus on defenses against effects that cause him to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]], slowed, or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]]. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 2nd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Leaping Attack (Ex)**: At 5th level, when a mobile fighter moves at least 5 feet prior to attacking, he gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 5th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1, 2, 3, and 4.

**Rapid Attack (Ex)**: At 11th level, a mobile fighter can combine a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Full Attack|full attack]] action with a single move. He must forgo the attack at his highest bonus but may take the remaining attacks at any point during his movement. This movement provokes [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] as normal. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 3.

**Fleet Footed (Ex)**: At 15th level, the mobile fighter's speed increases by 10 feet. He can take 10 on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks even while distracted or threatened, and can take 20 on an Acrobatics check once per day for every five fighter levels he possesses. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Whirlwind Blitz (Ex)**: At 20th level, a mobile fighter can make a full-attack action as a standard action. He may also use the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Whirlwind Attack|Whirlwind Attack]] feat as a standard action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-mastery|weapon mastery]].
