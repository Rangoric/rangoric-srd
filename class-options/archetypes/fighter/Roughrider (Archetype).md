# Roughrider

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Roughriders study and practice the fine points of mounted combat, drilling endlessly with warbeasts—from noble thoroughbreds to trained monsters—to form a perfect synergy between rider and steed.

**Steadfast Mount (Ex)**: At 2nd level, after a roughrider has spent 1 hour practicing with a mount, the mount gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC and a +1 morale bonus on saves, but only while the roughrider is mounted on it or adjacent to it. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels after 2nd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Armored Charger (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a roughrider no longer suffers armor check penalties on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] skill checks to ride. His mount's speed is not reduced when carrying a medium load or wearing medium barding. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1.

**Mounted Mettle (Ex)**: At 5th level, a roughrider and his mount gain a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls when he is mounted or adjacent to his mount. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels after 5th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1, 2, 3, and 4.

**Leap from the Saddle (Ex)**: At 7th level, after a roughrider's mount takes a single move, he may attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^fast-mount-or-dismount|fast dismount]] (DC 20 Animal Handling check). If he succeeds, he can take a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Full Attack|full attack]] action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 2.

**Relentless Steed (Ex)**: At 11th level, a roughrider's mount does not reduce its speed when wearing heavy barding or carrying a heavy load. The roughrider may also reroll an Animal Handling skill check to ride or a saving throw made by the mount once per day, but must use the second roll even if it is worse. This ability may be used one additional time per day for every four levels beyond 11th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 3.

**Ride Them Down (Ex)**: At 15th level, can spur his mount on while readying an attack. If a roughrider's mount takes a single move, the roughrider can make a full attack, taking his attacks at any point during his mount's movement. If he has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Trample|Trample]] feat, he may substitute an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun combat maneuver]] for each of his attacks. This movement provokes [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] against the roughrider but not his mount. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Unavoidable Onslaught (Ex)**: At 15th level, a roughrider's [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Mounted Combat|mounted]] [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] is not blocked by friendly creatures or difficult terrain. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Indomitable Steed (Ex)**: At 19th level, a roughrider and his steed gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR 5/—]] when mounted. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-mastery|armor mastery]].
