# Unarmed Fighter

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Not all fighters need weapons to make their mark on the world. The unarmed fighter picks up a weapon only rarely, and when he does, he prefers the weapons of the monk. There are even those who mistake them for monks, but these fighters enter the fight without _ki_, and instead have a tough perseverance that few can rival.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: An unarmed fighter is not [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-and-armor-proficiency|proficient]] with medium armor, heavy armor, or shields. An unarmed fighter is proficient with all [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-groups|monk weapons]], including exotic monk weapons.

**Unarmed Style**: At 1st level, an unarmed fighter gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]] feat and any single [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^style-feats|style feat]] as a bonus feat. The unarmed fighter need not meet all the prerequisites of the style feat he chooses, but style feats that grant additional uses of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]] feat cannot be taken until the unarmed fighter has that feat. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|bonus feat]] at 1st level.

**Harsh Training (Ex)**: At 2nd level, an unarmed fighter gains a +1 bonus on defenses against effects that cause the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] conditions or temporary penalties to ability scores. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels after 2nd (to a maximum of +5 at 18th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Tough Guy (Ex)**: At 3rd level, an unarmed fighter gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR/—]] equal to half his fighter level against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]] or damage taken while he is [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1.

**Weapon Training (Ex)**: At 5th level, an unarmed fighter gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with weapons in the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-groups|monk and natural weapon groups]], improving by +1 for every four levels beyond 5th (to a maximum of +4 at 17th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1, 2, 3, and 4.

**Clever Wrestler (Ex)**: At 7th level, an unarmed fighter takes no penalties to Dexterity or on attack rolls while grappled, and retains his Dexterity bonus to AC while [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Pinned (Condition)|pinning]] an opponent. The unarmed fighter can make attacks of opportunity even when grappled and even against creatures attempting to grapple him if the opponent has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]] feat or the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]] ability. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 2.

**Trick Throw (Ex)**: At 8th level, when an unarmed fighter successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trips]] an opponent with an unarmed attack, he can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] combat maneuver against that creature (before the opponent becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]) as an immediate action that does not provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]]. This ability replaces the 8th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|bonus feat]].

**Takedown (Ex)**: At 12th level, if an unarmed fighter succeeds on a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Drag|drag]] maneuver, he can attempt a trip maneuver against the same target as a swift action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity. At 15th level, he may do so after a successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] check. This ability replaces the 12th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|bonus feat]].

**Eye Gouge (Ex)**: At 13th level, if an unarmed fighter confirms a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] with his [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Unarmed Strike|unarmed strike]] or begins his turn grappled, he may attempt a dirty trick maneuver to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blind]] his target as a swift action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity. A target more than one size category larger is unaffected. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3.

**Sucker Punch (Ex)**: At 17th level, when an unarmed fighter strikes a creature that is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC, or that he has successfully pinned with a grapple check, he can attempt a dirty trick or trip combat maneuver against that target as a swift action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Sheer Toughness (Ex)**: At 19th level, an unarmed fighter becomes immune to nonlethal damage and the exhausted, fatigued, and staggered conditions. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-mastery|armor mastery]].

**Weapon Mastery (Ex)**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-mastery|This ability]] applies to unarmed strikes only.
