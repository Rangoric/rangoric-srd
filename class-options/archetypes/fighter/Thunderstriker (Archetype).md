# Thunderstriker

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

The thunderstriker adopts an unusual fighting style, gripping a heavy weapon with both hands and switching to a defensive posture with weapon and buckler, lashing out with the shield with surprising speed and power.

**Strapped Shield (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a thunderstriker takes no penalty on attack rolls when using a weapon in two hands while wearing a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Buckler|buckler]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1.

**Hardbuckler (Ex)**: At 7th level, a thunderstriker may make shield bash attacks with a buckler as if it were a light shield. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 2.

**Knockback Smash (Ex)**: At 11th level, when a thunderstriker uses his buckler to attack, he gains a bonus equal to the enhancement bonus of the buckler on both attack and damage rolls. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 3.

**Hammer and Anvil (Ex)**: At 13th level, a thunderstriker suffers only half the normal penalties for [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Two-Weapon Fighting|two-weapon fighting]] when using a buckler as his off-hand weapon. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3.

**Buckler Defense (Ex)**: At 15th level, a thunderstriker retains partial use of his buckler even when using a weapon in both hands or in each hand (rather than losing his shield bonus until the beginning of his next turn). He gains a +1 shield bonus to AC and may apply the benefits of any feats he knows that require the use of a shield, but does not benefit from any magical properties his shield may possess. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Balanced Bashing (Ex)**: At 17th level, a thunderstriker no longer suffers two-weapon fighting penalties when using a buckler as his off-hand weapon. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 4.

**Improved Buckler Defense (Ex)**: At 19th level, a thunderstriker does not forfeit his shield bonus to AC from a buckler when fighting two-handed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-mastery|armor mastery]].
