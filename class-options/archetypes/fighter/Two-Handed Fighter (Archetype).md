# Two-Handed Fighter

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some fighters focus their efforts on finding the biggest, heaviest, most imposing weapon they can find and training to manage and harness the weight of their massive weapons for maximum impact. These fighting school benefits only apply when using two-handed weapons.

**Shattering Strike (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a two-handed fighter gains a +1 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] attempts and on damage rolls made against objects. These bonuses increase by +1 for every four levels beyond 2nd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Overhand Chop (Ex)**: At 3rd level, when a two-handed fighter makes a single attack (with the attack action or a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]]) with a two-handed weapon, he adds double his Strength bonus on damage rolls. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1.

**Weapon Training (Ex)**: As the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|fighter class feature]], but the bonuses only apply when wielding two-handed melee weapons.

**Backswing (Ex)**: At 7th level, when a two-handed fighter makes a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Full Attack|full attack]] with a two-handed weapon, he adds double his Strength bonus on damage rolls for all attacks after the first. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 2.

**Piledriver (Ex)**: At 11th level, as a standard action, a two-handed fighter can make a single melee attack with a two-handed weapon. If the attack hits, he may make a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver against the target of his attack as a free action that does not provoke an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 3.

**Greater Power Attack (Ex)**: At 15th level, when using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]] with a two-handed melee weapon, the bonus damage from Power Attack is doubled (+100%) instead of increased by half (+50%). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Devastating Blow (Ex)**: At 19th level, as a standard action, a two-handed fighter may make a single melee attack with a two-handed weapon at a –5 penalty. If the attack hits, it is treated as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical threat]]. Special weapon abilities that activate only on a critical hit do not activate if this critical hit is confirmed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-mastery|armor mastery]].
