# Gladiator

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Most fighters battle out of necessity, with each battle a contest of life and death. Some, however, fight for glory and for the adulation of the crowd. The gladiator is both a cunning warrior and a consummate performer, knowing life and death are balanced not only on a sword's edge, but also on the cheers or jeers of the crowd.

**Skills**: A gladiator gains [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (act, comedy, and dance) (Cha) as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#Class Skills|class skills]].

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A gladiator is not [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-and-armor-proficiency|proficient]] with heavy armor or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Tower Shield|tower shields]]. Instead he gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Performance Weapon Mastery|Performance Weapon Mastery]] feat at 1st level as a bonus feat. This bonus feat is in addition to the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|bonus feat]] a fighter gains at 1st level. Furthermore, a gladiator using [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Piecemeal Armor|piecemeal armor]] is considered to be wearing a suit of armor as long as he wearing two or three armor pieces, gaining the +1 to armor bonus but still taking the +5% chance of arcane spell failure if the pieces are mixed.

**Bonus Feats**: A gladiator may choose to take [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Fighter Bonus|combat]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^performance-feats|performance feats]] as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|bonus feats]].

**Fame (Ex)**: At 2nd level, when a gladiator begins a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat|performance combat]], he always starts with at least 1 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat#^victory-points|victory point]]. If he already has victory points, he gains 1 extra victory point. At 10th level, the gladiator starts out with at least 2 victory points. If he already has victory points, he gains 2 extra victory points. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].
