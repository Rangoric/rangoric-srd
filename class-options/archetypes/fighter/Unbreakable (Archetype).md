# Unbreakable

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The unbreakable is a warrior of indomitable will, unstoppable and implacable once he has set his mind upon a course of action. The unbreakable endures any trial to do what must be done, and when his mind is set toward a goal, nothing can stop him from achieving it, though many may try. He is a juggernaut and a zealot, supremely confident in his own abilities.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: An unbreakable is not [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-and-armor-proficiency|proficient]] with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Tower Shield|tower shields]].

**Tough as Nails**: An unbreakable gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Die Hard]] as bonus feats. This ability replaces the fighter's 1st-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bonus-feats|bonus feat]].

**Unflinching (Ex)**: At 2nd level, an unbreakable gains a +1 bonus on Will Defense against mind-affecting effects. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels after 2nd level (to a maximum of +5 at 18th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Heroic Recovery (Ex)**: At 5th level, an unbreakable gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Heroic Recovery|Heroic Recovery]] feat as a bonus feat, if he does not have it already. If he already has this feat, the unbreakable can choose any [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Fighter Bonus|combat feat]] instead. In addition, he may use this feat one additional time per day for every four levels after 5th (to a maximum of 4 times per day at 17th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1.

**Heroic Defiance (Ex)**: At 9th level, an unbreakable gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Heroic Defiance|Heroic Defiance]] feat as a bonus feat, if he does not have it already. If he already has this feat, the unbreakable can choose any combat feat instead. In addition, he may use this feat one additional time per day for every four levels after 9th (to a maximum of 3 times per day at 19th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 2.

**Quick Recovery (Ex)**: At 11th level, an unbreakable needs only 15 minutes of rest or to be subject to a healing spell or effect to recover from the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] condition. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 3.

**Stalwart (Ex)**: At 13th level, when a spell or spell-like ability that has a partial effect even on a miss misses an unbreakable’s Fortitude or Will, he is completely unaffected by it. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3.

**Unlimited Endurance (Ex)**: At 15th level, when an unbreakable is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]], he only suffers the effects of the fatigued condition instead, but does require 1 hour of rest to reduce this condition to the actual fatigued condition. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Miraculous Recovery (Ex)**: At 17th level, when an ongoing effect attacks an unbreakable again, it rolls twice and takes the worse roll. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 4.

**Unbreakable Mind (Ex)**: At 20th level, an unbreakable becomes nearly impossible to sway with honeyed words or magic. He gains immunity to mind-affecting effects. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-mastery|weapon mastery]].
