# Cad

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

In combat, most fighters have some sort of code of honor. Some believe that one should not kick enemies when they are down, or should limit use of other such dirty tricks to the most dire of circumstances. The cad places no such limitations on himself. Battles are for winning, and anything that gives the cad the upper hand against his enemies is a legitimate tactic. No trick is too treacherous if it leads to victory.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A cad is not [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-and-armor-proficiency|proficient]] with medium armor, heavy armor, or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Tower Shield|tower shields]].

**Skills**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] (Dex), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] (Dex), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] (Dex), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex) are [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#Class Skills|class skills]] for a cad.

**Dirty Maneuvers (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a cad becomes skilled at deceiving and discomfiting his opponents. The cad gains a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]], and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] combat maneuver checks. The cad also gains a +1 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when attacked with the disarm, dirty trick, and steal combat maneuvers. These bonuses increase by 1 for every four levels after 2nd (to a maximum of +5 at 18th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]].

**Catch Off-Guard**: At 3rd level, the cad gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Catch Off-Guard|Catch Off-Guard]] feat. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1.

**Payback (Ex)**: At 5th level, a cad gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls against any creature that has attacked the cad since the beginning of his last turn. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 5th (to a maximum of +4 at 17th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 1, 3, and 4.

**Deadly Surprise (Ex)**: At 7th level, when a cad hits an opponent that is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against him with a weapon or unarmed attack, he may attempt a dirty trick combat maneuver as an immediate action as part of the attack. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 2.

**Razor-Sharp Chair Leg (Ex)**: At 9th level, as a swift action, a cad may alter the type of damage dealt by an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^improvised-weapons|improvised weapon]] to bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage. In addition, the cad has a critical threat range of 19–20/×2 with any improvised melee weapon. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 2.

**Craven Combatant (Ex)**: At 11th level, when [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^fighting-defensively-as-a-standard-action|fighting defensively]] or using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]], a cad cannot be [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]] except by a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja|ninja]] whose level is four or more higher than the cad's fighter level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 3.

**Sweeping Prank (Ex)**: At 13th level, as a standard action, a cad can use a dirty trick maneuver against any two adjacent opponents that he can reach, making a separate combat maneuver check against each opponent. He must use the same dirty trick maneuver against each opponent.

At 17th level, the cad can use this ability as a full-round action to attempt a dirty trick maneuver against a number of enemies equal to 2 + his Dexterity bonus (if any). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] 3 and 4.

**Treacherous Blow (Ex)**: At 15th level, when a cad confirms a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]], he can attempt a dirty trick combat maneuver as part of that attack as an immediate action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 4.

**Ultimate Payback (Ex)**: At 20th level, any critical threats a cad makes against an opponent that has attacked him since the beginning of his last turn are automatically confirmed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-mastery|weapon mastery]].
