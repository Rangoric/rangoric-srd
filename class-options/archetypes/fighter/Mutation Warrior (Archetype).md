# Mutation Warrior

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-archetypes|fighter]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While most fighters rely on physical fitness and rigorous training to achieve martial superiority, a few prefer to create and imbibe dangerous concoctions that mutate them into fearsome creatures.

**Mutagen (Su)**: At 3rd level, a mutation warrior discovers how to create a mutagen that he can imbibe in order to heighten his physical prowess at the cost of his personality. This ability functions as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|alchemist’s mutagen]] ability, using his fighter level as his alchemist level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 1.

**Mutagen Discovery (Su)**: At 7th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the mutation warrior can choose one of the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discoveries]] to augment his abilities: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Feral Mutagen (Discovery)|feral mutagen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Grand Mutagen (Discovery)|grand mutagen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Greater Mutagen (Discovery)|greater mutagen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Infuse Mutagen (Discovery)|infuse mutagen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Nauseating Flesh (Discovery)|nauseating flesh]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Preserve Organs (Discovery)|preserve organs]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Rag Doll Mutagen (Discovery)|rag doll mutagen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Spontaneous Healing (Discovery)|spontaneous healing]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Tentacle (Discovery)|tentacle]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Vestigial Arm (Discovery)|vestigial arm]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Wings (Discovery)|wings]]. The mutagen warrior uses his fighter level as his effective alchemist level for the purpose of these discoveries. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-training|armor training]] 2, 3, and 4 and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-armor-mastery|armor mastery]].
