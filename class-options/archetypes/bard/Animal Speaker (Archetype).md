# Animal Speaker

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-archetypes|bard]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

An animal speaker focuses not on the ears and minds of humans, but on the creatures of the wild and those in the underbellies of cities.

**Animal Friend**: An animal speaker selects a particular kind of animal, such as apes, badgers, bears, boars, cats, snakes, and so on. The bard gains a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks to influence animals of his chosen kind. Animals of this kind have a starting attitude of at least “indifferent” toward the bard and never attack him unless he attacks them first.

Animal companions and magically controlled animals can be directed to attack the bard if the controlling creature wins an opposed Charisma check against the bard (this check can be made once per round until it succeeds, after which no further checks are needed). Supernatural versions of animals (such as animals with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creature/Fiendish Creature|fiendish template]]) can attempt an opposed Charisma check against the bard with a +4 bonus on its roll to overcome this hesitation. This ability has no effect on creatures other than animals. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-fascinate|fascinate]].

**Nature's Speaker**: At 5th, 11th, and 17th level, the animal speaker selects another kind of animal friend. The bard can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ at will on animals of his selected kinds. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-well-versed|well-versed]].

**Bardic Performance**: An animal speaker gains the following types of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]].

**Soothing Performance**: At 3rd level, an animal speaker can use bardic performance to influence animals. This works like the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|druid ability wild empathy]], except he expends 1 round of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] and makes a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] check. If the bard already has wild empathy from another class, he adds the class levels that provide wild empathy to the result of his Performance check to influence an animal. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-competence|inspire competence]].

**Attract Rats**: At 5th level, the animal speaker can use bardic performance to summon 1d3 [[rangoric-srd/monsters/swarm/Rat Swarm|rat swarms]]; they remain as long as he continues performing. At 11th level, he summons 2d3 swarms instead of 1d3 and the swarms have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Advanced Creature|advanced creature simple template]]. At 17th level, the number of swarms he summons increases to 3d3. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-lore-master|lore master]].

**Summon Nature's Ally**: At 1st level, the animal speaker adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally I]]_ to his [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bard Spell List|bard spell list]] and bard spells known as a 1st-level spell. At 4th level (when he gains access to 2nd-level spells) he adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally II|summon nature’s ally II]]_ to his spell list and spells known as a 2nd-level spell, and so on every 3 levels thereafter, until 16th level when he adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally VI|summon nature’s ally VI]]_ to his 6th-level spell list and spells known.
