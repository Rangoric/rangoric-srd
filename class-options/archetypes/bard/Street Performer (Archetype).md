# Street Performer

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-archetypes|bard]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Whether acrobat, troubadour, or thespian, the street performer mixes with the masses, singing for his supper.

**Bardic Performance**: A street performer gains the following types of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]].

**Disappearing Act (Su)**: A street performer can use performance to divert attention from an ally. All creatures within 30 feet that a Will attack at 1/2 the bard's level + the bard's Cha modifier hits treat one creature chosen by the bard as if it were [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]]. This performance affects one additional creature at 5th level and every 6 levels thereafter. If the targets take any action that would cause them to become visible, they become visible to everyone. The bard cannot use this ability on himself. This ability is a mind-affecting effect that requires visual components. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-courage|inspire courage]].

**Harmless Performer (Su)**: At 3rd level, a street performer can use performance to appear meek and unworthy of being attacked. While using this performance, whenever an enemy targets the street performer, the performance attacks the enemy’s Will at 1/2 the bard's level + the bard's Cha bonus, and on a hit it is unable to attack the bard this round, as per _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sanctuary|sanctuary]]_. The enemy loses the attack that targeted the bard, but may spend additional attacks targeting other creatures. If the opponent was targeting the street performer with a spell, it must succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] at the same DC or lose the spell. If this check succeeds, it may target another creature with the spell instead. This mind-affecting ability requires audible or visual components. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-competence|inspire competence]].

**Madcap Prank (Su)**: At 9th level, a street performer can use performance to discomfit a target within 30 feet, causing its clothing to become tangled, its headgear to fall down over its eyes, or even causing it to slip and fall or otherwise be made to appear a fool. The performance attacks the target’s Reflex at 1/2 the bard's level + the bard's Cha modifier each round that it hears or sees the performance, and on a hit it takes one of the following random effects that round: 1—[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]], 2—[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]], 3—[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]], 4—[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]], 5—fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]], 6—[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]]. Each effect lasts 1 round. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-greatness|inspire greatness]].

**Slip through the Crowd (Su)**: At 15th level, a street performer's disappearing act enables affected creatures to move through [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#^crowds|crowd]] squares and enemy-occupied squares without impediment. Affected creatures are treated as if having _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_, but the effect attacks enemies again each time they are attacked, and a miss lets them notice them. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-heroics|inspire heroics]].

**Gladhanding**: A street performer earns double the normal amount of money from [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks. As a standard action, he may use a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check in place of a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] check to improve a creature's attitude for 1 minute, after which its attitude becomes one step worse than originally. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^countersong|countersong]].

**Streetwise**: A street performer gains a bonus equal to half his level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks made to influence crowds, and Persuasion checks to gather information (minimum +1). This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-knowledge|bardic knowledge]].

**Quick Change (Ex)**: At 5th level, a street performer can don a disguise as a standard action by taking a –5 penalty on his check. He can take 10 on Deception and Imitation checks and use Deception to create a diversion to hide as a swift action. He can take 20 on a Deception or Imitation check once per day, plus one time per six levels beyond 5th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-lore-master|lore master]].
