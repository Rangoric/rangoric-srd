# Detective

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-archetypes|bard]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Piecing together clues and catching the guilty with sheer cleverness, the detective is skilled at divining the truth.

**Bardic Performance**: A detective gains the following types of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]].

**Careful Teamwork (Su)**: A detective uses performance to keep allies coordinated, alert, and ready for action. All allies within 30 feet gain a +1 bonus on Initiative checks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks for 1 hour. They also gain a +1 insight bonus on Reflex Defense and to AC against traps and when they are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]]. These bonuses increase by +1 at 5th level and every six levels thereafter. Using this ability requires 3 rounds of continuous performance, and the targets must be able to see and hear the bard throughout the performance. This ability is language-dependent and requires visual and audible components. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-courage|inspire courage]].

**True Confession (Su)**: At 9th level, a detective can use performance to trick a creature into revealing its secrets. Using this ability requires a successful [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] check to see through a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|deception]] or notice mental compulsion. After 3 continuous rounds of performance, it attacks the target’s Will at 1/2 the bard's level + the bard's Cha modifier. A miss renders the target immune to this power for 24 hours. On a hit, a liar inadvertently reveals the lie and the truth behind it. A creature under a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Charm and Compulsion|charm or compulsion]] reveals the nature of its enchantment and who placed it (if the creature knows) and the enchantment attacks it again, a miss freeing it. This ability is language-dependent and requires audible components. Using this power requires only 2 rounds of performance at 15th level, and 1 round of performance at 20th level. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-greatness|inspire greatness]].

**Show Yourselves (Ex)**: At 15th level, a detective can use performance to compel creatures to reveal themselves when hiding. It attacks the Will of all enemies within 30 feet at 1/2 the bard's level + the bard's Cha modifier. On a hit, they must cease using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]], unlock and open doors between themselves and the detective, and dismiss, suppress, or dispel if necessary magical effects that grant [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisibility]] or any other form of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] from the detective. As long as they can hear the performance, affected creatures may not attack or flee until they have eliminated every such effect, though they are freed from this compulsion immediately if attacked. It attacks creatures in the area again each round the bard continues his performance. This ability is language-dependent and requires audible components. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-heroics|inspire heroics]].

**Eye for Detail (Ex)**: A detective gains a bonus equal to half his level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks, as well as [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks to gather information (minimum +1). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-knowledge|bardic knowledge]].

**Arcane Insight (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a detective can find and disable magical traps, like a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|rogue’s trapfinding]] ability. In addition, he gains a +4 bonus on defenses against illusions and a +4 bonus on caster level checks and to defenses to see through disguises and protections against divination (such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Aura|magic aura]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Misdirection|misdirection]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]_). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-well-versed|well-versed]].

**Arcane Investigation**: In addition, a detective's class [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bard Spell List|spell list]] includes the following: 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Chaos|detect chaos]]/[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|evil]]/[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|law]]/[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|good]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Zone of Truth|zone of truth]]_; 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Eye|arcane eye]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Plants|speak with plants]]_; 4th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]]_; 5th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prying Eyes|prying eyes]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Tell|stone tell]]_; 6th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Location|discern location]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Find the Path|find the path]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prying Eyes, Greater|greater prying eyes]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Moment of Prescience|moment of prescience]]_. A detective may add one of these spells or any divination spell on the bard spell list to his list of spells known at 2nd level and every four levels thereafter. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-versatile-performance|versatile performance]].
