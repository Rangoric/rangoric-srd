# Prankster

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-archetypes|bard]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnomes]]._

The prankster sees humor as the highest form or art, and pranks as the highest form of humor. In addition to setting friends up for light-hearted pranks, the prankster can use his quick wit and cruel sense of humor to enrage foes before incapacitating them with a clever turn of phrase.

**Bardic Performance**: A prankster's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] functions like a bard's, but some of its performances are exchanged for those listed below.

_Mock (Su)_: At 1st level, the prankster can use his performance to cause one or more creatures to become furious with him. Each creature to be mocked must be within 90 feet, able to see, hear, and understand the prankster, and capable of paying attention to him. The prankster must also be able to see the creatures affected. For every three levels the prankster has attained beyond 1st, he can target one additional creature with this ability.

It attacks the Will of each creature within range at 1/2 the prankster's level + the prankster's Charisma modifier, and a miss negates the effect. If it misses a creature, the prankster cannot successfully mock that creature for 24 hours. If it hits, the creature is angered by the performance and seeks to harm the prankster. While the prankster maintains the mocking, the target takes a –2 penalty on all attack rolls and skill checks until it has successfully attacked the prankster with a melee or ranged attack, or has harmed the creature with a spell that deals damage.

Mock is an enchantment ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Charm and Compulsion|compulsion]]) mind-affecting ability. Mock relies on audible and visual components in order to function. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-fascinate|fascinate]].

_Punchline (Sp)_: At 6th level, the prankster can use his performance to tell a punchline to amuse a creature he has already mocked (see above), goading it into _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hideous Laughter|hideous laughter]]_ (as the spell). Using this ability does not disrupt the mock effect, but it does require a standard action to activate (in addition to the free action to continue the mock effect). A prankster can use this ability more than once against an individual creature during an individual performance.

Telling a punchline does not count against a prankster's daily use of bardic performance. It attacks Will at 1/2 the bard's level + the bard's Charisma modifier, and a miss negates the effect. This ability affects only a single creature. Punchline is an enchantment (compulsion), mind-affecting, language-dependent ability and relies on audible components. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-suggestion|suggestion]] ability.

_Mass Punchline (Sp)_: This ability functions just like punchline, but allows a prankster of 18th level or higher to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hideous Laughter|hideous laughter]]_ simultaneously against any number of creatures that he has mocked. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-mass-suggestion|mass suggestion]] ability.

**Swap (Ex)**: A prankster can steal an object from a creature and replace it with another object of the same size or smaller that the prankster has in his hand. This functions as the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal combat maneuver]], but the prankster does not provoke an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]], and may use his [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] check in place of his combat maneuver check. If the prankster's check exceeds the target's CMD by 10 or more, the target is unaware the swap has been made until it tries to use the swapped object or the end of its next turn (whichever happens first). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-lore-master|lore master]].
