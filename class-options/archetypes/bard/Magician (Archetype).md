# Magician

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-archetypes|bard]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A magician dabbles in performance, but sees it as a means to tap into universal energies and channel them.

**Bardic Performance**: A magician gains the following types of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]].

**Dweomercraft (Su)**: A magician can use performance to manipulate magical energies. Allies of the magician gain a +1 bonus on caster level checks, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]], and attack rolls with spells and spell-like abilities. This bonus increases by +1 at 5th level and every six levels thereafter. This ability relies on visual and audible components. It replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-courage|inspire courage]].

**Spell Suppression (Su)**: At 8th level, a magician can use performance to counter the spells of his foes. Once the bard begins using this performance, he tracks the number of rounds it has been in use. While performing, as an immediate action, he can attempt to counter any spell that he can identify using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]], so long as that spell's level is equal or less than the total number of rounds he has been performing spell suppression. The attempt to counter the spell is made as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_, using the bard's level as the caster level. If successful, the bardic performance immediately ends. This ability requires audible components. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-dirge-of-doom|dirge of doom]].

**Metamagic Mastery (Ex)**: At 14th level, a magician can use performance to apply a metamagic feat to a spell he is about to cast without increasing the casting time. The bard must still expend a higher-level slot to cast this spell. This causes the performance to immediately end. This ability requires audible components. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-frightening-tune|frightening tune]].

**Magical Talent (Ex)**: A magician gains a bonus equal to half his level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] checks. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-knowledge|bardic knowledge]].

**Improved Counterspell**: A magician gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Counterspell|Improved Counterspell]] as a bonus feat. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^countersong|countersong]].

**Extended Performance (Su)**: At 2nd level, a magician can extend the duration of bardic performance after he stops concentrating by sacrificing a spell slot as a swift action. The performance effect lingers for 1 extra round per level of the spell. Only one spell may be sacrificed per performance, and performance types that take affect after a specific number of rounds cannot be extended. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-well-versed|well-versed]].

**Expanded Repertoire (Ex)**: At 2nd level and every four levels thereafter, a magician can add one spell to his spells known from the spell list of any arcane spellcasting class. The spell must be of a level he can cast. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-versatile-performance|versatile performance]].

**Arcane Bond (Ex)**: At 5th level, a magician gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-arcane-bond|arcane bond]] ability as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]]. He may not choose a familiar or a weapon as a bonded item. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-lore-master|lore master]].

**Wand Mastery (Ex)**: At 10th level, when a magician uses a wand containing a spell on his spell list, he uses his Charisma bonus to set the wand's attack bonus. At 16th level, when using such a wand, he uses his caster level in place of the wand's caster level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-jack-of-all-trades|jack of all trades]].
