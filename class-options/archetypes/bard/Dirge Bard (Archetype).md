# Dirge Bard

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-archetypes|bard]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A composer of sonorous laments for the dead and elaborate requiems for those lost yet long remembered, dirge bards master musical tools and tropes that must appeal to the ears and hearts of both the living and the dead.

**Bardic Performance**: A dirge bard gains the following type of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]].

**Dance of the Dead (Su)**: At 10th level, a dirge bard can use his bardic performance to cause dead bones or bodies to rise up and move or fight at his command. This ability functions like _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]_, but the created skeletons or zombies remain fully animate only as long as the dirge bard continues the performance. Once it stops, any created undead collapse into carrion. Bodies or bones cannot be animated more than once using this ability. Unlike _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]_, dance of the dead requires no components and does not have the evil descriptor. This performance replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-jack-of-all-trades|jack-of-all-trades]].

**Haunted Eyes (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a dirge bard gains a +4 bonus on defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|energy drain]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Death Attacks|death effects]], and necromantic effects. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-well-versed|well-versed]].

**Secrets of the Grave (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a dirge bard gains a bonus equal to half his bard level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks made to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|identify undead creatures]] and their abilities. A dirge bard may use mind-affecting spells to affect undead as if they were living creatures, even if they are mindless (though spells that affect only humanoids do not affect them, even if they were humanoids in life). In addition, he may add one necromancy spell from the spell list of any arcane spellcasting class to his list of spells known at 2nd level and every four levels thereafter. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-versatile-performance|versatile performance]].

**Haunting Refrain (Su)**: At 5th level, a dirge bard is able to stir primal terrors in the hearts of listeners. He can use a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (keyboard) or Performance (percussion) check in place of an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to demoralize an opponent, with a bonus equal to half his bard level. In addition, any [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] effect he creates gains a +2 bonus on its attacks, and this bonus increases by +1 every 5 levels beyond 5th. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-lore-master|lore master]].
