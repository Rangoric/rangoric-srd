# Forgepriest

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-archetypes|warpriest]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Armorers of exquisite skill, forgepriests take inspiration from their deity to produce the most perfect weapons and armor they can, the better to equip the armies of the faithful.

**Blessings**: A forgepriest selects only one [[rangoric-srd/class-options/blessings/Blessings|blessing]]. This alters the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-blessings|blessings]] class feature.

**Smith's Spells**: A forgepriest adds the following spells to his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-spells|spell list]]: 1st—_jury rig_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heat Metal|heat metal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shatter|shatter]]_; 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Quench|quench]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Versatile Weapon|versatile weapon]]_; 4th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wreath of Blades|wreath of blades]]_; 5th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fabricate|fabricate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Creation|major creation]]_; 6th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Sword|mage's sword]]_.

**Forge Mastery (Ex)**: At 2nd level, the forgepriest adds a bonus equal to half his level to all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] checks to make metal items, armor, and weapons. (This benefit is gained from the altered version of blessings, above.)

**Bonus Feats**: A forgepriest can select [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Item Creation|item creation feats]] in addition to combat feats when he gains a bonus feat. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-bonus-feats|bonus feats]].

**Craft Magic Arms and Armor**: At 3rd level, a forgepriest gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]] as a bonus feat. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-bonus-feats|bonus feat]] gained at 3rd level.

**Creator's Bond (Su)**: At 4th level, when a forgepriest uses his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-sacred-weapon|sacred weapon]] ability with an item he created personally, he can expend two uses of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-fervor|fervor]] ability to increase the bonus granted by 1. Once the forgepriest has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-sacred-armor|sacred armor]] ability, he can also use this ability in conjunction with that. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-channel-energy|channel energy]].

**Heat of the Forge (Su)**: At 6th level, a forgepriest gains fire [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 5. At 13th level, this resistance increases to 10. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-bonus-feats|bonus feat]] gained at 6th level.
