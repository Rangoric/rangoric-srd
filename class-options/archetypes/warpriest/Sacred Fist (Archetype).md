# Sacred Fist

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-archetypes|warpriest]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Unlike many warpriests, sacred fists leave behind armor and shield and instead rely on their fists and whatever protection their deity bestows on them.

**Class Skills**: The sacred fist's class skills are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] (Dex), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] (Str), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] (Dex), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] (Wis), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] (Int). These skills replace the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#Class Skills|warpriest's class skills]].

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: Sacred fists are proficient with the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Club|club]], crossbow ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Light Crossbow|light]] or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Heavy Crossbow|heavy]]), [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dagger|dagger]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Handaxe|handaxe]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Javelin|javelin]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Kama|kama]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Nunchaku|nunchaku]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Quarterstaff|quarterstaff]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Sai|sai]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Shortspear|shortspear]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Short Sword|short sword]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Shuriken|shuriken]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Siangham|siangham]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Sling|sling]], and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Spear|spear]]. Sacred fists are not proficient with any armor or shields. This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-weapon-and-armor-proficiencies|warpriest's weapon and armor proficiency]].

**AC Bonus (Su)**: A deity protects her sacred fist as long as he is unarmored and unencumbered. A sacred fist adds his Wisdom modifier (minimum 0) to his AC and his [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]]. In addition, a sacred fist gains a +1 deflection bonus to AC and CMD at 4th level. This bonus increases by 1 for every 4 levels thereafter (to a maximum of +5 at 20th level).

These bonuses to AC apply even against touch attacks or when the sacred fist is flat-footed. He loses these bonuses when he is immobilized or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], when he wears any armor, when he carries a shield, or when he carries a medium or heavy load.

**Flurry of Blows (Ex)**: At 1st level, a sacred fist can make a flurry of blows attack as a full-attack action. This ability works like the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-flurry-of-blows|monk ability of the same name]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-sacred-weapon|sacred weapon]].

**Unarmed Strike**: At 1st level, a sacred fist gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]] as a bonus feat. He uses his warpriest levels as monk levels for determining the amount of damage dealt with an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|unarmed strike]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-focus-weapon|focus weapon]].

**Blessed Fortitude (Su)**: At 3rd level, a sacred fist can avoid even magical and unusual attacks with help from his deity. If he succeeds at a Fortitude saving throw against an attack that has a reduced effect on a successful save, he instead avoids the effect entirely. A helpless sacred fist does not gain the benefit of the blessed fortitude ability. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-bonus-feats|bonus feat]] gained at 3rd level.

**Bonus Style Feat**: At 6th level, the sacred fist gains a style feat as a bonus feat. The sacred fist must meet the style feat's prerequisites. He uses his warpriest levels as monk levels for the purposes of meeting the feat's prerequisites. At 12th and 18th levels, a sacred fist gains either another style feat or a feat that requires a style feat as a prerequisite. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-bonus-feats|bonus feats]] gained at 6th, 12th, and 18th levels.

**Ki Pool (Su)**: At 7th level, the sacred fist gains a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]]. This functions as the monk class feature, using the sacred fist's level – 3 as his monk level when determining the number of points in his pool and bonuses granted to his unarmed strike. Additionally, the sacred fist can as a swift action spend 1 point from his ki pool to grant himself a +1 insight bonus to his AC for 1 minute. (This is in addition to the normal ki ability to gain a dodge bonus to AC.) This insight bonus increases by 1 for every 3 levels above 7th (to a maximum of +5 at 19th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-sacred-armor|sacred armor]].

**Miraculous Fortitude (Su)**: At 9th level, the sacred fist's blessed fortitude ability improves. He still takes no damage or negative effect when he succeeds at a Fortitude save, but now when he fails a Fortitude saving throw against a spell or effect that deals damage (including ability damage and drain), he takes only half the amount of damage. A helpless sacred fist does not gain the benefit of miraculous fortitude. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-bonus-feats|bonus feat]] gained at 9th level.
