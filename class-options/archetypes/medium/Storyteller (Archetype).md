# Storyteller

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-archetypes|medium]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A storyteller fills his life with epic tales, at the cost of some of his spiritual power.

**Diminished Spirits (Ex)**: A storyteller does not receive his spirit's seance boon. He can't choose to channel a weaker spirit to reduce his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-influence|influence]] from spirit surge. Because of his obsession with legends, he begins with 2 points of influence after a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-seance|seance]] rather than 1. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit|spirit]].

**Knowledge of Tales (Ex)**: A storyteller gains a +1 bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] skill checks with Knowledge skills in which he's trained. This bonus increases by 1 for every 4 medium levels that he possesses. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit-bonus|spirit bonus]].

**Versatile Surge (Su)**: A storyteller's spirit surge can be used after the storyteller fails any ability check or skill check, or after an attack hits one of his defenses. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit-surge|spirit surge]].

**Storyteller's Performance (Su)**: At 2nd level, a storyteller gains the ability to recall snippets of tales that are relevant to the current situation. This ability is similar in all respects to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bardic performance]] as used by a bard of the storyteller's medium level (including interactions with feats, spells, and other abilities), and uses [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] as the storyteller's performance skill. However, a storyteller gains only the following types of bardic performance: inspire courage (2nd level), inspire competence (3rd level), inspire greatness (9th level), and inspire heroics (15th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-shared-seance|shared seance]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-taboo|taboo]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-haunt-channeler|haunt channeler]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-propitiation|propitiation]], and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-trance-of-three|trance of three]].

**Learn the Story (Sp)**: At 7th level, a storyteller becomes a master at learning a place's tales. He can perform for a full minute, spending 10 rounds of storyteller's performance, to gain the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Retrocognition|retrocognition]]_. At 10th level, he can spend 1d4 × 10 minutes and 10 rounds of storyteller's performance to gain the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]_. The subject must be at hand for the legend lore to function. At 19th level, the storyteller can spend a standard action and 1 round of storyteller's performance to gain the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vision|vision]]_ on any subject. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-connection-channel|connection channel]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit-mastery|spirit mastery]].

**Living Story (Sp)**: At 20th level, a storyteller gains the ability to sweep others away into a story of his own making. He must perform for 1 full round, telling a tale to set the snare for his living story and spending 1 round of his storyteller's performance. At the end of that round, he can affect up to 30 Hit Dice of listeners within range with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Microcosm (Spell)|microcosm]]_. Unlike with a normal microcosm, this is also a sonic and language-dependent ability, and a creature with 16 or more Hit Dice that the effect misses is immune to the storyteller's living story ability for 24 hours. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-astral-beacon|astral beacon]].
