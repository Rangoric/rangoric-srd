# Flying Blade

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-archetypes|swashbuckler]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While most swashbucklers prefer their battles up close, others prefer dealing death from a distance.

**Panache**: Unlike other swashbucklers, a flying blade regains panache only when she confirms a critical hit or makes a killing blow with a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dagger|dagger]] or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Starknife|starknife]]. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]].

**Deeds**: A flying blade gains the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|deeds]], each of which replaces an existing deed.

**Subtle Throw (Ex)**: At 1st level, a flying blade can spend 1 panache point as part of a ranged attack with a dagger or starknife to make it without provoking [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]]. At 6th level, as a swift action she can spend 1 panache point to make all of her ranged attacks with daggers or starknives without provoking attacks of opportunity until the start of her next turn. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Dodging Panache (Deed)|dodging panache]].

**Disrupting Counter (Ex)**: At 3rd level, when an opponent makes a melee attack against her, she can spend 1 panache point to make an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] against the attacking foe. This attack of opportunity can be made with either a dagger or a starknife. If the attack hits, the opponent takes a –4 penalty on all attack rolls until the end of its turn. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Kip-Up (Deed)|kip-up]].

**Precise Throw (Ex)**: At 3rd level, as long as she has at least 1 panache point, a flying blade can use her [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Precise Strike (Deed)|precise strike]] with a thrown dagger or starknife as long as the target is within 60 feet of her, and she increases the range increment of these weapons by 5 feet. She can spend 1 panache point when she throws a dagger or a starknife to ignore all range increment penalties with that ranged attack. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Menacing Swordplay (Deed)|menacing swordplay]].

**Targeted Throw (Ex)**: At 7th level, a flying blade can target individual body parts. This deed functions as the swashbuckler's [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Targeted Strike (Deed)|targeted strike]] deed, but the flying blade can also use this deed when making ranged attacks with either a dagger or a starknife as long as the target is within 60 feet of the flying blade. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Targeted Strike (Deed)|targeted strike]].

**Bleeding Wound (Ex)**: At 11th level, a flying blade can deal [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage as part of an attack. This deed functions as the swashbuckler's [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Bleeding Wound (Deed)|bleeding wound]] deed, but the flying blade can also use this deed when making ranged attacks with either a dagger or a starknife as long as the target is within 60 feet of the flying blade. This deed alters [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Bleeding Wound (Deed)|bleeding wound]].

**Perfect Throw (Ex)**: At 15th level, a flying blade can pool all of her attack potential into a single attack. This deed functions as the swashbuckler's [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Perfect Thrust (Deed)|perfect thrust]] deed, but the flying blade must use this deed when making ranged attacks with either a dagger or a starknife, and she can use this deed only on targets within 60 feet of her. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Perfect Thrust (Deed)|perfect thrust]].

**Flying Blade Training (Ex)**: At 5th level, a flying blade gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls when using daggers or starknives in combat. When a flying blade wields a dagger or starknife, she gains the benefit of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]] feat with those weapons. Additionally, a flying blade increases the range increment of a thrown dagger or starknife by 5 feet. The increase of range increment stacks with that of precise throw.

Every 4 levels thereafter, the bonus on attack and damage rolls increases by 1, and the range increment increases by 5 feet. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-weapon-training|swashbuckler weapon training]].

**Flying Blade Mastery (Ex)**: At 20th level, when an attack that a flying blade makes with a dagger or starknife threatens a critical hit, that critical hit is automatically confirmed. Furthermore, the critical modifiers of daggers and starknives increase by 1 (×2 becomes ×3, and so on). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-weapon-mastery|swashbuckler weapon mastery]].
