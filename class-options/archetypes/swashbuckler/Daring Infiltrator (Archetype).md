# Daring Infiltrator

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-archetypes|swashbuckler]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Not known for their flashy entrances or for standing out in a crowd, a daring infiltrator uses stealth, disguise, and ruthless guile to pursue her goals. Some of these swashbucklers work to undermine evil organizations, while others are master thieves or mysterious assassins.

**Class Skills**: A daring infiltrator adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] (Int) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] (Dex) to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#Class Skills|class skills]] and removes [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (Cha) from it. This alters the swashbuckler's class skill list.

**Bonus Feats**: In addition to combat feats, a daring infiltrator's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-bonus-feats|bonus feats]] at 2nd, 10th, and 18th level can come from the following list: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Antagonize|Antagonize]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cosmopolitan|Cosmopolitan]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disarming Threat Deed|Disarming Threat]], Prodigy, and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]]. This alters the bonus feat class ability.

**Quick-Tongued (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a daring infiltrator gains a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks. This bonus increases by 1 for every 4 levels beyond 2nd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-charmed-life|charmed life]].

**Deeds**: A daring infiltrator gains the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|deeds]], each of which replaces an existing deed.

**Clandestine Expertise (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a daring infiltrator with at least 1 panache point gains a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. Also, as long as she has at least 1 panache point, when she successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aids another]] with an Imitation or Stealth check, she grants the subject a +4 bonus on the skill check instead of the normal +2. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Swashbuckler Initiative (Deed)|swashbuckler initiative]].

**Silence Is Golden (Ex)**: At 3rd level, when a daring infiltrator with at least 1 panache point succeeds at a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver check, the target is rendered mute for 1 round. For every 5 by which the result of the combat maneuver check exceeds the opponent's [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]], the target remains mute for an additional round. A mute creature cannot speak, use language-dependent effects or verbal components, or use command words. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Menacing Swordplay (Deed)|menacing swordplay]].

**Authoritative Bluff (Ex)**: At 11th level, a daring infiltrator can spend 1 panache point to reroll a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check after the roll is made but before the results are revealed. She must take the result of the second roll, even if it is lower. Additionally, a daring infiltrator with at least 1 panache point gains a +5 bonus on Deception checks to pretend to be someone's superior (socially or in the military). If she succeeds at the check, the target obeys any reasonable orders she gives as it would those of an actual superior in the situation. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Bleeding Wound (Deed)|bleeding wound]].
