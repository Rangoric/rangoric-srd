# Picaroon

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-archetypes|swashbuckler]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While some swashbucklers take pride in their ability to wear down an opponent with great skill at arms and clever positioning, there are those who use firearms to get in close and hit hard.

**Weapon Proficiency**: A picaroon gains proficiency with all simple weapons and martial weapons, as well as one-handed [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearms]]. This replaces the swashbuckler's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#Class Features|weapon proficiency]].

**Panache**: Unlike other swashbucklers, a picaroon regains panache when she confirms a critical hit or makes a killing blow with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon or a one-handed firearm. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]].

**Deeds**: The picaroon gains the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|deeds]], each of which replaces an existing deed.

**Melee Shooter (Ex)**: At 1st level, as a swift action when wielding both a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon and a one-handed firearm, the picaroon can spend 1 panache point to avoid provoking [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] with the first ranged attack made by the one-handed firearm during her turn. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Opportune Parry and Riposte (Deed)|opportune parry and riposte]].

**Quick Clear (Ex)**: At 3rd level, as a standard action the picaroon can spend 1 panache point to remove the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition from a single one-handed firearm she is currently wielding, as long as the firearm gained that condition through a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms#Misfires|misfire]]. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Kip-Up (Deed)|kip-up]].

**Gun Feint (Ex)**: At 7th level, a picaroon can use the ferocious reputation of firearms to her advantage. A picaroon with at least 1 panache point can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] instead of attacking with her firearm as part of a full attack. She can spend 1 panache point to gain a +5 bonus on this check. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Superior Feint (Deed)|superior feint]].

**Lightning Reload (Ex)**: At 11th level, once per round the picaroon can spend 1 panache point to reload a single barrel of a one-handed firearm as a swift action. If she has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Reload|Rapid Reload]] feat or is using an alchemical cartridge, she can instead reload a single barrel of the weapon as a free action each round. Using this deed doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Bleeding Wound (Deed)|bleeding wound]].

**Two-Weapon Finesse (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a picaroon gains the benefits of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Finesse|Weapon Finesse]] feat with light or one-handed piercing melee weapons. She also gains the effects of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]] feat as long as she is wielding a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon in one hand and one-handed firearm in the other hand. This ability counts as having both the Weapon Finesse and Two-Weapon Fighting feats for the purposes of meeting feat requirements. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-finesse|swashbuckler finesse]].
