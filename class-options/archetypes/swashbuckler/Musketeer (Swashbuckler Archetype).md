# Musketeer

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-archetypes|swashbuckler]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

A number of organizations and kingdoms search for warriors who are brave (or foolish) enough to wield firearms on the battlefield. The daring nature of swashbucklers often makes them ideal candidates for such service.

**Weapon Proficiency**: A musketeer gains proficiency with all simple weapons and martial weapons, as well as one-handed and two-handed [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearms]]. This ability replaces the swashbuckler's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#Class Features|weapon proficiencies]].

**Musketeer Instruction (Ex)**: At 1st level, a musketeer gains the benefits of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Finesse|Weapon Finesse]] feat with the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Rapier|rapier]] (this counts as having the Weapon Finesse feat for purposes of meeting feat prerequisites), as well as both [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Reload|Rapid Reload]] ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Musket|musket]]) and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Gunsmithing|Gunsmithing]] feats as bonus feats. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-finesse|swashbuckler finesse]].

**Deed**: The musketeer gains the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|deed]], which replaces an existing deed.

**Quick Clear (Ex)**: At 1st level, as a standard action the musketeer can spend 1 panache point to remove the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition from a single firearm he is currently wielding, as long as the firearm gained that condition through a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms#Misfires|misfire]]. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Dodging Panache (Deed)|dodging panache]].
