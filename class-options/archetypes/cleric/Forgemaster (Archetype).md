# Forgemaster

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-archetypes|cleric]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarves]]._

Forgemasters are priestly dwarves who are ritual casters and expert enchanters, able to produce their rune-graven armaments with astonishing speed.

**Artificer**: A forgemaster gains only one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|domain]], which must be the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Artifice Domain|Artifice]] domain (not including subdomains). If she worships a deity, it must grant the Artifice domain.

**Steel Spells**: A forgemaster adds the following spells to her [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List|spell list]]: 1st—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Crafter’s Curse|crafter’s curse]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Crafter’s Fortune|crafter’s fortune]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lead Blades|lead blades]]_; 2nd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Metal|chill metal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heat Metal|heat metal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shatter|shatter]]_; 3rd—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Versatile Weapon|versatile weapon]]_; 8th—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Iron Body|iron body]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Repel Metal or Stone|repel metal or stone]]_.

**Divine Smith (Su)**: Whenever a forgemaster casts a spell that targets a weapon, shield, or armor, the spell takes effect at +1 caster level. If the spell has one or more [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#Metamagic Feats|metamagic feats]] applied, she reduces the total level adjustment to the spell by 1 (minimum 0).

**Runeforger (Su)**: A forgemaster may inscribe mystical runes upon a suit of armor, shield, or weapon as a full-round action, using this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + her Intelligence modifier. These runes last 1 round per cleric level, but inscribing the same rune twice on an item increases this duration to 1 minute per level, three times to 10 minutes per level, and four times to 1 hour per level. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Erase|Erase]]_ affects runes as magical writing. A forgemaster learns forgemaster's blessing at 1st level and may learn one additional rune at 2nd level and every 2 levels thereafter. Only one type of rune marked with an asterisk (\*) may be placed on an item at any given time. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-channel-energy|channel energy]].

_Ancient Splendor_: The inscribed weapon, armor, or shield grants a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks (+4 when interacting with dwarves).

_Bloodthirst_\*: The inscribed piercing or slashing weapon functions as if it had the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Wounding|wounding]]_ special ability, even if nonmagical. The forgemaster must be at least 4th level before learning this rune.

_Deathstrike_: The inscribed weapon stores a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Knell|death knell]]_ spell that triggers immediately if a blow from the weapon reduces a target to negative hit points. This expends all deathstrike runes on the weapon. The forgemaster must be at least 4th level before learning this rune.

_Durability_: The inscribed item's hardness increases by an amount equal to the forgemaster's Wisdom modifier, and its hit points increase by an amount equal to twice her level.

_Featherlight_: The inscribed item's weight is halved; a metal item's weight is reduced to 1/4 normal. If inscribed on a suit of armor, its [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Armor#Armor Qualities|armor check penalty]] for [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks is halved.

_Forgemaster's Blessing_: The inscribed nonmagical item functions as a masterwork item ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Masterwork Weapons|weapon]] or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Armor#Masterwork Armor|armor]]).

_Ghostglyph_\*: The inscribed weapon, shield, or armor gains the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]]_ special ability. The forgemaster must be at least 4th level before learning this rune.

_Glowglyph_: The inscribed item sheds light as a torch. As a standard action, the bearer can command the rune to erupt in a burst of light as a shield with the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Blinding|blinding]]_ special ability with a burst radius of 5 feet per glowglyph rune inscribed on the item. This expends all glowglyph runes on the item.

_Invulnerability_\*: The inscribed armor grants its wearer [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]]/magic equal to 1/2 her cleric level. The forgemaster must be at least 8th level before learning this rune.

_Powerstrike_\*: The inscribed weapon's critical threat range doubles. This does not stack with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]_, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]], or similar effects. The forgemaster must be at least 6th level before learning this rune.

_Return_\*: The inscribed weapon gains the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Returning|returning]]_ weapon special ability. The forgemaster must be at least 4th level before learning this rune.

_Spellguard_: The inscribed item gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] equal to 11 + her cleric level. This applies only to effects targeting the item itself.

_Spellglyph_\*: The inscribed weapon gains the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Spell Storing|spell storing]]_ special ability. The forgemaster must be at least 4th level before learning this rune.

_Thief-Curse_: The forgemaster designates one creature as the rightful owner of an item. Any other creature that intentionally grasps the item is cursed (as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]_) for the duration of the rune. The forgemaster must be at least 6th level before learning this rune.

_Tracer_: For as long as the rune lasts, the cleric may sense its location at will as a standard action, as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Object|locate object]]_.

**Craft Magic Arms and Armor**: The forgemaster gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|this]] as a bonus feat at 3rd level.

**Master Smith (Ex)**: At 5th level, a forgemaster can craft mundane metal items quickly, using half their gp value to determine progress, and can craft magical metal items in half the normal amount of time.
