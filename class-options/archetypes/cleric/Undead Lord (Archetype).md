# Undead Lord

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-archetypes|cleric]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

An undead lord is a cleric focused on using necromancy to control undead. Her flock is the walking dead and her choir the keening spirits of the damned. This unliving congregation is the manifestation of her unceasing love affair with death.

A cleric cannot take the undead lord archetype unless her deity's portfolio includes the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Death Domain|Death]] domain or a similar domain that promotes undeath. An undead lord has the following class features.

**Death Magic**: An undead lord must select the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Death Domain|Death]] domain (and the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Undead Subdomain|Undead]] subdomain, if available in the campaign). She does not gain a second domain. In all other respects, this works like and replaces the standard cleric's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|domain]] ability.

**Corpse Companion (Su)**: With a ritual requiring 8 hours, an undead lord can animate a single [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeletons/Skeleton|skeleton]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/zombies/Zombie|zombie]] whose Hit Dice do not exceed her cleric level. This corpse companion automatically follows her commands and does not need to be controlled by her. She cannot have more than one corpse companion at a time. It does not count against the number of Hit Dice of undead controlled by other methods. She can use this ability to create a variant skeleton such as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeletons/Alternate Skeleton#Bloody Skeleton|bloody]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeletons/Alternate Skeleton#Burning Skeleton|burning skeleton]], but its Hit Dice cannot exceed half her cleric level. She can dismiss her companion as a standard action, which destroys it.

**Bonus Feats**: All undead lords gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Command Undead (Feat)|Command Undead]] as a bonus feat. In addition, at 10th level, she may select one of the following as a bonus feat: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Channel Smite|Channel Smite]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Extra Channel|Extra Channel]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Channel|Improved Channel]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Channel|Quick Channel]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skeleton Summoner|Skeleton Summoner]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Undead Master|Undead Master]].

**Unlife Healer (Su)**: At 8th level, the undead lord's spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural abilities used to heal undead heal an extra 50% damage. At 16th level, these effects automatically heal the maximum possible damage for the effect + the extra 50%. This does not stack with abilities or feats such as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Empower Spell|Empower Spell]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Maximize Spell|Maximize Spell]].
