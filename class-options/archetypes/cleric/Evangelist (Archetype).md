# Evangelist

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-archetypes|cleric]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The evangelist is the voice of her religion in the world. Where others nurture the faith among believers, an evangelist proclaims the coming glory of her deific patron and issues the clarion call to all around to heed the truth, or obey the call to war and crusade against the enemies of the church.

**Single-Minded**: An evangelist focuses her skills and learning on proclamation rather than the fine details of the church's deeper mysteries or martial training. Thus, she may select only one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|domain]] and does not gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Armor Proficiency (Medium)|Medium Armor Proficiency]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Proficiency|Shield Proficiency]].

**Public Speaker**: An evangelist gains [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#Class Skills|class skill]]. In addition, she is trained to project her voice with great skill and effect; the DC to hear her speak in difficult conditions is reduced by an amount equal to her class level plus her Charisma modifier (minimum 0).

**Sermonic Performance**: An evangelist gains the ability to deliver a select number of supernatural and spell-like performances through the force and power of her divinely inspired preaching and exhortation. This ability is similar in all respects to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] as used by a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] of the same level (including interactions with feats, spells, and prestige classes), using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance (oratory)]] as the evangelist's performance skill. However, an evangelist gains only the following types of bardic performance: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^countersong|countersong]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-fascinate|fascinate]], and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-courage|inspire courage]] at 1st level; [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-greatness|inspire greatness]] at 9th level; and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-heroics|inspire heroics]] at 15th level. Sermonic performance replaces the 1st-, 9th-, and 15th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-channel-energy|channel energy]] abilities. This caps the cleric's channel energy damage at 7d6 points.

**Spontaneous Casting**: An evangelist does not gain the ability to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-spontaneous-casting|spontaneously cast cure or inflict spells]] by sacrificing prepared spells. However, an evangelist can spontaneously cast the following spells by sacrificing a prepared spell of the noted level or above:
