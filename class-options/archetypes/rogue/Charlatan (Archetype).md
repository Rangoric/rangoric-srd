# Charlatan

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A charlatan is a master of lies and deception. Whether creating simple cons or elaborately woven hoaxes capable of swaying the masses and those in positions of power, a charlatan is often a purveyor of snake-oil, forgeries, and rumormongering.

**Natural Born Liar (Ex)**: At 1st level, when a charlatan successfully deceives a creature with a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], that creature takes a –2 penalty on the charlatan's Deception checks for the next 24 hours. This ability does not stack with itself. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Grand Hoax (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the charlatan begins to master the art of the grand hoax and learns to create deceptions designed to bestow harm upon her foes. She gains the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rumormonger (Rogue Talent)|rumormonger]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]], even though she is not yet 10th level. This ability takes the place of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the charlatan archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Black Market Connections (Rogue Talent)|black market connections]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Coax Information (Rogue Talent)|coax information]], convincing lie, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Honeyed Words (Rogue Talent)|honeyed words]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Underhanded (Rogue Talent)|underhanded]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the charlatan archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hard to Fool (Rogue Talent)|hard to fool]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Skill Mastery (Rogue Talent)|skill mastery]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Unwitting Ally (Rogue Talent)|unwitting ally]].
