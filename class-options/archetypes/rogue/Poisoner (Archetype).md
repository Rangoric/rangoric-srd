# Poisoner

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some consider poisoning an evil act. The poisoner knows poison is just a tool toward an end, and is no different than any other weapon. Some poisoners see themselves as great equalizers, as they are able to craft weapons that the weakest of creatures can wield to devastating effect, but most have no lofty delusions about their work.

**Poison Use (Ex)**: At 1st level, a poisoner is trained in the use of poison and cannot accidentally poison herself when [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|applying poison]] to a blade. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Master Poisoner (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a poisoner can use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) to change the type of a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]]. This requires 1 hour of work with an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Alchemist's Lab|alchemist's lab]] and a Craft (alchemy) skill check with a DC equal to the poison's DC. If successful, the poison's type changes to contact, ingested, inhaled, or injury. If the check fails, the poison is ruined. The poisoner also receives a bonus on Craft (alchemy) skill checks when working with poison equal to 1/2 her rogue level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the poisoner archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Distracting Attack (Rogue Talent)|distracting attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Lasting Poison (Rogue Talent)|lasting poison]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Swift Poison (Rogue Talent)|swift poison]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Weapon Training (Rogue Talent)|weapon training]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the poisoner archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Deadly Cocktail (Rogue Talent)|deadly cocktail]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hunter's Surprise (Rogue Talent)|hunter's surprise]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Opportunist (Rogue Talent)|opportunist]].
