# Acrobat

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Agility and daring are both excellent rogue traits, and their confluence can create spectacular feats of acrobatics. Whether they are daring thieves, infiltrating assassins, or intrepid spies, proper training in acrobatics is a valuable boon for rogues.

**Expert Acrobat (Ex)**: At 1st level, an acrobat does not suffer any armor check penalties on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] skill checks while wearing light armor. When she is not wearing armor, she gains a +2 competency bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] skill checks (flying included). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Second Chance (Ex)**: At 3rd level, an acrobat can reroll any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] skill check or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check to climb she has just made. This reroll is made at a –5 penalty. She must take the second result, even if it is worse. An acrobat can use this ability only once on any given skill check. She can use this ability once per day at 3rd level, plus one additional time per day for every 3 levels beyond 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the acrobat archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Expert Leaper (Rogue Talent)|expert leaper]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Ledge Walker (Rogue Talent)|ledge walker]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Nimble Climber (Rogue Talent)|nimble climber]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Peerless Maneuver (Rogue Talent)|peerless maneuver]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rogue Crawl (Rogue Talent)|rogue crawl]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Stand Up (Rogue Talent)|stand up]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the acrobat archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Defensive Roll (Rogue Talent)|defensive roll]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Getaway (Rogue Talent)|fast getaway]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Tumble (Rogue Talent)|fast tumble]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Improved Evasion (Rogue Talent)|improved evasion]].
