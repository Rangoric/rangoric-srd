# Deadly Courtesan

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Vishkanya|vishkanyas]]._

Skilled at manipulation and diversion, the deadly courtesan builds up those around her and periodically takes them down. She can be a spy, entertainer, assassin, bodyguard, or just an intimate to someone who needs it most. A deadly courtesan has the following class features.

**Bardic Performance (Su)**: At 2nd level, a deadly courtesan gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-music|bardic performance]] ability and the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-fascinate|fascinate]] bardic performance. Her fascinate DC is 10 + 1/2 her rogue level + her Charisma modifier. She can use this bardic performance for a number of rounds per day equal to 1 + her Charisma modifier. At each level after 2nd, she can use bardic performance for 1 additional round per day. If the courtesan also has bard levels, she may use these rounds for either class's fascinate bardic performance, and her bard and rogue levels stack for determining her fascinate DC. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] gained at 2nd level.

**Inspire Competence (Su)**: At 3rd level, a deadly courtesan can use her bardic performance to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^inspire-competence|inspire competence]]. The bonus begins at +2 and increases by +1 for every six levels the courtesan has attained beyond 3rd (+3 at 9th and +4 at 15th). This ability replaces all levels of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Performance Strike (Sp)**: At 8th level, as a swift action, a deadly courtesan may expend rounds of bardic performance to gain a morale bonus on one attack roll. The amount of the bonus is equal to the number of bardic performance rounds expended (maximum bonus equal to 1/2 her deadly courtesan level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the deadly courtesan archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Finesse Rogue (Rogue Talent)|finesse rogue]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Stand Up (Rogue Talent)|stand up]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Charmer (Rogue Talent)|charmer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Coax Information (Rogue Talent)|coax information]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Fingers (Rogue Talent)|fast fingers]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Honeyed Words (Rogue Talent)|honeyed words]], convincing lie, and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Deft Palm (Rogue Talent)|deft palm]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the deadly courtesan archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slippery Mind (Rogue Talent)|slippery mind]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Master of Disguise (Rogue Talent)|master of disguise]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rumormonger (Rogue Talent)|rumormonger]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Unwitting Ally (Rogue Talent)|unwitting ally]].
