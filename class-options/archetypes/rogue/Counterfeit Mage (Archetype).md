# Counterfeit Mage

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Charlatans and stage magicians use sleight of hand to fake magic. A counterfeit mage goes a step further, parroting the motions and phrases used by arcane casters to activate wands or other magical accoutrements. While counterfeit mages rarely fool a real wizard, their command of the arcane is enough to convince most lay people.

**Magical Expertise (Ex)**: At 1st level, a counterfeit mage adds 1/2 his level to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks to disarm magical traps, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks to find magical traps, and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] checks to activate scrolls and wands. A counterfeit mage can use Investigation to find magic traps and Engineering to disarm them. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Signature Wand (Ex)**: At 4th level, a counterfeit mage can spend 1 hour practicing with a wand to designate it as his signature wand. He can draw that wand as a free action, and can activate it without having to succeed at an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] check. He can change his signature wand once per day. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] gained at 4th level.

**Wand Adept (Ex)**: At 6th level, a counterfeit mage can use his Dexterity modifier in place of his Charisma modifier when attempting [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] checks to activate wands.

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the counterfeit mage archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Deft Palm (Rogue Talent)|deft palm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Esoteric Scholar (Rogue Talent)|esoteric scholar]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Honeyed Words (Rogue Talent)|honeyed words]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Major Magic (Rogue Talent)|major magic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Minor Magic (Rogue Talent)|minor magic]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Trap Spotter (Rogue Talent)|trap spotter]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the counterfeit mage archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Dispelling Attack (Rogue Talent)|dispelling attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Familiar (Rogue Talent)|familiar]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slippery Mind (Rogue Talent)|slippery mind]].
