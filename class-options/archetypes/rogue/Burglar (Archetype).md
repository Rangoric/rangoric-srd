# Burglar

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Adept at infiltration, trap removal, and lock picking, burglars prey on the homes of the wealthy and forgotten tombs alike. Such riches are guarded with vicious traps and hazards, but the devious mind of the burglar allows her to find such dangers and avoid them.

**Careful Disarm (Ex)**: At 4th level, whenever a burglar attempts to disarm a trap using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], she does not spring the trap unless she fails by 10 or more. If she does set off a trap she was attempting to disarm, she adds double her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|trap sense bonus]] to avoid the trap. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]].

**Distraction (Ex)**: At 8th level, whenever a burglar is detected while using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]], she can immediately attempt a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] skill check opposed by the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] skill of the creature that spotted her. If this check succeeds, the target assumes that the noise was something innocent and disregards the detection. This only functions if the creature cannot see the rogue. This ability can only be used once during a given Stealth attempt. If the same creature detects the rogue's presence again, the ability has no effect. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].
