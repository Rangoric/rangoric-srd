# Sniper

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some say that the sniper is the worst kind of assassin: a killer who waits silently in the shadows and then strikes from a distance without remorse. Snipers, of course, understand that such protestations about “cowardice” and “honor” regarding their profession are in fact merely the bleatings of sheep fearing the slaughter and pay them no heed. Most snipers take pride in their formidable abilities, which allow them to take life quickly, quietly, and efficiently, then disappear into their surroundings without a trace.

**Accuracy (Ex)**: At 1st level, a sniper halves all range increment penalties when making ranged attacks with a bow or crossbow. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Deadly Range (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a sniper increases the range at which she can apply her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage by 10 feet. This range increases by 10 feet for every 3 levels after 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the sniper archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Camouflage (Rogue Talent)|camouflage]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Snap Shot (Rogue Talent)|snap shot]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Sniper's Eye (Rogue Talent)|sniper's eye]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Weapon Training (Rogue Talent)|weapon training]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the sniper archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Defensive Roll (Rogue Talent)|defensive roll]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Master of Disguise (Rogue Talent)|master of disguise]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Stealthy Sniper (Rogue Talent)|stealthy sniper]].
