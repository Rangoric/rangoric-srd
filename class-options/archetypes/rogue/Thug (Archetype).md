# Thug

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some criminals steal with finesse, their victims only discovering the crime when the rogue is long gone and the coin already spent. A thug, on the other hand, cares nothing for finesse. Through both threat and violence, the thug gets what she wants by the promise of force, and has no problem making good on that promise as needed.

**Frightening (Ex)**: Whenever a thug successfully uses [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] a creature, the duration of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] condition is increased by 1 round. In addition, if the target is shaken for 4 or more rounds, the thug can instead decide to make the target [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 round. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Brutal Beating (Ex)**: At 3rd level, whenever a thug deals [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage, she can choose to forgo 1d6 points of sneak attack damage to make the target [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 her rogue level. This ability does not stack with itself—only the most recent duration applies. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the thug archetype: offensive defense, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Powerful Sneak (Rogue Talent)|powerful sneak]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Strong Impression (Rogue Talent)|strong impression]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Weapon Training (Rogue Talent)|weapon training]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the thug archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Crippling Strike (Rogue Talent)|crippling strike]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Deadly Sneak (Rogue Talent)|deadly sneak]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Knock-Out Blow (Rogue Talent)|knock-out blow]].
