# Eldritch Raider

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Gillman|gillmen]]._

An eldritch raider is a rogue who seeks to unravel the mysteries of the destruction of the gillmen's homeland. They explore old ruins that date back to the days of the old human empire and track down relics and lore from its glory days. An eldritch raider has the following class features.

**Class Skills**: An eldritch raider adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#Class Skills|class skills]] and removes [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] from her list of class skills.

**Skill Ranks per Level**: 6 + Int modifier.

**Detect Magic (Sp)**: At 2nd level, an eldritch raider gains the ability to use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_ at will. The eldritch raider treats her rogue level as her caster level for this ability. This ability counts as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Minor Magic (Rogue Talent)|minor magic]] rogue talent for purposes of qualifying for other rogue talents. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] rogues gained at 2nd level.

**Eldritch Intuition (Ex)**: At 3rd level, an eldritch raider gains an intuitive sense that allows her to more easily activate sorcerer and wizard spell completion and spell trigger items. She gains a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] checks for this purpose. This bonus increases to +2 at 6th level, +3 at 9th level, +4 at 12th level, +5 at 15th level, and +6 at 18th level. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]] class feature.

**New Talents**: An eldritch raider has access to the following new advanced talents when selecting [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|rogue advanced talents]].

_Major Eldritch Magic (Sp)_: The eldritch raider gains the ability to cast a 3rd-level spell from the sorcerer/wizard spell list two times per day as a spell-like ability. The caster level for this ability is equal to her rogue level. The save DC for this spell is 13 + her Intelligence modifier. The eldritch raider must have an Intelligence of at least 13 to select this talent. The eldritch raider must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Major Magic (Rogue Talent)|major magic]], minor eldritch magic, and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Minor Magic (Rogue Talent)|minor magic]] rogue talents before choosing this talent.

_Minor Eldritch Magic (Sp)_: The eldritch raider gains the ability to cast a 2nd-level spell from the sorcerer/wizard spell list two times per day as a spell-like ability. The caster level for this ability is equal to her rogue level. The save DC for this spell is 12 + her Intelligence modifier. The eldritch raider must have an Intelligence of at least 12 to select this talent. The eldritch raider must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Major Magic (Rogue Talent)|major magic]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Minor Magic (Rogue Talent)|minor magic]] rogue talents before choosing this talent.

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the eldritch raider archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Major Magic (Rogue Talent)|major magic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Minor Magic (Rogue Talent)|minor magic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Quick Disable (Rogue Talent)|quick disable]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Trap Spotter (Rogue Talent)|trap spotter]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Picks (Rogue Talent)|fast picks]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Black Market Connections (Rogue Talent)|black market connections]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Esoteric Scholar (Rogue Talent)|esoteric scholar]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Ninja Trick (Rogue Talent)|ninja trick]] ([[rangoric-srd/class-options/ninja-tricks/Slow Metabolism (Ninja Trick)|slow metabolism]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ninja-tricks/Wall Climber (Ninja Trick)|wall climber]]).

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the eldritch raider archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Dispelling Attack (Rogue Talent)|dispelling attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Improved Evasion (Rogue Talent)|improved evasion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slippery Mind (Rogue Talent)|slippery mind]], major eldritch magic, minor eldritch magic, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Thoughtful Reexamining (Rogue Talent)|thoughtful reexamining]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Familiar (Rogue Talent)|familiar]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hard to Fool (Rogue Talent)|hard to fool]].
