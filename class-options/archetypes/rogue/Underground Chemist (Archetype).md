# Underground Chemist

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Underground chemists are part of the fetid underbelly of the alchemical world. While underground chemists can't hold a candle to dedicated alchemists, their use of alchemical substances and potions makes them tricky and dangerous.

**Chemical Weapons (Ex)**: At 2nd level, an underground chemist is able to retrieve an alchemical item as if drawing a weapon. She adds her Intelligence modifier to damage dealt with [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapons]], including any splash damage. She adds 1/2 her level to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) checks. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|evasion]].

**Precise Splash Weapons (Ex)**: At 4th level, an underground chemist can deal [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage with [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapons]]. The attack must be her first attack in that round, must qualify to be a sneak attack (such as an attack against a flat-footed target), and must be directed at a creature rather than a square. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] gained at 4th level.

**Discovery (Su)**: At 10th level, an underground chemist can select one of the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] in place of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]]: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Concentrate Poison (Discovery)|concentrate poison]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Dilution (Discovery)|dilution]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Enhance Potion (Discovery)|enhance potion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Extend Potion (Discovery)|extend potion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Mummification (Discovery)|mummification]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Nauseating Flesh (Discovery)|nauseating flesh]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Poison Conversion (Discovery)|poison conversion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Preserve Organs (Discovery)|preserve organs]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Spontaneous Healing (Discovery)|spontaneous healing]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Sticky Poison (Discovery)|sticky poison]]. She uses her rogue level as her effective alchemist level for determining the effects of her discoveries and whether she is able to select one. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talents]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the underground chemist archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Black Market Connections (Rogue Talent)|black market connections]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Fingers (Rogue Talent)|fast fingers]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Lasting Poison (Rogue Talent)|lasting poison]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Sniper's Eye (Rogue Talent)|sniper's eye]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Swift Poison (Rogue Talent)|swift poison]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the underground chemist archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Deadly Cocktail (Rogue Talent)|deadly cocktail]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Thoughtful Reexamining (Rogue Talent)|thoughtful reexamining]].
