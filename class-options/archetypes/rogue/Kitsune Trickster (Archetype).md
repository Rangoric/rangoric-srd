# Kitsune Trickster

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Kitsune|kitsune]]._

The kitsune trickster combines her sharpened wit with minor arcane powers of charm and persuasion. She uses her talents to spin convincing lies, riddles, and stories. A trickster has the following class features.

**Kitsune's Guile (Ex)**: At 1st level, a trickster relies on her intellect as much as her personality. She adds her Intelligence modifier on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Kitsune's Charm (Sp)**: At 3rd level, a kitsune trickster can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]_ once per day as a spell-like ability (caster level equal to her rogue level – 2). At 6th level, and every three levels thereafter, the kitsune trickster gains an additional daily use of this ability. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the kitsune trickster archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Major Magic (Rogue Talent)|major magic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Minor Magic (Rogue Talent)|minor magic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/False Friend (Rogue Talent)|false friend]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Obfuscate Story (Rogue Talent)|obfuscate story]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Steal the Story (Rogue Talent)|steal the story]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Charmer (Rogue Talent)|charmer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Coax Information (Rogue Talent)|coax information]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Honeyed Words (Rogue Talent)|honeyed words]], and convincing lie.

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the kitsune trickster archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Skill Mastery (Rogue Talent)|skill mastery]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slippery Mind (Rogue Talent)|slippery mind]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Master of Disguise (Rogue Talent)|master of disguise]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rumormonger (Rogue Talent)|rumormonger]].
