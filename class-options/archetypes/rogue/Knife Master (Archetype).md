# Knife Master

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The knife master is a trained killer who specializes in close-up combat and the wave and weave of knife fighting. In her hands, daggers and other similar light blades become truly deadly instruments.

**Hidden Blade**: A knife master adds 1/2 her level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made to conceal a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-groups|light blade]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Sneak Stab (Ex)**: A knife master focuses her ability to deal [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage with daggers and similar weapons to such a degree that she can deal more sneak attack damage with those weapons at the expense of sneak attacks with other weapons. When she makes a sneak attack with a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dagger|dagger]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Kerambit|kerambit]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Kukri|kukri]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Punching Dagger|punching daggers]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Starknife|starknife]], or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Swordbreaker Dagger|swordbreaker dagger]], she uses d8s to roll sneak attack damage instead of d6s. For sneak attacks with all other weapons, she uses d4s instead of d6s. This ability is identical in all other ways to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]], and supplements that ability.

**Blade Sense (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a knife master is so skilled in combat involving light blades that she gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC against attacks made against her with light blades. This bonus increases by +1 for every three levels, to a maximum of +6 at 18th level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the knife master archetype: befuddling strike, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Combat Trick (Rogue Talent)|combat trick]], offensive defense, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Underhanded (Rogue Talent)|underhanded]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Weapon Training (Rogue Talent)|weapon training]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the knife master archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Another Day (Rogue Talent)|another day]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Confounding Blades (Rogue Talent)|confounding blades]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Deadly Sneak (Rogue Talent)|deadly sneak]], entanglement of blades, and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Unwitting Ally (Rogue Talent)|unwitting ally]].
