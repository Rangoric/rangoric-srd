# Pirate

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A pirate breaks from the confines of country and king to commit her crimes upon the high seas. She holds allegiance only to her ship and its captain (if even that much is true), and lives a lawless life upon the waves, plundering ships and shorelines as suits her whim.

**Sea Legs (Ex)**: At 1st level, a pirate becomes adept at moving on ships, boats, and similar vessels. She gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus (Acrobatics)]] feat as a bonus feat. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Swinging Reposition (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a pirate incorporates a ship's masts, rigging, ropes, sails, and other such structures into her combat style. Provided she is wearing light armor or no armor, when fighting in an environment where such structures exist, the rogue incorporates them into her movement, and does not have to move in a straight line when making either a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] attack or a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] combat maneuver. Once she completes her attack or maneuver, she can reposition herself. Immediately after making the charge or bull rush, she can move 5 feet as a free action, even if the charge ends her turn. This movement does not provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]]. This ability replaces the 2nd-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]].

**Unflinching (Ex)**: Pirates are a salty and steadfast lot. At 3rd level, a pirate gains a +1 bonus on saving throws against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fear|fear]] and mind-affecting effects. This bonus increases by +1 for every three levels, to a maximum of +6 at 18th level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the pirate archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Black Market Connections (Rogue Talent)|black market connections]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Combat Trick (Rogue Talent)|combat trick]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Finesse Rogue (Rogue Talent)|finesse rogue]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Firearm Training (Rogue Talent)|firearm training]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hold Breath (Rogue Talent)|hold breath]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rope Master (Rogue Talent)|rope master]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Strong Stroke (Rogue Talent)|strong stroke]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the pirate archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Getaway Master (Rogue Talent)|getaway master]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hard to Fool (Rogue Talent)|hard to fool]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Unwitting Ally (Rogue Talent)|unwitting ally]].
