# Skulking Slayer

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orcs]].*

Pushed into a life of crime by the society around them, half-orcs gravitate toward criminal activities that suit them best. Half-orc rogues leave subtle tactics and finesse to halflings and elves, and rely on brute strength and thuggery when they go about making mischief. Skulking slayers have turned the use of raw strength and surprise into an art form. A skulking slayer has the following class features.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: The skulking slayer gains proficiency with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Greatclub|greatclubs]] and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Whip|whips]], but loses proficiency with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Rapier|rapiers]] and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Hand Crossbow|hand crossbows]].

**Class Skills**: The skulking slayer does not gain [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#Class Skills|class skills]].

**Skill Ranks per Level**: 6 + Int modifier.

**Pass for Human (Ex)**: At 1st level, when trying to conceal her half-orc heritage, a skulking slayer gains a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks equal to half her level. When using disguise to appear as a specific individual, skulking slayers ignore the normal –2 penalty to appear as another race.

**Underhanded Maneuvers (Ex)**: At 1st level, when she could normally make a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]], a slayer may instead make a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] combat maneuver with a bonus on her roll. This bonus is equal to her number of sneak attack dice for a dirty trick combat maneuver, or 1-1/2 × her number of sneak attack dice for a steal combat maneuver. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Bonus Feats**: At 2nd level, a skulking slayer can select the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Surprise Follow-Through|Surprise Follow-Through]] feat in place of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]]. At 10th level, she can select the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Surprise Follow-Through|Improved Surprise Follow-Through]] feat in place of an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talent]].

**Bold Strike (Ex)**: At 3rd level, when a skulking slayer [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charges]] and makes a sneak attack with a two-handed weapon, she rolls d8s instead of d6s for her sneak attack damage. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]] +1 and +4.

**Shifty (Ex)**: At 6th level, a skulking slayer gains a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] equal to half her level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]] +2.

**Unexpected Charge (Ex)**: At 9th level, a skulking slayer can make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check to feint as a swift action before a charge. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]] +3.

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the skulking slayer archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Combat Trick (Rogue Talent)|combat trick]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Combat Swipe (Rogue Talent)|combat swipe]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Powerful Sneak (Rogue Talent)|powerful sneak]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Terrain Mastery (Rogue Talent)|terrain mastery]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the skulking slayer archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Crippling Strike (Rogue Talent)|crippling strike]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Deadly Sneak (Rogue Talent)|deadly sneak]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Unwitting Ally (Rogue Talent)|unwitting ally]].
