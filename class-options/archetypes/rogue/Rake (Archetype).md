# Rake

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The rake is a rogue who is open about her skills and talents, often to the point of being boastful. Usually she has the protection of an important figure who finds her services useful, but sometimes her bravado is enough to keep enemies away. She is often used as a face for the group for diplomacy, gathering information, negotiations, or to gain the most lucrative contracts and quests from local authorities.

**Bravado's Blade (Ex)**: When a rake hits an opponent and deals [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage, she can forgo 1d6 points of that damage and make a free [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] the foe. For every additional 1d6 points of sneak attack damage she forgoes, she receives a +5 circumstance bonus on this check. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Rake's Smile (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a rake gains a +1 morale bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks. This bonus increases by +1 for every 3 levels beyond 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the rake archetype: befuddling strike, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Charmer (Rogue Talent)|charmer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Honeyed Words (Rogue Talent)|honeyed words]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the rake archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Another Day (Rogue Talent)|another day]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Redirect Attack (Rogue Talent)|redirect attack]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Skill Mastery (Rogue Talent)|skill mastery]].
