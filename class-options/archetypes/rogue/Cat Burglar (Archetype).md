# Cat Burglar

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|catfolk]]._

Gifted with finesse and stealth, catfolk make excellent burglars. Cat burglars are masters of breaking and entering, using their feline grace to make it seem as though no crime was ever committed in the first place. Few locks can withstand skilled cat burglars, and such nimble rogues are capable of bypassing traps without activating them and enabling associates to do the same. A cat burglar has the following class features.

**Phantom Presence (Ex)**: At 4th level, a cat burglar masters stealthy movement and leaves no trace of her passing in dungeons and cities. While in dungeon and urban environments, she leaves no trail and cannot be tracked, though she can choose to leave behind a trail if she so desires. Furthermore, she can always choose to take 10 when making a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] check. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]].

**Trap Saboteur (Su)**: At 8th level, a cat burglar becomes a master of avoiding and manipulating traps and locks. She can attempt to open a lock as a standard action and takes 1/2 the normal amount of time to disable traps (minimum 1 round). When she has bypassed a trap without disarming it, she can also choose to suppress its trigger for up to 1 minute. If she does, she can also choose to end this suppression prematurely as a free action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the cat burglar archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Stealth (Rogue Talent)|fast stealth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Quick Disable (Rogue Talent)|quick disable]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Nimble Climber (Catfolk Rogue Talent)|nimble climber]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Single-Minded Appraiser (Rogue Talent)|single-minded appraiser]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Picks (Rogue Talent)|fast picks]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Terrain Mastery (Rogue Talent)|terrain mastery]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the cat burglar archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Another Day (Rogue Talent)|another day]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Tumble (Rogue Talent)|fast tumble]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hide in Plain Sight (Rogue Talent)|hide in plain sight]].
