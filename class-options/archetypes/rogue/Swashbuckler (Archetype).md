# Swashbuckler

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-archetypes|rogue]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A paragon of mobile swordplay, the swashbuckler is a rogue who focuses almost exclusively on honing her skill at arms and perfecting daring acrobatic moves and elaborate flourishes that border on performance.

**Martial Training (Ex)**: At 1st level, the swashbuckler may select one [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Categories|martial weapon]] to add to her list of weapon proficiencies. In addition, she may take the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Combat Trick (Rogue Talent)|combat trick]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] up to two times. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]].

**Daring (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a swashbuckler gains a +1 morale bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks and to defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fear|fear]]. This bonus increases by +1 for every 3 levels beyond 3rd. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]].

**Rogue Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]] complement the swashbuckler archetype: offensive defense, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Positioning Attack (Rogue Talent)|positioning attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Powerful Sneak (Rogue Talent)|powerful sneak]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Weapon Training (Rogue Talent)|weapon training]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talents]] complement the swashbuckler archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Crippling Strike (Rogue Talent)|crippling strike]], entanglement of blades, and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Redirect Attack (Rogue Talent)|redirect attack]].
