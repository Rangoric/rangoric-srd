# Heretic

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-archetypes|inquisitor]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

While all inquisitors hunt the enemies of the faith, sometimes, either through political maneuvering by her enemies or an unyielding tenacity that breaks her faith's basic tenets, an inquisitor can find herself a heretic. Still unyielding in her cause, these heretics are accustomed to using guile and deception to hide themselves and their activities while they continue to hunt their enemies.

**Judgment (Su)**: A heretic gains the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|judgment]] in addition to the normal list of inquisitor [[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Judgments|judgments]].

**Escape (Su)**: Each time the inquisitor using this judgment hits an opponent with a melee or ranged attack, she can use a move action attempt to create a diversion to hide (see the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth skill]]).

**Lore of Escape (Ex)**: At 1st level, the heretic uses every trick she knows to escape those now pursuing her. She adds her Wisdom modifier on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] skill checks in addition to the normal ability score modifiers. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-monster-lore|monster lore]].

**Hide Tracks (Ex)**: At 1st level, a heretical inquisitor is adept at hiding her tracks. Creatures attempting to track her take a –5 penalty on rolls to find or follow her tracks.

**Ex-Inquisitors**: An inquisitor who becomes an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-ex-inquisitors|ex-inquisitor]] can, with the GM's permission, take the heretic archetype, replacing her class abilities with the appropriate archetype abilities. If the character atones or joins a different faith, she loses her heretic abilities and regains her previous inquisitor class abilities.
