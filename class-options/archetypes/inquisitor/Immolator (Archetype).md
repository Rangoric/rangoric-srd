# Immolator

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-archetypes|inquisitor]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|ifrits]]._

The immolator puts her pyromaniacal urges to work in the service of a deity. She brings burning retribution down upon the enemies of her faith, consigning their souls to the sacrificial flames. An immolator has the following class features.

**Servant of the Flame**: An immolator must worship a deity whose portfolio includes the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire]] domain. An immolator who selects the Fire domain (or one of its associated [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomains]], if available) uses her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-domain|domain]] powers at +1 caster level (this stacks with the ifrit's [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|fire affinity]] racial trait).

**Judgment (Su)**: An immolator gains the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|judgment]] instead of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Smiting (Judgment)|smiting]] judgment.

_Immolation_: The immolator channels purifying flame to consume her enemies. When dealing fire damage to an opponent, she treats the target's [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|fire resistance]] as 5 lower than normal (minimum 0). At 6th level, she treats the target's resistance as 10 lower than normal, 15 lower than normal at 11th level, and 20 lower than normal at 16th level.

**Burnt Offering (Su)**: At 5th level, as a swift action, an immolator can imbue one of her weapons with the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming|flaming]]* weapon special ability. Any creature slain by this weapon burns with magical flame; its body turns to ash, though its equipment is not harmed. This special ability only functions while the immolator wields the weapon. This ability lasts for a number of rounds per day equal to the immolator's level. These rounds do not need to be consecutive. At 12th level, this ability gives the immolator's weapon the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Flaming Burst|flaming burst]]_ weapon special ability. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-bane|bane]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-greater-bane|greater bane]].

**Judgment by Fire (Su)**: At 20th level, an immolator can call fiery judgment down upon a foe during combat. Whenever an immolator uses her judgment ability, she can invoke a judgment by fire on a foe as a swift action. Once declared, the immolator can make a single melee (or ranged attack, if the foe is within 30 feet) against the target. If the attack hits, the attack deals fire damage instead of weapon damage, and it attacks the target’s Fortitude, and on a hit the target dies (it misses creatures immune to fire automatically). Its attack bonus is equal to 1/2 the immolator's level + the immolator's Wisdom modifier. Whether it hits or misses, the target creature is immune to the immolator's judgment by fire ability for 24 hours. Creatures killed in this manner explode in a burst of fire, dealing 10d6 points of fire damage to every creature within 5 feet (attack bonus 1/2 the immolator's level + the immolator's Wisdom modifier vs Reflex; a miss halves). Once this ability has been used, it cannot be used again for 1d4 rounds. This ability modifies and functions as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-true-judgment|true judgment]].
