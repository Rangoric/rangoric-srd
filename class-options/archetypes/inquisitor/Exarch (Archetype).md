# Exarch

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-archetypes|inquisitor]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarves]].*

The gruff traditionalism of most dwarves finds its apex in those who adhere to a strict orthodoxy rooted in ancient principles and practices and who are not amenable whatsoever to change or innovation.

**Spells**: Exarchs cannot cast [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-spells|spells]] with the chaotic descriptor.

**Inflexible Will (Ex)**: At 1st level, an exarch gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insanity (Spell)|insanity]]* effects and effects with the chaotic descriptor. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-monster-lore|monster lore]].

**Detect Chaos (Sp)**: At will, an exarch can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Chaos|detect chaos]]*. This ability replaces *[[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-detect-alignment|detect alignment]]*.

**Fearsome Jurist (Su)**: At 5th level, an exarch can imbue one of her weapons with the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Jurist|jurist]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Menacing|menacing]]* weapon special ability as a swift action, and may switch between these properties as a swift action. When using either special ability, her weapon's critical threat range doubles against chaotic creatures. This does not stack with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]*, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]], or similar effects. This ability otherwise functions as and replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-bane|bane]].

**Aura of Repetition (Su)**: At 8th level, once per day while using her judgment, an exarch can project an aura of repetition, as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Toil Subdomain|Toil subdomain]] power. If the exarch takes [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Artifice Domain|Artifice]] ([[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Toil Subdomain|Toil]]) as her domain, the save DC of her aura increases by 2 but its duration does not increase. This ability replaces her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-second-judgment|second judgment]].

**Double Jeopardy (Su)**: At 12th level, whenever an exarch uses her fearsome jurist ability, she may choose to affect two weapons, with one gaining the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Jurist|jurist]]* weapon special ability and the other the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Menacing|menacing]]* special ability as above. Both special abilities may be combined in a single weapon, whose critical threat range doubles. This does not stack with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge|keen edge]]*, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]], or similar effects. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-greater-bane|greater bane]].

**Aura of Reversion (Su)**: At 16th level, while using her judgment, an exarch can project a 30-foot-radius emanation for a number of rounds per day equal to her inquisitor level. Any creature other than the exarch that is using a transmutation effect within this aura at the beginning of its turn becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]], or sickened and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] if using a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Polymorph|polymorph]] effect, including the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Change Shape|change shape]] ability (Fortitude negates; DC 10 + 1/2 the exarch's level + her Wisdom modifier). Continuous effects from permanent magical items do not cause this effect. Within the aura, dispel checks against transmutation effects gain a +4 bonus. This ability cannot be used simultaneously with aura of repetition. This ability replaces her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-third-judgment|third judgment]].
