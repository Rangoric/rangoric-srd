# Mooncaller

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A mooncaller is bound to the subtle influences of the ever-changing moon and its endless cycles from light to dark and back again.

**Night Sight (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a mooncaller gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]. If she already has low-light vision, she gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] with a range of 30 feet. If she already has darkvision, the range of her darkvision is increased by 30 feet. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Resist Call of the Wild (Ex)**: At 4th level, a mooncaller gains a +4 bonus on defenses to avoid [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confusion]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|daze]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feeblemind|feeblemind]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insanity (Spell)|insanity]]_ effects. She also gains a +4 bonus against the exceptional, spell-like, and supernatural abilities of creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^shapechanger|shapechanger]] subtype. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Purity of Body (Ex)**: At 9th level, a mooncaller gains immunity to all [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Disease|diseases]], including supernatural and magical diseases. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].

**Wolfsbane (Su)**: At 13th level, a mooncaller gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR 3/silver]], increasing to DR 4/silver at 16th level and DR 5/silver at 19th level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].
