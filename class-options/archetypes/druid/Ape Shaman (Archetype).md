# Ape Shaman

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A shaman with this totem calls upon the mighty ape, a peaceful but powerful simian whose strength is beyond compare. An ape shaman is a friendly protector of the forest, but will crush those enemies who rouse her anger.

**Nature Bond**: An ape shaman who chooses an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] must select an ape or related primate. If choosing a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|domain]], the ape shaman must choose from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Community Domain|Community]] ([[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Family Subdomain|Family subdomain]]), [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Destruction Domain|Destruction]] ([[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Rage Subdomain|Rage subdomain]]), and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain|Strength]] domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: An ape shaman can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] with apes and other primates as a full-round action with a +4 bonus.

**Totem Transformation (Su)**: At 2nd level, an ape shaman may adopt an aspect of the ape while retaining her normal form. The druid gains one of the following sets of bonuses and abilities: movement (climb speed 20 ft., +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb), senses ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]]), natural weapons (2 slams \[1d6 for a Medium shaman\], +2 on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver checks]] to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]]), or toughness (+2 natural armor bonus to AC, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]] feat).

While using totem transformation, the ape shaman may speak normally and can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ (primates only) at will. Using this ability is a standard action at 2nd level, a move action at 7th level, and a swift action at 12th level. The ape shaman can use this ability for a number of minutes per day equal to her druid level. These minutes do not need to be consecutive, but they must be used in 1-minute increments. This is a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Polymorph|polymorph effect]] and cannot be used while the druid is using another polymorph effect, such as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Totemic Summons (Su)**: At 5th level, an ape shaman may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]_ as a standard action when summoning primates, and these summoned creatures gain temporary hit points equal to her druid level. She can apply the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Young Creature|young template]] to any primate to reduce the level of the summoning spell required by one. She can also increase the level of summoning required by one in order to apply either the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Advanced Creature|advanced]] or the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Giant Creature|giant template]], or increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant templates. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: At 6th level, an ape shaman's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] ability functions at her druid level –2. If she takes on the form of an ape, she instead uses her druid level +2.

**Bonus Feat**: At 9th level and every four levels thereafter, an ape shaman gains one of the following bonus feats: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Toughness|Toughness]]. She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].
