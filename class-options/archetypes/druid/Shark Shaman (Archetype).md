# Shark Shaman

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Some druids emulate the deadly shark, a remorseless hunter that marine dwellers dread. Like a true shark, a shark shaman leaves blood and fear in her wake.

**Nature Bond**: A shark shaman who chooses an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] must select a shark. If choosing a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|domain]], a shark shaman may choose the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Death Domain|Death]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain|Water]] domain.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: A shark shaman can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] with fish as a full-round action with a +4 bonus.

**Totem Transformation (Su)**: At 2nd level, a shark shaman may adopt an aspect of the shark while retaining her normal form. This ability functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Dragon Shaman (Archetype)|dragon shaman's totem transformation ability]], but the druid may select from the following bonuses: movement (can breathe water, swim speed 30 feet), senses ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] 30 feet, scent 90 feet in water), natural weapons (bite 1d6 for a Medium shaman), or shark skin (+2 natural armor, creatures [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grappling]] the shaman take 1 point of slashing damage per round of grapple). While using totem transformation, the shark shaman may speak normally and can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ (fish only) at will. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Totemic Summons (Su)**: At 5th level, a shark shaman may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]_ as a standard action when summoning rays and sharks, and summoned rays and sharks gain temporary hit points equal to her druid level. This ability otherwise functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Dragon Shaman (Archetype)|dragon shaman's totemic summons ability]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: At 6th level, a shark shaman's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] ability functions at her druid level –2. If she takes on the form of a shark, she instead uses her druid level +2.

**Bonus Feat**: At 9th level and every 4 levels thereafter, a shark shaman gains one of the following bonus feats: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bleeding Critical|Bleeding Critical]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lightning Reflexes|Lightning Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]]), or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]]). She must meet prerequisites for these bonus feats. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].
