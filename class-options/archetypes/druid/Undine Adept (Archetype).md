# Undine Adept

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|undines]]._

An undine adept dedicates herself to preserving the knowledge of the first undines and ensuring her people's ancient connections to the natural world remain undisturbed. They serve as the keepers of the roots of the undine people and as their protectors. An undine adept has the following class features.

**Domains**: An undine adept who chooses a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|domain]] must choose the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Community Domain|Community]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain|Water]] domain, or any subdomain of those domains.

**Amphibious (Su)**: At 2nd level, the undine adept gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^aquatic|aquatic]] subtype and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^amphibious|amphibious]] universal monster ability, allowing her to breathe water or air. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Augment Summoning (Su)**: At 3rd level, any creature with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^water|water]] subtype the undine adept summons with either _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]_ gains the benefits of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Augment Summoning|Augment Summoning]] feat. This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]].

**Resist Water's Call (Su)**: At 4th level, an undine adept gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against the spell-like and supernatural abilities of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^outsider|outsiders]] with the aquatic or water subtype, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fey|fey]] with the aquatic or water subtype, and spells and effects with the water descriptor. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: At 6th level, an undine adept gains the ability to use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]]. When an undine takes the form of a creature with the aquatic or water subtype, this ability functions at her class level + 1. For all other forms, her effective druid level for the ability is equal to her actual undine adept level. This ability otherwise functions as and replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]].

**Commune with Water Spirits**: At 9th level, an undine adept adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]]_ to her [[rangoric-srd/spells/Druid Spell List|druid spell list]]. She may use this spell whether she worships a deity or elemental forces. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].
