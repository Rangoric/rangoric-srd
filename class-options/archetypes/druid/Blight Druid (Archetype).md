# Blight Druid

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The devoted servants of nature corrupted, ruined, and destroyed, blight druids are the caretakers of lands ravaged by natural disaster. While some are devoted to reforming and reclaiming lands despoiled by the ravages of civilization, others seek out the more rapacious violence inherent in nature and feed the creeping rot and decay that brings an end to all things.

**Nature Bond (Ex)**: A blight druid may not bond with an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]], but may either call a familiar as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-familiar|wizard]] of her druid level or select from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain|Darkness]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Death Domain|Death]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Destruction Domain|Destruction]] domains in addition to those normally available for her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|nature bond]].

**Vermin Empathy (Su)**: A blight druid can improve the attitude of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^vermin|vermin]] as a normal druid can with [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|animals]]. Vermin have a starting attitude of unfriendly. The blight druid can also improve the attitude of animals and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^mindless|mindless]] [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^undead|undead]] creatures that were formerly animals, but she takes a –4 penalty on the check unless the animal or undead has a disease special attack. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]].

**Miasma (Ex)**: Starting at 5th level, if a blight druid is adjacent to a creature at the beginning of its turn, the miasma attacks the creature’s Fortitude at 1/2 the druid's level + the druid's Wisdom modifier, and on a hit it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 round. A creature of the animal, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fey|fey]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^plant|plant]] type that fails its save is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round and sickened for 1 minute thereafter. If it misses the creature, the creature is immune to this effect for 24 hours, as are creatures immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Disease|disease]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Blightblooded (Ex)**: At 9th level, a blight druid gains immunity to all diseases, including natural and supernatural diseases. She also becomes immune to effects that would cause her to become sickened or nauseated. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].

**Plaguebearer (Su)**: Starting at 13th level, any creature that strikes a blight druid with a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^touch-attacks|touch attack]], unarmed strike, or natural weapon is attacked against its Fortitude at 1/2 the druid's level + the druid's Wisdom modifier, and on a hit contracts a disease, as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]_ spell. If it misses the creature, the creature is immune to this effect for 24 hours. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].
