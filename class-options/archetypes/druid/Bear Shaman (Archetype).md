# Bear Shaman

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Some druids form a close bond with one type of animal. The animal shamans (the bear shaman here and the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Eagle Shaman (Archetype)|eagle shaman]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Lion Shaman (Archetype)|lion shaman]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Serpent Shaman (Archetype)|serpent shaman]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Wolf Shaman (Archetype)|wolf shaman]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Ape Shaman (Archetype)|ape shaman]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Bat Shaman (Archetype)|bat shaman]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Boar Shaman (Archetype)|boar shaman]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Dragon Shaman (Archetype)|dragon shaman]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Saurian Shaman (Archetype)|saurian shaman]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Shark Shaman (Archetype)|shark shaman]]) explore the unique bond between a druid and her totem. They all possess similar powers, which are explained fully in this description.

A shaman with this focus calls upon the mighty bear, titan of the woodlands and mountains, a paragon of strength and ferocity, and yet also a quiet protector rich in wisdom.

**Nature Bond**: A bear shaman who chooses an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] must select a bear. If choosing a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|domain]], the bear shaman must choose from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain|Earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Protection Domain|Protection]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain|Strength]] domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: A bear shaman can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] with bears and wolverines as a full-round action with a +4 bonus.

**Totem Transformation (Su)**: At 2nd level, a bear shaman may adopt an aspect of the bear while retaining her normal form. She gains one of the following bonuses: movement (+10 enhancement bonus to land speed, +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim), senses ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]]), toughness (+2 natural armor bonus to AC, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]] feat), or natural weapons (bite \[1d6\] and 2 claws \[1d4\] for a Medium shaman, +2 to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]] on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] checks). While using totem transformation, the bear shaman may speak normally and can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]* (mammals only) at will. Using this ability is a standard action at 2nd level, a move action at 7th level, and a swift action at 12th level. The bear shaman can use this ability for a number of minutes per day equal to her druid level. These minutes do not need to be consecutive, but they must be used in 1-minute increments. This is a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Polymorph|polymorph effect]] and cannot be used while the druid is using another polymorph effect, such as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Totemic Summons (Su)**: At 5th level, a bear shaman may cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]* as a standard action when summoning bears, and summoned bears gain temporary hit points equal to her druid level. She can apply the young template to any bear to reduce the level of the summoning spell required by one. She can also increase the level of summoning required by one in order to apply either the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Advanced Creature|advanced]] or the giant template, or increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant templates. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: At 6th level, a bear shaman's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] ability functions at her druid level – 2. If she takes on the form of a bear, she instead uses her druid level + 2.

**Bonus Feat**: At 9th level and every 4 levels thereafter, a bear shaman gains one of the following bonus feats: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Great Fortitude|Improved Great Fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Toughness|Toughness]]. She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].
