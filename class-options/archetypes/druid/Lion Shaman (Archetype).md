# Lion Shaman

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A shaman with this totem calls upon the proud lion, imposing and majestic, the mighty leader of deadly hunters.

**Nature Bond**: A lion shaman who chooses an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] must select a lion. If choosing a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|domain]], the lion shaman must choose from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Glory Domain|Glory]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain|Nobility]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Sun Domain|Sun]] domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: A lion shaman can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] with felines as a full-round action with a +4 bonus.

**Totem Transformation (Su)**: At 2nd level, a lion shaman may adopt an aspect of the lion while retaining her normal form. This ability functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Bear Shaman (Archetype)|bear shaman ability]], but the druid may select from the following bonuses: movement (+20 enhancement bonus to land speed), senses ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]]), or natural weapons (bite \[1d4\], 2 claws \[1d4\] for a Medium druid, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^rake|rake]], +2 [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]] to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]]). While using totem transformation, the lion shaman may speak normally and can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ (felines only) at will. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Totemic Summons (Su)**: At 5th level, a lion shaman may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]_ as a standard action when summoning felines, and these summoned creatures gain temporary hit points equal to her druid level. This ability otherwise functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Bear Shaman (Archetype)|bear shaman ability]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: At 6th level, a lion shaman's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] ability functions at her druid level – 2. If she takes on the form of a feline, she instead uses her druid level + 2.

**Bonus Feat**: At 9th level and every 4 levels thereafter, a lion shaman gains one of the following bonus feats: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lunge|Lunge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Iron Will|Improved Iron Will]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Iron Will|Iron Will]], or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]). She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].
