# Sky Druid

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|sylphs]]._

Some druids develop ties not to a particular landscape, but instead to the endless blue expanse of the skies. Such are the sky druids, who are more at home soaring through air than standing on the ground. A sky druid has the following class features.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A sky druid loses medium armor proficiency.

**Nature Bond (Ex)**: At 1st level, a sky druid who chooses an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] must select one with a fly speed. If choosing a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|domain]], the sky druid must choose from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animals]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Liberation Domain|Liberation]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]] domains, or subdomains appropriate to those domains.

**Sky's Embrace (Su)**: At 2nd level, a sky druid no longer takes falling damage, as though she were constantly under the effect of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_. Additionally, she may use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|Acrobatics]] in place of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|Athletics]] when making jump checks. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Resist Storm (Ex)**: At 4th level, a sky druid gains a +4 bonus on defenses against spells with the air or electricity descriptors and against effects that control or modify the weather (such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleet Storm|sleet storm]]_). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Skymaster (Su)**: At 5th level, a sky druid can use the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]_ spell (self only) for a number of minutes per day equal to her druid level. These minutes do not need to be consecutive. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: At 6th level, a sky druid gains the ability to use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]]. When a sky druid takes the form of a creature with a fly speed, this ability functions at her class level + 1. For all other forms, her effective druid level for the ability is equal to her actual sky druid level. This ability otherwise functions as and replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]].

**Soaring Form (Ex)**: At 9th level, a sky druid is no longer affected by altitude sickness or natural or magical wind. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].

**Into the Wild Blue (Su)**: At 13th level, a sky druid gains a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|fly speed]] equal to twice her base land speed (good maneuverability). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].
