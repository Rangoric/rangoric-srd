# Nature Fang

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A nature fang is a druid who stalks and slays those who despoil nature, kill scarce animals, or introduce diseases to unprotected habitats. She gives up a close empathic connection with the natural world to become its deadly champion and avenger.

**Studied Target (Ex)**: At 1st level, a nature fang gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|slayer’s studied target]] class feature. At 5th level and every 5 levels thereafter, the nature fang's bonus against her studied target increases by 1. Unlike a slayer, a nature fang does not gain the ability to maintain more than one studied target at the same time. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-sense|nature sense]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]], and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Slayer Talent**: At 4th level and every 2 levels thereafter, a nature fang selects a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]]. Starting at 12th level, she can select an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talent]] in place of a slayer talent. She uses her druid level as her slayer level to determine what talents she can select. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]].

**Sneak Attack (Ex)**: At 4th level, a nature fang gains sneak attack +1d6. This ability functions as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|rogue sneak attack]] class feature. If the nature fang gets a sneak attack bonus from another source, the bonuses on damage stack. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Swift Studied Target**: At 9th level, a nature fang can study an opponent as a move or swift action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].

**Slayer Talents**: The following slayer talents complement the nature fang archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Foil Scrutiny (Slayer Talent)|foil scrutiny]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Poison Use (Slayer Talent)|poison use]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Ranger Combat Style (Slayer Talent)|ranger combat style]]. Additionally, the following rogue talents, which are also available to slayers, complement the archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Bleeding Attack (Rogue Talent)|bleeding attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Camouflage (Rogue Talent)|camouflage]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Stealth (Rogue Talent)|fast stealth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Finesse Rogue (Rogue Talent)|finesse rogue]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Lasting Poison (Rogue Talent)|lasting poison]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rogue Crawl (Rogue Talent)|rogue crawl]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slow Reactions (Rogue Talent)|slow reactions]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Swift Poison (Rogue Talent)|swift poison]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Terrain Mastery (Rogue Talent)|terrain mastery]].

**Advanced Talents**: The following slayer advanced talents complement the nature fang archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Assassinate (Slayer Talent)|assassinate]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Slayer Camouflage (Slayer Talent)|slayer camouflage]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Woodland Stride (Slayer Talent)|woodland stride]]. The rogue advanced talent [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Master of Disguise (Rogue Talent)|master of disguise]] also fits the archetype.
