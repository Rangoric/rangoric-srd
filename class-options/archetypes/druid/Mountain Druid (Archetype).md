# Mountain Druid

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

As more and more of the soft, easy lands become cultivated and civilized, many druids look for refuge and solitude among the eternal peaks of the highest mountains.

**Mountaineer (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a mountain druid gains a bonus on initiative checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks equal to half her druid level in mountainous terrain, and she cannot be tracked in such an environment. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Sure-Footed (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a mountain druid suffers no penalty to speed or on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks when walking across steep slopes, rubble, or scree. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]].

**Spire Walker (Ex)**: At 4th level, a mountain druid does not lose her Dexterity bonus when climbing. A mountain druid is immune to altitude sickness and ignores the effects of a cold climate as if under the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: A mountain druid gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|this ability]] at 6th level, except that her effective druid level for the ability is equal to her druid level – 2. A mountain druid cannot use wild shape to adopt a plant form. However, at 12th level she can assume the form of a Large [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^giant|giant]] as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Form I|giant form I]]_. At 16th level, she may assume the form of a Huge giant as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Form II|giant form II]]_.

**Mountain Stance (Ex)**: At 9th level, a mountain druid gains immunity to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrification]] and receives a +4 bonus on defenses or to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] to resist any attempt to push, pull, [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Drag|drag]] her, or to resist any other effect that would physically move her from her position (e.g., _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Repel Wood|repel wood]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reverse Gravity|reverse gravity]]_, or being [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blown-away|blown away]] by high winds). This does not protect her against being [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|tripped]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grappled]], or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].

**Mountain Stone (Ex)**: At 13th level, a mountain druid can transform her body into a weathered stone outcrop and back at will. This effect functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Statue|statue]]_. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].
