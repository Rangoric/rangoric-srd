# Cave Druid

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Far from the green fields of the world above lies a lightless expanse beneath the surface. This darkling fairyland is not without beauty and natural wonders of its own, and a few druids seek to preserve this hidden realm and purge it of the fell horrors that creep up from below.

**Cavesense (Ex)**: A cave druid adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] as a class skill and gains a +2 bonus on Dungeoneering and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] skill checks. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-sense|nature sense]] ability.

**Nature Bond**: A cave druid may select the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain|Darkness domain]] in addition to the choices normally allowed for her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|nature bond]], but may not select the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]] domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: A cave druid can influence [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^ooze|oozes]], rather than [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^magical-beast|magical beasts]], with a –4 penalty on her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] check.

**Tunnelrunner (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a cave druid can move through areas of rubble or narrow passages that require squeezing at her normal movement rate and without penalty. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Lightfoot (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a cave druid cannot be detected with [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]].

**Resist Subterranean Corruption (Ex)**: At 4th level, a cave druid gains a +2 bonus on defenses against exceptional, supernatural, and spell-like abilities of oozes and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^aberration|aberrations]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature’s lure]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: A cave druid gains this [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|ability]] at 6th level, except that her effective druid level for this ability is equal to her druid level – 2. She cannot use wild shape to adopt a plant form. At 10th level, the cave druid can assume the form of a Small or Medium ooze as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]_, and at 12th level that of a Tiny or Large ooze as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape IV|beast shape IV]]_ (treating the ooze as if it were a magical beast without a natural armor bonus). When in ooze form, the cave druid has no discernible anatomy and is immune to poison, sneak attacks, and critical hits.
