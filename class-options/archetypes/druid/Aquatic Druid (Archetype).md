# Aquatic Druid

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Shepherds of the lakes and seas, aquatic druids guard ecosystems ranging from shallows streams to deep ocean trenches, ministering to their residents and communing with the tides.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: An aquatic druid's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] functions only on creatures that have a swim speed or the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^aquatic|aquatic]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^water|water]] subtype; however, she can improve the attitude of any such creature with Intelligence 2 or less regardless of type, including [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^mindless|mindless]] creatures.

**Aquatic Adaptation (Ex)**: At 2nd level, an aquatic druid gains an insight bonus on initiative checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks equal to 1/2 her druid level in aquatic terrain, and she cannot be tracked such environments. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Natural Swimmer (Ex)**: At 3rd level, an aquatic druid gains a swim speed equal to half her land speed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]].

**Resist Ocean's Fury (Ex)**: At 4th level, an aquatic druid gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against spells of the water type or the exceptional or supernatural abilities of creatures with the aquatic or water subtype. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: An aquatic druid gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|this ability]] at 6th level, except that her effective druid level for the ability is equal to her druid level – 2.

**Seaborn (Ex)**: At 9th level, an aquatic druid gains the aquatic subtype, the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^amphibious|amphibious]] trait, and a swim speed equal to her land speed. She also can endure cold climate effects as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].

**Deep Diver (Ex)**: At 13th level, an aquatic druid gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]]/slashing or piercing equal to 1/2 her level. This damage reduction also applies against spells and spell-like abilities that inflict damage by grappling or crushing (e.g., _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Crushing Hand|crushing hand]]_). She never takes pressure damage from deep water. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].
