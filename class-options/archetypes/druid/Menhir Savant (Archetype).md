# Menhir Savant

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Some druids study the paths of nature's power through the nodes and ley lines that connect standing stones and megalithic circles, learning to tap into their energies.

**Spirit Sense (Sp)**: At 1st level, a menhir savant can detect the presence of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^undead|undead]]; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fey|fey]]; [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^outsider|outsiders]]; and astral, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Etherealness|ethereal]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures. This ability functions like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]*, and the druid detects all of these creatures rather than trying to detect one kind. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-sense|nature sense]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]].

**Place Magic (Su)**: At 2nd level, a menhir savant learns to identify and tap into ley lines in different types of terrain. As a free action, she can tap into the magic of a nearby ley line and increase her caster level by +1 for 1 round. She can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + her Wisdom bonus. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]].

**Walk the Lines (Su)**: At 9th level, a menhir savant can use her connection to ley lines to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Transport via Plants|transport via plants]]* a number of times per day equal to her Wisdom bonus.

**Empty Body (Su)**: At 13th level, a menhir savant can become ethereal as a standard action, as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt|ethereal jaunt]]*. She can remain ethereal for a number of rounds per day equal to her druid level. These rounds do not need to be consecutive. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].
