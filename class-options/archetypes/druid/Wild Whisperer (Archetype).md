# Wild Whisperer

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A wild whisperer is an expert at studying, predicting, and explaining animal behavior. She is less interested in plants, fey, and other aspects of the natural world, and uses her gifts to tame or relocate dangerous beasts and soothe the hurts of wounded and sickly creatures.

**Inspiration (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a wild whisperer gains an inspiration pool as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|investigator class ability]]. A wild whisperer uses her druid level as her investigator level when determining the effects of this ability. Instead of free uses of inspiration on Knowledge skill or Linguistics checks, the wild whisperer can use inspiration on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] skill checks without expending a use of inspiration, provided she's trained in the relevant skill. She can also use inspiration on any [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] check without expending a use of inspiration. A wild whisperer's inspiration pool refreshes each day when she prepares spells. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]], and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Wild Shape (Ex)**: At 4th level, a wild whisperer gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] ability, but she never gains access to any forms beyond Small and Medium animal forms, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]_. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]].

**Natural Expertise (Ex)**: At 6th level, a wild whisperer's powers of observation give her an advantage when she's fighting natural creatures. When using inspiration on an attack roll against an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|animal]] or a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^vermin|vermin]], or on a defense against an effect from an animal or a vermin, a wild whisperer has to expend only one use of inspiration instead of two. This ability replaces the additional use of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] at 6th level.

**Investigator Talent**: At 8th level, wild whisperer selects an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]]. This ability replaces the additional use of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] at 8th level.

**Investigator Talents**: The following investigator talents complement the wild whisperer archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Amazing Inspiration (Investigator Talent)|amazing inspiration]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Effortless Aid (Investigator Talent)|effortless aid]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Eidetic Recollection (Investigator Talent)|eidetic recollection]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Empathy (Ex, Su) (Investigator Talent)|empathy]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Inspirational Expertise (Investigator Talent)|inspirational expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Inspired Alertness (Investigator Talent)|inspired alertness]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Inspired Intimidator (Investigator Talent)|inspired intimidator]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Perceptive Tracking (Investigator Talent)|perceptive tracking]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Tenacious Inspiration (Investigator Talent)|tenacious inspiration]].
