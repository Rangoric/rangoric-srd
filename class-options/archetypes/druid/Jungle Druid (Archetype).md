# Jungle Druid

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

The fecund jungles of the equatorial regions are rich in life and ancient tradition; druidical guardians of sacred pools, elder trees, and trembling volcanoes watch over crumbling temples and the inevitable reclamation of lost civilizations by the beating heart of nature untamed.

**Jungle Guardian (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a jungle druid gains a bonus on initiative checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks equal to 1/2 her druid level in jungle terrain, and she cannot be tracked in such environments. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Woodland Stride (Ex)**: A jungle druid gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|this ability]] at 3rd level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]].

**Torrid Endurance (Ex)**: At 4th level, a jungle druid ignores the effects of a hot climate as if under the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_. She also gains a +4 bonus on defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Disease|disease]] and the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^extraordinary-abilities|exceptional abilities]] of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|animals]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^magical-beast|magical beasts]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: A jungle druid gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|this ability]] at 6th level, except that her effective druid level for the ability is equal to her druid level – 2.

**Verdant Sentinel (Ex)**: At 13th level, a jungle druid can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tree Shape|tree shape]]_ at will. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|a thousand faces]].
