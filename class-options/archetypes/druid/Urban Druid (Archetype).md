# Urban Druid

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

While many druids keep to the wilderness, some make their way within settlements, communing with the animals and vermin who live there and speaking for the nature that runs rampant in civilization's very cradle.

**Spontaneous Casting**: An urban druid can channel stored spell energy into domain spells that she has not prepared ahead of time. She can “lose” a prepared spell in order to cast any domain spell of the same level or lower. This ability replaces the ability to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-spontaneous-casting|spontaneously cast summon nature's ally spells]].

**Nature Bond (Ex)**: An urban druid may not select an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]]. Instead, she must choose from the following domains, rather than those usually available to druids: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Charm Domain|Charm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Community Domain|Community]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Knowledge Domain|Knowledge]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain|Nobility]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Protection Domain|Protection]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Repose Domain|Repose]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Rune Domain|Rune]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]].

**Lorekeeper (Ex)**: At 2nd level, an urban druid adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] to her list of class skills. She also receives a +2 bonus on these skill checks. This ability replaces a druid's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]] abilities.

**Resist Temptation (Ex)**: At 4th level, an urban druid gains a +2 bonus on saves vs. divinations and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Charm and Compulsion|enchantments]]. This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-resist-natures-lure|resist nature's lure]] ability.

**A Thousand Faces (Su)**: An urban druid gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-a-thousand-faces|this ability]] at 6th level.

**Wild Shape (Su)**: An urban druid gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|this ability]] at 8th level, except that her effective druid level for the ability is equal to her druid level – 4.

**Mental Strength (Ex)**: At 9th level, an urban druid gains immunity to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Charm and Compulsion|charm and compulsion]] effects. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].
