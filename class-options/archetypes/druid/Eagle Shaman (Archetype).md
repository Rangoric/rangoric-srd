# Eagle Shaman

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-archetypes|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A shaman with this totem calls upon the noble eagle, stern and proud, soaring high above the world with keen and pitiless eyes that miss nothing.

**Nature Bond**: An eagle shaman who chooses an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] must select a bird (eagle). If choosing a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|domain]], the eagle shaman must choose from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain|Nobility]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]] domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**: An eagle shaman can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] with birds as a full-round action with a +4 bonus.

**Totem Transformation (Su)**: At 2nd level, an eagle shaman may adopt an aspect of the eagle while retaining her normal form. This ability functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Bear Shaman (Archetype)|bear shaman ability]], but the druid may select from the following bonuses: movement (fly speed 30 feet \[average\], the druid must be 5th level to select this bonus), senses ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], +4 racial bonus to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]]), or natural weapons (bite \[1d4\], 2 talons \[1d4\] for a Medium shaman). While using totem transformation, the eagle shaman may speak normally and can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ (birds only) at will. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]].

**Totemic Summons (Su)**: At 5th level, an eagle shaman may cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]_ as a standard action when summoning [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Eagle|eagles]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Roc|rocs]], and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Eagle, Giant|giant eagles]] (added to the 4th-level list), and these summoned creatures gain temporary hit points equal to her druid level. This ability otherwise functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Bear Shaman (Archetype)|bear shaman ability]].

**Wild Shape (Su)**: At 6th level, an eagle shaman's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] ability functions at her druid level – 2. If she takes on the form of an eagle or roc, she instead uses her druid level + 2.

**Bonus Feat**: At 9th level and every 4 levels thereafter, an eagle shaman gains one of the following bonus feats: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flyby Attack|Flyby Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Lightning Reflexes|Improved Lightning Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lightning Reflexes|Lightning Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]]), or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wind Stance|Wind Stance]]. She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-venom-immunity|venom immunity]].
