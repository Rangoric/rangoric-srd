# Tranquil Guardian

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-archetypes|paladin]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|aasimars]]._

A tranquil guardian is a missionary of peace and tranquility, a soothing voice of succor in a violent and dangerous world. A tranquil guardian gains the following class features.

**Touch of Serenity (Su)**: At 1st level, a tranquil guardian gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Touch of Serenity|Touch of Serenity]] as a bonus feat, even if she does not meet the prerequisites. At 6th level, and every six levels thereafter, the duration of a tranquil guardian's Touch of Serenity increases by 1 round. Each round on its turn, the effect attacks the target’s Will again, and a miss ends it. The duration does not stack; only the longest remaining duration applies. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|smite evil]].

**Serene Strike (Su)**: At 3rd level, when a tranquil guardian confirms a critical hit, she may convert all damage from her attack to nonlethal damage, and when she does, she can activate Touch of Serenity through her weapon or unarmed strike. Using serene strike is a free action. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-courage|aura of courage]].

**Divine Bond (Su)**: A tranquil guardian who chooses a weapon as her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-bond|divine bond]] may only increase her weapon's enhancement bonus or add the following properties to her weapon: _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Conductive|conductive]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Defending|defending]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Disruption|disruption]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Grayflame|grayflame]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Merciful|merciful]]_.

**Aura of Calm (Su)**: At 8th level, a tranquil guardian is immune to all spells and spell-like abilities with the emotion descriptor, as well as all [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] effects. Each ally within 10 feet of her gains a +4 morale bonus on defenses against these effects.

This ability functions only while the tranquil guardian is conscious, not if she is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dead|dead]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-resolve|aura of resolve]].

**Waves of Peace (Su)**: At 11th level, a tranquil guardian may expend 2 uses of her Touch of Serenity to affect each opponent within 5 feet of her with that effect. She does not need to touch the creature for the effect to take hold. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-justice|aura of justice]].

**Apostle of Peace (Su)**: At 20th level, a tranquil guardian's [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]] increases to 10/evil, and whenever she channels positive energy or uses lay on hands to heal, she heals the maximum possible amount. In addition, any creature struck by her Touch of Serenity, even if the touch misses its defense, is attacked against its Will again at 1/2 her tranquil guardian level + her Charisma modifier the next time it tries to attack. On a hit, the attack (including spells or special abilities) automatically fails. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-holy-champion|holy champion]].
