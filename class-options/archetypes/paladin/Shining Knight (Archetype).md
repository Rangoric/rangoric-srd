# Shining Knight

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-archetypes|paladin]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While paladins often are seen mounted atop a loyal steed, the shining knight is the true symbol of mounted bravery. They are never far from their steeds and are always clad in brightly polished armor. The shining knight has the following class features.

**Skilled Rider (Ex and Su)**: At 3rd level, a shining knight does not take any penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks due to her [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Armor#Armor Qualities|armor check penalty]]. In addition, any mount she is riding gains the benefit of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-grace|divine grace]] class feature, adding her Charisma bonus (if any) to its defenses. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-health|divine health]].

**Divine Bond (Su)**: Upon reaching 5th level, a shining knight must form a bond with a mount ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-special-mount|special mount]]). This ability otherwise functions as the paladin ability.

**Knight's Charge (Su)**: At 11th level, whenever a mounted shining knight charges a foe, her movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity, for either her or her mount. In addition, if her target is also the target of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|smite evil]] ability and the charge attack hits, it also attacks the target’s Will, and on a hit the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 the shining knight's level. Its attack bonus is equal to 1/2 the shining knight's level + the shining knight's Charisma modifier. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-justice|aura of justice]].
