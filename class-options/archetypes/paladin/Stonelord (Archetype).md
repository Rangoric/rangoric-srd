# Stonelord

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-archetypes|paladin]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarves]].*

A stonelord is a devoted sentinel of dwarven enclaves, drawing the power of the earth and ancient stone to protect her people.

**Stonestrike (Su)**: Once per day per paladin level, a stonelord can draw upon the power of the living rock. As a swift action, she treats her melee attacks until the beginning of her next turn (whether armed or unarmed) as magical and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Special Materials|adamantine]], including ignoring hardness up to twice her paladin level, with a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls, as well as on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] checks. This bonus also applies to her CMD if she or her target is touching the ground or a stone structure. This bonus increases by +1 at 5th level and every 5 levels thereafter. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|smite evil]].

**Heartstone (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a stonelord's flesh becomes progressively rockier. She gains a +1 natural armor bonus to AC and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]]/adamantine equal to 1/2 her paladin level. The natural armor bonus increases by +1 at 6th level, and every four levels thereafter, to a maximum of +5 at 18th level. These benefits are halved when not touching the ground or a stone structure. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-grace|divine grace]].

**Stoneblood (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a stonelord's vitals begin to calcify and her blood transforms into liquid stone. She adds her paladin level on checks to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stable|stabilize]] at negative hit points and gains a 25% chance to ignore a critical hit or precision damage. This does not stack with *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]* armor or similar effects. At 9th level, this chance increases to 50% and she becomes immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrification]]. At 15th level, this chance increases to 75% and she becomes immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^bleed|bleed]] and blood drain effects. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-health|divine health]] and her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-mercy|mercies]] gained at 3rd, 9th, and 15th level.

**Defensive Stance (Ex)**: At 4th level, a stonelord gains the defensive stance ability, as a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Stalwart Defender|stalwart defender]], and may select one [[rangoric-srd/class-options/defensive-powers/Defensive Powers|defensive power]] at 8th level and every four levels thereafter. Levels of stalwart defender stack with her paladin levels when determining the total number of rounds that she can maintain her defensive stance per day. A stonelord does not gain any spells or spellcasting abilities, does not have a caster level, and cannot use spell trigger or spell completion magic items. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-spells|spells]].

**Earth Channel (Su)**: At 4th level, a stonelord gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Channel|Elemental Channel]] (earth) as a bonus feat, which she may activate by spending two uses of her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] ability, using her paladin level as her effective cleric level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-channel-positive-energy|channel positive energy]].

**Stone Servant (Su)**: At 5th level, a stonelord may call a Small [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Earth Elemental|earth elemental]] to her side, as a paladin calls her mount ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-special-mount|special mount]]). This earth elemental is Lawful Good in alignment and possesses the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Celestial Creature|celestial template]], and it increases in size as the stonelord gains levels, becoming Medium at 8th level, Large at 11th level, Huge at 14th level, Greater at 17th level, and Elder at 20th level. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-bond|divine bond]].

**Stonebane (Su)**: At 11th level, when using stonestrike, a stonelord's attack gains the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Bane (Weapon Special Ability)|bane]]* weapon special ability against creatures with the earth subtype and constructs or objects made of earth or stone. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-justice|aura of justice]].

**Phase Strike (Su)**: At 12th level, a stonelord's stonestrike may pass through stone and metal as if they weren't there. By spending 2 uses of her stonestrike ability, she may ignore any cover less than total cover provided by stone or metal, and she ignores any AC bonus from stone or metal armor or shields as if wielding a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Brilliant Energy|brilliant energy]]* weapon. A phase strike cannot damage constructs, objects, or creatures with the earth subtype, but unlike a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Brilliant Energy|brilliant energy]]* weapon, it can harm undead. This ability replaces her 12th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-mercy|mercy]].

**Mobile Defense (Ex)**: At 18th level, a stonelord can make one 5-foot step per round while maintaining her defensive stance. This ability replaces her 18th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-mercy|mercy]].

**Stone Body (Ex)**: At 20th level, a stonelord's body transforms into living stone. She no longer needs to eat, drink, breathe, or sleep, and she becomes immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Paralysis|paralysis]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|poison]], and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]]. She is also no longer subject to critical hits or precision-based damage. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-holy-champion|holy champion]].
