# Speaker for the Past

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-archetypes|shaman]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A speaker for the past is a shaman who specifically serves as the voice for spirits from her people's history. A speaker for the past is often an advocate of the ancestors of a specific group, the voice of experience, and a powerful resource that allows the past to aid the present.

**Mysteries of the Past**: A speaker for the past adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] (Cha), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] (Int), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] (Wis), and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] (Int) to her list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#Class Skills|class skills]]. She also adds the spells from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ancestor Mystery|ancestor]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Time Mystery|time]] oracle [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Mysteries|mysteries]] to her class spell list (as the cleric level for those spells).

This ability replaces the shaman's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit-animal|spirit animal]]. The speaker for the past must choose a time when she must spend 1 hour each day in quiet contemplation to regain her daily allotment of spells, but does not need to commune with a spirit animal during this time. Because she has no spirit animal, she does not gain a spirit animal feature from any spirit she bonds with.

**Revelations of the Past**: At 4th, 6th, 12th, 14th, and 20th levels, the speaker for the past can select a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] from the ancestor or time mysteries. She uses her shaman level as her oracle level for these revelations, and uses her Wisdom modifier in place of her Charisma modifier for the purposes of the revelation. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-wandering-spirit|wandering spirit]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-wandering-hex|wandering hex]].
