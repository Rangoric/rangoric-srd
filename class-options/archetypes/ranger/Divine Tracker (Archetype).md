# Divine Tracker

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-archetypes|ranger]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Blessed by his deity, a divine tracker hunts down those he deems deserving of his retribution. His weapon is likely to find purchase in his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]].

**Alignment**: A divine tracker's alignment must be within one step of his deity's, along either the law/chaos axis or the good/evil axis. This replaces the ranger's alignment.

**Favored Weapon**: At 1st level, a divine tracker is proficient with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-favored-weapon|favored weapon]] of his deity. If his deity's favored weapon is unarmed strike, he instead gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]] as a bonus feat. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-wild-empathy|wild empathy]].

**Blessings (Su)**: At 4th level, a divine tracker forms a close bond with his deity's ethos. He selects two [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-blessings|warpriest blessings]] from among those granted by his deity and gains their minor powers. A divine tracker can select an alignment blessing ([[rangoric-srd/class-options/blessings/Chaos (Blessing)|Chaos]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/blessings/Evil (Blessing)|Evil]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/blessings/Good (Blessing)|Good]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/blessings/Law (Blessing)|Law]]) only if his alignment matches that blessing. If a divine tracker isn't devoted to a particular deity, he still selects two blessings to represent his spiritual inclinations and abilities, subject to GM discretion. The restriction on alignment blessings still applies. A divine tracker uses his ranger level as his warpriest level to determine the effects of blessings. At 13th level, a divine tracker gains the major powers of his blessings. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-hunters-bond|hunter's bond]].
