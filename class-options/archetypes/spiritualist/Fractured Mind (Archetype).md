# Fractured Mind

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-archetypes|spiritualist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A fractured mind draws her powers from a fraction of her own soul resonating with powerful emotion; her phantom is an extension of her own thoughts and feelings rather than a spirit ally.

**Emotional Spellcasting**: A fractured mind's ability to cast spells is tied to the force of her own spirit rather than her connection to the spirit world. As a result, she uses her Charisma score rather than her Wisdom score to determine the highest spell level she can cast, the attack bonuses of spells she casts, and her bonus spells per day. This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-spells|spellcasting]].

**Emotional Power (Sp)**: The fractured mind gains a number of spell-like abilities, which are tied to her phantom's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-emotional-focus|emotional focus]]. She gains one spell-like ability at 5th level, a second at 7th level, a third at 9th level, and a fourth at 16th level. A fractured mind can use each of these abilities once per day, plus one additional time per day for every 4 spiritualist levels she possesses beyond the level at which she gained the spell-like ability. The attack bonuses for these spell-like abilities are equal to 1/2 the fractured mind's spiritualist class level + the fractured mind's Charisma modifier, rather than being based on the spell's level. The spell-like abilities granted by each emotional focus are listed below.

_[[rangoric-srd/class-options/emotional-focuses/Anger (Emotional Focus)|Anger]]_: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]_ (5th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]_ (7th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_ (9th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_ (16th).
