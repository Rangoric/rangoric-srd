# Onmyoji

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-archetypes|spiritualist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

An onmyoji calls her phantom through years of study in obscure divine traditions, and treats it as a partner and a tool in her work between the mundane and spirit worlds.

**Divine Spellcasting**: An onmyoji's spellcasting ability comes from divine rather than psychic power. As a divine caster, the onmyoji's spells use verbal components instead of thought components, and somatic components instead of emotion components ([[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Components|psychic components]]), and she uses an ofuda as a divine focus. Ofudas are scrolls with holy writings written on parchment, cloth, or wood (having the same cost as a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Holy Symbol, Wooden|wooden holy symbol]]) or metal (having the same cost as a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Holy Symbol, Silver|silver holy symbol]]). This ability alters [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-spells|spellcasting]].

**Spiritual Resistance (Su)**: An onmyoji's phantom provides her with protection from supernatural forces. While the phantom is confined within the onmyoji's consciousness (not stranded on the Ethereal Plane or fully manifested), it grants her a +2 bonus on saving throws against the spell-like and supernatural abilities of fey, outsiders, and incorporeal undead. At 6th level and every 6 levels thereafter, this bonus increases by 1 (to a maximum of +5 at 18th level). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-shared-consciousness|shared consciousness]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-fused-consciousness|fused consciousness]].

**Divine Teachings (Su)**: An onmyoji gains the ability to call upon her phantom to help her cast spells she normally couldn't. At 4th level and every 3 levels thereafter, an onmyoji can choose a single spell from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List|cleric spell list]] with a spell level she is currently able to cast, and add that spell to her list of spells known, at the same spell level as it appears on the cleric spell list. Because the onmyoji relies on her phantom's influence in order to cast these spells, she can cast spells added in this way only while the phantom is confined within her consciousness (not stranded on the Ethereal Plane or fully manifested). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-spiritual-interference|spiritual interference]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-greater-spiritual-interference|greater spiritual interference]].
