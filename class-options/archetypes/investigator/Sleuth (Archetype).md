# Sleuth

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-archetypes|investigator]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A sleuth is an investigator who relies on good fortune and guile rather than alchemy. Having no mystical energy intrinsic to her, she must forgo the more magical aspects of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-alchemy|alchemy]] to solve her mysteries with wits, gumption, and the fickle consideration of luck.

**Sleuth's Luck (Ex)**: At 1st level, a sleuth gains a fluctuating pool of luck, measuring her ability to get out of scrapes. At the start of each day, a sleuth has a number of luck points equal to her Charisma modifier (minimum 1). Her luck goes up or down throughout the day, but usually cannot go higher than her Charisma modifier (minimum 1), though feats, magic items, and spells that grant either [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] points can also grant a sleuth luck points equal to the amount of grit or panache they grant. A sleuth spends luck to accomplish deeds (see below), and regains luck in the following ways.

**Rolling a Natural 20 on a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] Check**: While on an investigation, a sleuth regains luck by uncovering secrets. Rolling a natural 20 on these skill checks while actively investigating causes the sleuth to regain 1 luck point. Such skill checks made for more mundane reasons (such as normal research or using Sense Motive to gain information during normal bargaining or while gambling) do not regain luck for the sleuth. Determining which skill checks qualify is up to the GM.

**Rolling a 6 or Higher on an Inspiration Roll**: When a sleuth rolls an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|inspiration]] die while on an investigation and the die comes up a 6 or higher, she regains 1 luck point. (If she rolls multiple inspiration dice at a time, she regains 1 luck point if the total is 6 or higher.) Like regaining luck via a natural 20 on a qualifying skill check, inspiration checks made for more mundane reasons don't qualify for regaining luck. The GM is the final arbitrator for what rolls qualify. It's possible for an investigator to regain 2 luck points on the same skill check, if both the qualifying skill check is a natural 20 and any inspiration roll made is a 6 or higher.

This and the 1st-level deeds below replace [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-alchemy|alchemy]]. A sleuth cannot take the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Alchemist Discovery (Investigator Talent)|alchemist discovery]] talent or any investigator talent that affects alchemy.

**Deeds**: Sleuths spend luck points to accomplish deeds. Most deeds grant a sleuth some momentary bonus or effect, but there are some that provide longer-lasting effects. Some deeds stay in effect as long as a sleuth has at least 1 luck point.

At 1st level, a sleuth gains the following deeds.

**Daring (Ex)**: At 1st level, a sleuth can spend 1 luck point when he makes an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (riding) check to roll d6 and add the result to the check. He can choose to add this die after he rolls. If the d6 roll is a natural 6, he rolls another d6 and adds it to the check. He can continue to do this as long as he rolls natural 6s, up to a number of times equal to his Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).

**Opportunistic Evasion (Ex)**: At 1st level, when an effect that still deals damage on a miss misses a sleuth’s Reflex, he can spend 1 luck point as an immediate action to instead take no damage for that effect.

**Sleuth's Initiative (Ex)**: At 1st level, as long as the sleuth has at least 1 luck point, he gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks. Furthermore, if he has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]] feat, his hands are free and unrestrained, and the weapon is not hidden, he can draw a single light or one-handed melee weapon as part of the initiative check. (Unlike with [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Deeds|swashbuckler deeds]], this does not have to be a piercing weapon.)

At 4th level, a sleuth gains the following deeds. These abilities replace [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-swift-alchemy|swift alchemy]].

**Make It Count (Ex)**: When a sleuth uses [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]], he can spend 1 luck point to apply an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] that he doesn't already know and which affects studied strike to his studied strike. He must be able to fulfill that talent's prerequisites.

**Run Like Hell (Ex)**: At 4th level, a sleuth can spend 1 luck point to gain a +20-foot bonus to his speed for 1 minute. Furthermore, while under the effect of this bonus, if he moves more than his normal speed in a round, he gains a +4 bonus to AC until the start of his next turn.

**Second Chance (Ex)**: At 4th level, when a sleuth rolls an inspiration die or uses daring deed, he can spend 1 luck point to reroll either the inspiration or the daring deed die. If he rolls a 6 or higher on this reroll, he does not regain a luck point, and no matter what he rolls on either roll, he must keep the reroll result, even if it is lower.
