# Blood Conduit

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-archetypes|bloodrager]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Blood conduits learn to channel their arcane might directly through their flesh, without the need for mystical words or gestures.

**Contact Specialist (Ex)**: At 1st level, a blood conduit selects a bonus feat from the following: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Reposition|Improved Reposition]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]]. He does not need to meet the prerequisites to take this feat. He also adds those feats to his list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodline|bloodline feats]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-fast-movement|fast movement]] and alters bloodline feats.

**Spell Conduit (Su)**: At 5th level, as long as a blood conduit is wearing light or no armor, he can deliver [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-spells|bloodrager spells]] with a range of touch through bodily contact. When he succeeds at a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Performing a Combat Maneuver|combat maneuver check]] to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^pinned|pin]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]], or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] an opponent, or makes an unarmed strike against an enemy, he can as a swift action cast a touch spell on the creature, requiring no further touch attack roll. If this spell would usually require a successful touch attack, his successful combat maneuver check or unarmed strike counts as this attack. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].

**Reflexive Conduit (Ex)**: At 14th level, a blood conduit can discharge his power into foes that attempt bodily contact with him. While wearing light or no armor, when the blood conduit is subject to a combat maneuver check made to bull rush, grapple, pin, reposition, or trip him, as an immediate action he can target his attacker with a bloodrager spell that has a range of touch. If the spell would normally require a touch attack, a blood conduit can attempt a combat maneuver check for this attack instead. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-indomitable-will|indomitable will]].
