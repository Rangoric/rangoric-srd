# Spelleater

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-archetypes|bloodrager]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Where other bloodragers learn to avoid or shrug off minor damage of all sorts, spelleaters tap into the power of their bloodline in order to heal damage as it comes, and can even cannibalize their own magical energy to heal more damage and continue taking the fight to the enemy.

**Blood of Life (Su)**: A spelleater's blood empowers him to slowly recover from his wounds. At 2nd level, while [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodraging]] a spelleater gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]] 1. At 7th level and every 3 levels thereafter, this increases by 1 (to a maximum of fast healing 6 at 19th level). If the spelleater gains an increase to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] from a bloodline, feat, or other ability, he is considered to have an effective damage reduction of 0, and the increase is instead added to this effective damage reduction. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-damage-reduction|damage reduction]].

**Spell Eating (Su)**: At 5th level, a spelleater can consume spell slots for an extra dose of healing. As a swift action, the spelleater can consume one unused [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-spells|bloodrager spell]] slot to heal 1d8 damage for each level of the spell slot consumed. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].
