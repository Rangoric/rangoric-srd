# Greenrager

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-archetypes|bloodrager]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Typically, nature finds its greatest harmony with divine magic, but sometimes a connection with the natural world manifests itself through the arcane current in the veins of the bloodragers called greenragers. These bloodragers funnel their eldritch heritage into abilities that allow them to call powerful allies from nature and empower them with their bloodrage.

**Unfettered Fury (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a greenrager's fury allows him to move through undergrowth with frightening speed and grace. This functions as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|druid]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]] class feature, but only while the greenrager is [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodraging]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-blood-sanctuary|blood sanctuary]].

**Summoning Rager (Sp)**: At 6th level, a greenrager's magic unlocks the secrets of summoning allies from nature. He adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally I]]_ to his list of 1st-level bloodrager spells known as a bonus spell, as if it were a bonus bloodrager spell. At 7th level, he adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally II|summon nature's ally II]]_ as a bonus 2nd-level bloodrager spell, at 10th level he adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally III|summon nature's ally III]]_ as a bonus 3rd-level bloodrager spell, and at 13th level he adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IV|summon nature's ally IV]]_ as a bonus 4th-level bloodrager spell. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodline|bloodline feat]] gained at 6th level.

**Furious Summoning (Su)**: At 9th level, creatures summoned by the bloodrager's _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally]]_ spell gain a +4 morale bonus to Strength and Constitution and gain the druid's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]] ability. At 11th level, this bonus increases to +6. At 20th level, it increases to +8. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodline|bloodline feat]] at 9th level.
