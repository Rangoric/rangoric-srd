# Bogborn Alchemist

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Grippli|gripplis]]._

Some grippli alchemists are particularly attuned to the swamps and the dangerous creatures that inhabit them; these serve as their laboratories and research subjects, respectively. These bogborn alchemists have the following class features.

**Class Skills**: A bogborn alchemist adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] (Str) to his list of class skills.

**Amphibious Mutagen (Ex)**: At 1st level, when a bogborn alchemist uses a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|mutagen]], he may choose to have his mutagen form enhanced for aquatic movement. This gives him the [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Amphibious|amphibious]] special quality, his feet elongate, and the webbing between his fingers and toes expands, granting a swim speed of 15 feet. This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-throw-anything|throw anything]].

**Discovery Options**: A bogborn alchemist can select the [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Toxic Skin|toxic skin]] [[rangoric-srd/races/Grippli|grippli]] racial trait in place of an alchemical [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]].

**Discoveries**: The following [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] complement the bogborn alchemist archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Chameleon (Discovery)|chameleon]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Concentrate Poison (Discovery)|concentrate poison]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Sticky Poison (Discovery)|sticky poison]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Nauseating Flesh (Discovery)|nauseating flesh]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Poison Conversion (Discovery)|poison conversion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Bottled Ooze (Discovery)|bottled ooze]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Tanglefoot Bomb (Discovery)|tanglefoot bomb]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Tentacle (Discovery)|tentacle]].
