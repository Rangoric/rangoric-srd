# Psychonaut

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

A psychonaut uses his knowledge to explore altered states of consciousness and even other planes of existence. A psychonaut has the following class features.

**Bomb**: A psychonaut's bombs deal damage one die step lower than normal (regular bombs deal d4s, concussive bombs deal 1d3s, and so on). This ability otherwise functions as and replaces the standard alchemist [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] class feature.

**Precognition**: At 5th level, a psychonaut adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]* to his formula book as a 2nd-level extract (this extract does not require a divine focus component). This replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison resistance]] +4.

**Psychic Senses**: At 8th level, a psychonaut adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Clairaudience-Clairvoyance|clairaudience/clairvoyance]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Scrying|detect scrying]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]* to his formula book as 3rd-level extracts (a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]* extract does not require a focus or divine focus component). When a psychonaut uses a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]* extract, he gains the power to hear the lingering spirit within a corpse rather than enabling the corpse to audibly speak, but the extract otherwise works as the spell. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison resistance]] +6.

**Remote Consciousness**: At 10th level, a psychonaut adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dream|dream]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection, Lesser|lesser astral projection]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nightmare (Spell)|nightmare]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sending|sending]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telepathic Bond|telepathic bond]]* to his formula book as 4th-level extracts (a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]* extract does not require a focus component). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison immunity]].

**Greater Precognition**: At 15th level, a psychonaut adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Moment of Prescience|moment of prescience]]* to his formula book as a 5th-level extract. This ability replaces the increase to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] damage at this level.

**Master Precognition**: At 17th level, a psychonaut adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Foresight|foresight]]* to his formula book as a 6th-level extract. This ability replaces the increase to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] damage at this level.

**Discoveries**: The following [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] complement the psychonaut archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Confusion Bomb (Discovery)|confusion bomb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Eternal Potion (Discovery)|eternal potion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Extend Potion (Discovery)|extend potion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Lingering Spirit (Discovery)|lingering spirit]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Madness Bomb (Discovery)|madness bomb]].
