# Preservationist

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Some alchemists are obsessed with collecting and preserving exotic creatures. These preservationists may use bottled animals and monsters as teaching tools, but some learn how to reanimate them for short periods to battle on the alchemist's behalf. A preservationist has the following class features.

**Bottled Ally I**: At 2nd level, a preservationist adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] (Cha) to his list of class skills. He adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally I]]* to his formula book as a 1st-level extract. When he prepares that extract, he actually prepares a tiny, preserved specimen in a bottle (as with a caster casting the spell, the preservationist doesn't have to choose the creature until he uses the extract). When the alchemist opens the bottle, the specimen animates and grows to normal size, serving the preservationist as per the spell and otherwise being treated as a summoned creature. When the duration expires, the preserved creature decays into powder. If the preservationist has the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Infusion (Discovery)|infusion]] discovery, another character can use the infused specimen. The [[rangoric-srd/feats/Augment Summoning|Augment Summoning]] feat can be applied to these specimens. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-use|poison use]].

**Bottled Ally II**: At 5th level, a preservationist adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally II|summon nature's ally II]]* to his formula book as a 2nd-level extract. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison resistance]] +4.

**Bottled Ally III**: At 8th level, a preservationist adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IV|summon nature's ally IV]]* to his formula book as a 3rd-level extract. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison resistance]] +6.

**Bottled Ally IV**: At 10th level, a preservationist adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally V|summon nature's ally V]]* to his formula book as a 4th-level extract. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison immunity]].

**Bottled Ally V**: At 14th level, a preservationist adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally VII|summon nature's ally VII]]* to his formula book as a 5th-level extract. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-persistent-mutagen|persistent mutagen]].

**Bottled Ally VI**: At 18 level, a preservationist adds *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IX|summon nature's ally IX]]* to his formula book as a 6th-level extract. This ability replaces his 18th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]].

**Discoveries**: The following [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] complement the preservationist archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Bottled Ooze (Discovery)|bottled ooze]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Preserve Organs (Discovery)|preserve organs]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Tentacle (Discovery)|tentacle]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Vestigial Arm (Discovery)|vestigial arm]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Wings (Discovery)|wings]].
