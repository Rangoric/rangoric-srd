# Fire Bomber

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|goblins]]._

Fire bombers are exceptionally good at using bombs to burn creatures and blow things up, but are not quite as good at creating other types of bombs or extracts. A fire bomber has the following class features.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A fire bomber treats [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Torch|torches]] as a simple weapon ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-proficiency|weapon and armor proficiency]]).

**Fire Bombardier (Su or Ex)**: At 1st level, when a fire bomber throws a bomb that deals fire damage, all creatures in the splash radius take an additional point of damage per die of fire damage dealt. Fire bombers only add their Intelligence bonus to damage from bombs or alchemical substances that deal fire damage. This otherwise works like the alchemist's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-throw-anything|throw anything]] abilities. This ability alters bomb and throw anything.

**Bonus Feats**: A fire bomber can select the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Burn! Burn! Burn!|Burn! Burn! Burn!]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fire Tamer|Fire Tamer]], or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flame Heart|Flame Heart]] feat in place of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]].

**Fiery Cocktail (Su)**: At 4th level, whenever a fire bomber uses a discovery that deals damage other than fire damage, he can split the damage dice evenly between the bomb's primary damage type and 1d6 points of fire damage; when there is an odd number of damage dice, the odd die of damage comes from the primary damage type. For example, an 8th-level fire bomber could throw a concussive bomb that deals 2d6 points of fire damage and 3d4 points of sonic damage. Additional effects from the bomb still apply, but the attack bonus for admixture bombs is reduced by 2. This replaces the alchemist's 4th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]].

**Fire Body (Ex)**: At 8th level, a fire bomber adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body I|elemental body I]]_ to his extract list as a 3rd-level extract. _Elemental body_ extracts prepared using fire body are limited to fire elementals only. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison resistance]] +6.

**Improved Fire Body (Ex)**: At 10th level, fire bombers add _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body II|elemental body II]]_ to their spell list as a 4th-level extract. _Elemental body_ extracts prepared using improved fire body are limited to fire elementals only. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison immunity]].

**Greater Fire Body (Ex)**: At 14th level, fire bombers add _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body IV|elemental body IV]]_ to their spell list as a 5th-level extract. _Elemental body_ extracts prepared using greater fire body are limited to fire elementals only. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-persistent-mutagen|persistent mutagen]].

**Discoveries**: The following [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] complement the fire bomber archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Fire Brand (Discovery)|fire brand]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Rocket Bomb (Discovery)|rocket bomb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Explosive Bomb (Discovery)|explosive bombs]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Fast Bombs (Discovery)|fast bombs]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Inferno Bomb (Discovery)|inferno bomb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Precise Bombs (Discovery)|precise bombs]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Breath Weapon Bomb (Discovery)|breath weapon bomb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Explosive Missile (Discovery)|explosive missile]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Immolation Bomb (Discovery)|immolation bomb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Bottled Ooze (Discovery)|bottled ooze]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Confusion Bomb (Discovery)|confusion bomb]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Strafe Bomb (Discovery)|strafe bomb]].
