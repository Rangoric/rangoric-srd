# Deep Bomber

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Svirfneblin|svirfneblin]]._

Consumed with keeping hidden from the horrors below the surface, svirfneblin use their proclivity for alchemy and talent for obfuscation to strike from the darkness and retreat unseen. A deep bomber has the following class features.

**Silent Bomb**: At 2nd level, when the deep bomber creates a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]], he can choose to have it explode without making any noise, although those damaged by it may cry out. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-use|poison use]].

**Targeting Bomb\***: At 3rd level, when the deep bomber creates a bomb, he can choose to have its detonation include a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_ effect that applies to all creatures within the splash radius (including the target, if any). This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-swift-alchemy|swift alchemy]] (the deep bomber gains swift alchemy at 18th level and never gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-instant-alchemy|instant alchemy]]).

**Stonekin**: At 6th level, the deep bomber automatically learns _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tree Shape|tree shape]]_ as a 2nd-level extract, except instead of a tree, he takes the form of a stalagmite that is the same size as his current size. At 7th level, he automatically learns _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]_ as a 3rd-level extract. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-swift-poisoning|swift poisoning]].

**Discoveries**: The following [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] complement the deep bomber archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Delayed Bomb (Discovery)|delayed bomb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Dispelling Bomb (Discovery)|dispelling bomb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Fast Bombs (Discovery)|fast bombs]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Infusion (Discovery)|infusion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Madness Bomb (Discovery)|madness bomb]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Poison Bomb (Discovery)|poison bomb]].
