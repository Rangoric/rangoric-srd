# Internal Alchemist

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

An internal alchemist studies medicine, diet, and the living body to purify the self in the hope of gaining immortality by means of alchemical concoctions and controlling vital energy. Internal alchemists develop unusual physical abilities from heightened knowledge of how their bodies work. An internal alchemist has the following class features.

**Breath Mastery**: At 1st level, the internal alchemist can control his breath and the flow of vital energy within his body. Without preparation, he can hold his breath for a number of minutes equal to his Constitution score (after this, he must begin making Constitution checks or risk [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Suffocation|suffocation]]); by spending a full-round action preparing himself, he can increase this duration to 1 hour per point of Constitution. The alchemist can survive twice as long as normal without [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Starvation and Thirst|food or water]] before he starts to take penalties. He can put himself into a state of suspended animation as a move action, and is then [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] and appears completely dead; he awakens at a preset time or in response to a condition set by him when he enters this state. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-throw-anything|Throw Anything]].

**Bonus Feats**: An internal alchemist can select [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Perception), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Extra Ki|Extra *Ki*]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]] (unarmed strike), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Iron Will|Iron Will]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lightning Reflexes|Lightning Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (unarmed strike) in place of an alchemical [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]].

**Disease Resistance**: At 3rd level, an internal alchemist gains a bonus on all defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]] equal to his alchemist class bonus against poison. When he gains the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-resistance|poison immunity]] alchemist class ability, he becomes immune to disease as well. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-swift-alchemy|swift alchemy]].

**Uncanny Dodge**: Starting at 6th level ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]]), an internal alchemist can react to danger before his senses would normally allow him to do so. He cannot be caught [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]], nor does he lose his Dexterity bonus to AC if the attacker is [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]]. However, he still loses his Dexterity bonus to AC if he is immobilized, or if an opponent successfully uses the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] action (see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]]) against him. If the internal alchemist already has uncanny dodge from a different class, he automatically gains improved uncanny dodge (as a rogue, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]]) instead. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-swift-poisoning|swift poisoning]].

**Discoveries**: The following [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] complement the internal alchemist archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Cognatogen (Discovery)|cognatogen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Elixir of Life (Discovery)|elixir of life]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Grand Cognatogen (Discovery)|grand cognatogen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Greater Cognatogen (Discovery)|greater cognatogen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Lingering Spirit (Discovery)|lingering spirit]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Mummification (Discovery)|mummification]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Spontaneous Healing (Discovery)|spontaneous healing]].
