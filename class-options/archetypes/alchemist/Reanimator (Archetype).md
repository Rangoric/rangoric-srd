# Reanimator

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A reanimator is an alchemist who has discovered how to infuse a corpse with a semblance of life. Many work in tandem with necromancers to explore the fine border between the worlds of the living and the dead. A reanimator has the following class features.

**Bomb**: A reanimator's bombs deal damage one die step lower than normal (regular bombs deal d4s, concussive bombs deal 1d3s, and so on). This ability otherwise functions as and replaces the standard alchemist [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] class feature.

**Simple Reanimation**: At 7th level, a reanimator adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead, Lesser|lesser animate dead]]_ to his formula book as a 3rd-level extract. When he uses that extract, rather than drinking it, he injects it into the corpse he intends to animate, which rises as an undead creature under his control 1 hour later. The extract can only create [[rangoric-srd/monsters/zombie/Zombie|zombies]] (including [[rangoric-srd/monsters/zombie/Alternate Zombie#Variant Zombies|variant zombies]]). This ability replaces the increase to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] damage at this level.

**Create Undead**: At 13th level, a reanimator adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead|create undead]]_ to his formula book as a 4th-level extract. When he uses that extract, rather than drinking it, he injects it into the corpse he intends to animate, which rises as an uncontrolled undead 1 hour later. The extract can only create corporeal undead. This ability replaces the increase to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] damage at this level.

**Create Greater Undead**: At 15th level, a reanimator adds _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Greater Undead|create greater undead]]_ to his formula book as a 5th-level extract. This otherwise acts similarly to a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead|create undead]]_ extract. This ability replaces the increase to [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] damage at this level.

**Discoveries**: The following [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] complement the reanimator archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Alchemical Zombie (Discovery)|alchemical zombie]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Elixir of Life (Discovery)|elixir of life]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Eternal Potion (Discovery)|eternal potion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Mummification (Discovery)|mummification]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Preserve Organs (Discovery)|preserve organs]].
