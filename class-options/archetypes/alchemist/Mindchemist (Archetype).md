# Mindchemist

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While most alchemists use mutagens to boost their physical ability at the cost of mental ability, some use alchemy for the opposite purpose—to boost the power of the mind and memory. A mindchemist can reach incredible levels of mental acuity, but suffers lingering debilitating effects to his physique. A mindchemist has the following class features.

**Cognatogen**: At 1st level, a mindchemist learns how to create a cognatogen, as per the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Cognatogen (Discovery)|cognatogen]] discovery. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|mutagen]] class ability (a mindchemist cannot create mutagens unless he selects [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Mutagen (Discovery)|mutagen]] as a discovery).

**Perfect Recall**: At 2nd level, a mindchemist has honed his memory. When making a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] check, he may add his Intelligence bonus on the check a second time. Thus, a mindchemist with 5 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] and a +2 Intelligence bonus has a total skill bonus of +9 (5 + 2 + 2) using this ability. The mindchemist can also use this ability when making an Intelligence check to remember something. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-use|poison use]].

**Bonus Feats**: A mindchemist may select [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]], any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]]) in place of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]].

**Languages**: A mindchemist may learn three languages in place of a discovery.

**Discoveries**: The following [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Discoveries|discoveries]] complement the mindchemist archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Grand Cognatogen (Discovery)|grand cognatogen]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Greater Cognatogen (Discovery)|greater cognatogen]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Infuse Mutagen (Discovery)|infuse mutagen]].
