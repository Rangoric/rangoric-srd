# Winged Marauder

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-archetypes|alchemist]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|goblins]].*

Goblin alchemists have created a mixture of pheromones that they use to manipulate and entice giant vultures or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dire-animal/Dire Bat|dire bats]] to do their bidding. These goblins strap harnesses to the chests of these flying animals and hang beneath them, dropping flaming alchemical solutions on the helpless folks below.

**Class Skills**: A winged marauder gains [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] as class skills. These replace [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] as class skills.

**Flying Beast Tamer (Ex)**: At 1st level, a winged marauder acquires a flying mount as an animal companion. This works as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|druid]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] class feature, except the marauder must select a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dire-animal/Dire Bat|dire bat]] or giant vulture. The marauder's effective druid level is equal to his alchemist level. A marauder and his companion don't gain the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^animal-companion-share-spell|share spells]] ability. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|mutagen]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-persistent-mutagen|persistent mutagen]] class features.

**Discovery**: A winged marauder can select the following additional [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]].

**Sipping Pet (Ex)**: As a standard action, the marauder can administer a dose of a liquid (such as an extract, infusion, potion, or an alchemical liquid like antitoxin) to his animal companion, even if the companion is unconscious. The alchemist must be adjacent to or riding the companion to use this ability. An alchemist must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Infusion (Discovery)|infusion]] discovery to select this discovery.
