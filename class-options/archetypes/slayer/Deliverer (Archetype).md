# Deliverer

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-archetypes|slayer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Also known as a divine assassin, god's blade, or wrath-bringer, a deliverer is a weapon chosen by a god to punish those who have committed an affront to that deity. Evil deities are more likely to use assassins than deliverers, but some good deities use deliverers to deal with problems of a subtler nature than a cleric, inquisitor, paladin, or warpriest can typically handle.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A deliverer gains proficiency with his deity's favored weapon, in addition to the slayer's normal weapon proficiencies. This ability alters the slayer's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#Class Features|weapon and armor proficiency]].

**Determined Zeal (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a deliverer shrugs off attempts by his moral opposite to control or kill him. On any round in which a deliverer attacks an opponent whose alignment is at least two steps away from his own (such as a lawful good deliverer fighting a chaotic good or lawful evil foe, or a neutral foe with no other alignment components), he gains a +2 bonus on Will saving throws against that opponent's abilities, as well as the benefit of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]] feat until the end of his next turn. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 2nd level.

**True Believer (Ex)**: At 6th level, mortal wounds cannot stop a deliverer from performing his divine duty. On any round in which he attacks an opponent whose alignment is at least two steps away from his own (see Determined Zeal, above) while benefiting from the Diehard feat, he isn't [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] from having negative hit points, nor does he automatically lose 1 hit point per round. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 6th level.

**Divine Anathema (Ex)**: At 10th level, as a free action a deliverer can declare a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied target]] to be his divine anathema. The studied target's alignment must be at least two steps away from that of the deliverer. Against this target, the deliverer's attacks deal an additional 2d6 points of damage; this damage is directly from divine power and does not stack with the bonus damage from a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Holy|holy]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Unholy|unholy]]* weapon, or a weapon with a similar weapon special ability or effect. A deliverer can have only one divine anathema at a time. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talent]] gained at 10th level.

**Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue Talent (Slayer Talent)|rogue talents]]) complement the deliverer archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Combat Trick (Rogue Talent)|combat trick]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Finesse Rogue (Rogue Talent)|finesse rogue]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Foil Scrutiny (Slayer Talent)|foil scrutiny]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Terrain Mastery (Rogue Talent)|terrain mastery]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Trap Spotter (Rogue Talent)|trap spotter]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Trapfinding (Slayer Talent)|trapfinding]].

**Advanced Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue and Ninja Advanced Talents (Slayer Talent)|advanced rogue talents]]) complement the deliverer archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Assassinate (Slayer Talent)|assassinate]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ninja-tricks/Evasion (Ninja Trick)|evasion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hunter's Surprise (Rogue Talent)|hunter's surprise]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Master of Disguise (Rogue Talent)|master of disguise]].

**Ex-Deliverers**: A deliverer who grossly violates the code of conduct required by his deity loses all class features granted by this archetype, except for the weapon proficiencies. (He does not gain the slayer class abilities that the archetype replaces.) He cannot thereafter gain levels as a deliverer of that deity until he atones for his deeds (see the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Atonement|atonement]]* spell description).
