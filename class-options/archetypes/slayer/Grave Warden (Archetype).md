# Grave Warden

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-archetypes|slayer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While paladins and inquisitors use their connection with the divine to fight undead hordes and other horrors of the night, a grave warden relies on knowledge, skill with weapons, and tenacity to put an end to these night-born terrors.

**Holy Water Sprinkler (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a grave warden can draw a flask of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Holy Water|holy water]] as if it were a weapon (and can thus use the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]] feat to draw flasks of holy water). As a swift action, he can open a flask of holy water and pour it onto a held or adjacent melee weapon. If the weapon successfully hits an undead creature before the end of the grave warden's next turn, the undead takes damage as if it took a direct hit from the holy water, in addition to the damage from the weapon, if any. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 2nd level.

**Death Ward (Sp)**: At 7th level, a grave warden learns to perform a short ritual that grants the benefits of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]_, using his slayer level as his caster level. Performing this ritual takes 1 minute and uses 4 flasks of holy water. The grave warden can protect only himself with this ability. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-stalker|stalker]].

**Dustbringer (Ex)**: At 10th level, a grave warden can slay a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied]] undead opponent. This functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Assassinate (Slayer Talent)|assassinate]] slayer talent, except it attacks Will instead of Fortitude, affects only undead, and destroys the target instead of killing it. If the attack misses the target, it becomes immune to that grave warden's dustbringer ability for 24 hours. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talent]] gained at 10th level.

**Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue Talent (Slayer Talent)|rogue talents]]) complement the grave warden archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Stealth (Rogue Talent)|fast stealth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Finesse Rogue (Rogue Talent)|finesse rogue]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Swift Poison (Rogue Talent)|swift poison]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Terrain Mastery (Rogue Talent)|terrain mastery]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Trap Spotter (Rogue Talent)|trap spotter]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Unwitting Ally (Rogue Talent)|unwitting ally]].

**Advanced Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talents]] (also available to rogues) complement the grave warden archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hunter's Surprise (Rogue Talent)|hunter's surprise]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Slayer Camouflage (Slayer Talent)|slayer camouflage]].
