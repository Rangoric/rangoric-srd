# Cutthroat

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-archetypes|slayer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]].*

Street-smart and extremely resourceful, cutthroats stalk city streets and alleyways, preying on those unfortunate enough to catch their eye. More opportunists than careful planners, they don't care who they rob or kill, and take advantage of whatever circumstances put them near a suitable target.

**Class Skills**: A cutthroat adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] (Cha) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] (Int) to his list of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#Class Skills|class skills]], and removes [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] (Int) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] (Wis).

**Street Stalker (Ex)**: At 1st level, a cutthroat selects the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] skill, or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb. Once selected, this cannot be changed. When in urban environments, the cutthroat adds 1/2 his slayer level (minimum 1) to checks attempted with this skill. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-track|track]].

**Opportune Target (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a cutthroat who catches a foe by surprise can quickly study that foe to find its weaknesses. If a cutthroat can act in the surprise round, once during that surprise round he can study a foe as a free action (instead of a move action) and select it as his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied target]]. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 2nd level.

**Stab and Grab (Ex)**: At 6th level, as a swift action a cutthroat can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] combat maneuver against a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dead|dead]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] target or a studied target he has just confirmed a critical hit against. A dead or unconscious opponent takes a –10 penalty to its [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against this maneuver. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 6th level.

**Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue Talent (Slayer Talent)|rogue talents]]) complement the cutthroat archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Fast Stealth (Rogue Talent)|fast stealth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Poison Use (Slayer Talent)|poison use]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Slowing Strike (Slayer Talent)|slowing strike]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Terrain Mastery (Rogue Talent)|terrain mastery]].

**Advanced Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue and Ninja Advanced Talents (Slayer Talent)|advanced rogue talents]]) complement the cutthroat archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Assassinate (Slayer Talent)|assassinate]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Knock-Out Blow (Rogue Talent)|knock-out blow]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Opportunist (Rogue Talent)|opportunist]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Slayer Camouflage (Slayer Talent)|slayer camouflage]].
