# Stygian Slayer

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-archetypes|slayer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

A stygian slayer crawls out of the darkest shadows to strike fear into the hearts of civilized folk. He's a merciless killer who can control a sliver of magic, allowing him to arrive unseen, commit murder, and depart without detection.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A stygian slayer is proficient with light armor, but not with medium armor, heavy armor, or any kind of shield (including tower shields). This replaces the slayer's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#Class Features|weapon and armor proficiency]].

**Invisibility (Sp)**: At 4th level, a stygian slayer can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_ once per day, using his slayer level as his caster level. The slayer uses his Intelligence modifier for [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] when using this ability. The slayer can use this an additional time per day at 8th level and every 4 levels thereafter. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 4th level.

**Spell Use**: At 7th level, a stygian slayer is able to use spell completion and spell trigger items as if he were an arcane caster with these spells on his spell list: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Forced Quiet|forced quiet]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Modify Memory|modify memory]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantom Steed|phantom steed]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]]_, and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] spells of the illusion school of spell level 0 through 4th. The slayer uses his class level as his caster level for this ability. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-stalker|stalker]].

**Shadowy Mist Form (Sp)**: At 10th level, a stygian slayer can transform into an inky black cloud of mist. This functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_, except it also obscures vision as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]_. The slayer can use this ability for a number of minutes per day equal to his level. These minutes need not be consecutive, but must be used in 1-minute increments. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talent]] gained at 10th level.

**Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue Talent (Slayer Talent)|rogue talents]]) complement the stygian slayer archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Bleeding Attack (Rogue Talent)|bleeding attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Lasting Poison (Rogue Talent)|lasting poison]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Poison Use (Slayer Talent)|poison use]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Powerful Sneak (Rogue Talent)|powerful sneak]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rogue Crawl (Rogue Talent)|rogue crawl]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slow Reactions (Rogue Talent)|slow reactions]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Swift Poison (Rogue Talent)|swift poison]].

**Advanced Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue and Ninja Advanced Talents (Slayer Talent)|advanced rogue talents]]) complement the stygian slayer archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Assassinate (Slayer Talent)|assassinate]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hunter's Surprise (Rogue Talent)|hunter's surprise]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Slayer Camouflage (Slayer Talent)|slayer camouflage]].
