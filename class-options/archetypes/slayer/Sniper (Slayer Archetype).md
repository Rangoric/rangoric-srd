# Sniper

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-archetypes|slayer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Whether it's with a bullet from a sling, a quarrel from a crossbow, or even an expertly thrown dagger, the sniper ensures that a single shot disables his targets.

**Accuracy (Ex)**: At 1st level, a sniper halves all range increment penalties when making ranged attacks with a bow, crossbow, or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-track|track]].

**Deadly Range (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a sniper increases the range at which he can apply his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage by 10 feet. Whenever the sniper is able to select a new [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]], he can instead choose to increase this range by an additional 5 feet. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 2nd level.

**Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue Talent (Slayer Talent)|rogue talents]]) complement the sniper archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Camouflage (Rogue Talent)|camouflage]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Snap Shot (Rogue Talent)|snap shot]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Sniper's Eye (Rogue Talent)|sniper's eye]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Weapon Training (Rogue Talent)|weapon training]].

**Advanced Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talents]] (which are also available to rogues) complement the sniper archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Deadly Sneak (Rogue Talent)|deadly sneak]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Master of Disguise (Rogue Talent)|master of disguise]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Stealthy Sniper (Rogue Talent)|stealthy sniper]].
