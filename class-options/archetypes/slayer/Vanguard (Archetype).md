# Vanguard

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-archetypes|slayer]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

Vanguards are battlefield commanders who focus on the brutality of combat and lead their allies to bloody victory. Quick to react to danger, a vanguard is a valuable scout, capable officer, and skilled tactician.

**Lookout (Ex)**: At 1st level, a vanguard adds 1/2 his level (minimum 1) to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity|initiative]] checks. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-track|track]].

**Tactician (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a vanguard receives a [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^teamwork-feats|teamwork feat]] as a bonus feat. He must meet the prerequisites for this feat. Once per day as a standard action, the vanguard can grant one of his teamwork feats to all allies within 30 feet who can see and hear him. Allies retain the use of this bonus feat for 3 rounds plus 1 round for every 2 levels the vanguard possesses. Allies do not need to meet the prerequisites of this bonus feat. Whenever the vanguard is able to select a new [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]], he can instead choose to gain an additional use per day of this ability. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 2nd level.

**Vanguard's Bond (Ex)**: At 4th level, a vanguard forms a bond with his fighting companions. As a move action, he can choose one of his current [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied targets]] and grant half his studied target bonus against that target to all allies within 30 feet who can see or hear him. This bonus lasts for a number of rounds equal to the slayer's Intelligence modifier (minimum 1). This bonus does not stack with any [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] or studied target bonuses possessed by his allies; they use whichever bonus is higher. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] gained at 4th level.

**Ever Ready (Ex)**: At 7th level, a vanguard is never considered an unaware combatant and is always able to act in the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]]. The vanguard is still [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] in the surprise round until he acts. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-stalker|stalker]].

**Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talents]] complement the vanguard archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Combat Trick (Rogue Talent)|combat trick]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Finesse Rogue (Rogue Talent)|finesse rogue]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Hard to Fool (Rogue Talent)|hard to fool]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rogue Crawl (Rogue Talent)|rogue crawl]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Surprise Attack (Rogue Talent)|surprise attack]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Terrain Mastery (Rogue Talent)|terrain mastery]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Weapon Training (Rogue Talent)|weapon training]].

**Advanced Slayer Talents**: The following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talents]] (and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Rogue and Ninja Advanced Talents (Slayer Talent)|advanced rogue talents]]) complement the vanguard archetype: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Feat (Rogue Talent)|feat]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Opportunist (Rogue Talent)|opportunist]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Slayer Camouflage (Slayer Talent)|slayer camouflage]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Unwitting Ally (Rogue Talent)|unwitting ally]].
