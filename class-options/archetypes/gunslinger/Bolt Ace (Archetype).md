# Bolt Ace

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-archetypes|gunslinger]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes|archetype]]._

While gunslingers are full of sound and fury, there is a class of gunslingers that never soil their hands with powder or feel the sting of gun smoke. They commit their deeds in a more hushed manner, but with just as much flair.

**Grit (Ex)**: A bolt ace regains grit when she scores a critical hit or deals a killing blow with any kind of crossbow. This ability modifies the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]] class feature.

**Deeds**: A bolt ace can perform the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-deeds|deeds]] with a crossbow instead of a firearm: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Gunslinger Initiative (Deed)|gunslinger initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Pistol-Whip (Deed)|pistol-whip]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Dead Shot (Deed)|dead shot]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Targeting (Deed)|targeting]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Bleeding Wound (Gunslinger Deed)|bleeding wound]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Death's Shot (Deed)|death's shot]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Stunning Shot (Deed)|stunning shot]]. The bolt ace swaps the following deeds.

**Sharp Shoot (Ex)**: At 1st level, a bolt ace can resolve an attack against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^touch-attacks|touch AC]] instead of normal AC when firing a crossbow at a target within its first range increment. Performing this deed costs 1 grit point. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Deadeye (Deed)|deadeye]].

**Vigilant Loading (Ex)**: At 1st level, as long as a bolt ace has at least 1 grit point, she does not provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] when loading a crossbow. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Quick Clear (Deed)|quick clear]].

**Shooter's Resolve (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a bolt ace can spend 1 grit point when making a crossbow attack as a standard action and ignore the effects of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] (though not total concealment) and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|cover]] (other than total cover) against that shot. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Utility Shot (Deed)|utility shot]].

**Distracting Shot (Ex)**: At 7th level, a bolt ace can spend 1 grit point and choose to miss a target that she could normally attack within her range with a crossbow attack. When she does, the target loses its Dexterity bonus to AC (if any) for 1 round. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Startling Shot (Deed)|startling shot]].

**Vigilant Shooter (Ex)**: At 11th level, as long as a bolt ace spends 1 grit point when she does so, she does not provoke attacks of opportunity when firing a crossbow. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Expert Loading (Deed)|expert loading]].

**Inexplicable Reload (Ex)**: At 11th level, loading a crossbow becomes unthinking and automatic for a bolt ace. As long as she has at least 1 grit point, she always starts each round of combat (even a surprise round) with her crossbow loaded. Also the amount of time needed to reload a crossbow decreases by one step: a standard action becomes a move action, a move action becomes a swift action, a swift action becomes a free action, and a free action becomes not an action. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Lightning Reload (Deed)|lightning reload]].

**Pinning Shot (Ex)**: At 15th level, the bolt ace can spend 1 grit point while shooting a crossbow and attempt to pin down its target with the bolt. If the crossbow attack hits, it pins the target to a nearby object, to a wall, or to the ground, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangling]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggering]] the target. While pinned by the bolt, the target cannot move out of its space until it takes a standard action to free itself from the pinning bolt, except by means of teleportation. This deed replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Menacing Shot (Deed)|menacing shot]].

**Crossbow Training (Ex)**: Starting at 5th level, a bolt ace can select one specific type of crossbow, such as [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Hand Crossbow|hand crossbow]] or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Heavy Crossbow|heavy crossbow]]. She gains a bonus on damage rolls equal to her Dexterity modifier with that crossbow. Furthermore, when she scores a critical hit with that type of crossbow, her critical modifier increases by 1 (a ×2 becomes a ×3, for example).

Every 4 levels thereafter, she can pick a different type of crossbow, gaining the bonus damage and increased critical multiplier with that type of crossbow as well. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-gun-training|gun training]].
