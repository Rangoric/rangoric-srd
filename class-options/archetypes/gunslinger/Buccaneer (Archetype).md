# Buccaneer

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-archetypes|gunslinger]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/Archetypes#Racial Archetypes|racial archetype]] for [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|humans]].*

Freebooters who cling to the convoluted codes that rule independent ships, the buccaneer is a gunslinger of the high seas. Their exploits are fueled by the thrill of danger and often by the temporary courage provided by grog. A buccaneer has the following class features.

**Deeds**: A buccaneer swaps four of the normal gunslinger [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-deeds|deeds]] for the following deeds.

**Seadog's Gait (Ex)**: At 1st level, the buccaneer gains Sea Legs as a bonus feat. If she spends 1 grit point, she can ignore [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement#Terrain and Obstacles|difficult terrain]] until the end of her turn. This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Quick Clear (Deed)|quick clear]] deed.

**Pirate's Jargon (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the buccaneer's baffling palaver of nautical jargon and piratical cant provides a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks. Furthermore, she can spend a swift action and 1 grit point to cause a single living creature within 30 feet to make a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 her buccaneer level + her Charisma modifier) or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting language-dependent effect. This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Pistol-Whip (Deed)|pistol whip]] deed.

**Rope Swing (Ex)**: At 7th level, as long as the buccaneer has at least 1 grit point, she gains a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks equal to her gunslinger level when climbing or swinging on a rope. If she spends 1 grit point, her move while climbing or swinging on a rope does not provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]]. This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Dead Shot (Deed)|dead shot]] deed.

**Captain's Curse (Sp)**: At 11th level, a buccaneer can spend 2 grit points to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Old Salt’s Curse|old salt's curse]]* as a spell-like ability. At 15th level, she may use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Mark|black mark]]* instead. The caster level for these spell-like abilities is equal to the buccaneer's class level, and the save DC is equal to 10 + her buccaneer level + her Charisma modifier. This replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Lightning Reload (Deed)|lightning reload]] deed.

**Grit (Ex)**: Like the sea itself, a buccaneer is a force of nature. Instead of using her Wisdom modifier to determine the number of grit points she gains at the start of each day, she uses her Charisma modifier. This ability works in all other ways like the gunslinger's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]] class feature.

**Liquid Courage (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a buccaneer gains the ability to fortify her grit with strong drink. The act of drinking is a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity, and each drink provides 1 grog point. Grog points can be used in place of grit points to fuel deeds or grit feats (including those requiring a minimum of 1 grit point to use). The buccaneer can gain a maximum number of grog points each day equal to her Constitution modifier (minimum 1), and they last for 1 hour or until used, whichever comes first. She gains a morale bonus on saves against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] and a dodge bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity equal to her current grog point total. This ability replaces the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-nimble|nimble]] ability.

**Bonus Feat**: In addition to combat and grit feats, a buccaneer can select from the following feats as her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-bonus-feats|bonus feats]]: Expert Driver (water vehicles only), Master Siege Engineer, Siege Engineer, Siege Gunner, Skilled Driver (water vehicles only).

**Exotic Pet (Ex)**: At 5th level, a buccaneer gains a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^familiar-basics|familiar]] as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] of half the buccaneer's class level (though the exotic pet never gains the ability to deliver touch spells or share spells). This pet is typically a monkey or parrot (use the stats for a raven familiar). Such animals are useful scouts, even in the thick of combat. As long as the pet is within 30 feet of the buccaneer, the buccaneer also gains the benefit of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|evasion]]. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-gun-training|gun training]] 1.

**Sword and Pistol**: At 9th level, a buccaneer gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sword and Pistol|Sword and Pistol]] as a bonus feat even if she does not meet the prerequisites. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-gun-training|gun training]] 2.

**Gun Training (Ex)**: A buccaneer gains this [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-gun-training|ability]] only at 13th level with a single type of firearm.

**Raider's Riposte (Ex)**: At 17th level, whenever an enemy misses a buccaneer with an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]], it provokes an attack of opportunity from the buccaneer. This ability replaces [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-gun-training|gun training]] 4.
