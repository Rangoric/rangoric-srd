# Gift of Will

_A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-tricks|mesmerist trick]] (Su)._

A small portion of the mesmerist’s vast mental power buttresses his ally’s mind. The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject attempts a Will saving throw or a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] check, or is targeted by an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to demoralize her. For a saving throw, the subject uses the mesmerist’s Will saving throw bonus instead of her own. For a Sense Motive check or Intimidation check, she adds the mesmerist’s Charisma bonus as a morale bonus on the check or to the DC. The mesmerist can’t implant this trick on himself.
