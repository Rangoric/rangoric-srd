# Linked Reaction

_A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-tricks|mesmerist trick]] (Su)._

A hive mind allows the mesmerist and an ally to share one another’s reaction times. The mesmerist can trigger this trick when either the subject or the mesmerist is surprised and the other isn’t (even if the mesmerist would normally be unable to take actions because of being surprised). Both the mesmerist and the subject can act in this [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]]. The mesmerist can’t implant this trick on himself.
