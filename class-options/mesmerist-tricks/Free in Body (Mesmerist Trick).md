# Free in Body

*A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-masterful-tricks|masterful trick]] (Su).*

When constrained, the subject can easily slip loose. The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject begins its turn [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]] or under an effect that impedes its movement (including [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralysis]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Solid Fog|solid fog]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]*). The subject gains the benefits of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]* for 1 minute.
