# Astounding Avoidance

_A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-tricks|mesmerist trick]] (Su)._

The subject can avoid damage from magical and unusual attacks. The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject attempts a saving throw against an attack or spell that normally deals half damage on a successful save, before the subject rolls the save. If the subject succeeds at her save, she takes no damage. If the mesmerist is 12th level or higher, the subject takes only half damage if she fails her save. The benefits of this trick don’t stack with those of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|evasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-stalwart|stalwart]] class features.
