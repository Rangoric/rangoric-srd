# Astounding Avoidance

_A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-tricks|mesmerist trick]] (Su)._

The subject can avoid damage from magical and unusual attacks. The mesmerist can trigger this trick when an attack or spell that normally deals half damage on a miss attacks one of the subject’s defenses, before the attack is rolled. If it misses her, she takes no damage. If the mesmerist is 12th level or higher, the subject takes only half damage if she fails her save. The benefits of this trick don’t stack with those of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|evasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-stalwart|stalwart]] class features.
