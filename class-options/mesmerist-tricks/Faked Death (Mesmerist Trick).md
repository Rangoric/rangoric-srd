# Faked Death

_A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-masterful-tricks|masterful trick]] (Su)._

The subject appears to die, but actually can stealthily move about. The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject is damaged by an attack. The subject seems to fall down dead (as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Programmed Image|programmed image]]_), but actually turns invisible (as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_) for a number of rounds equal to the mesmerist’s level or until she attacks. Once the subject becomes visible again, the illusory dead body disappears. These are illusion effects, and they attack the Will of a creature that interacts with the false body, which disbelieves the effect on a miss. The false body is a figment; the invisibility is a glamer.
