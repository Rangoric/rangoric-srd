# Spectral Smoke

_A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-tricks|mesmerist trick]] (Su)._

A cloud of smoke pops up around the subject, foiling attacks. The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject is targeted by an attack or by a spell that requires an attack roll. A smoke cloud appears in the subject’s square and in a 10-foot radius around it. This cloud lasts for 1 round per mesmerist level. The cloud functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]_, but is an illusion (figment) effect that can’t be dispersed by wind and can be used underwater. The cloud attacks the Will of a creature that interacts with it, which disbelieves the effect on a miss. The radius of the cloud increases by 5 feet for every 5 caster levels the mesmerist possesses, to a maximum of 30 feet at 20th level.
