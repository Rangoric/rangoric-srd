# Mesmerist Tricks

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist|mesmerist]]’s tricks, one note each. A mesmerist learns a trick at 1st level and every even level, and from 12th level can take a masterful trick instead, which uses two of his daily tricks. He implants a trick in an ally (or himself) and triggers it when its condition is met. The class note has the rules ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-tricks|mesmerist tricks]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-masterful-tricks|masterful tricks]]). Each note has `trick-type` (trick or masterful trick) and `level` (the mesmerist level it can be taken at).
