# Charm Blessing

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-blessings|warpriest blessing]], granted by deities that grant the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Charm Domain|Charm domain]].*

**Charming Presence (minor)**: At 1st level, you can touch an ally and grant an entrancing blessing. For 1 minute, the ally becomes mesmerizing to her opponents, filling them with either abject admiration or paralyzing fear. This effect functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sanctuary|sanctuary]]*, except if the ally attacks an opponent, the effect ends with respect to only that opponent. This is a mind-affecting effect.

**Dominance Aura (major)**: At 10th level, you can surround yourself with a tangible aura of majesty for 1 minute. While this aura is active, once per round as a swift action you can issue a command (as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]* spell) to one creature within 30 feet; the creature must succeed at a Will saving throw or submit for 1 round.
