# Blessings

The blessings a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest|warpriest]] chooses from, one note each. Each has a minor power (1st level) and a major power (10th level). A deity grants the blessings with the same names as its [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains|domains]]; the class note’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-blessings|Blessings]] feature has the rules (choosing two, alignment blessings, uses per day, the save DC, the action and caster level, and who benefits from blessings on weapons and armor).
