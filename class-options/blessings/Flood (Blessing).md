# Flood Blessing

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-blessings|warpriest blessing]]. You see proof of your convictions in the churning waters of a flood.*

**Deities**: Evil deities that offer the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/blessings/Water (Blessing)|Water blessing]] or nonevil deities with disasters in their portfolios.

**Lasting Breath (minor)**: You can touch an ally to bless it with an extra reserve of breath. For 1 minute, while the ally holds its breath, standard and full-round actions don’t decrease the number of rounds the ally can hold its breath (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Suffocation|holding one’s breath]]).

**Tsunami Burst (major)**: At 10th level, when you are hit by a melee attack, you can invoke this blessing as an immediate action. A wave of water blasts out from you at your attacker, attempting a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] combat maneuver check using your CMB but adding your Wisdom modifier instead of your Strength modifier.
