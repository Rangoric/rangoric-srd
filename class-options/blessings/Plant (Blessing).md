# Plant Blessing

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-blessings|warpriest blessing]], granted by deities that grant the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|Plant domain]].*

**Creeping Vines (minor)**: At 1st level, upon hitting with a melee attack, as a swift action you cause the creature you hit to sprout entangling vines that attempt to hold it in place, entangling it for 1 round (Reflex negates).

**Battle Companion (major)**: At 10th level, you can summon a battle companion. This ability functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IV|summon nature’s ally IV]]*, with a duration of 1 minute. This ability can summon only one animal, regardless of the list used, and the creature’s type changes to plant instead of animal. For every 2 levels beyond 10th, the level of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally]]* spell increases by 1, to a maximum of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IX|summon nature’s ally IX]]* at 20th level.
