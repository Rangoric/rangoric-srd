# Liberation Blessing

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-blessings|warpriest blessing]], granted by deities that grant the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Liberation Domain|Liberation domain]].*

**Liberation (minor)**: At 1st level, for 1 round as a swift action, you can ignore impediments to your mobility and effects that cause paralysis (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]*). You can activate this blessing even if you’re otherwise unable to take actions, but not if you’re unconscious.

**Freedom’s Shout (major)**: At 10th level, as a swift action you can emit a 30-foot aura that affects all allies with the liberation blessing described above. This effect lasts for 1 round.
