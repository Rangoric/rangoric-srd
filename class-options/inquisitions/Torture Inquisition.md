# Torture Inquisition

*An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/inquisitions/Inquisitions|inquisition]], which an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]] can choose in place of a domain with her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-domain|Domain]] feature.*

## Granted Powers

Only through pain can truth and justice come to the surface. And if that doesn't work, at least you've enacted divine vengeance.

**Torturer's Presence (Ex)**: You gain a +2 bonus when using the Intimidation skill. This is in addition to your bonus for [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-stern-gaze|stern gaze]].

**Torturer's Touch (Sp)**: You may use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Fatigue|touch of fatigue]]* as a spell-like ability. Creatures that are immune to pain effects are immune to this touch. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Critical Precision (Ex)**: At 8th level, when you roll a critical threat, you may expend one use of your torturer's touch ability to add +4 on your critical confirmation roll. This does not stack with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]].
