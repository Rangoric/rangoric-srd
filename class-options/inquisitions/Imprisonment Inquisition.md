# Imprisonment Inquisition

_An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/inquisitions/Inquisitions|inquisition]], which an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]] can choose in place of a domain with her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-domain|Domain]] feature._

## Granted Powers

Sometimes it is better to capture foes than to kill them—whether your intention is to punish them for their crimes or to torture them for information.

**Caging Strike (Su)**: With a devastating weapon strike, spectral chains wrap around your target for a short period of time. Whenever you confirm a critical hit with a melee or ranged weapon attack (including spells that require attack rolls), you can choose to also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangle]] that target for 1d4 rounds (vs Fortitude). You may use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Divine Prison**: At 8th level, once per day upon making a successful melee attack, you can affect your target with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Monster|hold monster]]_ (vs Will).
