# Valor Inquisition

*An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/inquisitions/Inquisitions|inquisition]], which an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]] can choose in place of a domain with her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-domain|Domain]] feature.*

## Granted Powers

It takes courage to confront the enemies of your faith.

**Touch of Resolve (Sp)**: You may use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]* on a single creature a number of times per day equal to your 3 + your Wisdom bonus.

**Fearless (Su)**: At 8th level, you become immune to fear.
