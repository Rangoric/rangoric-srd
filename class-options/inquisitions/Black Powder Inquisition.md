# Black Powder Inquisition

*An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/inquisitions/Inquisitions|inquisition]], which an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]] can choose in place of a domain with her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-domain|Domain]] feature.*

**Deities**: Any (with GM approval).

## Granted Powers

You gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] (firearms) and Gunsmithing as bonus feats. When you hit an arcane spellcaster or a creature that uses spell-like abilities with a firearm attack, that creature must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw. If the spellcaster fails, he takes a –4 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] for 1 round.
