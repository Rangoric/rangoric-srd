# Fate Inquisition

_An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/inquisitions/Inquisitions|inquisition]], which an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)|inquisitor]] can choose in place of a domain with her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-domain|Domain]] feature._

## Granted Powers

Fate is a powerful tool for those who dole out justice for their deity. You can read the strands of fate, and those strands guide your endeavors.

**Augury (Sp)**: Once per day, you can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]_ as a spell-like ability.

**Agent of Fate (Su)**: At 8th level, when you use your _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]_ spell-like ability, you gain a benefit that lasts until the end of your next combat. If the result was “weal,” all curing effects used on you cure an additional +1d10 hit points. If the result was “woe,” your melee attacks deal an additional +1d6 points of damage. If the result is “weal and woe” or “nothing,” you gain a +2 insight bonus to your Armor Class.
