# Ongoing Defense

*A psychic’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-amplifications|phrenic amplification]] (Su).*

The psychic can increase the duration of spells that improve her psychic defenses. She can spend 1 point from her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-pool|phrenic pool]] when she casts any *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Intellect Fortress I|intellect fortress]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mental Barrier I|mental barrier]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Thought Shield I|thought shield]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tower of Iron Will I|tower of iron will]]* spell to extend the spell’s duration by 1 round.
