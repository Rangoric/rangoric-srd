# Ongoing Defense

_A psychic’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-amplifications|phrenic amplification]] (Su)._

The psychic can increase the duration of spells that improve her psychic defenses. She can spend 1 point from her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-pool|phrenic pool]] when she casts any _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Intellect Fortress I|intellect fortress]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mental Barrier I|mental barrier]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Thought Shield I|thought shield]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tower of Iron Will I|tower of iron will]]_ spell to extend the spell’s duration by 1 round.
