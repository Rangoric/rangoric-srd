# Phrenic Amplifications

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)|psychic]]’s phrenic amplifications, one note each. A psychic learns an amplification at 1st level and every 4 levels from 3rd, and from 11th level can take a major amplification instead; each is fuelled by her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-pool|phrenic pool]] and modifies a spell she casts with [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic magic]]. The class note has the rules ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-amplifications|phrenic amplifications]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-major-amplifications|major amplifications]]). Each note has `amplification-type` (amplification or major amplification) and `level` (the psychic level it can be taken at).
