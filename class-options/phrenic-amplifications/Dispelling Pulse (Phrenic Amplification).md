# Dispelling Pulse

_A psychic’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-major-amplifications|major amplification]] (Su)._

The psychic can spend 3 points from her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-pool|phrenic pool]] to entwine dispelling magic with a targeted spell. If the linked spell hits the target, with an attack roll or against a defense, it’s affected as though the psychic had cast a targeted _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_ spell on it. This effect can dispel only spells of the linked spell’s level or lower. If the linked spell doesn’t already require an attack roll or attack a defense, the dispelling effect alone attacks the target’s Will; its attack bonus is calculated using the linked spell’s level (and all other bonuses that would apply if the linked spell attacked Will). This amplification can be linked only to a spell that targets one creature or object.
