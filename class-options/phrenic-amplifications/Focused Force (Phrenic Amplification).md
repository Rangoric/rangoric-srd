# Focused Force

_A psychic’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-amplifications|phrenic amplification]] (Su)._

When casting a force spell, the psychic can increase the spell’s damage by spending 1 point from her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-pool|phrenic pool]]. Increase the die size for the spell’s damage by one step (from 1d4 to 1d6, 1d6 to 1d8, 1d8 to 1d10, or 1d10 to 1d12). This increases the size of each die rolled, so a spell that dealt 4d6+3 points of force damage would deal 4d8+3 points of force damage instead. This amplification can be linked only to spells that deal force damage, and only if that damage includes a die value. A spell that already uses d12s for damage can’t be amplified in this way.
