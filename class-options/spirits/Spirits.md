# Spirits

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]]’s spirits, one note each. Each spirit note lists its spirit magic spells, the hexes it opens up, what it gives the shaman’s spirit animal, its spirit, greater and true spirit abilities, and its manifestation. The class note has the rules: bonding with a spirit and the save DC for its abilities ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit|Spirit]]), casting its spells ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit-magic|Spirit Magic]]), bonding with a second spirit each day ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-wandering-spirit|Wandering Spirit]]), and becoming the spirit at 20th level ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-manifestation|Manifestation]]).
