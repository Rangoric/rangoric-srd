# Intercept

*A [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Stalwart Defender#Defensive Powers|defensive power]] (Ex).*

Once a round as an immediate action, when a melee or ranged weapon would successfully strike an adjacent ally, the defender can choose to have the weapon strike him instead of the intended target. The attack automatically hits the defender, regardless his AC or any miss chance in effect, and he suffers the normal consequences of the attack.
