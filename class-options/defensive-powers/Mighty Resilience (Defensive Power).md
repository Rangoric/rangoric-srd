# Mighty Resilience

*A [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Stalwart Defender#Defensive Powers|defensive power]] (Ex).*

The defender automatically negates the additional damage of one successful critical hit or sneak attack, as well as other abilities or effects that rely on a successful critical hit or sneak attack (such as blinding from Blinding Critical or bleed from sneak attack). The defender takes only normal damage from the attack. This power may only be used once per defensive stance. The defender must be at least 6th level to select this power.
