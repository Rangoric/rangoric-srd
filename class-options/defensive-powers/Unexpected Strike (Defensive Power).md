# Unexpected Strike

*A [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Stalwart Defender#Defensive Powers|defensive power]] (Ex).*

The defender can make an attack of opportunity against a foe that moves into any square threatened by the defender, regardless of whether or not that movement would normally provoke an attack of opportunity. This power can only be used once per stance. A defender must be at least 4th level before selecting this power.
