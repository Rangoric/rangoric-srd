# Bulwark

*A [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Stalwart Defender#Defensive Powers|defensive power]] (Ex).*

The defender adds his armor check penalty as a bonus to the DC of opponents attempting to use Deception against him and Acrobatics checks to pass by him without provoking an attack of opportunity for moving through spaces he threatens.
