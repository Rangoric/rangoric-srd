# Elephant Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). The elephant is a symbol of both power and wisdom in many lands. Shifters who take this aspect learn the value of applying their strength with precision.*

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 enhancement bonus on bull rush and overrun combat maneuver checks.

At 8th level, this bonus increases to +6, and at 15th level it increases to +8.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a Large adolescent elephant (as an elephant, but with a space of 10 feet). While in this form, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]], a gore attack (1d6 damage), and a slam attack (1d8 damage) with a 5-foot reach. You also gain a +2 racial bonus to Strength and to natural armor. While in this form, you can carry a Medium bipedal creature on your back, allowing you to act as the creature’s mount.

At 8th level, you gain trample, as the universal monster rule, dealing your shifter claw damage.

At 15th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]] as a bonus feat, and your racial bonus to Strength and natural armor increases to +4.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Gore (P), slam (B)
