# Aspects

The aspects a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter|shifter]] can take with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] class feature. The shifter’s class note says how aspects work: how long a minor form lasts and how she takes it on ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]]), taking her major form ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-wild-shape|wild shape]]), and taking several minor forms at once ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-chimeric-aspect|chimeric aspect]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-greater-chimeric-aspect|greater chimeric aspect]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-final-aspect|final aspect]]). Each aspect note says only what its minor and major forms grant; the levels in them are shifter levels.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Each aspect ends with its alternate natural attacks. When a shifter in her natural form extends her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-claws|shifter claws]], she can make one claw an alternate natural attack listed under one of her aspects instead, such as a falcon’s talons or a bear’s bite, and she can have up to two different alternate natural attacks this way. An alternate natural attack changes only the attack’s type and its damage type; otherwise it works as her claw does. ^aspects-alternate-natural-attacks
