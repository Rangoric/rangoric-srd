# Lion Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). Lions hunt in prides, cooperating to bring down large opponents and defend their territory.*

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Intimidation checks.

This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th level.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a dire lion. While in this form, you gain a base speed of 40 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] (30 feet). Once per day for every 2 shifter levels you have, you can utter a mighty roar, allowing you to attempt an Intimidation check with a +5 circumstance bonus to demoralize enemies within 30 feet. Allies gain a +1 morale bonus on attack and damage rolls against creatures affected by this ability.

At 8th level, you gain one of the following teamwork feats as a bonus feat: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Outflank|Outflank]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Pack Attack|Pack Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Paired Opportunists|Paired Opportunists]], or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shake It Off|Shake It Off]]. You can grant allies within 30 feet the benefits of this feat as a swift action for a number of rounds equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

At 15th level, you provide a flanking bonus to any ally who threatens the same opponent as you do, and you can change places with an adjacent ally as a move action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity, as if using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Swap Places|Swap Places]] feat.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Bite (B, P, S)
