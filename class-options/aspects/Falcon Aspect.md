# Falcon Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). The falcon senses all and has the speed to hunt down even the most elusive prey. Those who take this aspect gain enhanced abilities to perceive and reconnoiter the wilderness—little escapes this shifter’s attention.*

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Perception checks.

This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th level.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a Small falcon. While in this form, you gain a base speed of 30 feet, a bite attack (1d4 damage), two claw attacks (1d3 damage), a fly speed of 60 feet (good), [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], and a +4 racial bonus on vision-based Perception checks.

At 8th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] with a range of 120 feet, and your racial bonus on Perception checks increases to +6.

At 15th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blindsense|blindsense]] with a range of 60 feet and your fly speed increases to 90 feet (perfect).

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Bite (B, P, S), talon (S)
