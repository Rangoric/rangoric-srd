# Bull Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). The bull is a fierce charger and is adept at trampling its foes, pounding them to a bloody pulp.*

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Strength score.

At 8th level, the bonus increases to +4, and at 15th level it increases to +6.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a Large bull. While in this form, you gain a base speed of 40 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]], and a gore attack (1d8 damage) with powerful charge (+1d8 on your gore attack).

At 8th level, you gain the trample as per the universal monster rule (dealing gore damage plus 1-1/2 times your Strength modifier).

At 15th level, your gore damage increases to 2d8 and you gain the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Awesome Blow|Awesome Blow]] feat.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Gore (P), hoof (B), slam (B)
