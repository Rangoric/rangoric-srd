# Stag Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). The stag can outrun or stand its ground against almost any foe. This aspect offers majestic grace and swift speed.*

**Minor Form**: You gain an enhancement bonus of +5 feet to your base speed.

This bonus increases to +10 feet at 8th level and +20 feet at 15th level.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a Large stag, but its space changes to 10 feet. While in this form, you gain a base speed of 50 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] (30 feet), a gore attack (1d6 damage), and two hoof attacks (1d4 damage).

At 8th level, you gain a +4 racial bonus on Athletics checks to jump.

At 15th level, you gain the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Awesome Blow|Awesome Blow]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Natural Attack|Improved Natural Attack]] feats for gore attacks, and you can use Awesome Blow against a creature of your size or smaller.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Gore (P), hoof (B)
