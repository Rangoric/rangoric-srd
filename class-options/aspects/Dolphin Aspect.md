# Dolphin Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). The aspect of the dolphin imparts clever mastery of the seas. Shifters with this aspect also embody the size and ferociousness of large dolphins, such as orcas.*

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|checks to identify creatures’ abilities and weaknesses]].

This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th level.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a Large dolphin, but your space is 10 feet instead of 5 feet. While in this form, you gain a swim speed of 60 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blindsense|blindsense]] with a range of 10 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], a bite attack (1d8), and the ability to hold your breath for a number of minutes equal to 6 times your Constitution score before you risk drowning.

At 8th level, the range of your blindsense increases to 20 feet.

At 15th level, the range of your blindsense increases to 60 feet and your swim speed increases to 80 feet.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Bite (B, P, S), slam (B)
