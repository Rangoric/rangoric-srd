# Scorpion Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). The aspect of the scorpion invokes a hidden menace with a poisonous sting.*

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on Stealth checks and grapple combat maneuver checks.

This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th level.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a giant scorpion. While in this form, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] with a range of 60 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] with a range of 10 feet, two primary claw attacks (1d6) with the grab ability, and a secondary sting attack (1d6).

At 8th level, the range of your tremorsense increases to 20 feet, and you gain a +4 racial bonus on Athletics checks to climb and on Perception checks.

At 15th level, when you make an attack of opportunity with your sting attack or maintain a grapple and deal damage with your sting attack, that attack also applies the following [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]].

*Poison (Ex):* Sting—injury; save Fort DC = 10 + half your shifter level + your Constitution modifier; frequency 1/round for 6 rounds; effect 1d3 Strength damage; cure 2 consecutive saves.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Pincers (B), sting (P)
