# Horse Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). The aspect of the horse grants speed and maneuverability.*

**Minor Form**: You gain the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]] feat as well as an enhancement bonus of +5 feet to your base speed.

At 8th level, you gain the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Run|Run]] feat. If you already have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Run|Run]] feat, you instead can run at 6 times your base speed when using the Run feat.

At 15th level, your enhancement bonus to your base speed increases to +15.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a horse. While in this form, you gain a base speed of 50 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] (30 feet), and the ability to move before and after an attack, as if you had the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ride-By Attack|Ride-By Attack]] feat.

At 8th level, you can move through threatened squares without provoking attacks of opportunity, and you gain trample, as the universal monster rule, dealing your shifter claw damage.

At 15th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Overrun|Improved Overrun]] as a bonus feat, even if you do not meet the prerequisites.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Hoof (B)
