# Snapping Turtle Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). Long-lived and resilient, turtles embody wisdom and endurance in many cultures. Those who take this aspect gain profound insight as well as remarkable resilience.*

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to Wisdom.

At 8th level, this bonus increases to +4, and at 15th level, it increases to +6.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a Large giant snapping turtle. While in this form, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] (30 feet), a swim speed of 20 feet, and a bite attack (1d8 + 1-1/2 your Strength modifier damage).

At 8th level, you gain an insight bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier to your CMD against bull rush, drag, overrun, reposition, and trip attempts.

At 15th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]] 5/silver.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Bite (B, P, S)
