# Spider Aspect

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] (Su). The spider is a patient hunter, sensing foes that struggle in its webs.*

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on Athletics checks to climb, Stealth checks, saving throws against web effects (magical or otherwise), and checks to break free of webs.

This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th level.

**Major Form**: Your shape changes to that of a giant spider. While in this form, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] with a range of 60 feet, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] with a range of 10 feet, a climb speed of 30 feet, the web special attack, and a bite attack (1d6).

At 8th level, you gain a +4 racial bonus on Perception checks, and the range of your tremorsense increases to 20 feet.

At 15th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Natural Attack|Improved Natural Attack]] (bite), and the range of your tremorsense increases to 30 feet.

**Alternate Natural Attacks**: Bite (B, P, S)
