# Bold Stares

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist|mesmerist]]’s bold stare improvements, one note each. At 3rd level and every 4 levels thereafter, a mesmerist chooses one, and it adds to the effect of his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-hypnotic-stare|hypnotic stare]] on its target; the class note has the rules ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-bold-stare|bold stare]]).
