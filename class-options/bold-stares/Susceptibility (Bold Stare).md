# Susceptibility

_A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-bold-stare|bold stare]] improvement (Su)._

The hypnotic stare penalty also applies to the target’s [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks to oppose [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks, and to the DCs of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks made against the target.
