# Sluggishness

_A mesmerist’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-bold-stare|bold stare]] improvement (Su)._

The target of the hypnotic stare has all of its speeds reduced by 5 feet (to a minimum of 5 feet), and the hypnotic stare penalty also applies to the target’s Reflex Defense.
