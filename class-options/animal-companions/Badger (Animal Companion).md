# Badger

*An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/animal-companions/Animal Companions|animal companion]] that a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|druid]] can choose in place of a base creature ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Badger|badger]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Wolverine|wolverine]]).*

- **Size**: Small
- **Speed**: 30 ft., burrow 10 ft., climb 10 ft.
- **AC**: +2 natural armor
- **Attack**: bite (1d4), 2 claws (1d3)
- **Ability scores**: Str 10, Dex 17, Con 15, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 10
- **Special attacks**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] (as a barbarian for 6 rounds per day)
- **Special qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]]

## 4th-Level Advancement

- **Size**: Medium
- **Attack**: bite (1d6), 2 claws (1d4)
- **Ability scores**: Str +4, Dex -2, Con +2
