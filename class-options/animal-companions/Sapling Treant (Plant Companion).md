# Sapling Treant

_A plant companion that a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/druid/Treesinger (Archetype)|treesinger]] can choose for her plant bond._

- **AC**: +1 natural armor
- **Attack**: 2 slams (1d6)
- **Ability scores**: Str 15, Dex 10, Con 12, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 7
- **Special qualities**: double damage against objects, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]
