# Camel

*An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/animal-companions/Animal Companions|animal companion]] that a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|druid]] can choose in place of a base creature ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Camel|camel]]).*

- **Size**: Large
- **Speed**: 50 ft.
- **AC**: +1 natural armor
- **Attack**: bite (1d4) or spit (ranged touch attack, target is sickened for 1d4 rounds, range 10 feet)
- **Ability scores**: Str 18, Dex 16, Con 14, Int 2, Wis 11, Cha 4
- **Special qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]]

## 4th-Level Advancement

- **Ability scores**: Str +2, Con +2
