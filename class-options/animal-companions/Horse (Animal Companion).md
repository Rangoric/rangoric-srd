# Horse

_An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/animal-companions/Animal Companions|animal companion]] that a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|druid]] can choose in place of a base creature ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/horses/Horse|horse]])._

- **Size**: Large
- **Speed**: 50 ft.
- **AC**: +4 natural armor
- **Attack**: bite (1d4), 2 hooves\* (1d6)
- **Ability scores**: Str 16, Dex 13, Con 15, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 6
- **Special qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]]
- \*This is a secondary natural attack.

## 4th-Level Advancement

- **Ability scores**: Str +2, Con +2
- **Special qualities**: combat trained (see the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] skill)
