# Animal Companions

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|druid]]’s animal companion can be a base creature from the druid’s own lists, with the table under [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#The Druid’s Animal Companion|The Druid’s Animal Companion]], or one of the 31 companions here, each with a stat block of its own. The two ways sit side by side: the druid picks one when she gets her companion. A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-companion|hunter]], who may choose any animal available to a druid, can choose these too.

## The Companions

Each animal companion has a different starting size, speed, attack, ability scores and special qualities. All of an animal’s attacks are made at its full base attack bonus unless noted. An animal’s attacks add its Strength modifier to damage, except that a lone attack adds 1-1/2 times its Strength modifier. Some have special abilities, such as [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]]; follow the link for how they work. ^pathfinder-companions

- **The stat block replaces the base creature.** A companion here takes the place of the base creature in the druid’s lists, so its starting statistics are the ones on its note, and the druid’s table adds its Bonus HD, natural armor, Strength and Dexterity and Bonus Tricks on top. The starting statistics are for a companion with 2 Hit Dice, the Hit Dice a first-level druid’s companion has. ^companion-hit-dice
- **Advancement.** As the druid gains levels, her companion improves as well, usually at 4th or 7th level, in addition to the table’s improvements. The note gives the druid level and what changes: AC and ability score entries are added to the starting values, the attacks listed replace the starting ones, and special attacks and qualities are gained. Instead of taking the advancement, the druid can choose to increase the companion’s Dexterity and Constitution by 2. ^companion-advancement
- **Skills and feats.** An animal companion’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^animal-companion-skills|skills]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^animal-companion-feats|feats]] are in the druid’s note.
- The companions listed here are by no means the only ones available: the base creatures of the druid’s lists are too.

## Vermin Companions

In addition to the normal choices of animal companions, a druid who is so inclined may select a vermin as her companion. Vermin companions follow the same rules as animal companions, advancing their Hit Dice and other abilities per the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#The Druid’s Animal Companion|animal companion base statistics table]]. Vermin companions can be trained as if they were animals using the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] skill.

**Mindless**: Vermin companions have no Intelligence score and possess the mindless trait. In spite of this, vermin companions may learn one trick, plus additional bonus tricks. If a vermin animal companion gains an ability score increase (at 4 Hit Dice, 8 Hit Dice, and so on), the druid can apply this increase to the companion's Intelligence, changing it from — to 1, at which point the companion loses the mindless quality and is able to know up to 3 tricks per point of Intelligence, plus the additional bonus tricks. Vermin companions have no skill points or feats as long as they have the mindless quality.

**Trip**: Because many vermin have multiple limbs, they are very difficult to trip. As a result, a CMD entry has been included for each, indicating the bonus to CMD such vermin receive against trip attacks.
