- **Speed**: 20 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **AC**: +4 natural armor
- **Attack**: bite (1d6)
- **Ability scores**: Str 15, Dex 14, Con 15, Int 1, Wis 12, Cha 2
- **Special qualities**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Hold Breath|hold breath]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]
