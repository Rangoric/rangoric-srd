# Emotional Focuses

The emotional focuses a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)|spiritualist]]’s phantom can have, one note each. The class note’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-emotional-focus|Emotional Focus]] has the rules (what a focus decides: the phantom’s bonus skill ranks and good saves, the spiritualist’s Skill Focus, and the abilities it gains as the spiritualist rises in level), and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-phantoms|Phantoms]] the phantom itself.
