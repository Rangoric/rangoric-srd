# Resistance

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|inquisitor’s judgment]].*

The inquisitor is shielded by a flickering aura, gaining 2 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|energy resistance]] against one energy type (acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic) chosen when the judgment is declared. The protection increases by 2 for every three inquisitor levels she possesses.
