# Piercing

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|inquisitor’s judgment]].*

This judgment gives the inquisitor great focus and makes her spells more potent. This benefit grants a +1 sacred bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] and caster level checks made to overcome a target's spell resistance. This bonus increases by +1 for every three inquisitor levels she possesses.
