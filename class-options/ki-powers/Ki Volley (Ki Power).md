# Ki Volley

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Monk#^unchained-monk-ki-powers|ki power]] (Su).*

When a targeted spell or spell-like ability fails to overcome the monk's spell resistance from [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ki-powers/Diamond Soul (Ki Power)|diamond soul]], he can spend 2 ki points as an immediate action to send that spell back at its caster as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]]*. A monk must be 16th level or higher and possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ki-powers/Diamond Soul (Ki Power)|diamond soul ki power]] before selecting this ki power.
