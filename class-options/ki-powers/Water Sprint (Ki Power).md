# Water Sprint

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Monk#^unchained-monk-ki-powers|ki power]] (Su)._

A monk with this ki power can spend 1 point from his ki pool as a swift action to gain the ability to walk on water, as if under the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Walk|water walk]]_. Once activated, this ability lasts for 1 minute per monk level. A monk must be at least 6th level before selecting this ki power.
