# Diamond Mind

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Monk#^unchained-monk-ki-powers|ki power]] (Su)._

By spending 1 point from his ki pool as a swift action, a monk with this power can suppress a fear effect affecting him as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]_, using his monk level as his caster level. He can spend 2 ki points to activate this ability even when frightened or panicked. A monk must be at least 6th level before selecting this ki power.
