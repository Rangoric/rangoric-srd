# Light Steps

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Monk#^unchained-monk-ki-powers|ki power]] (Ex).*

A monk with this ki power can move effortlessly across nearly any surface. Whenever the monk activates his [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ki-powers/Feather Balance (Ki Power)|feather balance ki power]], he can also ignore all difficult terrain. In addition, while active, he can cross any solid surface, even if it would normally not support his weight. This does not allow him to walk on liquids. A monk must be at least 8th level and have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ki-powers/Feather Balance (Ki Power)|feather balance ki power]] before selecting this ki power.
