# Cobra Breath

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Monk#^unchained-monk-ki-powers|ki power]] (Su).*

Whenever a monk with this ki power uses [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ki-powers/Diamond Body (Ki Power)|diamond body]] to neutralize a poison, he can instead release that poison as a ranged touch attack against a single foe within 30 feet. If the attack hits, the foe must succeed at a saving throw (using the poison's original DC) or suffer the poison's effects, even if it was not originally a contact poison. A monk must be at least 12th level and must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ki-powers/Diamond Body (Ki Power)|diamond body ki power]] before selecting this ki power.
