# High Jump

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Monk#^unchained-monk-ki-powers|ki power]] (Ex).*

A monk with this ki power adds his level as a bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|jump]], both for vertical jumps and horizontal jumps. In addition, he always counts as having a running start when attempting [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|jump]]. By spending 1 point from his ki pool as a swift action, the monk gains a +20 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|jump]] for 1 round.
