# Deceptive Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the deceptive combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disengaging Feint|Disengaging Feint]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ranged Feint|Ranged Feint]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Feint|Two-Weapon Feint]].
- **From 6th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Feint|Greater Feint]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Misdirection Tactics|Misdirection Tactics]].
- **From 10th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disengaging Shot|Disengaging Shot]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Passing Trick|Passing Trick]].
