# Two-Weapon Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the two-weapon combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Double Slice|Double Slice]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Shield Bash|Improved Shield Bash]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]].
- **From 6th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Two-Weapon Fighting|Improved Two-Weapon Fighting]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Defense|Two-Weapon Defense]].
- **From 10th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Two-Weapon Fighting|Greater Two-Weapon Fighting]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Rend|Two-Weapon Rend]].
