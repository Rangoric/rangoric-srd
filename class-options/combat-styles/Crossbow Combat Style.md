# Crossbow Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the crossbow combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deadly Aim|Deadly Aim]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Focused Shot|Focused Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Reload|Rapid Reload]].
- **From 6th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Crossbow Mastery|Crossbow Mastery]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Precise Shot|Improved Precise Shot]].
- **From 10th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Pinpoint Targeting|Pinpoint Targeting]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shot on the Run|Shot on the Run]].
