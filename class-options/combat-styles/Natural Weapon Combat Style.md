# Natural Weapon Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the natural weapon combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: Aspect of the Beast, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Natural Attack|Improved Natural Attack]], Rending Claws, and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]].
- **From 6th level**, also: Eldritch Fangs and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]].
- **From 10th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Multiattack|Multiattack]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Vital Strike|Improved Vital Strike]].
