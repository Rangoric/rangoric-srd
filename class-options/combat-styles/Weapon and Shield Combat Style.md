# Weapon and Shield Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the weapon and shield combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Shield Bash|Improved Shield Bash]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Focus|Shield Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Slam|Shield Slam]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]].
- **From 6th level**, also: Saving Shield and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Master|Shield Master]].
- **From 10th level**, also: Bashing Finish and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Shield Focus|Greater Shield Focus]].
