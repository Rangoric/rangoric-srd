# Menacing Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the menacing combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Enforcer|Enforcer]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Gory Finish|Gory Finish]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intimidating Prowess|Intimidating Prowess]].
- **From 6th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Martial Dominance|Martial Dominance]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shatter Defenses|Shatter Defenses]].
- **From 10th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dreadful Carnage|Dreadful Carnage]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]].
