# Mounted Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the mounted combat combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Combat|Mounted Combat]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Archery|Mounted Archery]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ride-By Attack|Ride-By Attack]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Trick Riding|Trick Riding]].
- **From 6th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Shield|Mounted Shield]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spirited Charge|Spirited Charge]].
- **From 10th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Skirmisher|Mounted Skirmisher]] and Unseat.
