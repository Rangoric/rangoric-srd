# Thrown Weapon Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the thrown weapon combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Distance Thrower|Distance Thrower]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]].
- **From 6th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Close-Quarters Thrower|Close-Quarters Thrower]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/False Opening|False Opening]].
- **From 10th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Pinpoint Targeting|Pinpoint Targeting]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shot on the Run|Shot on the Run]].
