# Combat Styles

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] picks one combat style at 2nd level, and takes his combat style feats from its list. A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer|slayer]] can pick one through the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Ranger Combat Style (Slayer Talent)|ranger combat style]] talent.
