# Combat Styles

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] picks one combat style at 2nd level, and takes his combat style feats from its list. Archery and two-weapon are Pathfinder's core styles; the rest are from the Advanced Player's Guide.
