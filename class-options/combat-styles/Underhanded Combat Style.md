# Underhanded Combat Style

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-combat-style|ranger]] who selects the underhanded combat style can choose from the following feats whenever he gains a combat style feat, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

- **From 2nd level**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Dirty Trick|Improved Dirty Trick]], and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Steal|Improved Steal]].
- **From 6th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Dirty Trick|Greater Dirty Trick]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Steal|Greater Steal]].
- **From 10th level**, also: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Dirty Trick|Quick Dirty Trick]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Steal|Quick Steal]].

If he chooses Combat Expertise, he counts as having 13 Intelligence for the purpose of selecting that feat and others that have Combat Expertise as a prerequisite.
