# Inspirational Expertise

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex).*

When an investigator succeeds at a Knowledge check to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|identify a monster’s special powers or vulnerabilities]], he can expend one use of inspiration as a swift action to grant allies within 30 feet that can hear him a +4 insight bonus on attack rolls against that monster or type of monster for 1 round. An investigator must be at least 7th level to take this talent.
