# Underworld Inspiration

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex).*

An investigator can use his inspiration on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks without expending uses of inspiration, provided he’s trained in the skill.
