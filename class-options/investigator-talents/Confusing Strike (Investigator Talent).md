# Confusing Strike

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex)\*._

When the investigator deals damage with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]], the strike also attacks the opponent’s Fortitude, and on a hit the opponent becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d4+1 rounds. A miss reduces the duration to 1 round. The attack bonus is equal to 1/2 the investigator’s level + his Intelligence modifier. Constructs, mindless creatures, oozes, plants, undead, incorporeal creatures, and creatures immune to critical hits are not affected by this ability. An investigator must be at least 19th level to select this talent.
