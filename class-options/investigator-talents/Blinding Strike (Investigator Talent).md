# Blinding Strike

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex)\*._

When the investigator deals damage with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]], the strike also attacks the opponent’s Fortitude, and on a hit the opponent is permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]]. A miss reduces this to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1d4 rounds. The attack bonus is equal to 1/2 the investigator’s level + his Intelligence modifier. This talent has no effect on creatures that do not rely on eyes for sight or creatures with more than two eyes (although multiple critical hits might cause blindness, at the GM’s discretion). Blindness can be cured by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regeneration]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Blindness-Deafness|remove blindness]]_, or similar abilities. An investigator must be at least 17th level to select this talent.
