# Empathy (Ex, Su)

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]].*

When attempting a Sense Motive check, the investigator makes two d20 rolls and takes the higher result. If an investigator uses [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|inspiration]] on a Sense Motive check, he rolls the inspiration dice twice and takes the higher result. Once per day, the investigator can expend one use of inspiration to target a single creature that he can see and hear within 30 feet. Upon doing so, the investigator detects the surface thoughts of the target’s mind, as if he concentrated for 3 rounds while using the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* spell, unless the creature succeeds a Will saving throw. The DC of this save is 10 + 1/2 the investigator’s level + his Intelligence modifier. If the target fails, the investigator can continue to detect the surface thoughts of the target creature for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 his investigator level. An investigator must be at least 5th level to select this talent.
