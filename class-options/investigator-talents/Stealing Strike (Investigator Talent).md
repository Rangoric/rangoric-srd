# Stealing Strike

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex)\*.*

When the investigator deals damage with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]], he can perform a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] combat maneuver as a free action against the creature damaged by studied strike. This steal does not provoke attacks of opportunity. An investigator must be at least 13th level to select this talent.
