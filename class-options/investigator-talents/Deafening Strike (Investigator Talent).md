# Deafening Strike

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex)\*.*

When an investigator deals damage with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]], the opponent must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or be permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]]. A successful Fortitude saving throw reduces the duration of this effect to 1 round. The DC for this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 the investigator’s level + his Intelligence modifier. This talent has no effect on deaf creatures. Deafness can be cured by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regeneration]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Blindness-Deafness|remove blindness]]*, or similar effects. An investigator must be at least 15th level to select this talent.
