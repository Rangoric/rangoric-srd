# Deafening Strike

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex)\*._

When an investigator deals damage with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]], the strike also attacks the opponent’s Fortitude, and on a hit the opponent is permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]]. A miss reduces the duration of this effect to 1 round. The attack bonus is equal to 1/2 the investigator’s level + his Intelligence modifier. This talent has no effect on deaf creatures. Deafness can be cured by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regeneration]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Blindness-Deafness|remove blindness]]_, or similar effects. An investigator must be at least 15th level to select this talent.
