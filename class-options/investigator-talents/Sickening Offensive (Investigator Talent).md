# Sickening Offensive

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex)\*.*

When the investigator damages a studied target, that creature is also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 round. An investigator must be at least 7th level to select this talent.
