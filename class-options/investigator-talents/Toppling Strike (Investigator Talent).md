# Toppling Strike

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]] (Ex)\*._

When the investigator deals damage with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]], he can perform a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver as a free action against the creature damaged by studied strike. This trip does not provoke attacks of opportunity. An investigator must be at least 9th level to select this talent.
