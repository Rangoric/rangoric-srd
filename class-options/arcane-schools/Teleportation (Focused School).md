# Teleportation (Focused School)

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-focused-schools|focused school]] of conjuration.*

**Associated school**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Conjuration School|conjuration]].

**Replacement powers**: This subschool replaces the *acid dart* power of the conjuration school.

**Shift (Su)**: At 1st level, you can *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]]* to a nearby space as a *swift action* as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]*. This movement does not provoke an *attack of opportunity*. You must be able to see the space that you are moving into. You cannot take other creatures with you when you use this ability (except for familiars). You can move 5 feet for every two wizard levels you possess (minimum 5 feet). You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your *Intelligence* modifier.
