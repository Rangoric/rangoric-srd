# Shadow (Focused School)

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-focused-schools|focused school]] of illusion.*

**Associated school**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Illusion School|illusion]].

**Replacement powers**: The following school powers replace the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blinding Ray|blinding ray]]* and *invisibility field* powers of the illusion school.

**Binding Darkness (Sp)**: As a *standard action*, you cast a weave of shadows at any foe within 30 feet as a ranged *touch attack*. The shadows *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]* your foe for 1 round plus 1 additional round for every five wizard levels you possess. In conditions of bright light, this duration is halved (minimum 1 round). A creature *entangled* by your shadows has *concealment* from those without *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkvision|darkvision]]* or the ability to see in darkness, and other creatures likewise have *concealment* relative to it. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your *Intelligence* modifier.

**Shadow Step (Sp)**: At 8th level, you can use this ability to walk through the Shadow Plane and reappear as a *standard action*. You can travel up to 30 feet per wizard level per day in this fashion, either in a single round or broken up across multiple shadow steps. This movement must be used in 5-foot increments and does not provoke an *attack of opportunity*. Travel through the Shadow Plane is imprecise; when you arrive, you re-enter 1 square off target, as per the rules for [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|thrown splash weapons]]. If this would place you in an occupied square, you instead arrive in the nearest safe location. When you arrive, you are cloaked in shadow and gain *concealment* as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]* spell for 1 round. You may bring other willing creatures with you, but you must expend an equal amount of distance for each additional creature brought with you. They likewise re-enter off target (roll location for each creature) and are cloaked in shadow for 1 round.
