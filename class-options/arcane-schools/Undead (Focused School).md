# Undead (Focused School)

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-focused-schools|focused school]] of necromancy.*

**Associated school**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Necromancy School|necromancy]].

**Replacement powers**: The following school power replaces the *grave touch* powers of the necromancy school.

**Bolster (Sp)**: As a *standard action*, you can touch an *undead* creature and infuse it with negative energy. It gains a +1 *profane bonus* on all attack rolls and *saving throws*, as well as 1 *temporary hit point* per Hit Die and a +2 bonus to its turn resistance. The bonus on attack rolls and *saving throws* increases by +1 for every 5 wizard levels you possess. These bonuses last for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your wizard level (minimum 1 round). The bonuses and *temporary hit points* are immediately dispelled if the creature is within the area of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]* spell. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your *Intelligence* modifier.
