# Undead (Focused School)

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-focused-schools|focused school]] of necromancy._

**Associated school**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Necromancy School|necromancy]].

**Replacement powers**: The following school power replaces the _grave touch_ powers of the necromancy school.

**Bolster (Sp)**: As a _standard action_, you can touch an _undead_ creature and infuse it with negative energy. It gains a +1 _profane bonus_ on all attack rolls and _defenses_, as well as 1 _temporary hit point_ per Hit Die and a +2 bonus to its turn resistance. The bonus on attack rolls and _defenses_ increases by +1 for every 5 wizard levels you possess. These bonuses last for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your wizard level (minimum 1 round). The bonuses and _temporary hit points_ are immediately dispelled if the creature is within the area of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]_ spell. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your _Intelligence_ modifier.
