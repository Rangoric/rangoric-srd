# Spell Blending

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-magus-arcana|magus arcana]] (Ex).*

When a magus selects this arcana, he must select one spell from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|wizard spell list]] that is of a magus spell level he can cast. He adds this spell to his spellbook and list of magus spells known as a magus spell of its wizard spell level. He can instead select two spells to add in this way, but both must be at least one level lower than the highest-level magus spell he can cast. A magus can select this magus arcana more than once.
