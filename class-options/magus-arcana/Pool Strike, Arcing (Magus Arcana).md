# Pool Strike, Arcing

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-magus-arcana|magus arcana]] (Su).*

The magus can expend 1 additional point from his arcane pool when using the pool strike arcana. If his attack hits, the magus can target a number of enemies within 15 feet equal to his Intelligence modifier (minimum 0) with a ranged touch attack as a free action. Those struck take the same energy damage as the primary target of the pool strike, including increased damage on a critical hit. The magus must be at least 12th level and must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/magus-arcana/Pool Strike (Magus Arcana)|pool strike]] magus arcana before selecting this magus arcana.
