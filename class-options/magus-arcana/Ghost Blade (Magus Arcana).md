# Ghost Blade

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-magus-arcana|magus arcana]] (Su).*

Whenever the magus enchants his weapon using his arcane pool, he may spend 1 additional point from his arcane pool to add the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Brilliant Energy|brilliant energy]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]]* special abilities to the list of available options. The magus must be at least 9th level before selecting this arcana.
