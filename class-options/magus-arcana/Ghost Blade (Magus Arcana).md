# Ghost Blade

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-magus-arcana|magus arcana]] (Su)._

Whenever the magus enchants his weapon using his arcane pool, he may spend 1 additional point from his arcane pool to add the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Brilliant Energy|brilliant energy]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]]_ special abilities to the list of available options. The magus must be at least 9th level before selecting this arcana.
