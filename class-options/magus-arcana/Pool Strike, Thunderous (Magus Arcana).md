# Pool Strike, Thunderous

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-magus-arcana|magus arcana]] (Su).*

The magus can expend 1 additional point from his arcane pool when making a pool strike. His pool strike deals sonic damage and deafens a single target for 1 round (DC 10 + 1/2 magus level + Intelligence modifier). The magus must be at least 6th level and must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/magus-arcana/Pool Strike (Magus Arcana)|pool strike]] magus arcana before selecting this magus arcana.
