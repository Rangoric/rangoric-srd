# Arcane Redoubt, Greater

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-magus-arcana|magus arcana]] (Su)._

Whenever the magus uses his [[rangoric-srd/class-options/magus-arcana/Arcane Redoubt (Magus Arcana)|arcane redoubt]] magus arcana, he may spend an additional point from his arcane pool in order to apply his shield bonus to AC (including any enhancement bonus) as a bonus on Reflex Defense until the beginning of his next turn. If an effect attacks his Reflex while he is using this power, he may spend 2 points from his arcane pool as an immediate action to grant himself evasion, or 4 points to grant himself improved evasion. The magus must have the arcane redoubt magus arcana and be at least 12th level to select this magus arcana.
