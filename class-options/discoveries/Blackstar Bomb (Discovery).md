# Blackstar Bomb

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su)\*, available to drow alchemists.*

This bomb crushes the target like a void bomb, then repels nearby creatures with a burst of gravity. The target takes damage as if hit by a void bomb. All other creatures within 5 feet of the target are subject to a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]]. The alchemist uses his alchemist class level in place of his [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|Combat Maneuver Bonus]], and adds his Intelligence modifier in place of his Strength or Dexterity modifier to resolve the bull rush attempts. Creatures moved by a blackstar bomb are pushed directly away from the target’s square. An alchemist must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Void Bomb (Discovery)|void bomb]] discovery to create blackstar bombs.
