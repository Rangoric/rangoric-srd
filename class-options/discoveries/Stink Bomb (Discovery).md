# Stink Bomb

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]]\*._

The effects of the smoke created by an alchemist’s bomb can duplicate the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stinking Cloud|stinking cloud]]_ instead of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]_, filling an area equal to twice the bomb’s splash radius for 1 round. An alchemist must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Smoke Bomb (Discovery)|smoke bomb]] discovery before selecting this discovery.
