# Stink Bomb

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]]\*.*

The effects of the smoke created by an alchemist’s bomb can duplicate the effects of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stinking Cloud|stinking cloud]]* instead of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]*, filling an area equal to twice the bomb’s splash radius for 1 round. An alchemist must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Smoke Bomb (Discovery)|smoke bomb]] discovery before selecting this discovery.
