# Philosopher’s Stone

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-grand-discovery|alchemist grand discovery]].*

The alchemist learns how to create a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/artifacts/Philosopher’s Stone|philosopher’s stone]]*, and can do so once per month at no cost. Creating a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/artifacts/Philosopher’s Stone|philosopher’s stone]]* takes 1 day of work.
