# Philosopher’s Stone

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-grand-discovery|alchemist grand discovery]]._

The alchemist learns how to create a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/artifacts/Philosopher’s Stone|philosopher’s stone]]_, and can do so once per month at no cost. Creating a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/artifacts/Philosopher’s Stone|philosopher’s stone]]_ takes 1 day of work.
