# Mummification

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Ex).*

The alchemist has mastered preserving flesh and applied this knowledge to his own body, turning himself into an undeadlike creature. After learning this discovery, the alchemist must perform a 30-day regimen of a special diet, rigorous exercise, and drinking a mildly poisonous alchemical tea. At the end of this regimen, he falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] for 24 hours, then awakens as a “living mummy.” The alchemist’s type does not change, but he becomes immune to cold, nonlethal damage, paralysis, and sleep. An alchemist must be at least 10th level and must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Preserve Organs (Discovery)|preserve organs]] discovery before selecting this discovery.
