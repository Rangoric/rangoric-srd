# True Mutagen

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-grand-discovery|alchemist grand discovery]].*

The alchemist’s mutagen now grants a +8 natural armor bonus and a +8 alchemical bonus to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution. The alchemist takes a –2 penalty to his Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma as long as the mutagen persists (see [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-mutagen|Mutagen]]). An alchemist must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Grand Mutagen (Discovery)|grand mutagen]] discovery before selecting this discovery.
