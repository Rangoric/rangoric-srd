# Greater Plague Bomb

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]].*

The effects of the smoke created by an alchemist’s bomb duplicates the effects of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion, Greater|greater contagion]]* instead of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]*, filling an area equal to twice the bomb’s splash radius for 1 round per level. An alchemist must be at least 16th level and must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Plague Bomb (Discovery)|plague bomb]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Smoke Bomb (Discovery)|smoke bomb]] discoveries before selecting this discovery.
