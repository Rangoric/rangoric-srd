# Greater Plague Bomb

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]]._

The effects of the smoke created by an alchemist’s bomb duplicates the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion, Greater|greater contagion]]_ instead of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]_, filling an area equal to twice the bomb’s splash radius for 1 round per level. An alchemist must be at least 16th level and must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Plague Bomb (Discovery)|plague bomb]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Smoke Bomb (Discovery)|smoke bomb]] discoveries before selecting this discovery.
