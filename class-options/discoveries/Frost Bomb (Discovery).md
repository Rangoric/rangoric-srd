# Frost Bomb

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]]\*.*

When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it inflict cold damage. Creatures that take a direct hit from a frost bomb are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] on their next turn unless they succeed on a Fortitude save.
