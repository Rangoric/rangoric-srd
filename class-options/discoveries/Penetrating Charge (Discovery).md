# Penetrating Charge

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]].*

A penetrating charge is a bomb specifically designed to disable locks and similar mechanisms, though not other means of sealing a door, a container, or other sorts of devices. As a full-round action, the alchemist can place a penetrating charge on a lock. Instead of dealing damage, the penetrating charge seeps into the mechanism and eats away at it, granting a +5 circumstance bonus on any subsequent [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks to open the lock. A creature can spend 10 minutes and attempt an appropriate [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] check (DC 15) to fix the lock and remove this bonus.
