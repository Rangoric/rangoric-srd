# Grounding Goo

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su).*

The alchemist’s bomb applies a sticky residue after any damage from the bomb is resolved. If a creature damaged by the bomb has a nonmagical fly speed, its flight is severely impaired, and it takes a penalty equal to the alchemist’s level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks made to fly for 1 minute.
