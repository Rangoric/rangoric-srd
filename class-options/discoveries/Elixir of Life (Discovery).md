# Elixir of Life

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]].*

Once per day, the alchemist can brew an elixir of life. This special concoction costs 25,000 gp to create and takes 1 hour of work. An elixir of life, when administered by the alchemist who brewed it, restores life to a dead creature as per the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]*. Alternatively, the alchemist himself may drink the elixir of life, after which point he is immediately targeted with a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resurrection|resurrection]]* spell the next time he is killed. Used in this manner, the effects of an elixir of life persist only for a number of days equal to the alchemist’s Intelligence modifier; if he does not die before that time expires, the effects of the elixir of life end. An alchemist must be at least 16th level before selecting this discovery.
