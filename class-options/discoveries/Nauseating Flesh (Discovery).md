# Nauseating Flesh

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]].*

The alchemist’s body is suffused with poison. Any creature that bites, engulfs, or swallows the alchemist must make a Fortitude saving throw or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d4 rounds. An alchemist must be at least 12th level before selecting this discovery. The alchemist with this discovery does not suffer the effect of the poison of his own nauseating flesh.
