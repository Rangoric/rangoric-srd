# Inspired Bomb

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su)._

An alchemist with this discovery and a pool of inspiration (by way of a class feature such as the investigator’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|inspiration]], a feat, or the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Inspiring Cognatogen (Discovery)|inspiring cognatogen]] discovery) can expend two uses of inspiration to increase the bomb’s damage by 1d6. The type of damage the bomb deals does not change. Unlike other a discoveries that affect bombs, this one can be applied to a bomb with another bomb discovery, though it cannot be applied to the same bomb more than once.
