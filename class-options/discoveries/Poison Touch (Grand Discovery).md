# Poison Touch

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-grand-discovery|alchemist grand discovery]]._

The alchemist gains a poisonous touch, as if under the effects of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]_ spell. He can suppress or activate this ability as a free action. The physical appearance of how the alchemist generates and delivers his poisonous touch varies from alchemist to alchemist.
