# Rocket Bomb

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su), available to goblin alchemists.*

Alchemists with this discovery can prepare special rockets to deliver their bombs. Rocket bombs travel farther and explode bigger than normal [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bombs]], but cannot target individual creatures. Rocket bombs explode in a 20-foot radius, and all creatures in that area take the alchemist’s normal splash damage. The range increment on a rocket bomb is 50 feet. Rocket bombs cannot be used with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Precise Bombs (Discovery)|precise bomb]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Fast Bombs (Discovery)|fast bomb]] discoveries. An alchemist must be at least 6th level before selecting this discovery.
