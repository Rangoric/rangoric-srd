# Scrap Bomb

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su), available to goblin alchemists._

When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it explode into shards of shrapnel that deal piercing damage. A creature that takes a direct hit from a scrap bomb takes 1 point of bleed damage per die of bomb damage if the bomb also hits its Reflex.
