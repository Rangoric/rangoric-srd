# Lingering Plague

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]]._

Any creature attacked by a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]] caused by the alchemist’s extract or other class ability is attacked again 1 round later, and a hit doubles the duration of the disease effect. If the disease doesn’t have a duration, this discovery reduces its onset time and frequencies by half. An alchemist must be at least 8th level before selecting this discovery.
