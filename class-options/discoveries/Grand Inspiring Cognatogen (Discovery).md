# Grand Inspiring Cognatogen

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su)._

The alchemist’s inspiring cognatogen now grants a +4 dodge bonus to AC and a +4 bonus to Reflex Defense; a –6 penalty to Strength and Constitution; and the effects of having the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Item Lore (Investigator Talent)|item lore]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Tenacious Inspiration (Investigator Talent)|tenacious inspiration]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/investigator-talents/Underworld Inspiration (Investigator Talent)|underworld inspiration]] investigator talents. An alchemist must be at least 16th level and have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Greater Inspiring Cognatogen (Discovery)|greater inspiring cognatogen]] discovery to select this discovery.
