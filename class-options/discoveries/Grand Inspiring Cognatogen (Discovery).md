# Grand Inspiring Cognatogen

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su).*

The alchemist’s inspiring cognatogen now grants a +4 dodge bonus to AC and a +4 bonus to Reflex saving throws; a –6 penalty to Strength and Constitution; and the effects of having the item lore, tenacious inspiration, and underworld inspiration investigator talents. An alchemist must be at least 16th level and have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Greater Inspiring Cognatogen (Discovery)|greater inspiring cognatogen]] discovery to select this discovery.
