# Blinding Bomb

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su)\*.*

When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose for it to detonate very brightly. Creatures that take a direct hit from a blinding bomb are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1 minute unless they succeed at a Fortitude save. Creatures in the splash area that fail their saves against the bomb are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute. This is a light effect. An alchemist must be at least 8th level before selecting this discovery.
