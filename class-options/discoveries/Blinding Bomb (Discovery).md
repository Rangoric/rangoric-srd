# Blinding Bomb

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su)\*._

When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose for it to detonate very brightly. Creatures that take a direct hit from a blinding bomb are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1 minute if the bomb also hits their Fortitude. Creatures in the splash area that the bomb hits are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute. This is a light effect. An alchemist must be at least 8th level before selecting this discovery.
