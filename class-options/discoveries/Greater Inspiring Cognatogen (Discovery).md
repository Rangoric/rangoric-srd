# Greater Inspiring Cognatogen

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su).*

The alchemist’s inspiring cognatogen now grants a +2 dodge bonus to AC and a +2 bonus to Reflex saving throws; a –4 penalty to Strength and Constitution; and the effects of having the amazing inspiration, eidetic recollection, and expanded inspiration investigator discoveries. An alchemist must be at least 12th level and must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Inspiring Cognatogen (Discovery)|inspiring cognatogen]] discovery or class feature to select this discovery.
