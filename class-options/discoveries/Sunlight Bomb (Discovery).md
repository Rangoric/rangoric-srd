# Sunlight Bomb

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su)\*.*

The alchemist can throw bombs that explode with a searing radiance equivalent to that of sunlight and that act as blinding bombs. Undead, fungi, molds, oozes, slimes, and creatures to which sunlight is harmful or unnatural take +2 damage per die from the bomb. Undead that are harmed by sunlight and that fail their saves against the bomb are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. An alchemist must be at least 10th level and must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Blinding Bomb (Discovery)|blinding bomb]] discovery before he can select this discovery.
