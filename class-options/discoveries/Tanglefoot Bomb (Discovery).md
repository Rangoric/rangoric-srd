# Tanglefoot Bomb

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su)\*.*

A creature that takes a direct hit from a tanglefoot bomb must save against the bomb’s DC or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] and glued to the floor as if it had failed its save against a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services|tanglefoot bag]]. Creatures in the splash area that fail their saves are entangled but not glued to the floor; those who make this save are not entangled at all.
