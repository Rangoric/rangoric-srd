# Tanglefoot Bomb

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|alchemist discovery]] (Su)\*._

A creature that takes a direct hit from a tanglefoot bomb is attacked by the bomb (its attack bonus against Reflex), and on a hit is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] and glued to the floor as if a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tanglefoot Bag|tanglefoot bag]]. Creatures in the splash area that the bomb hits are entangled but not glued to the floor; those it misses are not entangled at all.
