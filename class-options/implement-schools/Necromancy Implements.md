# Necromancy Implements

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-implements|implement school]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]]._

Implements that draw power from necromancy can control undead and harm the living.

**Implements**: Bone, coin, doll, drum, robe, skull.

**Spells**: the necromancy spells on the occultist spell list: [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ0-necromancy|knacks]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ1-necromancy|1st]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ2-necromancy|2nd]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ3-necromancy|3rd]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ4-necromancy|4th]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ5-necromancy|5th]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ6-necromancy|6th]].

**Resonant Power**: Each time the occultist invests [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-mental-focus|mental focus]] into a necromancy implement, the implement grants the following resonant power. The implement’s bearer gains the benefits of this power until the occultist refreshes his focus.

**Necromantic Focus (Su)**: The implement grants its possessor greater power over the undead. Whoever possesses the implement can control an additional 2 Hit Dice of undead for every point of mental focus invested in the item (to a maximum number of Hit Dice equal to 4 × your occultist level). Any spellcaster who bears the implement can add it as an additional focus component for a necromancy spell he casts to create undead. If he does so, he can create an additional number of HD of undead equal to the additional HD the implement allows him to control (apply this effect after doubling the HD of undead for _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]]_, if applicable). Undead creatures take a –1 penalty on defenses against spells cast by the bearer of this implement for every 4 points of mental focus invested in this implement. ^necromancy-resonant-power

**Base Focus Power**: All occultists who learn to use necromancy implements gain the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-focus-powers|focus power]].

**Mind Fear (Sp)**: As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to cause a living creature to succumb to fear. The target must be within 30 feet of you, and the power attacks its Will, a miss negating the effect. If it hits and the target has a number of Hit Dice less than or equal to yours, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1d4 rounds. If it hits and the target has a number of Hit Dice greater than yours, it is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d4 rounds. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. ^necromancy-base-focus-power
