# Illusion Implements

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-implements|implement school]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]]._

Illusion implements allow the occultist to distort the senses and cloak creatures from sight.

**Implements**: Crystal, hat, mask, prism, ring.

**Spells**: the illusion spells on the occultist spell list: [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ0-illusion|knacks]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ1-illusion|1st]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ2-illusion|2nd]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ3-illusion|3rd]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ4-illusion|4th]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ5-illusion|5th]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Occultist Spell List#^occ6-illusion|6th]].

**Resonant Power**: Each time the occultist invests [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-mental-focus|mental focus]] into an illusion implement, the implement grants the following resonant power. The implement’s bearer gains the benefits of this power until the occultist refreshes his focus.

**Distortion (Sp)**: The implement allows its bearer to distort his form and location, protecting him from harm. As a standard action, the bearer can gain a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] miss chance equal to 5% for every point of mental focus invested in the implement (to a maximum of 5% + 5% for every 2 occultist levels you possess) until the next time the bearer makes an attack. If this miss chance reaches 50%, it doesn’t increase further, but the bearer gains all the benefits of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisibility]]. Creatures with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_, or similar abilities ignore the miss chance from this ability. ^illusion-resonant-power

**Base Focus Power**: All occultists who learn to use illusion implements gain the following [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-focus-powers|focus power]].

**Minor Figment (Sp)**: As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to create a minor figment. This can function as either _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Minor Image|minor image]]_. In either case, the effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to your occultist level. If you are 7th level or higher, the figment lasts for a number of minutes equal to your occultist level, and you can cause the figment to change once during its duration when a specified condition occurs. ^illusion-base-focus-power
