# Earth Element

*An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements|element]] a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] can choose with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|elemental focus]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]].*

Kineticists who focus on the element of earth are called geokineticists. Geokineticists manipulate the earth itself, and they are masters of defensive techniques.

**Class Skills**: A geokineticist adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] to her list of class skills.

**Kinetic Blasts and Defense**: Simple blast: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Blast|earth blast]]. Composite blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Magma Blast|magma blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Metal Blast|metal blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Mud Blast|mud blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Sandstorm Blast|sandstorm blast]]. Defense: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Flesh of Stone|flesh of stone]].

**Wild Talents**: 1st—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Basic Geokinesis|basic geokinesis]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Walk|earth walk]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Cover|kinetic cover]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Pushing Infusion|pushing infusion]]†; 2nd—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Bowling Infusion|bowling infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Climb|earth climb]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Entangling Infusion|entangling infusion]]†; 3rd—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Impale|impale]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Jagged Flesh|jagged flesh]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Magnetic Infusion|magnetic infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Rare-Metal Infusion|rare-metal infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Tremorsense (Wild Talent)|tremorsense]]; 4th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Enduring Earth|enduring earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Shift Earth|shift earth]]; 5th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Glide (Wild Talent)|earth glide]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Stone Sculptor|stone sculptor]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Tremorsense, Greater|tremorsense, greater]]; 6th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Deadly Earth|deadly earth]]†; 7th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Fragmentation|fragmentation]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Shift Earth, Greater|shift earth, greater]]; 9th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Seismic Master|seismic master]]. Infusion wild talents are marked with a dagger (†); the others are utility wild talents.
