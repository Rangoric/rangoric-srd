# Water Element

*An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements|element]] a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] can choose with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|elemental focus]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]].*

Kineticists who focus on the element of water are called hydrokineticists. Hydrokineticists master the flow of water or the numbing chill of cold, and they are known for their versatility and flowing forms, like the ever-changing currents.

**Class Skills**: A hydrokineticist adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] to her list of class skills.

**Kinetic Blasts and Defense**: Simple blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Cold Blast|cold blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Water Blast|water blast]]. Composite blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blizzard Blast|blizzard blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Charged Water Blast|charged water blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Ice Blast|ice blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Mud Blast|mud blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Steam Blast|steam blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Winter Blast|winter blast]]. Defense: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Shroud of Water|shroud of water]].
