# Aether Element

_An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements|element]] a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] can choose with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|elemental focus]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]]._

Kineticists who focus on the element of aether—a rare substance formed when elemental energy affects the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Ethereal Plane|Ethereal Plane]]—are called telekineticists. Telekineticists use strands of aether to move objects with their minds.

**Class Skills**: A telekineticist adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] to her list of class skills.

**Kinetic Blasts and Defense**: Simple blast: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Blast|telekinetic blast]]. Composite blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Aetheric Boost|aetheric boost]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Force Blast|force blast]]. Defense: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Force Ward|force ward]].

**Wild Talents**: 1st—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Basic Telekinesis|basic telekinesis]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Cover|kinetic cover]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Healer|kinetic healer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Pushing Infusion|pushing infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Finesse|telekinetic finesse]];
