# Air Element

_An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements|element]] a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] can choose with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|elemental focus]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]]._

Kineticists who focus on the element of air are called aerokineticists. Aerokineticists often control air flow or electricity, specializing in mobility and ranged combat.

**Class Skills**: An aerokineticist adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] to her list of class skills.

**Kinetic Blasts and Defense**: Simple blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Air Blast|air blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Electric Blast|electric blast]]. Composite blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blizzard Blast|blizzard blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Charged Water Blast|charged water blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Plasma Blast|plasma blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Sandstorm Blast|sandstorm blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Spring Blast|spring blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Thunderstorm Blast|thunderstorm blast]]. Defense: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Enveloping Winds|enveloping winds]].
