# Fire Element

*An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements|element]] a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] can choose with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|elemental focus]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]].*

Kineticists who focus on the element of fire are called pyrokineticists. Pyrokineticists wield elemental fire as a potent weapon, and they possess a powerful offense. (The psionic [[rangoric-srd/psionics/Pyrokineticist|pyrokineticist]] prestige class is something else.)

**Class Skills**: A pyrokineticist adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] to her list of class skills.

**Kinetic Blasts and Defense**: Simple blast: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Fire Blast|fire blast]]. Composite blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blue Flame Blast|blue flame blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Magma Blast|magma blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Plasma Blast|plasma blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Steam Blast|steam blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Summer Blast|summer blast]]. Defense: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Searing Flesh|searing flesh]].
