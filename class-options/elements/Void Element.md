# Void Element

_An [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements|element]] a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] can choose with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|elemental focus]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]]._

Like [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]], void forms where elemental energy meets another material, in this case the substance of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#Negative Energy Plane|Negative Energy Plane]]. Chaokineticists command this strange force in a way that in some respects resembles the manipulation of aether.

**Class Skills**: A chaokineticist adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] to her list of class skills.

**Kinetic Blasts and Defense**: Simple blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Gravity Blast|gravity blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Negative Blast|negative blast]]. Composite blasts: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Gravitic Boost|gravitic boost]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Negative Admixture|negative admixture]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Void Blast|void blast]]. Defense: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Emptiness|emptiness]].

**Wild Talents**: 1st—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Basic Chaokinesis|basic chaokinesis]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Pulling Infusion|pulling infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Pushing Infusion|pushing infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Void Healer|void healer]]; 2nd—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Eyes of the Void (Wild Talent)|eyes of the void]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/No Breath (Wild Talent)|no breath]]; 3rd—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Darkness Infusion|darkness infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Gravity Control|gravity control]]; 4th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Healing Burst|healing burst]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Singularity|singularity]]†; 5th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Eyes of the Void, Greater|eyes of the void, greater]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Gravity Control, Greater|gravity control, greater]]; 6th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Darkness Infusion, Greater|darkness infusion, greater]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Suffocate|suffocate]]; 7th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Enervating Infusion|enervating infusion]]†; 9th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Gravity Master|gravity master]]. Infusion wild talents are marked with a dagger (†); the others are utility wild talents.
