# Elements

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] can choose from among the following elements with her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-focus|elemental focus]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]] class features. Each element’s note gives the class skills it adds, its kinetic blasts and defense, and the utility and infusion [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents|wild talents]] it offers: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Void Element|void]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]].

## Universal

The following wild talents are available to all kineticists: 1st—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Draining Infusion|draining infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Extended Range|extended range]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Blade|kinetic blade]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Fist|kinetic fist]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Skilled Kineticist|skilled kineticist]]; 2nd—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Skilled Kineticist, Greater|skilled kineticist, greater]]; 3rd—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Elemental Grip|elemental grip]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Extreme Range|extreme range]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Flurry of Blasts|flurry of blasts]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Whip|kinetic whip]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Mobile Blast|mobile blast]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Snake (Wild Talent)|snake]]†; 4th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Expanded Defense|expanded defense]]; 5th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Grappling Infusion|grappling infusion]]†, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Form|kinetic form]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Spark of Life|spark of life]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wall (Wild Talent)|wall]]†; 6th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Ride the Blast|ride the blast]]; 8th—[[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Reverse Shift|reverse shift]]. Infusion wild talents are marked with a dagger (†); the others are utility wild talents.
