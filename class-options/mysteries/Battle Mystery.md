# Battle Mystery

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|oracle mystery]].* The shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Battle Spirit|battle spirit]] shares its name; a character with both must choose matching ones (see the shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit|spirit]]).

**Class Skills**: An oracle with the battle mystery adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] to her list of class skills.

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]* (2nd), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]* (4th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Vestment|magic vestment]]* (6th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Fire|wall of fire]]* (8th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]* (10th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength, Mass|mass bull's strength]]* (12th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]]* (14th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Earthquake|earthquake]]* (16th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Storm of Vengeance|storm of vengeance]]* (18th).

## Final Revelation

Upon reaching 20th level, you become an avatar of battle. You can take a full-attack action and move up to your speed as a full-round action (you can move before or after the attacks). Whenever you score a critical hit, you can ignore any DR the target might possess. You gain a +4 insight bonus to your AC for the purpose of confirming critical hits against you. When you are below 0 hit points, you do not die until your negative total is in excess of twice your Constitution score. ^battle-final-revelation
