# Lore Mystery

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|oracle mystery]].* The shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Lore Spirit|lore spirit]] shares its name; a character with both must choose matching ones (see the shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit|spirit]]).

**Class Skills**: An oracle with the lore mystery adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] and all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]] to her list of class skills.

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Identify|identify]]* (2nd), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]* (4th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Object|locate object]]* (6th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Legend Lore|legend lore]]* (8th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contact Other Plane|contact other plane]]* (10th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom, Mass|mass owl's wisdom]]* (12th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vision|vision]]* (14th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Moment of Prescience|moment of prescience]]* (16th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Time Stop|time stop]]* (18th).

## Final Revelation

You gain the ability to take 20 on all Knowledge skill checks. Your understanding of the fundamental underpinnings of reality has also become so advanced that once per day, you can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]*. This ability does not require a material component, but the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]* cannot be used to grant ability score bonuses, nor can it be used to replicate spells with expensive material components. ^lore-final-revelation
