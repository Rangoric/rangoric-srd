# Mysteries

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle|oracle]]’s mysteries, one note each. Each mystery note lists the class skills it adds, its bonus spells, its revelations (each its own note in [[rangoric-srd/class-options/revelations/Revelations|Revelations]], embedded) and its final revelation. The class note has the rules: choosing a mystery and learning its spells ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|Mystery]]), learning revelations and their save DC ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|Revelation]]), and the final revelation at 20th level ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-final-revelation|Final Revelation]]).
