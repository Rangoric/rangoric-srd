# Ancestor Mystery

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|oracle mystery]].*

**Class Skills**: An oracle with the ancestor mystery adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] and all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]] to her list of class skills.

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unseen Servant|unseen servant]]* (2nd), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spiritual Weapon|spiritual weapon]]* (4th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]* (6th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spiritual Ally|spiritual ally]]* (8th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]* (10th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater|greater heroism]]* (12th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt|ethereal jaunt]]* (14th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vision|vision]]* (16th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection|astral projection]]* (18th).

## Final Revelation

Upon reaching 20th level, you become one with the spirits of your ancestors. You gain a bonus on Will saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier, blindsense out to a range of 60 feet, and a +4 bonus on your caster level for all divination spells. You can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection|astral projection]]* as a spell-like ability once per day without requiring material components. ^ancestor-final-revelation
