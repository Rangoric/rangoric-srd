# Outer Dark Mystery

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|oracle mystery]]._

**Class Skills**: An oracle with the outer dark mystery adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] to her list of class skills.

**Bonus Spells**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entropic Shield|entropic shield]]_ (2nd), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dust of Twilight|dust of twilight]]_ (4th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]_ (6th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]_ (8th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feeblemind|feeblemind]]_ (10th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_ (12th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insanity (Spell)|insanity]]_ (14th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reverse Gravity|reverse gravity]]_ (16th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Interplanetary Teleport|interplanetary teleport]]_ (18th).

## Final Revelation

Upon reaching 20th level, you become a truly alien and unnatural creature. You gain damage reduction 5/— and immunity to acid, critical hits, and sneak attacks. Once per day, you can cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shapechange|shapechange]]_ as a spell-like ability without requiring a material component. ^outer-dark-final-revelation
