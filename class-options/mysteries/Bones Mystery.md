# Bones Mystery

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|oracle mystery]].* The shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Bones Spirit|bones spirit]] shares its name; a character with both must choose matching ones (see the shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit|spirit]]).

**Class Skills**: An oracle with the bones mystery adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] to her list of class skills.

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]* (2nd), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life|false life]]* (4th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]* (6th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]* (8th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]* (10th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Circle of Death|circle of death]]* (12th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Undead|control undead]]* (14th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Horrid Wilting|horrid wilting]]* (16th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wail of the Banshee|wail of the banshee]]* (18th).

## Final Revelation

Upon reaching 20th level, you become a master of death. Once per round, you can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stabilize (Spell)|stabilize]]* as a free action. If you are brought to below 0 hit points, you automatically stabilize. You can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]* at will without paying a material component cost (although you are still subject to the usual Hit Die control limit). Once per day, you can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Power Word Kill|power word kill]]*, but the spell can target a creature with 150 hit points or less. ^bones-final-revelation
