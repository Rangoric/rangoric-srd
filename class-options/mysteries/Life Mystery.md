# Life Mystery

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|oracle mystery]].* The shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Life Spirit|life spirit]] shares its name; a character with both must choose matching ones (see the shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit|spirit]]).

**Class Skills**: An oracle with the life mystery adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] to her list of class skills.

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]* (2nd), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]* (4th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]* (6th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]* (8th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]* (10th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]* (12th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Greater|greater restoration]]* (14th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal, Mass|mass heal]]* (16th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]* (18th).

## Final Revelation

Upon reaching 20th level, you become a perfect channel for life energy. You become immune to bleed, death attacks, exhaustion, fatigue, nausea effects, negative levels, and sickened effects. Ability damage and drain cannot reduce you below 1 in any ability score. You automatically make saving throws against massive damage. When you are below 0 hit points, you do not die until your negative total is in excess of twice your Constitution score. ^life-final-revelation
