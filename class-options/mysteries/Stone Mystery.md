# Stone Mystery

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-mystery|oracle mystery]].* The shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Stone Spirit|stone spirit]] shares its name; a character with both must choose matching ones (see the shaman’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-spirit|spirit]]).

**Class Skills**: An oracle with the stone mystery adds [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] to her list of class skills.

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Stone|magic stone]]* (2nd), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Call|stone call]]* (4th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]* (6th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Stone|wall of stone]]* (8th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]* (10th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Tell|stone tell]]* (12th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Statue|statue]]* (14th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Repel Metal or Stone|repel metal or stone]]* (16th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Clashing Rocks|clashing rocks]]* (18th).

## Final Revelation

Upon reaching 20th level, you become a master of acid and earth. You can apply any one of the following feats to any acid or earth spell without increasing the level or casting time: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Enlarge Spell|Enlarge Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Extend Spell|Extend Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Silent Spell|Silent Spell]], or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Still Spell|Still Spell]]. ^stone-final-revelation
