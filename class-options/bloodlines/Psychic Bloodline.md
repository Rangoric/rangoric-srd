# Psychic Bloodline

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-bloodline|sorcerer bloodline]].* Psychic power runs through your blood, from a family predisposition or from exposure to a powerful psychic phenomenon.

**Class Skill**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]].

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust I|mind thrust I]]* (3rd), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Id Insinuation I|id insinuation I]]* (5th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ego Whip I|ego whip I]]* (7th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Intellect Fortress I|intellect fortress I]]* (9th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Psychic Crush I|psychic crush I]]* (11th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mental Barrier V|mental barrier V]]* (13th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tower of Iron Will III|tower of iron will III]]* (15th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bilocation|bilocation]]* (17th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Microcosm (Spell)|microcosm]]* (19th).

**Bonus Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Focused Spell|Focused Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Heighten Spell|Heighten Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intuitive Spell|Intuitive Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Iron Will|Iron Will]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Logical Spell|Logical Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|Quicken Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Sense Motive), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]].

**Bloodline Arcana**: Your sorcerer spells and spell-like abilities count as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic]] instead of arcane. You use thought and emotion components instead of verbal and somatic components when casting your spells (see [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Components|Psychic Components]]).

## Bloodline Powers

Your psychic powers make you a master mentalist, but your soul struggles with the urge to transcend into a pure thought-form.

**Psychic Strike (Su)**: At 1st level, you can overwhelm the minds of those nearby. You can target one creature within 30 feet that you can see; that creature must succeed at a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier) or it takes 1d6 points of damage + 1 point for every 2 sorcerer levels you possess and becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. ^psychic-bloodline-psychic-strike

**Mental Resistance (Ex)**: At 3rd level, your mind blocks attempts to assail it. You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting effects. At 9th level, this bonus increases to +4. ^psychic-bloodline-mental-resistance

**Undercasting Prodigy (Sp)**: Starting at 9th level, whenever you gain a new level of spells, you automatically replace any psychic bloodline spells that can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Undercasting|undercast]] with the highest-level version you can cast in your list of spells known. For example, at 9th level, you would replace *mind thrust I*, *id insinuation I*, and *ego whip I* with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust IV|mind thrust IV]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Id Insinuation III|id insinuation III]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ego Whip II|ego whip II]]*, respectively, as spells known. ^psychic-bloodline-undercasting-prodigy

**Thoughtsense (Sp)**: At 15th level, you can detect the thoughts of others around you, as a constant *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Thoughtsense|thoughtsense]]* effect with a range of 30 feet instead of 60 feet. ^psychic-bloodline-thoughtsense

**True Thought-Form (Sp)**: At 20th level, you can shed the restrictions of the flesh and become a thought-form, a being that exists independently of its substance. Whenever a creature would kill you, you can attempt to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Swap|mind swap]]* against that creature as an immediate action just before dying. ^psychic-bloodline-true-thought-form
