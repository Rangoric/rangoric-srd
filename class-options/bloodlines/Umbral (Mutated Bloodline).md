# Umbral

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines#Mutated Bloodlines|mutated bloodline]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Shadow Bloodline|shadow bloodline]], for a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/sorcerer/Wildblooded (Archetype)|wildblooded]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]._

Your nature is to gather the darkness into yourself.

**Associated Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Shadow Bloodline|Shadow]].

**Bloodline Arcana**: Whenever you cast a spell in an area of dim light or darkness, your effective caster level is increased by 1.

**Bloodline Powers**: Your shadow magic is more defensive than offensive.

**Cloak of Shadows (Sp)**: At 1st level, as a standard action, you can grant one target a cloak of shadows. This cloak gives the target a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks made in areas of dim or no light equal to 1/2 your sorcerer level for 1 round per 2 sorcerer levels you possess (minimum +1 bonus for 1 round). You may use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Shadow Bloodline|shadowstrike]].
