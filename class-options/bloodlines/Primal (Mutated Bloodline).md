# Primal

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines#Mutated Bloodlines|mutated bloodline]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Elemental Bloodline|elemental bloodline]], for a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/sorcerer/Wildblooded (Archetype)|wildblooded]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]._

Your powers are attuned to the concentrated core of the elemental plane.

**Associated Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Elemental Bloodline|Elemental]].

**Bloodline Arcana**: Whenever you cast a spell with an energy descriptor that matches your elemental bloodline's energy type, that spell deals +1 point of damage per die rolled.

**Bloodline Powers**: You can infuse raw energy into your summoned minions.

**Elementalist Summoning (Su)**: At 9th level, whenever you summon a creature, it gains energy resistance 10 against the energy type that matches your elemental bloodline (if it already has such resistance, its resistance increases by +5), and its natural attacks deal an additional 1d6 points of damage of the same energy type. This bloodline power replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Elemental Bloodline|elemental blast]].
