# Anarchic

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines#Mutated Bloodlines|mutated bloodline]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Protean Bloodline|protean bloodline]], for a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/sorcerer/Wildblooded (Archetype)|wildblooded]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]].*

Your magical power taps into pure anarchy.

**Associated Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Protean Bloodline|Protean]].

**Bloodline Arcana**: Whenever you fail a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] to cast a spell, a cantrip effect is created. Randomly determine one of your cantrips known (for example, if you know 6 cantrips, roll 1d6). There is a 50% chance this cantrip affects a target of your choice within 60 feet; otherwise it affects you.

**Bloodline Powers**: Raw magical energy lashes out when you destroy or negate hostile spells.

**Wild Feedback (Su)**: At 3rd level, when you successfully dispel or counterspell an opponent's spell, the caster (if it is within 100 feet) takes 1d6 points of damage +1 per level of the spell affected. This bloodline power replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Protean Bloodline|protean resistances]].
