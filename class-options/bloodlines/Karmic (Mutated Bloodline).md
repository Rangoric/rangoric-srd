# Karmic

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines#Mutated Bloodlines|mutated bloodline]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Destined Bloodline|destined bloodline]], for a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/sorcerer/Wildblooded (Archetype)|wildblooded]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]._

The universe punishes those who interfere with destiny.

**Associated Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Destined Bloodline|Destined]].

**Bloodline Arcana**: If you are casting defensively because a creature is threatening you and you fail your concentration check, one of the creatures that is threatening you provokes an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] from you or from one ally who is adjacent to the enemy. You decide which creature provokes this attack and which of its adjacent opponents may make the attack.

**Bloodline Powers**: Your blood responds angrily when you are attacked.

**Fate's Retribution (Su)**: Starting at 1st level, when you are hit by a melee attack, as an immediate action, you can curse the creature that struck you. The target takes a –2 penalty on all attack and damage rolls for 1d4 rounds. It attacks Will at 1/2 your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier, and a miss negates this effect. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Destined Bloodline|touch of destiny]].
