# Bedrock

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines#Mutated Bloodlines|mutated bloodline]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Deep Earth Bloodline|deep earth bloodline]], for a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/sorcerer/Wildblooded (Archetype)|wildblooded]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]._

Your earth magic is more defensive than offensive.

**Associated Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Deep Earth Bloodline|Deep Earth]].

**Bloodline Arcana**: Whenever you cast a spell of the summoning subschool, the creatures summoned gain DR/adamantine equal to 1/2 your sorcerer level (minimum 1). This does not stack with any DR the creature might have.

**Bloodline Powers**: Rock is timeless; it endures.

**Iron Hide (Sp)**: At 9th level, as a swift action, you can grant yourself [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]] 10/adamantine for a number of rounds per day equal to your sorcerer level. The rounds do not need to be used consecutively. This bloodline power replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Deep Earth Bloodline|crystal shard]].
