# Seaborn

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines#Mutated Bloodlines|mutated bloodline]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Aquatic Bloodline|aquatic bloodline]], for a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/sorcerer/Wildblooded (Archetype)|wildblooded]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]._

Your powers rise like the tides.

**Associated Bloodline**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Aquatic Bloodline|Aquatic]].

**Bloodline Arcana**: When you are in a body of water large enough to float in, your effective caster level is increased by 1.

**Bloodline Powers**: You prefer to attack with water rather than to leech it away.

**Water Blast (Sp)**: As a standard action, you can fire a bolt of water at a foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack. The foe is knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]], and at your option may be pushed 5 feet directly away from you. It attacks Reflex at 1/2 your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier, and a miss negates this effect. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Aquatic Bloodline|dehydrating touch]].
