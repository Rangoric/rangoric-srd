# Ectoplasm Bloodline

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-bloodline|sorcerer bloodline]]._ Your family has a connection with ectoplasm and the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Ethereal Plane|Ethereal Plane]].

**Class Skill**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]].

**Bonus Spells**: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_ (3rd), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Whip|ghost whip]]_ (5th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ectoplasmic Snare|ectoplasmic snare]]_ (7th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spirit-Bound Blade|spirit-bound blade]]_ (9th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Ectoplasm (Spell)|wall of ectoplasm]]_ (11th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt|ethereal jaunt]]_ (13th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ectoplasmic Eruption|ectoplasmic eruption]]_ (15th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Demiplane|create demiplane]]_ (17th), _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Etherealness|etherealness]]_ (19th).

**Bonus Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ectoplasmic Spell|Ectoplasmic Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lingering Spell|Lingering Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Silent Spell|Silent Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Acrobatics or Stealth), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]].

**Bloodline Arcana**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|Incorporeal]] creatures take 75% of the normal amount of damage from your damaging spells, and when your non-damaging spells attack incorporeal creatures' defenses, they roll twice and take the higher result.

## Bloodline Powers

You can control and emit ectoplasm, leaving you less grounded in the physical world.

**Entangling Ectoplasm (Sp)**: At 1st level, you can create a ball of ectoplasm and hurl it at targets within 30 feet. This ectoplasm acts as a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tanglefoot Bag|tanglefoot bag]] that can also entangle incorporeal creatures. The ectoplasm dissipates within 1d3 rounds. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. ^ectoplasm-entangling-ectoplasm

**Ectoplasmic Reach (Su)**: At 3rd level, you can exude small tendrils of ectoplasm that can be used to make melee touch attacks as part of a spell with a range of touch. You can make melee touch attacks as if you had an additional 5 feet of reach. You must have a free hand with which to direct the ectoplasm. This ability does not otherwise increase your threatened area. At 11th level, this additional reach increases to 10 feet. At 17th level, this additional reach increases to 15 feet. ^ectoplasm-ectoplasmic-reach

**Ectoplasmic Form (Sp)**: At 9th level, you can transform into a cloud of ectoplasm. Treat this as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_, except that you can fly at a speed of 30 feet, and you can solidify your form sufficiently to carry small objects, though fine manipulation is impossible. You can use this ability for a number of minutes per day equal to your sorcerer level. These minutes need not be consecutive, but must be used in 1-minute increments. ^ectoplasm-ectoplasmic-form

**Malevolent Ectoplasm (Sp)**: At 15th level, your ectoplasmic tendrils can lash out at creatures around you. Treat this as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]_ effect centered on your position, but the tentacles can also grapple ethereal and incorporeal creatures, and they do not affect you (though they still affect your allies). You can use this ability a number of rounds per day equal to your sorcerer level. These rounds need not be consecutive. ^ectoplasm-malevolent-ectoplasm

**Ectoplasmic Body (Ex)**: At 20th level, you are more ectoplasm than flesh. You become immune to sneak attacks and critical hits and gain DR 5/slashing. ^ectoplasm-ectoplasmic-body
