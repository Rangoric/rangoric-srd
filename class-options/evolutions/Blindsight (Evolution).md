# Blindsight

*A 4-point evolution (Ex) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon’s senses sharpen even further, granting it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsight]] out to a range of 30 feet. The eidolon can maneuver and attack as normal, ignoring darkness, invisibility, and most forms of concealment as long as it has line of effect to the target. The eidolon must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Blindsense (Evolution)|blindsense]] evolution to take this evolution. The summoner must be at least 11th level before selecting this evolution.
