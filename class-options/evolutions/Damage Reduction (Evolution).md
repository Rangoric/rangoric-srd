# Damage Reduction

*A 3-point evolution (Su) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon’s body becomes resistant to harm, granting it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]]. Choose one alignment: chaotic, evil, good, or lawful. The eidolon gains DR 5 that can be bypassed by weapons that possess the chosen alignment. The alignment must be opposite to one of the alignments possessed by the eidolon. At 12th level, this protection can be increased to DR 10 by spending 2 additional evolution points. The summoner must be at least 9th level before selecting this evolution.
