# Reach

_A 1-point evolution (Ex) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon._

One of an eidolon’s attacks is capable of striking at foes at a distance. Pick one attack. The eidolon’s reach with that attack increases by 5 feet.
