# Dimension Door

*A 4-point evolution (Sp) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon learns to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimension Door|dimension door]]* as a spell-like ability once per day. The caster level for this evolution is equal to the eidolon’s Hit Dice. The save DC for this spell is 10 + 1/2 the eidolon’s HD + the eidolon’s Charisma modifier. The eidolon must have a Charisma score of at least 14 to take this evolution. The summoner must be at least 13th level before selecting this evolution.
