# Ultimate Magic

*A 4-point evolution (Sp) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon learns to cast a powerful spell as a spell-like ability. Select one spell from the following list: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Sight|arcane sight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Food and Water|create food and water]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lightning Bolt|lightning bolt]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Image|major image]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stinking Cloud|stinking cloud]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues|tongues]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]*. This spell can be cast once per day as a spell-like ability. The caster level for this evolution is equal to the eidolon’s Hit Dice – 2. The save DC for this spell is 10 + 1/2 the eidolon’s HD + the eidolon’s Charisma modifier. The eidolon must have a Charisma score of at least 13 and must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Major Magic (Evolution)|major magic]] evolution to take this evolution.

The summoner must be at least 11th level before selecting this evolution. This evolution can be selected more than once. Each time an eidolon selects this evolution, it applies to a new spell from the above list.
