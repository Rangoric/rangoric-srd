# Weapon Training

*A 2-point evolution (Ex) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon learns to use a weapon, gaining [[rangoric-srd/feats/Simple Weapon Proficiency|Simple Weapon Proficiency]] as a bonus feat. If 2 additional evolution points are spent, it gains proficiency with all martial weapons as well.
