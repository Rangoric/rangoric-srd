# Low-Light Vision

_A 1-point evolution (Ex) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon._

An eidolon gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], enabling it to see twice as far as a human in conditions of dim light.
