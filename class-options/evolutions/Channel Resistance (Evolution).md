# Channel Resistance

_A 2-point evolution (Ex) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon._

An eidolon becomes less easily affected by the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]] ability of clerics or paladins. The eidolon gains a +2 bonus on defenses against the effects of channel energy, including effects that rely on the use of channel energy (such as the Command Undead feat). At 7th level, this bonus can be increased to +4 by spending 2 additional evolution points. The eidolon must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Undead Appearance (Evolution)|undead appearance]] evolution to take this evolution.
