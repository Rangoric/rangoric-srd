# Lifesense

*A 4-point evolution (Su) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon can pinpoint living creatures with ease. The eidolon notices and locates living creatures within 60 feet, just as if it possessed the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Blindsight (Evolution)|blindsight]] evolution. The eidolon must possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Undead Appearance (Evolution)|undead appearance]] evolution to take this evolution. The summoner must be at least 11th level before selecting this evolution.
