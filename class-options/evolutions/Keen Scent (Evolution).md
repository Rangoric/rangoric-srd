# Keen Scent

*A 2-point evolution (Ex) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon’s sense of smell becomes even more acute. The eidolon can notice other creatures by scent in a 180-foot radius underwater and can detect blood in the water at ranges of up to a mile. The eidolon must possess the gills and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Scent (Evolution)|scent]] evolutions to take this evolution.
