# Shadow Blend

_A 2-point evolution (Su) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon._

In any condition of illumination other than bright light, the eidolon disappears into the shadows, giving it [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] (20% miss chance). If it has the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Shadow Form (Evolution)|shadow form]] evolution, it instead gains [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|total concealment]] (50% miss chance). The eidolon can suspend or resume this ability as a free action.
