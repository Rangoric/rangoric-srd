# Claws

*A 1-point evolution (Ex) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon has a pair of vicious claws at the end of its limbs, giving it two claw attacks. These attacks are primary attacks. The claws deal 1d4 points of damage (1d6 if Large, 1d8 if Huge). The eidolon must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Limbs (Evolution)|limbs]] evolution to take this evolution. This evolution can only be applied to the limbs (legs) evolution once. This evolution can be selected more than once, but the eidolon must possess an equal number of the limbs evolution.
