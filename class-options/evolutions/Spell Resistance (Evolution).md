# Spell Resistance

*A 4-point evolution (Ex) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon.*

An eidolon is protected against magic, gaining [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]]. The eidolon’s spell resistance is equal to 11 + the summoner’s level. This spell resistance does not apply to spells cast by the summoner. The summoner must be at least 9th level before selecting this evolution.
