# Damage Reduction

_A 3-point evolution (Su) for an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon._

The eidolon’s body becomes more resistant to harm. Increase the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] granted by the eidolon’s subtype by 5. _Requirements_: Summoner level 15th, damage reduction granted by the eidolon’s subtype.
