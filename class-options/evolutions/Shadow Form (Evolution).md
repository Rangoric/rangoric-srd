# Shadow Form

*A 2-point evolution (Su) for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]]’s or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon.*

The eidolon's body becomes shadowy and more indistinct. This shadow form grants the eidolon constant [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] (20% miss chance), and its melee attacks affect [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures as if it had the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]]* weapon property. The eidolon's melee attacks deal only half damage to corporeal creatures.
