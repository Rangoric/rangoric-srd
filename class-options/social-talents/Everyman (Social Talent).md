# Everyman

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-social-talent|social talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante can take on the appearance of a specific individual whenever he assumes a mundane guise using his [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Many Guises (Social Talent)|many guises]] social talent. The individual must be a farmer, laborer, or peasant. While disguised as this individual, the vigilante receives a +20 circumstance bonus on Imitation checks to appear as that individual. Any spell or ability designed to locate the individual has a 50% chance of finding the vigilante instead of the actual individual, and divination spells and abilities used on the vigilante give results as if he were the actual individual. Although this doesn’t grant the vigilante any special knowledge of the individual, the vigilante’s training grants him a +10 circumstance bonus on Deception checks to properly play the part of the individual.

A vigilante must be at least 11th level and have the many guises social talent to select this talent.
