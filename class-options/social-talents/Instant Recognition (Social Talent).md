# Instant Recognition

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-social-talent|social talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante is so well known that his name precedes him, even to other communities. It only takes 4 hours of effort to get the word out to transfer his renown benefits to a new community.

A vigilante must be at least 13th level and have both the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Incredible Renown (Social Talent)|incredible renown]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Triumphant Return (Social Talent)|triumphant return]] social talents to select this talent.
