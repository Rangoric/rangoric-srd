# Incredible Renown

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-social-talent|social talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante is incredibly famous in both identities. He can gain renown in a single community of up to 25,000 individuals (a large city) or up to two smaller cities of no more than 10,000 individuals each (two small cities). The bonus while he is in his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-dual-identity|social identity]] remains unchanged, but the circumstance bonus to Intimidation from his [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Great Renown (Social Talent)|great renown]] social talent while he is in his vigilante identity increases to +8.

A vigilante must be at least 11th level and have the great [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Renown (Social Talent)|renown]] social talent to select this talent.
