# In Vogue

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-social-talent|social talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante’s crafting or professional business is always at the height of the local trends, allowing the vigilante to gain more profits than usual. Goods he crafts with a Craft skill he chose with social grace are worth 1/3 more gp than normal due to his celebrity, without increasing the cost to create. Whenever he uses a Profession skill he chose with social grace to make money, he makes twice as much money.

A vigilante must be at least 5th level and have both the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Double Time (Social Talent)|double time]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Social Grace (Social Talent)|social grace]] social talents to take this talent.
