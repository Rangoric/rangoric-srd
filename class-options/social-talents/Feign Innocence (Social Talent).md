# Feign Innocence

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-social-talent|social talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-dual-identity|social identity]] seems so innocent that it is hard to believe he was involved in wrongdoing. Within his area of renown, this provides a nonmagical effect identical to the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Innocence|innocence]]* spell.

A vigilante must be at least 5th level and have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Renown (Social Talent)|renown]] social talent to take this talent.
