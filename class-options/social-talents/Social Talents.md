# Social Talents

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante|vigilante]]’s social talents, from Pathfinder’s *Ultimate Intrigue*. [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Beast Speech (Social Talent)|Beast Speech]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Songbird (Social Talent)|Songbird]] are from *Heroes of Golarion*. The vigilante’s class note says how social talents are gained and the rules they share ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-social-talent|social talent]]); each note says only what that talent does and who can take it. Talents named like another class’s option carry the bracket “(Social Talent)”.
