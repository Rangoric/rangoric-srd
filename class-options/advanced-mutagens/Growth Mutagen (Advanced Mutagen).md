# Growth Mutagen

*A [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Master Chymist#^master-chymist-advanced-mutagen|master chymist’s advanced mutagen]] (Su).*

When the chymist assumes her mutagenic form, she increases one size category, as if under the effects of an *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]* spell. The character must have an effective alchemist level (alchemist level plus chymist level) of at least 16 and must know the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Form I|giant form I]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph|polymorph]]* extract to select this ability.
