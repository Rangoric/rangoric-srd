# Dual Mind

_A [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Master Chymist#^master-chymist-advanced-mutagen|master chymist’s advanced mutagen]] (Ex)._

The chymist’s alter ego gives her a +2 bonus on Will Defense in her normal and mutagenic forms. If an enchantment spell or effect hits her, it attacks her again 1 round later with the same bonus; if it misses, she is free of the effect (as if the first attack had missed) and immediately changes to her mutagenic form or back to her normal form. If she has no more uses of the mutate ability remaining for the day, she cannot use dual mind. The character must have an effective alchemist level (alchemist level plus master chymist levels) of at least 10 to select this ability.
