# Stunning Stab

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|swashbuckler deed]] (Ex), 19th level.*

At 19th level, when a swashbuckler hits a creature with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon, she can spend 2 panache points to stun the creature for 1 round. The creature must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 the swashbuckler's level + the swashbuckler's Dexterity modifier) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. Creatures that are immune to critical hits are also immune to this effect.
