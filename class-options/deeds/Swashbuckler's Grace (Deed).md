# Swashbuckler's Grace

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|swashbuckler deed]] (Ex), 7th level.*

At 7th level, while the swashbuckler has at least 1 panache point, she takes no penalty for moving at full speed when she uses [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] to attempt to move through a threatened area or an enemy's space.
