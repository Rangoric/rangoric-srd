# Startling Shot

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-deeds|gunslinger deed]] (Ex), 7th level.*

At 7th level, a gunslinger with at least 1 grit point can spend a standard action to purposely miss a creature that she could normally hit with a firearm attack. When she does, that creature becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] until the start of its next turn.
