# Lightning Reload

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-deeds|gunslinger deed]] (Ex), 11th level.*

At 11th level, as long as the gunslinger has at least 1 grit point, she can reload a single barrel of a one-handed or two-handed firearm as a swift action once per round. If she has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Reload|Rapid Reload]] feat or is using an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^alchemical-cartridges|alchemical cartridge]] (or both), she can reload a single barrel of the weapon as a free action each round instead. Furthermore, using this deed does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
