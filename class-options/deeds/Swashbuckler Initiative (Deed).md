# Swashbuckler Initiative

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|swashbuckler deed]] (Ex), 3rd level.*

At 3rd level, while the swashbuckler has at least 1 panache point, she gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks. In addition, if she has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]] feat, her hands are free and unrestrained, and she has any single light or one-handed piercing melee weapon that isn't hidden, she can draw that weapon as part of the initiative check.
