# Deeds

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler|swashbuckler]]’s deeds, from Pathfinder’s *Advanced Class Guide*. A swashbuckler gains every deed of her level or lower and spends [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] to perform them; the class note says how. Each note has `level` (swashbuckler level) and `panache-cost` (points spent; 0 means it works while she has at least 1 panache point). Characters without the class can gain a deed through feats.
