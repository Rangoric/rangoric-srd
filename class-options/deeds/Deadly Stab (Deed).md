# Deadly Stab

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|swashbuckler deed]] (Ex), 19th level._

At 19th level, when the swashbuckler confirms a critical hit with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon, in addition to the normal damage, she can spend 1 panache point to inflict a deadly stab. It attacks the target’s Fortitude, and on a hit the target dies. Its attack bonus is 1/2 the swashbuckler's level + the swashbuckler's Dexterity modifier. This is a death attack. Performing this deed does not grant the swashbuckler a panache point.
