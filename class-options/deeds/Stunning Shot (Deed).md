# Stunning Shot

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-deeds|gunslinger deed]] (Ex), 19th level._

At 19th level, when a gunslinger hits a creature, she can spend 2 grit points to stun the creature for 1 round. The shot attacks the creature’s Fortitude at 1/2 the gunslinger's level + the gunslinger's Wisdom modifier. On a hit, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. Creatures that are immune to critical hits are also immune to this effect.
