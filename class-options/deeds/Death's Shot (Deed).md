# Death's Shot

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-deeds|gunslinger deed]] (Ex), 19th level.*

At 19th level, when the gunslinger scores a critical hit, she can spend 1 grit point to deal normal damage, and the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw with a DC equal to 10 + 1/2 the gunslinger's level + the gunslinger's Dexterity modifier. On a failed saving throw, the target dies. This is a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Death Attacks|death attack]]. Performing this deed does not allow the gunslinger to regain grit from confirming a critical hit or making a killing blow.
