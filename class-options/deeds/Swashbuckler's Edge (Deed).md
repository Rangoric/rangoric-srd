# Swashbuckler's Edge

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-deeds|swashbuckler deed]] (Ex), 15th level.*

At 15th level, while the swashbuckler has at least 1 panache point, she can take 10 on any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] check, or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] check to ride, even while distracted or in immediate danger. She can use this ability in conjunction with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Derring-Do (Deed)|derring-do]] deed.
