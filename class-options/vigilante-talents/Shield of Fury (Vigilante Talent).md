# Shield of Fury

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-vigilante-talent|vigilante talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Shield Bash|Improved Shield Bash]] as a bonus feat. If he already has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Shield Bash|Improved Shield Bash]] feat, he can immediately swap it for another feat for which he qualified at the level he chose Improved Shield Bash.

In addition, if he is at least 6th level, he is treated as if he has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]] feat as long as one of the weapons used is a shield. This allows him to take feats that require [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]] as a prerequisite without meeting the Two-Weapon Fighting or Dexterity prerequisites, but these feats only apply while he is wielding a shield as one of his weapons (unless he takes Two-Weapon Fighting as normal, in which case this restriction is lifted).
