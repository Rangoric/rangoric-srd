# Mad Rush

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-vigilante-talent|vigilante talent]] (Ex).*

Whenever the vigilante charges, he can make a full attack. Whenever he uses this ability, he takes a –4 penalty to his AC until the start of his next turn (in addition to the AC penalty for charging).

Only an avenger vigilante of at least 12th level can select this talent.
