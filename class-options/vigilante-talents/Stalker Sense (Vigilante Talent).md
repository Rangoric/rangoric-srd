# Stalker Sense

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-vigilante-talent|vigilante talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante has a sense for danger. He always acts on the surprise round.

At 6th level, he gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]], and at 12th level, he gains [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-improved-uncanny-dodge|improved uncanny dodge]].

Only a stalker can select this talent.
