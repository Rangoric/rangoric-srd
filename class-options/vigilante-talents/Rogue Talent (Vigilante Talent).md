# Rogue Talent

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-vigilante-talent|vigilante talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante gains a single [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Rogue Talents|rogue talent]] (not an advanced talent) of his choice. If he selects a rogue talent marked with an asterisk (*), that talent applies to his hidden strikes instead of to sneak attacks, and the talent counts as a stalker vigilante talent with an asterisk (*) for the limitation of only one applying to a given [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-specialization|hidden strike]]. Only a stalker vigilante can select this talent.
