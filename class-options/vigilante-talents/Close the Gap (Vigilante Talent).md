# Close the Gap

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-vigilante-talent|vigilante talent]] (Ex).*

Each round, at the start of his turn, the vigilante can designate one foe within 20 feet that isn’t adjacent to him. When he moves, he doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity from that foe as long as he ends his move adjacent to that foe. If he [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charges]] that foe, he does not take the –2 penalty to his AC on any attacks made by the designated foe.
