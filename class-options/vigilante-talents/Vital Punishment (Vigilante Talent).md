# Vital Punishment

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-vigilante-talent|vigilante talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]] as a bonus feat, though he does not have to meet the prerequisites for this feat. Once per round, before he makes an attack of opportunity, he can declare it to be a vital punishment. If the attack hits, the vigilante can apply the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]] feat. The vigilante can apply the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Vital Strike|Improved Vital Strike]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Vital Strike|Greater Vital Strike]] feat as his vital punishment instead if he later gains those feats.

A vigilante must be at least 6th level to select this talent.
