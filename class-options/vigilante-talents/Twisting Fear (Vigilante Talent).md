# Twisting Fear

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-vigilante-talent|vigilante talent]] (Ex).*

Whenever the vigilante causes an opponent to gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] condition, that opponent takes an amount of nonlethal damage equal to the vigilante’s reduced [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-specialization|hidden strike]] damage, as the stress wears upon its body. A creature can’t take damage from twisting fear more than once per round. Panicked creatures that take damage from twisting fear are too winded even to scream as they flee in terror.

Only a stalker vigilante can select this talent.
