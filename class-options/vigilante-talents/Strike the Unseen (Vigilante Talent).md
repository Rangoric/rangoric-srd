# Strike the Unseen

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-vigilante-talent|vigilante talent]] (Ex).*

The vigilante gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]] as a bonus feat. He can also deal [[rangoric-srd/classes/Vigilante#^vigilante-specialization|hidden strike]] damage against targets with total concealment.

At 10th level, he gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Blind-Fight|Improved Blind-Fight]] as a bonus feat and can deal all types of precision damage against targets with concealment or total concealment.

At 16th level, he gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Blind-Fight|Greater Blind-Fight]] as a bonus feat.

The vigilante does not have to meet the prerequisites for either Improved Blind-Fight or Greater Blind-Fight to gain these feats with this talent.
