# Revolution Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Liberation Domain|Liberation]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Liberation Domain#Granted Powers|freedom's call]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Liberation Domain|Liberation]] domain.

**Powerful Persuader (Su)**: At 8th level, when you make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check, you can roll twice and take the higher result. Using this ability is a free action. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, plus one additional time per day for every 2 levels beyond 8th.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Persuasion\|Symbol of Persuasion]] | Triggered rune charms nearby creatures. |
