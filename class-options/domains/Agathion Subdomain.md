# Agathion Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Good Domain|Good]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Good Domain#Granted Powers|holy lance]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Good Domain|Good]] domain. Agathions are a race of neutral good outsiders.

**Protective Aura (Su)**: At 8th level, you can emit a 30-foot protective aura as a standard action. Allies in this aura receive a +2 deflection bonus to AC and a +2 resistance bonus on all saving throws. In addition, allies in the area gain the benefits of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Evil|protection from evil]]* (although the AC bonus and saving throw bonus do not stack with those granted by this effect). You can use this ability for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. These rounds do not have to be consecutive.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Tongues\|Tongues]] | Speak any language. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally\|Planar Ally]] (agathions only) | As lesser planar ally, but up to 12 HD. |
