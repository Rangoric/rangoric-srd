# Growth Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|Plant]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain#Granted Powers|wooden fist]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|Plant]] domain.

**Enlarge (Su)**: As a swift action you can enlarge yourself for 1 round, as if you were the target of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]* spell. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person\|Enlarge Person]] (M) | Humanoid creature doubles in size. |
| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might\|Righteous Might]] | Your size increases, and you gain combat bonuses. |
