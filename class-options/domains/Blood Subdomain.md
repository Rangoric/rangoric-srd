# Blood Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain#Granted Powers|weapon master]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War]] domain.

**Wounding Blade (Su)**: At 8th level, you can give a weapon that you touch the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Wounding|wounding]]* special weapon quality for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, and an additional time per day for every four levels beyond 8th.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch\|Vampiric Touch]] | Touch deals 1d6/two levels damage; caster gains damage as hp. |
| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Thorns\|Wall of Thorns]] | Thorns damage anyone who tries to pass. |
| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Serious Wounds, Mass\|Mass Inflict Serious Wounds]] | Deals 3d8 damage +1/level to many creatures. |
