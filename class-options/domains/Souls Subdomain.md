# Souls Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Repose Domain|Repose]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Repose Domain#Granted Powers|gentle rest]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Repose Domain|Repose]] domain.

**Touch the Spirit World (Su)**: With a touch, you can empower a weapon to affect incorporeal creatures. The weapon touched deals half damage to incorporeal creatures, or full damage if it is a magic weapon. This benefit lasts for a number of rounds equal to your cleric level. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Trap the Soul\|Trap the Soul]] (M) | Imprisons subject within gem. |
