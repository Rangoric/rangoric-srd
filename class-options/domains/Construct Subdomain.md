# Construct Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Artifice Domain|Artifice]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Artifice Domain#Granted Powers|dancing weapons]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Artifice Domain|Artifice]] domain.

**Animate Servant (Su)**: At 8th level, as a standard action, you can give life to inanimate objects. This ability functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Objects|animate objects]]* using your cleric level as the caster level. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, and one additional time per day for every four levels beyond 8th.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish\|Limited Wish]] (XP) | Alters reality—within spell limits. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph Any Object\|Polymorph Any Object]] | Changes any subject into anything else. |
