# Enchantment Domain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric domain]] from Faeries. Deities: any.* It is one of the domains the optional [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fey Class Adjustments|fey class adjustments]] offer a cleric.

## Granted Powers

**Granted Power**: You may cast the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daze|daze]]* spell a number of times per day equal to 2 + your Charisma modifier.
