# Seasons Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain#Granted Powers|storm burst]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]] domain.

**Untouched by the Seasons (Su)**: By touching a creature, you can grant it the benefits of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*, which last for 1 hour per cleric level. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Goodberry\|Goodberry]] | 2d4 berries each cure 1 hp (max 8 hp/24 hours). |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blight\|Blight]] | Withers one plant or deals 1d6/level damage to plant creature. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunburst\|Sunburst]] | Blinds all within 10 ft., deals 6d6 damage. |
