# Loss Subdomain

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain|Darkness]] domain._

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain#Granted Powers|eyes of darkness]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain|Darkness]] domain.

**Aura of Forgetfulness (Su)**: At 8th level, you can emit a 30-foot aura of forgetfulness for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. The aura attacks the Will of creatures you target in this area, and on a hit they have no memory of any time spent inside the area. In addition, spellcasters in the area lose one prepared spell or available spell slot per round spent in the area, starting with 1st-level spells and going up through higher-level spells. The aura attacks spellcasters each round, and a miss negates this loss (this attack is separate from the memory loss attack). These rounds do not need to be consecutive.

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.
