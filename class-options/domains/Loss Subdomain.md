# Loss Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain|Darkness]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain#Granted Powers|eyes of darkness]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Darkness Domain|Darkness]] domain.

**Aura of Forgetfulness (Su)**: At 8th level, you can emit a 30-foot aura of forgetfulness for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. Creatures you target in this area must make a Will save or have no memory of any time spent inside the area. In addition, spellcasters in the area lose one prepared spell or available spell slot per round spent in the area, starting with 1st-level spells and going up through higher-level spells. Spellcasters are allowed a save each round to negate this loss (this save is separate from the memory loss save). These rounds do not need to be consecutive.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation\|Enervation]] | Subject gains 1d4 negative levels. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Modify Memory\|Modify Memory]] | Changes 5 minutes of subject’s memories. |
| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Energy Drain\|Energy Drain]] | Subject gains 2d4 negative levels. |
