# Thievery Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain|Trickery]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain#Granted Powers|master's illusion]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain|Trickery]] domain.

**Thief of the Gods (Su)**: At 8th level, when you make an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] check, you can roll twice and take the higher result. Using this ability is a free action. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, plus one additional time per day for every 2 levels beyond 8th.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Object\|Locate Object]] | Senses direction toward object (specific or type). |
| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt\|Ethereal Jaunt]] | You become ethereal for 1 round/level. |
