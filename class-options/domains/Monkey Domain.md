# Monkey Domain

*A druid domain, which a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]] can take through her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-nature-bond|nature bond]]; clerics can’t take it.*

## Granted Powers

The balanced wisdom, skill, agility, and playfulness of the monkey are the model for your spiritual discipline.

**Monkey Athletics (Su)**: As a free action, you may add a competence bonus equal to half your druid level (minimum of +1) on one Acrobatics, Athletics, Engineering, or Sleight of Hand check. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Ranged Legerdemain (Su)**: At 6th level, you can use the Engineering and Sleight of Hand skills at a range of 30 feet. Working at a distance increases the normal skill check DC by 5, and you cannot take 10 on this check. Any object to be manipulated must weigh 5 pounds or less. You can only use this ability if you have at least 1 rank in the skill being used.
