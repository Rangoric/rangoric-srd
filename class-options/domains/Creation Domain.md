# Creation Domain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric domain]].*

## Granted Powers

**Granted Power**: Cast conjuration (creation) spells at +2 caster level. (Those with access to both the Artifice and Creation domains cast conjuration (creation) spells at +3 caster level.)

## Domain Spells

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water\|Create Water]] | Creates 2 gallons/level of pure water. |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Minor Image\|Minor Image]] | As silent image, plus some sound. |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Food and Water\|Create Food and Water]] | Feeds three humans (or one horse)/level. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Minor Creation\|Minor Creation]] | Creates one cloth or wood object. |

| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Permanent Image\|Permanent Image]] | Includes sight, sound, and smell. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/True Creation (Spell)\|True Creation]] | Creates permanent nonmagical object. |
| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Genesis (Spell)\|Genesis]] | Creates a small demiplane. |
