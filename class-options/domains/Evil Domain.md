# Evil Domain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric domain]].*

**Subdomains**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Daemon Subdomain|Daemon]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Demon Subdomain|Demon]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Devil Subdomain|Devil]].

## Granted Powers

You are sinister and cruel, and have wholly pledged your soul to the cause of evil.

**Touch of Evil (Sp)**: You can cause a creature to become sickened as a melee touch attack. Creatures sickened by your touch count as good for the purposes of spells with the evil descriptor. This ability lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1). You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Scythe of Evil (Su)**: At 8th level, you can give a weapon touched the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Unholy|unholy]]* special weapon quality for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, and an additional time per day for every four levels beyond 8th.

**Granted Power (3.5)**: You cast evil spells at +1 caster level.
