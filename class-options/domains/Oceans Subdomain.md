# Oceans Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain|Water]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain#Granted Powers|icicle]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain|Water]] domain.

**Surge (Su)**: As a standard action, you can cause a mighty wave to appear that pushes or pulls a single creature. Make a combat maneuver check against the target, using your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier as your CMB. If successful, you may pull or push the creature as if using the bull rush or drag combat maneuver. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Tsunami\|Tsunami]] | Huge wave damages and sweeps up all in its path. |
