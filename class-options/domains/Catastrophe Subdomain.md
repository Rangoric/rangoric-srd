# Catastrophe Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Destruction Domain|Destruction]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Destruction Domain#Granted Powers|destructive aura]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Destruction Domain|Destruction]] domain.

**Deadly Weather (Su)**: At 8th level, you can unleash a furious call to the heavens, summoning forth an ever-changing storm of destruction for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. This storm has a radius of 5 feet per cleric level. Each round, the storm has one of the following effects: driving rain (–4 on all Perception checks and ranged attack rolls), howling winds (–8 on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|Acrobatics]] checks to fly and ranged attack rolls), heavy snow (all terrain is considered difficult), or lightning bolt (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning|call lightning]]*). Other effects depending on the weather might also apply (at the GM's discretion). You choose which effect takes place each round, but no effect may be repeated on the following round. These rounds do not need to be consecutive.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind\|Gust of Wind]] | Blows away or knocks down smaller creatures. |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning\|Call Lightning]] | Calls down lightning bolts (3d6 per bolt) from sky. |
| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather\|Control Weather]] | Changes weather in local area. |
