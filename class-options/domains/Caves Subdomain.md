# Caves Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain|Earth]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain#Granted Powers|acid resistance]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain|Earth]] domain.

**Tunnel Runner (Su)**: At 8th level, you can move through tunnels and caves with ease. Activating this ability is a standard action. You can move across any stone surface as if under the effects of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]*. You can also see very well in darkness, gaining darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. If you already possess darkvision, extend the range by 60 feet. While underground, you also gain an insight bonus equal to your cleric level on Stealth skill checks and an insight bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier on initiative checks. You can use this ability for 1 minute per day per cleric level you possess. These minutes do not need to be consecutive, but they must be spent in 1-minute increments.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Pit\|Create Pit]] (F) | Creates an extradimensional pit. |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spiked Pit\|Spiked Pit]] (F) | As create pit, but filled with spikes. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hungry Pit\|Hungry Pit]] (F) | As create pit, but dealing 4d6 damage to those in it as it closes. |
