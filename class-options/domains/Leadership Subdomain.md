# Leadership Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain|Nobility]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain#Granted Powers|inspiring word]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain|Nobility]] domain.

**Inspiring Command (Su)**: As a standard action, you can issue an inspiring command to your allies. The inspiring command affects one ally plus one additional ally for every three cleric levels you possess, who must all be within 30 feet of you. Affected allies gain a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls, AC, combat maneuver defense, and skill checks for 1 round. This is a language-dependent mind-affecting effect.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless\|Bless]] | Allies gain +1 on attack rolls and saves against fear. |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer\|Prayer]] | Allies +1 bonus on most rolls, enemies –1 penalty. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Brilliant Inspiration\|Brilliant Inspiration]] | Take best of two d20 rolls. |
