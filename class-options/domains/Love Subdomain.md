# Love Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Charm Domain|Charm]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Charm Domain#Granted Powers|dazing touch]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Charm Domain|Charm]] domain.

**Adoration (Su)**: As an immediate action, you can attempt to thwart a melee or ranged attack that targets you. This ability functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sanctuary|sanctuary]]*, but only against one individual attack. You must use the ability after the attack is declared but before the roll is made. The creature attacking you receives a Will save to negate this effect. If a creature has more than one attack, this ability only affects one of the attacks. You can use the ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier. This is a mind-affecting effect.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.
