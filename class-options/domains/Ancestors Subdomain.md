# Ancestors Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Repose Domain|Repose]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Repose Domain#Granted Powers|ward against death]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Repose Domain|Repose]] domain.

**Speak With Dead (Su)**: At 8th level, as a standard action, you can ask one question of a dead creature as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]*. The dead creature you are questioning does not gain a Will saving throw if your alignments are different. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to your cleric level.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Rest Eternal\|Rest Eternal]] (M) | Dead creature cannot be revived. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Geas-Quest\|Geas-Quest]] | As lesser geas, but affects any creature. |
