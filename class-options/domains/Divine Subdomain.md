# Divine Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Magic Domain|Magic]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Magic Domain#Granted Powers|hand of the acolyte]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Magic Domain|Magic]] domain.

**Divine Vessel (Su)**: Whenever you are the target of a divine spell, you can, as a swift action, grant each ally within 15 feet of you a divine boon. This boon grants a +2 bonus on the next attack roll, skill check, or ability check made before the end of their next turn. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Resurrection\|Resurrection]] | Fully restore dead subject. |
| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle\|Miracle]] | Requests a deity’s intercession. |
