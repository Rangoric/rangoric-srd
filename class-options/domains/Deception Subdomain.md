# Deception Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain|Trickery]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain#Granted Powers|copycat]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Trickery Domain|Trickery]] domain.

**Sudden Shift (Sp)**: In the blink of an eye, you can appear somewhere else. As an immediate action, after you are missed by a melee attack, you can teleport up to 10 feet to a space that you can see. This space must be inside the reach of the creature that attacked you. You can use this power a number of times each day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Project Image\|Project Image]] (M) | Illusory double can talk and cast spells. |
