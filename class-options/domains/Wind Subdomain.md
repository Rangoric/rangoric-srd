# Wind Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain#Granted Powers|lightning arc]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]] domain.

**Wind Blast (Su)**: As a standard action, you can unleash a blast of air in a 30-foot line. Make a combat maneuver check against each creature in the line, using your caster level as your base attack bonus and your Wisdom modifier in place of your Strength modifier. Treat the results as a bull rush attempt. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Whispering Wind\|Whispering Wind]] | Sends a short message 1 mile/level. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Walk\|Wind Walk]] | You and your allies turn vaporous and travel fast. |
| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Winds of Vengeance\|Winds of Vengeance]] | Gives you the ability to fly and attack with wind. |
