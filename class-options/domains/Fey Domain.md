# Fey Domain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric domain]]. Deities: deities related to nature, and chaotic deities not tied to mortal races.* The [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Faemancer#^faemancer-domain|faemancer]] can also choose it.

## Granted Powers

**Granted Power**: You can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bad Luck|bad luck]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Good Luck|good luck]]* a number of times per day equal to your Constitution modifier. These additional spells do not count against your daily allotment of spells.

## Domain Spells

Where a level has two spells, the cleric prepares [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^domains-two-spells|either one]] in the domain spell slot.

| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Disrobe\|Disrobe]] | Strip a creature of items worn or carried. |
