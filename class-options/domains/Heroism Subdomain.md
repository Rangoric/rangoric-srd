# Heroism Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Glory Domain|Glory]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Glory Domain#Granted Powers|divine presence]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Glory Domain|Glory]] domain.

**Aura of Heroism (Su)**: At 8th level, you can emit a 30-foot aura of heroism for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. Using this ability is a swift action. Allies in the area are treated as if they were under the effects of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]]*. These rounds do not need to be consecutive.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism\|Heroism]] | Gives +2 bonus on attack rolls, saves, skill checks. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater\|Greater Heroism]] | Gives +4 bonus on attack rolls, saves, skill checks; immunity to fear; temporary hp. |
