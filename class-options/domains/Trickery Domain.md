# Trickery Domain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric domain]].*

## Granted Powers

You are a master of illusions and deceptions. [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] are class skills.

**Copycat (Sp)**: You can create an illusory double of yourself as a move action. This double functions as a single *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]* and lasts for a number of rounds equal to your cleric level, or until the illusory duplicate is dispelled or destroyed. You can have no more than one copycat at a time. This ability does not stack with the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]* spell. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Master's Illusion (Sp)**: At 8th level, you can create an illusion that hides the appearance of yourself and any number of allies within 30 feet for 1 round per cleric level. This ability otherwise functions like the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Veil|veil]]*. The save DC to disbelieve this effect is equal to 10 + 1/2 your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier. The rounds do not need to be consecutive.

**Granted Power (3.5)**: Add [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] to your list of cleric class skills.
