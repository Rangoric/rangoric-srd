# Ferocity Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain|Strength]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain#Granted Powers|strength surge]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain|Strength]] domain.

**Ferocious Strike (Su)**: Whenever you make a melee attack, you can designate that attack as a ferocious strike. If the attack hits, it deals additional damage equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1). You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage\|Rage]] | Subjects gains +2 to Str and Con, +1 on Will saves, –2 to AC. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength, Mass\|Mass Bull’s Strength]] | As bull’s strength, affects one subject/level. |
