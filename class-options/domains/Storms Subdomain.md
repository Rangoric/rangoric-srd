# Storms Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain#Granted Powers|lightning lord]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Weather Domain|Weather]] domain.

**Gale Aura (Su)**: At 6th level, as a standard action, you can create a 30-foot aura of gale-like winds that slows the progress of enemies. Creatures in the aura cannot take a 5-foot step. Enemies in the aura treat each square that brings them closer to you as difficult terrain. They can move normally in any other direction. You can use this ability for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. The rounds do not need to be consecutive.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sirocco\|Sirocco]] (M) | Hot wind does 4d6 damage, fatigues those damaged, and knocks creatures prone. |
