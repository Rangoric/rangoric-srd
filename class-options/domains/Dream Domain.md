# Dream Domain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric domain]] from Faeries. Deities: any deities related to sleep or rest, darkness or night, knowledge, stories, and the mind.* The [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Faemancer#^faemancer-domain|faemancer]] can also choose it.

## Granted Powers

**Granted Power**: You can cast Dream domain spells and spells involving sleep at +1 caster level. You can also cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Snooze|snooze]]* as a 0-level spell.

## Domain Spells

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep\|Sleep]] | Put 2d4 HD of creatures into comatose slumber. |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dreamspeak\|Dreamspeak]] | Compel target creature to speak. |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Nap\|Nap]] | A full night’s sleep condensed into 4 hours of sleep. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fitful Sleep\|Fitful Sleep]] | Curse that denies restful sleep and spell preparation until removed. |
| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleepwalking\|Sleepwalking]] | As *suggestion*, but cast upon sleeping target. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleepless Curse\|Sleepless Curse]] | Ability damage until death or curse is dispelled. |
| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Improved Nap\|Improved Nap]] | A full night’s sleep condensed into 1 hour of sleep. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Recurring Nightmare\|Recurring Nightmare]] | Creature gains negative levels until death or curse is removed. |
| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Blank\|Mind Blank]] | Subject is immune to mental/emotional magic and scrying. |
