# Fur Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain#Granted Powers|speak with animals]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal]] domain.

**Predator's Grace (Su)**: You can, as a swift action, grant yourself a +10-foot bonus to your base speed for 1 round. This bonus increases by 5 feet for every 5 cleric levels you possess. In addition, you gain low-light vision for 1 round. If you already possess low-light vision, the range of your sight becomes three times that of a human in dim light for 1 round. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.
