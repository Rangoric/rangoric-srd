# Tactics Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain#Granted Powers|battle rage]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|War]] domain.

**Seize the Initiative (Su)**: Whenever you and your allies roll for initiative, you can grant one ally within 30 feet the ability to roll twice and take either result. This decision is made before results are revealed. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid\|Aid]] | +1 on attack rolls and saves against fear, 1d8 temporary hp +1/level (max +10). |
| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Command, Greater\|Greater Command]] | As command, but affects one subject/level. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally, Greater\|Greater Planar Ally]] | As lesser planar ally, but up to 18 HD. |
