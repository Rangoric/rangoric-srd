# Restoration Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Healing Domain|Healing]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Healing Domain#Granted Powers|rebuke death]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Healing Domain|Healing]] domain.

**Restorative Touch (Su)**: You can touch a creature, letting the healing power of your deity flow through you to relieve the creature of a minor condition. Your touch can remove the dazed, fatigued, shaken, sickened, or staggered condition. You choose which condition is removed. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease\|Remove Disease]] | Cures all diseases affecting subject. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison\|Neutralize Poison]] | Immunizes subject against poison, detoxifies venom in or on subject. |
| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment\|Break Enchantment]] | Frees subjects from enchantments, alterations, curses, and petrification. |
