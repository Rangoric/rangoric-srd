# Good Domain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric domain]].*

## Granted Powers

You have pledged your life and soul to goodness and purity.

**Touch of Good (Sp)**: You can touch a creature as a standard action, granting a sacred bonus on attack rolls, skill checks, ability checks, and saving throws equal to half your cleric level (minimum 1) for 1 round. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Holy Lance (Su)**: At 8th level, you can give a weapon you touch the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Holy|holy]]* special weapon quality for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, and an additional time per day for every four levels beyond 8th.

**Granted Power (3.5)**: You cast good spells at +1 caster level.
