# Resolve Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain|Strength]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain#Granted Powers|might of the gods]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Strength Domain|Strength]] domain.

**Bestow Resolve (Su)**: At 8th level, you can bless creatures with the boldness of your deity. You can bestow a number of temporary hit points equal to your level + your Wisdom modifier to all allies within 20 feet. The temporary hit points remain for 1 minute. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, plus one additional time per day for every 4 levels you possess beyond 8th.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless\|Bless]] | Allies gain +1 on attack rolls and saves against fear. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroes’ Feast\|Heroes’ Feast]] | Food for one creature/level cures and grants combat bonuses. |
