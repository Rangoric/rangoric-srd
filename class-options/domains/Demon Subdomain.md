# Demon Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Evil Domain|Evil]] domains, taken in place of one of them.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain#Granted Powers|touch of chaos]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Chaos Domain|Chaos]] domain or the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Evil Domain#Granted Powers|touch of evil]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Evil Domain|Evil]] domain. Demons are a race of chaotic evil outsiders.

**Fury of the Abyss (Su)**: As a swift action, you can give yourself an enhancement bonus equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1) on melee attacks, melee damage rolls, and combat maneuver checks. This bonus lasts for 1 round. During this round, you take a –2 penalty to AC. You can use this ability for a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of that domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Doom\|Doom]] | One subject takes –2 on attack rolls, damage rolls, saves, and checks. |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage\|Rage]] | Subjects gains +2 to Str and Con, +1 on Will saves, –2 to AC. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding\|Planar Binding]] (demons only) | As lesser planar binding, but up to 12 HD. |
