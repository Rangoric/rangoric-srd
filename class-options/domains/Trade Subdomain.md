# Trade Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Travel Domain|Travel]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Travel Domain#Granted Powers|agile feet]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Travel Domain|Travel]] domain.

**Silver-Tongued Haggler (Su)**: Whenever you make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] check, you can, as a free action, grant yourself a bonus on the roll equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1). You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Overland Flight\|Overland Flight]] | You fly at a speed of 40 ft. and can hustle over long distances. |
| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate\|Gate]] | Connects two planes for travel or summoning. |
