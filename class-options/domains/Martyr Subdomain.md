# Martyr Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain|Nobility]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain#Granted Powers|leadership]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Nobility Domain|Nobility]] domain.

**Sacrificial Bond (Su)**: At 8th level, when an ally within 30 feet takes damage from an attack, you can, as an immediate action, transfer this damage to yourself. This power also transfers any effects that accompany the damage. The damage done to you cannot be reduced in any way. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, plus on additional time per day at 14th level and 20th level.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield Other\|Shield Other]] (F) | You take half of subject’s damage. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sacrificial Oath\|Sacrificial Oath]] | Take damage for an ally for many rounds. |
