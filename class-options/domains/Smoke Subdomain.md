# Smoke Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain#Granted Powers|fire bolt]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire]] domain.

**Cloud of Smoke (Su)**: As a standard action, you can create a 5-foot-radius cloud of smoke. This power has a range of 30 feet. Creatures inside the cloud take a –2 penalty on attack rolls and Perception checks for as long as they remain inside and for 1 round after exiting the cloud. Creatures inside the cloud gain concealment from attacks made by opponents that are not adjacent to them. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.
