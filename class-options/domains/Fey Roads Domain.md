# Fey Roads Domain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-domains|cleric domain]]. Deities: deities related to nature or travel, and chaotic deities not tied to mortal races.* Its spells find, open and make the fey realm’s [[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Doorways|doorways and roads]].

## Granted Powers

**Granted Power**: You can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Doorway|detect doorway]]* at will. These additional spells do not count against your daily allotment of spells.

## Domain Spells

Where a level has two spells, the cleric prepares [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^domains-two-spells|either one]] in the domain spell slot.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Fey Doorway\|Detect Fey Doorway]] | Reveals doorways to fey roads within 60 ft. |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Object\|Locate Object]] | Senses direction toward object (specific or type). |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Befriend Doorway Guardian\|Befriend Doorway Guardian]] | As *charm monster*, except on fey only, plus information can be obtained. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Route\|Discern Route]] | Learn destination of a fey road. |
| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fork Path\|Fork Path]] | Allows caster to create a fork in a fey path. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Find the Path\|Find the Path]] | Shows most direct way to a location. |
| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Doorway and Backroad\|Create Doorway and Backroad]] | Creates doorways and a fey road between them. |
| 7th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Stray From Path\|Stray From Path]] | Allows caster and recipients to step off their fey path for the duration. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Doorway and Sideroad\|Create Doorway and Sideroad]] | As *create doorway and backroad*, except creates a fey road from a mortal world to the fey realm. |
| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate\|Gate]] | Connects two planes for travel and summoning. |
