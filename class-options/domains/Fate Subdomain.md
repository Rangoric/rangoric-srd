# Fate Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Luck Domain|Luck]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Luck Domain#Granted Powers|good fortune]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Luck Domain|Luck]] domain.

**Tugging Strands (Su)**: At 8th level, you can force a creature within line of sight to reroll any one roll that it has just made before the result of the roll is revealed. The result of the reroll must be taken, even if it is worse than the original roll. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, and one additional time per day for every 6 levels beyond 8th.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury\|Augury]] (M, F) | Learns whether an action will be good or bad. |
| 3rd | Borrow Fortune | Retry attack or check, but do worse on next two. |
