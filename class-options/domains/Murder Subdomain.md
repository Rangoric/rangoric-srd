# Murder Subdomain

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains#^subdomains|subdomain]] of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Death Domain|Death]] domain.*

## Replacement Power

This power replaces the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Death Domain#Granted Powers|death's embrace]] power of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Death Domain|Death]] domain.

**Killing Blow (Su)**: At 8th level, weapons you use become infused with the power of death. Whenever you confirm a critical hit with a melee or ranged weapon, your attack deals an additional amount of bleed damage equal to half your cleric level. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level, plus one additional time per day for every four levels beyond 8th.

## Replacement Domain Spells

Each spell takes the place of the domain’s spells at its level (both, where it lists two); its spells at the other levels stay.

| Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3rd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Keen Edge\|Keen Edge]] | Doubles normal weapon’s threat range. |
| 5th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Suffocation\|Suffocation]] (M) | Target quickly suffocates to death. |
| 9th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Suffocation, Mass\|Mass Suffocation]] (M) | One creature/level suffocates to death. |
