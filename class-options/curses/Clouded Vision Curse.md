# Clouded Vision Curse

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-curse|oracle’s curse]].*

Your eyes are obscured, making it difficult for you to see. You cannot see anything beyond 30 feet, but you can see as if you had [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]]. At 5th level, this distance increases to 60 feet. At 10th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blindsense|blindsense]] out to a range of 30 feet. At 15th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsight]] out to a range of 15 feet.
