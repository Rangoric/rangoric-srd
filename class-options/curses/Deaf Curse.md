# Deaf Curse

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-curse|oracle’s curse]].*

You cannot hear and suffer all of the usual penalties for being [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]]. You cast all of your spells as if they were modified by the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Silent Spell|Silent Spell]] feat. This does not increase their level or casting time. At 5th level, you receive a +3 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks that do not rely upon hearing, and the initiative penalty for being deaf is reduced to –2. At 10th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] and you do not suffer any penalty on initiative checks due to being deaf. At 15th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] out to a range of 30 feet.
