# Infested Curse

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-curse|oracle’s curse]], available to [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|goblin]] oracles._

Insects, spiders, grubs, and other vermin plague you constantly. They crawl in your hair, fill your clothes, and fly or crawl into your mouth, nose, and ears. You take a –4 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks, and when you first meet someone, the person's initial attitude toward you starts one level lower than normal. Animals shy away from you: you take a –4 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks. You gain a +2 bonus on Fortitude saves versus poisons from insects, scorpions, and spiders, as well as the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Distraction|distraction]] ability of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^swarm|swarms]] of such creatures. At 5th level, you add _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vomit Swarm|vomit swarm]]_ to your list of spells known. At 7th level, your bonus on poison and distraction saves increases to +4, and you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|DR]] 10/— against all swarm damage. At 13th level, you add _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Swarm Skin|swarm skin]]_ to your list of spells known. At 17th level, you become immune to the swarm damage and the distraction ability of insect, scorpion, and spider swarms.
