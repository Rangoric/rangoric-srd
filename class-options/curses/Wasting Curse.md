# Wasting Curse

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-curse|oracle’s curse]]._

Your body is slowly rotting away. You take a –4 penalty on Charisma-based skill checks, except for [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]]. You gain a +4 competence bonus on defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]]. At 5th level, you are immune to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] condition (but not nauseated). At 10th level, you gain immunity to disease. At 15th level, you are immune to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] condition.
