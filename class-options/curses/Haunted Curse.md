# Haunted Curse

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-curse|oracle’s curse]].*

Malevolent spirits follow you wherever you go, causing minor mishaps and strange occurrences (such as unexpected breezes, small objects moving on their own, and faint noises). Retrieving any stored item from your gear requires a standard action, unless it would normally take longer. Any item you drop lands 10 feet away from you in a random direction. Add *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]* to your list of spells known. At 5th level, add *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Minor Image|minor image]]* to your list of spells known. At 10th level, add *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]* to your list of spells known. At 15th level, add *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reverse Gravity|reverse gravity]]* to your list of spells known.
