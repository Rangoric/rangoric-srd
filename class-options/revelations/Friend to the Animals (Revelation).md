# Friend to the Animals

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Ex) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Nature Mystery|nature]] mystery._

Add all _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]_ spells to your spell list. You must still select these spells using your allotment of spells known. Animals within 30 feet of you receive a bonus on all defenses equal to your Charisma modifier.
