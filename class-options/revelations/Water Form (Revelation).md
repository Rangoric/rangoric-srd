# Water Form

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Waves Mystery|waves]] mystery.*

As a standard action, you can assume the form of a Small water elemental, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body I|elemental body I]]*. At 9th level, you can assume the form of a Medium water elemental, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body II|elemental body II]]*. At 11th level, you can assume the form of a Large water elemental, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body III|elemental body III]]*. At 13th level, you can assume the form of a Huge water elemental, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body IV|elemental body IV]]*. You can use this ability once per day, but the duration is 1 hour/level. You must be at least 7th level to select this revelation.
