# Spray of Shooting Stars

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Heavens Mystery|heavens]] mystery._

As a standard action, you can unleash a ball of energy that explodes in a 5-foot-radius burst dealing 1d4 points of fire damage per level. It attacks Reflex, and a miss halves this damage. This attack has a range of 60 feet. You can fire one explosive ball per day, plus one additional ball per day at 5th level and for every 5 levels thereafter. You can fire more than one ball at a time, but creatures caught inside more than one simultaneous explosions only take damage once.
