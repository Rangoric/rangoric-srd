# Speed or Slow Time

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Sp) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Time Mystery|time]] mystery._

As a standard action, you can speed up or slow down time, as either the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ spell. You can use this ability once per day, plus one additional time per day at 12th level and 17th level. You must be at least 7th level before selecting this revelation.
