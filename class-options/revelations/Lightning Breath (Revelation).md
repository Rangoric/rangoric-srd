# Lightning Breath

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Wind Mystery|wind]] mystery._

As a standard action, you can breathe a 30-foot line of electricity. This line deals 1d4 points of electricity damage per oracle level. It attacks Reflex, and a miss halves this damage. You can use this ability once per day, plus one additional time per day at 5th level and every five levels thereafter.
