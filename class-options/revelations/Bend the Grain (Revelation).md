# Bend the Grain

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Sp) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Wood Mystery|wood]] mystery.*

Once per day as a standard action, you can shape or warp wooden objects. This functions as either *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wood Shape|wood shape]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Warp Wood|warp wood]]*. At 11th level, you can use this ability to push wood away from you, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Repel Wood|repel wood]]*. At 7th level, and again at 14th level, you can use this ability an additional time per day.
