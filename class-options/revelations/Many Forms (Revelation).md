# Many Forms

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Outer Dark Mystery|outer dark]] mystery.*

As a standard action, you can assume the form of a Small or Medium humanoid, as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]* spell. At 7th level, you can assume the form of a Small or Medium animal, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]*. At 11th level, you can assume the form of a Small or Medium magical beast, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]*. At 15th level, you can assume a variety of forms, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph, Greater|greater polymorph]]*. You can use this ability for 1 minute per day per oracle level. This duration does not need to be consecutive, but it must be spent in 1-minute increments. You must be at least 3rd level to select this revelation.
