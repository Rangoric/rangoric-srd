# Sidestep Secret

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Lore Mystery|lore]] mystery._

Your innate understanding of the universe has granted you preternatural reflexes and the uncanny ability to step out of danger at the very last second. Add your Charisma modifier (instead of your Dexterity modifier) to your Armor Class and Reflex Defense. Your armor's maximum Dexterity bonus applies to your Charisma instead of your Dexterity.
