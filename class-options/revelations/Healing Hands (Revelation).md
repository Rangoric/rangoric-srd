# Healing Hands

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Ex) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Life Mystery|life]] mystery.*

You gain a +4 bonus on Medicine checks. You may provide first aid to two people or treat two people for poison as a standard action (make a separate Medicine check for each creature). When using the Medicine skill to treat wounds from caltrops (and so on), treat deadly wounds, treat poison, treat disease, or treat long-term care, you may treat double the normal number of people you may treat at the same time. You may provide long-term care for yourself.
