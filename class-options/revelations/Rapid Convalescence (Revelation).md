# Rapid Convalescence

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ascetic Mystery|ascetic]] mystery.*

You reduce the number of consecutive successful saves needed to recover from a disease or poison by 1 (to a minimum of 1). Additionally, you can sacrifice one of your unused spell slots for the day to gain an enhancement bonus equal to that spell slot’s level on your next save to resist the effects of poison or disease, provided the save is attempted within 1 minute per level of spell expended.
