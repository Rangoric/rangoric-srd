# Rapid Convalescence

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ascetic Mystery|ascetic]] mystery._

You reduce the number of consecutive misses needed to recover from a disease or poison by 1 (to a minimum of 1). Additionally, you can sacrifice one of your unused spell slots for the day to gain an enhancement bonus equal to that spell slot’s level on your defense against the next attack of a poison or disease, provided the attack comes within 1 minute per level of spell expended.
