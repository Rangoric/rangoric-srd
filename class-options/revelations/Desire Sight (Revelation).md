# Desire Sight

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Sp) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Intrigue Mystery|intrigue]] mystery._

As a standard action, you can learn the desires of any creature you can see within 100 feet, as if you had concentrated until the third round of detect desires. As usual for detect desires, it attacks the target’s Will, and a miss negates the effect.

You can use this ability once per day at 1st level, plus one additional time per day at 5th level and for every 5 levels beyond 5th.
