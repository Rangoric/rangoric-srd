# Undead Servitude

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Bones Mystery|bones]] mystery.*

You gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Command Undead (Feat)|Command Undead]] as a bonus feat. You can channel negative energy (see [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]]) a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier, but only to use Command Undead. You can take other feats to add to this ability, such as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Channel|Improved Channel]], but not feats that alter this ability, such as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Alignment Channel|Alignment Channel]].
