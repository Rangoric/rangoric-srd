# Burning Magic

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Flame Mystery|flame]] mystery._

Whenever one of your spells hits a creature’s defense and deals fire damage to it, it catches on fire. This fire deals 1 point of fire damage per spell level at the beginning of the burning creature's turn. The fire lasts for 1d4 rounds, but as a move action the creature can make the fire attack its Reflex again (with the spell's attack bonus), and a miss extinguishes the flames. Dousing the creature with water as a standard action grants a +2 bonus to its Reflex Defense against this attack, while immersing the creature in water automatically extinguishes the fire. Spells that do not attack a defense do not cause a creature to catch on fire.
