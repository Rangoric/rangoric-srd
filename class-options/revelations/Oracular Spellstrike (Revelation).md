# Oracular Spellstrike

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Ascetic Mystery|ascetic]] mystery.*

By harnessing a synergy of unarmed combat and spellcasting, you have uncovered a means of channeling spell energy quickly through your unarmed strikes. This resembles the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus|magus]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-spellstrike|spellstrike]] ability, except it works with unarmed strikes only, no matter what other abilities you have. Additionally, it uses spells from the cleric spell list, not the magus spell list.

You must be at least 7th level before selecting this revelation.
