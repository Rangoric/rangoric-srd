# Lignification

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Wood Mystery|wood]] mystery.*

Once per day, you can turn a creature into wood. As a standard action, you may direct your gaze against a single creature within 30 feet. The targeted creature (along with all its carried gear) must make a Fortitude save or turn into a mindless, inert statue made out of wood for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your oracle level. This ability otherwise functions as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]* spell, except the target turns to wood instead of stone. This can be reverse by any effect that can reverse *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flesh to Stone|flesh to stone]]*. At 15th level, you can use this ability twice per day. You must be at least 11th level to select this revelation.
