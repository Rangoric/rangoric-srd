# Freezing Spells

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Waves Mystery|waves]] mystery._

Whenever one of your spells hits a creature’s defense and deals cold damage to it, it is slowed (as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ spell) for 1 round. Spells that do not attack a defense do not slow creatures. At 11th level, the duration increases to 1d4 rounds.
