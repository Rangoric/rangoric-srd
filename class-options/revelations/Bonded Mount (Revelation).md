# Bonded Mount

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Nature Mystery|nature]] mystery.*

You gain the service of an unusually intelligent, strong, and loyal mount. The creature must be one that you are capable of riding and is suitable as a mount. A Medium oracle can select a camel or a horse. A Small oracle can select a pony or wolf, but can also select a boar or a dog if she is at least 4th level. This mount functions as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|druid’s animal companion]], using your oracle level as your effective druid level. Bonded mounts have an Intelligence score of at least 6.
