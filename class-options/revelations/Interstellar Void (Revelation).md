# Interstellar Void

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Heavens Mystery|heavens]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Outer Dark Mystery|outer dark]] mysteries._

You call upon the frigid depths of outer space to bring a terrible chill to your enemies. As a standard action, one target within 30 feet is cloaked in the void and takes 1d6 points of cold damage per level. It attacks Fortitude, and a miss halves this damage. At 10th level, the interstellar void is so extreme that enemies it hits are fatigued. At 15th level, creatures it hits are exhausted and stunned for 1 round. You can use this ability once per day plus one additional time per day at 10th level.
