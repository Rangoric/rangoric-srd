# Raise the Dead

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Bones Mystery|bones]] mystery._

As a standard action, you can summon a single skeleton or zombie to serve you. The undead creature has a number of Hit Dice equal to your oracle level. It remains for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier. At 7th level, you can summon a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeleton/Alternate Skeleton#Bloody Skeleton|bloody skeleton]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/zombie/Alternate Zombie#Fast Zombie|fast zombie]]. At 15th level, you can summon an advanced skeleton or zombie. You can use this ability once per day plus one additional time per day at 10th level.
