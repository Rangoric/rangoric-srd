# Speak with Animals

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Ex) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Nature Mystery|nature]] mystery.*

Choose a specific kind of animal (eagle, fox, dog, and so on). You gain the ability to converse with that type of animal as if you were under the effects of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animal]]*. You gain the ability to communicate with an additional kind of animal for every 3 oracle levels you have attained.

*The shaman has a hex of the same name: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Speak with Animals (Hex)|speak with animals]].*
