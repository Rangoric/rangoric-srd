# Fluid Nature

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Ex) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Waves Mystery|waves]] mystery.*

You receive a +4 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense against bull rush, drag, grapple, reposition, and trip attempts. A creature trying to confirm a critical hit against you has a –4 penalty on its confirmation roll. At 5th level, you gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]] as a bonus feat. You do not need to meet the prerequisite to gain this feat.
