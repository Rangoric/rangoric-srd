# Iron Skin

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Battle Mystery|battle]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Metal Mystery|metal]] mysteries._

Once per day, your skin hardens and takes on the appearance of iron, granting you DR 10/adamantine. This functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]_, using your oracle level as the caster level. At 15th level, you can use this ability twice per day. You must be at least 11th level to select this revelation.
