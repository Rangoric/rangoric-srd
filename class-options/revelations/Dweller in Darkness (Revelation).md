# Dweller in Darkness

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Sp) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Heavens Mystery|heavens]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Outer Dark Mystery|outer dark]] mysteries._

Once per day, you cast your psyche into the void of space to attract the attention of a terrible otherworldly being. The dweller in darkness behaves in all ways as if you had cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantasmal Killer|phantasmal killer]]_. At 17th level, the dweller in darkness can be perceived by more than one creature, as if you had cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Weird|weird]]_. You must be at least 11th level to choose this revelation.
