# Molten Skin

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle#^oracle-revelation|revelation]] (Ex) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/mysteries/Flame Mystery|flame]] mystery.*

You gain resist fire 5. This resistance increases to 10 at 5th level and 20 at 11th level. At 17th level, you gain immunity to fire.
