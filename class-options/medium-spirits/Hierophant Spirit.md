# Hierophant Spirit

*A legend of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit|spirits]] for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)|medium]].* A hierophant spirit is a being of true and pure faith.

**Spirit Bonus**: When you channel a hierophant, your spirit bonus applies on Wisdom checks, Wisdom-based skill checks, and Will saves. ^hierophant-spirit-bonus

**Seance Boon**: Your healing spells and abilities heal an additional 2 points of damage to each target. This does not affect healing conferred by magic items, nor does it add to fast healing or similar effects. ^hierophant-seance-boon

**Favored Locations**: Altars, churches, sacred groves, shrines.

**Influence Penalty**: You must strike for nonlethal damage in combat whenever possible. You take a penalty equal to your spirit bonus on all Charisma checks and Charisma-based skill checks involving worshipers of faiths other than the spirit’s, except checks to convince others to convert to the spirit’s faith. ^hierophant-influence-penalty

**Taboos**: Choose one: you must strongly revere nature and may not wear metal armor or shields; you must follow a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-code-of-conudct|paladin]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Antipaladin#^antipaladin-code-of-conduct|antipaladin]] code as appropriate for the spirit’s deity; you may not deliberately speak any lies, including bluffing, exaggerating, stating half-truths with intent to deceive, lies by omission, and so on, and if you know the answer to a question that someone asks you, you must give the answer. ^hierophant-taboos

## Spirit Powers

**Divine Surge (Lesser, Su)**: This power functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/medium-spirits/Archmage Spirit#^archmage-arcana|archmage arcana]] spirit power, except that you add spells from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List|cleric spell list]] instead of the sorcerer/wizard list (these spells count as divine), and you need a divine focus if the spell requires one. ^divine-surge

**Energy Font (Intermediate, Su)**: You can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]] a number of times per day equal to 1 + your Charisma modifier. Choose whether you channel positive or negative energy each time you contact a hierophant spirit; this choice must match the spirit’s faith. If you choose positive energy, add *cure* spells of each level you can cast from the cleric list to your medium spell list and spells known. Otherwise, add *inflict* spells in the same way. These spells count as divine, as in the divine surge spirit power. ^energy-font

**Overflowing Grace (Greater, Su)**: When you heal a creature to full hit points or a creature already at full hit points with your positive or negative energy, that creature gains a +1 sacred bonus on attack rolls, skill checks, ability checks, and saving throws for 1 round. The bonus is sacred if you use positive energy and profane if you use negative energy. If you destroy or kill one or more creatures with positive or negative energy, you gain a +1 bonus of the same type on attack rolls, skill checks, ability checks, and saving throws for 1 round. ^overflowing-grace

**Legendary Hierophant (Supreme, Su)**: Once per day, you can request a minor miracle. This request must be in line with the options for *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]* that don’t cost powdered diamond. As usual for a *miracle*, the deity or other being the spirit worships can choose whether or not to grant the request at its whim. ^legendary-hierophant
