# Medium Spirits

The six legends a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)|medium]] channels spirits from, one note each: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/medium-spirits/Archmage Spirit|Archmage]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/medium-spirits/Champion Spirit|Champion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/medium-spirits/Guardian Spirit|Guardian]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/medium-spirits/Hierophant Spirit|Hierophant]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/medium-spirits/Marshal Spirit|Marshal]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/medium-spirits/Trickster Spirit|Trickster]]. The class note’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit|Spirit]] has the rules (the seance, influence, and when each spirit power is gained), with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit-bonus|spirit bonus]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit-surge|spirit surge]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-shared-seance|shared seance]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-taboo|taboo]].

Each legend gives a spirit bonus (the checks and statistics it applies to), a seance boon, its favored locations, an influence penalty (taken while the spirit has 3 or more points of influence over the medium), example taboos, and four spirit powers: lesser (1st level), intermediate (6th), greater (11th) and supreme (17th).
