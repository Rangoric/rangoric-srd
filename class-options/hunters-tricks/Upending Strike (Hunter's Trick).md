# Upending Strike

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hunters-tricks/Hunter's Tricks|hunter’s trick]] (Ex), which a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/ranger/Skirmisher (Archetype)|skirmisher]] ranger learns._

The ranger can use this trick as a free action just before he makes a melee attack. If the attack hits, he may make a free [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver against the target.
