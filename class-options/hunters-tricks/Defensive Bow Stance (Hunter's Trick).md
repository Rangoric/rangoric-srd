# Defensive Bow Stance

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hunters-tricks/Hunter's Tricks|hunter’s trick]] (Ex), which a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/ranger/Skirmisher (Archetype)|skirmisher]] ranger learns._

The ranger can use this trick as a swift action. Until the start of his next turn, his ranged attacks do not provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]].
