# Hunter’s Tricks

The [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/ranger/Skirmisher (Archetype)|skirmisher]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]’s hunter’s tricks, one note each. They typically grant a boon or bonus to the ranger or a nearby ally. A skirmisher learns one at 5th level and another at 7th level and every two levels thereafter, as the archetype says. A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-companion|hunter]] may teach her animal companion hunter’s tricks instead of standard tricks.

## Using Hunter’s Tricks

A ranger can use these tricks a total number of times per day equal to 1/2 his ranger level + his Wisdom modifier. Tricks are usually swift actions, but sometimes move or free actions that modify a standard action, usually an attack action. Once a trick is chosen, it can’t be retrained. A ranger cannot select an individual trick more than once. ^hunters-tricks-uses
