# Surprise Shift

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hunters-tricks/Hunter's Tricks|hunter’s trick]] (Ex), which a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/ranger/Skirmisher (Archetype)|skirmisher]] ranger learns._

The ranger can move 5 feet as a swift action. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity and does not count as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Take 5-Foot Step|5-foot step]].
