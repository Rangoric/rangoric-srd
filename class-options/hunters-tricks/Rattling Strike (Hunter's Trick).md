# Rattling Strike

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hunters-tricks/Hunter's Tricks|hunter’s trick]] (Ex), which a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/ranger/Skirmisher (Archetype)|skirmisher]] ranger learns.*

The ranger can use this trick as a free action before he makes a melee attack. If the attack hits, the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d4 rounds.
