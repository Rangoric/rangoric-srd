# Orders

The orders a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Samurai|samurai]] can pledge himself to. The class’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-order|order]] feature says how an order works: its edicts, the extra effect it adds to the cavalier’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-challenge|challenge]], its class skills, and an order ability at 2nd, 8th and 15th level. Members of an order aren’t necessarily bound together, but some organizations are made up of cavaliers who all belong to one order.

**Cavalier**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Beast|Order of the Beast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Blue Rose|Order of the Blue Rose]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Cockatrice|Order of the Cockatrice]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Dragon|Order of the Dragon]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Lion|Order of the Lion]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Paw|Order of the Paw]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Seal|Order of the Seal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Shield|Order of the Shield]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Star|Order of the Star]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Sword|Order of the Sword]], and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Tome|Order of the Tome]].

**Cavalier or samurai**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Order of the Warrior|Order of the Warrior]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/orders/Ronin|Ronin]].
