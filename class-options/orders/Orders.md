# Orders

The orders a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier|cavalier]] can pledge himself to. The class’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-order|order]] feature says how an order works: its edicts, the extra effect it adds to the cavalier’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-challenge|challenge]], its class skills, and an order ability at 2nd, 8th and 15th level. Members of an order aren’t necessarily bound together, but some organizations are made up of cavaliers who all belong to one order.
