# Expert Leaper

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

When using the Acrobatics skill to jump, a rogue with this talent is always considered to have a running start and adds her rogue level to the check result. Whenever she deliberately falls, a successful DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check allows her to ignore the first 20 feet fallen. For every 5 by which she exceeds the DC of this check, she can ignore an additional 10 feet of distance fallen.
