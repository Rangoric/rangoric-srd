# Light Walker

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex).*

The rogue can move at full speed through difficult terrain and can take 5-foot steps into difficult terrain. A rogue must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Ledge Walker (Rogue Talent)|ledge walker]] rogue talent before choosing light walker.
