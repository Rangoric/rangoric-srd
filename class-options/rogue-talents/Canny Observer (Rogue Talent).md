# Canny Observer

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

When a character with this talent makes a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check to hear the details of a conversation or to find concealed or secret objects (including doors and traps), she gains a +4 bonus.
