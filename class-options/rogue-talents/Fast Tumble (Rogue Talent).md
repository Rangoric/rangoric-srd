# Fast Tumble

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex).*

When a character with this talent uses Acrobatics to move at full speed through a threatened square without provoking an attack of opportunity, the DC of the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check does not increase by 10.
