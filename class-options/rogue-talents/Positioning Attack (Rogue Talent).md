# Positioning Attack

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

Once per day, when a character with this talent hits a creature with a melee attack, she can move up to 30 feet without provoking attacks of opportunity. The movement must end in a space adjacent to the creature hit with the melee attack.
