# Hard to Fool

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

Once per day, a rogue with this talent can roll two dice while making a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] check, and take the better result. She must choose to use this talent before making the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] check. A rogue can use this ability one additional time per day for every 5 rogue levels she possesses.
