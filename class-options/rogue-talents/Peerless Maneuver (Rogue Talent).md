# Peerless Maneuver

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex)._

Once per day, a rogue with this talent can roll two dice while making an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check, and take the better result. She must choose to use this talent before making the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check. A rogue can use this ability one additional time per day for every 5 rogue levels she possesses.
