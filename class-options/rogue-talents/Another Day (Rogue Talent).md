# Another Day

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex).*

Once per day, when the rogue would be reduced to 0 or fewer hit points by a melee attack, she can take a 5-foot step as an immediate action. If the movement takes her out of the reach of the attack, she takes no damage from the attack. The rogue is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round on her next turn.
