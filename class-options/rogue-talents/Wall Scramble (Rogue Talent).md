# Wall Scramble

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

A character with this talent rolls twice when making [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks and takes the better of the two rolls. If she already rolls twice while making a Athletics check because of another ability or effect, she gains a +2 insight bonus on both of those rolls instead. If the character is under the effect of a spell or ability that forces her to roll two dice and take the worse result, she only needs to roll 1d20 while making Athletics checks.
