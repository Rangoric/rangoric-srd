# Deft Palm

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

A character with this talent can make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] check to conceal a weapon while holding it in plain sight, even while she is being observed.
