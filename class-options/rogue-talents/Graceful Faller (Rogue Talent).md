# Graceful Faller

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex) that only [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|catfolk]] can take.*

A catfolk rogue with this talent lands on her feet even when she takes lethal damage from a fall. If the catfolk rogue also has the [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Nimble Faller|nimble faller]] racial trait, she takes damage from any fall as if it were 20 feet shorter than it actually is.
