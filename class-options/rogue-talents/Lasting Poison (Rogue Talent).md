# Lasting Poison

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex)._

A rogue with this talent can apply poison to a weapon in such a way that it is effective for a number of successful attacks equal to her Dexterity modifier (minimum two) instead of one. This poison has a reduced effect, however, and its attack bonus takes a –2 penalty. Applying poison in this way is a full-round action, or a standard action if the rogue has the swift poison rogue talent.
