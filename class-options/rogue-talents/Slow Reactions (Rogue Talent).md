# Slow Reactions

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex). It adds to sneak attack: only one such talent applies to an attack (see [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]]).*

Opponents damaged by the rogue's sneak attack can't make attacks of opportunity for 1 round.
