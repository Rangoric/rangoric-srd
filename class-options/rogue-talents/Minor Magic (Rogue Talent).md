# Minor Magic

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Sp)._

A rogue with this talent gains the ability to cast a 0-level spell from the sorcerer/wizard spell list. This spell can be cast three times a day as a spell-like ability. The caster level for this ability is equal to the rogue's level. The attack bonus for this spell is the rogue's Intelligence modifier. The rogue must have an Intelligence of at least 10 to select this talent.
