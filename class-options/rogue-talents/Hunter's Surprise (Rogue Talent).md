# Hunter's Surprise

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex).*

Once per day, a character with this talent can designate a single enemy she is adjacent to as her prey. Until the end of her next turn, she can add her sneak attack damage to all attacks made against her prey, even if she is not flanking it or it is not [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]].
