# Guileful Polyglot

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex)._

A rogue with this talent who has at least one rank in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] gains four additional languages. A rogue with this talent who does not have any ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] gains two additional languages. If the rogue later gains ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]], she gains two additional languages, to a total of four additional languages above those granted by the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] skill itself.
