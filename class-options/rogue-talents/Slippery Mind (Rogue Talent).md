# Slippery Mind

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex)._

This ability represents the rogue's ability to wriggle free from magical effects that would otherwise control or compel her. If an enchantment spell or effect hits a rogue with slippery mind, she can make it attack her again 1 round later with the same attack bonus. She gets only this one extra chance for it to miss.
