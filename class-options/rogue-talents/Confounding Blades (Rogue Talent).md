# Confounding Blades

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex). It adds to sneak attack: only one such talent applies to an attack (see [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]]).*

When a character with this talent hits a creature with a melee weapon that deals sneak attack damage, her target cannot make attacks of opportunity for 1d4+1 rounds. The rogue must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Slow Reactions (Rogue Talent)|slow reactions]] rogue talent before she can choose this talent.
