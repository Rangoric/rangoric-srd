# Dispelling Attack

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Su). It adds to sneak attack: only one such talent applies to an attack (see [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]]).*

Opponents that are dealt sneak attack damage by a rogue with this ability are affected by a targeted *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*, targeting the lowest-level spell effect active on the target. The caster level for this ability is equal to the rogue's level. A rogue must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Major Magic (Rogue Talent)|major magic]] rogue talent before choosing dispelling attack.
