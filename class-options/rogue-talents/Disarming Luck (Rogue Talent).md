# Disarming Luck

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex) that only [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|catfolk]] can take._

Once per day, when a catfolk rogue attempts to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|disable a device]] and fails by 5 or more, she can reroll the check as a free action. She must take the result of the reroll, even if it's worse than the original roll.
