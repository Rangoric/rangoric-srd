# Defensive Roll

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex)._

With this advanced talent, the rogue can roll with a potentially lethal blow to take less damage from it than she otherwise would. Once per day, when she would be reduced to 0 or fewer hit points by damage in combat (from a weapon or other blow, not a spell or special ability), the rogue can attempt to roll with the damage. To use this ability, the rogue must attempt a Reflex check (1d20 + her Reflex save bonus; DC = damage dealt). If the check succeeds, she takes only half damage from the blow; if it fails, she takes full damage. She must be aware of the attack and able to react to it in order to execute her defensive roll—if she is denied her Dexterity bonus to AC, she can't use this ability. Since this blow is not an attack against her Reflex Defense that deals half damage on a miss, the rogue's evasion ability does not apply to the defensive roll.
