# Stand Up

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

A rogue with this ability can stand up from a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] position as a free action. This still provokes attacks of opportunity for standing up while threatened by a foe.
