# Unwitting Ally

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex).*

A character with this talent can spend a swift action to attempt to make an opponent act like an ally for purposes of providing a flank until the beginning of the character's next turn. The opponent must be able to hear and see the character, and the character must succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check opposed by the opponent’s Sense Motive. If the check succeeds, the opponent acts as an ally for the purpose of providing a flank. Whether or not the check succeeds, the character cannot use this trick again on the same opponent for the next 24 hours. If the character fails the check by 5 or more, she cannot use the unwitting ally ability on any opponent within line of sight of the failed attempt for 24 hours.
