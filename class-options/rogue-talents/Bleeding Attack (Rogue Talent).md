# Bleeding Attack

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] and a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-tricks|ninja trick]] (Ex). It adds to sneak attack: only one such talent or trick applies to an attack (see [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talents]]).*

A rogue or ninja with this ability can cause living opponents to bleed by hitting them with a sneak attack. This attack causes the target to take 1 additional point of damage each round for each die of her sneak attack (e.g., 4d6 equals 4 points of bleed). Bleeding creatures take that amount of damage every round at the start of each of their turns. The bleeding can be stopped by a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check or the application of any effect that heals hit point damage. Bleeding damage from this ability does not stack with itself. Bleeding damage bypasses any damage reduction the creature might possess.
