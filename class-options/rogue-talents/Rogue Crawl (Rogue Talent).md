# Rogue Crawl

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

While [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]], a rogue with this ability can move at half speed. This movement provokes attacks of opportunity as normal. A rogue with this talent can take a 5-foot step while crawling.
