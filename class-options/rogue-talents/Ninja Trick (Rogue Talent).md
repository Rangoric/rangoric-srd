# Ninja Trick

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

A rogue with this talent can choose a trick from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ninja-tricks/Ninja Tricks|ninja trick]] list. The rogue can choose but cannot use talents that require ki points, unless she has a ki pool. A rogue can pick this talent more than once. The rogue cannot choose a ninja trick with the same name as a rogue talent.
