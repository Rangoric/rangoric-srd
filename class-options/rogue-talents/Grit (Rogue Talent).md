# Grit

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]] (Ex).*

A character with this talent gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Gunslinger|Amateur Gunslinger]] feat and one grit feat of her choice. She must fulfill the prerequisites of the grit feat in order to choose it. A character must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Firearm Training (Rogue Talent)|firearm training]] talent before choosing this one.
