# Deadly Range

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] (Ex).*

A slayer with this talent increases the range at which he can deal [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage by 10 feet. A slayer can select this talent more than once; its effects stack. A slayer must be at least 4th level before selecting this talent.
