# Foil Scrutiny

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] (Ex).*

A slayer with this talent is able to obscure his thoughts and intentions. He gains a +2 bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks, as well as on Will saving throws to resist mind-reading spells and effects (such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]]*).
