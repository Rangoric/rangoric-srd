# Trapfinding

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|slayer talent]] (Ex).*

The slayer gains [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] as class skills (Pathfinder's Disable Device). The slayer also gains the rogue's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]] (which counts as trap sense), using his slayer level as his effective rogue level.
