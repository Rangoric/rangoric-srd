# Slayer Camouflage

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced talent]] (Ex).*

A slayer can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] skill to hide in any of his favored terrains, even if the terrain doesn't grant cover or concealment. A slayer must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Terrain Mastery (Rogue Talent)|terrain mastery]] talent to select this talent.
