# Staff-Like Wand

_An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-arcane-discoveries|arcane discovery]]._

Your research has unlocked a new power in conjunction with using a wand. Similar to using a magic staff, you use your own Intelligence score and relevant feats to set the attack bonus of spells you cast from a wand, and you can use your caster level when activating the power of a wand if it's higher than the caster level of the wand. You must be at least an 11th-level wizard and must have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]] feat to select this discovery.
