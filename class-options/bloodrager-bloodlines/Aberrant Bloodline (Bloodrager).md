# Aberrant Bloodline (Bloodrager)

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodline|bloodrager bloodline]]. The [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Aberrant Bloodline|sorcerer’s aberrant bloodline]] shares the name but not the powers.*

There is a taint in your blood that is both alien and bizarre. When you bloodrage, this manifests in peculiar and terrifying ways.

**Bonus Feats**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Disarm|Improved Disarm]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Iron Will|Iron Will]].

**Bonus Spells**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|Enlarge person]]* (7th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]* (10th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]* (13th), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Black Tentacles|black tentacles]]* (16th).

## Bloodline Powers

While bloodraging, you gain the abilities and immunities of some aberrations, but show signs of your tainted heritage.

**Staggering Strike (Su)**: At 1st level, when you confirm a critical hit the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 your bloodrager level + your Constitution modifier. These effects stack with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Staggering Critical|Staggering Critical]] feat; the target must save against each effect individually.

**Abnormal Reach (Su)**: At 4th level, your limbs elongate; your reach increases by 5 feet.

**Aberrant Fortitude (Su)**: At 8th level, you become immune to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] conditions.

**Unusual Anatomy (Su)**: At 12th level, your internal anatomy shifts and changes, giving you a 50% chance to negate any critical hit or sneak attack that hits you. The damage is instead rolled normally.

**Aberrant Resistance (Su)**: At 16th level, you are immune to disease, exhaustion, fatigue, and poison, and to the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] condition.

**Aberrant Form (Su)**: At 20th level, your body becomes truly unnatural. You are immune to critical hits and sneak attacks. In addition, you gain blindsight with a range of 60 feet and your bloodrager [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-damage-reduction|damage reduction]] increases by 1. You have these benefits constantly, even while not bloodraging.
