# Shadow Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Silent Image\|Silent Image]] | Creates minor illusion of your design. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness\|Darkness]] | 20-ft. radius of supernatural shadow. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness\|Deeper Darkness]] | Object sheds supernatural shadow in 60-ft. radius. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Conjuration\|Shadow Conjuration]] | Mimics conjuration below 4th level, but only 20% real. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Evocation\|Shadow Evocation]] | Mimics evocation below 5th level, but only 20% real. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk\|Shadow Walk]] | Step into shadow to travel rapidly. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Conjuration, Greater\|Greater Shadow Conjuration]] | As shadow conjuration, but up to 6th level and 60% real. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Evocation, Greater\|Greater Shadow Evocation]] | As shadow evocation, but up to 7th level and 60% real. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shades\|Shades]] | As shadow conjuration, but up to 8th level and 80% real. |
