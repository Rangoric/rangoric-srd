# Transformation Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Jump (Spell)\|Jump]] | Subject gets bonus on Athletics checks. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance\|Bear’s Endurance]] | Subject gains +4 to Con for 1 min./level. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I\|Beast Shape I]] | You take the form and some of the powers of a Small or Medium animal. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape II\|Beast Shape II]] | You take the form and some of the powers of a Tiny or Large animal. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III\|Beast Shape III]] | You take the form of a Diminutive or Huge animal, or Small or Medium magical beast. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Form of the Dragon I\|Form of the Dragon I]] | You become a Medium dragon. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Form of the Dragon II\|Form of the Dragon II]] | You become a Medium or Large dragon. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Form of the Dragon III\|Form of the Dragon III]] | You become a Medium, Large or Huge dragon. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shapechange\|Shapechange]] | Transforms you into any creature, and change forms once per round. |
