# Plague Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead\|Detect Undead]] | Reveals undead within 60 ft. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Command Undead\|Command Undead]] | Undead creature obeys your commands. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion\|Contagion]] | Infects subject with chosen disease. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead\|Animate Dead]] | Creates undead skeletons and zombies. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Vermin\|Giant Vermin]] | Turns centipedes, scorpions, or spiders into giant vermin. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Undead\|Create Undead]] | Create ghouls, ghasts, mummies, or mohrgs. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Undead\|Control Undead]] | Undead don’t attack you while under your command. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Greater Undead\|Create Greater Undead]] | Create shadows, wraiths, spectres, or devourers. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Energy Drain\|Energy Drain]] | Subject gains 2d4 negative levels. |
