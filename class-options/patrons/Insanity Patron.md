# Insanity Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Memory Lapse\|Memory Lapse]] | Subject forgets events back to last turn. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hideous Laughter\|Hideous Laughter]] | Subject loses actions for 1 round/level. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Distracting Cacophony\|Distracting Cacophony]] | Noise makes it difficult to cast. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion\|Confusion]] | Subjects behave oddly for 1 round/level. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Fog\|Mind Fog]] | Subjects in fog get –10 to Wis and Will checks. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Envious Urge\|Envious Urge]] | Targets steal from or disarm others. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Insanity (Spell)\|Insanity]] | Subject suffers continuous confusion. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Insanity\|Symbol of Insanity]] | Triggered rune renders nearby creatures insane. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Overwhelming Presence\|Overwhelming Presence]] | Creatures bow before you as if you were divine. |
