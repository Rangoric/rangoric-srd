# Death Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Deathwatch\|Deathwatch]] | Reveals how near death subjects within 30 ft. are. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blessing of Courage and Life\|Blessing of Courage and Life]] | Grants a +2 bonus on saves vs. fear and death. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead\|Speak with Dead]] | Corpse answers one question/two levels. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Rest Eternal\|Rest Eternal]] | Dead creature cannot be revived. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Suffocation\|Suffocation]] | Target quickly suffocates to death. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Circle of Death\|Circle of Death]] | Kills 1d4/level HD of creatures. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Finger of Death\|Finger of Death]] | Kills one subject. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death\|Symbol of Death]] | Triggered rune slays nearby creatures. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Power Word Kill\|Power Word Kill]] | Kills one creature with 100 hp or less. |
