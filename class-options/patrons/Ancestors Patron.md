# Ancestors Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless\|Bless]] | Allies gain +1 on attack rolls and saves against fear. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Aid\|Aid]] | +1 on attack rolls and saves against fear, 1d8 temporary hp +1/level (max +10). |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer\|Prayer]] | Allies +1 bonus on most rolls, enemies –1 penalty. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Blessing of Fervor\|Blessing of Fervor]] | Gives allies a choice of benefits. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune\|Commune]] | Deity answers one yes-or-no question/level. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater\|Greater Heroism]] | Gives +4 bonus on attack rolls, saves, skill checks; immunity to fear; temporary hp. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Refuge\|Refuge]] | Alters item to transport its possessor to you. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Euphoric Tranquility\|Euphoric Tranquility]] | Makes a creature friendly. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Weird\|Weird]] | As phantasmal killer, but affects all within 30 ft. |
