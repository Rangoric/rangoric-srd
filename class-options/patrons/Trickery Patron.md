# Trickery Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Rope\|Animate Rope]] | Makes a rope move at your command. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image\|Mirror Image]] | Creates decoy duplicates of you (1d4 +1 per three levels, max 8). |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Major Image\|Major Image]] | As silent image, plus sound, smell and thermal effects. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hallucinatory Terrain\|Hallucinatory Terrain]] | Makes one type of terrain appear like another (field into forest, or the like). |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirage Arcana\|Mirage Arcana]] | As hallucinatory terrain, plus structures. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mislead\|Mislead]] |  |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Reverse Gravity\|Reverse Gravity]] | Objects and creatures fall upward. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Screen\|Screen]] | Illusion hides area from vision, scrying. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Time Stop\|Time Stop]] | You act freely for 1d4+1 rounds. |
