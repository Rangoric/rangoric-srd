# Water Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless Water\|Bless Water]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Curse Water\|Curse Water]] | Makes holy water. Or: Makes unholy water. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Slipstream\|Slipstream]] | Wave boosts creature's speed. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing\|Water Breathing]] | Subjects can breathe underwater. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water\|Control Water]] | Raises or lowers bodies of water. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Geyser\|Geyser]] | Creates a geyser of boiling water. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body III\|Elemental Body III]] (water only) | You become a Small, Medium or Large elemental. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body IV\|Elemental Body IV]] (water only) | You become a Small to Huge elemental. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Seamantle\|Seamantle]] | Sheathes you in protective water. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Tsunami\|Tsunami]] | Huge wave damages and sweeps up all in its path. |
