# Time Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste\|Haste]] | One creature/level moves faster, +1 on attack rolls, AC, and Reflex saves. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Threefold Aspect\|Threefold Aspect]] | Appear older or younger. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport\|Teleport]] | Instantly transports you as far as 100 miles/level. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate\|Disintegrate]] | Makes one creature or object vanish. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Expend\|Expend]] | Wastes creatures' limited use magical ability. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Temporal Stasis\|Temporal Stasis]] | Puts subject into suspended animation. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Time Stop\|Time Stop]] | You act freely for 1d4+1 rounds. |
