# Endurance Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements\|Endure Elements]] | Exist comfortably in hot or cold environments. |

| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy\|Protection from Energy]] | Absorb 12 points/level of damage from one kind of energy. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Immunity\|Spell Immunity]] | Subject is immune to one spell per four levels. |

| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Greater\|Greater Restoration]] | As restoration, plus restores all levels and ability scores. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Iron Body\|Iron Body]] | Your body becomes living iron. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle\|Miracle]] | Requests a deity’s intercession. |
