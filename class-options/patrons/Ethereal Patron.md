# Ethereal Patron

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it._

| Witch Level | Spell                                                              | Brief Description                                                                                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2nd         | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead\|Detect Undead]]               | Reveals undead within 60 ft.                                                                                                              |
| 4th         | [[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility\|See Invisibility]]         | Reveals [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)\                                                                         |
| 6th         | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ectoplasmic Snare\|Ectoplasmic Snare]]       | Tendril of ectoplasm grapples a creature and tethers you to it.                                                                           |
| 8th         | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Condensed Ether\|Condensed Ether]]           | Creates a planar conjunction that slows movement, penalizes AC and Reflex Defense, and imposes a miss chance on ranged attacks.           |
| 10th        | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Envelope\|Ethereal Envelope]]       | Shroud your unconscious self in a cocoon on the Ethereal Plane.                                                                           |
| 12th        | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt\|Ethereal Jaunt]]             | You become ethereal for 1 round/level.                                                                                                    |
| 14th        | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ectoplasmic Eruption\|Ectoplasmic Eruption]] | Deal 6d6 points of damage and entangle creatures in a 30-ft. radius, and push ethereal and incorporeal creatures onto the Material Plane. |
| 16th        | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Envelopment\|Ethereal Envelopment]] | As ethereal envelope, but able to affect an unwilling creature.                                                                           |
| 18th        | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Etherealness\|Etherealness]]                 | Travel to Ethereal Plane with companions.                                                                                                 |
