# Agility Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Jump (Spell)\|Jump]] | Subject gets bonus on Athletics checks. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace\|Cat’s Grace]] | Subject gains +4 to Dex for 1 min./level. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste\|Haste]] | One creature/level moves faster, +1 on attack rolls, AC, and Reflex saves. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement\|Freedom of Movement]] | Subject moves normally despite impediments. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph\|Polymorph]] | Gives one willing subject a new form. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace, Mass\|Mass Cat’s Grace]] | As cat’s grace, affects one subject/level. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ethereal Jaunt\|Ethereal Jaunt]] | You become ethereal for 1 round/level. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Animal Shapes\|Animal Shapes]] | One ally/level polymorphs into chosen animal. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shapechange\|Shapechange]] | Transforms you into any creature, and change forms once per round. |
