# Vengeance Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands\|Burning Hands]] | 1d4/level fire damage (max 5d4). |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Gaze\|Burning Gaze]] | Inflict 1d6 fire damage to creature by looking at it. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Pain Strike\|Pain Strike]] | Inflicts 1d6 nonlethal damage 1 round/level. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout\|Shout]] | Deafens all within cone and deals 5d6 sonic damage. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Pain\|Symbol of Pain]] | Triggered rune wracks nearby creatures with pain. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Pain Strike, Mass\|Mass Pain Strike]] | As pain strike, but affects multiple creatures. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantasmal Revenge\|Phantasmal Revenge]] | Ghost from corpse hunts killer. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Incendiary Cloud\|Incendiary Cloud]] | Cloud deals 4d6 fire damage/round. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Winds of Vengeance\|Winds of Vengeance]] | Gives you the ability to fly and attack with wind. |
