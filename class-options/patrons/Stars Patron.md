# Stars Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire\|Faerie Fire]] | Outlines subjects with light, canceling blur, concealment, and the like. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dust of Twilight\|Dust of Twilight]] | Black particles extinguish light sources within area. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Guiding Star\|Guiding Star]] | Know approximate distance from where you cast this spell. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wandering Star Motes\|Wandering Star Motes]] | Outlines subject and produces light as a sunrod. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dream\|Dream]] | Sends message to anyone sleeping. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cloak of Dreams\|Cloak of Dreams]] | Causes living creatures within 5 ft. to fall asleep. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Circle of Clarity\|Circle of Clarity]] | Emanation hampers illusions and stealth. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Euphoric Tranquility\|Euphoric Tranquility]] | Makes a creature friendly. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection\|Astral Projection]] | Projects you and companions onto Astral Plane. |
