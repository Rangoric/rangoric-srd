# Wisdom Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield of Faith\|Shield of Faith]] | Aura grants +2 or higher deflection bonus. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Owl’s Wisdom\|Owl’s Wisdom]] | Subject gains +4 to Wis for 1 min./level. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Vestment\|Magic Vestment]] | Armor or shield gains +1 enhancement per four levels. |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Globe of Invulnerability, Lesser\|Lesser Globe of Invulnerability]] | Stops 1st- through 3rd-level spell effects. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dream\|Dream]] | Sends message to anyone sleeping. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Globe of Invulnerability\|Globe of Invulnerability]] | As lesser globe of invulnerability, plus 4th-level spell effects. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning\|Spell Turning]] | Reflect 1d4+6 spell levels back at caster. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Spells\|Protection from Spells]] | Confers +8 resistance bonus. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Disjunction\|Mage’s Disjunction]] | Dispels magic, disenchants magic items. |
