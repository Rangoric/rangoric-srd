# Strength Patron

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|witch’s patron]]. At each level shown, the patron adds its spell to the witch’s spells known, and her familiar stores it.*

| Witch Level | Spell | Brief Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor\|Divine Favor]] | You gain +1 per three levels on attack and damage rolls. |
| 4th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength\|Bull’s Strength]] | Subject gains +4 to Str for 1 min./level. |
| 6th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon, Greater\|Greater Magic Weapon]] | +1 bonus/four levels (max +5). |
| 8th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Power\|Divine Power]] | You gain attack bonus, +6 to Str, and 1 hp/level. |
| 10th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might\|Righteous Might]] | Your size increases, and you gain combat bonuses. |
| 12th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength, Mass\|Mass Bull’s Strength]] | As bull’s strength, affects one subject/level. |
| 14th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Form I\|Giant Form I]] | Turns you into a Large giant. |
| 16th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Giant Form II\|Giant Form II]] | Turns you into a Huge giant. |
| 18th | [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shapechange\|Shapechange]] | Transforms you into any creature, and change forms once per round. |
