# Future Gaze

*A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Divination Implements|divination implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp).*

As a standard action, you can gain an insight into the future by expending 1 point of mental focus. This functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]*, using your occultist level as the caster level.
