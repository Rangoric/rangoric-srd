# Future Gaze

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Divination Implements|divination implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp)._

As a standard action, you can gain an insight into the future by expending 1 point of mental focus. This functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Augury|augury]]_, using your occultist level as the caster level.
