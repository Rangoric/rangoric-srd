# Telekinetic Mastery

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Transmutation Implements|transmutation implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp)._

As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to grant yourself telekinetic power. This functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_, except you don’t need to concentrate on the effect to maintain it, and using any one of its effects is a standard action. This effect lasts for 1 round per occultist level you possess. You must be at least 9th level to select this focus power.
