# Danger Sight

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Divination Implements|divination implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp)._

As an immediate action, you can protect yourself from harm by expending 1 point of mental focus. You can use this ability whenever you are the target of an attack against your AC or a special ability, spell, or trap attacks one of your defenses. Doing so grants you an insight bonus to your AC or that defense equal to 1/2 your occultist level. This bonus applies only to the next attack against you, and if not applied by the end of the round, the protection fades and you gain no benefit. You must be at least 3rd level to select this focus power.
