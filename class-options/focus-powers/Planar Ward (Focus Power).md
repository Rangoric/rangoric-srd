# Planar Ward

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Abjuration Implements|abjuration implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp)._

As a standard action, you can expend 2 points of mental focus to surround yourself with a planar ward. Creatures not native to the plane that you are currently on take a –4 penalty on attacks against you, and you receive a +4 circumstance bonus on defenses against the spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural abilities of such creatures. This ward lasts for 1 minute.
