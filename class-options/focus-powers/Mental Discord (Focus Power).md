# Mental Discord

*A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Enchantment Implements|enchantment implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp).*

As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to disrupt the mind of one living creature within 30 feet. The creature can attempt a Will saving throw to negate the effect. If it fails the save, the creature can’t concentrate on any spell effects and must succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] to cast any spell (DC = 15 + double the spell level). The affected creature takes a –4 penalty on this check if the spell includes a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Components|thought component]]. The creature takes a –2 penalty on all Will saving throws while under this effect. This effect lasts for 1 round per occultist level you possess.
