# Pain Wave

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Necromancy Implements|necromancy implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp)._

As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to unleash a wave of pain. This wave hits all creatures other than you in a 20-foot-radius burst centered on a point that you designate within 100 feet. All living creatures in this area are wracked with pain, gaining the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] condition for 1 round per occultist level you possess. It attacks the Will of affected creatures, and a miss reduces the duration to just 1 round. This is a mind-affecting pain effect. You must be at least 7th level to select this focus power.
