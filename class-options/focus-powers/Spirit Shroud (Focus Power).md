# Spirit Shroud

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Necromancy Implements|necromancy implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Su)._

As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to surround yourself with a shroud of spirit energy. You gain a number of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Temporary Hit Points|temporary hit points]] equal to 1d6 + your occultist level. This shroud lasts for 1 minute per occultist level or until the temporary hit points are expended, whichever comes first. These temporary hit points stack with those from other sources, but not with those gained through multiple uses of this ability. At 4th level, as long as the shroud remains, you also gain a resistance bonus on all defenses against death effects, fear effects, and any spells or effects that bestow [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative levels]] or deal negative energy damage (if the spells or effects attack a defense). This bonus is equal to 1 + 1 for every 4 occultist levels you possess beyond 4th. You must be at least 3rd level to select this focus power.
