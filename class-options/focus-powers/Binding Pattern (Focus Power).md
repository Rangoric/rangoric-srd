# Binding Pattern

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Enchantment Implements|enchantment implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp)._

As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to create a binding pattern of psychic energy that causes a living creature to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]]. The target must be within 30 feet of you, and the power attacks its Will, a miss negating the effect. On a hit, it is paralyzed for 1 round for every 2 occultist levels you possess. At the end of each of its turns, it can make the power attack its Will again, and a miss ends the paralyzed effect and instead be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for the remaining duration. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect. You must be at least 7th level to select this focus power.
