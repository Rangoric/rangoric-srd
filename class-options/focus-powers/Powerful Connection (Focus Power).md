# Powerful Connection

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Divination Implements|divination implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Su)._

When casting a divination spell (such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]_) that imposes a penalty on defenses based on the physical connection you have to the target, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to double the penalty from a possession, garment, or body part. In addition, when casting any other divination spell that attacks a defense, you can increase its attack bonus by 2 by adding an object, garment, or body part that belonged to the target as a focus component for the spell and expending 1 point of mental focus.
