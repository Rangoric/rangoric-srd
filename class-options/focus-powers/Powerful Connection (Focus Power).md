# Powerful Connection

*A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Divination Implements|divination implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Su).*

When casting a divination spell (such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]*) that imposes a penalty on saving throws based on the physical connection you have to the target, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to double the penalty from a possession, garment, or body part. In addition, when casting any other divination spell that allows a saving throw, you can increase the DC of that save by 2 by adding an object, garment, or body part that belonged to the target as a focus component for the spell and expending 1 point of mental focus.
