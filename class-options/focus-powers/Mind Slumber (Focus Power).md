# Mind Slumber

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Enchantment Implements|enchantment implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp)._

By expending 1 point of mental focus as a standard action, you can lull a living creature into a deep mental slumber, forcing it to view the world as a dream. The target must be within 30 feet of you, and the power attacks its Will, a miss negating the effect. On a hit, the creature falls asleep, although it still perceives the world around it as a dream. This doesn’t cause the creature to fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]], but it does drop whatever it is holding. The creature can’t take any actions while in this state, but it isn’t [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]]—it perceives danger as part of its dream. The creature remains asleep for a number of rounds equal to your occultist level or until it takes any amount of damage, which immediately wakes it. At the end of each of its turns, the effect attacks its Will again, and a miss ends it. The creature can be manipulated or examined without waking it, but it can’t be moved from its square without waking it. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect. Whether it hits or misses, the target is then immune to this effect for 24 hours. You must be at least 3rd level to select this focus power.
