# Size Alteration

*A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Transmutation Implements|transmutation implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp).*

As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus and touch a creature to alter its size. You can increase or decrease the creature’s size by one step, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]* but not limited by the creature’s type. If the creature is hostile toward you, using this ability requires a successful melee touch attack, and the creature can attempt a Fortitude save to negate the effect. This effect lasts for 1 round per occultist level you possess. You can’t use this ability on a creature that is already subject to an effect that alters its size.
