# Obey

*A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Enchantment Implements|enchantment implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp).*

As a standard action, you can issue a command to one living creature by expending 1 point of mental focus. This functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]*. The target must be within 30 feet and capable of understanding your order. The target can attempt a Will save to negate this effect. If the creature is the same creature type as you, it takes a –2 penalty on this saving throw.
