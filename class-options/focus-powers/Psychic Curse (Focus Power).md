# Psychic Curse

_A focus power of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Necromancy Implements|necromancy implements]], for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]] (Sp)._

As a standard action, you can expend 1 point of mental focus to curse a living creature within 30 feet with your psychic magic. You can choose one of the following effects: memory lapse, mental block, or pain confusion. If you choose memory lapse, the target loses its memory of the past 1 hour per occultist level you possess. If you choose mental block, you can proscribe one specific action, such as attacking with a type of weapon, casting a specific spell, or something else suitably specific (subject to the GM’s discretion). Each time the target tries that action, the curse attacks its Will, and on a hit it can’t take that action (it can take other actions instead). If it attempts the prohibited action again on subsequent rounds, it gains a cumulative +2 bonus to its Will Defense against this attack until the curse misses. If you select pain confusion, the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d4 rounds whenever it is damaged by a critical hit. It attacks the target’s Will, and a miss negates this effect. This effect lasts for 1 day per occultist level you possess. A creature can’t be under the effects of more than one psychic curse at a time. This is a mind-affecting curse effect. You must be at least 5th level to select this focus power.
