# Healing Burst

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Void Element|void]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 4
- **Burn**: 1
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Healer|kinetic healer]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Void Healer|void healer]]
- **Saving Throw**: none
- **Spell Resistance**: yes

You use [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Healer|kinetic healer]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Void Healer|void healer]] (whichever you have, or either if you have both) to heal all creatures in a 30-foot radius around you. Healing burst heals half as much damage as normal, unless your healing amount is based on [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Positive Blast|positive blast]], in which case it heals the full amount. Unlike kinetic healer, you must accept the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] yourself. This counts as kinetic healer for the purpose of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/kineticist/Kinetic Chirurgeon (Archetype)|kinetic chirurgeon]] archetype (the 17th-level [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/kineticist/Kinetic Chirurgeon (Archetype)#^kinetic-chirurgeon-metahealer|metahealer]] ability heals the kinetic chirurgeon twice—once for half and once for full).
