# Engulfing Winds

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 0
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Enveloping Winds|enveloping winds]]

You bend your winds into a formidable barrier. Until the start of your next turn, you lose the benefits of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Enveloping Winds|enveloping winds]], but can create a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Wall|wind wall]]_ as the spell. You can extend the wall’s duration to 1 round per kineticist level by accepting 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]].
