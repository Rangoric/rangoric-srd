# Wind Manipulator

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 6
- **Burn**: 0
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Engulfing Winds|engulfing winds]]

You can call forth mighty winds in an enormous area. As a standard action, you can alter the wind around you as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Winds|control winds]]_, but the change remains only for as long as you continue to take a standard action to concentrate on this effect. If you accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]], you can extend the duration to 10 minutes per kineticist level you possess without your needing to concentrate.
