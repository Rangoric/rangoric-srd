# Forest Siege

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 9
- **Burn**: 0

You can transform available plant life into a besieging army. This functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Siege of Trees, Greater|greater siege of trees]]_, except you need to concentrate or the effect ends, and you can change targets as a free action.

If you accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]], you can prolong the effect without the need for concentration until the next time you recover burn, but you need to spend a standard action to change targets.
