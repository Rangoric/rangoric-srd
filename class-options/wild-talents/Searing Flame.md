# Searing Flame

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 2
- **Burn**: —
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Burning Infusion|burning infusion]]

Over time, your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Burning Infusion|burning infusion]] sears away your foe’s fire resistance. When you roll [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] damage from burning infusion against a foe, decrease the foe’s fire resistance by an amount equal to the unmodified burn damage; don’t apply fire resistance to this roll for the purpose of determining the reduction. This decrease lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your kineticist level. These decreases stack, to a minimum resistance of 0.
