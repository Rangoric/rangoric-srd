# Skilled Kineticist, Greater

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements#Universal|universal]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 2
- **Burn**: —
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Skilled Kineticist|skilled kineticist]]

By delving into study of the Ethereal and Elemental Planes, you are able to use your primary element to assist with your skills in an unusual way. Add [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] to your list of class skills. Choose one of your kineticist class skills that wasn’t granted by your primary element to also receive the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Skilled Kineticist|skilled kineticist]].
