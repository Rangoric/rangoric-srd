# Skilled Kineticist

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]].*

  - **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements#Universal|universal]]
  - **Type**: utility (Su)
  - **Level**: 1
  - **Burn**: —

You gain a bonus equal to 1/2 your kineticist level on skill checks with the skills your primary element added to your class skill list, and you can use the knowledge skill your primary element adds to your class skills ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]]) to identify elementals of your primary element’s subtype.
