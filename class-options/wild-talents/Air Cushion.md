# Air Cushion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 0

You cushion a fall from any height. You are constantly under the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_, and you count as one size category larger for the purpose of determining how you are affected by wind.
