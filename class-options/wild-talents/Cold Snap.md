# Cold Snap

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]].*

  - **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]]
  - **Type**: utility (Sp)
  - **Level**: 3
  - **Burn**: 1
  - **Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Cold Adaptation|cold adaptation]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Shroud of Water|shroud of water]]

You chill your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Shroud of Water|shroud of water]] and send the cold around you, creating an aura of numbing cold around yourself. Until the next time your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] is removed, you can begin or end the cold aura at will as a swift action. The temperature in the area 30 feet around you lowers by 5° F per kineticist level you possess, to a minimum modified temperature of –10° F. If this brings the temperature in the area below 40° F, all creatures within 5 feet of you take a –4 penalty to Dexterity. You are immune to these effects, as are creatures that are immune to cold or that benefit from effects that protect against temperature, such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*.
