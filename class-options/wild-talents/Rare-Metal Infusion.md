# Rare-Metal Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 2
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Metal Blast|metal]]
- **Defense**: none

You can control the properties of metal, allowing your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Metal Blast|metal blast]] to bypass [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] as if it were adamantine, cold iron, or silver (your choice). If your target has an unusual type of damage reduction that could be overcome with the correct type of metal (such as DR/gold), you can also select that metal for this purpose. The metal you create with this infusion crumbles away shortly thereafter and has no gp value.
