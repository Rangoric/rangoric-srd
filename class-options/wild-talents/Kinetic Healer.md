# Kinetic Healer

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 1; see text
- **Defense**: none
- **Spell Resistance**: yes

With a touch, you can heal a willing living creature of an amount of damage equal to your kinetic blast’s damage. Instead of paying the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] cost yourself, you can cause the recipient to take 1 point of burn. If you do so, the recipient takes 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]] per Hit Die he possesses, as usual for burn; this damage can’t be healed by any means until the recipient takes a full night’s rest.
