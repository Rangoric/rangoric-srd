# Fire's Fury

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: —

When using fire blasts or composite blasts that include fire, add your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-overflow|elemental overflow]] bonus to the damage dealt. If the kinetic blast normally adds double your elemental overflow bonus to damage, these effects stack.
