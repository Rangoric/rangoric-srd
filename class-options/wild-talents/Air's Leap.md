# Air's Leap

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]].*

  - **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
  - **Type**: utility (Su)
  - **Level**: 1
  - **Burn**: 0

You are always considered to have a running start when jumping, you add your kineticist level as a bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|Athletics]] checks to jump, you jump twice as far or high as the results of your check indicate, and you can accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] when jumping to double the distance you jump again (to a total of four times as far).
