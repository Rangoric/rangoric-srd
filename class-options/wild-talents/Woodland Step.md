# Woodland Step

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 0

You gain [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]], as per the druid ability. Due to your ability to manipulate plants, you can attempt a caster level check (DC = 11 + the spell’s caster level) as a standard action to also ignore magically manipulated plants (but not creatures of the plant type).
