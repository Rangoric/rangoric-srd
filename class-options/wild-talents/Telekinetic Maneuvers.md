# Telekinetic Maneuvers

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 4
- **Burn**: 0
- **Defense**: none
- **Spell Resistance**: yes

You can perform combat maneuvers as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Telekinesis|telekinesis]]_, but you use your Constitution modifier to determine your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|Combat Maneuver Bonus]] rather than your Intelligence or Charisma modifier. If you possess the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Finesse|telekinetic finesse]] wild talent, add [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] combat maneuvers to the list of combat maneuvers you can perform; when performing these maneuvers, determine your Combat Maneuver Bonus using your Dexterity modifier instead of your Constitution modifier.
