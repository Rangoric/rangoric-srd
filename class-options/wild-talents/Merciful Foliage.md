# Merciful Foliage

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 2
- **Burn**: 0

The [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Fey Realm|fey realm]] is a place of rampant life, and you can use some of its energy to ease the danger of your attacks. You can deal [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]] without taking a penalty on attack rolls with your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wood Blast|wood blasts]] or composite blasts that include wood.
