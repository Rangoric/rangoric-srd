# Spark of Life

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements#Universal|universal]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 5
- **Burn**: 0

You breathe a semblance of life into elemental matter, which takes the form of a Medium elemental of any of your elements as if summoned by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster IV|summon monster IV]]_ with a caster level equal to your kineticist level, except the elemental gains the mindless trait. Each round on your turn, you must take a move action to guide the elemental or it collapses back into its component element. By accepting 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]], you can pour a bit of your own sentience into the elemental, removing the mindless quality and allowing it to persist for 1 round per kineticist level without requiring any further actions. At 12th level, you can choose to form a Large elemental as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster V|summon monster V]]_; at 14th level, you can choose to form a Huge elemental as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]_; at 16th level, you can choose to form a greater elemental as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VII|summon monster VII]]_; and at 18th level, you can choose to form an elder elemental as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VIII|summon monster VIII]]_.
