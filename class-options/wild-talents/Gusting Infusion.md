# Gusting Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 1
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Air Blast|air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blizzard Blast|blizzard]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Sandstorm Blast|sandstorm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Thunderstorm Blast|thunderstorm]]
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates

The wind from your infusion causes your blast to act as an instantaneous _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]_. If your blast has a clear path, you can accept 2 additional points of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] or reduce the damage to 0 in order to cause the gust of wind effect to persist for 1 round along that path.
