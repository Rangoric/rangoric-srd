# Deadly Earth

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|form infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: form infusion
- **Level**: 6
- **Burn**: 4
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Extended Range|extended range]]
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Autumn Blast|autumn]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Blast|earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Magma Blast|magma]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Metal Blast|metal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Mud Blast|mud]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Spring Blast|spring]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Summer Blast|summer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Verdant Blast|verdant]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Winter Blast|winter]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wood Blast|wood]]
- **Defense**: none

You infuse the ground in a 20-foot radius anywhere within 120 feet of you on an earthen surface (if you are using a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Metal Blast|metal blast]], this infusion affects a metal surface instead). The ground roils and buckles beneath the affected area. All creatures and objects in contact with the ground within the area when you create it automatically take 1/4 of the normal damage from your blast, with no attack against their defenses. Any time a creature enters the area, it takes half the normal damage from your blast, as do creatures that end their turns in the area. The entire area counts as [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement#Terrain and Obstacles|difficult terrain]]. This infusion lasts for a number of rounds equal to your Constitution modifier or until you use it again.
