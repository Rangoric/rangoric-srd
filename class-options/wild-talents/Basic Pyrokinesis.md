# Basic Pyrokinesis

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 0

You can use your inner flame to reproduce the effects of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spark|spark]]_ cantrip, except that the light you create with light produces heat like a normal flame; using any of the three abilities ends any previous light effect from this wild talent.
