# Jagged Flesh

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 1
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Flesh of Stone|flesh of stone]]

You painfully push forth jagged chunks of rock from your flesh. Until the next time your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] is removed, while your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Flesh of Stone|flesh of stone]] is active, any creature that strikes you with an unarmed strike or natural weapon or that grapples you takes 1d6 points of piercing damage. Weapons that strike you also take this damage, though the damage is unlikely to penetrate the weapon’s hardness.
