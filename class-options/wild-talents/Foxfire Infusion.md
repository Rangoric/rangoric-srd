# Foxfire Infusion

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]].*

  - **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
  - **Type**: substance infusion (Sp)
  - **Level**: 3
  - **Burn**: 2
  - **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Foxfire|foxfire]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Positive Blast|positive blast]]
  - **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blue Flame Blast|blue flame]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Fire Blast|fire]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Positive Blast|positive]]
  - **Saving Throw**: Will partial

Your blast leaves behind flickering flames that reveal the target’s location as per *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]* for 1 minute (or until your next turn if the target succeeds at its save). This effect occurs even if the foe doesn’t take damage, as long as the attack overcomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] (if any). You can reduce the blast’s damage by half to increase the DC of this saving throw by 2.

A creature can remove the revealing flames early by taking a full-round action to extinguish them.
