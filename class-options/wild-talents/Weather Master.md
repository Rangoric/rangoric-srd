# Weather Master

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 8
- **Burn**: 0

By focusing on your connection to air and seeding the air around you for 10 minutes, you can create powerful weather effects, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]]_. You can create tornadoes or hurricane-force winds, as appropriate for the season. If you have access to the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire element]], you can create hot weather, a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Heat Wave|heat wave]], a thaw, or an early spring. If you have access to the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water element]], you can create all other types of weather listed in control weather.
