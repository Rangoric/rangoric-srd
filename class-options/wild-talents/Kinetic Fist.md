# Kinetic Fist

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|form infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements#Universal|universal]]
- **Type**: form infusion
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 1
- **Associated Blasts**: any
- **Defense**: none

You surround your body with energy or elemental matter from your kinetic abilities. You can use this form infusion as part of an attack action, a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge action]], or a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Full Attack|full-attack action]] to add damage to each of your natural attacks and unarmed strikes until the beginning of your next turn. Since kinetic fist is part of another action, using this wild talent doesn’t provoke any additional [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]]. You deal an additional 1d6 points of damage per 3 dice of your kinetic blast’s damage (minimum 1d6), and this damage is of the same type as your kinetic blast’s damage. This extra damage ignores [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] and doesn’t apply any modifiers to your kinetic blast’s damage, such as your Constitution modifier.
