# Plasma Blast

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#^composite-blasts|composite blast wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]].*

  - **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]]
  - **Type**: composite blast (Sp)
  - **Level**: —
  - **Burn**: 2
  - **Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Air Blast|air blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Fire Blast|fire blast]]
  - **Blast Type**: physical
  - **Damage**: half bludgeoning, half fire

You launch a gust of air and then use your fire to superheat the gust into plasma just as it strikes a single foe.
