# Smoke Storm

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 0
- **Defense**: Fortitude; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: no

You transform a source of open flame within 120 feet into a cloud of choking smoke, filling a 20-foot-radius spread and affecting vision like a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Smokestick|smokestick]]. All creatures that begin their turns inside the area become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] as long as they remain in the smoke and for 1d4+1 rounds thereafter (vs Fortitude).
