# Unraveling Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 5
- **Burn**: 3
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blue Flame Blast|blue flame]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Fire Blast|fire]]
- **Defense**: none

Your kinetic blast burns so hot that it melts away your foe’s magical effects. Whenever your infused blast hits a foe and penetrates its [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]], you can attempt a caster level check as if using a targeted _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_ before determining whether the foe takes damage from the blast. You can choose to reduce the blast’s damage by half to increase your bonus on the caster level check by 2. If you don’t choose a specific spell effect to attempt to dispel, you automatically attempt to dispel effects that would protect the target from fire damage before other spells.
