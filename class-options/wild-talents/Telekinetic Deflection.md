# Telekinetic Deflection

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 8
- **Burn**: 0

You can deflect your foes’ attacks, as the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deflection|deflection]]_ with a duration of 1 round. You can accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] to increase the duration to 1 round per kineticist level you possess.
