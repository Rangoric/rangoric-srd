# Suffocate

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Void Element|void]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 6
- **Burn**: 0
- **Defense**: Fortitude partial
- **Spell Resistance**: yes

You use aether to choke a creature within 120 feet, air to create an area of unbreathable air around it, or water to fill its throat with water. If your target needs to breathe, it must hold its breath for as long as you concentrate. On each of its turns, it can make the effect attack its Fortitude again, and on a miss it can speak that turn, but it can breathe only if your concentration breaks, you leave the 120-foot range, or you break line of effect. You can accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] when activating this wild talent in order to expel the air from your target’s lungs. If you do so, the effect attacks the target’s Fortitude each round, and on the first hit the target becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^disabled|disabled]] and is reduced to 0 hit points, and on the second hit, it falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] and is reduced to –1 hit points.
