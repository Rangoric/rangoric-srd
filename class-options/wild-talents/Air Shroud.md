# Air Shroud

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 0

You are always surrounded by air, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Air Bubble|air bubble]]_. As a standard action, you can accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] to extend the effect to a number of additional creatures equal to your Constitution modifier for 1 minute per kineticist level you possess.
