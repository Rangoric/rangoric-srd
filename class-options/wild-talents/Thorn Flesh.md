# Thorn Flesh

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 1
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Flesh of Wood|flesh of wood]]

You cover your body in barbed thorns that injure foes who strike you. This functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Jagged Flesh|jagged flesh]] wild talent.
