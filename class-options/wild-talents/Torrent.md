# Torrent

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|form infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]]
- **Type**: form infusion
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 2
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Air Blast|air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blizzard Blast|blizzard]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blue Flame Blast|blue flame]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Charged Water Blast|charged water]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Fire Blast|fire]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Magma Blast|magma]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Mud Blast|mud]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Plasma Blast|plasma]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Sandstorm Blast|sandstorm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Steam Blast|steam]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Thunderstorm Blast|thunderstorm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Water Blast|water]]
- **Defense**: Reflex half

A torrent of energy buffets or burns all foes in a 30-foot line, dealing half your normal amount of blast damage (or full damage for energy blasts). The attack bonus is Dexterity-based.
