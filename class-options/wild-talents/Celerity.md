# Celerity

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 0

You can galvanize the flow of electricity within your allies to enhance their speed. This acts as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_ except it lasts for 1 round. You can accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] to increase the duration to 1 round per kineticist level you possess.
