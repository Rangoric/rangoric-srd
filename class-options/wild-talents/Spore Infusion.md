# Spore Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 5
- **Burn**: 3
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Autumn Blast|autumn]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Spring Blast|spring]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Summer Blast|summer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Verdant Blast|verdant]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Winter Blast|winter]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wood Blast|wood]]
- **Defense**: Fortitude

Creatures that take piercing or slashing damage from your blast are infected with spores. The spores attack the target’s Fortitude, and on a hit it takes 1d6 points of damage per round for 10 rounds as plants and fungi grow out of its body. At the end of that time, the target is exposed to the pulsing puffs [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]]. This infusion is a disease effect. When using a wood kinetic blast against a creature infected by this infusion, you gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls, to attack bonuses against defenses, and on caster level checks to overcome [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]].

_Pulsing Puffs_: Blast—injury; _attack_ +8 vs Fortitude; _onset_ 1 minute; _frequency_ 1/day; _effect_ 1d6 Dex damage; _cure_ 2 consecutive misses.
