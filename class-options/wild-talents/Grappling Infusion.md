# Grappling Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements#Universal|universal]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 5
- **Burn**: 3
- **Associated Blasts**: any
- **Defense**: none

Your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Cloud|cloud]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Deadly Earth|deadly earth]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wall (Wild Talent)|wall]] blast grows tendrils that hold your enemies in place. Whenever a creature takes damage from the infused blast, you can attempt a combat maneuver check to have the blast [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] that opponent. Your bonus on this check is equal to 2 + your kineticist level + your Constitution modifier (treat the blast as a Huge creature). The blast only grapples opponents; it can’t perform other functions of grapple maneuvers such as pinning or moving the opponent. If the blast is already grappling an opponent, it receives a +5 bonus on its combat maneuver check to maintain the grapple, as normal. You can use this infusion only if you also use the cloud, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Deadly Earth|deadly earth]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wall (Wild Talent)|wall]] form infusion.
