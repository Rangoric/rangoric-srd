# Basic Hydrokinesis

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 0

You can _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]_ as the cantrip create water, purify water as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Purify Food and Drink|purify food and drink]]_, and dry wet creatures and objects as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_. While you cannot lift water into the air using this ability, you can create mild currents in a body of water by concentrating. These currents are strong enough to run a water mill as if the mill were being turned manually by a creature with a Strength score equal to your Constitution score.
