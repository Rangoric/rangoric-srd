# Pulling Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Void Element|void]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 1
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Gravity Blast|gravity]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Void Blast|void]]
- **Saving Throw**: none

Your kinetic blast pulls foes toward you. Attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Drag|drag]] combat maneuver check against each target damaged by your infused blast (the blast always drags the foe closer to you), using your Constitution modifier instead of your Strength modifier to determine your bonus. This infusion can pull a foe a maximum of 5 feet. You can increase the maximum distance pulled by 5 feet per additional point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] accepted.
