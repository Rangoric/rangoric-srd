# From the Ashes

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 9
- **Burn**: 2

When you are about to fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] or die from hit point damage, as an immediate action you can burst into flames that leave behind only ash, which cannot be appreciably affected by most attacks or effects, though a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_ spell or similar magic can annihilate it. At the start of your next turn, unless the ash has been completely destroyed, you arise in the square where the most ash is located, having taken the damage from the triggering effect but having also healed 5 points of damage per kineticist level you possess.
