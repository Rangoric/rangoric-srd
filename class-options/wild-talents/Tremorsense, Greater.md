# Tremorsense, Greater

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 5
- **Burn**: 0
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Tremorsense (Wild Talent)|tremorsense]]

You can spend 10 minutes to extend your [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] outward while in a natural underground setting, letting you learn three facts as if using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune with Nature|commune with nature]]_, except your tremorsense can’t tell if a creature is a woodland creature or a powerful unnatural creature.
