# Wild Talents

Kineticists’ wild talents shape the expression of their powers. How a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]] learns and uses them, their effective spell levels and attack bonuses are in her [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-wild-talents|wild talents]] class feature; which ones she can choose depends on her [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements|element]]. Each wild talent has its own note, listed below.

## Kinetic Blasts

Kinetic blasts comprise simple and composite blasts.

### Simple Blasts

A kineticist gains a simple blast from her primary element at 1st level—some elements offer more than one option. When a kineticist gains a new element via the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]] class feature, she gains a simple blast from that element as well. Each simple blast is either a physical blast or an energy blast. Physical blasts are ranged attacks that deal an amount of damage equal to 1d6+1 + the kineticist’s Constitution modifier, increasing by 1d6+1 for every 2 kineticist levels beyond 1st. [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|Spell resistance]] doesn’t apply against physical blasts. Energy blasts are ranged touch attacks that deal an amount of damage equal to 1d6 + 1/2 the kineticist’s Constitution modifier, increasing by 1d6 for every 2 kineticist levels beyond 1st. ^simple-blasts

**Simple blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Air Blast|air blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Cold Blast|cold blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Blast|earth blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Electric Blast|electric blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Fire Blast|fire blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Gravity Blast|gravity blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Negative Blast|negative blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Positive Blast|positive blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Blast|telekinetic blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Water Blast|water blast]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wood Blast|wood blast]].

### Composite Blasts

Composite blasts combine elements to form a new blast. When a kineticist gains a new element through [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-expanded-element|expanded element]], she gains access to all composite blasts for which she qualifies. Most composite blasts are either physical or energy blasts, like simple blasts. Physical composite blasts deal an amount of damage equal to 2d6+2 + the kineticist’s Constitution modifier, increasing by 2d6+2 for every 2 kineticist levels beyond 1st; energy composite blasts deal an amount of damage equal to 2d6 + 1/2 the kineticist’s Constitution modifier, increasing by 2d6 for every 2 kineticist levels beyond 1st. ^composite-blasts
