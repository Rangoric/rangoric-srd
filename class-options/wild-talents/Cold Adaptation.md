# Cold Adaptation

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Fire Element|fire]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 0

You are constantly protected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_ against cold temperatures only. You gain cold resistance equal to twice your current amount of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]].
