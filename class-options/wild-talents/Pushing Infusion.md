# Pushing Infusion

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]].*

  - **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
  - **Type**: substance infusion
  - **Level**: 1
  - **Burn**: 1
  - **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Air Blast|air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Autumn Blast|autumn]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blizzard Blast|blizzard]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Charged Water Blast|charged water]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Blast|earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Magma Blast|magma]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Metal Blast|metal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Mud Blast|mud]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Sandstorm Blast|sandstorm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Spring Blast|spring]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Steam Blast|steam]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Summer Blast|summer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Blast|telekinetic]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Verdant Blast|verdant]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Water Blast|water]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Winter Blast|winter]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wood Blast|wood]]
  - **Saving Throw**: none

The momentum of your kinetic blast knocks foes back. Attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] combat maneuver check against each target damaged by your infused blast, using your Constitution modifier instead of your Strength modifier to determine your bonus. This infusion can push a foe back by a maximum of 5 feet. You can increase the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] cost of this infusion to increase the maximum distance pushed by 5 feet per additional point of burn accepted. You can’t use this infusion with a form infusion such as cloud that causes your kinetic blast to lack a clear direction to push.
