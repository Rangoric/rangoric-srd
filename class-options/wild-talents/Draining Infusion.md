# Draining Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Elements#Universal|universal]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 1
- **Associated Blasts**: any [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#^simple-blasts|simple]]
- **Defense**: Fortitude partial; see text

You can drain elemental energy and matter from your foes to strengthen your next attack. When you use this infusion, your kinetic blast affects only creatures with a subtype matching your kinetic blast’s element (for instance, a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Fire Blast|fire blast]] would damage only creatures of the fire subtype). Against such creatures, your blast targets touch AC (if it requires an attack roll) and always allows [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]]. Because you are draining energy from the target, your blast doesn’t apply your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-overflow|elemental overflow]] bonuses or Constitution modifier. It attacks Fortitude, and on a miss the target takes 1/4 the normal amount of damage. Draining infusion ignores any [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]], resistances, and immunities the creature might possess. If your draining infusion hits at least one creature, you can reduce the total [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] cost of any one blast wild talent used before the end of your next turn by 1, or by 2 if you have the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-supercharge|supercharge]] ability. If you use draining infusion again before applying this reduction, you still deal the damage from that draining infusion, but the burn reductions don’t stack.
