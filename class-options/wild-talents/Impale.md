# Impale

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|form infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: form infusion
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 2
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Autumn Blast|autumn]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Blast|earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Ice Blast|ice]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Metal Blast|metal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Spring Blast|spring]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Summer Blast|summer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Verdant Blast|verdant]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Winter Blast|winter]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wood Blast|wood]]
- **Defense**: none

You extend a long, sharp spike of elemental matter along a line, impaling multiple foes. Make a single attack roll against each creature or object in a 30-foot line, starting with the closest target. If the result is a critical threat, roll to confirm against only the first target you hit. Calculate damage against each target you hit, starting from the closest. If your attack hits a target but fails to deal physical damage to that target, the spike fails to penetrate and the infused blast doesn’t continue. A blast with the impale infusion can penetrate solid barriers, but it must deal enough damage to destroy the barrier in order to continue through. Infused earth and metal blasts always deal piercing damage.
