# Touchsight

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 0

You attach strands of aether to everything your telekinesis touches. Any time you damage a creature using your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Blast|telekinetic blast]], as long as that creature doesn’t use a teleportation effect, you can see that creature at any distance as if using [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsight]] until the end of your next turn. Immediately after damaging a creature, you can accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] to strengthen the strands of aether attached to that creature, increasing the duration of the touchsight on that creature to 1 round per kineticist level.
