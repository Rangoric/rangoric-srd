# Telekinetic Haul

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 2
- **Burn**: 0
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Basic Telekinesis|basic telekinesis]]

When using [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Basic Telekinesis|basic telekinesis]], you can move an object that weighs up to 100 pounds per kineticist level you possess. When using your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Blast|telekinetic blast]], you can throw an object weighing up to 100 pounds per kineticist level you possess, but this doesn’t increase the damage. If you accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]], the maximum weight increases to 1,000 pounds per kineticist level you possess and the duration increases to 1 minute per kineticist level you possess.
