# Windsight

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]].*

  - **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
  - **Type**: utility (Su)
  - **Level**: 3
  - **Burn**: —

You can see through mist and fog (including *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]* and similar magic). In areas of moderate or stronger wind, you can see and hear as if you were standing at both your own position and a position a number of feet in the wind’s direction equal to the wind’s speed in miles per hour, potentially allowing you to see around corners and other obstacles.
