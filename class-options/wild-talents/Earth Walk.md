# Earth Walk

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: —

Because you meld very slightly into the earth, when you are standing on an earthen surface, you ignore [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement#Terrain and Obstacles|difficult terrain]] caused by rocks, earth, or mud, and while on such surfaces you add your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-elemental-overflow|elemental overflow]] bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against being moved or tripped and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to balance.
