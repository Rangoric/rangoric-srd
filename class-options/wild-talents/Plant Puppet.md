# Plant Puppet

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 5
- **Burn**: 0

You can control plants, causing them to attack your foes. This functions as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Aether Puppet|aether puppet]] wild talent, except it controls plants instead of objects.

At 12th level, you can grant hardness 5 to Large and larger animated plants.
