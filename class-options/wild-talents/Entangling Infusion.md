# Entangling Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 2
- **Burn**: 2
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Autumn Blast|autumn]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blizzard Blast|blizzard]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Cold Blast|cold]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Earth Blast|earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Ice Blast|ice]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Magma Blast|magma]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Metal Blast|metal]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Mud Blast|mud]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Sandstorm Blast|sandstorm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Spring Blast|spring]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Summer Blast|summer]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Verdant Blast|verdant]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Winter Blast|winter]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wood Blast|wood]]
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex negates

Your kinetic blast surrounds your foes in elemental matter. Whenever a blast with this infusion deals damage to a foe, that foe becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] for 1 minute. The foe can remove this condition as a standard action with a successful [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] or Strength check (with the same DC as for saves against your kinetic blast) or by dealing an amount of damage to the entangling matter equal to double your kineticist level (the matter has hardness 0). If the foe was already entangled by this infusion and fails its save against a second instance of this infusion, the increased amount of elemental matter fuses to the ground, causing the foe to be rooted in place as though anchored to an immobile object.
