# Ice Path

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 6
- **Burn**: 0
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Icewalker|icewalker]]

You freeze water vapor in the air, allowing you to travel above the ground as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Air Walk|air walk]]_ by walking along the ice, and leaving a path of ice behind you that lasts for 1 round before it melts.
