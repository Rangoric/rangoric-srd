# Telekinetic Invisibility

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 0

You weave strands of aether, bending light and dampening sound; this works as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_ except that the aetheric bending is easier to notice than normal invisibility, so your bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks is halved (+10 while moving and +20 while perfectly still). However, the dampened sound allows you to avoid automatic detection via sound-based [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsense]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsight]], but you do not receive the bonus on Stealth checks from this wild talent against a creature with such abilities.
