# Disintegrating Infusion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|substance infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]]
- **Type**: substance infusion
- **Level**: 6
- **Burn**: 4
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Force Blast|force]]
- **Defense**: Fortitude partial; see text

You can use force to rip your targets apart. Against creatures, your kinetic blast deals double its normal amount of damage, but it attacks the targets’ Fortitude, and a miss reduces the damage to half the blast’s normal amount of damage (for a total of 1/4 of the blast’s increased damage). Any creature reduced to 0 or fewer hit points by the blast is disintegrated, as the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_. You can use the infused blast to destroy force effects or objects as disintegrate, but for each force effect or 10-foot cube of nonliving matter you destroy in this way, you must accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]], which can’t be reduced by effects such as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-infusion-specialization|infusion specialization]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-gather-power|gather power]].
