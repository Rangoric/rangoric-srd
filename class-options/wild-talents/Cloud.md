# Cloud

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|form infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Elements**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Water Element|water]]
- **Type**: form infusion
- **Level**: 7
- **Burn**: 4
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Extended Range|extended range]]
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Blizzard Blast|blizzard]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Sandstorm Blast|sandstorm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Steam Blast|steam]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Thunderstorm Blast|thunderstorm]]
- **Defense**: none

You release your power in a diffuse cloud. You can center this 20-foot-radius spread anywhere within 120 feet of you. All creatures and objects within the cloud when you create it automatically take 1/4 the normal amount of damage from your blast, with no attack against their defenses. Any time a creature enters the cloud, it takes half the normal amount of damage from your blast, as do creatures that end their turns in the cloud. Your cloud obscures vision as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]_. It lasts for a number of rounds equal to your Constitution modifier or until you use the cloud infusion again.
