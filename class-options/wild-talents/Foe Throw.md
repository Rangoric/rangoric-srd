# Foe Throw

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Infusion Wild Talents|form infusion wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Aether Element|aether]]
- **Type**: form infusion
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 2
- **Associated Blasts**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Blast|telekinetic]]
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates; see text

Your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Telekinetic Blast|telekinetic blast]] can throw a Large or smaller creature instead of an unattended object; you can increase the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] cost by 1 to affect a creature of a larger size. If the creature you attempt to throw succeeds at a Fortitude save, it negates the blast entirely. Otherwise, if your blast hits, both the thrown creature and the target take the full amount of damage from your telekinetic blast, and the thrown creature falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] in the last unoccupied space along its path. If your blast misses, the thrown creature can choose to occupy any space within 30 feet of the target, it doesn’t fall prone, and it takes half the normal amount of damage from your blast. The movement doesn’t provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]].
