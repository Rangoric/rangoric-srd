# Tremorsense

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 0

You can take a move action to gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] 30 feet for 1 round on any earth or stone surface that you touch. You can accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] to increase the duration to 1 round per kineticist level you possess. While benefiting from this tremorsense, your earth blasts and composite blasts that include earth components don’t suffer a miss chance from concealment or total concealment against creatures you detect with your tremorsense.
