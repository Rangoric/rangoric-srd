# Negative Blast

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#^simple-blasts|simple blast wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Void Element|void]]
- **Type**: simple blast (Sp)
- **Level**: —
- **Burn**: 0
- **Blast Type**: energy
- **Damage**: negative energy

You blast your foe with negative energy. This blast and its composite blasts never heal creatures, even if they would normally be healed by negative energy (though such creatures still take no damage).
