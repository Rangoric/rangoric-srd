# Shift Earth

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Earth Element|earth]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 4
- **Burn**: 0
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Kinetic Cover|kinetic cover]]
- **Defense**: see text
- **Spell Resistance**: no

As a standard action, you can push or pull a 5-foot cube of earth or unworked stone within 30 feet, moving the cube 5 feet in any direction. You can create raised platforms, stairs up a cliff, holes, or other useful features. This doesn’t cause the earth to float in the air, although in areas with plenty of earth, you can move a cube upward, creating a short pillar. If you move the earth beneath a creature’s feet, it attacks the creature’s Reflex at +10, and on a miss the creature leaps elsewhere and avoids moving along with the earth.
