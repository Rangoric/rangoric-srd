# Basic Chaokinesis

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Void Element|void]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 1
- **Burn**: 0

You can create a shadow that protects a target from bright light. You can also change gravity to increase a creature’s [[rangoric-srd/rules/Carrying, Movement, and Exploration#Carrying Capacity|carrying capacity]] by half or grant a creature a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|Athletics]] checks to jump. Each benefit lasts 1 hour or until you use basic chaokinesis again.
