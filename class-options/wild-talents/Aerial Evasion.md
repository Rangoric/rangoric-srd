# Aerial Evasion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Air Element|air]]
- **Type**: utility (Su)
- **Level**: 3
- **Burn**: 1
- **Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Enveloping Winds|enveloping winds]]

You use wind to buffet yourself away from area attacks. Until the next time your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] is removed, you gain [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|evasion]], as the rogue class feature.
