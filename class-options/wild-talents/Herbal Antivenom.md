# Herbal Antivenom

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/wild-talents/Wild Talents#Utility Wild Talents|utility wild talent]] of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]]._

- **Element**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/elements/Wood Element|wood]]
- **Type**: utility (Sp)
- **Level**: 4
- **Burn**: 0

Your body can produce the herbal remedies necessary to counter almost any poison. You gain a +5 alchemical bonus on defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]], as if you were always under the effect of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Antitoxin|antitoxin]]. You can take the [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Poison|treat poison]] action as a standard action with a mere touch, without a healer’s kit, and even on yourself; if you succeed, you grant the touched creature a +5 alchemical bonus against that specific instance of poison in addition to the normal +4 bonus from treat poison.

You can accept 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]] while using the treat poison action in this way to also produce the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]]_.
