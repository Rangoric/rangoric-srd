# Protean Eidolon

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner#^unchained-summoner-eidolon-subtypes|eidolon subtype]] for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon.*

Serpentine beings of pure chaos, proteans seek to reshape reality. Protean eidolons appreciate creative summoners who often rebuild their forms and make liberal use of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Transmogrify|transmogrify]]* spell. Beyond that, protean eidolons are happy to work with their summoners for any purpose, though they are quick to remind their summoners that while they have a mutually beneficial relationship, they are not servants.

*Alignment*: Chaotic neutral.

*Base Form*: Serpentine (bite, grab [tail slap], tail, tail slap).

*Base Evolutions*:
  - At 1st level, protean eidolons gain the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Resistance (Evolution)|resistance]] (acid) evolution as well as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Grab (Evolution)|grab]] evolution, tied to an attack type of the summoner’s choice. Whenever the summoner changes the protean eidolon’s evolutions, he can also change the attack type for grab.
  - At 4th level, protean eidolons gain electricity resistance 10 and sonic resistance 10.
  - At 8th level, protean eidolons gain the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Constrict (Evolution)|constrict]] evolution.
  - At 12th level, protean eidolons gain DR 5/lawful. They also gain the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Blindsense (Evolution)|blindsense]] evolution and the ability to fly without wings with perfect maneuverability, as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Flight (Evolution)|flight]] evolution with 4 points spent.
  - At 16th level, protean eidolons lose the resistance (acid) evolution and instead gain the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Immunity (Evolution)|immunity]] (acid) evolution. They also gain the amorphous anatomy ability (see the protean subtype).
  - At 20th level, protean eidolons gain constant *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]* and the protean version of the change shape ([[rangoric-srd/spells/Polymorph|greater polymorph]]) ability (see the protean subtype).
