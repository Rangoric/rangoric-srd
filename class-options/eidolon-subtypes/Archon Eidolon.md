# Archon Eidolon

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner#^unchained-summoner-eidolon-subtypes|eidolon subtype]] for the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner|unchained summoner]]’s eidolon.*

Summoned from heaven, archon eidolons are the embodiments of righteousness. They often take on powerful, graceful forms, rippling with muscles but refined in their movements. Archon eidolons are totally dedicated to the cause of good, and have little patience for those who violate their oaths and ideals—including the summoner who called them. Archons will do whatever it takes to stop the advance of evil and protect the innocent.

*Alignment*: Lawful good.

*Base Form*: Biped (limbs [arms], limbs [legs], slam).

*Base Evolutions*:
  - At 1st level, archon eidolons gain the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Resistance (Evolution)|resistance]] (electricity) evolution and the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Skilled (Evolution)|skilled]] (Intimidation) evolution. They also gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against poison.
  - At 4th level, archon eidolons add 1 point to their evolution pools.
  - At 8th level, archon eidolons gain the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Ability Increase (Evolution)|ability increase]] evolution, applied to an ability score the summoner chooses.
  - At 12th level, archon eidolons gain DR 5/evil. They also gain immunity to petrification and the truespeech ability (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^archon|archon subtype]]).
  - At 16th level, archon eidolons lose the resistance (electricity) evolution and skilled (Intimidation) evolution, and instead gain the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Immunity (Evolution)|immunity]] (electricity) evolution. In addition, they gain the aura of menace ability (see the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^archon|archon subtype]]).
  - At 20th level, archon eidolons gain the ability to use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|greater teleport]] at will, as the spell (caster level 14th), except they can teleport only themselves and up to 50 pounds of carried objects.
