# Dares

Dares are similar to [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Deeds|deeds]] in that they grant a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger|gunslinger]] or a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler|swashbuckler]] extra abilities based on either [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]], but unlike deeds, dares become active when a member of these classes runs out of her respective pool, and become inactive until the character regains points of their respective pool. They grant the character a benefit and a new ability to regain or increase the ability to regain either grit or panache.

A dare can be taken in place of one of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-bonus-feats|bonus feats]] a gunslinger or a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-bonus-feats|swashbuckler]] gains at 4th level and every 4 levels thereafter. A gunslinger or a swashbuckler can choose to have multiple dares, but can only pick one to be active each time she reaches 0 grit or panache points. Once chosen, the active dare cannot be changed until the gunslinger or swashbuckler reaches 0 grit or panache points again. ^dares
