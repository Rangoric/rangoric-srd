# Leech

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/hunter/Verminous Hunter (Archetype)|vermin focus]] (Su), which a verminous hunter takes in place of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]]._

The creature gains a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver checks, and deals 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage every time it succeeds at a grapple combat maneuver check to damage an opponent. These benefits increase to a +4 bonus and 3 points of bleed at 8th level, and a +6 bonus and 5 points of bleed at 15th level. Bleed damage from this ability does not stack with itself.
