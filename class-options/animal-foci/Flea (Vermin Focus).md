# Flea

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/hunter/Verminous Hunter (Archetype)|vermin focus]] (Su), which a verminous hunter takes in place of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]]._

The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on defenses against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions|disease]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to jump. These bonuses increase to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th level.
