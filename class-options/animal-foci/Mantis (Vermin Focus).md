# Mantis

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/hunter/Verminous Hunter (Archetype)|vermin focus]] (Su), which a verminous hunter takes in place of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]].*

The creature gains a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] attack rolls and a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver checks. These bonuses increase to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th level.
