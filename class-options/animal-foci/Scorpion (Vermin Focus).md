# Scorpion

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/hunter/Verminous Hunter (Archetype)|vermin focus]] (Su), which a verminous hunter takes in place of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]]._

The creature gains a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver checks. These bonuses increase to +6 and +4 respectively at 8th level, and +8 and +6 at 15th level.
