# Frog

*An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]] (Su).*

The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim and to jump. These bonuses increase to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th level.
