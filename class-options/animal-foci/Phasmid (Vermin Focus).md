# Phasmid

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/hunter/Verminous Hunter (Archetype)|vermin focus]] (Su), which a verminous hunter takes in place of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]].*

The creature gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] and a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks in forests or plains. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th level.
