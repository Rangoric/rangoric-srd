# Worm

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/hunter/Verminous Hunter (Archetype)|vermin focus]] (Su), which a verminous hunter takes in place of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-focus|animal focus]]._

The creature gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] 1 and a 25% chance to treat any critical hit or sneak attack as a normal hit (as the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]_ armor special ability). This increases to fast healing 2 and 50% at 8th level, and fast healing 3 and 75% at 15th level.
