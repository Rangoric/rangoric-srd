# Forced Reincarnation

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-grand-hex|grand hex]] (Su).*

The witch causes a creature within 30 feet to die and be immediately *reincarnated* into a new body. A Will save negates this effect. Those that fail are slain and immediately brought back to life with the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reincarnate|reincarnate]]*. Whether or not the save is successful, a creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 1 day.
