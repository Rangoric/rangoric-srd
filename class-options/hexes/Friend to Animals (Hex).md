# Friend to Animals

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Nature Spirit|nature spirit]]._

The shaman can spontaneously cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally]]_ spells as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]]. In addition, all animals within 30 feet of the shaman receive a sacred bonus on all defenses equal to the shaman's Charisma modifier.
