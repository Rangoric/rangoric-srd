# Evil Eye

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|witch hex]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su).*

The witch or shaman can cause doubt to creep into the mind of a foe within 30 feet that she can see. The target takes a –2 penalty on one of the following (the hexer's choice): AC, ability checks, attack rolls, saving throws, or skill checks. This hex lasts for a number of rounds equal to 3 + her Intelligence modifier (Wisdom for a shaman). A Will save reduces this to just 1 round. This is a mind-affecting effect. At 8th level the penalty increases to –4.

**Shaman**: the shaman's version doesn't call this a mind-affecting effect, so a shaman's evil eye works on creatures immune to mind-affecting effects.
