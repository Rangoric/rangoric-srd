# Crystal Sight

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Ex) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Stone Spirit|stone spirit]].*

The shaman sees through stone, earth, or sand as easily as if it were transparent crystal. Her gaze can penetrate a number of feet equal to her shaman level (or 1/12th this thickness of metal). The shaman can use this ability a number of rounds per day equal to her shaman level, but these rounds do not need to be consecutive.
