# Shapeshift

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su).*

The shaman transforms herself into another form for a number of minutes per day equal to her level, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]*. This duration does not need to be consecutive, but must be spent in 1-minute increments. Changing form (including changing back) is a standard action that doesn't provoke an attack of opportunity. At 8th level, this ability works as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape I]]*. At 12th level, this ability works as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape II|beast shape II]]*. At 16th level, this ability works as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]*. At 20th level, this ability works as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape IV|beast shape IV]]*.
