# Flame Curse

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Flame Spirit|flame spirit]]._

The shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to become vulnerable to fire until the end of the shaman's next turn. If the creature is already vulnerable to fire, this hex has no effect. Fire immunity and resistances apply as normal, and a miss by the effect that caused the damage reduces it as normal. At 8th and 16th levels, the duration of this hex is extended by 1 round. After the vulnerability ends, the creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 24 hours.
