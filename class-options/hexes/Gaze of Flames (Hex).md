# Gaze of Flames

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Flame Spirit|flame spirit]].*

The shaman sees through fire, fog, and smoke without penalty as long as there is enough light to otherwise allow her to see normally. At 7th level, the shaman can gaze through any source of flame within 10 feet per shaman level, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Clairaudience-Clairvoyance|clairvoyance]]*. The shaman can use this ability a number of rounds per day equal to her shaman level, but these rounds do not need to be consecutive.
