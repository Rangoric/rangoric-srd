# Stone Stability

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Ex) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Stone Spirit|stone spirit]].*

The shaman receives a +4 bonus to her CMD when resisting bull rush or trip attempts as long as she is standing on the ground. At 5th level, the shaman receives [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]] as a bonus feat. At 10th level, the shaman receives [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Trip|Greater Trip]] as a bonus feat. The shaman does not need to meet the prerequisites of these feats.
