# Eternal Slumber

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-grand-hex|grand hex]] (Su)._

The witch can touch a creature, causing it to drift off into a permanent slumber. It attacks the creature’s Will, and a miss negates this effect. On a hit, the creature falls asleep and cannot be woken. The effect can only be removed with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ or similar magic, although slaying the witch ends the effect. The witch can use this ability to poison food or drink, attacking the Will of those who ingest it, and on a hit they fall into an eternal slumber. She can only have one such dose of poison at any one time, and it loses its potency after 1 minute if not consumed. Whether it hits or misses, a creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 1 day.
