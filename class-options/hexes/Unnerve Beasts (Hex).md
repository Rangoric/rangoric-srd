# Unnerve Beasts

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (Su)._

The target becomes offensive to animals (vs Will). Animals become distraught and aggressive in the victim's presence—horses buck, dogs snap and bark, bulls charge, and so on. The hex lasts a number of hours equal to the witch's Intelligence modifier. A creature the hex misses cannot be affected by the hex for 1 day. The reaction of the animals is a mind-affecting charm effect, but the hex on the target is not.
