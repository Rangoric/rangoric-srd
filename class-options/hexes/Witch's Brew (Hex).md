# Witch's Brew

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-major-hex|major hex]] (Ex).*

When the witch uses her cauldron to brew a potion, she may spend double the cost to create 2 identical potions that day instead of just 1. At 15th level, she may spend triple the cost to create 3 identical potions that day. The witch must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Cauldron (Hex)|cauldron hex]] to select this hex.
