# Chant

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Ex).*

A shaman can chant as a move action. Any creature that is within 30 feet that is under the effects of the shaman's [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Charm (Hex)|charm]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Evil Eye (Hex)|evil eye]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Fortune (Hex)|fortune]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Fury (Hex)|fury]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Misfortune (Hex)|misfortune]] hex has that effect's duration extended by 1 round. A shaman cannot select both this hex and the witch's [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Cackle (Hex)|cackle]] hex.
