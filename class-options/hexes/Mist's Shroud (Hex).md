# Mist's Shroud

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Waves Spirit|waves spirit]].*

The shaman touches a willing creature (including herself) and enshrouds that creature in mist. This grants the creature concealment as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]* spell. The mist dissipates after it causes an attack to miss because of concealment or after 1 minute, whichever comes first. At 8th and 16th levels, the mist lasts for one additional attack. Once this mist around it dissipates, the creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 24 hours.
