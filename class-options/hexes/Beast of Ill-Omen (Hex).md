# Beast of Ill-Omen

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (Su)._

The witch imbues her familiar with strange magic, putting a minor curse upon the next enemy to see it. It attacks the enemy’s Will, and on a hit the enemy is affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bane|bane]]_ (caster level equal to the witch's level). The witch can use this hex on her familiar at a range of up to 60 feet. The affected enemy must be no more than 60 feet from the familiar to trigger the effect; seeing the familiar from a greater distance has no effect (though if the enemy and familiar approach to within 60 feet of each other, the hex takes effect). The _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bane|bane]]_ affects the closest creature to the familiar (ties affect the creature with the highest initiative score). Whether it hits or misses, the creature cannot be the target of the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bane|bane]]_ effect for 1 day (later uses of this hex ignore that creature when determining who is affected).
