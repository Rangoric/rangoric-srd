# Bone Lock

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Bones Spirit|bones spirit]]._

With a quick incantation, the shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to suffer stiffness in the joints and bones, causing the target to be staggered 1 round. It attacks Fortitude, and a miss negates this effect. At 8th level, the duration is increased to a number of rounds equal to her shaman level, though if the first attack hits, the target can make the hex attack it again each round, and a miss ends the effect. At 16th level, the target can no longer make the hex attack it again each round, although a miss by the first attack against its Fortitude still negates the effect entirely.
