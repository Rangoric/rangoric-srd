# Healing

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|witch hex]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su).*

A witch or shaman can soothe the wounds of those she touches. This acts as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]* spell, using her caster level. Once a creature has benefited from the healing hex, it cannot benefit from it again for 24 hours. At 5th level, this hex acts like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]*.
