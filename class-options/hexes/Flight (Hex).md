# Flight

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (Su).*

The witch grows lighter as she gains power, eventually gaining the ability to fly. At 1st level, the witch can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]* at will and gains a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim. At 3rd level, she can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]* once per day. At 5th level, she can fly, as per the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]* spell, for a number of minutes per day equal to her level. These minutes do not need to be consecutive, but they must be spent in 1-minute increments. This hex only affects the witch.
