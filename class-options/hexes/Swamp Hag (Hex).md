# Swamp Hag

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (Sp).*

While traveling through swamps, mires, bogs, and similar terrain, the witch leaves no trail and cannot be tracked, as the druid’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-trackless-step|trackless step]]. The witch can walk through mud and even quicksand as if it were normal ground.
