# Battle Master

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Ex) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Battle Spirit|battle spirit]].*

The shaman makes an extra attack of opportunity each round. This ability stacks with the attacks of opportunity granted by the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]] feat. At 8th level, the shaman gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Specialization|Weapon Specialization]] feat in a weapon of her choice as a bonus feat. At 16th level, the shaman gains the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Weapon Focus|Greater Weapon Focus]] feat as a bonus feat, for the same weapon chosen for Weapon Specialization. The shaman doesn't need to meet the prerequisites of these feats.
