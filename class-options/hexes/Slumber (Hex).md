# Slumber

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (Su)._

A witch can cause a creature within 30 feet to fall into a deep, magical sleep, as per the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]_. It attacks the creature’s Will, and a miss negates the effect. On a hit, the creature falls asleep for a number of rounds equal to the witch's level. This hex can affect a creature of any HD. The creature will not wake due to noise or light, but others can rouse it with a standard action. This hex ends immediately if the creature takes damage. Whether it hits or misses, a creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 1 day.
