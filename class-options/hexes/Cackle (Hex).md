# Cackle

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-hex|hex]] (Su).*

A witch can cackle madly as a move action. Any creature that is within 30 feet that is under the effects of an [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Agony (Hex)|agony hex]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Charm (Hex)|charm hex]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Evil Eye (Hex)|evil eye hex]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Fortune (Hex)|fortune hex]], or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/hexes/Misfortune (Hex)|misfortune hex]] caused by the witch has the duration of that hex extended by 1 round.
