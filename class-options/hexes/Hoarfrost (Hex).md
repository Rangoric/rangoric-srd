# Hoarfrost

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-major-hex|major hex]] (Su).*

The target is rimed with a shell of frost needles that slowly work their way into its flesh (Fortitude negates). The target turns pale and blue, and takes 1 point of Constitution damage per minute until it dies, saves (once per minute), or is cured. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|Break enchantment]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]*, and similar spells end the effect. If the target saves, it is immune to this hex for 1 day. This is a cold effect.
