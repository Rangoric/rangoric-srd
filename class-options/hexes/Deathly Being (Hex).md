# Deathly Being

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Bones Spirit|bones spirit]]._

If the shaman is a living creature, she has [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Negative Energy Affinity|negative energy affinity]]. If she's an undead creature or a creature with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Negative Energy Affinity|negative energy affinity]] ability, she gains a +1 bonus to her channel resistance. At 8th level, if she's a living creature she gains a +4 bonus on defenses against death effects and effects that drain energy, or if she's an undead creature her bonus to channel resistance increases to +2.

At 16th level, if the shaman is a living creature, she takes no penalties from energy drain effects, though she can still be killed if she accrues more negative levels than she has Hit Dice. Furthermore, after 24 hours any negative levels the shaman has are removed without their making a further attack against her Fortitude. If the shaman is an undead creature, her bonus to channel resistance increases to +4.
