# Speak in Dreams

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-major-hex|major hex]] (Sp).*

The witch can contact a creature as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dream|dream]]*. The witch can use this ability on a number of creatures per day equal to her Intelligence bonus, but can dream-speak to those creatures as often as desired throughout that time period.
