# Fearful Gaze

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Bones Spirit|bones spirit]]._

With a single shout, the shaman causes one target creature within 30 feet to become shaken for 1 round. It attacks Will, and a miss negates this effect. At 8th level, she makes the target frightened instead. At 16th level, she makes it panicked instead. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. A creature affected by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.
