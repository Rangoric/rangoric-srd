# Ward of Stone

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-hex|shaman hex]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Stone Spirit|stone spirit]].*

The shaman touches a willing creature (including herself) and grants a ward of stone. The next time the warded creature is struck with a melee attack, it is treated as if it has DR 5/adamantine. This ward lasts for 1 minute, after which it fades away if not already expended. At 8th and 16th levels, the ward lasts for one additional attack. Once this ward ends, the creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 24 hours.
