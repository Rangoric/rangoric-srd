# Good for what Ails you

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex)._

While raging, the barbarian who takes a drink of alcohol may make one of the following conditions that may be affecting her attack her again: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]], dazzled, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]]. If it misses, the effect is suppressed for the duration of the rage. She also may make a poison affecting her attack her again; a miss counts against those required for a cure, but a hit has no ill effect.
