# Chaos Totem, Lesser

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su)._

While raging, the barbarian gains a +1 deflection bonus to AC against the attacks of lawful creatures and a +1 resistance bonus on defenses against confusion, insanity, polymorph, and effects with the lawful descriptor. This bonus increases by +1 for each chaos totem rage power she possesses.
