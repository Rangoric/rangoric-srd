# Bestial Climber

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex)._

While raging, the barbarian gains a natural climb speed equal to her land speed.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Raging Climber (Rage Power)|raging climber]] rage power and be at least 6th level before selecting this rage power.
