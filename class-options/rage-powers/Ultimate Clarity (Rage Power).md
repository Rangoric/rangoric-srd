# Ultimate Clarity

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Rage Powers|rage power]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|barbarian]] and the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-rage-powers|skald]].*

Once per rage, the character can refine her senses, seeing through normal and magical darkness (including from magical effects such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]*), [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisibility]], and illusions; she can also discern the exact locations of creatures with [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]]. This lasts for 1 round.

A character must be at least 6th level and have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Moment of Clarity (Rage Power)|moment of clarity]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Perfect Clarity (Rage Power)|perfect clarity]] rage powers to select this rage power.

The character does not need to be using [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Moment of Clarity (Rage Power)|moment of clarity]] to use this rage power.
