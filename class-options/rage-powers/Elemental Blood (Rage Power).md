# Elemental Blood

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su)._

While raging, the barbarian gains energy resistance 10 against the energy type chosen when she took lesser elemental blood.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Elemental Blood, Lesser (Rage Power)|lesser elemental blood]] rage power and be at least 6th level before selecting this rage power.
