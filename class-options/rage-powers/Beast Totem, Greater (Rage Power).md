# Beast Totem, Greater

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su).*

While raging, the barbarian gains the pounce special ability, allowing her to make a full attack at the end of a charge. In addition, the damage from her claws increases to 1d8 (1d6 if Small) and the claws deal ×3 damage on a critical hit. A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Beast Totem (Rage Power)|beast totem]] rage power to select this rage power.

A barbarian must be at least 10th level to select this rage power.
