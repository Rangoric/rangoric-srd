# Regenerative Vigor

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex)._

After using her [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Renewed Vigor (Rage Power)|renewed vigor]] rage power until her current rage ends, the barbarian gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] 1 for every 6 barbarian levels she has (maximum fast healing 3). She regains hit points from fast healing at the start of each of her turns.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Renewed Vigor (Rage Power)|renewed vigor]] rage power and be at least 6th level before selecting this rage power.
