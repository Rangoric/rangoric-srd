# Lethal Accuracy

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

While in the accurate stance, the barbarian's critical multiplier for damage increases by 1 (a ×2 multiplier becomes ×3, a ×3 multiplier becomes ×4, and a ×4 multiplier becomes ×5). A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Accurate Stance (Rage Power)|accurate stance]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Deadly Accuracy (Rage Power)|deadly accuracy]] rage powers and be at least 16th level to select this rage power.
