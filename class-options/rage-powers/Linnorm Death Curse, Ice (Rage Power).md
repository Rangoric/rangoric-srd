# Linnorm Death Curse, Ice

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Rage Powers|rage power]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|barbarian]] and the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-rage-powers|skald]].*

The character channels the power of an ice linnorm. The character's melee attacks deal an additional 1 point of cold damage. If the character is knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] or killed, the attacker suffers the curse of frost.

A character must be at least 4th level to select this rage power.

*Curse of Frost*: *save* Will DC 10 + 1/2 character's level + character's Charisma bonus; *effect* target gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Vulnerabilities|vulnerability to cold]].
