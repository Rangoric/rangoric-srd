# Celestial Blood, Greater

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su)._

Once per rage, the barbarian can reroll one ability check or skill check she just made, or make an attack just rolled against her defense reroll. She must take the second result, even if it’s lower. She must decide to use this ability after the die is rolled but before the result is revealed.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Celestial Blood (Rage Power)|celestial blood]] rage power and be at least 10th level before selecting this rage power.
