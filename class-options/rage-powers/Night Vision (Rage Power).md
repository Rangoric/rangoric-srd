# Night Vision

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

The barbarian's senses grow incredibly sharp while raging and she gains darkvision 60 feet. A barbarian must have [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Low-Light Vision (Rage Power)|low-light vision]] as a rage power or a racial trait to select this rage power.
