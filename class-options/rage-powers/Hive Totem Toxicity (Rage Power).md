# Hive Totem Toxicity

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su)._

While raging, the barbarian increases her bite damage die type with the animal fury rage power by one die type and decreases the penalty on attack rolls with that bite to –2. Once per rage, a bite that hits can deliver an injury toxin that has a frequency of once per round for 4 rounds, deals 1d3 points of Constitution damage, and has a cure of 1 miss. It attacks Fortitude at 1/2 the barbarian’s level + the barbarian’s Constitution modifier.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Hive Totem (Rage Power)|hive totem]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Hive Totem Resilience (Rage Power)|hive totem resilience]] rage powers and be at least 8th level before selecting this rage power.
