# Terrifying Howl

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

The barbarian unleashes a terrifying howl as a standard action. All [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] enemies within 30 feet must make a Will save (DC equal to 10 + 1/2 the barbarian's level + the barbarian's Strength modifier) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for 1d4+1 rounds. Once an enemy has made a save versus terrifying howl (successful or not), it is immune to this power for 24 hours. A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Intimidating Glare (Rage Power)|intimidating glare]] rage power to select this rage power. A barbarian must be at least 8th level before selecting this power.
