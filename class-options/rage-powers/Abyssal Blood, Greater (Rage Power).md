# Abyssal Blood, Greater

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su)._

While raging, the barbarian gains resistance 5 to acid, cold, and fire.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Abyssal Blood (Rage Power)|abyssal blood]] rage power and be at least 10th level before selecting this rage power.
