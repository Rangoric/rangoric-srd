# Draconic Blood, Greater

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su)._

While raging, the barbarian gains a breath weapon that she can use once per day. For every 2 barbarian levels, this breath weapon deals 1d6 points of damage of the energy type she chose for the draconic blood power, either in a 30-foot cone (if cold or fire) or a 60-foot line (if acid or electricity). The breath attacks the Reflex of those caught in its area, and a miss halves the damage. The attack bonus is equal to 1/2 the barbarian’s level + her Constitution modifier.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Draconic Blood (Rage Power)|draconic blood]] rage power and be at least 10th level before selecting this rage power.
