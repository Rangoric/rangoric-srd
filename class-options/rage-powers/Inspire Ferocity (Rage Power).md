# Inspire Ferocity

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

While in the reckless stance, the barbarian imparts the stance's bonus and penalty to all willing allies within 30 feet of her. A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Reckless Stance (Rage Power)|reckless stance]] rage power to select this rage power.
