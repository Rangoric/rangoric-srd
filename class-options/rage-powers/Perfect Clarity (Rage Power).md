# Perfect Clarity

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex)._

While in the calm stance, the barbarian can roll twice for any miss chances, taking the better result, and illusions attacking her Will disbelief roll twice and take the worse result. A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Calm Stance (Rage Power)|calm stance]] rage power to select this rage power.
