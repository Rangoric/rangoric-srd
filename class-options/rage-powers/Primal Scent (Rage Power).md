# Primal Scent

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

When using her scent rage power, the barbarian adds half her barbarian level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks made to track by scent and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to pinpoint the location of creatures she cannot see. If she pinpoints the location of a creature that has total concealment, she treats it as having concealment.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Scent (Rage Power)|scent]] rage power and be at least 8th level before selecting this rage power.
