# Undead Blood

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su)._

While the barbarian is raging, her melee attacks are treated as if they were made with a weapon with the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]]_ weapon special ability.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Undead Blood, Lesser (Rage Power)|lesser undead blood]] rage power and be at least 6th level before selecting this rage power.
