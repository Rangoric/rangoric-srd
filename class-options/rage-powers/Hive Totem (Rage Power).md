# Hive Totem

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su).*

While raging, the barbarian takes half damage from attacks by swarms of vermin, including spells and effects that act like a swarm of vermin (like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Swarm|Summon Swarm]] used to summon spiders or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Insect Plague|Insect Plague]]). She also gains a +1 bonus on Strength ability checks and to CMD against bull rush, drag, and trip attacks for every four barbarian levels she has (maximum +5). A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Animal Fury (Rage Power)|animal fury]] power and be at least 4th level before selecting this rage power.
