# Ground Breaker

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

The barbarian can attack the floor around her as a full-round action. This attack automatically hits and deals damage normally. If the barbarian deals more damage than the floor's hardness, the space she occupies and all of the squares adjacent to her become difficult terrain. Creatures in those squares, except the barbarian, must succeed at DC 15 Reflex saves or be knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. A barbarian must be at least 6th level to select this rage power.
