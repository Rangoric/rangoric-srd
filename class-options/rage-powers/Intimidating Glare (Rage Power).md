# Intimidating Glare

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

The barbarian can make an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check against one adjacent foe as a move action. If the barbarian successfully demoralizes her opponent, the foe is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d4 rounds + 1 round for every 5 points by which the barbarian's check exceeds the DC.
