# Abyssal Blood

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su).*

Once per day when the barbarian enters a rage, she can choose to grow one size category larger than her base size (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*, even if the barbarian isn’t humanoid).

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Abyssal Blood, Lesser (Rage Power)|lesser abyssal blood]] rage power and be at least 6th level before selecting this rage power.
