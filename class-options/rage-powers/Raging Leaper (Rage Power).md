# Raging Leaper

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex)._

When raging, the barbarian adds her level as an enhancement bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks made to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|jump]]. When making a jump in this way, the barbarian is always considered to have a running start.
