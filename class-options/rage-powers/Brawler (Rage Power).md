# Brawler

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

While raging, the barbarian is treated as if she has [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]]. If she already has the feat, her unarmed strikes deal 1d6 points of damage (1d4 if she is Small).
