# Lindwurm Death Curse, Cairn

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Rage Powers|rage power]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|barbarian]] and the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-rage-powers|skald]]._

The character channels the power of a cairn lindwurm. The character's melee attacks deal an additional 1 point of negative energy damage. If the character is knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] or killed by an attack or spell, the attacker suffers the curse of decay (vs Will).

A character must be at least 8th level to select this rage power.

_Curse of Decay_: _attack_ vs Will at 1/2 character's level + character's Charisma bonus; _effect_ target takes 1 point of Con [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Ability Score Loss|damage]] per day and ages at a rate of 1 year per day (eventually incurring all of the penalties of old age but none of the bonuses).
