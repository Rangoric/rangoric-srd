# Eater of Magic

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Su).*

When the barbarian succeeds at a saving throw to resist a spell, supernatural ability, or spell-like ability, she gains temporary hit points equal to the spell's level (in the case of spells or spell-like abilities) or 1/2 the CR of the effect's creator (in the case of supernatural abilities). These temporary hit points disappear when the barbarian's rage ends, and they don't stack with other temporary hit points gained from this rage power. A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Superstition (Rage Power)|superstition]] rage power and be at least 10th level to select this rage power.
