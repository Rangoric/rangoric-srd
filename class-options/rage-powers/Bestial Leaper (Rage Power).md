# Bestial Leaper

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex)._

While raging, the barbarian can take a move action to move and can take her normal standard action at any point during the move.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Raging Leaper (Rage Power)|raging leaper]] rage power and be at least 6th level before selecting this rage power.
