# Renewed Life

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex)._

While raging, the barbarian ignores the effect of one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-level-save|temporary negative level]] per four barbarian levels she has (maximum 5). Once per day, when a barbarian with temporary negative levels ends her rage, as many of her temporary negative levels as she could ignore while raging are attacked again right away, as in the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-level-save|daily attack]], and each miss removes that negative level.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Renewed Vitality (Rage Power)|renewed vitality]] rage power and be at least 6th level before selecting this rage power.
