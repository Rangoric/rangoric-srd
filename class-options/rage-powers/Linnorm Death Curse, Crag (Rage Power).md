# Linnorm Death Curse, Crag

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Rage Powers|rage power]] (Su) of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|barbarian]] and the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-rage-powers|skald]]._

The character channels the power of a crag linnorm. The character's melee attacks deal an additional 1 point of fire damage. If the character is knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] or killed by an attack or spell, the attacker suffers the curse of fire (vs Will).

A character must be at least 4th level to select this rage power.

_Curse of Fire_: _attack_ vs Will at 1/2 character's level + character's Charisma bonus; _effect_ target gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Vulnerabilities|vulnerability to fire]].
