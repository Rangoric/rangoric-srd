# Sharpened Accuracy

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

While in the accurate stance, the barbarian ignores the miss chance for concealment and treats total concealment as concealment. She also ignores cover penalties except those from total cover. A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Accurate Stance (Rage Power)|accurate stance]] rage power and be at least 8th level to select this rage power.
