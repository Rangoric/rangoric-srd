# Roaring Drunk

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex)._

While raging, the barbarian gains a +1 morale bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks and to the attack bonus of any fear effects she creates for each alcoholic drink she has consumed during her rage, to a maximum of +1 per four barbarian levels.
