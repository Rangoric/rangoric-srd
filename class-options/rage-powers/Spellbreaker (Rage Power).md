# Spellbreaker

_A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]]._

When raging, the barbarian gains [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spellbreaker|Spellbreaker]] as a bonus feat.

A barbarian must have the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Disruptive (Rage Power)|disruptive]] rage power and be at least 12th level to select this rage power.
