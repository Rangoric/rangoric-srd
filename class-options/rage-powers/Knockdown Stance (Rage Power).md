# Knockdown Stance

*A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage-powers|rage power]] (Ex).*

The barbarian can focus on toppling her foes. Once per round, she can make a trip attack against one target in place of a melee attack. If she succeeds, the target is knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. This [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|trip]] attempt doesn't provoke an attack of opportunity. This is a stance rage power.
