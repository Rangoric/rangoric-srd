# Ranger Traps

The [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]]’s traps: simple improvised traps that sometimes incorporate the ranger’s magical abilities, one note each. Rangers are legendary for their ability to hunt down and capture their prey, and some can create elaborate traps out of scraps, vines, and perhaps a little bit of specialized magic. A character learns how to set them with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/ranger/Trapper (Archetype)|trapper]] ranger archetype, which says how many it knows and how often it can set them, or with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Learn Ranger Trap|Learn Ranger Trap]] feat. How traps work in general is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps|traps]].

## Setting a Ranger Trap

Setting a trap is a full-round action that provokes attacks of opportunity. A trap fills a single 5-foot square, and cannot be placed in the same area as another ranger trap or a magical trap such as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glyph of Warding|glyph of warding]]_. The ranger only needs simple materials to create the trap, such as a piece of cloth, a small amount of metal (such as a dagger, iron spike, or a few nails), a foot of rope or vine, and so on.

The DCs for [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks to notice the trap, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks to disable it, and saving throws to avoid it are equal to 10 + 1/2 the character’s level + the character’s Wisdom bonus. All ranger traps have a location trigger and no reset (they work once). ^ranger-traps-dc

Ranger traps are either extraordinary or supernatural; extraordinary traps are mechanical, and supernatural traps are magic. Some traps may be set as extraordinary traps or as magical (supernatural) traps; learning such a trap lets a ranger set it either way. An extraordinary trap has a –2 penalty to its DC. An extraordinary trap lasts 1 day per ranger level or until it is triggered, whichever comes first. A magical trap lasts only 1 hour per level or until triggered, whichever comes first. A trap’s DC decreases by 1 for each full day (or hour, for a magical trap) that passes after it is set. ^ranger-traps-duration

Add-on traps add to or modify the effects of another trap; a ranger can add one of them to any standard ranger trap (this costs 1 additional use of the trap ability). ^ranger-traps-add-on
