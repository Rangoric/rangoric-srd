# Freezing Trap

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps|ranger trap]] (Su; supernatural only)._

- **Type**: magic
- **Trigger**: location; **Reset**: none; [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-dc|DCs]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-duration|duration]] as for every ranger trap
- **Effect**: The trap creates a burst of ice that damages and encases the triggering creature. The creature takes 1d3 points of cold damage + 1/4 the ranger’s level and is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] and stuck to the ground, similar to the effect of a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tanglefoot Bag|tanglefoot bag]]. It attacks Reflex, and a miss halves the damage and means the creature is not stuck to the floor by the ice. The ice is 1 inch thick per 2 Hit Dice of the ranger (minimum 1), has hardness 0 and 3 hit points per inch of thickness, and melts in 2d4 rounds.
