# Tar Trap

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps|ranger trap]] (Ex or Su).*

- **Type**: mechanical or magic
- **Trigger**: location; **Reset**: none; [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-dc|DCs]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-duration|duration]] as for every ranger trap
- **Effect**: The target is coated in a thin layer of sticky tar unless it succeeds at a Reflex save. It is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]], and becomes susceptible to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|catching fire]] from any source of flame. If lit on fire, the tar burns intensely for 1 round; it deals 2d6 points of fire damage and is destroyed in the process. Unless burned away, the tar remains for 1 round per ranger level. If this is an extraordinary trap, the ranger must supply a dose of tar.
