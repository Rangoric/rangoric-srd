# Burning Trap

_An add-on [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps|ranger trap]] (Ex or Su): set with another ranger trap, for 1 more use of the trap ability._

- **Type**: mechanical or magic
- **Trigger**: location; **Reset**: none; [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-dc|DCs]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-duration|duration]] as for every ranger trap
- **Effect**: A ranger can only add this to a [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Fire Trap (Ranger Trap)|fire trap]]. If the fire trap hits the triggering creature’s Reflex, it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|catches on fire]], taking 1d6 points of fire damage at the start of its turn for 1d4 rounds. As a full-round action, the burning creature can make the fire attack its Reflex again, and a miss extinguishes the flames. Dropping and rolling on the ground grants a +4 bonus to its Reflex Defense against this attack.
