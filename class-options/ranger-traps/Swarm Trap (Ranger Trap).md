# Swarm Trap

_A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps|ranger trap]] (Ex or Su)._

- **Type**: mechanical or magic
- **Trigger**: location; **Reset**: none; [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-dc|DCs]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-duration|duration]] as for every ranger trap
- **Effect**: The trap releases a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/swarm/Bat Swarm|bat swarm]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/swarm/Rat Swarm|rat swarm]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spider/Spider Swarm|spider swarm]] that attacks all creatures in the area. The swarm remains in the general area for no longer than 1 round per ranger level, after which it disperses. If the trap is an extraordinary trap, the ranger must provide the creatures of the swarm (typically in a small box, cage, or crate); these creatures may die of starvation before the trap is triggered (an extraordinary trap lasts 1 day per level).
