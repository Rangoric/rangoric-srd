# Poison Trap

*A [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps|ranger trap]] (Ex or Su).*

- **Type**: mechanical or magic
- **Trigger**: location; **Reset**: none; [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-dc|DCs]] and [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ranger-traps/Ranger Traps#^ranger-traps-duration|duration]] as for every ranger trap
- **Effect**: The trap poisons the creature that triggers it. If it is a supernatural trap, the poison deals 1d2 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|Con damage]] per round for 6 rounds. If it is an extraordinary trap, the ranger must provide 1 dose of contact, inhaled, or injury [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]] when setting the trap, and the trap uses that poison’s effects and DC.
