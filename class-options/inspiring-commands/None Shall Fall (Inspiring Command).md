# None Shall Fall

_A [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Battle Herald#^battle-herald-inspiring-command|battle herald inspiring command]] (Ex); it affects only a certain number of allies._

A number of allies equal to the battle herald's inspiring command bonus heal 1d6 points of damage. Targeted allies who are poisoned may make the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]] attack them again, with a bonus to their defense equal to the battle herald's inspiring command bonus; a hit has no additional effect, but a miss counts toward curing the poison. This healing is not positive energy and can heal undead creatures.
