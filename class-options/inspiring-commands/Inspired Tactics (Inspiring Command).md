# Inspired Tactics

_A [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Battle Herald#^battle-herald-inspiring-command|battle herald inspiring command]] (Ex)._

Allies apply the battle herald's inspiring command bonus on critical hit confirmation rolls, on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] checks, and as a dodge bonus to AC against any [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] provoked by combat maneuvers.
