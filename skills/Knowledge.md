# Knowledge

The Knowledge skills (the [[rangoric-srd/Tag Register|tag]] *knowledge*) each cover a body of lore, possibly an academic or even scientific discipline:

- Core skills: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Psionics (Skill)|Psionics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Tactics|Tactics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]].
- Background skill: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]], one narrow subject per skill.

These rules apply to every Knowledge skill.

**Check**: Answering a question within your field of study has a DC of 10 (for really easy questions), 15 (for basic questions), or 20 to 30 (for really tough questions).

You can also use a Knowledge skill to identify creatures (see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|Identifying Creatures]]) and, with a Magic skill, magic (see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Identifying Magic]]).

**Action**: Usually none. In most cases, making a Knowledge skill check doesn’t take an action—you simply know the answer or you don’t.

**Try Again**: No. The check represents what you know, and thinking about a topic a second time doesn’t let you know something that you never learned in the first place.

**Untrained**: An untrained Knowledge skill check is simply an Intelligence check. Without actual training, you know only common knowledge (DC 10 or lower).
