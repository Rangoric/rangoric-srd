# Tactics

Tactics covers how forces fight: formations and maneuvers, ambushes, sieges and defenses, the ranks and organization of armies and other fighting forces, how commanders think, and famous battles and what they teach. It came from Modern's Knowledge (tactics) (2026-10-31). It is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]], so its checks work as described there.

Building and working siege engines is [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]; Tactics is knowing where to put them.

**Unlocks**: Tactics uses the Knowledge skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge#Unlocks|Knowledge]]).
