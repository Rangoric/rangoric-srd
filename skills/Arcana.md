# Arcana

Arcana covers ancient mysteries, magic traditions, arcane symbols, cryptic phrases, arcane magic, magical constructs, dragons and magical beasts. It is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]], so its checks work as described there.

**Identifying Creatures**: Arcana identifies magical constructs, dragons and magical beasts; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|Identifying Creatures]].

**Identifying Magic**: Identifying an arcane spell or effect is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] check; Arcana is what you know about arcane magic and its traditions.

**Unlocks**: Arcana uses the Knowledge skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge#Unlocks|Knowledge]]).

## Occult Unlocks

Occult skill unlocks need the ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spells]] or the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] feat; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^occult-unlocks|Occult Skill Unlocks]] for who can use them and how often.

### Phrenology

You examine the skulls of intelligent creatures to analyze their psychological attributes, since the shape of the brain influences the shape of the skull.

**Items that help**: a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Phrenologist's Kit|phrenologist's kit]].

**Check**: Once per day, you can use your fingertips to examine the shape of a creature’s skull. Reading a skull takes 1 minute of concentration and scrutiny while you handle it. You can do this to a willing, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] or paralyzed creature, or to decomposed remains (as long as the skull is intact or can be pieced together). It doesn’t work on creatures without discernible skulls, such as constructs, elementals, oozes or plants. After the minute, the GM attempts a secret Arcana check, against a DC usually modified by the subject’s Hit Dice. With one check, you learn everything whose DC you meet: for instance, with a result of 22 against a creature with 2 HD, you learn its race and age, gender, alignment and class, but not its level or HD.

| Task                   | DC                   |
| ---------------------- | -------------------- |
| Determine race and age | 10                   |
| Determine gender       | 15                   |
| Determine alignment    | 15 + creature’s HD\* |
| Determine class        | 20 + creature’s HD\* |
| Determine level or HD  | 25 + creature’s HD\* |

\* A dead creature’s skull uses the HD it had when alive.

**Action**: Reading a skull takes 1 minute of uninterrupted study.

**Try Again**: Yes. Reexamining a skull may give new insights, but you can attempt only one such check per day.
