# Psychic

Psychic covers psychic magic and the occult: psychic traditions, thoughts and emotions, auras, the mind and its defenses, spirits and the soul. It is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]], so its checks work as described there. It is kept apart from [[rangoric-srd/skills/Psionics (Skill)|Psionics]], the Knowledge skill for psionics.

**Identifying Magic**: Identifying a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spell]] or effect is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] check; Psychic is what you know about psychic magic and its traditions.

**Unlocks**: Psychic uses the Knowledge skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge#Unlocks|Knowledge]]).
