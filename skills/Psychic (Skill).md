# Psychic

Psychic covers psychic magic and the occult: psychic traditions, thoughts and emotions, auras, the mind and its defenses, spirits and the soul. It is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]], so its checks work as described there. It is kept apart from [[rangoric-srd/skills/Psionics (Skill)|Psionics]], the skill for psionic powers.

**Magic Skill**: Psychic is the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] for [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spells]]: those cast by psychic spellcasters. Identifying psychic spells as they are cast, or spells already in place, is in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Identifying Magic]].

**Unlocks**: Psychic uses the Knowledge skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge#Unlocks|Knowledge]]) and the Magic skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic#Unlocks|Identifying Magic]]).
