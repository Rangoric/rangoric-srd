# Piloting

Piloting is steering and navigating vehicles, from wagons and sailing ships to cars, aircraft and starships. It came from Modern’s Drive, Pilot and Navigate and Starfinder’s Piloting (2026-10-31). Handling the animals that pull a vehicle is [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]], and finding your way on foot is [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]].

Rules for specific vehicles and starships come later; until then the GM sets the DCs for maneuvers.

## Driving and Flying

**Check**: Ordinary driving, sailing or flying needs no check. You make one in bad conditions (storms, ice, heavy seas), when you’re being chased or attacked, when time is short, or when you try a maneuver or stunt: something complex done quickly or in a tight space.

**Action**: A move action.

**Try Again**: Usually not; a failed check tends to have consequences.

**Special**: You can take 10, but not 20.

## Navigating

**Check**: You can plot a course for a ship, aircraft or other vehicle over a long distance, by charts, instruments or the stars. The DC depends on the length of the trip. If you succeed, you travel by the best reasonable course. If you fail, you still arrive, but it takes twice as long. If you fail by more than 5, you travel the expected time but only get halfway before you realize you’re lost.

| Length of Trip | DC |
| --- | --- |
| Short (a few hours) | 20 |
| Moderate (a day or two) | 22 |
| Long (up to a week) | 25 |
| Extreme (more than a week) | 28 |

A lost navigator can make a DC 20 check to find the way again. On a success, the trip takes twice the normal time in all; on a failure, half a day is lost before you can try again.

With a clear view of the night sky, you can work out your position by the stars with a DC 15 check.

Plotting a course between worlds or star systems uses how familiar the destination is instead: DC 10 for one you visit often, 15 for one you’ve seldom visited, and 25 for one you don’t know. The GM can change these by 5 to 10 for what you know about the route and how hard it is.

**Action**: A full-round action for a check on the way; plotting a long course takes at least 10 minutes.

**Special**: You can take 10. You can take 20 only to work out your position, not when traveling.
