# Theology

Theology covers gods and goddesses, mythic history, ecclesiastic tradition, holy symbols, divine magic, the Inner and Outer Planes, the Astral and Ethereal Planes, undead, outsiders, elementals, and magic related to the planes. It is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]], so its checks work as described there.

**Identifying Creatures**: Theology identifies undead, outsiders and elementals; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|Identifying Creatures]].

**Identifying Magic**: Identifying a divine spell or effect is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] check; Theology is what you know about the gods, their magic and their traditions.

**Unlocks**: Theology uses the Knowledge skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge#Unlocks|Knowledge]]).
