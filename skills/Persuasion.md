# Persuasion

Persuasion covers changing people's attitudes and finding things out by talking to them. It joins Diplomacy and Gather Information.

## Influencing Attitudes

**Check**: You can change the attitudes of others (nonplayer characters) with a successful Persuasion check; see the Influencing NPC Attitudes sidebar, below, for basic DCs. If your result is 5 or more below the DC to improve the creature’s attitude by one step, its attitude gets one step worse instead; this takes the place of the "Less than" entries in the table, except for a helpful creature. An attitude changed this way usually lasts 1d4 hours, though it can last much longer or shorter depending on the situation.

You can’t influence a creature that doesn’t understand you or has an Intelligence of 3 or less. Persuasion is generally ineffective in combat, and against creatures that intend to harm you or your allies in the immediate future.

In negotiations, participants roll opposed Persuasion checks, and the winner gains the advantage. Opposed checks also resolve situations when two advocates or diplomats plead opposite cases in a hearing before a third party.

**Action**: Changing others’ attitudes with Persuasion generally takes at least 1 full minute (10 consecutive full-round actions). In some situations, this time requirement may greatly increase. A rushed Persuasion check can be made as a full-round action, but you take a –10 penalty on the check.

**Try Again**: You can’t use Persuasion to influence a given creature’s attitude more than once in 24 hours. If a request is refused, more checks don’t change the answer, though other requests can be made.

### Advanced Uses

The character can turn a person into a fanatic follower. The attitude tables above include it.  

*Fanatic*: The attitude of fanatic is added to the tables above. In addition to the obvious effects, any NPC whose attitude is fanatic gains a +2 morale bonus to Strength and Constitution scores, a +1 morale bonus on Will saves, and a -1 penalty to AC whenever fighting for the character or his or her cause. This attitude will remain for one day plus one day per point of the character’s Charisma bonus, at which point the NPC’s attitude will revert to its original attitude (or indifferent, if no attitude is specified).

Treat the fanatic attitude as a mind-affecting enchantment effect for purposes of immunity, save bonuses, or being detected by the Sense Motive skill. Since it is nonmagical, it can’t be dispelled; however, any effect that suppresses or counters mind-affecting effects will affect it normally. A fanatic NPC’s attitude can’t be further adjusted by the use of skills.  

## Gathering Information

**Check**: An evening’s time, a few gold pieces for buying drinks and making friends, and a DC 10 Persuasion check get you a general idea of a city’s major news items, assuming there are no obvious reasons why the information would be withheld. The higher your check result, the better the information.

If you want to find out about a specific rumor, or a specific item, or obtain a map, or do something else along those lines, the DC for the check is 15 to 25, or even higher.

**Action**: A typical Persuasion check takes 1d4+1 hours.

**Try Again**: Yes, but it takes time for each check. Furthermore, you may draw attention to yourself if you repeatedly pursue a certain type of information.

### Advanced Uses

The character can gather information without eliciting suspicion.  

| Task            | Check Modifier |
| --------------- | -------------- |
| Avoid suspicion | -20           |

*Avoid Suspicion*: By accepting a -20 penalty on his or her Persuasion check, a character can avoid any suspicions that might otherwise be aroused by someone pursuing sensitive information.

## Unlocks

You gain these only through [[rangoric-srd/feats/Signature Skill|Signature Skill]] or a class feature such as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-edge|rogue’s edge]]; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^skill-unlocks|Skill Unlocks]].

**5 Ranks**: The time required to influence a creature's attitude or gather information is halved.

**10 Ranks**: You can attempt to adjust a creature's attitude in 1 round by taking a –10 penalty. If you take 1 minute to adjust a creature's attitude, add your Charisma bonus to the number of hours that attitude change persists.

**15 Ranks**: You can attempt to adjust a creature's attitude in 1 round with no penalty. If you take 1 minute to adjust a creature's attitude, the duration of the resulting change is measured in days, not hours. You can gather information in 10 minutes by taking a –5 penalty.

**20 Ranks**: You can attempt to adjust a creature's attitude in 1 round with no penalty. If you take 1 minute to adjust a creature's attitude, the duration of the resulting change is measured in weeks, not hours. You can gather information in 1d4 minutes with no penalty.
