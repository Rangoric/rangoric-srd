# Perception

Perception replaces Spot and Listen: it covers noticing things by sight and by hearing. Finding concealed or secret doors, traps and other small details is still [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]].

**Check**: Perception is used primarily to notice characters or creatures who are trying not to be noticed. Your Perception check is opposed by the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] check of the creature trying not to be seen or heard; one Perception check against one Stealth check covers both. Sometimes a creature isn’t intentionally hiding but is still difficult to see or hear, so a successful Perception check is necessary to notice it.

Perception can also make you aware of an [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] creature near you, though you can’t actually see it.

Perception is also used to detect someone in disguise (see the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] skill), and to read lips when you can’t hear or understand what someone is saying.

Perception checks may be called for to determine the distance at which an encounter begins.

*Hearing*: To hear a noise, your Perception check is made against a DC that reflects how quiet the noise is, or it is opposed by the Stealth check of someone trying to be quiet.

| Perception DC | Sound                                                                                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| –10           | A battle                                                                                     |
| 0             | People talking (1)                                                                           |
| 5             | A person in medium armor walking at a slow pace (10 ft./round) trying not to make any noise  |
| 10            | An unarmored person walking at a slow pace (15 ft./round) trying not to make any noise       |
| 15            | A 1st-level rogue using Stealth to sneak past the listener                                   |
| 15            | People whispering (1)                                                                        |
| 19            | A cat stalking                                                                               |
| 30            | An owl gliding in for a kill                                                                 |

1 *If you beat the DC by 10 or more, you can make out what’s being said, assuming that you understand the language.*

In the case of people trying to be quiet, the DCs given on the table could be replaced by Stealth checks, in which case the indicated DC would be their average check result.

*Modifiers*: These apply to every Perception check, by sight or by hearing, unless noted.

| Condition                         | Penalty |
| --------------------------------- | ------- |
| Per 10 feet of distance           | –1      |
| Distracted (not being observant)  | –5      |
| Through a door (hearing)          | –5      |
| Through a stone wall (hearing)    | –15     |
| Asleep (hearing)                  | –10     |

*Read Lips*: To understand what someone is saying by reading lips, you must be within 30 feet of the speaker, be able to see him or her speak, and understand the speaker’s language. (This use of the skill is language-dependent.) The base DC is 15, but it increases for complex speech or an inarticulate speaker. You must maintain a line of sight to the lips being read.

If your Perception check succeeds, you can understand the general content of a minute’s worth of speaking, but you usually still miss certain details. If the check fails by 4 or less, you can’t read the speaker’s lips. If the check fails by 5 or more, you draw some incorrect conclusion about the speech. The check is rolled secretly in this case, so that you don’t know whether you succeeded or missed by 5.

**Action**: Varies. Every time you have a chance to notice something in a reactive manner (such as when someone makes a noise or you move into a new area), you can make a Perception check without using an action. Trying to notice something you failed to see or hear previously is a move action. To read lips, you must concentrate for a full minute before making a Perception check, and you can’t perform any other action (other than moving at up to half speed) during this minute.

**Try Again**: Yes. You can try to notice something that you failed to see or hear previously at no penalty. You can attempt to read lips once per minute.

**Special**: When several characters are listening to the same thing, a single 1d20 roll can be used for all the individuals’ Perception checks.

Being [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] makes Perception checks made as reactions harder.

A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|ranger]] gains a bonus on Perception checks when using this skill against a favored enemy.

Some races are better at noticing things; see the [[rangoric-srd/rules/PC Races|race]] for its racial bonuses.

The masters of some [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiars]] gain a bonus on Perception checks.

A sleeping character may make Perception checks to hear things (with the asleep penalty above). A successful check awakens the sleeper.

## Advanced Uses

The character can pinpoint the location of an invisible creature, or detect an illusion with a visual or auditory component.  

| Task                                                     | DC |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -- |
| Defeat illusion                                          | 80 |

*Invisible Creatures*: A high enough Perception check can reveal an [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] creature and even pinpoint where it is; the DCs are with invisibility.

*Defeat Illusion*: The character can automatically detect any illusion with a visual or auditory component for what it truly is. No Will save is required, and the character doesn’t have to interact with the illusion (but he or she must be able to see it or hear its auditory component).

*Using Perception to Read Lips*

| Task                                       | DC Modifier |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| Read lips while moving at up to full speed | +20        |
| Pronounce unfamiliar language              | +20        |

*Pronounce Unfamiliar Language*: This use of the skill allows a character to repeat the speech of an observed creature, potentially allowing a comrade to translate the speech. It doesn’t grant the character any ability to understand the language spoken.

## Unlocks

You gain these only through [[rangoric-srd/feats/Signature Skill|Signature Skill]] or a class feature such as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-edge|rogue’s edge]]; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^skill-unlocks|Skill Unlocks]].

**5 Ranks**: You remain alert to sounds even in your sleep, and the normal DC increase to Perception checks when you are sleeping is halved. The distance modifier on the DC of Perception checks you attempt is reduced to +1 per 20 feet.

**10 Ranks**: The distance modifier on the DC of Perception checks you attempt is reduced to +1 per 30 feet. In addition, you gain a +5 bonus on Perception checks to notice or locate an invisible creature or object.

**15 Ranks**: You remain alert to sounds even in your sleep, and the normal DC increase to Perception checks when you are sleeping doesn't apply to you. The distance modifier on the DC of your Perception checks is reduced to +1 per 40 feet.

**20 Ranks**: You gain a +10 bonus on Perception checks to notice invisible creatures or objects. The distance modifier on the DC of Perception checks you attempt is reduced to +1 per 60 feet.
