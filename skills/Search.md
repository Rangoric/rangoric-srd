# Search

> [!note] Replaced by [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]]
> Search is now Investigation, which also analyzes clues and does research (2026-10-31; see [[Working Folder/Done/plans/New Skills from Modern and Starfinder|New Skills from Modern and Starfinder]]). The text below is kept for reference.

## Text before the rename

**Check**: You generally must be within 10 feet of the object or surface to be searched. The table below gives DCs for typical tasks involving the Search skill.

| Task                                                | Search DC                               |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Ransack a chest full of junk to find a certain item | 10                                      |
| Notice a typical secret door or a simple trap       | 20                                      |
| Find a difficult nonmagical trap (rogue only) (1)   | 21 or higher                            |
| Find a magic trap (rogue only) (1)                  | 25 + level of spell used to create trap |
| Notice a well-hidden secret door                    | 30                                      |
| Find a footprint                                    | Varies (2)                              |
| Sense magic | 60 |

1 *Dwarves can also find traps built into or out of stone; see the dwarf’s [[rangoric-srd/rules/PC Races#^dwarf-stonecunning|stonecunning]].*

2 *A successful Search check can find a footprint or similar sign of a creature’s passage,  
but it won’t let you find or follow a trail. See the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Track|Track]] feat for the appropriate DC.*

**Action**: It takes a full-round action to search a 5-foot-by-5-foot area or a volume of goods 5 feet on a side.

**Special**: Elves and half-elves are better at Search checks, and an elf can notice a secret door just by passing near it; see the [[rangoric-srd/rules/PC Races#Elves|elf]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/PC Races#Half-Elves|half-elf]].

The spells explosive runes, fire trap, glyph of warding, symbol, and teleportation circle create magic traps that a character with trapfinding can find by making a successful Search check and then can attempt to disarm by using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]. Identifying the location of a snare spell has a DC of 23. Spike growth and spike stones create magic traps that can be found using Search, but against which Engineering checks do not succeed. See the individual spell descriptions for details.

Active abjuration spells within 10 feet of each other for 24 hours or more create barely visible energy fluctuations. These fluctuations give you a +4 bonus on Search checks to locate such abjuration spells.

**Restriction**: Anyone can use Search to find simple traps, but finding harder ones takes [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]]. (Exception: The spell find traps temporarily enables a cleric to use the Search skill as if he were a rogue.)

A dwarf, even one without trapfinding, can use the Search skill to find difficult traps built into or out of stone; see the dwarf’s [[rangoric-srd/rules/PC Races#^dwarf-stonecunning|stonecunning]].

## Advanced Uses

The character can sense the presence of magic in an area.

*Sense Magic*: The character senses the presence of any active magical effects in the area being searched. The character can’t determine the number, strength, or type of the effects.
