# Society

Society covers people and how they live, now and in the past. It took over from History (2026-10-31) and added Modern's society fields and Starfinder's Culture:

- **Peoples and cultures**: customs, traditions, legends, religion as people practice it, and inhabitants and personalities.
- **Law and government**: laws, legal rights and duties, rulers, political and governmental bodies and how they work.
- **Nobility and lineage**: royalty, noble houses, lineages, heraldry, family trees and mottoes.
- **The past**: wars, colonies, migrations, the founding of cities, archaeology and antiquities.
- **Lands and settlements**: lands and their borders, cities, and what each is known for. Terrain and climate are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]].
- **Trade and business**: guilds, merchants, business practices and how to get things done through officials.
- **The street**: local gossip, the underworld and its personalities, and in modern settings current events and popular culture.

It is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]], so its checks work as described there. Reading unfamiliar writing is [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]].

**Identifying Creatures**: Society identifies humanoids; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|Identifying Creatures]].

**Unlocks**: Society uses the Knowledge skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge#Unlocks|Knowledge]]).
