# Stealth

Stealth replaces Hide and Move Silently: it covers staying unseen and staying unheard.

**Check**: Your Stealth check is opposed by the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check of anyone who might see or hear you; one Stealth check against one Perception check covers both. You can move up to one-half your normal speed and use Stealth at no penalty. When moving at a speed greater than one-half but less than your normal speed, you take a –5 penalty. It’s practically impossible (–20 penalty) to use Stealth while attacking, running or charging.

A creature larger or smaller than Medium takes a size bonus or penalty on Stealth checks depending on its size category: Fine +16, Diminutive +12, Tiny +8, Small +4, Large –4, Huge –8, Gargantuan –12, Colossal –16.

You need cover or concealment in order to attempt a Stealth check against someone who could see you. Total cover or total concealment keeps you from being seen, but anyone nearby may still hear you, so you still make a Stealth check against them.

If people are observing you, even casually, you can’t use Stealth against them. You can run around a corner or behind cover so that you’re out of sight and then use Stealth, but the others then know at least where you went. If your observers are momentarily distracted (such as by a Deception check; see below), though, you can attempt to use Stealth. While the others turn their attention from you, you can attempt a Stealth check if you can get to a hiding place of some kind. (As a general guideline, the hiding place has to be within 1 foot per rank you have in Stealth.) This check, however, is made at a –10 penalty because you have to move fast.

Noisy surfaces, such as bogs or undergrowth, are tough to move silently across. When you try to sneak across such a surface, you take a penalty on your Stealth check as indicated below.

| Surface                                                       | Check Modifier |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Noisy (scree, shallow or deep bog, undergrowth, dense rubble) | –2             |
| Very noisy (dense undergrowth, deep snow)                     | –5             |

*Sniping*: If you’ve already successfully used Stealth at least 10 feet from your target, you can make one ranged attack, then immediately use Stealth again. You take a –20 penalty on your Stealth check to conceal yourself after the shot.

*Creating a Diversion to Hide*: You can use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] to help you hide. A successful Deception check can give you the momentary diversion you need to attempt a Stealth check while people are aware of you.

**Action**: Usually none. Normally, you make a Stealth check as part of movement or other activity, so it doesn’t take a separate action. However, using Stealth immediately after a ranged attack (see Sniping, above) is a move action.

**Special**: Being [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] gives you a large bonus on Stealth checks.

Higher-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-camouflage|rangers]] can use Stealth in natural terrain without cover or concealment, and later even while observed.

Some races are better at staying hidden; see the [[rangoric-srd/rules/PC Races|race]] for its racial bonuses.

The masters of some [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiars]] gain a bonus on Stealth checks.

## Advanced Uses

The character can hide others as well as hiding him or herself.  

| Task         | Check Modifier |
| ------------ | -------------- |
| Hide another | -30           |

*Hide Another*: By accepting a -30 penalty on his or her Stealth check, a character can hide another adjacent creature whose size is no more than one category larger than the character’s own. Modifiers to the check for the size of the creature still apply, as do all other penalties, including those for moving faster than half speed. Likewise, a character can only hide another creature when it is not under direct observation by a third party. The creature the character hides remains hidden until it is spotted or it takes some other action that breaks its concealment, as normal.

## Unlocks

You gain these only through [[rangoric-srd/feats/Signature Skill|Signature Skill]] or a class feature such as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-edge|rogue’s edge]]; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^skill-unlocks|Skill Unlocks]].

**5 Ranks**: Reduce the Stealth penalty from sniping by 10.

**10 Ranks**: Stealth check penalties for moving quickly are halved, including the ability unlocked at 5 ranks, moving full speed, and reaching concealment after creating a distraction.

**15 Ranks**: If you attack after successfully using Stealth, your target is denied its Dexterity bonus against all attacks that you make before the end of your turn (this overlaps the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Owl Style|Owl Style]] feat).

**20 Ranks**: If you attack after successfully using Stealth, your target is denied its Dexterity bonus against all attacks that you make before the beginning of your next turn.
