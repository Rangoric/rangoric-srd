# Lore

Lore covers one narrow subject in depth: a single city, a noble house and its heraldry, a guild, a sport, a kind of wine, one kind of creature. Each subject is its own skill, written with the subject in brackets, such as Lore (heraldry).

A Lore subject has to be narrow, far narrower than the Knowledge skill it sits inside. A good test is whether one person could know nearly everything about it. Heraldry, one city, one guild or one sport are narrow enough; cities, monsters or magic are not. A broad field such as [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] covers a Lore subject and much more besides. Lore is how a character with only a spare point or two can still be the one who knows about it.

**Check**: Lore checks work like any Knowledge check; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] for the DCs, the action, trying again and untrained checks. Most questions about a narrow subject don’t get harder as you go up in level.

A Lore about one kind of creature (dragons, undead, the giants of one range) can be used to identify creatures of that kind, as a Knowledge skill can.

**Example Topics**:

- *Lore (fey)*: fey creatures and their courts, customs, bargains and weaknesses, the fey realm, and the places where it touches the world. A DC 15 check tells you whether a place is apt to be used as a crossing into the fey realm. It identifies fey, as Nature does.
- *Lore (astrology)*: the movements of the stars and constellations, the seasons in which each star rises and sets, birth signs, and reading the sky for good and bad days. It's what the astrologers of [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|star magic]] study.
- *Lore (folk tales)*: the folklore and myths of one people or region, and where its uncommon place names come from.

**Special**: Lore is a Background skill and a Knowledge skill (see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^core-and-background|Core and Background Skills]]), so it can be bought with background points, and it counts wherever a rule says "any Knowledge skill".

**Unlocks**: Lore uses the Knowledge skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge#Unlocks|Knowledge]]).
