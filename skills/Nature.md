# Nature

Nature covers the natural world, living or not:

- **Living things**: animals, fey, giants, monstrous humanoids, plants and vermin.
- **Weather and seasons**: seasons and cycles, weather and climate.
- **The land**: terrain, rocks, soil, minerals, earthquakes and volcanoes.
- **Waters**: rivers, lakes, tides and the sea.
- **The sky**: the sun, moons, stars and their movements, and the calendars that follow them.
- **Nature magic.**

In settings with science, Nature is the science of living things and of the earth and sky: biology, geology, meteorology, oceanography and astronomy. Physics, chemistry and materials are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]].

It is a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]], so its checks work as described there.

**Identifying Creatures**: Nature identifies animals, fey, giants, monstrous humanoids, plants and vermin; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|Identifying Creatures]].

**Magic Skill**: Nature is the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] for divine spells that come from nature: those of druids and rangers; see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Identifying Magic]].

**Unlocks**: Nature uses the Knowledge skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge#Unlocks|Knowledge]]) and the Magic skills’ unlocks (in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic#Unlocks|Identifying Magic]]).
