# Appraise

**Check**: You can appraise common or well-known objects with a DC 12 Appraise check. Failure means that you estimate the value at 50% to 150% (2d6+3 times 10%) of its actual value.

Appraising a rare or exotic item requires a successful check against DC 15, 20, or higher. If the check is successful, you estimate the value correctly; failure means you cannot estimate the item’s value.

A [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services|magnifying glass]] helps with Appraise checks on small or highly detailed items, such as a gem, and a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services|merchant’s scale]] helps with items valued by weight, including anything made of precious metals. These bonuses stack.

**Action**: Appraising an item takes 1 minute (ten consecutive full-round actions).

**Try Again**: No. You cannot try again on the same object, regardless of success.

**Special**: Dwarves are better at appraising items made of stone or metal; see the [[rangoric-srd/rules/PC Races#Dwarves|dwarf]].

The master of a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|raven familiar]] gains a bonus on Appraise checks.

**Untrained**: For common items, failure on an untrained check means no estimate. For rare items, success means an estimate of 50% to 150% (2d6+3 times 10%).

## Advanced Uses

The character can sense magical auras in objects.  

| Task         | DC |
| ------------ | -- |
| Detect magic | 50 |

*Detect Magic*: The character can sense if an item has a magical aura. He or she can then use Magic skill to learn more about the item as if he or she had already cast detect magic on the item. This requires a full-round action.
