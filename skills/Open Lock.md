# Open Lock

> [!note] Replaced by [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]]
> Open Lock joined Sleight of Hand (2026-10-22; see [[Working Folder/Done/plans/Skill List Rework|Skill List Rework]]). The original text is kept below for reference.

## Original text

Without a set of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services|thieves’ tools]] an Open Lock check is harder, even if a simple tool is employed; masterwork thieves’ tools make it easier.

**Check**: The DC for opening a lock varies from 20 to 40, depending on the quality of the lock, as given on the table below.

| Lock             | DC | Lock         | DC |
| ---------------- | -- | ------------ | -- |
| Very simple lock | 20 | Good lock    | 30 |
| Average lock     | 25 | Amazing lock | 40 |

**Action**: Opening a lock is a full-round action.

**Special**: The [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Fingers|Nimble Fingers]] feat gives a bonus on Open Lock checks.

**Untrained**: You cannot pick locks untrained, but you might successfully force them open.

### Advanced Uses

The character can open locks more quickly than normal.  

| Open lock as . . .     | DC Modifier |
| ---------------------- | ----------- |
| Move-equivalent action | +20        |
| Free action            | +50        |
