# Ghost Wolf

_Conjuration (Creation)_

- **Level**: Blr 4, Psy 4, Sor/Wiz 4, Sum 2, SumU 2
- **Components**: V, S, F (dire wolf tooth)
- **Casting Time**: 10 minutes
- **Range**: 0 ft.
- **Target**: One quasi-real wolflike creature
- **Duration**: 1 hour/level (D) or 1 round/level; see text
- **Defense**: None (see description)
- **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell conjures a Large, quasi-real, wolflike creature made of roiling black smoke. It functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantom Steed|phantom steed]]_, except as noted above. In addition, the creature radiates an aura of fear. Any creature with fewer than 6 Hit Dice within 30 feet (except the ghost wolf‘s rider) is attacked against Will, and shaken for 1d4 rounds on a hit (this is a mind-affecting fear effect). A creature the aura misses is unaffected by the steed's fear aura for 24 hours.

The ghost wolf may also be used in combat. Once per round, the rider may direct the ghost wolf to attack in battle as a free action (bite +10, 1d8+6 points of damage); unlike an animal mount, this does not require a Animal Handling check or any training. Once the ghost wolf attacks, it lasts for only 1 round per level thereafter.
