# Grease

_Conjuration (Creation)_

- **Level**: Brd 1, Mag 1, Mes 1, Skd 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Sum 1, SumU 1
- **Components**: V, S, M
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)

Target or Area: One object or a 10-ft. square  
Duration: 1 round/level (D)  
Defense: See text  
Spell Resistance: No

A grease spell covers a solid surface with a layer of slippery grease. The spell attacks the Reflex of any creature in the area when it is cast, and on a hit the creature falls. The spell attacks again on your turn each round that the creature remains within the area. A creature can walk within or through the area of grease at half normal speed with a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check. Failure means it can’t move that round (and the spell then attacks its Reflex, making it fall on a hit), while failure by 5 or more means it falls (see the Acrobatics skill for details).

The spell can also be used to create a greasy coating on an item. Material objects not in use are always affected by this spell, while the spell attacks the Reflex of a creature wielding or employing an object, a miss avoiding the effect. If this first attack hits, the creature immediately drops the item. The spell attacks again in each round that the creature attempts to pick up or use the greased item. A creature wearing greased armor or clothing gains a +10 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] checks and on grapple checks made to resist or escape a grapple or to escape a pin.

_Material Component_: A bit of pork rind or butter.
