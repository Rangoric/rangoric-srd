# Sculpt Corpse

_Necromancy_

- **Level**: Occ 1, Sor/Wiz 1
- **Components**: V, S, M (lump of wax)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: One dead creature touched
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Will partial; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: No

With a clammy caress and a word to the powers lurking beyond death, you can reshape a dead body look like another creature or even a specific person so long as you have first-hand knowledge of how that creature or person actually looks. You can make the corpse one size larger or smaller and change anything else about it including its apparent type, gender, or age. The spell attacks the Will of creatures with a reason to suspect deception, which realize that the corpse was altered on a miss. If you chose to make the corpse look like a specific individual, it attacks the Will of anyone who knows that individual, who realizes that the corpse is not actually that person on a miss. However, if it hits a creature by 5 or less, that creature believes the corpse is that of someone who closely resembled the person they knew rather than a deliberate fake. This spell merely changes the appearance of the corpse. Any spell or effect that targets the corpse (such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Raise Dead|raise dead]]_) treats it as if it still had its original appearance.
