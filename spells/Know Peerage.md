# Know Peerage

*Divination \[MindAffecting\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 1, Clr 2, Inq 2, Pal 1, Skd 1, Sor/Wiz 2
  - **Components**: V, S, M (thread from a tabard or livery)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creature touched
  - **Duration**: 10 \* caster level minute(s)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You impart your knowledge of nobility and peerage to the target, allowing her to recognize members of noble households, differentiate one set of heraldry from another, and otherwise identify who's who at a royal gala or other noteworthy social event. The target is able to identify noble individuals, noble family names, and noble crests, signets, heraldry, and other symbols. The target treats her number of ranks in Society as though it were equal to your number of ranks in Society, to a maximum of 5 ranks and a minimum of 0. If the target's number of ranks is greater than yours, she uses her own number of ranks instead. In addition, if the target's new total skill bonus on Society checks is at least +0, she automatically succeeds on all Society checks with a DC of 10 or lower.
