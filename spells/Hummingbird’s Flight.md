# Hummingbird’s Flight

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 3
  - **Components**: V, S, M
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Targets**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell allows you to fly with greater dexterity. For the duration of the spell, your maneuverability while flying is increased by 2 steps, to a maximum of *good*. For example, if you could fly with *clumsy* maneuverability, casting this spell allows you to fly at *average* maneuverability.

The material component for this spell is the corpse of a small bird.
