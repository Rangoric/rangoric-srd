# Amplify Stench

_Transmutation_

- **Level**: Clr 2, Drd 2, Hun 2, Psy 2, Sor/Wiz 2
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 10 minutes/level

You amplify your natural stench special ability—its attack bonus increases by 2, and creatures your stench hits become nauseated rather than sickened. If your stench ability normally causes a creature to become nauseated (such as with the foul stench ability), the radius of your stench doubles instead. This spell has no effect if you don't possess the stench special ability.
