# Spit Venom

_Transmutation \[Poison\]_

- **Level**: Clr 4, Drd 3, Hun 3, Sha 4, Wit 3
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Effect**: One stream of venom
- **Duration**: Instantaneous; see text
- **Defense**: Fortitude partial
- **Spell Resistance**: No

You spit a stream of venom at a target using a ranged touch attack. If the venom hits, it causes blindness for 1 round. The venom also attacks the target’s Fortitude, and on a hit poisons it with [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Black Adder Venom|black adder venom]]; the poison’s attack bonus in successive rounds is equal to the spell's.
