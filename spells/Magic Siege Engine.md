# Magic Siege Engine

_Transmutation_

- **Level**: Apl 2, Clr 2, Inq 2, Occ 2, Pal 2, Sor/Wiz 2
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: One [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines|siege engine]] touched
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: Will (harmless, object)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless, object)

This spell permits an indirect fire siege engine to bombard its targets with greater accuracy, delivering more damage. The siege weapon receives a +1 enhancement bonus on targeting rolls and damage rolls. If used on a direct fire siege weapon, this spell acts a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon|magic weapon]]_.
