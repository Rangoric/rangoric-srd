# Magic Siege Engine

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Clr 2, Pal 2, Sor/Wiz 2
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: One siege engine touched
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless, object)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless, object)

This spell permits an indirect fire siege engine to bombard its targets with greater accuracy, delivering more damage. The siege weapon receives a +1 enhancement bonus on targeting rolls and damage rolls. If used on a direct fire siege weapon, this spell acts a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon|magic weapon]]*.
