# Summon Nature’s Ally IX

*Conjuration (Summoning) \[see text\]*

  - **Level**: Drd 9
  - **Effect**: One or more creatures, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally I]], except that you can summon one 9th-level creature, 1d3 8th-level creatures of the same kind, or 1d4+1 lower-level creatures of the same kind.

When you use a summoning spell to summon an air, chaotic, earth, evil, fire, good, lawful, or water creature, it is a spell of that type.
