# Symbol of Slowing

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Clr 4, Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M (mercury and phosphorous, plus powdered diamond and opal worth a total of 1,000 gp)

## Saving Throw

Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]]*, except all creatures within 60 feet of a *symbol of slowing* are slowed (as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]* spell) for 1 round per caster level.

*Symbol of slowing* can be made permanent with a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Permanency|permanency]]* spell by a caster of 11th level or higher for the cost of 10,000 gp.
