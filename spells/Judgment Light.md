# Judgment Light

*Evocation \[Light\]*

  - **Level**: Inq 4
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Area**: 30-ft.-radius burst
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous, see text

An inquisitor may only cast this spell while she has a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|judgment]] active. When she does cast this spell, it causes one or more of the following effects based on the inquisitor's active judgments.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Destruction (Judgment)|Destruction]]*: Red light erupts from the caster. Enemies in the area take 4d8 points of damage and become shaken for 1d4 rounds. A successful Will saving throw halves the damage and negates the shaken effect.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Healing (Judgment)|Healing]]*: Green light erupts from the caster. Allies in the area regain a number of hit points equal to 1d8 + the caster's Wisdom modifier.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Justice (Judgment)|Justice]]*: Blue light erupts from the caster, revealing hidden and invisible creatures to all. The light outlines such creatures for 1 round per caster level, imposing a –20 penalty on Stealth checks on those creatures.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Piercing (Judgment)|Piercing]]*: Violet light erupts from the caster, reducing the spell resistance of enemies within the burst by 5 for 1 round per caster level.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Protection (Judgment)|Protection]]*: Amber light erupts from the caster, granting allies in the burst a +2 sacred bonus to AC and CMD, and a +4 bonus to AC against critical confirmation attack rolls for 1 round per caster level.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Purity (Judgment)|Purity]]*: White light erupts from the caster, allowing each ally within the burst to attempt a saving throw against a single effect that allows a saving throw with a +2 sacred bonus on the roll.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Resiliency (Judgment)|Resiliency]]*: Golden light erupts from the caster, granting allies within the burst DR 3/magic or the alignment your judgment allows for 1 round per caster level.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Resistance (Judgment)|Resistance]]*: Copper light erupts from the caster, granting allies within the burst resistance 5 against the energy the judgment protects the inquisitor against when she casts this spell for 1 round per caster level.

*[[rangoric-srd/class-options/judgments/Smiting (Judgment)|Smiting]]*: Silver light erupts from the caster, making allies' weapons within the burst count as magic, the alignment her weapon is, and adamantine for purposes of overcoming damage reduction for 1 round per caster level.
