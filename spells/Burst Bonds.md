# Burst Bonds

_Evocation_

- **Level**: Inq 1
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: One object or creature restraining you
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Fortitude half (object)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You blast your restraints with divine wrath, dealing 1d6 points of damage per caster level to the target object (maximum 5d6), ignoring hardness of 10 or less. The spell attacks the Fortitude of magical restraints, and a miss halves the damage; it hits other restraints automatically.

You can also target a creature that is grappling or otherwise restraining you (such as by adhesive or the swallow whole ability) with the spell. You don't need to make a touch attack to use _burst bonds_ against a creature, and you can cast this spell without making a concentration check while grappled or pinned. The creature does not take damage as above. If you are grappled and the spell hits your target, you may make an immediate attempt to break the grapple (but not reverse it) as a free action, with a +1 luck bonus to your CMB per caster level (maximum +5). If the spell hits the target creature and you are restrained by an ability that attacked one of your defenses, _burst bonds_ makes that ability attack you again, with a +1 luck bonus to your defense per two caster levels (maximum +5), and a miss frees you.
