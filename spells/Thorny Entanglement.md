# Thorny Entanglement

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Drd 3, Rgr 3
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Saving Throw**: Reflex partial (see text)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]*, except the plants also grow thorns and can fling them up to 15 feet. Any creature that moves within 15 feet of the spell's area is attacked once on its turn by the animated plants. The plants use your caster level as their base attack bonus and add your spellcasting ability score modifier (Intelligence for witches; Wisdom for druids, rangers, and shamans) to the attack roll. A volley of thorns counts as a thrown weapon and deals 2d6 points of piercing damage on a successful hit. A creature that enters the spell's area or ends its turn there automatically takes 2d6 points of piercing damage.
