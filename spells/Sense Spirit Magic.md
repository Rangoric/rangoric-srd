# Sense Spirit Magic

*Divination*

  - **Level**: Sha 1
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 30 ft.
  - **Area**: 30-ft.-radius emanation centered on you
  - **Duration**: 24 hours
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

You gain greater sensitivity to magic associated with your primary spirit and wandering spirits (any spells on the spirit magic spell lists for these spirits, as well as spell-like abilities that duplicate the effects of those spells). You gain a +10 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] checks to identify these spells if they are cast within the area, and a +2 circumstance bonus on saving throws to resist the effects of such spells. If any of these spells require an attack roll, you are never considered flat-footed against such attacks.
