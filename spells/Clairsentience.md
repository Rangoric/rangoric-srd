# Clairsentience

*Divination*

  - **Level**: Brd 5, Skd 5, Sor/Wiz 5

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Clairaudience-Clairvoyance|clairaudience-clairvoyance]]*, except that the caster can see, hear, feel, smell, or taste anything that could be sensed as if he were there. Heat, cold, wind, and vibration can be felt, odors and airborne vapors sensed through smell and/or taste, sounds heard and sights seen. Odors or vapors and poisons are not harmful when sensed through this spell, but harmful sounds or sights (such as a medusa or basilisk gaze or a banshee cry) retains their dangerous properties. The range of each sense is dependent on the sense in question and the local conditions (wind, light, etc.).

The character can move the sensor at a rate of 5 feet every five rounds. Surfaces and vibrations can be felt, substances tasted, etc. if the sensor is brought in contact with the appropriate substance/surface.
