# Hollow Heroism, Greater

*Enchantment (Compulsion) \[MindAffecting, Ruse\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 6, Skd 6, Sor/Wiz 7, Wit 7
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creature touched
  - **Duration**: Caster level minute(s)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater|Greater Heroism]], except that you can reverse the spell by issuing a special command as a standard action if you are within medium range (100 feet + 10 feet per caster level) of the target. The target loses the bonuses and instead takes a -4 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks until the spell's duration ends (no save), increasing to a -8 penalty against fear effects. Additionally, she takes damage equal to your caster level when you reverse the spell. Attempts to identify *greater hollow heroism* with a skill check incorrectly identify it as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism, Greater|greater heroism]]* (see the ruse descriptor on page 192).
