# Frozen Note

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting, Sonic\]_

- **Level**: Brd 5, Skd 5
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Target**: Any number of creatures within area
- **Area**: 30-ft.-radius emanation centered on you
- **Duration**: Concentration (up to 1 round/level); see text
- **Defense**: Will partial; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You produce and sustain a single perfect musical note that holds nearby creatures, friend as well as foe, utterly spellbound until you stop singing. Affected creatures are both paralyzed and oblivious to their surroundings so long as you maintain the note. Maintaining this note requires your absolute attention; if you take damage or take any other action, including moving from your current square, the spell instantly ends. The spell attacks a creature it missed again each round the creature spends within the area of the spell, until it hits and the creature becomes captivated. Creatures with 4 or more Hit Dice greater than your caster level are not affected by this spell. However, those with 4 or more Hit Dice less than your caster level are hit automatically. The note is so clear and flawless that less than extraordinary attempts to avoid hearing it merely grant targets a bonus to their Will Defense against it. Targets get a +2 circumstance bonus to their Will Defense for each barrier between your voice and their ears. This includes such things as a creature stopping up its ears with wax, closing all windows and doors in a nearby building, or even crawling into a barrel and pulling down the top. If a target the spell had previously missed loses the benefit of one or more of its barriers, the spell immediately attacks it again. Similarly, the spell attacks any target it had previously hit again each time that target gains the protection of a barrier, and a miss frees it.
