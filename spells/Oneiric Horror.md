# Oneiric Horror

_Illusion (Phantasm) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 2, Med 1, Mes 2, Psy 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 3
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Target**: One living creature
- **Duration**: Caster level round(s)
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You cause the subject to believe it is being attacked by a creature out of its nightmares. Each round, the subject makes a full-attack action against the creature. A flying creature can still attempt a Acrobatics check to hover. Each round on its turn after making a full attack against the imaginary creature, the subject can make the spell attack its Will again, and a miss ends the effect. The subject is fatigued for 1 minute after the spell ends.
