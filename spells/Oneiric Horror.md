# Oneiric Horror

*Illusion (Phantasm) \[MindAffecting\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 3
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Target**: One living creature
  - **Duration**: Caster level round(s)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You cause the subject to believe it is being attacked by a creature out of its nightmares. Each round, the subject makes a full-attack action against the creature. A flying creature can still attempt a Acrobatics check to hover. Each round on its turn after making a full attack against the imaginary creature, the subject can attempt a new saving throw to end the effect. The subject is fatigued for 1 minute after the spell ends.
