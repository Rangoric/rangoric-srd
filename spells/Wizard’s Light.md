# Wizard’s Light

*Divination*

  - **Level**: Brd 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 2
  - **Components**: V, F

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]*, except that only the caster can see objects illuminated by this light source. All other creatures perceive the area per the normal lighting conditions. A creature using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]* can also see by the light of the *wizard’s light*. The focus is a lens ground from black volcanic glass worth at least 25 gp.
