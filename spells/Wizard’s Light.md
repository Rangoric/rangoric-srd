# Wizard’s Light

_Divination_

- **Level**: Brd 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 2
- **Components**: V, F

As _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_, except that only the caster can see objects illuminated by this light source. All other creatures perceive the area per the normal lighting conditions. A creature using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_ can also see by the light of the _wizard’s light_. The focus is a lens ground from black volcanic glass worth at least 25 gp.
