# Burning Gaze

_Evocation \[Fire\]_

- **Level**: Blr 2, Drd 2, Hun 2, Mag 2, Mes 2, Occ 2, Sha 2, Sor/Wiz 2, Wit 2
- **Components**: V, S, M/DF (eye of a mundane salamander)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Fortitude (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Your eyes burn like hot coals, allowing you to set objects or foes alight with a glance.

As a standard action as long as this spell's effects persist, you may direct your _burning gaze_ against a single creature or object within 30 feet of your location. The gaze attacks a targeted creature’s Fortitude, and on a hit deals 1d6 points of fire damage. It hits unattended objects automatically. The spell then attacks the Reflex of each creature it damaged, and on a hit the creature catches fire. Each round, it attacks a burning creature’s Reflex again, and a miss quenches the flames; a hit deals another 1d6 points of fire damage. It also attacks the Reflex Defense of flammable items worn by a creature, and items it hits take the same damage as the creature. If a creature or object is already on fire, it suffers no additional effects from _burning gaze_.

Note that this spell does not grant an actual gaze attack—foes and allies are not in danger of catching on fire simply by meeting your gaze.
