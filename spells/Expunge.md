# Expunge

_Evocation \[Good or Evil\]_

- **Level**: Clr 4, Pal 2
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: See text
- **Range**: Personal
- **Duration**: See text

Casting this spell is a free action, like casting a _[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]_ spell, and it counts toward the normal limit of one _[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]_ spell per round. If the caster [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channels energy]] in the same round, the channeled energy deals an extra 1d6 points of damage; if the energy is used to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turn or rebuke undead]], the turning damage is 1d6 higher. If the caster channels positive energy, this is a good spell. If the caster channels negative energy, this is an evil spell.
