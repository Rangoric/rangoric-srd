# Expunge

*Evocation \[Good or Evil\]*

  - **Level**: Clr 4, Pal 2
  - **Components**: V
  - **Casting Time**: See text
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Duration**: See text

Casting this spell is a free action, like casting a *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell, and it counts toward the normal limit of one *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell per round. If the caster [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channels energy]] in the same round, the channeled energy deals an extra 1d6 points of damage; if the energy is used to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turn or rebuke undead]], the turning damage is 1d6 higher. If the caster channels positive energy, this is a good spell. If the caster channels negative energy, this is an evil spell.
