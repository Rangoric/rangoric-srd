# Microcosm

_Illusion (Phantasm) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Psy 9
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One or more creatures
- **Duration**: Permanent
- **Defense**: Will partial (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You plunge the targets' minds into a veiled [[rangoric-srd/rules/Mindscapes#Immersive Mindscapes|immersive mindscape]] of your own design. You can affect any number of creatures whose combined total Hit Dice do not exceed 30. The spell hits a creature of 10 HD or fewer automatically; one of 11-15 HD escapes after 10 minutes per level you possess if the spell misses its Will, and a creature of 16 HD or more negates the effect on a miss. Given time, the bodies of creatures whose minds are trapped in a _microcosm_ can die of starvation and thirst without care. Creatures can neither escape from nor enter a _microcosm_, even via a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mindscape Door|mindscape door]]_. _Microcosm_ can be ended only by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_. Creatures inside a _microcosm_ can be affected by mind-affecting effects targeted against their real bodies. However, they perceive them as natural events within their inner mindscape. Multiple creatures affected by the same _microcosm_ can interact with one another within the mindscape.
