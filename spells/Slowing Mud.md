# Slowing Mud

_Conjuration (Creation)_

- **Level**: Drd 4, Hun 4, Sha 4
- **Components**: V, S, M (earth and water)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Targets**: One creature/level, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
- **Duration**: 1 round/level (see text)
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You coat the targets in thick, sticky mud. The mud acts as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ spell and also blinds the targets. A creature can use a standard action to remove the mud from its eyes or the eyes of an adjacent creature, ending the blinded condition for that individual. Removing enough mud to end the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]_ effect is a full-round action. Jumping into a lake or being subject to an effect that creates a lot of water (such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|hydraulic push]]_) removes all the mud in 1 round.

A _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_ effect cast on a creature slowed by the mud allows it to act normally for the duration of the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|Slow]]_ does not stack with the effects of _slowing mud_. This spell cannot counter or dispel _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_.
