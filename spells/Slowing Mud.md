# Slowing Mud

*Conjuration (Creation)*

  - **Level**: Drd 4, Hun 4, Sha 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M (earth and water)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Targets**: One creature/level, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level (see text)
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You coat the targets in thick, sticky mud. The mud acts as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]* spell and also blinds the targets. A creature can use a standard action to remove the mud from its eyes or the eyes of an adjacent creature, ending the blinded condition for that individual. Removing enough mud to end the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]]* effect is a full-round action. Jumping into a lake or being subject to an effect that creates a lot of water (such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|hydraulic push]]*) removes all the mud in 1 round.

A *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]* effect cast on a creature slowed by the mud allows it to act normally for the duration of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|Slow]]* does not stack with the effects of *slowing mud*. This spell cannot counter or dispel *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*.
