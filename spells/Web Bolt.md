# Web Bolt

_Conjuration (Creation)_

- **Level**: Blr 1, Mag 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Wit 1
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Effect**: Fist-sized blob of webbing
- **Duration**: 1 min./level
- **Defense**: Reflex; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: No

You launch a ball of webbing at a target, which is affected (vs Reflex) as if by a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]_ spell occupying only the creature's space. If the spell misses or the creature breaks free of the webbing, the remaining webs dissolve and the square is not considered difficult terrain. The spell has no effect if the target is not on or adjacent to a solid surface that can support the webbing.
