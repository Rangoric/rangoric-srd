# Scorching Ash Form

_Transmutation \[Fire\]_

- **Level**: Alc 4, Sor/Wiz 4
- **Components**: S, M (a bit of gauze and a handful of ashes)
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions like _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]_, except the target becomes a visible swirl of hot ash and smoke instead of harmless translucent gas. The target gains the fire subtype. Any creature that begins its turn sharing a space with the target takes 2d6 points of fire damage and the smoke attacks its Fortitude (+5, +1 per previous attack), and on a hit it suffers the effects of smoke inhalation.
