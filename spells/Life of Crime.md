# Life of Crime

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Curse, MindAffecting\]_

- **Level**: Apl 4, Brd 6, Mes 6, Psy 8, Skd 6, Sor/Wiz 8
- **Components**: V, S, M (a black mask)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One living creature
- **Duration**: Permanent
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You unleash the basest instincts of iniquity in the target and cause them to become his overriding reason for being. The target neither gains nor provides benefit from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^teamwork-feats|teamwork feats]] or the aid another action and can't willingly accept harmless magical effects from others. The target moves by Stealth whenever possible, and lies and deceives others instinctively to further its personal agenda. In addition, when the target is conscious, the spell attacks its Will each hour (or each round during combat or a similarly stressful situation), and on a hit it behaves as if affected by a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Crime Wave|crime wave]]_ spell for 1 round. _Life of Crime_ is particularly difficult to remove. Only a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ with a higher caster level than _life of crime's_ caster level, or a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_ can remove its effects.
