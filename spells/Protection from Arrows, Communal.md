# Protection from Arrows, Communal

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Alc 3, Psy 3, Sor/Wiz 3, Sum 3, SumU 3
- **Components**: V, S, F (a piece of tortoiseshell or turtle shell)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Creatures touched
- **Duration**: 1 hour/level or until discharged
- **Defense**: Will (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Arrows|protection from arrows]]_, except you [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^communal-spells|divide the duration]] in 1-hour intervals among the creatures touched. Once the spell absorbs 10 points of damage per caster level (maximum 100 points) for a subject, the spell's effects end for that subject.
