# Spirit-Bound Blade

_Evocation_

- **Level**: Med 4, Occ 4, Sha 4, Spi 3
- **Components**: S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Weapon touched
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: Will (harmless, object)
- **Spell Resistance**: No

You focus emotional energy and weave it into a shroud of hardened ectoplasm around the weapon you touch, infusing it with a ghostly glow and great power. The weapon becomes a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]] weapon, and gains one of the following additional benefits, depending on the type of emotion you infuse into the weapon. If a special ability wouldn't apply to the chosen weapon (such as vicious on a ranged weapon), the weapon doesn't gain that benefit.

**Anger**: The weapon also gains the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Vicious|vicious]] special ability.

**Dedication**: The weapon also gains the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Returning|returning]] special ability.

**Despair**: The weapon also gains the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Cruel|cruel]] special ability.

**Fear**: The weapon also gains the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Menacing|menacing]] special ability.

**Hatred**: The weapon also gains the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Cunning|cunning]] special ability.

**Jealousy**: The weapon also gains the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Mimetic|mimetic]] special ability.

**Zeal**: The weapon also gains the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Keen|keen]] special ability. If you are a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)|spiritualist]] and the type of emotional energy you choose matches the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-emotional-focus|emotional focus]] of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-phantom|phantom]], the weapon grants its wielder a +2 bonus on skill checks with both skills you gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] in from your phantom's emotional focus.
