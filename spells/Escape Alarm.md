# Escape Alarm

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Brd 2, Hun 2, Rgr 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 2
  - **Components**: V, S, F
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Area**: Ten 10-foot cubes/level
  - **Duration**: 24 hour(s)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

You place a ward on an area that notifies you when a creature exits it.

This functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]], except as noted. It alerts you when a creature leaves, rather than enters, the area, and you can't select a password to bypass its effects. Instead, when you place an escape *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]*, you can attune up to one additional creature per caster level to the spell. You are automatically attuned to your own escape *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]* and don't count against the limit. Attuned creatures can enter and exit the spell's area without triggering the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]*. If you select a mental *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]* rather than an audible one, all attuned creatures receive the mental alert when someone exits the warded area.
