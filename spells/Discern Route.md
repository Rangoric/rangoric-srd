# Discern Route

*Divination*

  - **Level**: Brd 4, Clr 4, Drd 4, Fey 4, Fey Roads 4, Fhn 4, Fpk 4, Hun 4, Msl 4, Rgr 4, Skd 4, Sor/Wiz 5
  - **Components**: V, S, M, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Target**: One fey crossroad or *doorway*
  - **Duration**: 1 minute

This spell imparts the caster with the knowledge of where a selected [[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Fey Roads|fey road]] terminates.

*Material Component*: A silver coin balanced on the caster’s forefinger and flicked into the air by his thumb.
