# Wooden Phalanx

_Conjuration (Creation)_

- **Level**: Clr 9, Sor/Wiz 9
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Effect**: Three or more wood golems, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart (see text)
- **Duration**: 1 hour/level (D)
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

You create 1d4+2 wood golems with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/simple-templates/Advanced Creature|advanced template]]. The golems willingly aid you in combat or battle, perform a specific mission, or serve as bodyguards. You can only have one _wooden phalanx_ spell in effect at one time. If you cast this spell while another casting is still in effect, the previous casting is dispelled.
