# Complete Invisibility

*Illusion (Glamour)*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]*, except that the subject gains a +30 to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks and cannot be detected with the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]* spells or other magical means. In addition, the subject cannot be detected with Blindsight, Scent, Tremorsense or other similar abilities.
