# Complete Invisibility

_Illusion (Glamour)_

- **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
- **Duration**: 1 round/level

As _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]_, except that the subject gains a +30 to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks and cannot be detected with the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_ spells or other magical means. In addition, the subject cannot be detected with Blindsight, Scent, Tremorsense or other similar abilities.
