# Enchantment Foil

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Alc 4, Clr 4, Inq 4, Mes 3, Occ 4, Psy 4, Sor/Wiz 4, Wit 4
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 hour/level (see text)

You gain a +4 bonus on defenses against enchantment effects. If an enchantment effect misses you, you identify the effect as if you had succeeded at a Mysticism check to do so. Furthermore, you can choose to act as if it had hit you. If you do so, you gain a +20 bonus on Deception checks to convince others that it hit you and you are under the enchantment's effects. A creature that attempts to use magic to detect this ruse or to make you speak truthfully about it must succeed at a caster level check (DC 15 + your caster level) to do so.
