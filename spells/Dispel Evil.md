# Dispel Evil

_Abjuration \[Good\]_

- **Level**: Clr 5, Good 5, Inq 5, Pal 4, Sha 5
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch

Target or Targets: You and a touched evil creature from another plane; or you and an enchantment or evil spell on a touched creature or object  
Duration: 1 round/level or until discharged, whichever comes first  
Defense: See text  
Spell Resistance: See text

Shimmering, white, holy energy surrounds you. This power has three effects.

First, you gain a +4 deflection bonus to AC against attacks by evil creatures.

Second, on making a successful melee touch attack against an evil creature from another plane, you can choose to drive that creature back to its home plane. The effect attacks the creature’s Will, and a miss negates it (spell resistance applies). This use discharges and ends the spell.

Third, with a touch you can automatically dispel any one enchantment spell cast by an evil creature or any one evil spell. Exception: Spells that can’t be dispelled by dispel magic also can’t be dispelled by dispel evil. This effect hits automatically, and spell resistance does not apply to it. This use discharges and ends the spell.
