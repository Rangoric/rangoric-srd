# Telempathic Projection

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Emotion, Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Mes 1, Psy 1, Spi 1
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: Will (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You alter the target's mood, adjusting its attitude toward you or another creature you designate by one step either positively or negatively (see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion#Influencing Attitudes|Persuasion]]). You can instead use this spell to assist your own or an ally's [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] check, granting that check a +5 insight bonus against the target of your _telempathic projection_ with no attack against a defense. In this case, the spell's duration expires immediately when the skill check is complete.
