# Enforcer I

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Components**: V
  - **Casting Time**: See text
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Duration**: See text

Casting this spell is a free action, like casting a *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell, and it counts toward the normal limit of one *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell per round. If the caster casts another spell in the same round, the level of that spell is considered to be 5 for purposes of determining the save DC, if any. This spell does not affect spells of 6th level or higher.
