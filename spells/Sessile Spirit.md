# Sessile Spirit

*Necromancy*

  - **Level**: Clr 5, Wit 5
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Target**: One creature or object (see text)
  - **Duration**: Caster level round(s)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You reach into the target creature and cause that spirits within to become dormant and inactive. If the target is a medium with a spirit inhabiting its body or a spiritualist with a phantom inhabiting its consciousness (or a member of another class using the spirit or phantom class features), a failed save renders that spirit or phantom powerless, suppressing any benefits the spirit or phantom normally provides to its host, including bonus feats and spells known. If a spirit within the target is a creature using a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession|possession]]* effect, including *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possess Object|possess object]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession|possession]]*, or a ghost's malevolence, the spell targets the possessing creature rather than the creature it inhabits. On a failed save, the possessing spirit isn't exorcised from the target but is dazed for the duration of the spell.
