# Deathless

*Necromancy*

  - **Level**: Alc 4, Clr 4, Inq 4, Psy 4, Sor/Wiz 4, Wit 4
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Living creature touched
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

The target's soul is strongly anchored to its body, preventing death from hit point damage. No matter how low the target's hit points get, it remains alive (though not necessarily [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death|conscious]]).

This spell doesn't prevent death from sources other than hit point damage—such as Constitution [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Death Attacks|death effects]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|energy drain]]. Spells that cure hit points affect the creature normally.
