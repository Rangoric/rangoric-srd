# Hillsong

_Transmutation_

- **Level**: Brd 3, Skd 3
- **Components**: V, M
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 20 ft.
- **Target**: All willing creatures in range
- **Duration**: 10 minutes/level
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell imparts upon the recipient the racial abilities of Halflings (see the [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]] race) for the spell’s duration. They temporarily replace the character’s own racial traits. The spell does not change his appearance or size and does not modify ability scores. If he does not already have it, the character also gains a temporary fluency in the Halfling language that lasts for one full day. Halflings not affected by this spell.

_Material Components:_ A strand of Halfling hair and a handful of hearth ash.
