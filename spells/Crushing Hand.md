# Crushing Hand

*Evocation \[Force\]*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 9, Strength 9
  - **Components**: V, S, M, F/DF

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Interposing Hand|interposing hand]], except that the hand can interpose itself, push, or crush one opponent that you select.

The crushing hand can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] an opponent like grasping hand does. Its [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] for grapple checks use your caster level in place of its base attack bonus, with a +12 bonus for its Strength score (35) and a +1 bonus for being Large (its Dexterity is 10, granting no bonus to the CMD). The hand deals 2d6+12 points of damage (lethal, not nonlethal) on each successful grapple check against an opponent.

The crushing hand can also interpose itself as interposing hand does, or it can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] an opponent as forceful hand does, using the same bonuses outlined above.

Directing the spell to a new target is a move action.

Clerics who cast this spell name it for their deities.

*Arcane Material Component*: The shell of an egg.

*Arcane Focus*: A glove of snakeskin.
