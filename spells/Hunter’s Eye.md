# Hunter’s Eye

*Divination*

  - **Level**: Hun 2, Inq 3, Rgr 2
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Target**: One creature
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Sharpening your perceptive abilities and tuning them against obfuscating effects, *hunter's eye* greatly enhances your senses against the target creature. You gain the ability to perceive the target when it is invisible or ethereal as though using the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]* spell, and receive a +20 competence bonus on Perception checks to locate the target. You ignore concealment provided by fog or mist, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blur|blur]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, and similar effects affecting the target, but not concealment provided by darkness. This spell provides no benefits to your allies, and no benefits against creatures other than the target.
