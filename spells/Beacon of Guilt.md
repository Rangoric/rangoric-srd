# Beacon of Guilt

_Evocation \[Curse\]_

- **Level**: Brd 2, Inq 2, Occ 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 2
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: One object
- **Duration**: 24 hours or until discharged, then instantaneous
- **Defense**: Will (object); see text
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You place an invisible ward upon an object that is triggered the first time a creature tries to move the object from its current location. The next creature to touch the object is cursed to become obvious to everyone around it. The curse attacks the creature’s Will, and on a hit it is surrounded in an aura of twinkling red light that functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]] (spell resistance applies). The curse bestowed by this spell cannot be dispelled, but a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ spell can remove it.
