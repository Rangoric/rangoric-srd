# Hold Monster

*Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 4, Inq 4, Law 6, Skd 4, Sor/Wiz 5, Wit 5
  - **Components**: V, S, M/DF
  - **Target**: One living creature

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]], except that it affects any living creature that fails its Will save.

*Arcane Material Component*: One hard metal bar or rod, which can be as small as a three-penny nail.
