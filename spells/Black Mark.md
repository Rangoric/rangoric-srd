# Black Mark

*Necromancy \[Curse, Fear\]*

  - **Level**: Drd 7
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a flask of seawater)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: One creature
  - **Duration**: Permanent
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You mark the target with a black marking on its skin; the mark's exact appearance determined by you, but can be no larger than your hand. The *black mark* functions as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mark of Justice|mark of justice]]*, and when the mark is activated, the target becomes shaken anytime it is on or in the water more than a 5 feet from shore. In addition, as long as the *black mark* is active, the target is affected as if subject to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nature’s Exile|nature's exile]]* , but all creatures with the aquatic or water subtype or with a swim speed are made hostile, even those not of the animal type, though non-aquatic animals are not.
