# Heroism, Greater

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 5, Psy 6, Skd 5, Sor/Wiz 6, Spi 6, Sum 5, SumU 6, Wit 6
- **Duration**: 1 min./level

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Heroism|heroism]], except the creature gains a +4 morale bonus on attack rolls, defenses, and skill checks, immunity to fear effects, and temporary hit points equal to your caster level (maximum 20).
