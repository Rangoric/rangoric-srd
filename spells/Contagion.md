# Contagion

_Necromancy \[Disease, Evil\]_

- **Level**: Apl 3, Blr 4, Clr 3, Destruction 3, Drd 3, Hun 3, Sor/Wiz 4
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Living creature touched
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The subject contracts a disease selected from the table below. The disease is contracted immediately (the onset period does not apply); use _contagion_’s attack bonus for the first attack against Fortitude. Use the disease’s listed frequency and attack bonus to determine further effects; the attack bonus in the table is for those later attacks. For more information see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|diseases]].
