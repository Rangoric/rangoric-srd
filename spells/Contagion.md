# Contagion

*Necromancy \[Disease, Evil\]*

  - **Level**: Apl 3, Blr 4, Clr 3, Destruction 3, Drd 3, Hun 3, Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Living creature touched
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The subject contracts one of the following diseases: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Blinding Sickness|blinding sickness]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Bubonic Plague|bubonic plague]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Cackle Fever|cackle fever]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Leprosy|leprosy]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Mindfire|mindfire]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Red Ache|red ache]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Shakes|shakes]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Slimy Doom|slimy doom]]. The disease is contracted immediately (the onset period does not apply). Use the disease’s listed frequency and save DC to determine further effects. For more information see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|diseases]].
