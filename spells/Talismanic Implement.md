# Talismanic Implement

_Evocation_

- **Level**: Occ 3
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 10 minutes
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 hour/level (D) or until discharged
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contingency|contingency]]_, but you invest a spell whose level doesn't exceed one-quarter of your level (rounded down, maximum spell level 3rd) into one of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-implements|implements]] that holds 2 or more points of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-mental-focus|mental focus]]. The spell's school must match that of the implement, and the spell must be one that affects only your person. Casting _talismanic implement_ reduces the selected implement's mental focus by 2, including for the purposes of determining the effects of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Implement Schools|resonant powers]]. When you refresh your mental focus, _talismanic implement_ ends automatically, even if the duration hasn't elapsed. Unlike _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contingency|contingency]]_, you may have multiple _talismanic implements_ simultaneously, though a single implement can hold only one _talismanic implement_ spell, and if you have any _talismanic implements_, you can't have any other kind of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contingency|contingency]]_ in effect.
