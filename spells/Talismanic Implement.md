# Talismanic Implement

*Evocation*

  - **Level**: Occ 3
  - **Components**: V
  - **Casting Time**: 10 minutes
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level (D) or until discharged
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contingency|contingency]]*, but you invest a spell whose level doesn't exceed one-quarter of your level (rounded down, maximum spell level 3rd) into one of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-implements|implements]] that holds 2 or more points of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-mental-focus|mental focus]]. The spell's school must match that of the implement, and the spell must be one that affects only your person. Casting *talismanic implement* reduces the selected implement's mental focus by 2, including for the purposes of determining the effects of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Implement Schools|resonant powers]]. When you refresh your mental focus, *talismanic implement* ends automatically, even if the duration hasn't elapsed. Unlike *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contingency|contingency]]*, you may have multiple *talismanic implements* simultaneously, though a single implement can hold only one *talismanic implement* spell, and if you have any *talismanic implements*, you can't have any other kind of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contingency|contingency]]* in effect.
