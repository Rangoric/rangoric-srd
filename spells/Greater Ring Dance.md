# Greater Ring Dance

*Evocation*

  - **Level**: Clr 7, Drd 7, Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Components**: V, S, M/DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: 2 creatures/level
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Lesser Ring Dance|lesser ring dance]]*, except the number of ring dance participants increases to two for every caster level.

*Material Component*: a finely made fully functional miniature musical instrument (such as a fiddle, lute, or drum) worth at least 500 gp and made of precious wood, gold or silver inlay, and painted or varnished with exotic materials.
