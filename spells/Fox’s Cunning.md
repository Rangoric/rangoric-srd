# Fox’s Cunning

_Transmutation_

- **Level**: Alc 2, Brd 2, Med 2, Psy 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 2, Sum 2, SumU 2
- **Components**: V, S, M/DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Creature touched
- **Duration**: 1 min./level
- **Defense**: Will (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The transmuted creature becomes smarter. The spell grants a +4 enhancement bonus to Intelligence, adding the usual benefits to Intelligence-based skill checks and other uses of the Intelligence modifier. Wizards (and other spellcasters who rely on Intelligence) affected by this spell do not gain any additional bonus spells for the increased Intelligence, but the attack bonuses of spells they cast while under this spell’s effect do increase. This spell doesn’t grant extra skill points.

_Arcane Material Component_: A few hairs, or a pinch of dung, from a fox.
