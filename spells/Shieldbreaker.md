# Shieldbreaker

_Evocation \[Force\]_

- **Level**: Sor/Wiz 5
- **Defense**: Fortitude (object, see text)

As _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Missile|magic missile]]_, except only a single missile is fired. This missile deals 2d4+4 damage to the target shield (ignore hardness). The missile also ignores _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_ spell effects, dispels a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shield|shield]]_ spell if active, and causes a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Brooch of Shielding|brooch of shielding]]_ to shatter if the missile hits its Fortitude.
