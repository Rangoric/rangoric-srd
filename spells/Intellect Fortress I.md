# Intellect Fortress I

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Psy 4
  - **Components**: V
  - **Casting Time**: 1 immediate action
  - **Range**: 20 ft.
  - **Area**: 20-ft.-radius emanation centered on you
  - **Duration**: 1 round
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

Using the power of pure logic, you disrupt mental attacks. *Intellect fortress I* suppresses all effects with the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^emotion-component|emotion]] and fear descriptors for its duration.
