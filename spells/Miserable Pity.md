# Miserable Pity

_Abjuration \[Emotion, Mind-affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 2, Mes 2, Psy 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 2, Wit 2
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Creature touched
- **Duration**: 1 round/level and 1 minute; see text
- **Defense**: Will (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sanctuary|sanctuary]]_, except creatures attempting to attack the target whose Will the spell hits also feel a strong sense of pity toward the target, as if it were weak, pathetic, and not a threat. If the target breaks the spell by attacking, any creature the spell hit gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls against the target for 1 minute.
