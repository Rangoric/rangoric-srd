# Disrupt Silence

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Brd 2, Clr 3, Inq 3, Psy 3, Skd 2
- **Components**: S, M (tiny silver bell, chime, or gong)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Area**: 10-ft.-radius emanation centered on a creature, object, or point in space
- **Duration**: Caster level round(s)
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You suppress magical sound-dampening effects within the area.

_Disrupt silence_ temporarily negates magical _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ within its area, so that normal sounds can be heard within the overlapping areas of effect. Additionally, _disrupt silence_ can automatically counter or dispel any magical _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ effect of equal or lower level cast upon the same target, such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_. If you cast _disrupt silence_ on the target of a higher-level _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]_ effect, it functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]] instead of its normal function.
