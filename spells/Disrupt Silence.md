# Disrupt Silence

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Brd 2, Clr 3, Inq 3, Skd 2
  - **Components**: S, M (tiny silver bell, chime, or gong)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Area**: 10-ft.-radius emanation centered on a creature, object, or point in space
  - **Duration**: Caster level round(s)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You suppress magical sound-dampening effects within the area.

*Disrupt silence* temporarily negates magical *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]* within its area, so that normal sounds can be heard within the overlapping areas of effect. Additionally, *disrupt silence* can automatically counter or dispel any magical *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]* effect of equal or lower level cast upon the same target, such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*. If you cast *disrupt silence* on the target of a higher-level *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]* effect, it functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]] instead of its normal function.
