# Permanent Hallucination

_Illusion (Phantasm) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Psy 7, Sor/Wiz 7
- **Components**: S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: Permanent
- **Defense**: Will disbelief
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Audiovisual Hallucination|audiovisual hallucination]], except that the phantasm you create includes visual, auditory, olfactory, tactile, and thermal components, and the phantasm follows a complex script. The phantasm follows that script without your having to concentrate on it and can react to stimuli the target perceives, as appropriate for the script. Unlike most illusions that can be disbelieved, if the target disbelieves a _permanent hallucination_, she can choose to end the effect entirely at any time.
