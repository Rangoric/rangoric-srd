# Marks of Forbiddance

_Abjuration \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Pal 3
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Targets**: One enemy and one ally
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Will; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

_Marks of Forbiddance_ magically brand two creatures, preventing them from committing violence against one another. When you cast this spell choose one enemy and one ally within range. Both targets become branded with the sigil of forbiddance. Until the end of the spell's duration, in order for the two targets to attack one another, even with a targeted spell, the spell attacks each attacker’s Will. On a miss, that target can attack the other normally. On a hit, the target can't follow through with the attack and the action is lost. This spell does not prevent the targets from attacking each other with area of effect spells or abilities.
