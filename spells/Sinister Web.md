# Sinister Web

*Conjuration*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Saving Throw**: Reflex negates, Fortitude negates (see text)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]*, except the webs conjured are not particularly vulnerable to flame attacks. The webs do not burn unless in constant contact with fire, and it takes 2 rounds to burn away a 5 foot square area.

In addition, any creature caught in the webs must make a Fortitude Save or be paralyzed for 1d6+1 rounds. For each round that a creature tries to move through the webs, a new Fortitude Save is necessary.
