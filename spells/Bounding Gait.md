# Bounding Gait

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 2
  - **Components**: V, S, F
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Targets**: One creature
  - **Duration**: 5 rounds/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

The transmuted creature may run with a fast, bounding step. Walking and running movement rates are increased by half. As part of normal movement, obstacles and hazards up to 10 feet wide and 5 feet high may be cleared with ease. Once per round, the subject may leap over one such obstacle without making a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check. Additional obstacles in one round require a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check to clear, but a +15 circumstance bonus applies to these rolls. As a full-round action, the subject may make a single [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check with a +40 bonus to the roll. While this spell is in effect, the usual maximums for jumping distance do not apply to the subject.

*Arcane focus:* the preserved leg from a frog or toad.
