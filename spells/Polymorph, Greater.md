# Polymorph, Greater

*Transmutation (Polymorph)*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a piece of the creature whose form you choose)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Living creature touched
  - **Duration**: 1 min/level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell transforms a willing creature into an animal, magical beast, elemental, humanoid, plant, or dragon of your choosing; the spell has no effect on unwilling creatures, nor can the creature being targeted by this spell influence the new form assumed (apart from conveying its wishes, if any, to you verbally). If you use this spell to cause the target to take on the form of an animal or magical beast, it functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape IV|beast shape IV]]*. If the form is that of an elemental, the spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Body III|elemental body III]]*. If the form is that of a humanoid, the spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]*. If the form is that of a plant, the spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plant Shape II|plant shape II]]*. If the form is that of a dragon, the spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Form of the Dragon I|form of the dragon I]]*. The subject may choose to resume its normal form as a full-round action; doing so ends the spell.
