# Recurring Nightmare

*Illusion (Phantasm) \[Mind-Affecting\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 6, Clr 6, Drd 6, Dream 8, Hun 6, Skd 6, Sor/Wiz 6
  - **Components**: V, S, M
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Target**: One creature
  - **Duration**: Permanent
  - **Saving Throw**: See text
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Hideous creatures haunt the victim’s dreams. Each night the target sleeps, he gains 1d2 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative levels]] unless the victim manages a successful Will save to temporarily stave off the effects for that night. If the number of negative levels ever equals or exceeds his HD, he dies.

*Recurring nightmare* cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed with a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]* spell.

*Material Component*: A blue lotus flower burned in a brazier.
