# Hold Monster, Mass

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Psy 9, Sor/Wiz 9, Wit 9
- **Targets**: One or more creatures, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]], except that it affects multiple creatures and holds any living creature whose Will it hits.
