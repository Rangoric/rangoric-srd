# Focused Scrutiny

*Divination*

  - **Level**: Brd 2, Sha 2, Skd 2
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 10 minutes/level (D)

You gain heightened sensitivity to the mannerisms, reactions, and body language of one creature you can perceive, allowing you to sense its state of mind and easily influence it. You gain a +10 bonus on Perception, Sense Motive, and Fieldcraft checks attempted against the target creature, as well as a +5 bonus on Persuasion and Intimidation checks attempted against it.
