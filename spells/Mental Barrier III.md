# Mental Barrier III

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Psy 4
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 immediate action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mental Barrier I|mental barrier I]]_, but the shield bonus to AC increases to +8. In addition, you are immune to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust I|mind thrust I]]_, and you take half damage from _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust II|mind thrust II]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust III|mind thrust III]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust IV|mind thrust IV]]_ (reduced to one-quarter on a miss against your Will). This spell can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^undercasting|undercast]].
