# Mental Barrier III

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Psy 4
  - **Components**: V
  - **Casting Time**: 1 immediate action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 round
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mental Barrier I|mental barrier I]]*, but the shield bonus to AC increases to +8. In addition, you are immune to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust I|mind thrust I]]*, and you take half damage from *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust II|mind thrust II]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust III|mind thrust III]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust IV|mind thrust IV]]* (reduced to one-quarter on a successful Will save). This spell can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^undercasting|undercast]].
