# Open Doorway

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Brd 6, Clr 6, Drd 6, Hun 6, Skd 6, Sor/Wiz 6
  - **Components**: V, S, M
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Target**: One doorway
  - **Duration**: Permanent
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell opens a fey *[[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Doorways|doorway]]* without having the key or meeting the normal conditions of entry. This spell can reopen a one-way *doorway* without a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Doorway Guardian|guardian]] for a short period of time, or temporarily negate the effects of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gate Seal|gate seal]]* spell (1 round/caster level).

*Material Component*: A large, ornate silver key forged with two ounces of *[[rangoric-srd/rules/Faercana#^magic-sand|magic sand]]* (worth 50 gp.).
