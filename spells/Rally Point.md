# Rally Point

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Emotion, Good, Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Pal 1
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 5 ft.
- **Area**: One 5-ft. square
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level (D)
- **Defense**: Will (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

You create a spot that has the power to briefly hearten any good creature who comes into contact with it. A good creature who enters this square (even if simply as part of its normal move) gains a +2 morale bonus on attacks and to defenses, and 2 temporary hit points per caster level for 1 round. Nongood creatures gain no benefit from this spell. A creature cannot benefit more than once from the same casting of this spell.
