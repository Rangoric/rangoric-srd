# Symbol of Fear

_Necromancy \[Fear, Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Clr 6, Occ 4, Sor/Wiz 6, Wit 6
- **Defense**: Will

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]], except that all creatures within 60 feet of the symbol of fear instead become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for 1 round per caster level.

_Note_: Magic traps such as symbol of fear are hard to detect and disable. A rogue (only) can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] skill to find a symbol of fear and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 31 for symbol of fear.

_Material Component_: Mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal with a total value of at least 1,000 gp.
