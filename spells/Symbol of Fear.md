# Symbol of Fear

*Necromancy \[Fear, Mind-Affecting\]*

  - **Level**: Clr 6, Sor/Wiz 6, Wit 6
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]], except that all creatures within 60 feet of the symbol of fear instead become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for 1 round per caster level.

*Note*: Magic traps such as symbol of fear are hard to detect and disable. A rogue (only) can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] skill to find a symbol of fear and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 31 for symbol of fear.

*Material Component*: Mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal with a total value of at least 1,000 gp.
