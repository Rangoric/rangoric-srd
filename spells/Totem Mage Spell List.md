## Spell List

### Totem Mage Spells

The spells every [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can prepare. Each totem adds five more, one each of 1st to 5th level, listed in its note ([[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Bear (Totem)|Bear]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Boar (Totem)|Boar]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Cat (Totem)|Cat]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Dog (Totem)|Dog]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Eagle (Totem)|Eagle]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Horse (Totem)|Horse]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Insect (Totem)|Insect]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Rat (Totem)|Rat]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Wolf (Totem)|Wolf]]). These spells carry "Tot" levels.
