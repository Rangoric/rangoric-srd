# Summon Fey VI

*Conjuration (Summoning) \[see text\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 6, Clr 6, Drd 6, Hun 6, Skd 6, Sor/Wiz 6
  - **Components**: V, S, F/DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 round
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Effect**: One or more summoned creatures, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Fey I|summon fey I]]*, except you can summon one fey from the 6th-level list, 1d3 fey of the same type from the 5th-level list, or 1d4+1 fey of the same type from a lower-level list.
