# Word of Resolve

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Pal 1
- **Components**: V, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 immediate action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One ally
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Will negate (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

You focus your aura on one ally, allowing it to make a charm or fear effect that hit it reroll its attack, with a +4 sacred bonus to its defense against the reroll. If you do not have the aura of courage class ability, this spell has no effect on the target's fear. If you do not have the aura of resolve class ability, this spell has no effect on any charm effects on the target.
