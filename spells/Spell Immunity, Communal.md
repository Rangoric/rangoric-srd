# Spell Immunity, Communal

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Clr 5, Inq 5
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creatures touched
  - **Duration**: 10 min./level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Immunity|spell immunity]]*, except you divide the duration in 10-minute intervals among the creatures touched.
