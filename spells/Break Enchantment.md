# Break Enchantment

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Brd 4, Clr 5, Inq 5, Luck 5, Pal 4, Sha 5, Skd 4, Sor/Wiz 5, Wit 5
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 minute
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Targets**: Up to one creature per level, all within 30 ft. of each other
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: See text
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell frees victims from enchantments, transmutations, and curses. Break enchantment can reverse even an instantaneous effect. For each such effect, you make a caster level check (1d20 + caster level, maximum +15) against a DC of 11 + caster level of the effect. Success means that the creature is free of the spell, curse, or effect. For a curse with a DC of its own, such as a cursed magic item or a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Curse|curse affliction]], the DC is equal to the DC of the curse.

If the spell is one that cannot be dispelled by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone to Flesh|stone to flesh]]*, *break enchantment* works only if that spell is 5th level or lower.

If the effect comes from a permanent magic item, *break enchantment* does not remove the curse from the item, but it does free the victim from the item’s effects.
