# Holy Whisper

_Evocation \[Good\]_

- **Level**: Pal 3
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 30 ft.
- **Area**: Cone-shaped burst
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Fortitude; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You whisper a single word in the primordial language of good that is anathema to the minions of evil and strengthens the resolve of good creatures. Evil creatures within the burst are attacked against Fortitude, and on a hit become sickened for 1 round/level. Evil outsiders with the evil subtype, evil-aligned dragons, and undead in the burst also take 2d8 points of damage if the spell hits them. Good-aligned creatures in the burst gain a +2 sacred bonus on attack and damage rolls for 1 round.
