# Grant

_Transmutation_

- **Level**: Clr 5, Drd 5, Hun 5, Sor/Wiz 4
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: See text
- **Range**: Personal
- **Duration**: See text

Casting this spell is a free action, like casting a _[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]_ spell, and it counts toward the normal limit of one _[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]_ spell per round. If the caster casts another spell of 3rd level or lower in the same round, and that spell has a range of Personal, the caster may change that spell’s range to Touch. The spell takes effect on the touched creature instead of you.
