# Grant

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Clr 5, Drd 5, Hun 5, Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Components**: V
  - **Casting Time**: See text
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Duration**: See text

Casting this spell is a free action, like casting a *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell, and it counts toward the normal limit of one *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell per round. If the caster casts another spell of 3rd level or lower in the same round, and that spell has a range of Personal, the caster may change that spell’s range to Touch. The spell takes effect on the touched creature instead of you.
