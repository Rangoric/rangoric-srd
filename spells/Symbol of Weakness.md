# Symbol of Weakness

*Necromancy*

  - **Level**: Clr 7, Sor/Wiz 7, Wit 7

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]], except that every creature within 60 feet of a symbol of weakness instead suffers crippling weakness that deals 3d6 points of Strength damage.

Unlike symbol of death, symbol of weakness has no hit point limit; once triggered, a symbol of weakness simply remains active for 10 minutes per caster level.

*Note*: Magic traps such as symbol of weakness are hard to detect and disable. A rogue (only) can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] skill to find a symbol of weakness and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 32 for symbol of weakness.

*Material Component*: Mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal with a total value of at least 5,000 gp.
