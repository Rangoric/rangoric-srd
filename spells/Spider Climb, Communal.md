# Spider Climb, Communal

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Drd 3, Hun 3, Sor/Wiz 3
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a live spider)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creatures touched
  - **Duration**: 10 min./level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]*, except you divide the duration in 10-minute intervals among the creatures touched.
