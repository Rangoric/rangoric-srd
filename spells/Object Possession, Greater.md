# Object Possession, Greater

*Necromancy*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Target**: Unattended Gargantuan or smaller object (minimum Tiny) or construct
  - **Duration**: 10 \* caster level minute(s)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Object Possession|Object Possession]], except as noted above. The possessed animated object has a number of Construction Points appropriate for its size (up to 5 CP for Gargantuan objects). Your silver cord extends to medium range (100 ft. + 10 ft./level). You can attempt to possess a construct instead of an unattended object as your first possession with this spell. If you do, this spell acts as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Construct|Control Construct]], except as noted above.
