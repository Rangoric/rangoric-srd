# Thought Shield I

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Psy 2
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 immediate action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

Sensing an intrusion, you throw up a defense to protect your mind from attack or analysis. This grants you a +4 circumstance bonus on Will Defense against mind-affecting effects. As long as this spell lasts, spells and effects that allow a creature to read your thoughts receive no information from you.
