# Create Mindscape, Greater

*Illusion (Phantasm) \[MindAffecting\]*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 round
  - **Range**: Long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
  - **Target**: One creature/level
  - **Duration**: Caster level day(s)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will disbelief; see text
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Mindscape|create mindscape]], except it can affect more creatures, the mindscape lasts longer, and you can choose the mindscape's magic trait. You can also direct a number of believable creatures at a time equal to your caster level. Greater *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Mindscape|create mindscape]]* can be made permanent with a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Permanency|permanency]] spell by a caster of 15th level or higher at a cost of 17,500 gp. You and other creatures aware of a permanent mindscape can come and go using the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Mindscape Door|mindscape door]] spell.
