# Shriek Horrisonant

_Necromancy \[Sonic\]_

- **Level**: Clr 6
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Area**: Spread, 25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels radius spread
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Living creatures within the area whose Will the spell hits are dealt 1d3 negative levels and are frightened for one round per caster level.

Undead within the area are healed 5 hit points per four caster levels (25 maximum).
