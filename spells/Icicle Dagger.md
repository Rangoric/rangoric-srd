# Icicle Dagger

*Conjuration (Creation) \[Cold\]*

  - **Level**: Blr 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Sum 1, SumU 1, Wit 1
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 0 ft.
  - **Effect**: One icicle
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

You create a masterwork dagger out of ice. The dagger deals 1 point of cold damage in addition to normal dagger damage. If the dagger leaves your hand for more than 1 round, it melts and the spell ends. At 6th level, the dagger functions as a *+1 frost dagger*. At 11th level, it gains the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Returning|returning]]* property when thrown, melting away and reforming in your hand just before your next turn.
