# River Whip

_Conjuration (Creation) \[Water\]_

- **Level**: Mag 2, Sor/Wiz 2, Wit 2
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 0 ft.
- **Effect**: Whip of water
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level (D) or until discharged (see text)
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

You create a coil of flowing fresh water, functioning a whip appropriate for your size, except you make a melee touch attack instead of a regular attack. Anything you strike with the whip takes damage and is doused with 1 pint of water. A creature with the fire subtype takes an additional 1d6 points of damage. If the target is on fire, it gains a +2 bonus to its Reflex Defense against the fire’s next attack, and a miss extinguishes the flames.

After you have successfully used the whip to hit a creature a number of times equal to your caster level, the water is expended and the spell is discharged. The water created by this spell is otherwise identical to that produced by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]_.
