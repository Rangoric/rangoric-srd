# Summon Minor Ally

*Conjuration (Summoning)*

  - **Level**: Drd 1, Rgr 1
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 round
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Effect**: 1d3 summoned creatures
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally I]]*, except you can summon 1d3 Tiny or smaller animals, such as bats, lizards, monkeys, rats, ravens, toads, or weasels. The summoned animals must all be the same type of creature.
