# Summon Minor Ally

_Conjuration (Summoning)_

- **Level**: Drd 1, Hun 1, Rgr 1
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 round
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Effect**: 1d3 summoned creatures
- **Duration**: 1 round/level (D)
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature's ally I]]_, except you can summon 1d3 Tiny or smaller animals, such as bats, lizards, monkeys, rats, ravens, toads, or weasels. The summoned animals must all be the same type of creature.
