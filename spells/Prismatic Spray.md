# Prismatic Spray

*Evocation*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 60 ft.
  - **Area**: Cone-shaped burst
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: See text
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell causes seven shimmering, intertwined, multicolored beams of light to spray from your hand. Each beam has a different power. Creatures in the area of the spell with 8 HD or less are automatically [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 2d4 rounds. Every creature in the area is randomly struck by one or more beams, which have additional effects.

| 1d8 | Color of Beam | Effect                                                                   |
| --- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Red           | 20 points fire damage (Reflex half)                                      |
| 2   | Orange        | 40 points acid damage (Reflex half)                                      |
| 3   | Yellow        | 80 points electricity damage (Reflex half)                               |
| 4   | Green         | Poison (Kills; Fortitude partial, take 1d6 points of Con damage instead) |
| 5   | Blue          | Turned to stone (Fortitude negates)                                      |
| 6   | Indigo        | Insane, as insanity spell (Will negates)                                 |
| 7   | Violet        | Sent to another plane (Will negates)                                     |
| 8   |               | Struck by two rays; roll twice more, ignoring any “8” results.           |
