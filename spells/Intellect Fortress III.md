# Intellect Fortress III

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Psy 6
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 immediate action
- **Range**: 20 ft.
- **Area**: 20-ft.-radius emanation centered on you
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Intellect Fortress II|intellect fortress II]]_, but creatures within the area that a fear or emotion effect misses suffer no effects, even if there is usually a partial effect on a miss. This spell can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^undercasting|undercast]].
