# Terrible Remorse

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Emotion, Mind-affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 3, Clr 4, Inq 3, Mes 3, Psy 4, Skd 3, Sor/Wiz 4, Spi 4
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: 1 living creature
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Will partial (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You fill a target with such profound remorse that it begins to harm itself. Each round, the spell attacks the target, and on a hit it deals 1d8 points of damage + its Strength modifier to itself using an item held in its hand or with unarmed attacks. On a miss, it is staggered for 1 round and takes a -2 penalty to Armor Class, after which the spell ends.
