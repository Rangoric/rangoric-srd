# Good Hope

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 3, Skd 3
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Targets**: One living creature/level, no two of which may be more than 30 ft. apart
- **Duration**: 1 min./level
- **Defense**: Will (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell instills powerful hope in the subjects. Each affected creature gains a +2 morale bonus on defenses, attack rolls, ability checks, skill checks, and weapon damage rolls.

Good hope counters and dispels crushing despair.
