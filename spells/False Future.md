# False Future

_Illusion (Glamer)_

- **Level**: Brd 3, Clr 4, Med 3, Mes 3, Psy 4, Skd 3, Sor/Wiz 5, Spi 4, Wit 4
- **Components**: V, S, M (crushed jade worth 100 gp)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Creature or object touched
- **Duration**: Caster level hour(s)
- **Defense**: Will or Will disbelief (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You interfere with attempts to predict the target's future by preventing divinations from revealing what the target will do and what will befall the target while under the spell's effects. Instead of the target's true actions or experiences, divinations resolve as if the target will experience some different future you describe as you cast _false future_. The spell attacks the target creature’s Will, and a miss avoids the initial effect, and it attacks the Will of creatures using divinations, which disbelieve the illusion on a miss. This spell doesn't prevent divinations cast after the duration's end from determining what the subject actually did during the time you obscured using _false future_. _False future_ can't be detected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Identify|identify]]_, but _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic, Greater|greater detect magic]]_ can detect it.
