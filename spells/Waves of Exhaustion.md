# Waves of Exhaustion

*Necromancy*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7, Wit 7
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 60 ft.
  - **Area**: Cone-shaped burst
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: No
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Waves of negative energy cause all living creatures in the spell’s area to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]]. This spell has no effect on a creature that is already exhausted.
