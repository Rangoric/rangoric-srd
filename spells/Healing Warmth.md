# Healing Warmth

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Clr 4, Drd 4, Hun 4
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level

This spell grants you temporary immunity to fire damage as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]*. As a standard action, you may sacrifice 12 points of remaining energy absorption from the spell to heal a touched creature of 1d8 points of damage. Healing a creature provokes an attack of opportunity. When the spell has absorbed 12 points of fire damage per caster level (to a maximum of 120 points at 10th level), it is discharged.
