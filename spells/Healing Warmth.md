# Healing Warmth

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Alc 4, Clr 4, Drd 4, Hun 4, Inq 4, Psy 4
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level

This spell grants you temporary immunity to fire damage as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]_. As a standard action, you may sacrifice 12 points of remaining energy absorption from the spell to heal a touched creature of 1d8 points of damage. Healing a creature provokes an attack of opportunity. When the spell has absorbed 12 points of fire damage per caster level (to a maximum of 120 points at 10th level), it is discharged.
