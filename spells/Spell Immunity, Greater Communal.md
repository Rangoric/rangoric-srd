# Spell Immunity, Greater Communal

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Clr 9, Psy 9
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Creatures touched
- **Duration**: 10 min./level
- **Defense**: Will (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Immunity, Greater|greater spell immunity]]_, except you [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^communal-spells|divide the duration]] in 10-minute intervals among the creatures touched.
