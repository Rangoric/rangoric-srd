## Spell List

### Occultist Spells

The spells an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-spells|occultist]] can learn, all cast as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spells]], by level and then by [[rangoric-srd/class-options/implement-schools/Implement Schools|implement school]]. A spell is on an occultist’s list only once he knows its implement school ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-implements|implements]]); once he does, every spell of that school below counts as on his list for spell trigger and spell completion items.
