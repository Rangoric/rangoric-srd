# Color Spray

_Illusion (Pattern) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Blr 1, Illusion 1, Mag 1, Mes 1, Psy 1, Sor/Wiz 1
- **Components**: V, S, M
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 15 ft.
- **Area**: Cone-shaped burst
- **Duration**: Instantaneous; see text
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

A vivid cone of clashing colors springs forth from your hand, causing creatures to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], perhaps also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]], and possibly knocking them [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]].

Each creature within the cone is affected according to its Hit Dice.

_2 HD or less_: The creature is unconscious, blinded, and stunned for 2d4 rounds, then blinded and stunned for 1d4 rounds, and then stunned for 1 round. (Only living creatures are knocked unconscious.)

_3 or 4 HD_: The creature is blinded and stunned for 1d4 rounds, then stunned for 1 round.

_5 or more HD_: The creature is stunned for 1 round.

Sightless creatures are not affected by color spray.

_Material Component_: A pinch each of powder or sand that is colored red, yellow, and blue.
