# Undetectable Trap

*Illusion (Glamer)*

  - **Level**: Rgr 2
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a square of black silk worth 50 gp)
  - **Casting Time**: 10 minutes
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Trap touched
  - **Duration**: Caster level day(s)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

You shroud a single trap with a powerful illusion to make it more difficult to locate. Spells like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]* can't locate any magic aura from either the target trap or from *undetectable trap*. Furthermore, a character under the effect of the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Find Traps|find traps]]* spell doesn't receive an automatic chance to locate the target trap when she comes within 10 feet of it, and the bonus on Perception checks from *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Find Traps|find traps]]* doesn't apply to attempts to notice the target trap. Add 1/2 your caster level to the DC for any creature without the trapfinding class ability to notice the target trap with Perception checks.

A ranger with the ranger traps class feature can cast this spell on a ranger trap he creates as part of the same action he uses to prepare a ranger trap. This doesn't reduce the spell's casting time if such a ranger casts this spell on an ordinary trap, even one that he created himself using Craft (traps).
