# Intractable Grip

*Enchantment*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 3
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./level)
  - **Target**: One creature
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Any object held by the subject seems to have a life of its own. Rope, tools and other items used in conjunction with skills lose any bonuses they grant to those skills, and the subject suffers a further 5 circumstance penalty if the tools or objects are required for the use of the skill.

Spellcasters using material components or foci must succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] with a DC of 20+ spell level. In this rare case, use the Dexterity modifier in place of the Constitution modifier.

A subject that tries to use a weapon or magic item must make a Dexterity check against DC 20 or lose that action. This spell has little effect on a subject that holds nothing, but even the act of drawing a weapon could fail.
