# Etheric Shards

_Evocation \[Force\]_

- **Level**: Occ 4, Psy 5, Spi 4
- **Components**: S, M (broken glass)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Area**: One 10-ft. cube/level
- **Duration**: 1 hour/level (D)
- **Defense**: Reflex partial or Reflex (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: No

You harden interpenetrated ethereal substance into deadly transdimensional razors that are invisible to normal sight. Movement through an area of _etheric shards_ is halved, even for incorporeal creatures, and creatures entering a 5-foot cube filled with _etheric shards_ take 1d8 points of piercing and slashing damage and the shards attack their Reflex; on a hit they take 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage. This bleed damage stacks with itself and other sources of bleed damage. A creature standing within an area of _etheric shards_ takes no damage as long as it remains completely motionless, but even the minor movements involved in attacking or defending in combat make the shards attack a stationary creature’s Reflex. On a miss, it avoids damage completely for that round, but on a hit, it takes hit point damage and bleed damage as though it had moved through the square.

A creature forcibly moved through an area of _etheric shards_, such as by a bull rush or drag combat maneuver, takes a -4 penalty to its Reflex Defense against them, but a creature able to see invisible or ethereal objects gains a +4 bonus, and damage to it is completely negated on a miss.

_Etheric shards_ are a magic trap, and a creature with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trapfinding|trapfinding]] can use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] to find them. The DC of this check is equal to 25 + the spell's level. _Etheric shards_ can't be disabled with the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] skill.
