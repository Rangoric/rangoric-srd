# Symbol of Laughter

*Enchantment (Charm) \[Mind-affecting\]*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 4, Wit 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M (mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal worth a total of 1,000 gp)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]]*, except that all creatures within 60 feet are affected with uncontrollable laughter (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hideous Laughter|hideous laughter]]*) for 1 round per caster level.

Unlike *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]]*, *symbol of laughter* has no hit point limit; once it is triggered, a *symbol of laughter* remains active for 1 minute per caster level.

*Symbol of laughter* can be made permanent with a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Permanency|permanency]]* spell by a caster of 10th level or higher at a cost of 5,000 gp.
