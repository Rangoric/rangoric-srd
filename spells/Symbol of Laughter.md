# Symbol of Laughter

_Enchantment (Charm) \[Mind-affecting\]_

- **Level**: Mes 3, Occ 3, Sor/Wiz 4, Wit 4
- **Components**: V, S, M (mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal worth a total of 1,000 gp)
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]]_, except that all creatures within 60 feet are affected with uncontrollable laughter (as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hideous Laughter|hideous laughter]]_) for 1 round per caster level.

Unlike _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]]_, _symbol of laughter_ has no hit point limit; once it is triggered, a _symbol of laughter_ remains active for 1 minute per caster level.

_Symbol of laughter_ can be made permanent with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Permanency|permanency]]_ spell by a caster of 10th level or higher at a cost of 5,000 gp.
