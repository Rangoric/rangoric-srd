# Restoration

*Conjuration (Healing)*

  - **Level**: Alc 4, Clr 4, Inq 4, Pal 4, Sha 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]], except that it also dispels temporary [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative levels]] or one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^permanent-negative-levels|permanent negative level]]. If this spell is used to dispel a permanent negative level, it has a material component of diamond dust worth 1,000 gp. This spell cannot be used to dispel more than one permanent negative level possessed by a target in a 1-week period.

Restoration cures all temporary [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|ability damage]], and it restores all points permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drained]] from a single ability score (your choice if more than one is drained). It also eliminates any fatigue or exhaustion suffered by the target.

*Material Component*: Diamond dust worth 100 gp (or 1,000 gp to dispel a permanent negative level) that is sprinkled over the target.
