# Glue Seal

_Conjuration (Creation)_

- **Level**: Brd 1, Mag 1, Psy 1, Skd 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Sum 1, SumU 1
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One object or one 5-ft. square
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level (D)
- **Defense**: See text
- **Spell Resistance**: No

You cover a solid surface with a layer of sticky glue. The spell attacks the Reflex of anyone in the area when it is cast. Those it hits become entangled, but can break free by succeeding at a combat maneuver check or an Escape Artist check as a standard action against a DC of 10 + this spell’s attack bonus. The area of the spell is considered difficult terrain. A creature moving through the glue must succeed at a combat maneuver check or an Escape Artist check as part of its move action (DC = 10 + the spell's attack bonus). Creatures that fail lose their movement and become entangled in the first square they enter.

The spell can also be used to create a sticky coating on an item. The spell attacks the Reflex of a creature holding a targeted item. If this first attack misses, the item is unaffected. If it hits, the item is stuck in place. If an affected item is connected to another item (such as a sword in a sheath or a cork in a potion bottle), it cannot be separated from the other item unless the creature succeeds at a combat maneuver check or a Strength check as a move action to free it (DC = 10 + the spell's attack bonus). The spell attacks a creature’s Reflex each time it attempts to use the sticky item; on a hit, the item becomes stuck to the creature. Sticky armor or clothing imposes a –10 circumstance penalty on Escape Artist checks and combat maneuver checks to escape a grapple attempted by the wearer, as well as to the wearer's CMD to avoid being grappled.
