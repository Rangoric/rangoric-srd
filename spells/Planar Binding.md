# Planar Binding

*Conjuration (Calling) \[see text for lesser planar binding\]*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 6
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Targets**: Up to three elementals or outsiders, totaling no more than 12 HD, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart when they appear

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding, Lesser|lesser planar binding]], except that you may call a single creature of 12 HD or less, or up to three creatures of the same kind whose Hit Dice total no more than 12. Each creature gets a save, makes an independent attempt to escape, and must be individually persuaded to aid you.
