# Vocal Alteration

_Transmutation_

- **Level**: Alc 1, Brd 1, Inq 1, Med 1, Mes 1, Occ 1, Psy 1, Skd 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Wit 1
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One humanoid creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You alter the target's voice to something else. For example, you could make the target's voice high-pitched, husky, or nasal, or change its accent to an accent you are familiar with. If this spell is used as part of a disguise, the target gets a +10 bonus on the Imitation check when trying to fool a listener.

The target can vary the disguised voice just as it could its normal voice. For example, a halfling female given a male dwarf noble's voice and accent could speak in falsetto, with a rural halfling accent, and so on.
