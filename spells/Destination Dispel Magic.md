# Destination Dispel Magic

*Abjuration \[Teleportation\]*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Components**: V
  - **Casting Time**: See text
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Duration**: See text

Casting this spell is a free action, like casting a *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell, and it counts toward the normal limit of one *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell per round. If the caster casts another spell in the same round, and that spell has the Teleportation descriptor, then an *area dispel*, exactly as described in *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*, takes effect an instant before the teleported creature or object appears at the destination. The maximum bonus to the dispel check is +10.
