# Charm Person, Mass

*Enchantment (Charm) \[MindAffecting\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 4, Skd 4, Sor/Wiz 5, Wit 5
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Target**: One or more humanoid creatures, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
  - **Duration**: Caster level hour(s)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]], except that mass *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]* affects a number of humanoid creatures whose combined Hit Dice don't exceed twice your level. If there are more potential targets than you can affect, you choose them one at a time until you reach the limit of HD you can affect. If you cast mass *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]* on only one creature, you ignore the spell's HD limit.
