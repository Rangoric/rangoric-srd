# Vengeful Gaze of God

Transmutation   
Casting DC: 419  
Components: V, S  
Casting Time: 1 standard action  
Range: 12,000 ft.  
Target: One creature, or up to a 10-foot cube of nonliving matter in line of sight  
Duration: Instantaneous  
Saving Throw: Fortitude half  
Spell Resistance: Yes  
To Develop: 3,771,000 gp; 76 days; 150,840 XP. Seed: destroy (DC 29). Factor: increase damage to 305d6 (+570 DC), 1-action casting time (+20 DC). Mitigating factor: 200d6 backlash (-200 DC).  

The target of this spell is subject to 305d6 points of damage (or half of that if a Fortitude save succeeds). If the damage [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#^death|kills]] the target (or reduces a construct, object, or undead to 0 hit points), it is utterly destroyed as if disintegrated, leaving behind only a trace of fine dust. The caster is likewise dealt 200d6 points of damage
