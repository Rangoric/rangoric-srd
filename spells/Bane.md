# Bane

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Fear, Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Apl 1, Clr 1, Inq 1, Mes 1, Sha 1
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 50 ft.
- **Area**: All enemies within 50 ft.
- **Duration**: 1 min./level
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Bane fills your enemies with fear and doubt. Each affected creature takes a –1 penalty on attack rolls and a –1 penalty on defenses against fear effects.

Bane counters and dispels bless.
