# Targeted Bomb Admixture

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Alc 1
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level

Upon drinking an extract created with this formula, you make a significant change to your magical reserve that modifies the nature of all [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bombs]] you create and throw during this extract's duration. This effect on your magical reserve has no effect on any [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discoveries]] that you use to modify your bombs, but you can only have one admixture effect (formula with the word “bomb admixture” in its title) active at a time. If you drink another bomb admixture, the effects of the former bomb admixture end and the new one becomes active.

When you throw bombs, they can only hit a direct target; they do not splash. However, the bomb deals its base damage plus double your Intelligence modifier instead of just its base damage plus your Intelligence modifier.
