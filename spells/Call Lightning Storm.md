# Call Lightning Storm

*Evocation \[Electricity\]*

  - **Level**: Drd 5, Hun 5, Sha 5
  - **Range**: Long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning|call lightning]], except that each bolt deals 5d6 points of electricity damage (or 5d10 if created outdoors in a stormy area), and you may call a maximum of 15 bolts.
