# Faerie’s Gold

*Illusion (Glamer)*

  - **Level**: Brd 1, Fpk 1, Skd 1, Sor/Wiz 1
  - **Components**: V, S, M
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Target**: 144 cu. in./level
  - **Duration**: 1 hr./level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fool’s Gold (Faeries)|fool’s gold]]*, but this spell transforms 144 cubic inches of materials per level of the caster into solid gold. The area of effect (1 in. x 12 in. x 12 in.) is equivalent to about 1800 coins. Any creature seeing the resulting transformed metal is entitled to a Will save. If successful, the creature sees the metal for what it really is.

*Material Component*: Though not required, powdered gemstones used in the casting of this spell aid in its success.
