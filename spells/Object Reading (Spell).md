# Object Reading

_Divination_

- **Level**: Med 1, Mes 2, Occ 1, Psy 2, Spi 2
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: One touched object
- **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 round/level (D)
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

You read the psychic impressions left upon an object by emotionally or psychically charged events in the item's history, or the information imprinted by the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charge Object|charge object]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Implant False Reading|implant false reading]]_ spells. This spell returns the same information as the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise#Psychometry|psychometry]] occult skill unlock, but gives 1 piece of information when cast and requires 1 round of concentration per additional piece of information instead of 1 minute. You must still attempt an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] check to see how much information you gain. You gain a +10 competence bonus on the check.
