## Spell List

### Hunter Spells

The spells a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-spells|hunter]] can learn: the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Druid Spell List|druid spells]] of 6th level and lower and the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ranger Spell List|ranger spells]], at the lower level when a spell is on both. The hunter’s class note holds that rule; this list collects what it gives.
