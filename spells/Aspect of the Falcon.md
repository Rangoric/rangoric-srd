# Aspect of the Falcon

*Transmutation (Polymorph)*

  - **Level**: Drd 1, Rgr 1
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level

You take on an aspect of a falcon. Your eyes become wide and raptor-like, and you grow feathers on the sides of your head. You gain a +3 competence bonus on Perception checks, a +1 competence bonus on ranged attacks, and the critical multiplier for your bows and crossbows becomes 19–20/×3. This effect does not stack with any other effect that expands the threat range of a weapon, such as the Improved Critical feat or a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Keen|keen]]* weapon.
