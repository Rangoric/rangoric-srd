# Cackling Skull

_Illusion (Glamer)_

- **Level**: Wit 3
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One skull
- **Duration**: Permanent until discharged
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Mouth|magic mouth]]_, except rather than creating an illusory mouth on any surface, it affects a skull. In addition, when the skull's magic is triggered, it releases wild and unnerving cackles before speaking its message. The cackles attack the Will of all creatures that can hear them, and on a hit they become shaken for 1d4 rounds.
