# False Life, Greater

_Necromancy_

- **Level**: Alc 4, Blr 4, Med 3, Mes 3, Occ 4, Psy 4, Sha 4, Sor/Wiz 4, Spi 4, Wit 4
- **Components**: V, S, M (a drop of blood)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 hour/level or until discharged; see text

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life|false life]]_, except you gain temporary hit points equal to 2d10 + 1 point per caster level (maximum +20). The effects of this spell do not stack with those of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life|false life]]_.
