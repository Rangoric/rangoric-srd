# False Life, Greater

*Necromancy*

  - **Level**: Sha 4, Sor/Wiz 4, Wit 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a drop of blood)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level or until discharged; see text

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life|false life]]*, except you gain temporary hit points equal to 2d10 + 1 point per caster level (maximum +20). The effects of this spell do not stack with those of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/False Life|false life]]*.
