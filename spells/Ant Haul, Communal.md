# Ant Haul, Communal

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Alc 2, Clr 2, Drd 2, Hun 2, Rgr 2, Sor/Wiz 2
  - **Components**: V, S, M/DF (a small pulley)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creatures touched
  - **Duration**: 2 hours/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ant Haul|ant haul]]*, except you may divide the duration in 2-hour intervals among the creatures touched.
