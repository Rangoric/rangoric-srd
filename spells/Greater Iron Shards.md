# Greater Iron Shards

*Conjuration (Creation) \[Cold Iron\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 4, Skd 4, Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Effect**: Ray of needle-like shards of iron
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You unleash a small spray of iron shards from your hand directly along your line of sight at your target. You must make a single ranged attack to hit a single target. If you miss, there is no damage due to scattering shards. If you have three or more dice, the shards can be split among additional targets, provided they are within the path of the spell and along the same line of sight. Each target requires a separate ranged attack roll, regardless of how many shards fly toward each target. If the attack roll misses, the target suffers no damage.

Non-fey creatures take 1d4 points of damage per caster level + the caster’s level, while fey creatures take 1d6 points of damage per caster level + the caster’s level, to a maximum of ten dice (as in the example below, a 9th-level caster deals 9d6+9 to a fey creature). The damage counts as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^cold-iron-descriptor|cold iron]].

The shards are non-magical cold iron, and remain in the target for one round per level of the caster. It takes 1 round to remove a damage die worth of shards. Embedded iron shards do an additional point of damage per die per round to fey targets until removed. For example, a fey creature is the target of *greater iron shards* cast by a 9th level caster. The initial damage is 9d6+9 (2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 6, and 6 +9) totaling 44 points of damage. The creature decides to remove the shards rather than take the additional damage. It will take nine rounds to remove the shards, causing the creature to suffer an additional 45 points of damage (9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1), totaling 89 points of damage. If the creature had instead decided to “wait out” the life of the shards, the damage would be 81 points instead, totaling 95 points of damage.

*Material Component*: Two iron slivers or sewing needles thrown at the target.
