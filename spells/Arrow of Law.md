# Arrow of Law

_Evocation \[Lawful\]_

- **Level**: Clr 2, Pal 2
- **Components**: V, S, DF (a holy symbol)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Effect**: Arrow-shaped projectile of lawful energy
- **Duration**: Instantaneous (1 round); see text
- **Defense**: Will partial (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You fire a shimmering arrow of pure order from your holy symbol at any one target in range as a ranged touch attack. A chaotic creature struck by an _arrow of law_ takes 1d8 points of damage per two caster levels (maximum 5d8). A chaotic outsider instead takes 1d6 points of damage per caster level (maximum 10d6) and is dazed for 1 round. The arrow attacks Will, and a miss reduces the damage to half and negates the daze effect. This spell deals only half damage to creatures that are neither chaotic nor lawful, and they are not dazed. The arrow has no effect on lawful creatures.
