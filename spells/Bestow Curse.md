# Bestow Curse

_Necromancy \[Curse\]_

- **Level**: Apl 3, Blr 4, Clr 3, Fpk 3, Med 2, Mes 3, Msl 3, Occ 3, Sha 3, Sor/Wiz 4, Spi 3, Wit 3
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Creature touched
- **Duration**: Permanent
- **Saving Throw**: Will negates
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You place a curse on the subject. Choose one of the following three effects.

- –6 decrease to an ability score (minimum 1).

- –4 penalty on attack rolls, saves, ability checks, and skill checks.

- Each turn, the target has a 50% chance to act normally; otherwise, it takes no action.

You may also invent your own curse, but it should be no more powerful than those described above.

The curse bestowed by this spell cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ spell.

_Bestow curse_ counters _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_.
