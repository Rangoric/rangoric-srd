# Ego Whip V

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Emotion, Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Psy 7
- **Components**: S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Will partial
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ego Whip I|ego whip I]]_, but the target takes -10 penalty to the chosen ability score and is staggered for 1d10 rounds on a hit. This spell can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^undercasting|undercast]].
