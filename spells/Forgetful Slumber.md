# Forgetful Slumber

*Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-affecting\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 4, Skd 4, Sor/Wiz 4, Wit 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a few drops of river water)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 round
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Target**: One living creature
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell acts as the *deeper slumber* spell, but only affects one creature of 10 Hit Dice or fewer. In addition, a creature affected by this spell awakens with no knowledge of the events that led to the spell's casting. The target loses all memory from the 5 minutes prior to falling asleep. No effect short of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]* can restore memories lost by this spell.
