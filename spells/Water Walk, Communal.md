# Water Walk, Communal

_Transmutation \[Water\]_

- **Level**: Clr 4, Hun 4, Psy 4, Rgr 4
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Creatures touched
- **Duration**: 10 min./level (D)
- **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Walk|water walk]]_, except you [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^communal-spells|divide the duration]] in 10-minute intervals among the creatures touched.
