# Water Walk, Communal

*Transmutation \[Water\]*

  - **Level**: Clr 4, Hun 4, Rgr 4
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creatures touched
  - **Duration**: 10 min./level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Walk|water walk]]*, except you divide the duration in 10-minute intervals among the creatures touched.
