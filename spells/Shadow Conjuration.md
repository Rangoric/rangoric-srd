# Shadow Conjuration

_Illusion (Shadow)_

- **Level**: Brd 4, Mes 4, Skd 4, Sor/Wiz 4, Spi 4
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: See text
- **Effect**: See text
- **Duration**: See text
- **Defense**: Will disbelief (if interacted with); varies; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes; see text

You use material from the Plane of Shadow to shape quasi-real illusions of one or more creatures, objects, or forces. Shadow conjuration can mimic any sorcerer or wizard conjuration (summoning) or conjuration (creation) spell of 3rd level or lower.

Shadow conjurations are actually one-fifth (20%) as strong as the real things, though creatures who believe the shadow conjurations to be real are affected by them at full strength.

The conjured object, force, or creature attacks the Will of any creature that interacts with it, which recognizes its true nature on a miss.

Spells that deal damage have normal effects if they hit the affected creature’s Will; on a miss, the creature disbelieves. Each disbelieving creature takes only one-fifth (20%) damage from the attack. If the disbelieved attack has a special effect other than damage, that effect is only 20% likely to occur. Whether or not the attack to be disbelieved hits, the spell being simulated also attacks any defense it normally attacks, but its attack bonus is set according to shadow conjuration’s level (5th) rather than the spell’s normal level. In addition, any effect created by shadow conjuration allows spell resistance, even if the spell it is simulating does not. Shadow objects or substances have normal effects except against those who disbelieve them.

Against disbelievers, they are 20% likely to work.

A shadow creature has one-fifth the hit points of a normal creature of its kind (regardless of whether it’s recognized as shadowy). It deals normal damage and has all normal abilities and weaknesses. Against a creature that recognizes it as a shadow creature, however, the shadow creature’s damage is one-fifth (20%) normal, and all special abilities that do not deal lethal damage are only 20% likely to work. (Roll for each use and each affected character separately.) Furthermore, the shadow creature’s AC bonuses are one-fifth as large.

A creature the spell misses sees the shadow conjurations as transparent images superimposed on vague, shadowy forms.

This spell automatically misses objects’ Will.
