# Icy Prison

_Evocation \[Cold\]_

- **Level**: Sor/Wiz 5
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (10 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level; see text
- **Defense**: Reflex partial
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You trap the target in solid ice 1 inch thick per caster level. If the spell hits the creature, it is helpless, but can still breathe (the ice blocks line of effect to the target). If it misses, the target gains the entangled condition but can otherwise act normally. Whether the spell hits or misses, the target takes 1 point of cold damage per caster level each round it is helpless or entangled in the ice. The ice has hardness 0 and 3 hit points per inch of thickness; if broken, the creature is freed. A creature can break the ice as a full-round action with a successful Strength check (DC 15 + your caster level).
