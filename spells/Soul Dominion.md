# Soul Dominion

Divination, Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]  
Casting DC: 72  
Components: V, S  
Casting Time: 10 minutes  
Range: See text  
Target: One other living creature  
Duration: 20 minutes (D)  
Defense: Will (see text)  
Spell Resistance: No  
To Develop: 648,000 gp; 13 days; 25,920 XP. Seeds: contact (DC 23), reveal (DC 19), compel (DC 19). Factors: apply to all five senses (+8 DC), total compulsory control (+10 DC), stricter compulsion of any creature (ad hoc +11 DC). Mitigating factor: increase casting time by 9 minutes (-18 DC).

When a character casts this spell, he or she is temporarily able to take control of another sentient creature with whom the character is familiar (by meeting, observing, or successfully scrying the subject). The spell attacks the target’s Will, and a miss prevents the character from making the telepathic connection. The target is aware of the attempted takeover as a strange, momentary tingling. On a hit, the character is able to control the subject’s body as if it were his or her own, hearing, seeing, feeling, smelling, and tasting everything the target senses. Once the character dismisses the spell or its duration ends, the target resumes control of its body, fully aware of all events that occurred, having been a helpless witness trapped inside its own body. The target knows the name and general nature of its possessor if a second attack against its Will misses. A character cannot control undead or incorporeal creatures with soul dominion.
