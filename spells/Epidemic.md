# Epidemic

_Necromancy \[Disease, Evil\]_

- **Level**: Clr 6, Drd 6, Hun 6, Sor/Wiz 7, Wit 6
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: Living creature
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The target contracts one of the following diseases: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Blinding Sickness|blinding sickness]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Bubonic Plague|bubonic plague]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Cackle Fever|cackle fever]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Leprosy|leprosy]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Mindfire|mindfire]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Red Ache|red ache]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Shakes|shakes]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Slimy Doom|slimy doom]], as the spell _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]_. However, the disease is highly contagious, and any creature that touches the infected target, is touched by the target, or spends more than an hour in a confined space with the target is attacked by the disease with its normal attack bonus (not the spell’s), and contracts the disease on a hit. If the initial target overcomes the disease with the required number of misses, it remains a carrier of the disease for a length of time equal to the disease's frequency, and can continue to infect others during this time.
