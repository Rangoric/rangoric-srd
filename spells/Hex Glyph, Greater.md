# Hex Glyph, Greater

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Wit 5
  - **Components**: V, S, M (powdered diamond worth 400 gp)
  - **Saving Throw**: See text
  - **Spell Resistance**: No (object) and yes (see text)

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hex Glyph|hex glyph]]*, except you can store a hex or a major hex (but not a grand hex). You trace the glyph with incense, which must first be sprinkled with powdered diamond (as noted in the material line).

*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|Read magic]]* allows its caster to identify a *greater hex glyph* with a DC 16 Arcana check.
