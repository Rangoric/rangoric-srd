# Archon’s Aura

_Evocation \[Good, Lawful\]_

- **Level**: Clr 3, Pal 3
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 20 ft.
- **Area**: 20-ft. radius centered on you
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You gain a powerful aura, similar to an archon's aura of menace. This aura attacks the Will of any hostile creature within a 20-foot radius of you. On a hit, the creature takes a –2 penalty on attack rolls and defenses and to Armor Class for the duration of this spell, or until it successfully hits you with an attack. A creature that has resisted or broken the effect cannot be affected again by this particular casting of _archon's aura_.
