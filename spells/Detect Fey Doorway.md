# Detect Fey Doorway

*Divination*

  - **Level**: Brd 1, Clr 1, Drd 1, Fey Roads 1, Fhn 2, Hun 1, Msl 2, Rgr 2, Skd 1, Sor/Wiz 2
  - **Components**: V, S, F
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: See text
  - **Area**: Cone emanating from you to the extreme of the range
  - **Duration**: Concentration up to 10 minutes/level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Doorway|detect doorway]]*, but specifically in reference to fey *doorways* that lead to the fey realm. With the specific focus of this spell, the caster can see the *doorway* in the mortal world and can also get a vague visual idea of what the other end of the *sideroad* looks like (and possibly know where it goes if well versed in the fey realm’s geography).

*Arcane Focus*: a dowsing stick (forked wooden stick).
