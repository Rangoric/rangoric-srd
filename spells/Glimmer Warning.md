# Glimmer Warning

*Evocation \[Light\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 1, Clr 1, Drd 1, Hun 1, Skd 1, Sor/Wiz 1
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell provides both a moment’s early warning and a tiny bit of illumination when the caster becomes the target of a spell. While this spell is in effect, any time the caster is targeted by a spell, a 5-foot circle of light surrounds him. This circle is bright enough to allow mirror magic to work and provides a +1 circumstance bonus to any attempts by a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Reflecting Master|reflecting master]] to deflect the incoming spell ([[rangoric-srd/rules/mirror-magic/Mirror Magic#Reflection|reflection]]). This spell suppresses *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]* or similar spells during the round in which it creates light.
