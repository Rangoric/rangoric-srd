# Mental Barrier IV

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Psy 5
  - **Components**: V
  - **Casting Time**: 1 immediate action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 round
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mental Barrier III|mental barrier III]]*, but if you are struck by a critical hit or sneak attack, there is a 25% chance that the additional damage is negated (as [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|light fortification]]). This does not stack with similar effects that negate the additional damage from a critical hit or sneak attack. In addition, you are immune to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust I|mind thrust I]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust II|mind thrust II]]*, and you take half damage from *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust III|mind thrust III]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust IV|mind thrust IV]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust V|mind thrust V]]* (reduced to one-quarter on a successful Will save). This spell can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^undercasting|undercast]].
