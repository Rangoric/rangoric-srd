# Mental Barrier IV

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Psy 5
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 immediate action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Saving Throw**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mental Barrier III|mental barrier III]]_, but if you are struck by a critical hit or sneak attack, there is a 25% chance that the additional damage is negated (as [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|light fortification]]). This does not stack with similar effects that negate the additional damage from a critical hit or sneak attack. In addition, you are immune to _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust I|mind thrust I]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust II|mind thrust II]]_, and you take half damage from _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust III|mind thrust III]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust IV|mind thrust IV]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust V|mind thrust V]]_ (reduced to one-quarter on a successful Will save). This spell can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^undercasting|undercast]].
