# Delay Poison, Communal

*Conjuration (Healing)*

  - **Level**: Brd 3, Clr 3, Drd 3, Pal 3, Rgr 3, Wit 3
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creatures touched
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delay Poison|delay poison]]*, except you divide the duration in 1-hour intervals among the creatures touched.
