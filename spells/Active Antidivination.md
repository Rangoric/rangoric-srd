# Active Antidivination

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 6
  - **Components**: V, S, F
  - **Casting Time**: 1 round
  - **Area**: Personal
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

Spells such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]* passively protect the caster from divination spells. *Active antidivination* prevents any divination spell, spell-like ability, or item ability from functioning if it includes the caster in its area of effect. Divination spells cast upon the caster, or that include the caster in their area of effect, are countered as if counterspelled. If this spell is cast after a divination spell has been cast, and if that divination is still functioning on or around the caster, the divination is subject to a dispel check, with the caster’s effective level increased by half (+10 maximum). If an item was used to scry upon the caster (such as a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Crystal Ball|crystal ball]]*), the item ceases to function for one full round, after which it begins functioning but is unable to scry upon any creature currently affected by this spell.

*Arcane focus:* a small silver spring, twisted so that its ends meet.
