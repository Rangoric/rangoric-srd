# Immolation

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 6
  - **Area**: a cylinder 10 ft. high and 15 ft. diameter centered on the caster

As the warm version of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Shield|fire shield]]*, except that the flames extend 10 feet high and 5 feet from you in every direction. If the caster is struck by a natural or melee weapon attack, this effect deals 2d6 points of damage plus 1 point per caster level; all damage dealt is fire damage. Creatures using reach weapons take damage when striking the caster unless the reach of the weapon is 15 feet or greater.
