# Ooze Form III

*Transmutation (Polymorph)*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a bit of the creature whose form you plan to assume)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: Caster level minute(s)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ooze Form II|ooze form II]] except you can also assume the form of a Huge ooze. You gain blindsight 60 feet and a +8 resistance bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting effects and poison as well.

**Huge Ooze**: If you assume this form, you gain a +4 size bonus to your Strength, a +10 size bonus to your Constitution score, a -10 penalty to your Dexterity score, base speed 20 feet, climb speed 20 feet, swim speed 30 feet, a slam attack (2d6 plus 2d6 acid and grab), and constrict (2d6).
