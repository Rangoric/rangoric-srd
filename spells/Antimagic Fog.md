# Antimagic Fog

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level

As _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]_, except that the area within the fog behaves as an _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antimagic Field|antimagic field]]_. The vapors created by the spell are indistinguishable from those created by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]_. Magical winds do not dissipate the fog, but they do push it 1 ft. each round per mile an hour of wind speed. A wind blowing at 10 mph, for example, pushes the cloud 10 ft. each round in the direction the wind is blowing.
