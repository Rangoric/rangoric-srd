# Antimagic Fog

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Duration**: 1 minute/level

As *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]*, except that the area within the fog behaves as an *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antimagic Field|antimagic field]]*. The vapors created by the spell are indistinguishable from those created by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]*. Magical winds do not dissipate the fog, but they do push it 1 ft. each round per mile an hour of wind speed. A wind blowing at 10 mph, for example, pushes the cloud 10 ft. each round in the direction the wind is blowing.
