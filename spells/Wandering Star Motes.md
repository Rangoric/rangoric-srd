# Wandering Star Motes

_Illusion (Pattern) \[Light, Mind-affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 4, Occ 4, Psy 4, Sha 4, Skd 4, Sor/Wiz 4, Wit 4
- **Components**: V, S, M (a sprinkle of flash powder)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One living creature and special; see text
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Will; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You create sparkling motes of bright light that shoot toward the target and swirl around it in a complex pattern. The pattern clearly outlines the target and radiates light as if it were a sunrod, negating any concealment for the target. The spell attacks the target’s Will. If it hits, the target is dazed for 1 round, and the spell attacks again on its next turn, dazing it again for 1 round on a hit. The spell continues attacking the target’s Will each round. If the spell misses a target, the _wandering star motes_ jump to the nearest enemy within 30 feet, whose Will they now attack every round, dazing it on a hit. Any time the spell misses a target, the _wandering star motes_ jump to the next nearest enemy within 30 feet. A given creature can only be affected by the _wandering star motes_ once; once the spell has missed a target, it cannot be affected again. If there are no new targets within 30 feet of a target the spell has missed, the spell immediately ends. The spell only affects enemy creatures; your allies are not affected.
