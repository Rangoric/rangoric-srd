# Magical Deviation

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 3 rounds
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell causes any spell that targets you to swerve unpredictably away rather than striking you as intended. The spell’s effect deviates as if it were a grenadelike weapon; the distance of the deviation is equal to one-half of the distance between the caster of the spell and you. Spells that require a single target, rather than spells with an area of effect, retarget on the next available subject closest to the point of deviation, provided there is a valid target within the spell’s range. Casting this spell is draining and leaves the caster shaken for 3 rounds following its casting.
