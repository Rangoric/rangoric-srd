# Astral Projection, Lesser

*Necromancy*

  - **Level**: Clr 5, Sor/Wiz 5
  - **Components**: V, S, M (jacinth worth 1,000 gp)
  - **Casting Time**: 30 minutes
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Targets**: You plus one additional willing creature touched per two caster levels
  - **Duration**: See text
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection|astral projection]]*, except you cannot leave the Astral Plane and explore other planes (though you can still return to the plane you were on when you cast this spell).
