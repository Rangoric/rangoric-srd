# Freedom

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 9
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels) or see text
  - **Target**: One creature
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The subject is freed from spells and effects that restrict its movement, including [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spells P to R|binding]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangle]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^grappling|grappling]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Imprisonment|imprisonment]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Maze|maze]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralysis]], petrification, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^pinned|pinning]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Slow|slow]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunning]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Temporal Stasis|temporal stasis]], and [[rangoric-srd/spells/Web|web]]. To free a creature from imprisonment or maze, you must know its name and background, and you must cast this spell at the spot where it was entombed or banished into the maze.
