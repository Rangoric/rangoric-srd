# Gaze Trigger

*Divination*

  - **Level**: Brd 5, Clr 5, Drd 5, Hun 5, Skd 5, Sor/Wiz 5
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 move action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: One magic mirror
  - **Duration**: 1 week/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

When this spell is cast upon a magic mirror ([[rangoric-srd/rules/mirror-magic/Mirror Magic|mirror magic]]), another spell stored in the same mirror is named. When the mirror is looked upon for more than a brief moment, the named spell is cast on the individual gazing into the mirror’s surface. The activated spell is treated as if it were cast by the same being that cast the *gaze trigger*. While the spell is used as a trap, it can be used to cast a protective or beneficial spell from a mirror—looking into the mirror requires a standard action, but no other effort.
