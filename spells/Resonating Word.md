# Resonating Word

_Transmutation \[Sonic\]_

- **Level**: Brd 5, Psy 7, Skd 5, Sor/Wiz 7
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: 3 rounds
- **Defense**: Fortitude partial
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You speak a terrible word of power, setting up potentially lethal vibrations in the chosen target. The spell attacks the target’s Fortitude once each round on your turn, and the effects grow stronger each time it hits.

On the first round, the target takes 5d6 points of sonic damage and is staggered for 1 round. A miss halves the damage and negates the staggered effect.

On the second round, the target takes 5d6 points of damage and is stunned for 1 round. A miss halves the damage and negates the stunning effect.

On the third round, the target takes 10d6 points of damage and is stunned for 1d4+1 rounds. A miss halves the damage and negates the stunning effect.

The resonating word has no power after the third round, even if the spell's duration is increased.
