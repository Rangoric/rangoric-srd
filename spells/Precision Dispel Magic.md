# Precision Dispel Magic

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Sor/Wiz 4
- **Targets**: One creature or object, plus one creature or object/5 levels

As _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_, except that the caster may make several targeted dispel checks. The caster can affect one target, plus an additional target per five caster levels. No individual target can be the subject of more than one dispel check. All targets must be within 30 feet of one another.
