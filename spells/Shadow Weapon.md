# Shadow Weapon

_Illusion (Shadow) \[Shadow\]_

- **Level**: Blr 1, Occ 1, Psy 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Wit 1
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 0 ft.
- **Effect**: One shadow weapon
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: Will disbelief (if interacted with)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Drawing upon the Plane of Shadow, you shape a quasi-real masterwork melee weapon of a type you are proficient with. You may use this weapon to make attacks as if it were a real weapon, dealing normal damage for a weapon of its type. The first time you hit a creature with the weapon, the weapon attacks its Will to be believed; a hit means the weapon deals damage normally, a miss means it only takes 1 point of damage from the weapon's attacks. The weapon only deals 1 point of damage to objects.

If an attacked creature has spell resistance, you make a caster level check (1d20 + caster level) against that spell resistance the first time the _shadow weapon_ strikes it. If the weapon is successfully resisted, the spell is dispelled. If not, the weapon attacks the target’s Will to be believed as normal.

At 5th level, the weapon gains a +1 enhancement bonus. At 10th-level, you may increase the enhancement bonus to +2 or add the _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Frost|frost]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Keen|keen]]_ weapon property. The frost and keen properties have no effect if the weapon’s attack against the target’s Will misses.

The spell ends if the weapon leaves your possession.
