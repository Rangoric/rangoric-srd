# Feast of Ashes

_Transmutation \[Curse\]_

- **Level**: Drd 2, Hun 2, Occ 2, Psy 2, Wit 2
- **Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of ash)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft/2 levels)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: 2 days/level (D)
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You curse the target with a hunger no food can assuage. On a hit, the target begins to starve. The effects of this spell cannot inflict more nonlethal damage than the target has hit points. No amount of eating can counter the effect and, further, even attempting to eat makes the spell attack the target’s Fortitude at +2, and on a hit the target is nauseated for 1 round. The target still experiences the other normal effects of anything consumed (such as poisons or potions). _Feast of ashes_ can be removed with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ spell, but effects suffered as a result of starvation must be healed by normal means.
