# Unshakable Zeal

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Emotion, Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 5, Inq 6, Psy 7, Skd 5, Spi 6
- **Components**: S, F
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Target**: Creature touched
- **Duration**: Caster level hour(s)
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You fill the target with boundless enthusiasm and faith in its ultimate triumph. Whenever the target fails an attack roll, a skill check, a concentration check, or an ability check, or an attack hits one of its defenses, the target receives a +4 morale bonus on its next attempt at the failed check, or to that defense against the next such attack, within 1 round (this includes attack rolls against the same foe, attacks against the same defense from the same foe, and so on). In addition, when the target would be affected by a fear or emotion effect, it can instead dismiss _unshakable zeal_ without spending an action to negate the effect on itself.
