# Symbol of Stunning

*Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]*

  - **Level**: Clr 7, Sor/Wiz 7
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]], except that all creatures within 60 feet of a symbol of stunning instead become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d6 rounds.

*Note*: Magic traps such as symbol of stunning are hard to detect and disable. A rogue (only) can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] skill to find a symbol of stunning and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 32 for symbol of stunning.

*Material Component*: Mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal with a total value of at least 5,000 gp.
