# Peacebond

_Abjuration_

- **Level**: Inq 1, Occ 1, Psy 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Wit 1
- **Components**: S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One sheathed or slung weapon
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: Will (object)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (object)

You lock a weapon in place on its owner's body, or within the weapon's sheath or holster. Anyone who then tries to draw the weapon must spend a standard action and succeed at a Strength check (DC equal to 10 + the spell's attack bonus) to do so, provoking attacks of opportunity whether the attempt succeeds or fails.
