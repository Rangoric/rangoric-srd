# Flaming Sphere, Greater

_Evocation \[Fire\]_

- **Level**: Blr 4, Drd 4, Hun 4, Mag 4, Occ 4, Sor/Wiz 4
- **Components**: V, S, M/DF (tallow, brimstone, and powdered iron)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Effect**: 5-ft.-diameter sphere
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Reflex (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flaming Sphere|flaming sphere]]_, except that it deals 6d6 points of fire damage to any creature it strikes. Any creature the sphere hits catches on fire. If a creature catches on fire, the DC to extinguish the flames is equal to the DC of this spell.
