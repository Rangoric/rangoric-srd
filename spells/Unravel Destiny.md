# Unravel Destiny

_Divination_

- **Level**: Clr 3, Sor/Wiz 3, Wit 3
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Short (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You utter a dire portent, causing destiny and fate to unravel around the target. This profoundly disturbing effect causes the target to suffer a cumulative –2 penalty on all ability checks, attack rolls, defenses, and skill checks for every [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points|hero point]] it possesses. The target can reduce this penalty by spending hero points normally, but it takes 2d6 points of damage for each hero point spent while this spell is in effect.
