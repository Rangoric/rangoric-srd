# Mantle of Calm

_Enchantment (Compulsion)_

- **Level**: Clr 3, Inq 3, Mes 2, Pal 3, Psy 3, Sha 3
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 round/level (D)
- **Defense**: None and Will (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: No and yes (see text)

You surround yourself with a mantle of calm serenity. You take a –2 penalty on attack rolls, and opponents gain a +2 bonus on defenses against spells you cast. Any creature affected by a rage effect ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian|barbarian]]'s rage, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager|bloodrager]]'s bloodrage, blood rage monster ability, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rage|rage]]_ spell, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald|skald]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-inspired-rage|inspired rage]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]], and so on) that strikes you with a melee attack is attacked against Will, without the rage effect's bonus to Will Defense; a hit means the rage effect ends (as if it were dispelled or the creature voluntarily ended it, as appropriate).

If you become affected by a rage effect while this spell is active, this spell immediately ends. If your rage effect comes from a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald|skald]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]], then it ends only for you, and you cannot re-accept the effects of the song.
