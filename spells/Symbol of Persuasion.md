# Symbol of Persuasion

*Enchantment (Charm) \[Mind-Affecting\]*

  - **Level**: Clr 6, Sor/Wiz 6, Wit 6
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]], except that all creatures within the radius of a symbol of persuasion instead become charmed by the caster (as the charm monster spell) for 1 hour per caster level.

Unlike symbol of death, symbol of persuasion has no hit point limit; once triggered, a symbol of persuasion simply remains active for 10 minutes per caster level.

*Note*: Magic traps such as symbol of persuasion are hard to detect and disable. A rogue (only) can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] skill to find a symbol of persuasion and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 31 for symbol of persuasion.

*Material Component*: Mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal with a total value of at least 5,000 gp.
