# Enshroud Thoughts

_Abjuration \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Alc 2, Brd 2, Hun 3, Inq 2, Med 2, Mes 2, Psy 2, Rgr 3, Skd 2, Wit 2
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 10 \* caster level minute(s)
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You become warded against the mental prying of others, including the effects of divination spells such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Seek Thoughts|seek thoughts]]_, as well as enchantment spells such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Modify Memory|modify memory]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Memory Lapse|memory lapse]]_. If another creature attempts to target you with a mind-affecting spell that detects or alters your thoughts or memories, the caster must succeed at a caster level check against a DC equal to 11 + your caster level or the spell fails. Only spells that detect or alter your thoughts or memories are blocked by this spell; effects such as _clairaudience/clairvoyance_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]]_ continue to affect you as normal.
