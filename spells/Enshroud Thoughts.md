# Enshroud Thoughts

*Abjuration \[MindAffecting\]*

  - **Level**: Brd 2, Rgr 3, Skd 2, Wit 2
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 10 \* caster level minute(s)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You become warded against the mental prying of others, including the effects of divination spells such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Seek Thoughts|seek thoughts]]*, as well as enchantment spells such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Modify Memory|modify memory]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Memory Lapse|memory lapse]]*. If another creature attempts to target you with a mind-affecting spell that detects or alters your thoughts or memories, the caster must succeed at a caster level check against a DC equal to 11 + your caster level or the spell fails. Only spells that detect or alter your thoughts or memories are blocked by this spell; effects such as *clairaudience/clairvoyance*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]* , and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Locate Creature|locate creature]]* continue to affect you as normal.
