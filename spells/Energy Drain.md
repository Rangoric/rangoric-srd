# Energy Drain

_Necromancy_

- **Level**: Clr 9, Sha 9, Sor/Wiz 9
- **Defense**: Fortitude partial; see text for enervation

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]], except that the creature struck gains 2d4 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|negative levels]], and the negative levels last longer.

The negative levels hit automatically, with no attack against a defense, but 24 hours after the subject gains them, the spell attacks its Fortitude once for each negative level, with the spell’s attack bonus. If an attack misses, that negative level is removed. If it fails, that negative level becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^permanent-negative-levels|permanent]].

An undead creature struck by the ray gains 2d4x5 temporary hit points for 1 hour.
