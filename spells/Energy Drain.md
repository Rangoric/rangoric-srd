# Energy Drain

*Necromancy*

  - **Level**: Clr 9, Sha 9, Sor/Wiz 9
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude partial; see text for enervation

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]], except that the creature struck gains 2d4 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^energy-drained|negative levels]], and the negative levels last longer.

There is no saving throw to avoid gaining the negative levels, but 24 hours after gaining them, the subject must make a Fortitude saving throw (DC = energy drain spell’s save DC) for each negative level. If the save succeeds, that negative level is removed. If it fails, that negative level becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^permanent-negative-levels|permanent]].

An undead creature struck by the ray gains 2d4x5 temporary hit points for 1 hour.
