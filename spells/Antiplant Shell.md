# Antiplant Shell

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Drd 4, Hun 4
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 10 ft.
  - **Area**: 10-ft.-radius emanation, centered on you
  - **Duration**: 10 min./level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The antiplant shell spell creates an [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]], mobile barrier that keeps all creatures within the shell protected from attacks by plant creatures or animated plants. As with many abjuration spells, forcing the barrier against creatures that the spell keeps at bay strains and collapses the field.
