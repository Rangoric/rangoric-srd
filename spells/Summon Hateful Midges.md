# Summon Hateful Midges

_Conjuration \[Summoning\]_

- **Level**: Sor/Wiz 3
- **Components**: V, S, M
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Targets**: One creature
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Fortitude partial (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: No

A cloud of miniscule flying, biting insects surrounds the subject of this spell. As a full-round action, the subject may fend off the insects; the midges don't attack its defenses in this case. If the subject wishes to act (anything other than a 5 foot step), the midges deal one point of Strength, Dexterity or Constitution damage (equal chance) and attack its Fortitude, making it lose a full round action anyway on a hit. If the subject is the target of any area effect energy attack, the midges are destroyed.

_Material Components:_ A vial of stagnant water. The water is consumed but the vial itself is not.
