# Mage Armor

*Conjuration (Creation) \[Force\]*

  - **Level**: Blr 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Wit 1
  - **Components**: V, S, F
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creature touched
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

An [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] but tangible field of force surrounds the subject of a mage armor spell, providing a +4 armor bonus to AC.

Unlike mundane armor, mage armor entails no armor check penalty, arcane spell failure chance, or speed reduction. Since mage armor is made of force, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures can’t bypass it the way they do normal armor.

*Focus*: A piece of cured leather.
