# Synaptic Pulse, Greater

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Mes 4, Psy 5
- **Components**: V
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 30 ft.
- **Area**: 30-ft.-radius spread centered on you
- **Duration**: 1d4 rounds; see text
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You emit a pulsating mental blast that stuns all creatures in range of your psychic shriek for 1d4 rounds. On a miss, a creature is instead sickened for 1 round.
