# Summon Fey VIII

_Conjuration (Summoning) \[see text\]_

- **Level**: Clr 8, Drd 8, Sor/Wiz 8
- **Components**: V, S, F/DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 round
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Effect**: One or more summoned creatures, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
- **Duration**: 1 round/level (D)
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

As _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Fey I|summon fey I]]_, except you can summon one fey from the 8th-level list, 1d3 fey of the same type from the 7th-level list, or 1d4+1 fey of the same type from a lower-level list.
