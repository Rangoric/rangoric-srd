# Recoil Fire

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 2, Wit 2
  - **Components**: V, S, M (hoof shavings from a mule)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Target**: 1 firearm
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (object)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (object)

The target [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]] suddenly fires of its own volition, even if it is unloaded, generating a recoil so great that the wielder or carrier of the firearm must scramble to hold onto it and becomes flat-footed until the start of his next turn. If the wielder is already flat-footed, he is instead knocked prone. If the firearm was loaded, that ammunition is wasted.
