# Protective Spirit

_Conjuration (Creation)_

- **Level**: Hun 2, Rgr 2
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Personal
- **Target**: You
- **Duration**: 1 round/level

A _protective spirit_ is an invisible, mindless, shapeless force that hovers about you, defending you against unexpected attacks. When a creature makes an attack of opportunity against you, the _protective spirit_ makes an immediate attack using your base attack bonus plus your Dexterity modifier. On a successful hit, the spirit does no damage, but it causes the attack of opportunity to automatically miss. A _protective spirit_ can defend against a number of attacks of opportunity equal to your Dexterity bonus (minimum 1) per round.

A _protective spirit_ occupies your space, and moves with you. It cannot be attacked or harmed by physical attacks, but _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_, a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/artifacts/Sphere of Annihilation|sphere of annihilation]]_, or a _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rods/Rod of Cancellation|rod of cancellation]]_ affects it. A _protective spirit_'s AC against touch attacks is 10 + your Dexterity modifier.
