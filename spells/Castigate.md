# Castigate

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Emotion, Fear, Language-dependent, Mind-affecting\]_

- **Level**: Inq 2, Mes 2
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One living creature
- **Duration**: 1 round/level or 1 round; see text
- **Defense**: Will partial
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You compel the target to beg for forgiveness. On a hit, the target cowers with fear. On a miss, it is shaken for 1 round. Each round on its turn, a cowering subject may make the spell attack its Will again, and a miss ends the effect. A creature who worships the same god as you takes a –2 penalty to its Will Defense against the spell.
