# Halt Undead

_Necromancy_

- **Level**: Inq 3, Sor/Wiz 3
- **Components**: V, S, M
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Targets**: Up to three undead creatures, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Will (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell renders as many as three undead creatures immobile. The spell hits a nonintelligent undead creature automatically; it attacks the Will of an intelligent undead creature. If the spell is successful, it renders the undead creature immobile for the duration of the spell (similar to the effect of hold person on a living creature). The effect is broken if the halted creatures are attacked or take damage.

_Material Component_: A pinch of sulfur and powdered garlic.
