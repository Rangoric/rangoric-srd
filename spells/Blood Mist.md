# Blood Mist

_Conjuration (Creation) \[Poison\]_

- **Level**: Drd 8, Sha 8
- **Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of dried red algae)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100ft. +10ft./level)
- **Area**: 60-ft. radius
- **Duration**: 1 minute/level
- **Defense**: Fortitude (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell summons forth a misty cloud of rust-red toxic algae. Any creature within the mist is coated by it, turning the creature the same reddish color. All targets within the mist gain concealment. The mist attacks the Fortitude of any creature within it, and on a hit the creature takes 1d4 points of Wisdom damage and becomes enraged, attacking any creatures it detects nearby (as the “attack nearest creature” result of the confused condition). An enraged creature remains so as long as the spell is in effect. The mist attacks a creature only once each time it is within the mist (though leaving and returning means another attack).
