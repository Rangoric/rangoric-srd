# Debilitating Portent

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Clr 4, Psy 4, Spi 4, Wit 4
- **Components**: V, S, DF
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: 1 round/level (D) see text
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The target is surrounded by a glowing green aura of ill fate. Each time the spell's subject makes an attack or casts a spell, the spell attacks its Will with an attack bonus = 1/2 caster level + Charisma (in the case of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Oracle|oracles]]), Intelligence (in the case of witches), or Wisdom (in the case of clerics). On a hit, it deals half damage with the attack or spell. You can dismiss this spell as an immediate action when the target confirms a critical hit; doing so negates the critical hit. The attack that you negated still hits, but only deals half damage.
