# Cup of Dust

_Transmutation \[Curse\]_

- **Level**: Drd 3, Hun 3, Mes 3, Occ 3, Psy 3, Wit 3
- **Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of dust)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: 1 day/level (D)
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You curse the target with a thirst no drink can quench. On a hit, the target begins to dehydrate. The effects of this spell cannot inflict more nonlethal damage than the target has hit points. No amount of drinking can counter this effect, although the target still experiences the other normal effects of any liquid imbibed (such as poisons or potions). _Cup of dust_ can be removed with a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ spell, but effects suffered as a result of dehydration must be healed by normal means.
