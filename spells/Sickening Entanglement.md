# Sickening Entanglement

_Transmutation \[Poison\]_

- **Level**: Drd 2, Hun 2, Rgr 2, Sha 2
- **Components**:
- **Defense**: Reflex partial and Fortitude (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]_, except the plants are coated with an irritating sap that creates a painful, itching rash on creatures that touch them. The spell attacks the Fortitude of any creature that enters the area or ends its turn there, and on a hit it is sickened as long as it remains in the area and for 1d4 rounds afterward. This is a poison effect.
