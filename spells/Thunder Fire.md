# Thunder Fire

_Transmutation_

- **Level**: Brd 2, Skd 2, Sor/Wiz 2, Wit 2
- **Components**: V, S, M (a piece of spent thunderstone)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: 1 loaded firearm
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Will (object), see text
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes (object), see text

The [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]] targeted creates a thunderous report, even if the firearm is not loaded. Every creature within 15 feet of the creature wielding or carrying the firearm is attacked against Fortitude, and deafened for 1 minute on a hit. The creature wielding or carrying the firearm takes a –4 penalty to his Fortitude Defense against this attack, and his [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms#Misfires|misfire]] range increases by 1 for 1d4 rounds. If the firearm was loaded when it was targeted by this spell, that ammunition is wasted.
