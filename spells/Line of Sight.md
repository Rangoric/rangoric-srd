# Line of Sight

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 8
  - **Components**: V, F
  - **Casting Time**: See text
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Duration**: See text

Casting this spell is a free action, like casting a *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell, and it counts toward the normal limit of one *[[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|quickened]]* spell per round. If the caster casts another spell in the same round, and that spell has a range of Close, Medium or Long, that spell’s range is extended to reach a single creature that the caster can see. Magical and mundane aids cannot be used to further extend the range of that spell. The spell cast may not be of the highest level that the caster is capable of casting.

*Arcane focus:* a miniature spyglass made of copper and two round diamonds.
