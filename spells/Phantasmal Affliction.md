# Phantasmal Affliction

_Illusion (Phantasm) \[Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Mes 2, Psy 3, Sor/Wiz 3, Wit 3
- **Components**: V, S, M (a drop of cod liver oil)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: Living creature
- **Duration**: See text
- **Defense**: Will disbelief, then Fortitude or Will (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You cause the creature to believe she has a debilitating affliction. The spell attacks the target’s Will, and on a miss it recognizes the affliction as unreal. On a hit, the creature suffers an imaginary affliction of your choice.

Since the affliction exists entirely in the creature’s mind, phantasmal affliction is not affected by normal cures like neutralize poison or remove disease, or other effects like delay poison or the Medicine skill. Ordinary immunities do not apply in this case (though a creature immune to the affliction receives a +4 bonus to its Will Defense against the illusion). Constitution damage from the affliction can’t kill the target. Instead, it causes the target to fall unconscious like other forms of ability damage. Phantasmal affliction is a spell effect and can be dispelled normally.

_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Placebo Effect|Placebo effect]]_ counters and dispels phantasmal affliction.
