# Symbol of Insanity

*Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]*

  - **Level**: Clr 8, Sor/Wiz 8
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Symbol of Death|symbol of death]], except that all creatures within the radius of the symbol of insanity instead become permanently insane (as the insanity spell).

Unlike symbol of death, symbol of insanity has no hit point limit; once triggered, a symbol of insanity simply remains active for 10 minutes per caster level.

*Note*: Magic traps such as symbol of insanity are hard to detect and disable. A rogue (only) can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] skill to find a symbol of insanity and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 33 for symbol of insanity.

*Material Component*: Mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal with a total value of at least 5,000 gp.
