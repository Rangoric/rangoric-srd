*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Clr 2, Drd 2, Pal 2, Rgr 2, Sor/Wiz 2

  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Creatures touched

  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

This spell functions like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*, except you divide the duration in 1-hour increments among the creatures touched.
