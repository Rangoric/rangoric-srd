# Honeyed Tongue

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Brd 2, Inq 2, Pal 1, Skd 2
  - **Components**: V, M/DF (a drop of honey)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 10 minutes/level

This spell augments your diplomacies. While under the effects of spell, you roll two dice each time you make a Persuasion check to change a creature's attitude, taking the highest roll. If this results in a roll low enough to reduce the creature's attitude by a step, that creature gets some clue that you are manipulating it with a spell. Alternatively you can cast this spell before making a Persuasion check to gather information, gaining a +5 competence bonus on the check.
