# Geomessage

*Illusion (Figment)*

  - **Level**: Brd 3, Hun 3, Rgr 3, Skd 3, Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a scrap of vellum)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 minute
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Surface touched
  - **Duration**: Caster level day(s)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

You create a written message of 50 words or fewer, or else an image, a drawing, or a similar diagram. The figment hangs invisible and obscured upon the target surface for the duration of the spell. Another casting of *geomessage* is needed to cause the figment to arrange itself into the intended message. When you cast the spell, you can choose to incorporate a passphrase into the spell. If you do so, the message is protected as if *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]* had been cast on it for the full duration of the spell, though creatures that cast *geomessage* on the surface and speak the passphrase ignore the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]* effect and cause the image to become visible until the caster speaks the passphrase again. Without a passphrase, the message simply remains invisible to those who cast *geomessage*. Detection methods like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]* indicate the message's presence but do not reveal its contents.
