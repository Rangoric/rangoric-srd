# Dragon’s Breath

_Evocation \[Acid, Cold, Electricity, Fire\]_

- **Level**: Alc 4, Blr 4, Mag 4, Sor/Wiz 4
- **Components**: V, S, M (a dragon scale)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 30 ft. or 60 ft.
- **Area**: Cone-shaped burst or line
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Reflex half
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You breathe out a blast of energy. Creatures in the affected area take 1d6 points of energy damage per caster level (maximum of 12d6). The breath attacks Reflex, and a miss halves the damage. The spell's effect and energy type depend on the type of dragon scale used:

_Black dragon:_ 60-foot line of acid.

_Blue or bronze dragon:_ 60-foot line of electricity.

_Green dragon:_ 30-foot cone of acid.

_Gold or red dragon:_ 30-foot cone of fire.

_Silver or white dragon:_ 30-foot cone of cold.

_Brass dragon:_ 60-foot line of fire.

_Copper dragon:_ 60-foot line of acid.
