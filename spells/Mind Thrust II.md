# Mind Thrust II

_Divination_

- **Level**: Psy 2
- **Components**: S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Will half
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mind Thrust I|mind thrust I]]_, but the target takes 1d8 points of damage per caster level (maximum 5d8). This spell can be [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^undercasting|undercast]].
