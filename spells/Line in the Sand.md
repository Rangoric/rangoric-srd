# Line in the Sand

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Blr 1, Mag 1, Sor/Wiz 1
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 5 ft.
  - **Area**: 5-ft. burst centered on you
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level

You create a glowing crimson line around the area. Against creatures in the area, you can attempt a number of additional attacks of opportunity per round equal to your spellcasting ability score modifier (Intelligence for [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus|magi]] and wizards, Charisma for bloodragers and sorcerers), although this does not allow you to make more than one attack against a creature per action that provokes.
