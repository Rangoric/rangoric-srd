# Entice Fey, Greater

*Conjuration (Calling)*

  - **Level**: Drd 9
  - **Components**: V, S, M, DF (offerings worth 2,500 gp plus payment)
  - **Casting Time**: 10 minutes
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Effect**: Up to three called fey, totaling no more than 18 Hit Dice, no two of which can appear more than 30 ft. apart
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Entice Fey, Lesser|lesser entice fey]], except the spell's whimsical calling can produce a single fey of 18 Hit Dice or less, or up to three fey of the same kind whose Hit Dice total no more than 18.
