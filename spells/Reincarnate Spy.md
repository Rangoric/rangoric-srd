# Reincarnate Spy

*Conjuration (Healing)*

  - **Level**: Drd 6, Hun 6, Wit 7
  - **Components**: V, S, M, DF (oils worth 2,500 gp and a possession or piece of the body of the creature to resemble)
  - **Casting Time**: 10 minutes
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Dead creature touched
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: None (see text)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

This spell functions as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Reincarnate|reincarnate]] except that you can cause the new body to resemble a particular creature, matching its age category and sex and rerolling any race result that would be the wrong size category. The subject further gains a +5 bonus on Imitation checks to impersonate the chosen creature due to similar features, although it might take a penalty for being the wrong race. The spell automatically leaves you with a small piece of the creature's new body, typically a lock of hair (useful for *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]* and other such spells).
