# Geas/Quest

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Language-Dependent, Mind-Affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 6, Clr 6, Enchantment 7, Inq 5, Mes 6, Psy 6, Skd 6, Sor/Wiz 6, Wit 6
- **Casting Time**: 10 minutes
- **Target**: One living creature
- **Defense**: None

This spell functions similarly to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Geas, Lesser|lesser geas]], except that it affects a creature of any HD and hits automatically, with no attack against a defense.

Instead of taking penalties to ability scores (as with lesser geas), the subject takes 3d6 points of damage each day it does not attempt to follow the geas/quest. Additionally, each day the spell attacks its Fortitude, and on a hit it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]]. These effects end 24 hours after the creature attempts to resume the geas/ quest.

A remove curse spell ends a geas/quest spell only if its caster level is at least two higher than your caster level. Break enchantment does not end a geas/quest, but limited wish, miracle, and wish do.

Bards, sorcerers, and wizards usually refer to this spell as geas, while clerics call the same spell quest.
