# Chill Touch

_Necromancy_

- **Level**: Blr 1, Mag 1, Sha 1, Sor/Wiz 1, Spi 1, Wit 1
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Touch
- **Targets**: Creature or creatures touched (up to one/level)
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Fortitude partial or Will; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

A touch from your hand, which glows with blue energy, disrupts the life force of living creatures. Each touch channels negative energy that deals 1d6 points of damage. The touched creature also takes 1 point of Strength damage if the spell hits its Fortitude. You can use this melee touch attack up to one time per level.

An undead creature you touch takes no damage of either sort, but the spell attacks its Will, and on a hit it flees as if [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] for 1d4 rounds +1 round per caster level.
