# Magic Fang, Greater

*Transmutation*

  - **Level**: Drd 3, Rgr 3
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Target**: One living creature
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level

This spell functions like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Fang|magic fang]], except that the enhancement bonus on attack and damage rolls is +1 per four caster levels (maximum +5).

Alternatively, you may imbue all of the creature’s natural weapons with a +1 enhancement bonus (regardless of your caster level).

Greater magic fang can be made permanent with a permanency spell.
