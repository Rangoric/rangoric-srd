# Cloak of Dreams

_Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-affecting\]_

- **Level**: Brd 5, Mes 5, Occ 6, Psy 6, Sha 7, Skd 5, Sor/Wiz 6, Wit 6
- **Components**: V, S, M (a rose petal and a drop of perfume)
- **Casting Time**: 1 round
- **Range**: 5 ft.
- **Area**: 5-ft.-radius emanation centered on you
- **Duration**: 1 round/level (D)
- **Defense**: Will
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

You are surrounded by a soporific aroma that causes living creatures that begin their turn or end their movement within 5 feet of you to fall asleep for 1 minute. The aroma attacks a creature’s Will each time it begins its turn or ends its movement within the _cloak of dreams_, even if it has missed the creature before. Sleeping creatures are helpless but can be awakened with a standard action or after being wounded. Creatures with the scent special quality have a –4 penalty to their Will Defense against it.
