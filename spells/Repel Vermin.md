# Repel Vermin

*Abjuration*

  - **Level**: Brd 4, Clr 4, Drd 4, Rgr 3, Sha 4
  - **Components**: V, S, DF
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 10 ft.
  - **Area**: 10-ft.-radius emanation centered on you
  - **Duration**: 10 min./level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: None or Will negates; see text
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

An [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] barrier holds back vermin. A vermin with Hit Dice of less than one-third your level cannot penetrate the barrier.

A vermin with Hit Dice of one-third your level or more can penetrate the barrier if it succeeds on a Will save. Even so, crossing the barrier deals the vermin 2d6 points of damage, and pressing against the barrier causes pain, which deters most vermin.
