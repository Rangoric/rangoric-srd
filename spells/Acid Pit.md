# Acid Pit

*Conjuration (Creation) \[Acid\]*

  - **Level**: Sor/Wiz 4
  - **Components**: V, S, M (drop of acid), F (Fine shovel worth 10 gp)
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Effect**: 10-ft.-by-10-ft. hole, 10 ft. deep/2 levels
  - **Duration**: 1 round + 1 round/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Reflex negates; see text
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

This spell functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Pit|create pit]]*, except that it places a 5-foot-deep pool of acid at the bottom of the pit. The pit has a maximum depth of 100 feet. Creatures who fall into the pit take falling damage as normal (the acid counts as a yielding surface), plus 2d6 points of acid damage per round spent in contact with the acid. In addition, exposed items carried by a creature in the pit may be harmed. Refer to Table 9–2: Items Affected by Magical Attacks. Items are affected one at a time in the order listed on the table, and must make Fortitude saves after 3 consecutive rounds in the acid or gain the broken condition. Objects with the broken condition (regardless of how they became broken) must make a Fortitude saving throw each round spent within the acid or be destroyed. The walls of the pit are quite slippery and have a Athletics DC of 30.
