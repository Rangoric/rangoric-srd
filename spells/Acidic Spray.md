# Acidic Spray

_Conjuration (Creation) \[Acid\]_

- **Level**: Mag 5, Sor/Wiz 5
- **Components**: V, S, M (a vial of acid worth 10 gp)
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: 60 ft.
- **Effect**: 60-ft. line
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: Reflex partial (see text)
- **Spell Resistance**: Yes

A spray of acid erupts from your outstretched hand, dealing 1d6 points of acid damage per caster level (maximum 15d6) to each creature within its area (vs Reflex; a miss halves). This acid continues to burn for 1 round, dealing 1d6 points of acid damage per two caster levels (maximum 7d6) to any creature the spell hit (the acid attacks the creature's Reflex again on its turn, and a miss negates this additional damage).
