# Pantheons

The gods of six old mythologies, ready for any campaign to use, change or replace. Each entry gives the god's alignment, what the god is god of, and the domains a cleric of that god can choose from. Where another people knew the same god by another name, that name is in brackets. A campaign with its own gods can use these as models.
