# Caustic Cloud

*One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Acid Words|Acid group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]].*

**School** conjuration (creation) \[acid\]

**Level** sorcerer/wizard 7, witch 6

**Duration** 1 round/level

**Saving Throw** Fortitude half

**Spell Resistance** no

**Target Restrictions** burst

A wordspell with this effect word creates a cloud of noxious green vapor that obscures vision. Creatures caught in the cloud take 1d6 points of acid damage per caster level (maximum 20d6) and are fatigued. Creatures caught in the cloud receive a Fortitude save to halve the damage and to negate the fatigued effect. Creatures that remain in the cloud take a cumulative –2 penalty on the save each round they remain in the cloud, but spending just 1 round outside the cloud's area resets this penalty. Creatures in the cloud that are fatigued become exhausted on a failed saving throw. A strong wind, such as that created by a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]*, disperses this cloud immediately.
