# Servitor VII

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Summoning Words|Summoning group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** conjuration (summoning)

**Level** cleric 7, druid 7, sorcerer/wizard 7, summoner 5, witch 7

**Duration** 1 round/level

**Saving Throw** none

**Spell Resistance** no

**Target Restrictions** selected

This effect word functions as _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words/Servitor I|servitor I]]_ except that it summons one creature from the 7th-level list of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VII|summon monster VII]]_. If a wordspell with this effect word is cast by a druid, it instead summons a creature from the 7th-level list of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally VII|summon nature’s ally VII]]_. If the target word is boosted, a wordspell with this effect word summons 1d4+1 of the chosen creatures.
