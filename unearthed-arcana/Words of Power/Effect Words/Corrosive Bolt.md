# Corrosive Bolt

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Acid Words|Acid group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** conjuration (creation) \[acid\]

**Level** magus 2, sorcerer/wizard 2

**Duration** 2 rounds

**Saving Throw** none

**Spell Resistance** no

**Target Restrictions** selected

A wordspell with this effect word deals 1d4 points of acid damage per level of the wordcaster (maximum 5d4). On the following round, the target takes this damage again. Hitting a target with a wordspell with this effect word requires a ranged touch attack.
