# Thunder Strike

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Electricity Words|Electricity group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** evocation \[electricity\]

**Level** druid 8, sorcerer/wizard 8, witch 9

**Duration** instantaneous

**Saving Throw** Reflex half

**Spell Resistance** yes

This effect word deals 1d8 points of electricity damage per caster level (maximum 20d8). In addition, a target that fails its saving throw against this damage is deafened for 1 round per caster level.
