# Fog Bank

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Weather Words|Weather group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** conjuration (creation)

**Level** cleric 1, druid 1, sorcerer/wizard 1, witch 1

**Duration** 1 minute/level (D)

**Saving Throw** none

**Spell Resistance** no

**Target Restrictions** barrier, burst (emanation)

This effect word creates a thick fog in its area of effect. It obscures all sight, including darkvision, beyond 5 feet. Creatures 5 feet away have concealment, whereas creatures farther away have total concealment. A moderate wind or fire effect removes the fog in the affected area. The fog is stationary.

**Boost**: If a wordspell with this effect word uses the _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Target Words/Burst (Target Word)|burst]]_ target word, it can be directed to move up to 10 feet each round by the caster as a swift action.
