# Monstrous Form

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Change Words|Change group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** transmutation (polymorph)

**Level** alchemist 5, druid 5, magus 5, sorcerer/wizard 5

**Duration** 1 minute/level

**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless)

**Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

**Target Restriction** personal

This effect word functions as _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words/Bestial Form|bestial form]]_ except that the target of this wordspell receives a +6 size bonus. In addition to those benefits available in _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words/Altered Form|altered form]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words/Bestial Form|bestial form]]_, the target can select from the following: a burrow speed of 30 feet, blindsense out to a range of 30 feet, constrict (dealing the same damage as the grab attack + the target's Str modifier), ferocity, resist 20 against one energy type, or a +4 natural armor bonus.

**Boost**: The target receives four benefits instead of three.

**Boost**: This wordspell can use the _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Target Words/Selected|selected]]_ target word. Boosting this effect word increases its level by 1.
