# Dimensional Hop

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Teleportation Words|Teleportation group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** conjuration (teleportation)

**Level** bard 3, magus 3, sorcerer/wizard 3, summoner 3, witch 3

**Duration** instantaneous

**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless)

**Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

**Target Restrictions** selected

A wordspell with this effect word teleports willing targets to another location within line of sight of the wordcaster and within range of the wordspell.
