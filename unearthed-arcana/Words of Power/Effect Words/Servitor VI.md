# Servitor VI

*One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Summoning Words|Summoning group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]].*

**School** conjuration (summoning)

**Level** bard 6, cleric 6, druid 6, sorcerer/wizard 6, summoner 4, witch 6

**Duration** 1 round/level

**Saving Throw** none

**Spell Resistance** no

**Target Restrictions** selected

This effect word functions as *[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words/Servitor I|servitor I]]* except that it summons one creature from the 6th-level list of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]*. If a wordspell with this effect word is cast by a druid, it instead summons a creature from the 6th-level list of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally VI|summon nature’s ally VI]]*. If the target word is boosted, a wordspell with this effect word summons 1d4+1 of the chosen creatures.
