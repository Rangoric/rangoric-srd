# Ice Blast

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Cold Words|Cold group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** evocation \[cold\]

**Level** druid 4, magus 4, sorcerer/wizard 4, witch 4

**Duration** instantaneous

**Saving Throw** Reflex half

**Spell Resistance** yes

This effect deals 1d6 points of cold damage per caster level (maximum 10d6). A target that fails its saving throw is entangled by the ice for 1d4 rounds.
