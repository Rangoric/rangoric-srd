# Radiance

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Illumination Words|Illumination group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** evocation

**Level** bard 1, cleric 1, druid 1, inquisitor 1, magus 1, sorcerer/wizard 1, witch 1

**Duration** 10 minutes/level

**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless)

**Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

**Target Restrictions** burst (emanation)

The area of a wordspell with this effect word radiates light like a torch for the duration, shedding normal light in the wordspell's area and increasing the light level for an additional 20 feet by one step, up to normal light.
