# Servitor III

*One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Summoning Words|Summoning group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]].*

**School** conjuration (summoning)

**Level** bard 3, cleric 3, druid 3, ranger 3, sorcerer/wizard 3, witch 3

**Duration** 1 round/level

**Saving Throw** none

**Spell Resistance** no

**Target Restrictions** selected

This effect word functions as *[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words/Servitor I|servitor I]]* except that it summons one creature from the 3rd-level list of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster III|summon monster III]]*. If a wordspell with this effect word is cast by a ranger or druid, it instead summons a creature from the 3rd-level list of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally III|summon nature’s ally III]]*. If the target word is boosted, a spell with this effect word summons 1d4+1 of the chosen creatures.
