# Bestial Form

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Change Words|Change group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** transmutation (polymorph)

**Level** alchemist 4, druid 4, magus 4, sorcerer/wizard 4

**Duration** 1 minute/level

**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless)

**Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

**Target Restriction** personal

This effect word functions as _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words/Altered Form|altered form]]_ except that the target of this wordspell receives a +4 size bonus to Strength and can select three benefits. In addition to those available in _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words/Altered Form|altered form]]_, the target can select from among the following benefits: a fly speed of 30 feet (average maneuverability), grab (with any one natural attack), pounce, trip (with any one natural attack), or a +4 natural armor bonus.

**Boost**: The target receives four benefits instead of three.

**Boost**: This wordspell can use the _[[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Target Words/Selected|selected]]_ target word. Boosting this effect word increases its level by 1.
