# Discordant Note

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Sonic Words|Sonic group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** evocation \[sonic\]

**Level** bard 2, cleric 2, magus 2

**Duration** instantaneous

**Saving Throw** Fortitude half and partial

**Spell Resistance** yes

This effect word deals 1d4 points of sonic damage per caster level (maximum 5d4) and causes the targets to become staggered for 1 round. A successful Fortitude save halves the damage and negates the staggered condition.
