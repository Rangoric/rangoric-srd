# Acid Wave

_One of the effect words ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Effect Words#Acid Words|Acid group]]) of the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Words of Power/Words of Power|words of power]]._

**School** conjuration (creation) \[acid\]

**Level** magus 4, sorcerer/wizard 4

**Duration** 2 rounds

**Saving Throw** Reflex half and partial (see below)

**Spell Resistance** no

A wordspell with this effect word deals 1d6 points of acid damage per caster level (maximum 10d6). Targets damaged by this effect word are sickened for 1 round per caster level, or 1 round if the saving throw against the wordspell with this effect word was successful.
