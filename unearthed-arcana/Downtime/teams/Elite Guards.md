# Elite Guards

- **Upgrade to**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Elite Soldiers|Elite Soldiers]]
- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Guards|Guards]]
- **Create**: 3 Goods, 1 Influence, 4 Labor (170 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp, Influence, or Labor +4
- **Time**: 2 days
- **Size**: 5 people

Like Guards, this team defends a location but doesn't take on an active role. An Elite Guards team consists of 3rd-level warriors (_NPC_ _Codex_ 267), each wearing a chain shirt or banded mail and using a heavy steel shield and shortspear, a guisarme, or a halberd.
