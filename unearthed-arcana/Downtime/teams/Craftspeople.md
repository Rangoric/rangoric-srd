# Craftspeople

- **Create**: 3 Goods, 2 Influence, 4 Labor (200 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp, Goods, or Labor +4
- **Time**: 2 days
- **Size**: 3 people

Craftspeople are trained in a particular craft or profession [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] and make a living using that skill. Examples of this team are alchemists, carpenters, leatherworkers, masons, and smiths. A typical carpenter is a 4th-level expert with 4 ranks each in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background (carpentry)]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]]. Craftspeople in other fields have a similar skill arrangement.
