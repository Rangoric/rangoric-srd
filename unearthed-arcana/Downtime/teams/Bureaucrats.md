# Bureaucrats

- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Scofflaws|Scofflaws]], [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Soldiers|Soldiers]]
- **Create**: 2 Goods, 4 Influence, 2 Labor (200 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp or Influence +4
- **Time**: 2 days
- **Size**: 5 people

Bureaucrats interface with local government and deal with annoying paperwork related to running a business or organization. This type of team could include accountants, diplomats, lawyers, and scribes. A typical bureaucrat is a 1st-, 2nd-, or 3rd-level expert with ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (barrister, clerk, or scribe).
