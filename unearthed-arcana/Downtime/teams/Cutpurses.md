# Cutpurses

- **Upgrade to**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Robbers|Robbers]]
- **Create**: 3 Influence, 1 Labor (110 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp, Goods, or Labor +3
- **Time**: 1 day
- **Size**: 5 people

Cutpurses are minor thieves (often youths) who earn money from petty crimes such as pickpocketing or stealing items from market stalls. They are typically 1st-level commoners, experts, or rogues with 1 rank in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]].
