# Elite Soldiers

- **Upgrade to**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Cavalry (Downtime)|Cavalry]], [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Elite Archers|Elite Archers]]
- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Elite Guards|Elite Guards]], [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Soldiers|Soldiers]]
- **Create**: 5 Goods, 3 Influence, 7 Labor (330 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp, Influence, or Labor +6
- **Time**: 5 days
- **Size**: 5 people

Like Soldiers, this team will take aggressive action and seek out your enemies to kill them. An Elite Soldiers team consists of 3rd-level warriors, each wearing a chain shirt or banded mail and using a guisarme, a halberd, or a heavy steel shield and a shortspear.
