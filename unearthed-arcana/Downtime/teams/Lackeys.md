# Lackeys

- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Driver (Downtime)|Drivers]], [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Laborers|Laborers]]
- **Create**: 1 Goods, 2 Influence, 2 Labor (120 gp)
- **Earnings**: Influence or Labor +2
- **Time**: 1 day
- **Size**: 5 people

Lackeys wait on you hand and foot and take care of common domestic and traveling issues; their ranks include butlers, valets, maids, heralds, footmen, ladies-in-waiting, and similar service personnel. Skilled Lackeys anticipate your needs and coordinate with your other employees as well as those of your guests or host. A typical Lackey is a 1st-level commoner or expert with ranks in a subset of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]].
