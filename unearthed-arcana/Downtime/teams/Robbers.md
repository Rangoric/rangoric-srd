# Robbers

- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/teams/Cutpurses|Cutpurses]]
- **Create**: 1 Goods, 4 Influence, 3 Labor (200 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp, Goods, or Influence +4
- **Time**: 3 days
- **Size**: 5 people

Robbers are expert thieves who commit burglary, robbery, and other violent crimes for profit. A typical robber is a 3rd-level expert or rogue with 3 ranks each in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]].
