# Battle Ring

- **Upgrade to**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Sports Field|Sports Field]]
- **Create**: 18 Goods, 4 Influence, 16 Labor (800 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp or Influence +15
- **Benefit**: contestant gains a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat|performance combat]] checks
- **Time**: 40 days
- **Size**: 40-100 squares

This enclosed field is used for some form of dangerous contest, from nonlethal sports like wrestling or boxing to lethal blood sports such as animal fights or gladiatorial combat. It includes seating for spectators, appropriate flooring (padded or sandy), and often some manner of barrier between the audience and combatants. Each day, the person in charge of the Battle Ring can grant one combatant a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat|performance combat]] checks. This benefit applies only within the settlement.
