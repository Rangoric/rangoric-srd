# Sitting Room

- **Create**: 12 Goods, 12 Labor (480 gp)
- **Earnings**: Influence +4
- **Benefit**: bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks
- **Time**: 24 days
- **Size**: 6-10 squares

This is a room used for meeting and entertaining in a relaxed, comfortable setting, such as a den, dining room, or smoking room. It has furnishings appropriate to its function (chairs for a sitting room, table and chairs for a dining room, and so on). By spending an hour conversing with guests in a social manner, the host of the room gains a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks to influence or learn about those guests for the next 24 hours.
