# Storage

- **Upgrade to**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Office|Office]], [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Vault (Downtime)|Vault]]
- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/False Front|False Front]], [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Shack|Shack]]
- **Create**: 3 Goods, 3 Labor (120 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp +2
- **Time**: 8 days
- **Size**: 4-8 squares

Storage is any room used to store objects, keeping them out of the way for later use. Most Warehouses are just multiple Storage rooms built into a single building. A low-cost shop may allow its customers to browse items in the Storage area. A door to a Storage room includes an average lock.
