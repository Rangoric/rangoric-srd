# Ceremonial Room

- **Upgrade to**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Throne Room|Throne Room]]
- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Common Room|Common Room]]
- **Create**: 16 Goods, 2 Influence, 15 Labor, 5 Magic (1,180 gp)
- **Earnings**: Capital +10
- **Benefit**: bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks (see below)
- **Time**: 40 days
- **Size**: 40-100 squares

This is a large, open room for important events such as religious services, town meetings, and weddings. It often features an elevated area for the focus or leader of the event, and might have seats for others in attendance. A person leading or officially speaking at the event gains a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks to influence others at the event. This bonus ends when the event ends.
