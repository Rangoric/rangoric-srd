# Vault

- **Upgrade to**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Secret Room|Secret Room]]
- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Reliquary|Reliquary]], [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Storage|Storage]]
- **Create**: 8 Goods, 7 Labor (300 gp)
- **Time**: 16 days
- **Size**: 4-8 squares

This is a secure room designed to keep out intruders. The access to this space is controlled by an iron door with a good lock. If you upgrade this room to a Secret Room, the door retains its material and lock and also becomes a secret door.
