# Confessional

- **Create**: 2 Goods, 3 Labor (100 gp)
- **Benefit**: bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks
- **Time**: 4 days
- **Size**: 2-4 squares

This pair of tiny, linked rooms allows for private conversations. Alternatively, you may construct a hidden space that allows you to watch another room without being observed, such as through a peephole in a tapestry or mosaic. One side of this room provides a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks to hide from creatures in the adjoining room. A Confessional can be constructed in a way that allows this bonus to apply to creatures in both sides or just in one.
