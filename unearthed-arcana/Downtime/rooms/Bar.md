# Bar

- **Create**: 6 Goods, 1 Influence, 5 Labor (250 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp or Influence +10
- **Benefit**: bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks to gather information
- **Time**: 16 days
- **Size**: 10-20 squares

A Bar stores a selection of drinks and includes a counter for preparing them. After spending an hour with local people in this room, for the next 24 hours you gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks you make to gather information in the settlement.
