# War Room

- **Create**: 8 Goods, 7 Labor (300 gp)
- **Benefit**: bonus on mass combat and skill checks while scheming
- **Time**: 16 days
- **Size**: 4-12 squares

This is a room for planning military maneuvers, plotting grand heists, or providing briefings. It contains a large central table with plenty of chairs, maps, and figures to simulate troops and structures. When it's used for planning a battle ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Mass Combat/Mass Combat|Mass Combat]]), your army gains a +2 bonus on attack rolls and morale checks for their next battle within 24 hours. To grant the army this bonus, the army's commander must be present at the planning meeting for the battle or you must have some way of communicating these instructions to the commander. When used to scheme for an upcoming adventure (see [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/Downtime Activities#Scheme for an Upcoming Adventure|Scheme for an Upcoming Adventure]]), a War Room allows you to use the scheme's planning bonus on two skill checks instead of one.
