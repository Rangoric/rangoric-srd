# Summoning Chamber

- **Create**: 11 Goods, 4 Influence, 10 Labor, 5 Magic (1,040 gp)
- **Earnings**: Magic +3
- **Benefit**: bonus on Charisma, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks
- **Time**: 28 days
- **Size**: 6-16 squares

A Summoning Chamber is used to perform magical rituals to conjure outsiders. It contains a well-drawn, nearly complete magic circle on the floor—which you can complete with just a few chalk marks—suitable for use with _magic circle_ spells, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]_ spells, and so on. A person who uses a Summoning Chamber gains a +3 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks relating to a creature being called or summoned, and a +3 bonus on Charisma checks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks, and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks to influence or bargain with a creature called or summoned here.
