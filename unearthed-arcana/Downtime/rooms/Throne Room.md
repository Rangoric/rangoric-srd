# Throne Room

- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Ceremonial Room|Ceremonial Room]], [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Common Room|Common Room]]
- **Create**: 25 Goods, 5 Influence, 25 Labor, 5 Magic (1,650 gp)
- **Earnings**: Influence +15
- **Benefit**: bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks
- **Time**: 40 days
- **Size**: 40-80 squares

A Throne Room is used to receive important visitors, such as nobles. The room contains a throne, various decorations, and a few seats for visitors. By spending an hour conversing with visitors, the host of the room gains a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks to influence or learn about those guests for the next 24 hours.
