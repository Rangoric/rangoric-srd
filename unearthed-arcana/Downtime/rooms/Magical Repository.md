# Magical Repository

- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Book Repository|Book Repository]]
- **Create**: 9 Goods, 3 Influence, 8 Labor, 3 Magic (730 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp, Influence, or Magic +12
- **Benefit**: bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]], and spell research checks
- **Time**: 20 days
- **Size**: 4-12 squares

A Magical Repository is similar to a Book Repository, but specific to the study of the arcane arts. It contains shelves of books, comfortable chairs, and tables for studying and for scribing notes and scrolls. If you construct this room from scratch, it grants someone who studies there for 1 hour a +3 bonus on an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] check to answer a question. If you upgrade a Book Repository into this room, you either keep the original Book Repository's bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] checks or change its skill to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]]. An hour of study in this room also grants a +3 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] checks. The room grants an additional +1 bonus on Arcana and Mysticism checks for spell research (see [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/Downtime Activities#Research a Spell|Research a Spell]]) and crafting magic items.
