# Book Repository

- **Upgrade to**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Magical Repository|Magical Repository]]
- **Create**: 8 Goods, 2 Influence, 7 Labor, 1 Magic (460 gp)
- **Earnings**: gp or Influence +8
- **Benefit**: bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] checks of one type
- **Time**: 16 days
- **Size**: 4-12 squares

A Book Repository contains shelves upon shelves of books, in addition to chairs, desks, and tables for reading and studying. Most repositories contain books on a wide array of topics providing a general wealth of information, but some contain books focused on a specific topic. When you construct a Book Repository, select one [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] skill. If someone has a question relating to that [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] skill and is able to spend 1 hour researching in the Book Repository, she gains a +3 bonus on the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] check to answer the question.
