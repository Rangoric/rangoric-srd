# Torture Chamber

- **Create**: 7 Goods, 3 Influence, 5 Labor (330 gp)
- **Benefit**: bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks
- **Time**: 20 days
- **Size**: 6-16 squares

This grim room is used for interrogation as well as torture and other morally questionable acts. It contains structures to bind humanoids and various implements designed to inflict pain and discomfort. Interrogators in this room gain a +3 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks to influence captives.
