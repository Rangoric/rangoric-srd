# Escape Route

- **Upgrade from**: [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Downtime/rooms/Secret Room|Secret Room]]
- **Create**: 9 Goods, 9 Labor (360 gp)
- **Time**: 16 days
- **Size**: 6-12 squares

This is a hallway or tunnel leading to a hidden exit from the building. The exit door is typically a simple wooden door with an average lock ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] DC 20 to open). Either or both ends of the hallway can be secret doors ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] DC 20 to notice).
