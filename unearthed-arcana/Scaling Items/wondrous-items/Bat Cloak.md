# Bat Cloak

*A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|prize]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels.*

Fashioned of dark brown or black cloth, this cloak resembles gigantic bat wings. It allows its wearer to hang upside down from the ceiling like a bat.

**7th Level***:* The cloak grants its wearer a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.

**10th Level**: The wearer can grasp the edges of the cloak to fly(as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]* spell) for up to 7 minutes three times per day, but only in dim light or lower. After each use of this ability, the wearer must wait for at least as long as the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]* effect lasted until using this ability again.

**12th Level**: For each daily use of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]*, the wearer can instead choose to transform into an ordinary bat as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]* for the same duration.

**14th Level**: The wearer can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]* any number of times per day, but is still subject to the dim light restriction and the limit on using the ability again after a previous use.

**16th Level**: The wearer can also transform into a dire bat with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]*, and he can summon a swarm of bats at will as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Swarm|summon swarm]]*.

**18th Level**: The wearer constantly gains the echolocation of a bat. He can hear high-pitched sounds such as bat echolocation, and he gains blindsense 20 feet. This blindsense is sound-based, and is blocked by spells such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Silence|silence]]*.

**20th Level**: The wearer's blindsense increases to blindsense 60 feet, like that of a dire bat. Instead of using *beast shape* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]*, he can transform himself into a bat swarm, gaining the abilities of a bat as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]* plus the bat swarm's distraction, immunity to weapon damage, swarm traits, and wounding abilities.

Faint transmutation; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape III|beast shape III]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Echolocation|echolocation]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Swarm|summon swarm]]*; Price 900 gp; Cost 66,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
