# Lantern Pendant

_A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|prize]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels._

This small pendant of brass and glass is crafted in the shape of a lantern and continuously sheds light as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_ spell. As a move action, the wearer can open the miniature _lantern pendant_ and direct the tiny phantasmal fire within to float to any point within 110 feet. This functions as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, but the wearer can create a number of lights equal to 1/2 his character level, up to a maximum of four, and can create only torchlike lights, not glowing spheres or humanoid shapes. While the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_ are active, the light of the _lantern pendant_ itself is extinguished.

**6th Level**: Once per day, the wearer can command the phantasmal fire to fly forth and limn all creatures in an area as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_. Just as when using _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, the pendant's light is extinguished throughout the duration.

**8th Level**: The wearer can choose between _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glitterdust|glitterdust]]_ (Will DC 13) when he uses the 6th-level ability.

**10th Level**: Once per day, the wearer can focus the lantern's power, causing its light to increase from the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_ spell to the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_ spell, with a duration as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_. The _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_ is suppressed whenever he activates any other ability that extinguishes the pendant's light, but such time still counts against its duration.

**13th Level**: Once per day, the wearer can release the lantern's lights in the form of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wandering Star Motes|wandering star motes]]_(Will DC 16). During the duration, the pendant's light is extinguished.

Faint evocation; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wandering Star Motes|wandering star motes]]_; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 10,500 gp.
