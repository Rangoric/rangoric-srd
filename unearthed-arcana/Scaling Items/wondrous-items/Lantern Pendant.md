# Lantern Pendant

*A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|prize]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels.*

This small pendant of brass and glass is crafted in the shape of a lantern and continuously sheds light as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]* spell. As a move action, the wearer can open the miniature *lantern pendant* and direct the tiny phantasmal fire within to float to any point within 110 feet. This functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, but the wearer can create a number of lights equal to 1/2 his character level, up to a maximum of four, and can create only torchlike lights, not glowing spheres or humanoid shapes. While the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]* are active, the light of the *lantern pendant* itself is extinguished.

**6th Level**: Once per day, the wearer can command the phantasmal fire to fly forth and limn all creatures in an area as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*. Just as when using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, the pendant's light is extinguished throughout the duration.

**8th Level**: The wearer can choose between *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Glitterdust|glitterdust]]* (Will DC 13) when he uses the 6th-level ability.

**10th Level**: Once per day, the wearer can focus the lantern's power, causing its light to increase from the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]* spell to the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]* spell, with a duration as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*. The *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]* is suppressed whenever he activates any other ability that extinguishes the pendant's light, but such time still counts against its duration.

**13th Level**: Once per day, the wearer can release the lantern's lights in the form of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wandering Star Motes|wandering star motes]]*(Will DC 16). During the duration, the pendant's light is extinguished.

Faint evocation; CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wandering Star Motes|wandering star motes]]*; Price 1,000 gp; Cost 10,500 gp.
