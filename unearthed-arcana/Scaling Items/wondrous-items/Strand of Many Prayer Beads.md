# Strand of Many Prayer Beads

*A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|prize]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels.*

This item appears to be a simple string of prayer beads until the owner casts a divine spell while carrying the beads. Once that occurs, she learns how to activate one of the strand's special beads—a *bead of blessing*—to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]* once per day.

**9th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's *bead of healing* to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]* once per day.

**10th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's *bead of healing* to cast *remove blindness/deafness* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]* instead of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]*.

**14th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's *bead of smiting* to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Order’s Wrath|order's wrath]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]* (whichever was used to craft the item) once per day.

**16th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's *bead of karma* to cast her spells with a +4 caster level bonus for 10 minutes once per day.

**19th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's *bead of wind walking* to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Walk|wind walk]]* once per day.

Faint (many schools); CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]*, *remove blindness/deafness*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Walk|wind walk]]*, and either *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Order’s Wrath|order's wrath]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]*; Price 900 gp; Cost 51,375 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
