# Strand of Many Prayer Beads

_A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|prize]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels._

This item appears to be a simple string of prayer beads until the owner casts a divine spell while carrying the beads. Once that occurs, she learns how to activate one of the strand's special beads—a _bead of blessing_—to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_ once per day.

**9th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's _bead of healing_ to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]_ once per day.

**10th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's _bead of healing_ to cast _remove blindness/deafness_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]_ instead of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]_.

**14th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's _bead of smiting_ to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Order’s Wrath|order's wrath]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_ (whichever was used to craft the item) once per day.

**16th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's _bead of karma_ to cast her spells with a +4 caster level bonus for 10 minutes once per day.

**19th Level**: The owner can also activate the strand's _bead of wind walking_ to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Walk|wind walk]]_ once per day.

Faint (many schools); CL 4th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]_, _remove blindness/deafness_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wind Walk|wind walk]]_, and either _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chaos Hammer|chaos hammer]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Smite|holy smite]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Order’s Wrath|order's wrath]]_, or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Unholy Blight|unholy blight]]_; Price 900 gp; Cost 51,375 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
