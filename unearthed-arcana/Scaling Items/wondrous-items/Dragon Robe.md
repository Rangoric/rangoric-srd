# Dragon Robe

*A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|wonder]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels.*

Embroidered dragons of gold thread adorn this red silk robe. The wearer gains a +3 enhancement bonus to natural armor and fire resistance 10. Once per day, the wearer can gain the benefit of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkvision|darkvision]]*, as the spell. A typical *dragon robe* is attuned to red dragons, though variants attuned to other types of dragons exist.

**14th Level**: The wearer can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dragon’s Breath|dragon's breath]]* (red dragon) once per day. The save DC is equal to 13 + the wearer's Constitution modifier, and spell resistance does not apply.

**16th Level**: The wearer's enhancement bonus to natural armor increases to +4, and his fire resistance increases to 20.

**18th Level**: The wearer can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dragon’s Breath|dragon's breath]]* three times per day. The wearer can also use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Form of the Dragon III|form of the dragon III]]* once per day, with a save DC equal to 22 + the wearer's Constitution modifier.

**20th Level**: The wearer's enhancement bonus to natural armor increases to +5 and his fire resistance to 30. In addition, any *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dragon’s Breath|dragon's breath]]* effect created by this item is maximized.

Strong transmutation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], Maximize Spell*, darkvision*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dragon’s Breath|dragon's breath]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Form of the Dragon III|form of the dragon III]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]*; Price 38,000 gp; Cost 132,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
