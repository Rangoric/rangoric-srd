# Glorious Gorget

_A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|prize]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels._

This gilded neckpiece is inlaid with sunstone and diamond and engraved with wreaths of laurel. The wearer possesses great eloquence and gravitas, gaining a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (oratory) checks.

**10th Level**: The wearer's speeches can fill her allies with purpose. As a full-round action, she can attempt a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (oratory) check to affect all allies who can hear her with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Good Hope|good hope]]_ for 1 round. If she fails the check, all allies who heard her ineloquent speech cannot benefit from this ability for 24 hours.

**12th Level**: The wearer can improve someone's attitude up to three steps when using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]]. When she uses [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] to influence someone, she can present herself as glorious and powerful without threatening them, causing the attempt to operate like a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] check to influence instead.

**14th Level**: When the wearer uses the gorget's _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Good Hope|good hope]]_ ability, it lasts 1 additional round for every 10 by which her result exceeds the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (oratory) check DC. Once per day, she can extend the duration to the full duration of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Good Hope|good hope]]_.

Moderate enchantment; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Good Hope|good hope]]_; Price 5,000 gp; Cost 13,875 gp; Weight 2 lbs.
