# Cloak of the Elven Spirit

*A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|bauble]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels.*

When this plain gray cloak is worn with the hood drawn up around the head, the wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. If the wearer spends at least 1 minute concentrating on blending the cloak's colors to match her surroundings, she can attempt [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks without cover or concealment until she makes an attack or moves more than half her base speed on her turn. Once she breaks this effect, she must spend another minute to reactivate it.

**14th Level**:The cloak grants its wearer the camouflage ranger ability in forested areas.

**18th Level**: At will, when the wearer envelops herself in the cloak as a standard action, she can become invisible as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*.

**20th Level**: If the wearer also wears the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/wondrous-items/Boots of the Elven Spirit|boots of the elven spirit]], she can focus the cloak's *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]* effect. As a free action, she can choose to make it so her attacks do not end the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]* effect for 1 round. She can use this ability for 10 rounds per day, but the rounds need not be consecutive.

Strong transmutation; CL 12th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blend|blend]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility, Greater|greater invisibility]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]*, creator must be an elf; Price 5,400 gp; Cost 22,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
