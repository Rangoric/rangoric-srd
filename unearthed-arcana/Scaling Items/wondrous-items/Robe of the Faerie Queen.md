# Robe of the Faerie Queen

_A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|wonder]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels._

When donned, this sheer, sleeved robe fades to near invisibility. It grants the wearer a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks against fey. On command, the mantle glows in delicate _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_ of any color of the wearer's choice, and a second command word ends the glow.

**8th Level**: The mantle magnifies and sharpens the wearer's beauty such that her +4 competence bonus now applies on all Charisma checks, Charisma-based skill checks, and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks against any creature. Against fey, the bonus increases to +6.

**10th Level**: The mantle's wearer becomes manipulative and eloquent in social situations. Whenever she converses with creatures, she can insert a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_ (Will DC 14 negates) for every 10 minutes of conversation by succeeding at a DC 14 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (oratory) check. If she ever fails the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (oratory) check to use this ability, all creatures who witnessed her misstep are immune to this ability for 24 hours.

**12th Level**: Once per day, the wearer can release the glow within the mantle in a single coruscating burst of color. This blinds creatures in a 10-foot radius (Reflex DC 19 negates) for 1d4 rounds and completely dispels any darkness spells of 6th level or lower whose source is within its area.

**14th Level**: The wearer grows a pair of gossamer wings. The wings are delicate and weak, carrying the wearer only with difficulty. She gains a fly speed of 30 feet (poor maneuverability).

**16th Level**: The wearer's wings grow stronger, more agile, and more beautiful, and iridescent patterns trace along them. Her fly speed increases to 60 feet (good maneuverability), and she gains a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks.

**18th Level**: The wearer gains a small domain in the fey realm. She can access her domain from any location, creating a silvery gateway. Treat her domain as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Magnificent Mansion|mage's magnificent mansion]]_ except that its size equals 20 10-foot cubes/level, the domain appears as wilderness instead of a mansion, and the servants are tiny fey instead of nearly transparent liveried servants.

**20th Level**: The wearer's type changes to fey, and she gains DR 10/cold iron. If the wearer's type was already fey and she already possessed DR/cold iron, her DR increases by 10 and magic weapons with enhancement bonuses of +3 or greater do not count as cold iron for the purpose of bypassing her DR.
