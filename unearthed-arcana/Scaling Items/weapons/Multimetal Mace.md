# Multimetal Mace

_A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|prize]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels._

This _+1 light mace_ has bands of several different metals around its hilt, each band one inch thick. Once per day, the mace's owner can transform the mace into a different metal as a standard action. The _multimetal mace_ remains that type of metal until transformed again. To begin with, it can transform to silver or back to steel. It retains all magical abilities when it transforms, along with any ongoing spells and effects currently affecting it.

**8th Level**: The _multimetal mace_ can be transformed into cold iron.

**10th Level**: The _multimetal mace_ can be transformed into adamantine.

**12th Level**: The _multimetal mace_ is a _+2 light mace_.

Moderate transmutation; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heart of the Metal|heart of the metal]]_; Price 2,650 gp; Cost 11,405 gp; Weight 4 lbs.
