# Staff of Ways

_A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|prize]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels._

This wooden staff is carved with intricate patterns of waves, wind, trails, and vines. As the wielder goes up in level, the patterns begin to move, slowly at first, but gradually increasing in speed and magnitude. At the highest levels, the staff appears to be filled with a twisting and complex nature scene, with forking paths and raging tsunamis. This staff allows the use of the following spells.

- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_ (1 charge)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_ (2 charges)
- _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Monkey Fish|monkey fish]]_ (2 charges)

**12th Level**: The staff allows the use of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_ for 2 charges. The cost of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Monkey Fish|monkey fish]]_ each reduce to 1 charge.

**14th Level**: The staff allows the use of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]_ for 2 charges. The cost of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]_ decreases to 1 charge.

**16th Level**: The staff grants its owner a permanent +10-foot enhancement bonus to her base movement speed, a swim speed of 10 feet, and a climb speed of 10 feet. The staff can no longer be used to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Longstrider|longstrider]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Monkey Fish|monkey fish]]_. The staff allows the use of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_ for 2 charges and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_ for 3 charges. The cost of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fly|fly]]_ decreases to 1 charge.

**18th Level**: The staff allows the use of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Find the Path|find the path]]_ for 3 charges. The cost for _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]_ decreases to 1 charge.

**20th Level**: The staff grants its owner a fly speed of 30 feet (poor maneuverability). _Find the path_ now costs 2 charges, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_ costs only 1 charge. If the staff's wielder breaks the staff in two as a full-round action, she can transport herself and up to 19 allies to a safe location she knows extremely well. This ability ignores local conditions as if it were the transport travelers effect of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_.
