# Staff of the Pyre Lord

*A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|wonder]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels.*

This staff is carved entirely of polished obsidian inset with rubies. Flickering flames seem to dance across its surface. As the wielder goes up in level, the illusory flames grow larger and change in color, starting out a faint yellow and eventually becoming a deep blue. This staff allows the use of the following spells.

- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spark|spark]]* (1 charge)
- *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]* (2 charges)

**10th Level**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]* now costs only 1 charge, and the staff allows the use of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]* for 2 charges.

**12th Level**: The staff's *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]* spells become *empowered fireball*. The staff counts as a *+1 flaming quarterstaff* when used as a weapon.

**14th Level**: The staff allows the use of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]* for 2 charges to summon fire elementals only.

**16th Level**: The staff replaces *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]* with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VIII|summon monster VIII]]* for 3 charges to summon fire elementals only. It also allows the use of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]]* for 2 charges.

**18th Level**: The staff allows the use of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]]* for 3 charges. The cost for *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VIII|summon monster VIII]]* decreases to 2 charges, and *empowered fireball* now costs 1 charge.

**20th Level**: The cost of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]]* each decrease to 1 charge.

Moderate evocation; CL 8th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Staff|Craft Staff]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Empower Spell|Empower Spell]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scorching Ray|scorching ray]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spark|spark]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VIII|summon monster VIII]]*; Price 8,800 gp; Cost 132,000 gp; Weight 1 lb.
