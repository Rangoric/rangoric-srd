# Ring of the Earthlord

*A scaling item ([[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Scaling Items/Scaling Items#^value-of-scaling-items|wonder]]): its powers unlock as its wielder gains levels.*

This stone ring is constantly covered in a thin layer of dirt. It grants its wearer the ability to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]* once per day.

**8th Level**: The wearer can also use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Soften Earth and Stone|soften earth and stone]]* once per day. She can speak Terran, and she gains a +2 resistance bonus on saving throws against attacks by creatures from the Plane of Earth and a +4 morale bonus on attack rolls against such creatures. Additionally, these creatures take a –1 penalty on attack rolls against her. The wearer takes a –2 penalty on all saving throws against air or electricity effects.

**11th Level**: Earth elementals can't attack the wearer or even approach within 5 feet of her. If she wishes, the wearer can attempt to use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]* on an earth elemental (DC 17 Will save negates). If she fails the attempt, she loses the protection against that elemental and can't attempt to charm that elemental again.

**14th Level**: The wearer can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Shape|stone shape]]* twice per day and use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]* on herself once per week.

**16th Level**: The wearer can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Stone|wall of stone]]* once per day and can now use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]* three times per day.

**17th Level**: The wearer can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Passwall|passwall]]* once per week and can now use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]* at will.

**18th Level**: The wearer can now use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Passwall|passwall]]* twice per week and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Soften Earth and Stone|soften earth and stone]]* at will.

Moderate conjuration; CL 6th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forge Ring|Forge Ring]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meld into Stone|meld into stone]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Passwall|passwall]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Soften Earth and Stone|soften earth and stone]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stone Shape|stone shape]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster VI|summon monster VI]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Stone|wall of stone]]*; Price 4,800 gp; Cost 79,500 gp.
