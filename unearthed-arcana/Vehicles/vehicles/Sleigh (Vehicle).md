# Sleigh (Vehicle)

- **Vehicle type**: land
- **Size**: Large
- **Squares**: 4 (10 ft. by 10 ft.; 3 feet high or 13 feet high with sail)
- **Cost**: 100 gp
- **AC**: 9
- **Hardness**: 5
- **HP**: 60 (29)
- **Base save**: +1
- **Maximum speed**: twice the speed of the pulling creature(s) (muscle), or 100 ft. (current)
- **Acceleration**: half the speed of the pulling creature(s) (muscle), or 30 ft. (current)
- **CMB**: +1
- **CMD**: 11
- **Ramming damage**: 1d8
- **Propulsion**: muscle (pulled; 4 Medium creatures or 1 Large creature; on ice, the creatures must be clawed rather than hoofed) or current (air; 8-square sail; hp 40)
- **Driving check**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Piloting|Piloting]] (in the case of creatures with animal intelligence), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] (in the case of intelligent creatures pulling the vehicle), or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] (in the case of air current; +10 to the DC)
- **Forward facing**: toward the creatures pulling the vehicle or the front of the sleigh in the case of air current propulsion
- **Driving device**: reins (muscle propulsion) or rigging (air current propulsion)
- **Driving space**: the two most forward squares of the sleigh
- **Decks**: 1
