# Carriage (Vehicle)

- **Vehicle type**: land
- **Size**: Large
- **Squares**: 6 (10 ft. by 15 ft.; 6 feet high)
- **Cost**: 100 gp
- **AC**: 9
- **Hardness**: 5
- **HP**: 90 (44)
- **Base save**: +1
- **Maximum speed**: twice the speed of the pulling creature(s)
- **Acceleration**: the speed of the pulling creature(s)
- **CMB**: +1
- **CMD**: 11
- **Ramming damage**: 1d8
- **Propulsion**: muscle (pulled; 6 Medium creatures or 2 Large creatures)
- **Driving check**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Piloting|Piloting]] (in the case of creatures with animal intelligence pulling the vehicle), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] (in the case of intelligent creatures pulling the vehicle)
- **Forward facing**: toward the creatures pulling the vehicle
- **Driving device**: reins
- **Driving space**: the most forward squares if the carriage's upper deck.
- **Decks**: 2; a lower cab with enough room for four passengers, and an upper deck with room for a driver and another passenger.
