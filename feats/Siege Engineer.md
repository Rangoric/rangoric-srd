# Siege Engineer

You are proficient with all siege weaponry.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 5 ranks or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (siege engineer) 5 ranks, proficiency with one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines|siege engine]].

**Benefit**: You are considered to be proficient with all [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines|siege engines]]. Also, when you are crew lead for a siege engine, you do not generate [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Mishaps and Misfires|mishaps]] on the roll of a natural 1.

**Normal**: Each siege engine is an exotic weapon.
