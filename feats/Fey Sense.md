# Fey Sense

You have the ability to sense the presence of fey beings.

**Prerequisites**: See Special.

**Benefit**: As a free action, you can sense fey within 100 feet: how many there are and their general direction, but not their kinds or exact places. This doesn’t let you see invisible fey. You also gain a +5 bonus on Perception checks to notice creatures and objects made invisible by fey powers and abilities (not by spells).

**Special**: You can take this feat only if you have been exposed to the fey or have fey blood.
