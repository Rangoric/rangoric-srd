# Mystical Affinity

> [!note] Replaced by [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]]
> Mystical Affinity gave +2 on Emulation and one magic Knowledge field. The two-skill feats were removed; Skill Focus, which now rises to +6 at 10 ranks, takes their place (2026-10-23; see [[Working Folder/Done/plans/Skill Ranks Rework|Skill Ranks Rework]]). The original text is kept below for reference.

## Original text

You have a knack for magical and psionic devices and lore.

**Benefit**: You get a +2 bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] checks, and on checks with one magic [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] field of your choice: arcana, nature, theology, or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge (Psionics)|psionics]]. You choose the field when you take the feat.
