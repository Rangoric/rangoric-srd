# Greater Trip

You can make free attacks on foes that you knock down.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], base attack bonus +6, Int 13.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to trip a foe. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]]. Whenever you trip an opponent, it provokes attacks of opportunity.

**Normal**: Creatures don’t provoke attacks of opportunity from being tripped.
