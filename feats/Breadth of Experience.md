# Breadth of Experience

Although still young for your kind, you have a lifetime of knowledge and training.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]], or [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]]; 100+ years old.

**Benefit**: You get a +2 bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] skill checks, and can make checks with those skills untrained.
