# Greater Penetrating Strike

Your attacks penetrate the defenses of most foes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Penetrating Strike|Penetrating Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], fighter level 16th.

**Benefit**: Your attacks with weapons in which you have Weapon Focus ignore up to 10 points of damage reduction. This is reduced to 5 points for damage reduction without a type (such as DR 10/—).
