# Combat Medic

You know the urgency of treating wounds in the heat of battle, applying first aid to your allies with such speed that you assure no one gets left behind.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] to [[rangoric-srd/actions/Stabilize (Action)|stabilize]] an ally who also has this feat, [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Wound|treat its caltrop wounds]], or [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Poison|treat its poison]], you provoke no [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]], and can take 10 on the check. Unlike with other teamwork feats, allies that are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]], or cannot otherwise act still count for the purposes of this feat.
