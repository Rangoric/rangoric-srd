# Sense Link

When you and your eidolon share senses, your combined minds grant you exceptional powers of observation.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-bond-senses|Bond senses]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When sharing the senses of your eidolon, you gain a +4 competence bonus on Perception checks for the duration of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-bond-senses|bond senses]] ability.
