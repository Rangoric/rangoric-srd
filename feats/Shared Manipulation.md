# Shared Manipulation

You can subtly bolster allies’ ability to misdirect and infuriate their enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]].

**Benefit**: As a move action, you can grant all friendly creatures within 30 feet who can see or hear you a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks (choose which skill to affect each time you use this ability) for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 round).
