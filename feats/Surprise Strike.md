# Surprise Strike

You actually seem to do more damage when frantically trying to avoid your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cautious Fighter|Cautious Fighter]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Desperate Swing|Desperate Swing]], base attack bonus +6, [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]].

**Benefit**: Once per day, when fighting defensively or making an attack of opportunity while taking the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]] action with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Desperate Swing|Desperate Swing]] feat, you take no penalty on the attack roll.
