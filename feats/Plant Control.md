# Plant Control

Prerequisites: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Plant Defiance|Plant Defiance]], ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Plants|speak with plants]].  

Benefit: You can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|rebuke or command]]plant creatures as an evil cleric rebukes undead. To command a plant, you must be able to speak with it via a speak with plants effect, though it may do so mentally if desired. This ability is usable a total number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. You use your highest caster level to determine the level at which you rebuke plants.
