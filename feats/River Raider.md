# River Raider

You're skilled at creeping up on watercraft without being noticed and getting the jump on their occupants.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks while swimming in calm or rough water (but not stormy water). If you can act in a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]] and start it in water, you can take both a move and a standard action. You still can’t take a full-round action in the surprise round.

**Normal**: You can take only a move or a standard action in the surprise round.
