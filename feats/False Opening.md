# False Opening

When you make a ranged attack while threatened, you can fool your opponent into thinking he has an opening.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Close-Quarters Thrower|Close-Quarters Thrower]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Master|Point Blank Master]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with the selected ranged weapon.

**Benefit**: Choose a ranged or thrown weapon. When you make a ranged attack with it, you can choose to provoke attacks of opportunity from one or more opponents who threaten you. You gain a +4 dodge bonus to AC against those attacks. An opponent that makes such an attack and misses loses its Dexterity bonus to AC against you until the end of your turn.
