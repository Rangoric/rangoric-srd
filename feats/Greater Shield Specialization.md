# Greater Shield Specialization

Your shield protects you from critical hits.

**Prerequisites**: Proficiency with the selected shield, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Shield Focus|Greater Shield Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Focus|Shield Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Specialization|Shield Specialization]] with the selected shield, fighter level 12th.

**Benefit**: Choose a type of shield for which you have Shield Specialization. With it, you gain a +2 bonus to AC against critical hit confirmation rolls (stacking with Shield Specialization), and once per day you can negate a critical hit; the damage is rolled normally instead.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects don’t stack. Each time, it applies to a new type of shield.
