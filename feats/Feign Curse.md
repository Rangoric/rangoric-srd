# Feign Curse

You can fool others into believing you have ensorcelled them.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 5 ranks, 1 rank in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can pretend to curse a target. It must make a Sense Motive or Magic skill check (whichever is higher) against a DC of 15 + your ranks in Deception + your Charisma modifier, adding any bonus it has on saves against curses. If it fails, it is plagued by self-doubt: for its next two attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks or ability checks, it rolls twice and takes the lower result, plus one more roll for every 5 ranks of Deception you have beyond 5. This is a mind-affecting effect and doesn’t work on targets immune to curses. Once you try this on a creature, you can’t again for 24 hours, and if it sees through you, it gains a +10 bonus against later attempts.
