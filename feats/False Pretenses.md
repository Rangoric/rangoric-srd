# False Pretenses

Those who try to charm you get an unpleasant surprise.

**Prerequisite:** Exposure to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Person|dominate]] spell.

**Benefit:** When a charm or compulsion effect misses you, the character trying to charm or compel you believes that it hit. You can play along voluntarily if you wish to. if the charm or compulsion involves telepathic commands, you continue to receive them, although you aren't obligated to follow them.
