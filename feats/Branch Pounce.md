# Branch Pounce

You are adept at exploiting higher ground to its greatest advantage in battle, and can leap from above to deal incredible damage to your foes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] a target by jumping down on it from above (such as out of a tree), you can soften your fall with a melee attack. If the attack at the end of your charge hits, it deals its normal damage plus the falling damage for your fall (1d6 for a 10-foot fall, 2d6 for 20 feet and so on), which isn’t multiplied on a critical hit. You land in an empty square of your choice next to the target and take falling damage as if the fall were 10 feet shorter. You can make an Acrobatics check as normal to treat it as 10 feet shorter still. If the attack misses, you land [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] in a random square next to the target and take the full falling damage.
