# Opening Volley

Your ranged assault leaves your foe disoriented and vulnerable to your melee attack.

**Benefit**: Whenever you deal damage with a ranged attack, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on the next melee attack roll you make against that opponent, as long as it is before the end of your next turn.
