# Boar Ferocity

Your flesh-ripping unarmed strikes terrify your victims.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Boar Style|Boar Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can also deal piercing damage with your unarmed strikes, and you gain a +2 bonus on Intimidation checks to demoralize opponents. While using Boar Style, whenever you tear an opponent’s flesh, you can make an Intimidation check to demoralize it as a free action.
