# Ranged Feint

You can mislead foes about your aim with ranged attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +2, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] with a ranged weapon by throwing a thrown weapon or firing one piece of ammunition. It takes the same action as a normal feint, though you may need to reload or draw another weapon afterward. A successful ranged feint denies the enemy its Dexterity bonus to AC against your ranged attacks as well as your melee attacks, for the usual time. If your feints normally deny a foe its Dexterity bonus against other creatures’ attacks too, that applies only to their melee attacks.

**Normal**: You can feint only in melee, as the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] rule says.
