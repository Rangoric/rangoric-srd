# Tail Terror

You have strengthened your tail enough to make slap attacks with it.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]].

**Benefit**: You can make a tail slap attack with your tail. This is a secondary [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^natural-weapons|natural attack]] that deals 1d4 points of bludgeoning damage.
