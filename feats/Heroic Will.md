# Heroic Will

Your indomitable will breaks free from mental shackles.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Iron Will|Iron Will]], base Will save +4, [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]].

**Benefit**: Once per day as a standard action, you may make a harmful condition affecting you that attacks your Will attack you again, and a miss ends it. If you are dominated, controlled, or cannot take an action because of the effect you are trying to make attack again, you can have it attack at the start of the turn as no action, but on a miss, your turn ends. You cannot use this feat to remove instantaneous effects, effects that do not attack Will, or effects that make no attack against a defense.
