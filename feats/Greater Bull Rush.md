# Greater Bull Rush

Your bull rush attacks throw enemies off balance.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6, Str 13.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to bull rush a foe. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]]. Whenever you bull rush an opponent, its movement provokes attacks of opportunity from all your allies (but not you).

**Normal**: Creatures moved by bull rush don’t provoke attacks of opportunity.
