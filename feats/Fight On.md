# Fight On

You can keep fighting even after you should be dead.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13; [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]], or orc.

**Benefit**: Once per day, you can gain a number of temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier. You can activate this feat as an immediate action when reduced to 0 or fewer hit points. You can use this feat to prevent yourself from [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]]. These temporary hit points last for 1 minute. If your hit points drop below 0 due to the loss of these temporary hit points, you fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] and are dying as normal. If you also have a ferocity racial trait (such as the half-orc’s [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|orc ferocity]]), you can use that once you have lost the temporary hit points from this feat.
