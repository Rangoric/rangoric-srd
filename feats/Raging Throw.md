# Raging Throw

You expend some of your rage to throw one opponent at another.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, Con 13, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: While raging, when you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] combat maneuver, you can spend 1 additional round of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] as a swift action to add your Constitution bonus on your combat maneuver check. Further, if you bull rush an opponent into a square another creature occupies or into a solid object, the opponent and the creature or object take bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier + your Constitution modifier.
