# Aquatic Spell

You can cast your spells underwater or into water with little difficulty.

**Benefit**: An aquatic spell works normally underwater, with no check needed to cast it even if it has the fire descriptor (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Underwater Combat|Underwater Combat]]). It can also be cast from the surface into water and still take effect. A aquatic spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
