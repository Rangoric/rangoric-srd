# Field Repair

You can repair your broken weapon or armor to serviceability even without the benefits of artisan tools.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (any one craft) 4 ranks.

**Benefit**: If you are trained in a craft Background that fits a broken item, you can repair it with no raw materials and no penalty for using improvised tools. Each day you spend, the item regains 1 hit point plus one-quarter of its original hit points. Alternatively, if the item gained the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition because it is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]] that has [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms#Misfires|misfired]] or a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines|siege engine]] that suffered a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Mishaps and Misfires|mishap]], or has the broken condition because it is fragile (as the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dogslicer|dogslicer]] is) or has some similar flaw, you can make a Background check to make things with the DC it takes to craft that item (see the table below). If the check succeeds, the item loses the broken condition.

| Item                        | Subject                     | making DC    |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------ |
| A fragile weapon            | Weaponsmithing or bowmaking | Normal DC –5 |
| One-handed firearm          | Gunsmithing                 | 20           |
| Two-handed firearm          | Gunsmithing                 | 20           |
| Siege firearm, heavy        | Gunsmithing                 | 25           |
| Siege firearm, medium       | Gunsmithing                 | 30           |
| Siege firearm, light        | Gunsmithing                 | 35           |
| Ranged siege engine, heavy  | Siegecraft                  | 20           |
| Ranged siege engine, medium | Siegecraft                  | 25           |

**Normal**: Improvised tools give a –2 penalty on Background checks to make things, and repairs need raw materials.
