# Field Repair

You can repair your broken weapon or armor to serviceability even without the benefits of artisan tools.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] 4 ranks.

**Benefit**: If you are trained in a Craft skill that fits a broken item, you can repair it with no raw materials and no penalty for using improvised tools. Each day you spend, the item regains 1 hit point plus one-quarter of its original hit points.

**Normal**: Improvised tools give a –2 penalty on Craft checks, and repairs need raw materials.
