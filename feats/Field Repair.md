# Field Repair

You can repair your broken weapon or armor to serviceability even without the benefits of artisan tools.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] 4 ranks.

**Benefit**: If you are trained in a Craft skill that fits a broken item, you can repair it with no raw materials and no penalty for using improvised tools. Each day you spend, the item regains 1 hit point plus one-quarter of its original hit points. Alternatively, if the item gained the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition because it is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]] that has [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms#Misfires|misfired]], or has the broken condition because it is fragile (as the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dogslicer|dogslicer]] is) or has some similar flaw, you can make a Craft check with the DC it takes to craft that item (see the table below). If the check succeeds, the item loses the broken condition.

| Item | Craft Skill | Craft DC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A fragile weapon | Weapons or bows | Normal DC –5 |
| One-handed firearm | Firearms | 20 |
| Two-handed firearm | Firearms | 20 |

**Normal**: Improvised tools give a –2 penalty on Craft checks, and repairs need raw materials.
