# Psychic Virtuoso

You can use all your occult skill unlocks more often, and you are more talented at using them.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] or the ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spells]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^occult-unlocks|occult skill unlocks]]. If you have more than 10 ranks in the unlock’s skill, the bonus increases to +4. The bonus doesn’t apply on other checks with those skills, only on checks for occult skill unlocks.
