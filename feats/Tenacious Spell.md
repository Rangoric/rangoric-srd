# Tenacious Spell

Your magic is difficult to unravel.

**Benefit**: The DC of caster level checks to counter or dispel a tenacious spell is 2 higher. If a tenacious spell is dispelled or dismissed, it lasts 1d4 more rounds (but no longer than its normal duration) before ending. This doesn’t happen if *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antimagic Field|antimagic field]]* or a similar effect suppresses or ends it without dispelling or dismissing it. A tenacious spell’s lingering aura lasts twice as long as usual for *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*. A tenacious spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
