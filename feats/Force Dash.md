# Force Dash

You can absorb the magic in your force spells to give you a concentrated burst of speed.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +4, ability to cast 2nd-level arcane spells, ability to cast at least one arcane force spell.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, you can give up a prepared arcane force spell (or, if you cast spontaneously, a spell slot of the same level as an arcane force spell you know) to gain an enhancement bonus to speed of 10 feet × the spell’s level. The bonus applies to all your kinds of movement and lasts 1 round. If you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] while it lasts, you don’t take the charge’s –2 penalty to AC.
