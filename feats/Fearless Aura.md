# Fearless Aura

Your aura of courage becomes more potent, as your steadfast resolve is also manifested by your allies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-courage|Aura of courage]] class feature, caster level 8th.

**Benefit**: Your aura of courage expands to a 20-foot-radius emanation. Allies within the aura are immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] effects.

**Normal**: The aura of courage reaches only as far as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-courage|class feature]] says, and gives allies a bonus against fear effects rather than immunity.
