# Great Hatred

Your rage burns with vast intensity.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|hatred]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: You gain an additional +1 bonus on melee and thrown weapon attacks against targets of your [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|hatred]] racial trait.
