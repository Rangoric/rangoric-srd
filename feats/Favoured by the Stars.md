# Favoured by the Stars

You were born under many stars, and their light favors you.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus on saving throws against [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|stars and constellations]] that target you. It doesn't help against one whose area you are merely in.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
