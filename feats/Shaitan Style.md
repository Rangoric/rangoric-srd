# Shaitan Style

You strike with the caustic forces from within the earth.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13, Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +9 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: You gain one more Elemental Fist attempt per day. While using this style and Elemental Fist to deal acid damage, you gain a bonus on acid damage rolls equal to your Wisdom bonus. If your Elemental Fist attack misses while you are using it to deal acid damage, you still deal 1d6 points of acid damage to your target.
