# Arc Slinger

You can twirl your sling in a way that maximizes its effectiveness.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], proficient with sling or halfling sling staff.

**Benefit**: When using a sling or sling staff, you reduce your penalty on ranged attack rolls due to range by 2. [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]]’s damage bonus applies within the first normal range increment of your sling (50 feet) or sling staff (80 feet).
