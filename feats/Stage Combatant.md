# Stage Combatant

You are a master of stage and nonlethal combats.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], base attack bonus +5.

**Benefit**: When you attack with a weapon in which you have Weapon Focus, you take no penalty on the attack roll for an attack that deals no damage or nonlethal damage.

**Normal**: Attacks that deal no damage or nonlethal damage take a –4 penalty on attack rolls.
