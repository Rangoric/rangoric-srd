# Wood Crafter

Eschewing the use of metals in your craft, you excel at crafting armor from wood.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (armorsmithing) 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 bonus on Background (armorsmithing) checks to make armor or shields from wood or special materials that contain wood (such as [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Special Materials|darkwood]]). If you beat the DC by 5 or more, you also make the item for 10% less.
