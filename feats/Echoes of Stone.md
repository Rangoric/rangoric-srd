# Echoes of Stone

Your senses are keener among the rocks and stones.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|Oread]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks underground, and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to avoid becoming lost in caverns and rocky areas.
