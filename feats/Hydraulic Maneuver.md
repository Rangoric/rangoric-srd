# Hydraulic Maneuver

You can use your *[[path srd 3.5/spells/Hydraulic Push|hydraulic push]]* to disarm or trip.

**Prerequisites**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|Hydraulic push]]* spell-like ability, [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|undine]].

**Benefit**: You may use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|hydraulic push]]* to attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] (blind or dazzle only), or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver. Each time you use hydraulic push, you must decide which of the allowed combat maneuvers you want to perform. You may use this feat with your hydraulic push racial spell-like ability, your class-granted use of hydraulic push, or any hydraulic push spells you cast, but not with magic items or other external sources that use that spell.

**Normal**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|Hydraulic push]]* can only be used to make a bull rush combat maneuver.
