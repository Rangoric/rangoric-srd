# Trick Riding

You are not only skilled at controlling a horse in combat; you can make it look like art.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 9 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Combat|Mounted Combat]].

**Benefit**: While wearing light or no armor, you don’t need to make Animal Handling checks for any riding task with a DC of 15 or lower. You don’t take the –5 penalty for riding bareback. You can use Mounted Combat to negate a hit on your mount twice per round instead of once.
