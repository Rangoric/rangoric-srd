# Sabotage Specialist

You have an amazing knack for sabotaging devices.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Engineering or Sleight of Hand), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you use Engineering to rig a simple or tricky device (such as a saddle or wagon wheel) to work for a while and then fail, you can choose when it fails, up to 60 minutes after you rig it. It fails 1d6 – 3 rounds after that time (a result of –1 or –2 means it fails 1 or 2 rounds early).

**Normal**: A rigged device fails after 1d4 rounds or minutes of use.
