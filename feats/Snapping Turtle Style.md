# Snapping Turtle Style

Your deft unarmed style allows you to shield your body from harm.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +1 or monk level 1st.

**Benefit**: While using this style with at least one hand free, you gain a +1 shield bonus to AC.
