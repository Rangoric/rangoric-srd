# Night Stalker

You excel at hunting humans in the dead of night and murdering them quietly.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]]), [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]], half-ogre or [[rangoric-srd/races/Ogre|ogre]], size Large or larger.

**Benefit**: While in areas of dim lighting or natural darkness, you ignore all size penalties on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks and gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] enemies.
