# Improved Whip Mastery

You are able to entangle opponents with the coils of your whip.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (whip), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Whip Mastery|Whip Mastery]], base attack bonus +5.

**Benefit**: While wielding a whip, you threaten the area of your natural reach plus 5 feet. You can also use a whip to grab an unattended Small or Tiny object within its reach and pull it into your square, by hitting AC 10 with a melee touch attack. And you can wrap 5 feet of your whip around an object within reach, like a grappling hook, and swing on the rest like a rope. You can let go of the object as a free action, but you can’t attack with the whip while it is holding something.
