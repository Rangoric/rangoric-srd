# Neither Elf nor Human

You have removed yourself from your heritage so thoroughly that even magic does not recognize you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exile's Path|Exile's Path]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Seen and Unseen|Seen and Unseen]], character level 11th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]].

**Benefit**: You are not considered elven or human for the purpose of harmful spells or effects based on your type, like a [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Bane (Weapon Special Ability)|bane]] weapon or a ranger’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] class feature.
