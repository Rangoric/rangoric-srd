# Mounted Combat

**Prerequisite**: Animal Handling 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Once per round when your mount is hit in combat, you may attempt an Animal Handling check (as a reaction) to negate the hit. The hit is negated if your Animal Handling check result is greater than the opponent’s attack roll. (Essentially, the Animal Handling check result becomes the mount’s Armor Class if it’s higher than the mount’s regular AC.)

**Special**: A fighter may select Mounted Combat as one of his fighter bonus feats.
