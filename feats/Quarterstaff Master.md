# Quarterstaff Master

You can wield a quarterstaff as either a two-handed or one-handed weapon.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (quarterstaff), base attack bonus +5.

**Benefit**: You can wield a quarterstaff as a one-handed weapon. At the start of your turn, you decide whether to wield it one-handed or two-handed. While you wield it one-handed, your other hand is free and you can’t use the staff as a double weapon. You can take [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Specialization|Weapon Specialization]] in the quarterstaff even if you have no fighter levels.
