# Street Style

You use the urban environment to your advantage, thrashing and bashing your foe and driving it back.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +4 or monk level 3rd.

**Benefit**: While using this style, once per round when you hit a target with an unarmed strike, you can spend a swift action to deal an extra 1d6 points of damage and try to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] that target.

**Special**: You can enter this style’s stance only in urban terrain, and it ends as soon as you leave urban terrain.
