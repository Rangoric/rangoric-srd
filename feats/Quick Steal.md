# Quick Steal

You are adept at relieving foes of their belongings even while you strike.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Steal|Improved Steal]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: On your turn, you can attempt a single [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] combat maneuver in place of one of your melee attacks. You must use the attack with your highest base attack bonus for it.

**Normal**: A steal combat maneuver is a standard action.
