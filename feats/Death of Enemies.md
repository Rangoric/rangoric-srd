# Death of Enemies

Prerequisites: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bane of Enemies|Bane of Enemies]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 27 ranks, five or more favored enemies.

Benefit: Any time the character scores a critical hit against one of his or her favored enemies, the critical also attacks its Fortitude, its attack bonus 1/2 character’s ranger class level + character’s Wisdom modifier; on a hit, it dies instantly.

Special: Creatures immune to critical hits can’t be affected by this feat.
