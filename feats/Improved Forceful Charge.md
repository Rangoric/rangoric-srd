# Improved Forceful Charge

Enemies fall before this animal’s charge.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Forceful Charge|Forceful Charge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]].

**Benefit**: This animal can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] a target that is up to two size categories larger than itself. If it pushes the target at least 10 feet as a result of this bull rush, it can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver check against the target as a free action. This trip combat maneuver provokes attacks of opportunity as normal.
