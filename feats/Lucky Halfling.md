# Lucky Halfling

You bring luck to those with whom you travel.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]].

**Benefit**: Once per day, when an effect attacks the defense of one of your allies within 30 feet, you may have it attack the same defense of yours as if you were the one subject to it. You may use this ability after the attack against your ally is rolled, but before the GM declares if it hit or missed. Your ally may choose to use the result against your defense in place of its own.
