# Siege Gunner

Aiming outsized siege weapons poses little difficulty for you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Siege Engineer|Siege Engineer]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (siege engineer) 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You take no size penalty for aiming a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Direct-Fire Ranged Siege Engines|direct-fire siege weapon]] larger than yourself. If you operate an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Indirect-Fire Ranged Siege Engines|indirect-fire siege weapon]] and miss, you misdirect fire by 1 square per range increment.

**Normal**: Direct-fire weapons impose a –2 attack roll penalty per size category by which the weapon is larger than the creature aiming it. An indirect-fire weapon that misses misdirects fire by 1d4 squares per range increment.
