# Double Slice

Your off-hand weapon while dual-wielding strikes with greater power.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]].

**Benefit**: Add your full Strength bonus to damage rolls made with your off-hand weapon.

**Normal**: You add only half your Strength bonus to damage rolls made with a weapon in your off hand.
