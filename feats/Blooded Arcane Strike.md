# Blooded Arcane Strike

Arcane energy guides your devastating attack.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Arcane Strike|Arcane Strike]], ability to cast arcane spells, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodrage]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While you are [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodraging]], you don’t need to spend a swift action to use your [[rangoric-srd/feats/Arcane Strike|Arcane Strike]]—it is always in effect. When you use this ability with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Vital Strike|Improved Vital Strike]], or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Vital Strike|Greater Vital Strike]], the bonus on damage rolls for Arcane Strike is multiplied by the number of times (two, three, or four) you roll damage dice for one of those feats.
