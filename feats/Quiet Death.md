# Quiet Death

You have learned to dispatch your enemies with the silence and grace of an accomplished assassin.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 19, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 10 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] level 10th.

**Benefit**: When you ambush enemies that are unaware of you, you can make a Stealth check at a –5 penalty. Until an opponent’s first action, the result is the Perception DC to hear your attacks (instead of the usual DC of –10 to hear a fight); after that, it goes back to –10. Other enemies nearby can still see the attack: Quiet Death only keeps the sound of fighting from alerting more enemies.
