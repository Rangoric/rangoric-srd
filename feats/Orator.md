# Orator

You're able to construct statements and commands that are much more convincing than their content would otherwise suggest.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]]).

**Benefit**: You can make a Linguistics check instead of a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check to tell a falsehood or hide information, instead of a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] check to change a creature’s attitude, or instead of an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to force a creature to cooperate. You must speak in a language the target understands.
