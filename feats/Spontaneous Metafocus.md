# Spontaneous Metafocus

You can focus to combine one of your known spells and metamagic feats.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, one metamagic feat, able to cast spells spontaneously.

**Benefit**: Choose one spell you can cast spontaneously. When you apply metamagic feats to it, you cast it in its normal casting time instead of the longer one.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time, choose a new spell you can cast spontaneously.
