# Spirit Focus

You have a strong connection to a particular legend, which empowers the spirits you channel.

**Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirit-bonus|Spirit bonus]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Select a legend of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/medium-spirits/Medium Spirits|spirits]]. Your spirit bonus from spirits of that legend increases by 1.
