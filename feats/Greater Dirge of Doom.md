# Greater Dirge of Doom

The haunting sound of sepulchral intonations chills the most stalwart adversary to its very core.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Dirge of Doom|Improved Dirge of Doom]], ability to perform [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-dirge-of-doom|dirge of doom]].

**Benefit**: The effect of your dirge of doom lingers with a target for 2 rounds after the creature leaves the dirge’s area of effect. If you use your dirge on a creature that is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]]. If you use it on a creature that is frightened, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]].

**Normal**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|Fear]] effects on a creature end immediately once it leaves the area of dirge of doom. A fear effect cannot be made more extreme by dirge of doom and can be changed only to the frightened condition by the Improved Dirge of Doom feat.
