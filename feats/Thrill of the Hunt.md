# Thrill of the Hunt

The pursuit of prey invigorates you. You revel in tracking down and slaying your quarry.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 1 rank; base attack bonus +4 or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|track]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Once per day, when you succeed at a Fieldcraft check to find or follow a creature’s [[rangoric-srd/feats/Track|tracks]], you can designate that creature to be your prize for a number of hours equal to 4 + your ranks in Fieldcraft. You gain a +2 morale bonus on Fieldcraft checks to follow your prize’s tracks and on weapon damage rolls against your prize. If you find and subsequently render your prize dead or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], you gain a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls, saves, and skill checks for a number of hours equal to your ranks in Fieldcraft.

**Special**: An animal companion can choose this feat as if it were an animal feat.
