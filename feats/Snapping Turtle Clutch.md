# Snapping Turtle Clutch

Your unarmed style allows you to turn your opponent's attack into an opportunity.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snapping Turtle Style|Snapping Turtle Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +3 or monk level 3rd.

**Benefit**: While using Snapping Turtle Style, the style’s shield bonus also applies to your CMD and touch AC. Whenever an opponent misses you with a melee attack, you can spend an immediate action to try to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] it, with a –2 penalty.
