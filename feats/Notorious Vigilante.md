# Notorious Vigilante

You are widely known as dangerous and fearsome.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], any nongood alignment, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Great Renown (Social Talent)|great renown]] social talent, proficiency with chosen weapon.

**Benefit**: Your presence can act as a fast and effective Dazzling Display. As a standard action, you use the benefit of Dazzling Display even when you are not wielding a weapon in which you have Weapon Focus. If you are wielding such a weapon, you gain a +2 bonus on the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check.
