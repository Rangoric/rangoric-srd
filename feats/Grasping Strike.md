# Grasping Strike

The surrounding foliage lurches forth at your enemies in concert with your strikes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nature Magic|Nature Magic]] or the ability to cast druid or ranger spells, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: A number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1), when you hit a creature using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Vital Strike|Improved Vital Strike]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Vital Strike|Greater Vital Strike]], you can use this feat as a free action. The plants nearby reach out and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangle]] the creature if it fails a Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier). This lasts 1 minute, or until it breaks free with a Strength or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] check against the save DC. This is a supernatural ability.
