# Wolf Trip

You have studied the manner in which wolves bring down their prey.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wolf Style|Wolf Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using Wolf Style, you gain a +2 bonus on trip combat maneuver checks made as part of an attack of opportunity. Whenever you trip a creature, as a free action you can choose where it lands prone: any open space adjacent to both you and the space it was in.
