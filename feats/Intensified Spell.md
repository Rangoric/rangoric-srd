# Intensified Spell

Your spells can go beyond several normal limitations.

**Benefit**: An intensified spell’s maximum number of damage dice is 5 levels higher. You must have the caster levels to go past the normal maximum to benefit. Nothing else about the spell changes, and spells whose damage doesn’t depend on caster level aren’t affected. A intensified spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
