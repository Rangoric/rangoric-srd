# Janni Style

Your whirling fighting technique makes you difficult to hit.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (dance) 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using this style, you take only a –1 penalty to AC for charging, and opponents that flank you gain only a +1 bonus on attack rolls against you.
