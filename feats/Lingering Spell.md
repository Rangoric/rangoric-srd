# Lingering Spell

Your spell clings to existence, slowly fading from the world.

**Benefit**: An instantaneous spell that affects an area persists until the start of your next turn. Creatures already in the area take no further harm, but other creatures or objects entering it are subject to its effects. A lingering spell with a visible effect obscures vision, giving concealment (20% miss chance) beyond 5 feet and total concealment (50% miss chance) beyond 20 feet. A lingering spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
