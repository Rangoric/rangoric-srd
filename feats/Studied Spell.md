# Studied Spell

You use your knowledge about the target to bypass its resistances and damage reduction.

**Benefit**: When you cast a studied spell, choose one target it affects and make the Knowledge check for that creature’s type (see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|Identifying Creatures]]), DC 20 + the creature’s CR from its race alone (not class levels or templates; a creature defined by its class levels counts as CR 0). On a success, the spell ignores any energy resistance and damage reduction the target has from its race, and any racial bonus it has on saves against the spell. It doesn’t ignore resistance, reduction or save bonuses from spells and other effects. On a failure, the spell has its normal effect. A studied spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
