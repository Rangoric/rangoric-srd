# Improved Reposition

You have learned how to force your enemies to move around the battlefield.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]].

**Benefit**: You don’t provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]]. You gain a +2 bonus on checks to reposition a foe, and a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when an opponent tries to reposition you.

**Normal**: You provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a reposition combat maneuver.
