# Improved Sabotaging Sunder

You become further adept at sabotaging your foes' items.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Sunder|Improved Sunder]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sabotaging Sunder|Sabotaging Sunder]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 9 ranks.

**Benefit**: You don’t provoke an attack of opportunity when you use the special sunder maneuver from Sabotaging Sunder. You can also use it on items the target is holding, though still not on items hidden in a bag, pack or other container.
