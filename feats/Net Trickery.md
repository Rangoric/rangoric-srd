# Net Trickery

You have become very proficient at using your net to hinder your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] (net), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Net Adept|Net Adept]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Net Maneuvering|Net Maneuvering]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: In place of one of your melee attacks, you can use your net to attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] to blind an opponent. If you have an opponent entangled in your net, you can attempt to trip it as long as it is within your net’s reach or you control the trailing rope. You also gain a +2 bonus on drag and reposition checks you make with your net.
