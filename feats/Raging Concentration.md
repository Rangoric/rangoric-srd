# Raging Concentration

When you are in the heat of rage, not even pain can break your concentration.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-blood-casting|Blood casting]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While in a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bloodrager#^bloodrager-bloodrage|bloodrage]], you gain a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] equal to the morale bonus granted to your Constitution by the bloodrage.
