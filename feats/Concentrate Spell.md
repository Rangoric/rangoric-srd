# Concentrate Spell

You can increase the damage a spell does by reducing its area of affect.

**Benefit**: You can shrink a spell’s area to 25% of normal and double the damage dice it rolls. For example, a concentrated *fireball* explodes in a 5-foot-radius sphere but deals 2d6 points of damage per caster level instead of 1d6. A concentrated spell uses a spell slot three levels higher than its actual level.
