# Tower Shield Specialist

You wield tower shields with ease.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Focus|Shield Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tower Shield Proficiency|Tower Shield Proficiency]], base attack bonus +11 or fighter level 8th.

**Benefit**: The armor check penalty of a tower shield is 3 lower for you.
