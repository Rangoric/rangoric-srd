# Harder They Fall

You can work with an ally to move or knock over a foe thats too large for either of you to overcome alone.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]].

**Benefit**: When you use the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] action to grant an ally who also has this feat a +2 bonus on a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver check, the ally can attempt that maneuver even against foes two or more size categories larger than herself.
