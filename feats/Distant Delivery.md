# Distant Delivery

Your phantom can deliver spells at a greater range than usual.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-phantom|phantom]] class feature, phantom with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-deliver-touch-spells|deliver touch spells]] ability.

**Benefit**: The distance from you at which your phantom can deliver touch spells increases by 20 feet.
