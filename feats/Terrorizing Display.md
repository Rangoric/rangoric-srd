# Terrorizing Display

Your battle prowess frightens friend and foe alike.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Demoralizing Lash|Demoralizing Lash]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Taskmaster|Taskmaster]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 10 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]], proficiency with selected weapon.

**Benefit**: When you use [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], in addition to its normal effects, you can use it to spur allies within 30 feet as if you had used the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Taskmaster|Taskmaster]] feat. Use the same Intimidation check to determine the effects of Dazzling Display and this feat.
