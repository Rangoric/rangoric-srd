# Tiger Claws

You can sacrifice multiple attacks to make a single devastating strike.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tiger Style|Tiger Style]], base attack bonus +6 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: While using Tiger Style with both hands free, you can use a full-round action to make a single unarmed strike with both hands at your highest base attack bonus. Roll unarmed strike damage for each hand separately, and multiply both on a critical hit. If you use Power Attack with this attack, you can add half your Strength bonus to one of the damage rolls. If you hit, you can try a bull rush with a +2 bonus on the combat maneuver check. It doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity, but you can’t move with the opponent if it succeeds.
