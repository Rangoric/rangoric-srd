# Claw Pounce

You can charge and make an attack with your paws.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Striker|Nimble Striker]], base attack bonus +10, [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|catfolk]], [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Cat's Claws|cat’s claws]] racial trait or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Aspect of the Beast|Aspect of the Beast]] (claws of the beast manifestation).

**Benefit**: When you make a charge, you can make a full attack with your claws.

**Normal**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|Charging]] is a special full-round action that limits you to a single attack.
