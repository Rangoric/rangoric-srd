# Jaguar Pounce

Your ambushes are especially lethal.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: When you charge or use [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spring Attack|Spring Attack]] against an opponent who is flat-footed or helpless, you treat your first melee attack against it as if you had [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]].
