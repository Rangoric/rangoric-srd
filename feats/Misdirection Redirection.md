# Misdirection Redirection

After misdirecting your opponent's weapon attack, you trick it into striking someone else.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Misdirection Tactics|Misdirection Tactics]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 10 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you use Misdirection Tactics to make a melee weapon attack miss, you trick your foe into striking another creature of your choice within its melee reach. Your foe makes a new attack roll against the new target.
