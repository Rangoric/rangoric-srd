# Guardian of the Wild

Your mystic connection with the wilderness enhances your ability to react to threats.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Attuned to the Wild|Attuned to the Wild]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]].

**Benefit**: When you are in a terrain type you have selected the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Attuned to the Wild|Attuned to the Wild]] feat for, you gain a +2 dodge bonus to Armor Class. If you are in an area that qualifies as more than one kind of terrain, these bonuses do not stack; you receive the bonus for only one of the terrain types.
