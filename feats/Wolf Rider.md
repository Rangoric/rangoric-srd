# Wolf Rider

Others can keep their horses. You know that wolves are better mounts.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Undersized Mount|Undersized Mount]] or effective level 7th in the class that grants the mount; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 1 rank; [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-special-mount|special mount]] or mount class feature.

**Benefit**: You can select a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Wolf|wolf]] in place of the normal mount available via your special mount. In addition, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] is always a class skill for you.
