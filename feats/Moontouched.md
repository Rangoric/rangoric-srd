# Moontouched

Moonlight has a strange effect on you, making your body stronger but your mind weaker.

**Benefit**: At night, when at least part of the moon is showing and you can see it, you gain a +2 bonus on Fortitude and Reflex Defense but take a –2 penalty on Will Defense.
