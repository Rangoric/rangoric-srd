# Mantis Wisdom

Your knowledge of vital areas allows you to land debilitating strikes with precision.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mantis Style|Mantis Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: Count half your levels in classes other than monk as monk levels for which conditions you can apply with Stunning Fist (as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]]’s Stunning Fist class feature says). You can also remove a Stunning Fist effect you applied to a target with a standard action and a successful melee touch attack.

While using Mantis Style, you gain a +2 bonus on unarmed attack rolls with which you use Stunning Fist attempts.
