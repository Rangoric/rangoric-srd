# Swipe and Stash

Your fast fingers allow you to deftly deposit an item on someone else.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can make a DC 20 Sleight of Hand check to plant a small object on a creature. The target makes a Perception check opposed by your Sleight of Hand check to notice. This doesn’t make it wear, wield or use the item; you just plant it on it. The DC is 30 if the target has no clothing, armor, harness or similar gear to hide the item in.

In combat, you must succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] maneuver to plant an item, while making a Sleight of Hand check opposed by the target’s Perception to do it unnoticed. If you succeed and have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Steal|Greater Steal]], you are unnoticed automatically.

If you steal an object with a Sleight of Hand check or a steal maneuver, you can use this feat as a swift action to plant it on another creature within reach before the end of your turn.

**Special**: If you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Walking Sleight|Walking Sleight]], you can take either or both actions of Swipe and Stash at any point during your movement that round.
