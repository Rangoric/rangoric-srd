# Disheartening Display

Your show of prowess further demoralizes others.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], base attack bonus +6, proficiency with the chosen weapon.

**Benefit**: When you successfully use Dazzling Display against shaken, frightened or panicked opponents, their fear increases by one step. An already [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] creature demoralized this way [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^cowering|cowers]]. Once affected by this feat, a creature can’t be affected by it again (by you or anyone else) for 24 hours.
