# Redirect Attack

When you dodge an attack, you can deflect it toward another opponent.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, Int 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]].

**Benefit**: Once per turn, when an opponent that only you threaten misses you with an attack, you can redirect the attack at another foe adjacent to you and within the attacker’s reach. Make a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] check; if the result beats both the attacker’s CMD and the new target’s AC, the attack hits the new target and deals its normal damage.
