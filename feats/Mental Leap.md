# Mental Leap

You can make amazing jumps.  

Prerequisite: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 2 ranks.

Benefit: To use this feat, you must expend your psionic focus. You gain a +10 bonus on an Athletics check.
