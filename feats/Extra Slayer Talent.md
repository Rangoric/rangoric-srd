# Extra Slayer Talent

Through long practice, you have learned how to perform a special talent.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-talents|Slayer talent]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Slayer Talents|slayer talent]]. You must meet the prerequisites for this slayer talent.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, you gain another slayer talent.
