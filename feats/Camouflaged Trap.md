# Camouflaged Trap

You excel at hiding your traps in the wilderness, making them more difficult for your enemies to locate and avoid.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (traps) 4 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 4 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you make and set a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps|trap]] in the wilderness, the DC of the Perception check to find it increases by 5.
