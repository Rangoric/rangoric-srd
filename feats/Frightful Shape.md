# Frightful Shape

Your wild shape form strikes fear into your opponents.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 19, base attack bonus +9, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|wild shape]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you use wild shape, your form is supernaturally ferocious and disquieting in appearance, such that when you attack a creature, you can use this ferocity to frighten observers. Opponents within 30 feet of you when you attack must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half your character level + your Charisma modifier) to resist being affected by your frightful shape. On a failed save, creatures with fewer Hit Dice than you become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] if they have 4 Hit Dice or fewer; in either case, the effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to your character level. An opponent that succeeds at its saving throw is immune to your frightful shape for 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] effect.
