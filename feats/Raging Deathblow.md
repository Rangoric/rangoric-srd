# Raging Deathblow

Every killing blow gives you a surge of vitality, further fueling your rage.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-greater-rage|Greater rage]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While raging, whenever your attack reduces your opponent to –1 or fewer hit points, you gain 1 extra round of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] for that day. If that attack was a critical hit, you gain 1 more extra round for that day. Whenever you rest to renew your rounds of rage per day, any extra rounds you still have from this feat are lost.
