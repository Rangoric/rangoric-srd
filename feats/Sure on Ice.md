# Sure on Ice

You move on ice and snow as if it were dry ground.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giants/Frost Giant|Frost giant]].

**Benefit**: You can move across icy and snowy surfaces without penalty, and don't need to attempt [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to run or charge on ice. You gain a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb ice.
