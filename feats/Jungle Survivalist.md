# Jungle Survivalist

Normally pestilential rain forests are a safe haven and home to you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-terrain|Favored terrain]] (jungle) class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against diseases, poisons, and the distraction ability of creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^swarm|swarm]] subtype. You also gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks when climbing trees and a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks against creatures that gain a racial bonus on Stealth checks in vegetation.
