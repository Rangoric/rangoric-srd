# Silent Kill

You are quite adept at killing your prey without making noise or alerting others.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 12 ranks; [[rangoric-srd/class-options/slayer-talents/Assassinate (Slayer Talent)|assassinate]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-advanced-talents|advanced slayer talent]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ninja-tricks/Assassinate (Ninja Trick)|assassinate]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-master-tricks|master ninja trick]].

**Benefit**: When you kill a creature during a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]], you can attempt a Stealth check, opposed by the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks of potential observers, to prevent them from noticing your action and subsequently identifying you as the assailant.
