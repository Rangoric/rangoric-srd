# Improved Stonecunning (Feat)

Your sense for stonework is uncanny.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf#^dwarf-stonecunning|stonecunning]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: You gain the earth dwarf’s [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Improved Stonecunning|Improved Stonecunning]] racial trait: a bigger [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf#^dwarf-stonecunning|stonecunning]] bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks to notice unusual stonework, in place of the normal one.
