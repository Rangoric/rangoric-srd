# Crane Riposte

You use your defensive abilities to make overpowering counterattacks.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Crane Style|Crane Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Crane Wing|Crane Wing]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +8 or monk level 7th.

**Benefit**: You take only a –1 penalty on attack rolls for fighting defensively. Whenever you use Crane Wing against an opponent’s attack, you can make an attack of opportunity against that opponent after the attack.
