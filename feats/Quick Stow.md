# Quick Stow

You effortlessly stow items and sheathe weapons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You don’t provoke an attack of opportunity when you sheathe a weapon, and you can sheathe a weapon as part of a regular move action (so you can both stow and draw a weapon in the same move action). If you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], you can sheathe two light or one-handed weapons in the time it normally takes to sheathe one.

You can also stow an item in a backpack or other container you’re wearing or carrying as part of the action you use to pick it up (including stealing it with [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]]). You can try to hide the object as you do, with a Sleight of Hand check at a –20 penalty opposed by each opponent’s [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check.
