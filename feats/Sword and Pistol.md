# Sword and Pistol

You effortlessly pair melee and ranged weaponry.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point-Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Shot|Rapid Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snap Shot|Snap Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: When you use the Two-Weapon Fighting feat while wielding a melee weapon and a crossbow or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]], your attacks with the crossbow or firearm provoke no attacks of opportunity from foes that you threaten with your melee weapon.

**Normal**: Making a ranged attack provokes attacks of opportunity.
