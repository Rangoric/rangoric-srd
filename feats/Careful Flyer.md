# Careful Flyer

You move with ease when you fly at a slower rate.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Acrobatics), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: As long as you take a move action to fly, even moving less than half your speed, you don’t need an Acrobatics check to keep flying. When you move less than half your speed in a round, you also gain a +2 bonus on Acrobatics checks to avoid attacks of opportunity, and a +2 bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity you provoke by moving.

**Normal**: You must succeed at an Acrobatics check to keep flying unless you move at least half your speed.
