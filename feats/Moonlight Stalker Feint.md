# Moonlight Stalker Feint

You strike through the shadows so quickly that your opponent can barely react to your attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Moonlight Stalker|Moonlight Stalker]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 6 ranks, darkvision or low-light vision as a racial trait.

**Benefit**: Once per round, against an opponent from whom you have [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]], you can spend a swift action to make a Deception check to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]].

**Normal**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|Feinting]] is a standard action.
