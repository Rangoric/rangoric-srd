# Extra Evolution

Your eidolon has more evolutions.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|Eidolon]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Your eidolon’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-evolutions|evolution pool]] increases by 1.

**Special**: This evolution can be taken once at 1st level, and again at 5th, 10th, 15th and 20th.
