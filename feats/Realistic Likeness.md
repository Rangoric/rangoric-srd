# Realistic Likeness

When you are in human form, you can take the shape of a specific individual.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Kitsune|Kitsune]].

**Benefit**: You can precisely mimic the physical features of any individual you have encountered. When you use your racial change shape ability (see [[rangoric-srd/races/Kitsune|kitsune]]), you can attempt to take the form of an individual, granting you a +10 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks made to fool others with your impersonation.
