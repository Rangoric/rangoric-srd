# Dramatic Display

Your skill with your weapon is obvious to enemies and onlookers alike.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]].

**Benefit**: When you spend a swift action to make a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat#^performance-combat-check|performance combat check]], you exude an aura of awe-inspiring skill. You gain a +2 bonus on your performance combat check, and gain a +2 bonus on all attack rolls and combat maneuver checks until the end of your next turn.
