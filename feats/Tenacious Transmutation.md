# Tenacious Transmutation

Your mastery of the magic of changing makes your transmutations more durable.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (transmutation).

**Benefit**: The DC of caster level checks to dispel or remove your transmutations increases by 2. Even if one is negated, its effects last 1 more round before they end.
