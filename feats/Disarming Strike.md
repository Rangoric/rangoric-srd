# Disarming Strike

Your critical hits can disarm your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Disarm|Improved Disarm]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a critical hit with a melee attack, you can also disarm your opponent, on top of the attack’s normal damage. If your confirmation roll beats the opponent’s [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]], you disarm it as if with the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] combat maneuver. This doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity.

**Normal**: You must use the disarm combat maneuver to disarm your opponent.

**Special**: You can apply only one of these feats to a given critical hit: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bull Rush Strike|Bull Rush Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disarming Strike|Disarming Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Repositioning Strike|Repositioning Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sundering Strike|Sundering Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tripping Strike|Tripping Strike]]. You can choose to use this feat after you make your confirmation roll.
