# Vicious Stomp

You take advantage of the moment to brutally kick an enemy when he is down.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: Whenever an opponent falls prone adjacent to you, it provokes an attack of opportunity from you. This attack must be an unarmed strike.
