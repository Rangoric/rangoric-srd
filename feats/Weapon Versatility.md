# Weapon Versatility

You can use your favored weapons in unconventional ways.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When wielding a weapon in which you have Weapon Focus, you can shift your grip as a swift action so that it deals bludgeoning, piercing or slashing damage instead of its normal type. You can switch back, or to another type, as a swift action. If your base attack bonus is +5 or higher, this is a free action instead.
