# Improved Eldritch Heritage

The power of your discovered bloodline continues to grow.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Eldritch Heritage|Eldritch Heritage]], character level 11th.

**Benefit**: You gain either the 3rd-level or the 9th-level power (your choice) of the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines|bloodline]] you selected with the Eldritch Heritage feat. For purposes of using that power, treat your sorcerer level as equal to your character level – 2, even if you have levels in sorcerer. You do not gain any of the other bloodline abilities.

**Special**: You may select this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you select the feat, it applies to a different bloodline power for that bloodline available at sorcerer level 3 or 9.
