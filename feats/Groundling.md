# Groundling

You can speak with burrowing animals.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome magic]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: You can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]_ as a spell-like ability at will, but only to communicate with burrowing animals like gophers, moles, and the like. You can still use your gnome _speak with animals_ ability once per day to speak with any animal, as usual.
