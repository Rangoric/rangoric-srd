# Groundling

You can speak with burrowing animals.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome magic]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: You can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]* as a spell-like ability at will, but only to communicate with burrowing animals like gophers, moles, and the like. You can still use your gnome *speak with animals* ability once per day to speak with any animal, as usual.
