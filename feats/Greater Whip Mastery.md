# Greater Whip Mastery

You can use a whip to make combat maneuvers with ease.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Whip Mastery|Improved Whip Mastery]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (whip), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Whip Mastery|Whip Mastery]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: You never drop your whip because of a failed disarm or trip attempt. You can also [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] with your whip, using the normal grapple rules with these changes:

- **Attack**: You can’t attack with your whip while you are using it to grapple.
- **Damage**: When you damage the creature you are grappling, you deal your whip’s weapon damage instead of your unarmed strike damage.
- **Free hands**: You take no penalty on the combat maneuver check for having fewer than two hands free.
- **Reach**: Instead of pulling the creature next to you when you grapple it or move the grapple, you must keep it within your whip’s reach minus its own reach. If that is less than 0 (as with a Medium whip wielder and a Gargantuan creature), you can’t grapple it with your whip. If you have to pull a creature next to you to grapple it, you still provoke an attack of opportunity from it unless you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]].
- **Tie up**: While next to the creature, you can try to tie it up with your whip. If it is grappled rather than pinned, you take only a –5 penalty on the check instead of –10.
