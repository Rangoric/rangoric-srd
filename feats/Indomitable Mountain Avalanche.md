# Indomitable Mountain Avalanche

You are an impassable mountain, hurling your enemies back.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Indomitable Mountain Peak|Indomitable Mountain Peak]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Indomitable Mountain Style|Indomitable Mountain Style]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: If an opponent fails a combat maneuver against you, you can spend an attack of opportunity to make a melee attack against it at your highest attack bonus. If the attack hits, instead of dealing damage you can push the opponent back 5 feet for every 5 by which it failed its combat maneuver check (minimum 5 feet). When you hit with an unarmed attack of opportunity against an opponent that provoked it by failing an Acrobatics check to move through a square you threaten, you can likewise push it back 5 feet instead of dealing damage. The opponent moves back in a straight line, stopping before it would hit an obstacle or hazard.
