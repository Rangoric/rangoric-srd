# Improved Bull Rush

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You don’t provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]]. You gain a +2 bonus on checks to bull rush a foe, and a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when an opponent tries to bull rush you.

**Normal**: You provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a bull rush combat maneuver.
