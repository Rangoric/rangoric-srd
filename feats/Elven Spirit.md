# Elven Spirit

Although you are of mixed heritage, you are closer to your elven relatives and the magic in their blood flows freely in your veins.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-elf]].

**Benefit**: You possess the elven magic racial trait of the [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elves]]. Alternatively, you can instead gain any one racial trait that elves can exchange for the elven magic racial trait (see the elf’s [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf#Alternate Racial Traits|alternate racial traits]]).

**Special**: You can only take this feat at 1st level. If you take this feat, you cannot take the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Human Spirit|Human Spirit]] feat.
