# Kobold Ambusher

You are adept at moving fast and staying hidden.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 4 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]].

**Benefit**: You take no penalty on Stealth checks when you move up to your full speed. You can use the run or charge action and remain hidden, taking a –10 penalty on the check to do so.

**Normal**: Moving faster than half your speed, or running or charging, gives the penalties on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks that the skill lists.
