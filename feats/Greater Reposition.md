# Greater Reposition

When you reposition foes, they are thrown off balance.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Reposition|Improved Reposition]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to reposition a foe. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Reposition|Improved Reposition]]. Whenever you reposition a foe, its movement provokes attacks of opportunity from all your allies (but not you).

**Normal**: Creatures moved by reposition don’t provoke attacks of opportunity.
