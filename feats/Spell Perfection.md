# Spell Perfection

You are unequaled at the casting of one particular spell.

**Prerequisites**: 15 ranks in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]], at least three metamagic feats.

**Benefit**: Choose one spell you can cast. Whenever you cast it, you can apply any one metamagic feat you have to it without changing its level or casting time, as long as the modified spell wouldn’t use a slot above 9th level. Also, if you have other feats that give a set number bonus to some part of this spell (such as Spell Focus, Spell Penetration or Weapon Focus (ray)), double that bonus for this spell.
