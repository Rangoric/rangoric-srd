# Spirit of the Wild

Your mystic connection with one type of wilderness has grown even stronger.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Attuned to the Wild|Attuned to the Wild]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Guardian of the Wild|Guardian of the Wild]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]].

**Benefit**: When you are in a terrain type you have selected the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Attuned to the Wild|Attuned to the Wild]] feat for, you gain a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks made to act in the surprise round of combat. If you act in the surprise round, you gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blindsense|blindsense]] out to a range of 30 feet during the surprise round. If you are in an area that qualifies as more than one kind of terrain, these bonuses do not stack; you receive the bonus for only one terrain type.
