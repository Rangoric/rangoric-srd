# Destroyer's Blessing

Breaking things adds to your power.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-orc]] or orc, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you are raging and you succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] combat maneuver, you regain 1 round of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]]. If the sunder attempt causes the object to gain the broken condition, you heal 1 hit point. You can only gain these benefits once per round.
