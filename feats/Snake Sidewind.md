# Snake Sidewind

Your sensitive twisting movements make you difficult to anticipate combat.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snake Style|Snake Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 bonus to CMD against trips, and on Acrobatics checks and saving throws to avoid being knocked prone. While using Snake Style, when you threaten a critical hit with your unarmed strike, you can make a Sense Motive check in place of the attack roll to confirm it. When you score a critical hit with your unarmed strike, you can spend an immediate action to take a 5-foot step even if you have moved this round.

**Normal**: You can take a 5-foot step only if you haven’t otherwise moved this round.
