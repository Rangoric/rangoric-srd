# Disrupting Shot

With a well-placed shot, you make it harder for a nearby opponent to cast spells.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], fighter level 6th.

**Benefit**: If you ready an action to shoot an opponent casting a spell within 30 feet and hit it with a ranged attack, the DC of its [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] to cast the spell is 4 higher.
