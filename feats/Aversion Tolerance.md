# Aversion Tolerance

You have been hunted for so long that you have developed resistances to slayers’ usual scare tactics.

**Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/vampire/Alternate Vampire|Vampire]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on defenses against aversions to objects, sounds, and materials appropriate to your vampire type (such as garlic, holy symbols, and mirrors for a moroi; see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/vampire/Alternate Vampire#Vampire Templates|vampire templates]]). When confronted with such things, the aversion makes its attack against your defense immediately instead of after 1 round.
