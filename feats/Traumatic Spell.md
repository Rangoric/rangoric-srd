# Traumatic Spell

Creatures harmed by your spells relive their torments in nightly terrors.

**Benefit**: A traumatic spell causes lingering memories of pain and suffering in creatures harmed by it. When a traumatic spell hits a creature’s defense, the next time it sleeps the spell attacks its Will with the original spell’s attack bonus; on a hit, it is affected as if by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nightmare (Spell)|nightmare]]_. Each time it hits, it attacks again the following night, and on a hit the target is affected by another nightmare, though its attack bonus decreases by 2 each night after the first. This metamagic feat can be applied only to spells with the emotion or fear [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Spell Descriptions|descriptor]]. A traumatic spell uses up a spell slot 2 levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
