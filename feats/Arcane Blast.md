# Arcane Blast

You can convert any spell into an attack.

**Prerequisites**: Arcane spellcaster, caster level 10th.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can give up a prepared spell or unused spell slot of 1st level or higher and turn it into a ray, a ranged touch attack against any foe within 30 feet. It deals 2d6 points of damage plus 1d6 more for each level of the spell or slot you gave up. You can’t give up 0-level spells this way.
