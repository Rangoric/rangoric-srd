# Intrusive Presence

You can rifle through the thoughts and memories of your host.

**Prerequisites**: Int or Cha 21, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hidden Presence|Hidden Presence]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Manipulative Presence|Manipulative Presence]], ability to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession|possession]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession, Greater|greater possession]]_.

**Benefit**: You can use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_ at will as a spell-like ability against a host creature you [[rangoric-srd/rules/Possession (Rules)|possess]]. The host receives no saving throw, and the effect functions as if you had concentrated for 3 rounds. You can also use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Seek Thoughts|seek thoughts]]_ on your host as a spell-like ability once during the possession. The host can attempt a Will save with a DC equal to the DC of the original possession effect. If the host succeeds, the effect fails and the host becomes aware of the possession if it wasn’t already.
