# Rending Fury

You easily tear your enemies limb from limb with your natural attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +6, rend special attack.

**Benefit**: You deal rend damage if you hit with half the natural attacks your rend normally needs. For example, a troll with this feat can rend when it hits with one claw attack, while a girallon must hit one target with two claw attacks. You can make this rend attack only once per round.
