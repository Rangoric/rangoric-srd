# Demoralizing Lash

You cow your enemies with the lash of a whip.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]].

**Benefit**: To use this feat, you must use a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Whip|whip]] (or another weapon in the flails [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-groups|weapon group]]) to attack a foe [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralized]] by an Intimidation check. If the attack hits, you extend the duration the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] by 1 round. This feat has no effect on creatures whose shaken conditions are from sources other than Intimidation checks.
