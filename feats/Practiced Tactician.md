# Practiced Tactician

With only a few quick gestures and commands, you can direct others in combat.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-tactician|Tactician]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can use your tactician ability to grant allies a [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^teamwork-feats|teamwork feat]] one additional time per day.

**Special**: You can gain Practiced Tactician multiple times. Its effects stack.
