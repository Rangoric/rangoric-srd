# Pure Faith

Not only are you immune to disease, like most paladins, but you also are highly resilient to poisons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-health|Divine health]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 sacred bonus to saving throws against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|poison]].
