# Improved Conceal Spell

You can conceal the mightiest spells in the subtlest fashions.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Conceal Spell|Conceal Spell]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] 10 ranks, ability to cast 3rd-level spells or use a 3rd-level spell-like ability.

**Benefit**: When you use Conceal Spell, creatures no longer gain a bonus equal to the spell’s level on their checks to notice it.

**Normal**: Creatures trying to notice a spell hidden with Conceal Spell gain a bonus equal to its level.
