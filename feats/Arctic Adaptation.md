# Arctic Adaptation

You are comfortable in the driving snow and glaring ice of frigid climes, and you can survive much longer in such harsh environments than those who are unaccustomed to the cold.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-terrain|Favored terrain]] (cold) class feature.

**Benefit**: You treat [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Cold Dangers|cold environments]] as though they were one step less severe than they normally are. Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks against creatures that gain a racial bonus on Stealth checks in snowy conditions, and you gain a +4 bonus on saving throws and checks to avoid becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] by ice or snow glare.
