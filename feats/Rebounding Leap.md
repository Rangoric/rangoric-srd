# Rebounding Leap

Your riding and lancing expertise allows you to enter and leave the saddle with great speed.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/fighter/Dragoon (Archetype)|Leaping lance]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 11 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you succeed at the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|Athletics check to jump]] as part of your leaping lance class feature, you can remount your steed as a swift action.
