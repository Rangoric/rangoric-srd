# Spellbreaker

You can strike at enemy spellcasters who fail to cast defensively when you threaten them.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disruptive|Disruptive]], fighter level 10th.

**Benefit**: Enemies in your threatened area who fail their checks to cast spells on the defensive provoke attacks of opportunity from you.

**Normal**: Enemies who fail to cast spells on the defensive don’t provoke attacks of opportunity.
