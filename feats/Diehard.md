# Diehard

**Prerequisite**: Endurance.

**Benefit**: When your hit point total is below 0, but you aren’t dead, you automatically become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stable|stable]]. You don’t have to make a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#^stabilizing|Constitution check]] each round to avoid losing hit points.

When reduced to negative hit points, you may choose to act as if you were disabled, rather than dying. You must make this decision as soon as you are reduced to negative hit points (even if it isn’t your turn). If you do not choose to act as if you were disabled, you immediately fall unconscious.

When using this feat, you are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]]. You can take a move action without further injuring yourself, but if you perform any standard action (or any other action deemed as strenuous, including some free actions, such as casting a quickened spell) you take 1 point of damage after completing the act. If your hit points fall low enough to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#^death|kill you]], you immediately die.

**Normal**: A character without this feat who is reduced to negative hit points is unconscious and dying.
