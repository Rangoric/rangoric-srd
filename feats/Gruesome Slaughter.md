# Gruesome Slaughter

You can inspire extreme revulsion when you slaughter your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intimidating Prowess|Intimidating Prowess]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Killing Flourish|Killing Flourish]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 11 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer|slayer]] level 11th.

**Benefit**: Creatures you demoralize by using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Killing Flourish|Killing Flourish]] feat must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your character level + the higher of your Strength or Dexterity modifier) or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute.
