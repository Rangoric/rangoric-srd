# Pack Flanking

You and your companion creature are adept at fighting together against foes.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], ability to acquire an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]].

**Benefit**: When you and your companion creature have this feat, your companion creature is adjacent to you or sharing your square, and you both threaten the same opponent, you are both considered to be [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] that opponent, regardless of your actual positioning.

**Normal**: You flank only when positioned as the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] rule says.
