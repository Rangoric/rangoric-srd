# Canny Tumble

Your acrobatic maneuvers leave foes reeling.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Tumbling|Acrobatics]] to move through an opponent’s threatened area or space without provoking an attack of opportunity from it, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on your next melee attack roll against that opponent, as long as you make it before the start of your next turn.
