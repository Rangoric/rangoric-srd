# Uncanny Defense

Your instinct for self-preservation gives you many advantages.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cautious Fighter|Cautious Fighter]], base attack bonus +3, [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]].

**Benefit**: While fighting defensively or taking the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]] action, you gain a bonus on your Reflex saving throws and to your CMD equal to 1/2 of the dodge bonus to AC you gained from taking that action.
