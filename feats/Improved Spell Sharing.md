# Improved Spell Sharing

Your link with your companion creature allows you to share your magic with it.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to acquire an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]], eidolon, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-special-mount|special mount]].

**Benefit**: When you are adjacent to or sharing a square with your companion creature and that companion creature has this feat, you can cast a spell on yourself and divide the duration evenly between yourself and the companion creature. You can use this feat only on spells with a duration of at least 2 rounds. For example, you could cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]]* on yourself, and instead of the spell lasting 1 minute per level on yourself, it lasts 5 rounds per level on yourself and 5 rounds per level on your companion.

Once the spell is cast, you and the companion creature can move farther apart without ending the effect.

**Normal**: With [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^familiar-share-spells|share spells]], a spell you cast on yourself affects your companion creature for as long as that ability says, and only while the companion stays as close as it says.
