# Ectoplasmic Spell

Your spells breach the gulf between dimensions, sending ghostly emanations into the ether.

**Benefit**: An ectoplasmic spell has full effect against incorporeal and ethereal creatures. A ectoplasmic spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
