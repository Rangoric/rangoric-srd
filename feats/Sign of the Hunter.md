# Sign of the Hunter

The Hunter stood over your birth and guides your hand on the hunt.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on damage rolls with the shortspear, javelin, longspear, shortbow, composite shortbow, longbow and composite longbow. With a ranged attack, the bonus applies only against a target within 30 feet.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
