# Cunning Intuition

You can read your foe’s body language to gauge your foe and gain an edge in combat.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]]), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lightning Reflexes|Lightning Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ready for Anything|Ready for Anything]], base attack bonus +13 or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] level 13th, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 13 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Special Initiative Actions#Ready|ready]] an action, you don’t need to declare what action you will take, only the trigger and an action type (standard, move, swift or free). If you choose the standard action type, you can take a move action instead when your readied action triggers. When the condition triggers, you choose a specific action of that type to take.
