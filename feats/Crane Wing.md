# Crane Wing

You move with the speed and finesse of an avian hunter, your sweeping blocks and graceful motions allowing you to deflect melee attacks with ease.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Crane Style|Crane Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +5 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: Once per round, while fighting defensively with at least one hand free, you can choose one melee attack being made against you, before it is rolled. You gain a +4 dodge bonus to AC against that attack.
