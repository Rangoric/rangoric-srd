# Persuasive Bribery

You know how to get the most out of bribes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation).

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on Persuasion checks to bribe someone, and on Persuasion or Charisma checks to bargain with a called creature (such as with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Ally|planar ally]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Planar Binding|planar binding]]*), on top of any bonus from the bribe itself. The first time someone refuses a bribe you offer, their attitude toward you doesn’t worsen, even if the offer would normally offend them.
