# Improved Vital Strike

You can make a single attack that deals a large amount of damage.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: When you use the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Attack|attack action]], you can make one attack at your highest base attack bonus that deals additional damage. Roll the weapon’s damage dice for the attack three times and add the results together before adding bonuses from Strength, weapon special abilities (such as *flaming*), precision-based damage and other damage bonuses. These extra weapon damage dice aren’t multiplied on a critical hit, but are added to the total.
