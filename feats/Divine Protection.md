# Divine Protection

Your deity protects you against deadly attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 5 ranks, ability to cast 2nd-level divine spells; blessings, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains|domains]] or mystery class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain a bonus equal to your Charisma modifier on all saving throws. If your Charisma modifier is already applied as a bonus on all saving throws (such as from the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-grace|divine grace]] class feature), you instead gain a +1 bonus on all saving throws.
