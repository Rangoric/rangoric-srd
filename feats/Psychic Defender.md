# Psychic Defender

You are adept at defending yourself during psychic duels.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] or the ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spells]] or use psychic spell-like abilities, character level 3rd.

**Benefit**: Each time you enter a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Psychic Duels|psychic duel]], you gain [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Temporary Hit Points|temporary hit points]] equal to your [[rangoric-srd/rules/Psychic Duels#Generating Manifestation Points|manifesting level]]. They last until the end of the psychic duel.
