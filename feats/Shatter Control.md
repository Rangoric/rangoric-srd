# Shatter Control

You can infuse your attacks with divine power capable of harming the undead and shattering the bond between the undead and its controller.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel positive energy]] of 4d6 or more.

**Benefit**: As a special full-round action, you can make a single melee attack, or a single ranged attack within 30 feet, against an undead creature. If it hits, it deals your normal damage. If that undead is controlled by another creature (by a spell, because it is mindless and under that creature’s control, or under the command of another undead), and the controller is within 120 feet of you, the blow also attacks the controller’s Will, its attack bonus 1/2 your level + your Cha modifier; on a hit, the controller loses control of the undead for 10 minutes. In that time the undead seeks out and attacks its former controller until control is regained.
