# Shatter Control

You can infuse your attacks with divine power capable of harming the undead and shattering the bond between the undead and its controller.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel positive energy]] of 4d6 or more.

**Benefit**: As a special full-round action, you can make a single melee attack, or a single ranged attack within 30 feet, against an undead creature. If it hits, it deals your normal damage. If that undead is controlled by another creature (by a spell, because it is mindless and under that creature’s control, or under the command of another undead), and the controller is within 120 feet of you, the controller must succeed on a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your level + your Cha modifier) or lose control of the undead for 10 minutes. In that time the undead seeks out and attacks its former controller until control is regained.
