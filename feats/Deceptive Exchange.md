# Deceptive Exchange

You trick an adversary into grabbing an object you hand them, even in the midst of combat.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]].

**Benefit**: If you successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] an opponent, you can trick it into taking a one-handed object you are holding instead of denying it its Dexterity bonus to AC against your next attack. The opponent must have a free appendage able to hold the object.
