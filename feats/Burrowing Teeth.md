# Burrowing Teeth

You have teeth and claws that suit you well to digging.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sharpclaw|Sharpclaw]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tunnel Rat|Tunnel Rat]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|ratfolk]].

**Benefit**: You gain a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|burrow speed]] equal to 1/2 your base speed. You can burrow through sand, dirt, clay, gravel, or similar materials, but not solid stone. You do not leave a hole behind, nor is your passage marked on the surface.
