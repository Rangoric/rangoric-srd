# Improved Two-Weapon Feint

Your primary weapon keeps a foe off balance, allowing you to slip your off-hand weapon past his defenses.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 17, Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Two-Weapon Fighting|Improved Two-Weapon Fighting]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Feint|Two-Weapon Feint]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: While using Two-Weapon Fighting to make melee attacks, you can forgo your first primary-hand melee attack to make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] an opponent. If you succeed, that opponent is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC until the end of your turn.
