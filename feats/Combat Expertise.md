# Combat Expertise

**Prerequisites**: Int 13.

**Benefit**: When you make an attack or a full attack with a melee weapon, you can take a penalty on melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks and gain the same number as a dodge bonus to AC. The most you can choose is 1, plus 1 more for every 4 points of base attack bonus (2 at +4, 3 at +8, and so on). You choose the number when you declare the attack, and the effects last until your next turn.

**Normal**: A character without the Combat Expertise feat can fight defensively while using the attack or full attack action to take a –4 penalty on attack rolls and gain a +2 dodge bonus to Armor Class.
