# Bleeding Stare

Your stare causes your foe to bleed out of its eyes.

**Prerequisites**: Mesmerist level 5th, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-painful-stare|painful stare]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you trigger your painful stare, the target takes an amount of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage equal to 1/3 your mesmerist level. Bleed damage from multiple uses of Bleeding Stare doesn’t stack.
