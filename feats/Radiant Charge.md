# Radiant Charge

When you charge, you do so with the power of faith.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|Lay on hands]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you hit with a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] attack, you can expend all your remaining uses of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] to deal extra damage equal to 1d6 per use expended + your Charisma bonus. This damage comes from holy power and isn’t subject to damage reduction, energy immunities or energy resistances.
