# Sign of the Scroll

The Scroll stood over your birth, and you know a little of everything.

**Benefit**: Your untrained [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] checks can answer harder questions than common knowledge.

**Normal**: An untrained Knowledge check reveals only common knowledge (DC 10 or lower).

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
