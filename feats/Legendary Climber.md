# Legendary Climber

Prerequisites: Dex 21, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 9 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 21 ranks.  

Benefit: The character can ignore any check penalties applied for accelerated climbing or rapid climbing.

Normal: Without this feat, a character takes a -5 penalty on Athletics checks when attempting to cover his or her full speed in climbing distance in a round, or a -20 penalty when attempting to cover twice his or her speed in climbing distance in a round.
