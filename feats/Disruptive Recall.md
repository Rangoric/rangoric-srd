# Disruptive Recall

You can disrupt an enemy caster’s spells to fuel your own arcane power.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-spell-recall|Spell recall]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you use a melee attack to successfully disrupt an arcane spellcaster’s spell, you can immediately use your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-spell-recall|spell recall]] class feature to regain a magus spell you have already cast. This ability functions as if you had expended a number of points from your arcane pool equal to the level of the spell you disrupted, up to the maximum level spell you can cast.
