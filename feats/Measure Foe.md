# Measure Foe

You can read a foe's martial skill from its body language.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 2 ranks, base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You can make a Sense Motive check as a free action to judge a foe’s martial training after watching its movements for at least 1 minute or its attacks for at least 2 rounds. You take a –10 penalty if you are watching movements rather than attacks. The DC is 20 + the creature’s base attack bonus, or 10 + its Deception or Imitation modifier, whichever is highest.

If you succeed, you learn the foe’s base attack bonus and one combat feat it has, plus one more combat feat for every 5 points by which you beat the DC. You also gain a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls against that foe and to your AC against it, until it gains a level or otherwise improves. If you fail against a foe, you can’t try again against it until you gain more ranks in Sense Motive.
