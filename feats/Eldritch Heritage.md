# Eldritch Heritage

You are descended from a long line of sorcerers, and some portion of their power flows in your veins.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] in a class skill of the bloodline selected for this feat (see below), character level 3rd.

**Benefit**: Select one sorcerer [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Bloodlines|bloodline]]. You must have Skill Focus in a class skill that bloodline grants to a sorcerer at 1st level (for example, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] for the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Celestial Bloodline|celestial bloodline]]). This bloodline cannot be a bloodline you already have. You gain the 1st-level bloodline power for the selected bloodline. For purposes of using that power, treat your sorcerer level as equal to your character level – 2, even if you have levels in sorcerer. You do not gain any of the other bloodline abilities.
