# Knock-Down

Prerequisites: Base attack bonus +2, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], Str 15.

Benefit: Whenever you deal 10 or more points of damage to your opponent in melee, you make a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attack as a free action against the same target.
