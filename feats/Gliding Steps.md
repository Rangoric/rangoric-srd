# Gliding Steps

You skate across the surface of the earth as if gliding on ice.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Moves|Nimble Moves]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]].

**Benefit**: If you have at least 1 ki point in your ki pool, when you move you do not provoke attacks of opportunity when leaving the first square of that movement. You can spend 1 ki point to avoid provoking attacks of opportunity during that entire move.
