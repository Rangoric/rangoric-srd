# Greater Weapon of the Chosen

Your deity guides your hand when you fight with her favored weapon.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Weapon of the Chosen|Improved Weapon of the Chosen]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/War Domain|deity’s favored weapon]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon of the Chosen|Weapon of the Chosen]], must worship and receive spells from a deity.

**Benefit**: When you make a single attack with your deity’s favored weapon using the attack action, you roll two dice for the attack roll and take the higher result. You don’t need to use Weapon of the Chosen to gain this benefit.
