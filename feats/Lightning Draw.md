# Lightning Draw

You can draw your weapon with startling speed.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Swashbuckler Initiative (Deed)|swashbuckler initiative]] deed, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler|swashbuckler]] level 7th.

**Benefit**: You can spend 1 panache point to draw one or more light or one-handed piercing weapons, whether hidden or not, when you roll initiative, even at the start of a surprise round in which you can’t act. Drawing these weapons does not take an action.

If you have the instant unveil deed, it doesn’t cost any panache to use the ability described in the previous paragraph as long as you have at least 1 point of panache, and you can spend 1 panache point to draw a single light or one-handed piercing weapon, hidden or not, as an immediate action whenever a creature attacks you.
