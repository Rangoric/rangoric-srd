# Item Breaker

You are able to accurately target specific items held or worn by your opponents with ranged attacks.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Base Attack Bonus|Base attack bonus]] +3, proficiency with the weapon or attack.

**Benefit**: When targeting an item that is worn or held by an opponent, you receive a +2 competence bonus on your ranged attack. When attacking a weapon or shield, your ranged attack is considered to target a Medium weapon.
