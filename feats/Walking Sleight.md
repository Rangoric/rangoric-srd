# Walking Sleight

You've developed your skills at picking up items while on the move.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Engineering or Sleight of Hand), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You no longer take the –20 penalty for making a Sleight of Hand check as a move action. You can also make a Sleight of Hand check as a standard action in the middle of a move action.

**Normal**: A Sleight of Hand check as a move action takes a –20 penalty.

**Special**: This overlaps [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand#Unlocks|Sleight of Hand]]’s unlocks, which lower that penalty to –10 at 10 ranks and remove it at 20 ranks. This feat removes it at 5 ranks.
