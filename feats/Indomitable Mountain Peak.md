# Indomitable Mountain Peak

You become more difficult to move or pass, an elusive peak your enemies cannot surmount.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Indomitable Mountain Style|Indomitable Mountain Style]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: Whenever an opponent succeeds at a combat maneuver against you, or at an Acrobatics check to move through a square you threaten without provoking an attack of opportunity, you gain a +2 morale bonus to your CMD against the next combat maneuver made against you, and the DC of the next such Acrobatics check increases by 2. This bonus stacks each time, until a combat maneuver or Acrobatics check against you fails.
