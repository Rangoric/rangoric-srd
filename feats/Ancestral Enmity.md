# Ancestral Enmity

You can counter the special defensive training the stouter races have against your kind.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^giant|Giant subtype]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on melee attack rolls against [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarves]] and [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnomes]].

**Special**: You may select this feat twice. Its effects stack.
