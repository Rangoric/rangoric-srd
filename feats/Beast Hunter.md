# Beast Hunter

Thanks to your experience hunting in the wilds, you are capable of tracking animals in your most often traveled terrains, and you can easily take down animals larger than yourself.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Choose one of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^table-ranger-favored-terrains|ranger’s favored terrains]]. You gain a +2 bonus on Fieldcraft checks to track animals native to it. Against animals native to it that are at least one size category larger than you, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC and a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time, choose another favored terrain.
