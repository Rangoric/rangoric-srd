# Gloom Sight

With a combination of strange shadow magic and chirurgery, your eyes are permanently modified to see farther in darkness.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|Fetchling]].

**Benefit**: You gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] 90 ft., but gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Sensitivity|light sensitivity]] weakness.
