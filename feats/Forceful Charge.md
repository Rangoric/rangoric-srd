# Forceful Charge

This animal’s charges move its enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]].

**Benefit**: Once per round when this animal companion hits an opponent with a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] attack, it can initiate a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] against that target as a free action. If it moves its full speed as part of the charge, it gains a +4 bonus on its combat maneuver check to resolve this bull rush.
