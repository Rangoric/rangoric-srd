# Elemental Spell

You can manipulate the elemental nature of your spells.

**Benefit**: Choose an energy type: acid, cold, electricity or fire. You can replace a spell’s normal damage with that energy type, or split the damage so that half is that type and half its normal type. A elemental spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time, choose a different energy type.
