# Psicrystal Containment

Your psicrystal has advanced enough that it can hold a psionic focus that you store within it.  

Prerequisites: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psicrystal Affinity|Psicrystal Affinity]], manifester level 3rd.

Benefit: You can spend a full-round action attempting to psionically focus your [[rangoric-srd/psionics/Psion#Psicrystals|psicrystal]]. At any time when you need to expend your psionic focus, you can expend your psicrystal’s psionic focus instead, as long as the crystal is within 5 feet of you. Psionically focusing your psicrystal works just like focusing yourself. The psicrystal cannot focus itself - only the owner can spend the time to focus the crystal.
