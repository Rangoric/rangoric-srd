# Improved Feint Partner

Knowledge of your companions’ tricks and techniques allow you to take even greater advantage of your allies’ feints.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feint Partner|Feint Partner]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: Whenever an ally who also has this feat successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feints]] against an opponent, that opponent provokes an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] from you.
