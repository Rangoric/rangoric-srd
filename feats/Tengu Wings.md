# Tengu Wings

You can grow wings that allow you to fly.

**Prerequisites**: Character level 5th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|tengu]].

**Benefit**: Once per day, you can sprout a pair of giant black crow’s wings, granting you a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|fly speed]] of 30 feet (average maneuverability). This spell-like ability otherwise functions as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Beast Shape I|beast shape i]]* (though you do not gain any other benefits of that spell) with a caster level equal to your level.
