# Serpentine Compression

You can squeeze through small areas thanks to your slippery scales and lithe musculature.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/serpentfolk/Serpentfolk|Serpentfolk]].

**Benefit**: You gain the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Compression|compression]] universal monster ability, allowing you to move through an area as small as one-quarter your space without squeezing, or one-eighth your space when squeezing.
