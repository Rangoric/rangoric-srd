# Cloud Step

Your tread is of unearthly lightness.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spider Step|Spider Step]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] level 12th.

**Benefit**: As a move action, you can _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Air Walk|air walk]]_ (as the spell) up to half your slow fall distance, to a maximum of 50 feet. You must reach a solid, level surface by the end of your turn or you fall.
