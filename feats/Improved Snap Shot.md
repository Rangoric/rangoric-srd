# Improved Snap Shot

You can take advantage of your opponent's vulnerabilities from a greater distance, and without exposing yourself.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Shot|Rapid Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snap Shot|Snap Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: You threaten 10 more feet with Snap Shot.

**Normal**: Snap Shot threatens squares within 5 feet.
