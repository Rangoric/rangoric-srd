# Disorienting Maneuver

Your erratic movements disorient your opponent.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: If you use Acrobatics to tumble through an opponent’s space, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on attack rolls against it until the start of your next turn. If you try to trip it in that time, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on the combat maneuver check.
