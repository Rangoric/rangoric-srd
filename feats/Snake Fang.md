# Snake Fang

You can unleash attacks against an opponent that has dropped its guard.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snake Sidewind|Snake Sidewind]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snake Style|Snake Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 9 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using Snake Style, when an opponent’s attack misses you, you can make an unarmed strike against it as an attack of opportunity. If that hits, you can spend an immediate action to make another unarmed strike against the same opponent.
