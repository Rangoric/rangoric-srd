# Verdant Spell

Your magical connection to nature allows you to entice, fool, and misdirect plants and fungi as though they were people.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (enchantment), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: A verdant spell affects plant creatures (even mindless ones) as if they weren’t immune to mind-affecting effects, but it has no effect on other creatures. Only mind-affecting spells can be verdant. A verdant spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.

**Normal**: Plant creatures are immune to mind-affecting effects.
