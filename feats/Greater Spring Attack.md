# Greater Spring Attack

You are a scything wind cutting through the battlefield as you topple your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 17, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Acrobatic Steps|Acrobatic Steps]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Spring Attack|Improved Spring Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Moves|Nimble Moves]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spring Attack|Spring Attack]], base attack bonus +16.

**Benefit**: When you use Spring Attack, you can attack three targets during your movement instead of one. The second attack is made at your full base attack bonus –5, and the third at your full base attack bonus –10. All of Spring Attack’s restrictions apply to each target, and your movement doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity from any of them. You can’t target the same creature more than once.
