# Improved Drow Nobility

Your magical heritage is more potent than that of your peers, as demonstrated by your superior spell-like abilities.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Drow Nobility|Drow Nobility]], able to use [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]] spell-like abilities, [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]].

**Benefit**: You may use your _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]_ spell-like abilities twice per day. Your _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]_ spell-like ability instead becomes _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]_, which you may use twice per day.
