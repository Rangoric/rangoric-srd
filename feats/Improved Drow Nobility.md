# Improved Drow Nobility

Your magical heritage is more potent than that of your peers, as demonstrated by your superior spell-like abilities.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Drow Nobility|Drow Nobility]], able to use [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]] spell-like abilities, [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]].

**Benefit**: You may use your *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]* spell-like abilities twice per day. Your *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]* spell-like ability instead becomes *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]*, which you may use twice per day.
