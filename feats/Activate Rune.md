# Activate Rune

Having gained a basic understanding of runes and their operation you may activate inscribed runes.

**Prerequisites**: Con 12.

**Benefit**: You may activate [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#Inscribing Runes|inscribed runes]] as a standard action.

**Special**: You can’t cast rune spells or use runestones with this feat. It’s part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]].
