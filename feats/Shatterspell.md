# Shatterspell

Your mighty blows shatter your enemy’s magic.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disruptive|Disruptive]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spellbreaker|Spellbreaker]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]], 10th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can attempt to sunder an ongoing spell effect as if you had the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Spell Sunder (Rage Power)|spell sunder]] rage power. You may use this feat once per day, plus one additional time per day for every 5 points by which your base attack bonus exceeds +10.
