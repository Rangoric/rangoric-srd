# Body Shield

With a sly maneuver, you force a grappled opponent into the path of an incoming attack.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: As an immediate action while you are grappling an adjacent creature, you can make a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver check against it to gain cover against a single attack. If you succeed and the attack misses you, it targets the creature you used as cover, with the same attack roll. You can’t use this against a creature that is grappling you, and the cover ends once that attack is resolved.
