# Focused Phantom

Your close bond with your phantom helps you to focus your concentration.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-spiritual-interference|spiritual interference]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While you are adjacent to your phantom or your phantom is within your consciousness, you receive a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]].
