# Improved Charging Hurler

Every muscle in your body adds its force to your thrown weapons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Charging Hurler|Charging Hurler]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]].

**Benefit**: When you use Charging Hurler, your target can be at any range up to your weapon’s maximum range. If it is within 30 feet, you gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls.

**Normal**: Charging Hurler requires you to end your movement within 30 feet of your opponent.
