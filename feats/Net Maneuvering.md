# Net Maneuvering

With sweeping movements and brute force, you can use your net to put foes at a disadvantage.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] (net), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Net Adept|Net Adept]], base attack bonus +3.

**Benefit**: In melee, you can use a net to trip or disarm opponents instead of entangling them, with a +2 bonus on disarm checks made with it. If you have an opponent entangled in your net, you can attempt to drag or reposition it as long as it is within your net’s reach or you control the net’s trailing rope.
