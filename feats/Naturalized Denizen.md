# Naturalized Denizen

You are unusually anchored to your location.

**Prerequisite:** Exposure to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dimensional Anchor|dimensional anchor]] spell.

**Benefit:** You are never treated as an extraplanar creature (and you lose the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^extraplanar|extraplanar subtype]]). Thus, you can't be affected by a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Banishment|banishment]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Dismissal|dismissal]] spell or similar effects that send extraplanar creatures back to their home planes.
