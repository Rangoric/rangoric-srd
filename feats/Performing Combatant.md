# Performing Combatant

You treat every combat as a performance, bringing flare and showmanship.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], any one [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^performance-feats|performance feat]].

**Benefit**: You can make [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat#^performance-combat-check|performance combat checks]] in any combat. When making a performance check outside of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat|performance combat]], you can pick a single performance feat to use. You automatically gain any bonus on the performance combat check the feat grants, and then you make a DC 20 performance combat check. On a success, you gain the full effect of the performance feat you chose.
