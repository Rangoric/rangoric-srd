# Catch Off-Guard

Foes are surprised by your skilled use of unorthodox and improvised weapons.

**Benefit**: You take no penalties for using an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Categories|improvised weapon]] in melee. Unarmed opponents are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] against any attacks you make with an improvised melee weapon.

**Normal**: You take a –4 penalty on attack rolls made with an improvised weapon.
