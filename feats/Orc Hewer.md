# Orc Hewer

You are ferocious at hewing your enemies, especially orcs.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Goblin Cleaver|Goblin Cleaver]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]].

**Benefit**: This feat functions as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Goblin Cleaver|Goblin Cleaver]], but your additional attacks can be made against creatures your size or smaller. In addition, any such attacks that you make against humanoids (orc) gain a +2 circumstance bonus on attack rolls.
