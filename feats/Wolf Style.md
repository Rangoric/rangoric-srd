# Wolf Style

While in this style, you hamper foes that turn their backs on you.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using this style, whenever you deal at least 10 points of damage to a foe with an attack of opportunity, its base speed drops by 5 feet until the end of its next turn, plus another 5 feet for every 10 points of damage beyond the first 10. If this reduces its base speed to 0 or less, you can try to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] it as a free action after the attack of opportunity.
