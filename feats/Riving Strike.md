# Riving Strike

When you infuse your weapon with arcane might, your attacks make foes more susceptible to magic.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Arcane Strike|Arcane Strike]], ability to cast arcane spells.

**Benefit**: When you damage a creature with a weapon strengthened by your Arcane Strike, it takes a –2 penalty on saving throws against spells and spell-like abilities for 1 round.
