# Craft Toxins

You can create poisonous substances of any form or level of virulence, from discomforting to fatal.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) 5 ranks or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (herbalist) 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]].

**Benefit**: You can create any and all types of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions|poisons]], provided you meet the prerequisites. Making poisons and toxins takes a number of hours equal to the creation DC of the toxin. Each toxin has a base price equal to its caster level + DC x 120 gp. To create such substances, the creator must expend 2% (1/50) of the item’s base price in XP and use up raw materials costing 30% of the base price. (The savings alchemy or herbalism give other item creation feats, in [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemical and Herbal Items#Related Feats & Skill Adjustments|Related Feats & Skill Adjustments]], are already part of this feat.)

**Special**: This feat deals with alchemical or herbal poisons and toxins. If the character also has at least 2 ranks in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]], he or she can use this feat to process and administer animal-based venoms as well.

This feat is part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|alchemy and herbalism]].
