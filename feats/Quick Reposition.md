# Quick Reposition

Your opponent becomes an unwitting dance partner, following your lead while you fight.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Reposition|Improved Reposition]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: On your turn, you can attempt a single [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]] combat maneuver in place of one of your melee attacks. You must use the attack with your highest base attack bonus for it.

**Normal**: A reposition combat maneuver is a standard action.
