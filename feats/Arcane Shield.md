# Arcane Shield

You can convert any spell into a defense.

**Prerequisites**: Arcane spellcaster, caster level 10th.

**Benefit**: As an immediate action, you can give up a prepared spell or unused spell slot of 1st level or higher to gain a deflection bonus to AC equal to its level for 1 round. You can’t give up 0-level spells this way.
