# Chokehold

While grappling, you can cut off an opponent's air and blood supply.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +6 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: While you are grappling an opponent no more than one size category larger than you, you can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver at a –5 penalty. If you succeed, you have [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Pinned (Condition)|pinned]] the opponent in a chokehold, and maintaining the grapple maintains the chokehold. A creature in a chokehold can’t breathe or speak, so it can’t cast spells with verbal components, and it must hold its breath or begin to suffocate (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Suffocation|Suffocation]]). Creatures that don’t breathe, or are immune to bleed or to critical hits, are immune to your chokehold. When the grapple ends, so does the chokehold.
