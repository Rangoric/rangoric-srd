# Improved Dirge of Doom

The foreboding tone of your dirge is especially effective at unsettling your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to perform [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-dirge-of-doom|dirge of doom]].

**Benefit**: The range of your dirge of doom ability is extended to 60 feet. Additionally, if a creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] from another effect, the effect of your dirge of doom is changed to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for that specific creature. This benefit cannot cause a creature to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], even if a target is already frightened from another effect.

**Normal**: Dirge of doom reaches only as far as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-dirge-of-doom|class feature]] says, and a creature that is already shaken cannot become frightened by it.
