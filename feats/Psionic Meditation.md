# Psionic Meditation

> [!note] Replaced by [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quickened Focus|Quickened Focus]]
> Psionic Meditation was renamed Quickened Focus, with a manifester level prerequisite instead of Concentration ranks (2026-10-22; see [[Working Folder/Done/plans/Skill List Rework|Skill List Rework]]). The original text is kept below for reference.

## Original text

You can focus your mind faster than normal, even under duress.  

Prerequisite: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Concentration|Concentration]] 7 ranks.

Benefit: You can take a move action to become psionically focused.

Normal: A character without this feat must take a full-round action to become psionically focused.
