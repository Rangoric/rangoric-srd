You are a master of fighting in confined spaces.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|Ratfolk]], swarming racial trait.

**Benefit**: You count as one size smaller than normal for the purpose of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement|squeezing]].
