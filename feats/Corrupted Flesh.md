# Corrupted Flesh

You have sickened, rotting flesh from some deformity or vestigial limb that has turned fetid and necrotic, yet refuses to heal or fall off.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, 6 HD, [[rangoric-srd/races/Ogre|ogre]].

**Benefit**: You gain the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Stench|stench]] special ability (DC 10 + 1/2 your HD + your Constitution modifier).
