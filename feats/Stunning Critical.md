# Stunning Critical

Your critical hits cause opponents to become stunned.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Staggering Critical|Staggering Critical]], base attack bonus +17.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]], your opponent becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1d4 rounds. The stun attacks the opponent’s Fortitude, its attack bonus your base attack bonus; on a miss, the opponent is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d4 rounds. The effects of this feat do not stack. Additional hits instead add to the duration.
