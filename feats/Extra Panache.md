# Extra Panache

You have more panache than the ordinary swashbuckler.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Swashbuckler|Amateur Swashbuckler]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain two more panache points at the start of each day, and your maximum panache increases by two.

**Special**: If you have levels in the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler|swashbuckler]] class, you can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, these benefits increase by two.
