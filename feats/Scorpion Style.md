# Scorpion Style

You can perform an unarmed strike that greatly hampers your target's movement.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, make a single unarmed attack. If it hits, you deal damage normally, and the blow also attacks the target’s Fortitude, its attack bonus 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier; on a hit, its base land speed drops to 5 feet for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier.
