# Awesome Charge

When you charge, you can send your opponent flying.

**Prerequisites**: Str 25, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Awesome Blow|Awesome Blow]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]].

**Benefit**: When you hit your opponent with a charge attack, you can attempt an [[rangoric-srd/feats/Awesome Blow|awesome blow]] combat maneuver against that opponent as a free action.
