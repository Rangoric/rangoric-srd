# Crusader's Fist

You pour divine energy into the enemy you strike.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|Lay on hands]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: When you hit a creature that you can harm with your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] with an unarmed strike, you can use a swift action to expend a daily use of lay on hands to deal its normal damage, as if you had hit with its touch attack. This extra damage isn’t multiplied on a critical hit.
