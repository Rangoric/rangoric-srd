# Coordinated Reposition

You are skilled in coordinating combat positioning with your allies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Step Up|Step Up]].

**Benefit**: Whenever an ally with this feat who is threatening a creature you are threatening takes a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Take 5-Foot Step|5-foot step]], you can move 5 feet as an immediate action. This movement does not provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]].
