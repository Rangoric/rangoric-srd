# Deadly Finish

Your attacks don't just fell your opponents--they kill them outright.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: When you hit with a melee attack and reduce your opponent to –1 or fewer hit points, you can force it to succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 15 + the damage your attack dealt) or die.
