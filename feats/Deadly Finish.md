# Deadly Finish

Your attacks don't just fell your opponents--they kill them outright.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: When you hit with a melee attack and reduce your opponent to –1 or fewer hit points, you can have the blow attack its Fortitude, its attack bonus 5 + the damage your attack dealt; on a hit, it dies.
