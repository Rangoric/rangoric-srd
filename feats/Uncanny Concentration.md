# Uncanny Concentration

You have learned to enter a deeper state when casting spells, allowing you to shrug off distractions, damage, weather effects, and even the effects of other spells.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Casting|Combat Casting]].

**Benefit**: You don’t need to make [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] for vigorous or violent motion or for violent weather. You gain a +2 bonus on all other concentration checks.
