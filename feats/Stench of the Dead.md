# Stench of the Dead

The odor of decay hangs heavy on you, causing others to gasp and retch.

**Prerequisite:** Exposure to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghoul Touch|ghoul touch]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]] spell.

**Benefit:** You exude a carrion stench that attacks the Fortitude of any creature adjacent to you, its attack bonus 2 + your Cha modifier; on a hit, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] as long as it remains adjacent to you and for 1d4 rounds thereafter. You can't suppress the stench voluntarily.
