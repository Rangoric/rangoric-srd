# Janni Rush

When you leap to the attack, your blows are like bolts from on high.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Janni Style|Janni Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Janni Tempest|Janni Tempest]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 8 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (dance) 8 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using Janni Style, you always count as having a running start when jumping. If you jump as part of a charge and hit the opponent you charged with an unarmed strike, roll the unarmed strike’s damage dice twice and add them together before adding modifiers (such as Strength) or extra dice (such as precision damage). The extra dice aren’t multiplied on a critical hit.
