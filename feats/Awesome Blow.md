# Awesome Blow

**Prerequisites**: Str 25, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], size Large or larger.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, the creature can make an awesome blow [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]]. If it succeeds against a corporeal opponent smaller than itself, the opponent takes damage (typically slam damage plus Strength bonus), is knocked flying 10 feet in a direction of the attacking creature’s choice, and falls prone. The attacking creature can only push the opponent in a straight line, and the opponent can’t move closer to it than the square it started in. If an obstacle stops the opponent’s movement, the opponent and the obstacle each take 1d6 points of damage, and the opponent is knocked prone in the space next to the obstacle.
