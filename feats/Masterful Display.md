# Masterful Display

You craft a special victory performance that causes the crowd to go wild.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], any two [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^performance-feats|performance feats]].

**Benefit**: Choose the effects of any two performance feats you have. When you make a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat#^performance-combat-check|performance combat check]], you gain the benefits of those two feats, but you only gain a +2 bonus on the performance combat check.
