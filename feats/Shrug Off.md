# Shrug Off

You are able to shrug off part of an attack using your damage reduction.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|Damage reduction]] 1/— or better.

**Benefit**: At the beginning of each round of combat, you must declare one opponent against which your damage reduction increases. Your damage reduction is increased by 2 against any damage caused by that opponent (though the type of weapon needed to overcome the reduction remains the same).
