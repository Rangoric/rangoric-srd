# Greater Grapple

Maintaining a grapple is second nature to you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +6, Dex 13.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to grapple a foe. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]]. Once you have grappled a creature, maintaining the grapple is a move action. You can make two grapple checks each round (to move, harm or pin your opponent), though you don’t have to, and you need succeed on only one to maintain the grapple.

**Normal**: Maintaining a grapple is a standard action.
