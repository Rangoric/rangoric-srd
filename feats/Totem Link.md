# Totem Link

You have a special connection to a certain type of animal, increasing the power of your familiar if it is of the appropriate type and increasing your ability to interact with animals of your totem.

**Benefit**: You receive a +2 bonus on all skill checks involving your totem animal, and your caster level is treated as two levels higher when determining your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar]]’s abilities if your familiar is of the appropriate type. The totem must be one of these unless the GM creates new ones: [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Bear (Totem)|Bear]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Boar (Totem)|Boar]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Cat (Totem)|Cat]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Dog (Totem)|Dog]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Eagle (Totem)|Eagle]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Horse (Totem)|Horse]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Insect (Totem)|Insect]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Rat (Totem)|Rat]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Wolf (Totem)|Wolf]]. Not all of these may be available as familiars, so certain totems may be more advantageous than others.

**Special**: You can take this feat more than once, each time for a different totem. It’s the way into [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]].
