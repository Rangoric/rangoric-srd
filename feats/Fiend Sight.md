# Fiend Sight

Your eyes develop keener sight in dim light and darkness.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|tiefling]].

**Benefit**: You gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] and your [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] improves to 120 ft.

**Special**: You can take this feat twice. When you take it a second time, you gain the see in darkness ability.
