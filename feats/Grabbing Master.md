# Grabbing Master

You can grapple two foes as easily as one.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Grabbing Drag|Grabbing Drag]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Grabbing Style|Grabbing Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]]; base attack bonus +12, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler|brawler]] level 8th, or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] level 8th.

**Benefit**: When you are grappling two opponents while using Grabbing Style, you can use your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] to move or damage one or both opponents you are grappling, instead of just one.
