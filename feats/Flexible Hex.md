# Flexible Hex

Your wandering hex shifts at your command.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-wandering-hex|Wandering hex]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Once per day as a swift action, you can change your wandering hex. When you change your hex, the new hex must be associated with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#^shaman-wandering-spirit|wandering spirit]] with which you are currently bonded. At 12th level, you can change either or both of your wandering hexes with the feat.

**Normal**: Shamans select their hexes every day when they prepare their spells and cannot change them.
