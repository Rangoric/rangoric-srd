# Defiant Luck

You can sometimes defiantly shrug off spells and attacks that would kill a lesser creature.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]].

**Benefit**: Once per day, after an attack against your defense rolls a natural 20 or a critical hit is confirmed against you, you can either make the attacker reroll that attack, or force the creature that confirmed the critical hit against you to reroll the critical confirmation roll. This does not stack with other effects that allow you to make an attacker reroll or reroll an attack roll. You may only make one reroll.

**Special**: If your game uses [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points|hero points]], you can also spend 1 hero point when a critical hit is confirmed against you to have the opponent reroll the critical hit confirmation roll.
