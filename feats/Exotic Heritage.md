# Exotic Heritage

Your blood carries hints of an extraplanar ancestor, granting you a talent for a certain skill.

**Benefit**: Choose a skill. You gain a +3 bonus on all checks involving that skill. If you have 10 or more ranks in the chosen skill, this bonus increases to +6. This bonus does not stack with that granted by [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]]. This feat counts as Skill Focus with the chosen skill for the purpose of meeting the prerequisites of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Eldritch Heritage|Eldritch Heritage]] feat.
