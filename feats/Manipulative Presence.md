# Manipulative Presence

When you discard your host, you alter or obliterate its memory of your handiwork.

**Prerequisites**: Int or Cha 19, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hidden Presence|Hidden Presence]], ability to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession|possession]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession, Greater|greater possession]]_.

**Benefit**: Upon completion of your [[rangoric-srd/rules/Possession (Rules)|possession]], you can alter up to 5 minutes of your host’s memories as you see fit. This functions as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Modify Memory|modify memory]]_ spell. Your host’s awareness of the possession doesn’t impact this ability, and if your control of the host lasted less than 5 minutes, you can cause it to forget the possession entirely. The host can attempt a Will save with a DC equal to that of the original possession effect to resist this alteration.
