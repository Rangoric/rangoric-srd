# Kyton Style

Your fighting style emulates the disciplined nature of the kytons, with passion and pain held tightly in chains.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] (spiked chain), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While using this style, you treat spiked chains as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-flurry-of-blows|special monk weapons]]. If you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], you can spend 1 point from your ki pool to make a Stunning Fist attack with your spiked chain instead of an unarmed strike. This still uses a Stunning Fist attempt.
