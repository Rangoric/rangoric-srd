# Sharp Senses

Your senses are especially sharp, even for your kind.

**Prerequisites**: Keen senses racial trait ([[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]], or [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]]).

**Benefit**: You receive a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks. This replaces the normal Perception bonus from the keen senses racial trait.

**Normal**: The keen senses racial trait gives its own, smaller bonus on Perception checks.
