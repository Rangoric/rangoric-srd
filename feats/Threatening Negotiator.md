# Threatening Negotiator

Those who you intimidate know that you'll go through with your threats.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nerve-Racking Negotiator|Nerve-Racking Negotiator]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Intimidation).

**Benefit**: When you use Intimidation to make an opponent act friendly toward you, the change lasts 2d6 hours.

**Normal**: An intimidated opponent acts friendly for 1d6 × 10 minutes.
