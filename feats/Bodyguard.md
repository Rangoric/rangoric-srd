# Bodyguard

Your swift strikes ward off enemies attacking nearby allies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]].

**Benefit**: When an adjacent ally is attacked, you can use an attack of opportunity to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] to improve that ally’s AC. You can’t use this to improve the ally’s attack roll.

**Normal**: Aid another is a standard action.
