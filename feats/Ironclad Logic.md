# Ironclad Logic

You appeal to sound reasoning and fact, rather than to emotion, when attempting to sway others.

**Prerequisites**: Int 19, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 bonus on Persuasion checks whenever you add your Charisma modifier to them. If you have at least 10 ranks in Persuasion and Intelligence 27 or higher, the bonus increases to +8. It doesn’t stack with Skill Focus (Persuasion), but this feat counts as Skill Focus (Persuasion) for prerequisites.
