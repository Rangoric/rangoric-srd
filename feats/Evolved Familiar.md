# Evolved Familiar

Your familiar is different from others of its kind.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Select an evolution from the list of [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Evolutions#Summoner Evolutions|1-point evolutions]] available to a summoner’s eidolon. Your familiar has this evolution. The familiar must conform to any limitations of the evolution. For instance, no familiars can benefit from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Mount (Evolution)|mount]] evolution and only familiars with wings can take the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/evolutions/Wing Buffet (Evolution)|wing buffet]] evolution.

If you gain a new familiar, your old familiar loses all evolutions, and you can select a new 1-point evolution for the new familiar.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects stack, granting a new 1-point evolution to your familiar each time you gain this feat.
