# Critical Focus

You are trained in the art of causing pain.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: You receive a +4 circumstance bonus on attack rolls made to confirm [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hits]].
