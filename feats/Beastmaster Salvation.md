# Beastmaster Salvation

Your presence grants your companion the toughness and will to resist all manner of threats.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Perception), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Beastmaster Style|Beastmaster Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: If you are adjacent to your animal companion when it attempts a saving throw, you can make an Animal Handling check as an immediate action. Your companion uses your check result as the result of its saving throw instead of rolling.
