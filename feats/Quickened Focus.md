# Quickened Focus

You can focus your mind faster than normal, even under duress.  

Prerequisite: Wis 13, manifester level 4th (or 4th level in a psionic class that doesn’t manifest powers).

Benefit: You can take a move action to become [[rangoric-srd/psionics/Psionics#Psionic Focus|psionically focused]].

Normal: A character without this feat must take a full-round action to become psionically focused.
