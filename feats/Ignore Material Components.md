# Ignore Material Components

Prerequisites: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Eschew Materials|Eschew Materials]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 22 ranks, ability to cast 9th-level arcane or divine spells.  

Benefit: The character may cast spells without any material components. This feat does not affect the need for a focus or divine focus.
