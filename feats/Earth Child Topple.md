# Earth Child Topple

Your mastery of balance and momentum allows you to bring down giants with your bare hands.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, dwarf or gnome, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Earth Child Style|Earth Child Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] a giant of up to Huge size. While using Earth Child Style, you add your Wisdom bonus to combat maneuver checks to trip a giant and to attack rolls to confirm a critical hit against one.

**Normal**: You can trip only opponents no more than one size category larger than you.
