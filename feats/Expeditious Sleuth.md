# Expeditious Sleuth

Your mind is meticulous and sharp, making it easy for you to thoroughly search an area.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] 3 ranks, inspiration or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-lore-master|lore master]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can take 20 on an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] check in only 10 times the usual amount of time, and gain a +2 bonus on Investigation checks when you take 20.

**Normal**: Taking 20 takes as long as the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview|taking 20]] rule says.
