# Scribe Epic Scroll

Prerequisites: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Scribe Scroll|Scribe Scroll]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 21 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] 21 ranks.

Benefit: The character can scribe scrolls that exceed the normal limits for such items. Even this feat does not allow the character to scribe a scroll with an epic spell.
