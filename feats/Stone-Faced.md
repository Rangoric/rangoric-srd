# Stone-Faced

Rocks show more expression than you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]].

**Benefit**: You receive a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to lie or conceal your feelings or true motives, but not to feint in combat or deliver secret messages. In addition, the DC for [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks to get a hunch about you is 25, rather than the normal hunch DC.
