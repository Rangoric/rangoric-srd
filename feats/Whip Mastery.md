# Whip Mastery

Your superior expertise with this weapon does not provoke attacks of opportunity from your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (whip), base attack bonus +2.

**Benefit**: You no longer provoke attacks of opportunity when attacking with a whip. You can deal lethal damage with a whip, though you can still deal nonlethal damage when you want. You can deal damage with a whip despite a creature’s armor bonus or natural armor bonus.

**Normal**: Attacking with a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons|whip]] provokes attacks of opportunity as if you used a ranged weapon. A whip deals no damage to a creature with an armor bonus of +1 or a natural armor bonus of +3.
