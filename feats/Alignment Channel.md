# Alignment Channel

**Prerequisites**: The ability to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]].

**Benefit**: Choose chaotic, evil, good or lawful. Instead of its normal effect, your channel energy can heal or harm outsiders with that alignment subtype. You choose each time you channel; if you choose this, your channel energy has no effect on other creatures. The amount healed or dealt and the save DC are unchanged.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects don’t stack. Each time, it applies to a new alignment subtype; whenever you channel, choose which one.
