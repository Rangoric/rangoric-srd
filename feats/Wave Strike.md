# Wave Strike

You present a serene facade until you unsheathe your weapon and strike in one fluid motion.

**Prerequisites**: Weapon expertise class feature or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: If on your first turn of combat you draw a melee weapon to attack an opponent within your reach, you can spend a swift action to make a Deception check to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] against that opponent.
