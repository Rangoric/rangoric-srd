# Extreme Prejudice

Your hatred of a particular type of creature has honed your killer instinct.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Seething Hatred|Seething Hatred]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] +3d6, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied target]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you succeed at a sneak attack against a creature you selected as the target of your [[rangoric-srd/feats/Seething Hatred|Seething Hatred]] feat, you use d8s to roll sneak attack damage instead of d6s.
