# Maddening Obliteration

With a mighty killing blow, your unsettling punch vaporizes your foe.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 19, Cha 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Maddening Strike|Maddening Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Maddening Style|Maddening Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 10 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]] class feature, nongood alignment.

**Benefit**: When you damage a living creature with an unarmed strike while using Maddening Style, and you have at least 1 point in your ki pool, that creature takes a –2 penalty on saving throws against any confusion, madness and insanity effects (including your [[rangoric-srd/feats/Maddening Strike|Maddening Strike]]) for 1 round. Additionally, if you reduce a creature to fewer than 0 hit points with a Stunning Fist attack, you can spend 2 points from your ki pool as a free action to obliterate your opponent, reducing its body to fine dust as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]* does. A living creature can attempt a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) to negate this effect.
