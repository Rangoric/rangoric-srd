# Burning Spell

You cause creatures to take extra damage when you affect them with a spell that has the acid or fire descriptor.

**Benefit**: The acid or fire of the spell clings to the creature. A creature that takes acid or fire damage from the spell takes damage equal to 2 × the spell’s actual level at the start of its next turn, acid or fire as the spell’s descriptor says. If the spell has both descriptors, you choose which. A burning spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
