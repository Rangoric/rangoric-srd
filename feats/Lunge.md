# Lunge

You can strike foes that would normally be out of reach.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You can increase the reach of your melee attacks by 5 feet until the end of your turn by taking a –2 penalty to AC until your next turn. You must decide to do this before making any attacks.
