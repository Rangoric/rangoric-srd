# Charging Hurler

You know how to use your momentum to enhance your thrown weapon attacks.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]].

**Benefit**: You can use the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] rules to make a thrown weapon attack. Everything about a charge applies, except that you must only move closer to your opponent and must end within 30 feet of it. If you do, you can make a single thrown weapon attack against it, with the charge’s +2 bonus on the attack roll and –2 penalty to AC until the start of your next turn.
