# Ranged Disable

You can disable devices with ranged weapons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Far Shot|Far Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 9 ranks.

**Benefit**: As a full-round action, you can attempt an Engineering check to disable a device with a ranged weapon in which you have Weapon Focus. This works only on a simple device (one that normally takes a full-round action to disable), and not if the device has any cover or concealment. The shot deals no damage to the device. You count as using the wrong tools for the job (–4 on the check), and you apply the weapon’s range penalty to the check, but no bonuses that would apply to attack rolls.
