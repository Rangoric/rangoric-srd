# Intimidate Animals

You are skilled at intimidating animals with your bluster and fury.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Wild Empathy|Greater Wild Empathy]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You add your Wisdom modifier on Intimidation checks against animals in addition to your Charisma modifier. An animal with 4 or fewer Hit Dice is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] instead of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] on a successful Intimidation check to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] it.
