# Rending Claws

Your claw attacks do greater harm to your enemy.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, two claw natural attacks, base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: If you hit a creature with two claw attacks in the same turn, the second claw attack deals an extra 1d6 points of damage. This is precision damage and isn’t multiplied on a critical hit. You can use this feat once per round.
