# Djinni Spirit

By calling upon the spirits of storms, you can manipulate lightning to protect yourself and buffet your enemies with peals of thunder.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Djinni Style|Djinni Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +11 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: You gain one more Elemental Fist attempt per day. While using Djinni Style, you gain electricity resistance equal to your base attack bonus, or your monk level plus any base attack bonus from other classes, whichever is higher. While you are denied your Dexterity bonus to AC, you are denied this resistance too. Creatures that take electricity damage from your Elemental Fist must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] for 1d4 rounds. Those damaged by your Djinni Spin are deafened even if they save.
