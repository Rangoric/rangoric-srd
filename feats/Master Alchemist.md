# Master Alchemist

Your mastery of alchemy is nearly supernatural.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on Background (alchemy) checks, and you make mundane alchemical items much faster. When making poisons, you can make a number of doses equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) at once; the extra doses take no more time but do cost more raw materials.

When you make alchemical items or poisons with Background (alchemy), use the item’s price in gp as its value in sp to work out your progress (don’t multiply it by 10).
