# Extra Lay On Hands

You can use your lay on hands ability more often.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|Lay on hands]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can use your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] two additional times per day.

**Special**: You can gain Extra Lay On Hands multiple times. Its effects stack.
