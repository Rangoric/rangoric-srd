# Defensive Combat Training

You excel at defending yourself from all manner of combat maneuvers.

**Benefit**: You treat your total Hit Dice as your base attack bonus when working out your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|Combat Maneuver Defense]].
