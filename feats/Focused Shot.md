# Focused Shot

Your anatomical insight adds deadliness to your shots.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can make an attack with a bow or crossbow and add your Intelligence modifier to the damage roll. You must be within 30 feet of your target to deal this extra damage. Creatures immune to critical hits and sneak attacks are immune to it.
