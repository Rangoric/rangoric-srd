# Snap Shot

With a ranged weapon, you can take advantage of any opening in your opponent's defenses.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Shot|Rapid Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: While wielding a ranged weapon with which you have Weapon Focus, you threaten squares within 5 feet of you and can make attacks of opportunity with that weapon. You don’t provoke attacks of opportunity when you make a ranged attack as an attack of opportunity.

**Normal**: While wielding a ranged weapon, you threaten no squares and can’t make attacks of opportunity with it.
