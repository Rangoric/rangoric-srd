# Esoteric Linguistics

You can decipher even the most exotic of writings, including those that contain magic.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]]).

**Benefit**: You can make a Linguistics check instead of an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] check to activate a scroll, or instead of a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] check to identify a scroll. You must still have (or emulate) the ability score needed to cast the spell, and you can’t use Linguistics to emulate that score.
