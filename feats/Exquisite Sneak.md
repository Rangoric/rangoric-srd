# Exquisite Sneak

You know how to move silently even while wearing some forms of armor.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Careful Sneak|Careful Sneak]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: You don’t apply the armor check penalty for light and medium armor to Stealth checks, however fast you move. You still can’t use Stealth while running or charging.
