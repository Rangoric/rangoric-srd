# Versatile Channeler

You can choose to channel positive and negative energy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel energy]] class feature, and you’re a neutral cleric who chose at 1st level which kind of energy to channel.

**Benefit**: Each time you channel energy, you can choose to channel the other kind, as if your effective cleric level were 2 lower. You count as able to channel both positive and negative energy for prerequisites.

**Special**: Clerics whose alignment or deity decides which energy they channel can’t take this feat.
