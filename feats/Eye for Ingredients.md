# Eye for Ingredients

Your keen eyes help you find better deals on magical components for your spells.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Eschew Materials|Eschew Materials]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] 6 ranks, 6 ranks in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]].

**Benefit**: In a large city or bigger settlement, you can spend 4 hours searching the markets to buy material components for your spells at a 10% discount, up to 1,000 gp worth (for 900 gp) each day. These components work perfectly well, but they also resell for 10% less.
