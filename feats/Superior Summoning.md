# Superior Summoning

You can summon more creatures.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Augment Summoning|Augment Summoning]], caster level 3rd.

**Benefit**: Each time you cast a summoning spell that conjures more than one creature, it conjures one more.
