# Wings of Air

The winds lift you, carrying you where you want to go.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Airy Step|Airy Step]], character level 9th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|sylph]].

**Benefit**: Your bonus on saves against effects with the air or electricity descriptors and effects that deal electricity damage increases to +4. In addition, you gain a supernatural [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|fly speed]] equal to your base speed (good maneuverability). You may only fly with this ability when wearing light armor or no armor.
