# Celestial Servant

Rather than being a normal animal or beast, your companion or familiar hails from the heavenly realms.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|Aasimar]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar]], or mount class feature (such as the paladin’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-special-mount|special mount]]).

**Benefit**: Your animal companion, familiar, or mount gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Celestial Creature|celestial template]] and becomes a magical beast, though you may still treat it as an animal when using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]], or any other spells or class abilities that specifically affect animals.
