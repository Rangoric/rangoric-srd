# Merciful Spell

Your damaging spells subdue rather than kill.

**Benefit**: A spell that deals damage can deal nonlethal damage instead. Damage of a particular type (such as fire) becomes nonlethal damage of the same type. A merciful spell does not use up a higher-level spell slot than the spell’s actual level.
