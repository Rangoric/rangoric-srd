# Shatter Defenses

Your skill with your chosen weapon leaves opponents unable to defend themselves if you strike them when their defenses are already compromised.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], base attack bonus +6, proficiency with the weapon.

**Benefit**: Any shaken, frightened or panicked opponent you hit this round is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] to your attacks until the end of your next turn, including any further attacks you make this round.
