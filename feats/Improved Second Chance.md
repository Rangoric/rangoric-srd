# Improved Second Chance

You can turn a missed strike into a second chance without sacrificing later attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Second Chance (Feat)|Second Chance]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: When you reroll a missed attack with Second Chance, you can still make the rest of your attacks that turn, at a –5 penalty on each.

**Normal**: Using Second Chance means giving up the rest of your attacks that turn.
