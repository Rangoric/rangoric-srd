# Spirit Sense

You sense the presence of the unseen.

**Prerequisite**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] or the ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spells]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +5 bonus on checks to notice haunts. In addition, when you target an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] creature with a corporeal spell or effect that doesn’t deal damage, you have a 75% chance of affecting the target.

**Normal**: Corporeal spells and effects that don’t deal damage have only a 50% chance of affecting an incorporeal creature.
