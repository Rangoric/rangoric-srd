# Pass For Human

You’re easily mistaken for a human rather than a member of your own race.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-elf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]], or [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]] (see Special).

**Benefit**: You receive a +10 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks to disguise yourself as a human, and do not receive the penalty for disguising yourself as a member of another race when you do so. In areas largely populated or settled by humans, you can take 10 on your Imitation check, meaning most people tend to assume you are human unless given a reason to think otherwise.

**Special**: A halfling may take this feat, but must have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Childlike|Childlike]] feat as a prerequisite. The benefits of the two feats stack.
