# Enfilading Fire

Your ranged attacks take advantage of the flanking maneuvers of allies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]], one other [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^teamwork-feats|teamwork feat]].

**Benefit**: You receive a +2 bonus on ranged attacks made against a foe [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]] by 1 or more allies with this feat.
