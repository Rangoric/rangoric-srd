# Mind Over Body

Your ability damage heals more rapidly.  

Prerequisite: Con 13.

Benefit: You heal [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|ability damage]] and ability burn damage more quickly than normal. You heal a number of ability points per day equal to 1 + your Constitution bonus.

Normal: You heal ability damage and ability burn damage at a rate of 1 point per day.
