# Improved Awesome Blow

You are skilled at sending your opponents flying.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Awesome Blow|Awesome Blow]] or awesome blow class feature; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]].

**Benefit**: You receive a +2 bonus on checks to perform an awesome blow [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Performing a Combat Maneuver|combat maneuver]]. You also gain a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] whenever an opponent tries to perform an awesome blow combat maneuver against you. Whenever you successfully perform an awesome blow combat maneuver, your opponent’s movement provokes attacks of opportunity from all your allies (but not you).

**Normal**: Creatures moved by awesome blow do not provoke attacks of opportunity.
