# Powerful Shape

Your wild shapes are mighty and muscular.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|Wild shape]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid|druid]] level 8th.

**Benefit**: When in wild shape, treat your size as one category larger for the purpose of calculating [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]], carrying capacity, and any size-based special attacks you use or that are used against you (such as grab, swallow whole and trample).
