# Animal Call

You've learn how to replicate a number of animal calls native to wilderness environments.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Choose one of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^table-ranger-favored-terrains|ranger’s favored terrains]]. You can use Deception to mimic the calls of animals native to it. Creatures with ranks in Nature can use Nature instead of Sense Motive to notice the sound is false.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time, choose another favored terrain.
