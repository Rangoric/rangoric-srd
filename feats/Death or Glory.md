# Death or Glory

Even when facing a larger foe, you aren't afraid to take great risks in order to finish the fight.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: Against a creature of size Large or larger, you can make a single melee attack as a full-round action, with a +4 bonus on the attack roll, damage roll and critical confirmation roll. The bonus increases by 1 at base attack bonus +11, +16 and +20 (to a maximum of +7). After you resolve your attack, the opponent you attacked can spend an immediate action to make a single melee attack against you with the same bonuses.

**Special**: You can combine this attack with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Vital Strike|Improved Vital Strike]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Vital Strike|Greater Vital Strike]].
