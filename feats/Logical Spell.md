# Logical Spell

You can eschew emotion components by exercising logic.

**Benefit**: A logical spell can be cast without [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^emotion-component|emotion components]]. Spells that don’t require emotion components are not affected. A logical spell uses up a spell slot 1 level higher than the spell’s actual level.
