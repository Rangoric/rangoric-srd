# Sleep Venom

You can change the nature of your toxic spittle to put your enemies to sleep.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Vishkanya|Vishkanya]].

**Benefit**: As a swift action, you may alter the effects of your venom so the target falls unconscious. This changes the initial and secondary effect of your venom to the following: _initial effect_ [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d4 rounds; _secondary effect_ unconsciousness for 1 minute. You must make the decision to alter your venom before you apply it to a weapon.

**Normal**: Vishkanya venom has the effect the [[rangoric-srd/races/Vishkanya|vishkanya]]’s toxic racial trait gives.
