# Barroom Brawler

You have learned how to mimic the combat tricks and forms of others.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: Once per day as a move action, you can gain the benefit of a combat feat you don’t have for 1 minute. You must otherwise meet the feat’s prerequisites.

**Special**: If you have the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler#^brawler-martial-flexibility|martial flexibility]] class feature, this feat instead grants you one additional use per day of that ability.
