# Focused Eidolon

Your bond with your eidolon helps to focus your concentration.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-shield-ally|Shield ally]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While you are adjacent to your eidolon, you receive a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]].
