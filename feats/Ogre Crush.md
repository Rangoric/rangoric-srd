# Ogre Crush

Your thick, muscular arms are capable of landing powerful blows and crushing the life out of the smaller creatures you embrace.

**Prerequisites**: Str 25, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Ogre|ogre]], size Large or larger.

**Benefit**: Your unarmed strike damage increases by one step when you're [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grappling]] creatures smaller than you. You gain the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Constrict|constrict]] monster special ability, dealing an amount of damage equal to either the slam damage for a creature of your size or your unarmed strike damage, whichever is greater.
