# Kyton Cut

You lash yourself and share the pain with your opponent.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 17, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] (spiked chain), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Kyton Shield|Kyton Shield]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Kyton Style|Kyton Style]], base attack bonus +8 or monk level 6th, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While using Kyton Style, before you make an attack roll with a spiked chain you can choose to make it a Kyton Cut. You take 1d6 points of nonlethal damage, which can’t be avoided or reduced in any way (if you are immune to nonlethal damage, you take normal damage instead). If the attack hits, the target takes an extra 1d6 points of nonlethal damage and a –4 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] for 1 round. The penalty is a pain effect.
