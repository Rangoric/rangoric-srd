# Bashing Finish

You follow a powerful blow from your weapon with an opportunistic bash from your shield.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Shield Bash|Improved Shield Bash]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Master|Shield Master]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a critical hit with a melee weapon, you can make a shield bash attack against the same target with the same bonus, as a free action.
