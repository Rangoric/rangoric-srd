# Throw Anything

You are used to throwing things you have on hand.

**Benefit**: You take no penalties for using an improvised ranged weapon. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on attack rolls made with thrown splash weapons.

**Normal**: You take a –4 penalty on attack rolls made with an improvised weapon.
