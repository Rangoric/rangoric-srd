# Heroic Defiance

You struggle on when others would fall.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], base Fortitude save +8.

**Benefit**: Once per day as an immediate action, you can delay one harmful condition or affliction (such as panicked, paralyzed or stunned), including permanent and instantaneous ones, until the end of your next turn. Then it takes its normal effect. This feat doesn’t affect hit point damage or ability damage.
