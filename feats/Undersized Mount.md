# Undersized Mount

You've learned techniques that allow you to ride beasts of smaller sizes than normal.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: You can ride creatures of your own size category, though encumbrance or other factors might limit how you can use this ability.

**Normal**: A mount suited for you is usually at least one size category larger than you.
