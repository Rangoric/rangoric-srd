# Reward of Grace

When you lay on hands, divine energy ripples through you, granting you grace.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|Lay on hands]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Each time you use your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]], you gain a +1 sacred bonus on all attack rolls for 1 round.
