# Flanking Foil

Fighting multiple foes is easy for you.

**Benefit**: Whenever you hit an adjacent opponent with a melee attack, until the start of your next turn that opponent gains no [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] bonus on attack rolls while flanking you and can’t deal sneak attack damage to you. It can still provide a flank for its allies.
