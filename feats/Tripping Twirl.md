# Tripping Twirl

You can make a trip attack with a quarterstaff on all adjacent enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tripping Staff|Tripping Staff]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (quarterstaff), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Specialization|Weapon Specialization]] (quarterstaff), base attack bonus +12.

**Benefit**: As a full-round action while wielding a quarterstaff in two hands, you can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver against each enemy adjacent to you.
