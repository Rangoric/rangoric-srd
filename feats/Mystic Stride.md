# Mystic Stride

Enchanted vegetation does not bar your path.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Moves|Nimble Moves]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-woodland-stride|woodland stride]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can move at full speed even through thorns, briars and overgrown areas that are enchanted or magically manipulated to impede motion, even if those areas confer the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] condition.

**Normal**: Woodland stride does not apply to thorns, briars and overgrown areas that are enchanted or magically manipulated.
