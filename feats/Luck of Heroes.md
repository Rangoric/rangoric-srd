# Luck of Heroes

To others, it seems that you always have a bit of luck around you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hero's Fortune|Hero's Fortune]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you spend a [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points|hero point]] to reroll a die roll or to grant yourself a bonus before a die roll is made, there is a chance that the hero point is not spent. Whenever you spend a hero point, roll a d20. If the result is greater than 15, the hero point is not spent. You cannot use this feat when you [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points#^cheat-death|cheat death]].
