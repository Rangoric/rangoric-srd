# Lucky Strike

Your luck increases the potency of your weapon attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +5, [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Adaptable Luck|adaptable luck]] racial trait, [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]].

**Benefit**: Spend a use of your [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Adaptable Luck|adaptable luck]] racial trait to reroll the damage from a single weapon attack. You deal damage equal to the new damage roll, or the original roll, whichever is greater.
