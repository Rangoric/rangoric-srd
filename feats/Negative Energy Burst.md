# Negative Energy Burst

Prerequisites: Cha 25, ability to rebuke or command undead, ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Inflict Critical Wounds|inflict critical wounds]], any evil alignment.

Benefit: The character can use one rebuke or command undead attempt to unleash a wave of negative energy in a 60-foot-burst. Roll a normal rebuke (or command) check, except that the negative energy burst affects living creatures rather than undead. Any creature that would be rebuked by this result gains one negative level. Any creature that would be commanded by this check gains two negative levels. Each day these levels attack the creature’s Fortitude again, as temporary negative levels do, their attack bonus 1/2 the character’s effective turning level + the character’s Charisma modifier.
