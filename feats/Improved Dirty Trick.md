# Improved Dirty Trick

You are skilled at pulling dirty tricks on your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]].

**Benefit**: You don’t provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]]. You gain a +2 bonus on checks to attempt a dirty trick, and a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when an opponent tries a dirty trick on you.

**Normal**: You provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a dirty trick combat maneuver.
