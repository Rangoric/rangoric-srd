# Warmonger

You are adept at instigating wars among those you advise or antagonize.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]].

**Benefit**: You gain [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] as class skills. In addition, you receive a +4 competence bonus on Deception and Persuasion skill checks when using either skill to convince another character to go to war against or attack another creature or group.
