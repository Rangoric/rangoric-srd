# Bestow Luck

You are extremely lucky and sometimes your allies are as well.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Defiant Luck|Defiant Luck]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Inexplicable Luck|Inexplicable Luck]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]].

**Benefit**: You gain an extra use per day of your [[rangoric-srd/feats/Defiant Luck|Defiant Luck]] ability. You can also use your [[rangoric-srd/feats/Inexplicable Luck|Inexplicable Luck]] ability to grant an ally that can see and hear its benefit as an immediate action.
