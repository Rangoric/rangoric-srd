# Binding Throw

You can strike your enemy and use the blow as an opportunity to grab and hold him.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ki Throw|Ki Throw]].

**Benefit**: After you successfully use the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ki Throw|*Ki* Throw]] feat on an opponent, you can use a swift action to attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver against that opponent.

**Normal**: The [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver is a standard action.

**Special**: A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] may gain Binding Throw as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feat]] at 14th level.
