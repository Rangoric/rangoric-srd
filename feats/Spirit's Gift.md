# Spirit's Gift

You commune with a spirit of your choice, and it grants your animal companion or familiar a boon.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|Animal companion]] class feature or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar]] class feature.

**Benefit**: At the start of the day, you can choose to commune with a single [[rangoric-srd/class-options/spirits/Spirits|shaman spirit]]. (Although a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman|shaman]] would need an hour of preparation, this takes you no time.) For the next 24 hours, that shaman spirit augments your animal companion or familiar, as its [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shaman#Spirit Animal|spirit animal]] ability.
