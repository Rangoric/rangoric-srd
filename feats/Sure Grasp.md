# Sure Grasp

Your quick reflexes and skill at climbing keep you from falling to your doom.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Roll twice and take the higher result on Athletics checks to climb and on Reflex saves to avoid falling.
