# Sure Grasp

Your quick reflexes and skill at climbing keep you from falling to your doom.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Roll twice and take the higher result on Athletics checks to climb, and attacks against your Reflex Defense to make you fall roll twice and take the lower.
