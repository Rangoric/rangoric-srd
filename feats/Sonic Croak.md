# Sonic Croak

Your croak creates powerful sound waves that you can direct at fragile targets.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Throat Pouch|Throat Pouch]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggards/Boggard|boggard]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggards/Boggard#^terrifying-croak|terrifying croak]].

**Benefit**: When you use your [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggards/Boggard#^terrifying-croak|terrifying croak]] ability, instead of it imparting its normal effect, you can choose to deal 1d4 points of sonic damage to creatures in a 15-foot cone (Fortitude half). The attack bonus of this Fortitude attack is the same as that of your [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggards/Boggard#^terrifying-croak|terrifying croak]]. For every 3 Hit Dice you possess, the damage dealt increases by 1d4.

**Special**: The Throat Pouch feat increases the size of this feat's cone by 5 feet for every 6 HD you have, not every 2 HD.
