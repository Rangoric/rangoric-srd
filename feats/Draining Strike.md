# Draining Strike

With a well-aimed strike, you cause your target to become fatigued or exhausted.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: You must declare that you are using this feat before you make your attack roll (so a miss wastes the attempt). A foe damaged by your unarmed attack takes damage normally and must also succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] for 1 minute or until a spell or effect heals its hit point damage. If your base attack bonus is +14 or higher, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]] for that long instead. You can attempt Draining Strike once per day for every four character levels you have, and no more than once per round. Constructs, incorporeal creatures, plants, undead and creatures immune to critical hits can’t be affected.
