# Pile On

You can keep a foe shuddering in fear.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hurtful|Hurtful]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]].

**Benefit**: Once per round, when you damage a creature that is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], you can deal half your normal damage to make its fear last 1 round longer.
