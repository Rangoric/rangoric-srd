# Piercing Spell

Your studies have helped you develop methods to overcome spell resistance.

**Benefit**: When you cast a piercing spell against a target with spell resistance, treat its spell resistance as 5 lower than it is. A piercing spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
