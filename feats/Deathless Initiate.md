# Deathless Initiate

For you, impending death is a call to wrath.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, Con 13, orc or [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You are not limited to a single move or standard action while using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]] feat, but if you take both a move and a standard action, or a full-round action, while you are at 0 or fewer hit points, you take 1 point of damage. Further, while using the Diehard feat, you gain a +2 bonus on melee attacks and damage rolls.

**Note**: Pathfinder’s version reads “You are not staggered while using the Diehard feat”, since Pathfinder’s Diehard leaves you staggered. Rangoric keeps 3.5’s [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]] for now; this line is to look at again when the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death|dying and stabilization rules]] move to Pathfinder’s.
