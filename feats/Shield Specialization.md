# Shield Specialization

You are skilled at using your shield to protect yourself from critical hits.

**Prerequisites**: Proficiency with the selected shield, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Focus|Shield Focus]], fighter level 4th.

**Benefit**: Choose one type of shield (buckler, light, heavy or tower shield). With it, you gain a +2 bonus to AC against critical hit confirmation rolls, and you add your base shield bonus (including Shield Focus, but not enhancement bonuses) to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]].

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects don’t stack. Each time, it applies to a new type of shield.
