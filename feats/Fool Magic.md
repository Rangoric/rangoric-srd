# Fool Magic

You can mislead magic items by virtue of your disguise.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: When you are disguised as a member of a particular race or as someone of a particular alignment, you can use your Imitation bonus instead of your Emulation bonus to emulate that race and alignment to activate a magic item.
