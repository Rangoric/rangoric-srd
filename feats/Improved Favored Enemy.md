# Improved Favored Enemy

Prerequisite: Five or more favored enemies.  

Benefit: Add +1 to the bonus on Deception, Perception, Sense Motive, and Fieldcraft checks and damage rolls against all the character’s favored enemies.

Special: This feat may be taken multiple times. Its effects stack.
