# Lunging Spell Touch

You can extend your reach to touch foes that would normally be too far away.

**Prerequisites**: 6 ranks in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]].

**Benefit**: You can increase the reach of your spells’ melee touch attacks by 5 feet until the end of your turn by taking a –2 penalty to AC until your next turn. You must decide before you make any attacks that turn.
