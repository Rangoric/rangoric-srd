# Foebane Magic

The spells you cast are particularly effective against your favored enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast 1st-level spells, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Your favored enemies take a –1 penalty on saving throws against spells you cast. Furthermore, you gain your favored enemy bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] checks to identify spells cast by favored enemies.
