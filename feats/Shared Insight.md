# Shared Insight

You deftly direct others’ attentions where you wish.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]].

**Benefit**: As a move action, you can grant all friendly creatures within 30 feet who can see or hear you a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1 round).
