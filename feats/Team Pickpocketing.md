# Team Pickpocketing

You distract a mark with friendly conversation while your partner robs the victim blind.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Whenever an ally with this feat succeeds at a Deception check to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] an opponent, if you are adjacent to that creature, you can spend an immediate action to make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] check to pickpocket that opponent and gain a +4 bonus on that attempt.
