# Feint Partner

A little diversion is all you need to slip through your foe’s defenses.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Whenever an ally who also has this feat successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feints]] an opponent, that opponent also loses his Dexterity bonus to AC against the next attack you make against him before the end of the feinting ally’s next turn.
