# Bonebreaker

When your opponent is unable to adequately defend against them, your precise unarmed strikes break bone and tear tissue.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Jawbreaker|Jawbreaker]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 9 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you make a successful Stunning Fist attempt against an opponent that is [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], you can forgo any other Stunning Fist effect to deal 1d6 points of Strength or Dexterity [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]] to that opponent.
