# Incite Paranoia

You can convince anyone of her allies' ill intent.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Feint|Greater Feint]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: When you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] in combat and succeed by 5 or more, until the start of your next turn the target no longer provides flanking for its allies, and neither gains nor grants the benefits of teamwork feats. Also, when you use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] to tell the target a lie that one or more of its allies have betrayed it or are secretly against it, the lie counts as one step easier to believe (a bluff that is hard to believe is only a little hard to believe, and one that is a little hard to believe is believable).
