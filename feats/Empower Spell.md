# Empower Spell

**Benefit**: All variable, numeric effects of an empowered spell are increased by one-half. Attack rolls against defenses and opposed rolls are not affected, nor are spells without random variables. An empowered spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
