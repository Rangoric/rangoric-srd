# Crushing Blow

Your focus allows you to smash your enemy’s defenses.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]].

**Benefit**: You can make a Stunning Fist attempt as a full-round action. If it succeeds, instead of stunning your target, you reduce its AC by an amount equal to your Wisdom modifier for 1 minute. This penalty doesn’t stack with other penalties from Crushing Blow.
