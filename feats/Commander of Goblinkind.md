# Commander of Goblinkind

You employ ruthlessness and malice to command other races of goblinoids to do your bidding.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]].

**Benefit**: You receive a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks and Charisma-based skill checks regarding other [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^goblinoid|goblinoids]]. If you have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Leadership|Leadership]] feat, treat your leadership score as 2 higher when taking followers or a cohort with the goblinoid subtype.
