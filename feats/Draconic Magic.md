# Draconic Magic

Your draconic aspect manifests as magical might.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Aspect|Draconic Aspect]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]].

**Benefit**: You gain a group of spell-like abilities based on the color of your dragon aspect. Each spell-like ability can be used once per day, and the DC of any of these spell-like abilities is 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier.

- **Black**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Corrosive Touch|corrosive touch]]*\*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*.
- **Blue**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Minor Image|minor image]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]*\*.
- **Green**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Entangle|entangle]]*\*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]*.
- **Red**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]*\*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pyrotechnics|pyrotechnics]]*.
- **White**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]*\* (deals cold damage instead of fire), *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]*.

**Special**: You can use this feat instead of [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Breath|Draconic Breath]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Glide|Draconic Glide]] to qualify for the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Paragon|Draconic Paragon]] feat. If you have this feat and Draconic Paragon, you can use the spell-like ability marked with a \* twice per day.
