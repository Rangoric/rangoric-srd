# Compounded Pain

Your painful stare causes two additional effects.

**Prerequisites**: Mesmerist level 13th, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-painful-stare|painful stare]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can apply the effects of two [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^stare-feats|stare feats]] to your painful stares.

**Normal**: You can apply the effects of only one stare feat to a given painful stare.
