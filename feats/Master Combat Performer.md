# Master Combat Performer

You are a master of the techniques and weapons of the arena and the stage.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Performing Combatant|Performing Combatant]] or at least three [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^performance-feats|performance feats]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You can make [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat#^performance-combat-check|performance combat checks]] as a free action. You are proficient in all weapons with the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Performance (Quality)|performance]] special quality.
