# Blindsight,5-Ft.radius

Prerequisites: Base attack bonus +4, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]], Wisdom 19.  

Benefit: Using senses such as acute hearing and sensitivity to vibrations, you detect the location of opponents who are no more than 5 feet away from you. [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|Invisibility]] and darkness are irrelevant, though it you discern incorporeal beings.
