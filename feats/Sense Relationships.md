# Sense Relationships

You can quickly sense relationships between others and use that knowledge to manipulate them.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: After 1 minute or more of talking with or watching two creatures together, you can attempt a DC 20 Sense Motive check to get a hunch about their relationship. You take a –5 penalty if you don’t understand the language they speak, and another –5 if they are a different type of creature from you.

If you succeed by 5 or more, for the next hour you gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to lie about one of them to the other, and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks to ask one to act against the other. If you fail, you can’t try again on the same creatures until you gain another rank in Sense Motive.
