# Energy Channel

You distill your channeled energy into your weapon to empower it.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel energy]] class feature; [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Air Domain|Air]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Earth Domain|Earth]], [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Fire Domain|Fire]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Water Domain|Water]] [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains|domain]] or blessing.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, you can expend one use of channel energy to grant your weapon attacks a bonus on damage rolls equal to twice the number of dice rolled for your channel energy. This additional damage is of an energy type determined by your domain: acid (Earth), cold (Water), electricity (Air) or fire (Fire). If you have more than one of those domains, you must choose one of those damage types when you use this ability. This effect lasts for your next three weapon attacks or until the end of combat, whichever comes first.
