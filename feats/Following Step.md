# Following Step

You can follow foes as they move away from you.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Step Up|Step Up]].

**Benefit**: When you use Step Up to follow an adjacent foe, you can move up to 10 feet. You can still take a 5-foot step during your next turn, and this movement doesn’t reduce your movement during your next turn.

**Normal**: Step Up lets you take only a 5-foot step to follow an opponent.
