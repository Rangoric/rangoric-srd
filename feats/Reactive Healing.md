# Reactive Healing

You can channel healing energy in response to an attack that would knock you unconscious.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Channel|Quick Channel]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quicken Spell|Quicken Spell]]; [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When an attack or effect would reduce you to 0 hit points or fewer, you can spend one use of channel energy (of a kind that would heal you) or lay on hands as an immediate action to heal yourself. The healing affects only you, even if it would normally affect others.
