# Strangler

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] +1d6, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you successfully maintain a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] and choose to deal damage, you can spend a swift action to deal your sneak attack damage to the creature you are grappling.
