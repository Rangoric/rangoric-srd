# Quick Dirty Trick

You can perpetrate a dirty trick and deliver an attack before your opponent is the wiser.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Dirty Trick|Improved Dirty Trick]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: On your turn, you can attempt a single [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] combat maneuver in place of one of your melee attacks. You must use the attack with your highest base attack bonus for it.

**Normal**: A dirty trick combat maneuver is a standard action.
