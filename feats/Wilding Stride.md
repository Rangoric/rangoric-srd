# Wilding Stride

Your wild heart and honed body let you move with great swiftness.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wilding|Wilding]].

**Benefit**: Your base speed increases by 10 feet. This doesn't stack with the fast movement class feature or similar effects, unless they give an enhancement bonus.
