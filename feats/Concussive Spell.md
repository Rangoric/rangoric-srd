# Concussive Spell

You cause creatures to be disoriented when you affect them with a spell that has the sonic descriptor.

**Benefit**: A creature that takes damage from a concussive spell takes a –2 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks and ability checks for a number of rounds equal to the spell’s actual level. Only spells with the sonic descriptor can be concussive. A concussive spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
