# Bookish Rogue

Thanks to your preparation, your arcane ability is more varied than most.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Minor Magic (Rogue Talent)|Minor magic]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-talents|rogue talent]].

**Benefit**: By studying a spellbook for 10 minutes, you can change one spell you are able to cast using your [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Minor Magic (Rogue Talent)|minor magic]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Major Magic (Rogue Talent)|major magic]] rogue talent to one sorcerer/wizard spell of the same level contained in the spellbook. This change is permanent until you take the time to change it via this feat again.
