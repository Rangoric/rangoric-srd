# Sleeper Hold

By maintaining a grapple hold on your opponent, you can cause it to lose consciousness.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Grapple|Greater Grapple]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: You must declare that you are using this feat before your check to maintain a grapple (so a failed check ruins the attempt). If you maintain a grapple for a number of consecutive rounds equal to your opponent’s Constitution bonus (minimum 1 round), you can try to knock it out: this attacks its Fortitude, its attack bonus 1/2 your character level + your Str modifier; on a hit, it falls unconscious for 1d4 rounds. Each further round you try this, the attack gains a cumulative +1 bonus. While you use this feat, you take another –2 penalty to AC. Creatures immune to bleed, stunning or critical hits are immune to this.
