# Sleeper Hold

By maintaining a grapple hold on your opponent, you can cause it to lose consciousness.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Grapple|Greater Grapple]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: You must declare that you are using this feat before your check to maintain a grapple (so a failed check ruins the attempt). If you maintain a grapple for a number of consecutive rounds equal to your opponent’s Constitution bonus (minimum 1 round), you can try to knock it out: it must succeed on a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Str modifier) or fall unconscious for 1d4 rounds. Each further round you try this, it takes a cumulative –1 penalty on the save. While you use this feat, you take another –2 penalty to AC. Creatures immune to bleed, stunning or critical hits are immune to this.
