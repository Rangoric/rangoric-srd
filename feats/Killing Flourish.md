# Killing Flourish

You inspire dread in other foes when you slay an enemy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intimidating Prowess|Intimidating Prowess]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 4 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer|slayer]] level 4th.

**Benefit**: When you reduce your target to below 0 hit points with a melee attack, as a swift action you can attempt an Intimidation check to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] all foes within 30 feet who can see your attack.
