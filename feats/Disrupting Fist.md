# Disrupting Fist

Your powerful fists can reduce undead foes to dust.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Channel Smite|Channel Smite]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +9, [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel positive energy]] of 4d6 or more.

**Benefit**: When you confirm a critical hit with an unarmed strike against an undead creature, you can spend two uses of channel energy as a swift action to destroy it. It can negate this with a Will save (DC 10 + your base attack bonus).
