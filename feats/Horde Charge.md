# Horde Charge

When you charge with an ally, you are more deadly.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]] or orc.

**Benefit**: When [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charging]] during the same round as an ally with this feat, you gain a +2 bonus on attack and damage rolls in addition to the normal bonus for charging. If you can make multiple attacks on a charge, this bonus only applies to the first attack.
