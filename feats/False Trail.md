# False Trail

You create a false trail to throw off pursuers, enabling you to increase your lead on those following you or make them lose your trail entirely.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can lay a false trail in the wilderness with misleading footprints, dropped items, torn scraps of cloth and other signs, choosing the direction it leads. A creature tracking you must succeed at a Perception or Fieldcraft check (DC 10 + half your character level + your Wisdom modifier) to see the trail is fake when it first comes to it. On a success, it keeps tracking you as normal. On a failure, how far it follows the false trail depends on how long you spent making it:

- **Quick**: 10 minutes to make; the tracker follows it for 1d4 × 1,000 feet.
- **Normal**: 1 hour to make; the tracker follows it for 1d4 miles.
- **Elaborate**: 4 hours to make; the tracker follows it for 2d6 miles.

After following it that far, the tracker can make another Perception or Fieldcraft check with a +5 bonus. On a success, it realizes the trail is false and can go back to where you left it and track you as normal. On a failure, it follows the false trail another mile, then can check again, with a cumulative +5 bonus each mile.
