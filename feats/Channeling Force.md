# Channeling Force

You distill your channeled energy into a sheen of force that surrounds your weapon for a limited time.

**Prerequisites**: The ability to cast at least one force spell, [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, you can spend one use of channel energy to give your weapon attacks a bonus on damage rolls equal to the number of dice of your channel energy. The extra damage is force damage. It lasts for your next three weapon attacks or until the end of combat, whichever comes first.
