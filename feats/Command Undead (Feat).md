# Command Undead (Feat)

Using foul powers of necromancy, you can command undead creatures, making them into your servants.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|Channel negative energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can use one of your uses of channel negative energy to enslave undead within 30 feet. The channel attacks the Will of each undead, its attack bonus 1/2 your effective cleric level + your Charisma modifier, and a miss negates the effect. Undead it hits fall under your control, obeying your commands to the best of their ability, as if under the effects of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Undead|control undead]]_. The channel attacks intelligent undead again each day, and a miss frees them from your command. You can control any number of undead, so long as their total Hit Dice do not exceed your effective cleric level. If you use channel energy in this way, it has no other effect (it does not heal or harm nearby creatures). If an undead creature is under the control of another creature, you must make an opposed Charisma check whenever your orders conflict.

**Normal**: A character who channels negative energy can also spend a use of it on a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turning attempt]] to rebuke or command undead, which uses a turning check and turning damage rather than an attack against Will Defense.
