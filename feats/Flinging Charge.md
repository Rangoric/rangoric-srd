# Flinging Charge

You hurl a weapon during your charge before drawing a new one to strike, enabling you to make another attack at the expense of the latter attack's accuracy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: If you have a thrown weapon in hand when you begin a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]], you can make a ranged attack with it against the target of your charge at any point during the charge, with the charge’s +2 bonus on the attack roll. You can then draw a melee weapon during your charge. You take a –5 penalty on the melee attack at the end of the charge. When charging this way, you can’t use any effect that gives you more than one attack at the end of your charge (such as pounce), nor abilities that give you other ranged attacks on a charge (such as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Hurling Charge (Rage Power)|hurling charge]] rage power).
