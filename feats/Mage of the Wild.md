# Mage of the Wild

Your mystic connection with the wilderness enhances your spellcasting.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Attuned to the Wild|Attuned to the Wild]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]].

**Benefit**: When you are in a terrain type you have selected the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Attuned to the Wild|Attuned to the Wild]] feat for, you gain a +2 bonus on caster level checks, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] checks, and checks with your [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]]. If you are in an area that qualifies as more than one kind of terrain, these bonuses do not stack; you receive the bonus for only one of the terrain types.
