# Staggering Blow

You know just where to strike to temporarily stagger a foe.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: When you attack a foe using Vital Strike, you can choose to take a –2 penalty on your attack roll to also make the attack a staggering strike. You must choose before making the attack roll. If the attack hits, your target must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Strength modifier) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] until the start of your next turn. Once a creature that failed its save recovers, it is immune to this feat for 24 hours. Constructs, oozes, plants, undead, incorporeal creatures and creatures immune to critical hits are immune to this effect.
