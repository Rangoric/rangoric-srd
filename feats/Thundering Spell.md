# Thundering Spell

You can conjure your spells into existence with blaring thunder or fearful shrieks, deafening creatures damaged by their effects.

**Benefit**: A creature that takes damage from a thundering spell is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] for a number of rounds equal to the spell’s original level. If the spell attacks a defense, a miss negates the effect; if it doesn’t, the effect attacks the target’s Fortitude at the spell’s attack bonus, and a miss negates it. If the spell already deafens the creature, this duration is added to the spell’s. Spells that deal no damage don’t benefit from this feat. A thundering spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
