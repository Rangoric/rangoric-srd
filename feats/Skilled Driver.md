# Skilled Driver

You are more skilled when driving one type of vehicle.

**Benefit**: Choose a type of vehicle (air, land, or water). You gain a +4 bonus on driving checks with your chosen vehicle (see [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Vehicles/Vehicles|Vehicles]]).
