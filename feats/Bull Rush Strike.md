# Bull Rush Strike

Your critical hits can push back your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a critical hit with a melee attack, you can also push your opponent back, on top of the attack’s normal damage. If your confirmation roll beats the opponent’s [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]], you push it back (you don’t have to move with it) as if with the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] combat maneuver. This doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity.

**Normal**: You must use the bull rush combat maneuver to push your opponent back.

**Special**: You can apply only one of these feats to a given critical hit: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bull Rush Strike|Bull Rush Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disarming Strike|Disarming Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Repositioning Strike|Repositioning Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sundering Strike|Sundering Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tripping Strike|Tripping Strike]]. You can choose to use this feat after you make your confirmation roll.
