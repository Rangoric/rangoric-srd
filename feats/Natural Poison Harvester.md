# Natural Poison Harvester

You are exceedingly proficient at harvesting and distilling poison from venomous monsters.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on Background (alchemy) checks to make poison harvested from poisonous monsters. When you make such a poison, its DC increases by 2.
