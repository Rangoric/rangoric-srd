# Fleeting Spell

Your spells vanish with unusual speed.

**Benefit**: A fleeting spell is dismissible if it isn’t already, and you can dismiss it as a swift action. Once dismissed, its lingering aura can’t be detected by magic unless the searcher succeeds on a caster level check (DC 11 + your caster level). The DC of dispel checks to counter a fleeting spell is 2 lower, and once it’s active, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]* removes it with no caster level check. A fleeting spell lasts half its normal duration (an extended fleeting spell lasts its normal duration). Only spells with a duration of at least 2 rounds can be fleeting; instantaneous and permanent spells can’t. A fleeting spell does not use up a higher-level spell slot than the spell’s actual level.

**Normal**: Dismissing a spell is a standard action, and only spells marked (D) can be dismissed (see [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview|Magic Overview]]).
