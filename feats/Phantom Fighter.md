# Phantom Fighter

Your phantom is a deadly foe of incorporeal adversaries.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-phantom|phantom]] class feature, phantom with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)#^spiritualist-magic-attacks|magic attacks]] ability.

**Benefit**: Your phantom’s natural weapons are treated as having the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]] property. In addition, when you cast a touch spell to be delivered by your phantom, you can modify the spell as if you possessed the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ectoplasmic Spell|Ectoplasmic Spell]] metamagic feat.
