# Greater Shield Focus

You are skilled at deflecting blows with your shield.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Focus|Shield Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Proficiency|Shield Proficiency]], base attack bonus +1, fighter level 8th.

**Benefit**: The AC bonus from any shield you are using increases by 1 more. This stacks with Shield Focus.
