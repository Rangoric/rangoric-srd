# Vampiric Companion

Just as your undead existence mocks nature, so too does your twisted companion reflect the vile nature of vampirism.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dhampir|Dhampir]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Vampire|vampire]], nongood alignment, 10th level in a class that grants a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]].

**Benefit**: Your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#Familiars|familiar’s]] type changes to [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^undead|undead]]. The creature gains [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fast-healing|fast healing]] 5 as well as your [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Vampire#Vampire Weaknesses|vampire]] or [[rangoric-srd/races/Dhampir|dhampir]] weaknesses.

**Special**: If your animal companion or familiar is destroyed, dismissed, or lost, you can apply the effects of this feat to the replacement creature. If you are destroyed, the creature retains its undead type but loses all other special abilities from this feat. If you have more than one animal companion or familiar, choose one of them when you select this feat and apply its effects to that creature.

You can select this feat more than once. Each time you select the feat, it applies to a different animal companion or familiar.
