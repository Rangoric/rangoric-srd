# Gang Up

You are adept at using greater numbers against foes.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]].

**Benefit**: You are considered to be [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] an opponent if at least two of your allies are threatening it, wherever you are.

**Normal**: You must be opposite an ally to flank an opponent.
