# Expanded Fiendish Resistance

You gain extra fiendish resistances.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|Tiefling]].

**Benefit**: Pick one of the following energy types that you do not already have resistance to: acid, cold, electricity, or fire. You gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 5 to that energy type.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, pick another energy type you do not have resistance to. You gain resistance 5 to that energy type.
