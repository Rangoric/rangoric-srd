# Blood of the Fey

You are descended from a fey who visited the mortal world many years ago. The magical power inherent in fey blood pulses stronger throughout your body.

**Benefit**: You gain a +3 bonus on saving throws against enchantment spells and spell-like abilities. Add +2 to the save DCs of enchantment spells you cast.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level, and only if you aren’t a fey. You can’t have both this feat and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Insight of the Fey|Insight of the Fey]].
