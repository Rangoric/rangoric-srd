# Echoing Spell

You have learned how to release most, but not all, of a spell’s potential when you cast it.

**Benefit**: An echoing spell doesn’t vanish entirely from memory: you can cast it one more time that day. If you prepare spells, the second casting needs no other prepared slot; if you cast spontaneously, it uses no other spell slot. No effect that lets you reprepare or recast a spell can affect the echoed spell. A echoing spell uses up a spell slot three levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
