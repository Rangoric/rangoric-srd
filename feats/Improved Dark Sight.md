# Improved Dark Sight

With further modification, your eyes see clearly in not only normal darkness, but in magical darkness as well.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dark Sight|Dark Sight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Gloom Sight|Gloom Sight]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|fetchling]].

**Benefit**: You gain the see in darkness ability and lose the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Sensitivity|light sensitivity]] weakness, but gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Blindness|light blindness]] weakness.
