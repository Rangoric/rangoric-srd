# Maximized Spellstrike

You deal brutal damage against opponents caught unawares.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/magus-arcana/Maximized Magic (Magus Arcana)|Maximized magic]] magus arcana, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Samurai#^samurai-weapon-expertise|weapon expertise]] class feature or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]].

**Benefit**: When you make a melee attack and successfully use your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-spellstrike|spellstrike]] ability against an opponent denied his Dexterity bonus to AC, you can spend 3 points from your arcane pool to maximize the spell delivered through your spellstrike as if using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Maximize Spell|Maximize Spell]] metamagic feat.
