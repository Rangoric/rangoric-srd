# Death from Below

You have been trained to fight flying creatures.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on attack rolls against flying opponents when you have set a weapon against a charge, or when you make an attack of opportunity against a flying opponent.
