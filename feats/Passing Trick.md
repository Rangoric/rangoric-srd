# Passing Trick

Slipping past a foe gives you the chance to feint.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: Whenever you succeed at an Acrobatics check to move through an opponent’s space, you can spend a swift action to make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] it.

**Special**: If you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Underfoot|Underfoot]] and the opponent is larger than you, you gain a +2 bonus on the Deception check.
