# Missile Shield

You are skilled at deflecting ranged attacks with your shield.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Focus|Shield Focus]].

**Benefit**: While using a light, heavy or tower shield, once per round when you would be hit by an attack from a ranged weapon (not spell effects, natural attacks or massive ranged weapons), you can deflect it so you take no damage, as with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deflect Arrows|Deflect Arrows]]. You must be aware of the attack and not flat-footed.
