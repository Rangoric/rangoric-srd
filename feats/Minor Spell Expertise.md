# Minor Spell Expertise

You are able to cast a 1st-level spell as a spell-like ability.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast 4th-level spells.

**Benefit**: Choose one spell of 1st level that you know. You can cast it twice per day as a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions|spell-like ability]]. Its caster level equals your caster level in the class whose spell list it comes from, and its save DC is Charisma-based. You can’t choose a spell with an expensive focus or material component, and you can’t apply metamagic feats to it.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Its effects don’t stack. Each time, it applies to a new spell.
