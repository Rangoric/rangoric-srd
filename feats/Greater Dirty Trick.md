# Greater Dirty Trick

When you perform a dirty trick, your foe is seriously hindered.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Dirty Trick|Improved Dirty Trick]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to attempt a dirty trick. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Dirty Trick|Improved Dirty Trick]]. When your dirty trick succeeds, its penalty lasts 1d4 rounds, plus 1 round for every 5 by which your check beats the target’s CMD, and removing it takes the target a standard action.

**Normal**: The condition lasts 1 round, plus 1 round for every 5 by which your check beats the target’s CMD, and removing it takes a move action.
