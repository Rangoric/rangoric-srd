# Greater Blighted Critical

Your critical hit from a spell or spell-like ability afflicts the target with a major spellblight.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blighted Critical|Blighted Critical]], caster level 12th.

**Benefit**: Whenever you confirm a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] with a touch spell, ranged touch spell, or spell-like ability against an opponent, the victim gains a random major [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-major-spellblights|spellblight]].
