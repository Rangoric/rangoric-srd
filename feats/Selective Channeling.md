# Selective Channeling

You can choose whom to affect when you channel energy.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you channel energy, you can choose a number of targets in the area up to your Charisma modifier. They aren’t affected by your channeled energy.

**Normal**: Everything in the burst is affected when you channel energy; you choose only whether you are.
