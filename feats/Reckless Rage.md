# Reckless Rage

Your boundless rage grants your blows extra strength, though at the expense of accuracy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you use Power Attack while [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|raging]] or while using [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]], you take an additional –1 penalty on melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks, and you gain an additional +2 bonus on melee damage rolls. Modify this damage bonus appropriately based on the type of weapon you are using, as normal for Power Attack.
