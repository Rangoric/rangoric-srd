# Neckbreaker

With a quick jerk, you snap an enemy’s neck.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bonebreaker|Bonebreaker]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Grapple|Greater Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Jawbreaker|Jawbreaker]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 12 ranks.

**Benefit**: If an opponent your size or smaller is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Pinned (Condition)|pinned]] by you, after you initiate or maintain a grapple you can make a Stunning Fist attempt against it at a –5 penalty on the attack roll. If it succeeds, you wrench that opponent’s neck, dealing 2d6 points of Strength or Dexterity [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]]. If the targeted ability score is reduced to 0, any remaining damage is dealt to the opponent’s Constitution. A creature that is immune to critical hits or has no discernible head and neck is immune to this feat.
