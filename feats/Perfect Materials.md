# Perfect Materials

You know the alchemical methods to refine materials into their strongest or purest forms or strengthen them beyond their normal hardness.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) 9 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Create Alchana|Create Alchana]], ability to cast 4th-level spells.

**Benefit**: You have advanced your knowledge of Alchana and can create Greater [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Temperant Baths|Temperant Baths]] or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Emerald Tablet|Emerald Tablets]]. The base price to create these baths takes the item’s caster level, adds its creation DC, and then multiplies the total by 1500 gp. To create such substances, the creator must expend 4% (1/25) of the item’s base price in XP and use up raw materials costing 30% of the base price. (The savings alchemy or herbalism give other item creation feats, in [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemical and Herbal Items#Related Feats & Skill Adjustments|Related Feats & Skill Adjustments]], are already part of this feat.)

**Special**: This feat can also be used to repair and restore [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment General|masterwork]] items, provided all the component pieces of the item are together and can be reassembled before placing the item in the temperant bath.

This feat is part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|alchemy and herbalism]].
