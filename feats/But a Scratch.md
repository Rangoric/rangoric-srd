# But a Scratch

Your bravado convinces your enemies that their deadliest attacks are mere trifles, making you seem nigh invulnerable.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 4 ranks.

**Benefit**: When an opponent confirms a critical hit against you with a melee weapon, you can make a Deception check against it as an immediate action, with the same DC as demoralizing it with [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]]. If you succeed, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round, as if demoralized; beating the DC by more doesn’t lengthen this. If you fail, you can still try against opponents who saw you fail, but at a cumulative –2 penalty on those checks against them for 24 hours.
