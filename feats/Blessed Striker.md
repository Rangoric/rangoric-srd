# Blessed Striker

You attacks are augmented by the strength of your and your deity's shared alignment.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +11, ability to cast divine spells, alignment within one step of your deity’s.

**Benefit**: All your attacks count as having the alignments you share with your deity for overcoming damage reduction.

**Special**: If you grossly violate your deity’s code of conduct or stop worshiping your deity, you lose this feat’s benefit until you atone (see the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Atonement|atonement]]* spell).
