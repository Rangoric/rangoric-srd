# Nature's Aspect

You are able to gain Aspects of Nature after meditation in a natural setting.

**Benefit**: You can take any Aspect of Nature whose prerequisites you meet (see [[rangoric-srd/races/aspects-of-nature/Aspects of Nature|Aspects of Nature]]). Like an enchantment of the flesh, this process requires the expenditure of experience points.

**Special**: This feat is sometimes granted to non-fey races and fey creatures during an Aspect of Nature ceremony. The creature honored during the ceremony need not wait until it has an available feat slot to make use of this feat.
