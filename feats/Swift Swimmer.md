# Swift Swimmer

You are a faster swimmer than other members of your race.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Lizardfolk|Lizardfolk]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|swim speed]].

**Benefit**: Your swim speed increases by 15 feet.
