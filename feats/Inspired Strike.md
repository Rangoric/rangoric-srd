# Inspired Strike

You can turn your natural talent as easily to weapon-work as to skills.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-combat|Studied combat]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you damage a creature by using the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-combat|studied combat]] class feature, you can expend one use of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|inspiration]] to roll an inspiration die and increase the damage by the number rolled.
