# Agile Tongue

Your long pink tongue is capable of manipulating small items and even stealing objects.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Grippli|Grippli]].

**Benefit**: You have a prehensile tongue with a range of 10 feet. You can pick up items weighing no more than 5 pounds, make [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks, perform the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] combat maneuvers, or make melee touch attacks with your tongue.
