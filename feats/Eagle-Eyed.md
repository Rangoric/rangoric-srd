# Eagle-Eyed

Your distance vision is exceptionally keen, enabling you to see well in both normal and dim lighting conditions and providing you an advantage when making ranged attacks.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: The distance modifier to the DC of your sight-based Perception checks drops to +1 per 50 feet in bright or normal light, or +1 per 20 feet in dim light. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus on ranged attacks against targets more than 100 feet away.

**Special**: This overlaps [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception#Unlocks|Perception]]’s unlocks, which lower the distance modifier to +1 per 30 feet at 10 ranks. Use whichever gives the lower modifier.
