# Cartogramancer

You combine your knowledge of geography with the art of teleportation.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] 10 ranks, ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]*.

**Benefit**: If you cast *greater teleport* (or a similar teleportation effect that needs a reliable description of the place) without a reliable description of the destination, you can attempt a DC 25 Society check before casting to gain a reliable description of some place within 100 miles of it. If you beat the DC by 10, the place is within 50 miles; by 20, within 25 miles. Once you have tried this for a place, you can’t try again, and trying for somewhere else in the same general area gives the same result. For example, if you don’t know where the villain’s castle is, you also don’t know a place within 100 miles of the castle’s stables, or of the town half a mile away.
