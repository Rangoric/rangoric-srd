# Natural Poison Antitoxin

You have learned how to create antitoxin that can resist natural poisons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Natural Poison Harvester|Natural Poison Harvester]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) 8 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 8 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you succeed at a Craft (alchemy) check to make antitoxin from a dose of natural poison you harvested, the antitoxin lasts 4 hours, plus 1 hour for every 5 by which you beat the DC.
