# Extra Investigator Talent

You learn a new way to use your training and inspiration.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|Investigator talent]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-investigator-talent|investigator talent]]. You must meet the prerequisites for this investigator talent.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, you gain another investigator talent.
