# Focusing Blow

You and your allies work together to shake off mental effects.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hobgoblin Discipline|Hobgoblin Discipline]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]].

**Benefit**: An ally who also has this feat can deal damage to you in order to break an ongoing mind-affecting effect that attacks a defense. The ally must cause at least 5 points of damage to you with an attack, spell, or other ability. The effect then attacks your defense again, with a –1 penalty for every 5 additional points of damage the attack caused. If it misses, the mind-affecting effect ends. Only damage actually dealt counts for purposes of this feat; nonlethal damage and damage reduced or eliminated by [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]], resistances, and so on does not qualify.
