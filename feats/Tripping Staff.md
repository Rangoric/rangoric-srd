# Tripping Staff

You can make a trip attack with your quarterstaff.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (quarterstaff), base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You can make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks with a quarterstaff, as with other weapons that can trip (see [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons|Weapons]]).
