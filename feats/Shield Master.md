# Shield Master

Your mastery of the shield allows you to fight with it without hindrance.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Shield Bash|Improved Shield Bash]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Proficiency|Shield Proficiency]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Slam|Shield Slam]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: You take no penalties on attack rolls made with a shield while you are wielding another weapon. Add your shield’s enhancement bonus to attack and damage rolls made with the shield as if it were a weapon enhancement bonus.
