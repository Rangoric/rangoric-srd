# In Harm's Way

You put yourself in danger’s way to save your allies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bodyguard|Bodyguard]].

**Benefit**: While using aid another to improve an adjacent ally’s AC, you can intercept a successful attack against that ally as an immediate action, taking all the damage and any other effects of the attack (bleed, poison and so on) yourself. A creature can’t benefit from this feat more than once per attack.
