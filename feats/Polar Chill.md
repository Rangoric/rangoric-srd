# Polar Chill

You can call forth the cold of the arctic regions, making movement and fighting difficult for the unprepared.

**Prerequisite:** Exposure to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cone of Cold|cone of cold]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Ice Storm|ice storm]] spell.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can make the ground icy in a 20-foot-radius spread around you. Each square in that area becomes covered with ice, so it takes two squares of movement to enter each square, and the DC of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks there increases by 5. A DC 10 Acrobatics check is required to run or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] across the ice.

The ice remains for 1 minute or until exposed to fire.

You can't use this feat if you aren't touching the ground, and it doesn't work if the air temperature is above 100 degrees.
