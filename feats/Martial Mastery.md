# Martial Mastery

You broaden your study of weapons to encompass multiple similar weapons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Martial Versatility|Martial Versatility]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]] level 16th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]].

**Benefit**: Each combat feat you have that applies to a specific weapon (e.g., [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]]) can be used with all weapons in the same [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Groups|weapon group]].
