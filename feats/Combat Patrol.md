# Combat Patrol

You are a whirlwind, threatening a wide area around you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], base attack bonus +5.

**Benefit**: As a full-round action, you can set up a combat patrol: your threatened area grows by 5 feet for every 5 points of your base attack bonus. Until the start of your next turn, you can make attacks of opportunity against any opponent in that area that provokes them, and you can move as part of these attacks, as long as your total movement before your next turn is no more than your speed. Your movement provokes attacks of opportunity as normal.
