# Greater Drow Nobility

You have mastered the lesser spell-like abilities of the drow, demonstrating true nobility.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Drow Nobility|Drow Nobility]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Drow Nobility|Improved Drow Nobility]], able to use [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]] spell-like abilities, [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]].

**Benefit**: Your *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]* spell-like ability is now constant. You may use your *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Deeper Darkness|deeper darkness]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Levitate|levitate]]* spell-like abilities at will.
