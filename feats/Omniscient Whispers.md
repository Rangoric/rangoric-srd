# Omniscient Whispers

A constant, barely audible muttering echoes in your ears, usually beyond your comprehension. But if you focus all your energy on listening, you sometimes catch a sentence or two that bears directly on your current situation.

**Prerequisite:** Exposure to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Contact Other Plane|contact other plane]] spell.

**Benefit:** Once per week, you can tune into the voices you hear, getting the answer to a question much as if you had asked it with a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune|commune]] spell.

Using this feat renders you [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].
