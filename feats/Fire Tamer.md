# Fire Tamer

You know your way around even magical fire.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on defenses against spells with the fire descriptor. Additionally, your scars mark you as a talented fire tamer, granting you a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks when dealing with other goblins.
