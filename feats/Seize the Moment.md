# Seize the Moment

You and your allies are poised to pounce whenever one of you scores a telling blow.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]].

**Benefit**: When an ally who also has this feat confirms a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] against an opponent that you also threaten, you can make an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] against that opponent.
