# Kirin Style

Your study and your grace allows you to exploit your enemies' weaknesses.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: While using this style, you can spend a swift action to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|identify]] a single creature with a check of the right skill (DC 15 + the creature’s CR, for this feat). If you succeed, while you keep using this style you gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against that creature’s attacks and a +2 dodge bonus to AC against its attacks of opportunity. If you stop fighting the creature and fight it again later, you can try again.
