# Fetid Breath

The rotting of your flesh has spread to your lungs and fills them with putrid fluids.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Corrupted Flesh|Corrupted Flesh]], 6 HD, [[rangoric-srd/races/Ogre|ogre]].

**Benefit**: You can exhale a 30-foot cone of vile-smelling, moist air mixed with phlegm and remnants of old meals. Creatures in the area must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Constitution modifier) or they are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d6 minutes. Affected creatures that spend a full-round action gasping for fresh air and scraping off your oral debris may attempt another save to overcome the effect. You can use this [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Breath Weapon|breath weapon]] twice per day, plus one additional time per day for every 6 HD you possess beyond 6.
