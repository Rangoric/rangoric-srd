# Greater Power Penetration

Your powers are especially potent at breaking through power resistance.  

Prerequisite: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Penetration|Power Penetration]].

Benefit: To use this feat, you must expend your psionic focus. You get a +8 bonus on manifester level checks to overcome a creature’s power resistance. This bonus overlaps with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Penetration|Power Penetration]].
