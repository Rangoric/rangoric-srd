# Boar Style

Your sharp teeth and nails rip your foes open.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can deal bludgeoning or slashing damage with your unarmed strikes; changing the type is a free action. While using this style, once per round when you hit a single foe with two or more unarmed strikes, you can tear its flesh, dealing 2d6 [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage with the attack.
