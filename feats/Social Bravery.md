# Social Bravery

Even the most stressful social situations don’t daunt you, and you weather them as easily as a battle with a dragon.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]] class feature.

**Benefit**: In social situations, your bravado protects you and makes you harder to read. Your bravery bonus is added to the DCs of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks to demoralize you, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] against you, and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks to change your attitude or convince you to perform a request (this also applies against [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cutting Humiliation|Cutting Humiliation]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intoxicating Flattery|Intoxicating Flattery]]). Additionally, you gain a morale bonus equal to your bravery bonus on Deception and Intimidation checks.
