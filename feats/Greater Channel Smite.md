# Greater Channel Smite

You empower your weapon with the might of your deity, which you discharge as you strike your foes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel energy]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Channel Smite|Channel Smite]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: Before making any melee attacks on your turn, you can spend one use of channel energy as a swift action. Its dice form a pool of extra damage dice you can use against creatures harmed by the energy you channel (undead for positive energy, living creatures for negative). Before each melee attack, assign dice from the pool as extra damage if it hits. The target can make a Will save, as normal, to halve the extra damage, and it isn’t multiplied on a critical hit. If you miss, the dice stay in the pool, but any left at the end of your turn are lost.
