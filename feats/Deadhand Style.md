# Deadhand Style

Your study of death allow you to exploit your foes' fears.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 4 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]] class feature, nongood alignment.

**Benefit**: While using this style with at least 1 point in your ki pool, you gain a +2 bonus on defenses against fear effects, and the DC of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks against you increases by 4. As a swift action, you can spend 1 point from your ki pool to empower your unarmed strikes; they attack the Will of creatures you hit with them, their attack bonus 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier, and on a hit the creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.
