# Twin Thunders Master

With thunderous simultaneous strikes, you can batter a mighty giant into submission.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]] or [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]]; [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^defensive-training|defensive training]] racial trait; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Two-Weapon Fighting|Improved Two-Weapon Fighting]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-flurry-of-blows|flurry of blows]] class feature; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Twin Thunders|Twin Thunders]]; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Twin Thunders Flurry|Twin Thunders Flurry]]; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with both wielded weapons; base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: Whenever you deal an opponent extra damage with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Twin Thunders|Twin Thunders]] feat, that opponent is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round. You also force that opponent to succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC 10 + half your level + your Str modifier) or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. If you use this feat to render staggered an opponent that is already staggered, you [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|daze]] that opponent instead. In a similar way, you can [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stun]] an opponent that is already dazed.
