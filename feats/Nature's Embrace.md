# Nature's Embrace

Being in natural surroundings aids your natural healing.

**Prerequisites**: Base Will save +3, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: You heal at twice the normal rate while in a wild, undeveloped natural setting.
