# Elven Accuracy

Your sharp eyesight makes difficult shots easier.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]].

**Benefit**: If you miss due to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] when making a ranged attack with a longbow or shortbow (including composite bows), you can reroll your miss chance roll one time to see if you actually hit.
