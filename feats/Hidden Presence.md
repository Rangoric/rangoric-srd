# Hidden Presence

You are adept at hiding within your host.

**Prerequisites**: Int or Cha 17, ability to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession|possession]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Possession, Greater|greater possession]]_.

**Benefit**: While [[rangoric-srd/rules/Possession (Rules)|possessing]] another creature, you can hide yourself from divinations and pass through wards such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]_ or the effects of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Forbiddance|forbiddance]]_ spell. The spell attacks your Will at its attack bonus (even if it doesn’t normally attack a defense). If it misses, the spell treats you as if you were your host (for instance, divinations would reveal only the host’s alignment, and _forbiddance_ keyed to the host’s alignment wouldn’t damage you). If it hits, the spell functions against you as normal; if the spell normally attacks a defense, this first hit doesn’t count as a hit against the spell’s normal effects.
