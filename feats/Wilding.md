# Wilding

You were touched by nature at an early age and share a kinship with wild creatures. Your body might bear animal features, such as bestial ears or a tail, or your presence may be subtly unlike that of others.

**Prerequisites**: Any neutral alignment. You can take this feat only at 1st level.

**Benefit**: You gain the druid's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-empathy|wild empathy]] ability, using your character level as your druid level. If you have levels in a class that grants wild empathy, you instead gain a +3 bonus on wild empathy checks. You are treated as an animal for harmful mind-affecting effects that target animals, such as [[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Animal|charm animal]].
