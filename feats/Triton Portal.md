# Triton Portal

You can channel your inner magic to summon allies.

**Prerequisites**: Character level 5th, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|hydraulic push]]_ spell-like ability, [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|undine]].

**Benefit**: Once per day, you may expend your racial _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hydraulic Push|hydraulic push]]_ ability to instead cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally III|summon nature’s ally III]]_ as a spell-like ability with a caster level equal to your character level. This use of the ability can only summon a Small [[rangoric-srd/monsters/elemental/Water Elemental|water elemental]] or 1d3 dolphins. Using this ability is a full-round action.
