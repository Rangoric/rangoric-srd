# Kirin Path

You turn knowledge of your enemy into a flawless defense.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Kirin Strike|Kirin Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Kirin Style|Kirin Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 12 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can take 10 on checks to identify a creature, even when stress or distractions would normally prevent it. While using Kirin Style against a creature you identified with it, if the creature ends its turn in your threatened area, you can spend one of your attacks of opportunity that round to move up to 5 feet × your Intelligence modifier (minimum 5 feet). You must end this move in a square the creature threatens, and it doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity.
