# Improved Studied Combatant

Deep understanding of your foes’ tactics grants you the upper hand in combat.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Investigator|Amateur Investigator]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Studied Combatant|Studied Combatant]], base attack bonus +8, 1 rank in at least one [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] skill, no levels in a class that has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|inspiration]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Your bonuses for Studied Combatant increase to a +4 insight bonus on melee attack rolls and a +4 bonus on damage rolls. This feat otherwise works like the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Studied Combatant|Studied Combatant]] feat.
