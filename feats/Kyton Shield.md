# Kyton Shield

Whether in your hand or draped around your body, the spiked chain serves as both weapon and shield.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] (spiked chain), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Kyton Style|Kyton Style]], base attack bonus +5, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While using Kyton Style and wielding a spiked chain, you gain a +1 shield bonus to AC. If the spiked chain is magic, add half its enhancement bonus to this shield bonus; if it has the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Vicious|vicious]] ability, add its full enhancement bonus instead.
