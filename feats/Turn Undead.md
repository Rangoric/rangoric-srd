# Turn Undead

Calling upon higher powers, you cause undead to flee from the might of your unleashed divine energy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|Channel positive energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can, as a standard action, use one of your uses of channel positive energy to cause all undead within 30 feet of you to flee, as if [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]]. The channel attacks the Will of each undead, its attack bonus 1/2 your effective cleric level + your Charisma modifier, and a miss negates the effect. Undead it hits flee for 1 minute. The channel attacks intelligent undead again each round, and a miss ends the effect. If you use channel energy in this way, it has no other effect (it does not heal or harm nearby creatures).

**Normal**: A character with channel energy can also spend a use of it on a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turning attempt]], which uses a turning check and turning damage rather than an attack against Will Defense.
