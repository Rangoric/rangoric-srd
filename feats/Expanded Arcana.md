# Expanded Arcana

Your research has revealed new spells.

**Prerequisites**: Caster level 1st, see Special.

**Benefit**: Add one spell from your class’s spell list to your spells known, on top of those you gain for your level. Or add two spells from your class’s list, as long as both are at least one level lower than the highest-level spell you can cast in that class. Once made, these choices can’t be changed.

**Special**: You can take this feat only if you have levels in a class that casts from a limited list of spells known, such as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard|bard]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerer]]. You can take it multiple times.
