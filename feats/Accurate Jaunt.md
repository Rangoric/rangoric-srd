# Accurate Jaunt

You have an instinctive sense of interplanar travel.

**Prerequisite:** Exposure to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]], or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]] spell.

**Benefit:** When you use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport|teleport]], you can roll the targeting roll twice, taking whichever result you prefer. When you use [[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]], you likewise make the roll to determine your destination twice, choosing the better (usually lower) result.
