# Touch of Serenity

With a single touch you can reduce the threat of even the most savage of foes.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 18, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: You must declare that you are using this feat before you make your attack roll (so a miss wastes the attempt). On a hit, the attack deals no damage and has no other effect or condition, but the target can’t cast spells or attack (including attacks of opportunity and attacks made as immediate actions) for 1 round unless it succeeds at a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier). You can attempt a Touch of Serenity once per day for every four character levels you have (but see Special), and no more than once per round.

**Special**: A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] can attempt a Touch of Serenity a number of times per day equal to her monk level, plus one more time for every four levels she has in other classes.
