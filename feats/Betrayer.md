# Betrayer

You can charm people into lowering their defenses, allowing you to ambush them more effectively.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation), base attack bonus +3.

**Benefit**: When you succeed at a Persuasion check to change a creature’s attitude, you can draw a weapon and make a single melee attack against it as an immediate action. If you made it friendly or better, it is flat-footed against this attack. If it survives, it takes a –2 penalty on its initiative check for this combat.

Once you attack a creature, its attitude becomes hostile.
