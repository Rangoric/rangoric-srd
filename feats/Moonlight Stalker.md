# Moonlight Stalker

You are adept at using shadows to conceal your attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 3 ranks, darkvision or low-light vision as a racial trait.

**Benefit**: While you have [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] from an opponent, you gain a +2 bonus on attack and damage rolls against it.
