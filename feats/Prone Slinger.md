# Prone Slinger

Your sideways sling release allows you to launch bullets and stones even while prone.

**Benefit**: While prone, you can make ranged attacks with a sling.

**Normal**: A crossbow is the only ranged weapon you can use while [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]].
