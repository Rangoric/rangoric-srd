# Shadow Strike

You accurately strike even those you cannot clearly see.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You can deal precision damage, such as sneak attack damage, against targets with [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] (but not total concealment).
