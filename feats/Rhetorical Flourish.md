# Rhetorical Flourish

You rapidly change topics and employ confusing rhetoric to distract people from your true intent.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation).

**Benefit**: When you use Persuasion to make a request or change a creature’s attitude, you can misdirect it with words: make a Deception check against it. If you succeed, you gain a +4 bonus on your next Persuasion check against it within the next minute. If you fail by 5 or more, you take a –2 penalty on that check instead.

Or you can use this feat to retry one failed Persuasion check against a creature made within the past minute, taking a –4 penalty on the Deception check. If you succeed, you retry the Persuasion check instead of gaining the bonus.
