# Djinni Spin

You can surround yourself with the power of the storm, spinning like a hurricane to unleash a violent blast of electrical energy.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, Wis 17, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Djinni Style|Djinni Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Djinni Spirit|Djinni Spirit]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +13 or monk level 11th.

**Benefit**: While using Djinni Style, as a standard action you can spend two Elemental Fist attempts to unleash a whirlwind of electrified air around you; it affects adjacent creatures. Creatures in it take your unarmed strike damage plus your Elemental Fist electricity damage, and are deafened for 1d4 rounds. A Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) halves the damage and avoids being deafened.
