# Piercing Grapple

You can use small but deadly weapons to restrain a foe, forcing it to choose between pain and capitulation.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 7 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you start a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]], you can draw a light or one-handed piercing weapon and stab your opponent with it. You take a –2 penalty on the check to start the grapple, but not the usual –4 for having a weapon in hand. If you succeed, you grapple your opponent as normal, and the weapon doesn’t count as being in your hand, for that penalty or for dealing damage while you maintain the grapple. If your opponent breaks the grapple, it takes the weapon’s base damage plus 1d4 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage. If you end the grapple yourself, you can choose not to deal the bleed damage, but your opponent still takes the base weapon damage. While you maintain this grapple, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Intimidation checks against your opponent.
