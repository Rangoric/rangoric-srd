# Mounted Skirmisher

You are adept at attacking from upon a swift moving steed.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 14 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Combat|Mounted Combat]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Trick Riding|Trick Riding]].

**Benefit**: If your mount moves its speed or less, you can still make a full attack.

**Normal**: If your mount moves more than 5 feet, you can only make a single attack.
