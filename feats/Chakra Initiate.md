# Chakra Initiate

You have an extra wellspring of kundalini energy that you can use to open your chakras.

**Prerequisite**: Ability to awaken the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Chakras#^chakras-root|root chakra]].

**Benefit**: You gain a pool that holds 3 points of serpent-fire ki, which you can use only to open or maintain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Chakras|chakras]]. These serpent-fire ki points are replenished at the same time you replenish the points in your regular ki pool.
