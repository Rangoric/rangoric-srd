# Ferocious Summons

Your summoned creatures gain your ferocity.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Augment Summoning|Augment Summoning]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (conjuration), [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]] or [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc|orc]].

**Benefit**: Creatures you summon gain the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Ferocity|ferocity]] ability.
