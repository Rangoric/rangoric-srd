# Precise Strike

You are skilled at striking where it counts, as long as an ally distracts your foe.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: Whenever you and an ally who also has this feat are [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] the same creature, you deal an additional 1d6 points of precision damage with each successful melee attack. This bonus damage stacks with other sources of precision damage, such as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]]. This bonus damage is not multiplied on a critical hit.
