# Teleport Tactician

You are adept at noticing when opponents attempt to teleport around you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disruptive|Disruptive]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spellbreaker|Spellbreaker]].

**Benefit**: Any creature that uses a teleportation effect to enter or leave a square you threaten provokes an attack of opportunity from you, even if it casts on the defensive or uses a supernatural ability.
