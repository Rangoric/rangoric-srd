# Dastardly Finish

You can take advantage of an enemy’s debilitated state to attempt a coup de grace.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|Sneak attack]] +5d6.

**Benefit**: You can deliver a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Helpless Defenders|coup de grace]] to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^cowering|cowering]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] targets.

**Normal**: You can deliver a coup de grace only to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] targets.
