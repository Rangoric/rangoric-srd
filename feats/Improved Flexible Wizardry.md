# Improved Flexible Wizardry

You possess an impressive flexibility with casting that other wizards lack.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Flexible Wizardry|Flexible Wizardry]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Mastery|Spell Mastery]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] level 8th.

**Benefit**: When you use Flexible Wizardry, you can flexibly prepare a number of spell slots equal to your Intelligence modifier + 4, and you can finalize a slot as a standard action instead of a full-round action.
