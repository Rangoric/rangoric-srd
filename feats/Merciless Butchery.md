# Merciless Butchery

Your talent for slaughter lets you dispatch helpless opponents before they can recover.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dastardly Finish|Dastardly Finish]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] +5d6, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied target]] class feature.

**Benefit**: As a swift action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity, you can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Helpless Defenders|coup de grace]] against a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^cowering|cowering]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] opponent that you have designated as your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Slayer#^slayer-studied-target|studied target]].
