# Blinding Critical

Your critical hits blind your opponents.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], base attack bonus +15.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]], your opponent is permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]]. The blindness attacks the opponent’s Fortitude, its attack bonus your base attack bonus; on a miss, the opponent is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1d4 rounds. This feat has no effect on creatures that do not rely on eyes for sight or creatures with more than two eyes (although multiple critical hits might cause blindness, at the GM’s discretion). Blindness can be cured by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Blindness-Deafness|remove blindness/deafness]]_, or similar abilities.
