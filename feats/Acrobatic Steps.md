# Acrobatic Steps

You can easily move over and through obstacles.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Moves|Nimble Moves]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you move, you can move through up to 15 feet of difficult terrain each round as if it were normal terrain. This stacks with Nimble Moves, for a total of 20 feet.
