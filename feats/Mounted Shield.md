# Mounted Shield

Your defensive tactics defend both you and your mount.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Combat|Mounted Combat]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Focus|Shield Focus]].

**Benefit**: You can add your base shield bonus (including the bonus from Shield Focus, but not enhancement bonuses) to your mount’s AC. You can also add it to your Animal Handling check to negate a hit on your mount with Mounted Combat.
