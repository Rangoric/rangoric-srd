# Owl Dive

Your martial training helps you fly and charge through others' spaces.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 17, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Owl Style|Owl Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Owl Swoop|Owl Swoop]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Stealth), base attack bonus +7 or monk level 5th, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: While using Owl Style, you can charge through other creatures’ spaces, though moving through an enemy’s space takes a successful Acrobatics check, as normal. If you fail such a check during a charge, your charge stops just before you enter that enemy’s space; if you threaten that enemy, you can make your charge attack against it.
