# Preferred Spell

You find it very easy to cast one particular spell.

**Prerequisites**: 5 ranks in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Heighten Spell|Heighten Spell]].

**Benefit**: Choose one spell you can cast. You can cast it spontaneously by giving up a prepared spell or spell slot of its level or higher. You can apply any metamagic feats you have to it when you do; this raises the minimum level of the spell or slot you give up, but not the casting time.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Its effects don’t stack. Each time, it applies to a different spell.
