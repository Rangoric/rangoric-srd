# Mantis Torment

Your knowledge of the mysteries of anatomy allows you to cause debilitating pain with a simple touch.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 9 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mantis Style|Mantis Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mantis Wisdom|Mantis Wisdom]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]].

**Benefit**: You gain one more Stunning Fist attempt per day. While using Mantis Style, you can make an unarmed attack that uses two daily Stunning Fist attempts. If it hits, the opponent must succeed at a save against your Stunning Fist or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] with crippling pain until the start of your next turn, and then [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]].
