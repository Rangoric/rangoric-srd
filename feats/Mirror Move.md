# Mirror Move

By tracking an opponent's movements, you can attempt to duplicate his techniques.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: If you are the only character threatening an opponent and it uses a combat feat (including taking an action that benefits from one, or gaining a bonus to AC, attack rolls, damage rolls or saves from one), you can take an immediate action to gain that feat’s benefit until the end of your next turn. You must meet all the feat’s prerequisites. If the feat has a daily limit, such as Stunning Fist, uses while you mirror it count against your own daily limit for it. You can use Mirror Move once per day for every 4 character levels you have.
