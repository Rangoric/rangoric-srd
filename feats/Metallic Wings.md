# Metallic Wings

Your wing feathers are made of gleaming metal.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Angelic Blood|Angelic Blood]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Angelic Flesh|Angelic Flesh]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Angel Wings|Angel Wings]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|aasimar]], character level 11th.

**Benefit**: You gain two wing attacks. These are secondary natural attacks that deal 1d4 points of slashing damage (or 1d3 if you are Small).
