# Shaitan Skin

You can manipulate acid to shield yourself and disable your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shaitan Style|Shaitan Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +11 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: You gain one more Elemental Fist attempt per day. While using Shaitan Style, you gain acid resistance equal to your base attack bonus, or your monk level plus any base attack bonus from other classes, whichever is higher. While you are denied your Dexterity bonus to AC, you are denied this resistance too. Creatures that take acid damage from your Elemental Fist must succeed at a Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round.
