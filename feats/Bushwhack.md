# Bushwhack

You can subdue an unsuspecting foe quickly.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: When a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] creature is unaware of your presence and you successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] it, you can attempt a second grapple combat maneuver check to pin the creature as a free action. If you have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Chokehold|Chokehold]] feat, you can instead use this second combat maneuver check to initiate a chokehold.
