# Sliding Dash

Your seemingly reckless advance becomes an agile strike at the last moment.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 10 ranks or acrobatic charge class feature, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: When [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charging]], instead of moving to the closest space from which you can attack your target, you can move to the space adjacent to your target and on the other side of it, as long as you move through the closest space from which you can attack the target and through the target’s space to get there. When you move through the target’s space, you must attempt an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check with a DC equal to 10 + your opponent’s [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]]. Success allows you to move through the target’s space without provoking an attack of opportunity from that target, and when you arrive in the destination space and make your attack, the target is considered [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]] for that attack (or the first attack if you have more than one attack on a charge). On a failed check, you instead provoke an attack of opportunity and complete the charge as normal. Whether or not you succeed at the Acrobatics check, you take a –4 penalty to your AC until the start of your next turn, instead of the charge’s usual penalty.

**Normal**: When charging, you must end the movement part of a charge in the closest space from which you can attack your target.
