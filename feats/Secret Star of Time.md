# Secret Star of Time

You know the Secret Star of Time, which stretches a moment long enough to shape a calling.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast 3rd-level arcane spells spontaneously.

**Benefit**: When you call a star or constellation spontaneously, you can apply one other metamagic feat to it and still call it as a standard action. With two other metamagic feats, it takes a full-round action.

**Normal**: A spontaneous caster who applies a [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#Metamagic Feats|metamagic feat]] takes a full-round action to cast the spell.
