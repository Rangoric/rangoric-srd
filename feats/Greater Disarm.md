# Greater Disarm

You can knock weapons far from an enemy’s grasp.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Disarm|Improved Disarm]], base attack bonus +6, Int 13.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to disarm a foe. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Disarm|Improved Disarm]]. When you disarm an opponent, the weapon lands 15 feet away from its wielder, in a random direction.

**Normal**: Disarmed weapons and gear land at the feet of the disarmed creature.
