# Rebuffing Reduction

Your damage reduction can turn the force of blows back on your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, damage reduction, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: Whenever an adjacent opponent fails to get through your damage reduction with a melee attack, you can spend an immediate action to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] that opponent. If you succeed, you can’t move with it.

**Normal**: A bull rush combat maneuver is a standard action.
