# Shadecall

You can create undead.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shadehand|Shadehand]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shadetongue|Shadetongue]], Cha 15.

**Benefit**: By focusing necromantic energy into a corpse, you can create undead. You must concentrate on the corpse to be animated for 5 uninterrupted rounds. At the end of those 5 rounds, you must make a Charisma check (DC 20) to create a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Zombie|zombie]] or a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeleton/Skeleton|skeleton]], or a Charisma check (DC 25) to create a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/shadow/Shadow|shadow]]. The undead creature created by this ability rises at the end of the round in which the successful check was made, under the control of its creator. Undead created by this ability respond to vocal commands, and remain under their creator’s control indefinitely, until they are released. Regardless of the type of undead, you cannot create an undead creature with more HD than you have character levels with this ability.

You can maintain control over a maximum of 1/2 HD worth of undead creatures per character level. If you exceed this number, the newest creatures fall under your control, and excess undead you have had under your control become uncontrolled. You may choose which of the previous creatures are released. You may attempt to rebuke or command uncontrolled creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shadetongue|Shadetongue]] feat or with any other ability you have to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Evil Clerics and Undead|rebuke or command]] undead. Undead controlled with the Shadetongue feat or other rebuking abilities do not count toward this limit.

This requires a mostly intact corpse or skeleton and otherwise works as the spell *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animate Dead|animate dead]]*. Abilities granted by this feat are spell-like abilities. Using this feat to create a zombie or a skeleton causes 10 points of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]], while creating a shadow causes 15 points of nonlethal damage.

**Special**: It’s a [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Shadetouched Feats|shadetouched feat]]: it has uses per day and costs hit points as [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Shadetouched Feats|the Path of Shades]] says. [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadeseer|Shadeseers]] gain an [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadeseer#^shadeseer-enhanced-shadetouched-feats|enhanced version]] of this feat free at 5th level. Shadeseers may take this feat earlier, whenever they may select a feat, but they do not receive a bonus feat at 5th level to compensate.
