# Dazzling Display

Your skill with your favored weapon can frighten enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], proficiency with the selected weapon.

**Benefit**: While wielding the weapon in which you have Weapon Focus, you can perform a bewildering show of prowess as a full-round action. Make an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to demoralize all foes within 30 feet who can see your display.
