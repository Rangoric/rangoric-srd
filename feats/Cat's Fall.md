# Cat's Fall

Much like a cat, you can instantly shift your balance when you fall and roll with the impact, avoiding serious injury and landing on your feet.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: When you succeed at the DC 15 Acrobatics check to treat a fall as shorter, treat it as 20 feet shorter, and the damage from the next 10 feet of the fall is nonlethal. You land on your feet as long as you take less than 20 points of damage from the fall.

**Normal**: The DC 15 check treats the fall as 10 feet shorter, and you fall prone if you take any falling damage.

**Special**: This overlaps the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Tumbling|Acrobatics]] DC 30 check, which also treats a fall as 20 feet shorter. This feat gets there with the DC 15 check.
