# Sap Adept

You know just where to hit to knock the sense out of your foe.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|Sneak attack]] +1d6.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use a bludgeoning weapon to deal [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal]] sneak attack damage, you gain a bonus on your damage roll equal to twice the number of sneak attack damage dice you rolled.
