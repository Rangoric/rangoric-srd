# Go Unnoticed

Your small size lets you quickly duck out of sight.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: During the first round of combat, flat-footed opponents count as not having noticed you yet for [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]], so you can make a Stealth check that round to hide from them.
