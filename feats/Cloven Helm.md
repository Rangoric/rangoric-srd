# Cloven Helm

Your helm turns aside lethal blows.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dented Helm|Dented Helm]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hard-Headed|Hard-Headed]], base attack bonus +11, [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]].

**Benefit**: When wearing a helmet, you add +1 to your AC against critical hit confirmation rolls; this benefit stacks with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dented Helm|Dented Helm]]. When you use Dented Helm to deflect a critical hit, you may apply all damage from the critical hit to your helmet before applying any damage to yourself. If you take no damage, any additional effects, such as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^critical-feats|critical feats]] or poison, are negated.
