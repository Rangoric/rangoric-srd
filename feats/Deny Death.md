# Deny Death

Your ki is so strong that it can deny death.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|Ki pool]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]].

**Benefit**: As long as you have 1 ki point in your ki pool, when you fail your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#^stabilizing|Constitution check to stabilize]], you do not lose 1 hit point. If you succeed at the check, you can spend 1 ki point to heal 1d6 hit points. If you roll a natural 20 on the check to stabilize, you can spend 1 ki point to heal 2d6 hit points of damage instead.
