# Betrayal Sense

You can sense betrayal and intrigue as well as physical traps.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] level 3rd, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-trap-sense|trap sense]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to pierce another creature’s disguise and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks to avoid being surprised by an attack. These bonuses are equal to the bonus you gain from trap sense, or from [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-trap-sense|danger sense]] if you have it instead.
