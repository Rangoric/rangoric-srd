# Arcane Armor Mastery

You have mastered the ability to cast spells while wearing armor.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Arcane Armor Training|Arcane Armor Training]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Armor Proficiency (Medium)|Armor Proficiency (Medium)]], caster level 7th.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, reduce the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Armor|arcane spell failure]] chance from the armor you are wearing by 20% for any spells you cast this round. This bonus replaces, and does not stack with, the bonus granted by [[rangoric-srd/feats/Arcane Armor Training|Arcane Armor Training]].
