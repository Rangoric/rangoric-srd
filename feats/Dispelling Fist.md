# Dispelling Fist

By focusing on your knowledge of magic and spells that negate its powers, you use your bare hands to rip magical defenses from your enemy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +11, ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]*, caster level 7th.

**Benefit**: If you have *dispel magic* prepared or can cast it spontaneously, you can cast it as a swift action after hitting an opponent with an unarmed strike. It is a targeted dispel against the opponent you hit.
