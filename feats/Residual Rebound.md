# Residual Rebound

Sometimes spells cast at you rebound on the caster instead.

**Prerequisite:** Exposure to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]] spell.

**Benefit:** If you roll a natural 20 on a save against a targeted spell, it turns back on the caster as if affected by a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]] spell. Unlike spell turning, however, the Residual Rebound feat potentially functions against touch range spells as well. Residual Rebound only works on targeted spells that allow a saving throw, so a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]] won't rebound, nor will a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Power Word Stun|power word stun]].
