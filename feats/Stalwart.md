# Stalwart

You adopt a defensive stance that allows you to absorb and redirect hits.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: While using the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]] action, fighting defensively or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], you can give up the dodge bonus to AC you would gain to gain the same amount of damage reduction instead (to a maximum of DR 5/—) until the start of your next turn. This damage reduction stacks with damage reduction from class features, such as the barbarian’s, but not with damage reduction from any other source. If you are denied your Dexterity bonus to AC, you are also denied this damage reduction.
