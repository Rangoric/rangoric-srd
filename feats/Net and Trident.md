# Net and Trident

Your skill with lighter weapons allows you to wield one alongside your net.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] (net), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Net Adept|Net Adept]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]].

**Benefit**: You can treat a net as a one-handed ranged weapon, so you can wield a light or one-handed melee weapon and still make ranged attacks with your net. When you use your light or one-handed melee weapon to attack an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] opponent, you gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls and on attack rolls to confirm a critical hit.

**Normal**: A net is a two-handed ranged weapon.
