# Shield of Swings

A wild frenzy of attacks serves to bolster your defenses.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When you make a full attack while wielding a two-handed weapon, you can halve your damage to gain a +4 shield bonus to AC and CMD until the start of your next turn. The damage stays halved until then.
