# Shocking Bellow

You can demoralize a foe with a sudden ferocious scream.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intimidating Prowess|Intimidating Prowess]].

**Benefit**: If you act in the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]], you can try to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] a creature within 30 feet that can clearly see or hear you, as a free action.
