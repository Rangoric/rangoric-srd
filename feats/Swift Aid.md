# Swift Aid

With a quick but harmless swipe, you can aid an ally's assault.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, you can use [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] to give an ally either a +1 bonus on its next attack roll or a +1 bonus to AC.

**Normal**: Aid another is a standard action.
