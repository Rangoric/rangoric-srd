# Aberrant Tumor

To the surprise of others, that strange growth on you is actually your spellcasting companion.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Aberrant Bloodline|Aberrant bloodline]].

**Benefit**: You gain a tumor familiar, as the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/discoveries/Tumor Familiar (Discovery)|tumor familiar]] alchemist discovery, with an effective alchemist level equal to the level of the class that grants your aberrant bloodline for determining the tumor familiar’s abilities. If multiple classes grant you the aberrant bloodline, those class levels stack for determining your effective alchemist level.
