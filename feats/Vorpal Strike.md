# Vorpal Strike

Prerequisites: Str 25, Wis 25, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]] (unarmed strike), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Keen Strike|Keen Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], ki strike (adamantine).  

Benefit: The character’s unarmed strike is considered to be a slashing vorpal weapon. (At the character’s option, any unarmed strike can do bludgeoning damage instead, but it loses the vorpal quality.) This ability doesn’t stack with similar abilities
