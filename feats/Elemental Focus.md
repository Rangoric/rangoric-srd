# Elemental Focus

Your spells of a certain element are more difficult to resist.

**Benefit**: Choose one energy type (acid, cold, electricity or fire). Add +1 to the DC of all saving throws against your spells that deal damage of that type.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Its effects don’t stack. Each time, it applies to a new energy type.
