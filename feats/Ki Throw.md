# Ki Throw

Your physical control and mastery of momentum allows you to throw enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: On a successful unarmed [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attack against a target your size or smaller, you may throw the target [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] in any square you threaten rather than its own square. This movement does not provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]], and you cannot throw the creature into a space occupied by other creatures.

**Special**: A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] may gain _Ki_ Throw as a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-bonus-feat|bonus feat]] at 10th level. A monk with this feat can affect creatures larger than his own size by spending 1 [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki* point]] per size category difference.
