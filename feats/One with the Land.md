# One with the Land

Your connection to your favored terrain is so intense that you draw strength from the power of nature itself.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-terrain|Favored terrain]] class feature.

**Benefit**: In your favored terrain, you require only half the normal amount of food, water, and sleep, and your rate of natural healing is doubled. You add half your favored terrain bonus as a bonus on saving throws and Constitution checks to stave off the effects of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Cold Dangers|cold exposure]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Heat Dangers|heat exposure]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Starvation and Thirst|starvation]], and thirst in your favored terrain.
