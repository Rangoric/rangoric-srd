# Outflank

You look for every edge when flanking an enemy.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: Whenever you and an ally who also has this feat are [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] the same creature, your flanking bonus on attack rolls increases to +4. In addition, whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] against the flanked creature, it provokes an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]] from your ally.
