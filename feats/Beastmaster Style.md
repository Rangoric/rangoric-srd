# Beastmaster Style

Your animal companion is your most treasured friend, and you steadfastly protect it from your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 1 rank, must have an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]].

**Benefit**: While using this style, when an attack is made against your animal companion while you are adjacent to both it and the attacker, you can make an Animal Handling check as an immediate action. If your result is higher than the attack roll, the hit is negated.

**Special**: You can’t use this style while mounted on your animal companion.
