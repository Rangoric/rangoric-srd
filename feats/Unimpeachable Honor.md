# Unimpeachable Honor

Your devotion to your principles and your allies makes acting against them unthinkable.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Iron Will|Iron Will]].

**Benefit**: Whenever a charm or compulsion effect would make you act against your fundamental nature, plainly break the strictures of your religion, or commit an act of an alignment directly opposed to yours (such as a chaotic or evil act if you are lawful good), you gain a +4 bonus on any saving throw or opposed Charisma check to resist the command or break free of the effect. Whenever you are compelled to attack an ally, you take a –4 penalty on your attack and damage rolls.
