# Stouthearted

You fight back your fear by voicing the song in your heart.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|Raging song]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While you are maintaining a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Skald#^skald-raging-song|raging song]], if a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] effect hits your defense, you can expend a round of raging song to make it reroll the attack as an immediate action. You must keep this second result, even if it is lower.
