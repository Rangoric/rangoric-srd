# Shapeshifter Foil

Your command of shapeshifting magic can disrupt similar effects in others.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 5 ranks, ability to use any polymorph effect.

**Benefit**: A creature you damage has trouble using or keeping polymorph effects until the end of your next turn: to use one, it must succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] (DC 15 + twice the effect’s level). If you damage an opponent under a polymorph effect, it must succeed at a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or return to its own form. If you score a critical hit against it, it gets no save.
