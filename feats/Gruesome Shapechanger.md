# Gruesome Shapechanger

When you change shape, it is a violent and bloody affair.

**Prerequisites**: Shapechanger subtype.

**Benefit**: When you change shape, your old form sloughs off in a spray of blood and viscera that congeals into a thick slime, dissolving within 24 hours. Until it dissolves or is cleaned away, the space where you changed (or the space below, if you were in the air) is slippery: it counts as difficult terrain and adds 5 to the DC of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks there. The change attacks the Fortitude of any foe who sees it, its attack bonus 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Con modifier; on a hit, the foe is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute.

**Special**: Changing this way is tiring: after you use your change shape ability, you must wait 1d4 rounds before using it again. Taking this feat changes how your shapechanging works; you can’t choose to avoid its effects.
