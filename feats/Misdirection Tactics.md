# Misdirection Tactics

You have learned to use deception and trickery to misdirect your opponent's weapon attack.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 4 ranks.

**Benefit**: While you are using the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]] action, if a melee attack would still hit your AC, you can make a Deception check as an immediate action with a DC equal to the attack roll. If you succeed, the attack misses. If it still hits, you can’t use this feat against that opponent for 24 hours.
