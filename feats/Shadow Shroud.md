# Shadow Shroud

Using a portion of your innate magic, you cloak your body in darkness to evade attacks.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]* as a racial spell-like ability.

**Benefit**: As an immediate action, you can wrap yourself in a shifting haze of darkness that gives you concealment against one attack. The haze fades once the attack is resolved. You can do this once per day, and one more time per day when your caster level for the racial darkness ability reaches 5th, 10th, 15th and 20th.
