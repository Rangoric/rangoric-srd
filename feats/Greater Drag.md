# Greater Drag

When you drag foes, they are thrown off balance.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Drag|Improved Drag]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to drag a foe. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Drag|Improved Drag]]. Whenever you drag a foe, its movement provokes attacks of opportunity from all your allies (but not you).

**Normal**: Creatures moved by drag don’t provoke attacks of opportunity.
