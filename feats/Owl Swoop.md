# Owl Swoop

Your martial training helps you move with acrobatic poise.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Owl Style|Owl Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Stealth), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: While using Owl Style, you can use your base attack bonus in place of your ranks in Acrobatics to work out your Acrobatics modifier. You can make Acrobatics checks to cross narrow or slick surfaces and to move past foes without provoking attacks of opportunity while charging, taking the same penalties and increased DCs as for moving at full speed.
