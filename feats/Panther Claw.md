# Panther Claw

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Panther Style|Panther Style]].

**Benefit**: While using Panther Style, a retaliatory unarmed strike is a free action instead of a swift action. You can make a number of retaliatory unarmed strikes on your turn equal to your Wisdom modifier.
