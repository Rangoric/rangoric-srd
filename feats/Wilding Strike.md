# Wilding Strike

Filled with the might of nature, you need no weapons of steel or wood. Your fists and feet are as potent as any creation of the forge.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wilding|Wilding]].

**Benefit**: The damage die of your unarmed strikes increases to 1d6 (or 1d4 if you are Small). This doesn’t stack with other effects that increase the damage die of your unarmed strikes, including class levels such as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|monk]]’s. Increases to your actual size category (such as from *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*) still increase the damage die as normal.
