# Coordinated Charge

You are an expert at leading your allies into the fray.

**Prerequisites**: You have at least two other [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^teamwork-feats|teamwork feats]], base attack bonus +10.

**Benefit**: When an ally with this feat charges a creature that is no further away from you than your speed, you can, as an immediate action, [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] that creature. You must be able to follow all of the normal charge rules.
