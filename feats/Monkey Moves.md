# Monkey Moves

You scramble around your foes, moving and striking in an erratic fashion.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Monkey Style|Monkey Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 8 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 8 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using Monkey Style, you add your Wisdom bonus to Athletics checks to climb. You can climb and crawl at half your speed, take a 5-foot step by jumping, crawling or climbing, and keep your Dexterity bonus to AC while climbing. When you hit an opponent with your unarmed strike twice or more on your turn, you can spend a swift action to take a 5-foot step even if you have moved this round.

**Normal**: You climb at one-quarter your speed and lose your Dexterity bonus to AC while climbing. A 5-foot step uses your normal movement, and you can take one only if you haven’t otherwise moved this round.

**Special**: Keeping your Dexterity bonus while climbing overlaps [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Unlocks|Athletics]]’ 5-rank unlock, which you already have for this feat’s prerequisites.
