# Sign of the Crown

The Crown stood over your birth, and you carry yourself like a ruler.

**Benefit**: Once per day, you can make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] check as a free action to overawe a creature. If it fails a Will save with a DC equal to your check result, you gain a +10 bonus on your next Persuasion, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check against it.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
