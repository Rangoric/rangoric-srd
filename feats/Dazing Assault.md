# Dazing Assault

You can daze foes with wild attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: You can choose to take a –5 penalty on all melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|daze]] opponents you hit with your melee attacks for 1 round, in addition to the normal damage the attack deals. The daze attacks their Fortitude, its attack bonus your base attack bonus, and a miss negates the effect. You must choose to use this feat before making the attack roll, and its effects last until your next turn.
