# Sea Hunter

Your blows knock swimming opponents off balance.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Merfolk|merfolk]].

**Benefit**: When you make a successful melee attack against a swimming target, as a free action you can attempt to knock the target off balance. Treat this as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver. If you succeed, the target is considered off balance until it recovers its balance, usually by making an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check to swim on its turn. This feat has no effect on creatures with a swim speed, those using magic such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]*, and creatures that can’t be tripped.
