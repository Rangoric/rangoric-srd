# Gluttonous Gobbler

Some ogres are able to quickly choke down even the largest of meals so they can get on with other business.

**Prerequisites**: Both [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]] or the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Grab|grab]] ability, [[rangoric-srd/races/Ogre|ogre]], size Large or larger.

**Benefit**: If you start your turn [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grappling]] another creature at least two size categories smaller than you, you can put the grappled creature in your mouth as a move action and attempt another grapple combat maneuver check (as though attempting to pin the opponent). If you succeed, you swallow the opponent as if you had the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Swallow Whole|swallow whole]] special ability.
