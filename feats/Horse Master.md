# Horse Master

You blend horsemanship skills from disparate traditions into a seamless mounted combat technique.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-expert-trainer|Expert trainer]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: Use your character level to determine your effective druid level for determining the powers and abilities of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-mount|mount]].

**Normal**: You use your cavalier level to determine your effective druid level for determining the powers and abilities of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cavalier#^cavalier-mount|mount]].
