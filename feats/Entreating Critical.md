# Entreating Critical

Your critical hit allows you a chance to make your foes briefly see reason.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Call Truce|Call Truce]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation), base attack bonus +11, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: Whenever you confirm a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]], the shock of the attack momentarily opens an opportunity to end hostilities with a quick entreaty. As an immediate action immediately after confirming the critical hit, you can attempt a Persuasion check to improve the target’s attitude as though you had spent 1 full round using the Call Truce feat. All other conditions and limitations of the Call Truce feat apply.
