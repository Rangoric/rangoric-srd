# Draconic Paragon

You can shrug off sleep and paralysis effects as well as any dragon, and your draconic aspects improve.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Aspect|Draconic Aspect]], either [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Breath|Draconic Breath]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Glide|Draconic Glide]], character level 10th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]].

**Benefit**: You gain an additional +2 bonus against sleep and paralysis effects. You can use your breath weapon twice per day and your breath weapon damage increases to 4d6 points of energy damage. Your wings from the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Glide|Draconic Glide]] feat grow stronger, granting you a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|fly speed]] of 20 feet (average maneuverability).
