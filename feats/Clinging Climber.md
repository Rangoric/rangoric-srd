# Clinging Climber

Using leverage and pure brawn, you can cling to a cliff face, ladder, or rope with your legs to free both of your hands to take other actions.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: As a move action while climbing, you can cling to the surface with your legs by succeeding at an Athletics check with the same DC as climbing it. If you succeed, you can attack with a two-handed ranged weapon and reload ranged weapons until you start climbing again. The GM can rule that this doesn’t work on some surfaces.
