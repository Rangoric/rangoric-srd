# Gory Finish

By drawing upon wells of savagery, you can slay your foe in creative and horrifyingly gruesome manners, intimidating nearby foes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]].

**Benefit**: When you use the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Attack|attack action]], you can make a single attack at your highest base attack bonus with a weapon in which you have Weapon Focus. If you reduce your target to negative hit points, you can spend a swift action to make an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to demoralize all foes within 30 feet who saw the attack.
