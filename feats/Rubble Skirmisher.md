# Rubble Skirmisher

You use difficult terrain to your advantage when performing combat maneuvers.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Moves|Nimble Moves]], base attack bonus +2.

**Benefit**: When you are in a square of naturally occurring difficult terrain (bushes, rubble, undergrowth and so on), you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on combat maneuver checks and to your CMD.
