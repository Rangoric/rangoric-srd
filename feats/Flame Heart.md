# Flame Heart

You have mastered fire magic and alchemy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fire Tamer|Fire Tamer]], character level 5th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|goblin]].

**Benefit**: You gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|fire resistance]] 5. When casting spells with the fire descriptor or throwing alchemist [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bombs]] that deal fire damage, treat your caster level or alchemist level as if you were 1 level higher.
