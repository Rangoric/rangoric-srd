# Sin Seer

Unlike others who have taken the oath against undeath, your preoccupation with the undead does not cloud your view of good and evil.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/paladin/Oathbound Paladin (Archetype)#Oath against Undeath|Detect undead]] paladin class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-detect-evil|detect evil]] class feature. You may use it or the detect undead class feature, but not at the same time.
