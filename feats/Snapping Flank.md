# Snapping Flank

You can make a swift bite attack against a flanked foe.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +9, bite attack.

**Benefit**: Whenever you are [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] with an ally who also has this feat, as a swift action you can make a bite attack against the opponent you and your ally are flanking.
