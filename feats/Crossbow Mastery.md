# Crossbow Mastery

You can load crossbows with blinding speed and even fire them in melee with little fear of reprisal.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Reload|Rapid Reload]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Shot|Rapid Shot]].

**Benefit**: Reloading any crossbow is a free action for you, and you can fire a crossbow as many times in a full attack as you could attack with a bow. Reloading the type of crossbow you chose for Rapid Reload no longer provokes attacks of opportunity.
