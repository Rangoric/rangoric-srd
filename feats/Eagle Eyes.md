# Eagle Eyes

Your vision is especially keen.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, keen senses racial trait ([[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]], or [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]]).

**Benefit**: You ignore up to –5 in penalties due to distance on visual [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, allowing you to see accurately at much greater distances than most.
