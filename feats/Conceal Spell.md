# Conceal Spell

You can hide the evidence of spells you cast.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: When you cast a spell or use a spell-like ability, you can try to hide its verbal and somatic components among other speech and gestures, and hide the sight of casting, so others don’t realize you are casting until it’s too late. Hiding it slows your casting: a standard-action spell takes a full-round action, and a longer spell takes twice as long (swift-action spells still take a swift action). To see through it, a creature must succeed at a Perception, Sense Motive or Magic skill check (it rolls whichever has the highest bonus) against a DC of 15 + your ranks in Deception or Imitation (whichever is higher) + your Charisma modifier. It gains a bonus on the check equal to the spell’s level.

If the spell has a somatic component, any creature that can see you makes a Perception or Magic skill check (whichever is higher) against a DC of 15 + your ranks in Sleight of Hand + your Dexterity modifier, again with a bonus equal to the spell’s level. Since you hide the casting behind other actions, onlookers know you are doing something, even if they don’t know it’s a spell. A verbal component is still heard clearly, but the spell woven into it isn’t noticed. If an opponent fails its check, your casting doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity from it, and it can’t use readied actions that depend on noticing your spell, such as counterspelling. Spells with an obvious effect of their own, such as *fireball*, still have it, though who cast them might not be clear unless the effect comes from you.

If someone talks with you long enough to make a Sense Motive check without noticing your spells, they can use Sense Motive to get a hunch that you are acting oddly.
