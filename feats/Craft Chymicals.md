# Craft Chymicals

You can create chymicals, and miscellaneous special substances such as tanglefoot bags, tindertwigs, and various acids.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) 2 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Craft (alchemy)).

**Benefit**: Provided all item prerequisites are met, the alchemist can create any and all acids, chymicals, compounds, and miscellaneous alchemical substances not created by other Item Creation feats. Crafting, brewing, or mixing chymicals takes 1 day minimum, and any substances with creation DCs higher than 30 take 2 days. The base price of chymicals and acids equals their caster level x 25 gp. To create such substances, the creator must expend 2% (1/50) of the item’s base price in XP (round up) and use up raw materials costing 30% of the base price. (The savings alchemy or herbalism give other item creation feats, in [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemical and Herbal Items#Related Feats & Skill Adjustments|Related Feats & Skill Adjustments]], are already part of this feat.)

**Note**: The alchemical items of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services|Goods and Services]] are primarily chymical in nature, and can be created by this feat (or by the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) skill alone).

This feat is part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|alchemy and herbalism]].
