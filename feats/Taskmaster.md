# Taskmaster

You intimidate weaker allies into reckless ferocity.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Demoralizing Lash|Demoralizing Lash]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you spur an ally, who must have fewer Hit Dice than yourself, to reckless effort. For 1 minute, the ally gains a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, and Will saves against mind-affecting effects. However, the ally also takes a –2 penalty to AC and on skill checks. To affect an unwilling ally, you must succeed at an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check with the same DC used to demoralize your ally.

**Special**: If you have 10 or more ranks in Intimidation, the morale bonus increases to +2.
