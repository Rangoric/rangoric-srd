# Staggering Fist

When you engage in unarmed combat, you know just where to strike to stagger your foe.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +2.

**Benefit**: You must declare that you are using this feat before you make your attack roll (so a miss wastes the attempt). A foe damaged by your unarmed attack takes damage normally, and the blow also attacks its Fortitude, its attack bonus 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier; on a hit, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round (until just before your next turn). You can attempt Staggering Fist once per day for every four character levels you have, and no more than once per round. Constructs, incorporeal creatures, plants, undead and creatures immune to critical hits can’t be affected.
