# Human Spirit

Your blood burns with the passion and unyielding quest for self-improvement displayed so prominently by your human relatives.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-elf]].

**Benefit**: You receive 1 bonus [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^skill-points|skill rank]]. Whenever you gain another Hit Die, you gain an additional skill rank. You cannot gain more than four skill ranks in this way.

**Special**: You can only take this feat at 1st level. If you take this feat, you cannot take the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elven Spirit|Elven Spirit]] feat.
