# Ultimate Mercy

By using lay on hands, you can bring the dead back to life.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 19, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Mercy|Greater Mercy]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-mercy|mercy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can expend 10 uses of lay on hands to bring a single dead creature you touch back to life as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Raise Dead|raise dead]]* spell with a caster level equal to your paladin level. You must provide the material component for *raise dead* or choose to accept 1 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|temporary negative level]]; this level automatically goes away after 24 hours, never becomes a permanent negative level, and cannot be overcome in any way except by waiting for the duration to expire.
