# Beastmaster Ire

Seeing your animal companion attacked or hurt causes you to retaliate with a bestial fury of your own.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Perception), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Beastmaster Salvation|Beastmaster Salvation]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Beastmaster Style|Beastmaster Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 9 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you use Beastmaster Style’s benefit, you gain a +2 morale bonus on melee weapon attack rolls and a +4 morale bonus on weapon damage rolls against the attacker. If its attack dealt damage to your animal companion, these bonuses increase to +4 and +8. They last until the end of your next turn.
