# Saving Shield

You deflect attacks that could mean your ally's death.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Proficiency|Shield Proficiency]].

**Benefit**: Whenever an adjacent ally is the target of an attack, you can give it a +2 shield bonus to AC as an immediate action. You must be wielding a light, heavy or tower shield.
