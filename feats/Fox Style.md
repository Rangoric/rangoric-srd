# Fox Style

Your martial training helps you manipulate foes.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13.

**Benefit**: While using this style, you can use your base attack bonus in place of your ranks in Deception on Deception checks to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] in combat and to create a diversion to hide. If your Intelligence is 19 or higher, you also gain a +4 bonus on these checks.
