# Shield Slam

In the right position, your shield can be used to send opponents flying.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Shield Bash|Improved Shield Bash]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shield Proficiency|Shield Proficiency]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: Any opponent you hit with your shield bash is also hit with a free [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], using your attack roll as the combat maneuver check. This bull rush doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity. An opponent who can’t move back because of a wall or other surface is knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] after moving the most it can. You can move with your target if you are able to take a 5-foot step or to spend an action to move this turn.
