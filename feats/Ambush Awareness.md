# Ambush Awareness

You are always on your toes and are rarely caught off-guard for long, even when an enemy gets the jump on you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Perception).

**Benefit**: If you can’t act in the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]] because you failed a Perception check, you can still act on your initiative count in the surprise round, but only to take the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]] action.

**Normal**: If you can’t act in the surprise round because you failed a Perception check, you take no actions in it.
