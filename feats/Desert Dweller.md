# Desert Dweller

The endless sands and waterless wastes are your home, and neither the heat nor dehydration presents as lethal of a threat to you as it does to other travelers.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-terrain|Favored terrain]] (desert) class feature.

**Benefit**: You treat [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Heat Dangers|hot environments]] as though they were one step less severe; if you have a similar ability from another feat, such as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Torrid Tolerance|Torrid Tolerance]], the benefits stack and you treat hot conditions as if they were two steps less severe. You need to consume only half the normal amount of water for a creature of your size, and you gain a +4 bonus on Constitution checks to resist the effects of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Starvation and Thirst|thirst]]. You also gain a +4 bonus on saving throws and checks to avoid becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] by glare or being deceived by a desert mirage.
