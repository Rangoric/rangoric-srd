# Ferocious Resolve

Your orc heritage allows you to fight on.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc#^half-orc-orc-ferocity|orc ferocity]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: You gain the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Ferocity|ferocity]] ability, allowing you to continue fighting at negative hit points. When using this ability, you gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks.

**Normal**: A half-orc’s [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc#^half-orc-orc-ferocity|orc ferocity]] lets him fight on only briefly after he is brought below 0 hit points.
