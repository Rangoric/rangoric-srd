# Bouncing Spell

You can direct a failed spell against a different target.

**Benefit**: Whenever a bouncing spell that targets a single creature has no effect on its intended target (whether because of spell resistance or a miss against its defense), you can redirect it as a swift action to target another eligible creature within range. The redirected spell behaves in all ways as if its new target were the original target. A spell that affects its target in any way (including a lesser effect on a miss) can’t be redirected. A bouncing spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
