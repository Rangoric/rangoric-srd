# Extra Bombs

You can throw more bombs per day.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|Bomb]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can throw two additional [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bombs]] per day.

**Special**: You can gain Extra Bombs multiple times. Its effects stack.
