# Step Up and Strike

When a foe tries to move away, you follow and strike.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Following Step|Following Step]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Step Up|Step Up]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: When you use Step Up or Following Step to follow an adjacent foe, you can also make one melee attack against it at your highest base attack bonus. This attack counts as one of your attacks of opportunity for the round, and doesn’t count toward the actions you can normally take each round.

**Normal**: You can usually take only one standard action and one 5-foot step each round.
