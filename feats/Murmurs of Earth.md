# Murmurs of Earth

The earth opens up her secrets to your perceptions.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Echoes of Stone|Echoes of Stone]], character level 9th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|oread]].

**Benefit**: You gain a limited form of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Tremorsense|tremorsense]]. As a move action, you become aware of all creatures within 15 feet that are in contact with the ground at that moment.
