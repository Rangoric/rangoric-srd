# Twin Thunders Flurry

Your dual bludgeoning strikes are especially deadly when you are fighting giants.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]] or [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]]; [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^defensive-training|defensive training]] racial trait; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Two-Weapon Fighting|Improved Two-Weapon Fighting]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]], or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-flurry-of-blows|flurry of blows]] class feature; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Twin Thunders|Twin Thunders]]; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with both wielded weapons; base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] a creature with the giant subtype of up to Huge size, and you gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls against creatures of the giant subtype. Further, each time you hit a creature of the giant subtype with your off-hand weapon after you hit that creature with your primary weapon, you can deal the extra off-hand weapon damage [[rangoric-srd/feats/Twin Thunders|Twin Thunders]] grants you.
