# Psychic Adept

You have honed your mind to harness psychic power.

**Prerequisites**: Int 11, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]].

**Benefit**: You gain the ability to cast a 0-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-knacks|knack]] from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Psychic Spell List|psychic class’s spell list]]. You can cast this spell three times per day as a spell-like ability. The caster level for this ability is equal to your character level, and the save DC is equal to 10 + your Intelligence modifier.

**Special**: You can choose this feat multiple times, choosing a different knack each time.
