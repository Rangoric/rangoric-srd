# Fearless Curiosity

Your desire to see and experience the world overrides healthy caution.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on defenses against effects with the emotion descriptor. In addition, for any round in which you begin your turn affected by a fear effect, the fear effect attacks you again at the beginning of your turn, and a miss reduces its severity, from [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], frightened to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], and shaken to unaffected.
