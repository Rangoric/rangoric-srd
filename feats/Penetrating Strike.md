# Penetrating Strike

Your attacks are capable of penetrating the defenses of some creatures.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]], proficiency with the weapon, fighter level 12th, base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: Your attacks with weapons in which you have Weapon Focus ignore up to 5 points of damage reduction. This doesn’t apply to damage reduction without a type (such as DR 10/—).
