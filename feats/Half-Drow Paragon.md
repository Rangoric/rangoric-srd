# Half-Drow Paragon

Your drow blood is particularly strong.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Drow-Blooded|Drow-blooded]] and [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Drow Magic|drow magic]] racial traits, [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]].

**Benefit**: You count as a drow for any effects that relate to race. Furthermore, the spell-like abilities granted to you by your [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Drow Magic|drow magic]] racial trait count as drow spell-like abilities for the purposes of any feat prerequisites.
