# Live My Nightmare

Those who magically pry into your mind become privy to your most frightening dreams.

**Prerequisite:** Exposure to [[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantasmal Killer|phantasmal killer]] spell.

**Benefit:** Whenever someone successfully targets you with a divination spell or effect, you can send that caster a nightmarish vision. This vision functions as a [[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantasmal Killer|phantasmal killer]] spell, except that the form comes from your dreams, not the other creature's dreams. The nightmare attacks the other creature’s Will, its attack bonus 4 + your Cha modifier, and a miss disbelieves it; on a hit, it attacks the creature’s Fortitude with the same bonus, and on a hit the creature dies from fear.
