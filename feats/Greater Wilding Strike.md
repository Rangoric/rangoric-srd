# Greater Wilding Strike

You reach the pinnacle of your wilding strike abilities.

**Prerequisites**: Str 18, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Wilding Strike|Improved Wilding Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wilding|Wilding]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wilding Strike|Wilding Strike]], base attack bonus +16.

**Benefit**: The damage die of your unarmed strikes increases to 1d10 (or 1d8 if you are Small). This doesn’t stack with other effects that increase the damage die of your unarmed strikes, including class levels such as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|monk]]’s. Increases to your actual size category (such as from *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*) still increase the damage die as normal.
