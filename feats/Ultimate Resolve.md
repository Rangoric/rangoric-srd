# Ultimate Resolve

Your aura of resolve does not fall with you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-resolve|Aura of resolve]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Your aura of resolve is a 20-foot emanation, and does not end if you fall unconscious.

**Normal**: The aura of resolve reaches only as far as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-aura-of-resolve|class feature]] says, and ends when you fall unconscious.
