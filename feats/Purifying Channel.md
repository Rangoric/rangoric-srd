# Purifying Channel

Your healing energy also damages your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Selective Channeling|Selective Channeling]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you channel positive energy to heal, one creature you exclude from it takes fire damage equal to the healing you roll and is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round by the flames. A successful save against your channel energy halves the fire damage and negates the dazzle.
