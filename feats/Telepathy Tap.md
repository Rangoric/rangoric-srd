# Telepathy Tap

You are able to notice, intercept, and interpret telepathic communication around you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 10 ranks, ability to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Thoughts|detect thoughts]]_, or telepathy.

**Benefit**: When a creature within range of your telepathy (or within 60 feet, if you use _detect thoughts_) communicates by telepathy, including sending or receiving a magical message such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dream|dream]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sending|sending]]_, you can notice it with a DC 30 Sense Motive check. Then you can use your telepathy or spend a casting of _detect thoughts_ as an immediate action. If you do, for every 5 points by which your check beat the DC you learn one piece of the message, such as who sent it, its general nature, the mood of the sender or the receiver, or a person, place or thing it mentions. Your tap also attacks the creature’s Will (the spell’s attack bonus if you spent _detect thoughts_ or used telepathy from a spell, or 1/2 your racial Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier for racial telepathy). On a hit, you can listen to both sides of the communication as long as you keep concentrating as a standard action each round.
