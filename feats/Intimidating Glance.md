# Intimidating Glance

Your gaze strikes fear into your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-hypnotic-stare|hypnotic stare]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can attempt to demoralize the target of your hypnotic stare as a swift action (see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]]).

**Normal**: Attempting to demoralize an opponent is a standard action.
