# Skin Suit

During the daylight hours, you can cloak your unnatural form beneath a veil of flesh and blood.

**Prerequisites**: Int 7, undead that was once humanoid.

**Benefit**: Once per day as a full-round action, you can wrap your body in a thin suit of flesh that makes you look like yourself as a young adult humanoid. Your appearance changes as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alter Self|alter self]]*, but your ability scores don’t change and you gain no new abilities. While disguised, you can’t use slashing or piercing natural attacks (such as bites or claws) without destroying the suit, nor deliver energy drain or ability damage with your undead special attacks. Your alignment is masked as by a constant *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Undetectable Alignment|undetectable alignment]]*, and you have the aura of a living creature rather than an undead one for *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Undead|detect undead]]* and similar effects.

The suit dissolves into a puddle of bloody slurry at sundown, though you can remove it at any time as a standard action. It doesn’t protect you from direct sunlight. If you take lethal damage while wearing it, you must succeed on a Reflex save (DC equal to the damage) or the suit is destroyed.
