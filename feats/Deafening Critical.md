# Deafening Critical

Your critical hits cause enemies to lose their hearing.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], base attack bonus +13.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] against an opponent, the victim is permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]]. A successful Fortitude save reduces the deafness to 1 round. The DC of this Fortitude save is equal to 10 + your base attack bonus. This feat has no effect on deaf creatures. This deafness can be cured by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Blindness-Deafness|remove blindness/deafness]]*, or a similar ability.
