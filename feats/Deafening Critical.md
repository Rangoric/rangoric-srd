# Deafening Critical

Your critical hits cause enemies to lose their hearing.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], base attack bonus +13.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] against an opponent, the victim is permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]]. The deafness attacks the victim’s Fortitude, its attack bonus your base attack bonus; on a miss, the deafness lasts 1 round instead. This feat has no effect on deaf creatures. This deafness can be cured by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Blindness-Deafness|remove blindness/deafness]]_, or a similar ability.
