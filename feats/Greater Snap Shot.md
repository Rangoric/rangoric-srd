# Greater Snap Shot

You can prey on any gap in your foe's guard with impunity, and with even greater range.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 17, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Snap Shot|Improved Snap Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Shot|Rapid Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snap Shot|Snap Shot]], base attack bonus +12.

**Benefit**: Whenever you hit with an attack of opportunity using a ranged weapon, you gain a +2 bonus on the damage roll and on rolls to confirm a critical hit with that attack. These bonuses increase to +4 at base attack bonus +16, and to +6 at +20.
