# Secret Star of Change

You know the Secret Star of Change, which turns one spell into another.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Mastery|Spell Mastery]].

**Benefit**: You can give up a prepared arcane spell to call, in its place, a star or constellation you chose with Spell Mastery, using the spell's level as the call level. Make a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|caster level check]] (DC 10 + 3 × the spell's level). If it fails, the spell is lost and you take 1d6 points of damage per spell level.

**Special**: A lawful character can't take this feat.
