# Disarming Threat Deed

Even your threats are curiously charming.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Swashbuckler|Amateur Swashbuckler]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] class feature; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 2 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you succeed at an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to force an opponent to act friendly toward you, you can spend 1 panache point to cause the target to regard you with indifference when the duration of the effect expires. A target influenced in this manner is unlikely to report you to authorities.

**Normal**: An opponent forced to act friendly toward you by Intimidation becomes unfriendly when the duration expires, and is likely to report you to the authorities.
