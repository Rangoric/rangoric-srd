# Greater Overrun

Enemies must dive to avoid your dangerous move.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Overrun|Improved Overrun]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6, Str 13.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to overrun a foe. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Overrun|Improved Overrun]]. Opponents knocked prone by your overrun provoke attacks of opportunity.

**Normal**: Creatures knocked prone by your overrun don’t provoke attacks of opportunity.
