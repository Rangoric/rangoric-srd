# Ki Stand

If an opponent knocks you down, you swiftly rebound with an attack.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|Ki pool]].

**Benefit**: While you have at least 1 ki point in your ki pool, you can stand up from [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] as a swift action that provokes attacks of opportunity.

You may spend 1 point of your ki pool to stand up as a swift action without provoking attacks of opportunity.
