# Feline Grace

Your innate grace allows you to get out of the stickiest situations.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|catfolk]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]], and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuvers.
