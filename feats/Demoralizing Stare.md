# Demoralizing Stare

Your painful stare inspires fear in your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Mesmerist level 9th, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist#^mesmerist-painful-stare|painful stare]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you trigger your painful stare, the stare attacks the target’s Will, its attack bonus 1/2 your mesmerist level + your Charisma modifier; on a hit, the target becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round. This can’t cause the target to become frightened, even if the target is already shaken.
