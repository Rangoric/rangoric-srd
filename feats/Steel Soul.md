# Steel Soul

You are especially resistant to magic.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|hardy]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: You receive a +4 racial bonus on defenses against spells and spell-like abilities. This replaces the bonus against spells and spell-like abilities from your [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|hardy]] racial trait.

**Normal**: A dwarf’s bonus on these defenses comes from the [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|hardy]] racial trait.
