# Goblin Gunslinger

You have learned how to fire the big guns.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]].

**Benefit**: You can wield Medium [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearms]] without taking the penalty for an inappropriately sized weapon.

**Normal**: You take the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|penalty for an inappropriately sized firearm]].
