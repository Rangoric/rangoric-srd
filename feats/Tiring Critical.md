# Tiring Critical

Your critical hits cause opponents to become fatigued.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], base attack bonus +13.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]], your opponent becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]]. This feat has no additional effect on a fatigued or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]] creature.
