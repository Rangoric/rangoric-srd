# Bullying Blow

With a simple hit, you more easily intimidate an opponent.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc|orc]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you may make a melee attack with a –2 penalty on the attack roll. If the attack damages your opponent, you may make an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to demoralize that opponent as a free action.

**Normal**: Intimidating an opponent is a standard action.
