# Extra Amplification

You are a master at manipulating your mental energies to produce amplified effects.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-amplifications|phrenic amplification]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [[rangoric-srd/class-options/phrenic-amplifications/Phrenic Amplifications|phrenic amplification]]. This can’t be a major amplification.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, you gain another phrenic amplification.
