# Disposable Weapon

You ignore the limitations of your equipment, striking harder despite the damage it does to your weapon.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, proficient with weapon.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use a melee or thrown weapon with the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Fragile (Quality)|fragile]] quality to score a critical threat against an opponent, you can give your weapon the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition to automatically confirm the critical hit.
