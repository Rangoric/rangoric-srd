# Deadhand Master

Your death-infused punch drains life force from your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 23, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deadhand Initiate|Deadhand Initiate]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deadhand Style|Deadhand Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 14 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]] class feature, nongood alignment.

**Benefit**: While using Deadhand Style, when you damage a living creature with an unarmed strike, you can spend 2 points from your ki pool as a swift action to give your enemy 2 [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|negative levels]], with no attack against its defense. Effects that reduce the ki cost also reduce the number of negative levels you inflict, but effects that increase the ki cost do not increase the number of negative levels. As long as the foe has these negative levels, you can’t recover the ki points you spent, effectively reducing the size of your ki pool. Each day the negative levels attack the foe’s Fortitude again, as [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-level-save|temporary negative levels]] do, their attack bonus 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Wisdom modifier. Once the negative levels are gone, whether removed or ended by misses against the foe’s Fortitude, you can recover your spent ki points. If the foe dies before either happens, you can recover the spent ki after 24 hours.
