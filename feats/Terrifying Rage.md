# Terrifying Rage

Prerequisites: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 22 ranks, rage 5/day

Benefit: While you are raging, any enemy that views you must make a Will check (1d20 + Will save bonus) opposed by your Intimidation check or become panicked (if it has HD less than your character level) or shaken (if it has HD equal to or up to twice your character level) for 4d6 rounds.  An enemy with Hit Dice greater than twice your character level is not affected by this feat.
