# Foment the Blood

You can unleash a wave of energy that drives orcs into a frenzy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|Channel energy]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc|orc]].

**Benefit**: When you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]], instead of creating its normal effect, you can give orcs a bonus on weapon damage and critical hit confirmation rolls until your next turn. This bonus is equal to the number of dice your channeled energy normally heals or harms. Your channel has its normal effect on other creatures in the area.
