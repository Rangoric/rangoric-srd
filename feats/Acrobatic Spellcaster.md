# Acrobatic Spellcaster

Your skillful movements prevent foes from disrupting your spells.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Casting|Combat Casting]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Acrobatics).

**Benefit**: When you succeed at an Acrobatics check to move through a threatened square or an enemy’s space without provoking attacks of opportunity, the creatures your check denied attacks of opportunity also can’t make attacks of opportunity against you for casting spells for the rest of your turn.

**Normal**: Casting a spell within an enemy’s reach provokes attacks of opportunity even after you tumble past it.
