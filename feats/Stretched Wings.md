# Stretched Wings

You strengthen your crippled wings.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]), [[rangoric-srd/races/Strix|strix]], [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Wing-Clipped|wing-clipped]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: Your strix racial [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|fly speed]] increases to 60 feet (average). You ignore the wing-clipped trait’s Acrobatics check requirement to fly upward.
