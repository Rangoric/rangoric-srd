# Armor of the Pit

Your fiendish traits take the form of a protective scaly skin.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|Tiefling]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 natural armor bonus.

**Special**: If you have the [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Scaled Skin|scaled skin]] racial trait, you instead gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 5 to two of the following energy types that you don’t have resistance to already: cold, electricity, and fire.
