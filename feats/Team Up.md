# Team Up

When you are ganging up against an enemy, you can aid an ally with a quick feint.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Gang Up|Gang Up]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: When you and at least two of your allies are adjacent to an opponent, you can use [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] as a move action.

**Normal**: Aid another is a standard action.
