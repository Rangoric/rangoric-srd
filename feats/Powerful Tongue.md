# Powerful Tongue

Unlike others of your kind, you are able to pull your prey toward yourself.

**Prerequisites**: Str 17, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggard/Boggard#^sticky-tongue|sticky tongue]].

**Benefit**: Your tongue attack gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Pull|pull]] special ability. At the start of your turn, if your tongue is attached to a target of your size or smaller and you succeed at a combat maneuver check, you can pull the target 5 feet closer to you as a free action.
