# Skeleton Summoner

The walking dead respond to your call.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (necromancy), ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]*.

**Benefit**: Add the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeleton/Human Warrior Skeleton|human warrior skeleton]] to the creatures you can summon with *summon monster I*.

Once per day, when you cast *summon monster*, you can summon a skeletal version of one of the creatures on that spell’s list (the creature with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeleton/Skeleton|skeleton]] template).
