# Skeleton Summoner

The walking dead respond to your call.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (necromancy), ability to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]_.

**Benefit**: Add the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeletons/Human Warrior Skeleton|human warrior skeleton]] to the creatures you can summon with _summon monster I_.

Once per day, when you cast _summon monster_, you can summon a skeletal version of one of the creatures on that spell’s list (the creature with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/skeletons/Skeleton|skeleton]] template).
