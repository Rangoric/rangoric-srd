# Instinctive Reflection

Your reflexes instinctively assist you in deflecting spells cast at you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]].

**Benefit**: You receive a +3 natural bonus on all attempts to deflect spells with your magical mirrors ([[rangoric-srd/rules/mirror-magic/Mirror Magic#Reflection|reflection]]).

**Special**: It’s part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/mirror-magic/Mirror Magic|mirror magic]].
