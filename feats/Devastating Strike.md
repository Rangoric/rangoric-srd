# Devastating Strike

Pitting all of your strength and resolve against your enemy, you deliver a strike that is impossible to ignore.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Vital Strike|Improved Vital Strike]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Vital Strike|Greater Vital Strike]], you gain a +2 bonus on each extra weapon damage dice roll those feats grant (+6 maximum). This bonus damage is multiplied on a critical hit.
