# Phase Strike

You strike through the Ethereal Plane so your weapon phases through obstacles, armor, and even thick hide to strike true.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 10 ranks.

**Benefit**: As a move action, you can phase out one manufactured weapon or piece of ammunition you are holding into the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Ethereal Plane|Ethereal Plane]]. Your next melee attack or thrown attack with it before the end of your turn is resolved as a touch attack and ignores cover, but not armor bonuses from force effects. You can attack through total cover this way, though you must still pick the right square.

You can use this once per day, plus once more for every 10 ranks you have in Theology. It doesn’t work on planes that don’t touch the Ethereal Plane.
