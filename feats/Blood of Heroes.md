# Blood of Heroes

You have a sense of destiny about you and always seem to succeed, even when the odds are against you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Hero's Fortune|Hero's Fortune]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you gain a level, you gain 2 [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points|hero points]] instead of 1.

**Normal**: Whenever you gain a level, you gain 1 hero point.
