# Under and Over

You can slip under a foe trying to grab you, knocking him off balance.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Agile Maneuvers|Agile Maneuvers]], Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: If an opponent larger than you tries to grapple you and fails, you can make a trip attack against it as an immediate action, with a +2 bonus. This doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity.
