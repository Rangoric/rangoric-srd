# Scarred Legion

Your ritual scars make you more fearsome, and allies with similar scars grow bolder when they’re near you.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks. You also gain a +2 morale bonus on Will saving throws while adjacent to an ally with this feat. When you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]], if you started the charge adjacent to an ally with this feat, you retain the bonus on Will saves until the start of your next turn.
