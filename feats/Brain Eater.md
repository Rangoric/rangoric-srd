# Brain Eater

You gain some of the skills and knowledge of a consumed foe by eating its brain.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ghoul/Ghoul|Ghoul]], Int 17.

**Benefit**: If you eat part of the brain of a creature with Intelligence 3 or higher, you gain a +2 insight bonus on all skill checks and Will saves for 1 hour. Eating a brain is a full-round action, and the creature must be dead or helpless. If it is alive, you can try to eat its brain as a coup de grace, but you gain the bonus only if the attempt kills it.
