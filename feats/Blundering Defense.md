# Blundering Defense

Your feverish and sometimes comical defensive techniques offer enough distraction to aid allies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cautious Fighter|Cautious Fighter]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you fight defensively or use the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]] action, allies gain a luck bonus to AC and CMD equal to 1/2 the dodge bonus you gain from the action you are taking. Allies only gain this bonus while they are adjacent to you.
