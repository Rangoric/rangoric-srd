# Improved Beast Hunter

You are well trained in stalking and hunting the animals of the wild.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Beast Hunter|Beast Hunter]]; base attack bonus +3; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: When fighting animals that are at least one size category larger than you and that are native to the terrain you have chosen with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Beast Hunter|Beast Hunter]], you gain a +4 bonus on combat maneuver checks and a +4 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against such animals’ attempts to use combat maneuvers against you. Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus on Reflex saves against attacks by the animal that allow a Reflex save (such as attempting to avoid a trample attack).

**Special**: If you have selected Beast Hunter multiple times, you gain this benefit for all of the terrains you have chosen for those feats.
