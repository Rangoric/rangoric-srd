# Kobold Sniper

You snipe quickly, making it harder for others to find the location of your attack.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]].

**Benefit**: When you are sniping, you only take a –10 penalty on your Stealth checks to stay hidden.

**Normal**: Sniping gives the penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks that the skill lists.
