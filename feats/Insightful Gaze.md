# Insightful Gaze

In your personal interactions, you notice what others don’t. It’s hard to get anything past you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-stern-gaze|stern gaze]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: Whenever you make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] check to oppose someone’s [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check, you can roll two dice and take the higher result.
