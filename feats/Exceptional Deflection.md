Prerequisites: Dex 21, Wis 19, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deflect Arrows|Deflect Arrows]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].  

Benefit: The character can deflect any ranged attacks (including spells that require ranged touch attacks) as if they were arrows.
