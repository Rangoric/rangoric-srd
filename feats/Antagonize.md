# Antagonize

Whether with biting remarks or hurtful words, you are adept at making creatures angry with you.

**Benefit**: You can use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] to make a creature respond to you with hostility. Either way, it takes a standard action that doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity, with a DC of 10 + the target’s Hit Dice + its Wisdom modifier. You can’t do this to a creature that doesn’t understand you or has an Intelligence of 3 or lower. First, you can make a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] check as a swift action to gain an insight bonus equal to your Charisma bonus on these checks until the end of your next turn. This is a mind-affecting effect, and what happens depends on the skill:

- **Persuasion**: You fluster your enemy. For the next minute, it takes a –2 penalty on attack rolls against creatures other than you, and a 10% spell failure chance on spells that don’t target you or include you in their area.
- **Intimidation**: The creature flies into a rage. On its next turn it must try to attack you in melee, attack you at range, target you with a spell or include you in a spell’s area. The effect ends if it is kept from attacking you or trying would harm it (for example, if you are across a chasm or behind a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Fire|wall of fire]]*). If it can’t attack you on its turn, you can make the check again as an immediate action to extend the effect 1 round (but not again after that). The effect ends as soon as it attacks you. Once you have used this on a creature, you can’t target it again for 1 day.
