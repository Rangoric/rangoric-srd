# Wild Growth Channel

When you channel positive energy, you cause vines to grow and ensnare enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel positive energy]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|Plant domain]].

**Benefit**: When you channel energy, you can spend two more uses to make vines erupt from the ground in the area: a number of vine growths (AC 10, 5 hp) equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1), each filling a 5-foot square you choose. As an immediate action when you do this, and as a free action at the start of each of your turns, you can have the vines wrap the legs of a creature in their square: it must succeed on a Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 your class level + your Cha modifier) or be rooted to the spot, unable to move. The vines last a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1). The ground must be able to support plant life, such as soil.
