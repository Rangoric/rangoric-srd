# Accursed Critical

Your spells carry an embedded curse that manifests when they strike true.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Curse, Major|major curse]]*, caster level 9th.

**Benefit**: When you confirm a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] with a spell or spell-like ability, you may cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bestow Curse|bestow curse]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Curse, Major|major curse]]* on that target as an immediate action. This works even with ranged spells. You must have bestow curse or major curse prepared or otherwise available to cast, and using this ability casts the corresponding spell.
