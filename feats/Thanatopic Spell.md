# Thanatopic Spell

Your spells can pierce wards against negative energy and even affect undead targets.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (necromancy).

**Benefit**: A thanatopic spell gets through defenses and immunities against death effects, negative levels and energy drain as if they weren’t there. For example, a thanatopic _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Vampiric Touch|vampiric touch]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enervation|enervation]]_ affects a target protected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]_ normally. Attacks against defenses and spell resistance still apply.

Undead are affected too: the spell’s negative energy is retuned to harm them. A thanatopic spell that would kill a living creature (such as by giving it negative levels equal to its Hit Dice) destroys an undead creature, though undead that normally re-form (ghosts, liches, vampires) still can. Negative levels a thanatopic spell gives undead automatically miss their daily attack, so they are removed after 24 hours. A thanatopic spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.

**Normal**: Defenses such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]_ negate death effects, negative levels and energy drain, and undead are immune to them.
