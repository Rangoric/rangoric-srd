# Studied Combatant

Your inspiration guides your strikes when you take a moment to assess your foe.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Investigator|Amateur Investigator]], base attack bonus +6, 1 rank in at least one [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] skill, no levels in a class that has the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|inspiration]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can expend one use of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-inspiration|inspiration]] as a move action to study a single enemy that you can see. When you do so, you gain a +2 insight bonus on melee attack rolls and a +2 bonus on damage rolls for a number of rounds equal to your Intelligence modifier. The bonus on damage rolls is precision damage, and is not multiplied on a critical hit. Once affected by this feat, a target cannot be affect by your use of it again for 24 hours.
