# Ricochet Splash Weapon

Even when your thrown splash weapons miss, they are especially dangerous.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Throw Anything|Throw Anything]].

**Benefit**: Whenever your splash weapon misses and lands in a square with a creature in it, you can make an attack roll (at a –5 penalty) as if you had thrown the splash weapon at that creature. If it hits, the creature takes full damage instead of splash damage. Squares next to it still take splash damage as normal.

**Normal**: A [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]] that misses deals splash damage in the square where it lands and the squares next to it.
