# Faerie's Strike

Ephemeral wisps of nature spirits dance about those you strike.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nature Magic|Nature Magic]] or the ability to cast druid or ranger spells, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: A number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1), when you hit a creature using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Vital Strike|Improved Vital Strike]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Vital Strike|Greater Vital Strike]], you can use this feat as a free action. The creature must succeed at a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or be outlined as if by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]* for 1 minute. This is a supernatural ability.
