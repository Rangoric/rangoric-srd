# Horn Rider

You can defend yourself using the horn or tusk of a creature you're lashed to.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 shield bonus to AC when lashed into a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Horn Harness|horn harness]].

**Special**: A creature with this feat can take the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Archery|Mounted Archery]] feat without possessing 1 rank in the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] skill or the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Combat|Mounted Combat]] feat.
