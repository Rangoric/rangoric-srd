# Fey Magic

The magic sand of the fey realm has intriguing properties when used in conjunction with traditional spellcasting.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fey Spell Mastery|Fey Spell Mastery]].

**Benefit**: You can use [[rangoric-srd/rules/Faercana#^magic-sand|magic sand]] as a component for your spells to bolster them. This also adds one to each die rolled for the spell’s effect.

**Special**: The use of magic sand as a material component in ordinary spellcasting inserts a degree of randomness into the final spell result (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Faercana#^table-magic-sand-wild-characteristics|Table: Magic Sand Wild Characteristics]]).

A fey magic spell uses a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
