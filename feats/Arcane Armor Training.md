# Arcane Armor Training

You have learned how to cast spells while wearing armor.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Armor Proficiency (Light)|Armor Proficiency (Light)]], caster level 3rd.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, reduce the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Armor|arcane spell failure]] chance from the armor you are wearing by 10% for any spells you cast this round.
