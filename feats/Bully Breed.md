# Bully Breed

Your animal companion can terrify as well as fight.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 4 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 4 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-special-mount|special mount]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Your animal companion or mount gains [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] as a class skill. It can attempt to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] an opponent using Intimidation as a move action on the same round that it damages an opponent with a natural attack.

**Normal**: Animal companions and mounts with an Intelligence score of 2 or less must select feats from a limited list.

**Special**: Your animal companion can select the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shatter Defenses|Shatter Defenses]] feats if it meets the prerequisites. This feat does not allow your animal companion to use Intimidation to make a target friendly or gain its assistance.
