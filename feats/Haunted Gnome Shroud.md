# Haunted Gnome Shroud

Your disturbing glamer expands to make your exact location hard to pinpoint.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome magic]] racial trait, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Haunted Gnome|Haunted Gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Haunted Gnome Assault|Haunted Gnome Assault]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain another independent use of your [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome magic]] like that which [[rangoric-srd/feats/Haunted Gnome Assault|Haunted Gnome Assault]] grants. Further, while you are under the effect of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haunted Fey Aspect|haunted fey aspect]]*, you also have [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] (20% miss chance) against an opponent until that opponent deals you damage.
