# Sign of the Eye

The Eye stood over your birth, and you see what others miss.

**Benefit**: You know which square an [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] or otherwise totally concealed opponent is in, though you still have the 50% miss chance against it.

**Normal**: You must guess which square a totally concealed opponent is in.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
