# Sign of the Trumpet

The Trumpet sounds in your blood, and doom follows those who hunt you.

**Benefit**: Once per day as a standard action, you can call down doom. This attacks the Will of each hostile creature within 30 feet of you, its attack bonus 1/2 your character level + your Charisma modifier; on a hit, the creature takes a −1 morale penalty on attack and damage rolls for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier. This is a mind-affecting supernatural ability.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
