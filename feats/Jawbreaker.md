# Jawbreaker

You deliver a powerful strike to the mouth, breaking teeth and bone.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you make a successful Stunning Fist attempt against an opponent that is [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], instead of any other Stunning Fist effect, you can cripple that opponent’s mouth, dealing normal unarmed strike damage and 1d4 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage. Until the bleed damage ends, the target can’t use its mouth to attack, speak clearly or provide verbal spell components. A creature that is immune to critical hits or has no discernible mouth is immune to this feat.
