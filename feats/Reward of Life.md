# Reward of Life

When you lay on hands, you are also healed.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|Lay on hands]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Each time you use your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] to heal a creature other than yourself, you heal a number of hit points equal to your Charisma bonus. This has no effect when you use lay on hands to harm undead.
