# Play to the Crowd

You can glean the interests of others and use that information to strengthen your rhetoric.

**Prerequisites**: Int 19, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ironclad Logic|Ironclad Logic]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you use Persuasion to change a character’s attitude, you can first make a Sense Motive check against it to get a hunch, to tell whether it is under an enchantment, or to use [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sense Assumptions|Sense Assumptions]]. If the Sense Motive check succeeds, you gain a +2 bonus on the Persuasion check. Your target’s attitude doesn’t worsen if you fail the Persuasion check by 5 or more.
