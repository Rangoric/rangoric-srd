# Smell Fear

You can catch the sour scent of fear on the wind.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Keen Scent|Keen Scent]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]] or orc.

**Benefit**: You receive a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to detect creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] conditions using [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]]. You can substitute your Perception skill for [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] skill checks if the subject has one of the previous conditions or is attempting to cover up their fear in some way.
