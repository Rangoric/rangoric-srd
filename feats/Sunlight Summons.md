# Sunlight Summons

Your summoned minions shine with the power of the sun.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (conjuration), able to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally]]*.

**Benefit**: Creatures you summon shed light as a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]* spell. They are immune to blinding and dazzling effects, and their natural weapons count as magic for overcoming damage reduction.
