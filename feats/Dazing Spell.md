# Dazing Spell

You can daze creatures with the power of your spells.

**Benefit**: A creature that takes damage from a dazing spell is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for a number of rounds equal to the spell’s original level. If the spell allows a saving throw, a successful save negates the daze; if it doesn’t, the target can make a Will save to negate it. If the spell already dazes the creature, this duration is added to the spell’s. Spells that deal no damage don’t benefit from this feat. A dazing spell uses up a spell slot three levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
