# Voice of Beasts

Your deep connection with nature allows you to speak with all manner of living creatures.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|Wild shape]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While you are using wild shape, you gain the ability to communicate with all animals. This acts as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Animals|speak with animals]]*, though the effect is supernatural in nature and can’t be dispelled.
