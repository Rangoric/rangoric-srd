# Mountaineer

You are thoroughly at home in the high peaks and precipices of alpine territory.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-terrain|Favored terrain]] (mountain) class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb natural stone surfaces and a +4 bonus on Fortitude saves to avoid high-altitude [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigue]] and altitude sickness. If you spend 24 hours at a dangerous [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Mountain Travel|altitude]], you treat that altitude as if it were one altitude zone lower. If you spend at least 1 week at that altitude, you are immune to altitude fatigue or sickness. If you go to a lower altitude for more than 1 week, you lose this acclimation.
