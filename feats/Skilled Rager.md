# Skilled Rager

With practice, you’ve mastered your rage to allow yourself more versatility.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|Rage]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Choose one Charisma-, Dexterity- or Intelligence-based skill. While you are raging, you can use this skill.

**Normal**: While raging, you can’t use Charisma-, Dexterity- or Intelligence-based skills other than the few the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] names.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time, it applies to a different skill.
