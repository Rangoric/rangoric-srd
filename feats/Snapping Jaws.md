# Snapping Jaws

When enemies get too close, your jaws tear flesh and bone.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnoll|gnoll]].

**Benefit**: You can use your bite as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^natural-weapons|natural weapon]]. This is a primary attack that deals 1d6 points of damage.
