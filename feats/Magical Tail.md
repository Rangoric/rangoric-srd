# Magical Tail

You grow an extra tail that represents your growing magical powers.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Kitsune|Kitsune]].

**Benefit**: You gain a new spell-like ability, each usable twice per day, from the following list, in order: _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Person|charm person]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Misdirection|misdirection]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Confusion|confusion]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dominate Person|dominate person]]_. For example, the first time you select this feat, you gain disguise self 2/day; the second time you select this feat, you gain charm person 2/day. Your caster level for these spells is equal to your Hit Dice. The DCs for these abilities are Charisma-based.

**Special**: You may select this feat up to eight times. Each time you take it, you gain an additional ability as described above.
