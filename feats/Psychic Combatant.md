# Psychic Combatant

You are skilled at attacking opponents in psychic duels.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] or the ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spells]] or use psychic spell-like abilities, character level 3rd.

**Benefit**: You gain an extra pool of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Psychic Duels#Generating Manifestation Points|manifestation points]] equal to 1/2 your character level. You can use these points at any time during a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Psychic Duels|psychic duel]]. When you aren’t in a psychic duel, you can spend 1 of these points as a swift action to gain a +1 bonus on saving throws against psychic spells until the start of your next turn, or 3 points as a swift action to gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against psychic spells until the start of your next turn. The pool refreshes at the start of each day.
