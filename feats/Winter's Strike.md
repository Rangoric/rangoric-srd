# Winter's Strike

Your attacks afflict your foes with the relentless fatigue of a winter storm.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nature Magic|Nature Magic]] or the ability to cast druid or ranger spells, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: A number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1), when you hit a creature using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Vital Strike|Vital Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Vital Strike|Improved Vital Strike]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Vital Strike|Greater Vital Strike]], you can use this feat as a free action. The strike attacks the creature’s Fortitude, its attack bonus 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier; on a hit, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). This is a supernatural ability.
