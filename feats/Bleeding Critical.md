# Bleeding Critical

Your critical hits cause opponents to bleed profusely.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] with a slashing or piercing weapon, your opponent takes 2d6 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage each round on his turn, in addition to the damage dealt by the critical hit. The effects of this feat stack.
