# Grow Swathe

You can create swathes by placing magics within living plants or grafting them onto magical items.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (herbalist) 9 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Powders & Dusts|Craft Powders & Dusts]], ability to cast 3rd-level spells.

**Benefit**: You can add a living swathe of plants onto enchanted clothing or items or places and embed magics within the swathe to enhance and alter the existing magic or to add in new, nature-related magics or druid spells. The swathes act like *bracers, cloaks,* or other garments that hold spells and grant supernatural abilities while being worn.

There are two different levels of costs involved with this feat, depending on what the herbalist does:

- Swathe Graft: This treatment augments and adapts the magics already inherent in an item— usually a wand or a rod, though staves and boots are also common—so very little magic is necessary for the graft. The base price of a swathe graft is its caster level plus its DC, multiplied by 5 gp. To graft a swathe to an item, you must spend 1/50 (2%) of the new base price (item base price PLUS graft base price) in XP and use up raw materials costing one-quarter (25%) that new base price.

- Swathe Creation: This creates an independent living swathe not tied to a particular magical item, though these can be anchored to magical and nonmagical materials of leather or stronger substance. The base price of a living swathe takes the item’s caster level, adds its creation DC, and then multiplies the total by 200 gp. To create such substances, the creator must expend 4% (1/25) of the item’s base price in XP and use up raw materials costing 30% of the base price. (The savings alchemy or herbalism give other item creation feats, in [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemical and Herbal Items#Related Feats & Skill Adjustments|Related Feats & Skill Adjustments]], are already part of this feat.)

**Special**: Swathes cannot be created by the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]] feat unless the user meets all the prerequisites for this feat (herbal/plant knowledge).

This feat is part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|alchemy and herbalism]].
