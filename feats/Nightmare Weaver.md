# Nightmare Weaver

You can use your ability to create magical darkness to terrorize enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nightmare Fist|Nightmare Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 2 ranks, ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*.

**Benefit**: By spending a full-round action to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*, you can also make Intimidation checks to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] all foes in the spell’s initial area.

**Special**: This feat counts as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]] for qualifying for [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deadly Stroke|Deadly Stroke]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shatter Defenses|Shatter Defenses]].
