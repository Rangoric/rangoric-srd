# Sabotage Magic Item

You can cause magic items to suffer mishaps.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Emulation or a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]]), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can make an Emulation check to sabotage a magic item instead of activating it, with the same DC as activating it blindly. If you succeed, choose a condition under which the item will misfire, either dealing 2d6 points of damage to the creature trying to use it and failing to work, or affecting the wrong target. The condition must be something heard or seen, as with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Mouth|magic mouth]]*. If you fail, you suffer a mishap instead, as if you had failed to activate the item blindly by 10 or more.
