# Blood Beak

Your bleed attack is bloody and dangerous.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +5, natural weapon racial trait, [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|tengu]].

**Benefit**: Increase the damage of your beak (the tengu’s bite) attack to 1d6. Furthermore, when you confirm a critical hit with your beak attack, you also deal 1 point of bleed damage.

**Special**: The bleed effect from this feat stacks with that of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bleeding Critical|Bleeding Critical]] feat and similar effects, adding 1 point to your bleed damage.
