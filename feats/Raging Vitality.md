# Raging Vitality

While raging, you are full of vigor and health.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Whenever you are raging, the morale bonus to your Constitution increases by +2. Your rage doesn’t end if you become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]]. While unconscious, you must still spend a round of rage each round.
