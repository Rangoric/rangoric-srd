# Stone Sense

You can feel movement in the very earth and stones around you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Stonecunning (Feat)|Improved Stonecunning]] feat or the earth dwarf’s [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Improved Stonecunning|Improved Stonecunning]] racial trait, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] 10 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] to a range of 10 feet.
