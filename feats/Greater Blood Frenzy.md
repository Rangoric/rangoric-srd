# Greater Blood Frenzy

Your wounds move you into a deeper frenzy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|Blood frenzy]] ability, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sahuagin/Sahuagin|sahuagin]].

**Benefit**: You no longer take a penalty to AC while in a blood frenzy. In addition, if you strike an enemy with both of your claw attacks—or two or more claw attacks if you have extra limbs—while in a blood frenzy, you can [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Rend|rend]] (as the special attack), dealing an amount of additional damage equal to your claw damage plus 1-1/2 times your Strength bonus (minimum 0).
