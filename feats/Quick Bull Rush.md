# Quick Bull Rush

You can barrel into your opponent and follow this with an attack.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: On your turn, you can attempt a single [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] combat maneuver in place of one of your melee attacks. You must use the attack with your highest base attack bonus for it.

**Normal**: A bull rush combat maneuver is a standard action.
