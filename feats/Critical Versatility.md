# Critical Versatility

An open mind and combat training grant versatility to your critical hits.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|Fighter]] level 11th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]].

**Benefit**: Once per day, you can spend 1 hour practicing maneuvers to gain one single [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^critical-feats|critical feat]] that you meet the prerequisites for. You gain the benefits of the chosen critical feat until you choose to practice a different critical feat.
