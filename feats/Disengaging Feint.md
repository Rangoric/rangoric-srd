# Disengaging Feint

You can feint to disengage from combat.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] against an opponent. Instead of denying it its Dexterity bonus to AC, a successful feint lets you move up to your speed without provoking an attack of opportunity from that opponent for leaving the square you start in.
