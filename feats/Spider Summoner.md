# Spider Summoner

You gain the ability to summon powerful spiders.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast _summon monster_ or _summon nature’s ally_ spells, [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]].

**Benefit**: When casting either a _summon monster_ spell or a _summon nature’s ally_ spell, your options increase. Depending on the level of the spell, you can summon the spiders listed below.

- **_Summon Monster_**: 1st level, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spiders/Giant Crab Spider|giant crab spider]]\*; 4th level, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spiders/Giant Black Widow|giant black widow]]\*; 5th level, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spiders/Ogre Spider|ogre spider]]\*; 7th level, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spiders/Giant Tarantula|giant tarantula]]\*.
- **_Summon Nature’s Ally_**: 1st level, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spiders/Giant Crab Spider|giant crab spider]]; 4th level, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spiders/Giant Black Widow|giant black widow]]; 5th level, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spiders/Ogre Spider|ogre spider]]; 7th level, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/spiders/Giant Tarantula|giant tarantula]].

Creatures marked with an asterisk (\*) are summoned with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/celestial-creatures/Celestial Creature|celestial template]] if you are good, and the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/fiendish-creatures/Fiendish Creature|fiendish template]] if you are evil. If you are neutral, you may choose which template to apply to the creature.

Furthermore, when you summon spiders using _summon monster_ or _summon nature’s ally_, the DC of the summoned monster’s poison and web effects increases by 2.
