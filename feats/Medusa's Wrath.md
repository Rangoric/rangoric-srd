# Medusa's Wrath

You can take advantage of your opponent's confusion, delivering multiple blows.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Gorgon's Fist|Gorgon's Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Scorpion Style|Scorpion Style]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: Whenever you make a full attack and at least one unarmed strike, you can make two more unarmed strikes at your highest base attack bonus. These extra attacks must be against a dazed, flat-footed, paralyzed, staggered, stunned or unconscious foe.
