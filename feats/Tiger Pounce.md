# Tiger Pounce

Your unarmed strikes are as precise as they are powerful, but they leave you open and you can pursue foes with blinding speed.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tiger Claws|Tiger Claws]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tiger Style|Tiger Style]], base attack bonus +9 or monk level 8th.

**Benefit**: While using Tiger Style, you can apply Power Attack’s penalty to your AC instead of your attack rolls. Once per round as a swift action, you can move up to half your speed closer to a target you hit with an unarmed strike, or succeeded at a combat maneuver against, this turn or last turn.
