# Inspired by Fear

When spreading fear to your enemies, you further embolden your allies.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to perform [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bard-dirge-of-doom|dirge of doom]].

**Benefit**: Allies in the area of your dirge of doom receive a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fear|fear]] effects. At least one enemy must be in the dirge’s area and become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] by the dirge for you and your allies to receive this bonus.
