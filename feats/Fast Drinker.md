# Fast Drinker

You swiftly guzzle spirits to draw forth ki.

**Prerequisites**: Con 18, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/archetypes/monk/Drunken Master (Archetype)|drunken *ki*]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Drinking strong alcohol to gain temporary [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|*ki*]] takes a swift action rather than a standard action.
