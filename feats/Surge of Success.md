# Surge of Success

Your success drives your further actions.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]].

**Benefit**: When you confirm a critical hit or an attack against your defense rolls a natural 1, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on a single attack roll, skill check, or ability check, or on a defense against a single attack, of your choice before the end of your next turn. You must choose to use this bonus before you make the attack roll, skill check, or ability check, or before the attack against your defense is rolled.
