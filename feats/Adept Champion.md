# Adept Champion

You can alter your smite ability, channeling the power of your deity into divine inspiration that grants you greater aptitude for performing combat maneuvers.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|Smite evil]] class feature, base attack bonus +5.

**Benefit**: While using your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-smite-evil|smite evil]], as a swift action at the start of your turn, you can forgo the bonus on damage rolls and instead gain half that bonus as a bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] checks against the target of your smite. Your smite evil returns to normal at the start of your next turn.
