# Felling Escape

Through the use of fluid contortions and manipulations of leverage, you can throw your opponent to the ground after escaping a grapple.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]].

**Benefit**: When you break an opponent’s grapple with a combat maneuver check or an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] check, you can spend a swift action to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] that opponent.
