# Inner Flame

Your body generates so much heat that your mere touch scorches your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Scorching Weapons|Scorching Weapons]], character level 7th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|ifrit]].

**Benefit**: Your bonus on saves against fire attacks and spells with the fire descriptor or light descriptor increases to +4. When you use [[rangoric-srd/feats/Scorching Weapons|Scorching Weapons]], the affected weapons deal an additional 1d6 points of fire damage instead of 1, and when you are [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grappling]], you deal this damage to your grappling opponent on your turn.
