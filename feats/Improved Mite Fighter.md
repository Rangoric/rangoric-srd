# Improved Mite Fighter

Your ability to fight creatures smaller than yourself has been honed to a deadly art form.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mite Fighter|Mite Fighter]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Base Attack Bonus|base attack bonus]] +8.

**Benefit**: When fighting a creature smaller than yourself, you gain a +2 bonus on your attack rolls. This bonus stacks with the one from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mite Fighter|Mite Fighter]].
