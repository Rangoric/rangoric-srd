# Feinting Flurry

Your subtle flourishes mislead your enemy, tricking it into changing its footing.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-flurry-of-blows|flurry of blows]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]].

**Benefit**: While using [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-flurry-of-blows|flurry of blows]] to make melee attacks, you can forgo your first attack to make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]].
