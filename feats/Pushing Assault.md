# Pushing Assault

A strike made with a two-handed weapon can push a similar sized opponent backward.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When you hit a creature your size or smaller with a two-handed weapon attack using Power Attack, you can push it 5 feet directly away from you instead of dealing Power Attack’s extra damage. On a critical hit, you can push it 10 feet instead. This movement doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity, and the target must end it in a safe space where it can stand. You choose after the attack roll but before rolling damage.
