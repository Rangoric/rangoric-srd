# Fey Spell Versatility

You have learned magical secrets from the fey.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, 1 rank in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]], ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]] spells.

**Benefit**: Choose a 1st-, a 2nd-, a 3rd- and a 4th-level spell from the bard or sorcerer/wizard spell list, each either an enchantment or illusion spell or a curse. Add them to your ranger spell list. Once chosen, they can’t be changed.
