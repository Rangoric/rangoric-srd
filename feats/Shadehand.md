# Shadehand

You generate, store, and project necromantic energy from your body.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shadesight|Shadesight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]], Con 15.

**Benefit**: You can generate necromantic energy within your body and release it in a variety of ways. By expending a full-round action and making a Constitution check (DC 15), you generate one charge of necromantic energy. For every 5 points by which the check exceeds the base DC, you generate an additional charge. For each charge generated, you take 1d4 points of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]].

You may hold a number of generated charges equal to your Constitution score but take 2 points of damage per charge held at the start of each round. If you are slain or rendered unconscious while holding charges, the negative energy is released in a 10-foot-radius burst (centered on your body) that deals 1d4 points of damage to all living creatures in the area per charge held. The burst attacks the Fortitude of all creatures in the area at +5, and a miss avoids the damage. As a standard action, you may release up to 5 held charges to generate one or more of the effects detailed below. You may combine charge effects, as long as no more than 5 charges are released in a single action.

The available charge effects are:

- **1 charge**: A touch attack dealing 1d6 points of negative energy damage. It also attacks the target’s Will, its attack bonus your Charisma bonus, and a miss avoids the damage.
- **1 charge**: A touch attack that creates a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]_ effect, as the spell. It also attacks the target’s Will, its attack bonus your Charisma bonus, and a miss negates the effect.
- **1 charge**: Deliver either the negative energy or _cause fear_ effect as a 30-foot ranged touch attack.
- **1 charge**: Increase the attack bonus against Will for a negative energy or _cause fear_ effect by 5.
- **2 charges**: A touch attack dealing 1d4 points of temporary Strength [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]].
- **2 charges**: Project the damage or _cause fear_ effect as a 20-foot cone.
- **3 charges**: Project the damage or _cause fear_ effect as a 10-foot-radius burst centered on you.

You also gain a +2 competence bonus on Fortitude Defense to recover from [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|energy drain]] effects due to your experience with channeling negative energy through your body. Abilities granted by this feat are spell-like abilities.

**Special**: Your body temperature drops 20 degrees. It’s a [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Shadetouched Feats|shadetouched feat]]: it has uses per day and costs hit points as [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Shadetouched Feats|the Path of Shades]] says. [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadeseer|Shadeseers]] gain an [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadeseer#^shadeseer-enhanced-shadetouched-feats|enhanced version]] of this feat free at 1st level.
