# Shadehand

You generate, store, and project necromantic energy from your body.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shadesight|Shadesight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Fortitude|Great Fortitude]], Con 15.

**Benefit**: You can generate necromantic energy within your body and release it in a variety of ways. By expending a full-round action and making a Constitution check (DC 15), you generate one charge of necromantic energy. For every 5 points by which the check exceeds the base DC, you generate an additional charge. For each charge generated, you take 1d4 points of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]].

You may hold a number of generated charges equal to your Constitution score but take 2 points of damage per charge held at the start of each round. If you are slain or rendered unconscious while holding charges, the negative energy is released in a 10-foot-radius burst (centered on your body) that deals 1d4 points of damage to all living creatures in the area per charge held. All creatures in the area are permitted a Fortitude save (DC 15) to avoid the damage. As a standard action, you may release up to 5 held charges to generate one or more of the effects detailed below. You may combine charge effects, as long as no more than 5 charges are released in a single action.

The available charge effects are:

- **1 charge**: A touch attack dealing 1d6 points of negative energy damage. The target of this attack is permitted a Will save (DC 10 + your Charisma bonus) to avoid the damage.
- **1 charge**: A touch attack that creates a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]* effect, as the spell. The target of this attack is permitted a Will save (DC 10 + your Charisma bonus) to negate the effect.
- **1 charge**: Deliver either the negative energy or *cause fear* effect as a 30-foot ranged touch attack.
- **1 charge**: Increase the Will save DC for a negative energy or *cause fear* effect by 5.
- **2 charges**: A touch attack dealing 1d4 points of temporary Strength [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]].
- **2 charges**: Project the damage or *cause fear* effect as a 20-foot cone.
- **3 charges**: Project the damage or *cause fear* effect as a 10-foot-radius burst centered on you.

You also gain a +2 competence bonus on Fortitude saves to recover from [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Energy Drain and Negative Levels|energy drain]] effects due to your experience with channeling negative energy through your body. Abilities granted by this feat are spell-like abilities.

**Special**: Your body temperature drops 20 degrees. It’s a [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Shadetouched Feats|shadetouched feat]]: it has uses per day and costs hit points as [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Shadetouched Feats|the Path of Shades]] says. [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadeseer|Shadeseers]] gain an [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadeseer#^shadeseer-enhanced-shadetouched-feats|enhanced version]] of this feat free at 1st level.
