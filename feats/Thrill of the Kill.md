# Thrill of the Kill

Killing fuels your rage.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-orc]] or orc, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you are raging and your attack reduces an enemy to negative hit points or kills it, you regain 1 round of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]]. You may only use this feat if the fallen enemy had at least as many Hit Dice as you. You can only gain this benefit once per round.
