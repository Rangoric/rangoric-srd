# Perfect Strike

When wielding a monk weapon, your attacks can be extremely precise.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: You must declare that you are using this feat before you make your attack roll (so a miss wastes the attempt). The attack must be made with a kama, nunchaku, quarterstaff, sai or siangham. Roll the attack roll twice and take the higher result. If one of the rolls is a critical threat, the other roll is your confirmation roll (your choice if both are threats). You can attempt a Perfect Strike once per day for every four character levels you have (but see Special), and no more than once per round.

**Special**: A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] can attempt a Perfect Strike a number of times per day equal to her monk level, plus one more time for every four levels she has in other classes.
