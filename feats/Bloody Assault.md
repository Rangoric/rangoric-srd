# Bloody Assault

Sacrificing accuracy, you can inflict bloody wounds that are slow to heal.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You can choose to take a –5 penalty on all melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks to deal 1d4 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage with your weapon melee attacks, on top of their normal damage. The bleed doesn’t stack with itself. You must choose to use this feat before making the attack roll, and its effects last until your next turn (though the bleeding lasts until it is stopped, as normal).
