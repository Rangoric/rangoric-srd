# Charge Through

You can overrun enemies when charging.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Overrun|Improved Overrun]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]], you can try to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Overrun|overrun]] one creature in the charge’s path as a free action. If you succeed, you can complete the charge. If you fail, the charge ends in the space directly in front of that creature.

**Normal**: You must have a clear path toward the target of your charge.
