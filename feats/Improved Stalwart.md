# Improved Stalwart

You can roll with the punches while simultaneously striking back at your attackers.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stalwart|Stalwart]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: Double the damage reduction you gain from Stalwart, to a maximum of DR 10/—.
