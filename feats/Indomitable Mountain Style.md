# Indomitable Mountain Style

Like a rugged mountain, you are impassable and unmovable.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: While using this style, as long as you don’t take a move action, you gain a +4 morale bonus to your CMD and to the DC to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] you. The [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Tumbling|Acrobatics]] DC for creatures to move through squares you threaten without provoking attacks of opportunity also increases by 4.
