# Brilliant Planner

Your experience and intellect enable you to create prescient plans and contingencies.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, character level 5th.

**Benefit**: You can prepare for things to come without saying what the preparations are until they matter. While in a settlement for at least 24 hours, you can take 8 hours and set aside up to 50 gp per character level as your brilliant plan fund. While you have a brilliant plan pending, you always count as carrying 20 more pounds, even before you say what the plan is.

Once per day, you can take 10 minutes to enact a brilliant plan: you bring out an item that would have been for sale in a settlement you visited, or get a mundane service you arranged ahead of time. Subtract its price from the fund. An item must weigh 10 pounds or less, and the GM must approve any nonmagical service as fitting for the place.

Once you have spent the whole fund or brought out 20 pounds of objects, you can’t use this feat again until you refill the fund.
