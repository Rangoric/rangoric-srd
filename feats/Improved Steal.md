# Improved Steal

You have a knack for snatching items from your opponents.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]].

**Benefit**: You don’t provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]]. You gain a +2 bonus on checks to steal an item from a foe, and a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when an opponent tries to steal an item from you.

**Normal**: You provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a steal combat maneuver.
