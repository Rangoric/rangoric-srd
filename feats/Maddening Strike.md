# Maddening Strike

Your fists are weapons of mind-devouring madness.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 17, Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Maddening Style|Maddening Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 8 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]] class feature, nongood alignment.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, you can spend 1 point from your ki pool to imbue the next unarmed strike you make on this turn with the maddening power of the spaces between the stars. If that attack doesn’t hit, you take 2 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|Wisdom damage]]. If the attack hits, your opponent takes damage from your unarmed strike + 1d4+1 points of Wisdom damage. A successful Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) reduces this to 2 points of Wisdom damage.
