# Life Lure

Your channeled positive energy is irresistibly sweet to nearby undead.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel positive energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can channel positive energy to make all undead within 30 feet [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1). It attacks the Will of undead, its attack bonus 1/2 your effective cleric level + your Cha modifier, and undead it misses are unaffected. Channeling this way doesn’t heal or harm anything.
