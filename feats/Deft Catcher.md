# Deft Catcher

You possess a preternatural skill for catching items that slip from your grasp.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Sleight of Hand).

**Benefit**: Whenever you drop an item or are made to drop it (other than by a disarm or steal), you can make a DC 20 Sleight of Hand check as an immediate action to catch it. If something would disarm you or steal an item you are holding, such as a disarm or steal maneuver or a spell like *telekinesis*, you can also make a Sleight of Hand check as an immediate action to keep hold of it (DC 10 + the combat maneuver check result, or 20 + the spell’s DC if there was no maneuver check). Either way, if you fail by 10 or more, you fall prone.
