# Underfoot

You use your small size to slip past larger foes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 dodge bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Tumbling|Acrobatics]] checks to move past opponents larger than you without provoking attacks of opportunity. You also gain a +2 dodge bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity provoked when you move out of or within a larger opponent’s threatened area; this stacks with Mobility’s bonus.
