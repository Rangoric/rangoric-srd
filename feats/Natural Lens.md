# Natural Lens

Starlight flows through you so freely that pain and distraction can hardly break it.

**Benefit**: When you fail the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|caster level check]] to call a star or constellation, you can give up another prepared spell or unused spell slot of any level as a free action to try the check again at the same DC. If the second check succeeds, the star or constellation is called as normal. If it fails, both are lost and you take 1d6 points of damage from the strain.

**Normal**: A failed check loses the star or constellation.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
