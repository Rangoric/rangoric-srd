# Martial Versatility

You further broaden your study of weapons to encompass multiple similar weapons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|Fighter]] level 4th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]].

**Benefit**: Choose one combat feat you know that applies to a specific weapon (e.g., [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]]). You can use that feat with any weapon within the same [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Groups|weapon group]].

**Special**: You may take this feat more than once. Each time it applies to a different feat.
