# Unseat

You are skilled at unseating your mounted opponents.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mounted Combat|Mounted Combat]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] an opponent while mounted and wielding a lance, resolve the attack as normal. If it hits, you can immediately make a free [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] attempt on top of the normal damage. If it succeeds, the target is knocked off its mount and lands [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] in a space next to its mount, directly away from you.
