# Extra Discovery

You have made a new alchemical discovery.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|Discovery]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-discovery|discovery]]. You must meet all of the prerequisites for this discovery.

**Special**: You can gain Extra Discovery multiple times.
