# City Sprinter

You are skilled at moving quickly through the crowded and treacherous parts of cities.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: Entering a square with a crowd costs you no extra movement (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Urban Features|Urban Features]]). You also gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to move along rooftops and across slippery city streets and sewers.
