# Carrion Feeder

Like many scavengers, you can stomach foods that would make weaker creatures ill.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|Tengu]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 racial bonus on defenses against diseases and ingested poisons (but not other poisons). You receive a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to find food for yourself (and only yourself).
