# Disconcerting Knowledge

You unsettle your enemies by demonstrating that you know how to defeat them.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Bard#^bardic-knowledge|Bardic knowledge]] class feature or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Knowledge Domain|Knowledge domain]], 3 ranks in each of two [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]].

**Benefit**: You can use your knowledge to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] an enemy. When you use Intimidation as a standard action against a creature with a base CR of 2 or greater, you can use a Knowledge skill that could be used to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|identify the creature’s type]] in place of Intimidation when attempting to demoralize that creature. The target creature must be intelligent and understand the language that you are using when conveying the disconcerting knowledge.
