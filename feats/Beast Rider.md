# Beast Rider

You gain the service of a monstrous companion or mount.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|Animal companion]] or mount class feature (such as the paladin’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-special-mount|special mount]]), character level 7th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]] or orc.

**Benefit**: Select one of the following creature types: elephant, pteranodon, rhinoceros, stegosaurus, or triceratops. Add this creature type to your list of possible [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companions]] or mounts. When summoning a creature of the selected type to serve as a mount or companion, treat your effective druid level as if it were two levels higher (to a maximum of your character level). If the creature is large enough for you to ride, it gains the combat training general purpose (see [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]]) at no cost.
