# Twist Away

Your quick reflexes and dexterous movements make up for your lack of stamina.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|Evasion]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While you are wearing light armor or no armor, if you are forced to attempt a Fortitude save, you can use an immediate action to attempt a Reflex save (at the same DC) instead. If you succeed and the attack has a reduced effect on a successful save, you avoid the effect entirely. Whether the save succeeds or not, you are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] until the end of your next turn. If you are prevented from becoming staggered, you can’t use Twist Away, and you can’t ignore the staggered condition from Twist Away or remove it early.
