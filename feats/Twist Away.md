# Twist Away

Your quick reflexes and dexterous movements make up for your lack of stamina.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|Evasion]] class feature.

**Benefit**: While you are wearing light armor or no armor, if an effect attacks your Fortitude Defense, you can use an immediate action to have it attack your Reflex Defense instead (with the same attack bonus). If it misses and the effect is reduced on a miss, you avoid the effect entirely. Whether it hits or misses, you are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] until the end of your next turn. If you are prevented from becoming staggered, you can’t use Twist Away, and you can’t ignore the staggered condition from Twist Away or remove it early.
