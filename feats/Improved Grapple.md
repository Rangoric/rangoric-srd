# Improved Grapple

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: You don’t provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]]. You gain a +2 bonus on checks to grapple a foe, and a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when an opponent tries to grapple you.

**Normal**: You provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a grapple combat maneuver.

**Special**: A monk may select Improved Grapple as a bonus feat at 1st level, even if she does not meet the prerequisites.
