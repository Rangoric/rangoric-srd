# Duck and Cover

Your allies assist you in avoiding certain attacks.

**Benefit**: Whenever you are adjacent to an ally who also has this feat, and a spell or effect attacks the Reflex Defense of both of you, you may have the attack against you use the die roll of the attack against you or against your ally (your Reflex Defense still applies, regardless of which roll is used). If you take your ally’s result, you are knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] (or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] on your next turn, if you are already prone or cannot be knocked prone). In addition, you receive a +2 [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|cover]] bonus to your AC against ranged attacks as long as your ally is wielding a shield.
