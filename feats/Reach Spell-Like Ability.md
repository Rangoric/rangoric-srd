# Reach Spell-Like Ability

You can use a spell-like ability at a greater range.

**Prerequisites**: Spell-like ability at caster level 4th or higher.

**Benefit**: Choose one of your spell-like abilities that qualifies (below). You can use it as a reach spell-like ability three times per day (or fewer, if it’s normally usable only once or twice per day). Its range increases as if by [[rangoric-srd/feats/Reach Spell|Reach Spell]], one or more steps from touch to close, medium and long. Only an ability with a range of touch, close or medium qualifies, and only if it duplicates a spell whose level is no more than half your caster level (round down) – 1 for each step. An ability that needs a melee touch attack needs a ranged touch attack instead.

**Special**: You can take this feat more than once. Each time, choose a different spell-like ability.
