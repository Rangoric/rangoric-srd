# Defensive Weapon Training

You know how to defend yourself against a certain class of weaponry.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, base attack bonus +5.

**Benefit**: Choose a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Groups|weapon group]] (except natural). You gain a +2 dodge bonus to AC when an opponent attacks you with a weapon from that group. If you also have the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-weapon-training|weapon training]] class feature in that group, the bonus is +3.

**Special**: You can take this feat more than once. Its effects don’t stack. Each time, it applies to a different weapon group.
