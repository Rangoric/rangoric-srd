# Ready for Anything

You never let down your guard.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]]), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Initiative|Improved Initiative]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lightning Reflexes|Lightning Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]], base attack bonus +6 or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can always act in the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]], even if you fail a Perception check to notice foes, but you are still [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] until you take an action.
