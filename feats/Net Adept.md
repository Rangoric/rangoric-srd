# Net Adept

You have trained to use the net as a melee weapon.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] (net), base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You can treat a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Net|net]] as a one-handed melee reach weapon with a 10-foot reach. You take no penalty on melee attack rolls for using an unfolded net, and you can fold a net with one full-round action or two move actions.

**Normal**: A net is a ranged weapon with a –4 penalty on attack rolls once it is unfolded. Folding a net takes a proficient user 2 rounds.
