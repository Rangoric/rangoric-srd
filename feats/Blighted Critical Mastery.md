# Blighted Critical Mastery

You control the type of spellblight your critical hits give your opponent.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blighted Critical|Blighted Critical]], caster level 9th.

**Benefit**: Whenever you apply a [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights|spellblight]] by way of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blighted Critical|Blighted Critical]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Blighted Critical|Greater Blighted Critical]] feat, you can choose the spellblight you apply rather than determining it randomly.
