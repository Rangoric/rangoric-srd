# Live off the Land

You can easily pluck what you need from nature’s bounty as you travel.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-terrain|Favored terrain]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You (and any allies whom you assist with the Fieldcraft skill) can move at full speed while using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] to gather food and water, and you gain a +4 bonus on Fieldcraft checks to do so. If you cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Food and Water|create food and water]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]_, or any similar spell that creates edible and potable provisions while you are in your favored terrain, your caster level increases by an amount equal to half your favored terrain bonus.
