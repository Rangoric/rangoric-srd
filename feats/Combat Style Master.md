# Combat Style Master

You shift between combat styles, combining them to increased effect.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], two or more [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^style-feats|style]] feats, base attack bonus +6 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: You can enter a style’s stance, or switch to another, as a free action. At the start of combat, pick one of your styles: you start the combat in its stance, even in the surprise round.

**Normal**: Entering or switching a style’s stance takes a swift action, and you can’t enter one before combat begins (see [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^style-feats|style]] feats).
