# Hero's Fortune

Even at the start of your career, it was clear that you had a chance at greatness, and your legend continues to grow with every adventure.

**Benefit**: You gain a [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points|hero point]]. The maximum number of hero points you can have at any one time is increased to 5.

**Normal**: You can have no more than [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points#^hero-points-maximum|3 hero points]] at one time.

**Special**: NPCs who take this feat receive 1 hero point and can have up to 3 (not 5).
