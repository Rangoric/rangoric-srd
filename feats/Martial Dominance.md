# Martial Dominance

Your skill at arms intimidates your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +5, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: You can use your base attack bonus instead of your ranks in Intimidation to work out your Intimidation bonus. When you confirm a critical hit against a creature, you can make an Intimidation check to demoralize it as an immediate action.
