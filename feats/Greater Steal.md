# Greater Steal

You have a knack for snatching items from your opponents without them noticing.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Steal|Improved Steal]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to steal an item from a foe. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Steal|Improved Steal]]. If you steal an item from a foe during combat, it doesn’t notice until after combat ends or until it tries to use the item.

**Normal**: Creatures automatically notice items taken from them with the steal maneuver.
