# Close-Quarters Thrower

You are agile enough to avoid melee attacks while throwing weapons or bombs.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with the selected thrown weapon.

**Benefit**: Choose a type of thrown weapon. You don’t provoke [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] for making ranged attacks with it.

**Normal**: Making a ranged attack provokes attacks of opportunity.
