# Mighty Rage

Prerequisites: Str 21, Con 21, greater rage class feature, rage 5/day.

Benefit: When the character rages, he or she gains a +8 bonus to Strength and Constitution and a +4 morale bonus on Will Defense. (These bonuses replace the normal rage bonuses.)
