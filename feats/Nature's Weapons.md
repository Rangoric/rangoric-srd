# Nature's Weapons

You can make a weapon out of nearly anything that can be found in the wild.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Catch Off-Guard|Catch Off-Guard]]; base attack bonus +2; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can turn almost anything you find in the wilderness into an improvised melee weapon. It is one-handed and deals 1d6 points of damage for a Medium creature (1d4 for a Small one), bludgeoning, piercing or slashing as the GM rules. Grabbing one takes the same action as drawing a weapon. It must be made of a material that occurs naturally in the terrain.
