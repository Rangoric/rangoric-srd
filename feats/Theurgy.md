# Theurgy

You can blend the power of arcane and divine magic.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, Int or Cha 13, able to cast 1st-level arcane spells, able to cast 1st-level divine spells.

**Benefit**: You can strengthen your divine spells with arcane energy, and your arcane spells with divine energy.

When you cast a divine spell, you can give up an arcane spell slot or prepared arcane spell of that spell’s level or higher as a swift action. The divine spell’s caster level increases by 1.

When you cast an arcane spell, you can give up a divine spell slot or prepared divine spell of the same level or higher as a swift action. Half the damage the arcane spell deals becomes holy (if you channel positive energy) or unholy (if you channel negative energy).
