# Sickening Spell

You can sicken creatures with your spells.

**Benefit**: A creature that takes damage from a sickening spell is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for a number of rounds equal to the spell’s original level. If the spell allows a saving throw, a successful save negates the effect; if it doesn’t, the target can make a Fortitude save to negate it. If the spell already sickens the creature, this duration is added to the spell’s. Spells that deal no damage don’t benefit from this feat. A sickening spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
