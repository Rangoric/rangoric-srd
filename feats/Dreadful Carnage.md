# Dreadful Carnage

Slaying an enemy demoralizes your other nearby foes.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Furious Focus|Furious Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: Whenever you reduce an enemy to 0 or fewer hit points, you can make an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] check to demoralize all enemies within 30 feet as a free action. Enemies that can’t see both you and the enemy you reduced to 0 or fewer hit points are unaffected.
