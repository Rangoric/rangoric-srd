# Boar Shred

The wounds you inflict with your unarmed strikes bleed, giving you renewed vigor.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Boar Ferocity|Boar Ferocity]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Boar Style|Boar Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 9 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can make an Intimidation check to demoralize an opponent as a move action. While using Boar Style, whenever you tear an opponent’s flesh, it takes 1d6 bleed damage once per round at the start of its turn. This bleeding goes on even if you later switch to another style.
