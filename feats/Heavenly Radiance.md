# Heavenly Radiance

Your heavenly light can be used in a variety of ways.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|Aasimar]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]* spell-like ability, sufficiently high level (see below).

**Benefit**: You gain one additional use per day of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*. Choose one spell from the table below; by expending a use of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]*, you may use this spell as a spell-like ability. To select a spell, you must meet the minimum character level for its listing in the table. The save DC for this spell is Charisma-based.

| Spell-Like Ability | Minimum Character Level |
| --- | --- |
| *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare Burst|Flare burst]]* | 1st |
| *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wake of Light|Wake of light]]* | 3rd |
| *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Searing Light|Searing light]]* | 5th |
| *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wandering Star Motes|Wandering star motes]]* | 7th |
| *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sunbeam|Sunbeam]]* (one beam only) | 9th |

**Special**: You may take this feat multiple times. Each time you select it, you gain an additional use of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|daylight]]* and may select another spell from the table.
