# Siege Commander

Under your leadership, the time required to assemble and move a siege engine is greatly reduced.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (siegecraft) 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 5 ranks or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (siege engineer) 1 rank.

**Benefit**: When you lead a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Assembling Siege Engines|siege engine assembly]] crew, you grant all of its members a +4 competence bonus on checks to assemble or move the weapon. You also halve the time required to assemble a siege engine.
