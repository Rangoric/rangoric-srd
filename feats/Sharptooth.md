# Sharptooth

Your teeth are especially sharp and sturdy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|Ratfolk]].

**Benefit**: You gain a bite attack. This is a primary natural attack that deals 1d3 points of damage.
