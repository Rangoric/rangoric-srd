# Cat and Mouse

You can forgo an obvious counterstrike against an opponent to better spot breaches in its defenses.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Opportune Parry and Riposte (Deed)|opportune parry and riposte]] deed.

**Benefit**: When you use the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/deeds/Opportune Parry and Riposte (Deed)|opportune parry and riposte]] deed to successfully parry an opponent’s attack, if you have your immediate action available and choose not to attempt a riposte, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to your AC and a +2 bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] checks against the opponent you successfully parried for 1 round.
