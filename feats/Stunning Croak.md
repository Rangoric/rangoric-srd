# Stunning Croak

The terrifying force of your croak disorients and debilitates your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sonic Croak|Sonic Croak]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Throat Pouch|Throat Pouch]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggards/Boggard|boggard]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggards/Boggard#^terrifying-croak|terrifying croak]].

**Benefit**: When you use your [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggards/Boggard#^terrifying-croak|terrifying croak]] ability, instead of imparting its normal effect you can choose to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stun]] one creature within 30 feet for 1 round. If the croak misses the target’s Fortitude, it is instead [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. The attack bonus of this Fortitude attack is the same as that of your [[rangoric-srd/monsters/boggards/Boggard#^terrifying-croak|terrifying croak]] ability.
