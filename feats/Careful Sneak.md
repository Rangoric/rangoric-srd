# Careful Sneak

By taking your time, you negate penalties while sneaking in most armor.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You don’t apply the armor check penalty for light and medium armor to Stealth checks as long as you move half your speed or less. You still can’t run or charge while using Stealth this way.
