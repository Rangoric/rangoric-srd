# Desperate Swing

You land your most telling blows in desperate situations.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cautious Fighter|Cautious Fighter]], base attack bonus +1, [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]].

**Benefit**: Once per day, you can make a single melee attack while taking the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]] action. You take a –4 penalty on attack rolls when making this attack. You also gain a +4 bonus on critical confirmation rolls made while fighting defensively or making an attack of opportunity using this feat.
