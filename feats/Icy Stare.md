# Icy Stare

Your eyes burn like ice, allowing you to freeze and weaken foes with a glance.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Born of Frost|Born of Frost]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Frost Giant|frost giant]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can direct your icy stare against a single creature or object within 10 feet. The stare attacks a targeted creature’s Fortitude, its attack bonus 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier; on a hit, it takes 1d6 points of cold damage. The stare automatically hits unattended objects. The stare attacks the Fortitude of creatures it damages a second time, and on a hit they take 1 point of Strength damage.

This feat doesn't grant an actual [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Gaze Attacks|gaze attack]]: foes and allies are not in danger simply by meeting your gaze.
