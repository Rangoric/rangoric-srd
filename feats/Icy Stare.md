# Icy Stare

Your eyes burn like ice, allowing you to freeze and weaken foes with a glance.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Born of Frost|Born of Frost]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant/Frost Giant|frost giant]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can direct your icy stare against a single creature or object within 10 feet. Targeted creatures must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier) or take 1d6 points of cold damage. Unattended objects cannot attempt a saving throw. Creatures damaged by the stare must succeed at a Fortitude save or take 1 point of Strength damage.

This feat doesn't grant an actual [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Gaze Attacks|gaze attack]]: foes and allies are not in danger simply by meeting your gaze.
