# Parry Spell

You can throw an enemy spell back at its caster.

**Prerequisites**: 15 ranks in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Counterspell|Improved Counterspell]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you counter a spell, it turns back on its caster, exactly as with the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]]* spell.
