# Onslaught

No one is prepared for how hard you strike until they see it firsthand.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +1, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] class feature.

**Benefit**: If you use Power Attack with the first melee attack you make during a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprise round]], you can sneak attack even if you aren’t flanking your target and it isn’t denied its Dexterity bonus to AC.
