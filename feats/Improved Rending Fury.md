# Improved Rending Fury

Honing the deadliness of your claws, you are a living hurricane of rending fury.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rending Fury|Rending Fury]], base attack bonus +9, rend special attack.

**Benefit**: Whenever you rend an opponent, you deal an extra 1d6 damage. This damage isn’t multiplied on a critical hit.
