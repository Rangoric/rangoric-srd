# Epic Spell Focus

Prerequisites: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Spell Focus|Greater Spell Focus]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] in the school selected, ability to cast at least one 9th-level spell of the school to be chosen.

Benefit: Add +1 to the attack bonus of all spells from the school of magic the character selects to focus on. This stacks with the bonuses from Spell Focus and Greater Spell Focus.

Special: A character can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time the character takes the feat, it applies to a different school of magic.
