# Foeslayer

Your bitter feud with your enemies can be quenched only with blood.

A [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats|story feat]].

**Prerequisites**: You must have been defeated and robbed of at least half your possessions by a particular group of humanoids or monstrous humanoids, or have the An Eye for an Eye, Hated Foe, Raiders, or Vengeance background. You may choose a specific race, such as [[rangoric-srd/races/Duergar|duergar]], or a broader group, such as goblinoids. At the GM’s option, you may instead choose residents of a particular country, settlement, or tribe.

**Benefit**: The save DCs for your spells or abilities increase by 1 when you use them against the chosen group, and you gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC against their attacks.

**Goal**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats#^slay|Slay]] an [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats#^appropriate-number|appropriate number]] of [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats#^challenging-foe|challenging foes]].

**Completion Benefit**: You gain the benefits of the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Critical|Improved Critical]] feat on attacks made against members of your chosen race. Your dodge bonus to AC against such foes increases to +2.
