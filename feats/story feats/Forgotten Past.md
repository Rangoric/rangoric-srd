# Forgotten Past

A pivotal event from your past eludes your memory.

A [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats|story feat]].

**Prerequisites**: You must have suffered permanent memory loss or have the Reincarnated background.

**Benefit**: The duration of mind-affecting spells (even beneficial ones) is halved for you, to a minimum of 1 round. Your inquisitive nature gives you a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in Perception, this bonus increases to +4.

**Goal**: Regain a major portion of your lost memories. The exact means varies, possibly requiring a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]*, assistance from a divine being, reliving a past life, or confronting the situation that led to your memory loss. This process must involve encountering a [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats#^challenging-foe|challenging foe]], though possibly in ways other than direct confrontation.

**Completion Benefit**: You roll twice whenever you attempt a saving throw against a mind-affecting effect, keeping the better result.

**Special**: Restoration of memories by means less significant than *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]* does not qualify for the prerequisite.
