# Vengeance

The need to avenge those you loved drives you to great deeds.

A [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats|story feat]].

**Prerequisites**: You must have a close family member or other loved one slain by a specific [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats#^challenging-foe|challenging foe]] or that foe’s minions, or have the Raiders or Vengeance background.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on saving throws, attack rolls, and weapon damage rolls against your chosen foe and known minions of that foe.

**Goal**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats#^thwart|Thwart]] your chosen foe.

**Completion Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on all saving throws. This bonus stacks with this feat’s bonus against your foe and its minions, should they survive.
