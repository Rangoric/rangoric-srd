# Nation Builder

The land calls to you to conquer it and forge a realm all your own.

A [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats|story feat]].

**Prerequisites**: You must have explored and claimed an area of at least 100 square miles.

**Benefit**: When in either unclaimed wilderness or land under your own control, you receive a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks, and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in one of these skills, the bonus on that skill increases to +4.

**Goal**: Explore and conquer an area of at least 1,000 square miles, in the process [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats#^decisively-defeat|decisively defeating]] a [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats#^challenging-foe|challenging foe]]. You must also lay claim to the land either in your own name or in the name of a leader you directly report to, and establish a stronghold or other fortification to defend it.

**Completion Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on initiative checks, attack rolls, and saving throws while in the area you have claimed.
