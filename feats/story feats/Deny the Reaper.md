# Deny the Reaper

The lives you could not save stay with you to your final breath.

A [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats|story feat]].

**Prerequisites**: You must have witnessed the death of a close companion in battle—a death that could have been prevented, such as from bleeding, failure to stabilize, or ongoing poison damage—or have the Death in the Family or The War background.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in Medicine, this bonus increases to +4. You can apply [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|first aid]] as a move action and don’t take a penalty when [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Deadly Wounds|treating deadly wounds]] without a healing kit.

**Goal**: Bring an ally back from the dead, including by using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reincarnate|reincarnate]]*.

**Completion Benefit**: You and each ally within 10 feet of you gain a +2 bonus on saves against death effects. In addition, once per day you can spontaneously convert any 5th-level or higher conjuration (healing) spell into *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Breath of Life|breath of life]]*.
