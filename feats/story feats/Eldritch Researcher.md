# Eldritch Researcher

You seek new applications for magical energy.

A [[rangoric-srd/feats/story feats/Story Feats|story feat]].

**Prerequisites**: You must have [[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Magic|created a new spell]], or have The Way Things Work background.

**Benefit**: When casting a spell you’ve created, add 1 to your caster level. In addition, you gain a +2 bonus on checks with your [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]]. If you have 10 or more ranks in that skill, this bonus increases to +4.

**Goal**: Create a new spell of at least 6th level.

**Completion Benefit**: The save DCs for any spells you create increase by 1 when you cast them. In addition, when applying metamagic feats to self-created spells, reduce the total level adjustment by 1. You can’t reduce metamagic costs to lower than the spell’s original level in this manner.

**Special**: For a self-created spell to benefit from this feat, it must be a truly novel spell. Spells slightly altered from the original (for example, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Delayed Blast Fireball|delayed blast fireball]]* as compared to *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]*) gain no benefit. [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|Alchemists]] can benefit from this feat as though their formulae and extracts were spells.
