# Drunkard's Recovery

You need only a swallow of strong drink to bring you back from the brink of death.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13.

**Benefit**: If you are dying and a creature gives you at least a sip of alcohol (a standard action for an adjacent creature), you stabilize at once.
