# Deft Shootist Deed

You keep an eye out while focusing on your weapon, allowing you to avoid attacks while shooting and reloading firearms.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|Grit]] class feature or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Gunslinger|Amateur Gunslinger]] feat, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]].

**Benefit**: As long as you have at least 1 grit point, you do not provoke attacks of opportunity when shooting or reloading a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]].
