# Vigilant Charger

You can ready yourself to spring an attack against your enemies at a moment's notice.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]].

**Benefit**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Special Initiative Actions#Ready|Ready]] an action to charge when an enemy enters a space you choose. You must be able to charge that square both when you ready the charge and when it triggers, or you can’t take the readied action. A readied charge moves only up to your speed.

**Normal**: You can’t ready an action to charge. A charge lets you move up to twice your speed.
