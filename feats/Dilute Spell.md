# Dilute Spell

You can reduce the damage done by an area-effect spell and thereby affecting a larger area.

**Benefit**: You can double a spell’s area and reduce the damage it deals to 25% of the amount rolled. For example, a diluted _fireball_ fills a 40-foot-radius sphere, but its damage is a quarter of the total rolled. A diluted spell uses a spell slot one level higher than its actual level.
