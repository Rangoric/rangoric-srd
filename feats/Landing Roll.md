# Landing Roll

You can roll away from a foe who knocks you down.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]].

**Benefit**: If you are [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|tripped]], you can spend an immediate action to move 5 feet without provoking an attack of opportunity. This isn’t a 5-foot step. You fall prone after this movement.
