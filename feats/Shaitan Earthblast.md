# Shaitan Earthblast

With a forceful stomp you release a blast of acid from the earth to burn your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, Wis 17, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shaitan Style|Shaitan Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shaitan Skin|Shaitan Skin]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +13 or monk level 11th.

**Benefit**: While using Shaitan Style, as a standard action you can spend two Elemental Fist attempts to unleash a column of acid 20 feet high with a 5-foot radius, erupting from a point within 30 feet of you. Creatures in it take your unarmed strike damage plus your Elemental Fist acid damage, and are staggered for 1 round. A Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) halves the damage and avoids being staggered.
