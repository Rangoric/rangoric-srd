# Greater Hunter's Bond

Your allies can target additional foes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Hunter's Bond|Improved Hunter's Bond]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-hunters-bond|hunter’s bond]] (hunting companions) class feature, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]] level 12th.

**Benefit**: When you activate hunter’s bond, you can select a number of targets equal to your Wisdom modifier as long as they all count as your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]].

**Normal**: You select one target against which your allies receive your favored enemy bonus.
