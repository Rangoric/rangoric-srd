# Sign of the Sword

The Sword marked you three times at birth.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Martial Weapon Proficiency|Proficiency with all martial weapons]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls with swords, and take a −1 penalty on attack rolls with all other weapons.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level. You have three scar-like birthmarks: one on each shoulder and one on the top of your head.
