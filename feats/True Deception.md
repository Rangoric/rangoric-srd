# True Deception

Time spent learning the mystic arts of disguise has given you insight into the anonymity of the everyday.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 17, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] 17 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/ninja-tricks/Master Disguise (Ninja Trick)|master disguise]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-master-tricks|master ninja trick]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Master of Disguise (Rogue Talent)|master of disguise]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-advanced-talents|advanced rogue talent]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja|ninja]] level 10th or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue|rogue]] level 10th.

**Benefit**: When using the master disguise master ninja trick or the master of disguise advanced rogue talent to take on the appearance of a specific individual, you can fool divination magic and effects designed to locate the subject of your disguise as per the vigilante’s [[rangoric-srd/class-options/social-talents/Any Guise (Social Talent)|any guise]] social talent. In addition, you gain a +10 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to act like that individual.
