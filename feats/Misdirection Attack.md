# Misdirection Attack

After misdirecting your opponent's weapon attack, you leave your opponent open to further violence.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Misdirection Redirection|Misdirection Redirection]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Misdirection Tactics|Misdirection Tactics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 10 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you use Misdirection Tactics to make a melee weapon attack miss, the opponent provokes an attack of opportunity from you, even though you normally can’t make attacks of opportunity while using total defense. This is on top of Misdirection Redirection’s effect.
