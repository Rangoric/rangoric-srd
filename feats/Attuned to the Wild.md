# Attuned to the Wild

You share a strong mystic connection with one type of wilderness terrain.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]].

**Benefit**: Select one type of terrain from the ranger’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^table-ranger-favored-terrains|favored terrain]] class feature (except urban). While you are in your selected terrain type, your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Healing|natural healing]] rate (the amount of hit points and ability damage you heal from a full night’s rest) doubles.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take it, it applies to a different terrain type.
