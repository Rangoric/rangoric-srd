# One Eye Open

Your senses seek out threats even while you sleep.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Perception).

**Benefit**: The DCs of your Perception checks don’t increase while you are asleep. If you succeed at a Perception check to notice something dangerous while asleep, you can wake up to face it.

**Normal**: A sleeping creature takes a –10 penalty on Perception checks to hear.

**Special**: This overlaps [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception#Unlocks|Perception]]’s unlocks: the 5-rank unlock halves the asleep penalty and the 15-rank unlock removes it. This feat removes it without the ranks.
