# Insightful Advice

You teach others how to make the most of their skills.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can use [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] to help an ally within 30 feet with a skill in which you are trained. It takes 1 minute, and you need only speak and be heard. The bonus is +2, whatever else would change your aid another bonus, and it applies to all the ally’s checks with that skill for 1 day. It doesn’t stack with other aid another bonuses. Whether you succeed or fail, you can use this only once per day for each ally.
