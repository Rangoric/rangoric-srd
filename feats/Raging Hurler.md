# Raging Hurler

An opponent can do little to evade your wrathful pitching of weapons and objects.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|Rage]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Throw Anything|Throw Anything]].

**Benefit**: While raging, you can throw a two-handed weapon as a standard action, and you double the range increment for weapons you throw. If you also have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]] feat, you can throw two-handed weapons at your full normal rate of attacks. Further, you can pick up an unattended object within your reach that you can use as an improvised weapon as part of the attack action to throw it.
