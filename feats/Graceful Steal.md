# Graceful Steal

You can steal with surprising subtlety.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Agile Maneuvers|Agile Maneuvers]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Steal|Improved Steal]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you make [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks to lift or palm objects, you can use your CMB for a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Steal|steal]] combat maneuver in place of your Sleight of Hand modifier, using your Dexterity modifier rather than your Strength modifier. You can also steal items hidden in a bag or pack with the steal maneuver (as long as you can reach in), but the opponent gains at least a +5 bonus to its CMD, as for a fastened object.
