# Selective Spell

Your allies need not fear friendly fire.

**Prerequisites**: 10 ranks in a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]].

**Benefit**: When you cast a selective spell that has an area and an instantaneous duration, you can choose a number of creatures in the area up to the modifier of the ability score that gives you bonus spells for that spell. They are excluded from its effects. Spells without an area don’t benefit from this feat. A selective spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
