# Quick Favor

You can quickly take advantage of your charm.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation).

**Benefit**: Once per day, when you use Persuasion to gather information again just after you last did so in the same place (whether or not that attempt worked), it takes 4d10 minutes instead.

**Normal**: Gathering information takes 1d4 hours.
