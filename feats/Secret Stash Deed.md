# Secret Stash Deed

You are so skilled at stashing small packets of firearm ammunition and black powder on your person that you sometimes surprise yourself when you find them.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|Grit]] class feature or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Gunslinger|Amateur Gunslinger]] feat, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Spend 1 grit point while in combat to recover either 1 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Firearm Bullet|bullet]] and 1 dose of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Black Powder|black powder]] or 1 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^alchemical-cartridges|alchemical cartridge]] from a hidden stash on your person that you had, until now, forgotten about. If the bullet and black powder or the alchemical cartridges are normal shot, you do not need to pay for the ammunition. If you want to recover any other kind of ammunition, you must pay for it with gold pieces from your character's wealth. The grit cost of this deed cannot be decreased by the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Signature Deed|Signature Deed]] feat, the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-true-grit|true grit]] class feature, or any other similar effect that reduces the number of grit points you spend to use a deed. You also gain a +4 bonus on any Sleight of Hand checks made while gambling.
