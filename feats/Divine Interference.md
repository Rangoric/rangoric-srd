# Divine Interference

You can convert a spell to interfere with an enemy's attack.

**Prerequisites**: Divine spellcaster, caster level 10th.

**Benefit**: As an immediate action, when an enemy within 30 feet hits an ally with an attack, you can give up a prepared divine spell (or, if you cast spontaneously, an unused spell slot) of 1st level or higher to make the enemy reroll the attack roll, with a penalty equal to the spell’s level. Whether or not the second roll hits, you can’t use this on the same creature again for 1 day.
