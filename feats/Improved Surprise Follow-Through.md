# Improved Surprise Follow-Through

You follow up an attack with a surprising series of sweeping blows.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Cleave|Great Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Surprise Follow-Through|Surprise Follow-Through]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: When using Great Cleave, each opponent you attack on your turn other than the first is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against you.
