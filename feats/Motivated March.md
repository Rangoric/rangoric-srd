# Motivated March

Years of driving troops far beyond the limits of their endurance have made you an excellent taskmaster.

**Benefit**: When traveling overland, you and up to 10 allies per character level take half the nonlethal damage from a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Carrying, Movement, and Exploration#Overland Movement|forced march]]. Creatures benefiting from this feat can put off becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] from a forced march for a number of hours equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1). A creature that does becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]] instead of fatigued when it sets in.
