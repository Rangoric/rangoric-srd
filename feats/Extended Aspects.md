# Extended Aspects

You can invoke your aspect’s minor form longer than most.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|Shifter aspect]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Add your Wisdom bonus (minimum 1) to the number of minutes per day that you can use your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-aspect|shifter aspect]] ability to assume a minor form.
