# Sabotaging Sunder

Your can quickly sabotage a foe's worn or carried equipment.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Sunder|Improved Sunder]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 7 ranks.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can sabotage an item a foe is wearing with a special [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] combat maneuver. You use it in melee against an item that is neither held nor hidden in a bag, pack or other container. You need at least one hand free, and it provokes an attack of opportunity from the target even though you have Improved Sunder.

For the check, use your ranks in Engineering in place of your base attack bonus, and your Dexterity modifier in place of your Strength modifier. Bonuses on checks to sunder (such as Improved Sunder’s) apply, and so do the target’s bonuses to CMD against sundering. If you succeed, instead of dealing damage you sabotage the item, giving it the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition; an item that was already broken is destroyed or falls off. Items immune to sunder are immune to this, and it still can’t destroy or remove an item, such as an artifact, that can be destroyed or removed only by specific means.

**Normal**: A successful sunder deals weapon damage to the sundered item.
