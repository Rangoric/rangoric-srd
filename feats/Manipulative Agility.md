# Manipulative Agility

You can use your agility to manipulate people as well as small objects.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: You can use Sleight of Hand instead of Deception to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] in combat and to pass secret messages unnoticed (with gestures and body language). Either way, the effect is visual and doesn’t work if the target or the recipient can’t see you. The GM may limit what messages hand gestures and body language can carry.
