# Earth Child Style

Your martial training makes you a dangerous and elusive target for giants.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, dwarf or gnome, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using this style, your racial dodge bonus to AC against giants increases to +6, and you add your Wisdom bonus to your unarmed strike damage rolls against giants.
