# Agile Maneuvers

You've learned to use your quickness in place of brute force when performing combat maneuvers.

**Benefit**: You add your Dexterity modifier to your base attack bonus and size modifier to find your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|CMB]], instead of your Strength modifier.

**Normal**: You add your Strength modifier to your base attack bonus and size modifier to find your CMB.
