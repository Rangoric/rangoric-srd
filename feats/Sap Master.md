# Sap Master

You knock the sense out of foes with a well-timed surprise attack.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|Sneak attack]] +3d6, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sap Adept|Sap Adept]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you use a bludgeoning weapon to deal nonlethal sneak attack damage to a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]] opponent, roll your sneak attack dice twice, totaling the results as your nonlethal sneak attack damage for that attack.
