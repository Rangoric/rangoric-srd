# Extra Arcana

You have unlocked the secret of a new magus arcana.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-magus-arcana|Magus arcana]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [[rangoric-srd/class-options/magus-arcana/Magus Arcana|magus arcana]]. You must meet all the prerequisites for this magus arcana.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects stack, granting a new arcana each time you gain this feat.
