# Unsanctioned Detection

You can focus your ability to detect evil for more practical or mundane purposes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|Detect evil]] class feature.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, you can focus the clarity granted by your *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]* ability to heighten your awareness of other things. This gives you a +10 sacred bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks for one round. This expends your use of the detect evil class ability for the next 24 hours.
