# Intoxicating Flattery

You know how to compromise a target's judgment with lies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can flatter a creature over at least 1 minute to puff up its sense of its own worth and cloud its judgment. At the end, make a Deception check against a DC of 10 + its Hit Dice + its Wisdom modifier, or 10 + its Sense Motive modifier, whichever is higher. If you succeed, it takes a –2 penalty on Will saves, Wisdom-based skill checks and Wisdom checks for 1 hour, plus 1 hour for every 5 by which you beat the DC. It can end the effect early by taking 10 minutes to compose itself. If you fail, anyone who saw you fail is immune to your flattery for 24 hours. If you fail by 5 or more, the target’s attitude toward you worsens by one step.
