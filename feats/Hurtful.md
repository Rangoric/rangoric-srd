# Hurtful

You know how to add injury to insult.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]].

**Benefit**: When you [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] an opponent within your melee reach, you can make one melee attack against it as a swift action. If the attack doesn’t damage it, its [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] condition from being demoralized ends at once.
