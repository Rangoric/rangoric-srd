# Monkey Style

Your unarmed fighting style is nimble and unpredictable, full of ground rolls and short leaps.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You add your Wisdom bonus to Acrobatics checks. While using this style, you take no penalty on melee attack rolls or to AC while [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. You can crawl and stand up from prone without provoking attacks of opportunity, and you can stand up as a swift action with a DC 20 Acrobatics check.

**Normal**: While prone, you take a –4 penalty on melee attack rolls and to AC against melee attacks. Standing up is a move action that provokes attacks of opportunity.
