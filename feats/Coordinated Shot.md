# Coordinated Shot

Your ranged attacks against an opponent take advantage of your ally’s positioning.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]].

**Benefit**: If your ally with this feat is threatening an opponent and is not providing [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|cover]] to that opponent against your ranged attacks, you gain a +1 bonus on ranged attacks against that opponent. If your ally with this feat is [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanking]] that opponent with another ally (even if that other ally doesn’t have this feat), this bonus increases to +2.
