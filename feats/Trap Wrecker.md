# Trap Wrecker

You can smash traps instead of disarming them.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc|orc]].

**Benefit**: You can attempt to disarm a trap by striking it with a melee weapon instead of making an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] check. As a full-round action, make a melee attack against an Armor Class equal to the trap’s Engineering DC. If you miss, the trap activates. If you hit, roll damage. If this damage is at least half the trap’s Engineering DC, you disable the trap. If this damage is less than half the trap’s Engineering DC, the trap activates. You can only attempt this on nonmagical traps. You must be able to reach some part of the trap with your attack in order to use this feat. At the GM’s discretion, some traps may not be susceptible to this feat.
