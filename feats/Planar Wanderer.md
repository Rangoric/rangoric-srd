# Planar Wanderer

Your knowledge of the planes and teleportation allows you to travel with more precision.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cartogramancer|Cartogramancer]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] 10 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 10 ranks, ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]*.

**Benefit**: When you cast *plane shift* to a plane you have studied, you can attempt a DC 25 Theology check. On a success, you arrive 5d20 miles from where you meant to go instead of 5d% miles. If you beat the DC by 10 or more, you arrive 5d10 miles away; by 20 or more, 5d6 miles away.
