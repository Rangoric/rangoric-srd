# Mutual Hatred

Others hate you, and their hatred feeds your own.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls against creatures whose racial bonus against your kind, or whose favored enemy class feature, applies to you. For example, an orc gains this bonus against dwarves (whose racial attack bonus applies against orcs) and against rangers whose favored enemy is orcs.
