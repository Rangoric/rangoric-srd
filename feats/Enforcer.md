# Enforcer

You are skilled at causing fear in those you brutalize.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Whenever you deal nonlethal damage with a melee weapon, you can make an Intimidation check to demoralize your target as a free action. If you succeed, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for a number of rounds equal to the damage dealt. If the attack was a critical hit and you succeed, it is also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] for 1 round.
