# Insightful Delivery

Your knowledge of where to strike makes your poisons more deadly.

**Prerequisites**: Poison use class feature ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-poison-use|alchemist]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ninja#^ninja-poison-use|ninja]]), [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]] +4d6.

**Benefit**: When you use a poisoned weapon to attempt an attack in conjunction with [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]], the DC to resist the poison increases by half the number of your studied strike dice.
