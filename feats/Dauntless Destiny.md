# Dauntless Destiny

Your ability to avert disaster is impressive.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fearless Curiosity|Fearless Curiosity]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 10 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on defenses against effects with the emotion descriptor; this bonus stacks with those granted by [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fearless Curiosity|Fearless Curiosity]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intimidating Confidence|Intimidating Confidence]]. In addition, once per day you may make an attacker reroll a natural 20 on an attack against your defense, or reroll a natural 1 on an attack roll. If the reroll misses you or your attack roll hits, as a free action you can make an Intimidation check to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] the target of your attack or the creature whose attack you made it reroll, as long as the creature is within 30 feet and can see and hear you. This effect does not apply to attacks by traps or other objects. This does not stack with other effects that allow you to make an attacker reroll or reroll an attack roll. You may only make one reroll.
