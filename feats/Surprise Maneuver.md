# Surprise Maneuver

Your maneuvers are devastating against disadvantaged foes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]]; [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] +3d6 or studied strike +3d6.

**Benefit**: When you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuver]] check against a creature that you are flanking or that is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against your attack, you gain a bonus on the check equal to the number of sneak attack dice you roll.
