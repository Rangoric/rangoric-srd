# Starry Grace

Your quick and free starknife motions allow you to make deadly dexterous attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Finesse|Weapon Finesse]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (starknife).

**Benefit**: When wielding a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^starknife|starknife]], you can add your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier to its damage. The starknife must be one made for your size. You don’t gain this benefit while fighting with two weapons or using flurry of blows, or any time your other hand is otherwise occupied.

In addition, if you have the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] class feature, as long as you have at least 1 panache point, you gain a +5 bonus to your movement speed on your move action after taking an attack action with a starknife or on your [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spring Attack|Spring Attack]] or charge with a starknife.
