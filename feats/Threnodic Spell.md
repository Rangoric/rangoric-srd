# Threnodic Spell

You can convert mind-affecting magic to necromantic power capable of controlling undead.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (necromancy).

**Benefit**: A threnodic spell affects undead (even mindless undead) as if they weren’t immune to mind-affecting effects, but it has no effect on living creatures. Only mind-affecting spells can be threnodic. A threnodic spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell’s actual level.

**Normal**: Undead are immune to mind-affecting effects.
