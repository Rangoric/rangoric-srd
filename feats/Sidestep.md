# Sidestep

You can reposition yourself after a foe’s missed swing.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]].

**Benefit**: Whenever an opponent misses you with a melee attack, you can move 5 feet as an immediate action, as long as you stay within that opponent’s threatened area. This movement doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity. If you do, you can’t take a 5-foot step during your next turn, and if you take an action to move during your next turn, subtract 5 feet from your total movement.
