# Snapping Turtle Shell

Your guarding hand is almost magical in its skill at deflecting incoming blows.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snapping Turtle Clutch|Snapping Turtle Clutch]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Snapping Turtle Style|Snapping Turtle Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +5 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: While using Snapping Turtle Style, its shield bonus to AC increases to +2, and your enemies take a –4 penalty on rolls to confirm critical hits against you.
