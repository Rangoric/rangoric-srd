# Dragon Roar

The spirit of the dragon wells up inside you and bursts forth in a mighty roar.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dragon Style|Dragon Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 8 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain one more Stunning Fist attempt per day. While using Dragon Style, as a standard action you can spend two Stunning Fist attempts to roar in a 15-foot cone. Creatures in it take your unarmed strike damage and are shaken for 1d4 rounds. A Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) halves the damage and avoids being shaken.

**Special**: If you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]], you can spend a daily use of it to add your Elemental Fist damage to the roar. That damage isn’t halved on a save.
