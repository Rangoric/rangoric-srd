# Exhausting Critical

Your critical hits cause opponents to become exhausted.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tiring Critical|Tiring Critical]], base attack bonus +15.

**Benefit**: When you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] on a foe, your target immediately becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]]. This feat has no effect on exhausted creatures.
