# Touch of Evil

You instill unbidden thoughts and desires into another with your touch.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel negative energy]] of 6d6 or more, evil alignment.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can spend three uses of channel negative energy and make a melee touch attack. You can make an opposed [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] check against the target’s [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] to do it unnoticed. If the attack hits, the target must make a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Cha modifier). On a failure, you implant a suggestion, like *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Suggestion|suggestion]]*, except that it can be to do something harmful or destructive to another creature or object. If so, the target tries to carry it out in 1d6+1 days as it thinks it over and plans, and can make another Will save at the same DC just before it acts. If that fails, it gains a +4 circumstance bonus on one die roll made carrying out the suggestion. The planning costs it 1 point of Wisdom damage until the suggestion is carried out.
