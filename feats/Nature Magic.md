# Nature Magic

You are able to use simple spells by drawing on nature's raw majesty.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: You gain _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Know Direction|know direction]]_ as a constant spell-like ability, and one other druid orison of your choice as a spell-like ability once per day. Your caster level for both equals your character level.
