# Spawnlink

You can see through the eyes of your spawned minions.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, the create spawn ability.

**Benefit**: As a full-round action, you can link with one of the spawn you control on the same plane; its eyes glow bright red. While linked, you see through its eyes with its vision or yours, whichever is better, and your own body is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]]. You can also speak through your spawn (if it can speak) in your own voice. You can keep the link each round as a standard action, as if concentrating on a 3rd-level spell. If the spawn is reduced to 0 hit points or fewer while linked, you are blinded and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1d6 rounds, with no save.
