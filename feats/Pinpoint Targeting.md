# Pinpoint Targeting

You can target the weak points in your opponent's armor.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 19, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Precise Shot|Improved Precise Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]], base attack bonus +16.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, make a single ranged attack. The target gets no armor, natural armor or shield bonus to its AC against it. You don’t gain this benefit if you move this round.
