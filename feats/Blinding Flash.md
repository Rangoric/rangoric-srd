# Blinding Flash

You can temporarily blind your opponent by using your weapon or shield to flash sunlight into its eyes.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]].

**Benefit**: As a move action, you can angle the blade of your weapon or shield to reflect light into an opponent’s eyes, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzling]] it for 1 round. This works as a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Gaze Attacks|gaze attack]], and the target can resist with a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Dexterity modifier). It is a sight-dependent effect and doesn’t work on creatures that are already blinded or that don’t rely on sight as their main sense. You must be wielding a weapon or shield with a polished or reflective surface, in an area of bright light.
