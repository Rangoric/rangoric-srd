# Witch Knife

You empower your witch spells by incorporating the use of a special ceremonial knife during your castings.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch|Witch]] class.

**Benefit**: Each day, when you prepare your spells, you can select a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Masterwork Weapons|masterwork]] or magical dagger, transforming it into a witch knife, which serves as an additional focus component for witch [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-patron|patron]] spells. Add +1 to the DC of all your patron spells.
