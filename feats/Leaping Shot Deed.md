# Leaping Shot Deed

You leap through the air, guns blazing.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]] class feature or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Gunslinger|Amateur Gunslinger]] feat, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks made to jump. As a full-round action, you can move up to your speed and make firearm attacks at your highest base attack bonus with each loaded firearm you are wielding. You can make these attacks at any point during your movement, and if you are wielding two firearms, you can make the attacks at different points during the movement. At the end of this movement, you fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. This deed costs 1 grit point to perform.
