# Ledge Walker

You negotiate tiny ledges like a mountain goat.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Mountaineer (Racial Trait)|mountaineer]] or [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf#^stability|stability]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: You can move at full speed while using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] to balance on narrow surfaces, and you gain a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to catch yourself or another creature while falling. You also gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against effects that would cause you to fall [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] (such as earthquakes). This bonus does not apply to your CMD against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks.
