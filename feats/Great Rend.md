# Great Rend

You use your teeth and claws to rip your foes to shreds.

**Prerequisites**: Bite attack, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Rend|rend]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/troll/Troll|troll]].

**Benefit**: If you are able to use your [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Rend|rend]] ability on the same turn that you also succeed at a bite attack, your rend deals an amount of additional damage equal to half your Strength modifier.
