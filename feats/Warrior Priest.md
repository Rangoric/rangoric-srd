# Warrior Priest

Your religion is both a shield and a weapon in battle.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast divine spells, [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Domains|domain]] or mystery class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on initiative checks and a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] made to cast a spell or use a spell-like ability when casting defensively or while grappled.
