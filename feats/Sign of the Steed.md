# Sign of the Steed

The Steed stood over your birth, and the road is always easy for you.

**Benefit**: Terrain never slows your [[rangoric-srd/rules/Carrying, Movement, and Exploration#Overland Movement|overland movement]] below ×1/2.

**Normal**: The worst terrain cuts overland movement to ×1/4.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
