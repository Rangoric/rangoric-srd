# Disruptive

Your training makes it difficult for enemy spellcasters to safely cast spells near you.

**Prerequisites**: Fighter level 6th.

**Benefit**: The DC to cast spells [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|on the defensive]] increases by 4 for all enemies within your threatened area. This applies only if you know where the enemy is and can make an attack of opportunity against it; if you can make only one attack of opportunity per round and have already used it, it doesn’t apply.
