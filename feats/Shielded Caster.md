# Shielded Caster

Your allies cover you while you cast complicated spells.

**Benefit**: Whenever you are adjacent to an ally who also has this feat, you receive a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]]. If your ally is wielding a buckler or a light shield, this bonus increases by +1. If your ally is wielding a heavy shield or a tower shield, this bonus increases by +2. Finally, if an enemy threatening you and your ally has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disruptive|Disruptive]] feat, or another ability that increases the DC of concentration checks, the amount of the increase is halved.
