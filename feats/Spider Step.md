# Spider Step

Your physical mastery grants you an impossible stride.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] level 6th.

**Benefit**: As a move action, you can move up to half your slow fall distance across a wall or ceiling, or across ropes, branches, or even water or other surfaces that can’t hold your weight. You must reach a solid, level surface by the end of your turn or you fall.
