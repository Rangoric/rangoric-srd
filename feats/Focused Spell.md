# Focused Spell

When you cast a spell that affects more than one creature, one opponent finds it more difficult to resist.

**Benefit**: When you cast a spell that affects or targets more than one creature, choose one of them before casting. The spell’s attack bonus against that creature increases by 2. Spells that don’t attack a defense don’t benefit from this feat. A focused spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
