# Out of the Sun

You and your allies use the sun’s glare to your advantage.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you hit with a melee attack, you can attempt a Deception check as a move action to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]]. You do not gain the benefits of this feint; instead, if you succeed at your Deception check, an ally with this feat who is adjacent to you or your opponent gains the benefits, such that the opponent is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC (if any) against your ally’s next melee attack against that opponent. This attack must be made before the end of your ally’s next turn. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on your Deception check in bright light. This bonus increases to +4 in natural sunlight. You cannot use this feat in dim light or darkness.

**Special**: If you have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]] feat, you can feint with Out of the Sun as a swift action after you hit with a melee attack.
