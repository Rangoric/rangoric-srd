# Sign of the Fan

The Fan stood over your birth, and you turn a profit where others don’t.

**Benefit**: A week of dedicated work earns you gold pieces equal to your whole [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] check result.

**Normal**: A week's work earns half your Profession check result in gold pieces.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
