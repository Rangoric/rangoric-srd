# Punishing Kick

Your kicks are so powerful you use them to push or knock back your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13, Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: You must declare that you are using this feat before you make your attack roll (so a miss wastes the attempt). On a hit, the attack deals damage normally and you can either push the target 5 feet or try to knock it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. A pushed target moves 5 feet directly away from you; this movement doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity, and the target must end it in a safe space it can stand in. A target you try to knock prone can avoid it with a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier). You can attempt a Punishing Kick once per day for every four character levels you have (but see Special), and no more than once per round.

**Special**: A [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] can attempt a Punishing Kick a number of times per day equal to her monk level, plus one more time for every four levels she has in other classes.
