# Distracting Charge

Your ally’s charge exposes an opening you can exploit.

**Benefit**: When your ally with this feat uses the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] action and hits, you gain a +2 bonus on your next attack roll against the target of that charge. This bonus must be used before your ally’s next turn, or it is lost.
