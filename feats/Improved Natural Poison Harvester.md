# Improved Natural Poison Harvester

You excel at harvesting poison from the many toxic creatures of the natural world.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Natural Poison Harvester|Natural Poison Harvester]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) 9 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] 9 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you harvest poison from a hazard or dead creature, you gain 1d4 more doses.
