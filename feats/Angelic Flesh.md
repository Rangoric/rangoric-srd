# Angelic Flesh

Your skin shines like burnished metal.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Angelic Blood|Angelic Blood]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|aasimar]].

**Benefit**: You take a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks but gain one of the following benefits, depending on the metallic affinity of your flesh (choose one).

- **Brazen**: You gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|fire resistance]] 5 and a +2 bonus on saves against fire effects.
- **Golden**: You gain a +2 bonus on saves against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blindness]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzling]], patterns, and effects with the light descriptor. When you cast spells or use spell-like abilities that are from the illusion (pattern) subschool or have the light descriptor, you do so at +1 caster level.
- **Silver**: You gain a +2 bonus on saves against paralysis, petrification, and poison, and your unarmed strikes or natural weapons count as silver for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].
- **Steel**: You gain a +1 natural armor bonus to AC, and your unarmed strikes or natural weapons count as cold iron for the purpose of overcoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]].
