# Escape Route

You have trained to watch your allies’ backs, covering them as they make tactical withdraws.

**Benefit**: An ally who also has this feat provokes no [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attacks of opportunity]] for moving through squares adjacent to you or within your space.
