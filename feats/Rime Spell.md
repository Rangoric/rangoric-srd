# Rime Spell

Creatures damaged by your spells with the cold descriptor become entangled.

**Benefit**: The frost of your cold spell clings to the target. A creature that takes cold damage from a rime spell is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] for a number of rounds equal to the spell’s original level. Only spells with the cold descriptor can be rime spells. A rime spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
