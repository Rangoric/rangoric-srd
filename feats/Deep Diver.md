# Deep Diver

You are used to diving deeper than most would dare, and you run less risk of drowning than even other experienced swimmers.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 bonus on checks to dive into water without taking damage (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Falling|Falling into Water]]) and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks in dim light or darkness underwater. When you succeed on an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check to swim downward (at least 45 degrees below the horizontal), you can swim half your speed as a move action, or your speed as a full-round action. If you have a swim speed, it increases by 10 feet when you spend a move action to swim only downward.

You can hold your breath half again as long as normal, and you gain a +4 bonus on Constitution checks to keep holding it after that (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Drowning|Drowning]]) and on checks and saves against the cold of deep water. You take half damage from water pressure (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Water Dangers|Water Dangers]]).
