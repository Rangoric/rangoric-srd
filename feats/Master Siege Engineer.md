# Master Siege Engineer

You are significantly faster at loading a siege engine, as well as a better shot.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Siege Engineer|Siege Engineer]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] 10 ranks.

**Benefit**: If you are the crew lead on a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines|siege engine]], your crew can use move actions to load a siege engine. When you spend actions to aim a siege engine, you and your crew can use move actions instead of full-round actions to aim the siege engine.

**Normal**: Full-round actions are required to load and aim siege engines.
