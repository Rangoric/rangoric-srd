# Ray Shield

You can even deflect rays with your shield.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Missile Shield|Missile Shield]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spellbreaker|Spellbreaker]].

**Benefit**: While using a light, heavy or tower shield, once per round when you would be hit by a ranged touch attack (including rays and similar magical effects), you can deflect it so you take no damage from it. Your shield suffers the full effects of the spell or effect, if any.
