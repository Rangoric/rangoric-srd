# Aura Flare

You are able to flare your alignment aura to debilitate your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13; the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-aura|aura]] class feature (or a paladin’s or blackguard’s aura of good or evil); [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]] of 4d6 or more; a strong or overwhelming good or evil aura.

**Benefit**: Once per day when you channel energy, you can make your divine aura flare, affecting all good creatures in it (if you channel negative energy) or all evil ones (if you channel positive energy), on top of the channel’s normal effect. Choose one option for your aura’s strength or a weaker one: with a **strong** aura, affected creatures must succeed on a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your level in the class that grants channel energy + your Cha modifier) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] for 1d4 rounds; with an **overwhelming** aura, the same save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1d4 rounds.
