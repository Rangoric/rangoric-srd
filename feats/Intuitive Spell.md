# Intuitive Spell

You can avoid the need for thought components by trusting your instincts.

**Benefit**: An intuitive spell can be cast with no [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#^thought-component|thought components]]. Spells without thought components are not affected. An intuitive spell uses up a spell slot 1 level higher than the spell’s actual level.
