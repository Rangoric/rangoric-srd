# Cautious Fighter

You care more about survival than victory.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]].

**Benefit**: When fighting defensively or using [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Total Defense|total defense]], your dodge bonus to AC increases by 2.
