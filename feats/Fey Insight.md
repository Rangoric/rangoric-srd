# Fey Insight

You have insight into the weird, alien minds of fey and can use this knowledge to more handily negotiate with or manipulate the capricious creatures.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks when dealing with fey. If you have 10 or more ranks in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.
