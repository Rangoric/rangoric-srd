# Nimble Striker

You were born to charge your enemies and nobody does it better.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, base attack bonus +1, [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|catfolk]], [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Sprinter|sprinter]] racial trait.

**Benefit**: You do not take a –2 penalty to AC when you use the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]] feat, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Lunge|Lunge]] feat, or when you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]].
