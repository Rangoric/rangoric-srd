# Cosmopolitan

Living in large, exotic cities has put you in touch with many diverse civilizations, cultures, and races.

**Benefit**: You can speak and read two more languages of your choice. Also choose two Intelligence-, Wisdom- or Charisma-based skills. Those skills always count as class skills for you.
