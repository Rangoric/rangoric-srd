# Crippling Critical

You are able to maim a target and hinder its movement.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], base attack bonus +13.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] against an opponent, its speed is halved for 1 minute. This attacks its Fortitude, its attack bonus your base attack bonus; on a miss, the duration is 1d4 rounds instead. Against creatures with multiple types of movement, you must choose which movement type to affect. A flying creature hit by this attack must make a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|Acrobatics]] check to remain airborne, and has its [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|maneuverability]] reduced by one step.
