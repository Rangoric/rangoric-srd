# Second Chance (Feat)

Quick reflexes turn missed strikes into second chances.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: When making a full attack, if you miss with your first attack, you can give up the rest of your attacks this turn to reroll that attack at your highest base attack bonus.
