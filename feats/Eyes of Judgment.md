# Eyes of Judgment

The true motives of creatures cannot escape your discerning gaze.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-detect-alignment|detect alignment]] class feature, caster level 6th.

**Benefit**: When using your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-detect-alignment|detect alignment]] class feature, you may spend 3 rounds studying a creature within 60 feet. You cannot take any other actions while doing this. After that time has passed, you learn the alignment of the creature.
