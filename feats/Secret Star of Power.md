# Secret Star of Power

You know the Secret Star of Power, which makes another star’s light fiercer.

**Benefit**: Every die rolled for a star or constellation goes up one size: d4s become d6s, d6s become d8s, d8s become d10s and d10s become d12s. A d12 stays a d12 but gains +1 per die. The star or constellation uses up a spell slot two levels higher than its call level would.
