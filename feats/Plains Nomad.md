# Plains Nomad

You are a native of the wide and endless prairie, savanna, or steppe.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-terrain|Favored terrain]] (plains) class feature.

**Benefit**: You and a mount you are riding gain a +2 bonus on Constitution checks to avoid damage and fatigue from [[rangoric-srd/rules/Carrying, Movement, and Exploration#Overland Movement|hustling or a forced march]]. In [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Plains Terrain|plains terrain]], this bonus is doubled and also applies on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to avoid [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Getting Lost|getting lost]], to find food and water, to protect yourself from severe weather, and to predict the weather.
