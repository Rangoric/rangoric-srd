# Sickening Critical

Your critical hits cause opponents to become sickened.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]], your opponent becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute. The effects of this feat do not stack. Additional hits instead add to the effect’s duration.
