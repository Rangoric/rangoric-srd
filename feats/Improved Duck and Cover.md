# Improved Duck and Cover

Your allies’ ability to protect you from damage extends to area attacks.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Duck and Cover|Duck and Cover]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you use Duck and Cover, your ally has [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-evasion|evasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-improved-evasion|improved evasion]], and the attack misses your ally, half of the damage you would have taken is transferred to your ally. (This damage is not reduced by the ally’s evasion or improved evasion.)
