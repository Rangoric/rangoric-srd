# Ferocious Action

Your ferocity is quick but shorter lived.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc#^orc-ferocity|Ferocity]] racial trait, [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc|orc]].

**Benefit**: When you fall to 0 hit points or fewer, you lose 2 hit points each round, but you are not [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]]. If you are in a rage (such as that caused by the barbarian [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] class feature), you instead only lose 1 hit point per round.
