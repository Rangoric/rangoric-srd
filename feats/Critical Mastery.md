# Critical Mastery

Your critical hits cause two additional effects.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], any two [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^critical-feats|critical feats]], 14th-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter|fighter]].

**Benefit**: When you score a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]], you can apply the effects of two critical feats in addition to the damage dealt.

**Normal**: See [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^critical-feats|critical feats]].
