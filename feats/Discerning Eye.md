# Discerning Eye

You are not easily fooled by illusions and forgeries.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]] or [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]], keen senses racial trait.

**Benefit**: You receive a +2 racial bonus on defenses against illusion spells and effects and a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks to detect forgeries. You can use the Imitation skill to detect forgeries untrained.
