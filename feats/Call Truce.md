# Call Truce

You can passionately entreat your enemies with the aim of making combat unnecessary.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: In combat, as a 1-round action (like a spell with a 1-round casting time), you can call for a truce with any creatures that have Intelligence 4 or higher and understand you. You can’t be wielding a weapon or anything they might find threatening, such as a charged spell or a wand, and you must be in plain sight of most of them.

If any of your allies attack or threaten them before the start of your next turn, the call fails. At the start of your next turn, make a Persuasion check (DC 30 + the highest Charisma modifier in the opposing group). If you succeed, combat stops for 1 minute, or until any of them is threatened or attacked.

If you fail by 5 or more, you can’t use Persuasion with any of those creatures for 1d4 hours. If anyone in your group plans to use the truce to gain an advantage, the opponents can make a Sense Motive check against each of them to get a hunch, against 20 or that character’s Deception check, whichever is higher.

**Special**: If the truce would mean the opponents surrender or lose, if they are mind-controlled or fanatics, or if the GM rules other circumstances make it fitting, you might not be able to use this feat. For example, if the opponents’ main advantage is a short spell that would run out during the truce, you can’t use it. Circumstances can raise the DC by 5, 10 or even 20.
