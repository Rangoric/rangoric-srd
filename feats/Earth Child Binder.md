# Earth Child Binder

Even the greatest giants fear your technique.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]] or [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^defensive-training|defensive training]] racial trait, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Earth Child Style|Earth Child Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Earth Child Topple|Earth Child Topple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Trip|Greater Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 9 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] a creature of the giant subtype no matter its size. While you are using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Earth Child Style|Earth Child Style]], when a prone creature of the giant subtype stands up and provokes an attack of opportunity from you, if you make an unarmed strike, you can declare you are making a [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]] attempt after the attack hits. You gain a +4 bonus to the DC of any Stunning Fist effect you deliver in this way.

**Normal**: You can only trip creatures within the size limit given under [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]].
