# Step Up

You can close the distance when a foe tries to move away.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: Whenever an adjacent foe tries to take a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Take 5-Foot Step|5-foot step]] away from you, you can also take a 5-foot step as an immediate action, as long as you end adjacent to that foe. If you do, you can’t take a 5-foot step during your next turn, and if you take an action to move during your next turn, subtract 5 feet from your total movement.
