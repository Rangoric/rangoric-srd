# Nimble Moves

You can move across a single obstacle with ease.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13.

**Benefit**: Whenever you move, you can move through 5 feet of difficult terrain each round as if it were normal terrain. You can also take a 5-foot step into difficult terrain.
