# Narrow Mind

Your ability to concentrate is as keen as an arrowhead, allowing you to gain your psionic focus even in the most turbulent situations.  

Prerequisite: Wis 13.

Benefit: You gain a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Concentration|Concentration]] checks you make to become psionically focused.
