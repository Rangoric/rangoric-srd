# Fortified Armor Training

You can turn a deadly blow into a lesser one, at the cost of your gear.

**Prerequisites**: Proficiency with armor or a shield.

**Benefit**: If an opponent scores a critical hit against you, you can turn it into a normal hit. If you do, your armor or your shield (your choice) gains the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition.
