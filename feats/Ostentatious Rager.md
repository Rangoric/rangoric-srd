# Ostentatious Rager

You can enthrall onlookers with a show of ferocious might or compel enemies to regard you as a crucial threat.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can earn money with Intimidation by performing feats of strength and intimidation, exactly as if you were doing so with the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] skill.

Additionally, while you’re raging, if a foe would be [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralized]] by your Intimidation check and isn’t currently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] by your intimidation, you can instead distract that foe until the beginning of your next turn. During that time, the foe takes a –5 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks that don’t involve you and a –2 penalty to its AC against any creature other than you. These effects end early if a creature other than you attacks the foe, or if you demoralize the foe and make it shaken.
