# Dual Enhancement

You can enhance two weapons, or both ends of a double weapon, with a single act.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]]; [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-divine-bond|divine bond]] (weapon) or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Warpriest#^warpriest-sacred-weapon|sacred weapon]] class feature.

**Benefit**: When you use divine bond to improve your weapon, you can choose to enhance two weapons or both ends of a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Two-Weapon Fighting|double weapon]]. All weapons affected must be in hand or otherwise wielded. Any enhancement bonus you add to one of your weapons gets added to both automatically, but weapon special abilities must be accounted for separately. For example, if you can enhance up to the equivalent of a +2 bonus, you could give both your weapons an additional +1 bonus and apply the *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Keen|keen]]* special weapon ability to one of those weapons.
