# Pinning Rend

You tear flesh when you damage an opponent that you have pinned.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Grapple|Greater Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: While you have an opponent [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Pinned (Condition)|pinned]], when you succeed on a grapple check to deal it damage with an unarmed strike or a light or one-handed weapon, it also takes [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage equal to your unarmed strike or weapon damage dice. Creatures immune to critical hits are immune to this feat.
