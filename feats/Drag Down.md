# Drag Down

When you are knocked prone, you are skilled at bringing your opponent down with you.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]].

**Benefit**: Whenever an opponent trips you, you can try to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] that opponent as an immediate action.
