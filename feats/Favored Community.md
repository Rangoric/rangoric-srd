# Favored Community

You know your local community like the back of your hand.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] 2 ranks, must have lived in the settlement for at least 1 year.

**Benefit**: Choose a permanent settlement. While in it, you gain a +2 bonus on initiative checks and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], Perception, Stealth and Fieldcraft checks, as with the ranger’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|favored terrain]]. If you have that class feature and use its bonuses in your favored community, its bonus there increases by 2. These bonuses don’t stack with other effects that give or improve favored terrain bonuses.
