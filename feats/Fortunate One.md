# Fortunate One

You have an even greater knack than most halflings for adaptable luck.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Adaptable Luck|Adaptable luck]] racial trait, [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|halfling]].

**Benefit**: The number of times per day you can use the [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Adaptable Luck|adaptable luck]] racial trait increases by 1.
