# Ferocious Feint

Through training or inborn ability, the animal is naturally skilled at feinting against its foes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]].

**Benefit**: The animal companion uses half its master’s class level (minimum 1) in place of its Charisma bonus on Deception checks to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] an opponent. In addition, the animal companion can use Deception to feint an opponent as a move action.
