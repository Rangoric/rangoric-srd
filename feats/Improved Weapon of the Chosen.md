# Improved Weapon of the Chosen

You gain even greater favor when you use your deity's favored weapon.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains#War Domain|deity’s favored weapon]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon of the Chosen|Weapon of the Chosen]].

**Benefit**: This works as Weapon of the Chosen, except that you gain its benefits on all your attacks until the start of your next turn. Your attacks also count as one alignment of your deity (chaotic, evil, good or lawful) for overcoming damage reduction. If your deity is neutral with no other alignment, your attacks instead overcome damage reduction as if your weapon were both cold iron and silver.
