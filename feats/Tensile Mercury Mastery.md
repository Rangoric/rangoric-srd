# Tensile Mercury Mastery

You are a true master in the use of tensile mercury.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tensile Mercury Expertise|Tensile Mercury Expertise]].

**Benefit**: You can control or reshape tensile mercury weapons or armor as a free action, which doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity.
