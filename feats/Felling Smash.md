# Felling Smash

You commit all your focus to a devastating blow, trying to crush your opponent to the ground.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: If you use the attack action to make a single melee attack at your highest base attack bonus while using Power Attack, and you hit, you can spend a swift action to attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver against that opponent.
