# Favored Judgment

Your judgment against a particular type of creature is particularly harsh.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|judgment]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Select a favored race from the ranger’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^table-ranger-favored-enemies|favored enemies table]]. Any sacred or profane bonus you gain from a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-judgment|judgment]] is 1 higher for attacks you make against or take from creatures that match the selected favored enemy.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the feat, it applies to a different favored enemy.
