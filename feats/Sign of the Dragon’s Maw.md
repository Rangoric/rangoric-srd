# Sign of the Dragon’s Maw

The Dragon’s Maw stood over your birth, and its fury is in your blood.

**Benefit**: Before you make an attack roll, you can loose the Dragon’s Maw: you gain a +2 bonus on that attack roll and take a −2 penalty to AC, as on a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]]. As with a charge, you make only that one attack that round, and you can't use this feat in a round in which you charge.

**Special**: You can take this feat only at 1st level.
