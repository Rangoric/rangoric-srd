# Create Reliquary Arms and Shields

Your magical creations are infused with divine power.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Magic Arms and Armor|Craft Magic Arms and Armor]], ability to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Desecrate|desecrate]]_.

**Benefit**: When you make a magic weapon, armor or shield, you can add a casting of _consecrate_ or _desecrate_ to the process, for 250 gp more. The item becomes a reliquary and can be used as a holy (or unholy) symbol and divine focus of your deity. If you cast _consecrate_ or _desecrate_, your reliquary counts as a permanent fixture for that spell while it remains in the spell’s area.
