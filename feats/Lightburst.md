# Lightburst

You are able to convert spell energy into light.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to perform [[rangoric-srd/rules/mirror-magic/Mirror Magic|mirror magic]].

**Benefit**: When this ability is used, one spell level stored within a magic mirror is converted to a glowing halo of light around the mirror in which it is stored. This light flares out 60 feet in all directions and is the equivalent of bright daylight in intensity. The light persists for 1 hour per level of the caster. Mirror magic needs light to work ([[rangoric-srd/rules/mirror-magic/Mirror Magic#Light and Dark|light and dark]]).

**Special**: It’s part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/mirror-magic/Mirror Magic|mirror magic]].
