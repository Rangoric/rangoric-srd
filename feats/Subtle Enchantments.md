# Subtle Enchantments

Your enchantments influence targets in more subtle ways and are harder to notice.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Deception or Imitation), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (enchantment).

**Benefit**: When your enchantment spell or spell-like ability that would influence a foe’s attitude or actions misses its Will Defense, there is a 50% chance it doesn’t notice the attack (it can still try to identify the spell as normal). If it hits the foe or the foe is otherwise affected, the Sense Motive DC to notice it is under an enchantment increases by 5.

**Normal**: Anyone a spell misses notices the mental intrusion.
