# Ice Climber

You can move or clamber across slippery surfaces with great skill.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Arctic Adaptation|Arctic Adaptation]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mountaineer|Mountaineer]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: When moving across a slippery surface (including but not limited to icy surfaces), you gain a +5 bonus on Athletics checks to climb and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to maintain your balance. If you fail an Athletics check to climb, you fall only if you fail the check by 10 or more. You also gain a +5 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks to notice an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Avalanches (CR 7)|avalanche]] and a +2 circumstance bonus on Reflex saves to avoid an avalanche.
