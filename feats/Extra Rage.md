# Extra Rage

You can use your rage ability more than normal.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|Rage]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can [[rangoric-srd/classes/Barbarian#^barbarian-rage|rage]] for 6 additional rounds per day.

**Special**: You can gain Extra Rage multiple times. Its effects stack.
