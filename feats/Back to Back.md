# Back to Back

Your ally’s eyes are your own, and yours are his.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: While you are [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]] and adjacent to an ally with this feat, you receive a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against attacks from opponents flanking you.
