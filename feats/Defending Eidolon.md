# Defending Eidolon

You have trained your eidolon to protect you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-shield-ally|Shield ally]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Whenever you are adjacent to your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]], you can choose for the eidolon to take a –1 penalty on melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks to gain a +1 dodge bonus to your Armor Class. When your eidolon’s base attack bonus reaches +5, and for every +5 thereafter, the penalty increases by –1 and the dodge bonus increases by +1. You must choose to use this feat when your eidolon is making an attack or full-attack action with melee or natural weapons, and its effect lasts until your eidolon’s next turn, or until you are no longer adjacent to the eidolon, whichever occurs first.
