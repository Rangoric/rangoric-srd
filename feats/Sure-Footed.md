# Sure-Footed

Your footing is secure on unstable ground.

**Prerequisites**: Quadruped animal or magical beast.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to keep your balance and on Reflex saves to avoid falling. You can move at normal speed on steep slopes and stairs, and you don't risk falling when running or charging downhill.

**Normal**: See [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Hills Terrain|Hills Terrain]] for moving on steep slopes.
