# Favored Enemy Spellcasting

Your spells are more effective against creatures that you especially abhor.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast spells.

**Benefit**: Choose a creature type, or a humanoid or outsider subtype, from the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^table-ranger-favored-enemies|Ranger Favored Enemies]] table. Creatures of that type treat your spells’ save DCs as 1 higher. If you have the favored enemy class feature and that type is already one of your favored enemies, they treat your spells’ DCs as 2 higher.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time, it applies to a different creature type.
