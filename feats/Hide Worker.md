# Hide Worker

You excel at crafting armor made from the hides of animals or monsters.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (armor) 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 bonus on Craft (armor) checks to make armor or shields from the hides of creatures (such as [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Special Materials|dragonhide]]). If you beat the DC by 5 or more, you also make the item for 10% less.
