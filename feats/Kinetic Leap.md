# Kinetic Leap

Kinetic energy propels you when you jump.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 3 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-kinetic-blast|kinetic blast]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Once per day as a swift action, you can conjure a burst of energy from your kinetic blast to help you jump a long distance, adding a +10 bonus on your [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics#Jumping|Athletics]] check to jump; if you have at least 10 ranks in Athletics, the bonus increases to +20. By accepting 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist#^kineticist-burn|burn]], you can use this ability at will until your burn is removed.
