# Visceral Threat

Your ability to threaten foes is great enough to make them hesitate.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intimidating Prowess|Intimidating Prowess]].

**Benefit**: You can use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] instead of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] against a creature within your melee reach.
