# Tripping Strike

The force intrinsic to your critical hits can knock your foes down.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: Whenever you score a critical hit with a melee attack, you can also trip your opponent, on top of the attack’s normal damage. If your confirmation roll beats the opponent’s [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]], you knock it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] as if with the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver. This doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity.

**Normal**: You must use the trip combat maneuver to trip your opponent.

**Special**: You can apply only one of these feats to a given critical hit: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bull Rush Strike|Bull Rush Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disarming Strike|Disarming Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Repositioning Strike|Repositioning Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sundering Strike|Sundering Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Tripping Strike|Tripping Strike]]. You can choose to use this feat after you make your confirmation roll.
