# Shadetongue

You can command the undead.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shadesight|Shadesight]], Cha 15.

**Benefit**: You may rebuke or command undead as an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Evil Clerics and Undead|evil cleric]] of half your total character level, using the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turn or Rebuke Undead|turning]] rules; each use of this feat is one turning attempt. You must speak, and the undead must hear you.

This ability does not stack with any other turning or rebuking abilities you receive from other classes (such as uses of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Channel Energy|channel energy]]).

You also gain a +2 profane bonus on verbal [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks due to the power of your voice.

Abilities granted by this feat are spell-like abilities. Each use of this feat causes 10 points of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]].

**Special**: It’s a [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Shadetouched Feats|shadetouched feat]]: it has uses per day and costs hit points as [[rangoric-srd/rules/path-of-shades/The Path of Shades#Shadetouched Feats|the Path of Shades]] says. [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadeseer|Shadeseers]] gain an [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Shadeseer#^shadeseer-enhanced-shadetouched-feats|enhanced version]] of this feat free at 3rd level. Shadeseers may take this feat earlier, whenever they may select a feat, but they do not receive a bonus feat at 3rd level to compensate.
