# Djinni Style

Your hands sheathed in an auras of lightning, you move like the wind.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13, Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +9 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: You gain one more Elemental Fist attempt per day. While using this style and Elemental Fist to deal electricity damage, you gain a bonus on electricity damage rolls equal to your Wisdom bonus. While you use this style, you must deal electricity damage with Elemental Fist. While you have Elemental Fist attempts left, you also gain a +2 dodge bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity; anything that makes you lose your Dexterity bonus to AC also makes you lose this bonus.
