# Stunning Pin

You can render a pinned foe temporarily incapacitated.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Pinned (Condition)|pin]] an opponent, you can spend a swift action to make a Stunning Fist attempt against it.
