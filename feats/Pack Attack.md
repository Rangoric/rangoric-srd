# Pack Attack

You are skilled at surrounding your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When you are adjacent to an ally with this feat, the first time you melee attack an opponent, you can spend an immediate action to take a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Take 5-Foot Step|5-foot step]], even if you have otherwise moved this round.

**Normal**: You can take a 5-foot step only if you have not otherwise moved in a round.
