# Disruptive Spell

Your magical energies cling to enemies, interfering with their spellcasting.

**Benefit**: For 1 round, a target affected by a disruptive spell must make [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] to cast spells or use spell-like abilities (DC equal to the disruptive spell’s save DC + the level of the spell being cast). A target that avoids the spell’s effects avoids this one too. A disruptive spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell’s actual level.
