# Crossing Over

Crossing over allows someone, through force of will, to shorten his or her travel time while in the fey realm.

**Prerequisites**: Base Will save +3.

**Benefit**: A character using the Crossing Over feat can make an opposed Wisdom check versus the DC of the region of the fey realm to change the length of their journey within it. The governor of a region of the fey realm can set or modify its DC by using this feat. For more details, see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Travel Within the Fey Realm|travel within the fey realm]] and its [[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#^fey-distances|distances]].
