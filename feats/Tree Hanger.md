# Tree Hanger

You can use your tail to defend against trip attacks and to hang from nearby protrusions.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/races/Vanara|vanara]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against all [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks. If your square has a branch or other sturdy large object that you could hang from, as a swift action you may make a DC 15 Acrobatics check to jump upward and use your tail to hang from that object. While hanging, you can’t be tripped, you ignore the effects of difficult terrain in your square, and you gain a +2 bonus to your CMD against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Drag|drag]], and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]] attacks. If you leave that square (including if you are moved against your will), you lose your grip on the object and are no longer hanging. While hanging, you cannot use your tail for anything else.
