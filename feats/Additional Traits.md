# Additional Traits

You have more traits than normal.

**Benefit**: You gain two character traits of your choice (see [[rangoric-srd/traits/Character Traits|Character Traits]]). These traits must be chosen from different lists, and cannot be chosen from lists from which you have already selected a character trait. You must meet any additional qualifications for the character traits you choose.
