# Draconic Glide

You possess draconic defenses and wings that allow you to glide.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Draconic Aspect|Draconic Aspect]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus against sleep and paralysis effects. You grow a pair of wings that you can use to fall and glide at a safe pace. You can make a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check to fall safely from any height without taking falling damage, as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]*. When falling safely, you may make an additional DC 15 Acrobatics check to glide, moving 5 feet laterally for every 20 feet you fall.

**Special**: If you have the [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Gliding Wings|gliding wings]] racial trait, instead of the above abilities, your base speed increases to 30 feet.
