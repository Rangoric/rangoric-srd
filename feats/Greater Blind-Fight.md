# Greater Blind-Fight

Your enemies cannot hide from you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] 15 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Blind-Fight|Improved Blind-Fight]].

**Benefit**: Your melee attacks ignore the miss chance for less than total concealment, and you treat opponents with total concealment as if they had normal concealment (a 20% miss chance instead of 50%). You can still reroll a miss chance as normal.

If you pinpoint an invisible or hidden attacker, it gains no advantage on ranged attacks against you, whatever the range: you keep your Dexterity bonus to AC, and it doesn’t get the usual +2 bonus for being invisible.

**Special**: Greater Blind-Fight is no use against a creature under a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blink|blink]]* spell.
