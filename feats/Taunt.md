# Taunt

You may be small, but your remarks cut others down to size.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: You can demoralize opponents with [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] instead of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and you take no penalty on the check for being smaller than your target.
