# Astrologer (Feat)

You can read the stars for good and bad days.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (astrology) 1 rank.

**Benefit**: At the start of each day you can make a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (astrology) check. If it succeeds, roll 1d10 to learn how the stars stand for the coming day: on a 1–3 the day is ill-starred, and the GM can make you reroll one skill check you succeeded on; on a 4–7 it's an ordinary day; on an 8–10 the day is well-starred, and you can reroll one skill check you failed.

A DC 30 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Lore|Lore]] (astrology) check tells you when and where a child must be born to have a particular birth sign (a [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^sign-feats|sign feat]]). You can make it any time before or during the pregnancy.
