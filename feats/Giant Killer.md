# Giant Killer

Your cleaving strokes menace giants and larger foes.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Goblin Cleaver|Goblin Cleaver]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Orc Hewer|Orc Hewer]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Strike Back|Strike Back]], base attack bonus +11, [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]].

**Benefit**: This functions as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Goblin Cleaver|Goblin Cleaver]], but your additional attacks can be made against creatures one size category larger than you or smaller. In addition, any such attacks made against humanoids (giant) gain a +2 circumstance bonus on attack rolls.
