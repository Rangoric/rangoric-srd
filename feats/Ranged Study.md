# Ranged Study

You can use a limited form of studied combat and studied strike with a weapon of your choice.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with the chosen weapon, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-combat|studied combat]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Choose one kind of ranged weapon. You gain the bonuses for [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-combat|studied combat]] with your chosen weapon and can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-studied-strike|studied strike]] with your chosen weapon as long as the target of your studied strike is within 30 feet of you.

**Normal**: You gain the bonuses for studied combat and can use studied strike only with melee weapons.
