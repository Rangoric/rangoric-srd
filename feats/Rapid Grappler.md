# Rapid Grappler

You are a quick hand at grappling.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Grapple|Greater Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Grapple|Improved Grapple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use Greater Grapple to maintain a grapple as a move action, you can then spend a swift action to make a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] combat maneuver check.
