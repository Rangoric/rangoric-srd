# Disengaging Flourish

Distracting your opponents gives you the opportunity to make a swift retreat.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Disengaging Feint|Disengaging Feint]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]].

**Benefit**: As a standard action, make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] each opponent that threatens you. If you succeed against at least one, you can move up to your speed. This movement doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity from any opponent you succeeded in feinting.
