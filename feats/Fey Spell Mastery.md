# Fey Spell Mastery

You have learned to tap the innate supernatural powers of yourself and your magical ties to FaerieLand. As a result, you don’t need certain spell components when casting spells.

**Prerequisites**: Fey, or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blood of the Fey|Blood of the Fey]].

**Benefit**: You draw on your own fey power and your ties to the fey, so you can cast spells without material components worth less than 5 gp.
