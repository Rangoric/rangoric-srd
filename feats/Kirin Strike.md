# Kirin Strike

You have read the texts of the perfect way, and know how identify to your enemies' weak spot.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Kirin Style|Kirin Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 9 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 insight bonus on checks to identify creatures, including with Kirin Style. While using Kirin Style against a creature you identified with it, after you hit the creature with a melee or ranged attack you can spend a swift action to add twice your Intelligence modifier to the damage (minimum 2).
