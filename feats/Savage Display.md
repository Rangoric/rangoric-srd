# Savage Display

With your victory and a roar, you push yourself on with increased savagery.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]].

**Benefit**: When you spend a swift action to make a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat#^performance-combat-check|performance combat check]], you gain a +2 bonus on your performance combat check and gain a +1d6 bonus on damage rolls until the end of your next turn. This extra damage is not precision damage.
