# Ironguts

You have an especially strong stomach.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13; [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]], or orc.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against any effect causing the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] conditions and against all [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#^ingested-poison|ingested poisons]] (but not other poisons). In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] skill checks to find food for yourself (and only yourself).
