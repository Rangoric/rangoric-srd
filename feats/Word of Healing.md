# Word of Healing

Using the same divine energy as your lay on hands ability, you can heal others at a distance.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|Lay on hands]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You can use your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-lay-on-hands|lay on hands]] to heal another creature within 30 feet as a standard action that doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity. You must be able to speak and have a free hand. The target heals half the amount it would have if you had touched it, but gains the benefits of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin#^paladin-mercy|mercies]] as normal.
