# Street Carnage

For you, the urban environment becomes another brutal weapon you can use to shred flesh and break bones.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Street Style|Street Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Street Sweep|Street Sweep]], base attack bonus +8 or monk level 7th.

**Benefit**: While using Street Style, the critical multiplier of your unarmed strikes is ×3 instead of ×2.
