# Tail Weapon

You have strengthened your tail enough that you can use it to make slap attacks and augment your balance.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, [[rangoric-srd/monsters/serpentfolk/Serpentfolk|serpentfolk]].

**Benefit**: You can make a tail slap attack with your tail. This is a secondary natural attack that deals 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage. You also gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to balance and to your CMD against maneuvers that attempt to move you from your square.
