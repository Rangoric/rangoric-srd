# Detect Expertise

You can detect the mystic specialty of a foe.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Chaos|detect chaos]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|detect law]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*.

**Benefit**: When you use one of those detect spells on a creature, you may learn what spellcasting expertise it has. After you study it with the spell for 3 rounds, it must make a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your caster level + your Intelligence modifier). If it fails, you learn what bloodlines, domains, schools or similar spellcasting choices it has, if any. If it succeeds, it is immune to this feat for 24 hours.
