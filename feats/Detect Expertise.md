# Detect Expertise

You can detect the mystic specialty of a foe.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, ability to cast _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Chaos|detect chaos]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Good|detect good]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Law|detect law]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_.

**Benefit**: When you use one of those detect spells on a creature, you may learn what spellcasting expertise it has. After you study it with the spell for 3 rounds, the spell attacks its Will, its attack bonus 1/2 your caster level + your Intelligence modifier. On a hit, you learn what bloodlines, domains, schools or similar spellcasting choices it has, if any. On a miss, it is immune to this feat for 24 hours.
