# Splintering Weapon

Your fragile weapon works to your advantage, breaking off fragments in wounds you inflict.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, proficient with weapon, weapon made of a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Special Materials#Primitive Materials|primitive material]].

**Benefit**: Whenever you use a melee or thrown weapon with the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Fragile (Quality)|fragile]] quality or a similar quality and hit an opponent, you can give your weapon the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition to deal that opponent 1d4 points of [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage.
