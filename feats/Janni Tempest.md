# Janni Tempest

Your gale of attacks easily throws your opponents off balance.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Janni Style|Janni Style]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (dance) 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using Janni Style, whenever you hit an opponent with an unarmed attack, you gain a +4 bonus on checks to bull rush or trip it, as long as the maneuver is your next attack before the end of your turn. The maneuver doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity from that opponent.
