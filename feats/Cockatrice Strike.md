# Cockatrice Strike

With a single strike, you transmute flesh to stone.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Gorgon's Fist|Gorgon's Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Medusa's Wrath|Medusa's Wrath]], base attack bonus +16.

**Benefit**: As a full-round action, you can make a single unarmed strike against a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] foe. If the attack is a critical hit, the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^petrified|petrified]] unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier). This is a supernatural polymorph effect.
