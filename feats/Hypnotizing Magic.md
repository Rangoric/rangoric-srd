# Hypnotizing Magic

You have learned to bind the charming nature of the fey realm in your magic. Spells you cast have a secondary ability to hypnotize their victims.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fey Magic|Fey Magic]].

**Benefit**: Casting a spell altered by this feat causes *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hypnotism|hypnotism]]* (as the spell of the same name) to affect the target creature and 2d4 HD of creatures within a 15-foot radius centered on the original target. The altered spell gains the mind-affecting descriptor.

A hypnotizing spell uses a spell slot three levels higher than the spell’s actual level.
