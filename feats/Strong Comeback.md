# Strong Comeback

You learn quickly from past mistakes.

**Benefit**: Whenever you are allowed to reroll an ability check or skill check, or to make an attacker reroll an attack against your defense, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on the reroll (on a defense, a +2 circumstance bonus to that defense against the reroll).
