# Waterway Caster

You've learned to cast spells on a boat, or even while swimming in rough water.

**Benefit**: You automatically succeed on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] to cast a spell during vigorous or violent motion while you are swimming or aboard a ship or boat. You gain a +4 bonus on concentration checks to cast spells underwater.
