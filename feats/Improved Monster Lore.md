# Improved Monster Lore

You are obsessed with the abilities and weaknesses of monsters.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-monster-lore|monster lore]] class feature.

**Benefit**: You gain a sacred bonus on all skill checks to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|identify the abilities and weaknesses of creatures]] equal to 1/2 your level in classes that grant you the monster lore class feature.
