# Street Sweep

After driving your foe back, you can quickly knock it to the ground and slow it down.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bull Rush|Improved Bull Rush]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Street Style|Street Style]], base attack bonus +6 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: While using Street Style, the first time each round you deal damage with an unarmed strike to a foe you bull rushed with Street Style since the start of your last turn, the strike also attacks that foe’s Fortitude, its attack bonus your base attack bonus; on a hit, the foe is knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round.
