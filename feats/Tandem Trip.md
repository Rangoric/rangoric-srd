# Tandem Trip

You know how to work together to trip your foes.

**Benefit**: Whenever you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver against an enemy threatened by an ally with this feat, you roll twice and take the better result.
