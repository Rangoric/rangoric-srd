# Totemic Master

Through both might of arms and spiritual wisdom, you have attained full understanding of your barbaric totem.

**Prerequisites**: Str 17, Dex 13, Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Athletics), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totemic Disciple|Totemic Disciple]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totemic Initiate|Totemic Initiate]], base attack bonus +13, no barbarian [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rage-powers/Rage Powers|totem rage powers]] except those chosen with this feat, Totemic Disciple, or Totemic Initiate, nonlawful alignment.

**Benefit**: Choose one greater totem rage power that requires the totem rage power you chose with Totemic Disciple. While gaining the benefits of [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totemic Initiate|Totemic Initiate]], you also gain the benefits of this new rage power as well as the one you chose with Totemic Disciple. If you gain the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^pounce|pounce]] ability this way, you can make only natural attacks using your pounce ability.
