# Stealth Synergy

Working closely with an ally, you are able to move like twin shadows.

**Benefit**: While you can see one or more allies who also have this feat, whenever you and your allies make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] check, you all take the highest roll and add all your modifiers to Stealth.
