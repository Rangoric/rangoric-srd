# Coordinated Maneuvers

You are skilled at working with your allies to perform dangerous combat maneuvers.

**Benefit**: Whenever you are adjacent to an ally who also has this feat, you receive a +2 competence bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Performing a Combat Maneuver|combat maneuver checks]]. This bonus increases to +4 when attempting to break free from a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]].
