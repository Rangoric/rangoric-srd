# Aquatic Combatant

You have trained to fight while submerged in water.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on Athletics checks to swim, and you don’t take the usual penalties on melee attack rolls underwater. Your slashing melee attacks and bludgeoning unarmed attacks deal full damage underwater.

**Normal**: Underwater, most melee attacks take a –2 penalty and deal half damage.
