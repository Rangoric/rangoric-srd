# Monastic Legacy

Your formal unarmed training continues to bolster your training in other areas.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-still-mind|Still mind]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: Add half the levels you have in classes other than monk to your monk level to determine your effective monk level for your base [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^unarmed-strike|unarmed strike]] damage. This feat does not make levels in classes other than monk count toward any other monk class features.
