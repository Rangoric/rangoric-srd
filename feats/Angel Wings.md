# Angel Wings

Feathered wings sprout from your back.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Angelic Blood|Angelic Blood]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|aasimar]], character level 10th.

**Benefit**: You gain a pair of gleaming feathered wings that grant a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|fly speed]] of 30 feet (average maneuverability) if wearing light armor or unencumbered, or 20 feet (poor maneuverability) with a medium or heavy load or medium or heavy armor. [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] is a class skill for you.
