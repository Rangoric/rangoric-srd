# Wolf Savage

You savage your foes so badly that they can become supernaturally disfigured.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 17, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wolf Style|Wolf Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wolf Trip|Wolf Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 9 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using Wolf Style, when you deal at least 10 points of damage to a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]] opponent with a natural weapon or an unarmed strike, you can spend a swift action to savage it. It must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or take 1d4 points of Charisma or Constitution [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-damaged|damage]], or become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] (your choice). This ability damage can’t reduce the score to 0 or below.
