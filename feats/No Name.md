# No Name

You don't need an elaborate disguise to keep your identity under wraps.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|Grit]] class feature or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Amateur Gunslinger|Amateur Gunslinger]] feat, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 4 ranks.

**Benefit**: You often rely on surprise and misdirection in your social dealings. You gain a +2 bonus on Deception checks, and you can spend 1 grit point to gain a +10 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks for 10 minutes per your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger|gunslinger]] level (minimum 10 minutes). This deed does not actually change your appearance, but rather allows you to hide your identity in other ways.
