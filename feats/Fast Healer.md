# Fast Healer

You benefit greatly from your healing, be it from spells or natural healing.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]].

**Benefit**: When you regain hit points by resting or through magical healing, you recover extra hit points equal to half your Constitution modifier (minimum +1).
