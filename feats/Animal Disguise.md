# Animal Disguise

With a little work, you can convincingly disguise yourself as an animal.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can use Imitation to disguise yourself as an animal of your size category, if you have a fitting pelt and any other animal parts the disguise needs. You gain a +2 bonus on Imitation checks to disguise yourself as an animal. Creatures with ranks in Nature can use Nature instead of Sense Motive to see through this kind of disguise.
