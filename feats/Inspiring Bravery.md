# Inspiring Bravery

Your bravery inspires bravery in others.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Fighter#^fighter-bravery|bravery]] class feature.

**Benefit**: As long as you are conscious and not [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]], allies within 30 feet who can see and hear you gain your bravery bonus on saving throws against fear. If you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Bravery|Improved Bravery]], they gain your bravery bonus on saving throws against all mind-affecting effects. If you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Social Bravery|Social Bravery]], your bravery bonus is added to the DCs of checks to demoralize them, feint against them, change their attitude, or convince them to perform a request.
