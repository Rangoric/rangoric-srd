# Extra Summons

You can summon monsters more often each day.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-summon-monster|*summon monster* as a spell-like ability]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner|summoner]] 1st.

**Benefit**: You gain 1 additional use of your [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-summon-monster|*summon monster* spell-like ability]] per day.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times, but it can only be taken once for every five summoner levels you possess.
