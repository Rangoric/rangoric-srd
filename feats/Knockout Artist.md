# Knockout Artist

You can throw devastating knockout punches.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|Sneak attack]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: When you use your unarmed strike to deal [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]] and sneak attack damage to an opponent denied its Dexterity bonus to AC, you gain a +1 bonus on the damage roll for each sneak attack damage die you roll.
