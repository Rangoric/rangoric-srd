# Learn Rune

Because of your great insight and your deep connection with the runes, you are able to understand more runes than normal.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, Int 15, 1st-level rune spell slot.

**Benefit**: You may learn one rune more than normal.

**Normal**: Rune casters may learn a number of runes equal to their level in the Rune Caster class plus their Intelligence modifier ([[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#Casting Rune Spells|casting rune spells]]).

**Special**: This feat may only be taken once. It’s part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]].
