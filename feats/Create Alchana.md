# Create Alchana

You can create alchemical and magical items beyond those possible by magic or alchemy alone.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) 6 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Chymicals|Craft Chymicals]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]].

**Benefit**: You can create miscellaneous creations of alchemy unavailable to magic (or Craft Wondrous Item) or alchemy alone. Effectively, this feat allows you to create any new and wondrous alchemagical items, provided they are not arms and armor, wands, staves, rods, potions, or rings. The proof that an item is an alchana rather than just a miscellaneous magical item is this—its effects or usefulness are not canceled by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic|dispel magic]]* spells.

The base price to create alchana takes the item’s caster level, adds its creation DC, and then multiplies the total by 400 gp (CL + DC x 400). To create such substances, the creator must expend 4% (1/25) of the item’s base price in XP and use up raw materials costing 30% of the base price. (The savings alchemy or herbalism give other item creation feats, in [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemical and Herbal Items#Related Feats & Skill Adjustments|Related Feats & Skill Adjustments]], are already part of this feat.)

This feat is part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|alchemy and herbalism]].
