# Cutting Humiliation

You know how to cut a foe deeply with humiliation.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you demoralize a target with a spoken Intimidation check in a social situation (not through an ability such as Dazzling Display), you can humiliate it instead: it takes a –2 penalty on Charisma-based skill checks and Charisma checks for 1 hour, plus 1 hour for every 5 by which you beat the DC. It can end the effect early by taking 10 minutes to compose itself. If you fail, anyone who saw you fail is immune to your humiliation for 24 hours. If you fail by 5 or more, you are humiliated for 1 hour instead.
