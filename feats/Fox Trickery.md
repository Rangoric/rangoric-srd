# Fox Trickery

Whenever a foes drops his guard, you can make him suffer with a dirty trick.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fox Insight|Fox Insight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fox Style|Fox Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Dirty Trick|Improved Dirty Trick]].

**Benefit**: While using Fox Style, you can make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Dirty Trick|dirty trick]] combat maneuvers as attacks of opportunity. If your Intelligence is 19 or higher, you gain a +4 bonus on dirty trick combat maneuver checks.
