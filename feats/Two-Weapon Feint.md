# Two-Weapon Feint

You use one weapon to distract your enemy while slipping another past his defenses.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]].

**Benefit**: While using Two-Weapon Fighting to make melee attacks, you can forgo your first primary-hand melee attack to make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] an opponent.
