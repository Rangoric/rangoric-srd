# Blustering Bluff

You can verbally bully others into believing unlikely or outrageous lies.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: When you use Deception to fool someone with a bluff that is hard to believe, you can bully them, lowering the Sense Motive modifier for how hard the bluff is to believe by 5. If you do and your check wouldn’t have succeeded otherwise, after 1d6 × 10 minutes the target realizes you bullied it into believing a lie, treats you as unfriendly, and might report you or act against you.
