# Wilding Senses

The wilderness speaks to you, giving you an uncanny sense of approaching danger.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Wilding|Wilding]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks, or +4 to notice foes when deciding whether you are [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Surprise|surprised]]. If you have 10 or more ranks in Perception, these bonuses increase to +4 and +8.

**Special**: This bonus doesn't stack with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] in Perception. Wilding Senses counts as Skill Focus (Perception) for prerequisites.
