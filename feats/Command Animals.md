# Command Animals

You channel energy to get animals to do your bidding.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric Domains#Animal Domain|Animal domain]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can spend one use of channel energy to try to control animals within 30 feet. Each can make a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your class level + your Cha modifier) to negate the effect; those that fail obey you as if under *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]* with a caster level equal to your class level, and can make a new save each day. The animals you control can’t total more Hit Dice than your class level. Channeling this way doesn’t heal or harm anything. If an affected animal is controlled by, or a companion of, another creature, make an opposed Charisma check whenever your orders conflict.
