# Command Animals

You channel energy to get animals to do your bidding.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Animal Domain|Animal domain]], [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can spend one use of channel energy to try to control animals within 30 feet. The channel attacks each one’s Will, its attack bonus 1/2 your class level + your Cha modifier, and a miss negates the effect; those it hits obey you as if under _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Monster|charm monster]]_ with a caster level equal to your class level, and it attacks them again each day. The animals you control can’t total more Hit Dice than your class level. Channeling this way doesn’t heal or harm anything. If an affected animal is controlled by, or a companion of, another creature, make an opposed Charisma check whenever your orders conflict.
