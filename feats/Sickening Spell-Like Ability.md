# Sickening Spell-Like Ability

One of your spell-like abilities sickens those it harms.

**Prerequisites**: Spell-like ability at caster level 6th or higher.

**Benefit**: Choose one of your spell-like abilities that qualifies (below). You can use it as a sickening spell-like ability three times per day (or fewer, if it’s normally usable only once or twice per day). When you use it, it has the effect of [[rangoric-srd/feats/Sickening Spell|Sickening Spell]], with the ability’s equivalent spell level in place of the spell’s. If the ability doesn’t allow a save, the Fortitude save uses the DC it would have if it did. Only an ability that duplicates a spell whose level is no more than half your caster level (round down) – 2 qualifies.

**Special**: You can take this feat more than once. Each time, choose a different spell-like ability.
