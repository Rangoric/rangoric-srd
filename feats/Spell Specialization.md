# Spell Specialization

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]].

**Benefit**: Choose one spell of a school for which you have Spell Focus. Treat your caster level as two higher for all level-dependent effects of that spell.

Each time you gain an even level in the spellcasting class the spell comes from, you can choose a new spell to be your specialized spell instead.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Its effects don’t stack. Each time, it applies to a different spell.
