# Improved Arrow of Death

Prerequisites: Dex 19, Wis 19, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]], arrow of death class feature.  
Benefit: Add +2 to the DC of the character’s arrows of death. This feat may be taken multiple times. Its effects stack.
