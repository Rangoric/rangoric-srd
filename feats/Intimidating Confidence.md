# Intimidating Confidence

You have boundless faith in your success.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fearless Curiosity|Fearless Curiosity]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 5 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]].

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on saving throws against effects with the emotion descriptor; this bonus stacks with the bonus granted by [[rangoric-srd/feats/Fearless Curiosity|Fearless Curiosity]]. When you confirm a critical hit, as a free action you can make an Intimidation check to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] one creature you threaten. If you have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dazzling Display|Dazzling Display]] feat, you can make Intimidation checks to demoralize all creatures you threaten instead. You gain a +2 bonus on this check if your weapon has a ×3 critical modifier, or +4 if it has a ×4 critical modifier.
