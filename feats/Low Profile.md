# Low Profile

Yours small stature helps you avoid ranged attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC against ranged attacks. You don’t provide [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|soft cover]] to creatures when ranged attacks pass through your square.
