# Ferocious Beast

Through training or natural ability, the animal is skilled at unnerving its enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 1 rank, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-animal-companion|animal companion]].

**Benefit**: The animal companion uses half its master’s class level (minimum 1) in place of its Charisma bonus on Intimidation checks to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|demoralize]] an opponent, as well as on Intimidation checks to use the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Antagonize|Antagonize]] feat. In addition, the animal companion can use Intimidation to demoralize an opponent as a move action.

**Special**: An animal companion can’t take both Ferocious Beast and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Intimidating Prowess|Intimidating Prowess]].
