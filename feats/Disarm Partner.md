# Disarm Partner

When your ally fails to disarm an opponent within your reach, you can try to disarm that same foe.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When an ally with this feat fails a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] combat maneuver check made on his turn against an opponent within your melee reach, as an immediate action you can attempt a disarm combat maneuver check against that foe.
