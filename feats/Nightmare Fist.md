# Nightmare Fist

You are even more deadly in magical darkness.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 1 rank, ability to create magical darkness.

**Benefit**: While fighting within an area of magical darkness, you gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls with unarmed strikes, or a +4 bonus against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]] opponents. You also gain a +2 morale bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and Intimidation checks.
