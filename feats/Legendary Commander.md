# Legendary Commander

Prerequisites: Cha 25, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Epic Leadership|Epic Leadership]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Leadership|Leadership]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] 27 ranks, must rule own kingdom and have a stronghold.  

Benefit: Multiply the number of followers of each level that the character can lead by 10. This has no effect on cohorts.
