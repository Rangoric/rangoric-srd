# Superior Scryer

You are adept at seeing more when scrying.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Focus|Spell Focus]] (divination), ability to cast at least one scrying spell.

**Benefit**: When you cast a scrying spell, you see through its sensor with darkvision 60 feet, whether or not you or the spell normally allow it. If the spell sends back sight only within 10 feet of the sensor (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying, Greater|greater scrying]]* do), this feat doesn’t extend that range. You gain a +2 bonus on Perception checks to notice things through a scrying sensor, and your *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prying Eyes|prying eyes]]* gain a +2 bonus on Perception checks too.

When you use *scrying* or *greater scrying*, your chance of casting spells through the sensor increases by 10%.
