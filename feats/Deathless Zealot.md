# Deathless Zealot

Only the most serious wounds can stop you.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, Con 17, orc or [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deathless Initiate|Deathless Initiate]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deathless Master|Deathless Master]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Diehard|Diehard]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Ironhide|Ironhide]], base attack bonus +12.

**Benefit**: Whenever a creature rolls to confirm a critical hit against you, it must roll twice and take the lowest result.
