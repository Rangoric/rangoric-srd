# Favored Defense

Your cunning is your shield against your quarry’s attacks.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|Favored enemy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Choose one of your favored enemy types. You add half your favored enemy bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] and as a dodge bonus to AC when attacked by a favored enemy.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a new favored enemy type.
