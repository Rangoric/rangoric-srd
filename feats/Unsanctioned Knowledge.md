# Unsanctioned Knowledge

You have searched though forbidden texts and are privy to powerful but proscribed magic.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, ability to cast 1st-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Paladin|paladin]] spells.

**Benefit**: Choose a 1st-, a 2nd-, a 3rd- and a 4th-level spell from the bard or cleric spell list. Add them to your paladin spell list as paladin spells of those levels. Once chosen, they can’t be changed.
