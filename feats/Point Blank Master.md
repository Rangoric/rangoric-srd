# Point Blank Master

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Specialization|Weapon Specialization]] with the selected ranged weapon.

**Benefit**: Choose one type of ranged weapon. You don’t provoke attacks of opportunity when you fire it while threatened.

**Normal**: Using a ranged weapon while threatened provokes attacks of opportunity.
