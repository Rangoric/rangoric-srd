# Diverse Palate

Your taste for blood is broader than that of other dhampirs.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blood Drinker|Blood Drinker]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Dhampir|dhampir]].

**Benefit**: Choose a humanoid subtype or the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^monstrous-humanoid|monstrous humanoid]] type. You may use your [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blood Drinker|Blood Drinker]] feat on creatures of this subtype or type.

**Special**: You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you select it, choose a new humanoid subtype or the monstrous humanoid type.
