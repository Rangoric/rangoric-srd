# Blighted Critical

With a critical hit from a spell or spell-like ability, you give the target a minor spellblight.

**Prerequisites**: Caster level 5th.

**Benefit**: Whenever you confirm a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] with a touch spell, ranged touch spell, or spell-like ability against an opponent, the victim gains a random minor [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-minor-spellblights|spellblight]].
