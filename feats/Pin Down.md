# Pin Down

You easily block enemy escapes.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], fighter level 11th.

**Benefit**: Whenever an opponent you threaten takes a 5-foot step or the withdraw action, it provokes an attack of opportunity from you. If the attack hits, you deal no damage, but the creature loses the move action that gave it the 5-foot step or the withdraw, and doesn’t move.
