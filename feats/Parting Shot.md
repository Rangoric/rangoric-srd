# Parting Shot

You are an expert skirmisher, able to rain missiles upon your enemies whether advancing or retreating.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Shot on the Run|Shot on the Run]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: Once per encounter, when you take the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Withdraw|withdraw]] action, you can make a single ranged attack at any point during your movement.

**Normal**: You can’t attack when you withdraw.
