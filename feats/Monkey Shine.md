# Monkey Shine

You combine acrobatics and opportunity to devastating effect against your opponent.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Monkey Moves|Monkey Moves]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Monkey Style|Monkey Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] 11 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] 11 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using Monkey Style, if you succeed at a Stunning Fist attempt, in addition to its normal effect you can spend a free action to enter a square adjacent to you that is within your opponent’s space. This movement doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity. While you are in your opponent’s space, you gain a +4 dodge bonus to AC and a +4 bonus on melee attack rolls against that opponent. If otherwise unhindered, the opponent can move away from you, but if it does, it provokes an attack of opportunity from you even if its movement normally wouldn’t.

**Normal**: You can’t enter an opponent’s space.
