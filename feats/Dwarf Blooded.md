# Dwarf Blooded

You have dwarven blood coursing through your veins.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|Oread]].

**Benefit**: Your dwarven heritage manifests in two ways: you gain the [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|dwarf]]’s slow and steady racial trait (your speed is never modified by armor or encumbrance) and its [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf#^dwarf-stonecunning|stonecunning]] racial trait.
