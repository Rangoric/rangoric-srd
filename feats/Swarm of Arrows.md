# Swarm of Arrows

Prerequisites: Dex 23, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Shot|Rapid Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (type of bow used).  

Benefit: As a full-round action, the character may fire an arrow at his or her full base attack bonus at each opponent within 30 feet.
