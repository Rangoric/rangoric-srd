# Flexible Wizardry

You have learned a trick that makes your spellcasting more flexible.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spell Mastery|Spell Mastery]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard|wizard]] level 1st.

**Benefit**: When you prepare your spells each day, you can keep a number of spell slots equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) flexibly prepared. Instead of preparing one spell in each of these slots, you partially prepare two. Any time after that, you can spend a full-round action to finalize one slot, choosing which of its two spells is fully prepared in it.
