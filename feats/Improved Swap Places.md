# Improved Swap Places

When you switch places with your comrade, your sizes don’t matter.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Swap Places|Swap Places]].

**Benefit**: When you and your ally use Swap Places, your ally can be up to one size larger or smaller than you, and your movement into the ally’s square does not provoke an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity#Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]]. If your ally cannot fit into the space you had been occupying and there are no available adjacent squares to accommodate the rest of the ally’s space, the ally must [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Movement#Terrain and Obstacles|squeeze]]. Alternatively, as part of its movement, the ally can attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] combat maneuver against a creature that occupies a space your ally would occupy, but this bull rush cannot move the creature more than 5 feet.

**Normal**: Using Swap Places requires you and your ally to be the same size, and your movement into the ally’s square provokes attacks of opportunity.
