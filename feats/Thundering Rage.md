# Thundering Rage

Prerequisites: Str 25, rage 5/day.

Benefit: Any weapon the character wields while in a rage is treated as a thundering weapon. The attack bonus of the Fortitude attack to deafen is equal to 1/2 the character’s level. This ability does not stack with similar abilities.
