# Improved Hunter's Bond

You deepen your connection to your allies, granting them higher bonuses.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-hunters-bond|Hunter’s bond]] (hunting companions) class feature, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger|ranger]] level 9th.

**Benefit**: When you activate hunter’s bond, you can grant your allies your full [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] bonus against a single target.

**Normal**: Your allies receive only part of your favored enemy bonus, as [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-hunters-bond|hunter’s bond]] says.
