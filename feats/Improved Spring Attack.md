# Improved Spring Attack

You dart through the press of battle like a breeze, assaulting foes as you pass.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Mobility|Mobility]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nimble Moves|Nimble Moves]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Spring Attack|Spring Attack]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: When you use Spring Attack, you can attack two targets during your movement instead of one. The second attack is made at your full base attack bonus –5. All of Spring Attack’s restrictions apply to both targets, and your movement doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity from either of them. You can’t target the same creature twice.
