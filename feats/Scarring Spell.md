# Scarring Spell

Your emotion-affecting magic causes psychic trauma in your opponents.

**Benefit**: When a scarring spell hits a creature’s defense, for the next 24 hours that creature takes a –2 penalty on defenses against emotion and fear effects you create, and a –1 penalty on defenses against other emotion and fear effects. Penalties from multiple scarring spells don’t stack. This metamagic feat can be applied only to spells with the emotion or fear [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Spell Descriptions|descriptor]]. A scarring spell uses up a spell slot 1 level higher than the spell’s actual level.
