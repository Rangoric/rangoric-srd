# Nightmare Striker

Your faerie fire not only illuminates your foes, but it also shows you their weaknesses.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nightmare Fist|Nightmare Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nightmare Weaver|Nightmare Weaver]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Stunning Fist|Stunning Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 5 ranks, ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]*.

**Benefit**: While a *faerie fire* you have cast (not one from a spell completion or spell trigger item) outlines an opponent, the DC for that opponent to resist your Stunning Fist attempts increases by +2. If you hit an opponent with a Stunning Fist attempt and it fails its save, you can make it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d2 rounds plus 1 round for every 5 by which it failed the save.
