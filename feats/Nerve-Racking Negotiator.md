# Nerve-Racking Negotiator

You've mastered the art of intimidation to get what you want without suffering unwanted repercussions.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Persuasion or Intimidation).

**Benefit**: When you use Intimidation to make an opponent act friendly toward you, it must make a Will save (DC 10 + your ranks in Intimidation) once the intimidation wears off. If it fails, it goes back to the attitude it had toward you before (usually indifferent) and won’t report you to the authorities for intimidating it.

**Normal**: When the intimidation ends, the target becomes unfriendly toward you and may report you to the authorities.
