# Improved Drag

You are skilled at dragging foes around the battlefield.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You don’t provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Drag|drag]]. You gain a +2 bonus on checks to drag a foe, and a +2 bonus to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when an opponent tries to drag you.

**Normal**: You provoke an attack of opportunity when you attempt a drag combat maneuver.
