# Owl Style

You can move with the quiet grace of an owl.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Stealth), [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] 1 rank.

**Benefit**: While using this style, you can use your base attack bonus in place of your ranks in Stealth to work out your Stealth modifier (this doesn’t replace your ranks for anything else, such as Skill Focus or skill unlocks). You can use Stealth while you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]], taking a –10 penalty on top of the penalty for moving at full speed. Foes that fail their Perception checks and don’t otherwise notice you are denied their Dexterity bonus to AC against all your attacks during or at the end of that charge, instead of just the first.

**Normal**: Using Stealth while charging takes a –20 penalty.

**Special**: Denying a foe its Dexterity bonus against all your attacks overlaps [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth#Unlocks|Stealth]]’s 15-rank unlock.
