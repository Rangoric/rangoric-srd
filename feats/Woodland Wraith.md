# Woodland Wraith

You put the terrain between yourself and your opponents to avoid attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Dodge|Dodge]], base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: If you take two actions to move, or the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Withdraw|withdraw]] action, in a turn, and you start or end that movement in naturally occurring difficult terrain (bushes, brambles and so on) or in a space where a natural object (a tree, a rock) gives you at least partial cover, you gain concealment until you attack, move out of that space, or reach the end of your next turn, whichever comes first.
