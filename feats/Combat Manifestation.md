# Combat Manifestation

You are adept at manifesting powers in combat.  

Benefit: You get a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Concentration|Concentration]] checks made to manifest a power or use a psi-like ability while on the defensive or while you are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^grappling|grappling]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^pinned|pinned]].
