# Trap Sense

Prerequisites: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] 22 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] 22 ranks, trapfinding class ability.  

Benefit: If the character passes within 5 feet of a trap, he or she is entitled to an Investigation check to notice it as if the character was actively looking for it.
