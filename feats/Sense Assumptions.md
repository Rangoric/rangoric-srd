# Sense Assumptions

You can easily intuit an opponent's preconceptions.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: After 1 minute of conversation, you can make a Sense Motive check to work out some of another person’s assumptions. The DC is 20 or 10 + its Deception modifier, whichever is higher. If you succeed, you learn whether a lie of your choice about the subject would be hard for it to believe. If you fail by 4 or less, you learn nothing. If you fail by 5 or more, it realizes you are fishing for information. You can try again, but the DC rises by 5 for each earlier failure against that target.
