# Crashing Wave Style

You relentlessly push your enemies around, as the sea moves those within and upon it against their will.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Drag|Improved Drag]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Reposition|Improved Reposition]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]].

**Benefit**: While using this style, when you successfully [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Drag|drag]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Reposition|reposition]] an opponent, at any point during the movement you can move 5 feet as an immediate action, even into a square the opponent occupied and even if you have already taken a move action this round. This movement doesn’t provoke attacks of opportunity.
