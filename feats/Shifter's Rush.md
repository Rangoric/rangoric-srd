# Shifter's Rush

You can use your wild shape ability as you are moving toward your enemy.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-wild-shape|Wild shape]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter|shifter]] level 4th.

**Benefit**: When you use a move action to move 10 feet or more or when you [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]], you can use [[rangoric-srd/classes/Shifter#^shifter-wild-shape|wild shape]] as a free action during that movement.
