# Diagnosis

Your training in the herbal healing arts grants you insight into deducing ailments or afflictions. Your diagnosis can even detect poisons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] 2 ranks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (apothecary) 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: The character gains a +3 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks. This bonus stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Bodily Awareness|Bodily Awareness]].

**Special**: If successful in the use of Medicine, the character’s proper diagnosis allows the victim to gain 1 hit point back in 1d8 hours (instead of 24).

This feat is part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|alchemy and herbalism]].
