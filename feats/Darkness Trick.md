# Darkness Trick

You can manipulate the luminescence of magic weapons.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]] 5 ranks, ability to cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*.

**Benefit**: When wielding a magic weapon that sheds light or is made partly of light, such as a *flaming* or *brilliant energy* weapon, you can turn its light off as a swift action and back on at any time as a free action. While the light is off, any magic tied to it, such as a *flaming* weapon’s extra damage, doesn’t work, and the part of a *brilliant energy* weapon that is made of light doesn’t exist.

If the weapon ever leaves your possession (for example, if you drop it or hand it to someone), its light and the abilities tied to it come back at once.
