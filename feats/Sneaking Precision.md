# Sneaking Precision

Your knowledge of your enemies’ vulnerable spots is especially punishing.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|Sneak attack]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]], any [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^critical-feats|critical feat]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: Whenever you successfully [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] an opponent for a second time on your turn, you can spend a swift action to apply the effects of one [[rangoric-srd/feats/Feats Overview#^critical-feats|critical feat]] you know to that opponent.
