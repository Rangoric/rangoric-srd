# Bodily Awareness

Your training and knowledge provide you with focus and the ability to discern how herbal, alchemical, and magical substances best interact with the bodies of animals and sentients.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) 2 ranks or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (herbalist) 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: The character gets a +2 bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (apothecary) checks.

This feat is part of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemy and Herbalism|alchemy and herbalism]].
