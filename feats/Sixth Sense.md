# Sixth Sense

Sixth Sense allows mortals to sense a fey _doorway_ within range.

**Benefit**: The character has an uncanny ability to detect the presence of a fey _[[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Doorways|doorway]]_. As a free action, the character can detect the presence and direction of a fey _doorway_ within a half-mile radius. The character does not know the specific location of the _doorway_, but merely the direction in which it lies. It also does not grant the ability to see the _doorway_.
