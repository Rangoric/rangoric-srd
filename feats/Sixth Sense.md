# Sixth Sense

Sixth Sense allows mortals to sense a fey *doorway* within range.

**Benefit**: The character has an uncanny ability to detect the presence of a fey *[[rangoric-srd/rules/Fey Roads and Doorways#Doorways|doorway]]*. As a free action, the character can detect the presence and direction of a fey *doorway* within a half-mile radius. The character does not know the specific location of the *doorway*, but merely the direction in which it lies. It also does not grant the ability to see the *doorway*.
