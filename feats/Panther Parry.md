# Panther Parry

Your vicious strikes impair your foe's ability to attack you when you move.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Reflexes|Combat Reflexes]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Panther Claw|Panther Claw]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Panther Style|Panther Style]].

**Benefit**: While using Panther Style, your retaliatory unarmed strikes are resolved before the attacks that trigger them. If one deals damage to the opponent, it takes a –2 penalty on attack and damage rolls with its attack of opportunity.
