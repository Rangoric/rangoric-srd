# Intimidating Gaze

There is something in your eyes that scares people.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13+, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Inquisitor (Class)#^inquisitor-stern-gaze|stern gaze]] class feature, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: Once per day, as a free action, when making an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] skill check, you can roll two dice and take the higher result.
