# Improved Back to Back

After much practice, you and an ally have become adept at fighting in close proximity to one another.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Back to Back|Back to Back]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: While you are adjacent to an ally who is [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Flanking|flanked]] and also has this feat, you can spend a swift action to gain a +2 bonus to AC against all flankers until the start of your next turn.
