# Greater Rending Fury

When your claws latch on to an opponent, the effect is bloody and horrific.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Rending Fury|Improved Rending Fury]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rending Fury|Rending Fury]], base attack bonus +12, rend special attack.

**Benefit**: Whenever you rend an opponent, you also deal 1d6 [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Bleed (Condition)|bleed]] damage to it.
