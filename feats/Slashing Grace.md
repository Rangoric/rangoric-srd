# Slashing Grace

You can stab your enemies with your sword or another slashing weapon.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Finesse|Weapon Finesse]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] with the chosen weapon.

**Benefit**: Choose one kind of light or one-handed slashing weapon (such as the longsword). When wielding your chosen weapon one-handed, you can add your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier to that weapon’s damage, and you can treat it as a one-handed piercing melee weapon for all feats and class abilities that need one (such as a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Duelist|duelist]]’s precise strike). The weapon must be one made for your size. You don’t gain this benefit while fighting with two weapons or using flurry of blows, or any time your other hand is otherwise occupied.
