# Surprise Follow-Through

When striking one opponent, you catch its ally off guard.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Cleave|Great Cleave]], the second foe you attack on your turn is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against you.
