# Crashing Wave Fist

In addition to simply moving it, you are able to strike your enemy while pushing it around.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Crashing Wave Buffet|Crashing Wave Buffet]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Crashing Wave Style|Crashing Wave Style]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Drag|Improved Drag]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Reposition|Improved Reposition]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: When you drag or reposition an opponent, at any point during the movement you can make one unarmed attack against it at your highest attack bonus, plus one more attack for every 5 feet you move it beyond the first 5 feet. Each additional attack takes a cumulative –5 penalty.
