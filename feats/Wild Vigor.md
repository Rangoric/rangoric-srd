# Wild Vigor

The forces of nature bolster your vitality when you use wild shape.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-wild-shape|Wild shape]] class feature.

**Benefit**: A number of times per day equal to your Wisdom bonus (minimum 1), when you use wild shape, you can gain a number of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Temporary Hit Points|temporary hit points]] equal to your base attack bonus. These temporary hit points last for the duration of the wild shape or until they are lost.
