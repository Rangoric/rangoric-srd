# Angelbane Strike

You channel the power of your Abyssal patron through your weapon to punish the righteous.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Channel Smite|Channel Smite]], divine caster level 5th, [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|channel negative energy]] class feature, a demon lord as your patron deity.

**Benefit**: When you use Channel Smite against a living creature of good alignment, treat all 1s on the channel damage dice as 2s. If the target is a good outsider or a divine caster who serves a good deity, you count as 2 levels higher for how many channel energy dice you roll with Channel Smite.
