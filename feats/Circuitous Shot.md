# Circuitous Shot

You can bounce ranged attacks off objects to obscure their point of origin and surprise foes.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 19, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blind-Fight|Blind-Fight]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Precise Shot|Improved Precise Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Precise Shot|Precise Shot]], base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: You can take a –2 penalty on a ranged weapon attack to ricochet it off a stone or metal surface. For cover (but not concealment), resolve the attack as if it came from the ricochet point, and count the whole distance the weapon or ammunition travels for range penalties. This can let you attack a foe that has total cover against you by going around obstacles, but it still has total concealment against the attack.
