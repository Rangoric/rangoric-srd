# Haunted Gnome Assault

Discharging your disturbing glamer as you strike a telling blow, you haunt a foe with lingering fear.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome magic]] racial trait, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Haunted Gnome|Haunted Gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain one use of your [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome magic]] that is independent of your gnome magic spell-like abilities. When you wish to cast a gnome magic spell-like ability for which you have no daily uses remaining, you can expend this independent use to do so.

Further, while you are under the effect of *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haunted Fey Aspect|haunted fey aspect]]*, you can discharge that spell as a free action after you hit an opponent with a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Charge|charge]] attack or score a critical hit against an opponent. If you do, that opponent becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1 round.
