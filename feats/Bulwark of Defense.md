# Bulwark of Defense

Prerequisites: Con 25, defensive stance 3/day.  

Benefit: The character’s defensive stance bonuses increase to +4 Strength, +6 Constitution, +4 resistance bonus on all saves, and +6 dodge bonus to AC.
