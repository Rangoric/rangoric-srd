# Bolstered Resilience

You can dramatically increase your damage reduction in exchange for its temporary loss.

**Prerequisites**: Damage reduction.

**Benefit**: As an immediate action, you can double your damage reduction against a single attack, to a maximum of DR 20. The type of the DR stays the same. If the attack misses, the doubled damage reduction lasts until you are hit or until the start of your next turn, whichever comes first. At the start of your next turn, you become fatigued. You can’t use this feat while you are fatigued.
