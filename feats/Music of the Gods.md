# Music of the Gods

Prerequisites: Cha 25, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] 27 ranks, bardic music class feature.

Benefit: The character’s bardic music can affect even those normally immune to mind-affecting effects. However, such creatures gain a +10 bonus on Will Defense against such effects.
