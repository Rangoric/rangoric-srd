# Distant Shot

Prerequisites: Dex 25, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Far Shot|Far Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Point Blank Shot|Point Blank Shot]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] 17 ranks.  

Benefit: The character may throw or fire a ranged weapon at any target within line of sight, with no penalty for range.
