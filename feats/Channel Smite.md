# Channel Smite

You can channel your divine energy through a melee weapon you wield.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel energy]] class feature.

**Benefit**: Before you make a melee attack roll, you can spend one use of channel energy as a swift action. If you channel positive energy and hit an undead creature, it takes extra damage equal to your channel energy damage; if you channel negative energy and hit a living creature, the same. The extra damage attacks the target’s Will, as normal, and is halved on a miss. If you miss, the use of channel energy is spent with no effect.
