# Inner Breath

Your body is suffused with elemental air that provides for all your respiratory needs.

**Prerequisites**: Character level 11th, [[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|sylph]].

**Benefit**: You no longer need to breathe. You are immune to effects that require breathing (such as [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#^inhaled-poison|inhaled poison]]). This does not give you immunity to cloud or gas attacks that do not require breathing, such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cloudkill|cloudkill]]_.
