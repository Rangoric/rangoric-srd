# Cleaving Sweep

You can sweep multiple foes to the ground with a single blow of your axe.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Trip|Improved Trip]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] (greataxe), base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: When using a full-attack action with a two-handed weapon, you can give up your regular attacks and instead attempt a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuver at your highest base attack bonus against each opponent within reach. You must attempt a separate combat maneuver check against each opponent.
