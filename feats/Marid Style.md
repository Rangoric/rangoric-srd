# Marid Style

You conjure tendrils of icy water to strike your enemies from a distance.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13, Wis 15, [[rangoric-srd/feats/Elemental Fist|Elemental Fist]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]], base attack bonus +9 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: You gain one more Elemental Fist attempt per day. While using this style and Elemental Fist to deal cold damage, you gain a bonus on cold damage rolls equal to your Wisdom bonus. Your reach with your unarmed strike also increases by 5 feet while you deal cold damage this way.
