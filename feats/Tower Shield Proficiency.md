# Tower Shield Proficiency

**Prerequisite**: Shield Proficiency.

**Benefit**: You can use a tower shield and suffer only the standard penalties.

**Normal**: A character who is using a shield with which he or she is not proficient takes the shield’s armor check penalty on attack rolls and on all skill checks that involve moving, including riding checks.

**Special**: Fighters automatically have Tower Shield Proficiency as a bonus feat. They need not select it.
