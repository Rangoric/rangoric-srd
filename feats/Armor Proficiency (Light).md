# Armor Proficiency (Light)

**Benefit**: When you wear a type of armor with which you are proficient, the armor check penalty for that armor applies only to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.

**Normal**: A character who is wearing armor with which she is not proficient applies its armor check penalty to attack rolls and to all skill checks that involve moving, including riding checks.

**Special**: A class whose armor proficiencies include light armor gives this feat as a bonus feat, so its characters need not select it; each class says which armor it is proficient with.
