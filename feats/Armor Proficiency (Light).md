# Armor Proficiency (Light)

**Benefit**: When you wear a type of armor with which you are proficient, the armor check penalty for that armor applies only to Acrobatics, Athletics, Escape Artist, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth checks.

**Normal**: A character who is wearing armor with which she is not proficient applies its armor check penalty to attack rolls and to all skill checks that involve moving, including riding checks.

**Special**: All characters except wizards, sorcerers, and monks automatically have Armor Proficiency (light) as a bonus feat. They need not select it.
