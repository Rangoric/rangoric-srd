# Secret Star of Reality

You know the Secret Star of Reality, which lets you anchor starlight in things.

**Benefit**: You can make lenses, which raise the caster level of anyone calling one particular star or constellation. See [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^lenses|Lenses]] for how they are made.
