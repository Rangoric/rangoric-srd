# Greater Sunder

Your devastating strikes cleave through weapons and armor and into their wielders.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Sunder|Improved Sunder]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Power Attack|Power Attack]], base attack bonus +6, Str 13.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to sunder an item. This stacks with the bonus from [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Sunder|Improved Sunder]]. When you sunder to destroy a weapon, shield or suit of armor, any excess damage goes to the item’s wielder. No damage carries over if you choose to leave the item at 1 hit point.
