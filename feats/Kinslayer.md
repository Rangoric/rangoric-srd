# Kinslayer

Your intimate knowledge of anatomy, deep hatred of your own kind, and delight in spilling your people's blood make you a lethal combatant against your own race.

**Prerequisites**: Evil alignment, 3 ranks in the skill that [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Creatures|identifies creatures]] of your type.

**Benefit**: Your attacks deal an extra 1d6 points of precision damage against creatures of your own creature type (and subtype, if any). This stacks with other precision damage, such as sneak attack. If you have more than one subtype, choose one; the feat applies only to targets of that subtype. Once made, the choice can’t be changed.
