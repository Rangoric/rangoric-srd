# Skill Focus

Choose a skill.

**Benefit**: You get a +3 bonus on all checks involving that skill. If you have 10 or more ranks in that skill, the bonus increases to +6.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the feat, it applies to a new skill. [[rangoric-srd/feats/Signature Skill|Signature Skill]] gives this feat’s benefit too, along with the skill’s unlocks.
