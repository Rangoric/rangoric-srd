# Third Eye

You have a third eye you can open to see things as they really are and gain greater insight into auras.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] or the ability to cast [[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Overview#Psychic Magic|psychic spells]].

**Benefit**: Once per day as a standard action, you can open a spiritual third eye on your forehead, between and above your normal eyes. You can keep it open for up to 1 minute per character level. The duration doesn’t have to be consecutive, but it must be used in 1-minute increments. Once per day while your third eye is open, you can use the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception#Read Aura|read aura]] occult skill unlock after 1 minute of intense concentration, in addition to the normal daily use of read aura (which still takes 10 minutes). While your third eye is open, you gain a +4 bonus on Perception checks to read auras, and a +8 bonus on Perception checks to notice [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Invisibility (Condition)|invisible]] creatures or objects or to see through magical disguises (both illusions and those from the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Change Shape|change shape]] ability or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Polymorph|polymorph]] magic). When you close your third eye, by choice or at the end of the time limit, you are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] for as long as you kept it open.

**Normal**: Without this feat, you must spend 10 minutes in intense concentration to read one of a creature’s or item’s four [[rangoric-srd/rules/Auras|auras]], and you can do so only once per day.

**Special**: You can take this feat more than once. Its effects don’t stack. Each time you take it, the number of times per day you can open your third eye (and read an aura) increases by one.
