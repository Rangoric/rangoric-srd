# Channeled Shield Wall

You draw upon your channel energy to enhance the protective ability of your shield and those of allies while they are adjacent to you.

**Prerequisites**: [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#^channel-energy|Channel energy]] of 3d6 or more, proficiency with a shield.

**Benefit**: As a swift action, you can spend one use of channel energy to gain a +2 deflection bonus to AC while using a shield, for 1 minute per effective cleric level. While it lasts, allies with shields who are adjacent to you also gain a +2 deflection bonus.
