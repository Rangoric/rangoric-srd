# Twinned Feint

You can fool two opponents with a single feint.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can attempt to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Feint|feint]] a foe within your reach. If you succeed, you can attempt another feint against a foe adjacent to the first one and also within your reach. You can attempt only one extra feint per action with this feat. When you use it, you take a –2 penalty to AC until your next turn.

**Special**: If you have [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Feint|Improved Feint]], you can use this feat as a move action instead.
