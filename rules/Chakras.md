# Chakras

Ki gathers in seven vortices within a living creature’s body, called chakras, which distribute it through the body. On the creature’s etheric double (its image on the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Ethereal Plane|Ethereal Plane]]) they appear as small whirling disks of colored energy; they are part of what makes up its [[rangoric-srd/rules/Auras|aura]]. An initiate who has mastered ki can awaken his chakras one after another, from the root chakra at the base of the spine to the crown chakra at the top of the head, by channeling kundalini, the serpent-fire, through them. Each awakened chakra grants a supernatural ability.

## Activating Chakras

Awakening the chakras is possible only for an initiate with a ki pool (such as the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|monk’s ki pool]] or the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/rogue-talents/Ki Pool (Rogue Talent)|ki pool]] rogue talent) or some other means of using ki. The initiate must also have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Psychic Sensitivity|Psychic Sensitivity]] feat or levels in an occult class ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)|medium]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist|mesmerist]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)|psychic]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)|spiritualist]]). ^chakras-activating

Awakening chakras or maintaining awakened chakras is a swift action that costs 1 ki point. On the first round, this opens the root chakra. Each round after the first, it opens the next chakra in the sequence, up to the crown chakra on the seventh round. Once the initiate has begun awakening his chakras, each round he must continue opening chakras or maintain his awakened chakras, or all of his chakras close and he must begin again from the root chakra. If he pauses in the progression, he can resume it at any time, provided he has spent 1 ki point per round in the meanwhile to maintain his open chakras. Each round, when expending the ki for that round, the initiate can select the benefit from one chakra awakened up to that point. For example, if after 4 rounds the initiate has awakened three chakras, he can gain the ability of any of those three. To open a chakra, the initiate’s character level (or total Hit Dice) must equal at least double the chakra’s rank. For example, an initiate must be 14th level to open his crown chakra.

**Risk**: Every round in which a character has one or more awakened chakras, the dangers of the kundalini flow attack his Fortitude and Will Defenses, and he adds his Charisma modifier to each defense. The attack bonus is equal to the sum of the ranks of the awakened chakras. For example, awakening the root, sacral, and navel chakras results in an attack bonus of +6, and opening all seven chakras at once results in +28 attacks. The attack bonus totals are listed after each chakra’s rank and name below. If the attack hits his Fortitude, he takes 1d6 points of damage per awakened chakra. If the attack hits his Will, he is overcome with visions related to the emotions and concepts associated with the awakened chakras, causing him to be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|dazed]] for 1 round and causing all of his chakras to close. ^chakras-risk

The [[rangoric-srd/feats/Chakra Initiate|Chakra Initiate]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Chakra Adept|Chakra Adept]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Chakra Master|Chakra Master]] feats give a pool of serpent-fire ki for opening chakras and make them easier to hold.

## The Seven Chakras

**1. Root Chakra (+1)**: Associated with instinct, security, survival, and the potential for greater development. By awakening his root chakra, the initiate opens himself to an inflow of serpent-fire energy that strengthens his aura, granting him DR/— equal to the number of chakras opened for 1 round. The initiate must be at least 2nd level before he can open this chakra. ^chakras-root

**2. Sacral Chakra (+3)**: Associated with pleasure, addiction, creativity, emotional needs, and relationships. By awakening his sacral chakra, the initiate gains a fly speed equal to his base speed for 1 round with average [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|maneuverability]]. The movement must begin and end on a solid surface. The initiate must be at least 4th level before he can open this chakra. ^chakras-sacral

**3. Navel Chakra (+6)**: Also called the power chakra; associated with fire, anger, joy, fear, anxiety, and laughter. Upon awakening his navel chakra, the initiate can channel the serpent-fire as a breath attack that deals 2d8 points of damage in a 30-foot cone. This radiant gout of orange-red liquid flame resembles fire, but bypasses all forms of energy resistance, protection, and immunity. The damage increases by 1d8 for each open chakra beyond the 3rd, to a maximum of 6d8 if all of the chakras are awakened. The initiate must be at least 6th level before he can open this chakra. ^chakras-navel

**4. Heart Chakra (+10)**: Associated with compassion, tenderness, love, and rejection. Once the heart chakra has been awakened, the initiate can channel his serpent-fire into a torrent of healing energy. He can use this ability on himself or touch any adjacent living creature with this fire to heal an amount of damage equal to 1d8 + double the number of chakras he has awakened. In addition, he can remove one of the following conditions from the creature healed: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]], or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]]. This fire has no effect on constructs, undead, or other creatures that are not alive. The initiate must be at least 8th level before he can open this chakra. ^chakras-heart

**5. Throat Chakra (+15)**: The bridge between feeling and thinking, thrumming with the vibrations that govern existence. By awakening the throat chakra, the initiate taps into the primordial language underlying all things and slips into a kind of trance. He can take actions as normal, but he constantly mutters incoherent, often disturbing utterances. Each round, he can select one enemy within 30 feet. The initiate attacks that foe’s Will, and on a hit, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for 1 round. The attack bonus is 1/2 the initiate’s total Hit Dice + his Charisma modifier. This is a sonic mind-affecting effect. The initiate must be at least 10th level before he can open this chakra. ^chakras-throat

**6. Brow Chakra (+21)**: Also called the intuitive chakra; it governs occult vision. Once the brow chakra has been opened, the initiate gains the benefits of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_ for 1 round. A shining third eye of psychic energy opens on the initiate’s forehead; it is easily perceived by anyone looking at him, and persists as long as he maintains the brow chakra. The initiate must be at least 12th level before he can open this chakra. ^chakras-brow

**7. Crown Chakra (+28)**: Channeling serpent-fire through all seven chakras at once bathes the initiate’s aura in golden light like a halo and gives him enormous sway over probability. Once the crown chakra is opened, the initiate can gain the benefit of any two distinct chakras each round. He gains this benefit even if he doesn’t select the crown chakra. If he selects the crown chakra, every time he would roll a d20 during that turn (such as when attempting an attack roll or a skill check), he can roll twice and take the higher result, and any attack against his defenses during that turn is made with disadvantage. The initiate must be at least 14th level before he can open this chakra. ^chakras-crown
