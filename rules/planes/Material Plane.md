# Material Plane

The Material Plane is the center of most cosmologies and defines what is considered normal. It is the plane most campaign worlds occupy.

The Material Plane has the following traits (what each means is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#Planar Traits|planar traits]]):

- **Normal Gravity**
- **Normal Time**
- **Alterable Morphic**
- **No Elemental or Energy Traits**: Specific locations may have these traits, however.
- **Mildly Neutral-Aligned**: Though it may contain high concentrations of evil or good, law or chaos in places.
- **Normal Magic**

[[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes|Planes]] describes this plane too: see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Material Plane|The Material Plane]].

The planes of this cosmology are listed in [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/Cosmology|Cosmology]].
