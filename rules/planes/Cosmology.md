# Cosmology

In this cosmology, the planes form a vast, nesting sphere. At the heart of the sphere lie the Material Plane and its twisted reflection, the Shadow Plane, bridged by the mists of the Ethereal Plane. The elemental Inner Planes surround this heart. Farther out, beyond the void of the Astral Plane, sit the unimaginably vast Outer Planes, which are themselves surrounded and contained by the innumerable layers of the Abyss.

Each plane has a note of its own, with its traits. How planar traits work, and the standard descriptions of the Material, Transitive and Inner Planes, are in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes|Planes]].
