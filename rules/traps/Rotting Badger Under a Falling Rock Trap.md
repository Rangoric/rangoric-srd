# Rotting Badger Under a Falling Rock Trap

A sample trap; how traps work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps#Elements of a Trap|traps]]. Kobolds build this trap; other creatures can too, at the GM’s discretion.

- **CR**: 4
- **Type**: mechanical
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]]**: DC 25 to find
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]**: DC 25 to disable
- **Trigger**: location
- **Reset**: repair
- **Effect**: Atk +10 melee (1d6+6); rotting badger ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1d3 rounds, Fort DC 20 negates); multiple targets (all targets within 10 feet must attempt to save against the rotting badger)
