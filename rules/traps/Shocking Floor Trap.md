# Shocking Floor Trap

A sample trap; how traps work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps#Elements of a Trap|traps]].

- **CR**: 9
- **Type**: magic
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]]**: DC 26 to find
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]**: DC 26 to disable
- **Trigger**: proximity (*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]*)
- **Reset**: none
- **Duration**: 1d6 rounds
- **Effect**: spell effect (*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]*, Atk +9 melee touch [4d6 electricity damage]); multiple targets (all targets in a 40-ft.-square room)
