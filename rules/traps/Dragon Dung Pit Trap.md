# Dragon Dung Pit Trap

A sample trap; how traps work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps#Elements of a Trap|traps]]. Kobolds build this trap; other creatures can too, at the GM’s discretion.

- **CR**: 2
- **Type**: mechanical
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]]**: DC 20 to find
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]**: DC 20 to disable
- **Trigger**: location
- **Reset**: manual
- **Effect**: 20-ft.-deep pit (2d6 falling damage); dragon dung (Atk +10, creature stinks of dragon dung, –4 on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks for 24 hours, –8 on Stealth checks if detecting creature has the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] ability); Reflex DC 20 negates; multiple targets (all targets in a 10-ft.-square area)
