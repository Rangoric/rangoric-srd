# Camouflaged Spiked Pit Trap

A sample trap; how traps work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps#Elements of a Trap|traps]].

- **CR**: 8
- **Type**: mechanical
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]]**: DC 25 to find
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]**: DC 20 to disable
- **Trigger**: location
- **Reset**: manual
- **Effect**: 50-ft.-deep pit (5d6 falling damage); pit spikes (Atk +15 melee, 1d4 spikes per target for 1d6+5 damage each); DC 20 Reflex avoids; multiple targets (all targets in a 10-ft.-square area)
