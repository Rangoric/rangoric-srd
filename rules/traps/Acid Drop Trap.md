# Acid Drop Trap

A sample trap; how traps work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps#Elements of a Trap|traps]]. Kobolds build this trap; other creatures can too, at the GM’s discretion.

- **CR**: 2
- **Type**: mechanical
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]]**: DC 25 to find
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]**: DC 20 to disable
- **Trigger**: location
- **Reset**: none
- **Effect**: falling bag of acid (3d6 acid damage, Reflex DC 20 half)
