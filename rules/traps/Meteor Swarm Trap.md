# Meteor Swarm Trap

A sample trap; how traps work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps#Elements of a Trap|traps]].

- **Type**: magic
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]]**: DC 34 to find
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]**: DC 34 to disable
- **Trigger**: visual (*[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]*)
- **Reset**: none
- **Effect**: spell effect (*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]]*, 4 meteors at separate targets, +9 ranged touch, 2d6 plus 6d6 fire [–4 save on a hit], DC 23 Reflex save for half fire damage, 18d6 fire damage from other meteors, DC 23 Reflex save for half damage); multiple targets (four targets, no two of which can be more than 40 ft. apart)
