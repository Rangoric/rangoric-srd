# Empowered Disintegrate Trap

A sample trap; how traps work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Traps#Elements of a Trap|traps]].

- **CR**: 16
- **Type**: magic
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]]**: DC 33 to find
- **[[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]]**: DC 33 to disable
- **Trigger**: visual (_[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_)
- **Reset**: none
- **Effect**: spell effect (empowered _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disintegrate|disintegrate]]_, +9 ranged touch, 30d6 damage plus 50%, +9 vs Fortitude, a miss reducing the damage to 5d6 plus 50%)
