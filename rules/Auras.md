# Auras

Every living creature carries a spark of the cosmic fire that burns at the heart of the multiverse. This vital force animates beings, carrying directions from their minds to their bodies, and with practice an initiate in the occult arts can work with it. Traditions call it by different names (prana, odic force, orgone energy), but it is most commonly known as ki, the source of the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|monk’s]] inner power.

Practiced initiates can learn to pierce the veil between the Material Plane and the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#The Ethereal Plane|Ethereal Plane]] to watch the interplay of ki within a creature’s body. The energy gathers in seven swirling pools positioned throughout the body, called [[rangoric-srd/rules/Chakras|chakras]]. From there it flows through the creature’s form and even emanates 2 to 3 feet beyond it, making an ovoid nimbus around the creature: its aura.

The aura permeates a living creature’s body and spirit, influencing its health and vitality while being influenced in turn by its disposition, sense of self and emotional state. A creature’s aura is thus many auras mixed into one whole. The emanations of the creature’s ki form its **health aura**. Thought and belief issuing from its soul suffuse the health aura with patterns that can be read as its **alignment aura**. The flashes of color flowing through the whole reveal its attitude and disposition, and are its **emotion aura**. Last, an aura might hold emanations of magical origin, its **magic aura**, usually the work of outside forces on the creature.

Various spells detect parts of a creature’s or object’s aura. The _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Analyze Aura|analyze aura]]_ spell and the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception#Read Aura|read aura]] occult skill unlock are the most thorough ways to assess a creature’s whole aura.

## Alignment Aura

A creature’s alignment aura reflects the true nature of its beliefs and inner soul. It looks like a churning mist suffusing the health aura, and close study of the currents in this mist can identify the creature’s alignment. Objects or places infused with powerful magic attuned to an alignment (such as the blood-splattered altar where a debased cult holds its rituals, or a gilded cup holding the tears of a long-dead saint) sometimes have alignment auras too.

An alignment aura’s strength depends on the creature’s Hit Dice or the magic item’s caster level, as described in _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]_.

## Emotion Aura

A creature’s emotion aura reveals its current disposition and attitudes, which color the aura with a shimmer of hues called the emotional spectrum. Any combination of the colors below can make up a creature’s emotional state. Ways of perceiving the emotion aura usually reveal the emotions themselves as well as their colors: the secrets of the emotional spectrum are laid bare to the initiate, and shouldn’t stay a mystery to the player.

Since emotion auras are part of the health aura, objects and undead, which aren’t truly alive, seldom have them. Intelligent magic items sometimes have an emotion aura, and undead (particularly those on the Ethereal Plane, barred from the afterlife by emotional distress or unfinished business) may bear a tattered remnant of their auratic energy clinging to their haunted forms, though the life energy that made them ceased long ago.

### The Emotional Spectrum

Someone trained in auratic techniques sees an interplay of colors within a creature’s aura, and understanding this emotional spectrum can reveal a great deal about the creature. The colors blend and shift constantly as the creature’s thoughts, emotions and surroundings change, so a reading is a snapshot of its feelings and general character at one moment, and might not be accurate days or even minutes later.

## Health Aura

A creature’s health aura is a direct picture of the vital force flowing within its body. Ki comes from various sources in the outside world and flows into and throughout the nervous system through the swirling pools called [[rangoric-srd/rules/Chakras|chakras]]. Within the body, vital force has a faint rosy hue that fades outward into a vaporous white cloud, streaked by lines that stand out like bristles. When the creature is in normal health, these bristles stand stiffly; when it is ill, they droop like the whiskers of a weary animal. Reading the flow of vital force between chakras, and the fine lines running through the greater aura, lets an observer tell whether the creature is unharmed or wounded, whether it is poisoned or diseased, and whether it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^disabled|disabled]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^panicked|panicked]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]].

## Magic Aura

Magic auras are usually the result of outside forces working on an object, creature or place. Each magical effect swirls together with the others in the greater aura but stays distinct, so it’s possible to single out one effect within the aura, for example to dispel one magical effect and leave the others intact.

Other magic auras come from a creature’s own supernatural abilities, and can affect those who come too close. Such auras (such as a dragon’s frightful presence) often attack the defenses of creatures that come near. For more on magic auras, see _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]_.
