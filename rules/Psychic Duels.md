# Psychic Duels

Psychic duels are battles in which the combatants’ minds become intertwined within a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Mindscapes#Binary Mindscapes|binary mindscape]]. Though psychic spellcasters wield tremendous power in such battles, other creatures can also wield mental might with surprising potency.

The manifestations, manifestation points and manifesting level of a psychic duel have nothing to do with manifesting psionic powers: a combatant’s manifesting level isn’t a psionic [[rangoric-srd/psionics/Psionics#Manifester Level|manifester level]], and manifestation points aren’t [[rangoric-srd/psionics/Psionics#Powers and Power Points|power points]].

## Instigating a Psychic Duel

A psychic spellcaster creates a binary mindscape when _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Instigate Psychic Duel|instigate psychic duel]]_ hits a target. The caster’s and the target’s minds are then merged in a psychic duel within that mindscape until the spell’s duration ends or the duel reaches its conclusion (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Psychic Duels#Concluding a Psychic Duel|Concluding a Psychic Duel]]). If the duel begins during combat, each duelist keeps its place in the initiative order, and the duel goes on alongside the combat.

If the duel begins outside combat, the initiator can take a swift action to create a psychic manifestation upon entering the binary mindscape, before her opponent’s first turn.

While locked in a psychic duel, the combatants’ physical bodies are unresponsive. They keep their positions, but they can take no actions and lose their Dexterity bonuses to Armor Class for the duration of the duel.

## Running Psychic Duels

Each combatant in a psychic duel attacks her opponent and defends herself with the power of her imagination and the force of her personality. Combatants appear in the binary mindscape much as they appear in reality (unless they use a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Mindscapes#Mental Masks|mental mask]]), but the strange laws of the binary mindscape enhance their abilities and attacks in some ways and limit them in others.

While the illusion of physical movement exists in the mindscape, speed and position have little bearing on a psychic duel. Combatants are limited to purely mental actions, speech and the creation of psychic manifestations. With very few exceptions (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Psychic Duels#Concluding a Psychic Duel|Concluding a Psychic Duel]]), spells and spell-like abilities can’t be used.

When a character enters a binary mindscape, she uses these statistics as they were before she entered: her current hit points; ability scores; AC and touch AC; Fortitude, Reflex and Will Defenses; highest melee attack bonus; and highest ranged attack bonus. The attack bonuses apply on melee and ranged offensive manifestations. [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Regeneration|Regeneration]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] are the only special abilities that apply in a psychic duel; [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-uncanny-dodge|uncanny dodge]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Damage Reduction|damage reduction]], for example, don’t.

Bonuses and penalties from items, and from spells and effects active before the creature entered the mindscape, apply to these statistics, since such items and effects gain psychic equivalents when a creature enters a mindscape. While the mind is engaged in a psychic duel, though, only damaging and healing spells and effects cast on a combatant’s body have any effect on the battle.

Manifestations keep many of their creator’s combat capabilities. The binary mindscape translates martial prowess into psychic parallels, since such prowess comes from training, muscle memory and other subconscious parts of the mind. So while martially inclined characters have fewer resources in a psychic duel, they can still make powerful attacks against their foes with great accuracy.

Conditions gained during a psychic duel rarely carry over into the real world, but damage is dealt to the duelist’s physical body, so combatants can die. Likewise, unlike every other effect in the real world after a psychic duel begins, damage and healing to a duelist’s physical body carry over into the duel.

## Psychic Manifestations

While engaged in a psychic duel, the combatants don’t attack or cast spells normally. Instead, they create psychic manifestations, abilities that can be used only within a binary mindscape; they represent changes to the fabric of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Mindscapes|mindscape]].

### Generating Manifestation Points

Psychic manifestations are fueled by psychic energy, measured in manifestation points (MP). Each combatant has a manifesting level equal to its highest caster level or half its Hit Dice, whichever is higher. The most MP a combatant can spend on one manifestation is the level of the highest-level spell it can cast or 1/4 its Hit Dice (minimum 1, maximum 5), whichever is higher.

To create a psychic manifestation, a combatant generates MP in any of four ways: from ability scores, from class features, by sacrificing spells or by sacrificing spell-like abilities. She can use each of these methods only once per manifestation. For example, a gunslinger 1/wizard 8 could generate 1 MP from ability scores, 1 MP from grit and 2 MP by sacrificing a 2nd-level spell, reaching her limit of 4 MP per manifestation, but she couldn’t do so by sacrificing four different 1st-level spells. MP a combatant generates and doesn’t use at once are lost.

Generating MP from ability scores doesn’t deplete the scores, but it has other consequences for the combatant once the duel ends (below). Every other source of MP depletes the spent resources in the real world as well.

**From Ability Scores**: Every combatant begins a psychic duel with a pool of MP equal to its Hit Dice + the average of its Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma scores. As a free action, a combatant can draw from this pool to generate MP. A combatant who draws any points from the pool is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] upon leaving the binary mindscape. One who draws half or more of them is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]] upon leaving the mindscape, and takes a –4 penalty to Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma for 24 hours afterward. One who spends the entire pool becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] after leaving the mindscape. These conditions and the –4 penalty ignore all immunities and last for 24 hours; nothing but _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_ removes them sooner.

**From Class Features**: As a free action, a combatant with an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-arcane-pool|arcane pool]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-grit|grit]], a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk#^monk-ki-pool|ki pool]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Swashbuckler#^swashbuckler-panache|panache]] or a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)#^psychic-phrenic-pool|phrenic pool]] (or another similar class feature the GM chooses) can spend points from it to generate MP, at 1 MP per point spent.

**Sacrificing Spells**: As a free action, a combatant can sacrifice one spell slot (a spontaneous caster) or prepared spell (a prepared caster) to generate MP equal to the level of the slot or spell. [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-alchemy|Alchemists]] and [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-alchemy|investigators]] can sacrifice an extract slot as if it were a spell slot, but only if the slot is open and not already put into a physical extract.

**Sacrificing Spell-Like Abilities**: A combatant can sacrifice a use of a spell-like ability to generate MP the same way. Each at-will spell-like ability can be sacrificed only once in a psychic duel; it isn’t an endless source of MP.

### Creating Psychic Manifestations

There are three kinds of psychic manifestation: offensive manifestations, defensive manifestations and thought-form creatures. For [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]], creating a manifestation counts as casting a spell of a level equal to the MP spent on it, and the creator uses her manifesting level as her caster level.

#### Offensive Manifestations

_Full-round action._ An offensive manifestation is a direct attack on an opponent, a thought-form creature or both. It can attack a variety of defenses, depending on the MP spent and its creator’s wishes. Creating one takes three steps.

**Step 1, Sensory Effects**: Only the creator’s imagination limits a manifestation’s look, sound, feel and smell, and these sensory effects guide the later choices. For example, a combatant who pictures his offensive manifestation as a giant ball of lava hurled at his opponent might have it attack the opponent’s Reflex Defense rather than AC. Whatever its form, a manifestation’s damage is always untyped, and only defensive manifestations can reduce it.

**Step 2, Attack Type**: By default, an offensive manifestation is a melee or ranged attack (creator’s choice) against one target’s AC. For 2 more MP, it resolves against touch AC instead.

Alternatively, for 1 more MP, the creator can have the manifestation attack a defense of his opponent and any thought-form creature it controls. Its attack bonus is the total MP spent + the creator’s Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma bonus (whichever is highest). The creator chooses which defense it attacks: Fortitude, Reflex or Will. By default, a miss negates the manifestation’s damage; for 1 more MP, a miss halves it instead.

If a miss can negate or reduce an offensive manifestation’s effects, the creator can spend more to have the manifestation apply a condition (Step 3).

**Step 3, Damage and Conditions**: The creator then sets the manifestation’s number of damage dice. She must spend at least 1 MP on damage, which gives the manifestation 1 damage die, and can spend more for more dice, as the table shows.

| MP Spent on Damage | Damage Dice                                   |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1                  | 1 die                                         |
| 2                  | 4 dice                                        |
| 3                  | 6 dice                                        |
| 4                  | 8 dice                                        |
| 5                  | 10 dice                                       |
| 6                  | 12 dice                                       |
| 7                  | 14 dice                                       |
| 8                  | 16 dice                                       |
| 9                  | 1 die per manifesting level (maximum 20 dice) |

The damage die depends on where the manifestation’s MP came from. Sacrificed psychic spells and psychic spell-like abilities give d8s; arcane spells and other spell-like abilities give d6s; every other way of generating MP (including mixed ways) gives d4s. If the manifestation’s attack resolves against AC or touch AC, a natural 20 on the attack roll threatens a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]], for double damage.

If the manifestation attacks a defense, the creator can spend more MP to add one condition from the list below (she must still spend at least 1 MP on damage). A miss always negates the condition. Conditions gained in a psychic duel end if the duel ends before their normal duration does. Otherwise they follow the normal rules for those conditions, with these exceptions and additions.

- **[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|Blinded]] (3 MP)**: Affected creatures are blinded for 1 round, and can’t use offensive manifestations that attack a defense.
- **[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|Confused]] (3 MP)**: Affected creatures are confused for 1 round, which breaks concentration automatically. An affected creature forced to attack the nearest creature attacks its opponent’s thought-form creature if there is one, or the opponent if not. A confused combatant never attacks her own thought-form creature, or the other way round. Only for manifestations that attack Will Defense.
- **[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazed|Dazed]] (4 MP)**: Affected creatures are dazed for 1 round. A dazed combatant can go on concentrating on thought-form creatures and creating defensive manifestations, but can’t create thought-form creatures or offensive manifestations. Only for manifestations that attack Will Defense.
- **[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|Entangled]] (2 MP)**: Affected creatures are entangled until they take a full-round action to end the condition. Only for manifestations that attack Reflex Defense.
- **[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|Nauseated]] (4 MP)**: Affected creatures are nauseated for 1 round. A nauseated combatant can create only defensive manifestations. Only for manifestations that attack Fortitude Defense.
- **[[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|Shaken]] (1 MP)**: Affected creatures are shaken for 1d4 rounds. Only for manifestations that attack Will Defense.

#### Defensive Manifestations

_Action varies._ Defensive manifestations protect a combatant from her opponent’s manifestations. A combatant in a psychic duel can create these.

- **Evasive Mind** (1 or more MP; swift action): The combatant gains a +2 bonus on all defenses and gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Evasion and Improved Evasion|evasion]] for a number of rounds equal to the MP spent.
- **Hardened Mind** (1 to 4 MP; swift action): For 1 round, the combatant’s Armor Class increases by the MP spent, and her touch AC by half that (minimum +0).
- **Thought Barrier** (1 or more MP; swift action): The damage the combatant takes from offensive manifestations and thought-form creatures is reduced by 5 points, for a number of rounds equal to the MP spent.
- **Disruptive Counter** (variable MP; immediate action): When the combatant’s opponent creates a manifestation, the combatant can spend MP equal to that manifestation’s cost + 2 to counter it. When she does, any thought-form creature or defensive manifestations she has active are dismissed.
- **Nimble Mind** (2 MP; immediate action): When the combatant is attacked with an offensive manifestation that targets her touch AC, it targets her full AC instead.
- **Psychic Redirect** (1 MP; immediate action): When the combatant would take damage while she has a thought-form creature active, she can have her thought-form creature take it instead. If the attack targets both the combatant and the thought-form creature, the thought-form creature takes the damage for both and suffers any condition the attack confers, and the combatant suffers no effect.

#### Thought-Form Creatures

_1-round action._ A thought-form creature is a mentally constructed thrall that takes any form its creator wants. Creating one takes 1 full round (as casting a spell with a casting time of 1 round), and it lasts as long as its creator concentrates on its existence as a full-round action; a combatant can have only one thought-form creature at a time. A thought-form creature must attack the opposing thought-form creature if there is one; if not, it can attack its creator’s opponent. It makes its attacks when its creator uses a full-round action to concentrate on it. When a thought-form creature is reduced to 0 or fewer hit points, it disappears and its creator takes damage equal to its psychic backlash. A thought-form creature can be dismissed as a free action.

A basic thought-form creature costs 1 MP and has these statistics.

- **AC**: the creator’s AC; **touch AC**: the creator’s touch AC.
- **Hit Points**: 1/2 the creator’s current hit points.
- **Attack Bonus**: the creator’s manifesting level + the creator’s Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma modifier (whichever is highest).
- **Damage**: 1d6 + the creator’s Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma modifier (whichever is highest).
- **Defenses**: the creator’s defenses.
- **Psychic Backlash**: 1d10 + the thought-form creature’s MP cost.

**Augmentations**: When creating a thought-form creature, its creator can add any of these augmentations, up to her MP limit per manifestation.

- **Increase Attack Bonus**: Up to 4 more MP raise its attack bonus on all its attacks by the same amount.
- **Extra Attacks**: 2 more MP give it a second attack each round; 2 more on top of that give it a third attack, made at a –5 penalty.
- **Increase Armor Class**: Up to 4 more MP raise its AC by the same amount and its touch AC by half that.
- **Increase Defenses**: 2 or 4 more MP raise all its defenses by half that amount.
- **Increase Hit Points**: Up to 4 more MP raise its hit points by 10 × that amount.
- **Increase Damage**: 1 more MP makes the damage die of all its attacks a d8, or 2 more MP a d10. If it has only one attack, its damage bonus on that attack becomes 1-1/2 × the creator’s Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma bonus (whichever is highest).
- **Protective Ability**: 1 more MP gives the creator a +4 bonus to AC (normal and touch), a +2 bonus on Reflex Defense and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Evasion and Improved Evasion|evasion]] for as long as the thought-form creature exists; 2 more on top of that also make the creator immune to attacks against AC or touch AC for as long as it exists.

## Concluding a Psychic Duel

A psychic duel can end, and the combatants’ minds return to their bodies, in several ways besides the end of the _instigate psychic duel_ spell that started it. The duel ends at once if one of the combatants reaches 0 or fewer hit points, or if both combatants agree to end it; each can agree as a free action, even when it isn’t that combatant’s turn. Rarely, a spell or ability lets combatants leave a psychic duel: unlike most spells, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mindscape Door|mindscape door]]_ can be cast during a psychic duel, letting the caster leave the mindscape and end the duel.

Lastly, a creature adjacent to a combatant’s body can try to shake it out of its torpor as a standard action. This is a melee touch attack that deals no damage but makes the effect that started the psychic duel attack the combatant again. If it misses, the roused creature’s mind returns to its body and the duel ends. The effect can be made to attack again no more than once per round this way.
