# Blood Magic

Blood magic is the magical art of creating spells by supplying the needed magical energies from the magician’s own life force. Practiced by devotees called *[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Blood Mage|blood mages]]*, it is a dangerous art that requires the blood mage to give of his own body to cast spells. While a blood mage may cast more spells than other arcane spellcasters, he is also in far more danger from his mystical art than an ordinary wizard or sorcerer.

## What belongs to blood magic

- **Feat**: [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blood Power|Blood Power]], the way any arcane caster spends hit points instead of spell slots.
- **Prestige class**: the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Blood Mage|Blood Mage]], who lowers that cost and fuels spells with ability points, Fortitude, sacrificed hit points and the life force of others.
- **Items**: the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Blood Chalice|blood chalice]], and the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Bloodlinked (Armor Special Ability)|bloodlinked]] armor and [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Bloodlinked (Weapon Special Ability)|bloodlinked]] weapon special abilities, which store hit points for Blood Power.
- **Spell**: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bloodfire|bloodfire]]*, which spends the caster’s Constitution.
