# Confounded Casting

*Minor [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-minor-spellblights|spellblight]]*

A spellcaster with confounded casting has a mental disconnect between the spells she intends to cast and the spells she actually casts. The first time on her turn that she casts a spell or uses a spell-like ability, she makes a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] with a DC of 15 + twice the level of the spell being cast. If the concentration check fails, the caster must pick another spell or spell-like ability of the same level or lower to cast with the same casting time. If the spellcaster has no other spell or spell-like ability, the action is lost. The spell or spell-like ability she originally picked is not spent and she can attempt to cast that spell again later.

Confounded casting is cured by any effect that removes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confusion]] or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insanity (Spell)|insanity]]*. Creatures that are immune to mind-affecting effects are immune to this spellblight.
