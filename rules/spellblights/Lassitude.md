# Lassitude

*Minor [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-minor-spellblights|spellblight]]*

Whenever a spellcaster with this spellblight casts a spell, she must make a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] (DC 15 + twice the spell level of the spell being cast). If she fails the check, the spellcaster takes 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Nonlethal Damage|nonlethal damage]] per spell level (or 1 point of nonlethal damage when casting a 0-level spell or 1 point per 2 caster levels if using a spell-like ability). This nonlethal damage cannot be reduced in any way so long as the spellcaster suffers from lassitude.

Lassitude can be cured by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]*, or any effect that completely removes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhaustion]]. Simply resting has no effect. Creatures immune to nonlethal damage are immune to this spellblight.
