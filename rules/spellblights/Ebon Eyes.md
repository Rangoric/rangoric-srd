# Ebon Eyes

_Minor [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-minor-spellblights|spellblight]]_

A spellcaster with ebon eyes develops a jet-black film over her eyes, which inverts her capacity to perceive light and darkness. The creature treats darkness as bright light, dim light as normal light, normal light as dim light, and bright light as darkness. The ebon eyes protect against blinding, dazzling, patterns, or other visual effects, granting a +2 bonus on all defenses against those effects.

Ebon eyes can be removed with an effect that removes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blindness]].
