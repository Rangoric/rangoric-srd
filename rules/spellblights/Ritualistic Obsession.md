# Ritualistic Obsession

*Minor [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-minor-spellblights|spellblight]]*

A spellcaster afflicted with ritualistic obsession adds unnecessary gestures to her spellcasting activities. Any spell without a somatic component (even a spell cast with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Still Spell|Still Spell]] feat) now requires one, and any spell that already has a somatic component requires two free hands rather than one. Spell-like abilities now require a somatic component. The extra complexity increases swift action casting times to a standard action, standard action casting times to 1 round, and 1 round casting times to 2 rounds. Other casting times are not increased. The extra focus does serve to increase the efficacy of the caster’s spells. All save DCs for spells and spell-like abilities that have their casting time increased with ritualistic obsession are increased by 1.

Ritualistic obsession is cured by any effect that removes *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insanity (Spell)|insanity]]*. Creatures that are immune to mind-affecting effects are immune to ritualistic obsession.
