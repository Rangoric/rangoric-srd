# Eldritch Cataracts

_Major [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-major-spellblights|spellblight]]_

Each time a spellcaster with this spellblight casts a spell, her vision becomes fuzzier and fuzzier until eventually she is blinded. Each time the spellcaster casts a spell, the spellblight attacks her Fortitude, its attack bonus +5 + the spell’s level. On a hit, she takes a –1 penalty on vision-based [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks until the spellblight is removed. Further hits increase the penalty by 1 until the character reaches a –4 penalty, at which point she becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] instead.

Eldritch cataracts is a difficult spellblight to cure. Any effect that cures blindness reduces the penalty by 1. If the penalty is reduced to –3 or lower, any blindness is relieved until the spellblight hits the caster’s Fortitude enough times to bring the penalty back to –4. Reducing the penalty to 0 cures the spellblight.

Blind creatures and creatures that don’t use sight are immune to eldritch cataracts.
