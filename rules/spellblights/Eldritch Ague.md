# Eldritch Ague

*Minor [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-minor-spellblights|spellblight]]*

Eldritch ague manifests itself as a sickness that is debilitating to spellcasters. A spellcaster with this spellblight is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] until the curse is removed. When the subject of the curse casts a spell, she is overcome with shaking for 1 round, requiring any spellcasting or the use of a spell-like ability during that time to succeed at a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] (DC 15 + twice the spell level of the spell being cast). If she fails the check, the spell and the action to cast it are lost.

While a curse, eldritch ague acts much like a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Disease|disease]], and creatures with immunity to disease are also immune to eldritch ague. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]* cures eldritch ague; unlike with most spellblights, using this spell to remove the spellblight does not impose a [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#Removing Spellblights|–5 caster level check penalty]].
