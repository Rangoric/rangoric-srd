# Spellblights

Spellblights are rare and unusual magical conditions that uniquely affect spellcasters, including creatures that use spell-like abilities. Spellblights are curses, some functioning continuously and others manifesting only when the afflicted creature attempts to cast a spell or use a spell-like ability. A creature that lacks the ability to cast spells or use spell-like abilities cannot usually be afflicted by a spellblight.

Unlike many magical effects, a spellblight usually persists in an *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antimagic Field|antimagic field]]*, though because they often affect spellcasting, their effect is typically lessened in such a field.

Some of the effects of spellblights can be somewhat beneficial. Crafty spellcasters can make the best of even the worst situations, and the beneficial effects of any spellblights represent this.

## Gaining Spellblights

For now, a creature gains a spellblight from a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#^critical-hits|critical hit]] by a caster with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blighted Critical|Blighted Critical]] (a random minor spellblight) or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Greater Blighted Critical|Greater Blighted Critical]] (a random major one), or from one of the spells that give a spellblight: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Curse of Magic Negation|curse of magic negation]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Eldritch Fever|eldritch fever]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Steal Voice|steal voice]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prediction of Failure|prediction of failure]]*.

## Removing Spellblights

While the most severe symptoms of a spellblight may manifest only episodically, the condition is often tenacious and difficult to remove. Each spellblight lists conditions for its removal. Spellblights are also among the special curses that *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]* can’t simply remove: any spellblight can be removed as if it were a curse (with *remove curse*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]*, and so on), but doing so takes a caster level check (1d20 + caster level). The DC to remove a minor spellblight is 20; the DC to remove a major spellblight is 30. The DC is reduced by 1 with each day that passes (minimum DC 10). Using anything other than a curse-removing spell to remove a spellblight imposes a –5 penalty on the caster level check. *Break enchantment* uses this DC instead of its own, and its maximum of +15 on the check still applies.

Any spellblight can be removed automatically, without a caster level check, with *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Disjunction|mage’s disjunction]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]*, or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]*.

## Concentration Checks

If a spellblight calls for a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] based on the level of the effect, and the caster is using a spell-like ability that doesn’t have an effective level, use half the caster level of the spell-like ability instead.
