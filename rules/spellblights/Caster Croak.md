# Caster Croak

*Minor [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-minor-spellblights|spellblight]]*

The afflicted spellcaster’s throat or mouth is magically constricted. The spellcaster can barely make her words heard, and then only with great effort. A creature under this effect can only make itself heard by others by spending a swift action to focus its will on speech. Casting spells with a verbal component has a 20% chance of spell failure, and the save DCs of any spells she casts with the language-dependent descriptor are reduced by 4. Spell-like abilities are not affected by this spellblight because they lack verbal components. Shouting and screaming is impossible while the creature is affected by this spellblight.

A *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]* spell or similar effect cures this spellblight, as does casting the *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]* spell, though doing so is still affected by the spellblight’s effects.
