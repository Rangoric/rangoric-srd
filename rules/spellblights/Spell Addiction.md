# Spell Addiction

*Minor [[rangoric-srd/rules/spellblights/Spellblights#^table-minor-spellblights|spellblight]]*

A spell addict feeds off the elation of wielding magic, but suffers rapid withdrawal once spellcasting ends. After successfully casting a spell, the addict gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws until her next turn. On any round the spell addict does not cast a spell on her turn, however, she goes into withdrawal, and is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] until her next turn.

Spell addiction is cured by any effect that removes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confusion]], *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insanity (Spell)|insanity]]*, or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]]. Creatures that are immune to mind-affecting effects are immune to spell addiction.
