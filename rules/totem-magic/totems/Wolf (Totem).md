# Wolf

_A totem of [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat._

**Totem Shape**: At 1st level a totem mage of the wolf totem may transform into a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Wolf|wolf]].

**Spells** (the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can also prepare these, by level):

1. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon|magic weapon]]_
2. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spiritual Weapon|spiritual weapon]]_
3. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|prayer]]_
4. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Weapon, Greater|greater magic weapon]]_
5. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mark of Justice|mark of justice]]_

**Totem Ability**: The totem mage gains the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] ability.
