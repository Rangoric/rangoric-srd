# Dog

*A totem of [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat.*

**Totem Shape**: At 1st level the totem mage of the dog totem may use totem shape to turn into a normal [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dog|dog]] and a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dog, Riding|riding dog]] at 2nd level.

**Spells** (the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can also prepare these, by level):

1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless|bless]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Zone of Truth|zone of truth]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prayer|prayer]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Atonement|atonement]]*

**Totem Ability**: The totem mage receives a +1 luck bonus on all saving throws.
