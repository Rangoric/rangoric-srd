# Cat

_A totem of [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat._

**Totem Shape**: At 1st level a totem mage of the cat totem may use totem shape to turn into a normal [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Cat|cat]], a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Lion|lion]] at 3rd level, and a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tiger|tiger]] at 5th level.

**Spells** (the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can also prepare these, by level):

1. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Snares and Pits|detect snares and pits]]_
2. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cat’s Grace|cat’s grace]]_
3. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]_
4. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_
5. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_

**Totem Ability**: The totem mage receives a +2 bonus to her Dexterity.
