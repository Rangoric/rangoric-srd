# Insect

*A totem of [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat.*

**Totem Shape**: At 1st level a totem mage of the insect totem may transform into a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant-ant/Giant Ant, Worker|giant worker ant]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Bee|giant bee]], at 2nd level a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/giant-ant/Giant Ant, Soldier|giant soldier ant]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Praying Mantis|giant praying mantis]], or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Bombardier Beetle|giant bombardier beetle]], at 3rd level a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Wasp|giant wasp]], and at 4th level a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Giant Stag Beetle|giant stag beetle]].

**Spells** (the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can also prepare these, by level):

1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cause Fear|cause fear]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Swarm|summon swarm]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insect Plague|insect plague]]*

**Totem Ability**: The totem mage may use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spider Climb|spider climb]]* as a spell-like ability at will, with a caster level equal to her class level.
