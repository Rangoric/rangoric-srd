# Rat

*A totem of [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat.*

**Totem Shape**: At 1st level a totem mage of the rat totem may transform into a normal [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Rat|rat]].

**Spells** (the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can also prepare these, by level):

1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Contagion|contagion]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Poison|poison]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Slay Living|slay living]]*

**Totem Ability**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] become class skills for the totem mage.
