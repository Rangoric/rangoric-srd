# Boar

*A totem of [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat.*

**Totem Shape**: A totem mage of the boar totem can use totem shape to become a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Boar|boar]].

**Spells** (the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can also prepare these, by level):

1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Favor|divine favor]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Vestment|magic vestment]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Power|divine power]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*

**Totem Ability**: The totem mage receives a +2 bonus to her Strength.
