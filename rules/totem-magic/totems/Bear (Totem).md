# Bear

*A totem of [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat.*

**Totem Shape**: At 1st level a totem mage of the bear totem can use totem shape to become a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bear, Black|black bear]], at 3rd level a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bear, Brown|brown bear]], and at 5th level a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bear, Polar|polar bear]].

**Spells** (the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can also prepare these, by level):

1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Fear|remove fear]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear’s endurance]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*

**Totem Ability**: The totem mage receives a +2 bonus to her Constitution.
