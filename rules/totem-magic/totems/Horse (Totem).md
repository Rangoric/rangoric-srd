# Horse

*A totem of [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/Totem Magic|totem magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat.*

**Totem Shape**: At 1st level a totem mage of the horse totem may transform into a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/horse/Warhorse, Light|light warhorse]] and a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/horse/Warhorse, Heavy|heavy warhorse]] at 2nd level.

**Spells** (the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage#^totem-mage-spells-per-day|totem mage]] can also prepare these, by level):

1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mount|mount]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear’s endurance]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phantom Steed|phantom steed]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Plane Shift|plane shift]]*

**Totem Ability**: The totem mage adds 10 feet to her base speed.
