# Totem Magic

[[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|Totem mages]] are spellcasters who have answered the call of their totem creature, using their totemic connection to awaken new magical talents. This awakening gains the spellcaster the power to change shape into that of the totem, an action that cements the magical connection between spellcaster and totem. From this point, the totem mage learns the secrets of her totem, enabling her to assume more of its facets as her skill increases.

By far most totem mages are druids, both because of their interest in nature and because of their wild shape ability. Totem mages work with druids and rangers to protect wilderness regions, using the help of their totem animal to do so. Wizards rarely become totem mages, finding the feral nature of totem magic antithetical to the studious nature of wizardry. Sorcerers occasionally become totem mages, their inborn magical talents being somewhat akin in feeling to the powers granted by awakening a totem bond.

## Totems

Each totem listed below grants unique abilities that directly relate to its animal. The GM is free to create new totems to expand this list, and players are encouraged to work with their GM to create totems that match their character concepts.

- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Bear (Totem)|Bear]]
- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Boar (Totem)|Boar]]
- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Cat (Totem)|Cat]]
- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Dog (Totem)|Dog]]
- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Eagle (Totem)|Eagle]]
- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Horse (Totem)|Horse]]
- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Insect (Totem)|Insect]]
- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Rat (Totem)|Rat]]
- [[rangoric-srd/rules/totem-magic/totems/Wolf (Totem)|Wolf]]

A character links to a totem with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Totem Link|Totem Link]] feat, and a [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Totem Mage|totem mage]] builds on that link: totem shape (as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Changeform|changeform]]*), totem friendship, a totem companion, totem fear, totem facets, the totem’s spells and, at 9th level, its totem ability.
