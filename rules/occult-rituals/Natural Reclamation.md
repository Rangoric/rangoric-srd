# Natural Reclamation

*Conjuration; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 5*

  - **Level**: 5
  - **Casting Time**: 50 minutes
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a small model of the settlement worth 500 gp and the sacrifice of one sentient creature that lives in the ritual’s area), F (magically treated ivy), SC (up to 8)
  - **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] DC 29, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] DC 29, 2 successes
  - **Range**: touch
  - **Area**: 1,000-ft. radius/character level of the primary caster
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/character level of the primary caster
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates (object)
  - **Spell Resistance**: no
  - **Backlash**: All casters take 4d6 points of damage.
  - **Failure**: All nonmagical plants within 100 feet of the primary caster wither and die, and the casters suffer *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Horrid Wilting|horrid wilting]]* (no save), using the primary caster’s character level as the caster level.

This ritual can be cast only during the spring equinox. The primary caster binds the sacrifice in the treated ivy, places the model of the settlement on the sacrifice’s body, kills the sacrifice and smears the blood over the ivy; as the casters chant, the ivy grows and destroys the model. If the ritual succeeds, the ivy dives into the ground and reemerges throughout the ritual’s area. Vines and other plants native to the region grow over every building and structure in the area, dealing 3 points of damage to each every 10 minutes, ignoring hardness. A typical wooden building can withstand 100 points of damage before it is wrecked, and a typical stone building 150 before it is destroyed. The plants don’t damage creatures directly, though creatures that stay in a building may be hurt if it collapses. Heavily fortified stone structures, such as castles and city walls, are sturdy enough to withstand the ritual. A magical building or room can attempt a Fortitude save to repel the plants.
