# Welcome the Blighted Soul

_Necromancy \[Evil\]; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 9_

- **Level**: 9
- **Casting Time**: 9 hours (only during the new moon)
- **Components**: V, S, F (a ring of standing stones empowered with necromancy that cost 25,000 gp to create with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Wondrous Item|Craft Wondrous Item]]), sacrifice (18 living intelligent beings of the caster’s type, one sacrificed every 30 minutes of the casting)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] DC 31, 2 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] DC 31, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] DC 31, 4 successes
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: caster
- **Duration**: instantaneous
- **Saving Throw**: none
- **Spell Resistance**: no
- **Backlash**: The caster is reduced to –1 hit points.
- **Failure**: The caster is slain.

After sacrificing 18 lives, the caster sacrifices himself and at once rises from death as a siabrae.
