# Return to Dormancy

*Enchantment (Compulsion) \[Mind-Affecting\]; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 8*

  - **Level**: 8
  - **Casting Time**: 80 minutes
  - **Components**: V, S, M (a bone, piece of skin or scale belonging to the target), SC (at least 4 and up to 20)
  - **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] DC 36, 2 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] DC 36, 2 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (any; see text) DC 36, 4 successes
  - **Range**: medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./character level of the primary caster)
  - **Target**: one Colossal creature (minimum 20 HD)
  - **Duration**: instantaneous; see text
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: no
  - **Backlash**: All casters are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].
  - **Failure**: The primary caster becomes the sole focus of the target, which attacks the primary caster to the best of its ability for the next hour.

Many versions of this ritual exist, all meant to lull the rage of a gigantic, rampaging creature. The target is usually a unique creature that only a specific kind of Performance check affects (the GM decides which).

When the ritual is complete, the target, which must be a Colossal creature with at least 20 HD, attempts a Will save. On a failure, it stops what it is doing and retreats away from civilization for at least 1 year. If it succeeds, the ritual fails and its failure effect occurs at once. A creature with the kaiju subtype can’t use its recovery ability to attempt a new save against this ritual. If an affected creature is attacked and damaged, the ritual’s effect ends at once and its failure effect occurs.
