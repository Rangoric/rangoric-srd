# Atavistic Reversion

_Transmutation; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 5_

- **Level**: 5
- **Casting Time**: 5 hours
- **Components**: V, S, M (musky incense worth 500 gp), F (crown made of claws and fangs), SC (up to 8)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] DC 33, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] DC 33, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] DC 33, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] DC 33, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] DC 33, 1 success
- **Range**: touch
- **Area**: 1-mile radius/character level of the primary caster
- **Duration**: instantaneous
- **Defense**: Fortitude
- **Spell Resistance**: yes
- **Backlash**: All casters gain 1 temporary [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]] and become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].
- **Failure**: The primary caster gains the mind and body of a Tiny animal, as if she failed both saves against _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Baleful Polymorph|baleful polymorph]]_.

The primary caster lights a bonfire surrounded with incense, steps into the fire, places the crown on his brow and howls. As the ritual goes on, all humanoids in its area, the casters included, come to resemble their primal animal ancestors. At first these features have no game effect, and they disappear if the ritual fails.

If the ritual succeeds, the casters and those whose Fortitude it misses return to normal, while all other humanoids in the area regress further. They gain a +4 size bonus to Strength, a +4 natural armor bonus to AC and a bite attack (if they already had one, it counts as one size larger). If their Intelligence is greater than 2, it becomes 2, and they can’t cast spells, use other special abilities that require Intelligence, speak or wield manufactured weapons. The only skill checks they can attempt are [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]]. The effects on a creature can be removed with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Break Enchantment|break enchantment]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]_; removing the penalties also removes the benefits.
