# Unwavering Vigilance

_Conjuration (Teleportation) and Divination; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 8_

- **Level**: 8
- **Casting Time**: 80 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (one golden thread worth 1,000 gp for each caster), F (a miniature doll of golden thread and gemstones worth at least 25,000 gp), SC (at least 4 and up to 8)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] DC 33, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] DC 33, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] DC 33, 2 successes
- **Range**: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./character level of the primary caster)
- **Target**: all casters
- **Duration**: 1 day/character level of the primary caster
- **Saving Throw**: none (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: no
- **Backlash**: All casters take 6d6 points of damage, gain 1 temporary [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]] and become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].
- **Failure**: All casters gain 1 permanent [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]].

The secondary casters each hold one end of their golden threads, the other ends tied to the doll, and chant their shared values (often the vows of a cult or guild) with arms outstretched toward each other. The primary caster ties the doll to her wrist with her own thread, then touches each secondary caster on the forehead with it. Unlike with other occult rituals, more than four secondary casters don’t increase the bonus on the ritual’s skill checks.

When the ritual succeeds, each caster’s thread snaps; half melds into the caster and half into the doll (which can’t be this ritual’s focus again until the duration ends). Each caster gains the effects of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Status|status]]_ spell for each other caster. Whenever any caster is wounded, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^unconscious|unconscious]] (other than normal sleep), [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dying|dying]] or poisoned, any of the casters can, as a free action, use _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]_ to travel to that caster, as long as they are on the same plane. If that caster is in combat, the arriving casters roll initiative and enter combat on the next round, unless they have another reason to be ready sooner (for example, if they were _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Scrying|scrying]]_ on that caster). Each caster can teleport this way once per day.
