# Avoidance Ward

_Enchantment (Compulsion); [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 4_

- **Level**: 4
- **Casting Time**: 40 minutes
- **Components**: V, M (a piece of rowan wood charcoal), F (a small silver mirror worth 50 gp)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] DC 30, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] DC 30, 1 success
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: one dungeon door
- **Duration**: 8 hours
- **Defense**: Will (harmless, object)
- **Spell Resistance**: yes (harmless, object)
- **Backlash**: The caster takes 4d6 points of damage.
- **Failure**: The target door swings open and can’t be closed by the caster by any means short of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Limited Wish|limited wish]]_ for 24 hours.

The caster places a mirror on one side of the door and writes occult symbols on the other side that conform to one type or subtype of creature from the ranger’s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-favored-enemy|favored enemy]] list. When the ritual is complete, creatures of the chosen type or subtype can’t open, touch or even magically manipulate the door by any means (with no defense or spell resistance). A dungeon door can have only one avoidance ward at a time.
