# Blush of Youth

_Necromancy \[Evil\]; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 7_

- **Level**: 7
- **Casting Time**: 7 hours
- **Components**: V, S, M (a fist-sized onyx worth at least 5,000 gp; one creature younger than adult age, of the same race as the primary caster, per level of the primary caster), SC (see text)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] DC 33, 4 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] DC 33, 2 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] DC 33, 1 success
- **Range**: primary caster
- **Duration**: 1 month/character level of the primary caster; see text
- **Defense**: none
- **Spell Resistance**: no
- **Backlash**: If the ritual succeeds, the primary caster loses her memories from after the physical age of her new body (see below) for 24 hours. Otherwise, unlike normal, the secondary casters perish, and the primary caster suffers no further backlash.
- **Failure**: The primary caster takes damage to her highest ability score equal to that score’s modifier for 1 week, and no effect can remove, suppress or otherwise lessen this damage until then. All creatures gain a +5 bonus on Will Defense to disbelieve any illusion affecting the primary caster while this ability damage lasts. The primary caster can’t attempt this ritual again while she suffers this penalty.

The caster gathers her intended victims in a circle no more than 10 feet in radius around her and restrains them for the whole ritual. They take no part in it except as sacrifices, but they count as secondary casters for the caster’s skill check bonuses. Standing at the center, the caster chants while holding the onyx aloft, and the victims’ blood is drawn to the onyx and over the caster, until by the ritual’s end her body is encased in a shell of blood.

If the ritual succeeds, the shell hardens and cracks, and the caster’s body is young and attractive: whatever her actual age, she appears as she did when her beauty was at its height.

An undead caster returns to her creature type from before she died and became undead for the ritual’s duration, treating her Constitution score as equal to her Charisma score when needed. She counts as a living creature of that type, but keeps the immunities of the undead type and the special abilities of her kind of undead, except those that rely on appearing undead or on physical features her young living body lacks (she loses a horrific appearance or claws gained by becoming undead, but not a lich’s paralyzing touch). An incorporeal caster has no body and can’t use this ritual, nor can a non-undead creature that never ages, such as a construct.

For the duration, the caster gains a +5 competence bonus on Charisma-based skill checks other than [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]]. Whenever she uses an illusion spell or similar effect that makes her appear as someone or something she isn’t (including _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]_, _appearance of life_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Appearance of Life, Greater|greater appearance of life]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Veil|veil]]_, but not _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mirror Image|mirror image]]_), creatures take a –10 penalty on Will Defense to disbelieve the illusion, and divination magic that would normally see through illusions (such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_) fails to see through it. If the illusion has a harmful effect on creatures that fail to disbelieve it, the penalty doesn’t apply.
