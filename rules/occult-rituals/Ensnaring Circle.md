# Ensnaring Circle

_Abjuration \[variable; see text\]; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 4_

- **Level**: 4
- **Casting Time**: 40 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (powdered silver), F (a stylus made of mithral and diamond worth 5,000 gp), SC (up to 8)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] DC 30, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] DC 30, 1 success
- **Range**: 10 ft.
- **Effect**: a 20-foot-radius magic circle
- **Duration**: 1 day/character level of the primary caster
- **Defense**: Will (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: no; see text
- **Backlash**: The primary caster takes 2d6 points of damage.
- **Failure**: The casters believe their work succeeded, but when they try to use the circle, nothing happens.

The casters create a large magic circle outlined with the silver powder and shaped with the focus stylus. The circle is attuned to an alignment (chaos, evil, good or law) chosen when it is made, and the ritual gains that alignment’s descriptor. Once complete, the circle is invisible to all but the ritual’s casters and creatures with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/See Invisibility|see invisibility]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Seeing|true seeing]]_ or some other way of seeing invisible objects.

The finished circle can trap an outsider whose subtype matches the chosen alignment, as an inward-focused _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle]]_ spell of the matching kind (an ensnaring circle attuned to evil acts as an inward-focused _magic circle against evil_). The outsider’s entire space must be within the circle; the primary caster can then activate the circle as an immediate action, provided he is within 100 feet of it and can see it and the outsider. Several outsiders of the right subtype can be trapped at once if all are in the circle when it is activated. An outsider that isn’t completely within the circle isn’t caught, and is free to disrupt it as described in _magic circle against evil_.

As with a _magic circle_, a trapped outsider can’t cross the circle’s boundaries or reach over or beyond them, and can’t disrupt the circle directly or indirectly, though it can make ranged attacks beyond it. An outsider with spell resistance can test the trap once per day; if the primary caster fails to overcome its spell resistance, the circle is disrupted and the outsider is freed. An outsider capable of any form of dimensional travel can simply leave the circle that way. Unlike with a _magic circle_, no special diagram can be made to make an ensnaring circle more secure.
