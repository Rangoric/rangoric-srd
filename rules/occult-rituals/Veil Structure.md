# Veil Structure

_Illusion (Glamer); [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 8_

- **Level**: 8
- **Casting Time**: 80 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (a half-gallon of water from a fresh spring and a potion of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Invisibility|invisibility]]_), F (a pair of mithral pitchers and two golden aspergillums, worth 25,000 gp total), SC (at least 4 and up to 12)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] (see text) DC 32, 4 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] DC 32, 2 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] DC 32, 2 successes
- **Range**: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./character level of the primary caster)
- **Area**: one 20-ft. cube/character level of the primary caster
- **Duration**: permanent (D)
- **Defense**: Will disbelief (if interacted with)
- **Spell Resistance**: no
- **Backlash**: The primary caster gains 1 permanent [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]].
- **Failure**: For 1 year, no caster can be targeted by any beneficial glamer effect (such as _invisibility_). This is a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Curses|curse effect]], and can be removed with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ and similar effects.

The primary caster puts the spring water in one mithral pitcher and the potion in the other, pours the water into the pitcher holding the potion, then pours the mixture back; she repeats this six more times, then hands one pitcher to a secondary caster. In complete silence, the two splash the mixture with the aspergillums on the building or structure they wish to veil, which can be no larger than the ritual’s area. If the building or structure is in a natural environment, the ritual’s Nature or Society checks are Nature checks; if it is in an urban environment, they are Society checks.

When the ritual succeeds, the building is veiled: the area appears to hold nothing but its natural or urban surroundings. All the casters can still see the faintest outline of the building and interact with it normally. The primary caster can also touch up to 10 creatures per day to let each see the faintest outline of the building for 24 hours.
