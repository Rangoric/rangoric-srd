# Enter the Inner Circle

_Divination; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 4_

- **Level**: 4
- **Casting Time**: 40 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (the initiate’s blood), F (membership token), SC (at least 1, the initiate, and at most 20)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] DC 29, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] DC 29, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (oratory or sing) DC 29, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] DC 29, 1 success
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: creature touched
- **Duration**: instantaneous
- **Saving Throw**: none
- **Spell Resistance**: no
- **Backlash**: All casters are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].
- **Failure**: The primary caster, and any secondary caster who failed a skill check, is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] and unable to speak. This is a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Curses|curse effect]] that can be removed with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ and similar effects.

This ritual is the initiation ceremony of a secret society. Other organizations can have their own versions, replacing Perception and Stealth with two skills that suit the organization (such as Theology for a church). The primary caster and all secondary casters but one must be members of the organization, and exactly one secondary caster, the initiate, must not be.

The primary caster describes the society and the requirements of membership, looking for signs that the initiate understands and accepts them. She acknowledges any society leaders present and invites them to reject the initiate if they choose; if none are present, she looks for other signs of symbolic rejection. If she sees any, she can cancel the ritual before the skill checks are attempted: all casters then suffer the backlash, but there is no failure effect.

If the ritual goes on, the initiate must attempt either the Stealth or the Perception check as a test. If the initiate fails it, the ritual fails whatever the other successes, but if it would otherwise succeed, only the initiate suffers the failure effect. Finally, the primary caster collects the initiate’s blood and immerses a membership token in it while the initiate speaks the oaths of membership; when the last oath is spoken, the ritual is complete and the backlash occurs. If the ritual succeeds, each caster other than the initiate knows who and what the initiate is, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Blood Biography|blood biography]]_, and any lies the initiate deliberately and knowingly spoke during the ceremony, as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]]_. If no falsehood is revealed, the initiation succeeds and the token is attuned to the new member. When the member wears or carries it, the token grants a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to pass secret messages to members of the organization and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks to receive secret messages from them, besides any other properties it has.
