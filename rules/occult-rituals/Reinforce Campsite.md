# Reinforce Campsite

_Abjuration; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 4_

- **Level**: 4
- **Casting Time**: 40 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (flint and steel, tent), F (a set of magically attuned stones to encircle the campfire, worth at least 500 gp), SC (up to 5)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] DC 24, 2 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] DC 24, 2 successes
- **Range**: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./character level of the primary caster)
- **Effect**: a 30-foot-radius area
- **Duration**: 1 hour/character level of the primary caster
- **Backlash**: The primary caster is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].
- **Failure**: The campfire flares 200 feet into the air for 1 round, followed by a near-deafening boom. Besides alerting any nearby creatures, this gives an 80% chance of attracting a random encounter if the ritual was performed outdoors.

This ritual is cast while building a campsite: the casters secure a 30-foot perimeter around a central campfire, which is ringed with the warding stones (the ritual’s focus). A set of stones can be used only once every 7 days, though the primary caster can buy more sets to make several campsites in that time. If the ritual succeeds, these effects last for its duration or until the primary caster ends the ritual (a full-round action):

- **Alarm**: If a living creature that wasn’t in the area when the ritual was completed enters it, an audible alarm sounds for 2 rounds, loud enough to wake any creature resting in the campsite.
- **Defensive Ring**: The outermost 5 feet of the area become rough, counting as [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Caltrops|caltrops]] for any creature that wasn’t within the area when the ritual was cast.
- **Shelter**: Creatures within the area gain the benefits of _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_ (caster level = the primary caster’s character level) and a +4 circumstance bonus on defenses against weather effects.
