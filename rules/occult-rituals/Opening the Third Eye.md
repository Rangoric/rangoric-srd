# Opening the Third Eye

_Transmutation; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 5_

- **Level**: 5
- **Casting Time**: 50 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (incense), SC (up to 12)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] DC 34, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] DC 34, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] DC 34, 1 success
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: primary and secondary casters
- **Duration**: 1 day (D)
- **Defense**: Fortitude (harmless)
- **Spell Resistance**: yes (harmless)
- **Backlash**: All casters take 2d6 points of damage.
- **Failure**: All casters gain a temporary [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]] (attacking again each day after the first at +6 + the primary caster’s Charisma bonus), and the primary caster is afflicted with a more potent version of [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Mindfire|mindfire]] (+7; 2 misses; 1d4 Int; 1/day).

Through a ceremony of meditation and recitation, the casters become psychically attuned. Casters who don’t have levels in an occult class ([[rangoric-srd/classes/Kineticist|kineticist]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)|medium]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist|mesmerist]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]], [[rangoric-srd/classes/Psychic (Class)|psychic]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Spiritualist (Class)|spiritualist]]) gain these benefits:

- Each caster can choose one knack from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Psychic Spell List#0-Level Psychic Spells (Knacks)|psychic spell list]], plus one more for every 5 character levels she has (to a maximum of 5 knacks at 20th level). She can cast these knacks at will.
- A caster trained in a skill with an [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^occult-unlocks|occult skill unlock]] can use that skill’s occult unlock.

Casters who do have levels in an occult class gain these benefits:

- A caster trained in a skill with an occult skill unlock gains a +4 insight bonus on checks with that skill when using its occult unlock.
- A caster untrained in a skill with an occult skill unlock can still use that skill’s occult unlock.

Each caster can dismiss the ritual’s effects for herself.
