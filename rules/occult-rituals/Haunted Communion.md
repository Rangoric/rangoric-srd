# Haunted Communion

_Necromancy; [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 7_

- **Level**: 7
- **Casting Time**: 70 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (special paints and a small, salted pastry), F (silver bell worth 500 gp), SC (up to 18)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] DC 31, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] DC 31, 3 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] DC 31, 1 success
- **Range**: touch
- **Target**: one haunt within 60 feet
- **Duration**: 1 hour and 10 rounds (D); see text
- **Saving Throw**: Will negates
- **Spell Resistance**: yes
- **Backlash**: The primary caster is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhausted]].
- **Failure**: The casters gain 1 permanent [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]] and a –4 penalty on all skill and ability checks for as long as the negative level lasts.

This ritual must be performed within 60 feet of a haunt. The primary caster rings the silver bell with an object associated with the haunt’s origin or source of power, then draws an intricate circle of occult symbols around a plate bearing the salted pastry.

If the incantation succeeds, the haunt’s spirit enters the pastry (making the haunt inert for the ritual’s duration), and one of the casters must eat the pastry within the next hour or the ritual ends. If no one does, the haunt goes free, and for 24 hours the DCs of its effects increase by 4 and it gains a +4 bonus on attack rolls. Once a caster eats the pastry, the haunt’s spirit speaks through that caster and must truthfully answer 10 questions (1 question per round) before departing, though since a haunt is an emotional echo, its answers may be brief, cryptic or repetitive, as with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Speak with Dead|speak with dead]]_. Once the 10 questions are answered or the ritual is dismissed, the haunt can manifest as normal.
