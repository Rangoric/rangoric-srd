# Cats and Mice

_Transmutation (Polymorph); [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 5_

- **Level**: 5
- **Casting Time**: 50 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (one live mouse per target), F (a tiny cat figurine worth 500 gp and a golden cage worth at least 5,000 gp), SC (at least one per target)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] DC 27, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]] DC 27, 1 success; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] DC 27, 2 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] DC 27, 1 success
- **Range**: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./character level of the primary caster)
- **Target**: one [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]] creature/character level of the primary caster, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
- **Duration**: 1 hour/character level of the primary caster
- **Defense**: Will partial; see text
- **Spell Resistance**: yes
- **Backlash**: All casters are cursed with insatiable hunger, as a permanent _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feast of Ashes|feast of ashes]]_. The curse ends for a caster who eats the fresh flesh or blood of any target within the ritual’s duration. _Remove curse_ and similar effects can also remove it, but only after the ritual’s duration ends.
- **Failure**: Eating a target’s flesh or blood doesn’t end the backlash’s curse. For 1 hour, each target is affected by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]_. Each caster is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]].

The primary caster puts one live mouse per target into the golden cage, then puts the cat figurine in with them. If the ritual succeeds, the figurine briefly animates and kills each mouse; as each dies, a target’s head becomes a mouse’s head of a suitable size for the ritual’s duration. Each target gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] but can’t speak (and so can’t complete verbal components, speak command words, or activate spell completion and spell trigger items). Any [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Polymorph|polymorph]] effects on a target are dispelled, and for the duration it can’t use other polymorph spells or effects to take a new form. While transformed, the ritual attacks a target’s Will whenever she meets a cat, a catlike creature or a creature in a cat mask; on a hit she is affected as by _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fear|fear]]_, and on a miss she becomes immune to that effect from that feline.

A target that moves more than 1 mile from the cage per 5,000 gp of the cage’s value takes 1d6 points of nonlethal damage each round it stays beyond that range. This effect ends if the cage is destroyed or moved more than 100 feet from where it was at the ritual’s start.

Incorporeal and gaseous creatures are immune to the ritual, and a creature with the shapechanger subtype can return to its natural form as a standard action.
