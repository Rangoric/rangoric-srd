# Labyrinthine Wilds

_Illusion (Glamer); [[rangoric-srd/rules/occult-rituals/Occult Rituals|occult ritual]] level 5_

- **Level**: 5
- **Casting Time**: 50 minutes
- **Components**: V, S, M (bones of local wildlife carved into divining runes and daubed with specially treated wildlife blood, worth 2,000 gp), F (a compass with a highly sensitive magnetic needle worth 10,000 gp), SC (at least 4 and up to 10)
- **Skill Checks**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] DC 30, 2 successes; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] DC 30, 3 successes
- **Range**: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./character level of the primary caster)
- **Area**: a 2-square-mile region/character level of the primary caster
- **Duration**: permanent (D)
- **Defense**: Will disbelief (if interacted with)
- **Spell Resistance**: no
- **Backlash**: The primary caster gains 1 temporary [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]].
- **Failure**: All casters gain 2 temporary [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative levels]] and are permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] (the blindness is a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Curses|curse effect]], and can be removed with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ and similar effects).

To prepare, the primary caster walks a path along the outer edge of the area, likely taking longer than the casting time, marking it with tiny runes as she goes. Once she returns to her starting point, and as long as the area she outlined is less than 2 square miles per character level she has, the casting begins: all the casters cross into the region and wander erratic paths, each using the divining runes.

When the ritual is complete, the area within the border becomes an illusory replica of the local terrain that is hard to navigate. To travel to a chosen place within it, a creature must succeed at a DC 20 Intelligence check or a DC 30 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] check. On a success, it reaches its destination as normal. On a failure, the creature (and any allies it guides) spends 2d6 hours traveling, only to return to its starting point with no memory of its wanderings and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] from the journey.
