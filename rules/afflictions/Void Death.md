# Void Death

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is spread by the bite of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Akata|akata]]; an infected creature who dies of it rises as a void zombie 2d4 hours later, as described there.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +2 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 hour
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d2 Dexterity damage and 1d2 Constitution damage
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
