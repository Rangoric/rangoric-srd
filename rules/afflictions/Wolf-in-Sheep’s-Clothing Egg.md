# Wolf-in-Sheep’s-Clothing Egg

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the parasite eggs of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Wolf-in-Sheep’s-Clothing|wolf-in-sheep’s-clothing]].

- **Type**: disease, ingested
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 19
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d4 Strength damage until the host reaches 0, then 3d6 damage as the parasite bursts free
- **Cure**: 3 consecutive saves
