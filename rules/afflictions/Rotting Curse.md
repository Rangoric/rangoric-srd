# Rotting Curse

A curse; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the curse of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Augnagar|augnagar]]'s bite. A creature that suffers it displays hideous, festering wounds that exude a horrific stench. This functions as the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Stench|stench]] universal monster rule, save that it affects all creatures except those that are immune to poison. The stench makes the victim of this curse [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] with no defense, and attacks other creatures at +16 vs Fortitude; those it hits remain sickened as long as they remain within 30 feet of the cursed victim. The horrific stench also imparts a –8 penalty on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks made by the cursed victim. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

- **Type**: curse, injury
- **Attack**: +16 vs Fortitude
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d6 Constitution drain plus constant stench
