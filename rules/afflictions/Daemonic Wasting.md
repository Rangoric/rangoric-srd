# Daemonic Wasting

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the disease of the bite of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Meladaemon|meladaemon]]. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +12 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d4 Constitution damage and 1d4 Charisma damage
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
