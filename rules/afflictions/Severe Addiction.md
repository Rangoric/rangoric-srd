# Severe Addiction

A disease, caught from a drug; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]], and how an addiction is contracted, held off and cured is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|drugs and addiction]].

- **Type**: disease, drug
- **Attack**: per drug, vs Fortitude (the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|addiction attack bonus]] of the drug that caused it)
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: –2 penalty to Dex, Con, Str, and Wis; target cannot naturally heal ability damage caused by the drug that caused this addiction
- **Cure**: 3 consecutive misses
