# Paranoia

An insanity; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]], and how insanity is contracted and cured in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Insanity|insanity]].

- **Type**: insanity
- **Attack**: +7 vs Will
- **Onset**: 2d6 days
- **Effect**: –4 penalty on Will Defense and Charisma-based skill checks; cannot receive benefit from or attempt the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] action; cannot willingly accept aid (including healing) from another creature unless his insanity attacks his Will and misses

The paranoid character is convinced that the world and all that dwell within it are out to get him. Paranoid characters are typically argumentative or introverted.
