# Poison Oak (Severe Exposure)

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It comes from full body contact with poison oak, or breathing the smoke of it burning, the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Hazards#Poison Oak|poison oak hazard]] (CR 3).

- **Type**: poison, contact or inhaled
- **Attack**: +6 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 hour
- **Frequency**: 1/hour
- **Effect**: 2d4 Dex damage and 1d4 Con damage, and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] until the damage is healed/1 Con damage
- **Cure**: 1 miss
