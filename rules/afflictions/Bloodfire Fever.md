# Bloodfire Fever

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is carried by the saliva of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Trollhound|trollhounds]], which are themselves immune to it (as are trolls). It is a deep internal pain, as if the victim's blood were on fire, with loss of muscular coordination, pus-filled blisters and lethargy.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +4 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d3 Strength damage, 1d3 Dexterity damage and fatigued
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
