# Mania/Phobia

An insanity; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]], and how insanity is contracted and cured in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Insanity|insanity]].

- **Type**: insanity
- **Attack**: +4 vs Will
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Effect**: target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] (if manic) or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] (if phobic) as long as the source of the mania or phobia is obvious; chance of becoming fascinated or frightened (see below)

A mania is an irrational obsession with a (usually inappropriate) particular object or situation, while a phobia is an irrational fear of a (usually commonplace) object or situation. Additionally, if a manic or phobic character is directly confronted by his obsession (requiring a standard action), the insanity attacks his Will, and on a hit he becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fascinated|fascinated]] (if manic) or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^frightened|frightened]] (if phobic) by the object for 1d6 rounds.
