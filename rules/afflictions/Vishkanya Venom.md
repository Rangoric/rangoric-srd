# Vishkanya Venom

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the toxic saliva or blood a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Vishkanya|vishkanya]] applies to a weapon with its [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Toxic|toxic]] ability.

- **Type**: poison, injury
- **Attack**: +1/2 the vishkanya’s Hit Dice + the vishkanya’s Constitution modifier vs Fortitude
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 6 rounds
- **Effect**: 1d2 Dexterity damage
- **Cure**: 1 miss
