# Necrotic Boils

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is carried by the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Festrog|festrog]], whose boils rupture when it takes damage from a piercing or slashing weapon, squirting the attacker with the contagion.

- **Type**: disease, contact
- **Attack**: +1 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d4 Constitution damage
- **Cure**: 1 miss
