# Filth Fever (Huecuva)

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is carried by the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Huecuva|huecuva]], whose claws spread it. It is not the [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|Filth Fever]] of other creatures: it drains Strength rather than Constitution. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +2 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1d3 days
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d3 Dexterity damage and 1d3 Strength damage
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
