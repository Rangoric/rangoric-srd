# Psychosis

An insanity; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]], and how insanity is contracted and cured in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Insanity|insanity]].

- **Type**: insanity
- **Attack**: +10 vs Will
- **Onset**: 3d6 days
- **Effect**: character becomes chaotic evil; gains +10 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to hide insanity

This complex insanity fills the victim with hate for the world. He can try to suppress his psychosis for a period of 1 day: his insanity attacks his Will, and on a miss it is suppressed; otherwise he cannot help but plot and plan the death and destruction of his friends and enemies alike. For the most part, the impact of psychosis must be roleplayed, although not all players find entertainment in roleplaying a lunatic who's trying to do in his friends. In such cases, the GM should assume control of the character whenever his psychosis is in control.
