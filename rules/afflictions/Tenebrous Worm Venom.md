# Tenebrous Worm Venom

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the poison of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tenebrous Worm|tenebrous worm]]. It is delivered by the worm's bristles.

- **Type**: poison, injury
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 20
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 6 rounds
- **Effect**: paralysis for 1d4 rounds plus 1d2 Constitution damage (the duration of the paralysis is cumulative with each failed save)
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive saves
