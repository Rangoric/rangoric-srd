# Gloomwing Eggs

An infestation; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]], and why no number of saves ends an infestation in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Infestation|infestation]]. It is laid by the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Gloomwing|gloomwing]] in a helpless or dead creature.

- **Type**: infestation
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 14, each morning
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d4 Constitution damage
- **Cure**: any effect that cures disease destroys the eggs, though they are not treated as a disease for what creatures are immune to it. Within 24 hours of a creature's death from this damage, 1d4 young [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Tenebrous Worm|tenebrous worms]] emerge from the corpse, devouring it completely.
