# Mummy Rot

A curse; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. Spread by [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Mummy|mummies]].

- **Type**: curse and disease, injury
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 16
- **Onset**: 1 minute
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d6 Con damage and 1d6 Cha damage
- **Cure**: only by casting both *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]* within 1 minute of each other
