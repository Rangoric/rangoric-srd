# Tetanus

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is spread by the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/golem/Junk Golem|junk golem]]'s slam. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +2 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1d6 days
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d4 Dexterity damage. Each time someone takes Dexterity damage from tetanus, there is a 50% chance his jaw muscles stiffen, preventing speech and the use of spells with verbal components for 24 hours
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
