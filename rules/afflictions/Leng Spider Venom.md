# Leng Spider Venom

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the venom of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Leng Spider|Leng spider]]'s bite.

- **Type**: poison, injury
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 25
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 6 rounds
- **Effect**: 1d4 Constitution damage plus [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confusion]] for 1 round
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive saves

A Leng spider's venom causes flesh to blister and rot away and the mind to experience vivid and horrific hallucinations—these visions cause the poisoned creature to react in an unpredictable manner, as if confused. The hallucination element of this poison is mind-affecting.
