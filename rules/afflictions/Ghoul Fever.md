# Ghoul Fever

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is spread by the bite of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ghoul/Ghoul|ghouls]]; a humanoid who dies of ghoul fever rises as a ghoul, as described there.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +3 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d3 Con damage and 1d3 Dex damage
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
