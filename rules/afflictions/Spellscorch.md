# Spellscorch

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. Refined from rare underground crystals, this poison causes a burning headache that impedes spellcasting.

- **Type**: poison, injury
- **Attack**: +4 vs Fortitude
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 4 rounds
- **Effect**: for the next minute, the target must make a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration check]] with a DC equal to 10 + spell level to cast a spell, and all other concentration checks to cast spells take a –5 penalty
- **Cure**: 1 miss
- **Price**: 200 gp
