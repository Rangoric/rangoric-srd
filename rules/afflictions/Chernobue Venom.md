# Chernobue Venom

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the venom of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/qlippoth/Chernobue|chernobue]]. The thick, orange poison is semi-alive. As soon as the poisoned victim is cured of the poison (by making a save, being targeted with an effect like [[rangoric-srd/spells/Neutralize Poison|neutralize poison]], or enduring the full duration of the poison), the orange fluid bursts from the victim's body, causing 1d6 Charisma damage and rendering the victim unconscious for 2d6 rounds unless he makes a final DC 23 Fortitude save. The save DC is Constitution-based.

- **Type**: poison, injury
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 23
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 6 rounds
- **Effect**: 1d2 Constitution drain
- **Cure**: 1 save
