# Minor Addiction

A disease, caught from a drug; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]], and how an addiction is contracted, held off and cured is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|drugs and addiction]].

- **Type**: disease, drug
- **Save**: Fortitude, per drug (the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|addiction DC]] of the drug that caused it)
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: –2 penalty to Con
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive saves
