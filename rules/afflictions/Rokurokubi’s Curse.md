# Rokurokubi’s Curse

A curse; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the curse of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Rokurokubi|rokurokubi]]'s bite. Fully healing the Charisma damage cures the silence. The save DC is Charisma-based.

- **Type**: curse, injury
- **Save**: Will DC 23
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1 Charisma damage, and the victim is permanently silenced after 4 Charisma damage
