# Skaveling Ghoul Fever

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is carried by the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Skaveling|skaveling]], whose bite spreads it. It is [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Ghoul Fever|Ghoul Fever]] with a higher attack bonus, and a humanoid who dies of it rises as a ghoul in the same way. The attack bonus is Charisma-based.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +6 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d3 Constitution damage and 1d3 Dexterity damage
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
