# Cacodaemonia

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the disease of the bite of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/daemons/Cacodaemon|cacodaemon]]. In addition to the normal effects of the disease, as long as a victim is infected, the cacodaemon can telepathically communicate with the creature over any distance, as long as they remain on the same plane.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +2 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d2 Wisdom damage
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
