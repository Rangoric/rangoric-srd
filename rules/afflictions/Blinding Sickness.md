# Blinding Sickness

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. Spread in tainted water.

- **Type**: disease, ingested
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 16
- **Onset**: 1d3 days
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d4 Str damage; if it deals more than 2 points of Strength damage, the target must make another Fortitude save or be permanently [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]]
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive saves
