# Fungal Blood or Flesh

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the blood and flesh of a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Fungal Creature|fungal creature]] (its poisonous blood).

- **Type**: poison, ingested
- **Attack**: +1/2 the fungal creature’s racial Hit Dice + its Constitution modifier vs Fortitude
- **Frequency**: 1/minute for 6 minutes
- **Effect**: 1 Strength damage, 1 Dexterity damage, and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 minute
- **Cure**: 2 misses
