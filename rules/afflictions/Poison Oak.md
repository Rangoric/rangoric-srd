# Poison Oak

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It comes from contact with poison oak, the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Hazards#Poison Oak|poison oak hazard]] (CR 1).

- **Type**: poison, contact
- **Attack**: +3 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 hour
- **Effect**: 1d4 Dex damage, and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] until the damage is healed
- **Cure**: 1 miss
