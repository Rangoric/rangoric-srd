# Dorvae Poison

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the poison of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Dorvae|dorvae]]. It is delivered by the dorvae's writhing snakes.

- **Type**: poison, injury
- **Attack**: +10 vs Fortitude
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 12 rounds
- **Effect**: 1d4 Wisdom damage, and on the first failed save the target is susceptible to the dorvae's _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Geas, Lesser|lesser geas]]_ no matter its Hit Dice for 24 hours
- **Cure**: 3 misses
