# Zombie Rot

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. Spread by plague zombies: anyone who dies while infected rises as a plague zombie in 2d6 hours.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 10 + 1/2 the zombie’s Hit Dice + the zombie’s Cha modifier
- **Onset**: 1d4 days
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d2 Con damage
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive saves
