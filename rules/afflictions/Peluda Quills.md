# Peluda Quills

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the poison of the quills of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Peluda|peluda]], which strike a creature that hits it, or that it fires in its quill barrage. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

- **Type**: poison, injury
- **Attack**: +10 vs Fortitude
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 6 rounds
- **Effect**: 1d4 Constitution damage
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
