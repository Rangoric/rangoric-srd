# Fungal Spores

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is the poison of a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Fungal Creature|fungal creature]]’s poison spore cloud; a creature it kills becomes a fungal spawn (see the fungal creature’s create spawn).

- **Type**: poison, inhaled
- **Attack**: +1/2 the fungal creature’s racial Hit Dice + its Constitution modifier vs Fortitude
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 6 rounds
- **Effect**: 1d2 Constitution damage and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] for 1 minute
- **Cure**: 2 misses
