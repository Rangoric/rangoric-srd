# Slimy Doom

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. Victim turns into infectious goo from the inside out.

- **Type**: disease, contact
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 14
- **Onset**: 1 day
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d4 Con damage; the target must make a second Fortitude save or 1 point of the damage is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|drain]] instead
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive saves
