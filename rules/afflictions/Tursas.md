# Tursas

A disease; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]]. It is spread by the bite of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Iku-Turso|iku-turso]]. As long as a victim suffers any ability damage from tursas, it can breathe water. A creature reduced to 0 Charisma by it turns into a fully grown and healthy iku-turso: it forgets its previous life and abilities at once and seeks out the nearest iku-turso community to join it. A [[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]] or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]] can reverse the change. The attack bonus is Constitution-based.

- **Type**: disease, injury
- **Attack**: +10 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 minute
- **Frequency**: 1/day
- **Effect**: 1d3 Wisdom damage and 1d6 Charisma drain
- **Cure**: 2 consecutive misses
