# Rot Grubs

An infestation; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]], and why no number of misses ends an infestation in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Infestation|infestation]]. How a creature picks them up, and what else ends them, is under the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Hazards#Rot Grubs|rot grubs hazard]] (CR 4). A swarm of rot grubs infests its victims by its own [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Infestation|infestation]] ability instead.

- **Type**: infestation
- **Attack**: +7 vs Fortitude
- **Frequency**: 1/round
- **Effect**: 1d2 Con damage per grub
