# Eihwaz

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the rune caster.*

Rune of protection and endurance, Eihwaz is popular with rune warriors and the more combative rune casters. Those attuned to Eihwaz tend to be very steadfast and protective of others while always willing to demonstrate their own endurance.

**Special Power**: While attuned to the Eihwaz rune, rune casters may reduce the chance of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Armor|arcane spell failure]] due to armor by 20%.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear’s endurance]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Death Ward|death ward]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Stoneskin|stoneskin]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guards and Wards|guards and wards]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Spell Turning|spell turning]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Spells|protection from spells]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom|freedom]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +1 bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]].
