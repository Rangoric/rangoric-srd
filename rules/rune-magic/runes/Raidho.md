# Raidho

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

The rune of movement, Raidho draws its power from the action of changing location. Wanderers and nomads favor it. Those attuned to this rune suffer from wanderlust, always wanting to see what is over the next hill.

**Special Power**: For one round per day per rune caster level you may ignore any magical effects that impede movement. This ability activates anytime it is needed and the rune caster is attuned to Raidho. It deactivates as soon as it is no longer needed. This is a spell-like ability.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Know Direction|know direction]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Expeditious Retreat|expeditious retreat]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear’s endurance]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Haste|haste]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freedom of Movement|freedom of movement]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Passwall|passwall]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Find the Path|find the path]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Teleport, Greater|greater teleport]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Phase Door|phase door]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Astral Projection|astral projection]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks.
