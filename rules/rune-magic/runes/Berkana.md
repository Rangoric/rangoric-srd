# Berkana

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

A rune of plenty and fertility, Berkana is favored by farmers and druids for the effects it has on nature. Those attuned to Berkana become very animalistic and gain a distinct dislike for civilization and all its trappings.

**Special Power**: If a rune caster sows a field while attuned to Berkana, that field produces twice as much food as normal during the next harvest.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Animal|charm animal]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune with Nature|commune with nature]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally VI|summon nature’s ally VI]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Greater|greater restoration]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animal Shapes|animal shapes]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IX|summon nature’s ally IX]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks.
