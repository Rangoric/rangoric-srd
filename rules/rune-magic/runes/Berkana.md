# Berkana

_A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat._

A rune of plenty and fertility, Berkana is favored by farmers and druids for the effects it has on nature. Those attuned to Berkana become very animalistic and gain a distinct dislike for civilization and all its trappings.

**Special Power**: If a rune caster sows a field while attuned to Berkana, that field produces twice as much food as normal during the next harvest.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]_
1. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Charm Animal|charm animal]]_
2. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Lesser|lesser restoration]]_
3. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Disease|remove disease]]_
4. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration|restoration]]_
5. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Commune with Nature|commune with nature]]_
6. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally VI|summon nature’s ally VI]]_
7. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Restoration, Greater|greater restoration]]_
8. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Animal Shapes|animal shapes]]_
9. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally IX|summon nature’s ally IX]]_

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks.
