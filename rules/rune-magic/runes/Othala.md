# Othala

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

The rune of unearned possessions, Othala is favored by thieves, the greedy, and the paranoid. Those attuned to it become suspicious of others, thinking everyone is after their wealth, which they are always yearning to increase.

**Special Power**: Any time one of the trap-style spells of the rune caster, such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Trap|fire trap]]*, are triggered, the rune caster is permitted a Wisdom check (DC 15) to sense the activation. The rune caster knows the spell has triggered regardless of distance.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Mark|arcane mark]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Alarm|alarm]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Lock|arcane lock]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Explosive Runes|explosive runes]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Trap|fire trap]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Secret Chest|secret chest]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Guards and Wards|guards and wards]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mage’s Magnificent Mansion|mage’s magnificent mansion]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Wall|prismatic wall]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prismatic Sphere|prismatic sphere]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made to pick pockets.
