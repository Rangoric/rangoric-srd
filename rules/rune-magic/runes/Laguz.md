# Laguz

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the rune caster.*

The rune of water, Laguz is popular amongst sailors and alchemists. Those attuned to it change behavior quickly, going from calm to raging in mere seconds. This behavior makes others uncomfortable around rune casters known to favor Laguz.

**Special Power**: Rune casters attuned to Laguz may rebuke or command water elementals as an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Evil Clerics and Undead|evil cleric]] of the same level rebukes or commands undead, using the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turning Other Creatures|turning]] rules. This ability may be used a number of times per day equal to 3 plus the rune caster’s Constitution bonus.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Create Water|create water]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]* (cold only)
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Water Breathing|water breathing]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Water|control water]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wall of Ice|wall of ice]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Acid Fog|acid fog]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Horrid Wilting|horrid wilting]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Swarm|elemental swarm]]* (water elementals only)

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks made to swim.
