# Isa

_A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat._

Isa is commonly known as the rune of ice, but it also has influence over challenges and obstacles. Woodcutters, explorers, and those who enjoy a good challenge in the wilderness favor it. Those attuned to Isa can be distant and unfriendly, but they are always up for a challenge or contest.

**Special Power**: While attuned to the Isa rune, the character receives a +2 enhancement bonus to its defenses against spells with the cold descriptor.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Frost|ray of frost]]_
1. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]_ (cold only)
2. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]_ (cold only)
3. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleet Storm|sleet storm]]_
4. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ice Storm|ice storm]]_
5. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cone of Cold|cone of cold]]_
6. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freezing Sphere|freezing sphere]]_
7. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]]_
8. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Horrid Wilting|horrid wilting]]_
9. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Swarm|elemental swarm]]_ (water elementals only)

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]].
