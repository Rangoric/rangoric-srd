# Isa

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the rune caster.*

Isa is commonly known as the rune of ice, but it also has influence over challenges and obstacles. Woodcutters, explorers, and those who enjoy a good challenge in the wilderness favor it. Those attuned to Isa can be distant and unfriendly, but they are always up for a challenge or contest.

**Special Power**: While attuned to the Isa rune, the character receives a +2 enhancement bonus to any saving throws against spells with the cold descriptor.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ray of Frost|ray of frost]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Endure Elements|endure elements]]* (cold only)
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resist Energy|resist energy]]* (cold only)
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleet Storm|sleet storm]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ice Storm|ice storm]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cone of Cold|cone of cold]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Freezing Sphere|freezing sphere]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Horrid Wilting|horrid wilting]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Swarm|elemental swarm]]* (water elementals only)

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]].
