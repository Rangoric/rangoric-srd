# Jera

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

The rune of peace and healing, Jera is a popular rune with rune casters because of the healing powers it makes available. Those attuned to Jera tend to become less violent and more concerned with the well being of others.

**Special Power**: Any healing spells cast while the rune caster is attuned to this rune are cast at +1 caster level.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Minor Wounds|cure minor wounds]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Serious Wounds|cure serious wounds]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Critical Wounds|cure critical wounds]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds, Mass|mass cure light wounds]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal (Spell)|heal]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Heal, Mass|mass heal]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/True Resurrection|true resurrection]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks.
