# Teiwaz

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

The rune of law, justice, and responsibility Teiwaz is favored by leaders and judges. Many rune casters who favor Teiwaz serve as the lawgivers in their home areas. Rune casters attuned to Teiwaz become very concerned with upholding the law and seeing that justice is carried out. A rune caster attuned to Teiwaz may be attuned to Nauthiz at the same time.

**Special Power**: Once per day, the rune caster may create a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Zone of Truth|zone of truth]]*. This spell operates exactly as the spell of the same name.

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Virtue|virtue]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Command|command]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Calm Emotions|calm emotions]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hold Person|hold person]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Discern Lies|discern lies]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Mark of Justice|mark of justice]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks when detecting lies.
