# Uruz

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

Uruz is the rune of strength and health, embodying the idea of a perfectly fit physical form. Warriors, laborers, and healers favor this rune. Rune casters attuned to Uruz show off their strength at every opportunity, even when it is unnecessary.

**Special Power**: When the power of the Uruz rune is activated (as a standard action), the rune caster gains a bonus to his Strength equal to that of a *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]]* spell. This ability may be used up to three times each day.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear’s endurance]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Power|divine power]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Transformation|transformation]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Iron Body|iron body]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shapechange|shapechange]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +1 bonus on all Strength-based skill checks.
