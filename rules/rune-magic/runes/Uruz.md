# Uruz

_A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat._

Uruz is the rune of strength and health, embodying the idea of a perfectly fit physical form. Warriors, laborers, and healers favor this rune. Rune casters attuned to Uruz show off their strength at every opportunity, even when it is unnecessary.

**Special Power**: When the power of the Uruz rune is activated (as a standard action), the rune caster gains a bonus to his Strength equal to that of a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bull’s Strength|bull’s strength]]_ spell. This ability may be used up to three times each day.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Resistance|resistance]]_
1. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]_
2. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bear’s Endurance|bear’s endurance]]_
3. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]_
4. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Divine Power|divine power]]_
5. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Righteous Might|righteous might]]_
6. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Transformation|transformation]]_
7. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Regenerate|regenerate]]_
8. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Iron Body|iron body]]_
9. _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shapechange|shapechange]]_

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +1 bonus on all Strength-based skill checks.
