# Thurisaz

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

Thurisaz is the rune of ruin and flames. It is primarily concerned with destruction so the old can be moved aside to make room for the new. Those attuned to the Thurisaz rune seek to destroy, especially those that are corrupt or already damaged.

**Special Power**: Rune casters attuned to the Thurisaz rune may rebuke or command fire elementals as an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Evil Clerics and Undead|evil cleric]] of the same level rebukes or commands undead, using the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turning Other Creatures|turning]] rules. This ability may be used a total number of times per day equal to 3 plus the rune caster’s Constitution modifier.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flare|flare]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Burning Hands|burning hands]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Pyrotechnics|pyrotechnics]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fireball|fireball]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Shield|fire shield]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Flame Strike|flame strike]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Seeds|fire seeds]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fire Storm|fire storm]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Incendiary Cloud|incendiary cloud]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Meteor Swarm|meteor swarm]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +8 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks made while lighting a fire.
