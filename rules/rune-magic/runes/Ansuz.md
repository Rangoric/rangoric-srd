# Ansuz

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

Ansuz is the rune of air and communication, drawing its power from the power of the wind. Politicians and leaders favor Ansuz. Those attuned to the Ansuz rune are talkative, sometimes to the point of annoyance.

**Special Power**: Rune casters attuned to Ansuz may rebuke or command air elementals as an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Evil Clerics and Undead|evil cleric]] of the same level rebukes or commands undead, using the [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Turning Other Creatures|turning]] rules. This ability may be used a number of times per day equal to the rune caster’s Constitution modifier.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Obscuring Mist|obscuring mist]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Whispering Wind|whispering wind]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Gaseous Form|gaseous form]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Air Walk|air walk]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Winds|control winds]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Control Weather|control weather]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Whirlwind|whirlwind]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Elemental Swarm|elemental swarm]]* (air elementals only)

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks.
