# Algiz

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

Defenders against evil, those who favor Algiz work against the dark forces in the world and protect the good. Those attuned to Algiz stand steadfast against all evil, allowing them to do no wrong without resisting.

**Special Power**: While attuned to this rune, the character may *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Evil|detect evil]]* within a 60-foot radius as a full-round action.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

0. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disrupt Undead|disrupt undead]]*
1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Evil|protection from evil]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Consecrate|consecrate]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Magic Circle against Evil|magic circle against evil]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Remove Curse|remove curse]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hallow|hallow]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Banishment|banishment]]*
7. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Word|holy word]]*
8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Holy Aura|holy aura]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Soul Bind|soul bind]]*

**Associated Skill Bonus**: +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]] checks.
