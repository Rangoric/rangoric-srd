# Hagalaz

*A rune of [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic|rune magic]], for the [[rangoric-srd/prestige-classes/Rune Caster|rune caster]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Activate Rune|Activate Rune]] feat.*

The rune of storm and earthquake, Hagalaz represents the anger of nature. A popular rune with Warriors, this rune is a powerful if clumsy force. Those attuned to Hagalaz are violent and destructive but also respectful toward nature.

**Special Power**: While attuned to the Hagalaz rune, the rune caster receives a +4 enhancement bonus to all saving throws made to resist damage from exposure to the environment.

**Spells Available** (by level; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/rune-magic/Rune Magic#The Runes|the runes]]):

1. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shocking Grasp|shocking grasp]]*
2. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Produce Flame|produce flame]]*
3. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Call Lightning|call lightning]]*
4. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Rusting Grasp|rusting grasp]]*
5. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Insect Plague|insect plague]]*
6. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chain Lightning|chain lightning]]*

8. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Whirlwind|whirlwind]]*
9. *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Earthquake|earthquake]]*
