# Random Gems and Art Objects

_A gemstone or art object reward names a grade; roll d% on the table for that grade. Any gemstone can be replaced by a piece of simple jewelry one grade lower, or a piece of complex jewelry two grades lower. The [[rangoric-srd/rules/Treasure Generator|Treasure Generator]] lists the rewards; [[rangoric-srd/rules/Treasure|Treasure]] has Rangoric’s own Gems and Art Objects tables._
