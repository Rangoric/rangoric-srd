# Random Armor and Weapons

*These tables pick the kind of armor, shield or weapon for the armor and weapon rewards of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Treasure Generator|Treasure Generator]]’s treasure types. The enhancement of a magic armor or weapon reward comes from [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Armor and Weapons|Magic Armor and Weapons]]. [[rangoric-srd/rules/Treasure|Treasure]] and [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Expanded Random Magic Arms and Armor|Expanded Random Magic Arms and Armor]] have their own tables for the same choices.*
