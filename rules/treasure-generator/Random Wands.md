# Random Wands

_A wand reward gives a spell level on the first table, its rarity on the second, and the wand on the table for that level. The [[rangoric-srd/rules/Treasure Generator|Treasure Generator]] lists the rewards; [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Scrolls, Staffs, and Wands|Magic Scrolls, Staffs, and Wands]] has Rangoric’s own wand tables._
