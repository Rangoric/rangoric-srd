# Random Arcane Scrolls

_A scroll reward gives a spell level and caster level on the first table, its type (arcane or divine, common or uncommon) on the second, and the scroll on the table for that spell level. Divine scrolls are on [[rangoric-srd/rules/treasure-generator/Random Divine Scrolls|Random Divine Scrolls]]. The [[rangoric-srd/rules/Treasure Generator|Treasure Generator]] lists the rewards; [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Scrolls, Staffs, and Wands|Magic Scrolls, Staffs, and Wands]] has Rangoric’s own scroll tables._
