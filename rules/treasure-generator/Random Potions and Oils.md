# Random Potions and Oils

_A potion or oil reward gives a spell level on the first table, its rarity on the second, and the potion on the table for that level. The [[rangoric-srd/rules/Treasure Generator|Treasure Generator]] lists the rewards; potions and oils are also described in [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Potions, Rings, and Rods|Magic Potions, Rings, and Rods]]._
