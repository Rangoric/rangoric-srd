# The Eye

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Confusion|Confusion]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Light|Light]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Revelation|Revelation]]
  - **Call Level**: 5th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 10 min./level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

While the constellation lasts, any spell of 4th level or lower with *detect* in its name that you cast, such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, gives you at once everything that 3 rounds of study would, and you can cast it as a free action.

**Higher call levels**: Each call level above 5th gives you a +3 insight bonus on any roll the detect spell calls for, including the save against being stunned by an overwhelming aura.
