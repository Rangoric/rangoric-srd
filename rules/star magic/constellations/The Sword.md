# The Sword

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Destruction|Destruction]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Hope|Hope]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Wind|Wind]]
  - **Call Level**: 5th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Effect**: A longsword
  - **Duration**: 5 rounds/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

A +2 *keen shining* longsword appears in your hand. You're proficient with it while it lasts, if you weren't already. It vanishes if it leaves your hands for more than 1 round.

**Higher call levels**: Each call level above 5th gives you a +1 bonus to your base attack bonus while you wield the sword.
