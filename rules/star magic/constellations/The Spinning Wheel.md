# The Spinning Wheel

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Concealment|Concealment]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Protection|Protection]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Shadow|Shadow]]
  - **Call Level**: 5th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: One creature
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

The target is dressed in a fine outfit of dark cloth, cut and decorated as you choose, which vanishes when the constellation ends. While wearing it, the target gains a +4 deflection bonus to AC and a +5 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks, and has a 25% chance to turn a critical hit or sneak attack into a normal hit, as [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|light fortification]].

**Higher call levels**: Each call level above 5th adds +1 to the deflection bonus, +2 to the Stealth bonus and 10% to the chance.
