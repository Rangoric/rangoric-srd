# The Hound

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Concealment|Concealment]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Freedom|Freedom]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Hate|Hate]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Revelation|Revelation]]
  - **Call Level**: 7th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 1 mile/level
  - **Effect**: One incorporeal hound
  - **Duration**: Concentration
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

A waist-high hound of pale blue light appears and shares its senses with you. It goes wherever you will it at a speed of 100 feet, can pass through walls less than 1 foot thick, and has [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] to 100 feet, a +15 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Track|Track]] feat.

The hound can't deal damage and isn't harmed by physical attacks, but it vanishes if it is attacked or struck by any spell. The constellation ends if your concentration is broken for any reason.
