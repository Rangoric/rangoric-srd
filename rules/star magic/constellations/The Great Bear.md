# The Great Bear

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]* \[Mind-Affecting\]

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Fire|Fire]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Light|Light]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Water|Water]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Wind|Wind]]
  - **Call Level**: 7th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
  - **Area**: 40-ft.-radius spread
  - **Duration**: Concentration + 1 round/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Wavering lights pour down over the area. Each creature in it with fewer Hit Dice than your caster level must succeed on a Will save or be hypnotized, as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Hypnotism|hypnotism]]*, until the constellation ends.

**Higher call levels**: At the 8th call level, a creature coming out of the hypnosis must make a second Will save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^confused|confused]] for 1d4 rounds. At the 9th, the effect lasts 3 rounds per caster level after you stop concentrating, instead of 1.
