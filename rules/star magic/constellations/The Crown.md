# The Crown

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]* \[Mind-Affecting\]

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Compassion|Compassion]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Death|Death]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Hate|Hate]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Hope|Hope]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Justice|Justice]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Peril|Peril]]
  - **Call Level**: 9th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 60 ft.
  - **Target**: One creature/round
  - **Duration**: 2 rounds/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (see text)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

For the duration, you can use a standard action each round to start a contest of wills with a creature you can see within range. You must first overcome its spell resistance. You and the creature each roll 1d20 + your Will save bonus; if it loses, it obeys your unspoken commands without question until the constellation ends. A command that would get it killed outright starts another contest.
