# The Cloud

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Shadow|Shadow]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Thunder|Thunder]]
  - **Call Level**: 3rd ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Area**: 500-ft.-radius spread
  - **Duration**: 5 rounds/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Reflex half (see text)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

The sky over the area darkens at once, and black thunderclouds roll and rumble across it. At the 3rd call level they do nothing more: no rain, no harm, and they vanish when the constellation ends.

**Higher call levels**: Each call level above 3rd adds a 10% chance each round that lightning strikes 1d6 random creatures in the area. Each one struck takes 5d6 points of electricity damage, +1d6 per call level above 3rd (Reflex half). Spell resistance doesn't apply to the lightning.
