# The Lovers

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Compassion|Compassion]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Hope|Hope]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Life|Life]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Oaths|Oaths]]
  - **Call Level**: 7th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: Two willing creatures
  - **Duration**: Permanent
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (harmless)
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes (harmless)

You touch two willing creatures and bind them together for good.

- **Sense**: Either one can sense the other's hit points, general condition, and distance and direction (not exact location) as a free action.
- **Share**: Within 40 feet of each other, either one can transfer hit points to the other as a standard action: the giver loses 2 hit points for each 1 the other regains, and the receiver can't go above its maximum.
- **Shield**: Within 20 feet of each other, both use the better of their two bonuses on any saving throw.
- **Loss**: If one of them dies, the other loses 1d4 levels and must succeed on a Will save against the constellation's DC or fall into a coma and die within 1d4 days, unless saved by *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Miracle|miracle]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Wish|wish]]* or similar magic.
