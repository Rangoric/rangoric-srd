# The Gate

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Decay|Decay]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Freedom|Freedom]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Frost|Frost]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Peril|Peril]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Shadow|Shadow]]
  - **Call Level**: 9th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
  - **Area**: 60-ft.-radius burst
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

A wave of power that undoes arcane magic floods the area. It works as an area *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dispel Magic, Greater|greater dispel magic]]* on everything in it. For the duration, any spell cast into or within the area is countered at once, as if by a counterspell with *greater dispel magic*.
