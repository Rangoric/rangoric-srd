# The Hunter

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Compassion|Compassion]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Concealment|Concealment]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Death|Death]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Oaths|Oaths]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Silence|Silence]]
  - **Call Level**: 9th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 5 rounds/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

You draw a +5 [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Distance|distance]] longbow and a quiver of forty +3 [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Brilliant Energy|brilliant energy]] arrows out of the starlight, and you're proficient with longbows while they last. If you try to share the bow or the arrows with anyone, they vanish at once.
