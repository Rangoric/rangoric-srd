# The Reed

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Frost|Frost]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Water|Water]]
  - **Call Level**: 3rd ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Area**: 20-ft.-radius emanation centered on you
  - **Duration**: 2 rounds/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude half
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Frost gathers around you and steam rises from the ground. Each creature in the area takes 2d6 points of cold damage each round it spends there (1d6 on a successful Fortitude save). A creature that fails its save is also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] for a number of rounds equal to the call level.
