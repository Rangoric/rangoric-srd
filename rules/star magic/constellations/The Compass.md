# The Compass

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Constellation]]*

  - **Stars**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Concealment|Concealment]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Freedom|Freedom]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Oaths|Oaths]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Revelation|Revelation]]
  - **Call Level**: 7th ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You (see text)
  - **Duration**: 1 day/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

Name one creature, place or object. For the duration you know which way to go to reach it, even across planar boundaries. Protections such as *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Nondetection|nondetection]]* are contested as usual. A target in an area without arcane magic (an *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Antimagic Field|antimagic field]]*, a plane where arcane magic doesn't work) is out of reach. The constellation shows the way but gives no means of travel.

**Higher call levels**: Each call level above 7th gives you a +3 bonus on checks to get past protections against being found.
