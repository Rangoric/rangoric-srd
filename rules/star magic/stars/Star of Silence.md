# Star of Silence

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in winter, descendant in summer, on the horizon in spring and autumn ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Personal
  - **Target**: You
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level (D)
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

Quiet wraps around you. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus per call level on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks to avoid being heard. While the star lasts you can't speak or cast spells with verbal components, which includes calling stars. You can end it as a free action.
