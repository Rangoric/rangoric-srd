# Star of Confusion

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]* \[Mind-Affecting\]

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in autumn, descendant in spring, on the horizon in summer and winter ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: One creature or object
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The light muddles whatever you touch.

- **A creature** takes a −1 penalty per call level on all skill checks and can't make itself understood by others.
- **An object** that carries meaning (a book, sign, map or statue) has its meaning hidden: anyone trying to make sense of it must succeed on a saving throw against the star's DC or can't.
