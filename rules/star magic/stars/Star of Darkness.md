# Star of Darkness

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in winter, descendant in summer, on the horizon in spring and autumn ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Area**: 10-ft.-radius + 5 ft./level spread, centered on a fixed point
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

A ball of blackness goes to a fixed point you choose within range and drinks the light around it. The light in the area is dimmed by 30% per call level, and everyone in the area has [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Concealment|concealment]] with a miss chance equal to the dimming, up to total concealment at 100%.

- Darkvision and blind-fighting abilities ignore this concealment.
- Low-light vision ignores it until the dimming reaches 100%. From then on, creatures that have only low-light vision are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] in the area.
- Dimming beyond 100% also shortens darkvision in the area by the excess: at the 4th call level (120%) the area gives total concealment and darkvision there reaches 20% less far.

*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Daylight|Daylight]]* or the [[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/stars/Star of Light|Star of Light]] dispels this star.
