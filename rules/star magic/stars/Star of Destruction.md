# Star of Destruction

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in winter, descendant in summer, on the horizon in spring and autumn ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: 10 ft.
  - **Target**: All hostile creatures in the area
  - **Area**: 10-ft.-radius emanation centered on you
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

A wave of light bursts out from you. Each hostile creature in the area takes 1d4 points of force damage per caster level, to a maximum of 5d4 at the 1st call level. Each call level above 1st raises the maximum by 2d4.
