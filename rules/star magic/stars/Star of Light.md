# Star of Light

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in summer, descendant in winter, on the horizon in spring and autumn ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Area**: 20-ft.-radius burst
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous (light: 1 day/level)
  - **Saving Throw**: Reflex half
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

Light bursts from a point you choose. The burst deals 1d8 points of damage per call level to each undead creature in the area and to each spellcaster in it who has a necromancy spell prepared or known.

The area then stays filled with bright, white light for 1 day per caster level. The light has no source and casts no shadows, and it can't be covered or ended early. Only the first burst deals damage.
