# Star of Decay

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in winter, descendant in summer, on the horizon in spring and autumn ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
  - **Target**: One object/level, all within a 40-ft.-radius
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates (object)
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

The light makes things fall apart. Each target object takes damage equal to the call level every round. Unattended objects get no save and start decaying at once; an object held, worn or in use gets its owner's saving throw.
