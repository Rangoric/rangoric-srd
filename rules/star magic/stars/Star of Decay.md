# Star of Decay

_[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]_

- **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
- **Aspect**: Ascendant in winter, descendant in summer, on the horizon in spring and autumn ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
- **Target**: One object/level, all within a 40-ft.-radius
- **Duration**: 1 round/level
- **Defense**: Will (object)
- **Spell Resistance**: No

The light makes things fall apart. Each target object takes damage equal to the call level every round. Unattended objects have no defense and start decaying at once; an object held, worn or in use uses its owner's defense.
