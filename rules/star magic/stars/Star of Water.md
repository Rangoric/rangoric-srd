# Star of Water

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in spring, descendant in autumn, on the horizon in summer and winter ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: One object or spot
  - **Duration**: 1 hour/level
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

Pure water wells up from the object or spot you touch at 1 gallon per minute, until its container or space is full or the star ends. Anyone making a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check to treat someone who has drunk the water gains a +1 bonus per call level.
