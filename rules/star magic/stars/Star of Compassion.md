# Star of Compassion

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in summer, descendant in winter, on the horizon in spring and autumn ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Touch
  - **Target**: One creature touched per round
  - **Duration**: 1 round/level
  - **Saving Throw**: Will negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

While the star's light fills you, you can touch one creature each round and learn about its state. The creature must have an Intelligence of 3 or more and can't be an outsider. Touching an unwilling creature takes a touch attack.

What you learn depends on the call level, and each level also gives everything below it:

| Call level | You learn |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | Its mood (glad, grieving, angry) |
| 2nd | Its health (well, ill, wounded) |
| 3rd | Its spiritual standing (alignment, ties to divine powers) |
| 4th | Why it feels as it does |
| 5th | What lies behind its health (the illness, the old wound) |
| 6th | What lies behind its spiritual standing (an offended god, a broken vow) |
| 7th | How its mood came about, clearly enough to set it right |
| 8th | How to cure what ails it |
| 9th | How to mend its spiritual standing |
