# Star of Freedom

_[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]_

- **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
- **Aspect**: Ascendant in autumn, descendant in spring, on the horizon in summer and winter ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
- **Components**: V, S
- **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
- **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
- **Target**: One creature
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Defense**: None
- **Spell Resistance**: No

The light frees a mind. Each mind-affecting spell or effect that controls the target's actions at once attacks it again, and ends if it misses. At the 2nd call level and higher the target gains a +2 bonus on its defense against these attacks per call level above 1st.
