# Star of Freedom

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in autumn, descendant in spring, on the horizon in summer and winter ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
  - **Target**: One creature
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous
  - **Saving Throw**: None
  - **Spell Resistance**: No

The light frees a mind. The target at once gets a new saving throw against each mind-affecting spell or effect that controls its actions. At the 2nd call level and higher it gains a +2 bonus on these saves per call level above 1st.
