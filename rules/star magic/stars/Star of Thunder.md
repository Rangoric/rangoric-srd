# Star of Thunder

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic|Star]]*

  - **Call Level**: 1st ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^minimum-call-level|minimum]])
  - **Aspect**: Ascendant in spring, descendant in autumn, on the horizon in summer and winter ([[rangoric-srd/rules/star magic/Star Magic#^seasons|seasons]])
  - **Components**: V, S
  - **Casting Time**: 1 standard action
  - **Range**: Long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
  - **Area**: 10-ft.-radius burst
  - **Duration**: Instantaneous (see text)
  - **Saving Throw**: Fortitude negates
  - **Spell Resistance**: Yes

The light hangs for an instant and then bursts with a thunderclap. Every creature in the area, friend or foe, that fails its save can take no actions for a number of rounds equal to the call level. It isn't stunned and suffers nothing else, and the effect ends at once if it takes damage of any kind.
