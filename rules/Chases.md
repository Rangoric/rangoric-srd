# Chases

While chases are a signature action scene in countless stories, they present a singular challenge in the game, thanks to static movement rates. Since every creature in the game has a set movement rate, it might seem like you'd either automatically (or never) catch up to a fleeing foe! Obviously, this isn't the case, because there's more to catching a foe or avoiding being caught than simple speed.

A chase is laid out as a track of chase cards ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Building a Chase|Building a Chase]]), each with its [[rangoric-srd/rules/Chase Obstacles|obstacles]], and played out round by round ([[rangoric-srd/rules/Running a Chase|Running a Chase]]).
