# Puck

***AKA “Poukka”, “Robin Goodfellow”***

Son of a mortal human female and [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/King Oberon|Prince Oberon]], Puck is a feisty and mischievous personality who meddles in the affairs of fey and mortals alike with abandon. Possessing an evasive personality, he is best described as a good-natured fellow who enjoys the finer things in life, and will always find a means of obtaining them.
