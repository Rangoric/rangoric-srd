# Pixie Queen Sharla

***AKA “Queen of Pixies”***

Like many [[rangoric-srd/monsters/sprite/Pixie|pixies]], Queen Sharla is a prankster at heart, though the responsibilities of court weigh heavy on her conscience. She is always seeking ways to better protect her people, and keep [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/The Queen of Air and Darkness|the Queen of Air and Darkness]] from interfering with their way of life. She is benevolent but overly cautious in her decisions regarding outsiders. Too many residents of the Land of Eternal Summer have been killed, hurt, or otherwise wronged by ignorant mortals, so she now is very thorough when the safety of her subjects is at stake. She sometimes tests the mettle of visitors through spells, trickery, and even with an effect from her prized *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/rods/Rod of Wonder|rod of wonder]]*. The Pixie Queen holds her court high above the Lands of Eternal Summer amid a cloud of flying pixies, faeries, and other winged creatures. She rarely visits the ground or leaves her court unless she has to do so.
