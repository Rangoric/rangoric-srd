# Sand Man

***“Keeper of Dreams”, “Steward of the Sands of Time”, “Warden of Nightmares”***

The Sand Man appears as a tall and somewhat gaunt [[rangoric-srd/races/Feeorin|feeorin]] with light gray skin, midnight blue eyes, and charcoal gray hair. His features are slightly drawn, looking as if he is always in sore need of sleep. His motives are mysterious, and he tends to influence good and evil beings alike without prejudice.

The Sand Man always appears generally as above, though his primary form shifts over time. Some say the Sand Man used to be a [[rangoric-srd/races/Bogie|bogie]], others suggest it was a fae-touched elf, and still more suggest the Sand Man’s role is eternal, even if he is not. The latest Sand Man, court rumors suggest, is a feeorin who wandered into the Sands of Time to become the Sand Man when he failed to gain [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/Queen Titania|Titania]]’s hand in marriage.

Other than [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/The Holly King|the Holly King]], the Sand Man is the only being who seems to have any control over the flow of the Sands of Time. He visits mortal worlds and sprinkles a little magic sand into the eyes of mortals whereupon they dream. What mortals do not know is that their dreaming, which often spans more time in dreamland than in the mortal world, helps to stem the growth of the desert by creating an “errant” flow of time to an alternate destination.
