# The Holly King

*AKA The Gift Bringer, Kris Kringle, Santa Claus*

The Holly King is known far and wide among mortals, especially children, though he is called by a vast number of names and appears in an equally diverse quantity of forms. He is most famous for the Yule Gifting where he delivers gifts of food, toys, and clothing to those he deems have led a good and upstanding life over the past year. Kringle uses numerous magic items to accomplish this task, including the only ancient fey magic ritual currently in use. When performing the ritual, he redirects the flow of time from the mortal realm he is visiting and sends it into the Sands of Time desert. This ritual essentially stops the flow of time and enables him to perform his tasks between seconds of mortal time. This is how the Holly King can visit entire worlds within the confines of one mortal night.

The Holly King celebrates the Yule Gifting to promote goodness in the worlds he visits, hoping that eventually evil will no longer be a threat to good kind-hearted folk. He believes in this ideal so much that he will not give gifts to those who have been bad.
