# Sidhnaar DiCrystala

***AKA “Master of Tolls”, “Seneschal of Doors”***

Sidhnaar DiCrystala is a human who found himself the unwilling target of the Crystal-keeper’s affections. Unbeknownst to her then, Sidhnaar’s interests lay only with men, not women. Rather than torture her with his presence and the too-well-known pains of unrequited love, he delicately bid farewell and made his way to Market. Since those first few tumultuous days in FaerieLand, Sidhnaar has carved himself a niche in the world of the fey.

Eventually landing the job of Master of Tolls at Market, he is now in a position to meet new and interesting people for magical training, theatrical events, and romantic dalliances—especially with certain [[rangoric-srd/races/Feeorin|feeorin]] men (who he also happens to resemble).

Over his years spent in FaerieLand, Sidhnaar has also gained the honorary title of Seneschal of Doors. [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/Queen Titania|The Faerie Queen Titania]] gave him this title after he served as liaison between the Seelie Court and his original mortal homeworld. Now, as bearer of that title, he is often brought to Court to help explain the nuance of one party’s world in the other party’s terms.
