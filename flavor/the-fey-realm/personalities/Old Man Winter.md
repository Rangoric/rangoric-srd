# Old Man Winter

***“Master of the Winter”, “Biting Wind of the North”***

Old Man Winter’s strange coloration makes him look like a [[rangoric-srd/races/Feeorin|feeorin]] who has frozen to death; his skin is bluish tinged, his silver-white hair and mustacheless beard are laced with ice crystals, and his ice blue clothing shimmers with patches of frost.

Old Man Winter is old even by fey standards. He seldom comes down from his mountaintop Palace of Ice in the Land of Eternal Winter. When he does venture forth, it is usually to reinforce the presence of winter in FaerieLand and mortal lands.

When he chooses to speak with others, they find him ill-tempered, biting, cruel, and callous. Through interpreting his actions, all anyone has been able to learn about him is that he hates summer, fire-based creatures, and those who are careless or destructive with fire. His son [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/Jack Frost|Jack Frost]], who often visits him in his palace, has reaffirmed what many already know; Old Man Winter is obsessed with winter and all that comes with it: cold, biting winds, rain, snow, ice, and so on.
