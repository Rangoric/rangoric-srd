# King Oberon

***AKA “High Consort Oberon”, “Prince Oberon”***

Depending on when Oberon is encountered, he may either be Prince Oberon, consort to [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/Queen Titania|Queen Titania]], or King Oberon, her husband. Regardless of where in his life one might meet him, he is a rather short ash-colored [[rangoric-srd/races/Feeorin|feeorin]] further understated by his hunched shoulders. Some say this is a birth defect while others blame [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/Baba Yaga|Baba Yaga]] or the Sisters Three for cursing him while still in his mother’s womb. Despite his short stature and bent frame, he is a stalwart and strong man who acts younger than he appears. He is a good complement to Queen Titania’s rule, as he is a caring man who seeks to guide the younger and less experienced fey toward positive goals.

He enjoys visiting mortal worlds on faerie rades (fey hunting parties) and frequently stops such a troupe to make a brief hunt into the countryside. When he is not at the Great Stone Ring or among the Lands of Eternal Spring with the Seelie Court, he can be found hunting in the Lands of Eternal Autumn with his closest friend [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/King Finvarra|King Finvarra]].

Like nearly all fey, Oberon despises [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/personalities/The Queen of Air and Darkness|the Queen of Air and Darkness]] and works to halt any of her activities whenever he can. Queen Titania laid a restraining royal hand on his efforts—to which he is visibly irritated by—so that his infrequent but daunting temper and impetuousness does not disrupt her well-laid plans. Oberon hopes that someday he will have the opportunity to raise an army strong enough to defeat the Dark Queen. He also holds a secret dream of running her through with his sword. As of yet, he has not had either chance.
