# The Twilight Lands

Within the darkened border realm of Shadow is the home of most fey—the Twilight Lands. This is the heart of FaerieLand and its one true unified visage—this region reflects what the majority of fey creatures think of when they think of “home.”

**Distance:** Distant (DC 20)

**Regional Seat:** [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/places/The Great Stone Ring|The Great Stone Ring]]

**Regent:** Elected annually from among the regents of the Twilight Lands

**Prominent Features:** [[rangoric-srd/flavor/the-fey-realm/places/The Great Stone Ring|The Great Stone Ring]]

**Seasons:** All seasons feature prominently in the Twilight Lands, and in fact there are lands where each season never wanes.
