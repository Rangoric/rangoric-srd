# Flavor Overview

Setting and flavor text from the open content sources: worlds, places, peoples and lore. It's here for color and ideas, not rules; nothing else in the SRD depends on it.

## What goes here

- **Open Content only.** A book's text comes in only when its Open Content declaration covers it (see [[rangoric-srd/legal/Source Product Identity|Source Product Identity]]) and its Section 15 notice is in [[rangoric-srd/legal/Legal|Legal]]. Text a book keeps closed (such as star magic's, where only the game stats are open) stays out.
- **Read for Product Identity**, like everything else in the SRD: no other publishers' trademarks, settings or book titles, and no example character names.
- **One folder per setting**: `flavor/<setting>`, with a setting note that introduces it and links its notes. Places get one note each, in the setting's `places` folder, and so do personalities, in `personalities` (their descriptions only; stat blocks stay out).
- **Properties**: `srd-type: flavor` and the `flavor` tag, with the usual `based-on`, `status`, `change` and `pi-review`. Place notes add `place-kind` (region or site) and, for a site, its `region`.
- **Rules stay in the rules.** Where a book gives a game effect inside its flavor text, the flavor note keeps it as the book has it; a rule the SRD needs gets its home in the rules and the flavor points there.
