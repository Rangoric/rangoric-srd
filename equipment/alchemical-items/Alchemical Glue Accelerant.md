# Alchemical Glue Accelerant

This tiny dropper contains a liquid that speeds up the cure time of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Alchemical Glue|alchemical glue]]. Applying the accelerant is a standard action. Accelerated glue is tacky after 1 round and fully cured in 1 minute. The dropper contains 20 doses; each dose is sufficient to cure one small application of alchemical glue. The entire dropper must be used to cure a full square foot of glue.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 25 gp.
