# Shard Gel

This vivid green gel instantly crystallizes into needle-like shards upon contact with the air. You can throw a flask of shard gel as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]]. A direct hit deals 1d4 points of piercing damage as the shards penetrate the target's flesh. The target's square and each square adjacent to it are covered in shards that function like [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Caltrops|caltrops]]. The caltrops last for 5 rounds before dissolving into harmless green sludge. Any effect that deals sonic damage to an area (such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sound Burst|sound burst]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shout|shout]]_) immediately destroys all gel shards in that area. Crafting a flask of shard gel is a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) check.
