# Paper Candle Firework

This finger-sized explosive detonates noisily 1 round after lighting. The blast attacks the Fortitude of anyone in the same square as a paper candle when it explodes at +5, and on a hit they are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1d4 rounds. Crafting this item is a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 1 gp.
