# Flash Powder

This coarse gray powder ignites and burns almost instantly if exposed to flame, significant friction, or even a simple force such as throwing it against a floor (a standard action). Creatures within the 10-foot-radius burst are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1 round (Fortitude DC 13 negates).

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 50 gp.
