# Bottled Lightning

Electricity crackles along a metal filament inside this small glass bottle. You can open the bottle as a standard action, unleashing a small bolt of lightning toward an enemy within 20 feet of you. This is a ranged touch attack that deals 1d8 points of electricity damage. Any creature in a line between you and the target (including the target) takes 1 point of sonic damage from the terrific clap of thunder the bolt generates (+5 vs Reflex). Crafting bottled lightning is a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.
