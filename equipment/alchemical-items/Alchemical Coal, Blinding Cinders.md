# Alchemical Coal, Blinding Cinders

A [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]] who chews and swallows this alchemically treated piece of coal (a full-round action) can make one [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Breath Weapon|breath weapon]] attack during the next minute. If the kobold fails to make a breath attack before this minute expires, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 hour. Other humanoid creatures that eat alchemical coal are sickened for 1 hour (though certain other creatures may be able to use them, at the GM's discretion). Creatures that are immune to poison are immune to the sickened effect. The type of breath weapon depends on the type of alchemical coal that is chewed by the kobold.

This jagged bit of dusty red coal allows a [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]] to breathe a 30-foot line of coarse cinders. The breath weapon deals 1d6 points of fire damage and blinds creatures in the area for 1 round. The cinders attack each creature’s Reflex at +5; on a miss, it takes half damage and is not [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]].

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 100 gp.
