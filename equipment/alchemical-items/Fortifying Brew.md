# Fortifying Brew

_Remedy_

This liquid causes a pleasant warming sensation when consumed. For the next hour, you gain a +2 morale bonus on defenses against fear. Using more than 1 dose within 24 hours causes you to become [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 hour.

**Construction**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 20 gp; Weight 1 lb.
