# Blight Tonic

Blight tonic increases the potency of a creature's disease attack transmitted by physical contact, such as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dire-animal/Dire Rat|dire rat]]'s [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]]. The next time a creature must save against the drinker's disease save DC, the DC for the initial infection increases by +2. The tonic lasts for 10 minutes or until the next time a creature must save against the drinker's disease DC, whichever comes first.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 150 gp; Weight 1 lb.
