# Blight Tonic

Blight tonic increases the potency of a creature's disease attack transmitted by physical contact, such as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/dire-animals/Dire Rat|dire rat]]'s [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]]. The next time the drinker's disease attacks a creature, its attack bonus for the initial infection increases by +2. The tonic lasts for 10 minutes or until the next time the drinker's disease attacks a creature, whichever comes first.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 150 gp; Weight 1 lb.
