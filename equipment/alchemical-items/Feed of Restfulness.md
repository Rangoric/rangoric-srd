# Feed of Restfulness

*Herbal Restorative/Ingestible; Special*

A large animal or magical beast that consumes this feed gains the benefits of a full day’s (24 hours) rest with only 4 hours of sleep.

The standard packaging is a tightly packed pound of herbs and oats in a waxed-muslin bundle. The bundle is broken open and crumbled into the feedbag.

**Construction**: No aura; CL 3rd; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Ingestibles|Brew Ingestibles]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Herbal Restoratives|Craft Herbal Restoratives]]; DC: 16; Price: 235 gp/1 pound bag (1 meal)
