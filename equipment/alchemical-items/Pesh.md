# Pesh

A drug: a dose gives its effect and its ability damage at once, and risks [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Moderate Addiction|addiction]]. How drugs work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|drugs and addiction]].

- **Type**: ingested or inhaled
- **Addiction**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Moderate Addiction|moderate]], +10 vs Fortitude
- **Price**: 15 gp
- **Effect**: 1 hour; +1d2 alchemical bonus to Strength, –2 penalty on defenses against illusions and mind-affecting effects
- **Effect**: after 1 hour; 1d2 hours of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigue]]
- **Damage**: 1d2 Con and 1d2 Wis damage
