# Potion Condensor

*Chymical; Special*

The potion condensor is a chymical which can be mixed with any magical potion to condense that potion to a tincture (1 oz. potion to 0.25 ounce tincture). As a tincture, the potion delivers the same effects with a dosage of 2 drops, but only for 20% of its normal duration (minimum of 1 round or instantaneous, depending on the spell). The benefit is that the potion can now be used in tincture form in 2d3 doses of 2 drops each.

One of the drawbacks of the potion condensor is that it does not reduce curative potions—it destroys them and renders them inert without delivering their effects. Also, if any person takes a second dose while the previous dose’s effects are still active, they suffer 1d4 points each of normal and nonlethal damage (no save) as their body is shocked by an overdose of magic and chymicals. Despite the damage, the dose will deliver the effects of the tincture as per above.
