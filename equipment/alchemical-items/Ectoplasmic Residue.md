# Ectoplasmic Residue

This flask holds the remains of minor ectoplasmic creatures. You can throw it as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]]: a ranged touch attack with a range increment of 10 feet. A direct hit deals no damage, but it attacks the target’s Will at +3, and on a hit the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d4 rounds. It attacks the Will of every creature within 5 feet of where the flask hits at +3, and those it hits are shaken for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.
