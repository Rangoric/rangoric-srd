# Ectoplasmic Residue

This flask holds the remains of minor ectoplasmic creatures. You can throw it as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]]: a ranged touch attack with a range increment of 10 feet. A direct hit deals no damage, but the target must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] for 1d4 rounds. Every creature within 5 feet of where the flask hits must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be shaken for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 20 gp; Weight 2 lb.
