# Brewed Reek

Animal musk, spoiled meat, pungent plants, and any other foul-smelling substances on hand go into a batch of brewed reek. When boiled, this mixture becomes a thick alchemical slime that adheres to anything it touches. You can hurl brewed reek as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Splash|splash weapon]] with a range increment of 10 feet. The reek attacks the Fortitude of a creature struck with a direct hit at +5, and on a hit it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 2d6 rounds. If it hits, it attacks the target’s Fortitude a second time, and on a hit the target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round. It attacks the Fortitude of creatures in the splash area, and those it hits are sickened for 1 round. A creature that drinks brewed reek is hit automatically and is sickened for 2d6 rounds and nauseated for 1 round.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 40 gp; Weight 1 lb.
