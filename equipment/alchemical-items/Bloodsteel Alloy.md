# Bloodsteel Alloy

*Alchana; Special*

Bloodsteel is [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Bloodiron Alloy|bloodiron]] reforged and refined alchemically a second and third time to remove ferrous impurities and add in additional chymicals. In all ways but those noted below (including randomly generating bloodsteel items on the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Bloodiron Alloy|bloodiron]] table), bloodsteel should be viewed the same as bloodiron.

- Bloodsteel weapons inflict 2 points of alchemical damage with each successful hit, and this damage can only be healed naturally (not through use of magic).
- Bloodsteel weapons effect a subtype of creature rather than a whole broad category. Thus, after rolling for Blooded Foes, restrict the type to a subtype—evil outsiders or reptilians or fire creatures or aquatic creatures or dragons of one color (and their related kin, half-dragons).

**Construction**: Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (weaponsmithing), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Create Alchana|Create Alchana]]; DC: 27; Market Price: 12,000 gp + cost of weapon
