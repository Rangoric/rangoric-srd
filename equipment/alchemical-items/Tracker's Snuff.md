# Tracker's Snuff

When inhaled, this finely ground, alchemically treated tobacco greatly enhances your senses, especially your sense of smell. It grants the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]] ability and a +2 alchemical bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks for 1 hour. However, once the effect wears off your body is wracked with terrible pain as your joints begin to stiffen and seize, dealing 1d2 points of Dexterity damage to you.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 30; Price 200 gp.
