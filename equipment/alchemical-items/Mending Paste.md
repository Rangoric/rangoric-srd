# Mending Paste

You can use this smelly, silvery paste to temporarily repair metal objects. It is sold in a sealed flask and reacts with air, turning solid in 1d6+4 minutes. You must make an appropriate DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background#Making Things|Background]] check to repair a metal item with the [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] condition; success means the object loses the broken condition but gains the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Fragile (Quality)|fragile]] condition (for a weapon or armor, as that note says; other items have no further effect). Increase the DC by +5 if the broken object is a magic item or by +5 if it is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]] (add both modifiers if the item is a magical firearm). If the check fails, the mending paste is consumed but you may try again with another flask or conventional repair methods.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 25 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
