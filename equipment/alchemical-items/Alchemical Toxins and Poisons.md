# Alchemical Toxins and Poisons

Every toxin and poison of the alchemical and herbal catalog, grouped by how it is delivered. What the columns mean, and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Toxins|Craft Toxins]] rule for making them, are in [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemical and Herbal Items#Toxins & Poisons|Toxins & Poisons]]; the poisons themselves work by the core [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|poison]] rules. Poisons marked # are the core poisons, listed for their DC to create; an asterisk on ability damage marks a permanent drain. For treasure, each toxin is rolled on its own category’s table (its classification here).
