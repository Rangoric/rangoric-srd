# Alchemical Toxins and Poisons

Every toxin and poison of the alchemical and herbal catalog, the poisonous herbs, and the core poisons alchemists and herbalists can make with their skills. Each is an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|affliction]] and works by the core [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]] rules; the lists gather them from the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Toxins Index.base|Toxins Index]]. Making one follows [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#^making-poison|Making Poison]]: its DC to make is 10 + its attack bonus (+10 for one made as a potion), and a natural 1 on the check exposes a crafter without [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#^poison-use|poison use]]. Which skills and feats make which, and what the columns mean, are in [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Alchemical and Herbal Items#Toxins & Poisons|Toxins & Poisons]]. For treasure, each toxin is rolled on its own category’s table (its classification here).
