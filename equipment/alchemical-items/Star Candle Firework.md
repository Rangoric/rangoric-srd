# Star Candle Firework

When lit, this foot-long wooden tube launches a flaming pyrotechnic “candle” every round for 4 rounds. The projectiles deal 1 point of nonlethal damage and 1 point of fire damage if they hit; on a critical hit, the target is also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for 1 round. The projectiles shed light as candles for 1 round and have a range increment of 5 feet. Attacking with a star candle is a ranged touch attack and always has a –4 nonproficiency penalty. Crafting this item is a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.
