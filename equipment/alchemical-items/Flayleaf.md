- **Addiction**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Minor Addiction|minor]], +2 vs Fortitude

- **Effect**: 1 hour; +2 alchemical bonus on defenses against mind-affecting effects, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]]
- **Damage**: 1 Wis damage
