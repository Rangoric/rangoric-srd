# Flayleaf

A drug: a dose gives its effect and its ability damage at once, and risks [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Minor Addiction|addiction]]. How drugs work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|drugs and addiction]].

- **Type**: inhaled or ingested
- **Addiction**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Minor Addiction|minor]], Fortitude DC 12
- **Price**: 10 gp
- **Effect**: 1 hour; +2 alchemical bonus on saves against mind-affecting effects, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]]
- **Damage**: 1 Wis damage
