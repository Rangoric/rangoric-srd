# Ambrosia

Upon consumption, this heavenly elixir, brewed from holy water and blessed herbs, grants a +2 sacred bonus on defenses against negative energy, energy drain, and death effects for 1 hour, including the daily attacks of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative levels]]. Ambrosia affects undead and evil outsiders as [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Holy Water|holy water]].

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 100 gp/vial; Weight 1 lb.
