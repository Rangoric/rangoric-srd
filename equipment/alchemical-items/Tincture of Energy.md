# Tincture of Energy

*Herbal Restorative/Ingestible; Special*

The tincture of energy provides temporary relief from sleep and exhaustion for short periods of time. Ingesting 2 drops (2d4 doses per vial) allows a person to do without sleep for 2d6 hours, although they suffer minor effects (1d2 points of nonlethal damage). If the tincture is taken more than once in a 24-hour period, the imbiber suffers 1d6 points of nonlethal damage per additional dose. This nonlethal damage—little more than exhaustion due to lack of sleep—can only be removed by total rest and normal sleep.
