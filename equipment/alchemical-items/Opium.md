# Opium

A drug: a dose gives its effect and its ability damage at once, and risks [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Severe Addiction|addiction]]. How drugs work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|drugs and addiction]].

- **Type**: inhaled, ingested, or injury
- **Addiction**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Severe Addiction|severe]], Fortitude DC 20
- **Price**: 25 gp
- **Effect**: 1 hour; +1d8 [[rangoric-srd/combat/Injury and Death#Temporary Hit Points|temporary hit points]], +2 alchemical bonus on Fortitude saves, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]]
- **Damage**: 1d4 Con and 1d4 Wis damage
