# Twitch Tonic

_Remedy_

This thick syrup is extracted from bitter herbs and enhanced by alchemy to ward against sleep, paralysis, and the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^staggered|staggered]] condition. If you drink twitch tonic, you gain a +2 alchemical bonus on defenses against these effects for the next hour. If you drink twitch tonic while suffering from any of these effects, you may immediately make the effect attack you again (with the +2 bonus to your defense), and a miss ends it; you may gain this particular benefit only once per day. You can administer twitch tonic to an unconscious or paralyzed creature as a full-round action, similar to administering a potion.

**Construction**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 45 gp.
