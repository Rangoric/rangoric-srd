# Alkali Flask

This flask of caustic liquid reacts with an ooze's natural acids. You can throw an alkali flask as a splash weapon with a range increment of 10 feet. Against non-ooze creatures, an alkali flask functions as a normal flask of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Acid|acid]]. Against oozes and other acid-based creatures, the alkali flask inflicts double damage.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 15 gp; Weight 1 lb.
