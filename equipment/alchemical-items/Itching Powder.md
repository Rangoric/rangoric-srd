# Itching Powder

This fine gray powder causes targets to suffer from uncontrollable itching until they spend at least 1 round washing it off. Throwing a packet of itching powder is a splash attack with a range increment of 10 feet. The powder attacks the Fortitude of anyone standing on the square of impact at +2, and of those in adjacent squares at –2. Creatures it hits take a –2 penalty on attack rolls, defenses, skill checks, and ability checks until they wash the powder off. This is a poison effect. Crafting this item is a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.
