# Signal Rocket

You can use this rocket to deliver simple auditory and visual signals to an army from a distance. When used during [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Mass Combat/Mass Combat|mass combat]], a signal rocket allows a commander to change [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Mass Combat/Mass Combat#Strategy Track|strategy track]] with a higher degree of success than normal. A commander can launch a signal rocket while changing a strategy track by more than 1 step to grant his army a +5 circumstance bonus on its Morale check to implement his orders. Crafting this item requires a successful DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.
