# Spiderdraw

This alchemical fluid is viscous and clear, and it emits a strong chemical smell when exposed to air. Vermin are attracted to it and perceive anything marked with it as prey. Spiders, insects, and similar [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Vermin|vermin]] gain a +5 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks and attack rolls against a creature marked with spiderdraw; if these creatures have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]] ability, they can detect a marked creature at five times the normal range, note its direction as a free action, and pinpoint it when it is within 25 feet.

Drow sometimes use spiderdraw as a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#^contact-poison|contact poison]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]] against opponents, allowing vermin to focus on the marked targets while the drow retreat, regroup, or move to a more advantageous position. Washing off the spiderdraw is a full-round action requiring at least a gallon of water or other suitable washing liquid; otherwise, the effects last for 1 hour.

Crafting _spiderdraw_ requires a successful DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) check.
