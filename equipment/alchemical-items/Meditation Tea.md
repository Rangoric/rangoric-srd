# Meditation Tea

_Remedy_

Drinking this cloudy tea has a soothing effect that clarifies your thoughts. For 10 minutes after drinking the tea, you gain a +2 alchemical bonus against mind-affecting effects. If you drink meditation tea while suffering from a mind-affecting effect, you may immediately make the effect attack you again (with the +2 bonus to your defense), and a miss ends it; you may gain this particular benefit only once per day.

**Construction**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 30 gp.
