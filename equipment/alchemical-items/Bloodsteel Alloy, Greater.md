# Bloodsteel Alloy, Greater

*Alchana; Special*

Greater bloodsteel is [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Bloodsteel Alloy|bloodsteel]] refined to such an extreme degree that its effects are tied to one particular type of monster or species or race. In all ways but those noted below (including randomly generating items on the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Bloodiron Alloy|bloodiron]] table), greater bloodsteel should be viewed the same as bloodiron.

- Greater bloodsteel weapons inflict 3 points of alchemical damage with each successful hit, and this damage can only be healed naturally (not through use of magic).
- Greater bloodsteel weapons effect only a single race of creature—ettins instead of giants, red dragons instead of just chromatic dragons, pit fiends instead of just evil outsiders, etc.
- If used against a character race (human, elf, dwarf, etc.), greater bloodsteel weapons incur their effects on particular bloodlines (i.e. a hated foe and his relatives), not the entire race.

**Construction**: Moderate evocation; CL 9th; [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (weaponsmithing), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Create Alchana|Create Alchana]]; DC: 27; Market Price: 15,000 gp + cost of weapon
