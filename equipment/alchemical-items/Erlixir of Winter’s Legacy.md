# Erlixir of Winter’s Legacy

*Erlcana, Greater; Special*

The Erlixir of Winter’s Legacy, unlike the other erlixirs, is thicker and more an unguent than an imbibable drink. When poured or smeared upon a dead body, the erlixir transforms the body’s flesh and skin to a moss that swiftly hardens to wood, and, over the course of 1d4 days, reincarnates the dead body (and the person to whom it once belonged) into a plant, fey or magical creature.

The Erlixir of Winter’s Legacy comes in a small black ceramic bottle with a humaniform shape on one side of the bottle, and a bear in the same pose on the other. In all ways, the erlixir works as per the *reincarnate* spell, though GMs should roll on the incarnations table below to determine the character’s new form and abilities.
