# Troll Oil

_Remedy_

This crimson liquid is viscous and tastes foul. If you drink it, for the next hour you automatically stabilize when reduced to negative hit points (unless the damage is sufficient to instantly kill you) and have a 50% chance each round to end any [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^bleed|bleed]] effect on you. If you take fire or acid damage, the benefits of troll oil are suspended for 1 round.

**Construction**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) DC 30; Price 50 gp; Weight 1 lb.
