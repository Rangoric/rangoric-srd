# Gloom Sight Goggles

These nonmagical goggles are set with a piece of alchemically treated, black, obsidianlike stone found in the mountainous regions of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/planes/Shadow Plane|Shadow Plane]]. Gloom sight goggles interact with the unique eyes of fetchlings in such a way that when the goggles are worn over both eyes, they expand the range of a [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|fetchling]]'s [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] to 90 feet, but the fetchling also gains the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Light Sensitivity|light sensitivity]] weakness. Other races cannot see through the lenses of these goggles, and they have no effect on fetchlings whose eyes have been modified by the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Gloom Sight|Gloom Sight]] feat. Though they are alchemical rather than magical in nature, these goggles take up the magic item eye slot.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 200 gp.
