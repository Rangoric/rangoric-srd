# Bloodblock

This gooey, pinkish substance helps treat wounds. Using a dose gives you a +5 alchemical bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks for providing [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|first aid]], [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Wound|treating wounds]] made by [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Caltrops|caltrops]] or similar objects, or [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Deadly Wounds|treating deadly wounds]]. A dose of bloodblock ends a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^bleed|bleed]] effect as if you had made a DC 15 Medicine check. When treating deadly wounds, using a dose of bloodblock counts as one use of a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Healer's Kit|healer's kit]] (and grants the +5 bonus stated above).

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 25 gp.
