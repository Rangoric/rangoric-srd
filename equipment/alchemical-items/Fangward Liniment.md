# Fangward Liniment

*Unguent; Special*

Fangward liniment, when rubbed on the skin, reduces or prevents tooth and claw damage from nonmagical beasts. It toughens normal skin and provides a natural armor bonus of +3 but only against natural living weapons (tooth, claw, antler, etc.). It also provides damage reduction 5 against those same natural weapons.

It takes as much time to apply this liniment to the entire body as it does to put on a suit of plate armor, but other armor or clothing can be worn over it afterward. The fangliniment’s effects last 3d12 hours.

**Construction**: No aura; CL 10th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Unguents|Craft Unguents]]; DC: 30; Price: 5,000 gp/4 oz.
