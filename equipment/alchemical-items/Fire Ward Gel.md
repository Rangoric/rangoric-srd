# Fire Ward Gel

_Remedy_

When you apply this thin gel to your skin, armor, or clothing, it creates a resistant barrier that protects you from fire or heat damage. You gain fire resistance 5. The gel burns away as it absorbs fire damage; when it absorbs a total of 20 points of fire damage, it is discharged. Fire ward gel counts as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Protection from Energy|protection from energy]]_ for the purpose of stacking multiple fire protection effects. Applying fire ward gel takes 1 minute, and it lasts for 1 hour before losing its potency. Multiple applications of fire ward gel do not stack; applying more while a dose is active merely resets the potential amount of fire damage absorbed to 20 points.

**Construction**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 150 gp; Weight 1 lb.
