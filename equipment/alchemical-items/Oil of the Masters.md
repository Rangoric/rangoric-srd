# Oil of the Masters

This gold-tinted oil smells of wood shavings. When you apply it to the strings of a stringed instrument or the body of a woodwind, it improves the quality of the instrument's sound. For 1 hour, anyone playing the instrument gains a +2 alchemical bonus on the appropriate [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] check.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 50 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
