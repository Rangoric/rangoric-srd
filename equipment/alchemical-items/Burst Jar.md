# Burst Jar

This simple clay pot has two sealed, airtight inner chambers containing alchemical liquids. The jar is thrown as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]]. It shatters on impact, covering the target with a mixture of the two liquids. One round later on your turn, the liquids react and explode with concussive force. The target takes 1 point of sonic damage, is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] for 1d4 rounds, and must make a DC 12 Fortitude save or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for 1 round. Creatures within 5 feet of the explosion take 1 point of sonic damage. The target can use a full-round action to scrape or wash off the liquid before it detonates, depositing the material in its square or an adjacent square; the material detonates as normal, though the original target only takes damage as if it were within 5 feet of the explosion. Crafting this item is a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.
