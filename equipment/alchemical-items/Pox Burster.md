# Pox Burster

A pox burster is an alchemically preserved animal bladder or gourd that has been filled with toxic, rotting materials. You can throw a pox burster as a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Splash|splash weapon]]. Treat this attack as a ranged touch attack with a range increment of 10 feet. A direct hit attacks the target’s Fortitude at once at +3, and on a hit it contracts [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]]. Every space adjacent to the target square of the pox burster is covered in disease-causing filth. For the next minute, the filth also attacks the Fortitude of any creature that is injured while in one of these spaces at –1, and on a hit it contracts [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Filth Fever|filth fever]].

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 50 gp; Weight 1 lb.
