# Fuse Grenade

This hollow clay container holds a small charge of black powder and a slow-burning fuse. Lighting the fuse is a move action; 1d3 rounds later the grenade explodes, dealing 2d6 points of bludgeoning damage and 1d6 points of fire damage in a 10-foot-radius burst (+5 vs Reflex; a miss halves). You throw a fuse grenade as if it were a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]]. Crafting this item is a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.
