# Skyrocket Firework

When lit, this foot-long wooden tube begins to shake and emit a handful of white sparks, shedding light as a torch. One round later it takes flight, moving at a speed of 90 feet for 1d6 rounds before loudly exploding in a burst of light and sound, dealing 2d6 points of fire damage in a 10-foot-radius burst (+5 vs Reflex; a miss halves). Anyone who takes damage from the explosion is either [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]] (50% chance of either) for 1 round. Crafting this item is a DC 25 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.
