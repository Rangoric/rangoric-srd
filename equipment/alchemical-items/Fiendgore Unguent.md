# Fiendgore Unguent

When this unguent—prepared with vile alchemical reagents and the gore of fiends—is applied to a wounded [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|tiefling]] or evil outsider (not currently at maximum hit points), it momentarily transforms the essence of the target into something even more fearsome and demonic. While under the effects of a fiendgore unguent, a tiefling or an evil outsider gains a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks and a +1 circumstance bonus to the DC of all spells with the fear descriptor that they cast. Applying the unguent is a delicate process, requiring a full-round action, and can only be properly applied to a willing or helpless creature. If applied to a creature other than a tiefling or an evil outsider, it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickens]] the creature instead. The unguent's effects (either beneficial or harmful) last for 1 minute.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 75 gp; Weight 1 lb.
