# Tangleburn Bag

This sack contains tanglefoot bag materials and alchemical powders that burn at a high temperature. It functions like a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tanglefoot Bag|tanglefoot bag]], plus a direct hit on a creature deals 1d6 points of fire damage, and the fire attacks the creature’s Reflex at +10; on a hit, it catches on fire. If it catches on fire, for the next 2 rounds the flames attack at +15 instead of +5 when it tries to extinguish them, and using water to extinguish the flames creates a burst of burning material equivalent to [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Alchemist's Fire|alchemist's fire]] making a direct hit on the target (including splash damage). After the initial 2 rounds, the flames may be extinguished as normal. Crafting this item is a DC 30 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.
