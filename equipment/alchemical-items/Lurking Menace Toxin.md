# Lurking Menace Toxin

_Ingestible/Toxin; Special_

The lurking menace is a toxin that can be added to any and all potions during creation or added as a powder and mixed into an already made potion. As long as the magic is active (either in the bottle or after ingestion), nothing happens. When the potion’s effects wear off or if _dispel magic_ (cast on the potion or potion-user) succeeds in canceling the magical effects, the menace interacts with the base fluid and becomes a virulent poison. The poison attacks the victim’s Fortitude at once at +4, and on a hit the victim suffers 1d8 points of damage and severe nausea for a full round. After that, the poison attacks the victim’s Fortitude each round over the next 2d4 rounds at +5, and on each hit the victim suffers 1d4+1 points of damage.

Adding the lurking menace to nonmagical ingestibles is useless, as the toxin is generated by an alchemical interaction with the magical energies of the potion. Also, the only potions this cannot be effectively added to are those storing Healing domain spells.

**Poison**: As an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|affliction]]:

- **Type**: poison, ingested
- **Attack**: +5 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: when the potion’s magic ends or is dispelled
- **Frequency**: 1/round for 2d4 rounds
- **Effect**: 1d8 damage and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round/1d4+1 damage

**Construction**: No aura; CL 11th; [[rangoric-srd/feats/Brew Ingestibles|Brew Ingestibles]], [[rangoric-srd/feats/Craft Toxins|Craft Toxins]]; DC: 25; Price: 350 gp/1 oz.
