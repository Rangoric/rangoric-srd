# Reagent Paper

This slip of paper can aid in the identifying of liquids. Its color changes with basic traits such as acidity, salinity, and magic. Expending one sheet confers a +2 alchemical bonus on any [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check or a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] check made to identify potions or other liquids.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 15; Price 1 gp.
