# Snake Oil

This mildly toxic liniment numbs flesh and can soothe injuries. It's really a poison, but an imbiber who resists it recovers from minor injuries.

A poison; how afflictions work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|afflictions]].

- **Type**: poison, contact
- **Attack**: +2 vs Fortitude
- **Onset**: 1 round
- **Frequency**: 1/minute for 5 minutes
- **Effect**: on a hit, 1 point of nonlethal damage; on a miss, the imbiber instead heals 1d6 points of nonlethal damage
- **Cure**: 1 miss
