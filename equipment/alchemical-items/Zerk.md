# Zerk

A drug: a dose gives its effect and its ability damage at once, and risks [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Minor Addiction|addiction]]. How drugs work, and what each line means, is in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Drugs and Addiction|drugs and addiction]].

- **Type**: injury
- **Addiction**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/afflictions/Minor Addiction|minor]], +8 vs Fortitude
- **Price**: 50 gp
- **Effect**: 1 hour; +1 alchemical bonus to initiative. If addicted, the user also gains a +1d4 alchemical bonus to Strength for as long as he is addicted
- **Damage**: 1d2 Con damage
