# Stink Ink, Arcane

Most common among ratfolk alchemists, arcane stink ink is used to inscribe formulae or spells into formula books and spellbooks. Like normal stink ink, the arcane version can only be read by creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]] ability (although _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_ works normally on spells and arcane formulae inscribed with stink ink). Using arcane stink ink to inscribe a spell or formula into a book costs +10% of the normal amount.

Price special; Weight 1 lb.
