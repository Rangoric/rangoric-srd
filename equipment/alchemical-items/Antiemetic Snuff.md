# Antiemetic Snuff

_Remedy_

This snuff can be used to shake off the effects of nausea. If you take it before being exposed to an effect that would give you the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] condition and attacks a defense, the effect rolls twice and takes the worse result. A single dose provides this benefit for 1 hour.

**Construction**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 50 gp.
