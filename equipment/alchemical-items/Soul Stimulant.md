# Soul Stimulant

_Remedy_

This soothing elixir was created to counter the energy-draining effects of vampires, wights, and similar horrible creatures. If you have a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^negative-levels|negative level]] (whether temporary or permanent), you can drink a dose of soul stimulant, negating the negative level's penalty for 12 hours. You can only benefit from 1 dose of soul stimulant at a time, though you can continue to take a dose every 12 hours to stave off the negative level's effects.

**Construction**: [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 30; Price 300 gp.
