# Stillgut

Drinking a vial of this bland, bluish liquid grants you a +5 alchemical bonus on Fortitude Defense to avoid [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nausea]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickness]] for 1 hour. If you are already [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]], you can drink stillgut as a move action. Drinking it in this fashion makes the nausea attack you again (without the +5 bonus), and a miss ends it. Goblins often use stillgut so they can choke down meat or other foods in advanced stages of rot or decay.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 25; Price 50 gp/vial.
