# Fervor Juice

This foul-tasting alchemical concoction grants those that imbibe it [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Ferocity|ferocity]] for a short period of time. This functions as the monster ability of the same name, and lasts for 1 hour from the time you ingest the solution. Crafting this item requires a successful DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) check.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 20; Price 50 gp.
