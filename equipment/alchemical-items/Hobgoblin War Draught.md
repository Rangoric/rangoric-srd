# Hobgoblin War Draught

This drink is a foul-smelling alchemical blend of raw alcohol, mashed grubs, and medicinal mushrooms. A hobgoblin under the effects of hobgoblin war draught ignores all penalties resulting from the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigued]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^shaken|shaken]] conditions for 10 minutes, after which it attacks his Fortitude at +5, and on a hit he is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 hour. Anyone lacking the goblinoid subtype who drinks hobgoblin war draught is attacked against Fortitude at +5 at once, and on a hit is sickened for 1 hour; on a miss, the drinker ignores all penalties resulting from the fatigued and shaken conditions for 1 minute, after which he is sickened for 10 minutes. Hobgoblin war draught has no effect on creatures that are immune to poison.
