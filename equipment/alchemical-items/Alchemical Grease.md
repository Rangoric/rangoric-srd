# Alchemical Grease

Each pot of this slick black goo has sufficient contents to cover one Medium or two Small creatures. If you coat yourself in alchemical grease, you gain a +5 alchemical bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] checks, on combat maneuver checks made to escape a grapple, and to your [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] to avoid being grappled; this lasts 4 hours or until you wash it off.

[[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) DC 15; Price 5 gp; Weight 1 lb.
