# Ghast Retch Flask

Harvested and concentrated from the remains of slain ghasts, this foul-smelling powder is kept in tightly sealed flasks. It is thrown as a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]] and the flask breaks on impact, releasing noxious dust. The target is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 round and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 3 rounds after that. The retch attacks Fortitude at +2 for the nauseated condition, and a miss prevents it, but not the sickened condition. Creatures within 5 feet of where the flask hits are sickened for 1 round. This is a poison effect. Crafting this item is a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) check.
