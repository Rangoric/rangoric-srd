# Universalist’s Manuscript

Blood, dirt, wine, and more stain this white leather book. The owner’s name is stamped into the cover in peeling gold, yet no more information about the author is immediately evident. Several of the pages contain careful diagrams and illustrations done by a competent but uninspiring artist.

**Level**: 10 universalist

**Protection**: Average lock with arcane lock (DC 35) and explosive runes (Reflex DC 16)

**Value**: 2,820 gp
