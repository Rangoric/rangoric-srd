# Bryony

- **Rarity**: Uncommon
- **Environment**: Any marshlands or fresh water coastlands and riverbanks
- **Harvest**: Perennial
- **Cost**: 5 sp/ 9 berries (fresh or as 1 oz. maceration)
- **Game Use**: Component, Poison (attack bonus variable; Nausea/1d3 Str +1d2 Con)

**Poison**: As an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|affliction]]:

- **Type**: poison, ingested
- **Attack**: +7 vs Fortitude (berries; leaves and flowers +6, stalks or root +4)
- **Frequency**: 1/minute for 2 minutes
- **Effect**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 minute/1d3 Str damage and 1d2 Con damage

Bryony is a thick-rooted and fast-growing plant that climbs and clambers over most things in its path or along its rootpath, its brittle stems shooting up from the base with hairy leaves and greenish flowers (that become orange berries in early summer). All parts of this plant are poisonous, some more than others: stalks or root attack at +4, leaves and flowers at +6, and berries at +7, with the effects above.

Special measures are taken only when preserving bryony for spell components, and then it is only to crush together all parts of a ripe vine, then macerate it in oil to be used as components for numerous necromancy and transmutation spells.

An herb of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism|herbalism]]; its lines follow the [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Herbal Template|herbal template]], and its price changes with distance and season ([[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Variable Costs|Variable Costs]]).
