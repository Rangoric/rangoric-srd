# Caranator

- **Rarity**: Rare
- **Environment**: Any mountain forests
- **Harvest**: Biennial
- **Cost**: 12 gp/ 1 oz. of root, dried, 20 gp for whole root, fresh
- **Game Use**: Component (Item/Spells), Special (chew a thumb-sized piece of root to gain a Will save vs. *any* charms or enchantments immediately)

An ugly weed by all accounts, caranator is a tough rhizome that crawls and clambers at great speeds, its dark green heart-shaped leaves with black ribs and undersides hardly brightening the plant. While it can spread over a 5-foot-wide area, caranator’s only useful part is its tuberous central root—caranator root, when chewed, helps clear the mind and allows many enchantments to be fought and possibly overcome.

The root, if cut and dried and powdered, is more easily distributed (by dissolving it into wine or water). It also becomes highly useful as a component for illusions and enchantments.

An herb of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism|herbalism]]; its lines follow the [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Herbal Template|herbal template]], and its price changes with distance and season ([[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Variable Costs|Variable Costs]]).
