# Herbs

The herbal catalog of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism|herbalism]]: 59 herbs useful to alchemists, herbalists, natural healers and apothecaries. What each line means is in the [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Herbal Template|herbal template]]; prices change with distance and season ([[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Variable Costs|Variable Costs]]).

**WARNING:** Despite the many uses listed here, don’t try these herbs for real: their game uses are made up for play, not tested for safety.
