# Wormwood

- **Rarity**: Common
- **Environment**: Any temperate to warm hills and plains
- **Harvest**: Perennial
- **Cost**: 5 cp/ 1 oz. of dried leaves and stems
- **Game Use**: Herb/Food, Medicine (Infusions used as digestive agents and to help expel internal parasites), Poison (DC 11, Nausea/1d3 nonlethal)

**Poison**: As an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#The Affliction Block|affliction]]:

- **Type**: poison, ingested
- **Save**: Fortitude DC 11
- **Frequency**: 1/minute for 2 minutes
- **Effect**: [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nauseated]] for 1 minute/1d3 nonlethal damage

Wormwood is an ugly perennial with a tough, dyspeptic green stalk that grows three feet high and emits regular pale green three-bladed leaves and pale olive flowers come summer. Twigs, leaves, and flowers boiled in water create a common curative for digestive ills, though taken and chewed undiluted or fresh the plant is poisonous. Alchemists and brewers also boil and ferment (and sometimes further distill) wormwood to create and enhance stronger spiced wines.

An herb of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism|herbalism]]; its lines follow the [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Herbal Template|herbal template]], and its price changes with distance and season ([[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Variable Costs|Variable Costs]]).
