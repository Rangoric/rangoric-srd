# Balm of Gilead

- **Rarity**: Rare
- **Environment**: Warm plains and hills
- **Harvest**: Biennial
- **Cost**: 4 gp/oz. of dried bark/root or per bud/seed or per 1 oz. resin
- **Game Use**: Component (Item, Spell)

This aromatic evergreen buds in late spring and has no common uses. Its buds, resin, bark, and roots are useful to alchemists and spellcasters alike, the latter for assation and sublimation into various creations. The resin is a base for ingestibles and unguents, and the buds are potent components for charm and enchantment spells.

An herb of [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism|herbalism]]; its lines follow the [[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Herbal Template|herbal template]], and its price changes with distance and season ([[rangoric-srd/rules/alchemy-and-herbalism/Herbalism#Variable Costs|Variable Costs]]).
