# Performance

Performance is a weapon quality that grants bonuses when using the weapon in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat|performance combat]]. Performance weapons tend to be the preferred weapons of warriors who fight in the arena or some other forum where showmanship is just as important as scoring a debilitating blow or deadly hit, and these weapons are often well known to the spectators of such events.

When wielding a weapon with the performance quality, if an attack or combat maneuver made with this weapon prompts a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Performance Combat#^performance-combat-check|performance combat check]], you gain a +2 bonus on that check.

A weapon with this quality names it in its special features. A creature with the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Performance Weapon Mastery|Performance Weapon Mastery]] feat treats every weapon it is proficient with as having the performance quality, and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Master Combat Performer|Master Combat Performer]] feat grants proficiency with all weapons that have it.
