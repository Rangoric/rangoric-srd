# Alchemical Fire

_[[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Special Siege Engine Ammunition|Siege engine ammunition]]._ Price 200 gp (one use); Weight 10 lb. ^stats

This hard, ceramic canister of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Alchemist's Fire|alchemist's fire]] can be used as ammunition in catapults and trebuchets. When it hits its target square, it deals 4d6 points of fire damage to each creature and wooden structure within 5 feet of the target space, and the fire attacks each creature’s Reflex at +10; on a hit, it [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|catches on fire]] (wooden objects automatically catch on fire). The fire attacks the Reflex of every creature and wooden object within the area between 5 and 30 feet of the target space at +10, and those it hits take half the fire damage, but they do not catch on fire. On a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Mishaps and Misfires|siege engine mishap]], this ammunition explodes before it is launched, dealing its damage to the siege engine and all nearby creatures and wooden objects as if one of the spaces of the siege engine (crew leader's choice) were the target square. This alchemical fire ignores the hardness of wooden objects.
