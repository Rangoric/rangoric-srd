# Blast Shot

_[[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Special Siege Engine Ammunition|Siege engine ammunition]]._ Price 30 gp (one use); Weight 25 lb. ^stats

Instead of a single hard ball, this ammunition is a bundle of large pellets, balls, or pieces of scrap metal, propelled a short distance by black powder and attacking all creatures and objects within an area. Both [[rangoric-srd/equipment/siege-engines/Cannon (Siege Engine)|cannons]] and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/siege-engines/Fiend's Mouth Cannon|fiend's mouth cannons]] can fire this kind of ammunition. When such a siege engine fires this ammunition, it hits every creature and object within a 30-foot-cone burst. The siege engine makes attack rolls against each creature and unattended object in the burst. It must miss every creature or target to misfire, and a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Mishaps and Misfires|misfire]] generates the normal effect. It deals its normal damage on a hit, but does not ignore the hardness of objects.
