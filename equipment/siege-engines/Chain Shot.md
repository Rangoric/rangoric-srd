# Chain Shot

*[[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Special Siege Engine Ammunition|Siege engine ammunition]].* Price 50 gp (one use); Weight 30 lb. ^stats

This siege engine firearm ammunition can be loaded in a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/siege-engines/Cannon (Siege Engine)|cannon]] or a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/siege-engines/Fiend's Mouth Cannon|fiend's mouth cannon]]. It is especially good at tearing through [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Vehicles/Propulsion Devices|sails and dirigibles]], dealing double its normal damage to those forms of propulsion. When fired at a creature, on a hit, the creature must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude saving throw or be knocked [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]].
