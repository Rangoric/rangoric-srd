# Liquid Ice (Siege Ammunition)

_[[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Special Siege Engine Ammunition|Siege engine ammunition]]._ Price 400 gp (one use); Weight 20 lb. ^stats

This hard, ceramic canister filled with alchemical liquid ice can be used as ammunition in catapults and trebuchets. When it hits its target square, it deals 4d6 points of cold damage to each creature within 5 feet of the targeting space, and the ice attacks each creature’s Fortitude at +10; on a hit, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] for 1 round. The ice attacks the Fortitude of every creature within the area between 5 and 30 feet of the target space at +10, and those it hits take half damage. On a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Mishaps and Misfires|siege engine mishap]], this ammunition explodes before it is launched, dealing its damage to all nearby creatures as if one of the spaces of the siege engine (crew leader's choice) were the target square.

This is the siege ammunition, not the alchemical [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Liquid Ice|*liquid ice*]] (40 gp per flask), a different thing with the same name.
