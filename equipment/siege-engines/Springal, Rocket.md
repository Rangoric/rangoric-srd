# Springal, Rocket

_Huge [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Indirect-Fire Ranged Siege Engines|indirect-fire siege engine]], a siege [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Firearms|firearm]]._ Price 6,000 gp; Dmg 3d10; Critical ×4; Range 100 ft. (50 ft. min.); Type Fire; Crew 3; Aim 2; Load 3; Speed 0 ft.; Misfire 1 (20 ft.); Ammunition 500 gp, 30 lb. per use. ^stats

A springal uses a torsion-cranked composite paddle to strike a firing rack containing multiple arrows or black-powder-propelled rockets, which rain down in an arc over a burst area. Springals are indirect-fire weapons that affect an area centered on the target square.

This highly volatile springal propels a group of black-powder rockets that rain down on the targeted square and in a 15-foot burst around the area. If the rocket springal [[rangoric-srd/rules/Siege Engines#Mishaps and Misfires|misfires]], it explodes, dealing its damage in a 20-foot blast around the springal. This explosion destroys the rocket springal. One use of rocket springal ammunition costs 500 gp and weighs 30 pounds.
