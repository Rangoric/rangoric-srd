# Aura Goggles

These leather goggles have dark, purple-hued lenses coated with an alchemical substance that reveals psychic spectrums. To give any benefit, the goggles must be worn continuously for 1 hour, during which the wearer takes a –2 penalty on sight-based Perception checks. After that hour, the wearer can remove the goggles to gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Perception checks for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception#Read Aura|read aura]] occult skill unlock. This benefit fades after 1 hour.

Price 15 gp; Weight 1 lb.
