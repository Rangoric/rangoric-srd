# Portable Sweat Lodge

This large leather wrap holds a folding wooden framework on stilts, pliable slats of birch bark, more leather rolls and consecrated stones. Assembled, it makes a small domed hut, usable as a tent, that fits two crouching Medium or four Small humanoids. When the stones are heated and water poured over them to make a thick steam, the hut becomes a sweat lodge for healing ceremonies, and gives a +2 circumstance bonus on Medicine checks for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine#Faith Healing|faith healing]] occult skill unlock.

Price 25 gp; Weight 25 lb.
