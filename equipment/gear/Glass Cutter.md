# Glass Cutter

This small metal rod has a tiny sharp wheel on one end and a bulb on the other. You score the glass with the wheel and tap it with the bulb to break it. If you succeed at a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] check, you can break the glass very quietly (DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check to hear). Most thieves use [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Glue Paper|glue paper]] with a glass cutter to reduce noise and capture glass fragments.
