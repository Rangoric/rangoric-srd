# Balancing Pole

These flexible poles range from 15–30 feet in length and, when used properly, help you keep your balance when crossing narrow walkways. Using a balancing pole grants you a +1 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks made to traverse a narrow surface.

Price 8 sp; Weight 12 lb.
