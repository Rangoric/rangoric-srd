# Parrot (Animal)

These colorful and intelligent birds can be trained to mimic humanoid voices. They are often kept as pets by the wealthy and those who live in the tropical climates they favor. Parrots have the same statistics as [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Raven|ravens]].

Price 50 gp; Weight 4 lb.
