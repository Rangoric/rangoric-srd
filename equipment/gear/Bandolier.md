# Bandolier

This leather belt is worn over one shoulder and runs diagonally across the chest and back. It has small loops or pouches for holding eight objects the size of a flask or small dagger. You can use the "[[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Manipulate an Item|retrieve a stored item]]" action to take an item from a bandolier. You can wear up to two bandoliers at the same time (any more than this and they get in each other's way and restrict your movement).

Price 5 sp; Weight —.
