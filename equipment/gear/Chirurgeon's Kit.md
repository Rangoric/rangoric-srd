# Chirurgeon's Kit

This sturdy leather case straps to the waist or thigh, or can be slung over a shoulder. It consists of a leather pouch, a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Healer's Kit|healer's kit]], a bottle of strong brandy, a _potion of [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Light Wounds|cure light wounds]]_, a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Vial|vial]] of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Smelling Salts|smelling salts]], and 2 doses each of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Alchemist's Kindness|alchemist's kindness]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Antiplague|antiplague]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Antitoxin|antitoxin]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Bloodblock|bloodblock]], and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Soothe Syrup|soothe syrup]].

Price 400 gp; Weight 4 lb.
