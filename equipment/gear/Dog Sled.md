# Dog Sled

This [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Sled|sled]] is several feet long and is designed to be pulled over snow and ice by a team of trained [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Riding Dog (Mount)|riding dogs]]. Most sleds have runners at the back for a musher to stand on. A dog sled can carry up to the carrying capacity of all the dogs that pull it.

Price 20 gp; Weight 300 lb.
