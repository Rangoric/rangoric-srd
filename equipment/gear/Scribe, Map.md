# Scribe, Map

A scribe is a literate person with clear handwriting who can take dictation or transcribe text documents. You must provide [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Ink|ink]] and writing materials, though the scribe can acquire these for you at half price. Scribes cannot copy magical writing. A scribe who can accurately copy a map is rare and demands a higher wage. The listed price is per day.

Price 10 gp per day.
