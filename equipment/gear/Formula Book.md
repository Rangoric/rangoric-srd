# Formula Book

A formula book has 100 pages of parchment, and each alchemical extract formula takes up one page per extract level. A newly purchased formula book contains no extract formulas. An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]] character begins play with a formula book containing the extract formulae he knows. This is the alchemist equivalent of a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Spellbook, Wizard's|spellbook]] for wizards (a spellbook and a formula book are essentially the same item).

Price 15 gp; Weight 3 lb.

Four sample formula books are in [[rangoric-srd/equipment/spellbooks/Sample Spellbooks|Sample Spellbooks]].
