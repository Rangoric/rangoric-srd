# Hypnotist's Locket, Pocket Watch

This small, elliptical metal case holds a small portrait or keepsake, and can be attached to a belt or worn as a pendant. When hanging from a metal chain, a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Hypnotist's Locket|hypnotist's locket]] gives a +2 circumstance bonus on Persuasion checks for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion#Hypnotism|hypnotism]] occult skill unlock.

This kind is a pocket watch. It's worn the same way, still has a space for a portrait or keepsake and gives the same bonus for hypnotism, but it also works as a timepiece.

Price 250 gp; Weight 1 lb.
