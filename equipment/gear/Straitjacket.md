# Straitjacket

Made of durable canvas and leather straps, this restraint keeps its wearer from using his arms at all. Binding a creature in a straitjacket takes 1 minute, and the creature must be willing or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^helpless|helpless]]; it can be done in 5 rounds, but then the DC to escape is 5 lower. A creature in a straitjacket is [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Grappled (Condition)|grappled]], except that it can move. Escaping takes a DC 28 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] check (DC 30 for a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Straitjacket, Masterwork|masterwork straitjacket]]).

Price 5 gp; Weight 1 lb.
