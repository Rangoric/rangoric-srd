# Campsite Kit

This kit is actually four bundles of gear, designed so four individuals can share the load. It consists of four [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Bedroll|bedrolls]], four [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Blanket, Winter|blankets]], a day's worth of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Firewood|firewood]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Flint and Steel|flint and steel]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tindertwig|tindertwig]], four [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Mess Kit|mess kits]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Cooking Kit|cooking kit]], and 8 days of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rations, Trail|trail rations]] (with the expectation that adventurers will supplement the rations with a little hunting as they travel). Adventurers expecting inclement weather should also purchase one or more [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tent|tents]].

Price 12 gp; Weight 80 lb.
