# Bedroll

This consists of two woolen sheets sewn together along the bottom and one side to create a bag for sleeping in. Some have cloth straps along the open side so the bedroll can be tied closed while you are sleeping. It can be rolled and tied into a tight coil for storage or transport. Most people use a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Blanket, Winter|blanket]] with the bedroll to stay warm or provide a ground cushion.

Price 1 sp; Weight 5 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
