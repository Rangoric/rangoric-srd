# Air Bladder

Used by swimmers for longer dives, this bladder is similar to a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Waterskin|waterskin]], but is relatively airtight. If inflated, it holds enough air to sustain a Medium creature for 1 round or a Small creature for 2 rounds.

Price 1 sp; Weight 1/2 lb.
