# Training Harness

This specialized gear must be tailored to a specific type of animal. A training harness provides a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks made with an animal wearing it.

Price 10 gp; Weight 10 lb.
