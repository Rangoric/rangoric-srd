# Automatic Writing Planchette

This heart-shaped wooden board has two small wheels and an opening that holds a pencil as its third leg. Used on a tabletop covered in paper, it scribbles out messages for a creature using the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics#Automatic Writing|automatic writing]] occult skill unlock, and gives a +2 circumstance bonus on the Linguistics check. When used to channel a haunt capable of communication, the planchette doubles the rate of communication with the haunt and gives a +4 circumstance bonus on Linguistics checks to decipher its cryptic messages. These benefits don't stack with those of other items that give similar benefits (such as a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Talking Board|talking board]]).
