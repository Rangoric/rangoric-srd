# Ventriloquist's Dummy

This figure of finely sculpted and painted wood, about 2 feet tall, is shaped and dressed like a typical humanoid. An opening in its back lets a Small or Medium creature work it with one arm. Used as part of a routine, a ventriloquist's dummy gives a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (comedy) checks.

Price 100 gp; Weight 7 lb.
