# Juggler's Kit

This kit includes multiple sets of well-balanced objects, such as balls, clubs, knives, rings, and torches. It provides a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (act or comedy) checks and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks involving juggling.

Price 15 gp; Weight 10 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
