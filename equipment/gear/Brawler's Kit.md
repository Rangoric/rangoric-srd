A starting kit for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Brawler|brawler]]. It includes a backpack, a bedroll, a belt pouch, a flint and steel, rope, torches (10), trail rations (5 days), and a waterskin.

Price 9 gp; Weight 26 lb.
