# Loaded Dice, Superior

Most loaded dice are weighted—a heavier substance is included opposite the desired number. You can spot marks from tampering with a die in this fashion with a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check. Higher-quality dice (such as wooden dice carved around a naturally heavy inclusion) have DCs ranging from 20 to 30.
