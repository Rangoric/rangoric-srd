# Mesmerist's Kit

A starting kit for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Mesmerist|mesmerist]]. It includes a backpack, a bedroll, a belt pouch, an entertainer’s outfit, flint and steel, a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Hypnotist's Locket|hypnotist’s locket]], ink (1 ounce), an inkpen, an iron pot, stationery, torches (10), trail rations (5 days), and a waterskin.

Price 35 gp; Weight 26-1/2 lb.
