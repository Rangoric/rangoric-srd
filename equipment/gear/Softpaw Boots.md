# Softpaw Boots

These soft and subtle boots are constructed of silk and specially cured leather. They are specially designed for [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|catfolk]] feet. They work with the feline structure of the race's feet to soften footfalls and to reduce the imprints of their tracks. While wearing softpaw boots, a catfolk gains a +1 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. Furthermore, the DC to notice or follow the tracks of a catfolk wearing softpaw boots increases by +2. Magical softpaw boots exist as [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Daredevil Softpaws|daredevil softpaws]].

Price 25 gp; Weight 1 lb.
