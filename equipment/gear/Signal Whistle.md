# Signal Whistle

With a DC 5 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (wind instruments) check, you can use a whistle to signal the same sorts of situations as [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Signal Horn|signal horns]]. A whistle's piercing report can be clearly heard ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] DC 0) up to a quarter-mile away. For each quarter-mile beyond, Perception checks to hear a whistle take a –2 penalty. [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Silent Whistle|Silent whistles]] that only animals and other creatures with keen hearing can hear also exist.
