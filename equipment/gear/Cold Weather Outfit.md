# Cold Weather Outfit

A cold weather outfit includes a wool coat, linen shirt, wool cap, heavy cloak, thick pants or skirt, and boots. This outfit grants a +5 circumstance bonus on Fortitude Defense against exposure to cold weather.

Price 8 gp; Weight 7 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
