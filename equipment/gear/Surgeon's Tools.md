# Surgeon's Tools

When used in conjunction with a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Healer's Kit|healer's kit]], surgeon's tools raise the kit's bonus to a +3 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] checks to [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Wound|treat wounds]] or [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Deadly Wounds|deadly wounds]].

Price 20 gp; Weight 5 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
