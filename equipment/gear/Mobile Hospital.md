# Mobile Hospital

This kit for a wagon provides all the equipment needed to care for up to 10 sick or injured people at a time. It includes two large [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tent|tents]], 10 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Cot|cots]] with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Bedroll|bedrolls]], a sturdy table, a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Chirurgeon's Kit|chirurgeon's kit]], and five [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Healer's Kit|healer's kits]]. It grants anyone using it a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] checks to provide [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|first aid]], can be used to [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Deadly Wounds|treat deadly wounds]] with a single use of a healer's kit instead of two, and doubles the rate at which patients recover in long-term care.

Price 1,000 gp; Weight 500 lb.
