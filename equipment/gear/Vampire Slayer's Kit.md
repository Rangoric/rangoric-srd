# Vampire Slayer's Kit

This small, latched wooden case contains numerous tools and devices used in hunting and slaying vampires. It contains 10 cloves of garlic, four masterwork [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Wooden Stake|wooden stakes]], a hammer, a silvered mirror, 4 vials of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Holy Water|holy water]], 1 application of _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Silversheen|silversheen]]_, and a masterwork holy symbol. It also contains numerous reagents and powders used for treating wounds and making wards, salves, and other concoctions. In addition to its general usefulness, when displayed, a vampire slayer's kit grants its owner a +2 bonus on all Charisma-based skill checks made to investigate or seek out vampires. The [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Slayer's Kit|slayer's kit]] and the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Undead Slayer's Kit|undead slayer's kit]] are similar kits.

Price 500 gp; Weight 8 lb.
