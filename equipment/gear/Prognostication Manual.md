# Prognostication Manual

This small but thick booklet covers one form of folk divination, such as cartomancy, cheiromancy, crystallomancy or horoscopy. Using it while reading a creature's fortune cuts the time needed by 2 minutes, and it gives a +2 circumstance bonus on Sense Motive checks for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive#Prognostication|prognostication]] occult skill unlock. If the method needs a particular item, such as a deck of cards for cartomancy or animal bones for osteomancy, that item gives a +2 circumstance bonus that doesn't stack with the manual's. A prognostication manual is most useful for methods that need no special equipment, such as palm reading and horoscopy.

Price 30 gp; Weight 3 lb.
