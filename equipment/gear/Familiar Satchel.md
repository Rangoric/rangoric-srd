# Familiar Satchel

This armored case provides [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Modifiers#Cover|total cover]] to any Tiny or smaller creature contained within it. It includes air holes (which can be plugged with cork stoppers if you need to go underwater) and two receptacles for food and water.

Price 25 gp; Weight 6 lb.
