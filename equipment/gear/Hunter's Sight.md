# Hunter's Sight

This complicated lens fits over one eye and takes up the [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#Magic Items on the Body|eyes slot]] when in use. When using it in conjunction with a ranged weapon, you reduce any range penalty on your attacks by 2. Objects within 100 feet become difficult to see, however, and you take a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks based on sight while wearing the hunter's sight.

Price 100 gp; Weight —.
