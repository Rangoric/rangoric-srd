# Traveling Bee Hive

These straw baskets provide a portable home for bees. They are dome shaped, with a hole in the top that a smaller woven basket covers as a cap. This hole allows for the harvesting of small amounts of honey without destroying the entire hive. Believing that certain bees provide for a superior crop yield, some farmers pay beekeepers to travel to their farms with the bees.

Destroying a traveling bee hive causes the bees to swarm, creating a 5-foot-radius cloud. A creature is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] for as long as it remains in the cloud, and the bees attack its Fortitude at +2; on a hit, it becomes [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] for 1 minute. The sickened condition is a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|poison]] effect.

Price 10 gp; Weight 10 lb.
