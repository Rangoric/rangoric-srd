# Alchemy Crafting Kit

An [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist|alchemist]] with an alchemy crafting kit is assumed to have all the material components needed for his extracts, mutagens, and bombs, except for those components that have a specific cost. An alchemy crafting kit provides no bonuses on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) checks.

Price 25 gp; Weight 5 lb.
