# Bath, Cold

A cold bath usually involves someone else's used soapy bath water. A hot bath is a one-person washtub filled with hot water one pot at a time. A public bath is a bath facility (such as a bath house or resort) that provides hot water, [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Soap|soap]], and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Perfume or Cologne|cologne or perfume]], and may also offer wading pools, massages, or other services for an additional cost. Some public baths require membership or a minimum social status.

Price 2 cp.
