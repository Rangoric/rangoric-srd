# Wire

This 6-foot length of copper wire is strong and flexible. By attaching it to two [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Piton|pitons]] or iron spikes across an area, you can create a simple tripwire trap.

Price 5 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
