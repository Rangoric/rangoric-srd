# Sleigh

This [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Wagon|wagon]] has runners, making it an ideal conveyance for snow and ice travel. In general, two horses (or other beasts of burden) are needed to draw it. A sled comes with the harness required to pull it.

See also the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Sled|sled]].

Price 100 gp; Weight —; Passage 2 cp per mile.
