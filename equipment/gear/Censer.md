# Censer

This metal container has a small removable lid and holds a small crucible in which to burn [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Incense|incense]]. A censer used in ceremonies or rituals can hold up to 10 pieces of incense, and burns 1 piece per hour when lit.

Price 5 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
