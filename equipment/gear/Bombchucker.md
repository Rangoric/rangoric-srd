# Bombchucker

This curved wicker basket is worn like a glove over one hand. The basket extends approximately 2 feet in a steady curve, allowing you to launch a thrown [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Throw Splash Weapon|splash weapon]] at incredible speeds and over long distances. Using a bombchucker increases the range increment of any thrown splash weapon by 10 feet. Loading a bombchucker requires a free hand, as the hand wielding the bombchucker can't be used for any other purpose. A bombchucker doesn't get in the way of preparing and throwing a bomb created with the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Alchemist#^alchemist-bomb|bomb]] class feature.

Price 12 gp; Weight 1 lb.
