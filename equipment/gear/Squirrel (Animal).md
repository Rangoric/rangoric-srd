# Squirrel (Animal)

These tiny rodents are as common in the wild as rats are in cities. They typically eat nuts, seeds, and vegetation. Squirrels have the same statistics as [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Rat|rats]].

Price 1 gp; Weight 2 lb.
