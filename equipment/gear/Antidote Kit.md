# Antidote Kit

This small box contains a wide variety of remedies and treatments against not only specific poisons but also their most common methods of delivery, including such things as purgatives to eliminate ingested toxins. It grants a +3 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] checks made to [[rangoric-srd/actions/Treat Poison|treat poison]]. An antidote kit is exhausted after 10 uses.

Price 100 gp; Weight 3 lb.
