# Stove Can

This little metal container holds a waxy fuel that creates enough heat to cook with but only as much light as a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Candle|candle]]. To snuff the flame, close the metal lid on the container; it cools enough that it can be carried in a few minutes. Each lasts 5 hours (enough to cook about 10 meals).

Price 10 gp; Weight 1 lb.
