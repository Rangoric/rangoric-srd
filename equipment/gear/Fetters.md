# Fetters

Fetters are [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Manacles|manacles]] fitted around the ankles rather than the wrists, and use the same rules for breaking, escape, and cost relative to size. A creature in fetters is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] and can only move at half speed. In addition, a fettered creature must succeed at a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check to move more than its (reduced) speed in a round. If it fails the check by 5 or more, the creature falls [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]].

Price 15 gp; Weight 2 lb.
