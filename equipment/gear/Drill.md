# Drill

A drill can create a 1-inch-diameter hole in stone, wood, or metal as a standard action. Harder materials wear down or break the drill more quickly. Hearing the sound of drilling requires a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check.

Price 5 sp; Weight 1 lb.
