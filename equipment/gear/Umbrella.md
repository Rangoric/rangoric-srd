# Umbrella

An umbrella is a heavier, waterproof [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Parasol|parasol]] made of oiled or waxed cloth. It is intended to keep you dry in the rain or snow, but can still protect against sunlight just like a standard parasol.

Price 2 gp; Weight 3 lb.
