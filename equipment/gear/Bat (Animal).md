# Bat (Animal)

These Diminutive rodents are nocturnal fliers that eat insects and fruit. They rely primarily on sound to navigate rather than sight. They are absent only in desert and arctic regions. Some cultures keep bat houses near towns and villages to control insect problems.

The animal is described in [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bat|Bat]].

Price 5 gp; Weight 1 lb.
