# Sugar Glass Bottle

This bottle looks like it's made of glass, but is more fragile and doesn't cause damage when it strikes a creature or object. Highly prized by theater performers, sugar glass bottles grant a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks involving the creation of seemingly realistic fights.
