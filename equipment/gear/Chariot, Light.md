# Chariot, Light

This two-wheeled vehicle is drawn by a horse. These chariots are often used for racing or as the platform for a single archer charging across the battlefield at high speed.

See also the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Chariot, Medium|medium]] and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Chariot, Heavy|heavy]] chariots.
