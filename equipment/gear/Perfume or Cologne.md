# Perfume or Cologne

Most perfumes and colognes are derived from the essential oils of flowers. Perfume or cologne may (at the GM's discretion) provide a +1 circumstance bonus on several [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Charisma-based|Charisma-based skill]] checks.
