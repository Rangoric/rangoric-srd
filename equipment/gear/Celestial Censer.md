# Celestial Censer

This blessed thurible holds up to 10 pieces of incense, and burns at a rate of 1 stick per hour. If a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Smokestick|smokestick]] is added to the incense in the censer while it burns, creatures with the evil subtype are [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for as long as they remain within the area of the resulting smoke. The ordinary [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Censer|censer]] is a similar item.

Price 50 gp; Weight 3 lb.
