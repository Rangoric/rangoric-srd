# Cave Whistle

Fashioned from the spongy stem of a rare mushroom, this whistle produces a sound that only [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobolds]] and dragons can hear. Kobolds often create complicated codes made up of notes that they use to coordinate ambushes and raids.
