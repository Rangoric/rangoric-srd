# Portable Bridge

When staked down with eight [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Piton|pitons]] (four on each side of the gap), this rope and slat bridge can support up to 1,500 pounds and span a 30-foot gap. It rolls up into a parcel that can be carried slung over the back or by a pack animal. Convincing an animal to cross a portable bridge requires the come or heel [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling#Handling Animals|trick]].

Price 200 gp; Weight 60 lb.
