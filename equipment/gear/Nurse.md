# Nurse

A nurse is a person familiar with healing and medicine. A typical nurse is a 1st-level [[rangoric-srd/classes/Expert|expert]] with 1 rank in [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] and the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Medicine) feat. An experienced nurse is 3rd or 4th level, has additional ranks in Medicine, and is paid two to three times as much; however, these nurses are in high demand and are normally employed full-time by wealthy merchants or nobles to look after children and elderly family members. A nurse generally knows a little bit about what local magical healing can do and where to find it. The listed price is per day.

Price 7 sp per day.
