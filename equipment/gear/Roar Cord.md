# Roar Cord

This thin length of rope has many oddly shaped bits of hollow metal fixed along its length. As a standard action, a creature can swing a roar cord over its head to generate a variety of eerie noises. For the next round, any creature within 60 feet of the roar cord takes a –2 penalty on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks that rely on sound and a –1 penalty on defenses against fear effects. The roar cord can be used as a bardic instrument (string instrument) that grants the bard a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks when using the countersong bardic performance.

Price 15 gp; Weight 1 lb.
