# Yak (Animal)

These shaggy-haired relatives to cows are more at home in mountainous terrain where they are frequently used as pack animals and to pull plows. Yaks have the same statistics as [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Bison|bison]].

Price 24 gp; Weight 1,000 lb.
