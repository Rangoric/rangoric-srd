# Iron Spike

This foot-long iron spike is used to keep doors open or closed and to secure ropes for climbing. Hearing a spike being hammered in requires a DC 5 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check.

Price 5 cp; Weight 1 lb.
