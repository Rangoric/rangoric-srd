# Cork Vest

This fabric vest contains pockets full of cork, providing the wearer additional buoyancy. Originally worn by fishers and sailors, it protects against drowning. While wearing a cork vest, you take a –2 penalty on Dexterity checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim, but instead of going underwater after failing the check by 5 or more, you go underwater only if you fail by 10 or more. Additionally, you gain a +4 bonus on Athletics checks to avoid damage from fatigue while swimming. The cork vest can be worn under armor.

Price 25 gp; Weight 1 lb.
