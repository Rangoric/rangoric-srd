# Artist's Poster

This illustrated sheet of paper, typically 24 inches wide and 32 inches long, is an advertisement easily seen by crowds. Placing a poster within a settlement grants creatures a +1 circumstance bonus on Persuasion checks to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion#Gathering Information|gather information]] about the poster's subject. In a settlement of more than 1,000 people, one more poster must be placed for every additional 1,000 people (or portion thereof) to gain the bonus. The price includes commissioning an artist of suitable talent to make the poster; copies beyond the first cost 5 sp each.
