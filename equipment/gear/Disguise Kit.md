# Disguise Kit

The kit is the perfect tool for disguise and provides a +2 circumstance bonus on Imitation checks. A disguise kit is exhausted after ten uses.

Price 50 gp; Weight 8 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
