# Laundry, Standard

The listed price is for up to four items (such as trousers, an undergarment, shirt, and jacket or vest). Additional items may be washed for 3 cp each. Laundry is finished the next day unless you bring it to the washer early. Magical laundry service is a mage (typically an apprentice wizard) magically and instantly cleaning your clothes with _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]_.

Price 1 sp.
