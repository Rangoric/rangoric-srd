# Book of Puzzles

Each book contains 10 puzzles made to test the mind and stir the intellect. Solving a single puzzle requires at least an hour and a successful DC 10 Intelligence check, though every 5 points by which you exceed the check reduces the amount of time you need to solve the puzzle by 10 minutes (to a minimum of 10 minutes to solve an individual puzzle). Once you solve a puzzle, for the next 24 hours you can choose to roll twice on a single [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]], or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] skill check and take the higher result. Once all the puzzles are solved, the book is useless, though you can purchase another puzzle book with different puzzles.

Price 50 gp; Weight 1 lb.
