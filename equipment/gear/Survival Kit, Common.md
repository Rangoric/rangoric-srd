# Survival Kit, Common

A survival kit provides the necessary tools for day-to-day existence in a temperate wilderness. It typically contains a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Flint and Steel|flint and steel]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Mess Kit|mess kit]], two [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Waterskin|waterskins]] (enough to hold water for 1 person for 1 day), very basic maps showing major landmarks, and a small utility knife.

Price 5 gp; Weight 4 lb.
