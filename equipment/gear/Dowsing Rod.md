# Dowsing Rod

A dowsing rod is used by the psychically attuned to locate water, graves or minerals. Most are forked branches cut from hazel, peach or willow trees; some are bent, L-shaped rods of cold iron. A dowser can use any forked twig for dowsing, but these specially prepared rods give a +2 circumstance bonus on Fieldcraft checks for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft#Dowsing|dowsing]] occult skill unlock.

Price 10 gp; Weight 1 lb.
