# Veterinarian's Kit

This item is the equivalent of a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Healer's Kit|healer's kit]], except its materials are especially suitable for treating common animals (cats, dogs, goats, oxen, sheep, and so on). It provides a +3 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/actions/First Aid|First Aid]] checks to treat animals, but only a +1 circumstance bonus on First Aid checks on other creatures. A veterinarian's kit is exhausted after 10 uses.

Price 25 gp; Weight 1 lb.
