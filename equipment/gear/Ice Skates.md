# Ice Skates

Each of these calf-high boots bears a horizontal blade on the bottom, allowing you to travel swiftly on ice. Wearing ice skates allows you to move on ice at normal speed with a successful DC 5 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check (including running and charging), but your movement is reduced to half speed on all other terrain. Donning or removing an ice skate is a full-round action. Skating with only one is possible, but the DC of the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check rises to 15.

Price 1 gp; Weight 2 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
