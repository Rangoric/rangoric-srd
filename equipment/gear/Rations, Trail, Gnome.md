# Rations, Trail, Gnome

Almost any preserved food can be found in gnome trail rations, which are designed to keep a wandering gnome from needing to dine on the same meal twice in a week. If you are a gnome who subsists on nothing but these rations for at least 1 week, you are treated as being 1 Hit Die higher for the purposes of spells and supernatural abilities that have variable effects based on Hit Dice, such as _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Color Spray|color spray]]_ and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Sleep|sleep]]_. This does not actually improve your caster level, character level, or Hit Dice in any other way. This benefit lasts until you eat a meal other than the rations or go for a full day without eating a day's worth.

Ordinary [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rations, Trail|trail rations]] are a different item.

Price 2 gp per day; Weight 1 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
