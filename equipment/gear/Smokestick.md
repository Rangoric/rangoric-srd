# Smokestick

This alchemically treated wooden stick instantly creates thick, opaque smoke when ignited. The smoke fills a 10-foot cube (treat the effect as a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Fog Cloud|fog cloud]]_ spell, except that a moderate or stronger wind dissipates the smoke in 1 round). The stick is consumed after 1 round, and the smoke dissipates naturally.

It can be made by a character with the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) skill.

Price 20 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
