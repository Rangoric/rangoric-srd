# Hanging Tent

A hanging tent is a small tent (able to comfortably house a single Medium creature) with a teardrop shape, which hangs from a sturdy anchor rope. The rope can be tied to a tree limb, grappling hook, or even a piton hammered into a cliff side. Hanging tents are used primarily in mountainous terrain (where no flat surface may be available for camping) and swamps (where no dry ground may be available). Climbing in or out of a hanging tent is a full-round action that requires a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check. Failure indicates another attempt must be made, while failure by 5 or more indicates the camper has fallen from the tent.

Price 20 gp; Weight 15 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
