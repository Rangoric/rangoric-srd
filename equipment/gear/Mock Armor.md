# Mock Armor

When an arcane caster needs a disguise, she might don a suit of mock armor. Made from leather or thin wood such as balsa or bamboo and painted to look like metal, a suit of mock armor passes as full plate under all but the closest scrutiny. A character inspecting someone in mock armor must succeed at a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check to identify it as fake. Mock armor gives you no Armor Class bonus, doesn't affect your maximum Dexterity bonus, and has no armor check penalty, arcane spell failure chance, or speed reduction. It cannot be given magical armor abilities, whether enhancement bonuses or properties like _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Fortification|fortification]]_ or _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/armor-abilities/Ghost Touch (Armor Special Ability)|ghost touch]]_. When wearing mock armor, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to conceal your identity or allegiance as a spellcaster (for example, in cities where arcane spellcasting is illegal). The [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (carpentry) or Craft (leather) DC to create mock armor is 25.

Price 90 gp; Weight 10 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
