# Cheat Sheath

This ornate leather sheath is covered in tables of magical notation and arcane formulae, as well as common spell triggers and reagents. Referring to these handy bits of notation grants you a +2 circumstance bonus on the checks made to learn a spell from a spellbook or scroll or to prepare a spell from a borrowed spellbook (see [[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Magic#Adding Spells to a Wizard’s Spellbook|Arcane Magic]]), and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Mysticism|Mysticism]] checks made to identify the properties of a magic item using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]* or to decipher a scroll.

Price 100 gp; Weight 2 lb.
