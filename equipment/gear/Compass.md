# Compass

An ordinary compass that points to the magnetic north pole grants its user a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks made to avoid becoming lost. It also can be used to grant the same bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] checks made to navigate underground.

Price 10 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
