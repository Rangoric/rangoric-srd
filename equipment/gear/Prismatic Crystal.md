# Prismatic Crystal

This chunk of crystal shines with the colors of the rainbow. Used as a focus for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception#Read Aura|read aura]] occult skill unlock, it cuts the time needed to read an aura from 10 minutes to 5. Held by a psychic creature, the crystal also raises the light level in a 10-foot radius by one step (darkness becomes dim light, and dim light becomes normal light), but not above normal light.

Price 30 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
