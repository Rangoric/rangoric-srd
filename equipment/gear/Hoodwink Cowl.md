# Hoodwink Cowl

This uncomfortable mask is a black cloth hood with padded leather earmuffs and shuttered goggles that block vision. A creature wearing a hoodwink cowl is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^deafened|deafened]], and takes a –10 penalty on all other Perception checks.

Price 75 gp; Weight 3 lb.
