# Darklight Lantern

This lantern does not burn oil, but instead burns [[rangoric-srd/equipment/alchemical-items/Shadowcloy|shadowcloy]]. When shadowcloy is used as its fuel, this lantern creates a strange, hazy darkness that decreases the light level for 30 feet around it by one step. Unlike when shadowcloy is thrown at a single target, this haze does not decrease natural darkness to supernatural darkness. One flask of shadowcloy fuels a darklight lantern for 1 minute.

Price 20 gp; Weight 3 lb.
