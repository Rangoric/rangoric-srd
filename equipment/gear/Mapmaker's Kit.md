# Mapmaker's Kit

This small kit contains a simple slate with a grid carved into its surface and a number of differently colored pieces of chalk. If you use this kit to draw a map as you travel, you receive a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to avoid becoming lost.

Price 10 gp; Weight 2 lb.
