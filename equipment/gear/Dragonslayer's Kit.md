# Dragonslayer's Kit

Aimed more at hired blades than seasoned adventurers, this kit provides supplemental equipment for outfitting hirelings to hunt dragons. It includes a _potion of [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cure Moderate Wounds|cure moderate wounds]]_, _oil of [[rangoric-srd/spells/Bless Weapon|bless weapon]]_ (1 application), a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Longspear|longspear]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Backpack|backpack]], three large [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Sack|sacks]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Sunrod|sunrod]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/armor/Tower Shield|tower shield]], and 5 vials of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Alchemist's Fire|alchemist's fire]]. For hunting dragons that are immune to fire, the alchemist's fire can be replaced with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Acid|acid]] at the time of purchase, for a savings of 50 gp.

Price 485 gp; Weight 64 lb.
