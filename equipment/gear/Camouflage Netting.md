# Camouflage Netting

This loose mesh of twine and varicolored cloth is used to disguise the presence of creatures or large objects. Camouflage netting must be manufactured to provide camouflage in a single type of terrain, such as snow, sand, forest, grass, swamp, urban, or water. When you hide under the netting in the appropriate terrain, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks as long as you remain immobile and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^prone|prone]]. Covering up with camouflage netting is a full-round action requiring a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] check (failure means you do not cover yourself correctly with the netting and do not gain its bonus on Stealth checks). You have the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^entangled|entangled]] condition when using the netting, except your Dexterity penalty does not affect your initiative or Stealth checks. Taking off camouflage netting is a move action, or a swift action if combined with a move action to stand up from a prone position.

You may spend 1 minute covering an object with camouflage netting. Make a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] check with a –10 penalty and use the total as the Stealth check result for the object. Creatures who fail a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check against this Stealth DC do not recognize what the object is and usually treat it as a natural part of the terrain. For example, you can conceal a wagon as a rock or patch of brush. Covering a Large object requires two nets. Covering a Huge or Gargantuan object requires five nets and takes 10 minutes of work, and the Fieldcraft check penalty is –20 instead of –10. Camouflaging a Colossal object depends on its shape but generally requires at least 10 camouflage nets and takes 1 hour of work, and the Fieldcraft check penalty is –30 instead of –10.

A creature with the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] ability ignores the effect of camouflage netting and makes checks against your actual Stealth roll when in scent range. Any creature within 10 feet of a Large or larger object hidden by camouflage netting automatically recognizes it as an object covered in camouflage.

Price 20 gp; Weight 5 lb.
