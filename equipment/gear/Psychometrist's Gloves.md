# Psychometrist's Gloves

These layered lambskin gloves are psychically attuned to sharpen the wearer's sense of touch. To give any benefit, they must be worn for 8 uninterrupted hours, during which Dexterity-based skill checks that use the hands take a –2 penalty. After that time, the wearer can remove the gloves to gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Appraise checks for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise#Psychometry|psychometry]] occult skill unlock. This benefit fades after 1 hour.

Price 10 gp; Weight 5 lb.
