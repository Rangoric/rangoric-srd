# Footprint Cast

This quick-drying cast is perfect for preserving a set of footprints in order to examine them later. By spending 1 minute setting the cast and waiting for it to dry, you can copy footprints, allowing others to examine them without traveling to the scene, and preventing the DC of the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] check to analyze them from increasing because of time or weather.

Price 2 gp; Weight 1 lb.
