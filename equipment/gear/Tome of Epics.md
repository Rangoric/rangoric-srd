# Tome of Epics

This hefty book is bound in oilskin and decorated with scenes of glorious combat between ancient heroes and ferocious monsters. It contains several tales of valor, defeat, and victory, all with brightly colored illustrations. After consulting the book for 1 hour, for the next 24 hours you gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (oratory) and Performance (vocal) checks and a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks pertaining to heroic lineages.

Price 50 gp; Weight 3 lb.
