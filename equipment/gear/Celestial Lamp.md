# Celestial Lamp

This polished lantern contains a _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Continual Flame|continual flame]]_ and sheds light as a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Lamp, Common|common lamp]]. If its font of consecrated crystal and metalwork is filled with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Holy Water|holy water]], the lamp's light is sanctified for 24 hours, adding a +1 bonus to the attack bonus of channeled positive energy or energy channeled to harm evil outsiders within a 30-foot radius.

Price 300 gp; Weight 2 lb.
