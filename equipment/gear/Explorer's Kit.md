# Explorer's Kit

This kit consists of a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Backpack|backpack]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Bedroll|bedroll]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Pouch, Belt|belt pouch]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Mug or Tankard, Clay|clay mug]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dagger|dagger]], two [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Fishhook|fishhooks]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Flint and Steel|flint and steel]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Sewing Needle|sewing needle]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Signal Whistle|signal whistle]], 50 feet of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/String or Twine|string]], 50 feet of thread, a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Waterskin|waterskin]], a week's worth of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rations, Trail|trail rations]], and a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Whetstone|whetstone]]. For Small creatures, the weight of an explorer's kit is 7-1/2 pounds. Leaving the bedroll and rations at camp or on a mount reduces the weight by 12 pounds for Medium creatures and by 3 pounds for Small creatures.

Price 12 gp; Weight 22 lb.
