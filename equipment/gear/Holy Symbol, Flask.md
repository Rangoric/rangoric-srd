# Holy Symbol, Flask

This version of a standard holy symbol is oversized and has a watertight compartment that can hold 1 pint (the same volume as a flask of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Holy Water|holy water]]). Adventurers normally use the compartment to hold holy water or alcohol. A holy symbol flask is not meant for you to throw at enemies and is not fragile like a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Flask|flask]] or [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Vial|vial]]. Like using holy water on an [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^incorporeal|incorporeal]] opponent, you unstopper the flask and pour the contents onto an adjacent target. Detecting the compartment requires a successful DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check.
