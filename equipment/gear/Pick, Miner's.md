# Pick, Miner's

If a miner's pick is used in combat, treat it as a two-handed improvised weapon that deals piercing damage equal to that of a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Heavy Pick|heavy pick]] of its size.

Price 3 gp; Weight 10 lb.
