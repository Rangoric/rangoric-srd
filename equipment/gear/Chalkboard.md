# Chalkboard

A wooden frame approximately the same size as a large book surrounds this thinly sliced piece of polished black stone. Rubbing a simple damp cloth over the slate erases anything scribed with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Chalk|chalk]] on its surface.

Price 1 gp; Weight 2 lb.
