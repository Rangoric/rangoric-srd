# Chronicler's Kit

This bundle contains a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Case, Map or Scroll|map case]], two [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Vial|vials]] of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Ink|ink]], two [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Inkpen|inkpens]], 10 sheets of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Paper|paper]], two blank [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Journal|journals]], a pound of fine powder for drying ink, and a 20-foot measuring cord. The supplies usually suffice for chronicling a single expedition of not more than 2 months' duration.

Price 40 gp; Weight 4-1/2 lb.
