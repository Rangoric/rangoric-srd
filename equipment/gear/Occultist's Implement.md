# Occultist's Implement

This bauble, bit of clothing, or relic has some minor historical significance. Though not especially valuable, it’s useful to an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist|occultist]], who can use it as an [[rangoric-srd/classes/Occultist#^occultist-implements|implement]]. Any such item is nonmagical, even if it appears to be a rod or wand. Implements that already have a mundane version with a price higher than 10 gp (such as armor, shields, weapons, and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tarot Deck|tarot decks]]) use their normal item entries instead of this catchall.

Price 10 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
