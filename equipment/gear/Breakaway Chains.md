# Breakaway Chains

These masterwork chains are easy to break, even though they look like normal [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Chain|chains]] ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] DC 25 to notice the difference). A DC 10 Strength check breaks them instantly.

Price 65 gp; Weight 2 lb.
