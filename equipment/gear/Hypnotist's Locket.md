# Hypnotist's Locket

This small, elliptical metal case holds a small portrait or keepsake, and can be attached to a belt or worn as a pendant. When hanging from a metal chain, a hypnotist's locket gives a +2 circumstance bonus on Persuasion checks for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion#Hypnotism|hypnotism]] occult skill unlock.

A hypnotist's locket can also be a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Hypnotist's Locket, Pocket Watch|pocket watch]].

Price 15 gp; Weight 1 lb.
