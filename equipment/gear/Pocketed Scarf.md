# Pocketed Scarf

An elaborate design disguises several small pockets on one side of this scarf. This scarf grants you a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made to hide objects on your body. This bonus does not stack with the bonus wearing heavy clothing provides but does stack with bonuses for attempting to hide small objects.

Price 8 gp; Weight 1/2 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
