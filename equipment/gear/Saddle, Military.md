# Saddle, Military

A military saddle braces the rider, providing a +2 circumstance bonus on Animal Handling checks related to staying in the saddle. If you’re knocked unconscious while in a military saddle, you have a 75% chance to stay in the saddle (compared to 50% for a riding saddle).

Price 20 gp; Weight 30 lb.
