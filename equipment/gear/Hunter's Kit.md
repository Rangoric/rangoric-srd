# Hunter's Kit

A starting kit for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter|hunter]]. It includes a backpack, a bedroll, a belt pouch, a flint and steel, an iron pot, a mess kit, rope, torches (10), trail rations (5 days), and a waterskin.

Price 9 gp; Weight 28 lb.
