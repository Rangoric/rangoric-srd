# Pulley

A simple [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Block and Tackle|block-and-tackle]] pulley, when properly secured, adds a +5 circumstance bonus on Strength checks to lift heavy objects. Securing the pulley requires 1 minute.

Price 2 gp; Weight 10 lb.
