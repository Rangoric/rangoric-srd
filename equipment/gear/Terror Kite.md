# Terror Kite

This small [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Kite|kite]] is usually painted with a fierce face and bright colors and is edged with serrated wooden blades. Its twine is strengthened by soaking it in glue and sometimes with crushed glass to give it a slight cutting edge. The kite has hardness 5 and 3 hit points. Participants in a kite battle make alternating [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] combat maneuvers against each other's kites; each successful maneuver allows a competitor to roll 1d6 points of damage against the opponent's kite. When a kite reaches 0 hit points, it is broken or its string is cut, and its player loses the match. In some matches, points are awarded for touching the kite's top to the opponent's string, with the winner being the first to reach a set point total. Those interested in kite-fighting may select the terror kite as a weapon for the purpose of feats such as [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] and [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Specialization|Weapon Specialization]], and apply these bonuses on kite damage rolls and on their [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] combat maneuver attempts made while using terror kites.
