# Grappling Arrow

This small [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Grappling Hook|grappling hook]] is designed to be tied to a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rope, Silk|silk rope]] and fired from a bow. When fired, it has a range increment of 30 feet. An equivalent item exists for crossbows.

Price 1 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
