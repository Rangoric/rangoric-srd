# Thieves' Tools

This kit contains the tools you need to use the Engineering and Sleight of Hand skills. Without these tools, you must improvise tools, and you take a –2 circumstance penalty on Engineering and Sleight of Hand checks.

Price 30 gp; Weight 1 lb.
