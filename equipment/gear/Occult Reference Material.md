# Occult Reference Material

These books come in all shapes and sizes, from hardbound, illustrated manuscripts to collections of journals, notes and scrolls by many authors. Used as a reference (which typically takes 1d4 minutes of searching the text), occult reference material gives a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge]] checks about the occult topic it covers. Common topics include astral projection, [[rangoric-srd/rules/Auras|auras]], dreams, ley lines, [[rangoric-srd/classes/Medium (Class)#^medium-spirits|medium spirits]] and rituals. Material on a general subject (such as spirits as a whole) gives an overview but not enough detail for a skill bonus.

Price 80 gp; Weight 1 lb.
