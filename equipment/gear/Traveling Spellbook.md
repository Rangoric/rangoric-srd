# Traveling Spellbook

A traveling spellbook is lighter and less cumbersome than its full-size [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Spellbook, Wizard's|counterpart]]. It has 50 pages.

The alchemist's equivalent is the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Traveling Formula Book|traveling formula book]].

Price 10 gp per blank; Weight 1 lb.
