# Book Lariat

This braided metal cord comes with a clasp that affixes to the lock of a standard [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Spellbook, Wizard's|spellbook]]. The other end of the cord attaches to a belt or belt loop. The cord is 10 feet long and retractable. If you drop your spellbook while it's attached to the lariat, you can recover the spellbook as a standard action. While attached to you, the book can never be farther than 10 feet from you. Unclasping the book requires a move action, or the cord can be cut to free the book (hardness 5, hp 10).

Price 3 sp; Weight 1/2 lb.
