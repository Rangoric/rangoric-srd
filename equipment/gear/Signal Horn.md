# Signal Horn

Sounding a horn requires a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] (wind instruments) check and can convey concepts such as “Attack!”, “Help!”, “Advance!”, “Retreat!”, “Fire!”, and, “Alarm!” The report of a signal horn can be clearly heard ([[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] DC 0) up to a half-mile distant. For each quarter-mile beyond, Perception checks to hear the horn suffer a –1 penalty.

Price 1 gp; Weight 2 lb.
