# Saddle, Exotic Riding

An exotic saddle is like a normal [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Saddle, Riding|riding saddle]] except that it is designed for an unusual mount. Exotic saddles come in military, pack, and riding styles.

Price 30 gp; Weight 30 lb.
