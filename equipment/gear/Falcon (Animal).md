# Falcon (Animal)

This Tiny, long-winged hawk is a popular hunting bird among many aristocracies. Falcons are technically a subset of [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Hawk|hawks]] but are considered more “noble” in many cultures. Ownership of these birds is denied to commoners in many nations with hierarchical monarchies.

Price 40 gp; Weight 2 lb.
