# Bufo

This drink is a favorite of goblins, boggards, and other primitive humanoids. It is made by soaking a poisonous toad or frog (or its eggs) in weak beer, or by “milking” these animals for their poison and mixing it with the beer (which allows the animal to be used over and over again). Some tribes use wide-mouthed jugs and leave the dead animal inside as a crunchy treat for eating once the drink is gone. A creature that is drunk on bufo (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#^drunkenness|Drunkenness]]) is also [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]].

Price 1 gp per jug; Weight 2 lb.
