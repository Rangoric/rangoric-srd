# Talking Board

This thin, rectangular wooden board is printed with an arch of letters, numbers and short affirmative and negative messages, and comes with a small, heart-shaped pointer on short wooden legs. It can be used in place of a pencil and paper or an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Automatic Writing Planchette|automatic writing planchette]] for a Linguistics check with the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics#Automatic Writing|automatic writing]] occult skill unlock, and gives a +2 circumstance bonus on the check. The board is large enough that another psychically sensitive creature can help with a successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Special Attacks#Aid Another|aid another]] check.

A user alone can also use the board for the recall memory use of the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion#Hypnotism|hypnotism]] occult skill unlock, with herself as the subject; she attempts the check as normal and gains a +2 circumstance bonus on it.

When used to channel a haunt capable of communication, a talking board doubles the rate of communication with the haunt and gives a +4 circumstance bonus on Linguistics checks to decipher its cryptic messages.

Though a talking board can do some of what other gear does (such as an automatic writing planchette or a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Hypnotist's Locket|hypnotist's locket]]), these items can't be used together to gain several bonuses or to raise the rate of communication with a haunt above double.

Price 15 gp; Weight 2 lb.
