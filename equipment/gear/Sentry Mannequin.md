# Sentry Mannequin

After 5 minutes of assembly and coverage with the included loose hood and baggy clothing, this wooden frame looks like a vigilant humanoid figure. Creatures more than 30 feet distant from it must succeed at a DC 10 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check in order to realize it is not someone standing watch.

Price 30 gp; Weight 35 lb. ([[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Goods and Services#Weights|less for Small characters]]).
