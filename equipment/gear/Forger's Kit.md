# Forger's Kit

These inks, pens, papers, templates for certificates, and tools for modifying or copying official seals facilitate the creation of counterfeit documents. It grants a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks made to forge documents.

Price 200 gp; Weight 6 lb.
