# Shield Sconce

This metal frame holds a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Torch|torch]] and is designed to be strapped to the front of a light, heavy, or tower shield, allowing you to carry a torch without giving up your shield or occupying your weapon hand.

Price 1 gp; Weight 1/2 lb.
