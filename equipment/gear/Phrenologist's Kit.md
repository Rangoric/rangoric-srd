# Phrenologist's Kit

This leather doctor's bag holds what a phrenologist needs to measure skulls: metal skull calipers of several sizes, a labeled chalkware bust of a humanoid head, and numbered charts of the skulls of various species. The kit gives a +2 circumstance bonus on Arcana checks for the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana#Phrenology|phrenology]] occult skill unlock.

Price 50 gp; Weight 5 lb.
