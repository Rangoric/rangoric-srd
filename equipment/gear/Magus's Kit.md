# Magus's Kit

A starting kit for a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus|magus]]. It includes a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Backpack|backpack]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Bedroll|bedroll]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Pouch, Belt|belt pouch]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Flint and Steel|flint and steel]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Ink|ink]], an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Inkpen|inkpen]], an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Pot, Iron|iron pot]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Mess Kit|mess kit]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rope, Hempen|rope]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Soap|soap]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Spell Component Pouch|spell component pouch]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Torch|torches]] (10), [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rations, Trail|trail rations]] (5 days), and a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Waterskin|waterskin]]. The kit does not contain a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Spellbook, Wizard's|spellbook]] because a magus begins play with a spellbook.

Price 22 gp; Weight 31 lb.
