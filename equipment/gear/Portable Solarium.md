# Portable Solarium

This long wooden case holds a framework of brass armatures, balance weights, a folding mat and purple-hued lenses. Assembled, it becomes a small bed under a slowly revolving array of lenses that filter sunlight onto the mat. It works only on bright, sunny days, and has room for one Medium or two Small creatures to recline beneath it. The solarium can be used with [[rangoric-srd/actions/Long-Term Care|long-term care]]: a subject who basks in the filtered light for an 8-hour rest recovers 1 additional hit point per level or 1 additional ability score point if the caregiver succeeds at the check.

Price 800 gp; Weight 35 lb.
