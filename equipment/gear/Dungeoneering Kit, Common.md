# Dungeoneering Kit, Common

This bundle of essential supplies for exploring ruins and abandoned cities includes two [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Candle|candles]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Chalk|chalk]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Hammer|hammer]] and four [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Piton|pitons]], 50 feet of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rope, Hempen|hemp rope]], a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Lantern, Hooded|hooded lantern]] with 5 [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Flask|flasks]] of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Oil|oil]], two [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Sack|sacks]], two [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Torch|torches]], and four [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Tindertwig|tindertwigs]]. A deluxe dungeoneering kit contains two candles, chalk, a hammer and four pitons, 50 feet of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Rope, Silk|silk rope]], two sacks, three [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Sunrod|sunrods]], four tindertwigs, and an [[rangoric-srd/equipment/gear/Everburning Torch|everburning torch]].

Price 15 gp; Weight 25 lb.
