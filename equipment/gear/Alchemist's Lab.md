# Alchemist's Lab

This lab is used for making alchemical items, and provides a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) checks. It has no bearing on the costs related to the Background (alchemy) skill. Without this lab, a character with the Background (alchemy) skill is assumed to have enough tools to use the skill but not enough to get the +2 bonus that the lab provides.

Price 200 gp; Weight 40 lb.
