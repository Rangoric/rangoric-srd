# Bo Staff

_Exotic two-handed melee weapon (blocking, double, monk)._ Cost 1 gp; Dmg 1d4/1d4 (S), 1d6/1d6 (M); Critical x2; Weight 3 lb.; Type Bludgeoning. ^stats

Similar to a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Quarterstaff|quarterstaff]], only slightly more slender and tapered at one end, the bo staff is both a defensive device and a weapon.

A bo staff is a double weapon: you can fight with it as if fighting with two weapons, with all the normal attack penalties, just as if you were using a one-handed weapon and a light weapon. A creature wielding a bo staff in one hand can’t use it as a double weapon—only one end of the weapon can be used in any given round. A bo staff is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-blocking|blocking]] weapon. A bo staff is a special [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] weapon.
