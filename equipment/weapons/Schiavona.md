# Schiavona

*Martial one-handed melee weapon.* Cost 25 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 1d8 (M); Critical 18-20/x2; Weight 3 lb.; Type Piercing or slashing. ^stats

The schiavona soldier’s sword is suitable for both cutting and thrusting. Its extreme ease of use and excellent balance makes it equally suitable for both foot soldiers and cavalry. The hilt is a complex assembly of iron bars, which forms a basket that provides very good protection for the wielder’s hand without the bulk associated with most basket hilts. On the inner side of the cross guard is an iron thumb loop. A type of schiavona with a more elaborate guard in the form of interlocking leaves and stems is a favored sword among elves.

The sword’s blade is typically a bit less than four feet long with little taper until the very end, where it comes to a point. In most regards, the sword resembles a claymore and is commonly used in combination with a buckler.
