# Brass Knuckles

*Simple unarmed attack.* Cost 5 sp; Dmg 1d3 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x2; Weight 1 lb.; Type Bludgeoning. ^stats

Crude but effective weapons of the street, brass knuckles increase the damage inflicted by unarmed strikes, allowing a medium-sized character to deal 1d4 points of nonlethal damage in combat and a small-sized character to deal 1d3 points. The Weapon Finesse feat allows users to apply their Dexterity modifiers instead of Strength modifiers to attack rolls with brass knuckles. Available in three sizes: slender for elves and halflings; medium for humans; and extra-thick for half-orcs.
