# Poisoned Sand Tube

_Martial ranged weapon._ Cost 1 gp; Dmg special (S), special (M); Weight 1 lb. ^stats

Resembling a slender bamboo or metal scroll case, this tube is filled with fine sand that is soaked or coated with inhaled or contact [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]]. When loaded with 3 doses of these types of poison, the wielder blows into the tube, dispersing the poisoned sand into her enemies' faces in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone is affected as if afflicted with 1 dose of the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]]. Even when loaded with unpoisoned sand, the tube lets loose a powerful irritant, and it attacks the Fortitude of an opponent struck at +2; on a hit, it is [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round. Once expended, the tube must be repacked before it can be used again. Repacking a tube requires a full-round action, or a standard action if the wielder has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Reload|Rapid Reload]] feat.
