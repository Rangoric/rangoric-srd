*Exotic two-handed advanced firearm.* Cost 5,000 gp; Dmg 1d8 (S), 1d10 (M); Critical x4; Range Increment 80 ft.; Misfire 1; Capacity 1; Weight 12 lb.; Type Bludgeoning and piercing. ^stats
