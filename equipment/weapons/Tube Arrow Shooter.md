# Tube Arrow Shooter

_Martial ranged weapon._ Cost 3 gp; Dmg 1d3 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x2; Range Increment 40 ft.; Weight 1/2 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

This weapon consists of a small metal tube hidden within a sleeve; the tube holds a short, spring-loaded [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Bamboo Shaft Arrows|bamboo shaft]]. The wielder gains a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made to conceal a tube arrow shooter on his body. Releasing the fastener allows the spring to force the arrow out. Once a shaft is fired, the tube must be reloaded before it can be used again. Reloading a tube is a full-round action, or a standard action if the wielder has the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Rapid Reload|Rapid Reload]] feat.
