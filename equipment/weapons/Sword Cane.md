# Sword Cane

_Martial one-handed melee weapon._ Cost 45 gp; Dmg 1d4 (S), 1d6 (M); Critical x2; Weight 4 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

This slender light blade lies within a wooden container that serves as both its scabbard and hiding place. You can draw the blade from the cane as a swift action (or a free action if you have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]] feat). An observer must make a DC 20 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check to realize an undrawn sword cane is a weapon rather than a walking stick; the DC decreases to 10 if the observer is able to handle the weapon.

See also the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Sword Cane Pistol|sword cane pistol]], which combines this weapon with a coat pistol.
