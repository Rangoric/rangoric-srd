# Tetsubo

_Exotic two-handed melee weapon._ Cost 20 gp; Dmg 1d8 (S), 1d10 (M); Critical x4; Weight 10 lb.; Type Bludgeoning. ^stats

A slight improvement over the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Greatclub|greatclub]], this weapon consists of a long, slender length whose upper half is shod with a thick sheet of studded iron. The iron gives added weight and strength to the club, while the studs are designed to crack helmets and armor.
