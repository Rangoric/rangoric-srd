# Iron Brush

_Martial light melee weapon._ Cost 2 gp; Dmg 1d2 (S), 1d3 (M); Critical x2; Range Increment 10 ft.; Weight —; Type Piercing. ^stats

This is an iron-handled version of a scholar's brush with a sharpened handle. Though it does little damage, it is easily concealed (the wielder gets a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made to conceal an iron brush on her body) and can be used as a jabbing weapon or thrown short distances.
