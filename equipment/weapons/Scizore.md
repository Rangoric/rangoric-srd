# Scizore

*Martial one-handed melee weapon (performance).* Cost 20 gp; Dmg 1d8 (S), 1d10 (M); Critical x2; Weight 3 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

The scizore is a hardened tube that fits your forearm, ending in a semicircular blade used for devastating bladed punch attacks. The scizore grants a +1 shield bonus to AC, but if you attack with the blade, you lose the AC bonus that round and take a –1 penalty on attack rolls with the scizore. While wearing a scizore, you cannot use that hand for anything else. A scizore provides a +10 bonus to your CMD against being [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarmed]] of your scizore. Donning a scizore is a full-round action.

A scizore is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Performance (Quality)|performance weapon]].
