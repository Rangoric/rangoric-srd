# Sling Bullets

*Ammunition (10).* Cost 1 sp; Weight 5 lb. ^stats

Bullets come in a leather pouch that holds 10 bullets. A bullet that hits its target is destroyed; one that misses has a 50% chance of being destroyed or lost.
