# Cat Glove

*Exotic light melee weapon.* Cost 5 gp; Dmg 1d3 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x2; Weight 2 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

Cat gloves are tough yet supple leather gloves, each finger tipped with a small but sharp steel claw. These weapons are used to rake an opponent with a clawing action. Their surreptitious nature makes them a favorite weapon of many assassins and rogues. Opponents cannot disarm the wearer of a cat glove. An attack with a cat glove is considered an armed attack. The cost and weight given is for a single glove. The Weapon Finesse feat may be used to apply Dexterity modifiers instead of Strength modifiers to attack rolls.
