# Double-Headed Spear

*Exotic two-handed melee weapon.* Cost 5 gp; Dmg 1d6/1d6 (S), 1d8/1d8 (M); Critical x3; Range Increment 20 ft.; Weight 6 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

(*Shuang Tou Qiang*): A common martial artist’s weapon, the double-headed spear has a spearhead at each end of the shaft. A double-headed spear is a double weapon, and when used as such, incurs all the normal attack penalties associated with fighting with two weapons (although the off-hand weapon is considered light). A creature using a double-headed spear in one hand cannot use it as a double weapon. A double-headed spear may also be thrown effectively.
