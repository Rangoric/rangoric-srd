# Ranseur

*Martial two-handed melee weapon (reach).* Cost 10 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 2d4 (M); Critical x3; Weight 12 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

A ranseur has reach. You can strike opponents 10 feet away with it, but you can’t use it against an adjacent foe.

With a ranseur, you get a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|combat maneuver checks]] made to disarm an opponent.
