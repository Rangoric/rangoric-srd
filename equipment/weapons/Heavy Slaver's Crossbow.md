# Heavy Slaver's Crossbow

*Exotic ranged weapon.* Cost 400 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 1d8 (M); Critical 19–20/x2; Range Increment 80 ft.; Weight 12 lb.; Type Bludgeoning. ^stats

This crossbow shoots ammunition called [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Bolas Bolts|bolas bolts]] instead of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Crossbow Bolts|crossbow bolts]]. It comes in both light and heavy varieties (but not double, hand, or repeating crossbow varieties) with the same reload action as a conventional crossbow of its type.
