# Bardiche

*Martial two-handed melee weapon.* Cost 30 gp; Dmg 1d8 (S), 1d10 (M); Critical 19-20/x2; Weight 9 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

This is a heavy axe with a long, broad blade 2 to 3 ft. in length mounted by two rings onto a 4 ft. shaft. This is a two-handed weapon when wielded by a medium-sized or smaller creature.
