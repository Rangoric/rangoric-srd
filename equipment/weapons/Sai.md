# Sai

*Exotic light melee weapon.* Cost 1 gp; Dmg 1d3 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x2; Range Increment 10 ft.; Weight 1 lb.; Type Bludgeoning. ^stats

With a sai, you get a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|combat maneuver checks]] made to disarm an enemy.

The sai is a special monk weapon. This designation gives a monk wielding a sai special options.
