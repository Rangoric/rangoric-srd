# Double-Chained Axe

*Exotic two-handed melee weapon (trip).* Cost 30 gp; Dmg 1d6/1d6 (S), 1d8/1d8 (M); Critical x3; Weight 20 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

The double-chained axe is a long haft with a chain axe attached to either end. It may be used as a double weapon, but the user incurs all the normal attack penalties for fighting with two weapons, as if he were using a one-handed weapon and a light weapon. The double-chained axe may not be used as a double weapon if wielded in one hand.

This axe provides a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|combat maneuver checks]] made to disarm an enemy.

This weapon can make trip attacks. If tripped during a trip attempt, the wielder can drop the chained axe to avoid being tripped themselves.
