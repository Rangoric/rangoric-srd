# Axehead Arrows

*Ammunition (20) for bows.* Cost 5 gp; Critical x2; Range Increment Per bow; Weight 7 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

This arrow has a small curved blade affixed to the head of the shaft. In addition to causing Slashing damage instead of Piercing damage, axehead arrows may be used to cut strings, ropes or vines from distance.
