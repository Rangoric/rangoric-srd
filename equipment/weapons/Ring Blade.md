# Ring Blade

*Exotic one-handed melee weapon.* Cost 25 gp; Dmg 1d4 (S), 1d6 (M); Critical x2; Weight 4 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

This weapon consists of a circular blade with a crossbar running across its diameter. It can either be used as an exotic melee weapon or as a shield. Its wielder can switch between functions from round to round. If enchanted, its bonus applies offensively when used as a weapon, and defensively when used as a shield.
