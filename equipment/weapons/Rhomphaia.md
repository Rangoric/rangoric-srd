# Rhomphaia

_Martial two-handed melee weapon (reach)._ Cost 15 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 2d4 (M); Critical x3; Weight 10 lb.; Type Piercing or slashing. ^stats

This weapon is an early polearm consisting of a long, single-edged blade attached to a sturdy staff, sometimes slightly curved. It serves as both a cutting and a thrusting weapon.

A rhomphaia has reach. You can strike opponents 10 feet away with it, but you can’t use it against an adjacent foe. If you use a ready action to set a rhomphaia against a charge, you deal double damage on a successful hit against a charging character. A rhomphaia is [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Fragile (Quality)|fragile]].
