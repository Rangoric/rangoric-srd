# Grappling Arrows

*Ammunition (5) for bows.* Cost 50 gp; Dmg — (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x2; Range Increment ½ distance; Weight 3 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

The head of this arrow was probably developed by some ingenious gnome. It consists of two steel blades on a center pin, which are designed to spring out once fired from the bow. The shaft of this arrow is reinforced to hold 250 lbs. The arrow has half the normal range increment. A ranged attack is made with an AC of the target being 15.
