# Dwarven Double Waraxe

_Exotic one-handed melee weapon._ Cost 60 gp; Dmg 1d8 (S), 1d10 (M); Critical x3; Weight 12 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

This hefty waraxe is similar to the common [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Dwarven Waraxe|dwarven waraxe]], but its recurved blade spans forward and back from its head like a deadly butterfly. A dwarven double waraxe grants a +1 bonus on all attack rolls after the first when using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Cleave|Cleave]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Great Cleave|Great Cleave]].
