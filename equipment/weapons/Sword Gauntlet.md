# Sword Gauntlet

*Exotic one-handed melee weapon.* Cost 55 gp; Dmg 1d4 (S), 1d6 (M); Critical 19-20/x2; Weight 3 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

A sword gauntlet is a short sword blade attached to the back of a bracer and hand guard with a crosswise grip. The wielder holds the grip for leverage with the blade extending out from the back of the hand. Donning or removing this weapon is a move action (though it is a free action if the wielder has the Quick Draw feat). Sword gauntlets may not be disarmed, but prevent anything else from being held in the hand wielding the blade.
