# Weighted Greatsword

*Martial two-handed melee weapon.* Cost 80 gp; Dmg 1d12 (S), 2d8 (M); Critical 18-20/x2; Weight 12 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

This greatsword is a single-edged blade with a weight just over halfway up the length of the blade, attached to its back. The sword is weighted to such a finely balanced degree that it has no adverse effect on the wielder, however the extra weight allows the weapon more effective cutting power.
