# Kerambit

_Martial light melee weapon._ Cost 2 gp; Dmg 1d2 (S), 1d3 (M); Critical x3; Weight —; Type Slashing. ^stats

This small, curved pull dagger has a metal loop at the base of its handle allowing it to be secured with a pinky or worn on a string tied in the hair. While relatively small, the curved blade can create brutal wounds. Easily concealed (the wielder gets a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks made to conceal a kerambit on her body), it is a favorite concealed weapon of ninja and assassins.
