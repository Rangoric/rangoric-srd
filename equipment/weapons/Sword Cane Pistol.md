# Sword Cane Pistol

*Exotic one-handed early firearm.* Cost 775 gp; Dmg 1d3 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x3; Range Increment 10 ft.; Misfire 1 (5 ft.); Capacity 1; Weight 1 lb.; Type Bludgeoning and piercing. ^stats

A combination weapon, this gun mixes a coat pistol with a sword cane. The sword cane pistol is considered a double weapon for the purpose of creating masterwork or magical versions of this weapon. The pistol attachment makes the nature of the weapon a little more difficult to hide. An observer must make a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] check to realize that an undrawn sword cane pistol is a weapon rather than a walking stick; the DC decreases to 5 if the observer is able to handle the weapon. A sword cane pistol uses either a bullet and a single dose of black powder or an alchemical cartridge as ammunition. The sword part of the weapon must be drawn in order to load the pistol part of the weapon.
