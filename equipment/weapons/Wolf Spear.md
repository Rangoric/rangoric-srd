# Wolf Spear

*Martial two-handed melee weapon (reach).* Cost 20 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 1d8 (M); Critical x3; Weight 20 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

(*Lan Qiang*): An exceedingly rare weapon, the wolf spear is a polearm of up to 15 feet in length. The spear’s head is surrounded by barbed, metal, treelike branches. A wolf spear is a Reach weapon. It can be used to strike opponents up to 10 feet away, but cannot be used against an adjacent foe.
