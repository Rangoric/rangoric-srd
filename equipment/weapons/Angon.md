# Angon

*Simple ranged weapon.* Cost 2 gp; Dmg 1d4 (S), 2d4 (M); Critical 19-20/x2; Range Increment 20 ft.; Weight 3 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

This is a barbed javelin intended for throwing. Like a typical javelin, it can be used in melee combat, but not nearly as well (suffering -4 to melee attack rolls). The head of the javelin is characterized by a number of vicious barbs that inflict terrible wounds. When a critical hit is scored, the angon has become imbedded in the victim. This hinders combat (-2 to all actions until removed), but ripping the offending weapon out of a body causes an additional 2d4 points of damage. Extracting an angon is even challenging for a skilled healer: a successful [[rangoric-srd/skills/Medicine|Medicine]] check (DC 15) inflicts only 1d4 points of damage to the victim, while a DC 20 roll results in a clean removal without further damage.
