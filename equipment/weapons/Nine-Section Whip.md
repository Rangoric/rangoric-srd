# Nine-Section Whip

_Exotic one-handed melee weapon (blocking, distracting, monk, trip)._ Cost 8 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 1d8 (M); Critical 19–20/x2; Weight 3 lb.; Type Bludgeoning. ^stats

The nine-section whip consists of steel bars linked together. The final bar is a 6-inch weighted spike. This weapon can be wielded as a single-handed weapon, a two-handed weapon, or in pairs.

A nine-section whip is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-blocking|blocking]] weapon. A nine-section whip is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-distracting|distracting]] weapon. You can make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks with a nine-section whip. If you are tripped during your own trip attempt, you can drop it to avoid being tripped. A nine-section whip is a special [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] weapon.
