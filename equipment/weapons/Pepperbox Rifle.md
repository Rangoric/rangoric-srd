# Pepperbox Rifle

*Exotic two-handed advanced firearm.* Cost 7,000 gp; Dmg 1d8 (S), 1d10 (M); Critical x4; Range Increment 80 ft.; Misfire 1–2; Capacity 4; Weight 15 lb.; Type Bludgeoning and piercing. ^stats

The four barrels of this rifle are set into a turnable housing that can be quickly rotated by hand (a free action) between shots. A pepperbox rifle uses metal cartridges as ammunition.
