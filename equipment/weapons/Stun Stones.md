# Stun Stones

*Ammunition (20).* Cost 1 gp; Dmg 1d4 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x2; Range Increment –10 ft.; Weight 5 lb.; Type Bludgeoning. ^stats

Typically, sling stones are made of baked clay and are designed to injure or kill. However, some cultures use specialized weighted stones made from soft, unbaked clay designed to stun. This is particularly valuable when the victim needs to be taken alive, and as a result slavers in particular favor this ammunition. Wet clay is more limited in range (40 ft range increment instead of 50), but deals only nonlethal damage. On a critical hit, damage is doubled as normal, but the victim must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + attackers base ranged attack bonus) or be [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^stunned|stunned]] for one round.
