# Pen Polearm

*Exotic two-handed melee weapon (reach).* Cost 15 gp; Dmg 1d4 (S), 1d6 (M); Critical 18-20/x2; Weight 5 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

(*Bi Guo*): Not actually a pen at all, this weapon is a polearm with a metal head shaped like a hand clenching a sharpened metal brush. The middle finger and forefinger are also extended and tipped with sharp nails. As it is difficult to wield and used to strike at vital points, the pen is an Exotic weapon. The Weapon Finesse feat can be used to apply Dexterity modifiers instead of Strength to attack rolls with a pen.
