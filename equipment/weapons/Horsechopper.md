# Horsechopper

*Martial two-handed melee weapon (reach).* Cost 10 gp; Dmg 1d8 (S), 1d10 (M); Critical x3; Weight 12 lb.; Type Piercing or slashing. ^stats

Crafted by goblins to give themselves an advantage against horses, this weapon is essentially a halberd with an enlarged hook opposite the blade. A horsechopper has reach: you can strike opponents 10 feet away with it, but you can’t use it against an adjacent foe. You can use a horsechopper to make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks; if you are tripped during your own trip attempt, you can drop it to avoid being tripped.
