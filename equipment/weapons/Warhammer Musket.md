# Warhammer Musket

*Exotic two-handed early firearm.* Cost 1,600 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 1d8 (M); Critical x4; Range Increment 30 ft.; Misfire 1–2 (5 ft.); Capacity 1; Weight 6 lb.; Type Bludgeoning and piercing. ^stats

This musket has a warhammer head at the end of its barrel, which allows it to be used as both a musket and a warhammer. It is considered a double weapon for purposes of creating masterwork or magical versions of this weapon. If this firearm gains the broken condition, both the firearm component and the warhammer are considered [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]]. A warhammer musket uses either a bullet and a single dose of black powder or an alchemical cartridge as ammunition.
