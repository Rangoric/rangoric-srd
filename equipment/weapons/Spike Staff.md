# Spike Staff

*Simple two-handed melee weapon (double).* Cost 20 gp; Dmg 1d4/1d4 (S), 1d6/1d6 (M); Critical x2; Weight 6 lb.; Type Bludgeoning. ^stats

This staff is a combination between a quarterstaff and crowbar. It has the length of a quarterstaff and the same traits, such as being a double weapon. The top and bottom of the staff differ from the traditional one by coming to a point and being made of heavy iron. This has two unusual effects. The staff is treated as a masterwork quarterstaff for combat purposes, granting a +1 attack bonus. It also is specifically designed to lever things open and fit into one of the ends into cracks in walls for the purpose of breaking holes in them. This grants the wielder a +5 bonus against the break DCs of all types of wood, masonry, and stone walls.
