# Kusarigama

*Exotic two-handed melee weapon (reach, double).* Cost 12 gp; Dmg 1d2/1d4 (S), 1d3/1d6 (M); Critical x2; Weight 3 lb.; Type Slashing or bludgeoning. ^stats

This weapon has a single sickle held in the off-hand attached by 10 feet of fine chain to a weighted metal ball. The sickle is used to make trips, jabs, and blocks while the ball is whipped around at high speeds and then smashed into the opponent. A kusarigama is a double weapon (the sickle deals slashing damage, the ball bludgeoning): you can fight with it as if fighting with two weapons, with all the normal attack penalties, just as if you were using a one-handed weapon and a light weapon. It has reach. You can use a kusarigama to make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks; if you are tripped during your own trip attempt, you can drop it to avoid being tripped. It is a special monk weapon, and it has the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-grapple|grapple]] feature.
