# Staff-Spear

*Simple two-handed melee weapon (double).* Cost 45 gp; Dmg 1d4/1d4 (S), 1d6/1d6 (M); Critical x2; Weight 5 lb.; Type Bludgeoning or piercing. ^stats

This weapon resembles a normal quarterstaff and can be used as such. However, pressing two switches on the handle causes spear-heads to spring forth at each end of the staff, either simultaneously or one at time.

Characters that coordinate the release of the spear head with a successful attack roll inflict a bonus of +1d6 damage. Retracting a spear head is a standard action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity, and users can retract both heads as a full round action. The spearstaff may be used as a double weapon. Fighting with a spearstaff as a double weapon incurs all the normal penalties associated with fighting with two weapons as if the user was wielding a one-handed weapon and a light weapon.
