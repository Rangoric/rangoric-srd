# Tekko-Kagi

_Exotic light melee weapon (disarm)._ Cost 2 gp per iron claw; Dmg 1d2 (S), 1d3 (M); Critical x2; Weight —; Type Piercing. ^stats

Also known as the iron claw, this device consists of a fanlike structure of five 10-inch blades secured to a sturdy handle strapped to the forearm of the off-hand. It can be used as an offensive weapon, defensively like a buckler, or to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] an opponent's weapon without provoking an [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Initiative and Attacks of Opportunity|attack of opportunity]]. It provides its owner with a +2 circumstance bonus on attempts to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] swords or other slender-bladed weapons.

A tekko-kagi is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-disarm|disarm]] weapon.
