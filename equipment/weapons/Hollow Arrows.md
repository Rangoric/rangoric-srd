# Hollow Arrows

*Ammunition (20) for bows.* Cost 25 gp; Dmg 1d4 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x2; Weight 3 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

These appear to be normal arrows or crossbow bolts, but the shaft has been hollowed and waxed, allowing it to contain two doses of poison. When it hits its target, the shaft shatters and delivers the poison. Damage is reduced by the lighter weight and construction.
