# Madu, Leather

_Exotic light melee weapon (performance)._ Cost 40 gp; Dmg 1d3 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x2; Weight 5 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

The madu is a round, light shield with two animal horns—usually from an antelope—extending from opposite sides of the shield. If you are proficient with the madu, you may use it to fight defensively with a –2 penalty instead of the normal –4, and your attack penalty for using [[rangoric-srd/feats/Combat Expertise|Combat Expertise]] improves by +1 (minimum –1 penalty). You cannot hold anything else in the hand that bears a madu. If you are not proficient with the madu, treat it as a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Light Spiked Shield|light spiked shield]].

This is the leather madu, which weighs 5 lb.; the [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Madu, Steel|steel madu]] costs 50 gp and weighs 6 lb.

A madu is a [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Performance (Quality)|performance weapon]].
