# Double-Chained Kama

*Exotic two-handed melee weapon (double, monk, reach, trip).* Cost 8 gp; Dmg 1d4/1d4 (S), 1d6/1d6 (M); Critical x2; Weight 4 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

This weapon comprises a pair of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/weapons/Kama|kama]] connected with an 8-foot length of chain. The wielder can attack as if armed with a single kama in each hand or extend the chain to make a single reach attack. By swinging the rope, the wielder can whip the kama about to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] opponents. Furthermore, if one of the weapons is dropped, the wielder can retrieve as a free action by pulling on the chain.

A double-chained kama is a double weapon: you can fight with it as if fighting with two weapons, with all the normal attack penalties, just as if you were using a one-handed weapon and a light weapon. A creature wielding a double-chained kama in one hand can’t use it as a double weapon—only one end of the weapon can be used in any given round. When you extend the chain, a double-chained kama has reach: you can strike opponents 10 feet away with it, but you can’t use it against an adjacent foe. You can make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks with a double-chained kama. If you are tripped during your own trip attempt, you can drop it to avoid being tripped. A double-chained kama is a special [[rangoric-srd/classes/Monk|monk]] weapon.
