# Chainblade

*Exotic one-handed melee weapon (reach).* Cost 300 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 1d8 (M); Critical 17-20/x2; Weight 6 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

The chainblade has a handle and crosspiece like that of a sword with a 5 to 7 ft. long segmented blade. The segments are joined together by a slotted hinge that allows the blade as a whole to move freely along the thin axis while remain ridged along the flat, giving the weapon a snaky, whiplike motion.
