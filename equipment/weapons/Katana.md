# Katana

*Exotic one-handed melee weapon.* Cost 50 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 1d8 (M); Critical 18–20/x2; Weight 6 lb.; Type Slashing. ^stats

Specifically constructed for samurai, katanas employ multiple types of steel combined in a distinctive forging process. The results are swords noted for their wickedly sharp yet slender, gently curved blades, designed to make graceful hacking strokes capable of severing opponents’ heads and limbs. Though finely balanced, these blades are difficult to master. A character can use a katana two-handed as a martial weapon, but must take the Exotic Weapon Proficiency (katana) feat to use it one-handed. A katana is [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^weapon-deadly|deadly]].
