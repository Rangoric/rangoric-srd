# Claws of the Weasel

*Simple light melee weapon.* Cost 5 gp; Dmg 1d3 (S), 1d4 (M); Critical x4; Weight 1 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

These items fit like gauntlets, and have 6 inch metal claws that can be used for both climbing and for fighting. For combat purposes, they function the same as spiked gauntlets. When a character wears these when making a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] check, he receives a +4 circumstance bonus.
