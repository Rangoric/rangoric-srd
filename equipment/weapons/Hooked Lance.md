# Hooked Lance

_Martial two-handed melee weapon (reach, trip)._ Cost 3 gp; Dmg 1d6 (S), 1d8 (M); Critical x4; Weight 10 lb.; Type Piercing. ^stats

This slender, 10-foot-long polearm has an added hook protruding from the head that is used to snag the clothing and armor of opponents.

A hooked lance has reach. You can strike opponents 10 feet away with it, but you can’t use it against an adjacent foe. You can make [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attacks with a hooked lance. If you are tripped during your own trip attempt, you can drop it to avoid being tripped.
