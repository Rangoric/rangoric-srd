# Sap Hide

*Light armor.* Cost 450 gp; Armor Bonus +4; Maximum Dex Bonus +6; Armor Check Penalty –1; Arcane Spell Failure 10%; Speed 30 ft. (30 ft.), 20 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 15 lb. ^stats

Sap hide is a lightweight, yet durable armor made from the boiled and processed sap of the bowery tree. Sap hide must be carefully custom fitted to the body, which makes it not only fairly expensive, but difficult to transfer from one person to another. Sap hide is slightly thicker than leather, but much more flexible and more resistant to cuts. Sap hide has a firm but spongy feel to it and a smooth matte appearance. Sap hide is highly prized as it is comfortable enough to sleep in. Typically a suit of sap hide is made to cover the entire body, but it is not uncommon to see partial versions of the armor.

Any creature that wears a suit of sap hide that was not specifically tailored for him incurs an Armor Check penalty increase to -4. Masterwork sap hide does not have an increased armor bonus, it is simply fit for enchantment.
