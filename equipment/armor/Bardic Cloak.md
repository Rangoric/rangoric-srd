# Bardic Cloak

*Armor extra.* Cost 100 gp; Weight 5 lb. ^stats

This cloak is typically constructed of thick wool with several plates of either leather or thin metal sewn inside. Special flame-retardant oils used in its construction give this cloak a fire resistance of *2*. Bards often sell these cloaks in many of the major cities of the realms where adventurers seek protection from the elements.
