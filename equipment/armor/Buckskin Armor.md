# Buckskin Armor

*Light armor.* Cost 15 gp; Armor Bonus +3; Maximum Dex Bonus +6; Armor Check Penalty 0; Arcane Spell Failure 10%; Speed 30 ft. (30 ft.), 20 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 15 lb. ^stats

This armor is composed of reinforced buckskin.

Barbarians often gather the skin from young bucks to outfit their villages with this type of armor for tribal wars. Armor of this ilk is often the first set a tribal elder gives to a young barbarian once he comes of age to hunt with the adults.
