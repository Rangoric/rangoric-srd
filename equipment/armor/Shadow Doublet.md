# Shadow Doublet

*Light armor.* Cost 50 gp; Armor Bonus +1; Maximum Dex Bonus +8; Armor Check Penalty –1; Arcane Spell Failure 12%; Speed 30 ft. (30 ft.), 20 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 10 lb. ^stats

This type of armor is referred to as a “shadow doublet” due to its construction of black or dark blue velvet. A thin lining of hardened leather is sewn to the inner lining of the jacket, offering some protection against blows. The wearer’s [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks receive a circumstance bonus of +2 in dark or shadowy areas.
