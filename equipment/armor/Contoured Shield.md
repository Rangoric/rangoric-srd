# Contoured Shield

*Shield.* Cost 75 gp; Shield Bonus +2; Armor Check Penalty –2; Arcane Spell Failure 15%; Weight 15 lb. ^stats

Essentially a heavy shield rigged to work like a buckler, a contoured shield is strapped to the shoulder and forearm, leaving the hand free. It is shaped to fit most humanoids and allows the use of an item in the shield hand just as a buckler does. However, the contoured shield is much more unwieldy than a buckler, and it imposes a -1 penalty on all attacks made with bows, crossbows, two-handed melee weapons, and off-hand melee weapons. This penalty stacks with those for fighting with your off hand and, if appropriate, for fighting with two weapons. In any case, if you use a weapon in your off hand, you don’t get the contoured shield’s AC bonus for the rest of the round.
