# Hauberk

*Medium armor.* Cost 125 gp; Armor Bonus +3; Maximum Dex Bonus +4; Armor Check Penalty –2; Arcane Spell Failure 20%; Speed 20 ft. (30 ft.), 15 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 25 lb. ^stats

A hauberk is a one-piece set of chain armor. It is similar to a chain shirt, except that it hangs lower, to just below the knee. Most hauberks have a hood attached that protects the wearer’s head. A hauberk is cheaper and easier to manufacture than regular chainmail.
