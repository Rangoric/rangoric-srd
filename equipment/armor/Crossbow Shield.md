# Crossbow Shield

*Shield.* Cost 85 gp; Shield Bonus +2; Armor Check Penalty –1; Arcane Spell Failure 15%; Weight 6 lb. ^stats

This is a rectangular wooden shield mounted on front of a crossbow. It provides protection only on rounds when you hold the crossbow at the ready (making an attack with it or readying an action to do so). The shield does not interfere with the normal function of the crossbow.
