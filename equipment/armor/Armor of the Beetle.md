# Armor of the Beetle

*Heavy armor.* Cost 2,000 gp; Armor Bonus +9; Maximum Dex Bonus 0; Armor Check Penalty –8; Arcane Spell Failure 45%; Speed 20 ft. (2) (30 ft.), 15 ft. (2) (20 ft.); Weight 60 lb. ^stats

This armor is similar to full plate, except that it is even heavier and special care has been taken to reinforcing the weak points in armor such as joints and midpoints. The down-side is that this limits the range of movement of the wearer slightly. Many adventurers who brave the underground prefer this armor due to the fact that it increases the chances of surviving any sort of cave-in. While wearing this armor, a character caught in the bury zone of a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Cave-Ins and Collapses (CR 8)|structural collapse]] sustains only 1d6 points of damage rather than the standard 3d6, and takes only 1d4 points of damage per round if pinned.
