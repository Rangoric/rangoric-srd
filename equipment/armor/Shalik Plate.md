# Shalik Plate

*Medium armor.* Cost 4,000 gp; Armor Bonus +7; Maximum Dex Bonus +3; Armor Check Penalty –4; Arcane Spell Failure 25%; Speed 20 ft. (30 ft.), 15 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 30 lb. ^stats

Shalik plate is extremely rare, as it must be created in its entirety by someone with skill as an artificer. Shalik plate is essentially a duplicate of metallic plate armor made out of the artificial material shalik. Shalik plate is nonmetallic, light, and durable. It never rusts, decays or tarnishes, and wipes clean easily with a damp cloth. Its only drawback is that it can only be repaired magically (usually a *mending* spell is sufficient).
