# Steelwood Splint

*Heavy armor.* Cost 500 gp; Armor Bonus +6; Maximum Dex Bonus +2; Armor Check Penalty –7; Arcane Spell Failure 40%; Speed 20 ft. (2) (30 ft.), 15 ft. (2) (20 ft.); Weight 40 lb. ^stats

As its name implies, this sturdy looking suit of splint armor is crafted from the rare and valuable [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Special Materials#^steelwood|steelwood]] tree. It is just as strong and slightly lighter than its more common cousin. No metal pieces are used to make the armor, making it appropriate for characters whose spiritual vows prohibit such things.
