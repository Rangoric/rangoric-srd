# Crested Hide Mail

*Medium armor.* Cost 50 gp; Armor Bonus +3; Maximum Dex Bonus +5; Armor Check Penalty –3; Arcane Spell Failure 20%; Speed 20 ft. (30 ft.), 15 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 25 lb. ^stats

Crested hide is a unique blend of spiked armor and rawhide, ideal for both offensive and defensive use. On a successful [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|combat maneuver check]] to grapple, the horn spikes on this armor deal 1d4 points of damage to the victim.
