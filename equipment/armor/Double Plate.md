# Double Plate

*Heavy armor.* Cost 3,500 gp; Armor Bonus +12; Maximum Dex Bonus 0; Armor Check Penalty –8; Arcane Spell Failure 50%; Speed 15 ft. (2) (30 ft.), 10 ft. (2) (20 ft.); Weight 100 lb. ^stats

Double plate is rare, although it is occasionally crafted for the truly stalwart. As the name implies, double plate is full plate armor made from extra-thick pieces of metal. A Strength of 16 is required to even be able to stand and walk in this armor. This is the best protection money can buy, however, and most professional warriors set their sights on enchanting a suit as soon as their skills and coin purse allows. While the slower speed of this armor might seem a disadvantage, most warriors offset this with *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Leggings of Mobility|leggings of mobility]]* or *boots of striding and springing*.
