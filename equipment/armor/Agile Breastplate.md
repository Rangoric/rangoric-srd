# Agile Breastplate

_Medium armor._ Cost 400 gp; Armor Bonus +6; Maximum Dex Bonus +3; Armor Check Penalty –4; Arcane Spell Failure 25%; Speed 20 ft. (30 ft.), 15 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 25 lb. ^stats

This breastplate is specially crafted in a manner that allows extra maneuverability for some physical activities. The armor check penalty for [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks made to climb or jump is only –1 (not doubled; masterwork and mithral versions of this armor reduce this penalty as well as the normal penalty).
