*Medium armor.* Cost 100 gp; Armor Bonus +4; Maximum Dex Bonus +3; Armor Check Penalty –4; Arcane Spell Failure 27%; Speed 20 ft. (30 ft.), 15 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 30 lb. ^stats
