# Blue Bone Mail

*Heavy armor.* Cost 1,500 gp; Armor Bonus +7; Maximum Dex Bonus +2; Armor Check Penalty –5; Arcane Spell Failure 35%; Speed 20 ft. (2) (30 ft.), 15 ft. (2) (20 ft.); Weight 35 lb. ^stats

Blue bone mail is a nonmetallic plate mail made with the bony armor plates of a large beast stitched onto a leather backing. It is similar to gnarled mail, but slightly lighter. Most blue bone mail is unenameled, allowing the soft blue color of the natural bone to stand for itself.
