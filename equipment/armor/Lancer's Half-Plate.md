# Lancer's Half-Plate

*Heavy armor.* Cost 2,000 gp; Armor Bonus +7; Maximum Dex Bonus +1; Armor Check Penalty –6; Arcane Spell Failure 35%; Speed 20 ft. (2) (30 ft.), 15 ft. (2) (20 ft.); Weight 50 lb. ^stats

This finely crafted half-plate offers substantial benefits to mounted wearers. If used in conjunction with a military saddle, lancer’s half-plate adds a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks related to staying in the saddle. This bonus stacks with the bonus provided by the military saddle. If you’re knocked unconscious while wearing a lancer’s half-plate and using a military saddle, you have an 85% chance to stay in the saddle (compared to a 75% chance for a military saddle alone).
