# Iron Mantle

*Medium armor.* Cost 150 gp; Armor Bonus +5; Maximum Dex Bonus +2; Armor Check Penalty –5; Arcane Spell Failure 25%; Speed 20 ft. (30 ft.), 15 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 30 lb. ^stats

Iron mantle armor is made from a number of iron plates held together with leather straps, and covers the wearer’s shoulders and chest. Iron mantle armor causes its wearer to suffer only a -3 penalty to Fortitude saving throws against nonlethal damage from [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Heat Dangers|heat exposure]], instead of the usual -4. Iron mantle armor can be donned in 4 minutes, donned hastily in 1 minute, and removed in 1 minute.
