*Heavy armor.* Cost 400 gp; Armor Bonus +6; Maximum Dex Bonus +1; Armor Check Penalty –5; Arcane Spell Failure 40%; Speed 20 ft. (2) (30 ft.), 15 ft. (2) (20 ft.); Weight 40 lb. ^stats
