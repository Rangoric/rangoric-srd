# Light Steel Quickdraw Shield

_Shield._ Cost 59 gp; Shield Bonus +1; Armor Check Penalty –2; Arcane Spell Failure 5%; Weight 7 lb. ^stats

This light steel shield is specially crafted with a series of straps to allow a character proficient in shields to ready or stow it on her back quickly and easily. If you have a base attack bonus of +1 or higher, you may don or put away a quickdraw shield as a swift action combined with a regular move. If you have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Two-Weapon Fighting|Two-Weapon Fighting]] feat, you can draw a light or one-handed weapon with one hand and a quickdraw shield with the other in the time it would normally take you to draw one weapon. If you have the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Quick Draw|Quick Draw]] feat, you may don or put away a quickdraw shield as a free action.
