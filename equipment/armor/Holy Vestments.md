# Holy Vestments

*Light armor.* Cost 15 gp; Armor Bonus +1; Maximum Dex Bonus +8; Armor Check Penalty –1; Arcane Spell Failure 5%; Speed 30 ft. (30 ft.), 20 ft. (20 ft.); Weight 5 lb. ^stats

Clerical holy vestments are blessed to withstand attacks, protecting their wearers from harm. Often made from silk and wool, these robes are both comfortable and easy to move in. The symbol of the patron deity is often embroidered upon the center of the chest of the robes.
