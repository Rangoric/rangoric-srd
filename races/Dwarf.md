## Dwarves

Size: Medium
Base Speed: 20ft
Perks:

- +2 Constitution
- +2 Wisdom
- -2 Charisma
- Slow and Steady: A dwarf's speed is never modified by armor or encumbrance.
- Darkvision: Dwarves can see in the dark up to 60 feet. Darkvision is black and white only, but it is otherwise like normal sight, and dwarves can function just fine with no light at all. ^dwarf-darkvision
- Defensive Training: +4 dodge bonus to Armor Class against monsters of the giant type. Any time a creature loses its Dexterity bonus (if any) to Armor Class, such as when it’s caught [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]], it loses its dodge bonus, too.
- Craftsman: +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] checks that are related to stone or metal.
- Greed: +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Appraise|Appraise]] checks that are related to stone or metal items, or made to determine the price of nonmagical goods that contain precious metals or gemstones.
- Hatred: +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against orcs and goblinoids.
- Hardy: +2 racial bonus on saving throws against poison, spells, and spell-like effects.
- Stability: A dwarf gains a +4 bonus to its [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] and [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] when standing on the ground (but not when climbing, flying, riding, or otherwise not standing firmly on the ground). ^stability
- Stonecunning: This ability grants a dwarf a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks to notice unusual stonework, such as sliding walls, stonework traps, new construction (even when built to match the old), unsafe stone surfaces, shaky stone ceilings, and the like. Something that isn’t stone but that is disguised as stone also counts as unusual stonework. A dwarf who merely comes within 10 feet of unusual stonework can make an Investigation check as if he were actively searching, and a dwarf can use the Investigation skill to find stonework traps as a rogue can. A dwarf can also intuit depth, sensing his approximate depth underground as naturally as a human can sense which way is up. ^dwarf-stonecunning
- Weapon Familiarity: Dwarves are proficient with battleaxes, heavy picks, and warhammers, and treat any weapon with the word “dwarven” in its name as a martial weapon. ^dwarf-weapon-familiarity

Languages:

- Common
- Dwarven

Bonus Languages:

- Giant
- Gnome
- Goblin
- Orc
- Terran
- Undercommon
