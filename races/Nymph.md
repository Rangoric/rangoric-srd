## Nymphs

Type: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fey|Fey]]
Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- +3 Charisma
- +1 Wisdom
- -2 Strength
- -1 Constitution
- Spell-Like Abilities: A nymph gains her spellcasting and spell-like abilities from the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Nymph (Racial Class)|Nymph (Racial Class)]].
- Ageless: Nymphs do not age like mortals, and cannot die from old age. Upon reaching young adulthood (roughly 20 human years), nymphs cease to age. Unlike other fey species, they have no control over this part of their physiology.
- Fey Blood: Nymphs have a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against illusion and enchantment spells and spell-like abilities, which increases by +1 for every two levels they attain (see [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Fey Blood|fey blood]]).
- Low-Light Vision: Nymphs have [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]].

Languages:

- Fae
- Sylvan

Bonus Languages:

- Auran
- Celestial
- Common
- Elven

Level Adjustment: +1 (see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monsters as Races#^level-adjustment|level adjustment]]) with the racial class. A nymph that starts play with a regular class and all the abilities of her race instead has level adjustment +12.

Racial Class: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Nymph (Racial Class)|Nymph (Racial Class)]]

### Description

The parable “beauty is only skin deep” is often applied to nymphs, but those who learn more about them soon learn that they are more than meets the eye. While nymphs are unnaturally beautiful, they also represent the beauty of personality and belief. They are so stunningly beautiful in body and soul that they can blind and even kill if someone lays eyes on them.

**Personality:** Nymphs prefer to lead a solitary life, sequestered away from those things they hate: ugliness and evil. Occasionally, they join with others to further the cause of all that is good and beautiful against their rivals.

**Physical Description:** Nymphs always appear as young attractive women with pointed ears and exceptional figures, though they tend to appear most like the dominant race of the area around them (human in a human village, elf in a forest safehold, etc.). Their hair is luxuriant and their clothing always accentuates all these qualities.

**Relations:** Nymphs are solitary creatures by nature, though they will sometimes assist others in fighting evil. The native fauna, instead, are the closest thing to companions that nymphs have. Those who have been lucky enough to call them companions say that they are charming, alluring, and poised people gifted with a quick wit.

**Alignment:** Like all fey, nymphs are chaotic by nature, but they are always chaotic good.

**Nymph Lands:** Aside from their original home in the fey realm, nymphs have been seen in forested hills and meadows, and occasionally among field crops and urban parks.

**Religion:** Like all fey, nymphs do not have a specific religion other than that of Nature. They revere all of Mother Nature’s works and wonders, considering them sacred. In extremely rare moments, particularly among nymphs journeying the mortal realms, they have taken on the worship of a nature divinity or the local divinity of beauty and physical perfection.

**Language:** Nymphs speak the language Fae, a Sylvan dialect, as their native language. Since they seldom interact with mortals, they speak Common haltingly and with a heavy accent.

**Names:** Nymph names commonly come from words that represent beauty or beautiful objects. Even words that sound beautiful themselves are used.

**Adventurers:** Nymphs leaving their secluded homes only take up adventuring to help rid the world of evil and ugliness.
