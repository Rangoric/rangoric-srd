## Vishkanyas

Type: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^vishkanya|vishkanya]])
Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- +2 Dexterity
- +2 Charisma
- -2 Wisdom
- Low-Light Vision: Vishkanyas have [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]].
- Keen Senses: +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks.
- Limber: +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Escape Artist|Escape Artist]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks.
- Poison Resistance: A vishkanya has a racial bonus on saving throws against poison equal to its character level.
- Poison Use: Vishkanyas have [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Poison Use|poison use]].
- Toxic: A number of times per day equal to his Constitution modifier (minimum 1/day), a vishkanya can envenom a weapon that he wields with his toxic saliva or blood (using blood requires the vishkanya to be injured when he uses this ability). Applying venom in this way is a swift action. *Vishkanya Venom*: Injury; *save* Fort DC 10 + 1/2 the vishkanya's Hit Dice + the vishkanya's Constitution modifier; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d2 Dex; *cure* 1 save. *This poison is written in Pathfinder's format (frequency and cure, as the PSRD has it) until poisons get a topic of their own; how poisons work in general is under [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Poison|poison]].*
- Weapon Familiarity: Vishkanyas are always proficient with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Descriptions|blowguns]], [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Descriptions|kukri]], and [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Descriptions|shuriken]].

### Description

Vishkanyas are a race of exotic humanoids with poisonous blood. Possessed of an alien beauty, these graceful humanoids see the world through serpentine eyes of burnished gold. Their supple skin is covered with tiny scales, often of a light green, which are sometimes arrayed in patterns not unlike those of a serpent. They cannot be generalized as good or evil, but since they truly speak with forked tongues, they are content to accept the gold they're offered and leave questions of morality to others.
