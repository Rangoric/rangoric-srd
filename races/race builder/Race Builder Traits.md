# Race Builder Traits

The racial traits for the [[rangoric-srd/races/race builder/Race Builder|Race Builder]], by category, with their race point (RP) costs. Benefits are given in their full wording, from [[path srd 3.5/rules/advanced-race-guide/race-builder/Racial Traits (Race Builder)|Racial Traits (Race Builder)]]; a few traits whose wording names a setting’s places, or that it doesn’t list on their own, are given in short. Skill names are Rangoric’s. The **Notes** column links Rangoric’s note for a trait when it has one (an alternate racial trait or a trait in a race note).

**Costs** are the costs listed in each trait’s heading. "+1 each" means each extra purchase costs 1 RP more than the last.

**Tiers**: **S** standard, **A** advanced, **M** monstrous. A race takes at most 3, 4 or 5 traits per category by power level, and only traits of its tier or lower.

**Planes in prerequisites** are named by alignment ("a good-aligned plane", "an evil-aligned plane") or as "the deep underground"; the elemental planes and the Plane of Shadow keep their names.

The racial-trait notes that are builder traits carry their category and tier as tags (`racial-trait/defense`, `racial-trait/standard`) and their cost as `race-points`; [[rangoric-srd/races/race builder/Racial Traits by Category.base|Racial Traits by Category]] lists them.

## Feat and Skill Traits

Translating skills: Bluff → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], Diplomacy → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], Disguise → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], Survival → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]], Climb and Swim → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]], Ride and Handle Animal → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]], Disable Device and Knowledge (engineering) → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]], Knowledge (local) and (history) → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]], Knowledge (dungeoneering) → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]], Knowledge (planes) → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]], Knowledge (arcana) → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Arcana|Arcana]], Spellcraft to identify → the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|Magic skill]], Use Magic Device → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Emulation|Emulation]], Fly → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] to fly, Intimidate → [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]]. Perception in Rangoric is noticing by sight and sound; **finding** hidden things (traps, secret doors) is [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]], so a Pathfinder Perception bonus "to find" becomes Investigation.
