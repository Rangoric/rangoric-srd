# Aspect of the Dawn

One of the [[rangoric-srd/races/aspects-of-nature/Aspects of Nature|Aspects of Nature]], taken through the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nature's Aspect|Nature's Aspect]] feat.

Adherents to the Aspect of the Dawn find the rising sun symbolizes the opportunities of a new day. They also see the rising sun as a symbol of good triumphing over evil, and are known to extend this philosophy to daily life, seeking out the “rising sun” in the darker underbelly of society. They favor clothing in the soft yellows, pinks, and purples found in the sunrise sky, since these colors compliment their now fairer complexions.

**Skin**: Pale ‘peaches and cream’ to light mocha complexion; soft, slightly warmer than normal skin.

**Hair**: Lightens to a glossy gold-streaked blonde; straight with reflective qualities.

**Favored Colors**: Pale purple, pink, and light yellow.

**Manifestation**: There is a slight yellow glowing quality to the creature’s skin, but not luminescent enough to cast light or be visible in darkness. As creatures of the twilight, they gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] 60 ft. (if already possessed, this range increases by 30 ft.).
