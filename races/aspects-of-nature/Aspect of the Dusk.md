# Aspect of the Dusk

One of the [[rangoric-srd/races/aspects-of-nature/Aspects of Nature|Aspects of Nature]], taken through the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Nature's Aspect|Nature's Aspect]] feat.

Those following the Aspect of the Dusk see the coming night of each day as a daily symbol of the greater cycle of death and foreshadowed renewal. These creatures are gloomier than their Autumn-Aspected brethren, though they find them to be the most understanding of the other Aspects. They favor darker clothes that blend with the blues, indigoes, and violets found in the sunsets of the twilight sky.

**Skin**: Shadowed bluish tint; soft, cool, and slightly clammy to the touch.

**Hair**: Dark chocolate-brown to black with deep green or blue highlights; curly with reflective characteristics.

**Favored Colors**: Blue, indigo, and violet.

**Manifestation**: A slight purplish radiance to the creature’s skin but not strong enough to be visible in darkness. As creatures of the fading light of day, they gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] 60 ft. (if already possessed, this range increases by 30 ft.).
