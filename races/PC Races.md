##### Race and Languages

All characters know how to speak Common. A dwarf, elf, gnome, half-elf, half-orc, or halfling also speaks a racial language, as appropriate. A character who has an Intelligence bonus at 1st level speaks other languages as well, one extra language per point of Intelligence bonus as a starting character.

**Literacy**: Any character can read and write all the languages he or she speaks. ^literacy

**Class-Related Languages**: Clerics, druids, and wizards can choose certain languages as bonus languages even if they’re not on the lists found in the race descriptions. These class-related languages are as follows:  
 ^class-related-languages

_Cleric_: Abyssal, Celestial, Infernal.

_Druid_: Sylvan.

_Wizard_: Draconic.

##### Alternate Racial Traits

Each race lists alternate racial traits, notes in `races/racial-traits`. A character can exchange one or several of their race's normal traits for alternate ones, as each alternate trait says, but can't exchange the same racial trait more than once. A race's subtypes are bundles of its alternate traits. ^alternate-racial-traits

##### Defensive Training

A race with defensive training gets a +4 dodge bonus to Armor Class against monsters of the giant type. Any time a creature loses its Dexterity bonus (if any) to Armor Class, such as when it’s caught [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^flat-footed|flat-footed]], it loses its dodge bonus, too. ^defensive-training

##### Small Characters

A Small character gets a +1 size bonus to Armor Class, a +1 size bonus on attack rolls, and a +4 size bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks. A Small character’s carrying capacity is three-quarters of that of a Medium character.

A Small character generally moves about two-thirds as fast as a Medium character.

A Small character must use smaller weapons than a Medium character.
