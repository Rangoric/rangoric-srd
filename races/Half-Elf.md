## Half-Elves

Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- +2 to one ability score of the player's choice, chosen at character creation.
- Adaptability: Half-elves receive [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] as a bonus feat at 1st level.
- Elf Blood: For all effects related to race, a half-elf is considered both an [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]] and a [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]]. ^half-elf-elven-blood
- Elven Immunities: Immunity to sleep spells and similar magical effects, and a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against enchantment spells or effects.
- Keen Senses: +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks.
- Low-Light Vision: A half-elf can see twice as far as a human in starlight, moonlight, torchlight, and similar conditions of poor illumination. She retains the ability to distinguish color and detail under these conditions. ^half-elf-low-light-vision

Languages:

- Common
- Elven

Bonus Languages:

- Any (other than secret languages, such as Druidic)

### Subtypes

Bundles of alternate traits for half-elves of a particular background.

- **Drow-Descended**: Clearly bearing the features of their dark elf parents, and seen as a threat whatever their intent: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Drow-Blooded|Drow-Blooded]] and [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Drow Magic|Drow Magic]].
- **Elf-Raised**: Embraced by their elven relatives and raised with traditional elven training: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Ancestral Arms|Ancestral Arms]]. (The ARG adds arcane training, which is built on favored class and isn't in Rangoric.)
- **Human-Raised**: Born and raised in accepting human communities: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Integrated|Integrated]] and [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Wary|Wary]].

**Variants from Unearthed Arcana.** None has ability scores of its own; all keep the half-elf's +2 to one ability score.

- **Fire Half-Elf**: Enjoying the finer things in life, but tempered by their human side. Not the full fire race traits: +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against water creatures, including extraplanar creatures from the Elemental Plane of Water, and −1 penalty on all saving throws against spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural abilities used by water creatures, including extraplanar creatures from the Elemental Plane of Water.
- **Aquatic Half-Elf**: Children of aquatic elves and humans, often diplomats between the two. [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Aquatic Race Traits|Aquatic Race Traits]], with a swim speed of 40 feet. +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks.
- **Arctic Half-Elf**: Wanderers who carry messages and rumors between communities. [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Cold Endurance|Cold Endurance]]. +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks.
- **Desert Half-Elf**: Diplomats and negotiators for their human or elf communities. [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Heat Endurance|Heat Endurance]]. +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks. Keen senses covers Investigation only.
- **Jungle Half-Elf**: Born where elves bring in human blood to invigorate their families, or want a mixed-race emissary. +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks.
