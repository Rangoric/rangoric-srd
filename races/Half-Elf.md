## Half-Elves

Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- Immunity to sleep spells and similar magical effects, and a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against enchantment spells or effects.
- Low-Light Vision: A half-elf can see twice as far as a human in starlight, moonlight, torchlight, and similar conditions of poor illumination. She retains the ability to distinguish color and detail under these conditions. ^half-elf-low-light-vision
- +1 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks.
- +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks.
- Elven Blood: For all effects related to race, a half-elf is considered an [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]]. ^half-elf-elven-blood

Languages:

- Common
- Elven

Bonus Languages:

- Any (other than secret languages, such as Druidic)
