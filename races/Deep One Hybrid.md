## Deep One Hybrids

Type: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^human|human]], [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^deep-one|deep one]])
Size: Medium
Base Speed: 20ft, swim 30ft
Perks:

- +2 Constitution
- +2 Wisdom
- -2 Dexterity
- Slow: A deep one hybrid's base speed is 20 feet, as it walks with a shuffling gait.
- Low-Light Vision: Deep one hybrids have [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]].
- Natural Armor: A deep one hybrid's thick skin and dense fatty tissues grant it a +1 natural armor bonus to AC.
- Final Change (Su): A mere 1d12 months after a deep one hybrid reaches venerable age (at 60 years old), it dies a painful, agonizing death, only to have its body transform into that of a mature [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Deep One|deep one]]. This transformation functions as the _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reincarnate|reincarnate]]_ spell, with the newly formed deep one gaining the following modifications to its physical ability scores: +6 Strength, –2 Dexterity, and +6 Constitution.
- Sea Longing (Ex): Every 24 hours a deep one hybrid spends in an area more than 10 miles from the sea, its longing attacks its Will at +10, and on a hit it takes 1 point of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^ability-drained|Wisdom drain]].
- Take to the Water (Ex): A deep one hybrid can hold its breath 10 times longer than a human can, and gains a +2 bonus on Initiative checks and Reflex Defense while swimming. A deep one hybrid has a swim speed of 30 feet, which works as described under [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|movement modes]], and gains a +8 racial bonus on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim.

Languages:

- Common
- Aklo

Bonus Languages:

- Abyssal
- Aquan
- Dwarven
- Elven
- Giant
- Gnome
- Goblin
- Halfling
- Orc
- Undercommon

### Age, Height and Weight

A deep one hybrid ages at the same rate as a half-orc: use the [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc#Age, Height and Weight|half-orc]]'s age, height and weight tables. At venerable age it undergoes its final change (above).

### Description

Deep one hybrids are the spawn of humans and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Deep One|deep ones]]. They are most comfortable with others of their kind, and typically cluster in small, insular settlements where they can assume positions of authority. They keep any humans within their towns subservient and cowed, making sure those other residents know better than to act against local laws. Though deep ones are devoutly religious, they usually cloak their true beliefs under a facade of more conventional worship, believing that by doing so they can avoid suspicion from visitors and nearby societies.

Deep one hybrids are hearty and devout, but tend to be somewhat slow and awkward in their movements. They are defined by their class levels—they don't possess racial Hit Dice. A sample is the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/mythos/Deep One Hybrid, 1st-Level Cleric|deep one hybrid, 1st-level cleric]].
