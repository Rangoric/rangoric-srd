## Half-Orcs

Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- +2 Strength
- -2 Intelligence
- -2 Charisma
- A half-orc’s starting Intelligence score is always at least 3. If the adjustment would lower the character’s score to 1 or 2, his score is nevertheless 3.
- Darkvision: Half-orcs (and orcs) can see in the dark up to 60 feet. Darkvision is black and white only, but it is otherwise like normal sight, and half-orcs can function just fine with no light at all. ^half-orc-darkvision
- Orc Blood: For all effects related to race, a half-orc is considered an orc. ^half-orc-orc-blood

Languages:

- Common
- Orc

Bonus Languages:

- Draconic
- Giant
- Gnoll
- Goblin
- Abyssal
