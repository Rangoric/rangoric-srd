## Elves

Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- +2 Dexterity
- +2 Intelligence
- -2 Constitution
- Elven Immunities: Immunity to magic sleep effects, and a +2 racial saving throw bonus against enchantment spells or effects.
- Elven Magic: +2 racial bonus on caster level checks made to overcome spell resistance, and a +2 racial bonus on skill checks made to identify the properties of magic items.
- Keen Senses: +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks. An elf who merely passes within 5 feet of a secret or concealed door is entitled to an Investigation check to notice it as if she were actively looking for it.
- Low-Light Vision: An elf can see twice as far as a human in starlight, moonlight, torchlight, and similar conditions of poor illumination. She retains the ability to distinguish color and detail under these conditions. ^elf-low-light-vision
- Weapon Familiarity: Elves are proficient with longbows (including composite longbows), longswords, rapiers, and shortbows (including composite shortbows), and treat any weapon with the word “elven” in its name as a martial weapon.

Languages:

- Common
- Elven

Bonus Languages:

- Celestial
- Draconic
- Gnoll
- Gnome
- Goblin
- Orc
- Sylvan

Paragon Class: [[rangoric-srd/classes/Elf Paragon|Elf Paragon]]

### Subtypes

Bundles of alternate traits for elves of a particular background.

- **Arctic Elf**: Born and raised in frozen lands, with nights that last for weeks: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Darkvision (Elf Trait)|Darkvision]], [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Desert Runner|Desert Runner]], and [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Elemental Resistance|Elemental Resistance]].
- **Dusk Elf**: Linked to the night itself and attuned to darkness and shadow, though not to evil: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Arcane Focus|Arcane Focus]], [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Darkvision (Elf Trait)|Darkvision]], [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Dreamspeaker|Dreamspeaker]], and [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Silent Hunter|Silent Hunter]].
- **Savage Elf**: Adapted to lands where every day is a struggle to survive: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Eternal Grudge|Eternal Grudge]] and [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Fleet-Footed|Fleet-Footed]].
- **Tower Elf**: Clans who have lived for generations in schools of wizardry: [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Arcane Focus|Arcane Focus]] and [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Urbanite|Urbanite]].

**Variants from Unearthed Arcana.** A variant's ability scores take the place of the elf's, but keep the elf's +2 Intelligence.

- **Fire Elf**: Quick of action and thought, but sharing the elf's frailty. +2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, −2 Constitution, −2 Charisma. [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Fire Race Traits|Fire Race Traits]].
- **Aquatic Elf**: Elves of the kelp forests and sargasso groves beneath the waves. +2 Dexterity. [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Aquatic Race Traits|Aquatic Race Traits]], with a swim speed of 40 feet. [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Superior Low-Light Vision|Superior Low-Light Vision]] in place of low-light vision.
- **Arctic Elf**: Living in oases of warmth hidden among the ice, where hot springs steam in the freezing air. +2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, −2 Strength. [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Cold Endurance|Cold Endurance]]. +2 racial bonus on any one [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] skill (chosen at creation), and +2 on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks in arctic environments. Keen senses covers Perception only: no Investigation bonus and no secret-door sense.
- **Desert Elf**: Nomads who herd horses, cattle and goats across the sands, welcome everywhere for their news and trade goods. +2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, −2 Strength. [[rangoric-srd/races/racial-traits/Heat Endurance|Heat Endurance]]. Weapon familiarity: proficient with the scimitar, rapier and shortbow (including composite shortbow) in place of the elf's four weapons. +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks. Keen senses has no Perception bonus.
- **Jungle Elf**: More primal elves, the remnant of civilizations the jungle swallowed. Weapon familiarity: proficient with the handaxe, rapier, shortsword and shortbow (including composite shortbow) in place of the elf's four weapons. +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks. No secret-door sense.
