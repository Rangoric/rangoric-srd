## Humans

Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- +2 to one ability score of the player's choice, chosen at character creation.
- Bonus Feat: Humans select one extra feat at 1st level.
- Skilled: Humans gain one extra [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^skill-points|skill point]] at 1st level and one more whenever they gain a level.

Languages:

- Common

Bonus Languages:

- Any (other than secret languages, such as Druidic)
