# Tengu Rogue

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|tengu]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-class-bonuses|rogue]] level._

Choose a weapon from those listed under the tengu's [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|swordtrained]] ability. Add a +1/2 bonus on critical hit confirmation rolls with that weapon (maximum bonus +4). This bonus does not stack with [[rangoric-srd/feats/Critical Focus|Critical Focus]].
