# Goblin Rogue

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|goblin]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-class-bonuses|rogue]] level._

Add a +1 bonus on the rogue's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Rogue#^rogue-sneak-attack|sneak attack]] damage rolls during the surprise round or before the target has acted in combat.
