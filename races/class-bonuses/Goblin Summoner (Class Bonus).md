# Goblin Summoner

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|goblin]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-class-bonuses|summoner]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner#^unchained-summoner-class-bonuses|unchained summoner]] level._

Add +1/4 evolution point to the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]]'s [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-evolutions|evolution pool]]. These bonus evolution points must be spent on evolutions that deal fire damage or protect the eidolon from fire (for example, resistance, energy attacks, immunity, breath weapon, and so on).
