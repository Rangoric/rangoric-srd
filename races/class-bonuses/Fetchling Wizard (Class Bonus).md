# Fetchling Wizard

*A [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|fetchling]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-class-bonuses|wizard]] level.*

Add one spell from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|wizard spell list]] to wizard's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-spellbooks|spellbook]]. The spell must be at or below the highest level he can cast and be of the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Schools of Magic#Illusion|illusion (shadow) subschool]] or have the darkness descriptor.
