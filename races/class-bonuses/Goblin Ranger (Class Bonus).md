# Goblin Ranger

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|goblin]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-class-bonuses|ranger]] level._

Gain a +1/2 bonus on damage dealt to dogs (and doglike creatures) and horses (and horselike creatures).
