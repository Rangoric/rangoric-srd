# Human Fighter

Add +1 to the fighter's [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] when resisting two [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers|combat maneuvers]] of the character's choice.
