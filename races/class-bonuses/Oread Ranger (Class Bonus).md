# Oread Ranger

_An [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|oread]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-class-bonuses|ranger]] level._

Add +1/4 to the natural armor bonus of the ranger's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^ranger-animal-companion|animal companion]]. If the ranger ever replaces his animal companion, the new animal companion gains this bonus.
