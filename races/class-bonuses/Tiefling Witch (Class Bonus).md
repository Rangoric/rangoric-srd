# Tiefling Witch

*A [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|tiefling]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-class-bonuses|witch]] level.*

The witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-familiar|familiar]] gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 1 against cold, electricity, or fire. For each witch level after the first, increase the familiar's resistance to one of these energy types by 1 (maximum 5 for any one type). If the witch ever replaces her familiar, the new familiar has these resistances.
