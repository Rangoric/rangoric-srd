# Gnome Druid

*A [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Druid#^druid-class-bonuses|druid]] level.*

The druid gains energy resistance 1 against acid, cold, electricity, or fire. For each druid level after the first, increase her resistance to one of these energy types by +1 (maximum 10 for any one type).
