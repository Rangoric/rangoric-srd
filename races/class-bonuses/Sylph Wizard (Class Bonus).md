# Sylph Wizard

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|sylph]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-class-bonuses|wizard]] level._

Select one [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-arcane-school|arcane school power]] from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Air Elemental School|air]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/arcane-schools/Wood Elemental School|wood]] [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-elemental-schools|elemental schools]] that the wizard can currently use. The wizard treats her class level as though it were +1/6 higher (to a maximum of +2) when determining the effects of that power.
