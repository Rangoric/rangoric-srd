# Undine Wizard

_An [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|undine]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-class-bonuses|wizard]] level._

Add one spell from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Cleric Spell List|cleric]], [[rangoric-srd/spells/Druid Spell List|druid]], or [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|wizard spell list]] with the water descriptor to the wizard's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-spellbooks|spellbook]]. This spell must be at least one level below the highest spell level he can cast. This spell is treated as one level higher unless it also appears on the wizard spell list.
