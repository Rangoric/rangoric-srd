# Half-Elf Witch

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|half-elf]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-class-bonuses|witch]] level._

Add one spell from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Witch Spell List|witch spell list]] to the witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-familiar|familiar]]. This spell must be at least one level below the highest spell level she can cast. If the witch ever replaces her familiar, the new familiar knows these bonus spells.
