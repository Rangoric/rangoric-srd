# Gnome Gunslinger

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Gunslinger#^gunslinger-class-bonuses|gunslinger]] level._

The gunslinger reduces the amount of time needed to restore a [[rangoric-srd/rules/conditions/Broken (Condition)|broken]] firearm using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Gunsmithing|Gunsmithing]] feat by 5 minutes (maximum reduction of 50 minutes).
