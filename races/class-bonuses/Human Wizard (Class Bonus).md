# Human Wizard

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-class-bonuses|wizard]] level._

Add one spell from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|wizard spell list]] to the wizard's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Wizard#^wizard-spellbooks|spellbook]]. This spell must be at least one level below the highest spell level the wizard can cast.
