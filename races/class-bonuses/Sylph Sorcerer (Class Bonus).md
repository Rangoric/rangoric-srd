# Sylph Sorcerer

*A [[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|sylph]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-class-bonuses|sorcerer]] level.*

Choose a [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-bloodline|bloodline power]] from the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Djinni Bloodline|djinni]] or [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Elemental Bloodline|elemental (air) bloodline]] that the sorcerer can use. The sorcerer treats her class level as though it were +1/2 higher (to a maximum of +4) when determining the effects of that power.
