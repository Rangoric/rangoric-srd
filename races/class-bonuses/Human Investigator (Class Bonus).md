# Human Investigator

*A [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-class-bonuses|investigator]] level.*

Add one extract formula from the investigator's [[rangoric-srd/spells/Alchemist Formula List|formula list]] to his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Investigator#^investigator-alchemy-2|formula book]]. This formula must be at least 1 formula level below the highest level the investigator can create.
