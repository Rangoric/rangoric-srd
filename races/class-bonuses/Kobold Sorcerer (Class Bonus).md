# Kobold Sorcerer

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|kobold]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-class-bonuses|sorcerer]] level._

Choose acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage. Add +1/2 point of the chosen energy damage to spells that deal the chosen energy damage cast by the sorcerer.
