# Undine Cleric

_An [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|undine]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Cleric#^cleric-class-bonuses|cleric]] level._

Add a +1 bonus on caster level checks to overcome the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] of creatures with the aquatic or water subtype.
