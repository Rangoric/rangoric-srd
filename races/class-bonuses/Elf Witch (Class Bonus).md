# Elf Witch

*An [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|elf]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-class-bonuses|witch]] level.*

Add one spell from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Witch Spell List|witch spell list]] to the witch's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Witch#^witch-familiar|familiar]]. This spell must be at least one level lower than the highest-level spell she can cast. If the witch ever replaces her familiar, the new familiar knows these bonus spells.
