# Half-Orc Sorcerer

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-class-bonuses|sorcerer]] level._

Add +1/2 point of fire damage to spells that deal fire damage cast by the sorcerer.
