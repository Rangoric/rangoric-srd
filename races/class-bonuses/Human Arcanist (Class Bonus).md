# Human Arcanist

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-class-bonuses|arcanist]] level._

Add one spell from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|arcanist spell list]] to the arcanist's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Arcanist#^arcanist-spellbooks|spellbook]]. The spell must be at least 1 spell level below the highest level the arcanist can cast.
