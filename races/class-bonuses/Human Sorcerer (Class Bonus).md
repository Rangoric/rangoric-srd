# Human Sorcerer

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|human]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer#^sorcerer-class-bonuses|sorcerer]] level._

Add one spell known from the [[rangoric-srd/spells/Sorcerer and Wizards Spell List|sorcerer spell list]]. This spell must be at least one level below the highest spell level the sorcerer can cast.
