# Gnome Hunter

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-class-bonuses|hunter]] level._

Add DR 1/magic to the hunter's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Hunter#^hunter-animal-companion|animal companion]]. For each hunter level after the first, the DR increases by 1/2 (maximum DR 10/magic). If the hunter replaces her animal companion, the new companion gains this damage reduction.
