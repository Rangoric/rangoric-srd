# Half-Orc Magus

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|half-orc]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-class-bonuses|magus]] level._

Add +1/2 point of fire damage to spells that deal fire damage cast by the magus.
