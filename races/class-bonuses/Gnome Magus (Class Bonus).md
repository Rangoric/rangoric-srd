# Gnome Magus

_A [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|gnome]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-class-bonuses|magus]] level._

Add one of the following weapon special abilities to the list of weapon special abilities the magus may add to his weapon using his [[rangoric-srd/classes/Magus#^magus-arcane-pool|arcane pool]]: _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Defending|defending]], [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Ghost Touch (Weapon Special Ability)|ghost touch]], [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Merciful|merciful]], [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Mighty Cleaving|mighty cleaving]], [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Vicious|vicious]]_; _[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Allying|allying]], [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Conductive|conductive]], [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Corrosive|corrosive]], [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Acid Burst|corrosive burst]], [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/weapon-abilities/Menacing|menacing]]_. Once an ability has been chosen with this bonus, it cannot be changed.
