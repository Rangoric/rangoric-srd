# Fetchling Summoner

*A [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|fetchling]] [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#^class-bonuses|class bonus]], gained for each [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-class-bonuses|summoner]] or [[rangoric-srd/classes/Unchained Summoner#^unchained-summoner-class-bonuses|unchained summoner]] level.*

The summoner's [[rangoric-srd/classes/Summoner#^summoner-eidolon|eidolon]] gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 1 against either cold or electricity. For each summoner level after the first, he increases his eidolon's resistance to one of those energy types by 1 (maximum 10 for any one energy type).
