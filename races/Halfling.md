## Halflings

Size: [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#Small Characters|Small]]

- +1 Racial Bonus on saving throws

- +1 racial bonus with thrown weapons and slings
- +2 racial bonus on perception

- Common
- Halfling

- Elven
- Gnome

- Orc
