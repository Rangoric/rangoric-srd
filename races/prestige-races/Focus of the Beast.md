# Focus of the Beast

One of the [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Prestige Races|prestige races]]; how enchantments of the flesh work, and what they cost, is there.

Those that choose this focus are seeking to become more feral and wild, gaining natural armor, powerful natural weaponry, and various abilities related to natural animals.

| Step | Cost | Minimum Level | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| [[#Wild Creature\|Wild Creature]] | 4,500 XP | 5th | Feral |

### Feral Creature

**Example:** Feral Dwarf  
**Cost:** 3,500 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 4th  
**Prerequisite:** None  
**Unavailable to:** Creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Green|Focus of the Green]], [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Seersighted|Seersighted]], Serpent, or Wyrm

**Details:** A feral creature has grown a thick coat of heavy fur. This fur provides a slight armoring improvement, as well as a resistance to cold.

**Game Effects:** Gain a natural armor bonus of +2 and cold resistance 5 (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance to energy]]).

### Wild Creature

**Example:** Wild Dwarf  
**Cost:** 4,500 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 5th  
**Prerequisite:** Feral  
**Unavailable to:** Non-feral races.

**Details:** A wild creature has grown sharp fangs and wicked claws. These features can be used to make martial unarmed attacks. The character’s features change noticeably, with the jaw extending and the hands growing.

**Game Effects:** Gain natural weaponry: Claws do 1d6 damage, crit x2; Bite does 1d8 damage, crit x3.

### Snouted Creature

**Example:** Snouted Dwarf  
**Cost:** 5,500 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 6th  
**Prerequisite:** Wild  
**Unavailable to:** Non-wild races.

**Details:** A snouted creature has grown an enlarged, hypersensitive nose. The character’s features change noticeably, usually with the face elongating into an animal-like snout.

**Game Effects:** Gain the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Scent|scent]] special ability.

### Tailed Creature

**Example:** Tailed Dwarf  
**Cost:** 6,500 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 7th  
**Prerequisite:** Snouted  
**Unavailable to:** Non-wild races.

**Details:** A tailed creature has grown a long, prehensile tail that can be used to hold objects or to suspend himself from an overhead tree branch or pole. The tail may be used as a third limb to wield a weapon in combat, although the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Multiattack|Multiattack]] feat is required to do so without great penalties. The tail may be as long as the character is tall, and the player may, with the GM’s approval, choose the look and feel of the new appendage.

**Game Effects:** Gains a prehensile tail.
