# Focus of the Hordes

One of the [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Prestige Races|prestige races]]; how enchantments of the flesh work, and what they cost, is there.

Creatures choosing the Focus of the Hordes covet the aggressive power and stealthy nature goblins and their kin possess. Adherents to this path seek a means to enhance their own bodies in a similar vein. Following this focus slowly changes the creature into a more primal and aggressive form.

### Goblinoid Creature

**Example:** Goblinoid half-elf  
**Cost:** 2,800 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 3rd  
**Prerequisite:** None  
**Unavailable to:** Creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Changeling|Focus of the Changeling]], Fae-Sight, [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Fey|Fey]], [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Green|Green]], Serpent, or Wyrm

**Details:** The eyes of the goblinoid creature become sensitive to light and provide the creature with the ability to see in the dark. The creature becomes more alert to his surroundings and has learned to move in a stealthy manner.

**Game Effects:** The creature’s subtype becomes [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^goblinoid|goblinoid]]. Gains [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] (range of 60 ft.), [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] (Perception) as a bonus feat (in place of 3.5’s Alertness), and a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks to move silently.

### Orc-Blooded Creature

**Example:** Orc-blooded half-elf  
**Cost:** 4,000 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 4th  
**Prerequisite:** Goblinoid  
**Unavailable to:** Non-goblinoid creatures

**Details:** The creature’s physiology changes, mimicking that of an orc, providing greater fortitude and improved vision.

**Game Effects:** The creature’s subtype becomes [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^orc|orc]], and for all special abilities and effects, an orc-blooded character is considered an orc. Orc-blooded creatures can use special orc weapons or magic items with race-specific orc powers. Gain +2 inherent bonus to Constitution and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] (range of 120 ft.).

### Hobgoblinoid Creature

**Example:** Hobgoblinoid half-elf  
**Cost:** 4,800 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 5th  
**Prerequisite:** Orc-blooded  
**Unavailable to:** Non-orc-blooded races.

**Details:** A hobgoblinoid creature is more aggressive and stronger than the less evolved goblinoid creature. They are also stealthier and have a heightened alertness surpassing that of the goblinoid.

**Game Effects:** The creature’s subtype returns to goblinoid. Gain a +2 inherent bonus to Strength, and gain a +5 insight bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks. Gain a +4 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks.

### Ogrish Creature

**Example:** Ogrish half-elf  
**Cost:** 7,250 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 8th  
**Prerequisite:** Hobgoblinoid  
**Unavailable to:** Non-hobgoblinoid creatures

**Details:** The creature’s size increases to ogrish proportions, his skin toughens, and his muscles benefit from the primal blood now flowing through them.

**Game Effects:** The creature’s type becomes [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^giant|giant]], and its height increases to 10 feet, making him a [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#Large Characters|Large]] creature. Gains +3 natural armor bonus and a +2 inherent bonus to Strength.
