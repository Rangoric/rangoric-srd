# Focus of the Changeling

One of the [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Prestige Races|prestige races]]; how enchantments of the flesh work, and what they cost, is there.

Changelings are subtle, preferring to blend in and disappear rather than stand out in a crowd. They also use their skills and abilities to cleverly manipulate situations to their favor or bluff their way out of trouble. Those who chose the Focus of the Changeling tend to be quiet individuals who like to operate alone or in small groups using clandestine techniques.

This focus crosses evolutionary steps with the [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Shapeshifter|Focus of the Shapeshifter]]. Standard XP penalties apply if a character advances to the second step in the Focus of the Shapeshifter instead of remaining on the Changeling’s path.

### Bland Creature

**Example:** Bland Elf  
**Cost:** 1,400 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 2nd  
**Prerequisite:** None  
**Unavailable to:** GM specified races

**Details:** A bland creature has developed the ability to more adeptly hide in crowds, appearing as nearly anyone else in their size and general shape. The key to this talent is in making oneself hardly noticeable at all.

**Game Effects:** Gain a +5 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks and a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks.

### Chameleon Creature

**Example:** Chameleon Elf  
**Cost:** 2,500 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 3rd  
**Prerequisite:** Bland  
**Unavailable to:** Non-Bland creatures

**Details:** The Chameleon Creature takes the skills learned as a Bland Creature to new heights with the added ability to subtly shift her physical form.

**Game Effects:** Gain a +3 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks and an additional +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] checks. Gain the spell-like ability *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Disguise Self|disguise self]]* (3.0’s *change self*) for use at will. This ability is not an illusion, but rather a shifting of the physical structure of the creature. If a creature is killed while in the form of someone or something else, they revert to their natural form at death.

### Skinflow Creature

This step is shared with the [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Shapeshifter|Focus of the Shapeshifter]]: see [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Shapeshifter#Skinflow Creature|Skinflow Creature]] there.

### Changeling Creature

**Example:** Changeling Elf  
**Cost:** 6,000 XP  
**Minimum Level:** 6th  
**Prerequisite:** Chameleon and [[rangoric-srd/races/prestige-races/Focus of the Shapeshifter|Skinflow]]  
**Unavailable to:** Non-Skinflow creatures

**Details:** The Changeling Creature has attained mastery over the appearance of his body, and accentuates the physically pleasing characteristics (scent, touch, etc.) to help in certain situations.

**Game Effects:** Gain a +2 bonus to Charisma. The creature also gains a +2 competence bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks.
