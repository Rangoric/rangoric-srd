# Swift as Shadows

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]]. **Replaces**: sure-footed (its Acrobatics and Athletics bonus; the Stealth bonus stays).

Halflings possess incredible stealth even while moving through obstructed areas. Halflings with this racial trait reduce the penalty for using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] while moving by 5, and reduce the Stealth check penalty for sniping by 10.
