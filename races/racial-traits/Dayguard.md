# Dayguard

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Strix|Strix]]. **Replaces**: nocturnal.

Familiar with watching over its tribe during the day, the strix gains a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks and treats Perception as a class skill.
