# Surface Sprinter

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|Ratfolk]]. **Replaces**: darkvision and the base speed of 20 feet.

Some ratfolk spend as much time as they can aboveground and become very fast runners. This comes at the cost of their night vision, which becomes less acute after years of sunlight. These ratfolk gain [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] and have a base speed of 30 feet. This racial trait replaces darkvision and the base speed of 20 feet.
