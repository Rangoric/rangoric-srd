# Shiftless

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]]. **Replaces**: sure-footed (its Acrobatics and Athletics bonus; the Stealth bonus stays).

Halflings have a reputation for larceny and guile—and sometimes it's well deserved. Halflings with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks, and Sleight of Hand is always a class skill for them.
