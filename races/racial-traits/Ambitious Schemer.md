# Ambitious Schemer

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|Drow]]. **Replaces**: keen senses.

Seduction and treachery are tools for advancement in drow society, even for the martially inclined. Drow with this racial trait may choose either [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] as a class skill, and gain a +2 bonus on such skill checks.
