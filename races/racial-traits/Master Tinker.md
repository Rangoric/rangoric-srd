# Master Tinker

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: defensive training and hatred.

Gnomes experiment with all manner of mechanical devices. Gnomes with this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks, and on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] checks to open locks. They are treated as proficient with any weapon they have personally crafted.
