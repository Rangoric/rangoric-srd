# Halo

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|Aasimar]]. **Replaces**: darkvision.

Some aasimars possess the ability to manifest halos. An aasimar with this racial trait can create *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Light|light]]* centered on her head at will as a spell-like ability. When using her halo, she gains a +2 circumstance bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks against evil creatures and on saving throws against becoming [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^blinded|blinded]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^dazzled|dazzled]].
