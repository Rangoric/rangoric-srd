# Rock Stepper

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]. **Replaces**: stonecunning.

Dwarves with this racial trait can skillfully negotiate rocky terrain. They can ignore difficult terrain created by rubble, broken ground, or steep stairs when they take a [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Actions#Take 5-Foot Step|5-foot step]].
