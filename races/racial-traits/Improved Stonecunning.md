# Improved Stonecunning

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]. **Replaces**: stonecunning (it is stonecunning with a bigger bonus).

An earth dwarf's racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Investigation|Investigation]] checks to notice unusual stonework is +4 (instead of +2). Everything else about [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf#^dwarf-stonecunning|stonecunning]] is unchanged.
