# Air Race Traits

**Race**: variant races (see each race's subtypes). **Replaces**: nothing; added to the race.

Races with a strong affinity for the element of air (from the [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Elemental Racial Variants|elemental racial variants]]) have the following traits.

- +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against creatures of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^earth|earth]] subtype, including extraplanar creatures from the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes|Elemental Plane of Earth]].
- -2 penalty on all saving throws against spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural abilities with the earth subtype or used by creatures of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities|earth]]subtype, including extraplanar creatures from the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes|Elemental Plane of Earth]].
- Breathless (Ex): Members of air races do not breathe, so they have immunity to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Drowning|drowning]], [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Suffocation|suffocation]], and attacks that require inhalation (such as some types of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Poison|poison]]).

Used by: the [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]'s air gnome subtype; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]]'s air goblin.
