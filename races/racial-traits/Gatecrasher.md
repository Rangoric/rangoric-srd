# Gatecrasher

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: orc ferocity.

Many half-orcs revel in acts of wanton destruction. Half-orcs with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Strength checks to break objects and on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Sunder|sunder]] combat maneuver checks.
