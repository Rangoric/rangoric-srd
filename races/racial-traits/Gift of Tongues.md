# Gift of Tongues

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: defensive training and hatred.

Gnomes love languages and learning about those they meet. Gnomes with this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks, and they learn one language every time they put a rank in the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] skill (instead of one per 2 ranks).
