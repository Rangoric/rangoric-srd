# Tree Runner

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

In trackless rain forests and marshes, it can be difficult to find dry ground to build on. Goblin tribes living in such areas have learned to live in the treetops. These goblins gain a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb.
