# Emissary

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|Fetchling]]. **Replaces**: shadow blending.

Rare fetchlings excel in the role of emissary between the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Planes#Plane of Shadow|Plane of Shadow]] and the Material Plane. Once per day, such a fetchling can roll twice when making a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] check and take the better roll.
