# Xenophobic

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]. **Replaces**: the dwarf's normal languages.

Isolationist dwarves despise non-dwarven humanoids. They speak only Dwarven and do not gain any bonus languages from possessing a high Intelligence score. In addition, they learn only one language per 4 ranks of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] they possess. However, their untrusting nature gives them a +1 bonus against mind-affecting effects, except for fear effects.
