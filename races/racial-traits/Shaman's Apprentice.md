# Shaman's Apprentice

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: intimidating.

Only the most stalwart survive the years of harsh treatment that an apprenticeship to an orc shaman entails. Half-orcs with this trait gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Endurance|Endurance]] as a bonus feat.
