# Hydrated Vitality

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|Undine]]. **Replaces**: water affinity.

Undines with this racial trait mimic the healing abilities of the mephits, gaining [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] 2 for 1 round anytime they submerge completely within a body of natural salt water, fresh water, or brackish water. Stagnant, poisoned, or trapped water (such as an artificial pit or a *[[rangoric-srd/magic-items/wondrous-items/Bag of Holding|bag of holding]]*) does not activate this ability. Undines can heal up to 2 hit points per level per day with this ability, after which it ceases to function.
