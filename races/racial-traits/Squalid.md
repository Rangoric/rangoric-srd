# Squalid

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc|Orc]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc#^orc-ferocity|ferocity]] (for the half-orc, [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc#^half-orc-orc-ferocity|orc ferocity]]).

Some orcs exist in surroundings so filthy and pestilent that even other orcs would have difficulty living in them. Orcs, and half-orcs, with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws made to resist [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^nauseated|nausea]], the [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickened]] condition, and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Afflictions#Disease|disease]].
