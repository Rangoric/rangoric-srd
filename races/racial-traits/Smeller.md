# Smeller

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc|Orc]]. **Replaces**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Orc#^orc-ferocity|ferocity]] and weapon familiarity.

Orcs with this racial trait gain a limited [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Scent|scent]] ability with half the normal range. Because it's limited, it doesn't let them track by scent.
