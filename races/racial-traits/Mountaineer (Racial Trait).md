# Mountaineer

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Duergar|Duergar]] (through Dwarf Traits). **Replaces**: stability.

Mountain dwarves are skilled at climbing and navigating narrow ledges. Dwarves with this racial trait are immune to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Mountain Travel|altitude sickness]] and do not lose their Dexterity bonus to AC when making [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to cross narrow or slippery surfaces.
