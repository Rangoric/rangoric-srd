# Surface Infiltrator

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|Drow]]. **Replaces**: darkvision and light blindness.

Some drow dwell close to the surface lands, either because they serve drow causes or they were exiled. Drow with this racial trait gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], allowing them to see twice as far as humans in conditions of dim light.
