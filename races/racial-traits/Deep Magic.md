# Deep Magic

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Duergar|Duergar]]. **Replaces**: the enlarge person and invisibility spell-like abilities.

Duergar spellcasters labor long to overcome the inborn spell resistance held by so many of their underground foes. Duergar with this racial trait receive a +2 racial bonus on caster level checks made to overcome [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Spell Resistance|spell resistance]] and a +2 racial bonus on dispel checks.
