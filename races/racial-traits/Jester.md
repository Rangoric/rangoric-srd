# Jester

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|Kobold]]. **Replaces**: crafty.

Some kobolds swallow their pride and survive by groveling, placating, and amusing the powerful. Kobolds with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Performance|Performance]] checks. Persuasion and Performance are always class skills for them.
