# Heart of the Snows

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Humans born in chilly climes treat [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Cold Dangers|cold climates]] as one category less severe. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Fortitude saving throws against the effects of cold climates, on any check or saving throw to avoid slipping and falling, and to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] combat maneuvers. This bonus applies on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks made in slippery conditions.
