# Cave Crawler

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]]. **Replaces**: fast.

Some goblins are born and raised in caves and rarely see the light of day. Goblins with this trait gain a climb speed of 10 feet and the +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb associated with having a climb speed (see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|movement modes]]). Goblins with this racial trait have a base speed of 20 feet and lose the fast racial trait.
