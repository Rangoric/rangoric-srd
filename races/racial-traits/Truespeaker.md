# Truespeaker

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|Aasimar]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

There are some aasimars whose language transcends all boundaries. They gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Linguistics|Linguistics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks, and they learn one language each time they gain a rank in Linguistics (instead of one per 2 ranks).
