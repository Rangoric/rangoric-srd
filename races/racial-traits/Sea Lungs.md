# Sea Lungs

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Changeling|Changeling]] (a hag racial trait, for a changeling whose mother is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hag/Sea Hag|sea hag]]). **Replaces**: nothing.

The changeling may hold her breath for a number of rounds equal to three times her Constitution score before she risks drowning (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#^hold-breath|holding your breath]] for what happens then).
