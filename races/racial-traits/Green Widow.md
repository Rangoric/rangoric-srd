# Green Widow

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Changeling|Changeling]] (a hag racial trait, for a changeling whose mother is a [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hag/Green Hag|green hag]]). **Replaces**: nothing.

The changeling gains a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks against creatures that are attracted to her.
