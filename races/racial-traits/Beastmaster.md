# Beastmaster

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: orc ferocity.

Some half-orcs have a spiritual kinship with fantastical beasts, capturing them for sport or living and hunting with them. A half-orc with this trait treats whips and nets as martial weapons and gains a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks.
