# Sociable

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]. **Replaces**: adaptability.

Half-elves are skilled at charming others and recovering from faux pas. If half-elves with this racial trait attempt to change a creature's attitude with a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] check and fail by 5 or more, they can try to influence the creature a second time even if 24 hours have not passed.
