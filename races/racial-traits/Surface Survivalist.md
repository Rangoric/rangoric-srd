# Surface Survivalist

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]. **Replaces**: darkvision.

Some dwarves have dwelt so long aboveground they have lost their ability to see at night. However, their adaptation to extreme environments allows them to treat [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Winds|wind conditions]] (when determining whether or not they are checked or blown away) and either [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Heat Dangers|hot]] or [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Cold Dangers|cold]] climates (choose one) as one step less severe.
