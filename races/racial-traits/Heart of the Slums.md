# Heart of the Slums

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Humans who eke out a life in a city's teeming slums must be quick and clever. They gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks, and a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks in urban and underground settings. In addition, they may roll twice when saving against disease, taking the better roll.
