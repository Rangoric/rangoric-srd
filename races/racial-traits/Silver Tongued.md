# Silver Tongued

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Humans are often adept at subtle manipulation and putting even sworn foes at ease. Humans with this trait gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks.
