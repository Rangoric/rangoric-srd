# Fleet of Foot

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]]. **Replaces**: slow speed and sure-footed (its Acrobatics and Athletics bonus; the Stealth bonus stays).

Some halflings are quicker than their kin but less cautious. Halflings with this racial trait move at normal speed and have a base speed of 30 feet.
