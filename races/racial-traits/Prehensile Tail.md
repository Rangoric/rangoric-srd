# Prehensile Tail

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|Tiefling]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Vanara|Vanara]] (a base racial trait). **Replaces**: fiendish sorcery for the tiefling; nothing for the vanara.

Many tieflings have tails, but some have long, flexible tails that can be used to carry items, and every vanara has one. While they cannot wield weapons with their tails, they can use them to retrieve small, stowed objects carried on their persons as a swift action.
