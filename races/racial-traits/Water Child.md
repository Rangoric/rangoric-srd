# Water Child

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]. **Replaces**: adaptability.

Some half-elves are born of elves adapted to life on or near the water. These half-elves gain a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim, can always take 10 while swimming, and may choose Aquan as a bonus language.
