# Giant Hunter

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]. **Replaces**: hatred.

Dwarves with this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls against creatures of the giant type. Furthermore, they gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks to find and follow tracks made by giants.
