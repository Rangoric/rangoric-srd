# Wildfire Heart

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|Ifrit]]. **Replaces**: energy resistance.

Ifrits with this trait are as swift and dangerous as a blazing wildfire. They gain a +4 racial bonus on initiative checks.
