# Fire-Starter

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|Ifrit]]. **Replaces**: fire affinity.

Ifrits with this racial trait derive sadistic satisfaction from watching others burn. Anytime the ifrit causes a creature to [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Catching on Fire|catch fire]], he gains a +1 morale bonus on the next single attack roll, saving throw, skill check, or ability check that he makes in the next round. The ifrit only gains this bonus the first time he causes a particular creature to catch fire; subsequent times the creature catches fire provide no bonus.
