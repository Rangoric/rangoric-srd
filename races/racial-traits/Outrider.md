# Outrider

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]]. **Replaces**: sure-footed (its Acrobatics and Athletics bonus; the Stealth bonus stays).

Some halflings specialize in mounted combat. Halflings with this racial trait gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks.
