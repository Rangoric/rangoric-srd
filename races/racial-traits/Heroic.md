# Heroic

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: bonus feat.

Some humans are born heroes. In games that use [[rangoric-srd/unearthed-arcana/Hero Points|hero points]], each time these humans gain a level, they gain 2 hero points instead of 1. If they take the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Blood of Heroes|Blood of Heroes]] feat, they gain 3 hero points each level instead of 2.
