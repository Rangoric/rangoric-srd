# Envoy

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]. **Replaces**: elven magic.

Elves often have trouble relating to neighbors of other races, especially those with much shorter lifespans. As a result, some are trained in minor magics that are particularly useful when dealing with non-elves. Elves with this racial trait and an Intelligence score of 11 or higher gain the following spell-like abilities once per day: *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Comprehend Languages|comprehend languages]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]*. The caster level for these effects is equal to the elf's level.
