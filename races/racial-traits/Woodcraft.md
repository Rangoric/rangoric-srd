# Woodcraft

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]. **Replaces**: elven magic.

Elves know the deep secrets of the wild like no others, especially secrets of the forests. Elves with this racial trait gain a +1 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks. In forest terrain, these bonuses improve to +2.
