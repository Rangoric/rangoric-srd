# Knack with Poison

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: illusion resistance and obsessive.

Some gnomes have an instinctive understanding of poisons and their uses. Gnomes gain a +2 racial bonus on Fortitude saves against poison and a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) checks to make poison. The bonus on Fortitude saving throws against poisons increases to +4 if the gnome accidentally poisons himself when applying or readying the substance.
