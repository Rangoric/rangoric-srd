# Knack with Poison

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: illusion resistance and obsessive.

Some gnomes have an instinctive understanding of poisons and their uses. Gnomes gain a +2 racial bonus on Fortitude Defense against poison and a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Background|Background]] (alchemy) checks to make poison. The bonus on Fortitude Defense against poisons increases to +4 if the gnome accidentally poisons himself when applying or readying the substance.
