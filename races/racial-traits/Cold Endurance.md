# Cold Endurance

**Race**: variant races (see each race's subtypes). **Replaces**: nothing; added to the race.

Members of arctic races have a +4 racial bonus on Fortitude saves made to resist the effects of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Cold Dangers|cold weather or exposure]].

Used by: every arctic race subtype (Unearthed Arcana): the [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]'s arctic dwarf; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]'s arctic elf; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]'s arctic gnome; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]]'s arctic goblin; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]'s arctic half-elf; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]'s arctic half-orc; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]]'s arctic halfling; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|Kobold]]'s arctic kobold.
