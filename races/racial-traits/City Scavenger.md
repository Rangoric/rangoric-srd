# City Scavenger

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Goblins who live within the boundaries of human cities survive by scavenging for refuse and hunting stray animals. Goblins with this trait gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks, and can use Fieldcraft to forage for food while in a city.
