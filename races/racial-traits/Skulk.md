# Skulk

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|Ratfolk]]. **Replaces**: tinker.

Some ratfolk can blend easily into their environments, and move with surprising grace. Ratfolk gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks, and take only a –5 penalty on Stealth checks made to hide from creatures they have distracted with a [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] check (rather than the normal –10 penalty).
