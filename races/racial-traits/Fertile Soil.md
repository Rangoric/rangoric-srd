# Fertile Soil

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|Oread]]. **Replaces**: earth affinity.

Oread [[rangoric-srd/classes/Sorcerer|sorcerers]] with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/bloodlines/Verdant Bloodline|verdant bloodline]] treat their Charisma score as 2 points higher for all sorcerer spells and class abilities. Oread spellcasters with the [[rangoric-srd/class-options/domains/Plant Domain|Plant domain]] use their domain powers and spells at +1 caster level (opened from clerics to any spellcaster with the domain, as Earth Affinity was; decided 2026-11-10).
