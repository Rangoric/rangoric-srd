# Wanderlust

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]]. **Replaces**: fearless and halfling luck.

Halflings love travel and maps. Halflings with this racial trait receive a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks. When casting spells or using abilities that provide or enhance movement, halflings treat their caster level as +1 higher than normal.
