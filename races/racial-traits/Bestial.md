# Bestial

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: orc ferocity.

The orc blood of some half-orcs manifests in the form of particularly prominent orc features, exacerbating their bestial appearances but improving their already keen senses. They gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks.
