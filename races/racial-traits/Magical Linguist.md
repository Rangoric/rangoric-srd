# Magical Linguist

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: gnome magic and illusion resistance.

Gnomes study languages in both their mundane and supernatural manifestations. Gnomes with this racial trait add +1 to the attack bonus of spells they cast with the language-dependent descriptor or those that create glyphs, symbols, or other magical writings. They gain a +2 racial bonus on defenses against such spells. Gnomes with Charisma scores of 11 or higher also gain the following spell-like abilities: 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Arcane Mark|arcane mark]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Comprehend Languages|comprehend languages]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Message|message]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Read Magic|read magic]]_. The caster level for these effects is equal to the gnome's level.
