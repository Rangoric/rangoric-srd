# Spirit of the Waters

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]. **Replaces**: elven magic and weapon familiarity.

Some elves have adapted to life in tune with the sea or along the reedy depths of wild rivers and lakes. They gain a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim, can always take 10 while swimming, and may choose Aquan as a bonus language. They are proficient with longspear, trident, and net.
