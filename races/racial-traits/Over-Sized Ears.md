# Over-Sized Ears

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

While goblins' ears are never dainty, these goblins have freakishly large ears capable of picking up even the smallest sounds. Goblins with this racial trait gain a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks.
