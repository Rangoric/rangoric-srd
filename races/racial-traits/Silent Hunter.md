# Silent Hunter

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]. **Replaces**: elven magic.

Elves are renowned for their subtlety and skill. Elves with this racial trait reduce the penalty for using [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] while moving by 5 and can make Stealth checks while running at a –20 penalty (this number includes the penalty reduction from this racial trait).
