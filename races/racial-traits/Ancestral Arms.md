# Ancestral Arms

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]. **Replaces**: adaptability.

Some half-elves receive training in an unusual weapon. Half-elves with this racial trait receive [[rangoric-srd/feats/Exotic Weapon Proficiency|Exotic Weapon Proficiency]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Martial Weapon Proficiency|Martial Weapon Proficiency]] with one weapon as a bonus feat at 1st level.
