# Battle-Hardened

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|Hobgoblin]]. **Replaces**: sneaky.

Incessant drills make defense second nature to some hobgoblins. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain a +1 bonus to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]].
