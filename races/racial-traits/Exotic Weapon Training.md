# Exotic Weapon Training

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|Tengu]]. **Replaces**: swordtrained.

Instead of swords, some tengus are trained in exotic weaponry. Such tengus choose a number of [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#^eastern-weapons|eastern weapons]] equal to 3 + their Intelligence bonus, and gain proficiency with these weapons.
