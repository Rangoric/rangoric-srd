# Icecunning

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]. **Replaces**: nothing; adds to stonecunning.

Arctic dwarves can apply their [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf#^dwarf-stonecunning|stonecunning]] benefits to structures and natural features made of ice, as well as those made of stone.
