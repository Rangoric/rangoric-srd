# Crystalline Form

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|Oread]]. **Replaces**: earth affinity.

Oreads with this trait gain a +2 racial bonus to AC against [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Rays|rays]] thanks to their reflective crystalline skin. In addition, once per day, they can deflect a single ray attack targeted at them as if they were using the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Deflect Arrows|Deflect Arrows]] feat.
