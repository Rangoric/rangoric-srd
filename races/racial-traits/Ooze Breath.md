# Ooze Breath

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|Undine]]. **Replaces**: spell-like ability.

Some undines' outsider heritage can be traced to ooze mephits. These undines have a [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Breath Weapon|breath weapon]] that is a 5-foot cone of slime usable once per day. The slime deals 1d4 points of acid damage per two character levels (maximum 5d4) and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^sickened|sickens]] creatures in the area for 3 rounds. A successful Reflex saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 the undine's level + the undine's Constitution modifier) halves the damage and negates the sickened effect.
