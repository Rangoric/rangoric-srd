# Ocean's Daughter

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Changeling|Changeling]]. **Replaces**: sea lungs.

The changeling gains a +1 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim. She automatically succeeds at Athletics checks made to avoid nonlethal damage from swimming.
