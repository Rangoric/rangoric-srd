# Unfit

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|Hobgoblin]]. **Replaces**: sneaky.

Hobgoblins who failed to secure a position in the military hold the lowest status in hobgoblin society, and quickly learn the value of currying favor with their betters. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain proficiency in a single martial weapon and +1 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks.
