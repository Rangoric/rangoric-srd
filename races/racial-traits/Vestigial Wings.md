# Vestigial Wings

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|Tiefling]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Some tieflings possess a pair of undersized, withered, or stunted wings like a mockery of those of their fiendish forbearer. Sometimes these wings are leathery, like those of a bat. Other times they are covered with a scattering of black, red, or violet feathers. Rare manifestations can take even more bizarre forms. These wings do not provide the lift required for actual flight, but do have enough power to aid flight attained by some other method, and grant a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|fly]].
