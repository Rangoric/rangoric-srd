# Darkvision

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Merfolk|Merfolk]]. **Replaces**: low-light vision (for the gnome, low-light vision and keen senses; for the half-orc, orc ferocity).

Some members of these races are born with [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] rather than low-light vision, or with sharper darkvision than the rest of their race. Darkvision works the same for every race, but a race can have it at a range other than the usual 60 feet, as the half-orc's acute darkvision reaches 90 feet; each race below gives its range and anything that comes with it.

- **[[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]** (60 feet): Though uncommon, some groups of elves are born with darkvision. In many cases this is taken as a sign of a drow in the elf's ancestry, and can lead to persecution within the elf's home community. These elves also gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Sensitivity|light sensitivity]].
- **[[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]** (60 feet): Some gnome strains have lived in the underground depths for so long they have given up on light entirely. This also replaces the gnome's keen senses.
- **[[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]** (Acute Darkvision, 90 feet): Some half-orcs have exceptionally sharp darkvision, gaining darkvision 90 feet in place of their usual 60 feet. This replaces orc ferocity.
- **[[rangoric-srd/races/Merfolk|Merfolk]]** (60 feet): Some merfolk favor the lightless depths over shallower waters. These merfolk also gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Sensitivity|light sensitivity]].
