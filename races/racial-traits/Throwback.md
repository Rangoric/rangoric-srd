# Throwback

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gillman|Gillman]]. **Replaces**: aquatic, amphibious, the swim speed and water dependent.

Some gillmen are throwbacks to their land-dwelling human ancestors. Gillmen with this racial trait do not have the amphibious trait, have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^human|human subtype]] instead of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^aquatic|aquatic subtype]], have no swim speed or bonuses on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim, cannot breathe water, and do not have the water dependent racial trait.
