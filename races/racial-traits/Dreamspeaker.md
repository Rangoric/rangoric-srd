# Dreamspeaker

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]. **Replaces**: elven immunities.

A few elves have the ability to tap into the power of sleep, dreams, and prescient reverie. Elves with this racial trait add +1 to the saving throw DCs of spells of the divination school and sleep effects they cast. In addition, elves with Charisma scores of 15 or higher may use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dream|dream]]* once per day as a spell-like ability (caster level is equal to the elf's character level).
