# Heat Endurance

**Race**: variant races (see each race's subtypes). **Replaces**: nothing; added to the race.

Members of desert races gain a +4 racial bonus on Fortitude saves made to resist the effects of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Heat Dangers|hot weather]].

Used by: every desert race subtype (Unearthed Arcana): the [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]'s desert dwarf; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]'s desert elf; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]'s desert gnome; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]'s desert half-elf; the [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]'s desert half-orc.
