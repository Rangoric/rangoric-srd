# Blood Enmity

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Duergar|Duergar]]. **Replaces**: the invisibility spell-like ability.

Duergar have long warred against their dwarven cousins and the hated [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]]. Duergar with this racial trait receive a +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against humanoid creatures of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^dwarf|dwarf]] or [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^elf|elf]] subtypes.
