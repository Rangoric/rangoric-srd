# Adoptive Parentage

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: bonus feat.

Humans are sometimes orphaned and adopted by other races. Choose one humanoid race without the human subtype. You start play with that race's languages and gain that race's weapon familiarity racial trait (if any). If the race does not have weapon familiarity, you gain either [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] or [[rangoric-srd/feats/Weapon Focus|Weapon Focus]] as a bonus feat that is appropriate for that race instead.
