# Efreeti Magic

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|Ifrit]]. **Replaces**: spell-like ability.

Some ifrits inherit an efreeti ancestor's ability to magically change a creature's size. They can cast either *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Enlarge Person|enlarge person]]* or *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Reduce Person|reduce person]]* (the ifrit chooses when using this ability) once per day as a spell-like ability (caster level equal to the ifrit's character level). The ifrit can use this ability to affect other ifrits as though they were humanoid creatures.
