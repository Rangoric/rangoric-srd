# Celestial Crusader

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|Aasimar]]. **Replaces**: celestial resistance and skilled.

Some aasimars follow their destiny to war against the powers of ultimate evil. These individuals gain a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls and to AC against evil outsiders and a +2 racial bonus on checks to identify evil outsiders or items or effects created by evil outsiders; they can make these checks untrained.
