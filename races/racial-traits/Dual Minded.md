# Dual Minded

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]. **Replaces**: adaptability.

The mixed ancestry of some half-elves makes them resistant to mental attacks. Half-elves with this racial trait gain a +2 bonus on Will Defense.
