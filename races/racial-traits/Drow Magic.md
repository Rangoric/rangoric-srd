# Drow Magic

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]. **Replaces**: adaptability.

A few half-elves with drow ancestry exhibit the innate magic of that race. Half-elves with this trait have [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]] blood somewhere in their background, and can cast *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Dancing Lights|dancing lights]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Darkness|darkness]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Faerie Fire|faerie fire]]* each once per day, using the half-elf's character level as the caster level for these spell-like abilities.
