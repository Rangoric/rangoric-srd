# Bond to the Land

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: defensive training and hatred.

Some gnomes have strong ties to specific kinds of terrain, as a holdover from their fey origins. These gnomes gain a +2 dodge bonus to AC when in a specific terrain type selected from the [[rangoric-srd/classes/Ranger#^table-ranger-favored-terrains|ranger list of favored terrains]]. This choice is made at character creation, and cannot be changed.
