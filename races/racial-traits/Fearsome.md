# Fearsome

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|Hobgoblin]]. **Replaces**: sneaky.

Some hobgoblins scorn caution and subtlety for swagger and bluster. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]] checks.
