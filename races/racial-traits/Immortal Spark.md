# Immortal Spark

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|Aasimar]]. **Replaces**: skilled and spell-like ability.

Aasimars with this racial trait defy the powers of death. They gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks and saving throws against death effects and can use *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Age Resistance, Lesser|lesser age resistance]]* once per day as a spell-like ability.
