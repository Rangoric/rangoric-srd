# Gliding Wings

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|Kobold]], [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|Tengu]] (as Glide). **Replaces**: crafty for the kobold; gifted linguist for the tengu.

Some kobolds are born with wings that, while too weak for actual flying, do allow them to fall at a very slow and safe pace, and some tengus can use their feathered arms and legs the same way (Glide). A creature with this trait can use them to glide. It can make a DC 15 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] check to fall safely from any height without taking falling damage, as if using *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Feather Fall|feather fall]]*. When falling safely, it may make an additional DC 15 Acrobatics check to glide, moving 5 feet laterally for every 20 feet it falls.
