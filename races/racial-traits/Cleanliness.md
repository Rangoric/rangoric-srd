# Cleanliness

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|Ratfolk]]. **Replaces**: rodent empathy.

Ratfolk strive to keep themselves and each other immaculately clean, to make it difficult for disease to spread. A ratfolk with this racial trait gains a +2 bonus on defenses against disease, and whenever a disease’s attack misses her by 5 or more, she is treated as though it had missed her twice in a row. This racial trait replaces rodent empathy.
