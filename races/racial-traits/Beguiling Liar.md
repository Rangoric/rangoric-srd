# Beguiling Liar

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|Tiefling]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Many tieflings find that the best way to get along in the world is to tell others what they want to hear. These tieflings' practice of telling habitual falsehoods grants them a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks to convince an opponent that what they are saying is true when they tell a lie.
