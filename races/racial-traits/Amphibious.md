# Amphibious

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|Undine]]. **Replaces**: spell-like ability.

Some undines are born with a permanent bond to water. Undines with this racial trait gain the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^aquatic|aquatic subtype]] and [[rangoric-srd/monsters/abilities/Amphibious|amphibious]] special quality.
