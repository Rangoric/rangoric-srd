# Scent

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|Ratfolk]]. **Replaces**: tinker.

Some ratfolk have much more strongly developed senses of smell, instead of keen eyes and ears. These ratfolk have the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^scent|scent]] ability, but take a –2 penalty on all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] checks based primarily on sight or hearing.
