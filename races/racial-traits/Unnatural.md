# Unnatural

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ratfolk|Ratfolk]]. **Replaces**: rodent empathy.

Some ratfolk unnerve normal animals, and train to defend themselves against the inevitable attacks from such creatures. These ratfolk take a –4 penalty on all Charisma-based skill checks to affect creatures of the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^animal|animal]] type, and receive a +2 dodge bonus to AC against animals. An animal's starting attitude toward ratfolk is one step worse than normal.
