# Elemental Insight

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|Ifrit]] (as Fire Insight), [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|Oread]] (as Earth Insight), [[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|Sylph]] (as Air Insight). **Replaces**: the race's elemental affinity (fire affinity for the ifrit, earth affinity for the oread, air affinity for the sylph).

Spellcasters of these races sometimes find that their elemental heritage makes creatures of their element more willing to serve them. The *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Monster I|summon monster]]* and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Summon Nature’s Ally I|summon nature’s ally]]* spells that such a spellcaster casts last 2 rounds longer than normal when used to summon creatures with the subtype of the caster's element:

- **Fire Insight** ([[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|ifrits]]): creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^fire|fire subtype]].
- **Earth Insight** ([[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|oreads]]): creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^earth|earth subtype]].
- **Air Insight** ([[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|sylphs]]): creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^air|air subtype]].
