# Hard Head, Big Teeth

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Goblin|Goblin]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Goblins are known for their balloonlike heads and enormous maws, but some have even more exaggeratedly large heads filled with razor-sharp teeth. Goblins with this trait gain a bite attack as a primary [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^natural-weapons|natural attack]] that deals 1d4 points of damage.
