# City-Raised

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: weapon familiarity.

Half-orcs with this trait know little of their orc ancestry and were raised among humans and other half-orcs in a large city. City-raised half-orcs are proficient with whips and longswords, and receive a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks.
