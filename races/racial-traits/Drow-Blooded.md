# Drow-Blooded

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]. **Replaces**: low-light vision.

Some half-elves born of [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|drow]] parents exhibit more drow traits than others—particularly many of the physical features of the drow—and have [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] 60 feet and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Light Blindness|light blindness]].
