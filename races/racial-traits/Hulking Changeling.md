# Hulking Changeling

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Changeling|Changeling]] (a hag racial trait, for a changeling whose mother is an [[rangoric-srd/monsters/hag/Annis|annis]]). **Replaces**: nothing.

The changeling gains a +1 racial bonus on melee damage.
