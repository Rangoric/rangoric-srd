# Scion of Humanity

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|Aasimar]]. **Replaces**: the Celestial language, and alters the native subtype.

Some aasimars' heavenly ancestry is extremely distant. An aasimar with this racial trait counts as an outsider ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^native|native]]) and a humanoid ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^human|human]]) for any effect related to race, including feat prerequisites and spells that affect humanoids. She can pass for human without using the [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]] skill.
