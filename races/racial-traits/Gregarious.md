# Gregarious

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Kitsune|Kitsune]]. **Replaces**: agile.

Even among your own kind, your gift for making friends stands out. Whenever you successfully use [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] to win over an individual, that creature takes a –2 penalty to resist any of your [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Charisma-based|Charisma-based skill]] checks for the next 24 hours.
