# Arcane Training

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]. **Replaces**: adaptability.

Half-elves occasionally seek tutoring to help them master the magic in their blood. Half-elves with this racial trait choose one arcane spellcasting class at 1st level. They can use [[rangoric-srd/magic-items/Magic Item Basics#Using Items|spell trigger and spell completion]] items for that class as if one level higher (or as a 1st-level character if they have no levels in that class).
