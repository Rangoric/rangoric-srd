# Cat's Claws

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|Catfolk]]. **Replaces**: natural hunter.

Some catfolk have stronger and more developed claws than other members of their race, and can use them to make attacks. Catfolk with this racial trait have a pair of claws they can use as [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^natural-weapons|natural weapons]]. These claws are primary attacks that deal 1d4 points of damage.
