# Explorer

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: hatred and obsessive.

Many gnomes are obsessed with seeing as much of the world as possible, rather than perfecting some specific talent or vocation. These gnomes gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb and checks for one [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skill]] of their choice.
