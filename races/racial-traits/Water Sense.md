# Water Sense

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|Undine]]. **Replaces**: energy resistance.

Undines with this racial trait can sense vibrations in water, granting them [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Blindsight and Blindsense|blindsense]] 30 feet against creatures in contact with the same body of water.
