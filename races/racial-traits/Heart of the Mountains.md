# Heart of the Mountains

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Humans born in the mountains are skilled at negotiating heights and precipices. They gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] checks to move on narrow surfaces and uneven ground. Furthermore, they are considered acclimated to the effects of [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Mountain Travel|high altitude]].
