# Eye for Talent

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: bonus feat.

Humans have great intuition for hidden potential. They gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks. In addition, when they acquire an animal companion, bonded mount, cohort, or familiar, that creature gains a +2 bonus to one ability score of the character's choice.
