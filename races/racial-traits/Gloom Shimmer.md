# Gloom Shimmer

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|Fetchling]]. **Modifies**: spell-like abilities.

Some fetchlings can manipulate shadowy energy in order to displace their location instead of transporting between shadows. Upon reaching 9th level, instead of gaining _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Shadow Walk|shadow walk]]_ as a spell-like ability, these fetchlings gain _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Displacement|displacement]]_ as a spell-like ability usable twice per day (caster level equal to the fetchling's character level).
