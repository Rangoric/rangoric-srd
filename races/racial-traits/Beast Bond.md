# Beast Bond

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Kobold|Kobold]]. **Replaces**: crafty.

Some kobolds have a talent for training animals and beasts to help them both on and off the battlefield. Kobolds with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks. Animal Handling is always a class skill for them.
