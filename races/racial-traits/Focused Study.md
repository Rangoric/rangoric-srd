# Focused Study

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: bonus feat.

All humans are skillful, but some, rather than being generalists, tend to specialize in a handful of skills. At 1st, 8th, and 16th level, such humans gain [[rangoric-srd/feats/Skill Focus|Skill Focus]] in a skill of their choice as a bonus feat.
