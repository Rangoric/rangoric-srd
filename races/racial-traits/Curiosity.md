# Curiosity

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|Catfolk]]. **Replaces**: natural hunter.

Catfolk are naturally inquisitive about the world around them, though some are more curious than others. Such catfolk gain a +4 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks to gather information, and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] is always a class skill for them. If they choose a class that has Society as a class skill, they gain a +2 racial bonus on Society checks instead.
