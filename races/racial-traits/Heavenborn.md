# Heavenborn

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Aasimar|Aasimar]]. **Replaces**: skilled and spell-like ability.

Born in the celestial realms, aasimars with this racial trait gain a +2 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks and they cast spells with the good or light descriptor at +1 caster level.
