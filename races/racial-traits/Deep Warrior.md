# Deep Warrior

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]. **Replaces**: defensive training.

Dwarves with this racial trait grew up facing the abominations that live deep beneath the surface. They receive a +2 dodge bonus to AC against monsters of the aberration type and a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Bonus|combat maneuver checks]] made to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Grapple|grapple]] such creatures (or to continue a grapple).
