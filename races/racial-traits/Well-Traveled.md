# Well-Traveled

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: nothing; added in the aquatic gnome subtype.

Aquatic gnomes receive a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks. They always know when their welcome is wearing out.
