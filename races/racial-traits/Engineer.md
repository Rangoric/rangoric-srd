# Engineer

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|Hobgoblin]]. **Replaces**: sneaky.

Hobgoblin engineers tinker endlessly with fire, explosives, and the engines of war. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] (alchemy) and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Engineering|Engineering]] checks.
