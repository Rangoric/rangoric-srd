# Climber

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|Catfolk]]. **Replaces**: sprinter.

Catfolk hunters excel at hunting prey from trees and other high vantage points. Catfolk with this racial trait possess a climb speed of 20 feet (along with the +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb a climb speed affords; see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|movement modes]]).
