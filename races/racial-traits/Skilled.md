# Skilled

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: darkvision.

Second- and third-generation half-orcs often favor their human heritage more than their orc heritage. Half-orcs with this trait gain 1 additional [[rangoric-srd/skills/Skills Overview#^skill-points|skill point]] per level.
