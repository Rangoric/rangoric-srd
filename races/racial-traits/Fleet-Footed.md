# Fleet-Footed

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]. **Replaces**: keen senses and weapon familiarity.

While all elves are naturally lithe and agile, some also are naturally speedy and have a strong desire to rush into situations rather than worrying about looking ahead. Elves with this racial trait receive [[rangoric-srd/feats/Run|Run]] as a bonus feat and a +2 racial bonus on initiative checks.
