# Poison Use

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Drow|Drow]] (a base racial trait), [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]] (in the jungle halfling subtype). **Replaces**: nothing.

Drow are skilled in the use of poison and never risk accidentally poisoning themselves. Jungle halflings use poison extensively when hunting, and as a result never poison themselves when applying poison to a weapon or when using a poisoned weapon in combat. Jungle halflings make extensive use of poisons harvested from vermin, particularly those that damage Dexterity scores.
