# Poison Use

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]]. **Replaces**: nothing; added in the jungle halfling subtype.

Jungle halflings use poison extensively when hunting, and as a result never poison themselves when applying poison to a weapon or when using a poisoned weapon in combat. Jungle halflings make extensive use of poisons harvested from vermin, particularly those that damage Dexterity scores.
