# Clever Cat

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|Catfolk]]. **Replaces**: natural hunter.

Catfolk's generally friendly disposition doesn't preclude craftiness. Some of them see social obstacles as games to be played and won. These catfolk receive a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks.
