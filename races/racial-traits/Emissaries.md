# Emissaries

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: nothing; added in the aquatic half-orc subtype.

As emissaries for their tribes, aquatic half-orcs receive a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks. They aren't the best diplomats under the waves, but they're a far sight better than their orc cousins.
