# Bandy-Legged

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|Hobgoblin]]. **Replaces**: normal speed.

Bandy-legged hobgoblins have an even more exaggerated stature than other hobgoblins, with bowed legs and massive shoulders. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Animal Handling|Animal Handling]] checks to ride, and a +2 racial bonus to their [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Combat Maneuver Defense|CMD]] against [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Bull Rush|bull rush]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] attempts while on solid ground. The base speed of bandy-legged hobgoblins is reduced to 20 feet.
