# Integrated

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Elf|Half-Elf]]. **Replaces**: adaptability.

Many half-elves are skilled in the art of ingratiating themselves into a community as if they were natives. Half-elves with this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]], [[rangoric-srd/skills/Imitation|Imitation]], and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks.
