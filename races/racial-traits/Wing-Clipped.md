# Wing-Clipped

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Strix|Strix]]. **Modifies**: the fly speed.

The flight of wing-clipped strix is weaker than normal, whether from deformity or injury. Their fly speed is 20 feet (poor) instead of the normal fly speed, and they must make a DC 30 [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|Acrobatics]] check to fly upward. Ostracized by their tribes and forced to deal with other races, these strix compensate for their weakness by gaining a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Deception|Deception]] checks, [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb, and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks.
