# Forge-Hardened

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|Ifrit]]. **Replaces**: spell-like ability.

Not all ifrits are descended from efreet—some instead descend from azers or even salamanders. Such ifrits gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] checks to make armor and weapons and on saves to resist [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^fatigued|fatigue]] and [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#^exhausted|exhaustion]].
