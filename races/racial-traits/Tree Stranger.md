# Tree Stranger

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Vanara|Vanara]]. **Replaces**: the climb speed.

Some vanaras have spent their lives among humans in centers of learning instead of traditional treetop homes. These vanaras treat all [[rangoric-srd/skills/Knowledge|Knowledge skills]] (the _knowledge_ tag) as class skills, but lose their climb speed.
