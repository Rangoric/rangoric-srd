# Urbanite

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]. **Replaces**: keen senses.

Elves who live in cities for more than a century can grow to know the ebb and flow of social situations just as their forest-dwelling cousins know the rules of the wild. Elves with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] checks made to gather information and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks made to get a hunch about a social situation.
