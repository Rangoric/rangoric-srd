# Pit Boss

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|Hobgoblin]]. **Replaces**: sneaky.

Slave blood fuels the hobgoblin war machine. Pit bosses extract the last breath of labor from their charges with a liberal touch of the lash. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain proficiency with whips and a +1 racial bonus on combat maneuver checks made to [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Disarm|disarm]] or [[rangoric-srd/combat/Combat Maneuvers#Trip|trip]] with a whip.
