# Lorekeeper

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Dwarf|Dwarf]]. **Replaces**: greed.

Dwarves keep extensive records about their history and the world around them. Dwarves with this racial trait receive a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks that pertain to dwarves or their enemies. They can make such skill checks untrained.
