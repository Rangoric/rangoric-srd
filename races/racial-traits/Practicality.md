# Practicality

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Halfling|Halfling]]. **Replaces**: fearless and sure-footed (its Acrobatics and Athletics bonus; the Stealth bonus stays).

Halflings value hard work and common sense. Halflings with this racial trait gain a +2 bonus on any one [[rangoric-srd/skills/Craft|Craft]] or [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] skill, as well as on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Sense Motive|Sense Motive]] checks and saves against illusions.
