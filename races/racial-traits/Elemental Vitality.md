# Elemental Vitality

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|Ifrit]] (as Fire in the Blood), [[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|Oread]] (as Stone in the Blood), [[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|Sylph]] (as Storm in the Blood). **Replaces**: the race's elemental affinity (fire affinity for the ifrit, earth affinity for the oread, air affinity for the sylph).

Members of these races with this racial trait mimic the healing abilities of the mephits, gaining [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Fast Healing|fast healing]] 2 for 1 round when their element touches them, as each version says. They can heal up to 2 hit points per level per day with this ability, after which it ceases to function.

- **Fire in the Blood** ([[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|ifrits]]): anytime the ifrit takes fire damage (whether or not this fire damage gets through the ifrit's fire resistance).
- **Stone in the Blood** ([[rangoric-srd/races/Oread|oreads]]): anytime the oread is subject to acid damage (the acid damage does not need to overcome the oread's resistances or immunities to activate this ability).
- **Storm in the Blood** ([[rangoric-srd/races/Sylph|sylphs]]): anytime the sylph takes electricity damage (whether or not this electricity damage gets through the sylph's electricity resistance).
