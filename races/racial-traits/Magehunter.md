# Magehunter

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Hobgoblin|Hobgoblin]]. **Replaces**: sneaky.

Hobgoblins hate and fear arcane casters. A magehunter gains a +2 racial bonus on checks made to [[rangoric-srd/skills/Identifying Magic|identify a spell being cast]] and a +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against arcane spellcasters. He only gains this bonus against creatures that use spells, and not against those that only use spell-like abilities.
