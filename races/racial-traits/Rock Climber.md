# Rock Climber

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: intimidating.

Half-orcs from mountainous regions are excellent climbers, and sometimes ambush prey by leaping down from above. Half-orcs with this racial trait gain a +1 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb.
