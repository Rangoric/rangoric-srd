# Deepsight

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Undine|Undine]]. **Replaces**: darkvision.

The eyes of some undines are especially adapted to the lightless depths of the oceans, but not to air-filled environments. An undine with this racial trait has [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] 120 feet when underwater, but otherwise has no darkvision at all.
