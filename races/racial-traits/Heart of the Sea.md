# Heart of the Sea

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Human|Human]]. **Replaces**: skilled.

Humans born near the sea are always drawn to it. They gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Profession|Profession]] (sailor) checks and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim, and Profession and Athletics are always class skills for them. They can hold their breath twice as long as normal, and spellcasters gain a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/rules/Concentration (Check)|concentration checks]] when attempting to cast spells underwater.
