# Desert Mirage

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Ifrit|Ifrit]]. **Replaces**: fire affinity.

Ifrits thrive in the deserts of the world, where their keen instincts and resistance to heat give them a huge edge over their competitors. Those with this trait gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks in [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Desert Terrain|desert]] environments and on saves to resist [[rangoric-srd/rules/Wilderness, Weather, and Environment#Starvation and Thirst|starvation and thirst]].
