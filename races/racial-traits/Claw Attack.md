# Claw Attack

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tengu|Tengu]]. **Replaces**: swordtrained.

Tengus with this racial trait have learned to use their claws as natural weapons. They gain two claw attacks as primary [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^natural-weapons|natural attacks]] that deal 1d3 points of damage, and are treated as having the [[rangoric-srd/feats/Improved Unarmed Strike|Improved Unarmed Strike]] feat for the purpose of qualifying for other feats.
