# Cavewight

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Orc|Half-Orc]]. **Replaces**: intimidating.

Some half-orcs live far below the surface, seeking freedom in winding cave complexes. Half-orcs with this racial trait gain a +1 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Dungeoneering|Dungeoneering]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Fieldcraft|Fieldcraft]] checks made underground.
