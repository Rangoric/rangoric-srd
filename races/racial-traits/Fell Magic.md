# Fell Magic

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: gnome magic.

Gnomes add +1 to the DC of any saving throws against necromancy spells that they cast. Gnomes with Wisdom scores of 11 or higher also gain the following spell-like abilities: 1/day—*[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Touch|chill touch]]*, *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]*, and *[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Fatigue|touch of fatigue]]*. The caster level for these effects is equal to the gnome's level. The DC for these spells is equal to 10 + the spell's level + the gnome's Wisdom modifier.
