# Fell Magic

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Gnome|Gnome]]. **Replaces**: gnome magic.

Gnomes add +1 to the attack bonus of necromancy spells that they cast. Gnomes with Wisdom scores of 11 or higher also gain the following spell-like abilities: 1/day—_[[rangoric-srd/spells/Bleed|bleed]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Chill Touch|chill touch]]_, _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Detect Poison|detect poison]]_, and _[[rangoric-srd/spells/Touch of Fatigue|touch of fatigue]]_. The caster level for these effects is equal to the gnome's level. The attack bonus of these spells is the spell's level + the gnome's Wisdom modifier.
