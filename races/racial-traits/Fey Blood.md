# Fey Blood

**Race**: the fey races (a base racial trait). **Replaces**: nothing.

Born of a supernatural world where distances are fluid and based on misdirection or force of will, creatures with fey blood are resistant to illusion and enchantment spells. They have a racial bonus on saving throws against illusion and enchantment spells and spell-like abilities. The race note gives the starting bonus and how often it increases by +1 as the character gains levels.

Used by: the [[rangoric-srd/races/Bogie|Bogie]] (+1, increasing every three levels); the [[rangoric-srd/races/Deep Fey|Deep Fey]] (+2, increasing every four levels); the [[rangoric-srd/races/Faerie|Faerie]] (+1, increasing every four levels); the [[rangoric-srd/races/Feeorin|Feeorin]] (+2, increasing every two levels); the [[rangoric-srd/races/Half-Fey|Half-Fey]] (+1, increasing every four levels); the [[rangoric-srd/races/Nymph|Nymph]] (+2, increasing every two levels); the [[rangoric-srd/races/Scath|Scath]] (+1, increasing every four levels); the [[rangoric-srd/races/Sprite|Sprite]] (+1, increasing every four levels).
