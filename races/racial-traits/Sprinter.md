# Sprinter

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Catfolk|Catfolk]] (a base racial trait), [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|Tiefling]] (as Fiendish Sprinter). **Replaces**: nothing for the catfolk; skilled for the tiefling.

Catfolk, and tieflings with this trait (Fiendish Sprinter), gain a 10-foot racial bonus to their speed when using the charge, run, or withdraw actions. Such tieflings have feet that are more bestial than human, resembling those of a clawed predator or the cloven hooves common to many of their kind.
