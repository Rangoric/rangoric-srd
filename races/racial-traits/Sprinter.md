# Sprinter

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Tiefling|Tiefling]] (as Fiendish Sprinter). **Replaces**: skilled.

Some tieflings have feet that are more bestial than human. Whether their feet resemble those of a clawed predator or are the cloven hooves common to many of their kind, tieflings with this trait (Fiendish Sprinter) gain a 10-foot racial bonus to their speed when using the charge, run, or withdraw actions.
