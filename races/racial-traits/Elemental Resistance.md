# Elemental Resistance

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Elf|Elf]]. **Replaces**: elven immunities.

Elves who dwell in the most extreme environments, from arctic wastelands to volcanic plains, develop natural resistance to the dangers of their homelands over the course of a few generations. Elves with this racial trait gain [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Resistance to Energy|resistance]] 5 to acid, cold, electricity, or fire. This choice is made at character creation, and once made it cannot be changed.
