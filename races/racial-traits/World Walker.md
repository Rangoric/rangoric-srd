# World Walker

**Race**: [[rangoric-srd/races/Fetchling|Fetchling]]. **Modifies**: skilled.

Fetchlings who have spent most of their lives on the Material Plane can become more acclimated to their new environments. Instead of gaining a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Theology|Theology]] checks, these fetchlings gain a +1 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Nature|Nature]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Society|Society]] checks.
