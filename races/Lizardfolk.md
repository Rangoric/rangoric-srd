## Lizardfolk

Type: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^reptilian|reptilian]])
Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft, swim 30ft
Perks:

- +2 Strength
- +2 Constitution
- Natural Armor: Lizardfolk have tough scaly skin, granting them a +1 natural armor bonus.
- Swim Speed: Lizardfolk have a swim speed of 30 feet (along with the +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to swim a swim speed affords; see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|movement modes]]).
- Natural Weapons: Lizardfolk have a bite attack that deals 1d3 points of damage, a primary attack, or a secondary attack if the lizardfolk is wielding manufactured weapons, and two claw attacks that deal 1d4 points of damage, which are primary natural attacks (see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^natural-weapons|natural weapons]]).

Languages:

- Draconic

Bonus Languages:

- Aquan
- Common
- Gnoll
- Goblin
- Orc

Level Adjustment: +1 (see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monsters as Races#^level-adjustment|level adjustment]])

### Description

The Advanced Race Guide builds the lizardfolk as an example race and describes it no further; what lizardfolk are like, and their size, is in the monster [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Lizardfolk|Lizardfolk]] entry.
