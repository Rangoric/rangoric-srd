## Ogres

Type: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^giant-subtype|giant]])
Size: [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#Large Characters|Large]]
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- +4 Strength
- +2 Constitution
- -2 Intelligence
- -2 Charisma
- Natural Armor: Ogres have a +3 natural armor bonus.
- Reach: Ogres have a reach of 10 feet.
- Senses: Ogres can see in the dark up to 60 feet (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]]) and have [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]].

Languages:

- Giant

Bonus Languages:

- Common
- Dwarven
- Goblin
- Orc
- Terran

Level Adjustment: +2 (see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monsters as Races#^level-adjustment|level adjustment]])

### Description

The Advanced Race Guide builds the ogre as an example race and describes it no further; what ogres are like, and their size, is in the monster [[rangoric-srd/monsters/ogre/Ogre|Ogre]] entry.
