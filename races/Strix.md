## Strix

Type: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^strix|strix]])
Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft, fly 60ft (average)
Perks:

- +2 Dexterity
- -2 Charisma
- Fly Speed: Strix have a fly speed of 60 feet (average maneuverability), which works as described under [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|movement modes]], with flying as a use of [[rangoric-srd/skills/Acrobatics#Flying|Acrobatics]].
- Senses: Strix have [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] and can see in the dark up to 60 feet (see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]]).
- Hatred: Strix receive a +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against humanoid creatures with the [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^human|human subtype]] because of their special training against these hated foes.
- Nocturnal: Strix gain a +2 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Perception|Perception]] and [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks in dim light or darkness.
- Suspicious: Strix receive a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against illusion spells and effects.

Languages:

- Strix

### Description

Human neighbors tell horrific tales of slaughter woven with frightened suspicion when speaking of strix. Strix, however, tell a tale of encroachment and a struggle for land and resources. For ages, humans invaded strix lands fighting bloody battles against the fierce, black-skinned creatures they thought to be winged devils. Over time, strix have developed a hatred for humankind and now fiercely protect their dwindling numbers.
