## Gnolls

Type: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^gnoll|gnoll]])
Size: Medium
Base Speed: 30ft
Perks:

- +2 Strength
- +2 Constitution
- Natural Armor: Gnolls have a +1 natural armor bonus.
- Darkvision: Gnolls can see in the dark up to 60 feet; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] for how it works.

Languages:

- Gnoll

Bonus Languages:

- Common
- Draconic
- Elven
- Goblin
- Orc

Level Adjustment: +1 (see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monsters as Races#^level-adjustment|level adjustment]])

### Description

The Advanced Race Guide builds the gnoll as an example race and describes it no further; what gnolls are like, and their size, is in the monster [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Gnoll|Gnoll]] entry.
