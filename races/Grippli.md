## Gripplis

Type: [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^humanoid|Humanoid]] ([[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^grippli|grippli]])
Size: [[rangoric-srd/races/PC Races#Small Characters|Small]]
Base Speed: 30ft, climb 20ft
Perks:

- +2 Dexterity
- +2 Wisdom
- -2 Strength
- Climb Speed: Gripplis have a climb speed of 20 feet (along with the +8 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Athletics|Athletics]] checks to climb a climb speed affords; see [[rangoric-srd/monsters/Monster Types, Sybtypes, and Special Abilities#^movement-modes|movement modes]]).
- Darkvision: Gripplis can see in the dark up to 60 feet; see [[rangoric-srd/rules/Special Abilities and Conditions#Darkvision|darkvision]] for how it works.
- Camouflage: Gripplis receive a +4 racial bonus on [[rangoric-srd/skills/Stealth|Stealth]] checks in marshes and forested areas.
- Swamp Stride: A grippli can move through difficult terrain at its normal speed while within a swamp. Magically altered terrain affects a grippli normally.
- Weapon Familiarity: Gripplis are proficient with [[rangoric-srd/equipment/Equipment Weapons#Weapon Descriptions|nets]].

Languages:

- Common
- Grippli

Bonus Languages:

- Boggard
- Draconic
- Elven
- Gnome
- Goblin
- Sylvan

### Description

Gripplis stand just over 2 feet tall and have mottled green-and-brown skin. Most gripplis are primitive hunter gatherers, living on large insects and fish found near their treetop homes, and are unconcerned about events outside their swamps. The rare grippli who leaves the safety of the swamp tends to be a ranger or alchemist seeking to trade for metals and gems.
